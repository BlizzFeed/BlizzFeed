## Season 35 Battle Pass: Traveling Blade

[![Video](https://markdown-videos-api.jorgenkh.no/youtube/F3dBUsgM04k?width=1280&height=720)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F3dBUsgM04k)

Most scurry to obey the Great Families’ commandments. A rare few answer them with steel. The Season 35 Battle Pass seeps into Sanctuary on January 16, at 3 a.m. server time, consisting of 40 ranks worth of rewards, such as Crests, Hilts, Legendary Gems, and more.

Begin slaying demons and climbing your way through the ranks today before the Great Families seek retribution on February 13, 2:59 a.m. server time.

#### Reactivate Your Ephemeral Treasures with the Collector's Empowered Battle Pass

Cosmetics lost to time have reemerged in a new aspect of the Collector’s Empowered Battle Pass. Upon activating your Collector’s Empowered Battle Pass, you can choose two Ephemeral Treasures you already own to reactivate, meaning they can once again be displayed proudly. Each consecutive activation of a Collector’s Empowered Battle Pass will score you the ability to reactivate an additional Ephemeral Treasure, up to 5 at a time.

## Feature Updates

#### Modify Your Legendary Gem Drop Table

We’re cutting down the Standard drop table of Legendary Gems to 16 for Five Star and 15 for Two and One Star to improve the drop chances for the most popular and recent Legendary Gems. From now on, you can select the seventeenth Legendary Gem to complete your One, Two, and Five-Star Legendary Gem drop tables.

Additionally, the following gems will be available per quality:

Five-Star: Blood-Soaked Jade, Bottled Hope, Seeping Bile, Starfire Shard. Roiling Consequence, Gloom Cask, Echoing Shade, Void Spark, Frozen Heart, Spiteful Blood, Blessing of the Worthy, Stormvault, Maw of the Deep, Wulfheort, Hilt of Many Realms, Golden Firmament.

Two-Star: Lightning Core, Mother's Lament, Power & Command, Pain Clasp, Viper's Bite, The Hunger, Bloody Reach, Igneous Scorn, Volatility Shard, Cold Confidant, The Abiding Curse, Tear of the Comet, Kir Sling, Ironbane, Mossthorn.

One-Star:The Black Rose, Ca'arsen's Invigoration, Berserker's Eye, Everlasting Torment, Freedom and Devotion, Heartstone, Misery Elixir, Trickshot Gem, Pain of Subjugation, Lucent Watcher, Defiant Soul, Blessed Pebble, Respite Stone, Entropic Well, Mocking Laughter.

#### Challenge Dungeons

Challenge Dungeons are receiving a handful of changes to strengthen their gameplay loop.

- Sacrificial Power: Reduced life cost from 10% to 8%.
- Conjurer: Reduced duration from 12 to 6 seconds, increased doppelganger damage.
- You can no longer follow other players while inside Challenge Dungeons.

#### Spectral Allies

We’ve adjusted the way in which you utilize Spectral Allies, making them more intuitive.

#### Elite Quests

Elite Quests can now be replayed any time after you’ve completed them. Visit Taite in Westmarch to relive climactic moments from your past.

#### Inventory Stack Increase

Wildfire, Tremor, Jolt, Vengeance, Barrier, and Ravager Stones can now be stacked to 999 instead of 99 in your inventory.

Additionally, Voidblood Slag can now be stacked to 9999 instead of 999 in your inventory.

#### Battleground

Assist scoring has been changed to include instances where a teammate benefits from buffs through active skills and kills within the buff’s duration.

#### Dungeons

Those in a party who exit a Dungeon (Challenge Dungeons included) within a minute of starting it will be unable to join a new party for a short period.

The waiting period for checkpoints within certain Dungeons has been reduced from 15 to 8 seconds.

## Returning Events

#### Conqueror

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/PZ9SD0C0PHD91719013961764.jpg)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/PZ9SD0C0PHD91719013961764.jpg)

An ancient fighting pit where the Fahiran aristocracy held brutal games has been uncovered in Shassar. Fight tooth and nail **January 10, 3 a.m.–January 17, 3 a.m. server time** during the Conqueror limited time PvP event to bring honor to your name and riches to your collection.

Horadric Scholars have poured over their tomes to discover the origin of increased materialized wealth, while the merchants of Sanctuary have seized the opportunity to increase their abundance. The source: Winds of Fortune.

#### Survivor's Bane

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/Q98LDMU7BIQN1714945677293.jpg)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/Q98LDMU7BIQN1714945677293.jpg)

In Survivor’s Bane, you’ll begin each run without your character’s items and a random Skill from their Class. From **January 10, 3 a.m.–January 17, 3 a.m. server time** you can vanquish demons across three levels in succession—Shassar Sea, Ashwold Cemetery, and Frozen Tundra—to earn rewards and achievements.

As you move throughout the map, any Skills you have equipped will be automatically cast for you. Smiting Hell’s minions will grant you useful experience orbs that level up your character. Each time you level up, you’ll be presented with the option to equip a new Skill, up to six total can be equipped—the Skill you’re presented with can be from any Class regardless of your character’s Class—or to upgrade an equipped Skill.

#### Hungering Moon

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/J9TVDCM48DDI1711147235277.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/J9TVDCM48DDI1711147235277.png)

The moon, underfed, demands sustenance...providing you with an upcoming opportunity to sate its seemingly endless appetite for blood!

The moonlit path you’ll take to heed your lunar overlord’s howl is ready to be travelled. Players can complete up to three tasks a day between **January 11, 3 a.m.–January 18, 2:59 a.m. server time.**

#### Winds of Fortune Limited-time Event

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/5n/5N0IAO2QYZOL1723854257487.jpg)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/5n/5N0IAO2QYZOL1723854257487.jpg)

Horadric Scholars have poured over their tomes to discover the origin of increased materialized wealth, while the merchants of Sanctuary have seized the opportunity to increase their abundance. The source: Winds of Fortune.

From **January 15, 3:00 a.m.—January 22 3:00 a.m. local server time**, you will earn increased rewards!

While Winds of Fortune is live, activate your 24-hour buff to receive this duplicate effect. Once activated, the buff’s duration is in real time and will end 24 hours after it’s been initiated. If you have not activated the buff within 24 hours of the event ending, it will be enabled automatically. Any rewards not claimed before the end of the event will be auto-claimed on next log-in.

During Winds of Fortune, the 4-player party Normal Gems bonus drop will be doubled and unaffected by the daily cap, and only the first twelve Common Gems of the day will be tradable.

Any of these items will drop in duplicate quantities:

- Gold
- Experience
- Battle Pass Points
- Normal Gems
- Legendary Items
- Items

Items from these activities will also drop in duplicate quantities:

- Horadric Bestiary
- Challenge Rifts
- Bounties
- Fishing
- Dungeons
- Purge the Depths
- Accursed Towers
- Hidden Lairs
- Farming in the wilderness
- Codex Activities

Note that during this limited-time event, Battle Pass rewards will not be doubled, and the weekly limit on Battle Pass Points and Normal Gems will not change. Any bonus Experience gained will be affected by your current modifier.

Additionally, there are limits to each item that can be earned in duplicate quantities. You’ll earn double the amount until you reach the limit. Please see the event in-game for more details.

#### Wild Brawl

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/gallery/y7/Y7HEKDQ5TVN11735942488905.jpg)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/gallery/y7/Y7HEKDQ5TVN11735942488905.jpg)

Enlist your most trusted companions and outlast enemy teams in the Wild Brawl event. In this unique, limited-time PVP event from **January 17, 3 a.m.–January 24, 2024, 3 a.m. server time**, players start at Level 1, work together in teams of 6 to explore an expansive map, and endure to become the last surviving team. Equip discovered gear, gain experience to strengthen your power, and slay everything in sight to win the battle. This arena-style game mode ends when one team has outlasted the others to make their eternal mark on Sanctuary.

#### Shady Stock

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/gallery/k9/K9MQXLQRTDB91690170190995.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/gallery/k9/K9MQXLQRTDB91690170190995.png)

Yakin has once again come into possession of some liberated merchandise, likely plucked from a far-away excavation. His Shady Stock is in high demand and will be available to peruse from **January 18, 3 a.m.–January 25, 3 a.m. server time**, or until Westmarch’s guards raid his supply.
