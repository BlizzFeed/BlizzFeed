## Cast your Reel in Fisher’s Frenzy

Travel to the where the sunsets linger for an eternity, and the water teems so thick with fish one could walk across them. From **July 10−July 24**¸ voyage to the Shimmering Tropic for the kind of fishing most adventurers only dream of in **Fisher’s Frenzy**. Beware while casting in the Shimmering Tropic, your line can conjure all sorts of unexpected oddities from the murky waters below.

Local Fisherman have spoken of their fishing attracting groups of monsters to their presence-one swore they even saw a Treasure Goblin emerge from beneath. Seafaring folk are known to spin a tall tale, but various accounts tell of attacks from a large Sea Monster and mayhaps tantalizing treasure Chests emerging for the lucky angler.

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/0BP40OCLXRO21720216706657.jpg)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/0BP40OCLXRO21720216706657.jpg)

While you’re waiting for the catch of your life, keep a keen eye on your Fishing Rod. Perhaps you’ll tug on your line to speed up the progress of your catch, hopefully landing an absolute beast on your hook.

Casting your line in this limited-time event has the chance to earn you a Lost Jade Fishing Rod, a mythical piece of unique angler gear from days of old. Compete amongst the Leaderboard to see who can land the biggest fish. Complete tasks within the event to earn Legendary Items, Set Items, Gold, and more. Note that these Fishing changes for Fisher’s Frenzy only occur within the Shimmering Seas, all other areas for fishing remain unaffected.

## Fight Valiantly in Bout of Realms

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/Z4KCTPFLB5KK1720216706977.jpg)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/Z4KCTPFLB5KK1720216706977.jpg)

Something dastardly has whipped the warriors of the realm into a frenzy. Only the breaking of bones and shedding of blood can quench their thirst for battle. Gather your most steadfast allies to win the day in Bout of Realms, our first PvP group event!

The Bout of Realms unfolds in three phases: Registration, Preliminaries and Finals.

From **July 10−July 15, 3 a.m. local server time**, register for Bout of Realms by forming a party of up to 4 players. Party members can be swapped at any time within the registration period. Once registration has ended on July 15, parties are locked and cannot be changed. Choose your teammates wisely, for they will be your key to success.

From **July 15−July 22, 3 a.m. local server time**, during Preliminaries each team will participate in 6 matches, which take place at **9:30 p.m. local server time**.

Preliminaries matches grant:

- 3 points for a win
- 1 point for a loss
- 0 points for a forfeit

Ties are decided by the number of kills made during the matches. Participating in the Preliminaries earns you a Legendary item, Gold, scrap and Dust.

Once Preliminaries have concluded the top scoring teams will advance. Up to 16 teams will move forward to the Finals, depending on server size.

From **July 22−July 31, 3 a.m. local server time**, Finals will see 16 teams across servers compete for the top place at **9:30 p.m. local server time**. Teams that qualify for finals will earn an Igneous Nimbus aura cosmetic, and the top four finalists will earn a **Champion’s Fire kill streak visual effect** which they can wield for 30 days to flaunt their martial prowess.

Those who stand victorious as our finals champions will earn all previously mentioned rewards, and 10 Legendary Crests.

## Seek Legendary Gems with A Priority Drop Rate

The Horadric Mages have used their most clever magic to augment the ability to uncover a targeted collection of Legendary Gems.

From **July 10 3 a.m.−July 31 3 a.m. local server time**, there is a limited-time event that provides Priority Drop Rates in Elder Rifts when hunting for coveted 5-Star Legendary Gems. During the event, you can select eight 5-Star Legendary Gems for an improved chance for them to drop! Whenever a 5-Star Legendary Gem drops, there is a 60% chance it will be one of those eight you choose for the event.

Use this most curious magic to gain untold power!

## Returning Events

#### Survivor’s Bane

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/Q98LDMU7BIQN1714945677293.jpg)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/Q98LDMU7BIQN1714945677293.jpg)

In Survivor’s Bane, you’ll begin each run without your character’s items and a random Skill from their Class. From **July 12, 3 a.m.–July 19, 3 a.m. server time** you can vanquish demons across three levels in succession—Shassar Sea, Ashwold Cemetary, and Frozen Tundra—to earn rewards and achievements.

As you move throughout the map, any Skills you have equipped will be automatically cast for you. Smiting Hell’s minions will grant you useful experience orbs that level up your character. Each time you level up, you’ll be presented with the option to equip a new Skill, up to six total can be equipped—the Skill you’re presented with can be from any Class regardless of your character’s Class—or to upgrade an equipped Skill. For more information about Survivor’s Bane, please visit [this article](https://news.blizzard.com/diablo-immortal/24021895/).

#### Alley of Blood

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/TZQHHTKVVSJS1714945678584.jpg)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/TZQHHTKVVSJS1714945678584.jpg)

Quarreling forces threaten to desecrate hallowed grounds, marking a point in the conflict between the forces of Shadows and Immortals. Demolish opposing Sentry Towers and dismantle the enemy team’s base of operations by shattering their Crystal Heart. Imbue your adventurer with Specialist Skills for a tactical advantage, and guard and bolster your Champion’s Marks to claim the day. From **July 12, 3 a.m.–July 19, 3 a.m. server time**, fight alongside Sacred Guardians in the Alley of Blood.

Here are the rules of engagement:

- All characters will be set to Level 60.
- The Rank of all Legendary Gear and Set Gear will be set to 1.
- The Rank for all Legendary Gems on your character will be set to 10.

Bonuses from the following are disabled as well:

- Resonance
- Clans and Warbands
- Normal Gems and Charms
- Helliquary bonuses
- Stances
- Paragon Specialization

## Feature Updates

#### Tower War Season 3

The new Tower War Season will begin on **July 10, 3 a.m. server time** and ends on **August 12, 3 a.m. server time**. The rules of engagement remain the same as last season, go forth and best of luck to all adventurers!

#### Battleground Class Balance Adjustments

The following adjustments have been made to the following Class skills across Diablo Immortal.

**Wizard Items**

- Frozen Wellspring (Meteor): Initial action time reduced from 0.47 to 0.3 seconds. Damage increased by 25%.
- Csur's Fortuitous Ward (Teleport): The shield provided by Teleport also grants immunity to a single instance of total loss of control.

The following Class Balance changes will affect these Classes in Battlegrounds:

**Tempest Items**

- Zephyrguard (Flowing Strike): Cooldown increased from 6 to 7.5 seconds.

**Tempest Magic Affixes**

- Flowing Strike Magic Attribute: The shield granted by using Flowing Strike has had its absorb amount reduced by 15%.

**Wizard Items**

- Angmar's Repulsive Burden (Scorch): When paired with Vaetia's Resourceful Countenance (modifies Scorch to be a lightning orb that explodes, creating a field that pursues enemies) the knockback has an additional internal cooldown of a few seconds.

#### Helliquary Boss Matchmaking Changes

The Quick Match function for Helliquary Boss matchmaking has been expanded to support cross-server matchmaking. It should now be much quicker and easier to find a party.

> **Developer’s Note:** *These changes should make it easier to receive weekly Helliquary rewards and clear out bosses to unlock later tiers of Hell and Inferno difficulties.*

#### New Search Option in Essence Heritage

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/HLW81SLZ2GF21720216707091.jpg)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/HLW81SLZ2GF21720216707091.jpg)

When using Zamina to Extract Essence, there will be a drop-down menu at the top allowing you to quickly filter to only display Essences for the skills you want. You can select multiple skills at a time, allowing you to more rapidly search for synergies to create unique builds.

#### Rune System Changes

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/N95O63YUOXKV1720216706643.jpg)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/N95O63YUOXKV1720216706643.jpg)

When Runes are successfully socketed or removed from your items, a summary that displays the bonuses will be shown.

#### Abyssal Verge Changes

When playing in the Abyssal Verge, we’ve increased how far the Terror Incarnate will chase you. Their overall Health has also been decreased.

#### Castle Erebban Changes

If you fail to escape Castle Erebban and lose the final rewards, you will no longer lose any Legendary Items that you’ve picked up during the game mode. Additionally, we’ve made improvements to the UI to improve clarity and readiblity.

#### Familiar Optimizations

Familiars will now pick-up items more quickly. Who’s a good Duskprowler!

#### Side Quest Changes

When you have items needed for a Side Quest, the required items will be selected instead of needing to select them manually.

#### New Blacksmith in the Court of Whispers

A new Blacksmith has been added to the Court of Whispers, where you’ll be able to salvage Materials and perform all types of Smithery!

#### Increased Stash Inventory

In the Stash, the stacking limit for Purification Shards has been increased to 999, and the stacking limit for Spirit Essence has been increased to 9999.

#### Shadow Weekly Goals Updates

After you complete a Weekly Shadow Goal, it will generate a new goal ensuring there will always be 3 available to complete.
