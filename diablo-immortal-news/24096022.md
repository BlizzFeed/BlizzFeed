## Become The Storm

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/XBLTG1CQKJ2F1716249751449.jpg)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/XBLTG1CQKJ2F1716249751449.jpg)

Tempestuous winds are picking up, adventurer—let them sweep you up, then storm your way through the Burning Hells’ legions as the Tempest. Starting on May 23, there will be three ways you can try out the Tempest.

- Survive the storm in the **Tempest Origin Story quest, Relentless Tide**. No class change is necessary to undertake this journey as you learn of the harsh and frigid ancestral home of the Tempest, and the sacrifices they’ve undertaken to survive.
- Command the sea and sky in the **Tempest Trial Dungeon**. Once you’ve learned more about the Tempests’ origins, rain havoc with three strong, recommended builds without having to change your class or think about skill combinations.
- Enter the **Deluged Plane**, a 2-week-long event like the Fractured Plane, where all participants play as the Tempest. Test different builds and try different combinations, building affixes as you progress. Emerging triumphant will earn you a Tempest cosmetic and Legendary Gear.
- As always, you will be able to create a new character as the Tempest or use Class Change, which is now available once you reach Westmarch, rather than upon reaching Level 35.

#### Tempest Origin Story: Relentless Tide

Far north of Ivgorod lay the storm-wracked Cold Isles—all that remains of the once-mighty Pelghain empire. Savaged by torrential rain, incredible winds, deadly beasts, and a toxic, ever-present mist, it is only by the tireless protection of the Unmoored and the Tempest warriors that the nation survives. Now, as the waves threaten to drown one of Pelghain’s outlying islands, a group of Tempests set out to evacuate the isle before time runs out. Surviving the storm is always a constant struggle, but for one brave warrior, this mission will change everything.

Experience the new Tempest class story as we journey through the events leading up to the Tempest’s decision to leave the Cold Isles for the mainland.

#### Tempest Trial Dungeon

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/YKWFI36X9SMG1716249751588.jpg)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/YKWFI36X9SMG1716249751588.jpg)

Master the skies and seas with three strong, recommended builds for the Tempest without having to change your class. While in the dungeon, you will be equipped with the skills, gear, and Legendary gems recommended for the build you choose.

Claim a one-time reward (one Legendary gear item and 5000 gold) after completing the trial dungeon for the first time.

#### Deluged Plane Limited-Time Event

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/UXUP549TGP7K1716249752410.jpg)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/UXUP549TGP7K1716249752410.jpg)

Trouble is brewing, adventurers, and the seas are rough. To harness the storm and try out different combinations and builds, enter the Deluged Plane: a limited-time event where you can try playing the Tempest with no Class Change required.

Completing a certain number of tasks will grant you rewards:

- 2 tasks – 3 enchanted dust, Tempest class-bound, Horadrim armor cosmetic
- 4 tasks – 100k gold, Tempest class-bound, Horadrim weapon cosmetic
- 6 tasks – 1 Telluric Pearl, 3 normal gems
- 7 tasks – 1 bound Legendary Crest, 10 Aspirant’s Keys

Learn to command the wind and sea— the Deluged Plane is available from **May 23, 3 a.m. server time—June 6, 3 a.m. server time**.

## New Magic Affixes on Eternal Gear for Tempest

- **Flowing Strike:** Grants a shield that absorbs damage.
- **Rolling Surf:** Causes your Primary Attacks to launch water blades at enemies, dealing damage.
- **Vortex:** Range increased.
- **Cascade:** Range increased.
- **Wind Walk:** Restores a portion of your maximum Life every few seconds.
- **Stormfury:** Increases your Primary Attack Speed for a few seconds.
- **Blade Dance:** Increases Critical Hit Damage taken for enemies hit.
- **Zephyr Surge:** Critical Hit Damage increased for each Zephyr consumed.

## Feature Updates

#### Paragon, Difficulty Tiers and Levelling Changes

With this Content Update, we have made several changes to the Paragon System, Hell and Inferno difficulty levels, Helliquary boss access, and more.

To read full details on the changes, please see our [blog here](https://news.blizzard.com/diablo-immortal/24096019/).

#### Class Change Optimizations and Armory Updates

Class change is now unlocked when you reach Westmarch, rather than when you hit Level 35. Class change can be found in Selynne’s Basilica in Westmarch. You can also navigate here quickly by selecting this option in the Tempest Event hub from the main menu.

On **May 23**, you will have a free class change available to celebrate the windswept arrival of the Tempest. Additionally, until **June 13**, if you switch classes, you’ll have the ability to revert to your original class each day with no cooldown.
