> Gather round to read the prequel to the Flesh Harvest quest, releasing with the next Diablo Immortal major update on September 25.

---

Since assuming control of the Shardborne, Prince Albrecht has only ever sought peace and sanctuary for those like him. Deep in the thickets of the Sharval Wilds, Albrecht finds a new home for his people…and he will go to any extreme to protect it.

As the denizens of the Sharval Wilds fear for their safety, Albrecht steps in with a promise of salvation for all. The “Crimson Mass” comic is the prequel to the *Flesh Harvest* quest, which releases on **September 25** with our [next major update](https://news.blizzard.com/article/24226326/). Read it to learn about the plot that will threaten all of Sanctuary.
