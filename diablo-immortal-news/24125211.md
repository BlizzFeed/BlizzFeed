## Overflowing Arsenal Limited-Time Event

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/0U7B6BJZO7JT1722637107939.jpg)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/0U7B6BJZO7JT1722637107939.jpg)

Coveted panoplies swallowed up long ago by the Burning Hells’ emissaries have resurfaced in Sanctuary, and in suspicious amounts. Could this be an opportunity to recover gilded garments. . . or is it just another sinister illusion?

From **August 7, 3 a.m.–September 4, 3 a.m. server time**, in pursuit of the Overflowing Arsenal, you’ll cut down foes to complete 5 weekly tasks up to four times each, earning Overflowing Tokens for your valor. These Tokens will help you progress towards milestones that provide you with a choice between 2 item rewards. The items will always be of either Legendary or Set rarity. In total, there are 14 milestone rewards that can be completed up to 3 times. View the rewards you will choose between below:

Rewards:

- Round 1, milestone 1: Helmet or Chest Armor
- Round 1, milestone 2: Chest Armor or Shoulders
- Round 1, milestone 3: Shoulders or Pants
- Round 1, milestone 4: Pants or Main Hand
- Round 1, milestone 5: Main Hand or Off-Hand
- Round 1, milestone 6: Off-Hand or Helmet
- Round 1, milestone 7: Necklace or Belt
- Round 1, milestone 8: Belt or Gloves
- Round 1, milestone 9: Gloves or Boots
- Round 1, milestone 10: Boots or Left Ring
- Round 1, milestone 11: Left Ring or Right Ring
- Round 1, milestone 12: Right Ring or Necklace
- Round 1, milestone 13: Helmet or Chest Armor
- Round 1, milestone 14: Shoulders or Pants

Adventurers can also receive an additional reward for how consistently they protect Sanctuary. Log in on a Saturday during Overflowing Arsenal’s run and receive 50 Overflowing Tokens, 5,000 Gold, and 10 Beguiling Dust.

Those looking to blaze through the milestones like a bat out of the Burning Hells have an option available to them as well. For 160 Eternal Orbs, players can purchase a Legendary Crest bundle that comes with a 150% Overflowing Tokens acquisition bonus (50% faster than normal). This bundle can be found in the Overflowing Arsenal event menu.

## Sunfall Reliquary Grotto

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/3RU6PTKBTUBJ1722637108009.jpg)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/3RU6PTKBTUBJ1722637108009.jpg)

The Sunfall Reliquary Grotto is available from **August 7, 3 a.m.–August 28, 2:59 a.m. server time**. You can use **Reliquary Grotto Tokens** to purchase the ability to **draw one item at random** from the available item pool. There are 10 items total, and each can only be acquired once. Each time you draw an item, the cost of the next draw will increase. Performing 10 draws guarantees that you will receive all items available in the Sunfall Reliquary Grotto. Your first draw is free.

Reliquary Grotto Tokens can be purchased with Eternal Orbs, and any unspent Tokens will be converted into Platinum once the Sunfall Reliquary Grotto event ends. All items drawn will automatically go to your inventory.

Because items from the Sunfall Reliquary Grotto are acquired randomly per draw, we want to provide players with the initial draw rates for each item to aid in deciding if they would like to engage with the event past their first free draw.

***Note: The drop rates provided below are for the first draw only and will adjust to reflect the relative probabilities of the remaining items in the Revenant Market item pool.***

- 4 Legendary Crests: 0.94%

- A Masterwork Gem Random Chest: 0.23%
- A Masterwork Gem Random Chest: 0.16%
- A Masterwork Gem Selection Chest: 0.16%
- 40 Bound Gem Fragments: 0.12%

The cost of each draw is below. Eternal Orbs can be exchanged for the same amount of Reliquary Grotto Tokens.

## Returning Events

#### Survivor’s Bane

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/Q98LDMU7BIQN1714945677293.jpg)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/Q98LDMU7BIQN1714945677293.jpg)

In Survivor’s Bane, you’ll begin each run without your character’s items and a random Skill from their Class. From **August 9, 3 a.m.–August 16, 3 a.m. server time** you can vanquish demons across three levels in succession—Shassar Sea, Ashwold Cemetary, and Frozen Tundra—to earn rewards and achievements.

As you move throughout the map, any Skills you have equipped will be automatically cast for you. Smiting Hell’s minions will grant you useful experience orbs that level up your character. Each time you level up, you’ll be presented with the option to equip a new Skill, up to six total can be equipped—the Skill you’re presented with can be from any Class regardless of your character’s Class—or to upgrade an equipped Skill. For more information about Survivor’s Bane, please visit [this article](https://news.blizzard.com/diablo-immortal/24021895/).

Quarreling forces threaten to desecrate hallowed grounds, marking a point in the conflict between the forces of Shadows and Immortals. From **August 9, 3 a.m.–August 16, 3.am. server time**, fight alongside Sacred Guardians in the Alley of Blood. For more information about Alley of Blood, please visit [this article](https://news.blizzard.com/diablo-immortal/24008061/).

Here are the rules of engagement:

- All characters will be set to Level 60.
- The Rank of all Legendary Gear and Set Gear will be set to 1.

**Bonuses from the following are disabled as well:**

- Resonance
- Clans and Warbands
- Normal Gems and Charms
- Helliquary bonuses
- Stances
- Paragon Specialization

## Feature Updates

#### Battleground Zealous Idols

The resurrection time for the defending team during Phase 2 of Battleground has been increased. As the Idol gets closer to the end of the lane, the resurrection timer will increase.

> **Developer’s Note:** *This change is being made to aid the win rate of attackers since the current system created a win rate distribution that favors defenders by a considerable amount.*

#### Helliquary Bosses

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/G84R4YRENL0F1722637107862.jpg)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/G84R4YRENL0F1722637107862.jpg)

Four additional Helliquary Bosses can now be fought in Normal Difficulty in addition to Lassal the Flame-Spun: Vitaath the Shivering Death, Gorgothra the Claimer, Beledwe and Gishtur, and Izilech the Misshapen. The new bosses will provide a first kill bonus.

To unlock Hell Difficulty I, players still only need to defeat Lassal.

#### Abyssal Verge and Erebban Castle

Abyssal Verge and Erebban Castle have received a variety of updates to its drop rates and rewards.

- The drop rate of Runes received from defeating monsters has been increased.
- An Abyssal Essence is now guaranteed to drop in Level 5 Danger of Erebban Castle upon successful completion.
- Achieving a score of 1,000 or above guarantees an Abyssal Essence drop in Abyssal Verge.

> **Developer’s Note:** *We have received feedback stating how difficult it is to obtain Runes and Abyssal Essence from Abyssal Verge and Erebban Castle. These changes were made to aid players in acquiring these items. We will continue to monitor the rate at which players are obtaining Abyssal Essence and Runes.*

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/0GE35P2YWIPI1722637107849.jpg)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/0GE35P2YWIPI1722637107849.jpg)

Normal Gems can now be received as a reward from Abyssal Verge and Castle Erebban. In total, 63 Unbound Normal Gems and 63 Bound Normal Gems can be received from these activities and Hidden Lair each week.

#### Terror Essence Codex Task

The weekly Terror Essence Codex Task can now hold up to 2 weeks of rewards and will no longer reset partial progress weekly. The previous iteration of this feature was too absolute in your ability to pass or fail, so we’re creating more forgiveness for those who can’t complete their objective in a week’s time.

#### Item Name Display

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/81V6473R4Y5B1722637107852.jpg)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/81V6473R4Y5B1722637107852.jpg)

The color of loot displayed in the Wilderness has been adjusted to better match their appropriate rarities in the PC version of Diablo Immortal only.

#### New Challenge Rift Season

Fifty new levels will be added to Challenge Rifts on August 19, increasing the total from 250 to 300. Remember to cement your high score in the current season’s leaderboard before the next one begins. Conquest and first-time clear rewards can also be earned from each Challenge Rift Season, so look for opportunities to push your build to reach these goals.

#### Demonic Remains and Charms Lock Feature

A lock icon has been added to the item menu for Demonic Remains and Charms. Toggling this on stops this item from being accidentally Salvaged or Destroyed.

#### Balance Changes

Spartusk

- Cooldown increased from 9-10.5 seconds. Spartusk now removes all bonuses from Skills on its target.

Draff Cryptic

- Dark Curse now removes all bonuses from Skills on its target.

Eyes Ablaze

- The explosion from Eyes Ablaze will now only deal damage to a single target.
