## Feature Updates

#### Oceanic Server Matchmaking Queue Improvements for Battlegrounds

To improve matchmaking in Battlegrounds for Servers in the Oceanic regions, we are making several changes to allow players to join the fight more easily. Oceanic regions will stagger when Battlegrounds begins to capture a greater amount of players. When Battlegrounds starts at UTC+11, the start time will delay by 2 hours. When Battlegrounds start at UTC+10 it will delay by 1 hour.

Additionally, Oceanic, Korean, and Japanese based players will now be able to matchmake together.

#### Battlegrounds Rewards Changes

Players will now receive rewards from participating in Battlefield matches beyond the first 3 per day to better recognize your vigilant efforts in combat.

#### Battleground Resonance Adjustments

We are making further adjustments to matchmaking in Battlegrounds to facilitate better matchmaking between players of similar power levels. We'll achieve this in a couple of different ways.

After Legendary rank, we are reducing the importance that resonance has when matchmaking. Once you’ve reached the Legendary rank, you’ll still be able to match with players that have high resonance but it will be weighted less and eventually ignored once you’re a high enough power level.

Additionally, once a match is found, Armor and Armor Penetration values will be considered for which team players are assigned to.

#### Battlegrounds Server Merge Improvements

To help reduce matchmaking queues and facilitate players playing together across different servers, we have merged multiple servers and allowed players to play across servers.

## Region-specific Game Additions

#### Improved Jump Skill Functionality

Previously, we have released some features and feature updates at different intervals for the version of Immortal available in China and the version available for all other regions. The following changes are soon to be implemented in China for Diablo Immortal.

Several player skills that allow players to leap to new heights have also unintentionally allowed them to leap into unintended areas in Sanctuary, pushing past specific boundaries and disregarding some obstacles. The functionality for boundaries in game will be adjusted so these jumping skills can better avoid obstacles, improving skill functionality while also allowing traversal across the map.

Currently, we don’t have a firm date as to when these changes will be available in all other regions but we'll keep you updated.
