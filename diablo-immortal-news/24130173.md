#### Paragon Changes

Many players at Paragon Level 99 were stuck waiting to defeat Phangwrth, Warmth-Feaster before reaching Paragon Level 100 and Hell Difficulty II. If you chose or were not aware that you needed to beat Phangwrth to progress, you would not be able to go past Paragon Level 99 and would no longer progress, effectively in Paragon purgatory.

We have since removed the need to defeat Phangwrth to continue levelling to Paragon Level 100. This gives you the ability to gain more strength to return and take down Phangwrth to advance to Hell Difficulty II.

#### Inferno I Combat Rating Changes

We are lowering the requirements for Inferno I from Combat Rating 34900 to Combat Rating 30120 to make it easier for players to reach this increased challenge. We have also lowered the requirements for facing numerous Helliquary bosses in Inferno Difficulty I and Hell Difficulty III.

#### Battles Pass UI Updates

Starting with the Devouring Mists Battle Pass, we are updating to a redesigned Battle Pass Main Screen and Codex.

The themed UI on the Main Screen and Codex for each Battle Pass will be reduced to the Seasonal icon and color that represents the Battle Pass theme.

The new UI interface is inspired by Ancients’ Cradle, mixing angelic shapes and an inner demonic nature. The Codex now also displays a timer which indicates the remaining days of the Battle Pass left to make sure you don’t miss out on when the current one ends!

#### Battlegrounds Resonance Notification

In Battlegrounds, we have changed how we’re matchmaking to player power rather than simply using Resonance levels. Matchmaking for Battlegrounds based off Resonance levels didn’t fully capture a player’s true power, often leading to uneven matches when a player’s entire host of skills and abilities were considered.

We will now use a new system for matchmaking that takes comprehensive combat power for matchmaking, which better represents a player's overall power.

> **Developer’s Note:** *We found that using Combat Resonance and four-dimensional attributes (Level, Battlegrounds Match score, Resonance Level) to matchmake couldn’t fully capture a player’s overall power. This led to instances where players of a similar calculated rating could have vastly different player power, which was a frustrating experience.\
> To solve this problem, we instead chose to use comprehensive combat power for matchmaking. Comprehensive combat power is calculated based on all your player abilities and power. This includes Life, Damage, the previously mentioned four-dimensional attributes, damage reduction and damage increase of Daedessa, Legendary Gems, and more.\
> This should more accurately judge your overall combat ability, which combined with new matchmaking rules, can bring better balanced matches in Battlegrounds.*

#### Battlegrounds Legendary Rank Notification

Upon reaching Legendary rank in Battlegrounds, some players gave feedback that they started to encounter players with far higher Resonance Levels compared to their own. Once you reach Legendary Rank, Resonance Level isn’t as impactful when matchmaking. Upon reaching above 3500 Battleground Score, Resonance Level is no longer considered when matchmaking.

These rules have been in place for a long time, but to help avoid confusion we’ve added a notification that explains matchmaking at the Legendary rank.

Upon reaching Legendary rank, there will be a notification that explains the matchmaking changes relative to your Resonance Level. To view this information, you can select the info button within the main Battlegrounds screen to learn more.
