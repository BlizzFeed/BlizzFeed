## Season 43 Battle Pass: Everlasting Opulence

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/7XMWUV9TGTQN1755906138781.jpg)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/7XMWUV9TGTQN1755906138781.jpg)

[![Video](https://markdown-videos-api.jorgenkh.no/youtube/TNnKa3yVVUY?width=1280&height=720)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TNnKa3yVVUY)

Legends say the gilded skeletons within Aughild’s tomb were disobedient nobles submerged in molten gold. Only the daring attempt to plunder their remains, and only the bold will emerge with their spoils—and lives—intact.

The Season 43 Battle Pass bolsters itself into Sanctuary on **August 28, at 3 a.m. server time**, containing a Battle Pass with 40 ranks worth of rewards, such as Crests, Hilts, Legendary Gems, and more.

Begin dismembering demons and climbing your way through the ranks today before this age of opulence ends on **September 25, 3:00 a.m. local server time**.

August 27—September 3, 3 a.m. local server time

September 3—September 10, 3 a.m. local server time

September 10—September 17, 3 a.m. local server time

September 17—September 24, 3 a.m. local server time

Burn through these limited-time events to earn the following rewards:

- 12 Legendary Items
- 4 Set Items
- 10 1-Star Legendary Gems
- 800,000 Gold
- 2,800 Scrap
- 240 Enchanted Dust
- 28 Aspirant’s Keys
- 16 Normal Gems

## Revel in the Winds of Fortune

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/DHRSBG46EPI41755906138742.jpg)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/DHRSBG46EPI41755906138742.jpg)

Mages have poured over their tomes to discover the origin of increased materialized wealth, employed by the merchants of Sanctuary to seize this opportunity to increase their abundance. The Winds of Fortune are to thank for these unexpected riches.

From **August 27, 3:00 a.m.—September 3, 3:00 a.m. local server time**, you will earn increased rewards!

While Winds of Fortune is live, activate your 24-hour buff to receive this duplicate effect. Once activated, the buff’s duration is in real time and will end 24 hours after it’s been initiated. If you have not activated the buff within 24 hours of the event ending, it will be enabled automatically. Any rewards not claimed before the end of the event will be auto-claimed on the next log-in.

During Winds of Fortune, the 4-player party Normal Gems bonus drop will be doubled and unaffected by the daily cap, and only the first twelve Common Gems of the day will be tradable.

Any of these items will drop in duplicate quantities:

- Gold
- Experience
- Battle Pass Points
- Normal Gems
- Legendary Items
- Items

Items from these activities will also drop in duplicate quantities:

- Horadric Bestiary
- Challenge Rifts
- Bounties
- Fishing
- Dungeons
- Purge the Depths
- Accursed Towers
- Hidden Lairs
- Farming in the wilderness
- Codex Activities

Note that during this limited-time event, Battle Pass rewards will not be doubled, and the weekly limit on Battle Pass Points and Normal Gems will not change. Any bonus Experience gained will be affected by your current modifier.

Additionally, there are limits to each item that can be earned in duplicate quantities. You’ll earn double the amount until you reach the limit. Please see the event in-game for more details.

## Feature Updates

#### Set Item Hunter Event Time Change

We're extending the Set Hunter event through September 10th.

#### Notification Updates and Claim All button

We’ve added a “claim all” button to accept all outstanding rewards in the notification bell tray. We’ve also added a “mark as read” for lower priority notifications and removed some other notifications. Enjoy that clean inbox!

#### Blood Knight Skill Re-mapping

We have introduced the ability to re-map your secondary skills when using the Blood Knight’s Abomination. Previously, your secondary skills which only became available when using the Blood Knight’s transformation were unable to be re-mapped to your inputs of choice. You can now re-map these sub-skills through the Skill Book UI.

This functionality was recently released with the Druid, so it’s now being retroactively applied to the Blood Knight.

#### Elder Rift Replay

On completing an Elder Rift, you’ll now have the option to select Replay Elder Rift to run a new Elder Rift without needing to return to Westmarch.

#### Inventory Sorting

We’ve added the ability for players to sort items in the Miscellaneous tab in different ways.
