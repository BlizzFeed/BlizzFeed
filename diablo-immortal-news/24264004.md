Two Hells collide… The legion of Hell that invades Argent D’Nur now storms Sanctuary. Only you can stop them in the Diablo Immortal x DOOM: The Dark Ages crossover event: The Slayers Reign. ## Rip and tear through the Slayer’s Bane

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/PYOP1ZR965801775513387275.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/PYOP1ZR965801775513387275.png)

In this limited time reimagining of Survivor’s Bane, the brutality of *DOOM*: The Dark Ages tears its way into Sanctuary on April 15th. You will wield a selection of **iconic Slayer inspired weapon skills**, each reinterpreted to deliver relentless crowd control, devastating synergy, and unmistakable DOOM ferocity. Six unique skills are available during the event—each one drawn straight from the Slayer’s arsenal. From whirling shields to earthshattering strikes and sustained firepower, every ability is designed to reward aggression and momentum. Some skills work in deadly concert, amplifying damage against bleeding enemies and turning controlled chaos into total annihilation.

But the invasion doesn’t stop at weapons.

As you battle through this corrupted version of Survivor’s Bane, Imps stalk the battlefield, bringing the look and menace of Hell’s foot soldiers into Diablo Immortal. And looming over it all is a singular threat, **the Cyberdemon**.

#### The Lord in Chains: Face off against the Cyberdemon

The Ancient Elder of Hell enters Slayers Bane as the main boss. In this epic showdown this mutilated authority was created for one singular purpose, to stop the Scourge of Hell himself. Use your new Slayer skills and abilities to take the Cyberdemon down and claim victory in the Slayers Bane.

## Legendary Gem: The Crucible

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/36C4NPVZ5U5X1775513398600.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/36C4NPVZ5U5X1775513398600.png)

Wrapped in Wraith Forged energy, bleeding a haunting red to match the hells of Sanctuary, now offers you the power of the Night Sentinels’ most legendary artifact, The Crucible.

This 2-Star gem deals damage to enemies below a set maximum life causes you to Slaughter them, dealing damage, increasing as the targets Life gets lower. Enemies slain briefly after being Slaughtered grant you Slayer's Rage, increasing your damage and Movement Speed. Slayer's Rage stacks and slaying a player instantly grants you max stacks. Slaughter cannot occur more often than once every few seconds per enemy.
