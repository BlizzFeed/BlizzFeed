## Tong-Shi's Renewal Limited-Time Event

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/6l/6L3FS92J13IX1706319882524.jpg)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/6l/6L3FS92J13IX1706319882524.jpg)

Tong-Shi—a god of the Xian pantheon—invites you to his Renewal, a call to bask in the fading glow of a finished year and begin the cycle anew, and welcome a new year with open arms and optimism.

From **January 31, 3:00 a.m.–February 25 2:59 a.m. server time**, players can complete up to five tasks a day to earn festive rewards, such as **Red Bags** and **Tseym**.

The 5 tasks to complete for rewards include: Dungeons, Elder Rifts, killing elite monsters, Bounties, and defeating Jingun (who becomes available to destroy on February 9). Completing these tasks earns you Red Bags and Tseym. There is no limit to the number of times you can complete these tasks for rewards, but there is a weekly limit for how many rewards you can earn, to a limit of 30 Red Bags and 100 Tseym each week that Tong-Shi's Renewal is active.

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/4t/4TC3YCVKIX551706319974142.jpg)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/4t/4TC3YCVKIX551706319974142.jpg)

Red Bags are celebratory Tong-Shi’s Renewal rewards that can be gifted to a friend or to your Clan. Not only will you receive rewards when you open Red Bags sent to you, but you’ll also receive rewards for sending Red Bags. Spread the blessings of Tong-Shi’s Renewal by sending and receiving as many Red Bags as you can get your hands on! Don’t forget to claim a free Red Bag every day you log-in from the log-in rewards tab within the event, which will not contribute to your overall limit.

Completing daily event tasks can also yield Tseyms. Each time you contribute a Tseym, you’ll receiveve a random reward:

- Gold
- Scrap
- Dust
- Glowing Shards
- Gem

Turning in numerous Tseym will combine multiple amounts of the same reward (Ex: Turn in 10 Tseym, you may receive 50 gold). Each Tysem turned in during the limited-time event contributes 500 Gold to **Tong-Shi's Trove**. Tong-Shi's Trove will grant double the Gold deposited if it’s claimed after **February 17**.

Players can also receive rewards such as the bound **Legendary Crests** and **Spark Cask** for logging in on the following days: February 2, February 3, February 6, February 9, February 10 February 14, February 17, February 21 and February 24.

#### Jingun, Eater of Mountains

From **January 31–February 8, 3 a.m. local server time**, band together with fellow Adventurers and hunt down the fabled terror Jingun, Eater of Mountains. While the hunt for Jingun is afoot, you can complete a daily task that transports you outside the world of Sanctuary to locate Signs of Jingun, which reward you with Gold, Scrap, and Dust.

Once Jingun has been found on **February 8**, you’ll be able to claim 3 Rare Crests and begin creating raids to fight Jingun. Your first vanquishing of Jingun grants 1 Telluric Pearl, and 1 random Legendary item.

## Howler Hunt Limited-Time Event

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/I6SZUAVQEQ2W1706319824353.jpg)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/I6SZUAVQEQ2W1706319824353.jpg)

A full moon rises, and the blades that were once at the throat of your enemies rest warily next to your foes. From **January 31, 3 a.m.–February 21, 3 a.m. server time**, in the Howler Hunt event, one of you shall betray the other this night, but strike falsely and your ranks thin. The bloodthirsty Howler has infiltrated a group of Adventurers—find it and kill it, before it kills you.

Available at Level 30 and above, you have been tasked with obtaining Sacred Energy from Sacred Wards and submitting it to the Altar of Purging, located at the center of the map.

During the Match, one of your companions will become **The Howler**. The Howler is tasked with sabotaging their former companions’ progress, using additional threats to their advantage to disrupt any progress made. Howlers can summon Undead Incursions and blighted Sacred Wards to cause chaos and sow the seeds of dissent from the shadows.

Embodying the night terror of The Howler presents unbridled advantages, but those that can master their lunar urges will reign supreme. Howlers can instantly slay their nearest ally, but this will reveal your true Howler form for several seconds. Subsequent kills create longer cooldown times, so feast on your companions wisely.

Once a slain adventurer's corpse is located, a vote will be called to execute the adventurer suspected of the foul deed.

Adventurers and the treacherous Howler win through different means. If all Howlers are executed successfully through a vote, the Adventurers succeed in their grim task. Similarly, if the Howlers manage to execute all Adventurers, the night is theirs. If the Sacred Energy bar reaches 100% completion, the Adventurers win. If all Sacred Wards are destroyed, the Howlers win.

Rewards are determined by how much you assist—or deter—your side’s victory. Collecting and turning in Sacred Energy, participating in voting your fellow Adventurers out, slaying Adventurers as the Howler and dealing with threats will contribute to your rewards.

Fear the full moon and the stalwart companion, for the crimson reflected in the moon may be your own blood.

#### Rise to Meet New Challenges in Returning Events

Tong Shi’s Renewal is but one of many causes of celebration. Special modifiers will breathe new intensity and excitement into the Conqueror, Fractured Plane, Trial of the Hordes and Wild Brawl events for their durations. Two random modifiers from the list below will be in effect for each match and all players in the match will experience the same modifiers.

- Chain Lightning: Player attacks call Chain Lightning.
- Frenzy: Player Attack Speed increased by 20%.
- Rend And Tear: Player attacks cause the enemy to Bleed after three hits.
- Holy Vengeance: Players sometimes explode with Holy Vengeance.
- Will To Survive: Players take 5% reduced damage for every 10% Life they have lost.
- Hit And Run: Every 5 yards Players move without stopping increases skill damage by 10%.
- Fleet-Footed: Player movement speed increased by 20%.
- Pursuit Of Victory: Killing enemies grants 30% Movement and Attack Speed increase for 2 seconds.
- Ultimate Overflow: Player Ultimate skills charge faster.
- Shockwave: Player attacks have a chance to trigger a shockwave.
- Dirty Tricks: Players periodically Blind surrounding enemies.
- Terrified: Monsters will flee in Fear when damaged below 50% Life.
- Sacrificial Power: Skills have reduced cooldowns but cost Life.
- Lookout: Monsters sense players more easily.
- Flurry: Players gain stacks of Flurry that increase Attack Speed when dealing damage to monsters.
- Combo: Activating the empowered skill refreshes its cooldown.

## Feature Updates

#### Familiars Optimization

Previously, Familiars were not picking up items or following players in a timely manner. With this update, Familiars have had their movement speed adjusted to speed up when further away from the player, and to slow down as they approach the player. Pickup speed for item drops has also been increased, which is aimed to reduce player inconvenience when trying to pick up dropped items that have been marked by the Familiar for pickup.

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/XBT47X7G7Z1V1706319806350.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/XBT47X7G7Z1V1706319806350.png)

Additionally, you can now decide how confrontational your Familiar will be with Sanctuary’s demonic hordes using the new Attack and Defend modes. Attack sets your Familiar to proactively seek enemies to engage, while Defend sets your Familiar to only engage enemies that you are currently attacking.

Lastly, Familiar Stones have been further optimized, making them more effective in keeping the best Skills and Attributes on your Familiar.

#### Familiar Melding

When Melding two Familiars together, automatic recommendations will be shown to the player on a summary screen. The recommendation can either be accepted, or players can click or tap the edit button to manually tune their selections. Once the player has confirmed they are comfortable with either of these methods, the finalized Familiar will be shown, and the Meld will have been completed.

#### Familiars and Wynton’s Grand Market

After listing a Familiar in Wynton’s Grand Market, it will take a few hours before the listing appears to other players.

#### Familiars Shop

A new filter option for traits has been added to the Familiars Shop menu. It is left of the displayed price for items.

#### Familiar View

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/4Q4GNPA8YDDW1706319805939.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/4Q4GNPA8YDDW1706319805939.png)

You can now see the details of another player’s Familiar by clicking or tapping on that player, navigating to their Details menu, and selecting the Familiars slot displayed in their layout.

#### New Bounties

Thirteen new Bounties have been added for the Ancients’ Cradle zone. Set out to rid Sanctuary of these vile threats and earn rewards.

#### Challenge Rift

Starting on February 19, the upper limits of Challenge Rift floors will be increased from 500 to 550. Also, the difficulty of monsters on Floors 481–500 will be decreased.

#### Elder Rift

When setting up an Elder Rift run, you can now determine which Crests the Add 7 button prioritizes. To access these settings, select the Change Priority button located just above the displayed rewards on the Elder Rift menu.

#### Crusader’s Sacred Fire Skill

The visual effects associated with the Crusader’s Sacred Fire skill have been optimized to better communicate its use. This Skill functionally remains the same.

#### PC Text Chat

A suite of quality-of-life- improvements have been made to make text chat on PC more intuitive.

- Chat Box text can now be selected with your mouse or keyboard shortcuts.
- Copying, cutting, and pasting text is now supported.
- Pressing the Tab button while in the Chat Box will now move forward through the list of available chat channels. Shift + Tab will now move backwards through the list of chat channels.
- Shift + R can be used to respond to your latest Whisper.
- The ESC key will now deselect the Chat Box.

## Region-Specific Game Additions

Below you will find region-specific updates coming with this content update to the version of Diablo Immortal available in China.

#### Familiar Password Lock

Players can now protect their Familiar from being accidentally Converted, Melded, or Renewed with a new password system. Currently, we have no plans to bring this feature to all other regions.

#### Survivor’s Bane Updates

- Rewards will now be based upon kill count instead of combat duration.
- A variety of Skills have been strengthened.
- In Endless Mode, players will see a menu option roughly every 12, 17, 22, and 27 minutes that gives them the option to exit and claim their rewards or to continue playing; if they continue, the enemies will be stronger.

We plan to bring these changes to all other regions in a future content update. Once we have more information, we’ll be sure to let you know.
