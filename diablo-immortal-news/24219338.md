The Sharval Wilds teems with new life, shaping itself to the forces lurking beneath its mighty boughs. As with previous content updates, there will be server maintenance starting on **July 2, from 5–7 p.m. PDT** for Oceania, East Asia, Southeast Asia, and Europe servers, and on **July 3, from 1–3 a.m. PDT** for servers in the Americas. After the maintenance has concluded, all items mentioned below will be live—specific dates for any features going live afterward are provided below.

## Assume the Mantle of the Druid

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/HIBFI3ZINARV1751327356391.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/HIBFI3ZINARV1751327356391.png)

Deep within the Sharval Wilds are the Talons of Ifeh, a ferocious group of Druids capable of wielding wild, unpredictable, and uncontrollable magic. This content update introduces the ability to take up the mantle of this primal defender, and great power.

For more details on the Druid, you can read our full class overview [here](https://news.blizzard.com/article/24216435/).

Additionally, note that Primal Plane limited-time event will be the previous version of Fractured Plane. The recent improvements, optimizations, and upgrades (such as Nightmare mode and Charms) will not be present throughout the duration of the Primal Plane. Read more details about Primal Plane [here](https://news.blizzard.com/article/24216435/#Assume).

## Feature Updates

#### Market Items with the Same Price now Stack

Normal Gems, Terror Essences, and Runes posted on the Market at the same price will now be stacked in a single group. This should allow you to more easily purchase multiples of the same items at one time.

All items stacked this way will sell first-in, first-out. Excess items posted on the market that exceed the market stack cap will not be visible.
