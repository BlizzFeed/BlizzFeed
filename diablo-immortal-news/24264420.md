## Battle for the Beyond

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/7UJXRMRJ7K381778390787813.jpg)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/7UJXRMRJ7K381778390787813.jpg)

Engage in faction-driven warfare.

In this special version of the Conqueror mode, players will find **special faction themed shrines** across the map. Activating these shrines grants powerful **combat orbs**, each tied to a specific StarCraft faction:

- **Stim Pack (Terran):** Increases Attack Speed and Movement Speed by 30% for 8 seconds.
- **Poison (Zerg):** For a limited time, all attacks fire poison projectiles that apply a damage over time effect.
- **Shields (Protoss):** Grants a protective shield that lasts for a limited duration or until it absorbs a set amount of damage.

Only orbs matching the **faction you selected before the match** will drop during gameplay.

Conqueror will also feature **weekly faction-based leaderboards**, with players choosing their faction prior to each match and competing for ranking within that faction.

**Permanent Update:**

All players will have access to **all eight gear slots**. Players who have not yet unlocked the final two slots will have **default gear automatically equipped** in those slots.

Versatile Rings will become a permanent improvement to Ring Drops and no longer just a limited time event.

Following this update, all 3+2 and 3+3 quality Rings obtained through any means will feature a versatile socket, allowing Gems of any color to be socketed into them. The versatile socket is only available on newly acquired qualifying Rings.

## Increasing Battlegrounds Rewards

We want to ensure that you feel that the time you’re spending in Battlegrounds feels just as rewarding as the time you spend the open world of Diablo Immortal, so we’re making an adjustment to the rewards!

We’re increasing the rewards across the board by roughly 50-100% in some areas, like Gold and Gear Portions, adding Normal Gems (1 per run) to BG Participation, and giving a slight increase to the drop rate of more valuable gear.

Your first 3 Runs will still be the most rewarding, with a step down after those 3 runs, but both prize pools are getting a buff. Normal Gems earned in Battleground share the same cap as 4-Player Activities.

## Combat Rating & Progression Adjustments

We’re making changes intended to reduce system complexity and provide a more streamlined path to increasing power as you progress through Sanctuary.

- The Ancestral Tableau system has been removed.
- Defense of Cyrangar and Purge the Depths no longer grant Activity Points.
- Set Item Upgrades no longer grant Combat Rating.
- The Helliquary Upgrade system has been removed. Helliquary no longer provides Seeker Rating. All invested materials will be refunded.
- Players can access the Helliquary Reforge System without needing to fully max out levels.
- Demonic Remains no longer grant Combat Rating, but all other stats remain unchanged.
- Seeker's Path no longer grants Voidblood Slag and will now reward upgrade materials instead.
- When Paragon Level increases, players will now directly gain Combat Rating, Seeker Rating, and primary attributes.
- All stats, including Combat Rating previously tied to these systems, will now scale automatically with paragon Level progression.
- After the update, your actual Combat Rating will not be lower than before: the system will automatically compare the old and new values and apply the higher one.

## Set Item Drop Rules Revision

After this update, at Hell 1 difficulty, players can obtain and craft all 16 set items.

The drop rate for each individual set item remains unchanged before and after the adjustment. However, now that all difficulties have the same amount of set items in their drop pool, the overall set item drop rate for lower difficulties is being increased to match the drop rate of higher difficulties.

## Feature Updates

All Legendary gear reward selection screens have been updated with a **Battle Pass–style layout** featuring enhanced labels for easier comparison between owned and unowned items, and an added class selection dropdown.

The Cosmetics Backpack has been improved to make browsing and managing your collection more intuitive.

Quality-of-life improvements have been added for Ephemeral Treasure interactions. Players are now redirected directly to the **Treasure activation screen** after seasonal purchase. Tooltips now highlight other treasures within the same set. Reactivated Ephemeral Treasures now feature a **distinct visual glow** in the Inventory.

The **Realm of Damnation** and select previous main quest content now support Hell and Inferno Difficulty.

A new onboarding experience has been added to help players enter Battlegrounds more easily. At **Level 46**, first-time participation grants a **Battleground Intro Quest**. This Covers core rules, objectives, and flow of combat.
