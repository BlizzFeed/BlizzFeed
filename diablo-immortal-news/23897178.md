## Feature Updates

#### Additional Stash Tabs

The ability for players to have additional storage tabs in their Stash is one of our most requested feature updates. Now, **players can unlock up to two additional Stash tabs** from within the Stash menu for their character.

The first Stash tab can be permanently unlocked for **2,000 Platinum**.

The second Stash tab can be unlocked while you have an active Boon of Plenty and will only provide additional storage space during this duration. If your Boon of Plenty expires, any items stored within this Stash tab will remain there until you withdraw them. Additional items cannot be stored in this Stash tab unless you have an active Boon of Plenty.

#### Adjustments to Boss Invulnerable Phases

In our previous Content Update, we made a change that removed the invulnerability phases between killing Segithis’ Left and Right Legs. In this Content Update, we are removing or shortening the invulnerability phase duration from many boss encounters, such as the Ancient Nightmare and Glacial Colossus fights. Going forward, we will continue to evaluate the player’s experience in boss encounters and will make additional adjustments as needed.

> **Developer’s Note:** *Boss invulnerability phases break the flow of combat, making them one of the most noticeable forms of downtime in a dungeon, but they also allow for storytelling and gameplay pacing. In some cases, we can completely remove an invulnerability phase but in many we need to reduce the overall duration of the phase to ensure the encounter functions ideally. Our goal is to reduce the overall downtime in dungeons, especially during combat, but we don't want this reduction to come at a cost to players’ experiences. Many of these changes result in only a few seconds saved here and there, but added up, they should be noticeable.*

#### Path of Blood Rewards

Several improvements have been made to the Path of Blood system:

- Upon completion of a floor, players will now receive gold, experience, and items as a reward.
- You may now skip directly to the nearest boss floor to your current floor, such as Floor 10 if you have completed Floor 7. If you complete that boss floor, you’ll receive credit and rewards for all skipped floors, the boss floor, and will receive the option to skip directly to the next boss floor.

> **Developer’s Note:** *As players continue to flow through the Cycle of Strife, we understand that some players will successfully climb through the entire Path of Blood, only to be forced to slowly trudge back to the top in the next Cycle. While we still believe in the importance of this reset, we wanted to focus on improving the rewards and speed of progression for Path of Blood. This way, those players that wish to breeze through the system can more readily accomplish this feat, while also being better rewarded for their efforts.*

#### Hell Difficulty Notification

Even in a place like Sanctuary, reaching a new Hell Difficulty level calls for celebration. Now, when a player unlocks a new Hell Difficulty level, a congratulations screen detailing the new features available to them will pop up.

#### Auto-Follow Party Member

By clicking or tapping on a member of their Party, players can now automatically follow that player. Any movement or combat that you manually initiate will interrupt auto-following.

#### Player Profile Display

If a player is part of a Warband, their Warband’s name can be seen in the Player Profile menu.

#### Combat Rating Visibility

Combat Rating is an integral aspect of Diablo Immortal. To aid players in easily accessing their Combat Rating, we have moved this statistic to a higher location within the Attributes interface.

#### Bounty Rewards Increase

Any bounty that previously provided 1.2x rewards will now provide 5x rewards.

## Perils of the Fen Limited-Time Event

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/6M57CQK5HB1M1675449901497.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/6M57CQK5HB1M1675449901497.png)

A dangerous torrent of demonic destruction has flooded the murky waterways of Bilefen, grinding daily life to a sludgy halt. Sanctuary’s citizens who call this precarious network of swamps home are on their last leg—coming to their rescue is imperative.

Channel the arcane energy coursing through your mortal vessel to overcome the **Perils of the Fen limited-time event**. From **February 8 at 3 a.m.–February 16 at 3 a.m. server time**, as you complete Perils of the Fen tasks, you’ll receive rewards for your heroics. Racking up enough completed tasks will also unlock milestone rewards for you—a testament to your valor.

## Season 10 Battle Pass: Alchemist’s Admixture

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/Z9C6RU2LXGKQ1675449900822.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/Z9C6RU2LXGKQ1675449900822.png)

*“Despite centuries of setbacks and misunderstandings, the alchemist’s creed is unchanged: anything is possible if you are curious enough.”\
-Rogue Alchemist*

The Alchemist’s Admixture, a new Battle Pass devoted to unwavering curiosity, has been uncovered in Diablo Immortal, and with it, the Season 10 Battle Pass on **February 16, at 3:00 a.m. server time**. This Battle Pass hosts **40 ranks worth of challenges and rewards**, such as Legendary Gems, Crests, Hilts, and more.

The Season 10 Battle Pass only runs until **March 16, at 2:59 a.m. server time**—begin slaying demons and climbing your way through the ranks today!
