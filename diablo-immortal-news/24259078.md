> Gather round to hear the tale of the Warlock and their relentless battle between sin and sacrifice.

---

The journey of a Warlock’s life is never black and white. When faced with unspeakable horrors, Warlocks must make hard decisions – sometimes impossible ones. But when they wield the powers of Hell against its own demons, they must be prepared to accept the consequences.

Would you be willing to pay the steep price that victory demands? Experience the “Sin Eater” web and motion comic and decide for yourself.
