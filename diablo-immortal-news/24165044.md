# A Year of Valor and Adventure – Thank You!

![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/vk/VK6TL5H3CQ131734397130066.jpg)

> Look back at your time in Sanctuary this past year, plus get a sneak peek at next year with fellow adventurer and general manager of Diablo, Rod Fergusson.

---

Adventurers,

As the Hungering Moon sets for the final time in 2024, we take this moment to reflect on an unforgettable year in Diablo Immortal's history. Throughout the past months, you—the courageous defenders of Sanctuary—stood tall against overwhelming darkness, carving your legends into this perilous world. We also salute the many of you who joined us in the fight on the gamescom and CCXP show floors, locked in tournament to defeat hordes of Helliquary bosses.

Looking back, we saw the return of Diablo, the Lord of Terror, flanked by his mightiest lieutenants—Albrecht, the First of the Shardborne, and the Bride of Hell, who summoned legions of demonic armies. The introduction of the grim and haunting World’s Crown zone brought terrifying new challenges, each demanding your skill, courage, and resilience. The clash with these forces has shaken Sanctuary, leaving lasting scars that will shape its future for years to come.

Together, you’ve faced endless battles, embraced new ways to master your craft through fresh Paragon trees and relentless Helliquary bosses, and participated in monumental events like our first-ever in-game crossover with *World of Warcraft*. This event, *Eternal War*, allowed us to battle the Lich King himself and earn cosmetic rewards that span Blizzard’s universes and celebrate WoW’s 20-year legacy.

These adventures are no small feat and stand as a testament to your dedication and camaraderie. Together, you **created 980,000 Tempests**, lived through **480 million player deaths**, and spent over **2 million hours fishing**. Even the fiercest heroes need a break!

But this is where we turn the page into a new year. The Burning Hells never rest, and neither do we.

Sanctuary may have survived this year’s horrors, but even darker events loom on the horizon. In 2025, Diablo Immortal is poised to enter its **Epoch of Madness**. After turning on Diablo, **Albrecht returns** with even more sinister motives that will lead you deep into the mysterious darkness of a long-sought primeval forest, where nature itself poses threats beyond comprehension. This uncharted land is inhabited by ancient mystics and monstrous beasts and promises to test even the strongest among us.

New quests will plunge deep into the heart of Sanctuary, and amidst the chaos, you'll play in a new way as a **new class** emerges, plus additional crossover events we can't wait to show you. Raise your weapons, for the adventure continues. Sanctuary’s future—and its secrets—await discovery.

To each and every one of you, thank you. Thank you for jumping headfirst into the darkness. Thank you for your passion, your feedback, and your boundless love for the universe that unites us. You are the heart of *Diablo Immortal*, and your dedication fuels everything we do. We’re honored to fight alongside you as we write the next chapter of this dark and thrilling saga.

Happy Holidays, and we’ll see you in the Epoch of Madness.

Rod Fergusson\
General Manager, Diablo
