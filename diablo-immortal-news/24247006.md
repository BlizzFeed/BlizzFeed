> Gather round to hear the tale of the great conqueror Aughild, as told by Leoric to his son, Prince Albrecht.

---

Amid a sleepless night, a young Prince Albrecht seeks out his father, King Leoric, for counsel. To ease his son's troubled mind, Leoric regales him with the legend of the great conqueror, King Aughild, and his many feats throughout Entsteig. The tale is one Albrecht won’t soon forget—an ideal every king should aspire to.
