After the maintenance has concluded, all items mentioned below will be live—specific dates for any content going live afterward are provided below.

## Crimson Plane Limited-Time Event

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/S7MPMKY6W13X1689033373468.jpg)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/S7MPMKY6W13X1689033373468.jpg)

Every Blood Knight must accept their days roaming Sanctuary with sentience are numbered. While Fernam’s ritual will suppress their physical transition into an unholy abomination for a time, the battle to stay human is also fought mentally. Two halves of a troubled mind exist in conflict. Entering the Crimson Plane may be the only way to retain harmony.

Crimson Plane is a limited-time event where players can **get a taste of playing the Blood Knight**. A **15-floor gauntlet** awaits, inhabited by the fiercest minions the Hells can throw your way. Your journey in the Crimson Plane begins without your usual gear, gems, and skills. Instead, you **will be provided with a set of pre-selected skills for the Blood Knight**. To advance, you must rely solely on your vampiric instincts, unstable gear you find, and the Chaos Coins you receive from draining the blood of demonic hordes. Best of all, only unstable gear of Legendary rarity will drop in the Crimson Plane, and items you receive will complement the skills you begin with, equipping you with the ability to make, and tweak, your Blood Knight build on the fly. When your journey in the Crimson Plane is over, all the unstable gear you’ve collected will vanish.

The Chaos Coins you earn will also come to your aid—spend them at the Chaos Broker in exchange for new items or Shards of Fortune. These shards will increase your Fortune Rating, providing a greater chance of receiving higher-quality Legendary items from chests on each floor. If you’re resourceful enough to make it to the fifteenth floor without dying more than 5 times, you’ll receive a reward. Out of all the Legendary items you’ve encountered during your run, select 6, and out of those 6, you will be rewarded 1 at random to carry out of the Crimson Plane.

Every 5 floors a player completes will earn them **bonus rewards for their current Class**. These rewards reset weekly.

- **Floor 5:** Chaotic Chest.
- **Floor 10:** Chaotic Chest.
- **Floor 15:** Chaotic Chest and a random Legendary item.

Completing a new task each day for up to 7 days will score you progress toward **milestone rewards**.

- **Complete 2 tasks:** 10 Aspirant’s Keys.
- **Complete 4 tasks:** 1 Telluric Pearl.
- **Complete 6 tasks:** 1 Legendary Crest.
- **Complete 7 tasks:** Horadric Legacy Sanguine Cosmetic.

Put your troubled mind at ease before you succumb to the curse—the Crimson Plane is available from **July 13, 3 a.m.–August 2, 3 a.m. server time**.

## Feature Updates

#### Class Change

One of the ways you can pick up a polearm and impale foes as a Blood Knight is by using the Class Change feature. To celebrate the release of our seventh Class, from **July 13, 3 a.m.–August 2, 3 a.m. server time**, the cooldown to change from one Class to another will be reduced from 7 days to 24 hours. You will have the ability to revert to your previous Class after each change with no cooldown as well.

We have made additional permanent tweaks to the Class Change feature:

- The Primary Attribute (Strength or Intelligence) of equipped Normal, Magic, Rare, and Set items will now convert towards the Primary Attribute of your new Class. This also applies to items in your Inventory and Stash.
- For each Legendary item equipped, you will receive Legendary items with the exact same Attributes. The Primary Attributes of the items will be adjusted for the Class you’re changing to. All previously equipped Legendary items from your prior Class will be moved to your Inventory. As a result, you’ll receive Legendary items the first time you change to a brand-new Class.
- Any Demonic Remains will be converted to the class you’ve changed to and retain their stats.
- Beginning the second time you change to a Class, a set of Transfer Legendary items will be granted to you. These items cannot be Salvaged for materials or extracted for their Legendary Essence.
- Any empty Slots upon transferring will be filled with Transfer Legendary items.
- Paragon Point allocations will no longer reset after each time you change classes.

#### Legendary Items Transmogrification

After reaching Essence Mastery Level 1, players will unlock the new **Transmogrification feature**, allowing them to change the appearance of their gear with that of specific Legendary items.

Now, when a player extracts Essence from marked Legendary items at the Essence Transfer vendor, the appearance of that item will be added to the **Transmogrification Collection**—this is a location where players can view each transmog they’ve unlocked. Transmogs can be applied to any items in their corresponding slot. The equipment slot will display the chosen transmog’s appearance until it is removed via the Transmogrification Collection. Note that players cannot wear both Cosmetics and transmogs for the same slot. You CAN, for example, wear a Weapon Cosmetic with transmogs for your armor slots though.

This content update enables 126 existing Legendary items to be transmogable.

#### Battleground

A wall has been added for Defenders in Phase 2 of Battleground to amend an issue where Attackers would get boxed in and killed immediately after their spawn location shifted. When the spawn location shifts, Defenders will only be allowed to leave their previous spawn location and cannot re-enter.

#### Charms

A Destroy button has been added for Charms of Ranks 2–5.

#### Accursed Towers

- Players no longer need to sign up to contest each individual tower. Clan Leaders can now select which of their Clan members will contest a particular tower.
- If a PvP match begins without enough players from your Clan, you can jump into the match and fill an available slot. Players can only join one PvP match per day using this method.
- Players can now see if members of their Clan have joined a PvE or PvP match in the enrollment menu.

#### Blood Knight Cosmetics

With the introduction of the Blood Knight class, we have created a Blood Knight version of the below Cosmetics. Going forward, any Cosmetic that releases will include a version for the Blood Knight.

- Anointed Immortal (Armor and Weapon)
- Blood Rebirth (Armor, Weapon, and Portal)
- Bronze Wardens (Armor, Weapon, and Portal)
- Children of Inarius (Armor, Weapon, and Portal)
- Exalted Immortal (Armor and Weapon)
- Hatred's Heritage (Armor, Weapon, and Portal)
- Inferno Within (Weapon)
- Scions of the Storm Argent (Armor, Weapon, and Portal)

#### Beta Credit to Eternal Orbs Conversion

Any Beta Credit that players received from making purchases during Diablo Immortal’s Closed Beta will automatically convert to Eternal Orbs after the maintenance period concludes for your region. The Orbs and information on the conversation rate will be delivered to you via in-game mail.
