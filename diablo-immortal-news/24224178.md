## Fly Through the Rift Racer Limited-time Event

Legend of your demon-slaying prowess is already known across the furthest reaches of Sanctuary. Now it’s the time to put that deadly efficiency directly to the test against other adventurers of your excellence.

From **July 21, 3 a.m.—July 28, 3 a.m. server time**, team up with 3 other fellow adventurers to race your way to the end of an Elder Rift and compete for illustrious rewards. Your runs will be bolstered by five powerful Elixirs to grant you each an extra boost to complete the Elder Rift run.

- Vithu’s Elixir: Increases Critical Hit Chance when using a buff.
- Shal’baas’ Fury: Increases Primary Attack Speed when using your Primary Attack.
- Banquet of Blood: Increases Critical Hit Chance when you apply harmful effects.
- Mountebank’s Might: Deals damage to nearby enemies when you are shielded.
- Gloom’s Stride: Deals damage to nearby enemies when you use a Dash Skill at both -your current and end location.

This Rift Racer event will feature two different game modes: Normal and Nightmare Mode. Normal will feel similar to the Elder Rifts you know and love, while Nightmare Mode cranks up the heat with demonic twists and challenges. In Nightmare mode, you’ll have to steel yourself against a fearsome new Rift Guardian, The Jurat.

The Jurat stands in the way as the final threat to claim the mantle of being the fastest, but keep your senses heightened, for more threats hide in the shadows.

Throughout your Elder Rift Race, the underbrush scurries, rife with greedy goblins looking to cause chaos. Slay these goblins as soon as possible, for if left unchecked they’ll bolster the power of all remaining enemies in the Elder Rift. Look out for them at regular intervals of 25%, 50%, and 75% Rift completion.

Normal Rift Racer will be available at all times throughout the event, but Nightmare Mode has limited attempts and only stands to be challenged from 7:00—10:00 p.m. local server time. You’ll have one extra bonus attempt on **July 26 and 27 from 12:00—2:00 p.m**., local server time.

Complete several challenges within the event to earn Legendary Items, Set Items, and Legendary Crests. Hone your score to compete against the heroes of Sanctuary on your server, and across all servers to earn resplendent rewards. The top players on the Cross-Server Leaderboard will earn the following:

- Legendary Crests
- Set Item chests
- Mystery Legendary Gift Boxes
- Reinforced Scrap Materials
- Gold

Race through the Elder Rifts and strategize with your closest allies to emerge triumphant. We wish you good fortune and swift slaying in the races.

## Balance Changes

The Balance changes below will be effective everywhere in Diablo Immortal.

#### Druid Abilities

- Werewolf (Baseline): Damage dealt by the mark is increased by 50%.
- Howl (Werewolf): Damage dealt by the mark is increased by 50%.
- Lunge (Werewolf): You are immune to knockback while charging the skill.
- Surging Stone: Damage increased by 51% and energy cost reduced by 20%.
- Landslide: Improved targeting.
- Added the Healing tag to multiple skills.

#### Druid Legendary Items

- Mauler’s Mitt (Werebear Crush): Damage dealt is increased with each subsequent hit, up to a maximum of 150% of base damage.
- Coat of Fecundity (Thorn Armor): Smolder Seed explosion damage increased by 50%.
- Loamskull (Werewolf): Fire aura damage increased by 65%.
- The Ordered Crook (Werewolf): Healing increased by 14% and now heals every .5 seconds while an enemy is marked instead of when the mark expires.
- Snarling Face (Werewolf Lunge): During the initial dash you are immune to loss of control and knockbacks. The damage of the secondary attack is increased by 32%.
- Scar Carver (Werewolf Rend and Tear): Secondary attacks damage against low health enemies is increased by 50%.
- Tuskguard (Summon Oak Sage): Each Oak Sage now attacks 3 targets instead of 1.
- Flesh is Fertile Ground (Thorn Armor): Now provides 30% increased Attack Speed instead of damage reduction. Subsequent hits now deal 25% reduced damage instead of 50%.
- Loamskull (Werewolf): Reverting back to human form now occurs more quickly.
- Revert (Werewolf and Werebear): There is now a 2 second cooldown to revert back to human form after transforming into a Werebear or Were wolf.

## Feature Updates

#### Favorites Section

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/c2/C2M0M0TOMZ3C1752276702379.jpg)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/c2/C2M0M0TOMZ3C1752276702379.jpg)

You can now favorite and organize your most beloved stickers and emojis in a handy new Favorites section for faster access.

#### Silence Debuff Icon Addition

We’ve added a new Silence debuff icon in PvP areas to help bring better visibility for Legendary item affixes that use the effect.

#### Battlegrounds Updates for Dedesa

Shadow and Immortal Rank bonuses will now be normalized to a 16% damage and defense increase during Battleground and Convoy PvP matches. These bonuses vary from server to server based on when the Cycle of Strife refreshes, which makes you weaker immediately after your server overthrows an Immortal Clan. These bonuses are intended to impact your performance within your server, not your performance across servers so we’re normalizing this to always be a maximum value.

> **Developer’s Note:** *This was already active in other game modes before this update, and we’re further extending this to Battleground and Convoy to try for a better and more consistent PvP experience regardless of where your Server currently is in the Cycle of Strife.*

#### Deeds of Valor Score Updates

Your score in Deeds of Valor will now dynamically update in real time to better reflect your current progress towards ranking up.

#### Deeds of Valor Burden Reduction

The total activity needed to earn Deeds of Valor badges has been lowered requiring less work to earn higher level badges.

Additionally, Shadow Clans that win during the Right of Exile week will earn Impact Marks that will automatically assign to highly active members within the clan that have not previously participated in a Rite of Exile match.

> **Developer’s Note:** *These changes aim to grant you better access to the coveted 12% Deeds of Valor badges. Clan Leaders will have the ability to change who these Impact Marks are assigned to if they feel other clan members are more deserving of the honor.*

#### New Familiar Traits

We are improving these new Familiar Traits:

- Salvager: Allows remote salvaging.
- Appraiser: Allows remote identification.
- Park Mule: Allows remote Armory management.

You’ll now be able to salvage, identify, and manage your Armory remotely far and wide across Sanctuary in many areas where it was previously unavailable. If you already own these Traits, these updates will be automatically applied. Enjoy this updated flexibility, and happy adventuring.

#### Shadow Rank Changes

We’re removing the need to go to the Court of Whispers at certain thresholds to manually rank up. Now, the only time you’ll need to go in-person is the very first time you need to rank up.

#### Boss Fight Revives Updates

If you die during a boss fight and are out of revives, you will now automatically revive after the boss is defeated.
