## New Legendary Gem: Mother’s Lament

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/UFLOIV7E0RW91684869929133.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/UFLOIV7E0RW91684869929133.png)

Lilith’s influence is widespread. Her devotees will go to any length to worship their matriarch, even tainting trinkets in her honor. In celebration of Diablo IV’s release on June 6, we are introducing a new Legendary Gem on **June 8**. **The below values for this gem reflect its power at Rank 10**:

**Mother's Lament (Two-Star Gem):** When you deal damage, you have a 20% chance of gaining Maternal Disdain, increasing your Critical Hit Chance by 32% for 6 seconds. Cannot occur more often than once every 20 seconds. When Maternal Disdain ends, you release a blood spike toward nearby enemies dealing 54% base damage to any enemies it hits and also cause them to Bleed.

## Merciless Monstrosity Limited-Time Event

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/VG4ZIJYRSX6U1684869918260.jpg)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/VG4ZIJYRSX6U1684869918260.jpg)

**Ashava**, a whirlwind of pestilence, has recently emerged in Sanctuary. This gargantuan plague bearer wreaks havoc with her two massive forearm blades and ability to coat the ground in a viscous poison. If you are at least Level 30, have the guts to face Ashava, and come out on top, you’ll earn 10 **Mark of Pestilence**, 1 Telluric Pearl, 50 Scrap Materials, and 3 Enchanted Dust for your first kill. Three additional Marks of Pestilence can be earned from completing Elder Rifts, up to twice per day, and Ashava can be killed once a day for 10 Marks.

**One Mark of Pestilence can be traded for a random reward, which could be:** 1,000 Gold, 5 Scrap Materials, 3 Enchanted Dust, a Glowing Shard, or a One-Star Legendary Gem. **Rack up 88 Marks of Pestilence** and you can trade them for a random Legendary Item; this exchange can only be performed once.

Call upon members of your server to slay Ashava and trade in their Marks of Pestilence to earn rewards for everyone:

- **Trade in 888,888 Marks:** 20 Aspirant’s Keys
- **Trade in 1,888,888 Marks:** 38,888 Gold
- **Trade in 2,888,888 Marks:** 3 Rare Crests
- **Trade in 4,888,888 Marks:** Grim Mercy Portrait Frame
- **Trade in 6,888,888 Marks:** Mother’s Lament Two-Star Legendary Gem

Ashava can be fought through the Merciless Monstrosity event menu or randomly encountered in Elder Rifts from **June 8, 3 a.m.–June 22, 3 a.m. server time**.

## Season 14 Battle Pass: Children of Lilith

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/K6IQEA3K7SLY1684869918734.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/K6IQEA3K7SLY1684869918734.png)

The world was to be paradise for the Lilin. Now, everywhere they look, they see the thieves of their future. Reclaim your birthright as Lilith’s favored children with a new Battle Pass devoted to the Blessed Mother. The Season 14 Battle Pass emerges on **June 8, at 3 a.m. server time**. This Battle Pass hosts **40 ranks worth of challenges and rewards**, such as the **Mother’s Lament Two-Star Legendary Gem**, Crests, Hilts, and more.

Remember, new Rank rewards are available in the Empowered Battle Pass, and rotating Cosmetics can be obtained from the **Battle Archive Shop** for owners of the Collector’s Empowered Battle Pass—get yours today!

This Battle Pass will be lost to time on **July 6, at 2:59 a.m. server time**—begin slaying demons and climbing your way through the ranks today!

Her worshippers’ prayers have brought Lilith’s favor to Sanctuary. From **June 11, 3 a.m.–June 29, 3 a.m. server time**, players Level 30 and higher will receive a 100% bonus to all Battle Pass experience gains.

## Hatred’s Heritage Phantom Market

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/48JUO1RUGJPH1684869918272.jpg)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/48JUO1RUGJPH1684869918272.jpg)

A new Phantom Market offering a Cosmetic Set born from hatred-incarnate **Lilith** starts on **June 8, at 3 a.m. server time**. Using **Obols**, players will purchase the ability to **draw one item at random** from the available item pool. There are 10 items total, and each can only be acquired once. Each time you draw an item, the cost of the next draw will increase. Performing 10 draws guarantees that you will receive all items available in the Phantom Market, including the **Hatred’s Heritage Cosmetic Set**, which can be used with all of Diablo Immortal’s Classes. This cosmetic set also boasts a tailored visual effect when you rack up a killstreak against Hell’s minions.

Obols can be purchased with Eternal Orbs, and any unspent Obols will be converted into Platinum once the Phantom Market event ends on **July 6, at 2:59 a.m. server time**. **All players will receive enough Obols for a free first draw**—these are automatically added to your account.

Because items from the Phantom Market are acquired randomly per draw, we want to provide players with the initial draw rates for each item to aid in deciding if they would like to engage with the event past their first free draw.

***Note: the drop rates provided below are for the first draw only and will adjust to reflect the relative probabilities of the remaining items in the Phantom Market item pool.***

- Hatred’s Heritage Portal Cosmetic: 3.16%
- Inner Light Crimson Weapon Cosmetic: 1.77%
