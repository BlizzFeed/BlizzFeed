## Harvest Treasures Event

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/FS89O6HB7OEG1794618497362.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/FS89O6HB7OEG1794618497362.png)

**Event Starts:** November 18, 2 a.m. PST (after maintenance) - December 8, 2:59 a.m. PST

This new puzzle gameplay event features Abundant Treasure Cards with a 3x3 grid. Completing all manner of in-game tasks from logging in to defeating Helliquary bosses allows players to earn the ability to scratch off one grid square, which has a chance to grant a reward. Track your progress in the **Abundance and Treasure** tab of the Events UI.

## Phantom Gallery Event

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/QZASCR4B8IF41794618497370.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/QZASCR4B8IF41794618497370.png)

**Event Starts:** November 18, 2 a.m. PST - December 1, 2 a.m. PST

The Phantom Gallery is a festive way for players to acquire Legendary Gems, Legendary Crests, Gem Fragments, and more. Using Litra will purchase the ability to draw one item at random from the available item pool. There are ten items total, and each can only be acquired once. Each time you draw an item, the cost of the next draw will increase. Performing 10 draws guarantees that you will receive all available items in the pool.

Players can also earn additional rewards on draws 2, 4, 5, 7, 9, and 10.

- Masterwork Gem Selection Chest
- Eternal Orbs
- Legendary Crest
- Masterwork Gem Selection Chest
- Legendary Crest
- Builder’s Beacon (60 days)

Litra can be purchased with Eternal Orbs, and any unspent Litra will be removed once the Phantom Gallery event ends.

Note that the drop rates provided below are for the first draw only and will adjust to reflect the relative probabilities of the remaining items in the Hunter’s Moon Phantom Gallery item pool.

- Bound Gem Fragment x5: 74.08%
- Rare Crest x20: 22.01%
- Legendary Crest x10: 1.37%
- Bound Gem Fragment x30: 0.98%
- Masterwork Gem Chest x1: 0.25%
- Masterwork Gem Selection Chest x1: 0.58%
- Legendary Crest x15: 0.25%
- Sovereign Masterwork Gem Chest x1: 0.22%
- Masterwork Gem Chest x1: 0.18%
- Legendary Crest x25: 0.08%

## Quality of Life Changes

#### Helliquary Invite Permissions Now Persistent

Helliquary Invite Permission settings have been made persistent rather than automatically resetting twice per week.

#### Helliquary First Clear Leaderboard Added

We have added a First Clear Leaderboard to show all winning team member names, eliminating the need to click on a separate board to view them.

#### Prevent Wanted Mail from Accidental Deletion

Ever lost wanted mail when you used Delete All in your mailbox? You can now flag mail to exclude it from the Delete All function. Just open the mail you want to keep, select Flag Mail, and you’ll exclude it from Delete All.

#### MVP Display Improvements

The MVP display should present the player's appearance more clearly, with visuals matching the Outfit interface in the Backpack.

#### Battle Pass Interface Refresh

We've also updated Battle Pass rewards are displayed. You can now view and try on the current cycle's cosmetics directly from the rewards screen. In addition, we've optimized the unlock order of Battle Pass rewards; however, the total rewards are unchanged.

## Bug Fixes

- Fixed issues with the Bad Blood modifier in Challenge Dungeons.
  - The damage it deals will no longer interrupt player interactions.
  - The number of Bad Blood pools that spawn will now be properly capped.
- Fixed issues with Escape and other dash skills.
  - Skills and dashes should work as intended and can only be interrupted by enemy crowd control skills.
- Fixed issues with various skills granted by Legendary Essences were not working as intended.
- Fixed an issue where the Druid skill Thorn Armor appeared to have no effect when paired with the Affix and the Legendary Essence Flesh Is Fertile Ground (the effect was actually working as intended).
- Fixed an issue where the Crusader Legendary Essence Spurnmail did not function properly in the Cow Level activity.
- Fixed an issue where the Spear Flurry skill granted by the Blood Knight Legendary Essence Five-finger Spear was not properly tagged as a movement skill.
- Fixed an issue where the Necromancer skill Bone Wall granted by the Legendary Essence Gyreplate could be placed in incorrect locations in certain areas.
- Fixed an issue where the Druid skill Summon Oak Sage granted by the Legendary Essence Teeth of the Fen could spawn Sling Sages in incorrect locations in certain areas.
- Improved the casting experience of the Monk’s Flying Kick skill granted by the Legendary Essence Clashing Sky, particularly under high-latency conditions.
- Fixed an issue where the Crusader skill Draw and Quarter granted by the Legendary Essence Harras of the Steppes could cause the Crusader and horse to become separated under certain circumstances.
- Fixed an issue where the internal cooldown on the Wizard skill Sorceress granted by the legendary essence Rustic Wisdom did not function properly under certain circumstances, allowing it to stun the same enemy more frequently than intended.
