The dead are rising, but not due to the necromancer’s scythe. The realms of the living and the dead blur, eager for you to join their growing ranks. Read on for a preview of your salvation from these ghastly spirits... or your epitaph. ## Hallow’s Wake Returns

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/3X5A62F0Z6W51759525431394.jpg)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/3X5A62F0Z6W51759525431394.jpg)

The Hallow’s Wake Limited-time Event reemerges from the fading dusk with grisly fervor, adventurer.

To earn Hallowed Stones, slaughter goblins in either Survivor’s Bane or Howler’s Hunt and visit the Crone in Westmarch for daily rewards. The Crone’s wares are chocked full of holiday haunts. Trade Hallowed Stones for new Headless cosmetics, Legendary Items, Legendary Crests, and more.

#### Grisly Survivor’s Bane

From **October 15, 3 a.m.—October 29, 3 a.m. local server time**, Survivor’s Bane is rife with a seasonal fright. Among the ghastly demons that plague your every move, you’ll come face to face with haunting beings that only appear during Hallow’s Wake. Supernatural forces from beyond your realm may be at work, narrowing your vision and occluding the dangers that surround you. Beware, for terrors abound!

#### A Terrifying Howler Hunt

From **October 29, 3 a.m—November 12, 3 a.m. local server time**, Howler Hunt embodies a tradition from an otherworldly realm-letting you don the mask of a legendary Mythslayer.

Tonight, one of you shall betray the others, but strike falsely and your ranks thin! The traitor has infiltrated a group of Adventurers, find it and put an end to its betrayal, before it kills you!
