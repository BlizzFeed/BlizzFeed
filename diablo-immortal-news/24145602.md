After the maintenance has concluded, all items mentioned below will be live—specific dates for any content going live afterward are provided below.

#### Skein-Parter (Rolling Surf)

- Maximum number of water daggers reduced from 7 to 4 due to performance impact it was causing. To compensate, damage of each water dagger increased by 75%.

#### Spartusk (Demoralize)

- Cooldown increased from 12 to 25 seconds. Stun duration increased from 2 to 3 seconds.

## Feature Updates

#### Vanguard Rewards

You now have a chance to earn Set gear after successful Vanguard runs on certain difficulties. To do so, you must apply your limited-attempt rewards, which can be found on the Vanguard main menu.

#### Wynton’s Grand Market

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/1BZ3DLLXEWCS1728679975948.jpg)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/1BZ3DLLXEWCS1728679975948.jpg)

The price of unsold items in Wynton’s Grand Market can now be adjusted. You can do so by clicking on the listed item in the menu and selecting the “modify the price” button after adjusting the price.

#### Rite of Exile and Battleground

Damage dealt by players in Rite of Exile and Battleground will be reduced by 50%, previously 40%.

> **Developer’s Note:** *We reduced the damage dealt by players in a previous content update but continued to observe instances of excessive damage since. Increasing the damage reduction to 50% should address this issue and provide a more balanced experience for everyone.*

#### Challenge Rift Season 9

The next season will begin on October 28. It introduces Challenge Rift Levels 301–350 and a new seasonal Avatar Frame reward for the first person to complete the final Challenge Rift. This reward will expire at the end of the season and once again be awarded to the player who completes the highest Challenge Rift in each season.

#### Fangspitter (Multishot)

The visual effects of Multishot provided by the Demon Hunter Legendary item Fangspitter has been improved.

#### Crystalline Shadow (Disintegrate)

The Magic Affix that adds an additional beam to Disintegrate now works with this Legendary Equipment.

#### Prayer for Edwinter Set

The 2-Piece Set bonus has changed. Previously, the mark applied to bosses counted only as Chilled and Frozen for attacks from the player wearing the Set. Now, other players attacking a marked boss willtreat it as Chilled and Frozen for the sake of attacks that can exploit such conditions.

#### Helliquary Bosses

Iscea, Twofold Rebirth’s Life locking after taking lots of damage quickly has been optimized, resulting in a faster kill time.

Additionally, top contributor chests will open automatically at the end of a Helliquary Boss fight.

#### Inventory Stacking

Mystery Legendary and Set items can now be stacked in your Inventory.

#### Dungeons

The refresh time for Blacksmiths and exit portals has been adjusted across various Dungeons to help reduce downtime.

#### Hidden Lair

The Hidden Lair party bonus chest will now open automatically upon completion of the activity.
