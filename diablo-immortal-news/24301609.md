## Experience Optimization

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/zx/ZX6OV0O1D2FA1792195490627.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/zx/ZX6OV0O1D2FA1792195490627.png)

Fresh content is now easier to spot. When new activities, challenges, or objectives are added to an event, a “New” badge will appear on the corresponding tab. If an event contains new content, its main entry point will also display a “New” badge, making it easy to jump into the latest activities. Once you've viewed the new content, the badge will disappear until the next refresh.

New badges refresh every Wednesday at 3:00 AM server time alongside the weekly task reset, helping you quickly find newly available objectives and rewards.

We've also improved selectable Legendary equipment rewards. Instead of receiving a random Legendary from your chosen slot, you'll now receive a specific Legendary item for that slot, giving you greater control over the rewards you earn.

## Gifts of Corvus Balance Adjustments

- **Local Censure:** The additional skill suppression duration has been increased from 4 to 5 seconds, and its cast range has been increased.
- **Superior Bandage**: The amount of Life restored every 0.5 seconds has been increased from 10% to 15%.
- **Pulsing Shield**: The damage dealt to nearby enemies each second has been increased from 100% to 150% of your Shield amount.
- **Portal Backlash**: The fixed damage dealt to nearby enemies after using a portal has been increased from 35% to 45% of their maximum Life.
- **Into the Fray:** The resurrection time reduction has been increased from 50% to 75%.
- **Fleet Strike:** For every 1% Movement Speed bonus you have over an enemy, the damage bonus against that enemy has been increased from 1% to 2%.
- **Bondbreaker:** The duration of immunity to control and knockback granted when you are affected by either effect has been increased from 1 to 2 seconds.

Event Period: After maintenance on October 21, 2026 – November 16, 2026, at 2:59 a.m.

Tower War is available daily from **10 a.m.–12 p.m., 2–4 p.m.**, and **8–10 p.m. Chaos Convoy is available all day.**

**Players Level 46 and above can speak to the Battleground Captain to participate.**

## QoL Improvements

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/df/DFMV6XTL481N1792196387589.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/df/DFMV6XTL481N1792196387589.png)

### Pin Items in your Inventory

Keep your most frequently used items close at hand with the new Item Pinning feature inside of the Inventory screen! Pin up to 22 items to prioritize their placement in your inventory, making it easier to find and access the items you need. Pinned items are marked with a distinctive gilded outline, and you can unpin them at any time to restore their normal inventory order. Items of the same type can only be pinned once, helping you keep your inventory organized and your most important items readily accessible.

### Secondary Item Rank Increased to 30

As many adventurers continue progressing beyond the current Secondary Item Rank cap, we're expanding the system to support additional long-term character growth.

The maximum Secondary Item Rank will be increased from **25 to 30**. To keep this progression aligned with overall character advancement, higher ranks will unlock at specific Paragon milestones:

- Rank 26 unlocks at Paragon 1600
- Rank 27 unlocks at Paragon 1700
- Rank 28 unlocks at Paragon 1800
- Rank 29 unlocks at Paragon 2000
- Rank 30 unlocks at Paragon 2100

This update provides new goals for endgame players while ensuring character progression remains tied to continued advancement throughout Sanctuary.

Upgrade levels 21 and above require specific Paragon Level thresholds before they can be enhanced.
