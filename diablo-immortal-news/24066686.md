You can still claim rewards from the last cycle until the end of that cycle on **May 21**.

## Returning Events

#### Trials of the Warrior

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/JL2XGFIFYYVY1714945678199.jpg)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/JL2XGFIFYYVY1714945678199.jpg)

A true warrior isn’t measured by their title but by their feats. Add to your accolades from **May 8, 3 a.m.–May 16, 2:59 a.m. server time** by completing Daily and Challenge tasks from the Trials of the Warrior event. You can either complete Bounties or slay Elite monsters to earn treasures befitting their valor. Depending on the tier you reach by the event’s conclusion, you’ll receive a corresponding reward via in-game mail. You will only receive the reward for their highest tier achieved.

- **Bronze Tier (1 Bounty or 5 Elite monsters):** 1 random Legendary item.
- **Silver Tier (10 Bounties or 50 Elite monsters):** 2 random Legendary items.
- **Gold Tier (30 Bounties or 100 Elite monsters):** 2 random Legendary items and 1 random Set item.
- **Platinum Tier (40 Bounties or 150 Elite monsters):** 3 random Legendary item and 1 random Set item.
- **Diamond Tier (50 Bounties or 200 Elite monsters:** 3 random Legendary items and 2 random Set items.

You must log in the week following the event to receive their rewards, from **May 8, 3 a.m.–May 16, 3 a.m. server time**.

#### Wild Brawl

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/KHVMYJ29CITM1714945678238.jpg)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/KHVMYJ29CITM1714945678238.jpg)

Enlist your most trusted companions and outlast enemy teams in the Wild Brawl event. In this unique, limited-time PVP event from **May 10, 3 a.m.–May 17, 2:59 a.m. server time**, players start at Level 1, work together in teams of 6 to explore an expansive map, and endure to become the last surviving team. Equip discovered gear, gain experience to strengthen your power, and slay everything in sight to win the battle. This arena-style game mode ends when one team has outlasted the others to make their eternal mark on Sanctuary. For more information about Wild Brawl, please visit [this article](https://news.blizzard.com/diablo-immortal/23985784/).

#### Survivor’s Bane

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/Q98LDMU7BIQN1714945677293.jpg)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/Q98LDMU7BIQN1714945677293.jpg)

In Survivor’s Bane, you’ll begin each run without your character’s items and a random Skill from their Class. From **May 17, 3 a.m.–May 23, 3 a.m. server time** you can vanquish demons across three levels in succession—Shassar Sea, Ashwold Cemetary, and Frozen Tundra—to earn rewards and achievements.

As you move throughout the map, any Skills you have equipped will be automatically cast for you. Smiting Hell’s minions will grant you useful experience orbs that level up your Character. Each time you level up, you’ll be presented with the option to equip a new Skill, up to six total can be equipped—the Skill you’re presented with can be from any Class regardless of your Character’s Class—or to upgrade an equipped Skill. For more information about Survivor’s Bane, please visit [this article](https://news.blizzard.com/diablo-immortal/24021895/).

Demolish opposing Sentry Towers and dismantle the enemy team’s base by shattering their Crystal Heart. Imbue your adventurer with Specialist Skills for a tactical advantage, and guard and bolster your Champion’s Marks to claim the day. From **May 17, 3 a.m.–May 24, 3.am. server time**, fight alongside Sacred Guardians in the Alley of Blood. Here are the rules of engagement:

Bonuses from the following are disabled as well:

- Resonance
- Clans and Warbands
- Normal Gems and Charms
- Helliquary bonuses
- Stances
- Paragon Specialization

## Feature Updates

#### Class and Battleground Balance

The following adjustments have been made to the following Class skills across Diablo Immortal.

**Barbarian**

- Heartless Terror (Demoralize): Can now be used while affected by knockback or control effects. Burst/explosion effects should no longer follow the character.
- Hammer of the Ancients (Baseline): Point of impact has been moved slightly back to accommodate visual effects. Note that this will also apply to Hammer of the Ancients inscriptions using the same effects.
- Bitterwind (Whirlwind): Radius increased from 2 to 2.5 meters; visual effects have been adjusted accordingly.
- Fallow Field (Whirlwind): Radius increased from 2 to 2.5 meters; visual effects have been adjusted accordingly.
- North Wind (Whirlwind): Radius increased from 2 to 2.5 meters; visual effects have been adjusted accordingly.
- Bergbreaker (Wrath of the Berserker): Cooldown between retaliations reduced from 0.8 to 0.5 seconds. Damage increased by 22%.

**Blood Knight**

- Woundwell (Umbral Lance): Cooldown reduced from 9 to 6 seconds. Damage increased by 15%.
- Bardiche of Below (Whirling Strike): Damage increased by 15%. Blast effect now occurs sooner.
- Count of the Eves (Whirling Strike): Damage reduction per stack reduced from 5% to 4%.
- Oversight (Swarm of Bats): Amount healed as a percentage of damage dealt has been reduced across all mastery levels.
  - Level 1 Mastery: Reduced from 15% to 12%.
  - Level 2: Reduced from 19.5% to 15%.
  - Level 3: Reduced from 24% to 18%.
  - Level 4: Reduced from 28.5% to 21%.

**Crusader**

- Bowyer’s Remose (Spinning Shield): Cooldown reduced from 9 to 6 seconds. Damage increased by 15%.
- Conjuration of Light (Baseline): In Battleground and Rite of Exile, the duration of the immunity effect from Conjuration of Light will be reduced by 50% each time a player receives the buff again within 20 seconds. (First: 100%; second: 50%; third: 25%, etc.)
- Hale Pilgrim (Draw and Quarter): Duration of knockback immunity has been reduced across all mastery levels.
  - Level 1: Reduced from 3 to 2.5 seconds.
  - Level 2: Reduced from 3.6 to 3 seconds.
  - Level 3: Reduced from 4.2 to 3.6 seconds.
  - Level 4: Reduced from 4.8 to 4 seconds.
- Luminous Reign (Falling Sword): Damage reduced by 15%. Each time an instance of damage is resolved, an enemy will be damaged at most once.
- Air Splitter (Falling Sword): Cooldown reduced from 9 to 6 seconds. Damage increased by 15%.

#### Gauntlet Boss Rotation

You will now be able to collect first kill rewards before Helliquary Gauntlet bosses are rotated out. Stay tuned for the notice letting you know when to claim your rewards before Gauntlet rotation.

#### Shadow Assembly Mailbox Rewards

Shadow Assembly rewards will no longer be automatically deleted from your mailbox when the Immortals are overthrown. You can collect the rewards from the mailbox once a new reign has begun, but the Marks in your mailbox will not count towards Rank progression in the new reign.

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/DYS3K5ATRM0E1714945676919.jpg)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/DYS3K5ATRM0E1714945676919.jpg)

- **Gishtur and Beledwe**: Defeat Gishtur and Beledwe in Hell V in under 2 minutes.
- **Izilech the Misshapen**: Defeat Izilech the Misshapen in Hell VI in under 2 minutes.
- **Vhansoon, Desolation’s Scourge**: Defeat Vhansoon, Desolation’s Scourge in Inferno I in under 2 minutes.
