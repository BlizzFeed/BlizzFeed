Diablo Immortal is available on PC and is no longer in Open Beta. From the many changes made thanks to player feedback, we have fine-tuned this version of Immortal to deliver an experience we are proud to offer to all adventurers in Sanctuary. We will continue to update this list with any known issues that arise with the current PC version of Immortal.

As **patches and hotfixes are applied** to the PC version of Diablo Immortal, we will **update this list** with the corresponding changes. Hotfixes are typically applied in a higher frequency, addressing smaller, related issues. They don’t require a large download to be applied to the game. Patches are larger client-side updates that require a download for players to obtain. New patches will require players to update before they can continue playing.

## August 8, 2023—Patch Notes

We wanted to take a moment to thank our Diablo Immortal Community for enjoying our game not only on mobile, but also on PC since we launched in [Open Beta last June](https://news.blizzard.com/diablo-immortal/23787371/). The Team knew there were many improvements we wanted to make before leaving Open Beta. We have been hard at work implementing essential quality of life changes to bring Diablo Immortal on PC to the standard we set ourselves for all Blizzard titles on the platform.

With the [Destruction’s Wake major update](https://news.blizzard.com/diablo-immortal/23967319/), we are excited to announce that the team released a series of improvements that are comprehensive enough to remove the Open Beta tag on PC.

To our incredible community, **thank you** for your continued feedback and support of Immortal on PC! With your consistent communication on issues and features and a healthy dose of slaying demon hordes on the side, you have helped make Immortal what it is today. We could not have done it without you.

#### Gameplay

- All textures, including UI, characters, and VFX have been given a fidelity pass to ensure they are high-resolution (up to 4K).
- Added an interact button (“E” by default) to improve clickable hitboxes being inconsistent, improving overall interaction.

#### Chat Interface

- Improved chat communication with /Commands, a core feature for all Blizzard titles. This allows you to now use /whisper and / commands.
  - For example, if you used the /party chat command, you would send a message to all members of your current party.
- The Tab key can now be used to cycle channels and change conversations.

#### User Interface

- Window size and resolution can now be modified in the Settings menu under “Display”.

> **Developer’s Note:** *This has been a long-requested feature and we hope that you can all enjoy Diablo Immortal in your preferred window resolution. Don’t forget that we also added the Cursor Lock option in a previous update to help prevent accidental clicks outside of the game window!*

- Hovering over items in the Inventory and Stash tabs with your cursor will now provide a tooltip preview.
- Items in the Inventory and Stash tabs can now be equipped and stored using right-click.
- Press F12 to save screenshots to your local folder.
- The ESC key will now properly close the most recent window for all primary menu screens.

To celebrate Immortal leaving Open Beta, the limited-time Surrogate’s Mind bundle is now available exclusively through Diablo Immortal on PC. Download now to secure this limited-time offer.

Below is a list of features in Diablo Immortal we plan to improve next. We want all adventurers to have a smooth experience in Sanctuary, and implementing these features shall contribute to that.

We are always listening and looking for opportunities to improve your experience while annihilating hell spawn in Immortal. Thank you for your consistent feedback, and please let us know what features you would like to see improved for PC.

## July 7, 2022—Patch Notes

#### Demon Hunter Primary Attack

- Added a new option under “Controller” in the Settings Menu that allows players to have Demon Hunter primary attacks fire only when actively pressing the primary attack button.

> **Developer’s Note:** *This setting will not be turned on by default for existing Demon Hunters. However, this setting will be applied to all newly created Demon Hunters. This same change has been applied to the Barbarian’s Frenzy ultimate and the Wizard’s Electrocute ultimate.*

#### Mouse Cursor Visual Improvements

- Increased the mouse cursor’s appearance to be both larger and brighter, allowing it to be more visible.

#### Controller

- Fixed an issue that caused Xbox controllers to have input delay.

#### Gameplay

## PC Open Beta Known Issues

#### Controls

- Pressing ESC may open the Main Menu over other menus.
- Key binds do not currently support modifier keys such as ALT, CTRL, and SHIFT.

#### Gameplay

- General movement, interactions, and abilities may be inaccurate at times.
- Print Screen does not currently save screenshots into the directory folder.

#### User Interface

- Chat is currently missing features such as /commands, copy/paste, and select all text.
- Windowed mode does not currently support resizing.
- PC settings have been inserted into their respective sections within the menu and may require a bit of exploration to find.

#### Accessibility

- Voice-to-text transcription services may not be working for certain languages.
