## Feature Updates

#### Blessed Reign Adjustments

The conditions for Immortal Clans to earn a Blessed Reign have been updated. Now, it requires an Immortal Clan to claim victory during the Rite of Exile on Week 7 instead of winning during the Challenge of the Immortal battle.

Additionally, the number of victories for Shadows to win the Rite of Exile has been adjusted, during weeks 5-7 Shadows must win 4 battles during the Rite of Exile to win and begin the Challenge of the Immortal. Regardless of the outcome for the Rite of Exile, the Challenge of the Immortal will still happen on Week 7, so the top 10 Shadow Clans can all fight for the next Immortal reign.

This means that it would take 7 wins for the Immortals to win, and 4 for the Shadows to win.

Reigns that are currently ongoing will inherit these new rules.

#### Battlegrounds and Tower War Group Leader Changes

Some players use the feature of applying to become Group Leader maliciously when playing in Tower War or Battlegrounds. To prevent this from happening, applying to be the Group Leader now has a cooldown.

#### Familiar Conversion Preview UI Updates

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/D2J3AR5M8HPI1731114068290.jpg)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/D2J3AR5M8HPI1731114068290.jpg)

When Converting your Familiar, we’ve updated the UI so you'll see which Traits are being converted in a few different places. You’ll be able to see it under each Familiar’s name and in the Preview side panel.

Additionally, when selecting the detailed view toggle (located on the right of the Convert button), the side panel shows detailed information on each Skill and Trait listed in the pool.

This should help clarify exactly which Traits are contributing to the pool that the final Familiar will be pulling from.

#### Hell II Leaderboard Changes

Once you’ve unlocked Hell II, we’ve reordered the order of the Leaderboard to present more relevant content at the top of the list.

#### Wings Appearance Toggle

Wing cosmetics—such as the Prophet’s Progeny wings—can now be toggled to either reveal the enhanced effects, or display the default base wing appearance.
