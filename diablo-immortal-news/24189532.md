# Berserk Collides with Sanctuary for a Limited-Time

![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/ry/RY7QEU6NG3CC1744842603143.jpg)

> The world of Kentaro Miura’s Berserk and Sanctuary collide in Diablo Immortal with a new boss fight, limited-time Eclipse Survivor’s Bane, and more.

---

Guts, Griffith, gore galore. Straight from the pages of Kentaro Miura’s hellish dark fantasy manga, Berserk comes alive in Sanctuary with the Diablo Immortal x Berserk crossover event: **Struggler’s Path**. From **May 1—May 30**, battle against the fearsome and immortal Nosferatu Zodd, survive your sacrifice in Struggler’s Bane, wield fearsome power with the Crimson Behelit Legendary Gem, and much more.

Set in a medieval, dark fantasy world, Berserk centers on themes of betrayal, revenge, sacrifice, and monstrous power. Celebrate Berserk with grisly themed gameplay and limited-time cosmetics you can earn and collect so your characters can walk in the footsteps of these legendary figures. Don relics of war, symbols of ambition, and echoes of vengeance. Wear them, and become a part of the legend.

## Pay Death a Visit to fight Nosferatu Zodd

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/S3I2BZNEWZCH1744841716463.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/S3I2BZNEWZCH1744841716463.png)

Feared throughout Midland, warriors and noblemen tremble at the whisper of his name. Nosferatu Zodd is an immortal legendary warrior known for his ferocity and mercilessness; he’s said to have slain thousands on the battlefield. Zodd has now set his thirst for blood upon Sanctuary, intent on finding an opponent formidable enough for his might.

You’ll soon be able to challenge this embodiment of bloodlust; survive the storm of his attacks, and prove yourself his most worthy opponent in his over three hundred years of slaughter!

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/NXIJ1KNYBM651744841716549.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/NXIJ1KNYBM651744841716549.png)

Everything on the battlefield is a weapon to Zodd. The longer you fight him, the more he will relish the struggle, increasing the wrath you’ll face. Those that hold the **Crimson Behelit Legendary Gem** will have the ability to face an even more powerful Zodd. Execute your best pincer move and defeat this enraged Zodd to claim tantalizing rewards.

## Deny your Sacrifice in Struggler's Bane

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/MFI3AWJEYPDW1744841713924.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/MFI3AWJEYPDW1744841713924.png)

*“Pile it up, take all you have left, chant the words 'I sacrifice' in your heart, and you will be granted raven-black wings upon which you shall soar higher than any summit."*

As Griffith’s weakened husk clung to any semblance of life, the crimson Behelit lay within his withered hands. A wordless face swam in existence, eager to heed its master's call. The desire to summon the Godhand and acquire his own kingdom.

The infamous Eclipse comes to life within Survivor’s Bane. The Eclipse is an apocalyptic event within Berserk, where the Band of the Hawk was sacrificed by their former leader to exalt him to member of the Godhand. Within the Eclipse, these doomed warriors were teleported to a hellscape, mercilessly slaughtered after being marked with the Brand of Sacrifice.

While Survivor’s Bane is active during the Struggler’s Path event, you'll be able to fight within the Eclipse itself. Every other minute, you’ll experience the full Eclipse for 60 seconds. Rally as though you are side by side with the Band of the Hawk, fighting for your life despite the brand of Sacrifice freshly etched upon you.

The Struggler’s Bane brings fearsome new horrors. Terrifying demons will attempt to claim your life, and the land around you will be familiar despite being transformed into a hellscape. While the Eclipse is active, a new unique boss also awaits there to execute the Godhand’s bidding.

## Harness the Eclipse with the Crimson Behelit Legendary Gem

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/3RG6EA5873V61744841714371.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/3RG6EA5873V61744841714371.png)

The Crimson Behelit writhes in silent agony, eyes staring unfocused, ready to receive the sacrifice necessary to exalt a mere human to that of a Godhand. This fearsome new Legendary Gem arrives with the Struggler’s Path. The below value reflects its power at Rank 10.

Your Behelit activates in response to your overwhelming desire, granting you the Behelit Power you seek the most. While healthy, dealing damage grants you Behelit's Wrath, temporarily increasing your damage. When your Health is reduced, taking damage as you gain Behelit's Mercy, reducing your damage taken. When a Behelit Power triggers it releases a scream of terror at the enemy's location, Fearing nearby enemies temporarily. Each Behelit Power has its own separate cooldown.

## Shape Earth with the Golem Familiar

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/CEIVGEDH6HJR1744841715229.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/CEIVGEDH6HJR1744841715229.png)

Although they’re formed of earth and water, these stumbling creatures are sturdier than they appear. Golems are constructs capable of protecting their master, even in the deadliest of situations. You will soon have the opportunity to obtain these unique Familiar Skins, which give your Familiar the look of these earthen companions.

While the Struggler’s Path is active, your first summoned Familiar is guaranteed to be a Golem.

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/ASGZ0F67N8NH1744841713983.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/ASGZ0F67N8NH1744841713983.png)

Much like Nature, your Golem transform beautifully as you’ll also be able to customize this Familiar skin. Combine these decorations to make your Golem a companion unique to you, sure to catch the eye of your fellow strugglers.

## Defy your Destiny with Berserk Weapon Cosmetics

For the duration of the Struggler’s Path, you’ll be able to earn exclusive Berserk themed weapon cosmetics lethal enough to challenge the fiercest demons of Sanctuary. Eight skins are available to claim, one for each class.

Log in during the event to select your preferred tool of demonic dismemberment, the first of which will be specific to your class. Weapon skins available during the Struggler’s Path include the fearsomely massive Dragon Slayer greatsword!
