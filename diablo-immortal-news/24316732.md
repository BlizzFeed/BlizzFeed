## A Stranger Arrives in Westmarch

Cloaked in mystery (and a literal cloak), a stranger has arrived in Westmarch, muttering of ridiculous secrets and utter madness. An earthy smell wafts behind them wherever they tread. He whispers nonsense of disturbances in the rift, of bizarre invaders in the dark dungeons of the world, of strangely dressed hoarders of secret treasures wandering the wilds, and of a crown taken by an “unworthy tyrant”.

Clearly, this mysterious stranger cannot be trusted. Reports say that he’s been seen in the streets, squares, and alcoves of the city. Avoid him at all costs and ignore his lunacy.

## Season 3 of Rift Racer Challenge is Here

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/yx/YXJLGCVXCSX41793404402065.jpg)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/yx/YXJLGCVXCSX41793404402065.jpg)

From November 4-November 8, Rift Racer returns with updates to make the challenge easier to dive into, and more competitive across a wider range of players. Take on the challenge solo, compete for the fastest clear times, and earn valuable rewards.

### Difficulty Update

Five difficulties are available this season, choose a difficulty that suits your Combat Rating.

### Class-Based Leaderboards

Leaderboards are now separated by class, so you’ll compete for the fastest times against other players of the same class. If you make it to the top of some cross-server class leaderboards, you can earn limited-time exclusive Titles, materials, Set and Legendary items, currency, and more.

### Difficulty-Based Mechanics

Special mechanics are introduced at higher difficulties, dialing the intensity and skill needed as you progress.

### Returning Encounters

Goblins from the original Rift Racer return, while Nightmare difficulty brings back the Cross from Rift Racer Season 2 as the final boss to conquer.

### Reward Improvements

The cross-server leaderboard rewards have been expanded to 12 tiers. The top 50% of participating players will receive rewards. Additionally, the top-ranked player for each class will also receive the Rift Racer Champion Title, which lasts for 90 days.

Rewards will be distributed on November 9 during daily reset at 3:00 a.m. server time. Earn experience, currency, materials, items, along with additional bonuses available during the first few clears.

See the Rift Racer event page in-game for full details.

## Feature Updates

**War Games Updates**

We’ve added several new custom settings to War Games called Character Settings. Within the new Character Settings, the lobby host will be given the option to enable or disable some combat systems and even set upper limits for some stats. These settings will apply to all characters within the match, allowing you to create unique rules for your own War Games.

Character Settings include:

- Enable/Disable Familiars as well as setting upper limits for the number of active familiars
- Enable/Disable Awakened Legendary Gems
- Attribute Limits sets the upper limit for each of the following stats individually:
  - Resonance
  - Combat Rating
  - Life
  - Damage
  - Potency
  - Resistance
  - Armor Penetration
  - Armor

Note that these changes are temporary, so we can help evaluate their performance before deciding whether to make them permanent. These settings will be available from Nov 4, 2026, to Feb 17, 2027.

**Battleground Matchmaking and Skill Rating Improvements**

We’ve made several adjustments to the Battleground matchmaking rating (MMR) system to improve how your skill is evaluated, and to support more balanced matches over time.

These changes are intended to provide a more accurate foundation for matchmaking, particularly as skill ratings become more established throughout each season.

These improvements are being made in two key areas.

1. **More Accurate Skill Evaluation**: Refining how matchmaking ratings are calculated to better reflect your competitive performance and reduce unintended rating inflation.

**2. Improved Rating Consistency** Adjusting how ratings are updated following wins and losses, allowing the system to more consistency distinguish between players of different skill levels as the season progresses.

These changes are intended to provide a more accurate foundation for matchmaking, particularly as your skill ratings become more established throughout each season.

We do recognize that matchmaking quality depends on factors such as regional player populations, queue times, and player availability. We’ll continue monitoring match balance and matchmaking performance across different regions and time periods to identify further opportunities for improvement.

**Clearer Class Identity with Collaboration Avatars**

To help improve class readability and identification, we’re adding class icons next to your avatars in screens such as the Leaderboards, Party Finder, PvP, and more.

This should help you quickly identify player classes when queuing for activities, especially when they’re using avatars that don’t belong to a specific class.

**Auxiliary Gem Updates**

Auxiliary Gems can now be active above Rank 1 but each gem’s quality determines the maximum Rank it can be. With these changes, we aim to smooth the transition from older gems to newer gems. This also helps preserve one’s previous investments while also maintaining the value of higher quality Legendary Gems.

- A socketed dormant Legendary Gem can still have a Rank 1 Auxiliary Gem
- A 1-Star Auxiliary Gem can have a max Rank of 5
- A 2-Star Auxiliary Gem can have a max Rank of 6
- A 2/5-Star Auxiliary Gem can have a max Rank of 6
- A 3/5-Star Auxiliary Gem can have a max Rank of 7
- A 4/5-Star Auxiliary Gem can have a max Rank of 8
- A 5/5-Star Auxiliary Gem can have a max Rank of 9

The maximum Rank of an Auxiliary Gem cannot exceed the socketed Primary Gems Rank.

> **Developer’s Note:** *Auxiliary Gems still do not contribute Gem Power to empowering the socketed Legendary Gem like Resonance Gems do.*

**Spectral Allies for Difficulty 1-3 for Vanguard**

In difficulty 1-3 of Vanguard, we’ve added a button so that you can add Spectral Allies to your party manually. This change does not apply to difficulty level 4 of Vanguard; you’ll still need a full party.

## Bug Fixes

- Fixed an issue where the skill radius of the Barbarian Legendary Essence Gilded Hunter's Demoralize did not match its visual effect radius.
- Fixed an issue where the Barbarian Legendary Essence Polished Pestles' Leap Attack sometimes failed to stun enemies.
- Fixed an issue where the Monk Legendary Essence Clashing Sky's Kick could be interrupted by the Monk's own movement commands under high-latency conditions.
- Fixed an issue where the Crusader Legendary Essence Storm Saddle's Draw and Quarter effect sometimes failed to initiate a charge.
- Fixed an issue where the Crusader Legendary Essence Ordhelm' Holy Banner was incompatible with the Legendary Essences Lustre Titanic and Torrential Refrain.
- Fixed an issue where the Necromancer's Skeletal Mage failed to damage training dummies when using the Legendary Essence Black Wall together with Calcified Celerity.
- Fixed an issue where the slow effect applied by the Necromancer's Bone Wall from the Legendary Essence Gyreplate had an incorrect duration.
- Fixed an issue where the Tempest Legendary Essence Unmoored Eyes' Blade Dance sometimes failed to hit enemies.
- Fixed an issue where the Warlock Legendary Essence Watch Them Writhe's knockback range was incorrect when the skill was cast near the edge of terrain.
