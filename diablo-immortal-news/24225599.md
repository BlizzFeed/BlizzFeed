## Join the Bout of Realms

The bravest fighters of Sanctuary gather in one unified purpose, to determine who among them can crown themselves champion. The Bout of Realms is a multistage Tower War PvP competition that pits teams of 8 against other teams across servers within their home region. The winners of each region will earn themselves the eternal glory of being crowned the ultimate victor of the Bout of Realms, as well as earning illustrious cosmetic rewards, a slew of Legendary Crests, and more.

From **August 13–August 17, 3 a.m.**, Register for Bout of Realms by forming a party of up to 8 players from within your server. Party members can be swapped at any time within the registration period. Once registration has ended on **August 17**, parties are locked and cannot be changed. Choose your teammates wisely, for they will be your key to success.

From **August 18–August 22**, each team will participate in a total of 15 matches for Preliminaries. These take place each day local server time at:

- 9 p.m.
- 9:30 p.m.
- 10 p.m.

This is 5 days of 3 back-to-back battles, so be prepared before you commit yourself to a team. Your party will be counting on you.

Preliminary matches grant:

- 3 points for a win
- 1 point for a loss
- 0 points for a forfeit

Ties are decided by the number of kills made during the matches. Participating in the Preliminaries earns you a Legendary item, Gold, scrap and Dust. Once Preliminaries have concluded the top scoring teams will advance. Up to 64 teams will move forward to the Finals, depending on server size.

The Finals commence from **August 25–August 29**, where 64 teams across servers compete for the top 4 spots in a best of three elimination match each night at **9 p.m. local server time**. The winners advance to the next night, while the losers will be eliminated. After determining the winners of the Final 2 teams, the winners will progress to the Championship Match, while the runner ups will compete in the Third-Place Match.

The Third Place Match takes place on **August 30, 9 p.m.** local server time. The teams fighting for the Third Place finish will battle it out in a best of 5 format to determine the winner.

The Championship Match, the *final* showdown, takes place on **August 31** at **9 p.m.** local server time. The top 2 teams will battle it out in a best of five format against their designated opponents. The winning team in the Championship Match will be crowned the Champion, while the losing team will be runner-up.

## New Legendary Affixes

Wield these new Legendary Items to strike down the Burning Hell’s Legions with ferocious intent. These new Items can only be found in Inferno IV and higher.

#### Barbarian

- Helmet Splitter (Off-Hand): Wrath of the Berserker now increases your Movement Speed, greatly reduces damage taken, and reduces the duration of loss of control.
- Hunter's Snare (Chest): Chained Spear now empowers you to counterattack with spears when you take damage, pulling in enemies.
- Silvered Dragoon (Helm): Demoralize now empowers you to counterattack with a roar when you take damage, reducing enemy Attack Speed.
- Stone and Kettle (Shoulders): Wrath of the Berserker also reduces damage taken.
- Trapping's Spoils (Pants): Chained Spear also heals you for a percentage of its damage done.
- Tundra Burr (Main Hand): When Demoralize deals damage, it also reduces an enemy's damage for a limited-time, up to a maximum.

#### Blood Knight

- Chimeric Favor (Pants): Abomination gains an additional Skill, Blood Mirage. Blood Mirage consumes your Life to conjure an illusion of yourself that mimics your Primary Attack.
- Gnarly Gullet (Shoulders): Abomination no longer consumes Anger to transform and its Primary Attack now consumes Life to throw globs of gore at enemies.
- Mangler's Grove (Main Hand): Abomination also increases your Attack Speed by a percentage of maximum Life lost, up to a maximum buff for increased Attack Speed.
- Quenched Votary (Helm): Transfusion shields also increase your Life Drain by a percentage.
- Rose Toll (Off-Hand): Abomination Primary Attacks have a chance to trigger a bloody claw that deals additional damage. The bloody claw's chance to trigger increases as your remaining Life decreases, up to a maximum.
- Skin Upon Steel (Chest): Transfusion now grants only you a shield and while it remains active all Life Drain restores the absorb value of the shield instead of healing you. Transfusion will be automatically activated when your Life is low.

#### Crusader

- The Chainkeeper (Shoulders): Sacred Chain now empowers you for a limited time, during which holy links will form between you and nearby enemies, continually damaging them.
- Harmonic Collide (Chest): Condemn now empowers you, causing lightning to strike enemies after they are attacked a few times.
- Fugitive Daunt (Pants): Sacred Chain also disables enemy Dash skills for a limited time.
- Laden Axiom (Main Hand): Sweep Attack now swings two mystical chains towards each other in an arc, gathering enemies in the middle and Stunning them.
- Lash Recital (Helm): When Sacred Chain damages an enemy multiple times within a limited time, they are dealt additional damage.
- Vilification Protocol (Off Hand): Condemn also reduces enemy Movement Speed.

#### Demon Hunter

- Bewildering Arc (Main Hand): Smoke Screen also increases your Attack Speed for a limited time.
- Fume Threads (Chest): Smoke Screen now empowers you to launch grenades at nearby enemies as you move. Dash skills have a reduced distance requirement to trigger grenades.
- Let Them Sulk (Helm): Daring Swing cooldown is reduced by seconds for each enemy it hits, up to a maximum duration.
- Manymark (Pants): Spinning Chakram also applies a Chakram Mark for a limited time when it hits an enemy. When marked enemies are hit multiple times they are dealt additional damage (Cannot trigger more often that once every few seconds to the same enemy).
- Nimble Rival (Shoulders): Daring Swing now fires a hook in a direction that locks onto the ground and then pulls you along its path, damaging enemies and making you immune to knockback and loss of control as you travel.
- Supply Everlasting (Off Hand): Spinning Chakram now empowers you to launch chakrams at nearby enemies as you move. Dash skills have a reduced distance requirement to trigger chakrams.

#### Druid

- Cackling Frenzy (Pants): Rend and Tear (Werewolf) also increases your Life Drain against enemies hit for a limited-time, stacking up to a maxmium.
- Nyteflurry (Main Hand): Raven Swarm now empowers you, causing your Primary Attacks and Skills to conjure ravens that automatically attack enemies, who are Blinded when ravens hit them multiple times.
- Kiegfran (Shoulders): Raven Swarm also generates Primal Power when it hits an enemy, but this cannot occur more often than once every so often.
- Sharval's Blessing (Chest): While a Werewolf, you gain additional Life Drain against enemies below 50% Life.
- Wildbrute (Helm): Lunge (Werewolf) now causes you to flicker out of sight, becoming untargetable as you Dash forward damaging enemies along your path. Lunge can be used multiple times in quick succession.
- Wulftear (Off Hand): Rend and Tear (Werewolf) is now a series of slashing claw attacks that damage and Stun enemies. Using other skills beforehand increases the number of attacks.

- Lethal Embrace (Shoulders): Transforming into a Werewolf increases your Evasion Rating for a few seconds for every Primal Power you currently have, up to a maximum.
- Lunar Cradle (Main Hand): Summon Wolves also weaken nearby enemies, increasing the damage they take from you.
- Ravenous Wilds (Off Hand): Thorn Armor damage also shatters enemy armor, increasing the Critical Hit Damage they take for a limited time, up to a maximum.

- Veiled Vice (Helm): Howl (Werewolf) also applies Fear to enemies. Enemies below a percentage of Life are Feared for a limited time. Cannot Fear the same enemy more often than once every few seconds.

#### Monk

#### Wizard

- The Greebluk (Off Hand): Ice Armor now grants you invisibility, only revealing you if an enemy gets close.

## Balance Changes

The Balance changes below will be effective everywhere in Diablo Immortal.

#### Barbarian

- Terror Unburdened (Demoralize): The beneficial effects provided by Demoralize will now be disabled when the uncontrollable state is interrupted by another skill.

> **Developer’s Note:** *Instead of breaking the interaction entirely, we are disabling the benefits provided by Terror Unburdened when you have control of your character. The benefits of damage reduction, immunity to knockback, and were all meant to counteract that you couldn’t control your character. Considering you can control your character, these effects will be removed in this instance.*

#### Druid

- Werebear and Werewolf (Baseline): The transformations can now trigger while under the effects of total loss of control or knockback. Now drains 4 Primal Power per second down from 5.

> **Developer’s Note:** *We heard the feedback that maintaining transformation time was one of the more frustrating aspects of the Druid class, so we are lowering the Primal Power costs to allow you to remain in forms longer. Additionally, we recognize the frustration of not being able to transform when suffering crowd control effects or your transformations being interrupted by them, so you should now be able to transform more freely.*

#### Wizard

- Coat of the Astrum (Lightning Nova):
  - Lightning Nova projectiles will now disappear when the Wizard dies.
  - Lightning Nova projectiles will now prefer staying closer to the Wizard.
  - Lightning Nova deals reduced damage to enemies further away from the Wizard.

> **Developer’s Note:** *The wild arcing Lightning Nova provided by this affix was too dominant, especially given the introduction of the new Skybreaker set. The range of the effects made it difficult for enemies to fight Wizard characters, and even if you killed them, their Lightning Nova would persist and still kill you.\
> These changes are aimed to force Wizards into more dangerous situations to maintain pressure and keep up the high damage. We will continue to monitor other synergies of this build after these changes have gone live to see if further adjustments need to be made.*

#### Sets

- Skybreaker's Bolt 2 Set Bonus: If an enemy suffers from this effect again within the 6-second cooldown the duration will be reduced.

> **Developer’s Note:** *Multiple characters running this set effect lead to 100% uptime providing excessive value to other players, so to counter this, the effect will now have its own diminishing returns within the 6-second window.*

## Feature Updates

#### Set Bonus UI Updates

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/PTQRN7LJEO601754701781723.jpg)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/PTQRN7LJEO601754701781723.jpg)

The Equipment Powers interface has been tweaked to provide a snapshot of all equipped Set Item abilities at a glance. If you prefer the expanded view, you can still access it by selecting the ‘See All’ button.

#### AFK Farming Damage Re-enable

To help discourage AFK farming, we are reintroducing the acid pits that will spawn and kill players if too much time is spent away from their keyboard.

#### Familiar Swap Button Change

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/BT439VHILL9P1754701780458.jpg)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/BT439VHILL9P1754701780458.jpg)

We’ve added a button within the Familiars UI so that you can quickly swap a Bonded Familiar into the Companion Slot. Select a Bonded familiar and click the crossed swords next to **Remove** to swap with the Companion familiar.

#### Loyalty Bonus Reset Time Optimization

Loyalty Points will now reset and be converted into Legacy Loyalty Points when we introduce new Legendary Equipment instead of when the server reaches the Paragon Level for a new difficulty.

#### Season 14 Challenge Rifts

Challenge Rift levels are increasing from 550 to 600 and the Combat Rating requirements for 530 to 550 have been lowered. Additionally, the server first clear for the highest Challenge Rift level will record the top 3 players instead of only the first player to complete the Challenge Rift.

#### Damage Changes in Inferno I-VI

We are lowering the damage for the open world, hidden lairs, Helliquary bosses, and normal dungeons for Inferno I-VI by about 20%.
