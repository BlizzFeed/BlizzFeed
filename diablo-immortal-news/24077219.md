As with previous content updates, there will be server maintenance starting on **April 9, from 5 p.m.–7 p.m. PST** for Oceania, China, East Asia, Southeast Asia, and Europe servers, and on **April 10, from 1 a.m.–3 a.m. PST** for servers in the Americas. After the maintenance has concluded, all items mentioned below will be live—specific dates for any features going live afterward are provided below.

## Survivor’s Bane Updates

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/O3V5F2COHBJY1712353352732.jpg)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/O3V5F2COHBJY1712353352732.jpg)

Survivor’s Bane will feature some new variations when playing in Endless mode. We’re introducing a new system where you’ll receive a random buff at the start of the match to help aid you in vanquishing hellspawn.

We’re also introducing two new hellishly augmented versions of Survivor’s Bane which greatly increases the overall challenge. Legions increases enemy spawn rate by 100%, and experience earned by 50%. The second, Stampede, increases enemy density by 50%, and enemy movement speed by 25%.

New achievements will be yours to unlock once you’ve mastered these new modes by crushing demons in Survivor’s Bane. Partaking in these new modes will also earn you increased rewards from rising to meet this challenge with a fervor only Sanctuary’s finest could muster.

## Events Center Rewards Aplenty

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/NJGGW7MK3CDL1712353351900.jpg)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/NJGGW7MK3CDL1712353351900.jpg)

We are introducing an all-new Events Center to give you increased rewards for your various exploits in Sanctuary on a regular, rotating basis. The first cycle will be from **April 19—May 21**, play through the limited-time events to earn even more rewards. By finishing tasks and conquering the legions of the Burning Hells, you’ll earn **Seals of Triumph**, which will reward you in kind for the sheer number of deeds you can attribute to your name.

To see the full list of eligible events that reward Seals of Triumph, see the bottom- left side of the Events tab.

Participate as you would normally to earn these rewards,; the more events you complete, the more spoils shall be yours:

- 1-Star Legendary Gems
- Normal Gems
- Telluric Pearls
- Legendary Items
- A unique Avatar frame
- Aspirant’s Keys

## Returning Events

#### Wild Brawl

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/GGDYXCDXA7AM1712353353336.jpg)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/GGDYXCDXA7AM1712353353336.jpg)

Enlist your most trusted companions and outlast enemy teams in the Wild Brawl event. In this unique, limited-time PVP event from **April 12, 3 a.m.–April 19, 2024, 3 a.m. server time**, players start at Level 1 and work together in teams of six to explore an expansive map and endure to become the last surviving team. Equip discovered gear, gain experience to strengthen your power, and slay everything in sight to win the battle. This arena style game mode ends when one team has outlasted the others to make their eternal mark on Sanctuary. For more information about Wild Brawl, please visit [this article](https://news.blizzard.com/diablo-immortal/23985784/).

#### Survivor’s Bane

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/ZX5Q8E4T4XGB1712353353036.jpg)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/ZX5Q8E4T4XGB1712353353036.jpg)

In Survivor’s Bane, you’ll begin each run without your character’s items and a random Skill from their Class. From **April 19, 3 a.m.–April 26, 3 a.m. server time** you can vanquish demons across three levels in succession—Shassar Sea, Ashwold Cemetary, and Frozen Tundra—to earn rewards and achievements.

As you move throughout the map, any Skills you have equipped will be automatically cast for you. Smiting Hell’s minions will grant you useful experience orbs that level up your character. Each time you level up, you’ll be presented with the option to equip a new Skill, up to six total can be equipped—the Skill you’re presented with can be from any Class regardless of your character’s Class—or to upgrade an equipped Skill. For more information about Survivor’s Bane, please visit [this article](https://news.blizzard.com/diablo-immortal/24021895/).

For more information on the new Survivor’s Bane updates, you can view those [here](#Survivor).

## Feature Updates

#### Switch the New Magic Affix while Class Change

Legendary Items earned from Terror Rifts, Oblivion Pillars, and Hellslayer Bounties with Class specific Affixes will update when changing Classes. If you’ve obtained any of these items with a Class specific Affix, your Item will shift to a different Class specific Affix upon changing to a new Class. These Affixes are directly tied across Classes, an Affix on one Class will always update to the same Affix on a different Class.

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/RIRR3RBJ8Z891712353351584.jpg)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/RIRR3RBJ8Z891712353351584.jpg)

#### Battleground Compensation for AFK Players

For players that lose a Battleground match after a teammate is removed for inactivity or a disconnection, we’re compensating the loss of the match with a slight reimbursement of overall points.

#### Necromancer Legendary Affix Changes

The cooldown for the Corpse Lance Emperor of Worms Legendary Affix effect has been increased from 3 to 6 seconds. When striking an enemy, they’ll only be affected by the Immobilizing effect every 6 seconds instead of 3.

Corpse Lance now also reduces the Movement Speed of any enemies it strikes and immobilizes to prevent any dash skill for any enemy hit by a Lance 4 times for 4.8 seconds.

- Level 1: Immobility 2.4 seconds
- Level 2: Immobility 2.8 seconds
- Level 3: Immobility 3.2 Seconds
- Level 4: Immobility 3.6 Seconds

> **Developer’s Note:** *Considering the cooldown for Corpse Lance at level 4 was 3 seconds and stunned enemy players for 2.4 seconds, players were essentially able to stun lock their opponents with relative ease. This change to cooldown should allow for a fairer fight.*

#### Item Stack Inventory Changes

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/8WPFNL8VEC1B1712353352419.jpg)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/8WPFNL8VEC1B1712353352419.jpg)

Certain items now stack to higher amounts per space in your inventory to save space. The following items now stack further:

- Aspirant’s keys increased from 99 to 9,999 per slot.
- All types of Crests, increased from 99 to 9,999 per slot.
- Upgrade materials such as Scrap, increased from 9,999 to 99,999 per slot.
