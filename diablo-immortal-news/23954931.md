# We’re Raising Hell—Diablo Immortal is Turning One

- URL: https://news.blizzard.com/en-gb/article/23954931/were-raising-helldiablo-immortal-is-turning-one
![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/c1/C1GPJCIKPFFU1684343838977.jpg)

> The Gates of Hell are opening wide for the one-year anniversary of Diablo Immortal! Over the next two months, adventurers can expect a new Zone, Class, celebratory events, updates to existing features, and so much more.

---

Welcome, adventurer.

On June 2, the fiery pits of Hell will rejoice—Diablo Immortal turns one! This milestone could not have been achieved without your continuous support, and to honor you, we have a bloody wave of new additions hitting the shores of Sanctuary **over the next two months**.

It’s time to gaze into the flames and provide a bird’s-eye view of the **upcoming Diablo IV Launch Celebration x Diablo Immortal event and Lilith-themed Cosmetics, new Zone**, and **new Class**.

## Diablo IV Launch Celebration x Diablo Immortal

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/H0U52H1O8XY81684193799488.jpg)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/H0U52H1O8XY81684193799488.jpg)

The creation of Sanctuary by Inarius and Lilith is a storied tale; its impact can still be felt today. The **Merciless Monstrosity event, Children of Lilith Battle Pass**, and **Hatred’s Heritage Phantom Market** will be experienceable expressions of Sanctuary’s matriarch. Your opportunity to let sin flow freely will arrive in **early June**.

## Discover the Cradle of Sanctuary

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/4ZGYDVOU4F3F1684193799495.jpg)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/4ZGYDVOU4F3F1684193799495.jpg)

Your pursuit of the Cult of Terror will lead you and Zatham to a mystical island inhabited by a reclusive people—the locals refer to it as **Ancients’ Cradle**. In **mid-June**, you’ll embark on the next chapter of the Main Questline, and discover a new PvP Zone Event, Side Quests, Bounties, and enemy types along the way.

## A Monstrous New Class

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/c6/C6IREHOB9NPW1684343986421.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/c6/C6IREHOB9NPW1684343986421.png)

We’re nearly ready to **release a new Class** with which you can defend the world of Sanctuary. This Class bleeds new blood into the Diablo universe with its appearance in Immortal and favors a mid-range playstyle using a classic weapon type. The team is brimming with excitement, and we can’t wait for you to assume this new fantasy in **July**.

## Looking Forward

Thank you for reading through a small slice of what we have in store for Diablo Immortal over the next two months. We’ll provide more detail on each item mentioned as they are revealed in future content and major updates.

We’ll see you in Sanctuary!\
