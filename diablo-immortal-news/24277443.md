# Introducing Diablo Immortal’s Newest Class: Warlock

- URL: https://news.blizzard.com/en-gb/article/24277443/introducing-diablo-immortals-newest-class-warlock
![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/ro/ROHHTAKLC0W81781052690007.jpg)

> The new Warlock class enters the fight for Sanctuary in Diablo Immortal on June 17. Learn about its origins, skills, Legendary items, and more.

---

Vizjerei summoners brought darkness to Sanctuary when they conjured demons from the Burning Hells to serve and empower them. For that offense, the Vizjerei were stripped of their titles, forbidden to practice demonology, and branded as the worst and vilest humans alive after the Mage Clan Wars. But the Mage Clan Wars were a long time ago, and Sanctuary’s memory is as short as the lives of its people.

Though the Vizjerei no longer practice demonology, some of their tomes and writings survived confiscation by the Viz-Jaq’Taar Assassins. In the current, frightful age, lists of demons’ true names and old clan practices are being discovered by hedge mages and upstarts. Those who wield the forbidden powers of demonology—summoning and control of the Burning Hells—share the Vizjerei tolerance for risk and earn the contemptible name “Warlock.” In their ambition to make history and persevere in a devastated world, a Warlock compromises nearly everything about themselves.

As part of Diablo’s 30th Anniversary and the upcoming Bloodied Jewel (Patch 5.0) Major Update, the Warlock joins the fray as Diablo Immortal’s 10th class – a summoner of demons, master of portals, and wielder of Hell’s dark power.

On **June 16**, we’ll release a full content update with everything that will accompany the Warlock’s rise on **June 17, 2026**, so stay tuned!

***As this article is an early look at the Warlock, information conveyed in this article may be subject to change.***

## Origin of the Warlock: Power’s Price

"Power's Price" is a new origin quest accessible to any players interested in trying out the Warlock class before creating a new character or changing their class.

This quest will place you in the boots of a novice warlock and let you try out the basics of the class's summons and hellish magic. You'll race against a demonic adversary to recover lost knowledge from a ruined Vizjerei mage tower. Learn to command demons, hurl Hellfire, and even bind the mighty Soulgorger. But be warned: such dark power always comes at a cost.

## Embrace the Path of the Warlock

Players will be able to experience the Warlock in multiple ways:

- **Roll Fresh or Switch:** Create a new character or use Class Change and experience the Warlock.
- **Warlock Origin Quest:** Begin the journey of a Warlock in the Origin Quest, Power's Price, and learn to wield powerful demonic magics. Start the quest through the class change menu at the Shifting Flames in Westmarch, or through the Warlock Event Hub, Command the Hells.
- **Fractured Plane:** Play as a Warlock in the Fractured Plane event, a solo, roguelike limited-time event where you gain randomized Legendary essences and fight through the shifting corridors of your mind. This is a great place to try out all the Warlock's Legendary items, fine-tune your playstyle, and experience Hellish new essences.
- **Warlock Trial Dungeon:** Select from three pre-set builds and enter a trial dungeon based on Mad King’s Breach with full stats and pre-selected gems to experience the full potential of the Warlock.
- **Warlock Race:** Engage in the limited- time speedrun event to master Warlock builds, with the opportunity to win exclusive speedrun portals.

## Fight Demons With Demons in Sanctuary

With demons, Hellfire, brimstone, and shadows at their command, Warlocks have the potential to become Sanctuary’s greatest saviors or its worst nightmares unleashed.

#### Passive Ability

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/KE67SM1BDNV11781050668230.png)](#)

**Soulgorger:** Summon a primordial demon to fight by your side that is immune to total loss of control and knockback effects. The Soulgorger's primary attacks include claw swipes and a flame breath, and it can unleash these additional abilities:

- **Pummeling Eruption:** Slam the ground with extreme force, causing it to fracture. Enemies within the area are Stunned for a few seconds and take damage, then after a short delay the ground erupts with Hellfire, dealing additional damage to enemies within the area and knocking them back.
- **Voracious Sacrifice:** When your life drops below a threshold, your Soulgorger sacrifices a portion of its current Life to restore Life to you.
- **Ferocious Leap:** Command your Soulgorger to leap to a target.
- **Devour:** Your Soulgorger devours some of your other demons when they die, causing it to evolve and gain additional powers corresponding to the devoured demon. Up to two Devour effects can be active at one time.

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/FOSGDP9LQUGQ1781052349978.gif)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/FOSGDP9LQUGQ1781052349978.gif)

#### Primary Attacks

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/6YD1I6IWMVZ81781050670576.png)](#)

**Lunatic Rush:** Summon a Fallen Lunatic that rushes enemies, exploding on contact, dealing damage to nearby enemies.

**Lunatic Legion (Ultimate of Lunatic Rush):** Enhance Lunatic Rush for a few seconds, causing it to open a larger rift that summons 3 Fallen Lunatics to rush enemies, exploding on contact, dealing damage to nearby enemies.

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/J88KP9U25AWU1781050670089.png)](#)

**Hellfire Bolt (Unlocks at Level 34):** Throw a bolt of Hellfire that deals damage to enemies it strikes. You can move and attack at the same time, but your Movement Speed is reduced.

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/R8TPRO1ECG641781052346820.gif)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/R8TPRO1ECG641781052346820.gif)

**Rain of Chaos (Ultimate of Hellfire Bolt):** Enhance Hellfire Bolt for a few seconds, causing meteors of Hellfire to rain from the sky around you, dealing damage to enemies they strike. Additionally, your Primary Attacks call down larger meteors at a target location, dealing damage to enemies in the area.

#### Other Skills

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/KNZJTVH5MO6Q1781050670479.png)](#)

**Lash of Pain:** Rip molten chains from the ground, dealing damage to enemies within the area and knocking them up before swinging the blazing chains down on to them, dealing additional damage. If a demon under your control is within the area, the chains will strike them, dealing damage equal to a portion of their maximum Life and whipping them into a frenzy, increasing their Attack Speed and damage for a few seconds.

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/T5EOGREPSWHV1781052347317.gif)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/T5EOGREPSWHV1781052347317.gif)

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/EF3H74EORHGB1781050670089.png)](#)

**Hellswarm:** Unleash a swarm of Hellspawn that deals damage to enemies in their path and also embeds an infernal plague within them, dealing additional damage over time. Infected enemies that are slain hatch a Plagued Thornstrider that attacks enemies, dealing damage and inflicting the same plague with each attack. You can control up to 3 Parasitic Crawlers at one time and any additional hatchlings are consumed by your Soulgorger, healing it.

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/8UCLBWIMGS5D1781050669676.png)](#)

**Demonic Portal (Unlocks at Level 3):** Open a portal, allowing you to summon demons directly from Hell that fight by your side.

- **Bloodlord**
  - Charges forward, dealing damage and knocking back enemies in its path before following up with an overhead slam that deals damage and Stuns enemies within the area for a few seconds.
  - Devour: Your Soulgorger grows horns and gains the ability to charge forward, dealing damage and knocking back enemies in its path and dealing additional damage to nearby enemies at the end of the dash.
- **Demonic Soldier**
  - 3 Warriors emerge from the portal at a target location to aid you in battle for a short while. They use basic attacks to damage enemies.
- **Rasplet Hulk**
  - Emerges at target location, unleashing a series of strikes that deal damage and cause enemies to Bleed. The first strike also Stuns enemies for a few seconds.
  - Devour: Your Soulgorger grows thorned blades on its forearms, increasing its Primary Attack range and damage while also inflicting a Bleed. The series of attacks ends with a dash forward that deals damage.

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/0S0BBYTAPQMO1781052342835.gif)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/0S0BBYTAPQMO1781052342835.gif)

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/W8F7B8HSYQ0M1781050669374.png)](#)

**Burning Ascent (Unlocks at Level 8):** Summon a Hellflyer to breathe flames down on enemies within its path. As it passes by, you leap up grabbing its claws to catch a ride; at the end of the attack, you dismount the Hellflyer and gain increased Movement Speed for a few seconds.

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/FK9NMA3CZ8021781052343704.gif)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/FK9NMA3CZ8021781052343704.gif)

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/0FT3LGDKKOOC1781050669023.png)](#)

**Warp Strike (Unlocks at Level 15):** Command your Soulgorger to leap into a portal and exit at the target location, dealing damage to enemies within the area and knocking them up. Additionally, your Soulgorger's Attack Speed is increased for a few seconds.

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/2V76CY6Q6QZE1781050670158.png)](#)

**Infernal Eruption (Unlocks at Level 20):** Perform a destructive ritual at a target location, causing geysers of Hellfire to erupt randomly within the area, dealing damage to enemies and causing them to Burn. Charging longer makes the ritual location smaller, causing the eruptions to become more concentrated. If a demon under your control is hit by an eruption, they become engulfed in Hellfire, causing them to deal damage to nearby enemies every second and Burn them.

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/ARG9AN8RB6B51781052347116.gif)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/ARG9AN8RB6B51781052347116.gif)

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/OREXHHAW26XX1781050669284.png)](#)

**Brimstone Gateway (Unlocks at Level 28):** Open a portal at your current location and a target location, causing you to instantly traverse through Hell to the other portal. These connected portals will remain stable for a few seconds before they collapse, allowing you and allies to use them to instantly teleport to the other end. Portals can only be used once every few seconds.

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/23IB2NZM3YV61781050668257.png)](#)

**Spiteful Sacrifice (Unlocks at Level 38):** Empower yourself for a few seconds to spite your foes, causing your Primary Attacks to consume a portion of your current life, tearing open rifts in reality that unleash shadowy blasts that deal damage to their target and nearby enemies.

- **Sacrificial Power:** Rifts are now opened automatically, targeting random enemies with shadowy blasts that deal damage to their target and nearby enemies. The frequency of rifts opened increases with your Attack Speed.

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/ZPJQPD5CFPQX1781050669752.png)](#)

**Devouring Darkness (Unlocks at Level 41):** Create a spatial anomaly between Hell and a target location that pulls enemies to its center before releasing a series of shadowy explosions that deal damage to enemies within the area. Charging consumes a portion of your current Life, increasing the number of explosions.

- **Sacrificial Power:** Devouring Darkness is unleashed at maximum charge, dealing damage to enemies in the area and causing them to Burn.

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/42T3DYDNEIYW1781050668223.png)](#)

**Siphon Life (Unlocks at Level 44):** Perform a dark ritual, draining the Life from you or your Soulgorger to heal the other and empower them with increased damage.

- **Sacrificial Power:** You and your Soulgorger unleash a blast of dark magic, dealing damage to nearby enemies and restoring a portion of Life to each of you while also increasing the damage you both deal for a few seconds.

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/PPJ5B0XBGNMY1781050668928.png)](#)

**Tyrant's Grasp (Unlocks at Level 47):** A demonic hand reaches through a rift, grabbing enemies within the area, dealing damage to them. Enemies within the Tyrant's Grasp are immolated in flames, taking additional damage before the hand slams into the ground, dealing more damage to them and all enemies within the area.

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/DL3K6OAM8VKY1781052342727.gif)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/DL3K6OAM8VKY1781052342727.gif)

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/TMNM8D3Z1WT21781050669060.png)](#)

**Blood Offering (Unlocks at Level 50):** Blood Offering can be toggled on and off. While on, you forcefully extract a portion of Life from all your demons every second, causing them to explode, dealing damage to nearby enemies and inflicting a Bleed for additional damage.

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/ZZKZTPBMH03U1781050669572.png)](#)

**Demonic Pact (Unlocks at Level 53):** Sacrifice a portion of your current Life, signing a demonic pact to summon a Twisted Shaman for yourself and each nearby ally. Twisted Shaman hurl 3 bolts of Hellfire, dealing damage. Additionally, whenever the master of a Twisted Shaman falls below a threshold of maximum Life, their Twisted Shaman sacrifices itself to heal them and fulfill their pact.

## Wield Demonic Power Using Warlock Legendary Items

**Over 50 new Legendary items** are being released for the Warlock! We wanted to give you a variety of items to tinker with so you can get a sense of what the Class can do upon release. We love to see the creative builds the community cooks up and can’t wait for you to surprise us.

#### Main Hand

**Mordscrape:** Demonic Portal can now summon a Hellflyer that flaps its wings creating a vortex that follows enemies.

**Clawing End:** Siphon Life now binds your life in a ritual that accumulates each time you take damage. When the ritual is complete, you will be healed and gain increased Attack Speed and damage.

**Pointed Respite:** Demonic Portal can now summon a Fetish Chieftain, increasing the Primary Attack Speed of nearby summons while also healing them.

**Torment Anew:** Hellfire Bolt also Burns enemies, stacking.

**Cavalier Cutter:** If your Soulgorger would die, it instead goes into a blood frenzy, preventing its death for a few seconds while also increasing its damage and Movement Speed.

**Harsh Discipline:** Burning Ascent now empowers you with the demonic energy of a Hellflyer, breaking you free of total loss of control and silence effects while causing you to become untargetable and Dash forward, dealing damage to enemies in your path and granting you a shield when it ends.

**Cruentation Razor:** Siphon Life now reduces your Primary Attack damage to infuse your Soulgorger with its powers, increasing its Primary Attack Speed while also granting it additional powers based on your equipped Primary Attack.

**Crash and Burn:** Burning Ascent is now Channeled, allowing you to control the demon as it flies around the area unleashing blasts of wind below it, dealing damage to enemies within the area.

**Bloody Surrogate:** Siphon Life now sacrifices your Soulgorger to heal you and remove harmful effects and knockback effects.

**Bloodfray:** Lash of Pain also increases your Attack Speed for a few seconds when it Burns an enemy, stacking.

#### Off-Hand

**Flensed Skull of Klavar:** Demonic Portal can now summon a Doombound Cabalist that unleashes volleys of hellfire at enemies that explode on impact, dealing damage and causing them to Burn.

**Cruel Destination:** Spiteful Sacrifice's Critical Hit Chance is increased for a few seconds each time you take damage, stacking.

**Shornhollow:** Demonic Pact now sacrifices a portion of your Life to grant you and your summons increased damage and Movement Speed, but it also increases damage taken. Should any of your summons die, thier death will be briefly delayed.

**Jawless Grin:** Infernal Eruptions deals increased damage to Burning enemies.

**Muse of Blasphemies:** Warp Strike now commands your Soulgorger to leap through a portal, damaging enemies within the area and Stunning them with a follow-up tail sweep.

**Iron Reins:** Warp Strike now commands your Soulgorger to leap through a portal to a target location where it radiates demonic power, damaging, Stunning, and causing enemies within the area to take increased damage from you.

**Master's Furor:** Demonic Portal can now summon a Branded One at a target location that Stuns enemies before cloning itself, and then they all perform a dashing strike, damaging enemies within their path.

- Devour: Soulgorger's Primary Attacks are empowered, causing them to perform a dashing strike alongside 2 clones.

**Shadowed in Light:** Warp Strike now commands your demon into a portal. While inside the portal an echo of the Soulgorger strikes enemies whenever you deal damage to them.

**Gorewyrn:** Demonic Pact now heals you and nearby allies, purging loss of control and silence effects of those healed while also granting immunity to those effects and damage reduction for a short while.

**Dirty Tactics:** When one of your Fallen Lunatics explode and die, there is a chance it leaves behind a soul that is devoured by the Soulgorger, healing it and increasing its Attack Speed for a few seconds.

#### Chest

**Legion's Leavings:** Blood Offering now drains Life from your Soulgorger, creating a shadow copy of it with all the same effects it currently had active.

**The Dark Trade:** Spiteful Sacrifice's Critical Hits drain a portion of your current Life to increase its Critical Hit Damage. Cannot occur more often than once every second.

**Winnow Wrap:** Hellswarm also shatters the enemy's armor, increasing the damage they take for a few seconds, stacking.

**Watch Them Writhe:** Infernal Eruptions now conjures a sword that crashes down upon enemies and shatters the ground, dealing damage, knocking them up, and Burning enemies within the area.

**Heretic Pride:** Warp Strike also reduces the damage taken by your Soulgorger while also increasing its damage dealt.

**Fetter's Gift:** Spiteful Sacrifice's damage is increased and grants you Sacrificial Power if it is the only skill you have equipped that consumes Sacrificial Power.

**Under Pressure:** Blood Offering now empowers your Soulgorger for a short while, increasing its Critical Hit Chance and healing it periodically. If your Soulgorger is dead, you can also sacrifice a portion of your Life to summon it back immediately.

**Ashes of Liberty:** Infernal Eruption now conjures a ritual of flames around you that increases in intensity with your Movement Speed, dealing damage to enemies within the area and Burning them.

**Painstaking Experiment:** Lash of Pain's Critical Hit Chance is increased against enemies suffering from a continual damage effect.

**Total Annihilation:** Blood Offering now causes your summons to apply Oblation Marks to enemies they hit. When you hit a marked enemy, the mark detonates, dealing additional damage.

#### Legs

**Tattertooth Chausses:** Soulgorger's Devour also heals it and increases its Critical Hit Chance for a few seconds.

**Measured Halves:** Spiteful Sacrifice is now Channeled and can be cast while moving, causing you to conjure swords of dark energy that you hurl forward, damaging enemies in their path.

**Pawn Lure:** Blood Offering deals increased damage for each active summon, stacking.

**Rivenrile:** Lash of Pain also shatters the enemy's armor, increasing the damage they take, stacking.

**Woe Beclaimed:** Tyrant's Grasp now crushes enemies within a target location, damaging and preventing them from moving or attacking. All your focus is required, preventing you from moving or attacking as well.

**Pyrestorm:** Spiteful Sacrifice is now a Charge skill that drains your life. While charging it locks onto enemies within its range before releasing swords of dark energy at them, dealing damage and applying a Malignant Shadows curse that periodically deals damage.

**Helltracker:** Soulgorger deals increased Critical Hit Damage for each Devour evolution it has active.

**Margin of Error:** Infernal Eruption range is increased.

**Flood the Flames:** Infernal Eruption also Slows enemy Movement Speed for a few seconds, stacking.

**Supply and Demand:** Tyrant's Grasp now conjures a pair of demonic hands behind you that attack when you use your Primary Attack, dealing damage in an area in front of you.

#### Helmet

**Ridicule the Flames:** Soulgorger deals increased damage to Burning enemies.

**Giltjagg:** Siphon Life also increases your Movement Speed.

**Offal on the Spire:** Hellswarm now causes them to swarm around you. Periodically the Hellswarm will seek out nearby enemies, damaging and infesting them.

**Diabolist's Dream:** Brimstone Gateway now opens an unstable portal that teleports enemies trying to escape the area to its center, damaging and Stunning them.

**Vilest Freedom:** Brimstone Gateway now opens a portal that melds you with the shadows as you enter. You cannot be targeted or use attacks while melded, but you can use the skill again to exit the portal at a designated location.

**Hellscourge:** Hellswarm now empowers you, allowing you to swarm enemies with explosive locusts that apply harmful effects to enemies a second time when you apply them.

**Together in Peril:** Soulgorger takes reduced damage from players.

**Scarred Memories:** Burning Ascent's cooldown is reduced and restores energy based on the distance you travel.

**Split and Fracture:** Brimstone Gateway now allows you to place both portals down separately and at locations farther apart.

**Close Combat:** Tyrant's Grasp also reduces the damage enemies deal to you for a few seconds.

#### Shoulders

**Battleweight:** Soulgorger's Primary Attacks increase the damage enemies take from your summons, stacking. Maximum stacks are reduced against enemy players.

**Unholy Domain:** Devouring Darkness now drains your life to empower you for a short time, causing your damage to unleash blast waves of dark magic at your target's location, dealing damage to all surrounding enemies.

**Repeated Sins:** Soulgorger's summon cooldown is reduced and while on cooldown each summon that dies further reduces the remaining cooldown.

**Poppet Fingers:** Lash of Pain now conjures a demonic catapult that bombards enemies within an area, dealing damage and increasing the damage they take from Burning.

**Pit-Burnt Paulds:** Devouring Darkness now infuses dark magic into your Soulgorger, allowing them to shoot a beam across the ground that damages and knocks up enemy monsters. The knock up effect only affects monsters, not players.

**Necessary Nutrition:** Burning Ascent maximum charges increased.

**Living Armory:** Soulgorger's Critical Hit Chance is increased for a few seconds when taking damage, stacking.

**Impassioned Misery:** Burning Ascent's Critical Hit Chance is increased relative to your Movement Speed, stacking.

**Mourplume Shackles:** Demonic Pact cooldown reduced.

**Brutal Lessons:** Lash of Pain now empowers you, causing your Primary Attacks to swing molten chains that damage nearby enemies, knocks them back and Burns them.

## Warlock Class Gear Affixes

**Blood Offering:** Causes your demons to gain increased Primary Attack Speed for a few seconds when it is applied. Cannot occur more than once every few seconds.

**Brimstone Gateway:** Increases the Movement Speed of you or allies that use the portals for a few seconds.

**Burning Ascent:** Removes slow speed effects, then immunes the loss of control effects for a few seconds. Cannot occur more than once every few seconds.

**Demonic Pact:** Beneficial effects provided have their durations increased.

**Demonic Portal:** Has a chance to summon an additional demon that was not summoned.

**Devouring Darkness:** Range increased.

**Hellswarm:** Weakens enemies, reducing their Attack Speed for a few seconds, which stacks.

**Infernal Eruption:** Deals increased damage for each nearby Burning enemy or Burning summon you control.

**Lash of Pain:** Immobilizes enemies, preventing the use of Dash skills for a few seconds when striking them below a percentage of maximum Life. Cannot occur against the same target more than once every few seconds.

**Siphon Life:** Creates an aura of misery around the target for a few seconds that deals damage every second and Slows enemies. The same target cannot gain the aura more than once every few seconds.

**Spiteful Sacrifice:** Generates Sacrificial Power whenever it deals damage. Cannot occur more often than once every few seconds.

**Tyrant's Grasp:** Causes enemies to take increased damage for a few seconds after it inflicts them with a knockback or total loss of control effect.

**Warp Strike:** Cooldown reduced for each of your Soulgorger's attacks.

## Warlock Offensive and Defensive Stances

**Command:** Upon activation, you and your Summons’ Attack Speeds are increased by 20% for 3 seconds. After the initial duration ends, the increase is reduced to 10%.

**Restraint:** Upon activation, all nearby enemies are Stunned for 3 seconds. Your Primary Attacks now reduce the Movement Speed of enemies they hit by 40%. (Player Movement Speed is reduced by 20%.)
