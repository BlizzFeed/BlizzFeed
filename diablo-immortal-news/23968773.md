## Trial of the Hordes Challenge Event

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/GBYMN71O8L721687627282080.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/GBYMN71O8L721687627282080.png)

Experienced you may be, but emerging from the **Trial of the Hordes** victorious is the true mark of a champion. From **June 28, 3 a.m.–July 12, 3 a.m. server time** you can go head-to-head against another player while earning rewards and climbing your way through the event’s Ranks. Each player has an enclosed lane in which broods of demons will flood in waves—culling the demons from Hell with steadfast dedication is your goal.

As you put the sword to demons, more enemies will spawn on your opponent’s side and vice versa. Whoever can overwhelm their opponent with hell spawn—causing them to lose all their life—or has the most life by the match’s end, will win.

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/6SAC02J4JECI1687627283189.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/6SAC02J4JECI1687627283189.png)

Your journey in Trial of the Hordes is marked by the **Rank** system. As you win matches and increase your rank, you’ll not only earn rewards and enter new Rank tiers, but steadily climb your way up the Leaderboard. Here are some additional guidelines for Trial of the Hordes:

- Players will compete against other players of the same Class—time to showcase your prowess!
- Matchmaking for Trial of the Hordes is cross-server. We hope you’re ready for fiercer competition.
- Slain monsters can drop Combat Globes, use these to turn the tides of battle.
- Slicing through cabals of wicked demons will spawn vigorous Elite enemies on your opponent’s side.
- All gear, Legendary Gems, Badges, and other bonuses can be used.

A bounty of additional rewards can be yours if you **complete these daily tasks** while playing Trial of the Hordes:

- **Complete 1 match:** 3,000 Gold, 20 Scrap
- **Defeat 150 monsters:** 3,000 Gold, 20 Scrap
- **Pick up 5 Combat Globes:** 10,000 Gold, 20 Scrap
- **Defeat 1,000 monsters:** 10,000 Gold, 50 Scrap
- **Defeat 2,000 monsters:** 10,000 Gold, 50 Scrap
- **Win 3 matches:** 10,000 Gold, 80 Scrap, and 1 random Legendary Item

Strive for excellence; winning 3 matches will unlock an additional mystery task for those worthy enough. Grab your gear and get ready to put your skills to the test, adventurer!

## Feature Updates

#### Corvus Expedition

Corvus Expedition has been revamped in the following ways:

- Previously, 8 players were needed to begin an expedition—only 4 players are required now.
- Corvus Expeditions now reward more Gold, experience, and items.

#### Side Quests

The daily cap for Side Quests has been increased from 16 to 24.

#### Accursed Towers

We’re introducing some improvements to the map and map filter for Accursed Towers with the aim of providing players with increased information.

- Each tower will have a corresponding number that is displayed on the map to quickly identify them.
- The map filter will now display how many results your filter selection will yield prior to clicking or tapping the Ok button.
- Previously, the Contested, Occupied, and Unchallenged tower statuses were selected by default in the filter menu. However, this did not reflect the default state of the map, as the map by default shows all towers. Now, the filter opens with all tower statuses and buffs selected to accurately parallel what is shown by default on the map.

#### Clan and Shadow Clan Tabs

The number of towers a Clan currently occupies will now be displayed next to their name in the Clan and Shadow Clan tabs. This is so that players can make an informed decision to join a Clan based on their participation in Accursed Towers.

#### Dread Pilgrimage

A few quality-of-life updates have been implemented to streamline this event.

- You no longer need to use the Purify button to claim each reward—it will now be done automatically.
- The text descriptions for each challenge have been updated for additional clarity.

#### Hatred’s Heritage Phantom Market

The Hatred’s Heritage Phantom Market **will now end on July 12 at 3 a.m. server time** instead of July 6 at 3 a.m. server time. The new Class can don the Hatred’s Heritage Cosmetic Set, so we want to provide players with additional time to obtain it.
