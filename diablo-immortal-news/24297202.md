## New Zone Event – Twisting Torments

Twisting Torments is a new zone event set in the Rocky Waste subregion outside Lut Gholein. As Andariel and Duriel battle to see which evil reigns supreme, their hellish influence bleeds into Sanctuary. Minions of Pain and Anguish will swarm to sites of their master's power outside the city and be empowered with new affixes (such as the chains of Anguish and the maggot-spawn of the Pain Burrows) while this event is active. Defeating the empowered minions of Duriel and Andariel will provide increased rewards, including a weekly Legendary equipment drop.

## New Legendary Gems

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/1L7MXXL15CA11788911472865.jpg)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/1L7MXXL15CA11788911472865.jpg)

#### Enigma Latera (5 Star)

Periodically gain Enigma, causing your next Primary Attack to unleash Cruel Enigma that chains through nearby enemies. Cruel Enigma increases the damage you deal to afflicted enemies while also reducing the damage they deal to you, stacking for each beneficial effect they have. Additionally, Cruel Enigma siphons several seconds of one non-Skill beneficial effect from an afflicted enemy and grants you that effect for the same duration.

**Resonance Gems:** Tundra Blight, Fading Nostrum, Stubborn Oracle, Void Spark, Fated Trail

#### Prong Crag (2 Star)

While in combat you periodically gain Resolve for several seconds, increasing your damage done when you take damage from a single hit that exceeds a percentage of your maximum Life while also reducing your damage taken from that source, stacking. Additionally, Resolve increases your Critical Resistance.

**Resonance Gems:** Faltergrasp, Tundra Blight, Rampart Torch

## Battleground Updates

#### Battleground Theme Refresh

The demonic invasion is coming to an end.

With the battlefield no longer under demonic influence, Battlegrounds will **return** to the familiar Nephalem-themed visual style. Alongside this thematic refresh, we’re making several improvements to combat clarity and the overall Battleground experience based on player feedback.

While the demons may be retreating, not everything from the invasion is going away. Some of the changes players enjoyed will remain as Battlegrounds return to their Nephalem roots.

The Final Guardian buff is basically back to the previous version: defeating the Final Guardian grants the entire team *Nephalem Power*, allowing them to execute enemies below 20% Life, ignoring Undying, invulnerability, and similar effects.
