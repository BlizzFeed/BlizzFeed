## Find Power in Surrender—the Origin of the Talons of Ifeh Druids

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/3US7U616GIGD1750697934925.jpg)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/3US7U616GIGD1750697934925.jpg)

Deep within the Sharval Wilds are the **Talons of Ifeh**, a ferocious group of Druids capable of wielding wild, unpredictable, and uncontrollable magic. This ancient magic allows them to burn their enemies in rings of fire, shred skin with gravel bullets, and change their forms into that of beasts.

The Talons of Ifeh were led to Sharval long ago by **Ifeh, the First Archdruid of Sharval**, a member of the Druidic colleges of Scosglen. Ifeh was dissatisfied with the ways in which her peers sought power from the wilds. The other Druids believed that harmony with the land would lead to communion, but Ifeh instead surrendered her body and soul to the wilds. In return, she was granted a great yet ravenous power, one that would awaken the beast within.

Eventually, the bloodlust that would often overwhelm her in her hunts led to the murder of another Druid. She was banished from First College and exiled from her home. But Ifeh knew that there was another home for her and her students out there in Sanctuary. One that would welcome them.

Today, the Talons of Ifeh are few in number but mighty in strength. While most sequester themselves in the wilds, intent on protecting their own territory, some have heard nature’s call to defendall of Sanctuary from the demonic rot that threatens the land. The Talons of Ifeh fight with tooth and claw against the Burning Hells, and with each demon buried in the dirt, more life sprouts.

## Assume the Mantle of the Druid

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/SVJ7VDWCNTTZ1750697936080.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/SVJ7VDWCNTTZ1750697936080.png)

Starting on **July 3**, there will be **four ways** you can harness the power of nature as the Druid:

- Enter the **Primal Plane**, a three-week-long event like the Fractured Plane, but everyone plays as the Druid.
- Play the Druid Origin Quest, “Grovekeeper's Call,” and experience the Druid’s journey as they search for inner balance. Defend your land from demons, become a true Druid through a ritualistic battle, and learn about your role as nature’s guardian. Start the quest through Class Change or the Druid Event Hub.
- **Create a new character and select the Druid class.**
- Using **Class Change**, you can transfer your character from its existing Class to the Druid. Completing the Druid Origin Quest removes the Class Change Cooldown.

## Use Your Naturalistic Skills to Protect Sanctuary

With staff in hand, the Druid is a spellcaster who commands the forces of nature, and when dire threats arise, can transform into savage Werewolf and Werebear forms to unleash devastating melee attacks. If your foes number many, call upon transformations like Wolves, Bears, Ravens, or the noble Stag to back you up. Whatever the threat may be, the Druid has an answer.

### The Seed of Your Strength: Primal Power

Primal Power is a resource unique to Druids, represented by a green bar above the skill buttons. This resource is required to fuel your most impressive and powerful skills, notably Werewolf, Werebear, and Surging Stone. While certain powerful skills consume it, your other skills generate it through use, and you also passively generate a small amount each second from your equipped Primary Attack.

### Primary Attacks

Both druid Primary Attacks generate 2 Primal Power passively every second and generate 1 Primal Power whenever they hit an enemy.

[![...](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/4I58T8GXLD511750697928760.jpeg)](#)

#### Ferocious Strike:

Swing and slam your staff, dealing damage over three attacks. Enemies other than your primary target take only 25% damage.

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/K3FEU5JUVX701750697910051.gif)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/K3FEU5JUVX701750697910051.gif)

Fang and Claw (Ultimate of Ferocious Strike): Ferocious Strike is enhanced for 12 seconds, embracing a savage aspect of the bear to strike with massive claws, dealing damage in a sequence of 3 attacks. Fang and Claw also grants you a shield for 5 seconds that absorbs damage equal to 20% of your maximum Life.

[![...](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/6OI5JM0QXC8Q1750697929148.jpeg)](#)

#### Landslide (Unlocks at Level 34):

Crack the earth and call forth jagged rocks, projecting them forward in a line that bursts with spikes when it hits an enemy, dealing damage in a small area.

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/ALJM7C2H42CD1750697920487.gif)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/ALJM7C2H42CD1750697920487.gif)

Nature’s Wrath (Ultimate of Landslide): Landslide is enhanced for 12 seconds, conjuring 5 slabs of rock at a time for you to rain down on nearby enemies. A single enemy can take a maximum of 3 hits, with damage after the first hit reduced by 50%. Nature’s Wrath also grants you a shield for 5 seconds that absorbs damage equal to 20% of your maximum Life.

### Transformations

[![...](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/HWOXRIWDPB141750697931439.jpeg)](#)

#### Werewolf (Unlocks at Level 3):

Transform into a werewolf and dash forward, dealing damage and marking enemies along your path. When a marked enemy's Life drops below 50%, they will take additional damage. Movement speed is increased by 30% and you consume 5 Primal Power per second while transformed. Werewolf form replaces your equipped skills while active.

- Swipe (Primary Attack): A sequence of claw attacks where every third attack causes enemies to Bleed.
- Rend and Tear: Dash forward, dealing damage to enemies along your path. Enemies below 50% Life will be dealt additional damage and knocked airborne. Maximum 3 charges.
- Lunge: Run quickly as you charge the skill, increasing your Movement Speed by 30%. Release the skill to lunge forward, knocking back and damaging enemies in your path.
- Howl: Unleash a powerful howl, dealing damage to all nearby enemies and marking them. When a marked enemy's Life drops below 50%, they will take additional damage.
- Revert: End the transformation.

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/C6F5NXP68SJ61750697897178.gif)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/C6F5NXP68SJ61750697897178.gif)

[![...](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/0K55CULBIGSX1750697931066.jpeg)](#)

#### Werebear (Unlocks at Level 38):

Transform into a werebear and roar, dealing damage and Stunning nearby enemies for 2 seconds. Each time you are attacked while transformed, you gain 1 stack of Ursine Endurance, reducing the damage you take and the duration of loss of control effects by 3%, stacking up to 10 times. You consume 5 Primal Power per second while transformed. Werebear form replaces your equipped skills while active.

- Maul (Primary Attack): Swing your claws in wide arcs to damage enemies.
- Crush: Smash the ground with both hands, dealing damage to all nearby enemies and additional damage equal to 10% of your maximum Life. Maximum 3 charges.
- Bound: Dash forward, knocking back and damaging enemies in your path. You are immune to loss of control and knockback during the dash.
- Mangle: Grab two enemies in front of you and slam them together, creating an explosive force that deals damage to nearby enemies and Stuns them for 1 second. Deal additional damage equal to 10% of your maximum Life. The enemies grabbed and slammed together are Stunned for 3 seconds.
- Revert: End the transformation.

## Claw Through Brutish Nightmares Using Druid Legendary Items

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/8OSQVNQBJ6HQ1750697934942.jpg)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/8OSQVNQBJ6HQ1750697934942.jpg)

**60 new Legendary items** are being released for the Druid! We wanted to give you a variety of items to tinker with so you can get a sense of what the Class can do upon release. We love to see the creative builds the community cooks up and can’t wait for you to surprise us.

**Main Hand**

- Staff of Droves: Landslide also increases your Primary Attack Speed by 7% for 5 seconds when it hits an enemy, stacking up to 3 times.
- Loamskull: Werewolf now wreaths you in fire when you transform, damaging and Burning nearby enemies and causing explosions of flame when Primary Attacks Critically Hit.
- Branch of Growling Earth: Earthquake damage is increased by 20% when it hits a Slowed enemy.
- The Craw: Werebear now transforms you into a gigantic bear with increased attack range and maximum Life.
- The Ordered Crook: Werewolf transformation now causes you to leap to a target location, knocking enemies airborne and marking them. Your Critical Hit Chance is increased against the marked enemies and you restore Life when the marks expire.
- Frenzy Reed: Rabid Might duration is extended by 30% while transformed.
- Burnsocket: Fire Tornado generates an additional 1 Primal Power per enemy hit, up to a maximum of 10.
- Rattling Shell: Summon Wolves also increases your Movement Speed by 15% for each active wolf companion under your control.
- Hundred Wings’ Shade: Raven Swarm now dashes forward as a flock of ravens, during which you are untargetable, and damages enemies along your path. A portion of the cooldown is returned if no enemies are hit.
- Lift the Lowborn: Earthquake also grants you a shield for 4 seconds that absorbs damage and makes you immune to 1 total loss of control effect.

**Off-Hand**

- Goring Brace: Ferocious Strike generates an additional 5 Primal Power when hitting an enemy. This can only occur once every 3 seconds.
- Distant Hissing: Rend and Tear (Werewolf) now slashes with both claws, unleashing a claw wind at enemies in front of you. If Burning enemies are close to you, the range and damage of the claw wind increases, and it knocks back enemies.
- Hobble Tooth: Crush (Werebear) now slams the ground to create a shockwave, damaging and Slowing nearby enemies. Can be used during other skills.
- Ifeh’s Nails: Mangle (Werebear) consumes 15 Primal Power after use to reset its cooldown. This cannot occur more often than once every 12 seconds.
- Cedarkin: Summon Oak Sage now actively summons protective Oak Sages to orbit you, each granting damage reduction and immunity against one total loss of control effect.
- Mauler’s Mitt: Crush (Werebear) now empowers you, causing your Primary Attacks to smash the ground and damage nearby enemies, with Critical Hits causing spikes of rock to erupt from the ground for additional damage.
- Scar Carver: Rend and Tear (Werewolf) now causes you to dash forward and strike, and if the attack hits you can activate a powerful follow-up attack that sweeps in a wide arc, dealing additional damage to enemies at low Life.
- Tuskguard: Summon Oak Sage now actively summons 3 Oak Sages who infect enemies with Smolder Seeds that explode if the enemy is affected by Burning.
- Sapling Claw: Summon Oak Sage now actively summons a ring of Oak Sages that automatically seek out targets and self-destruct, damaging all nearby enemies.
- Hungry Blades: Your Summon Grizzly generates 2 Primal Power when it takes damage. Cannot occur more than once per second.

**Chest**

- Saved by Nature: Circle of Life now grants nearby allies a regenerative seed that continuously heals Life, and heals a large amount if their Life drops too low.
- Coat of Fecundity: Thorn Armor now creates an area of thorny vines that follows you, continuously damaging nearby enemies and infecting them with Smolder Seeds, which explode if the infected enemy is Burning.
- Earthbindings: Earthquake now smashes the ground and empowers you, causing your movement to unleash aftershocks every few yards.
- Life Begets: Circle of Life now passively heals you while you avoid damage and can be activated to heal you in a burst of energy that also damages and Stuns nearby enemies. The burst triggers automatically if your Life falls too low.
- Wild Surrender: Circle of Life is now channeled, continuously healing Life and generating Primal Power. You gain damage reduction but cannot move while channeling.
- Saw Notch: While a Werebear, each enemy you kill will increase your Attack and Movement Speeds by 8% for 4 seconds, stacking up to 3 times.
- Barbmantle: Thorn Armor now only shields you, and while active causes your attacks to scatter thorns, damaging nearby enemies. Skill can be triggered on Primary Attack.
- Flesh is Fertile Ground: Thorn Armor is now toggled on or off, and while on it consumes Primal Power to grant damage reduction to all nearby allies and continuously damage nearby enemies.
- Breakplates: Fire Tornado also shatters enemy armor, increasing the damage they take by 10% for 3 seconds.
- Whispers of the Soil: Earthquake now conjures a swirling maelstrom of rock at a target location, damaging and Petrifying enemies within. Allied summons in the maelstrom gain a protective shell, taking reduced damage and gaining immunity to knockback and loss of control effects.

**Legs**

- Unstoppable Redress: Rend and Tear (Werewolf) can be used while under the effects of total loss of control, consuming 15 Primal Power and removing the status.
- Thrivegreaves: Rend and Tear (Werewolf) consumes 5 Primal Power, increasing its Critical Hit Chance by 25%.
- Shattergrouse: Crush (Werebear) also shatters enemy armor, increasing the damage they take by 15% for 3 seconds.
- Scorched Berth: Stag Charge now leaps to a target location, generating Primal Power based on the number of enemies hit.
- Ravager’s Trews: Lunge (Wererwolf) deals 25% increased damage but consumes 5 Primal Power when used.

**Helmet**

- Trailing-Vine: Thorn Armor also increases your Critical Hit Chance by 8% while active.
- Spring Veil: Bound (Werebear) is now toggled on or off, and while on you consume Primal Power to continuously sprint forward, damaging and knocking back enemies in your path.
- Mask of Pouncing: Stag Charge also increases your Primal Power generation by 30% for 3 seconds.
- Snarling Face: Lunge (Werewolf) now causes you to perform a quick backstep before dashing forward. If you hit an enemy, trigger a powerful follow-up uppercut with both claws that knocks enemies airborne.
- Bounding Accuracy: Bound (Werebear) now leaps to a target, damaging all nearby enemies and increasing the Attack Speed and Critical Hit Chance of your Primary Attacks.
- Bonfire Crest: Fire Tornado now unleashes an explosion at a target location that damages and Burns nearby enemies. Skill can be triggered on a Critical Hit with a Primary Attack.
- The Yawning Maw: Summon Grizzly now summons a flaming bear that continuously Burns nearby enemies, and can be commanded to leap to a location, damaging enemies and increasing its Critical Hit Chance.
- Ash Monger: Fire Tornado now unleashes an expanding ring of fire, damaging and Burning enemies. Burning enemies explode when their Life falls too low.
- Gilded Majesty: Summon Grizzly now summons a bear imbued with the relentless power of the forest thicket, able to attack with Stunning thorns, consume Primal Power to resurrect, and be commanded to leap into a group of enemies and taunt them.

**Shoulders**

- Grand Endurance: Bound (Werebear) now causes you to jump to a target location, damaging nearby enemies and gaining an absorb shield. While the shield is active, you take reduced damage.
- Brae’s Bark: While a Werewolf, you deal 10% increased damage against enemies suffering from a continual damage effect.
- Peatspald: Bound (Werebear) increases your Movement Speed by 40% for 3 seconds when hitting an enemy.
- Promise the Obscure: Mangle (Werebear) now unleashes a sequence of four attacks as you move in a direction, knocking back enemies. You are immune to knockback and loss of control during these attacks.
- All Are Prey: While a Werewolf, you deal 20% increased damage to enemies, reduced by 5% for every ally within 5 yards of enemies.
- Pile of Plenty: Bound (Werebear) cooldown is reduced by 0.5 seconds each time your Primary Attack hits an enemy, up to a maximum of 2 seconds.
- Thrill of the Hunt: While a Werewolf, generate 3 Primal Power for each enemy killed. Can only trigger once every second.
- Bar-Ghaist Beacon: Summon Wolves now summons fire wolves to fight alongside you and Burn enemies, who can be commanded to breathe fire and increase their Attack Speed.
- Soarskull: Raven Swarm also increases your Movement Speed by 30% for 3 seconds.
- The Great One: Summon Wolves now summons a single dire wolf that takes reduced damage by consuming Primal Power when hit and can be commanded to dash toward enemies.
