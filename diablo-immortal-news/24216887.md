> Read “Tread Heavy” to learn about the new Druid class, releasing with the next Diablo Immortal content update on July 3.

---

Sanctuary is a volatile place—danger lurks at every turn. An eldritch evil rots at the heart of the First Forest. The fey sow ruin and molder wherever they go, consuming oak and creature and child alike. But where nature cries out its defiance, a druid must answer.

As Sanctuary’s denizens struggle to keep hope, the rich stories they’ve passed down have shined truth in the darkness. The “Tread Heavy” comic provides a glimpse at the role you shall play as the Druid in the greater fight against the Burning Hells’ legions. For more information about this primal new class, which releases on **July 3**, [visit this article](https://news.blizzard.com/article/24216435/).
