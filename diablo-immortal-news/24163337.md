## Winds of Fortune Limited-time Event

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/PAQYQ1NOD6FZ1726871391544.jpg)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/PAQYQ1NOD6FZ1726871391544.jpg)

Horadric Scholars have poured over their tomes to discover the origin of increased materialized wealth, while the merchants of Sanctuary have seized the opportunity to increase their abundance. The source: Winds of Fortune.

From **December 6, 3:00 a.m.—December 13, 3:00 a.m. local server time**, you will earn increased rewards!

While Winds of Fortune is live, activate your 24-hour buff to receive this duplicate effect. Once activated, the buff’s duration is in real time and will end 24 hours after it’s been initiated. If you have not activated the buff within 24 hours of the event ending, it will be enabled automatically. Any rewards not claimed before the end of the event will be auto-claimed on next log-in.

During Winds of Fortune, the 4-player party Normal Gems bonus drop will be doubled and unaffected by the daily cap, and only the first twelve Common Gems of the day will be tradable.

Any of these items will drop in duplicate quantities:

- Gold
- Experience
- Battle Pass Points
- Normal Gems
- Legendary Items
- Items

Items from these activities will also drop in duplicate quantities:

- Horadric Bestiary
- Challenge Rifts
- Bounties
- Fishing
- Dungeons
- Purge the Depths
- Accursed Towers
- Hidden Lairs
- Farming in the wilderness
- Codex Activities

Note that during this limited-time event, Battle Pass rewards will not be doubled, and the weekly limit on Battle Pass Points and Normal Gems will not change. Any bonus Experience gained will be affected by your current modifier.

Additionally, there are limits to each item that can be earned in duplicate quantities. You’ll earn double the amount until you reach the limit. Please see the event in-game for more details.

## Balance Changes

> **Developer’s Note:** *We are introducing a new “backlash” mechanic with this round of balance changes. It will put your targets in a state where using their skills increases the duration of the harmful effect you placed on them, further punishing builds that rely on heavy spam usage of skills. Opponents can choose to fight through the pain of the backlash effect or disengage to come back at full strength after the effect has expired.\
> Our intent is to work more on altering Legendary Affixes to impact gameplay more instead of flat-out nerfing Classes that might be too strong. While this is the beginning of a new approach to tuning, we will still adjust effects as needed, which is why there’s also a few nerfs to some overperforming classes in the list below. The goal is to make nerfs less severe by increasing the power of other Classes. This combined approach of reinvigorating old Legendary Affixes alongside a softer touch on nerfs will result in new combat experiences and more interesting choices, without it feeling as punishing as having the power of your skills heavily reduced.*

The balance changes below will be effective everywhere in Diablo Immortal.

#### Barbarian

- Flickertonne (Hammer of the Ancients): Cooldown increased from 0.5 to 1 second. Base damage was also reduced slightly, with fully charged damage remaining unchanged.

#### Blood Knight

- Transfusion: Shield no longer removes and grants immunity to all harmful effects and instead removes and grants immunity to loss of control effects.
- Vein Seeker (Tendrils of Blood): Cooldown increased from 0.5 to 1 second. Damage increased slightly.

#### Crusader

- Anointed Brimstone (Consecration): You are now immune to Chill and Frozen while active.
- Vainglory's Star (Judgment): Enemies struck by Judgment will now increase the vulnerability effect further each time they use a skill, gradually increasing the damage they take.

#### Demon Hunter

- Escalating Violence (Escape): No longer Slows and instead Immobilizes and disables Dash Skills.
- Puncta Obscura (Impale): Additionally reduces enemy healing by 80%.

#### Necromancer

- Studied Menace (Corpse Lance): Additionally reduces enemy healing by 80%.
- Revenant Wind (Wraith Form): No longer slows and instead weakens enemies, reducing the damage they deal. The duration of this effect is extended by 0.5 seconds with each skill the enemy uses while suffering from this effect, up to a maximum of an additional 4 seconds.
- Scurrying Ruin (Bone Wall): Bone Wall duration reduced from 8 to 4 seconds. Damage increased slightly.

## Feature Updates

#### Vanguard

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/DZGDS35ZFM1H1732317861791.jpg)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/DZGDS35ZFM1H1732317861791.jpg)

Remain steadfast in the face of amplified challenges and rewards new to Vanguard. December 5 marks the start of a fresh Vanguard season, complete with a new environment and bloodthirsty enemies from World’s Crown—the new Zone releasing with our next major update—have been injected into the mode to dial up the intensity. In the Ashen Ravine dungeon, you’ll need to band together with your shield brothers to escort siege engines so they can crumple demonic fortifications, clearing a path for you to strike through.

Three new Helliquary bosses have also found their way into Vanguard. Complete the herculean task of besting these titans of Hell on Difficulty III or higher to earn 3 new weapons infused with Helliquary Boss Affixes. These Affixes can be triggered by both Basic and Ultimate attacks. Here’s the new weapon Affixes:

Gorgothra the Claimer Helliquary Affix: Unleash the power of Gorgothra the Claimer, creating 4 statues that fire beams, each dealing damage to enemies.

Viaath the Shivering Death Hellquary Affix: Shivering Breath Unleash the power of Vitaath the Shivering Death, conjuring two clones of Vitaath to release waves of cold, dealing damage and Chilling enemies.

Gishtur & Beledwe Helliquary Affix: Unleash the power of the Twin Terrors, spinning massive blades that deal damage to all nearby enemies while pulling them in.

Call upon your allies to team up and overcome a fight suited for the most seasoned Vanguard enjoyers, and should you prevail, your name will be etched into the new Vanguard Leaderboard for all of Sanctuary to envy.

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/DHGXSL10K6S11732317861545.jpg)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/DHGXSL10K6S11732317861545.jpg)

#### Zone Side Quests

Zone Side Quests you have not yet completed will now appear first when speaking to Taite the Adventure Seeker in Westmarch. Exploration tasks in zones should be easier to complete now.

#### Trial of the Hordes

Matchmaking queues for Trial of the Hordes have been optimized to prioritize quicker match times.

#### New Combat Settings

The displaying of visual effects in different environments, such as the Wilderness or PvP activities, can now be hidden using a new option in the settings menu.

#### Clans

Clan Leaders and Officers can now view the history of personnel changes made via the new Clan Roster feature.

#### Normal Gems from Hidden Lairs

The number of Normal Gems you’ve acquired can be viewed in the bonus interface while inside Hidden Lairs.

#### Mourrneskull Legendary Gem

During the Eternal War crossover event, many of you have used Lich King’s power to eviscerate your foes with icy prejudice by way of the Mourrneskull Legendary Gem. Utilize the Mourrneskull Legendary Gems you receive from Elder Rifts, Shop bundles, or through crafting at the Apprentice Jeweler to upgrade your Gem’s Rank as much as possible during the event. Once this Eternal War concludes, you will only be able to upgrade this Legendary Gem at an Apprentice Jeweler using Telluric Pearls, so make the most of the event while you can.

#### PC Optimization

Player profiles can now be opened directly by clicking on their name. Additionally, the Alt-Click function can be used to open a player’s profile directly from viewing them in the wilderness.

Additionally, an option to have channeled skills follow your mouse cursor on PC has been added to the settings menu.
