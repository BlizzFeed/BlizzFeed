## The Epoch of Madness Main Quest

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/AENELK3PBPKW1765217330863.jpg)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/AENELK3PBPKW1765217330863.jpg)

## Protect yourself with new Ancient Legendary Items

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/YD5BMAPQABQY1765661606669.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/YD5BMAPQABQY1765661606669.png)

From the shadowed recesses of Sanctuary’s forgotten vaults, artifacts of unimaginable strength have stirred. Five Ancient Legendary Chest items, once thought only myth, now await those brave enough to claim their power.

These storied armaments are unlike any you have encountered. Each chest piece bears three pre-determined Magic Affixes, etched into its essence by forces beyond mortal comprehension, and a fourth random class Affix.

Crafting Ancient Legendaries: Once players reach Paragon level 1200 and unlock Inferno X, they can obtain Ancient Artifacts of the corresponding difficulty level from Helliquary Raids, Daily Quests, and Daily Dungeon Rewards. Collecting 100 Ancient Artifacts allows you to craft one Ancient Legendary piece of equipment at the Blacksmith.

Obtaining Ancient Legendaries in the Wild: Once players reach Paragon level 1200 and unlock Inferno X, they have a very low chance of directly obtaining Ancient Legendary equipment of the corresponding difficulty level as a drop in game modes such as Helliquary Raids, Helliquary Gauntlets, Vanguard, Hellslayer Bounties, Oblivion Pillars, and Terror Rifts.

#### The Future of Ancient Legendary Equipment

In the future, we plan to continue introducing types of Ancient Legendary equipment slots. However, each time a new slot is added, the oldest slot will be removed simultaneously, ensuring that a maximum of two Ancient Legendary equipment slots can exist at the same time.

Ancient Legendary equipment and Ancient Artifacts no longer drop in Inferno 7–9 and crafting Ancient Legendary equipment for Inferno 7–9 at the blacksmith will be disabled.

Low-difficulty Ancient Artifacts can now be exchanged for Ancient Scraps, used for enchanting Ancient Legendary equipment, at the Weapon Smith.

## Seek New Legendary Gems

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/9QMU753290XZ1765661603188.jpg)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/9QMU753290XZ1765661603188.jpg)

Three new Legendary Gems arrive in this next update. Each can elevate your character to new heights of power.

**Fated Trail (Five Star Gem)**

- Dealing damage grants you Rampant Growth for 6 seconds, causing brambles to grow beneath the enemy's feet when you deal damage to them. Brambles deal damage to nearby enemies equal to 10% of your maximum Life and can only be triggered once every second per enemy.
- Additionally, the brambles shatter enemy armor, increasing the damage you deal to them by 4% for 2 seconds, stacking up to 6 times. When an enemy is first struck by brambles they will be Immobilized for 1.5 seconds. Rampant Growth can only be gained periodically.
- Resonance Gems: Maw of the Deep, Blessing of the Worthy, Specter Glass, Mercy's Harvest, Ironbane

**Rampart Torch (Two Star Gem)**

- When attacked you gain a protective shield for 6 seconds that reduces the damage you take by a percentage of your base damage and reduces the continual damage you take. Cannot occur more often than once every 20 seconds.
- Resonance Gems: Power & Command, Stubborn Oracle, Misery Elixir

**Flaystone (One Star Gem)**

- You deal and increased damage and gain increased Critical Hit Chance against shielded enemies.
- Resonance Gems: Pain of Subjugation, Trickshot Gem

## Prepare to challenge new Helliquary Bosses: The Fleshwarped

Albrecht has prepared for war, gathering the strongest Shardborne in his army: the **Fleshwarped**.

These monstrosities were once Albrecht's Shardborne followers, but with the power of creation, they have evolved into living war machines. Most traces of their former humanity have vanished, and all that remains is a burning desire for freedom and blood.

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/CD4NOSFBFC6X1765661601055.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/CD4NOSFBFC6X1765661601055.png)

#### Bloatmaw

In his search for weapons of war, Albrecht sought to create a Shardborne capable of ending battles within seconds. With the power of creation, Albrecht continuously transformed one of his Shardborne followers into a completely new creature. With its swollen gullet and strong legs, Bloatmaw leaps across battlefields, crushing prey and spewing miasma. In an instant, the toxic gas envelops entire legions, warping and melting flesh in the Shardborne's twisted image.

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/4VR9HDGD2G5K1765661605410.jpg)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/4VR9HDGD2G5K1765661605410.jpg)

#### Marrowmount

When the Bravehull Stronghold received word that Albrecht's army was approaching, the Entsteig platoon readied themselves with barricades and ramparts. Yet no wall was strong enough to withhold Marrowmount. The twisted stallion broke through the stronghold's walls with one charge, and Shardborne soldiers swarmed the Entsteig army within. Though a group of Entsteig soldiers were able to hinder the Shardborne, it's only a matter of time before it continues its rampage.

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/LJ90I3KDC1KK1765661600042.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/LJ90I3KDC1KK1765661600042.png)

#### Sundered Goliath

Albrecht formed the Sundered Goliath with the grafted bodies of hundreds of his followers, hoping to create the perfect creature. A failed experiment, the Shardborne was uncoordinated and unwieldy, yet Albrecht sought it for a new and greater purpose. Within the hulking chamber on its back, this behemoth carries whole battalions of Shardborne soldiers into war. As it tramples its enemies, Shardborne swarm from its body and overrun the battlefield.

During the Corrupted Heroes event, while exploring any Wild zone, players may encounter a Cursed Heavy Ornate Chest, triggering Corrupted Heroes to appear to defend the Chest. Up to 4 players can participate, against 3 Corrupted Heroes.

The Corrupted Heroes’ HP, damage, and defense will scale based on the average combat rating of participating players, and Rewards increase with team participation and difficulty tier.

The Set tells a very grim fey tale about a noble from Entsteig who thought himself a trophy hunter and set out with his party to hunt the mythical nature spirits of the Sharval Wilds. ## Feature Updates

### Prodigy’s Path Changes

All aspects of Prodigy’s Path are being reduced, but the relative values of the offerings will slightly increase.

### Updates to Several Legendary Gems

We are slightly changing the functionality for a few Legendary Gems, and how they function when fighting other players: Viper’s Bite, Seeping Bile, and Everlasting Torment.

To limit the total duration of loss of control these gems shared cooldowns between players wearing the same one. If another player applies the corresponding debuff to an enemy for these gems, it will no longer prevent your gems from being applied to that enemy. Your gems will now correctly respect your own gems’ internal cooldowns, and not impacted by another player’s cooldowns.

> **Developer’s Note:** *The main function of these gems is to provide damage, so this setting meant less uptime for these effects if other players had the same ones equipped, which wasn’t a great feeling.*

### Introducing Demonic Reforging

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/DHH2G6ONTO2S1765661602229.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/DHH2G6ONTO2S1765661602229.png)

### New Paragon Skills

We're adding three new Paragon Skills which unlock at Paragon 1200. Use them to enhance your already renowned demon-slaying prowess.

#### Searing Flame

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/SC00IK6Z3N9W1765661596802.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/SC00IK6Z3N9W1765661596802.png)

Damaging a Burning enemy applies Searing Flame, damaging them every second for 3 seconds. Searing Flame's damage is increased for each additional Burn on the enemy, up to a maximum of 3 Burns. Enemies slain while afflicted by Searing Flame, unleash a fireball at other nearby enemies that explodes on impact dealing damage to all nearby enemies. Searing Flame cannot afflict the same target more often than once every 15 seconds.

#### Glacial Shards

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/JQ0D0A3KZVRW1765661596807.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/JQ0D0A3KZVRW1765661596807.png)

Chilling an enemy grants you 1 Glacial Shard, stacking up to a maximum of 6. Damaging an enemy with your Primary Attack unleashes all Glacials Shards at the enemy, damaging and Chilling them, reducing their Attack and Movement Speed by 60% for 3 seconds. Glacial Shards can only be unleashed once every 15 seconds.

#### Brewing Storms

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/M0L35AXWPXJ61765661596816.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/M0L35AXWPXJ61765661596816.png)

Shocking an enemy conjures a Brewing Storm above their head, that unleashes a lightning strike after 3 seconds, damage the enemy. Shocking enemies afflicted with Brewing Storm charges up the storm increasing the damage it deals and if the enemy is shocked X times the Brewing Storm will become fully charged, unleashing the lightning strike immediately, Stunning the enemy. Brewing Storms cannot afflict the same target more often than once every 15 seconds.

### The Curse is Lifted on Cursed Items

Beginning with this major update, newly dropped items will no longer include Cursed attributes. Additionally, the system will no longer distribute Purification Shards.

To try and preserve the gameplay depth previously tied to Cursed equipment, we’re introducing a brand-new Cursed Paragon Specialization.

#### Cursed Paragon Specialization

These will only feature the three most popular Cursed Effects: Lashed, Nettled, and Vexed. These Contain nodes that grant the full effects of the former two-piece, four-piece and six-piece Cursed sets. This also includes new passive nodes to improve various combat attributes, inspiring some hefty buildcraft.

Once the Cursed Paragon Specialization is activated, any Cursed attributes previously found on equipped items will immediately become inactive.

#### Transitioning Existing Items and Material

All Cursed items obtained before the update can be salvaged into Upgrade Material. Purification Shards can now also be exchanged at a 1:1 rate for Beguiling Dust.

### Set Item Crafting Changes

We’ll be making a change to reduce some of the clutter caused by Fabled Wisps, and how many different Materials the Set Item crafting system has.

These changes include introducing a new material called **Fabled Wisp**, which has no difficulty attached to it. Fabled Wisp is a universal material that will be used to craft lower difficulty Set Items.

On **December 17**, when we release Inferno X-XII, Fabled Wisps for Inferno VII-XII will now exist. You’ll receive and in-game message that automatically converts all Fabled Wisps from Hell I-Inferno VI in your inventory to the new Fabled Wisps.

These new universal Fabled Wisps can be converted up into Fabled Wisps in Inferno VII at a rate of 7:1.

Any older Fabled Wisps can be deleted if you so choose, we do not have any plans for them and will never have a purpose.
