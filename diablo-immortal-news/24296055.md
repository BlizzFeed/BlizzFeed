### Play The Game, Spin The Wheel, Win a Prize

Every player that plays and finished the Diablo Immortal demo experience will get to spin our wheel and win one of three BlizzCon exclusive Diablo Immortal pins. Try your luck, and walk away emblazoned with Diablo, Andariel, or Maltheal.

### Prove your Mettle in the Battle Arena

Put your skills to the test, and prove your might! Come together with your team to take on the Diablo Immortal Boss Gauntlet!

Players will form teams of 4 and take on two Helliquary Bosses, back to back, with the goal of recording the fastest time possible. All 4 members of the fastest teams of the day will walk away with a Diablo IV: Lord of Hatred Mephisto 26" Statue.

Additional prizes will be available for all 10 teams that place on the leaderboard, but only the fastest teams to beat [REDACTED] and [REDACTED] will walk away with the glorious tribute to Lord of Hatred.

### Summoning Spawn into Diablo Immortal: A Live Drawing Panel with Todd McFarlane

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/xd/XDYFHC566TK91787872430001.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/xd/XDYFHC566TK91787872430001.png)

On September 29th, Spawn and Diablo Immortal collide in an epic crossover event that brings two hells together like never before! To celebrate this cacophony of demonic turmoil, the legendary artist and creator of Spawn will be joining us for a special panel.

Join us Sunday, September 13th at 10 a.m. PDT for a Live-Drawing Panel and discussion with Todd McFarlane and our very own concept artist, Justin Murray. This panel will take place at the Diablo Immortal Battle Arena, but for those of you that can’t make it to BlizzCon, we’ll be posting a recording to [YouTube.com/DiabloImmortal](https://www.youtube.com/DiabloImmortal) later that day.

If you want a chance to have Todd answer a question, join our Discord Server and submit your question in the #ask-todd-mcfarlane channel. We’ll select some of our favourite questions submitted and ask Todd live during the panel.

### Meet Todd in-person

This is your chance to meet Todd and receive a limited-edition signed memento! To celebrate the Spawn x Diablo Immortal collaboration, we’ve created a limited edition run of prints of a custom poster, illustrated by Brett Booth and inked/colored by Todd McFarlane.

The first 100 guests in attendance of the panel will receive a ticket to a special signing/meet and greet with Todd McFarlane following the panel.

Please note that Todd will only be signing the Poster on this day, and time is very limited, so we must restrict guests from bringing any personal items for Todd to sign and cannot permit photos.
