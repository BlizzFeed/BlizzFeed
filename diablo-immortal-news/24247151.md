Hail, adventurer! As with previous content updates, there will be server maintenance starting on **January 6, from 4 p.m.–6 p.m. PST** for Oceania, East Asia, Southeast Asia, and Europe servers, and on **January 7, from 12 a.m.–2 a.m. PST** for servers in the Americas. After the maintenance has concluded, all items mentioned below will be live—specific dates for any features going live afterward are provided below.

## Shattered Wilds Limited-Time Event

With the siege of Sharval behind us, it’s time to put the pieces of the Shattered Wilds back together. From **January 7, 3 a.m.–February 4, 3 a.m. server time** you can earn points in the event by completing different tasks. Rewards such as 2 Telluric Pearls, Set items, Legendary items, and One-Star Legendary Gems can be earned from Shattered Wilds.

Shattered Wilds progress can be earned by participating in the following events:

**Survivor’s Bane**

January 7, 3 a.m.–January 14, 3 a.m. server time

**Trial of the Hordes**

January 14, 3 a.m.–January 21, 3 a.m. server time

Additional events that reward progress in the Shattered Wilds limited-time event will release with our next content update.

## Boreal Nomads Battlepass

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/mo/MOT4G6ZEES1V1766425271716.jpg)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/mo/MOT4G6ZEES1V1766425271716.jpg)

The World’s Crown was inhospitable before Terror’s dominion, yet many a stubborn sailor or exile settled its shores, protecting themselves with thick furs and bones from the savage creatures that dared to stop them.

The Boreal Nomads Battle Pass arrives in Sanctuary on **January 14, 3 a.m. local server time**, consisting of 40 ranks worth of rewards, such as Crests, Hilts, Legendary Gems, and more.

Begin freezing your enemies and climbing your way through the ranks before the Battlepass ends on February 11, 2:59 a.m. server time.
