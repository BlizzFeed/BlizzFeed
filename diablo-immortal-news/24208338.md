## Welcome to the Anniversary: Threefold Rebirth

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/WPNZFDIFIA6Y1748109551201.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/WPNZFDIFIA6Y1748109551201.png)

“By three, they come,” so it is said. With three years’ worth of slain demons, denizens saved, and friends made along the way, we welcome you to Diablo Immortal’s third anniversary! This celebration of all things Hell offers a smattering of activities suited for every adventurer. Glean some of the festivities to come below. The rest are detailed throughout this article.

- 6/1–6/19 3 a.m. Third Chime Phantom Gallery
- 6/1–6/19 3 a.m. Legendary Gem Trial

### 5-Star Blood Floe Legendary Gem Gift

What better way to celebrate our three-year anniversary than with a new Legendary Gem, coated in crimson and foretold to be introduced in our next major update. From **June 19– July 2**, you can [claim a Quality 2, 5-Star Blood Floe Legendary Gem as a gift from Battle.net](https://shop.battle.net/family/diablo-immortal).

To obtain this Legendary Gem you must be Level 30 or above and link your Diablo Immortal and Battle.net accounts. For more information on how to link your accounts, [visit here](https://battle.net/support/article/339559?flowTrackingId). Only one gift may be claimed per account.

## Trial of True Evils Anniversary Event

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/Y9AVABB0E1UQ1748109548669.jpg)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/Y9AVABB0E1UQ1748109548669.jpg)

The Butcher, Baal, Skarn, and Diablo have declared you their greatest threat—you’re wanted, dead or *dead*. May your soul be prepared to overcome the trial of true evils. It must, for Sanctuary’s sake.

Fight your way through Diablo Immortal’s history by facing off against adversaries from the pantheon of doom. During the event, a new boss may be challenged every few days, and towards the end of the event, you can take on these baddies back-to-back.

Don’t worry, this is a fair fight—the bosses will scale to your power, providing anyone Level 30 and above the chance to challenge this fearsome foursome from **June 1, 3 a.m.–June 19, 3 a.m. server time**, if you dare. . .

## Conditional Drop Rate Gem Event

From **June 1, 3 a.m.–June 19, 3 a.m. server time**, if you use 30 Eternal Legendary Crests or Legendary Crests in a row, and haven't received a 5-Star Legendary Gem, you will gain a bonus to your drop rates starting with the 31st Legendary Crest. The drop rate will be increased by 1.125% on your 31st attempt and will increase cumulatively for each subsequent attempt. Should you reach your 41st attempt, the drop rate will then be increased by 2.25% per attempt. Your 50th attempt drop rate is guaranteed.

## Balance Updates

#### Barbarian

- Fallow Field (Whirlwind): You are now immune to knockback while channeling Whirlwind. The amount of Life consumed per second increased from 2% to 3%.
- Ox's Horns (Whirlwind): The Bleed effect can now be stacked up to 3 times, dealing up to twice the damage.
- Five Fresh Claws (Whirlwind): Armor shatter effect stacks increased from 5 to 15 (up to 30% increased damage), but allied players can no longer benefit from the effect.

#### Demon Hunter

- Agony's Specter (Escape): If Escape is used while being affected by knockback, Primary Attack Speed and Damage will be increased by 50%.

#### Blood Knight

- Count of the Eves (Whirling Strike): Previously damage reduction was 4% per enemy hit, stacking up to 6 times (24% maximum). Now the damage reduction is frontloaded, with the initial stack providing 9%, and the remaining 5 stacks providing 3% each (still 24% maximum).
- Vow of Trickery (Whirling Strike): You now gain knockback and loss of control immunity for 1.5 seconds after successfully blocking a projectile with Whirling Strike.
- Transfusion (Base Skill): If Transfusion is applied to an ally while they are affected by knockback, the shield you both receive will also make you immune to knockback for its duration.

#### Monk

- Tranquil Palm (Exploding Palm): Cooldown increased from 6 to 8 seconds. Damage increased by 33%.

## Feature Updates

### Battle Pass Updates

We’re adding more rewards to the Standard Battle Pass, Premium Battle Pass, and Battle Archive Shop. Here’s a glance at some of the additions:

- Standard Battle Pass: At Rank 40, you can now choose between a 2-Star Legendary Gem Selection Chest and a Telluric Pearl.
- Premium Battle Pass: 2-Star Legendary Gem Selection Chests, Normal Gems, and Gem Fragments, have been added.
- Battle Archive Shop: You now have up to 5 instances of purchasing a 2-Star Legendary Gem Selection Chest.

### Crafting

The notification for having enough crafting materials to craft an item from Charsi can now be dismissed.
