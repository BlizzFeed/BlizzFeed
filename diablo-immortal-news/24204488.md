## Matriarch’s Retribution

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/56/56U0S96L6Y9O1746831689520.jpg)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/56/56U0S96L6Y9O1746831689520.jpg)

From now until May 31, 3 a.m. local server time, eviscerate Hell’s demons across limited-time events to collect Skeletal Spoils which can be used to unlock fearsome rewards.

The following Returning Events can be bested to carve your way through Matriarch’s Retribution:

- May 15—May 22, 3 a.m. [Conqueror](https://news.blizzard.com/article/23994437/)
- May 17—May 24, 3 a.m. [All Clans on Deck](https://news.blizzard.com/article/23938757/)
- May 22—May 31, 3 a.m. [Wild Brawl](https://news.blizzard.com/article/23985784/)
- May 24—May 31, 3 a.m. Shields of the Hearth 8-Day Event

Battling in Matriarch’s Retribution can earn you the following rewards:

- 2x Telluric Pearls
- 2x Legendary Items
- 4x Set Items
- 10x 1-Star Legendary Gems
- 800,000 Gold
- 2,800 Scrap Materials
- 240 Enchanted Dust
- 28 Aspirant’s Keys
- 16 Normal Gems

## Feature Updates

#### Challenge Dungeon Combat Rating Changes

When entering a Challenge Dungeon, player Combat Rating will always be checked to prevent being grouped into a Dungeon you cannot play.

> **Developer’s Note:** *Previously, if you were matched in a Challenge Dungeon within a Zone, there was a chance you could be placed in a group you didn’t qualify for. This change should fix any instances where this would happen.*

#### Dungeon Kick Feature Updates

You’ll now have the ability to kick other players from your party for the following reasons:

- AFK or inactive behavior
- Harassment or inappropriate remarks

Additionally, you cannot be kicked for being AFK if you’re doing the first or second highest most damage in the party. Also, you cannot be kicked for hateful or inappropriate remarks if you have not spoken in chat.

#### Spectral Allies in Inferno Difficulty

You can now summon Spectral Allies in Dungeons when in Inferno difficulty. Only the best allies would follow you into these lowest depths of hell.

#### Monster Essences at Castle Erebban

An additional Horadric Altar has been added into Cain's Workshop near the Gates for Abyssal Verge, Erebban and Vanguard.

#### Cancel Familiar Expeditions

You can now cancel Familiar Expeditions early. It’s ok, you missed them. We understand.
