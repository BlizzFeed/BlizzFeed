## Hallow’s Wake Returns

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/OOG5UUIJXP411727913290107.jpg)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/OOG5UUIJXP411727913290107.jpg)

The Hallow’s Wake Limited-time Event reemerges from the fading dusk with grisly fervor, adventurer. From **October 9, 3 a.m.—November 6, 2:59 a.m. local server time**, creep through a vicious Survivor’s Bane and terrifying Howler’s Hunt to earn **Hallowed Stones** and trade them for macabre rewards.

To earn Hallowed Stones, slaughter goblins in either Survivor’s Bane or Howler’s Hunt and visit the Crone in Westmarch for daily rewards. The Crone’s wares are chocked full of holiday haunts. Trade Hallowed Stones for new Headless cosmetics, Legendary Items, Legendary Crests, and more.

#### Grisly Survivor’s Bane

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/LC5KRDNDDIAS1727913290436.jpg)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/LC5KRDNDDIAS1727913290436.jpg)

From **October 9, 3 a.m.—October 23, 3 a.m. local server time**, Survivor’s Bane is rife with a seasonal fright. Among the ghastly demons that plague your every move, you’ll come face to face with haunting beings that only appear during Hallow’s Wake. Supernatural forces from beyond your plane may be at work, narrowing your vision and occluding the dangers that surround you. Beware the terrors abound!

#### A Terrifying Howler’s Hunt

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/MAC8O4FKV17H1727913291206.jpg)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/MAC8O4FKV17H1727913291206.jpg)

From **October 23, 3 a.m—November 6, 3 a.m. local server time**, Howler’s Hunt embodies a tradition from an otherworldy realm-letting you slip into the skin of a vicious Werewolf. Within your first match for the day, don the **Nightmoon Curse Cosmetic Set** to embrace your inner beast and strike fear into the hearts of enemies and allies alike.

Hallow’s Wake is a time for the dead and the mad to lose their heads. Use your Hallowed Stones to snatch the newest Headless cosmetics, which can be worn throughout the duration of Hallow’s Wake:

[![Headless Geyser](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/H3F83DGM8UFW1727913291479.jpg)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/H3F83DGM8UFW1727913291479.jpg)

Headless Geyser

[![Headless Fire](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/96EOD7A0T15A1727913291185.jpg)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/96EOD7A0T15A1727913291185.jpg)

Headless Fire

[![Headless Belfry](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/CEWQ09NYDIRQ1727913291445.jpg)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/CEWQ09NYDIRQ1727913291445.jpg)

Headless Belfry

Don’t forget to spend your Hallowed Stones before Hallow’s Wake ends on November 6, where they vanish into the air like a specter of the wind. Note that Headless Cosmetics will remain in your inventory once the event has finished, wear it to stir fear in the hearts of your fellow adventurers until the end of Hallow’s Wake.

## Verdant Pageant Phantom Market

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/FA4CRHZBB5PJ1727913290688.jpg)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/FA4CRHZBB5PJ1727913290688.jpg)

The barrier between the world of the living and that of the dead has become blurred, and the souls that have come before us return to make their presence known. Their intentions are unclear, but their familiarity is unmistakable. The blending of these realms brings back items from past Phantom Markets, returning cosmetics from both the **Arcane Calamity** and **Treacherous Reflections** Phantom Markets.

From **October 9, 3 a.m.—November 6, 3 a.m. local server time**, the Verdant Pageant Phantom Market materializes to decorate your wanderer with sanguine threads.

Considering items from the Phantom Market are acquired randomly per draw, we want to provide the initial draw rates for each item to aid in deciding if you would like to engage with the event past your first free draw.

Note: The drop rates provided below are for the first draw only and will adjust to reflect the relative probabilities of the remaining items in the Phantom Market item pool.

- Murderous Rage Emoji: 60.00%
- Arcane Calamity: Dark Orchid Weapon 15.63%
- Arcane Calamity: Gilded Weapon 15.63%
- Verdant Pageant Portal: 3.16%
- Treacherous Reflection: Blood Moon Weapon 1.77%
- Arcane Calamity: Dark Orchid Armor 1.14%
- Arcane Calamity: Gilded Armor 1.14%
- Treacherous Reflection: Blood Moon Armor 0.76%
- Verdant Pageant Ultimate: 0.63%
- Vedant Pageant Cosmetic Set: 0.13%

For more information about how the Phantom Market functions, visit [this article](https://news.blizzard.com/diablo-immortal/23954934/).

## Embrace the Nightmoon Curse Cosmetic

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/6PP43R9X2BEE1727913290056.jpg)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/6PP43R9X2BEE1727913290056.jpg)
