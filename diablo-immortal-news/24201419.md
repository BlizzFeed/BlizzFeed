## Harness the Crimson Behelit Legendary Gem

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/K7LGJRDA4H741745623206195.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/K7LGJRDA4H741745623206195.png)

The Crimson Behelit writhes in silent agony, eyes staring unfocused, ready to receive the sacrifice necessary to exalt a mere human to that of a Godhand. This fearsome new Legendary Gem arrives with the Struggler’s Path. The below value reflects its power at Rank 10.

Your Behelit activates in response to your overwhelming desire, granting you the Behelit Power you seek the most. While above 50% health, dealing damage grants you Behelit's Wrath, increasing your damage done by 30% for 6 seconds. When taking damage below 50% health you gain Behelit's Mercy, reducing the damage you take by 30% for 6 seconds. When a Behelit Power triggers it releases a scream of terror at the enemy’s location, Fearing nearby enemies for 1.6 seconds. Each Behelit Power has its own separate cooldown; Behelit's Wrath has a 20 second cooldown and Behelit's Mercy has a 60 second cooldown.

### Legendary Gem Bonus Drop Pool Selection

From **May 1, 3 a.m.— May 22, 3 a.m.** local server time, you’ll have the chance to greatly alter your Legendary Gem drop tables for a limited-time. You’ll be able to fully customize your 5-Star Legendary Gem pool of 16, selecting the exact pool of gems that have a chance to drop.

Additionally, once you’ve selected the total pool of 16, you can further select 8 of the 16 gems to have an increased chance to drop when a 5-Star Gem appears.

Similarily, for 2-Star Legendary Gems you can choose 17 Legendary Gems to make up the available Gems, and increase the chances for 8 of them to drop when a 2-Star Legendary Gem drops.

## Deny your Sacrifice in Struggler’s Bane

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/6TIVTVHPUUW41745623201447.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/6TIVTVHPUUW41745623201447.png)

*“Pile it up, take all you have left, chant the words 'I sacrifice' in your heart, and you will be granted raven-black wings upon which you shall soar higher than any summit."*

As Griffith’s weakened husk clung to any semblance of life, the crimson Behelit lay within his withered hands. A wordless face swam in existence, eager to heed its master's call. The deep desire within Griffith to summon the Godhand and acquire his own kingdom.

The infamous Eclipse comes to life within Survivor’s Bane. The Eclipse is an apocalyptic event within Berserk, where the Band of the Hawk was sacrificed by their former leader to exalt him to member of the Godhand. Within the Eclipse, these doomed warriors were teleported to a hellscape, mercilessly slaughtered after being marked with the Brand of Sacrifice.

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/1TJ1I2RUC88C1745623202215.gif)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/1TJ1I2RUC88C1745623202215.gif)

While Survivor’s Bane is active during the Struggler’s Path event, you'll be able to fight within the Eclipse itself. Every other minute, you’ll experience the full Eclipse for 60 seconds. Rally as though you are side by side with the Band of the Hawk, fighting for your life despite the brand of Sacrifice freshly etched upon you.

The Struggler’s Bane brings fearsome new horrors. Terrifying demons will attempt to claim your life, and the land around you will be familiar despite being transformed into a hellscape. While the Eclipse is active, a new unique boss also awaits there to execute the Godhand’s bidding.

## Fight Zodd in the Apostle’s Challenge

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/T7132P6RR7UW1745623205385.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/T7132P6RR7UW1745623205385.png)

Feared throughout Midland, warriors and noblemen tremble at the whisper of his name. Nosferatu Zodd is an immortal legendary warrior known for his ferocity and mercilessness; he’s said to have slain thousands on the battlefield. Zodd has now set his thirst for blood upon Sanctuary, intent on finding an opponent formidable enough for his might.

To fight Zodd, you’ll need to earn **Apostle’s Tributes**, which can be earned by completing specific daily tasks. Only one tribute is needed to fight Zodd, even when playing in party.

You’ll soon be able to challenge this embodiment of bloodlust—survive the storm of his attacks and prove yourself his most worthy opponent in his over three hundred years of slaughter!

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/4TY6D2N6LVR31745623205286.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/4TY6D2N6LVR31745623205286.png)

Everything on the battlefield is a weapon to Zodd. The longer you fight him, the more he will relish the struggle, increasing the wrath you’ll face. Those that hold the **Crimson Behelit Legendary Gem** will have the ability to face an even more powerful Zodd. Execute your best pincer move and defeat this enraged Zodd to claim tantalizing rewards.

Defeating Zodd earns you a chance to obtain weapon cosmetics from the Struggler’s Path, and a chance to obtain the fabled Crimson Behelit Legendary Gem.

Horadric Scholars have poured over their tomes to discover the origin of increased materialized wealth, while the merchants of Sanctuary have seized the opportunity to increase their abundance. The source: Winds of Fortune.

While Winds of Fortune is live, activate your 24-hour buff to receive this duplicate effect. Once activated, the buff’s duration is in real time and will end 24 hours after it’s been initiated. If you have not activated the buff within 24 hours of the event ending, it will be enabled automatically. Any rewards not claimed before the end of the event will be auto-claimed on next log-in.

## Feature Updates

#### Dungeon Affix Adjustments

We are changing some existing dungeon affixes:

Disruptive Portal: The Butcher’s Health has been reduced.

Electric: Cooldown has increased so the effect occurs less frequently. There’s now a 1 second delay after being marked before you can damage one another.

Chains: Cooldown has increased so the effect occurs less frequently. Chain duration is shortened when not broken by players, no longer occurs in elevators and other small areas in dungeons.

Explosive Reaction: damage is a fixed amount based on monster and difficulty level, not a percentage of player Health.

Sacrificial Power: Health cost is now based on skill cooldown rather than all skills having the same cost. Skills with a cooldown of 12 seconds will have the same cost as the current cost.

#### Destruction’s Call Optimization

Building off previous improvements, we’re adding Havoc Orbs to Destruction’s Call. Havoc Orbs will trigger one of the 4 effects for a short period of time.

Hastened Havoc Aura: Increases Move and Attack speeds of player and any teammates within the aura.

Critical Hit Havoc Aura: Increases your Critical Hit Chance and any teammates within the aura.

Healing Havoc Aura: Heals you and any teammates within the aura.

Havoc Guardians: Summons three Havoc Guardians to fight with you.

#### Mount Zavain and Black Forest Boss Timer

The World Map will now display a countdown timer for World Bosses in the Black Forest and Mount Zavain.

A gray icon on the map will indicate where the boss is, but that it’s inactive. A colored icon with a time indicates how much time remains until the boss spawns, and when the icon is colored and says “Active,” the boss is ready to spawn at any time.

#### Familiars Can Now Attack the Training Dummy

Familiars will now have their damage added when using the Training Dummy to better estimate your total damage output. You can also use the Breakdown menu to see how much damage your Familiar is specifically dealing.

#### Battleground Changes

We are making a couple changes to Battleground.

First, attacks by the Ancient Heart in the rebirth area will cause damage regardless of your immunity to damage and critical hits.

Secondly, the Lifesteal effect of damaging the Final Guardian has been reduced by 50%.
