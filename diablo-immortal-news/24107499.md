## Risk and Rally Limited-Time Event

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/1LXDQLIXJJ701721430736464.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/1LXDQLIXJJ701721430736464.png)

Speed shall pave your road to recognition in Risk and Rally. From **July 25, 3 a.m.–August 11, 3 a.m. server time**, a dungeon will be selected every 12 hours. You must cleanse this hive of Hell with the utmost swiftness to not only keep Sanctuary safe, but to cement yourself on the Risk and Rally leaderboards.

There will be a specific leaderboard for each current dungeon. Clear the dungeon by defeating its final boss as quickly as possible. Depending on your clear time, you’ll be added to that dungeon’s leaderboard, receiving points for your placement. You can run the current dungeon as many times as you’d like within the 12-hour instance—only your best time will be recorded, along with points received for that entry. The points you earn during each 12-hour instance will go towards your placement on the Risk and Rally leaderboard for the whole event. Both the current dungeon and Risk and Rally leaderboards are split out by Inferno and Hell difficulty and by class. You will only be competing against players on your same server.

This event is open to all players Level 30 and above. The selected dungeon can be any of the following: Cavern of Echoes, Forgotten Tower, Mad King’s Breach, Temple of Namari, Tomb of Fahir, or Tristram Cathedral.

Placing on the 12-hour leaderboards will grant you:

- Slots 1-3: 50 points, 10,000 Gold, 7 Beguiling Dust
- Slots 4-20: 45 points, 8,000 Gold, 6 Beguiling Dust
- Slots 21-150: 35 points, 6,000 Gold, 5 Beguiling dust
- Slots 151-500: 25 points, 4,000 Gold, 2 Beguiling Dust
- Slots 501-1,000: 10 points, 2,000 gold

If you’re mighty enough to place on the Risk and Rally leaderboard, you’ll receive:

- Slots 1-3: Demon’s Dash30-day Arcana Nimbus aura cosmetic, 5 random Legendary Items, 4 Legendary Crests, 100,000 Gold, 20 Beguiling Dust, 200 Scrap
- Slots 4-15: 14-day Arcana Nimbus aura cosmetic, 5 random Legendary Items, 3 Legendary Crests, 85,000 Gold, 14 Beguiling Dust, 150 Scrap
- Slots 16-100: 7-day Arcana Nimbus aura cosmetic, 5 random Legendary Items, a Legendary Crest, 65,000 Gold, 14 Beguiling Dust, 100 Scrap
- Slots 101-500: 5 random Legendary Items, 40,000 Gold, 10 Beguiling Dust, 50 Scrap

## Revenant Market

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/Z9NG56GJKUOS1721430736478.jpg)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/Z9NG56GJKUOS1721430736478.jpg)

A new type of market has come to Diablo Immortal. And with it, the opportunity to acquire two previously released Phantom Market Cosmetics: Hatred’s Heritage and Deathless Destiny.

The Revenant Market is available from **July 25, 3 a.m.–August 9, 2:59 a.m. server time**. You can use **Obols** to purchase the ability to **draw one item at random** from the available item pool. There are 10 items total, and each can only be acquired once. Each time you draw an item, the cost of the next draw will increase. Performing 10 draws guarantees that you will receive all items available in the Revenant Market. Your first draw from the Revenant Market is free.

Tokens are a new feature of the Revenant Market. Instead of providing you with one reward when pulled, you’ll be given a choice between two items of that type: Portal, Ultimate, or Cosmetic Set. Choose carefully—each Token type can only be acquired once.

Obols can be purchased with Eternal Orbs, and any unspent Obols will be converted into Platinum once the Revenant Market event ends. If you draw a Token for an item you already own, it’ll be converted into Eternal Orbs equivalent to the draw cost. Any unused Tokens will automatically be exchanged for the next available unowned Cosmetic item at the end of the event.

Because items from the Revenant Market are acquired randomly per draw, we want to provide players with the initial draw rates for each item to aid in deciding if they would like to engage with the event past their first free draw.

***Note: The drop rates provided below are for the first draw only and will adjust to reflect the relative probabilities of the remaining items in the Revenant Market item pool.***

- Covet Emoji: 60.00%
- Unholy Passengers Vermilion Weapon: 15,63%
- Unholy Passengers Flame Weapon: 15.63%
- Portal Token: 3.16% (Choose between the Hatred’s Heritage or Deathless Destiny Portal)
- Frostmoon Frame: 1.77%
- Unholy Passengers Vermilion Armor: 1.14%
- Unholy Passengers Flame Armor: 1.14%
- Borealis Portal: 0.76%
- Ultimate Token: 0.63% (Choose between the Hatred’s Heritage or Deathless Destiny Ultimate)
- Cosmetic Set Token: 0.13% (Choose between the Hatred’s Heritage or Deathless Destiny Cosmetic Set)

Current owners of the Hatred’s Heritage and Deathless Destiny Cosmetic Sets will automatically receive them for the Tempest class when the new Revenant Market begins. We are looking for more opportunities to bring Phantom Market Cosmetics from days past to the Tempest and Blood Knight, so keep your eyes peeled.

## Feature Updates

#### Health Bars in PvP

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/W7QLFW338DPC1721430736166.jpg)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/W7QLFW338DPC1721430736166.jpg)

In PvP, if you have a Cheat Death skill equipped, your Health bar will display with a silver frame around it.

> **Developer’s Note:** *We want to provide more information in PvP so all participants can better equip themselves for the activity. Here are some places where Cheat Death skills can be derived from:*
>
> - *The Savior Familiar Skill.*
> - *Class skills, such as the Barbarian’s Undying Rage.*
> - *The Phoenix Ashes Five-Star Legendary Gem.*
> - *The Cheat Death Paragon Specialization Skill.*

#### New Blacksmith Location

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/5CC672SSXWN71721430736137.jpg)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/5CC672SSXWN71721430736137.jpg)

A Blacksmith has been added to the Court of Whispers for convenient access.

#### Castle Erebban

There is now a chance to receive Monstrous Essence from opening Treasure Chests in Castle Erebban.

#### Battleground Zealous Idols

The resurrection time for defenders in a Zealous Idols match of Battleground will increase relative to the objective’s position. The idol with the greatest progress will be used to determine the defending team’s resurrection time.

#### Paragon Trees

Paragon Specializations can now be activated from the central node of the tree. Your last set of activated skills with each specialization will be remembered, making it more convenient to switch specializations.

#### Armory Optimization

The Armory will now have an option to save your selected Reforge Loadout as part of each profile. This option is enabled by default but can be disabled in the Armory options menu.

> **Developer’s Note:** *We will continue to improve the functionality of the Armory, expanding it to include more systems. Be on the lookout for additional improvements in the future.*

#### Automatic Navigation

Using Dash Skills, like the Wizard’s Teleport, while Auto Navigating will no longer interrupt the navigation.

#### Dream and Drown (Cascade) Legendary Item

Internal cooldown between triggered waves increased from 0.5 to 1 second. Damage of triggered waves increased by 100%.

#### Helliquary

Upon defeating a Helliquary boss, your teammates’ Heavy Austere Chests will automatically open.
