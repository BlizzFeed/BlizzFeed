## Battle Pass 46: Forbidden Palate

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_folder_media/ws/WS68JNFR4ZF71762560379036.jpg)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_folder_media/ws/WS68JNFR4ZF71762560379036.jpg)

Those who indulge in their brethren’s flesh learn that there is no sweeter sin. Though you may try to resist, the spirit craves that which it has previously been denied.

The Season 46 Battle Pass boldly enters Sanctuary on **November 20**, at **3 a.m. server time**, containing a Battle Pass with 40 ranks worth of rewards, such as Crests, hilts, Legendary Gems, and more.

Begin feasting on these desirable rewards by smashing monsters and rising through the ranks before your delicacy spoils and ends on **December 17, at 3 a.m. server time**.

## Wild Monsters Join the Fray

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/rl/RLFYWU74XK4K1762560573190.jpg)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/rl/RLFYWU74XK4K1762560573190.jpg)

The untamed lands of Sanctuary teem with dangerous creatures, from the Dark Wood to the Frozen Tundra. These blood-lusted creatures move with the land, and their animal perceptions are keen to recognize threats near their hovels and homes.

When fighting in Wild Zones, nearby monsters may detect the fighting and join in. If they notice your struggle and rise to quell the unknown presence, a horn will sound which alerts you to their presence.

Monsters will only be alerted to your presence after you attack one of them, so if you’re deep within enemy territory weigh your decisions wisely.

## Winds of Fortune Gust

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/o5/O512VCPORVSQ1762560866932.jpg)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/o5/O512VCPORVSQ1762560866932.jpg)

Horadric scholars are deep in study, unraveling the mysteries behind a sudden surge in materialized wealth. Meanwhile, Sanctuary’s merchants waste no time, capitalizing on the phenomenon to expand their fortunes.

From **November 12, 3:00 a.m.—November 18, 3:00 a.m. local server time**, you will be able to activate a special boost and earn increased rewards!

While Winds of Fortune is live, activate your 24-hour buff to receive this boost. Once activated, the buff duration is in real time and will end 24 hours after it’s been initiated. If you have not activated the buff within 24 hours of the event ending, it will be enabled automatically. Any rewards not claimed before the end of the event will be auto-claimed on the next log-in.

During Winds of Fortune, the 4-player party Normal Gems bonus drop will be doubled and unaffected by the daily cap, and only the first twelve Common Gems of the day will be tradable.

Any of these items will drop in duplicate quantities:

- Gold
- Experience
- Battle Pass Points
- Normal Gems
- Legendary Items
- Items

Items from these activities will also drop in duplicate quantities:

- Horadric Bestiary
- Challenge Rifts
- Bounties
- Fishing
- Dungeons
- Purge the Depths
- Accursed Towers
- Hidden Lairs
- Farming in the wilderness
- Codex Activities

Note that during this limited-time event, Battle Pass rewards will not be doubled, and the weekly limit on Battle Pass Points and Normal Gems will not change. Any bonus Experience gained will be affected by your current modifier.

Additionally, there are limits to each item that can be earned in duplicate quantities. You’ll earn double the amount until you reach the limit. Check out the event in-game for more details.

## Upcoming Changes to Selling Gems

In a future update, we are disabling the ability to sell Normal Gems ranked 2-5 in exchange for Platinum on the Market. If you have any Rank 2-5 Normals Gems and attempt to post them to the Market, they’ll be dismantled down to Rank 1 and posted as Rank 1 Gems.

This upcoming change will take effect on November 26th, 2025. During this time, all 2-5 Normal Gem posts on the Market will expire and be removed. You can collect them from the Market and re-post them, but the new changes will take effect. Vic in Westmarch will also offer the option to dismantle the Rank 2-5 Gems as well.
