Chaos is at your doorstep, adventurer. Can you quell it?

Welcome to Destruction’s Wake, our fourth and largest major update for Diablo Immortal thus far. It goes without saying that your support of Diablo Immortal for the past year is what has brought us to this colossal milestone, our anniversary celebration. We cannot thank you enough for all your valuable feedback and hours spent protecting Sanctuary from the demonic legions of the Burning Hells. **Thank you for making this possible!**

After the upcoming maintenance periods, players with a Paragon Level lower than their Server’s Paragon Level can receive up to a 1,500% experience bonus to help them catch up and experience the features detailed in this major update. We have also raised the Server Paragon Level for all Servers to 800.

In our next content update, which is coming in about two weeks, we will introduce another wave of Server Merges for Japanese servers.

As with previous Content Updates, there will be server maintenance starting on **June 14, from 5 p.m.–7 p.m. PDT** for Oceania, China, East Asia, Southeast Asia, and Europe Servers, and on **June 15, from 1 a.m.–3 a.m. PDT** for servers in the Americas. ## New Zone and Questline: Ancients’ Cradle

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/J7OJR5HXCZF11686623853061.jpg)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/J7OJR5HXCZF11686623853061.jpg)

Your next journey takes you to **Ancients’ Cradle**, a remote island in the Forgotten Sea. Meet the ancient **Lilin** and **Inari** peoples--whose lineage can be traced back to Sanctuary’s creation--as you continue to pursue the Cult of Terror following your retrieval of the Pathstone. You must keep your wits about you adventurer, for the Cult of Terror will stop at nothing to purify the Worldstone, putting the power of creation in the hands of Diablo himself.

To visit the Ancients’ Cradle Zone and embark on its **main questline**, you must first **reach Hell Difficulty VI** and complete the Fare You Well prologue quest. Speak with Zathan outside of Deckard Cain’s workshop to begin the prologue quest. The island is rife with sacred Lilin and Inari ruins to explore—you’ll also help quell rising tensions between the two groups, clean up corruption, and restore the island to its former glory across **8 new side quests**.

Don’t forget to hone your demon decimating skills before travelling to Ancient’s Cradle. This Zone is plagued by the **Afflicted** (locals who have been transformed into a new evil by the curse), Amalgamations (ravenous flora and fauna who will relegate you to the bottom of the island’s food chain), and **Cradleguard** (constructs sent into a never-ending frenzy because of the Pathstone’s removal from the island). There’s no shortage of new haunts from the Burning Hells to snuff out!

Tensions are rising, adventure. Will you rise to meet them?

## New Combat Mechanic: Stances

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/LIWG0RS6N1R91686623862417.jpg)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/LIWG0RS6N1R91686623862417.jpg)

A martial innovation from the times of yore has once resurfaced in Sanctuary. It is time you worked new **Stances** into your nightmare-slaying routine. To utilize Stances, you must be in the **Inferno Difficulty**. Once you’re playing on this difficulty, you’ll see a second set of weapon slots unlocked; equip items to these slots for Stances to automatically enable itself. The additional slots can have gems socketed into them to further enhance your character’s power.

Now that you’ve got the right equipment, it’s time to teach you how to use your Stances. All Classes have both an **Offensive and Defensive Stance**. They each provide unique passive bonuses that further the proficiencies of your Class and can be **shifted between every 30 seconds** while in combat. Upon switching Stances, the passive bonus will be twice as strong for the first 3 seconds, allowing you to immediately reap the benefits of your Offensive of Defensive stance.

Listed below are the Offensive and Defensive Stances and their passive bonuses for each Class.

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/jp/JPWG4JW74DXS1686628526916.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/jp/JPWG4JW74DXS1686628526916.png)

There are a few additional things to note about Stances:

- While 4 weapons are equipped, all Legendary Gems, Legendary Item Affixes, and Attributes will be active.
- Offensive bonuses of equipped weapons are reduced to 60% power while in the Defensive Stance.
- You may only equip one Legendary Item that affects each Skill while Stances are active.

Stances inject another layer of variety into the moment-to-moment combat of Diablo Immortal. We are excited to expand the ways in which players can react to whatever Hell throws their way and seeing our community take their builds to new heights.

## New Set: Seeds of Sown Gold

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/2D6TAS2E5F6I1686623862382.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/2D6TAS2E5F6I1686623862382.png)

Seeds of Sown Gold is a new item set that’s obtainable from dungeons in **Inferno Difficulty I and higher**. This set is centered around Channeled Skills and rewards players who continually channel to deal additional damage.

**Seeds of Sown Gold (2-Piece Bonus)**: Continually Channeling a skill against the same enemy gradually increases the damage of that skill up to a maximum of 30%.

**Seeds of Sown Gold (4-Piece Bonus)**: After you stop Channeling a skill, enemies hit by it take an additional explosion of damage for 30% of the damage dealt to them by that Channeled skill.

**Seeds of Sown Gold (6-Piece Bonus)**: After you start Channeling a skill, gain Artisan's Endurance for 8 seconds. While Channeling under the effect of Artisan's Endurance, you are immune to knockback effects and damage dealt to you is reduced by 40%. This effect cannot occur more often than once every 40 seconds.\
Seeds of Sown Gold items can be acquired from the following dungeons:

- **Artisan’s Care (Neck)**: Silent Monastery
- **Gleaming Gullers (Boots)**: Cavern of Echoes
- **Harvest of Dust (Gloves)**: Destruction’s End
- **Revolving Folly (Belt)**: Pit of Anguish
- **Roundray (Ring)**: Temple of Namari

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/7OKPBO88OWIM1686623861560.jpg)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/7OKPBO88OWIM1686623861560.jpg)

Starting on **June 15, 3:00 a.m.** reclaim Sanctuary with friends at your heels by using the **Recruit or Recall a Friend** feature.

**To recruit or recall players**, you must be at least Level 20. Each player has a unique code that can be found on the event’s page. Copy and share this code with your friend. Alternatively, you can recall a player via an in-game invitation system located within the event’s menu.

**To be recruited**, the player must be Level 20 or below and have not already been recruited by another player. The recruited player can accept an invitation by inputting the recruiter’s code into the Call of Allies event menu.

**To be recalled**, the player must be above Level 20 and not had any character on their account recalled previously or played Diablo Immortal in the last 14 days. Once you log in, input your friend’s code into the Reunited Allies event page within 24 hours.

The recruiting or recalling player will receive **10 Recruitment Points** the first time they complete each of the following tasks:

- **Send an invite using the in-game invitation system**: 10 Recruitment Points
- **Recruit or recall 1 player**: 10 Recruitment Points
- **1 Recruited or recalled player earns 27000 Battle Points**: 10 Recruitment Points
- **2 Recruited or recalled players earn 2700 Battle Points**: 10 Recruitment Points
- **3 Recruited or recalled players earn 2700 Battle Points**: 10 Recruitment Points

As you earn Recruitment Points, you’ll unlock:

- **10 Recruitment Points**: 2 Beguiling Dust, 30 Scrap, and 5K Gold
- **20 Recruitment Points**: 6 Rare Crests and 6 Normal Gems
- **30 Recruitment Points**: 1 Legendary Crest, 3 Rare Crests, and 6 Normal Gems
- **40 Recruitment Points**: 1 Legendary Crest, 3 Rare Crests, and 6 Normal Gems
- **50 Recruitment Points**: 3 Legendary Crests and 9 Rare Crests

The ability to recruit or recall players ends on **July 27 at 3:00 a.m. server time**, but players will have until **August 23** to complete tasks and earn rewards.

## New Legendary Items, Skills, and Class Balance

### New Skills

We’re adding an additional Skill for each Class that **unlocks at level 53**. The aim with these new Skills is to provide additional group utility or fill niches to better round out the toolkit of each Class.

#### Barbarian

**Sunder - 12 Second Cooldown – Damage**: Briefly launch into the air and smash the ground with your weapon, generating a huge shockwave that deals damage to all enemies in the area while also increasing all damage they take by 30% for 6 seconds. Players will take increased damage for 3 seconds.

#### Crusader

**Fist of the Heavens - 7 Second Cooldown – Damage**: Call down divine wrath to strike up to 8 nearby enemies damaging them. For every 10% of a targeted enemy's missing Life, the damage dealt to that target by Fist of the Heavens will be increased by 25%.

[![Video](https://markdown-videos-api.jorgenkh.no/youtube/KisjFJUwgcQ?width=1280&height=720)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KisjFJUwgcQ)

#### Demon Hunter

**Shadowsight - 24 Second Cooldown – Buff**: Immediately reveal all Bosses, Treasure Goblins, Elite monsters, and enemy players within 90 yards of you on the minimap and increase the Movement Speed of yourself and nearby allies by 20% for 12 seconds.

[![Video](https://markdown-videos-api.jorgenkh.no/youtube/kRA3686us8I?width=1280&height=720)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kRA3686us8I)

#### Monk

**Breath of Heaven - 12 Second Cooldown – Buff**: Immediately restore Life and grant 60% increased Movement Speed to yourself and all nearby allies. Using Breath of Heaven on the same target again within 9 seconds reduces the healing amount by 50%.

[![Video](https://markdown-videos-api.jorgenkh.no/youtube/VMuFUjMYE5M?width=1280&height=720)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VMuFUjMYE5M)

#### Necromancer

**Borrowed Time - 30 Second Cooldown – Buff**: Grant yourself or an ally borrowed time for 8 seconds. If the target takes fatal damage during this borrowed time, they will be resurrected at full Life, but with a curse. The curse will drain 5% of the target's maximum Life every second and lasts for a maximum of 16 seconds. The curse can be removed sooner if the target kills an enemy or assists in killing an enemy. Borrowed Time will be automatically used on you when you take fatal damage if it is not on cooldown.

[![Video](https://markdown-videos-api.jorgenkh.no/youtube/4fh8nqpdptk?width=1280&height=720)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4fh8nqpdptk)

#### Wizard

**Frostfield - 20 Second Cooldown – Control**: Create a field of gathering hoarfrost in an area. After a short delay, all enemies in the area will take damage and be Frozen in ice for 12 seconds. ## Feature Updates

### Combat Rating Adjustment

Combat Rating and Primary Attribute are now separate statistics. The Combat Rating for items will be displayed directly on them and is no longer based on the item’s attribute.

Items that drop will now have greater Primary Attribute diversity and an increased chance to have a higher Combat Rating. This is to help players grow their Combat Rating quickly and introduce more variety in their Item choices.

### Battleground and Challenge Rift Schedule Change

On some servers, Battleground would reset in as little as 3 weeks, not leaving enough time for players to push to their desired rank. While on other servers, an Immortal reign might be strong and last for longer periods of time, resulting in less rewards because the season doesn't end as frequently. Similarly, Challenge Rifts have weekly and monthly rewards that aren't directly tied to any in-game events. The following changes aim to update and unify the way this content in Diablo Immortal resets, to make the rewards and timing more consistent and forgiving.

Starting on June 16, all current Battleground seasons will end, and rewards will be distributed accordingly. Battleground and Challenge Rifts will now reset on a 10-week cycle that is independent of Cycle of Strife—the rewards are being updated accordingly as well. In addition to the seasonal one-time bracket and end of season rewards, Battleground will provide a weekly reward based upon your rank. Battleground participation is also being removed as a requirement for Badges of Honor because it's no longer tied to the reign being reset.

Battleground rewards:

- **Weekly Rewards**: Purification Shards, Charms, Scrap
- **End of Season Rewards**: Reforge Stones, Normal Gems

Challenge Rifts will continue to provide weekly rewards, but the monthly rewards will instead be distributed at the end of the season. The 2, 3, and 4 player Leaderboards have been removed as well. Our goal is to consolidate the seasonal gameplay into one event that happens on a fixed schedule. This is being done to provide players with more time to complete the activities and normalize the rewards for all players regardless of the Cycle of Strife cadence on their server.

Challenge Rift rewards:

- **Rift Weekly Rewards**: Gold, Hilts, Scrap
- **End of Season Rewards**: Enigmatic Crystals, Hilts

### Hidden Lair Rewards

Hidden Lairs now reward guaranteed Normal Gems for each objective, up to a weekly maximum of 21 Hidden Lairs (42 floors). The entrance menu will still indicate whether the player has reached the individual caps for each gem type, but it’s now a weekly total instead of daily. This should reduce the effort needed to earn all associated rewards and assist players who might be catching up.

### Accursed Towers

To give smaller Clans increased opportunity to claim towers and allow a smoother start to the season on servers with higher populations, we made some adjustments to Accursed Towers:

- The maximum number of towers available in each season has increased from 150 to 200.
- The number of regions has increased from 3 to 4.
- 2 Regions will unlock during the first week of each season.

### Ancestral Tableau

To support the adjustments to Combat Rating on gear, the Ancestral Tableau will now have a separate stat for Combat Rating. Max Bonus values for each stat, except Combat Rating, will use the highest value among items placed in the Tableau. The Max Bonus value for Combat Rating will be determined by averaging up to the top 4 Combat Rating values among items placed in the Tableau.

For example, if Player A has a triple stat item that has 200 Intellect, Willpower, and Fortitude—plus 500 Combat Rating—and Player B has a double stat weapon with 300 Vitality and Willpower—plus 550 Combat Rating—then the Max Bonus for the Ancestral Tableau would be: 200 Intellect and Fortitude, and 300 Vitality, Willpower, and 550 Combat Rating. Since there's no other weapons in the Ancestral Tableau in this example, the Max Bonus for Strength would be 0.

The drop rate of Ancestral Weapons has increased as well.

### Deeds of Valor Badges

The Akeba's Chosen Shadows Clan leader badge now requires that you be the leader of a top 10 Clan by the end of the Immortal reign. Additionally, Battleground will no longer count towards your Deeds of Valor badge because Battleground no longer resets with the Cycle of Strife.

> **Developer’s Note:** *This change is meant to prevent players from becoming the leader of a Shadow Clan at the end of a cycle to get the best badge possible. These badges are meant to reward the leadership and organization required to run a clan and be successful in Diablo Immortal.*

The requirements to earn the Indomitable Deeds of Valor badge have also been adjusted to make it more readily obtainable for Immortals.

### Shadows Daily Quest Change

The Shadows Daily Challenge that required players to “win a Battleground match” is now “play a Battleground match” to allow for quicker completion.

### Immortal Eternal Crown Bonuses

When a new Immortal reign starts, the Immortal's Eternal Crown bonuses will now begin as a 16% Damage Increase and 16% Defense Increase. The maximum benefit will persist as a 32% Damage Increase and 32% Defense Increase.

### Paragon Specialization in Battleground

Paragon Specializations can no longer be swapped during an active Battleground. They may still be swapped prior to the start of battle.

> **Developer’s Note:** *Just like a regular Skill, it was never intended for players to swap out of a Paragon Specializations while its prominent abilities were on cooldown. We believe this strategy only further increases PvP lethality without any real downsides to players who choose to abuse the ability to swap specializations. We want Paragon choices to matter and bypassing the cooldowns circumvented these design goals.*

### Codex Objectives for Inferno Difficulties

There are new Codex Objectives for Inferno Difficulties I, II, and III. They unlock at Paragon Level 800, 900, and 1,000 respectively.

### Prodigy’s Path Refresh

We're refreshing Prodigy's Path to complement the new Paragon Level cap.

- Prodigy's Path IV will unlock when players reach Level 700.
- Prodigy's Path I will disappear from the menu once players unlock Prodigy's Path IV. All unclaimed rewards will be sent to your Inbox.
- Players can continue to claim Prodigy's Path IV rewards after Paragon Level 800, based upon the Expected Paragon Level, if they choose to stop leveling at Paragon Level 799

### Hero's Journey

Following the maintenance windows for this major content update, all current progress for Hero’s Journey Chapters 1 through 6 will be paused and a new Hero’s Journey will begin. Only Chapters 1 through 4 will be available for the new Hero’s Journey, with Chapters 5 and 6 coming later. This Hero’s Journey is planned to be available for about six months, like previous iterations.

While completing their Chapters, adventurers will receive a bevy of rewards, and once Chapter 4 is completed, they will receive the **Resplendent Journey Frame**—a token of triumph.

### Activity Scheduler

Scheduled activities created by your fellow Warband or Clan members will now display a red dot to increase their visibility.
