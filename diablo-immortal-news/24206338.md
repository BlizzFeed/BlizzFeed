## *Prince of Freedom* Main Quest

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/M5HUS6ZFAL321749507268068.jpg)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/M5HUS6ZFAL321749507268068.jpg)

In *Prince of Freedom*, you'll follow the track of Albrecht and the Shardborne further into the Sharval Wilds. You'll return to Orthrup, visit the township of Highfork, and navigate the atmosphere of revolt bubbling throughout every corner of Sharval as you travel further west. Within the shadowed canopies of Sharval's deep woods, darker answers—and new questions—await. ## Clobber the Competition in Party Duels

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/1G87ESQEFTPO1749507268980.jpg)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/1G87ESQEFTPO1749507268980.jpg)

Want to prove you’re Sanctuary’s finest defender? Grab your best loadout and head to the wilderness with up to three other players in your party. Using the new Party Duels PvP feature, you can initiate a duel with another team of up to four players. To get the fight started, click/tap on another player and select the “Challenge” button. Once your invitation has been accepted, a warm-up phase will be initiated, and then the brawl begins!

To the winner go the spoils, which in this case are commemorative badges of your triumphant victory. All players can initiate a Party Duel, and any monsters in the vicinity of your duel will be excluded from the conflict.

Party Duels were created as a flexible, barrier-free PvP offering that can be engaged with on your terms. We look forward to seeing how you use this new way of playing.

**To unlock Inferno Difficulty VII, you must defeat Deosbrae, Sovereign Shade.**

## Ancient Legendary Items

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/VH77W71Q5X2P1749507266378.jpg)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/VH77W71Q5X2P1749507266378.jpg)

Powerful armor thought to exist only in tales told to fledgling warriors has been exhumed from the depths of Sanctuary. Five Ancient Legendary Helmets, each containing four Attributes and four Affixes, can be earned as a drop or crafted at the Blacksmith in Inferno Difficulty VII and higher. All Affixes on Ancient Legendary items are predetermined, with one new Affix being unique to each item. We’ll have more information about which Affixes can be found on these Helmets in a future article.

Ancient Legendary items can be acquired as a drop from the following activities: Helliquary, Helliquary Gauntlet, Vanguard, Terror Rifts, Oblivion Pillars, and Hellslayer Bounties.

To craft these Helmets, you’ll need to acquire 100 of a new resource called Ancient Artifacts, and visit a Blacksmith. This resource can be acquired from the following sources: Helliquary Bosses, daily Dungeon rewards, and Battle Pass Daily Chests.

Once acquired, these new Ancient Legendary items will not only boost your Combat Rating significantly, but the Affixes they provide create new directions to take your build in. We’re extremely excited for you to beat back Hell’s spawn using Ancient Legendary items.

Here’s a look at the pre-determined Attributes and Affixes for each Helmet:

**Hordesplitter's Spirit:**

- Increased 6% damage while moving (New Affix)
- Beneficial Effect Duration increased by 4.5%
- Critical Hit chance increased by 3%
- Critical Hit damage increased by 4.5%

**Unveiled Secrets:**

- When hitting an enemy from behind, the damage dealt is increased 6% (New Affix)
- Damage done to players increased by 1.5%
- Damage increased 6% to enemies below 30% Life
- Accuracy Rating increased by 9%

**First Strike:**

- Primary attacks have 8% chance to shatter on impact exploding to deal 100% of their damage to nearby enemies (New Affix)
- Primary Attack Damage increased by 4.5%
- Attack Speed increased by 3%
- Your primary attack has a 5% chance to trigger Shivering Breath when hitting and enemy

**Eagertrigger's Sight:**

- Picking up a Health Globe has 24% chance to reset the cooldown of one of your skills (New Affix)
- Attack Speed increased by 3%
- Skill cooldown reduced by 3%
- Skill Damage increased by 3%

**Graverunner:**

- Reduces the duration of Total Loss of Control effects by 6% (New Affix)
- [Endure] Evasion Rating is increased by 0.8% for every nearby enemy, up to a maximum of 8%
- [Endure] Evading an attack has a % chance to grant you a shield equal to 5% of your maximum Life for 5 seconds
- Damage taken from players reduced by 1.5%

## Set Hunter Event

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/VBFNIZUQZKDT1750120111421.jpg)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/VBFNIZUQZKDT1750120111421.jpg)

Acquiring that missing set item to finish your build just got easier! Before beginning your next dungeon run, select the set you wish to target on the event’s menu or using the option at the bottom-left corner of the dungeon menu, then start your run as normal. If you did not receive an item from your selected set as a reward, then you’ll have an increased chance to receive it during your next dungeon run.

Here’s how the drop chance increases each time you do not receive an item from your targeted set:

- **Run one:** 22% increased chance on your next run.
- **Run two:** 33% increased chance on your next run.
- **Run three:** 50% increased chance on your next run.
- **Run four:** 100% increased chance on your next run.

Dungeons will continue to only drop set items they’re able to reward. For more information about which Set Items drop from each dungeon, visit the Codex. This event will be active from **June 19–August 25, 3 a.m. server time**.

### Paragon Skills

We are introducing four new Legendary Paragon Skills. These skills can be progressively upgraded by spending Paragon Points to unlock additional attributes without requiring all 80 Paragon Points to fully unlock the skill. Each Legendary Paragon Skill contains 20 ranks costing 4 Paragon Points each to unlock. Each rank provides different attributes, and the final rank unlocks the skill for use.

The following Legendary Paragon Skills unlock at Paragon 900:

- Shepherd and Protector: Each of your Summons gains an absorb shield equal to 40% of your maximum Life for 3 seconds when they are first summoned.
- Curse of Weakness: Your loss of control effects also apply Curse of Weakness to enemies, reducing the damage they deal by 30% for 3 seconds. Cannot occur on the same target more often than once every 10 seconds.
- Soul Harvester: Harvest the soul of fallen foes, granting you an absorb shield equal to 10% of your maximum Life for 3 seconds. Cannot occur more often than once every 10 seconds. The shield's effectiveness is tripled when harvesting the soul of a player.
- Frigid Blast - Dash skills unleash a blast of frigid ice around you Chilling nearby enemies, reducing their Attack and Movement Speed by 30% for 3 seconds. Cannot occur more often than once every 10 seconds.

## New Familiar Passive Skills

Two new Familiar Passive Skills have been added:

- **Phalanx:** If your Life is above a set percentage, you receive a Damage increase. If your Life drops below a set percentage, you gain a Defense increase for a few seconds. This is increased by a set percentage for each additional Familiar applied status effect to a set limit.
- **Guardian:** The Familiar applies a Guardian status effect to you. If your Life drops below a set percentage, the effect ends and restores a percentage of your max Life. Recovery is increased for every nearby enemy when the status effect ends, to a maximum of 100%.

Additionally, the Familiar Passive Skill Savior has been modified to reduce the power of the Familiar’s Active Skill as a trade-off for saving the player from death.

## Feature Updates

### PC Key Shortcuts

- Added an auto-run key that causes your character to automatically follow the position of your mouse cursor. Default key: ` (the tilde key).
- Added a new setting that allows you to click on your skills to cast them. This setting is not enabled by default.
- You can now bind actions to side mouse buttons. Currently, only up to two side mouse buttons are supported, but we will be looking at supporting additional side mouse buttons in the future.
- You can now press Spacebar to skip cutscenes.

### Portal Interruption

Ever been caught off guard digging through your map to set up a portal, only to be stopped by a small spider? We’ve improved the portal experience by reducing interruptions that cancel the action of summoning a portal in the wilderness.

> **Developer’s Note:** *These changes minimize frustrating moments where players are unintentionally pulled into combat. Our goal is to make traversal feel smoother and more respectful of your intent. There will still be interruption rules for PvP and other events.*

### Stormpoint Keep

An additional waypoint was added to Stormpoint Keep near the southern ramparts, between the existing Stormpoints Battlements and the Barbican Traverse waypoints.

### Battleground Matchmaking

To continue our ongoing initiative to improve Battleground’s matchmaking system, we have introduced another round of updates. These updates aim to more fairly match you against other players of relative skill, resulting in more balanced games. We plan to continue fine-tuning this system in the future.

### Battleground Convoy

We’ve received feedback that players feel as though the default camera angle and a lack of information about what’s going on during a match have made it difficult to play Battleground Convoy. To address this feedback, we’ve made some changes:

- At any time during a match, you can tap and hold on the screen to pan your camera around the battlefield. Releasing will cause the camera to snap back to your character’s location. This action can be performed on PC by clicking and holding the scroll wheel of your mouse.
- You can ping anywhere on the battlefield to alert your team using a button next to the minimap.
- The requirement to press a button to revive has been removed.
- While waiting to review, you can now spectate your teammates.
- Voiceover callouts have been added to communicate when teammates die, objectives are captured, and other useful information.

> **Developer’s Note:** *We’re excited to see how these updates provide players with more battlefield awareness and aid performance. Going forward, we will continue to look for ways to improve Battleground Convoy, so please provide us with any feedback you may have.*

### Vanguard

We’re refreshing Vanguard mode to kick off a new cycle, beginning with a reimagined Lidwinter Woods. This update removes some frustrating mechanics that previously slowed progression and introduces new tiers of difficulty to better challenge and reward you. We’re also expanding the presence of Spectral Allies in the early difficulty tiers, providing you with more options and an engaging way to shape your playstyle from the start.

> **Developer’s Note:** *These changes mark the beginning of a broader effort to revitalize Vanguard mode and ensure each rotation feels fresh, rewarding, and worth returning to. Moving forward, we continue making changes to this mode that aligns with these goals.*

### Raid the Vault

Entering the Vault will no longer grant Activity Points towards your Deeds of Valor badge, and instead, you will earn 15 Activity Points when you open a chest inside the Vault. Fully clearing all four floors of the Vault and opening all chests grants 150 Activity Points, helping you to complete the weekly Activity requirements in as little as four full clears.

### Build Recommendations

Over the coming months, we’re updating the Build Recommendation page to better highlight the creativity and expertise of our community. The first update is a new tab that showcases the top PvP and PvE players from our leaderboards. You can now view, explore, and even save these builds directly into your Armory.

> **Developer’s Note:** *As we continue to evolve this feature, we plan to introduce more ways for players to share, discover, and celebrate builds created by the community.*

### Item Power UI

It currently takes a lot of taps/clicks to see all the equipped Set and Legendary powers that apply to your character, so we've added a Set and Legendary powers overview button to both the Inventory and Armory menus. This new page explains all applied powers and should be helpful for those looking to fine tune or share their builds with others.

### Hero’s Journey

Hero’s Journey will reset on June 19. The first four chapters of the next Hero’s Journey will be available for you to complete, with the Quill and Parchment Frame as your reward.

### Challenge Rift

Challenge Rift Season 13 runs from June 19–August 25, increasing the maximum floor level to 550 while also lowering the requirements for floors 481–500.

### Adventurer’s Chronicle

We've added 10 new tales for the Adventurer's Chronicle scattered throughout Sanctuary. Most can be added to your chronicle simply by locating them in the wilderness, but one will only be found through unconventional means.

### Cycle of Strife Burning Cloaks

Two new Burning Cloaks are available for worthy Immortals to snatch up!

Burning Cloak of the Boundless: Earned by spending 63 weeks as an Immortal with at least 8 Blessed Reigns.

Burning Cloak of the Immortal: Earned by spending 98 weeks as an Immortal with at least 11 Blessed Reigns.
