## New Treasure Goblins Scuttle Abound

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/GZQISDMYGXQN1760127532426.jpg)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/GZQISDMYGXQN1760127532426.jpg)

The underbrush rustles, shaken by creatures of the greediest inclinations. We’re updating the current goblin system with new goblin types, event mechanics, and optimizations to Avarice, the Gold Cursed.

#### New Goblin Types

- Mythstealer: Drops Set items
- Malevolent Tormentor: Drops Legendary Equipment
- Gem Hoarder: Drops Gems
- Odious Collector: Drops Flowing Shard, and other salvage
- Draff Gatherer: Drops Rhodolite and crafting materials

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/OV8JY2VLVK7V1760127531909.jpg)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/OV8JY2VLVK7V1760127531909.jpg)

#### Keep a Keen Eye for Increased Treasure Goblins

While Goblins randomly spawn in overworld zones and dungeons, now there’s the chance to activate a special Goblin event during any dungeon run.

If luck should fortune you, a Portal will appear at the entrance of your dungeon, transporting you to the final arena where resides Avarice the Gold-Cursed. Defeating him grants awards equivalent to if you had defeated the final boss in the dungeon.

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/GNBTGFTH05RD1760127529959.jpg)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/GNBTGFTH05RD1760127529959.jpg)

Avarice himself has been gilded with several improvements. His iconic portal dash has been restored, alongside several other minor optimizations and adjustments to his other skills. This should make him a more formidable and engaging fight to conquer.

## The Winds of Fortune Regales

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/3W1S6YOR7VGS1757014396810.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/3W1S6YOR7VGS1757014396810.png)

Horadric scholars are deep in study, unraveling the mysteries behind a sudden surge in materialized wealth. Meanwhile, Sanctuary’s merchants waste no time, capitalizing on the phenomenon to expand their fortunes.

From **October 15, 3:00 a.m.—October 22, 3:00 a.m. local server time**, you will be able to activate a special boost and earn increased rewards!

While Winds of Fortune is live, activate your 24-hour buff to receive this boost. Once activated, the buff duration is in real time and will end 24 hours after it’s been initiated. If you have not activated the buff within 24 hours of the event ending, it will be enabled automatically. Any rewards not claimed before the end of the event will be auto-claimed on the next log-in.

During Winds of Fortune, the 4-player party Normal Gems bonus drop will be doubled and unaffected by the daily cap, and only the first twelve Common Gems of the day will be tradable.

Any of these items will drop in duplicate quantities:

- Gold
- Experience
- Battle Pass Points
- Normal Gems
- Legendary Items
- Items

Items from these activities will also drop in duplicate quantities:

- Horadric Bestiary
- Challenge Rifts
- Bounties
- Fishing
- Dungeons
- Purge the Depths
- Accursed Towers
- Hidden Lairs
- Farming in the wilderness
- Codex Activities

Note that during this limited-time event, Battle Pass rewards will not be doubled, and the weekly limit on Battle Pass Points and Normal Gems will not change. Any bonus Experience gained will be affected by your current modifier.

Additionally, there are limits to each item that can be earned in duplicate quantities. You’ll earn double the amount until you reach the limit. Check out the event in-game for more details.

## Feature Updates

#### Blessed Hunter Event

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/OLT0FRZRBF2K1760127532598.jpg)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/OLT0FRZRBF2K1760127532598.jpg)

The New Blessed Hunter event will function very similar to the old Set Hunter event. From **October 15, 3:00 a.m.—November 3, 3:00 a.m. local server time**, when Set items drop within dungeons, they now have a chance to gain blessing when they're picked up.

Each item can be blessed up to 4 times by:

- increasing the items quality (by adding more attributes or magic properties)
- Increasing an attribute’s value
- Converting the Set item into another Set of your choice

When a set item drops, it’ll automatically roll four times, displaying the fully identified item within your bag. The diamonds underneath the blessing notification will help to show you the number of blessings you’ve received as well as the heart indicating your item was changed into one of your preselected Sets. You can also check “My Log” in your chat channels to see a breakdown of what blessings your item received.

Additionally, you can now also select individual Set items per item slot allowing you to tailor your own custom wish list of Set items instead of being forced to select the same Set for every slot. You still make these item selections at any Dungeon entrance or in the Random Dungeon UI.

#### Familiar Conversion Changes

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/SMEUOCTMHYI61760127531829.jpg)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/SMEUOCTMHYI61760127531829.jpg)

Based off community feedback, we are making the section that shows Conversion Stones in the UI permanently appear when converting Familiars. This stays visible regardless of whether you toggle the Conversion preview panel.

We’ve also increased the background of the screen to make it more prominent.

#### New Legendary Essences

New Legendary Essences will be released on **October 29**. The Loyalty points for the Loyalty Bonus system will be converted into Legacy Loyalty Points.

#### Region-Specific Update

**Item Drop Blessing**

We're working on a new daily bonus event based around item drops, which will come in the form of a daily 1-hour blessing. The blessing enhances the quality of item drops, the chance to obtain legendary items, and an option to earn items for another class. This feature will be tested in China for the 4.1.1 content update, and we’re targeting a release in all other regions in the next content update.
