# Diablo Immortal Bug Fixes and Patch Notes for All Platforms

- URL: https://news.blizzard.com/en-gb/article/24166781/diablo-immortal-bug-fixes-and-patch-notes-for-all-platforms
![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/vk/VK6TL5H3CQ131734397130066.jpg)

> We will continually update this blog with all bug fixes and patches the team implements for Diablo Immortal on all platforms.

---

The Diablo Immortal team has been diligently monitoring your feedback and working to introduce bug fixes and patches to further improve your playing experience. As we implement these types of changes for the iOS, Android, and PC versions of Immortal, we will update this list.

Bug fixes are typically applied at a higher frequency, addressing smaller, related issues. They don’t require a large download to be applied to the game. Patches are larger client-side updates that require a download for players to obtain. New patches will require players to update before they can continue playing.

***Please check out [here](https://news.blizzard.com/article/23826549/) for the previous patch notes.***
