## Server Merge

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/IWUNH5C8U5JN1667513797470.gif)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/IWUNH5C8U5JN1667513797470.gif)

Following Diablo Immortal’s scheduled maintenance on **November 9 at 3:00 a.m. server time**, we will be merging the populations of select servers. This change is being made so players will have an easier time finding other players to party up with regardless of the server they play on. We will continue to monitor player populations on all servers and merge additional servers as needed. At this time, we cannot say when additional server merges will occur, but we will keep players updated. Here’s a complete list of all servers that will merge in this wave:

This is a big change for everyone, and we understand that you may have questions regarding what you must do if you’re playing on a merging server or how your gameplay experience might change. Here’s how Server Merge will specifically work for players—**please note the below only applies to players on a merging server**:

- Once the Server Merge is complete, you will continue to log into your same server, but will notice you now share a population with the other servers in your grouping. No characters have been deleted.
- If your merged server contains duplicate character names, Warband names, or Clan names, a number will be automatically added to the end of each identical name and a free name change will be provided.
- Battle Pass progress will be shared across the merged servers.
- Your character will retain all of their items, such as equipment, resources, gems, etc.
- Your character will retain all players on their Friends List.
- Your character will remain in the same Clan.
- Your character will remain in the same Warband.
- Your Battlegrounds score and rank will be retained.
- All Deeds of Valor badges will be retained.
- All server first achievements for Challenge Rift will be retained.
- All leaderboards will be re-ranked following Server Merge.
- All Wynton’s Grand Market transactions will be retained on the merged server. The recommended price of all items in the market will become the average of the merged servers.
- The Immortal with the highest Dominance among the merged servers will become the new Immortal. All other Immortals will be reverted to Adventurers and receive compensation befitting their previous station.
- Previous Immortal Reigns will be retained on the Wall of Honor.
- Shadows and Adventurers will retain their progress on the merged server.
- Shadow War and Rite of Exile will be paused to give players time to adjust.
- The Server Paragon Level of the merged servers will be equal to the highest Server Paragon Level among the servers in your grouping.

## Feature Updates

#### Daily Activities Time Change

- The Shadows’ Assembly will now take place from **7:00-9:00 p.m. server time**. Previously, the Assembly ran from 6:00-8:00 p.m. server time.
- Wrathborne Invasion will now occur twice a day, first at 12:30 p.m. server time, and again at 9:00 p.m. server time.

> **Developer’s Note:** *Because of player feedback and data collected on play patterns, we decided to change the times that the Shadow Assembly and Wrathborne Invasion take place—this is to give a larger population of players an opportunity to play these activities at a more convenient time. It is our hope that even more players can now enjoy these activities on a regular basis.*

#### Set Items

- You can now craft specific Set items at Charsi’s forge in Westmarch using a new resource, Fabled Wisp—these can be acquired by salvaging Set items.
- Only Set items acquired from dungeon bosses and Heavy Austere Chests after the maintenance update that begins on November 8 can be salvaged into a Fabled Wisp.

> **Developer’s Note:** *We have been listening to a deluge of feedback regarding players having a hard time finding specific Set items. It is our belief that adding the ability to craft specific Set items will provide a path for players to reliably complete their sets.*

#### Legendary Gems

- There are now two types of Gem Fragments: Bound and Unbound. Neither of these Gem Fragments can be sold in Wynton’s Grand Market, but Unbound Gem Fragments allow a Legendary Gem to be upgraded without making it bound.
- Upgraded Legendary Gems can now be sold in Wynton’s Grand Market. To be eligible, the Legendary Gem must have never been equipped to your character, must have been previously upgraded using Unbound Gems or Unbound Gem Fragments only and must be rank five or lower.

#### Castle Cyrangar

- A Blacksmith and Identifier have been added to Castle Cyrangar’s courtyard to provide additional player ease.

#### Kion’s Ordeal

We have received feedback that managing other players in Kion’s Ordeal is quite a challenge. To alleviate organizational duties for the Immortal leader, we have added a few quality-of-life updates to this feature:

- A new management UI that displays the Paragon Level, Combat Rating, and Contribution Score of each of your players with the ability to sort each listing.
- The ability to set Combat Rating and Contribution Score requirements for raid entry.
- A Waitlist feature to help manage incoming applicants.
- An improved difficulty selection UI with clear descriptions for each tier.

Thank you for keeping Sanctuary safe!\
