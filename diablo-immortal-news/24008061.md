## Tristram Cathedral Dungeon and Main Quest

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/SQIQWWX92SGZ1695085752748.jpg)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/SQIQWWX92SGZ1695085752748.jpg)

Reports from Wortham suggest the townsfolk are having peculiar dreams. . .some have even disappeared during the night. Returning to Tristram Cathedral to investigate these disturbances will have you crossing cleavers with an old nemesis, the Butcher, along with confronting a malicious enemy imbued with the power of Diablo himself.

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/TANKXDVX7X471695085754133.jpg)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/TANKXDVX7X471695085754133.jpg)

Your descent will be hindered by new, unsightly abominations: the Shardborne. These grotesque beings are the unfortunate result of the corrupted Worldstone; shards penetrate the flesh of humans, reanimating the dead into lethal attackers, and slowly decay the living until they’re a twisted amalgamation of flesh. Tread carefully when in their presence—the slightest exposure to Worldstone shards will send them into a rabid craze.

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/0CQEIYBPYOY21695085750316.jpg)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/0CQEIYBPYOY21695085750316.jpg)

The Cult of Terror looks to unleash these nightmares upon Sanctuary. Navigating treacherous locations, such as the manor where the Mad King lost himself and the cathedral where Diablo was once confined, will be paramount in keeping the Burning Hells’ influence at bay. Sanctuary needs you, adventurer.

To embark upon this journey into the bowels of Tristram Cathedral you must have completed the Ancients’ Cradle main questline—beginning with *Fare You Well* and ending with *Creation’s Husk*—and reached Hell Difficulty III.

## Alley of Blood

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/KRO8THVIV4EM1695085754099.jpg)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/KRO8THVIV4EM1695085754099.jpg)

Alley of Blood is a new team-based, single-lane PvP activity changing how Shadow Clans prove themselves in the Shadow War, and how they clash against Immortals for bonuses in the new Vigil of Blades component of Cycle of Strife. A variant of Alley of Blood is also available to play during a new limited-time event.

In Alley of Blood, teams will battle fiercely over hallowed ground, aiming to overpower the opposing team by demolishing their Sentry Towers and ultimately destroying the base of their operations, their Crystal Heart. In cases where both teams are evenly matched and neither Crystal Heart is destroyed, the winner is decided by the team with more remaining structures and overall team performance. Your team won’t be in this fight alone—massive Sacred Guardians seek to triumph over their adversary as well and will spawn to attack the enemy’s Sacred Guardians, Sentry Tower, and Crystal Heart. These stone behemoths deal massive damage, so you’ll want to clear a path for them.

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/VEZRZT8N39UF1695085751462.jpg)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/VEZRZT8N39UF1695085751462.jpg)

#### Deciding Your Role: Specialist Skills

Because there is only one lane to victory, teams will need to coordinate their efforts to outsmart their foes and specialize their fighting style. Before a match begins, players will pick one of the below Specialist skills to add to an additional Skill slot. Teams can’t have more than two players per Skills category, but those players can select the same sub-Skill. These Skills are unique to Alley of Blood and can only be used in this activity.

**Focus**

- Anguish Brand: Mark one enemy player for 6 seconds. The marked enemy will drop one of their Champion's Marks for every 5% Life they lose. When the mark expires, that enemy takes damage equal to 30% of the Life lost while marked.
- Stone Hex: Bestows a curse upon a target enemy player, turning them to stone and making them vulnerable to attacks from friends or foes. The curse lifts after 6 seconds or if the target dies.

**Siege**

- Guardian's Call: Summon a single guardian that will move forward, attacking enemy Sacred Guardians and structures. The player must stay within proximity of the guardian, or it will stand still.
- Siege Weapon: Summons a turret at the player’s location that prioritizes targeting enemy structures, dealing area of effect damage where it strikes. The turret self-destructs after 8 seconds.

**Skirmish**

- Dwindlemurk: Releases a fan-shaped blast of fog. Enemy players affected by the fog have their Max Life decreased by 20%, and their size and damage are decreased by 30%. This effect lasts for 6 seconds.
- Executioner's Glare: Fires a torrent of energy in a line that pierce across the map. Deals damage to all enemies hit, and players with less than 10% Life are killed instantly.

**Support**

- Blessing of Momentum: Grant teammates immunity to loss of control and increase their Attack Speed for 4 seconds.
- Justice Shield: Generate a wall at the target location that absorbs projectiles that hit its front. Lasts for 6 seconds.

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/LMMJ73CRYFR21695085753858.jpg)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/LMMJ73CRYFR21695085753858.jpg)

#### The Key to Greatness: Champion’s Marks

Cutting down the enemy team’s forces doesn’t just bring you satisfaction in Alley of Blood; it can also bring power to your character in the form of Champion’s Marks. These shiny symbols of strength can be picked up after they drop from destroyed Sacred Guardians and felled players. You can have a maximum of 99 Champion’s Marks, with each one increasing your power and character’s size. But beware--upon your own death, you’ll drop a portion of your Marks for the enemy team to scoop up.

#### Alley of Blood Tips

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/T8XQ82CJ6TUA1695085751674.jpg)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/T8XQ82CJ6TUA1695085751674.jpg)

- Sentry Towers defend themselves and deal damage to Guardians in their proximity first, then the player. Towers prioritize dealing damage to players who attack a player allied to the tower while within the tower’s proximity.
- Should you die in Alley of Blood, you’ll be automatically resurrected after a period. Resurrection time increases as the match progresses.
- Health Potions are replaced with a bandage to self-heal. The bandage has a 60-second cooldown.
- Everyone starts the match with a 5-second boost to Movement Speed.

As we mentioned previously, Alley of Blood can be played in two styles: an 8v8 fashion in Shadow War, and a 4v4 fashion in the Alley of Blood limited-time event.

#### Come Out on Top in 8v8 Shadow War

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/0RZDPV0P614H1695085753468.jpg)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/0RZDPV0P614H1695085753468.jpg)

Alley of Blood will become the new way in which Shadow War is waged—the same goes for the new Vigil of Blades component of Cycle of Strife. The schedule for Shadow War is changing as well. Here’s the new weekly schedule:

- Tuesday, 9 p.m. server time: A list of the week’s matches is announced.
- Thursday, 7:30 p.m. server time: Preliminary round matches commence, and winners and losers are determined.
- Saturday, 7:30 p.m. server time: Final round matches commence.

Additionally, the structure of Shadow War is also changing. Clans are now divided across multiple groupings based upon their Clan’s classification and the matches themselves are broken into 4 categories: Eminent, Exalted, Famed, and Proud. Clans can battle in anywhere between 3–12 matches and receive points for their win based on the category of the match. In the event nobody from the opposing team shows up by the start of the match, you’ll win by default.

#### Vigil of Blades

Cast your blade into the fray to bring glory to your faction! After Shadow War concludes, and before Rite of Exile, the most prominent Shadow Clans will face off in an 8v8 battle against the Immortals in the Alley of Blood. Bring everything you’ve got and do not relent; the faction which wins 2 out of 3 matches will walk away with a bonus to their damage and health for fighting in the Rite of Exile. Vigil of Blades will only occur during the first three weeks of a new Immortal faction reign.

We’re also introducing two ways that anyone can be involved in Vigil of Blades and Rite of Exile—the Prediction system and Spectator mode.

In a new Cycle of Strife tab within the Faction menu, players can see the status of the conflict between Shadows and Immortals and cast their prediction for who will come out on top. Any player who casts a prediction will receive rewards, and those who predict correctly will get a sweet bonus reward.

#### Everyone Can Showcase their Talents in the Alley of Blood Event

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/3PKARG91006T1695085752030.jpg)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/3PKARG91006T1695085752030.jpg)

From **September 21, 3 a.m.–October 5, 3 a.m. server time** a 4v4 version of Alley of Blood will be available to players. This event is especially good for those new to Diablo Immortal because of the rules of engagement listed below, but it’s also great for players who don’t participate in Cycle of Strife or want some extra experience playing Alley of Blood.

Here are the rules of engagement:

- All characters will be set to Level 60.
- The Rank of all Legendary Gear and Set Gear will be set to 1.
- The Rank for all Legendary Gems on your character will be set to 10.

Bonuses from the following are disabled as well:

- Resonance
- Clans and Warbands
- Normal Gems and Charms
- Helliquary bonuses
- Stances
- Paragon Specialization

Playing in the Alley of Blood event can net you some helpful rewards such as: crafting materials, Battle Points, Legendary items, and Normal Gems. You must be Level 30 or higher to play the Alley of Blood event.

## 3 New Legendary Gems

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/SWF81X7E8KUW1695085750735.jpg)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/SWF81X7E8KUW1695085750735.jpg)

Three new Legendary Gems have joined the line up. Experiment with these gems to discover new synergies and make your character’s build blaze like the flames of the Burning Hells. The below values for each gem reflect their power at Rank-10:

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/T35ISMU292LN1695085750618.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/T35ISMU292LN1695085750618.png)

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/U0BIXNCR27WV1695085753375.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/U0BIXNCR27WV1695085753375.png)

Viper’s Bite (Two-Star Gem): Damaging enemies poisons them with Viper's Venom, causing them to take 45% base damage + 120 damage every second for 6 seconds. Enemies inflicted with Viper's Venom also have their Attack Speed reduced by 6%. Cannot occur on the same target more often than once every 20 seconds.

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/RO88T82T6YRI1695085753445.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/RO88T82T6YRI1695085753445.png)

Void Spark (Five-Star Gem): Dealing damage conjures a Void Spark for 6 seconds. While the Void Spark is active, dealing damage has a 30% chance of unleashing lightning that chains to a maximum of 8 nearby enemies, dealing 110% base damage + 446 to each enemy and increasing your Attack Speed by 16%. Lightning strikes can only trigger once every 1 second. Conjuring a Void Spark can only occur once every 20 seconds.

## Feature Updates

#### Legendary Gems

Legendary Gems can now be upgraded in smaller increments. Starting at Rank 5, Legendary Gems receive smaller incremental upgrades when combining each duplicate copy of the Gem, instead of requiring the full set to upgrade it to the next Rank. This change does not lower the overall number of Gems required to upgrade.

#### Skill Interruption

Abilities with short cast times—like the Crusader’s Holy Banner and Fist of the Heavens—will no longer be interruptible.

#### Random Dungeon Finder

Players now have a one button click way to jump into a random Dungeon’s depths.

While searching for a random Dungeon, players may be grouped with another team at any time, which reduces the overall time spent matchmaking. Players who enter a Dungeon using this feature will have an increased chance to receive a Set item as a reward.

#### First Kill of the Day Rewards

The rewards received for getting your first kill of the day are changing. This only applies to players who have achieved 7 or more first kills of a day ever on their respective character. Starting on your eighth kill of the day, you will receive the following rewards—this set of rewards will continue to recycle after completion.

- Day 1: 1 Rare Crest
- Day 2: 1 Charm
- Day 3: 1 random Legendary item
- Day 4: 200 Hilts
- Day 5: 5 Aspirant's Keys
- Day 6: 1 random Normal Gem
- Day 7: 1 random One-Star Legendary Gem

#### Rite of Exile

Immortal Clans can now strive to earn a Blessed Reign by successfully defending the crown for 7 weeks. During the first three weeks of a reign the top 3 Shadow Clans and the Immortal Clans will battle each other in the new Vigil of Blades game mode to earn a bonus to damage and Life during the Rite of Exile in the remaining weeks of the reign. The faction that wins 2 of the 3 battles will gain +4% Life and damage, which stacks up to a 12% bonus if they win all three weeks.

#### Battleground Idol Changes

We've increased the speed for the Idol during phase 2 of Battleground and Rite of Exile. The more players on the Idol—up to 4—will increase the movement speed by up to 30%. Additionally, we reduced the time required to activate the idols at the beginning of phase 2.

> **Developer’s Note:** *Overall, the goal is to help increase the rate of wins for attackers as they have fallen behind defenders again for total victories. The main area of improvement is centered around phase 2 of Battleground as it’s the most contested phase in the match.*

Skills that provide the player with untargetable status will also no longer be able to push the Idol. We will be working through Legendary Essences in the future to ensure consistency, but right now, the main offenders to fix are Sanguinate, Solemn Snare, Swarm of Bats, and Wraith Form.

When Attackers successfully push the Idol to the end during phase 2, there was a short delay between that and successfully completing the phase. These two events now occur at the same time. This prevents situations where Attackers could push the Idol to the end, but would still lose the match because the doors hadn't opened yet, and more time hadn’t been added to the clock.

#### Immortal Vault Defense Rewards

Immortal Vault defense rewards have been increased. You can now earn up to 200 Hilts for successfully defending the Vault, the weekly cap has been increased to 1400 Hilts.

#### Immortal Daily Quests

Daily Quests for Immortals have been updated and revamped to improve the overall player experience.

#### Immortal Clan Changes

Essentia is being removed from the game and will no longer be required as a resource for opening Kion's Vault. As a result, Shadows and Immortals will now fight over Vault Supplies in the Raid and Defend the Vault activities.

Additionally, adjustments have been made to how Kion's Ordeal interacts with Daedessa's Blessing so there will still be some initial conflict between the Shadows and the Immortals for this bonus.

Lastly, the damage bonuses received by Shadows when they increase their individual rank, and for Immortals when they increase their reign have been changed to progress at more even rates between the factions. The damage bonuses have also been lowered, with a new maximum benefit of 16%, previously 32%.

> **Developer’s Note:** *In our efforts to continue to make both sides of the Cycle of Strife appealing to all players, we evaluated several systems that interplay between the factions. We put a particular focus on improving the benefits of being an Immortal and making the progression of the Immortal and Shadows factions more evenly suited.*

#### Immortal Cloak Upgrade

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/R8GYL1RFVTHY1695085751231.jpg)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/R8GYL1RFVTHY1695085751231.jpg)

Players can now unlock 2 new cosmetics and several new Cloak options as part of the long-term progression as an Immortal.

Once a player has reached certain cumulative milestone weeks as an Immortal, they will unlock special rewards when their current reign ends. These cosmetics can be used by any class, regardless of current Immortal status. The most coveted cosmetic rewards will also require players to achieve the new Blessed Reign, by defending their Immortal crown for all 7 weeks.

- 4 Weeks: Burning Cloak of the Resilient
- 7 Weeks: Immortal Veteran Cosmetic
- 10 Weeks: Burning Cloak of the Enduring
- 14 Weeks: Burning Cloak of the Defiant
- 21 Weeks: Immortal Legacy Cosmetic
- 28 Weeks with 4 Blessed Reigns: Burning Cloak of the Unending
- 42 Weeks with 6 Blessed Reigns: Burning Cloak of the Eternal

> **Developer’s Note:** *We want players to strive to be Immortals; one of the unique aspects of being an Immortal is the faction-specific looks they can wear. While we want to retain the identity of being an Immortal and keep those cosmetics bound to them, it felt appropriate to give players permanent cosmetics to strive for.*

#### Shadow Assembly

We've received lots of feedback from players unable to participate during Shadow Assembly for a multitude of reasons. To allow more players to participate and earn the daily rewards from Shadow Assembly, it will be available from 12 p.m.–2 p.m. in addition to the original 7 p.m.–9 p.m. server time slot.

#### Shadow War

Shadow War start times are being pushed back to 7:30 p.m. server time, and the preparation phase is being reduced to 5 minutes.

> **Developer’s Note:** *We want to allow more time for players to participate in Shadow War and believe pushing the start time back should allow for increased participation.*

#### Shadows Daily Quests

Shadows will now have 21 tasks to complete each week instead of 3 tasks each day.

#### Elder Rifts

We are adding 8 all-new Elder Rift modifiers.

- Chain Reaction: Some enemies are unstable and explode upon taking damage.
- Combo: Activate the empowered skill to refresh the cooldown.
- Crusher: Molten Boulders crush anything in their path.
- Instability: Unstable Portals appear throughout the Rift. Enter at your own risk.
- Flurry: When damaging enemies, gain stacks of Flurry which increase your Attack Speed.
- Rampage: Your ultimate ability is replaced with Rampage, causing you to rush forward damaging enemies until you collide with a surface.
- Minefield: Damaging mines are armed throughout the Rift. Stepping on them causes them to explode after a brief delay, dealing damage to anything inside the area.
- Endless Treasure: A special Treasure Goblin appears in the Rift that drops rewards each time it takes damage.

#### Unlocking New Zones

The difficulty required to unlock certain Zones and Dungeons has been changed:

- Stormpoint requires Hell Difficulty I.
- Dread Reaver Dungeon requires Hell Difficulty II.
- Ancients’ Cradle requires Hell Difficulty III.

#### Hero’s Journey

Players will be able to unlock Chapters V and VI of the current Hero’s Journey. You cannot make progress towards the activities in these chapters without completing the previous chapter.

Chapter V Rewards:

- 30 Aspirant’s Keys
- 900 Hilts
- 1 Legendary Crest
- 50,000 Gold

Chapter VI Rewards:

- 36 Aspirant’s Keys
- 900 Hilts
- 1 Legendary Crest
- 1 Resplendent Journey Portal

#### Warband

Players that are behind on the Warband's Ancestral Tableau will now receive bonus points to help them catch up to other players. Players that are just starting will receive up to a 5x bonus that will decrease as they get closer to the limit.

Additionally, players no longer need to be in a Warband to participate in Defense of Cyrangar and Purge the Depths. Both activities can now be accessed in Westmarch through Valstus, the Warband Steward.

#### Defense of Cyrangar

The time it takes to complete rounds in the Defense of Cyrangar Endless Mode has been decreased.

#### Horadric Bestiary

Unveil 12 new pages for your Horadric Bestiary, covering the demons of the Hearthrot Helliquary and new threads from Ancients’ Cradle.

#### UI Changes to Immortal Treasury Rewards

We are making a series of updates to the Immortal Vault experience, including additional UI support. Vault items will now be available for selection during Sunday and delivered on Monday during the reset at 3 a.m. server time. Contribution for the week will end Sunday at 3 a.m. local server time, allowing players to know their exact ranking on Sunday when they select their desired items. Players with the highest contribution will be placed at the top of the list, making it easier to see who is poised to win. Items that were stolen from the Vault will no longer be displayed.

#### Prodigy’s Path

This Major update introduces a Seasonal Prodigy’s Path. The Seasonal Prodigy's Path refreshes every season. This Seasonal Prodigy's Path will be available on October 6, 3 a.m. server time— January 6, 2024, 3 a.m. server time.

The Seasonal Prodigy's Path unlocks when players reach Paragon Level 140. Players can win rewards by increasing 5 Levels every time until all have been claimed. Unclaimed free rewards will be sent via in-game mail 14 days after season end. Once the Seasonal Prodigy's Path has been purchased, all unclaimed—including locked—rewards will be sent via in-game mail 14 days after the season ends.

#### Miscellaneous

- The loadout slots in the Armory have increased from 5 to 10.
- The capacity of Gems in your inventory has increased from 40 to 80 rows.
- Players will now have a 30% Damage Reduction bonus in all Accursed Tower matches.
- Rewards chests in Dungeon runs with 4 players will now automatically open.
- New servers will provide players with an experience bonus until they reach max level. This bonus begins two weeks after the server goes live.
