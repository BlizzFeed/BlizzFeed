## The Warping of the Wilds Limited-Time Event

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/GJPN1BUQRP111757014397130.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/GJPN1BUQRP111757014397130.png)

Rejoice, child, for our time is near. The Shardborne Prophet, our Prince of Freedom, is coming to save you from your suffering. He will strengthen us against disease, famine, and the ills of the flesh.

Prepare yourself for the next chapter in Diablo Immortal’s story by participating in the Warping of the Wilds event from **September 18, 3 a.m.–September 28, 3 a.m. server time**. Log in on the second, fourth, and eighth days of the event to earn rewards, such as a Telluric Pearl, Legendary Crest, and Rare Crests. You’ll also receive a 100% bonus to experience gained from Battle Pass levels during the event’s run.

You must be at least Level 30 or above to experience the Warping of the Wilds.

## The Winds of Fortune Pick Up Once Again

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/3W1S6YOR7VGS1757014396810.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/3W1S6YOR7VGS1757014396810.png)

Horadric scholars are deep in study, unraveling the mysteries behind a sudden surge in materialized wealth. Meanwhile, Sanctuary’s merchants waste no time, capitalizing on the phenomenon to expand their fortunes.

From **September 18, 3:00 a.m.—September 25, 3:00 a.m. local server time**, you will be able to activate a special boost and earn increased rewards!

While Winds of Fortune is live, activate your 24-hour buff to receive this boost. Once activated, the buff’s duration is in real time and will end 24 hours after it’s been initiated. If you have not activated the buff within 24 hours of the event ending, it will be enabled automatically. Any rewards not claimed before the end of the event will be auto-claimed on the next log-in.

During Winds of Fortune, the 4-player party Normal Gems bonus drop will be doubled and unaffected by the daily cap, and only the first twelve Common Gems of the day will be tradable.

Any of these items will drop in duplicate quantities:

- Gold
- Experience
- Battle Pass Points
- Normal Gems
- Legendary Items
- Items

Items from these activities will also drop in duplicate quantities:

- Horadric Bestiary
- Challenge Rifts
- Bounties
- Fishing
- Dungeons
- Purge the Depths
- Accursed Towers
- Hidden Lairs
- Farming in the wilderness
- Codex Activities

Note that during this limited-time event, Battle Pass rewards will not be doubled, and the weekly limit on Battle Pass Points and Normal Gems will not change. Any bonus Experience gained will be affected by your current modifier.

Additionally, there are limits to each item that can be earned in duplicate quantities. You’ll earn double the amount until you reach the limit. Please see the event in-game for more details.

## Feature Updates

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/2m/2ML3MCOU13B61757024568312.jpg)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/2m/2ML3MCOU13B61757024568312.jpg)

#### Market Update: 5-Star Gems Cross Server Sales

As a first foray into a cross-server marketplace, 5-Star Gems will now be purchased and sold via the marketplace across all servers and regions around the world (excluding China).

We will be keeping a close eye on any issues or abuse that may arise with this new update to the market and work to resolve them as quickly as possible. If this does go well, we will explore expanding this to more items in the marketplace in the future.

#### PvP Optimizations – Loss of Control and Diminishing Returns

We’ve added a new Unstoppable Bar to PvP that sits just below your Life bar. It fills up as you get hit with knockbacks or total loss of control effects. If you’re hit with one of those effects, any additional ones within the next 6 seconds will be 30% less effective. If you receive a cumulative 3 seconds of knockback or hard crowd control within 8 seconds, you will gain a crowd control protection buff for 2.5 seconds (this buff's duration will not be extended), granting temporary immunity to knockbacks or crowd control effects. This will be active in all PvP modes.

We will be closely monitoring player feedback around this new system and may adjust as needed, potentially even disabling the feature if we are unsatisfied with how it is performing.

#### Bulk Buy

Bulk Buy is being re-added into the Market, allowing players to set a price range for items and buy larger quantities of items at different price points more quickly.

#### New Account Normal Gem Trade Restrictions

In our continued efforts to detect and prevent fraudulent behavior in-game, we will be making changes to prevent users from selling Normal Gems in the Market before they have been able to complete Inferno Difficulty 1. This will affect both new and existing accounts.

#### Set Item Hunter Event Time Change

We're extending the Set Hunter event through September 25th while we work through a new event that will make discovering set items even more exciting! The Set Item Hunter event allows you to target certain set items, to help you complete your build faster.

Before beginning your next dungeon run, select the set you wish to target on the event’s menu or using the option at the bottom-left corner of the dungeon menu, then start your run as normal. If you did not receive an item from your selected set as a reward, then you’ll have an increased chance to receive it during your next dungeon run.

#### Vanguard Co-Op Optimizations

The Ashen Ravine is receiving some changes with the other improvements for the Vanguard mode.

The Siege Engine is now more resilient, which allows it to benefit from a wider set of defensive and restorative Charm skills. The vehicle should now benefit more from your party’s loadouts and supportive skills. When playing in Normal difficulties, we’ve reduced the numbers of blockades from three to two to make it a bit more efficient.

Elite difficulties remain unchanged in length, as these are meant to be longer tests of endurance. Additionally, in Elite mode we’ve significantly ramped up the difficulty. Elite mode should now feel like a dramatic escalation, reserved for only those who are fully prepared to push their limits.

> **Developer’s Note:** *These changes should make the Ashen Ravine easier to understand if you’re a newer player and should introduce some increased challenge if you’re already enjoying this raid on Elite difficulties.*

#### Ancient Scrap

Ancient Legendary equipment can now be salvaged for Ancient Scrap and Ancient Artifacts can now be converted into Ancient Scrap at the Blacksmith Materials Vendor in Westmarch. Ancient Scrap can be used as a substitute material to Enchant your Ancient Legendary equipment by selecting the toggle next to the Enchant button. Different types of Enchants will cost different amounts of Ancient Scrap.

#### Elder Rift QoL Update

When a Rift Guardian appears in an Elder Rift, a portal leading directly to the Rift Guardian will appear next to players who are far away.

#### Wicked Hunt Event Correction

In our last update blog, we erroneously listed the Alley of Blood event as taking place from Sept 10-17, but these are dates for Fractured Plane. Alley of Blood will take place Sept 12-Sept 15.

Swing your blade, and make your way through the following events on their corrected dates to earn points in the Wicked Hunt event before September 24, 3 a.m. local server time:

**Trial of the Hordes**

September 3—September 10, 3 a.m. local server time

**Fractured Plane**

September 10—September 17, 3 a.m. local server time

**Wild Brawl**

September 17—September 24, 3 a.m. local server time

Burn through these limited-time events to earn valuable rewards from the Wild Hunt event!
