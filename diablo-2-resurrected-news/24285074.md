> Celebrate the victory of 300 stalwart adventurers who stared Death in the face -- and won the Hardcore Level 99 Race of the Demon Sorceress.

---

Heroes of Sanctuary,

From the depths of darkness, those who have endured relentless trials now stand at a decisive moment—**the time has come to carve their names into history**.

On February 21, 2026, at 10:00 AM, with the dawn of Diablo II: Resurrected Ladder Season 13, the **Hardcore Level 99 Race of the Demon Sorceress** began its relentless march. Now this grand saga has reached its final chapter.

Under the merciless laws of Hardcore, where a single misstep means oblivion, these heroes faced every terror Sanctuary could muster. At last, 300 among them have risen, having achieved the fabled Level 99.

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/ue/UE49KPMIU4J81781222472597.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/ue/UE49KPMIU4J81781222472597.png)

#### Selection Criteria

With honor comes scrutiny. Every record from this race has been rigorously examined by Blizzard.

To uphold the sanctity of fair competition, **any participant found to have reached their level through the use of unauthorized programs, including multi-loaders or other illicit means, has been excluded from this list**.

Only those who conquered Sanctuary through rightful means, only the true champions, are worthy of having their names inscribed within this Grimoire.
