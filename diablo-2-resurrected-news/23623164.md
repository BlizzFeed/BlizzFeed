Prepare to relive the epic adventure that defined the action-RPG genre for decades. Today at BlizzConline™, Blizzard Entertainment announced **Diablo II: Resurrected**, the definitive remastering of the classic *Diablo II* and its expansion *Diablo II: Lord of Destruction*.

[![Video](https://markdown-videos-api.jorgenkh.no/youtube/3nyey-q-bJM?width=1280&height=720)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3nyey-q-bJM)

Coming later this year to PC, Xbox Series X|S, Xbox One, PlayStation®5, PlayStation®4, and Nintendo Switch™, *Diablo II: Resurrected* brings back this timeless chapter of the Diablo saga for veteran heroes of Sanctuary and a whole new generation, with modern audio and visuals that take advantage of today’s gaming hardware.

To learn more, tune in to the Diablo II: Resurrected Deep Dive panel at BlizzConline, and visit the [Diablo II: Resurrected website](http://diablo2.blizzard.com) below.
