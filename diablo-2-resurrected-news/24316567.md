## Ladder Season 16 Schedule

**Ladder Season 15 Ends:** Monday, November 9, 11 a.m. PST

**Season 16 Patch begins rolling out:** Monday, November 9

**Ladder Season 16 Starts**: Friday, November 13, 5 p.m. PST

Ladder Season 16 will usher in a new opportunity for valiant adventurers to race to Level 99 and amass powerful loot along the way. We cannot wait to see which determined souls carve their name into the Leaderboard this time.

| **Version** | **Description** |
| --- | --- |
| Pre-Expansion Ladder | The standard version of Ladder play that encompasses only the original four acts. |
| Pre-Expansion Hardcore Ladder | The hardcore (only one life) version of Ladder play that encompasses only the original four acts. |
| Ladder | The standard version of Ladder play that encompasses five acts, as it includes the “Lord of Destruction” expansion content. |
| Hardcore Ladder | The hardcore (only one life) version of Ladder play that encompasses five acts, as it includes the “Lord of Destruction” expansion content. |

For those new to Ladder Seasons, you might be wondering what happens to your Shared Stash loot once the current season ends.

![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/C8K0PY1UZFZ41778536358635.png)
![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/X718PFSR0PL51778536358627.png)

Once Ladder Season 15 ends, all Ladder characters will be transferred to their respective non-ladder group. All items in that character group's Shared Stash go into a new Withdraw Only set of Shared Stash tabs, denoted by a check marked Past box. **Any items from Ladder Season 14 stored in the Withdraw Only tabs will be lost at this time.** You will have all of Ladder Season 16 to withdraw any items you would like to keep from Season 15. When Ladder Season 15 ends, this remove only set of Stash Tabs will be overridden with any items in the Ladder Season 15 Shared Stash. Be sure to get any items out before then, or they will be forever lost!

## Patch 3.4 Notes

### Character Conversion

We have made some adjustments to character conversions between partitions moving forward.

Starting November 16, players will no longer be able to convert Expansion (LoD) Online (Ladder and Non-Ladder) characters to Reign of the Warlock. Players will still be able to convert Offline characters and Classic to Expansion (LoD) conversions.

### Bug Fixes

- Fixed an issue where Jewels did not drop if the player killed the last Uber Ancients boss after staying out of the Colossal Summit for extended periods.
- Fixed an issue where health regen/degen effects stalled out by damage events in online games.
- Fixed an issue where a Warlock's bound demon would lose behavior when the Inferno skill was interrupted by the character traveling via a portal.
- Fixed an issue where some VFX decals were failing to render in HD mode.
- Fixed an issue where Terror Zone toggle for online gameplay didn't save the player's previous choice.
- Fixed an issue when interacting with the "Remove Only" checkbox while holding any items related to "Advanced Stash" would not interact with the checkbox.
- Fixed an issue where Searched games on Lobby didn't appear when hidden by the scroll list.
- Fixed issue where various errors preventing connection to the game would give the error text "You have not been online in the last 30 days" instead of intended error text.
- Fixed an issue with Auto-Party Invite not properly having players joining games.
- Fixed various game freezes and crash issues.
