Pause from your tome of demon summoning, Adventurer. These are the Patch notes for the 3.1.2 Client Patch for the Reign of the Warlock.

## Bug Fixes

#### General

- Fixed various crash and disconnect issues.
- Fixed various resolution and UI panel sizing issues.
- Fixed various graphics rendering issues across multiple areas.
- Fixed an issue where your Battle.net tag could be shown in game chat instead of their character name.
- Fixed an issue where players could retain Blood Oath even if they lost all skill points.
- Fixed an issue where Warlock pets could die when using Blood Boil multiple times in a short duration.
- Updated Warlock Death Hex status VFX to have lower volume.
- Updated the number of daggers that can be found on vendors.
