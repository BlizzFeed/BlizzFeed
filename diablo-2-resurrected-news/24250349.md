# 22 Nights of Terror Holiday Event

![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/16/16ZII1GXUDBP1732663857271.png)

> Sleigh your way through Diablo II: Resurrected this holiday season across 22 Nights of Terror, with frightfully festive gameplay modifiers.

---

The air stirs with whimsy, perhaps even magic.

A subtle yet joyous invocation permeates Sanctuary, casting a ray of holiday celebration upon its inhabitants. . . or is it demonic trickery? This magic from beyond is not cause for a ceasefire in the unremitting conflict between demon and mortal, but its nuances can still be felt, as disturbances are lurking about. Seeing this holiday miracle through to the end relies on you—sleigh your way through **22 Nights of Terror** during the Diablo II: Resurrected holiday event.

This year, instead of having buffs that rotate through the event, these will be active at all times during the 22 Nights of Terror! Enhance your holiday slaying with the following splendor:

- A Heart-Warming Experience: Terror zones grant an additional 25% Experience bonus
- Magic time of year: Heroes gain an additional 250 magic find
- Winter Terrorland: 19 Popular Terror Zones all day

From **December 16, 11:00 a.m.—January 7, 11:00 a.m. PST** the fabric of Sanctuary will be altered. The 22 Nights of Terror holiday event can be enjoyed in all online game modes except for Classic Diablo II.

Powerful loot and a dash of terror awaits you in Sanctuary this holiday season. Go forth in glory!

Happy holidays!\
-The Diablo II: Resurrected Team
