> Race to Level 99 on the Hardcore Realm as the Warlock for a chance to be immortalized in The Grimoire of the Unfallen.

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/4w/4WZF8EEQK8111771435175555.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/4w/4WZF8EEQK8111771435175555.png)

Heroes of Sanctuary, a new era has arrived. With Reign of the Warlock, an unwritten chapter of history begins—and it may bear your everlasting mark.

To celebrate, join the challenge worthy of the bravest souls. Carve your legend into history.

Starting on **February 20, 2026 at 5:00 p.m. PT.**, the first 300 verified Warlock players to reach Level 99 on the Hardcore Ladder will have their legacy engraved in a physical monument on Blizzard campus.

Note the start of the competition coincides with the Ladder Season 13 launch. For more details on the Ladder Season 13 reset, read our blog [here](https://news.blizzard.com/article/24246296/).

The Warlock Hardcore 99 ends once the final 300th player completes this pinnacle challenge.

With only 300 spots available, every moment counts. This race is a contest against both danger and time itself.

#### A Chance to be Immortalized in The Grimoire of the Unfallen

Should you prove yourself worth to be one of the first players to complete this ultimate challenge, your Battle.net tag will be engraved into "The Grimoire of the Unfallen," a commemorative record that will be displayed on Blizzard campus in Irvine, California.

It is a distinction that will endure, an honor reserved for the worthy few.

#### Eligibility and Requirements

To be eligible as a winner in this event, you must reach Level 99 with a Hardcore Warlock character in the Season 13 Ladder. There are no server restrictions; players from all regions are eligible to participate.

**Provide your submission using the official form here: [Submission Form](https://forms.gle/1jobVKneJpdSMVrs7)**

Participants **must** submit:

- Your Battle Tag
- A Screenshot proving Level 99 completion

All submissions will undergo review and verification by the Blizzard development team. The first 300 verified heroes will officially be inscribed into The Grimoire of the Unfallen.

Official rules for the Hardcore Warlock 99 Race can be found [here](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/d2r/t/warlock-hardcore-level-99-race-term-conditions/172282).
