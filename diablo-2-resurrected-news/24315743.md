# Celebrate Diablo’s 30th Anniversary with Legacy of Terror

- URL: https://news.blizzard.com/en-gb/article/24315743/celebrate-diablos-30th-anniversary-with-legacy-of-terror
![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/6o/6O2CMHTXLFIR1793910143270.png)

> Celebrate Diablo’s 30th Anniversary in Diablo 2: Resurrected with Legacy of Terror, six days of Diablo Clones, Terrorized zones, buffs, discounts, and, of course, cows.

---

**Event Starts:** November 13, 2026, 5:00 p.m. PST

**Event Ends:** November 19, 2026, 5:00 p.m. PST

Diablo’s 30th Anniversary in Diablo II: Resurrected brings Legacy of Terror, six days of spine-chilling threats and lucrative perks to launch your Season 16 Ladder experience like never before. This event is available for both Ladder and Non-Ladder players.

For six days, Sanctuary serves the following terrors and rewards:

- **Face the Diablo Clone once every six hours (Hell difficulty only).** This works like the current system where Diablo Clone replaces a Super Unique in Hell. During the event, it will be a guaranteed spawn once every six hours.
- **Battle through Terrorized Zones in every zone.** After defeating Baal in Normal, Nightmare, or Hell, that difficulty will feature simultaneous Terrorized Zones in all zones. (Players who do not wish to have all their zones Terrorized can still disable this feature using the Game Creation (gear) menu on the Character Select screen or in the Lobby when creating a game. Doing so will not affect any other features of the Legacy of Terror event).
- **Enter the Cow Portal in the Rogue Encampment at will.** After defeating Baal on a difficulty level, just go directly to Rogue Encampment to enter a special Cow Portal that will remain active at all times during the event. No side trip to Tristram is required!
- **Enjoy 100% Magic Find and a 15% discount on all vendors.** The discount includes Gamblers and works at all difficulty levels.
- **Slay monsters for a rare chance to reanimate them as Hell Bovine Minions.** This is available at all difficulty levels.

Now get out there and slay, slay, slay!
