With this year’s BlizzCon just around the corner, it’s time to stock up on your favorite items from the Blizzard Gear Store—including some brand-new additions!

Those attending the convention in person will also be able to order in advance for pickup on site with Blink shopping.

**Blizzard Gear Store Orders**

Some new BlizzCon items are already available online, and more will be added in the coming days! To place an order and get your loot shipped straight to your home address, head to the [Blizzard Gear Store](https://www.blizzardgearstore.com/stores/blizzard/en), create a Fanatics account, and start browsing. If you’re looking to have your parcel on your doorstep by a certain time, be sure to check out the shipping timeframes for [international orders](https://www.blizzardgearstore.com/stores/blizzard/en/help).

**Blink and On-Site Shopping for BlizzCon Attendees**

For those attending BlizzCon in person, Blink shopping is coming back this year! Starting October 28, head to our Blink store, log in using your Blizzard account, and stock up. Leave some room in your suitcase when you head to Anaheim—you’ll be able to pick up the entirety of your order at the on-site BlizzCon Store, which will be open Thursday, October 31 at its new location in the Anaheim Convention Center North Hall (Level 1).
