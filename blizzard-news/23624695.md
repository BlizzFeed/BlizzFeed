#### MAIN EVENT

Once the Opening Ceremony wraps, we’ll begin broadcasting panels and other BlizzConline events simultaneously across six channels. The Blizzard Channel features a mix of major events and panels from all six channels on both days of the show, and the festivities kick off Friday, February 19 at 23:00 CT with an hour-long opening ceremony that you won’t want to miss. The other five are focused on different games and universes: World of Warcraft, Overwatch, Hearthstone, Diablo, and a Strategy channel covering StarCraft, Warcraft III, and Heroes of the Storm.
