# Esports at BlizzCon: Watch Your Way

- URL: https://news.blizzard.com/en-gb/article/23193645/esports-at-blizzcon-watch-your-way
![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/hl/HLWYHA8IO1S41571855330967.jpg)

> The full list of regional streams for all esports goings-on at BlizzCon!

---

Would you believe it, it’s that time of year again! We’ve got the Hearthstone Global Finals, the StarCraft II World Championship Series Global Finals, the World of Warcraft Arena World Championship, the World of Warcraft Mythic Dungeon International, and the Overwatch World Cup returning to the Anaheim Convention center for 2019!

More details around BlizzCon for each of these programs can be found below:

- [Hearthstone Global Finals](https://playhearthstone.com/en-gb/blog/23187144)
- [Overwatch World Cup](https://www.overwatchleague.com/overwatch-world-cup)
- [StarCraft II World Championship Series Global Finals](https://wcs.starcraft2.com/en-gb/)
- [World of Warcraft Arena World Championship](https://worldofwarcraft.com/en-gb/esports/arena)
- [World of Warcraft Mythic Dungeon International](https://worldofwarcraft.com/en-gb/esports/mythic)

We are offering the broadcast for each of these events in several languages. You can find the links to these regional streams\* in the table below!

- [WoW MDI](#WOWMDI)
