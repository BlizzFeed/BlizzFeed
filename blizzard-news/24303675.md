# That’s a Wrap on BlizzCon® 2026

- URL: https://news.blizzard.com/en-gb/article/24303675/thats-a-wrap-on-blizzcon-2026
![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/8l/8LVJDQQ9YZG41787692116328.png)

> A look back at the highlights of BlizzCon 2026, from championship victories to unforgettable community moments.

---

BlizzCon® 2026 is officially in the books, wrapping up a weekend of community, connection, competition, and plenty to celebrate across Blizzard’s worlds. Day 1 kicked things off with major announcements during [**Opening Ceremony**](https://news.blizzard.com/article/24301453) and incredible creativity on display at [**Community Night**](https://news.blizzard.com/article/24302575). As Day 2 comes to a close, we’re celebrating the champions crowned across a weekend of intense esports competition and looking back at some of our favorite memories from the panels, show floor, and beyond.

### Overwatch® World Cup

![Gold Medal - KOREA.jpg](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/gallery/3v/3VLT6JWLN8YX1789351902502.jpg)After battling through the field, **South Korea (KOR)** emerged as the 2026 Overwatch World Cup champions, defeating **Saudi Arabia (KSA)** to take home the World Cup trophy.

### World of Warcraft® Mythic Dungeon International

![WoW MDI - LIQUID_CHAMPIONS.png](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/gallery/7a/7ADB021XIZTF1789351943129.png)Speed, execution, and precision were on full display in the World of Warcraft Mythic Dungeon International, where **Team Liquid** captured the championship against runners-up **Big Lucky**.

### World of Warcraft® Arena World Championship

![AWC - GATORSBACK_CHAMPIONS.png](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/gallery/ld/LDT425WID5KG1789351978526.png)In the World of Warcraft Arena World Championship, **Gators Back** claimed the AWC title against **Echo**, closing out the weekend as champions.

### Hearthstone® Masters Tour World Championship

![HS - OTGxhh - Champion.png](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/gallery/in/INZIIDXU128Z1789352028252.png)The Hearthstone Masters Tour World Championship came down to **OTGxhho** defeating **XiaoT**to become world champion.

### Blizzard Classic Cup

The Blizzard Classic Cup celebrated the history, impact, and fun of Blizzard esports across generations with both Entertainment Matches and Legacy Matches.

![TASTELESS FS_Champs Red_2.png](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/gallery/5m/5MI33BBCD8ZS1789352059055.png)Across the full slate of Classic Cup competition, **Team Tasteless** came out on top, defeating **Team Artosis** and taking home the trophy.

Congratulations to all of this year’s champions, and to every competitor who helped make BlizzCon an incredible weekend of esports action.
