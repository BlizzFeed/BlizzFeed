# Your BlizzCon 2026 Community Night Contest Winners!

> See who claimed the top prizes in the Cosplay, Art, Short Film, and Talent Contests at BlizzCon 2026.

---

Community Night at BlizzCon 2026 is officially in the books! This year’s event was an incredible celebration of the creativity and spirit that make the Blizzard community so special. Congratulations to everyone who participated, and to this year’s winners!

## Cosplay

This year’s cosplay creators delivered in both the Cosplay Contest and Cosplay Exhibition, bringing iconic characters to life through remarkable artistry, craftsmanship, and style.

![Master Crafter.png](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/gallery/i8/I8WK1ZRVEP401789319779744.png)

In the category of Master Crafter, congratulations to our winner **Oshley** as **Kael'thas Sunstrider**!

![Armories winner pro.jpg](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/gallery/2i/2IN7SKGW0S1H1789321827026.jpg)In the category of Armories & Artifacts, congratulations to our winner **Shannon Noelle** as **Azure Drake Brigitte**!

![Epic FX.png](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/gallery/1r/1ROIRFFNBBWR1789319498881.png)In the category of Epic FX, congratulations to our winner **Sparkz** as **Ceremonial Jacaranda Regalia with Sunny Floral Staff**!

![Dynamic resized.jpg](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/gallery/fp/FPON28NDE78B1789322392223.jpg)In the category of Dynamic Duo, congratulations to our winners **Zalkegeroth and Faltation** as **Fall of the Lich King**!

![Best in Show 2.png](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/gallery/29/29V5GEETPN031789319340880.png)Taking home the top prize of Best in Show across all Cosplay Contest categories, congratulations to **Sparkz** as **Ceremonial Jacaranda Regalia with Sunny Floral Staff**!

![Best in Show pro.jpg](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/gallery/4l/4L7GGHJ485F41789321859662.jpg)

## Art

This year’s Art Contest was bursting with creativity, with submissions that showcased an incredible range of skill, passion, and imagination from our community.

![Guldan Garrosh 2.png](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/gallery/0d/0D0982AMQKF71789319576703.png)

Congratulations to the winner of the Art Contest **Emma Nolan** for ***Gul’Dan & Garrosh***!

Rounding out the Art Contest honorees were **Marco Vinicio** in 2nd Place for *Mephisto Sculpture*, and **Lina Bang** in 3rd Place for *Overwatch Assemble*.

## Short Film

Filmmakers from our community took us on a journey with this year’s Short Film Contest, bringing their stories to life on screen with humor, heart, and their own unique perspectives.

![High Noon 2.png](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/gallery/m0/M0N9VL01X4OT1789319632500.png)

Congratulations to the winner of the Short Film Contest **Bark** for ***High Noon***!

Applause for Short Film honorees also goes to **Zachary Shives** in 2nd Place for *BigIron*, and **Victoria Vandenbroucke** in 3rd Place for *76*.

## Talent

Performers from our community wowed us in the Talent Contest with their energy, flair, and showmanship, featuring a broad range of skills and unmistakable personality.

![MRGL MRGL 2.png](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/gallery/ob/OBCA3SEXS3OZ1789319892102.png)

Congratulations to the winner of the Talent Contest **Ilya Zhigarev** for “**Mrgl Mrgl!”**

Our other exceptional Talent Contest honorees include **Christopher Lane** in 2nd Place for “Jetpack Cat Theme,” and **Prinxe of De Amani** in 3rd Place for “I’d Like to Apologize to Every Healer I Ever Had.”

## Here’s to You

Thank you to everyone who shared their creativity, passion, and talent with us, and congratulations again to all of this year’s winners. BlizzCon is what it is because of this incredible community, and we’re so grateful to have you be a part of it.
