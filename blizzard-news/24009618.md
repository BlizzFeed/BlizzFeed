# BlizzCon 2023: Additional tickets on sale September 29, in-person program announcements!

- URL: https://news.blizzard.com/en-gb/article/24009618/blizzcon-2023-additional-tickets-on-sale-september-29-in-person-program-announcements
![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/bq/BQE15VLU6ROI1695744729854.jpg)

> This will be the final opportunity to purchase tickets to this year’s event. Catch a sneak peek of some of this year’s highlights, just announced.

---

As we prepare to welcome our community to BlizzCon® 2023 on November 3-4, we’re thrilled to share that a third wave of ticket sales will take place on Friday, September 29 at 12pm PDT. Here’s a first glimpse into some of the events and programming you can expect in person.

![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/if/IFUXSEQ7N88I1695856024861.png)

**Tickets**

Tickets will be sold through [AXS](https://www.axs.com/blizzcon2023): if you don’t have an AXS account already, we recommend making one in advance. Tickets will go on sale Friday, September 29 at 12pm PDT. Please note that you can purchase a maximum of two tickets per transaction. You’ll find more information about BlizzCon tickets at [BlizzCon.com](https://www.blizzcon.com/). [Some content at BlizzCon is rated for Mature audiences](https://www.esrb.org/ratings-guide/) and is not suitable for children: children under 7 will not be permitted to enter, and anyone under the age of 17 must be accompanied by an adult.

![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/9l/9LIBAFU0XV3F1695744683543.jpg)

If you can’t make it to the show in person, BlizzCon 2023 will be live streaming all arena content for free. We’ll also have details soon on a new set of in-game goodies for this year’s event called the BlizzCon Collection (previously called Virtual Ticket) – stay tuned to find out more.

Whether you will be joining us in-person or online, we can’t wait to see you! To stay up to date with all things BlizzCon, be sure to [follow us on](https://www.twitter.com/Blizzard_Ent) [social media](http://www.twitter.com/Blizzard_Ent), and head to [BlizzCon.com](https://www.blizzcon.com/) for more information about the show.
