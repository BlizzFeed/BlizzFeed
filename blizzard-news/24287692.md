> We can’t wait to celebrate with LE SSERAFIM at BlizzCon weekend—and for their closing performance!

[![Video](https://markdown-videos-api.jorgenkh.no/youtube/fgq1lEOwxZQ?width=1280&height=720)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fgq1lEOwxZQ)

We’re thrilled to welcome LE SSERAFIM back to BlizzCon! Not only will LE SSERAFIM join the festivities, they will also take the Main Stage once again as this year’s closing musical act on Sunday, September 13. As a prelude to their upcoming U.S. tour—and following their standout performance at BlizzCon 2023—it’ll be the “Perfect Night” to celebrate with LE SSERAFIM in person at BlizzCon 2026, only at the Anaheim Convention Center.

LE SSERAFIM are a girl group with SOURCE MUSIC, a label under HYBE, consisting of five members: KIM CHAEWON, SAKURA, HUH YUNJIN, KAZUHA, and HONG EUNCHAE. The group’s name, LE SSERAFIM, is an anagram of the words “I’M FEARLESS,” capturing the bold spirit and confident sound that define their identity. Since their debut in 2022, the group has steadily built a strong international presence with multiple Billboard chart entries, emerging as one of the most talked-about acts in today's global music landscape. With a passionate global fanbase, LE SSERAFIM are no strangers to the Blizzard community, having previously teamed up with Overwatch for a collaboration that brought their signature energy and style to one of Blizzard’s most celebrated universes.
