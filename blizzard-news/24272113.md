BlizzCon® 2026 is almost here, and we’re bringing the loot in style with the [BlizzCon Digital Bundles and BlizzCon Ultimate Collection](http://www.blizzcon.com/bundles), featuring in-game items from Warcraft®, Diablo®, and Overwatch®. Plus, all players can claim two free in-game rewards for Heroes of the Storm® and StarCraft® II, no purchase necessary, for a limited time. Whether you’re soaring through Azeroth, throwing down in the Tavern, battling across Sanctuary, brawling in the Nexus, commanding forces in the Koprulu Sector, or locking in the perfect Overwatch play, we’ve got you covered with in-game items to help you celebrate what matters most to you. And remember, BlizzCon 2026 will be **livestreamed free** for everyone!

This year’s digital bundles are designed to give you more choice in how you celebrate BlizzCon and the worlds you call home:

- **Pick your favorites.** Grab any of the five BlizzCon Digital Bundles individually for World of Warcraft®, Overwatch, Diablo® IV, Diablo® Immortal, or Hearthstone®.
- **Or go all in with the BlizzCon Ultimate Collection.** This all-in-one treasure trove includes everything from all five BlizzCon Digital Bundles, plus additional in-game items spanning Blizzard’s universes.

If you start with one bundle and decide you want the whole stash later on, you can also upgrade to the BlizzCon Ultimate Collection.

The BlizzCon Ultimate Collection is included in the purchase of a **BlizzCon Pass**, so if you’re attending the show, you’re getting it all. Be sure to [link your BlizzCon Pass](https://blizzcon.com/blizzcon-pass-linking/) to your **Battle.net Account** before September 14 to receive your items.\* Visit our [FAQ](https://www.blizzcon.com/faq/) page for more info.

The BlizzCon Ultimate Collection\*\* and BlizzCon Digital Bundles are available now through September 28, 2026, on the [Battle.net Shop](https://www.blizzcon.com/bundles).
