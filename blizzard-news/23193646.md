Add some epic artwork to your personal stash—all for a good cause!

Beginning **Thursday, October 31** through **Tuesday, November 5**, we’re once again hosting our special online BlizzCon® Charity Auction via eBay. The auction features a variety of original Blizzard artwork, unique memorabilia, and other cool collectibles from our games. This is your chance to score awesome new loot for your collection, and the proceeds will directly benefit [CHOC Children’s](http://www.choc.org/).

All auction items will also be on display at BlizzCon in Hall B. Check out some of the epic items below!
