***JUNE 5 UPDATE:***

It’s not too late to add a little more Azeroth to your collection—we’ve extended the deadline to pre-purchase the BlizzCon 2019 Commemorative Collectible statues until Wednesday, **June 19, 2019**! If you want to make sure you get an Orc Grunt or Human Footman in this limited production run, make sure to lock in your order before then.

Check out our brand-new unboxing video to get a closer look at the statues as we get ’em ready for action.

[![Video](https://markdown-videos-api.jorgenkh.no/youtube/Qiy-gMLcm0A?width=1280&height=720)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Qiy-gMLcm0A)

***ORIGINAL POST:***

Last week we announced that everyone who buys a ticket to BlizzCon 2019 will receive their choice of [BlizzCon Commemorative Collectible](https://blizzcon.com/en-gb/news/22955737/blizzcon-2019-commemorative-collectible-celebrates-25-years-of-warcraft) statue—the mighty axe-wielding **Orc Grunt**, or the fierce and noble **Human Footman.**

Starting today and for a limited time, we’re inviting *everyone***—whether you’re going to BlizzCon or not—**to celebrate 25 years of Warcraft with us by pre-purchasing either statue (or both) at the Blizzard Gear Store, to be shipped to your home in time for BlizzCon.

- [PRE-PURCHASE NOW](https://gear.blizzard.com/blizzcon-2019-commemorative-collectible/?utm_source=blog&utm_medium=community&utm_content=blizzcon&utm_campaign=bzc-19-commemorative-collectible-050419)

These statues are being produced specially for BlizzCon 2019, and we’ll be doing a one-time, limited run based on the orders we receive—so if you want to make sure you get one, you’ll need to put in your order before **June 6, 2019** at 08:59 a.m. CEST.

Similar statues in the Blizzard Legends style go for €55 each, but we’re making these available at a special price of €45 in celebration of Warcraft’s 25th anniversary.

You don’t need a BlizzCon ticket to participate—whether you’re planning to watch from home, or you picked one of the statues with your BlizzCon pass purchase and want to complete the pair, you’re invited to get in on the action.

[Check ’em out on the Blizzard Gear Store](https://gear.blizzard.com/blizzcon-2019-commemorative-collectible/?utm_source=blog&utm_medium=community&utm_content=blizzcon&utm_campaign=bzc-19-commemorative-collectible-050419).
