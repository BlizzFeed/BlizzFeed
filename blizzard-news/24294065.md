## Before You Arrive

If you’ll be joining us at the Anaheim Convention Center, a little preparation will help you get into the show and start exploring more quickly.

Before you arrive at BlizzCon, be sure to:

- **Confirm that your BlizzCon Pass is assigned correctly to you on Tixr.** You will need to show your BlizzCon Pass at Registration to receive your badge, wristband, and commemorative items. See Registration details below. [Follow these instructions](https://fansupport.tixr.com/articles/11083937-how-to-change-the-name-on-your-tickets) to change the name on your BlizzCon Pass, if needed.
- **Bring a valid government-issued photo ID card** with you, such as a driver’s license or passport.
- **Link your BlizzCon Pass to your Battle.net Account.** This is how you will receive your BlizzCon Ultimate Collection of in-game items—as well as drops—included with the purchase of your BlizzCon Pass.
- **Review the programming schedule.** Plan your must-see moments and leave room to discover unexpected ones.
- **Download the [BlizzCon floor map](https://blizzcon.com/floor-map)** so you can navigate the convention center even if you’re offline.

## Registration – BlizzCon Pass and Add-On Experiences

BlizzCon Registration is located in **Hall E** of the Anaheim Convention Center. To get to Registration, make your way to the Grand Plaza between the Hilton Anaheim and Anaheim Marriott hotels. Once you have gone through the security checkpoint, follow the signs pointing to Hall E Registration.

Registration is open at the following times:

- Friday, September 11 – 1:00 p.m. to 9:00 p.m. PDT
- Saturday, September 12 – 7:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m. PDT
- Sunday, September 13 – 7:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m. PDT

When you arrive at Registration, have your BlizzCon Pass ready in the Tixr app along with a valid photo ID that [matches the name](https://fansupport.tixr.com/articles/11083937-how-to-change-the-name-on-your-tickets) on your pass.

After your BlizzCon Pass is scanned, you will receive:

- A BlizzCon badge
- An event wristband
- A voucher for your BlizzCon Commemorative Items

You must wear your BlizzCon badge and event wristband for the duration of your time at BlizzCon at the Anaheim Convention Center. Entry credentials may not be shared. Lost badges and wristbands will not be replaced. If a wristband must be removed for safety reasons, bring the removed wristband to Hall E Registration for replacement.

![Commemorative Items - Brightened Asset.png](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/gallery/7x/7XJT95ZRLKL01787710431954.png)\
A commemorative items voucher may be redeemed for one set of commemorative items. Vouchers must be redeemed when issued; they cannot be saved for later redemption. Lost vouchers cannot be replaced.

If you purchased an Add-On experience with your BlizzCon Pass, follow the signage at Registration to the Add-On line in Hall E to pick up your BlizzCon Pass, commemorative credentials, and other items included in the purchase of both your BlizzCon Pass and Add-On experience. You can pick up everything you need from this one line—you do not need to wait in two separate lines to receive your BlizzCon Pass and Add-On experience.

## BlizzCon Show Hours

BlizzCon is open at the following times:

- Saturday, September 12 – 9:00 a.m. to 7:00 p.m. PDT
- Sunday, September 13 – 9:00 a.m. to 7:00 p.m. PDT

If you plan on lining up before BlizzCon opens to gain access as early as possible, the Grand Plaza, attendee staging areas, and security checkpoints will open at 6:00 a.m. PDT on both Saturday and Sunday.

Once you’re inside, take advantage of the **free WiFi** by joining the network “Anaheim Free WiFi.”

## Opening Ceremony

The **BlizzCon 2026 Opening Ceremony** will take place in **Hall D**. Seating is **first-come, first-served** and will fill up quickly. If you’re unable to get a seat inside Hall D, you can watch the Opening Ceremony from stages and viewing locations throughout BlizzCon. Doors open at 9:00 a.m. PDT and Opening Ceremony begins at 10:30 a.m. PDT.

## BlizzCon Merch Store

This year, the **BlizzCon Merch Store** has been redesigned into a new walkthrough retail shop experience, allowing you to see and interact with the items available. Visit the BlizzCon Merch Store in the **North Hall 1st Floor** at the following times:

- Friday, September 11 – 1:00 p.m. to 9:00 p.m. PDT
- Saturday, September 12 – 9:00 a.m. to 7:00 p.m. PDT
- Sunday, September 13 – 9:00 a.m. to 7:00 p.m. PDT

Additionally, **merch kiosks** with select merch will be located around the show floor. Find them at the following spots, open during BlizzCon show hours:

- Between Hall A and Hall B
- Between Hall C and Hall D
- Arena Plaza (merch trailer located outside of the North Hall) – open Friday, September 11, 1:00-9:00 p.m. PDT as well as during show hours

As in years past, [**click-and-collect shopping**](https://blink.gear.blizzard.com/) will also be available, opening Tuesday, September 8, at 10:00 a.m. PDT. This year, click-and-collect will offer an early access assortment of available merchandise from September 8 through the opening of BlizzCon. During BlizzCon, click-and-collect will offer a limited selection of “event essentials” only. Please visit the BlizzCon Store in person in the North Hall 1st Floor as our main shopping experience. Items purchased via click-and-collect can be picked up during BlizzCon Merch Store hours.

Please note that BlizzCon is a **cashless event**. Be prepared to use credit cards, debit cards, or mobile payment methods throughout BlizzCon for all transactions including merchandise, food, and more.

## Add-On Experiences

Both the **BlizzCon Charity Night** add-on experience and **Darkmoon Faire After Hours** add-on experience take place outside of BlizzCon’s official show hours and require a pre-purchased event ticket for admission to these events. Both experiences are sold out.

**BlizzCon Charity Night** will take place Friday, September 11, from 6:00-10:00 p.m. PDT. For entry, ticketed attendees may proceed to The Westin Anaheim, Anabella ballroom. Please have your BlizzCon badge and Charity Night wristband on. Ticketed attendees will receive their Blizzard custom artwork at the event.

The **Darkmoon Faire After Hours** experience will take place Saturday, September 12, from 8:00-11:00 p.m. PDT. For entry, ticketed attendees may proceed to the North Hall 2nd Floor holding area beginning at 7:30 p.m. PDT.

BlizzCon is home to high-stakes [esports competitions](https://blizzcon.com/esports) including the **Overwatch® World Cup**, **World of Warcraft® Mythic Dungeon International**, **World of Warcraft® Arena World Championship**, **Hearthstone® Masters Tour World Championship**, and the [**Blizzard Classic Cup**](https://news.blizzard.com/article/24243865). ## Cosplay at BlizzCon

**Cosplay** has always been an incredible part of BlizzCon, and we can’t wait to see what our community brings to life this year. If you’re attending BlizzCon in costume, join us at the Grand Plaza Fountain on Saturday, September 12, at 3:00 p.m. PDT for a **group photo**!

To help create a safe and welcome experience for everyone, all costumes and props must comply with the BlizzCon [Cosplay & Props Policy](https://blizzcon.com/in-person/event-policies/). If you plan to attend BlizzCon in costume, please review the complete policy before arriving at the Anaheim Convention Center.

Additionally, we are grateful to our other attendees for respecting cosplayers: asking before taking a photo, not touching them or their props without permission, and being respectful of them if they’re taking a break.

## March of the Murlocs

A longstanding BlizzCon tradition, the **March of the Murlocs** will take place Sunday, September 13. For those wishing to participate, put on your Murloc gear and gather in the Arena Plaza at 11:45 a.m. PDT. This year’s color is emerald green. The blowing of the conch signaling the start of the March will take place at 12:00 p.m. PDT. Stick around for a **group photo** at the Grand Plaza Fountain as soon as the March concludes.

## Inclusion Nexus

Looking for a place to connect, recharge, and experience the people behind Blizzard's worlds? Stop by the **Inclusion Nexus** on the 2nd floor of the South Hall, a unique BlizzCon destination celebrating the culture, communities, and employees who help shape Blizzard every day.

Explore four immersive experiences:

- **Stage** – Live panels, discussions, art experiences, trivia, and employee-led conversations.
- **Archives** – A look at Blizzard's history through artifacts, memories, and employee stories.
- **Experiential Spaces** – Interactive installations where attendees can share reflections, celebrate our values, and contribute to community storytelling.
- **Café** – A welcoming space to relax, connect with others, and take a break from the show floor.

Throughout the weekend, enjoy programming featuring Blizzard developers, Employee Networks, career conversations, mentorship roundtables, accessibility and inclusion discussions, live art, networking opportunities, and more.

Be sure to check in for **exclusive giveaways**, including both Inclusion Nexus items and limited Employee Network-specific swag available while supplies last.

## Portfolio and Resume Reviews

Interested in a career in gaming? Stop by our **portfolio and resume review sessions** in 201C on the 2nd Floor from 2:00-5:00 p.m. PDT on Saturday and Sunday. Spots are available on a first-come, first-served basis and are expected to fill quickly, so reserve your time slot early by visiting the welcome desk in front of the room.

## Security and Event Policies

We want everyone at BlizzCon to feel like they're part of one big Blizzard community. We ask that everyone at the show be respectful and courteous to their fellow attendees.

If you witness or experience any activity that puts someone's safety or enjoyment of the show at risk, inform a staff member, security guard, police officer, or any guest services personnel. We have escalation procedures in place to handle these kinds of situations, and we will take the appropriate action from there.

In order to provide a safe, enjoyable, and welcoming environment for everyone, Blizzard reserves the right to remove from BlizzCon any person whose conduct is deemed to be violent, disorderly, unbecoming, who uses vulgar or abusive language, or in any way deemed non-compliant with our BlizzCon code of conduct or other rules and regulations.

Please review our [Event Policies](https://blizzcon.com/in-person/event-policies/) for more information. If you feel any concerns about your safety prior to the show, please email us at [bcreporting@blizzard.com](mailto:bcreporting@blizzard.com). If you have any concerns during the show, please speak to the nearest security team member or Blizzard employee wearing a BlizzCon staff shirt.
