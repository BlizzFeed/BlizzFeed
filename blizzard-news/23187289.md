> The next expansion, World of Warcraft®: Shadowlands, has been announced!

---

The next expansion, [World of Warcraft®: Shadowlands](https://blizz.ly/Shadowlands), has been announced!

*Sylvanas Windrunner, fallen leader of the Horde, has pierced the veil between Azeroth and the realm of the dead, setting in motion a series of events that threaten to upset the cosmic balance between life and death. Azeroth’s heroes will confront the wonders and horrors of the afterlife as they deal with the consequences of Sylvanas’ actions.*

[![Video](https://markdown-videos-api.jorgenkh.no/youtube/eAPLYMfRTvg?width=1280&height=720)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eAPLYMfRTvg)

*Shadowlands* is now available for pre-purchase in-game and through the [Blizzard Shop](https://blizz.ly/BuyShadowlands)! For more information on Shadowlands, visit the [official site](https://blizz.ly/Shadowlands) and stay tuned for the What’s Next panel on Friday, 2:30 p.m.–3:15 p.m. PDT (watch free [here](http://www.blizzcon.com/watch)) and the Deep Dive\* panel on Saturday, 11:15 a.m.–12:00 p.m PDT.

\*Viewable for BlizzCon Virtual Ticket holders.
