# Submit Your Questions for the BlizzConline Q&As

- URL: https://news.blizzard.com/en-gb/article/23624623/submit-your-questions-for-the-blizzconline-q-as
![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/ou/OUIG19Z3JQ1E1612817038420.jpg)

> Have a question for the developers of World of Warcraft, Hearthstone, or Diablo? Submit your questions in video, audio, or written form for the BlizzConline Q&A panels!

---

Got a burning question about the Burning Hells? Seeking arcane knowledge about Azeroth? Have an inquiry about dealings at the tavern?

On Saturday, February 20 at BlizzConline, the development teams of World of Warcraft, Hearthstone, Diablo IV, and Diablo Immortal will be answering community-submitted questions—and if you want to participate, all you have to do is ask!

Head over to the BlizzConline Q&A submission page below to drop us your query for consideration. Through this page, you’ll also have the unique option to submit questions in audio or video form. We’ll be accepting a limited number of submissions this way until February 18, but keep an eye out for other ways to submit your written questions on our community sites soon. We’ll also be opening up the inbox again for some additional video, audio, or text questions onFebruary 19, 2021 at 0.00 CET following the opening ceremony.

To watch the Q&As live, tune in to BlizzCon.com at these times and channels:

**World of Warcraft Q&A** – 21:00 – 22:00 CET on the World of Warcraft channel

**Hearthstone Q&A** – 21:00 – 21:35 CET on the Hearthstone channel

**Diablo Q&A –** 21:00 – 22:00 CET on the Diablo channel

If you miss them when they air, you can check them out later in the archives.

*To stay up to date on all things BlizzConline, follow along on [BlizzCon.com](http://blizzcon.com/ "Blizzcon.com") or on the Battle.net [desktop](https://www.blizzard.com/en-us/apps/battle.net/desktop "https://www.blizzard.com/en-us/apps/battle.net/desktop") or [mobile](https://www.blizzard.com/en-us/apps/battle.net/mobile "https://www.blizzard.com/en-us/apps/battle.net/mobile") apps.*
