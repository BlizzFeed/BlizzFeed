Experience the true essence of BlizzCon amongst friends and family, without the hassle of flights and entry lines! This year, Blizzard is partnering with the [Meltdown](https://www.meltdown.bar/) to organize unforgettable viewing parties for BlizzCon 2019, spreading the genuine BlizzCon spirit across the globe! Gather your friends, guildies, and team-mates to share your passion for all things Blizzard at your nearest Meltdown bar and watch the Opening Ceremony live! (November 1, 10:00 PDT / 19:00 CET)

Activities and events will vary from bar to bar, but expect to experience fun trivia, exciting contests, unique photo opportunities, live tournaments, and meet-ups with local influencers. And what party would be complete without prizes? Some lucky attendees and worthy contestants will walk away with their share of loot, ranging from the 15th Anniversary Collector Editions of World of Warcraft to figurines, apparel and other legendary physical and digital goodies.

With over 20 convenient locations across Europe, Meltdown bars will welcome hyped visitors on November 1 starting at 18:00 CET. Meltdown bars in Canada and Panama will also be hosting their own viewing parties, spreading the BlizzCon vibes even further! Check out the full list of venues below or head on over to the [Meltdown website](https://www.meltdown.bar/) to find the location nearest to you!
