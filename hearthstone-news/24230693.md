# The Hearthstone Summer Championship Is Here!

- URL: https://news.blizzard.com/en-gb/article/24230693/the-hearthstone-summer-championship-is-here
![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/7D2DIHCGJLTX1756839199030.png)

> Here is how to watch the greatest Hearthstone players fight for glory in the Masters Tour Summer Championship.

---

The wait for the Hearthstone Summer Championship is almost over! From September 12–14, the top ladder players will join the winners of the Summer Playoffs to battle it out for their share of $50,000 as well as one of four spots in the 2025 Hearthstone World Championship. Read on for everything you need to know to enjoy all the action on Twitch and YouTube.

## What You Need to Know

- **Dates**: Friday, September 12–Sunday, September 14, starting each day at 9 a.m. PDT (6 p.m. CEST)
- **Format**: 4-deck, Best-of-5 Conquest with a ban
  - **Day 1**: A&B Groups Play, Double Elimination Bracket
  - **Day 2**: C&D Groups Play, Double Elimination Bracket
  - **Day 3**: Top 8, Single Elimination Bracket
- **Prize Pool**: $50,000
  - The top 4 competitors will qualify for the Masters Tour World Championship later this year!
- **Drops**: Earn up to 3 The Lost City of Un’Goro packs while watching on Twitch (1 each day)
- **Choose Your Champion**: Choose a competitor in the Summer Championship, and the better they finish, the more packs you’ll earn!
  - You can participate in Choose Your Champion starting on Monday, September 8. It goes live the moment we make the announcement, so keep an eye on <https://x.com/playhearthstone>.
- **Co-streamers**: We loved the content we saw from our co-streamers during the last Masters Tour, so this time we’re hoping even more streamers join the fun!
  - If you’re interested in co-streaming the Masters Tour Summer Championship, [please fill out this application](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSdlXSGYhNiHeUdPA3GflkElQzr00J-Q_f9g4Km-ZHKM2kf5_g/viewform?usp=header) by 9:00 a.m. PDT Wednesday, September 10. If selected, we’ll invite you to co-stream the tournament.
- **Casters**: [Edelweiss](https://bsky.app/profile/edelweissccg.bsky.social), [Lorinda](https://bsky.app/profile/l0rinda.bsky.social), [Raven](https://x.com/RavenCasts), [Sottle](https://bsky.app/profile/sottle.bsky.social)
- **Streams**:
  - **Twitch**: <https://www.twitch.tv/playhearthstone>
  - **YouTube**: <https://www.youtube.com/Hearthstone>
