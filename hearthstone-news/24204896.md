# Adventure Calls from the Lost City of Un’Goro, Hearthstone’s Next Expansion

- URL: https://news.blizzard.com/en-gb/article/24204896/adventure-calls-from-the-lost-city-of-ungoro-hearthstones-next-expansion
![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/qd/QDH41X9ADHB51748555945972.jpg)

> A powerful new keyword, the return of Quests, and danger untold await in the Lost City of Un’Goro!

---

It turns out Un’Goro Crater is more than just a big bowl of deadly dinosaurs. Legend tells of a Lost City hidden in its depths, and it’s up to the great Tortollan explorer Loh to assemble a team of experts and find it! Between ancient Maps, perilous Quests, and Kindred spirits, Loh is going to have one shell of a Story to tell.

[![Video](https://markdown-videos-api.jorgenkh.no/youtube/b2ngyvLoYsI?width=1280&height=720)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=b2ngyvLoYsI)

The Lost City of Un’Goro releases on July 8 with 145 new cards! View the Un’Goro reveal schedule, and all cards already revealed, in the Official Card Library.

---

### Embark on Quests for Game-Changing Rewards

With Loh setting out on his last great adventure, Quest spells are making their return! Quests are 1-cost spells that start in your hand. Complete the Quest’s requirements to be granted a powerful reward. The Lost City of Un’Goro introduces a brand-new Legendary Quest card for each class!

### New Keyword: Kindred

The Un’Goro Crater surges with primal energy! Tap into this power with the new keyword, Kindred. Cards with the Kindred Keyword get a bonus effect if you played a card of the same minion type or spell school on your previous turn. Plan ahead to unleash these primal bursts of power!

![PRIEST_TLC_815_enUS_GravedawnVoidbulb-117316_NORMAL.png](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/A9BDMCH8TD2H1748554050429.png)

### Reacquaint Yourself with Old Friends and Mechanics

Loh’s team of adventurers includes a few familiar faces, including famous explorer Elise Starseeker, the group’s navigator, and former Tortollan leader Umbra, the group’s guide. Those who have journeyed to Un’Goro before will recognize cards and mechanics from Hearthstone’s history that are returning for this new adventure.

![NEUTRAL_TLC_100_enUS_ElisetheNavigator-117762_NORMAL.png](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/KC22CCDLKIF71748554050420.png)

### Relive Iconic Stories from Un’Goro Crater

Passing down knowledge of the Un’Goro Crater to the next generation is a tradition of the Tortollan people. There are seven Story cards in The Lost City of Un’Goro. Each Story harkens back to Quests undertaken the first time we journeyed into the Un’Goro Crater.

![HUNTER_TLC_826_enUS_StoryofCarnassa-117599_NORMAL.png](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/H1EVZZQ2LGSC1748554049959.png)![HUNTER_UNG_920t1_enUS_QueenCarnassa-41367_NORMAL.png](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/K8A1TGVTSPSE1748554050307.png)

### Find Your Way to Fortune with Maps

Loh’s adventuring party wouldn’t get very far without a good map! Play a Map to Discover a card, and if you play the chosen card on the same turn, then you’ll get to pick another one of the options. There are six Maps in The Lost City of Un’Goro, so use them to map out your road to victory!
