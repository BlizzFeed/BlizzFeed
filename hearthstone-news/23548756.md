> Hearthstone 2020 Masters Tour concludes this weekend, starting at 6 am PDT only on YouTube!

---

The final Masters Tour of 2020 is here! Over 300 invited players from more than 40 countries, will compete for the Masters Tour Online: Madrid trophy and their share of the $3,000,000 (USD) total prize pool for Hearthstone Masters Tour 2020. Join us this weekend for all of the competitive action only on [YouTube Gaming](https://www.youtube.com/HearthstoneEsports)!

After this weekend, the top total earners across all six 2020 Masters Tours in each region will earn invites to Hearthstone Grandmasters. There are currently a total of 10 spots open: three spots opened by Grandmasters Season 2 relegation in each region, and a fourth in Americas following Fei “[ETC](https://twitter.com/etc_hs0)” Liang retirement from competitive Hearthstone. The field is wide open for players to prove themselves worthy of joining Grandmasters and competing for their shot at the World Championship!

### How It Works

Hearthstone Masters Tour Online: Madrid will be played in the 4 deck, best-of-5 Conquest with a ban format. Players who manage at least a 7-2 result will qualify for first two Hearthstone Masters Tours of 2021!

Here’s how it works:

- Day 1: Five rounds of Swiss. Players will be dropped after receiving their third match loss.
- Day 2: Players with three or more match wins will play through four more rounds of Swiss.
- Day 3: Top 8 players will compete in the playoff, and a new Masters Tour Champion will be crowned!

\
For qualified players, please check [the full tournament schedule for information regarding mandatory player check-ins, round start times, and more](https://playhearthstone.com/en-us/esports/tournament/masters-tour-madrid/). Qualified players should familiarize themselves with our [rules and policies](https://playhearthstone.com/en-us/esports/programs/rules-and-policies). We’ll see you in the Tavern!
