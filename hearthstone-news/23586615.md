# Hearthstone 2020 World Championship - Viewer’s Guide

- URL: https://hearthstone.blizzard.com/en-gb/news/23586615/hearthstone-2020-world-championship-viewers-guide
![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/hb/HBT74Z09AQTF1607623763200.jpg)

> Hearthstone 2020 World Championship begins this weekend, starting December 12 at 8 am PST only on YouTube!

---

The Hearthstone 2020 World Championship is here! Beginning this Saturday, December 12, the top eight players in the world face-off to determine who will join the Hall of Champions and walk away with $200,000 (USD). Before the competition starts, be sure to vote on who you believe will be Champion and earn free Madness at the Darkmoon Faire packs, through [Choose Your Champion presented by T-Mobile](https://playhearthstone.com/esports/tournament/world-championship-2020/choose-your-champion/). Join us this weekend for all of the competitive action only on [YouTube Gaming](https://www.youtube.com/HearthstoneEsports)!

![HS_WC_Initial_Matches.PNG](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/gallery/2r/2RG6TMZJ4FZD1607470767504.PNG)

### Details

- **Venue:** Online - [YouTube.com/HearthstoneEsports](http://www.youtube.com/HearthstoneEsports)
- **Dates:** Saturday, December 12 – Sunday, December 13, 2020
- **Broadcast Start Time:** 8:00 AM PST
- **Casters:** TJ “[Azumo](https://twitter.com/Azumoqt)” Sanders, Darroch “[Darroch](https://twitter.com/DarrochBrown)” Brown, Dan “[Frodan](https://twitter.com/Frodan)” Chou, Jia “[Jia](https://twitter.com/jiadee_)” Dee, Alex “[Raven](https://twitter.com/RavenCasts)” Baguley, and Simon “[Sottle](https://twitter.com/coL_Sottle)” Welch
- **Prize Pool:** $500,000 (USD)
- **Format:** 4-deck, best-of-5 Conquest with a ban
- **Language Streams on YouTube:**
  - [English](https://www.youtube.com/HearthstoneEsports)
  - [Arabic](https://www.youtube.com/EmpirePlay)
  - [French](https://www.youtube.com/user/HearthstoneFRA/featured)
  - [Japanese](https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCrR37g6dpxX4kUitZR8b4YA)
  - [Korean](https://www.youtube.com/HearthstoneKR)
  - [LatAm Spanish](https://www.youtube.com/user/hearthstonelatam)
  - [Mandarin](https://www.youtube.com/HearthStoneZHTW)
  - [Portuguese](https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCiXMMud2GemSR8PhXhTHuyQ?view_as=subscriber)
  - [Russian](https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCv4IKw9phF1NSxXD1gMZjqg)
