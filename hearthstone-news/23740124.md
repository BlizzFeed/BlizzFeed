# Batten down the hatches, the Deadmines Mini-Set is here!

- URL: https://hearthstone.blizzard.com/en-gb/news/23740124/batten-down-the-hatches-the-deadmines-mini-set-is-here
![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/w4/W42CM3D5D61I1635284704967.jpg)

> Delve into the Deadmines in Hearthstone’s all-new, 35-card Mini-Set!

---

Edwin VanCleef has a bone to pick with everyone who found themselves so *United* in Stormwind. As you make your way through the Deadmines, you had better watch your back around the Defias Brotherhood. With new Pirates and Tradeable synergies, there’s a lot to look out for!

[![Video](https://markdown-videos-api.jorgenkh.no/youtube/8Zld2eXWcOM?width=1280&height=720)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8Zld2eXWcOM)

The Deadmines Mini-Set, launching worldwide on November 2, consists of 35 distinct cards: 4 Legendary cards, 1 Epic card, 14 Rare cards, and 16 Common cards. Those new cards can be found in United in Stormwind packs, or you can get the entire 66-card\* Mini-Set for $14.99 or 2000 Gold!

![HS_21p6_Deadmines_Mini_Blog_Inline_ShopImage_760x360_RN01.png](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/7n/7NXWDQ6830531635286167585.png)

\* *Includes one copy of each Legendary card and two copies of each other card, for a full playable set of all cards in the Mini-Set.*
