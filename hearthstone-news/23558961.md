# Madness at the Darkmoon Faire is NOW LIVE!

> Hearthstone’s newest expansion is live! Log-in now and bust open your packs!

---

Carnival goers beware, the Old Gods have returned and are running wild at the Darkmoon Faire! [*Madness at the Darkmoon Faire* is now live worldwide with 135 new collectible cards, the new Corrupt keyword, Old God artifacts, and so much more!](https://playhearthstone.com/cards)

[![Video](https://markdown-videos-api.jorgenkh.no/youtube/UbHaRnws9_8?width=1280&height=720)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UbHaRnws9_8)

#### What’s Included: Old Gods Return

The Old Gods—C’thun, N’Zoth, Yogg-Saron, and Y’Shaarj—are back! Each Old God drives a different deck archetype and has a potent Battlecry capable of turning the tide of a match. Players can also tamper with Old God artifacts: four ancient spells with zany effects that hearken back to the abilities of the original Old God designs!

#### New Keyword: Corrupt

Madness at the Darkmoon Faire introduces a new keyword: Corrupt! Corrupt cards upgrade in your hand after you play a higher cost card. Plan ahead and upgrade your Corrupt cards before playing them for a major payoff!
