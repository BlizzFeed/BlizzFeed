> Patch 32.4.2 brings balance changes to Standard, Wild, and Battlegrounds, as well as the return of Anomalies in Battlegrounds!

---

Patch 32.4.2 brings the mid-season Battlegrounds updates, along with a new Legendary Quest, bug fixes, and balance changes for Standard and Wild!

### New Legendary Quest Chain

We’re on the road to The Lost City of Un’Goro, where grand quests and glorious rewards await! Start your adventure early with a new Legendary Quest Chain. From the launch of Patch 32.4.2 until July 8, play Standard games to earn up to 3 The Lost City of Un’Goro packs, 1 Golden The Lost City of Un’Goro pack, and 1 Into the Emerald Dream pack!

### Hearthstone Updates

*Dev Comment: Standard currently offers a good variety of viable deck classes and archetypes, but there are a few outliers that we wanted to tone down as we prepare to step into The Lost City of Un’Goro. Just like with our other recent updates, these changes are mostly to make burst turns slower and clunkier, curbing both play experience and power outliers.*

*On the Wild side, even though we targeted Draka’s decks last patch, it has continued to create extreme play patterns. As such, we’re finishing the job with a concrete cap on Draka’s power.*

### Battleground Updates

*Dev Comment: Well-met! This season is slightly shorter than usual, so our mid-season shakeup looks a little different. For the first time ever in Battlegrounds, we’ll have two systems together: Trinkets and Anomalies! We’ve curated which Anomalies are returning to add variety to your games while omitting some of the more disruptive ones. We’ve also added three new Anomalies that meddle with the Trinket system itself!*

#### Returning Anomalies

- Audience's Choice
  - At the start of each turn, one player chooses a card for ALL players to get at end of turn.
- Bring Home the Bacon
  - Start with two upgrading 'Piggy Banks'. Break them open to gain Gold!
- Double Header
  - The first time you buy a card each turn, get an extra copy of it.
- Eleventh Hour
  - The first time your hero takes fatal damage in combat, prevent it and gain 11 Gold next turn instead.
- Elven Elite
  - The Tavern only offers cards of your Tier.
  - Bob-blehead and Innkeeper’s Stein are banned from this Anomaly.
- Factory Line
  - At the end of every 2 turns, get a plain copy of your left-most minion. (2 turns left!)
- False Idols
  - You only need 2 copies of a minion to make it Golden. Instead of a Triple Reward, get a Gold Coin.
  - Pilfered Lamps, Designer Eyepatch, and First Mate Pip are banned from this Anomaly.
- Golden Arrow
  - Every 3 turns, get a Golden Arrow.
- Golganneth's Tempest
  - Minions cost 2 Gold. You cannot Refresh the Tavern. It Refreshes itself after you buy a card.
  - Innkeeper's Stein, Dalaran Cheese Wheel, Guiding Candle, Recycling Sticker, Lubber Sticker, Slippery Slider and Bounding Felstalker are banned from this Anomaly.
- Grapnel of the Titans
  - The first minion you buy each turn is free.
- Instant Warband
  - Start with 3 different Tier 2 minions in hand.
- Marin's Treasure Box
  - Trinkets are in this game. All heroes are Marin the Manager.
- Mimiron's Clockwork Stadium
  - You cannot upgrade the Tavern with Gold. It upgrades itself every 2 turns. (2 turns left!)
  - Bartend-o-Tron's Oilcan and Investment Contract are banned from this Anomaly.
- No Face, No Case
  - Every 4 turns, get a Faceless Manipulator.
- Overseer's Orb
  - After you upgrade the Tavern, Refresh it with minions of your most common type.
- Path of the Treasure-Seeker
  - After you Refresh 5 times, find the Golden Monkey! (5 left!)
- Perfected Alchemy
  - Start with a Goldenizer.
- Scout's Honor
  - Start with a Golden Patient Scout in play.
- Secrets of Norgannon
  - Tavern Tier 7 exists. Start with 10 extra Armor.
- Treasure Hoard (turns 5, 7, and 8)
  - On Turn [5/7/8], Discover a Golden Tier [3/5/6] minion.
- Finicky Hourglass
  - Start at Tavern Tier 2.

#### New Anomalies

Impressive Foresight

### Bug Fixes and Game Improvements

- [Hearthstone] Fixed a bug where Endbringer Umbra would retrigger all Deathrattles granted to Deathrattle minions in her effect.
- [Battlegrounds] Triple Rewards now give a 3-Gold pouch if there are no valid minions to Discover.
- [Battlegrounds] Fixed a bug where Golden Maw Caster’s Battlecry could trigger multiple times with Brann-type effects. (It will now only grant 2 Discovers, since there is only one Undead minion to destroy.)
- [Arena] Fixed a bug where players could get fewer packs than shown on their Arena rewards screen. Players who missed packs this way have been granted their missing packs; another wave of missed pack grants will go out after this final fix goes live.
- [Arena] Fixed a bug where Mystery Dispense-o-bot (from Remixed Dispense-o-bot) wouldn’t grant a secret.
- [Arena] Fixed a bug where Death Knights could be offered unpickable cards outside of their runes during their draft. Players who are stuck in a draft pick with no valid options will have their draft automatically retired and their ticket(s) refunded.
- [Arena] Fixed a bug where players who haven’t unlocked The Underground could get in a locked state by spectating an Underground game.
