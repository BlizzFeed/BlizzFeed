> The plan is set. The guards are distracted. It’s time to Escape from Violet Hold!

---

Make a break for it! *Escape from Violet Hold* is now live with 135 new collectible cards. This jailbreak-themed Hearthstone expansion features new Legendary Rulebreaker minions, the new Prepare keyword, and sneaky new Disguise and Bribe cards.

## What’s Included: Rulebreakers

Vanessa VanCleef is orchestrating a daring breakout from Azeroth’s maximum security prison, the Violet Hold, with a crew of infamous inmates. Meet the Rulebreakers! Each class gets its own Legendary Rulebreaker minion that defies a core concept of Hearthstone. After all, rules are made to be broken.

## What’s Included: New Keyword – Prepare

Pulling off the perfect heist takes careful planning. The new Prepare keyword lets you set up explosive plays in advance. By dragging a Prepare card into your deck, you spend all your remaining mana to Prepare it, discounting the card by that amount, plus one. Then you can unleash it on a later turn for a big surprise play out of nowhere.

## What’s Included: Disguised Minions

For the first time in Hearthstone history, you can play minions onto your opponent’s board. Disguised minions give you flexible options: play them on your side to utilize their ability for yourself or send them to your opponent’s side to unleash effects that can disrupt their game plan.
