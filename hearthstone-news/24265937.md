# CATACLYSM Rewards Track Refresh

- URL: https://news.blizzard.com/en-gb/article/24265937/cataclysm-rewards-track-refresh
![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/9v/9VFSAI9I4MEP1772651161366.jpg)

> Collect cards, cosmetics and more from CATACLYSM with the new Rewards Track!

---

Deathwing’s *CATACLYSM* is bringing untold levels of destruction to Azeroth, but on the bright side, it’s also bringing a brand-new Rewards Track! When the Rewards Track refreshes with the launch of *CATACLYSM*, all players will automatically be granted any unclaimed rewards on the current free Rewards Track and the Tavern Pass track (if acquired), before a completely new *CATACLYSM* Rewards Track takes its place.

Take a look at all of the rewards waiting to be unlocked!

![HS_35p0_RewardsTrackRefresh_InBlog_InBlog_FreeTrack_760x428_SW01B.jpg](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/QPNCZWUZNXNY1772650375939.jpg)

## *CATACLYSM* Rewards Track

The free Rewards Track for *CATACLYSM* will include 14 Golden cards and the Genn, Cursed King Legendary card\*! Other goodies include a random Legendary Colossal minion from *CATACLYSM*, random Legendary card, random Epic card, Tavern Tickets, and a bounty of Gold and Packs.

\* *These 14 Golden cards (two copies of each of 7 cards from the set) and the Genn, Cursed King Legendary card earned on the Rewards Track can’t be crafted or disenchanted.*

## *CATACLYSM* Tavern Pass

With the launch of *CATACLYSM*, a new Tavern Pass arrives packed with cosmetic rewards, XP boosts, Signature cards, and expansion packs! Getting the Tavern Pass at any time will immediately grant you a 10% XP Boost that lasts for the rest of the *CATACLYSM* expansion, plus all items up to your current level on the paid track. Unlock your rewards even faster!

As you progress along the Tavern Pass during the *CATACLYSM* expansion, you can claim these rewards\*:

- Additional XP Boosts
- Archmage Kalec Diamond Legendary card
- Arisen Onyxia Signature Legendary Colossal card
- Warmaster Blackhorn Golden Legendary card
- Genn, Cursed King Golden Legendary card
- 2x Medivh's Triumph Golden Epic cards
- 2x Gronn Giant Signature Epic cards
- 2x Obsessive Technician Signature Common cards
- 2x Experimental Animation Signature Common cards
- 2x Drakeadon Mongrel Signature Common cards
- 1x new cosmetic coin
- 1x new card back
- 8x new Hero Skins
- 1x Golden *CATACLYSM* Pack
- 1x Golden *Across the Timeways* Pack
- 4x *CATACLYSM* Packs

\* *All Tavern Pass card grants are unable to be crafted or disenchanted.*

## New Cosmetic Coins

Two new cosmetic coins will be added with *CATACLYSM*: the Shattered Coin and the Alexstrasza Coin. The Shattered Coin is earned by reaching level 90 on the Rewards Track with the Tavern Pass. The Alexstrasza Coin is earned by completing the “Total Destruction “Achievement by collecting 135 *CATACLYSM* cards.
