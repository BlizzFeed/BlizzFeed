## Prepare for *CATACLYSM* on March 17

*CATACLYSM*, Hearthstone’s next expansion, launches on March 17! This 135‑card expansion brings the first‑ever Deathwing Hero Card to Hearthstone, alongside brand‑new Colossal minions empowered by the Herald keyword, cards for the Dragon Aspects, the new Shatter keyword, and more. See every new card in the Card Library today, and grab your Pre‑Purchase bundles before the Worldbreaker descends upon Azeroth!

![HS_34p6_Resize_InBlog_MegaBundle_PP_MM_760x428_DB01.png](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/aq/AQFGZTT73Z1B1770672492998.png)

With the launch of *CATACLYSM* comes [a new Rewards Track](https://hearthstone.blizzard.com/news/24265937), filled with all manner of rewards sure to help in the battle to come, like XP boosts, cards, Gold, card packs, and more!

## Free Access to Dream City Trial Cards

Starting March 10, you’ll have full access to every card from *Into the Emerald Dream* and *The Lost City of Un’Goro*, including Golden versions. These Dream City Trial Cards\* are free to use for the entirety of *CATACLYSM*. Use them to build your deck and jump right into the action!

![HS_35p0_Announce_Blog_Generous_Moment_Free_Cards_E2_Resize_760x428_DB01.jpg](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/4RUHCUX0Y2F81770233138197.jpg)

If you already own most of these cards, don’t worry, we’ve still got something cool for you! Earning the “Only Missing A Few (Emerald Dream)” achievement grants you a Signature Legendary Ysera, Emerald Aspect, while completing “Only Missing A Few (Lost City)” awards a Signature Legendary Endbringer Umbra.

![enUS_YseraEmeraldAspect_SIG.png](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/8MCRECX4JZ711770233137009.png)![enUS_EndbringerUmbra_SIG.png](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/KEAVLLQ08PJ21770233137027.png)

For even more free rewards, check out our Dragon's Hoard Guide to learn how to claim them.

\* *Login between 3/10/2026 PDT and 36.0 Expansion Launch to gain temporary access to cards from Into the Emerald Dream and The Lost City of Un’Goro until 36.0 Expansion Launch.*

### New Year Rotation and Core Set Update

When *CATACLYSM* launches on March 17, the Year of the Scarab will begin! At that time, cards from the Year of the Pegasus (Whizbang's Workshop, Perils in Paradise, and The Great Dark Beyond) will rotate out of Standard. Updates to the Core Set and the Event Set card pools will also happen at that time. Core Set card text changes will take place with the patch, on March 10. See the [Welcome to the Year of the Scarab blog](https://hearthstone.blizzard.com/news/24243444) for more details on the Core Set updates.

## Arena Updates

On March 10, with Patch 35.0, all ongoing Arena runs will end, and a new Arena season will begin. This season will use a fully curated card pool that includes all *CATACLYSM* cards, with the exception of Vyranoth, which will be banned. That's right, play with new cards early! Cards from *CATACLYSM* will be incorporated into the card pool on March 10, a week before the new expansion releases on March 17.

For this season, cards with the Herald mechanic will receive an occurrence bonus but be limited to a maximum of two copies in the initial draft.

### Arena Rewards

With the new season comes a fresh reward for achieving 12 wins in Underground: the Dragonsworn Varden Hero Skin. Starting on March 10, it will replace the previous reward Hero Skin. After unlocking it, any subsequent 12-win runs will earn you an alternate prize of 2 Golden *CATACLYSM* packs.

When *CATACLYSM* releases on March 17, all Arena and Underground pack rewards will update from *Across the Timeways* packs to *CATACLYSM* packs.
