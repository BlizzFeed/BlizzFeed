## Introducing: The Black Market

From September 15 - October 13, step into the **Black Market**, a makeshift bazaar run by mysterious vendors who play by their own rules, deal in unusual currency, and change their prices whenever it suits them. Play Ranked Hearthstone to earn a limited-time currency called Bloodstones and spend them on packs and a variety of new and returning cosmetics at the Black Market. Check back often because today's bargain could become tomorrow's luxury.

During the final week of the Black Market from October 6 - 13, you'll no longer be able to earn Bloodstones, but the market will remain open so you can spend any you've saved up before they disappear at the end of the event. Your Black Market currency does not carry over between Black Market events, so make sure to spend any you've saved up before the event ends. If you're just a few Bloodstones short of that last item, you'll be able to haggle with vendors during the final week of the event for a chance to lower its price and score a better deal.

Spend your Bloodstones on the following Black Market offerings:

- 1x Reign of the Black Empire Pack (Stock: 4)
- Taelan Fordring Signature Legendary Card
- Broxigar Hero Skin & Card Back for Demon Hunter Class
- Yogg-Ramen Hero Skin for Mage Class
- Champion Tyrande Hero Skin for Priest Class
- Fireside Card Back

If you already own an item, that reward slot will be replaced with Golden Standard Packs of equivalent value.

### New Minion Type: Aberrations

![HS_BlizzConRecap_InBlog_BGAberrations_DB_760x428_SW01.jpg](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/G0EOHYSJTE8T1789249575934.jpg)

Meet **Aberrations**, the newest Battlegrounds minion type. Built around discard synergies, Aberrations grow stronger as they work toward awakening their Deity. Different Aberration cards support this strategy in different ways, giving you multiple paths to unlock the power of the Old Gods.

How summoning a Deity works:

- Each time a friendly Aberration dies, it advances your Deity's progress.
- There are two Deities, C'Thun and Y'Shaarj, but only one will answer your call.
- Which Deity answers is chosen at random at the start of each game.
- Once three sacrifices have been made, your Deity joins for that combat with a game-changing effect.

Aberrations will appear in every lobby during the first two weeks following their debut, starting September 22.

With Aberrations joining Battlegrounds as the newest minion type, Naga will be temporarily rotated out of the minion pool. Naga-specific heroes and cards will be unavailable while Naga are out of the pool.

For a complete look at Patch 36.6.1, check out our [full Battlegrounds overview](https://hearthstone.blizzard.com/news/24302091).

## Bug Fixes

### Hearthstone

- Fixed a bug where Flames of Infinity always triggered first at the end of an opponent's turn.
- Fixed a bug where Medivh's Triumph could have an incorrect Cost after Soul Immolation was played.
- Fixed a bug where players could exceed the hand-size limit in certain situations involving Arcane Flow and The Forbidden Sequence.
- Fixed a bug where minions that target cards in hand could not be played without a valid target.
- Fixed a bug where Kazakus Potions generated by Aya, Lotus Kingpin were not copied correctly by Shadow of Demise.
- Fixed a bug where Harsh Sentence could cause issues when attempting to insert minions into a full deck.
- Fixed a bug where The Origin Stone and Sands of Time could interact in a way that caused the game to become stuck.
- Fixed a bug where Gorgonzormu's Delicious Cheese could be played with a full board.
- Fixed a bug where Bone Flurry displayed and dealt inconsistent damage when modified by Prophet Velen.

### Battlegrounds

- Fixed a bug where Neutral minions that became All-type minions did not correctly receive Elemental scaling bonuses.
- Fixed a bug where Rot Hide Gnoll could gain Attack from a teammate's minions dying.
- Fixed a bug where Power of the Storm interacted incorrectly with Cosmic Duality.
- Fixed a bug where Timewarped Sea Glass only doubled Attack instead of applying its full effect.
- Fixed a bug where Unforeseen Portal could activate multiple times in a single game.
- Fixed a bug where Sire Denathrius' Kidnap Sack rewarded two Spellcraft spells instead of one.
- Fixed a bug where Valithria Dreamwalker failed to receive buffs when Dragons were summoned during combat.
- Fixed a bug where Pyramad's Hero Power could be used when no Tavern minions remained.
- Fixed a bug where purchasing a Spell incorrectly counted as a Discover while using Magicfin Sticker.
- Fixed a bug where Arch-Villain Rafaam's Hero Power could trigger during the Shop Phase.
- Fixed a bug where Reclaimed Souls could be used after Aureate Laureate died in combat without granting the expected copy.

### Cosmetics

- Fixed a visual bug where several Signature cards briefly appeared as their normal versions during animations.
- Fixed a bug where the Dino Cloner Zerek Hero Skin was missing an in-combat voice line.
- Fixed a visual bug where opponent minions could retain a transparent Taunt effect after certain interactions involving Mister Clocksworth and Kayn Sunfury.
- Fixed a visual bug where cards could appear on the wrong layer after canceling a play while Underfel Rift was active.
