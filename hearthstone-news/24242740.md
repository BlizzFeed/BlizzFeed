*Dev Comment: Well met! We’d like to share a peek behind the curtain at a change coming to Hearthstone. Beginning with* CATACLYSM*, major expansions will return to a total of 135 cards. Neutral common cards haven't seen as much play as we’d like, so we’re removing 10 Neutral commons. But these cards aren’t really disappearing. Instead, they’re transforming into something more exciting.*

*Going forward, we’re using that design space to create new, higher-rarity event cards that you’ll be able to earn for free throughout the* CATACLYSM *expansion cycle. These changes make it easier for you to get your hands on exciting new cards, both in opening expansion packs and through events.*

*Those are just a few of several improvements rolling out this year. As highlighted in the Hearthstone Spotlight, we’re also introducing new Class Sets that give every class more impactful tools and shine an even brighter light on their unique playstyles. We can’t wait to share more with you soon.*
