> Meet the 2020 World Championship Contenders: Monsanto – The Comedian

---

Following two Grandmasters seasons and six Masters Tours, we are gearing up to conclude the year with the Hearthstone 2020 World Championship! With $500,000 (USD) on the line, the top eight players in the world are set to meet on December 12-13 to see who will join the Hall of Champions and finish off 2020 as the next Hearthstone World Champion.

We recently asked each of the competitors a series of quick-fire questions to learn more about them ahead of the 2020 World Championship. Let’s get to know Jerome “[Monsanto](https://twitter.com/Monsanto_HS)” Faucher.

### The Comedian

With a quick wit and dry sense of humor, Monsanto quickly endeared himself to the Hearthstone Esports team when he first landed on the scene in 2016. His good-natured spirit and lightheartedness mask one of the true workhorses of the competitive scene, dedicating hours of prep for each match while also hosting Hearthstone News on his own [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCnrT9AckW-mW5qJMgJp5MQQ) channel. Hailing from The Great White North, this Canadian all-star’s consistency and high-level play earned him a spot in the inaugural season of Grandmasters. Following his 3-2 defeat of Fei “[ETC](https://twitter.com/ETC_HS0)” Liang in the Grandmasters 2020 Season 2 finals, securing his first trip to a World Championship.

[![Video](https://markdown-videos-api.jorgenkh.no/youtube/1m6LxoMyY3U?width=1280&height=720)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1m6LxoMyY3U)

**HS Esports**: What is your greatest strength at Hearthstone?

***Monsanto**: Adaptability.*

**HS Esports**: Prior to Madness at the Darkmoon Faire, what has been your favorite deck from 2020?

***Monsanto**: Enrage Warrior. It's been a very difficult yet rewarding deck to play this whole year.*

**HS Esports**: What is your favorite Hearthstone card of all time?

***Monsanto**: The Curse of Rafaam one because it was so easy to equip.*

![Curse of Rafaam.png](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/gallery/nu/NU5WZHZEWKOV1605767443080.png)

**HS Esports**: What has been the highlight of 2020 so far?

***Monsanto**: Out-Turtle-Maging bloodyface's Turtle Mage with Liadrin Shadowstep Kobold Lackey and then losing my match anyway.*

[![Video](https://markdown-videos-api.jorgenkh.no/youtube/tQFhUmtAOoE?width=1280&height=720)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tQFhUmtAOoE)

**HS Esports**: Other than yourself, who do you think has the best chance to win the 2020 World Championship?

***Monsanto**: Bloodyface, because he comes from Americas and we all know it's the best region.*

**HS Esports**: Why do you think you are going to win the 2020 World Championship?

***Monsanto**: I’ve got a very powerful hat.*

**HS Esports**: What does becoming the World Champion mean for you?

***Monsanto**: It would mean it's all downhill from here, probably not the best outcome. Money though….*
