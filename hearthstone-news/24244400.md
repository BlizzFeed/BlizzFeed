Patch 34.4.2 rolls out today with balance changes for Hearthstone, Arena, and Battlegrounds.

## Hearthstone Updates

*Dev Comment: Well met! We’re pleased to see the meta in a solid spot. Your deckbuilding with the Echoes of the Infinite Mini-Set has sparked fresh archetypes like Imbue Rogue and Broxigar Demon Hunter, and it’s breathed new life into several existing decks.*

*Looking ahead, there are two major patches on the horizon: one featuring widespread balance changes, and the other bringing our annual set rotation and Core Set update. We expect some big meta shakeups in those patches, so we kept this update intentionally small. Morchie received a light buff to make her a more appealing option.*

*We’ve heard your feedback about Elise the Navigator, and she will be getting adjustments. Because she plays such a big role in the meta, we’re planning her update as part of those larger, more impactful patches. We appreciate your patience as we chart a new course for the famed explorer.*

## Battlegrounds Updates

*Dev Comment: Most of this patch focuses on tuning individual cards that were either overperforming or lagging behind. We also had two bigger goals in mind.*

*First, we wanted to dial back Deathrattles a bit. Deathrattle builds are fun and generally healthy for the game, but in the current meta they’ve become a little too dominant and too common. We’re adjusting Monstrous Macaw, Twilight Broodmother, Apexis Guardian, and Carapace Raiser to bring those strategies back to a more reasonable power level.*

*Our second goal was to give some love to Timewarped cards with low pick rates. These buffs should help them feel more rewarding and open up new options in your games. As always, we’ll be keeping an eye on how these changes play out and will make further updates if needed.*

*Our mid-season update is right around the corner, and we’re excited to share more soon. See you in the Tavern!*
