Patch 33.0 launches tomorrow, opening the path to the Lost City of Un’Goro, introducing the first-ever Hearthstone Pet, and more!

### Explore the Lost City of Un’Goro on July 8

The Lost City of Un’Goro, Hearthstone’s next expansion, launches on July 8! The new 145-card expansion includes the return of Quests, the powerful new Kindred keyword, and more! See all the new cards in the [Card Librar](https://hearthstone.blizzard.com/en-us/cards)y today. Don’t forget to grab your [Pre-Purchase bundles](https://us.shop.battle.net/product/hearthstone-the-lost-city-of-ungoro-mega-bundle) before setting out on your big adventure!

### Pre-Release Tavern Brawl and Community Days

The Lost City of Un'Goro Pre-Release Tavern Brawl begins tomorrow, with Patch 33.0, and runs until expansion launch on July 8. Crack open your packs early, build a deck, and compete in a mini competition to win more Lost City of Un’Goro prizes!

![HS_32p4_CommunityActivations_InBlog_PreReleaseTavernBrawl_760x572_SW01.jpg](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_folder_media/73/73QBKB1MNBL71750972676052.jpg)

Everyone will get one free entry into the Pre-Release Tavern Brawl. After that, entries will cost 2 Tavern Tickets, 300 Gold, or 400 Runestones. In each run, you will lock in a deck and then play until you get 6 wins or 3 losses, and then get prizes based on how well you do.

![HS_32p4_CommunityActivations_InBlog_PreReleaseBrawlChart_760x439_SW01.jpg](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_folder_media/qw/QW0WJCHNO6061750972722351.jpg)

In this Pre-Release Tavern Brawl, the eligible card pool will include the Core Set, the Event Set, Into the Emerald Dream, Embers of the World Tree Mini-set, and The Lost City of Un’Goro. Note that Creature of Madness and Hamuul Runetotem will be banned. The card pool will also include a selection of cards from other sets to help support some of the new Quests and cards from the Lost City of Un’Goro expansion:

- Hunter: Painted Cavasaur, Toyrannosaurus
- Priest: Nightshade Tea, Orbital Halo, Silvermoon Brochure
- Rogue: Agency Espionage, Adaptive Amalgam
- Warlock: Sweetened Snowflurry, Sketch Artist

See what your favorite creators are unearthing and earn more packs to use in the Pre-Release Tavern Brawl during an extended 5-day Community Days event! See the dedicated [Community Activations blog](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24209849/the-lost-city-of-ungoro-community-events) for more information about the Pre-Release Tavern Brawl and Community Days.

### Introducing Pets: King Krush Is Ready to Roar by Your Side

The cat’s out of the bag: Pets are coming to Hearthstone! King Krush, a mini version of the iconic green devilsaur, is our first interactive cosmetic companion. He’ll join you on the board, reacting to gameplay, emotes, and more with unique animations and sounds. Pet him, feed him, or play tug-of-war with his favorite toy. Earn XP to unlock new skins like Crystal Krush and King Plush.

![HS_32p4_Pets_DarkmoonFaireTreasures_Blog_InBlog_GIF_KingKrush_Idle_650_440_SW01.gif](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_folder_media/fe/FE8YILPSATYX1750972773373.gif)

King Krush is part of the debut collection for our new random pull system, Darkmoon Faire Treasures, running from July 8 to 29. Your first pull is free! Subsequent pulls require Runestones to continue playing. Every pull guarantees a new reward, which means no duds and no dupes! [Read more details on Darkmoon Faire Treasures pulls and odds](https://us.support.blizzard.com/en/article/32545). Explore the Faire’s magical rewards, including Signature cards, Hero Skins, and more.

![HS_32p4_Pets_DarkmoonFaireTreasures_Blog_InBlog_PrizeSpread_760x428_SW02.png](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_folder_media/91/912DTBKXDED81750972838320.png)

[Check out our full breakdown of Pet features and how to obtain prizes from Darkmoon Faire Treasures.](https://hearthstone.blizzard.com/news/24216434)

*Darkmoon Faire Treasures is not available to players under the age of 18. Battle.net Account required to access.*

### In-Game Event: The Lost City of Un’Goro

![HS_33p0_Patch_Notes_InBlog_Event_GIF_760x434_SW01.gif](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_folder_media/k9/K94FSE3UDZIH1750972905823.gif)

Set out to find the Lost City of Un’Goro by sky, by lake, or by cavern! From July 8 to July 29, choose to adventure with one of three iconic explorers and complete their Event Quests to earn Event XP on their respective event rewards track. Quest objectives will now refer to Traditional Modes for completion—Standard, Wild, Casual, Arena, and Tavern Brawl.

Complete the rewards track to earn:

- 6 The Lost City of Un'Goro Packs
- 3 Into the Emerald Dream Packs
- 3 Year of the Pegasus Packs
- 2 The Great Dark Beyond Packs
- 1 Perils in Paradise Pack
- 1 Hero Skin of the explorer you chose at the start of the event\*:
  - Dino Wrangler Brann Skin
  - Adventure Finley Skin
  - Relic Hunter Reno Skin

*\*The remaining two Hero Skins will be available for purchase in the shop after the event concludes!*

### Arena Updates

On July 1, with Patch 33.0, all ongoing Arena runs will end and a new Arena season will begin. This season will feature a curated card pool for each class with cards coming from across all sets. Play with new cards early! Cards from the Lost City of Un’Goro will be incorporated into the card pool a week before the new expansion releases on July 8. All of the new Quest cards from the Lost City of Un’Goro, as well as some returning Quests, will be draftable and come with five support cards in their Legendary Bucket.

*Dev Comment: After listening to player feedback, we've updated the rewards for Underground Arena, with a focus on making it easier to earn back your entry costs through deep runs. By further streamlining the reward structure and reallocating some of the Crowd’s Favor, we were able to provide better gold and ticket payouts, bringing back more of that “infinite run” feeling that’s been a hallmark of the classic Arena experience. We’ll be monitoring how things play out and will continue to adjust as needed.*
