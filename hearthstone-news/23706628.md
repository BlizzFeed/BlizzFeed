# United in Stormwind is Now Live

- URL: https://hearthstone.blizzard.com/en-gb/news/23706628/united-in-stormwind-is-now-live
![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/iy/IYXT4EJKRY0V1628022829499.jpg)

> Hearthstone’s newest expansion is now live worldwide.

---

Welcome to Stormwind, travelers. *United in Stormwind* is now live worldwide with 135 new collectible cards, the new Tradeable keyword, Questlines, profession tools, mounts, and so much more.

#### What’s Included: Tradeable

Cards with the new Tradeable keyword can be played like normal, or, for one mana, can be dragged back to your deck in exchange for another card.

![PRIEST_SW_441_enUS_ShardoftheNaaru-64391_NORMAL.png](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/u7/U7R4IPZVFL3O1627602387111.png)![SHAMAN_SW_114_enUS_Overdraft-64773_NORMAL.png](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/j6/J6KULGVLA4641628001367247.png)

#### What’s Included: Questlines

All 10 classes have access to a Questline. Questlines start in your hand and have three phases. Earn rewards for completing each phase of the Questline, culminating in a powerful Legendary mercenary minion.

![HS_21.0_NowLive_Blog_Inline__enUS.png](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/fr/FRX94GZJARAB1627602501827.png)

#### What’s Included: Profession Tools

Profession tools are weapons with special effects instead of attack values.

![MAGE_SW_001_enUS_CelestialInkSet-63673_NORMAL.png](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/f1/F1IEGQUU4G8C1627602710706.png)![PALADIN_SW_048_enUS_PrismaticJewelKit-64554_NORMAL.png](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/od/ODA8UD4DZADG1627602864669.png)

#### What’s Included: Mounts

Mounts are spells that buff a minion and then keep fighting even after the rider falls in combat.

![WARLOCK_SW_087_enUS_DreadedMount-64896_NORMAL.png](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/xz/XZB3IGDX1GC01627602746877.png)![HUNTER_SW_458_enUS_RammingMount-64965_NORMAL.png](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/r0/R0Z93ZAQLV0H1627602878514.png)

#### Battlegrounds Cosmetics
