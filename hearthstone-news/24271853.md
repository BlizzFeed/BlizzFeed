Patch 35.2 is rolling out with updates for the new season of Battlegrounds, new event cards, two Tavern Brawls, and more!

## Battlegrounds Updates

Answer the call to defend Azeroth in **Battlegrounds Season 13: CATACLYSM CALLS**! Explore new and returning Trinkets, utilize two new keywords, try out two new heroes, and more. The new season begins on April 14 at 10:00 a.m. PDT.

![HS_35p2_BGSeason13_Announce_InBlog_BGMarquee_Seasonal_760x710_SW01.jpg](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/1u/1UMV7OIA2UM11774552928199.jpg)

### What’s Coming in Season 13

- **Returning Mechanic: Trinkets.** They’re back with an enhanced system designed to offer you better, more synergistic Trinkets more often.
  - 33 new and 75 returning Lesser Trinkets
  - 40 new and 74 returning Greater Trinkets
- **New Keyword for Demons: Fodder.** Add Fodder tokens to your minion pool and watch your Demons feast on them to gain stats!
- **New Keyword for Dragons: Chromadrake.** Coming in five chromtastic colors, each Chromadrake provides a unique effect that enhances both your Dragons and your Tavern Spells.
- **Updated Minion Pool:**
  - 48 new minions
  - 61 returning minions
- **Two New Heroes:**
  - Genn, Worgen King - King of Duality
  - Mister Clocksworth - Double Time

For in-depth details on all this and more, check out our [Battlegrounds Season 13 reveal blog](https://hearthstone.blizzard.com/news/24244885). Check out r/hearthstone on Reddit for all of the [new and returning Trinkets](https://www.reddit.com/r/hearthstone/comments/1se5i05/battlegrounds_season_13_battlegrounds_card/) as well as all of the [changed, removed and returning Minions](https://www.reddit.com/r/hearthstone/comments/1se5n6g/battlegrounds_season_13_changed_returning_removed/). See the [Hearthstone Official Card Library](https://hearthstone.blizzard.com/battlegrounds) for the complete directory of updates.

### Battlegrounds Track Refresh

![HS_33p2_BGSeason13_Announce_InBlog_First4_760x435_SW01.png](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/NVKHFFHHP0AK1774553292203.png)

Then, progress through the Season Pass track to earn the new Cataclysmic Strike, 13 additional Hero Skins, and more! Or upgrade to the Season Pass + to get all the rewards from the Season Pass **plus one included Hero re-roll per game**.

## Deathling Flutters Menacingly into Darkmoon Faire Treasures

![HS_35p2_Patch_Notes_InBlog_DFTPrizeSpread_760x428_SW01.jpg](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/A4SK9UJM58E41775510456910.jpg)

From April 14 - May 5, behold the Dinky Destroyer. The Wittle Worldbreaker. The Black Scourgelet. **Deathling**. He’s just one of 10 fantastical prizes you can win at the Darkmoon Faire Treasures booth.

Deathling is the spawn of Deathwing, his most chaotic creation and his most loyal disaster. He may not have Deathwing’s size or world‑shattering might, but you would never guess it from how scrappy and bitey he is. Deathling reacts to your gameplay, celebrating your biggest plays with manic fervor and melting down over even the slightest setback. He flutters by your side as the first‑ever Hearthstone Pet to stay airborne.

Feed him one of his favorite snacks, an extra‑juicy Magmaw grub. Dig into his toy bag and pull out one of three surprises at random for him to play with: an Alexstrasza Plush, an N’Zoth Toy, or a pack of live dynamite?!

![HS_35p2_Patch_Notes_InBlog_Deathling_GIF_580x440_SW01.gif](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/W0EM69DM8SRL1775510517704.gif)

Earn XP to level up Deathling and unlock new skins, including Shadowflame Deathling, Mech‑ling, and Deathling, Worldbreaker.

![HS_35p2_Patch_Notes_InBlog_DeathlingSkins_760x354_SW03.jpg](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/ZBXEHZKQUNMB1775510452587.jpg)

Each Pet earns XP differently, rewarding you for the actions they like. Here’s how to earn XP with Deathling:

- Feed Deathling a Treat
- Summon a minion with 12 or more attack
- Cast a Fire spell
- Use Herald
- Play a 10-cost card
- Play a Dragon that costs 5 or more mana
- Discard a card
- Gain Armor
- In Battlegrounds, start a game as Deathwing, Ragnaros or Al'Akir
- In Battlegrounds, Triple a Dragon
- In Battlegrounds, play a Tavern Tier 6 minion
- In Battlegrounds, win a round of combat

*Darkmoon Faire Treasures is our random pull system that rewards Pets, Mythic Skins, Signature cards, Hero Skins, and more. The first pull is free, and subsequent pulls require Runestones to purchase. Every pull guarantees a prize. As the prize collections in the Treasures booth rotate, prices adjust to match the value of the current items. [Read more about the current value of Darkmoon Faire Treasures, as well as pull rates and odds](https://support.blizzard.com/article/000380896).*

## Bug Fixes and Game Improvements

- Hearthstone
  - Fixed a bug where Alexstrasza, Guardian of Life’s Health effect persisted on a hero’s Health after playing a Hero card.
  - Fixed a bug where Ysiel Windsinger’s Battlecry did not trigger while Naga Sea Witch was on the board.
  - Fixed a bug where Poisonous effects did not trigger after applying a buff from Avatar Form.
  - Fixed a visual bug where the Immovable Object animation played slowly when multiple instances of damage occurred in quick succession.
  - Fixed a visual bug where the Golden version of Wrathspike Brute was missing its animation.
  - Updated Warmaster Blackhorn’s VFX to only target the deck.
- Arena
  - When a run is retired due to a banned card, a new prompt now clearly shows that your Tavern Ticket entry fee has been refunded. This refund was always granted, but this update adds a visual confirmation.
- Battlegrounds
  - Fixed a bug where a copy of Secret Sinstone created by Search Through Time was added to the hand before resolving correctly.
  - Fixed a visual bug where Blood Gem VFX from Glowgullet Warlord traveled from the wrong corner of the board.
  - Fixed a bug where Colorful Compass always granted Naga after being obtained.
  - Fixed a bug where the Rocking and Rolling Darkmoon Prize did not stack.
  - Fixed a visual bug where Thornspike Pauldron displayed incorrect Blood Gem stats in the large card view.
  - Fixed a visual bug where the Golden version of Darkgaze Elder was missing the Blood Gem tooltip.
  - Fixed a bug where Splinter of Aurum did not trigger its effect.
  - Fixed a bug where Slamma Sticker caused summoned Beasts to have 1 Health.
  - Fixed a bug where Alliance Keychain granted stats based on a minion that attacked and died immediately instead of the first minion that died.
  - Fixed a bug where Murkbrine Expeditioner did not grant gained Health if the leftmost card in hand was a Spell.
  - Fixed a bug where Polarizing Beatboxer did not double its stats when Timewarped Wargear was magnetized to another minion.
- Cosmetics
  - Fixed an issue where Multiclass Quick Equip did not appear for the Mythic Arachnid Kerrigan Bundle.
  - Fixed a visual bug where the Mythic Lion’s Landing Variant Hero Skin had a delayed attack cancel return animation.
  - Fixed a visual bug where Demon Hunter Malfurion displayed incorrect art.
  - Fixed a visual bug Nathanos Blightcaller displayed temporary placeholder art.
