Patch 34.0, launching tomorrow, starts the countdown to the *Across the Timeways* expansion, kicks off a new Arena season, and more.

## Travel Across the Timeways on November 4

*Across the Timeways*, Hearthstone’s next expansion, launches on November 4! The new 145-card expansion includes the time travel Rewind keyword, Fabled Legendary minions from alternate timelines, and more! See all the new cards in the [Card Library](https://hearthstone.blizzard.com/cards) today. Don’t forget to grab your [Pre-Purchase bundles](https://shop.battle.net/product/hearthstone-across-the-timeways-mega-bundle?p=1984114) before time passes you by!

![HS_33p6_Expansion_Announcement_InBlog_MegaBundle_MM_760x428_SW01.jpg](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/C7L5UP5NN3BI1758307557467.jpg)

With the launch of *Across the Timeways* comes a new Rewards Track, filled with timeless treasures like XP boosts, cards, Gold, card packs, and more!

## Pre-Release Tavern Brawl and Community Days

The *Across the Timeways* Pre-Release Tavern Brawl begins tomorrow, with Patch 34.0, and runs until expansion launch on November 4. Crack open your packs early, build a deck, and compete in a mini competition to win more Across the Timeways prizes!

![HS_32p4_CommunityActivations_InBlog_PreReleaseTavernBrawl_760x572_SW01.jpg](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_folder_media/73/73QBKB1MNBL71750972676052.jpg)

Everyone will get one free entry into the Pre-Release Tavern Brawl. Ater that, entries will cost 2 Tavern Tickets, 300 Gold, or 400 Runestones. In each run, you will lock in a deck and then play until you get 6 wins or 3 losses, and then get prizes based on how well you do.

![HS_33p6_CommunityEventsBlog_InBlog_PreReleaseBrawlChart_760x439_SW01.jpg](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/BGDJJU42UTK81760028397725.jpg)

The eligible card pool for the brawl will be:

- Across the Timeways
- The Lost City of Un’Goro
- Day of Rebirth Mini-Set
- Core Set
- Event Set
- Note: Dive the Golakka Depths will be banned.

See what futuristic decks your favorite creators are making and earn more packs to use in the Pre-Release Tavern Brawl during an extended 5-day Community Days event! See the dedicated [Community Events blog](https://news.blizzard.com/24241327) for more information about the Pre-Release Tavern Brawl and Community Days.

A new Achievement has been added for you to pursue during this Pre-Release Tavern Brawl.

- Timeways Pre-Release Champion
  - Finish a Pre‑Release Tavern Brawl run with 6 wins for the *Across the Timeways* expansion.
  - Rewards: 2x Tavern Tickets and 1x Across the Timeways Pack

## Hearthstone Updates

*Dev Comment: Well met! It’s been great to see all the ways you're playing the new cards from Across the Timeways ahead of the November 4 launch. Based on your feedback, we’ve made some updates in this patch to help the Rewind effect feel a little snappier. Mister Clocksworth has been a standout in this regard, so we’re removing one of his Rewinds to improve the overall experience of playing with or against him. While this slightly tones him down, we think the change will make games more enjoyable for everyone. We’re also continuing to explore further improvements to Rewind for a future update. Thanks for your input and we hope you have a good time with the new set!*

Mister Clocksworth

- Old: Rewind x3.
- New: Rewind x2.

## Arena Updates

On October 28, with Patch 34.0, all ongoing Arena runs will end and a new Arena season will begin. The eligible sets that will make up this Arena card pool are:

- Across the Timeways\*
- The Lost City
- Into the Emerald Dream
- The Great Dark Beyond
- Perils in Paradise
- Whizbang's Workshop

\* *Please note that Fabled cards are not currently included in Arena, but they will be added in a future update.*

With the new season comes a fresh reward for achieving 12 wins in Underground: the Old Ways Ulfar Hero Skin. This replaces the previous reward Hero Skin. After unlocking it, any subsequent 12-win runs will earn you an alternate prize of 2 Golden Across the Timeways Packs. When *Across the Timeways* releases on November 4, all Arena and Underground pack rewards will update from The Lost City of Un’Goro Packs to Across the Timeways Packs.

## Arfus Bounds Into Darkmoon Faire Treasures

![HS_34p0_Patch_Notes_InBlog_ArfusDarkmoonFaire_MM%20760.png](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/1W8EFV1SQVLR1760991445278.png)

The dog days are back... from the dead! Arfus has arrived in Hearthstone as a new interactive Pet, and he’s just dying to play with you. This ghostly good boy will be your loyal companion during matches, reacting to your gameplay with baleful barks and ghoulish growls. Throw him a bone to snack on or play fetch with Frostmourne (just don’t tell Arthas).

Level up Arfus to unlock three extra skins: Hound of Menethil, Putrid Pupper, and Helmed Hound. Pets earn XP when they see you do things they like, with each action granting 5 XP. Here’s what gets Arfus’ tail wagging:

- Feed Arfus a bone treat
- Play fetch with Arfus using Frostmourne
- Spend a corpse
- Play a Lich King card
- Play a frost spell
- Play a minion with two types
- Play an undead minion with 5+ Attack
- A friendly minion dies during your turn or shop phase
- Fill up your entire hand with cards
- Summon a Deathrattle minion
- Summon an Avenge minion
- Win a round of combat in Battlegrounds
- Triple an undead minion in Battlegrounds
- Play as the Lich King in Battlegrounds

Available from November 4 - 25, Arfus is one of 10 prizes in the returning Darkmoon Faire Treasures\*, a random pull system where the other prizes include Signature cards, Hero Skins, and more. Each pull unlocks a new treasure. [Read more details on Darkmoon Faire Treasures pricing and odds](https://support.blizzard.com/article/000380896).

\* *Darkmoon Faire Treasures is not available to players under the age of 18. Battle.net Account required to access.*

## Bug Fixes and Game Improvements

- [Hearthstone] Fixed a bug where Beast Speaker Taka did not trigger its Deathrattle effect after being resummoned.
- [Hearthstone] Fixed a bug where Hallucination was playable on a full board.
- [Hearthstone] Fixed a bug where Dissolving Ooze gave max stats instead of current stats.
- [Hearthstone] Fixed a bug where Opu the Unseen failed to resolve minion deaths between the Battlecry and Combo triggers.
- [Hearthstone] Fixed various visual and audio bugs related to Rewind.
- [Hearthstone] Fixed an audio bug where music was missing when Beast Speaker Taka was played.
- [Hearthstone] Fixed a visual bug where the King Rastakhan Hero Skin lacked a Hero Power icon animation.
- [Battlegrounds] Fixed a bug where Hot-Air Surveyor playing extra Blood Gems only triggered cards like Defiant Shipwright once.
- [Battlegrounds] Fixed a bug where the token in Putricide's Creation did not display correctly when stitched with Jelly Belly.
- [Battlegrounds] Fixed a bug where Friendly Bouncer did not summon minion if hand was full.
- [Pets] Fixed various visual bugs related to Rabbit’ath.
- [Adventure] Fixed a bug where the game sometimes crashed while playing Dalaran Heist.
