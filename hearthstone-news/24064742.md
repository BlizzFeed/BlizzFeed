# Announcing Battlegrounds Season 7

- URL: https://hearthstone.blizzard.com/en-gb/news/24064742/announcing-battlegrounds-season-7
![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/8b/8BP1JV14L8FL1711669942221.jpg)

> Grab a friend and join us for a new chapter of Battlegrounds in Once Upon a Tavern.

---

Once Upon a Tavern, there was a new Battlegrounds season, filled with updates for Traditional Battlegrounds and a magical new Mode, Battlegrounds Duos! Jump into the fray with new fairy-tale themed cards and rewards. Whether you’re playing on your own or with a partner, **a new chapter in Battlegrounds unfolds on April 16!**

![HS_29p2_BG_Pullout_Blog_InBlog_BGMarquee_Seasonal_760x650_SW01.jpg](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/FYZL25QH9J731711663782034.jpg)

With Season 7, Battlegrounds Ratings will reset and Battlegrounds Quests will rotate out of the Tavern. Tavern Spells will stay in the Tavern for both traditional Battlegrounds and Battlegrounds Duos as a regular part of Battlegrounds going forward.

#### Play Battlegrounds Duos

![HS_29p2_BG_Pullout_Blog_InBlog_BGMarquee_Duos_760x507_SW01.jpg](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/CVUK4BGJRLDX1711663782032.jpg)

Grab a friend or make a new one, Battlegrounds Duos is nearly here! We’ve been hard at work on Duos since we first shared it with you all at BlizzCon, balancing and polishing the mode.

The basics of Duos are the same as when we first announced it. You and a partner queue together in a game against three other teams of two players. Teams have shared life totals, pass each other cards through the Portal (for 1 Gold), and fight in tag-team combat to take the crown!

![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_folder_media/ZPUWZ5J71ZIW1698443831938.gif)

You can swap between solo Battlegrounds and Battlegrounds Duos on the main Battlegrounds menu. From the Duos menu, you can invite a friend to play as your teammate, or you can queue on your own to get paired with a random player and make a new friend! There will be separate ratings for solo Battlegrounds and Battlegrounds Duos, and separate leaderboards.

![HS_29p2_BG_Pullout_Blog_InBlog_MenuChange_GIF_760x428_SW01.gif](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/L7KB2H9WC9AS1711663782854.gif)

#### New Duos Heroes and Minions

With the launch of Duos, we’ve added some Duos-specific Heroes and cards that take advantage of the mode’s new gameplay: 4 heroes, 16 minions, and 3 tavern spells. These will only be available in Battlegrounds Duos, not the regular Battlegrounds mode. Some of these will look familiar to people who played Duos at BlizzCon, maybe with a few updates, and some of these are brand new!

#### Huge Updates Across Solo and Duos

Battlegrounds Duos isn’t the only story to tell Once Upon a Tavern. Season 7 includes a massive minion update for both Duos and solo Battlegrounds, including 50 new minions, over a dozen returning minions, and a couple new Tavern Spells. There are changes across the whole game, including a rework to Beasts, big refreshes to Dragons and Mechs, and balance changes to tons of minions and heroes! All these updates apply to all Battlegrounds games, traditional and Duos.

#### Battlegrounds Track Refresh

![HS_29p2_BG_Pullout_Blog_InBlog_First4_760x435_SW01.png](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/ANOA5EMPOLDJ1711663782257.png)

On April 16, Battlegrounds Season 6 will end and Season 7 will begin! At that point, players will switch over to begin earning awards on the new Battlegrounds Season 7 Rewards Track. Any rewards that were already earned in the Season 6 Rewards Track, but not yet claimed, will be automatically claimed for you.

The Season 7 Battlegrounds Track includes storied fairy tale themed cosmetics. Pick up the Season Pass to instantly unlock +2 Hero options at the start of each game. Then progress through the track to earn the Endless Fables Legendary Strike, 18 additional Hero Skins, a new Bartender, and more!
