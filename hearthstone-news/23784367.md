> It’s almost time for the first Rewards Track refresh of the next Hearthstone year!

---

It’s almost time to dive into a new Rewards Track! When the Rewards Track refreshes with the *Voyage to the Sunken City* launch, all players will automatically be granted any unclaimed rewards on both the free Rewards Track and the Tavern Pass track (if purchased), and a completely new *Voyage to the Sunken City* Rewards Track of unlockable treasure will take its place.

![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_folder_media/4PT6QIWUQA6T1648080313389.jpg)

*Fractured in Alterac Valley Achievements will stop awarding XP with the launch of Patch 23.0 on April 5 whereas the Rewards Track will swap over with the launch of Voyage to the Sunken City on April 12.*

#### Voyage to the Sunken City Rewards Track

The free Rewards Track for *Voyage to the Sunken City* will include packs from *Voyage to the Sunken City*, Standard packs, Tavern Tickets, a random Epic card, two random Legendary cards, a Murky Epic Mercenary Card, a Sunken City card back, and a lot of Gold! There are also 14 Golden cards\* and the Ambassador Faelin Legendary minion, from the set. For finishing the Rewards Track, you’ll get to choose from one of 10 returning Hero Skins.

\* *Two copies each of seven different cards from the set. The free Rewards Track copies of Ambassador Faelin and the 14 Golden cards are uncraftable and can’t be disenchanted.*

*Sir Finley, Sea Guide and Golden copies of Ambassador Faelin will eligible for a full dust refund until April 13, at 10 a.m. Pacific.*

#### Voyage to the Sunken City Tavern Pass

With the launch of *Voyage to the Sunken City,* a new Tavern Pass will be made available for purchase, offering a sea of brand-new cosmetic rewards and XP boosts.

![HS_RewardsTrack23p0_InBlog_TavernPassTrack_760x380_SW02.jpg](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/vg/VGE38GOL1X671648852104703.jpg)

Purchasing the Tavern Pass at any point during the expansion will immediately grant the Diamond Sir Finley, Sea Guide Legendary minion, a 10% XP Boost that lasts for the rest of the duration of the expansion, and all other items up to your current level on the paid track. As you progress with the Tavern Pass during the *Voyage to the Sunken City* expansion, you can earn additional XP Boosts, the uncraftable Golden Blademaster Okani Legendary minion, the Ambassador Faelin hero skin and card back, three Murky Mercenary Portraits, a new Battlegrounds Bartender, eight Battlegrounds hero skins, eleven Hearthstone hero skins, and a new cosmetic coin.

*As a reminder, Battlegrounds Perks are now available as a standalone purchase, separate from the Tavern Pass. The free Rewards Track, the paid Tavern Pass, and Battlegrounds Perks, if purchased, all last until the end of the Voyage to the Sunken City expansion cycle.*

#### Diamond Cards in Voyage to the Sunken City

Sir Finley, Sea Guide and Commander Sivara will be the set’s two Diamond cards. The unlock criteria for those cards are as follows:

![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/UHOC9GJ8YHJV1648139609095.png)![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/K1P6DUQ0C4C71648139609097.png)

- Sir Finley, Sea Guide—Instantly obtained by purchasing the Tavern Pass and claiming the card on the Rewards Track.
- Commander Sivara—Obtained by completing the *Voyage to the Sunken City* Legendary Collector Achievement (for owning 25 *Voyage to the Sunken City* Legendary cards).

#### Ka-Ching! Two New Cosmetic Coins

Two new Cosmetic Coins are being added with *Voyage to the Sunken City*, the Sunken Coin and the Zin-Azshari Coin. The Sunken Coin is obtained by completing the first 90 levels of the Rewards Track with the Tavern Pass. The Zin-Azshari Coin is obtained by collecting 135 unique *Voyage to the Sunken City* cards.

![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/GGFVIY2L4JJG1648139609093.png)![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/UUWGS0039TPA1648139609171.png)

Keep in mind that *Voyage to the Sunken City* will have a 35-card Mini-Set that will make it easier to reach both the 25 total Legendary cards for the Commander Sivara Diamond Legendary minion and the 135 total cards for the Zin-Azshari Coin. We’ll have more information to share about the *Voyage to the Sunken City* Mini-Set in the coming months.
