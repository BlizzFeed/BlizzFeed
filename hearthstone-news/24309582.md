# Reign of the Black Empire Rewards Track Refresh

- URL: https://hearthstone.blizzard.com/en-gb/news/24309582/reign-of-the-black-empire-rewards-track-refresh
![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/ri/RIBKKP6OJW5Y1791312498615.jpg)

> Collect cards, cosmetics and more from Reign of the Black Empire with the new Rewards Track!

---

The Old Gods’ influence is spreading across Azeroth, and the new ***Reign of the Black Empire* Rewards Track** is packed with twisted treasures inspired by their return! When the Rewards Track refreshes with the launch of *Reign of the Black Empire*, all players will automatically receive any unclaimed rewards from the current free Rewards Track and Tavern Pass track (if purchased) before a brand-new *Reign of the Black Empire* Rewards Track takes its place.

![HS_37p0_RewardsTrackRefresh_InBlog_RewardsTrack_760x846.jpg](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/3395CWV969JQ1791312460809.jpg)

## Reign of the Black Empire Rewards Track

The free Rewards Track for *Reign of the Black Empire* will include 14 Golden cards and the Archaedas Legendary card\*! Other goodies include a random Legendary card, random Epic card, Tavern Tickets, and a bounty of Gold and Packs.

\* *These 14 Golden cards (two copies of each of 7 cards from the set) and the Archaedas Legendary card earned on the Rewards Track can’t be crafted or disenchanted.*

## Reign of the Black Empire Tavern Pass

With the launch of *Reign of the Black Empire*, a new Tavern Pass arrives packed with cosmetic rewards, XP boosts, Signature cards, and expansion packs!

Getting the Tavern Pass at any time will immediately grant you a **10% XP Boost** that lasts for the rest of the *Reign of the Black Empire* expansion, plus all items up to your current level on the paid track. **Unlock your rewards even faster!**

As you progress along the Tavern Pass during the *Reign of the Black Empire* expansion, you can claim these rewards\*:

- Rajh Diamond Legendary card
- Ammunae Diamond Legendary card
- M.O.T.H.E.R Golden Legendary card
- Archaedas Golden Legendary card
- 2x Mogu Warmaster Golden cards
- 2x Assimilating Ooze Signature cards
- 2x OC91 Chariot Signature cards
- 2x Primal Rockfang Signature cards
- 1x new cosmetic coin
- 1x new card back
- 8x new Hero Skins
- 1x Golden Standard Pack
- 4x Reign of the Black Empire Packs
- Additional XP Boosts

\* *All Tavern Pass card grants are unable to be crafted or disenchanted.*

## New Cosmetic Coins

Two new cosmetic coins will be added with *Reign of the Black Empire*: the **Reign of the Black Empire Coin** and the **Chaos Coin**. The Reign of the Black Empire Coin is earned by reaching level 90 on the Rewards Track with the Tavern Pass. The Chaos Coin is earned by completing the "Bow Down Mortals” Feat by collecting 135 Reign of the Black Empire cards.
