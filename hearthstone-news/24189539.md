# Into the Emerald Dream is NOW LIVE!

- URL: https://news.blizzard.com/en-gb/article/24189539/into-the-emerald-dream-is-now-live
![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/pn/PNE5R2E2AEE61742254592946.jpg)

> It’s time to step Into the Emerald Dream. Hearthstone’s latest expansion is now live with Imbued Hero Powers, Darks Gifts, Wild Gods, and more!

---

Wake up! Into the Emerald Dream is now live with 145 new collectible cards, Imbued hero powers, Dark Gifts, Wild Gods, dragons, and more!

[![Video](https://markdown-videos-api.jorgenkh.no/youtube/h5DejvWyoAQ?width=1280&height=720)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=h5DejvWyoAQ)

#### What’s Included: New Keyword – Imbue

![PALADIN_EDR_264_enUS_AegisofLight-116675_NORMAL.png](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/VIWAHKQB2MYA1742254953522.png)![MAGE_EDR_519_enUS_Wisprider-114292_NORMAL.png](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/MSKXW7C8RPDO1742254952574.png)![NEUTRAL_EDR_800_enUS_FlutterwingGuardian-114006_NORMAL.png](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/EKXPMRF2KHKN1742254952999.png)![NEUTRAL_EDR_888_enUS_MalornetheWaywatcher-115186_NORMAL.png](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/6AUDH86VOMZK1742254953274.png)

Druid, Hunter, Mage, Paladin, Priest, and Shaman have rallied together to defend the Emerald Dream. They draw strength from the World Tree, which has imbued them with its power! The first time one of those classes plays a card with Imbue, they get a new Imbued Hero Power, specific to their class. Each subsequent time they Imbue, all the numbers in its text improve by 1, making them stronger throughout the game!

![MAGE_EDR_851p_enUS_BlessingoftheWisp-114056_NORMAL.png](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/54YUIP1Z2CCQ1742254952631.png)![MAGE_EDR_851p_enUS_BlessingoftheWisp-114056_NORMAL_5.png](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/O39YHIUDXUD61742254952784.png)

#### What’s Included: New Keyword – Dark Gifts

The Old Gods have brought corruption to the Emerald Dream in hopes of turning it into a Nightmare! They offer Dark Gifts to their supporters as tempting offers of power. Dark Gifts are modifiers that are applied to certain Discover effects; there are 10 different Dark Gifts, each of which gives a boost to your Discover options.

#### What’s Included: Legendary Wild Gods

Wild Gods are massive, ancient beings that can turn the tide of battle! Each class gets one Legendary Wild God to support their cause.

#### What’s Included: Expanded Keyword – Choose One

The Emerald Dream is the realm from which Druidic magic flows. Here, each class gets a taste of that magic with their own Choose One card—available for the first time outside of the Druid class!
