# Escape from Violet Hold Rewards Track Refresh

- URL: https://news.blizzard.com/en-gb/article/24286285/escape-from-violet-hold-rewards-track-refresh
![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/ja/JA5NCQ0U5FMN1782257054945.jpg)

> Collect cards, cosmetics and more from Escape from Violet Hold with the new Rewards Track!

---

Escaping from Dalaran’s maximum security prison isn’t going to be easy, so pick up some handy *Escape from Violet Hold* goodies on the new Rewards Track! When the Rewards Track refreshes with the launch of *Escape from Violet Hold*, all players will automatically be granted any unclaimed rewards on the current free Rewards Track and the Tavern Pass track (if acquired), before a completely new *Escape from Violet Hold* Rewards Track takes its place.

![HS_36p0_RewardsTrackRefresh_InBlog_RewardsTrack_760x846_SW01.jpg](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/an/ANN22QYGLZ241782235996641.jpg)

## Free Rewards on the *Escape from Violet* *Hold* Track

The free Rewards Track for *Escape from Violet Hold* will include 14 Golden cards and the **Warden Maiev Legendary card**\*! Other goodies include a random Legendary card, random Epic card, Tavern Tickets, and a bounty of Gold and Packs.

*\* These 14 Golden cards (two copies of each of 7 cards from the set) and the Warden Maiev Legendary card earned on the Rewards Track can’t be crafted or disenchanted.*

## Upgrade to the *Escape from Violet Hold* Tavern Pass for More Epic Rewards

With the launch of *Escape from Violet Hold*, a new Tavern Pass arrives packed with expansion packs, XP boosts, cosmetic rewards, and Signature cards!

Getting the Tavern Pass at any time will immediately grant you a **10% XP Boost** that lasts for the rest of the *Escape from Violet Hold* expansion, plus all items up to your current level on the paid track. **Unlock your rewards even faster!**

As you progress along the Tavern Pass during the *Escape from Violet Hold* expansion, you can claim these rewards\*:

- Additional XP Boosts
- 4x Escape from Violet Hold Packs
- 2x Golden Standard Packs
- R4T-C4TCH3R Diamond Legendary card
- Vanessa the Ringleader Golden Legendary card
- Warden Maiev Golden Legendary card
- 2x Getaway Hogdriver Golden Epic cards
- 2x Black Market Auctioneer Signature Epic cards
- 2x Defias Smuggler Signature Common cards
- 2x Soothsayer Signature Rare cards
- 1x new cosmetic coin
- 1x new card back
- 11x new Hero Skins

\* *All Tavern Pass card grants are unable to be crafted or disenchanted.*

## New Cosmetic Coins

Two new cosmetic coins will be added with *Escape from Violet Hold*: the Dalaran Coin and the Contraband Coin. The Dalaran Coin is earned by reaching level 90 on the Rewards Track with the Tavern Pass. The Contraband Coin is earned by completing the “I'm Putting Together a Team" Achievement by collecting 135 *Escape from Violet Hold* cards.
