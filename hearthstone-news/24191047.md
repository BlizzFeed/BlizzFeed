Patch 32.0.3 brings balance changes to Hearthstone and Battlegrounds, and more!

### Hearthstone Updates

*Dev Comment: These changes are a continuation of the changes we made in Patch 32.0 to remove unfun play patterns and give Into the Emerald Dream the best chance to shine. Many of these changes are to cards and strategies that are bad experiences to play against even if they’re not overly powerful. We’re also adjusting a few early power outliers—including buffs! We don’t normally do balance changes this early after an expansion, but we thought making a few changes now would best align our goals for the set with our patch schedule.*

### Battlegrounds Updates

*Dev Comment: This is expected to be our last major balance adjustment to Battlegrounds before the next season. We’re overall happy with how Anomalies were received this season, and most of our adjustments to them this patch are focused on removing a few pain points with specific Anomalies. On the minion side, our aim this patch is to lower the power of Demons and Naga and buff Dragons and Mechs. We also decided to remove Captain Bonerender from the minion pool early, for sentiment reasons. Keep an eye out, we’ll be sharing news about the next Battlegrounds season soon!*

#### Anomaly Pool Updates

- Prudence of Amitus has been removed from the Anomaly Pool.
- Secrets of Norgannon and Cosmic Duality now appear less often.
- Guff Runetotem has been banned from the Secrets of Norgannon Anomaly.
- Pyramad has been banned from the Match Fixing, Golden Friendship, Emergency Landing, and Pooled Resources Anomalies.
- Saloon’s Finest (Tier 5 Tavern Spell) has been banned from the Golganneth’s Tempest Anomaly.
- The following heroes have been banned from being the second hero power option with the Cosmic Duality Anomaly: Lord Jaraxxus, Overlord Saurfang, The Jailer, Professor Putricide, Ragnaros, Tavish Stormpike, Cariel Roame, Sindragosa, Millificent Manastorm, Vanndar Stormpike, Rokara, and Sylvanas Windrunner.

### Arena Updates

Arena draft rates have been adjusted so that the following cards appear less frequently:

- Spirit Gatherer
- Aspect’s Embrace
- Fleeing Treant (in Mage, Priest, and Shaman)
- Rite of Atrocity

### Bug Fixes and Game Improvements

- [Hearthstone] Fixed a bug where Shaladrassil could get activated by your opponent playing cards that cost more than it.
- [Hearthstone] Fixed a bug where the dragon in the Horrible Egg (from Clutch of Corruption) would change if the source dragon was transformed.
- [Hearthstone] Fixed a bug where Doomkin’s stats weren’t reverted when its effect was reverted.
- [Hearthstone] Updated Merry Moonkin’s text and effect so that it gains all the Armor at once, instead of over multiple activations.
- [Hearthstone] Fixed a visual bug with Shaladrassil’s history tile.
- [Collection] Fixed a bug where Signature Nightmare Lord Xavius couldn’t be disenchanted.
- [Collection] Fixed a bug where Signature Gorgonzormu disenchanted for the wrong dust amount when eligible for a full dust refund. Due to this miss during last refund window, Signature Gorgonzormu has been added to this patch’s refund period.
