## Hearthstone Updates

### Standard Card Changes

*Dev Comment: Well met! With* CATACLYSM *now live and Standard freshly rotated, we’ve been closely watching the new Standard environment take shape as you experiment with a wide range of decks and strategies. With the meta still evolving, this patch focuses on reining in a few clear outliers and giving a small boost to some budding archetypes. As always, we will be keeping a close eye on how these changes land and look forward to seeing how the playing field continues to develop.*

## Battlegrounds Updates

*Dev Comment: This marks our final major balance patch of the season. We’re happy with where the meta and the Timewarped Tavern have landed overall, so we took a lighter approach this time around.*

- *We’ve toned down Metal Dispenser and Silky Shimmermoth, which have been overperforming, while offering modest buffs to Dragons, Pirates, Murlocs, and Elementals to help keep things fresh.*
- *For Timewarped cards, our focus was on improving or removing underperformers so your shop offerings stay fun and interesting.*
- *We brought back the Money Match and Golganneth’s Tempest Anomalies after hearing how much these fan favorites were missed.*

*With the next season just around the corner, we’re excited to share more details with you soon. We’ll see you in the Tavern!*
