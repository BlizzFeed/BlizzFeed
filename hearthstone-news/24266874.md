# 35.4 Patch Notes

- URL: https://news.blizzard.com/en-gb/article/24266874/35-4-patch-notes
![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/18/189RICBMS0IK1777312874659.jpg)

> This patch features the debut of Class Sets, the return of Dual Class Arena, new event rewards, and more!

---

Patch 35.4 launches tomorrow, bringing the debut of Class Sets in *Restoration of Azeroth*. This update also includes a new Arena season, new events, and some bug fixes.

## Restoring Azeroth, One Class at a Time

![HS_35p4_ClassSetAnnounce_InBlog_ClassSetGolden_DB_760x428_SW01.jpg](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/UD0D68AOIYXE1776809524288.jpg)

Deathwing devastated Azeroth, and now four classes are stepping up to heal what was broken. Introducing: Class Sets! **Druid, Hunter, Paladin, and Mage** each receive a *Restoration of Azeroth* Class Set, a focused release of new cards built entirely around a single class. Class Sets roll out in three waves over the course of the year, with four classes per wave, one after each major expansion. (Check out our [in-depth Class Sets blog](https://hearthstone.blizzard.com/news/24267730) for more details!)

Each *Restoration of Azeroth* Class Set includes seven unique cards: **1 Legendary, 2 Epics, 2 Rares, and 2 Commons**.

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/zc/ZCCE69ODAUJC1777398776890.jpg)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/zc/ZCCE69ODAUJC1777398776890.jpg)

*To view the image at full size, click the image above or [follow this link](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/zc/ZCCE69ODAUJC1777398776890.jpg).*

You can find these cards in *CATACLYSM* packs, craft them with dust, or purchase them directly. Buy an individual Class Set for one class, or grab the bundle to unlock all four Class Sets, totaling 28 new unique cards, plus a bonus Cultivating Sprite Signature Epic card and the Volcanic Krush Lite Hero Skin.

- Individual **Restoration of Azeroth Class Set** (Druid, Hunter, Paladin, or Mage)
  - Available for $9.99 USD or 1,200 Gold.
- **Restoration of Azeroth Class Sets Bundle**
  - Includes all four Class Sets, two copies of the Cultivating Sprite bonus card, and the Volcanic Krush Lite Hero Skin.
  - Available for $19.99 USD or 2,500 Gold.
- **Golden Restoration of Azeroth Class Sets Bundle**
  - Includes all cards in Golden form, two copies of the Cultivating Sprite Signature card, and the Volcanic Krush Lite Hero Skin.
  - Available for $79.99 USD or 12,000 Gold.

*Available purchase options vary by region.*

Check out the full list of *Restoration of Azeroth* cards in the [Official Hearthstone Card Llibrary](https://hearthstone.blizzard.com/cards/).

## Arena Updates

### Dual Class Arena Returns for a New Season

This season brings back Dual Class Arena! You'll choose two classes, then draft cards from both to create new combinations and strategies. With Patch 35.4, all ongoing Arena runs will end, and the new Arena season will begin.

#### Fully Curated Card Pool

This season features a fully curated card pool, hand-picked with Dual Class play in mind.

#### Updated Arena UI and Draft Experience

With Dual Class Arena returning, the UI has been updated to better support the experience. Both chosen classes are now displayed under your name during matches, Class Banners appear during the draft, and Hero Powers are offered first to clearly signal that you are drafting in a Dual Class season.

Step into the role of the Great Red Leviathan and face your foes with fully custom animations, from descending onto the board with a burst of red magic to rising from the Hero Portrait to deliver a magical dragon slash.
