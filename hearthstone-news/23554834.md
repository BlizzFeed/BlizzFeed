# The Masters Tour Online: Madrid Champion Has Been Crowned!

- URL: https://hearthstone.blizzard.com/en-gb/news/23554834/the-masters-tour-online-madrid-champion-has-been-crowned
![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/oy/OYEX12NM6S4S1603991535069.jpg)

> The Masters Tour Online: Madrid has concluded with the first Chinese Masters Tour champion!

---

Zheng “iGXc” Qianhao is the Masters Tour Online: Madrid champion! In the final Masters Tour event of 2020, with 304 players from more than 40 countries, iGXc secured the championship following an intense 3-1 final against Asia-Pacific Grandmaster Yashima “[Alutemu](https://twitter.com/Alutemu)” Nozomi. He is the first Chinese player to win a Masters Tour, earning the Masters Tour Online: Madrid trophy, and $32,500 in champion prizing!

![iGXc decks.PNG](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/gallery/en/EN2ESAFS14TF1603664734999.PNG)

With the relegation of nine Grandmasters following Grandmasters 2020 Season 2, as well as the retirement of Americas Grandmaster Fei “[ETC](https://twitter.com/etc_hs0?lang=en)” Liang, Masters Tour Online: Madrid was the last chance for players aiming to earn enough to advance to Grandmasters. The top three players from Asia-Pacific and Europe, and top four from Americas, based on total winnings from all six Master Tours in 2020 have earned invites to join the top-tier of competitive Hearthstone. Two invitees, David “[justsaiyan](https://twitter.com/justsaiyanhs)” Shan and Enzo “[Warma](https://twitter.com/Warma_HS)” Flock, were themselves relegated following Grandmasters 2020 Season 2 and have earned their shot at redemption!
