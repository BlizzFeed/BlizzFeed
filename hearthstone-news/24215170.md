### The Lost City of Un’Goro Tavern Pass

With the launch of the Lost City of Un’Goro, a new Tavern Pass will be available with new cosmetic rewards, XP Boosts, and expansion packs!

![HS_33p0_RewardsTrack_InBlog_TavernPass_MM_760x428_SW01.jpg](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_folder_media/rn/RNKKK4P3WYO01750975899594.jpg)

Getting the Tavern Pass at any time will immediately grant you a 10% XP Boost that lasts for the rest of the Lost City of Un’Goro expansion—plus all items up to your current level on the paid track!

As you progress along the Tavern Pass during the Lost City of Un’Goro expansion, you can unearth these rewards\*:

- Additional XP Boosts
- Elise the Navigator Diamond Legendary card
- 2 Ravenous Devilsaur Golden Epic cards
- 2 Supreme Dinomancy Signature Epic cards
- 2 Ambush Predators Signature Rare cards
- 2 Bonechill Stegodon Signature Common cards
- 1 new cosmetic coin
- 1 new card back
- 11 new Hero Skins
- 2 Golden Standard Packs
- 4 Lost City of Un’Goro Packs

*\* All Tavern Pass card grants are unable to be crafted or disenchanted.*

### New Cosmetic Coins

Two new cosmetic coins will be added with the Lost City of Un’Goro: the Tortollan Coin and the Un'Goro Coin. The Tortoallan Coin is earned by reaching level 90 on the Rewards Track with the Tavern Pass. The Un'Goro Coin is earned by completing the Achievement for collecting 145 Lost City of Un’Goro cards.

![NEUTRAL_TLC_COIN1_enUS_TheCoin-122529_NORMAL.png](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_folder_media/q7/Q7AC8KEZRX031750976053564.png)![NEUTRAL_TLC_COIN2_enUS_TheCoin-122530_NORMAL.png](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_folder_media/4j/4J41RR1NYSDT1750976073746.png)

The Lost City of Un’Goro will have a Mini-Set, coming in Patch 33.4, that will make it easier to complete the collection-based Achievements. We’ll share more information about that Mini-Set before it goes live.
