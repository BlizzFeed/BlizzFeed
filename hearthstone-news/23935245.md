# The Masters Tour Spring Championship is Here!

- URL: https://hearthstone.blizzard.com/en-gb/news/23935245/the-masters-tour-spring-championship-is-here
![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/x6/X69IW9JU9IET1681770217428.png)

> The first Masters Tour of the year takes the stage, live on Twitch!

---

The first Masters Tour of the year is this weekend, with players competing on the newest expansion, *Festival of Legends*! Sixteen of the top players from the January, February, and March Ladders will face off for their share of the $50,000 (USD) prize pool and an invite to the 2023 World Championship. Read on for everything you need to know to enjoy the action, only on [Twitch](https://www.twitch.tv/playhearthstone) and [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCVia_crjzJylRmGq7SHTiaw).

### What You Need to Know

- **Dates**: Saturday, April 22 - Sunday, April 23 at 9 am PDT
- **Format**: 4-deck, best-of-5 Conquest with a ban
- **Prize Pool**: $50,000 (USD)
- **Drops**: Earn up to two *Festival of Legends* packs while watching on Twitch all weekend.
- **Casters**:[Raven](https://twitter.com/RavenCasts), [Sottle](https://twitter.com/coL_Sottle), [Edelweiss](https://twitter.com/SwagoiEdelweiss), [TJ](https://twitter.com/Azumoqt), and [Lorinda](https://twitter.com/LorindaGames?ref_src=twsrc%5Egoogle%7Ctwcamp%5Eserp%7Ctwgr%5Eauthor)
- **Co-Streaming**: If you are interested in co-streaming future Hearthstone Esports events, live or during off-broadcast times more conducive to your local region, be sure to apply [here](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSdPWAFj9F5fSAdnGlN9sxkivINRuNYWzZyXgDtsUnxOgu4jUQ/viewform?usp=sf_link).
