## Upgraded Signature Card Features

Signature Cards have always been about celebrating Hearthstone’s art and adding a personal touch to your collection. We’ve heard your feedback on the upgrades you’d like to see, and now we’re rolling out some of those improvements along with a few ideas we think you’ll love. The biggest change? Card text now appears directly on the card instead of hiding in a tooltip that, let’s be honest, wasn’t always easy to read. You’ll also have the option to choose how the text appears or remove it entirely. Whatever feels right for you!

![HS_34p2_Patch_Notes_InBlog_Signature_Collection_Comparison_760x448_DB02.gif](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/936INYASFRIJ1764704029487.gif)

Read on for all the details about the new Signature Card experience.

### Customization Options

Head to **Settings → Options** and select your preference:

- **Never Show Text**: Doesn’t show any card text. Enjoy the full art!
- **Favor Art**: Displays cards with full artwork, showing text only when necessary. You can toggle text on or off where available.
- **Favor Text** (default): Displays cards with text, switching to full artwork view when appropriate. You can toggle text on or off where available.
- **Always Show Text**: Full card text is always shown. Ideal for streaming, Esports, and always knowing what the card does at a glance.

![HS_34p2_Patch_Notes_InBlog_Signature_Collection_Tyrande_760x522_DB01.gif](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/FQYY7QEXTEMG1764704043187.gif)

### Enhanced Readability

By moving Signature card text to be at the same position as on other cards, they are now easier to read and scan when looking at cards in your hand.

![HS_34p2_Patch_Notes_InBlog_Signature_Card_Play_760xx522_DB01.gif](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/JNNX9YBMEGNU1764704041118.gif)

### Improved Presentation Across Hearthstone

We’ve not only enhanced the text on Signature cards but also improved the overall presentation of Signature cards across Hearthstone. From the Collection Manager to Arena, Signature cards now feature a cleaner, more elegant layout for easier reading. For example, cards with related counterparts (such as tokens or Fabled cards) now display their text clearly in the Collection Manager.

![HS_34p2_Patch_Notes_InBlog_Signature_Collection_Rafaams_760x522_DB01.gif](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/Q9DCVSTUMD6D1764704030786.gif)

## Tavern Brawl

## Darkmoon Faire Treasures

The Darkmoon Faire Treasures booth is rolling out three new prize collections in the coming weeks, containing fantastical cosmetics inspired by Turalyon, Rafaam, Yogg‑Saron, and plenty more.

Darkmoon Faire Treasures is our random pull system that rewards Pets, Mythic Skins, Signature cards, Hero Skins, and more. The first pull is free, and subsequent pulls require Runestones to purchase. As the prize collections in the Treasures booth rotate, prices adjust to match the value of the current items. [Read more about the current value of Darkmoon Faire Treasures, as well as pull rates and odds](https://support.blizzard.com/article/000380896).
