#### United in Stormwind Rewards Track

The Rewards Track for *United in Stormwind* will include packs from *United in Stormwind*, Standard packs, the Stormwind Card Back, Tavern Tickets, a random Epic card, two random Legendary cards, and a lot of Gold. There are also 14 uncraftable Golden cards, and the uncraftable Legendary minion Lady Prestor. For finishing the track, you’ll get to choose from one of 10 returning Hero Skins.

#### Diamond Cards

Varian, King of Stormwind and Archbishop Benedictus will get Diamond treatment in *United in Stormwind.* The unlock criteria for those cards are as follows:

- Varian, King of Stormwind—Instantly obtainable by purchasing the Tavern Pass and claiming the card on the Rewards track.
- Archbishop Benedictus—Obtained by completing the *United in Stormwind* Legendary Collector 5/5 Achievement (for collecting a total of 25 *United in Stormwind* Legendary cards).

#### United in Stormwind Tavern Pass

The *Forged in the Barrens* Tavern Pass will expire with the launch of *United in Stormwind* and a new Tavern Pass will be made available for purchase—offering a host of brand-new cosmetic rewards and XP boosts.

![HS_RewardsTrack21p0_PaidTrack_760x310_2.png](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/qf/QFZDQ53Z0Q3T1626978753270.png)

Purchasing the Tavern Pass at any point during the expansion will immediately grant the Diamond Varian, King of Stormwind Legendary minion, a 10% XP Boost that lasts for the rest of the duration of the expansion, and all items up to your current level on the paid track. As you progress with the Tavern Pass during the *United in Stormwind* expansion, you’ll earn additional XP Boosts, the uncraftable Golden Legendary Flightmaster Dungar, the Yrel Card Back and Alternate Hero, and an all-new cosmetic coin. You’ll also get up to four new skins for *each* of Scabbs the Rogue, Guff the Druid, and Tamsin the Warlock.

*As a reminder, Battlegrounds Perks are now available as a standalone purchase, separate from the Tavern Pass.*

#### New Cosmetic Coins

Two new Cosmetic Coins are being added with *United in Stormwind*, the Stormwind coin and the Lordaeron coin. The Stormwind coin is obtainable by completing the first 90 levels of the Rewards Track with the Tavern Pass. The Lordaeron coin is obtainable by collecting 135 unique *United in Stormwind* cards.

![NEUTRAL_SW_COIN1_enUS_TheCoin-73367_NORMAL.png](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/2d/2DG3MUTKZRL91626978777016.png)![NEUTRAL_SW_COIN2_enUS_TheCoin-73368_NORMAL.png](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/t6/T66JPLHPH1641626978800044.png)

Keep in mind that **United in Stormwind will have a 35-card Mini-Set** that will make it easier to reach both the 25 total *United in Stormwind* Legendary cards for the Archbishop Benedictus Diamond card reward and the 135 total *United in Stormwind* cards for the Lordaeron Cosmetic Coin. We’ll have more information to share about the *United in Stormwind* Mini-Set in the coming months.
