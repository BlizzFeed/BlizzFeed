Grab your gear! The Lost City of Un’Goro is now live with 145 new collectible cards, the powerful new Kindred keyword, the return of Quests, Maps, Stories, Tortollans, dinosaurs, and more!

### What’s Included: New Keyword – Kindred

Those brave enough to venture into the Lost City of Un’Goro have uncovered a primal bond unlike any other: Kindred. This new keyword empowers those who walk in harmony with their ancestral spirits. Play a card of the same minion type or spell school as your last turn, and Kindred grants a powerful bonus effect. In Un’Goro, survival favors the prepared, so plan your plays wisely and harness the power of Kindred!

### What’s Included: Map Cards

Every great expedition starts with a map—just make sure you don’t confuse a coffee stain for a landmark, otherwise you may wind up in a volcano! In the Lost City of Un’Goro, Map cards help you chart your path to victory. Each Map lets you Discover a card, and if you play that card on the same turn, you’ll get to Discover again from the remaining options. With six unique Maps in the set, smart explorers can chain their choices and uncover powerful treasures buried deep in the jungle.
