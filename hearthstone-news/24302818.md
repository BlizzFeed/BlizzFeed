## Theorycrafting: A New Take on a Familiar Event

The Old Gods' corruption is spreading everywhere, and its reach knows no bounds! Theorycrafting has been infused with their dark power, expanding from a single day into nearly a full week of early access content. Join your favorite creators as they experiment with new decks, discover powerful interactions, and put *Reign of the Black Empire* through its paces before launch.

### Theorycrafting: Devs vs. Streamers

The battle for the Black Empire isn't limited to Azeroth.

During Theorycrafting, Team Dev and Team Streamer will clash for glory, bragging rights, and rewards. Every five victories earned by Team Streamer will unlock surprise rewards for randomly selected Theorycrafting participants to share with their community, so every win counts.

To make sure more players can join the action, this year's Theorycrafting event spans multiple regions and multiple days. Participating creators will stream from their own channels throughout the event, while our developers enter the fray during the opening play session.

Check the schedule below to see when your favorite creators will be live, then tune in to watch new decks emerge, mind-bending combos take shape, and the first *Reign of the Black Empire* experiments unfold.

Drops enabled! Watch any stream in the Hearthstone category to earn rewards.

- NA/LATAM/EMEA players will play on October 6 (8:00 a.m. PDT – 1:00 p.m. PDT) and on October 7 (12:00 p.m. PDT – 6:00 p.m. PDT)
- APAC players will play on October 7 (2:00 a.m. PDT – 8:00 a.m. PDT)
- CN players will play on October 8-9 (4:00 p.m. PDT – 8:00 a.m. PDT)

*Note: Theorycrafting participants will stream on their own channels.*

## Community Days and Pre-Release Tavern Brawl

### Community Days

The Black Empire's influence is still spreading!

From October 13 at 10:00 a.m. PDT - October 17 at 10:00 a.m. PDT, participate in Hearthstone Community Days and celebrate the discoveries, decks, and *Reign of the Black Empire*. Tune into any participating Hearthstone stream to earn up to 3x Standard Packs and 2x Tavern Tickets just for watching.

The whispers are calling. Will you answer?

### Pre-Release Tavern Brawl

Crack open the *Reign of the Black Empire* packs you’ve been earning, then jump into the Pre-Release Tavern Brawl where only *Reign of the Black Empire* cards are allowed. All *Reign of the Black Empire* Common and Rare cards available as free Trial Cards\* during the event. Choose from predefined multi-class combinations before constructing your deck. Most options combine three classes, while one special two-class combination offers enhanced C'Thun synergies.

- When: October 13, 10:00 a.m. PDT - October 20, 10:00 a.m. PDT

\* *Please note these* Reign of the Black Empire *trial Common and Rare Cards are available to play for this event only and not permanently added to your collection. The cards can be obtained for your collection by opening* Reign of the Black Empire *packs.*
