> Hearthstone’s newest expansion is now live worldwide!

---

Grab your coat and your faction’s tabard, adventurers, because [*Fractured in Alterac Valley* is now live worldwide](https://playhearthstone.com/expansions-adventures/fractured-in-alterac-valley/) with [135 new collectible cards, the return of Hero cards, the new Honorable Kill keyword, Objectives, and more!](https://playhearthstone.com/cards?set=fractured-in-alterac-valley)

#### What’s Included: Honorable Kill

Cards with the new Honorable Kill keyword get a bonus effect when they deal exact lethal damage to an enemy character on your turn.

![WARRIOR_AV_565_enUS_AxeBerserker-73459_NORMAL.png](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/mb/MBD4QN77ALSU1638315856139.png)![PALADIN_AV_340_enUS_Brasswing-70261_NORMAL.png](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/ep/EPI8AL1HUQP21638315868281.png)

#### What’s Included: Hero Cards

All 10 classes have access to a Legendary Hero card. Hero cards are cards that change your hero for the rest of the game. These hero cards all have powerful Battlecries and new hero powers that change the way you play!

![PRIEST_AV_207_enUS_XyrellatheDevout-67048_NORMAL.png](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/sy/SYRFSOH0G7LR1638315886880.png)![HUNTER_AV_113_enUS_BeaststalkerTavish-67803_NORMAL.png](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/s8/S8FQW4FOHJY51638315901100.png)

#### What’s Included: Objectives

Objectives are new spell cards with effects that last for three turns.

![MAGE_AV_290_enUS_IcebloodTower-70204_NORMAL.png](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/iw/IWYJNH41L57V1638315923369.png)![WARLOCK_AV_657_enUS_DesecratedGraveyard-72440_NORMAL.png](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/et/ETZ0C5R15UMA1638315935083.png)
