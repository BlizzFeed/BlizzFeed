Patch 29.4, launching tomorrow, brings an explosive new mini-set, Dr. Boom’s Incredible Inventions, and more!

#### Incredible New Inventions!

![HS_29p4_Patch_Notes_InBlog_GoldenMiniSet_SP_760x428_SW01Artboard%201.jpg](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/8QW9Z51ISZP91714599282837.jpg)

Dr. Boom’s tired of everyone calling Whizbang the world’s greatest inventor. So he kept tinkering away in the basement until he came up with something bombastic, something Gigantic, something... INCREDIBLE!

The Dr. Boom’s Incredible Inventions Mini-Set has 38 all-new cards. Get them in Whizbang’s Workshop packs or as a complete 72-card\* Mini-Set. Get the normal version for $14.99 or 2000 Gold, or get the all-Golden version for $69.99 or 10,000 Gold. The all-Golden version comes with a bonus Diamond copy of the Puppetmaster Dorian Legendary minion! You can see all the cards from Dr. Boom’s Incredible Inventions Mini-Set in the official [Card Library](https://hearthstone.blizzard.com/cards).

*\* 4 Legendary cards, 1 Epic card (x2), 17 Rare cards (x2), and 16 Common cards (x2).*

#### Gigantify Your Fun!

Dr. Boom’s Incredible Inventions include a new keyword: Gigantify! Gigantify is like Miniaturize, but bigger: whenever you play a card with Gigantify, you get an 8-mana, 8/8 Gigantic version added to your hand. That’s huge!

#### Hearthstone Balance Changes

*Dev Comment: Our [last balance update](https://hearthstone.blizzard.com/news/24087317/29-2-2-patch-notes) was a massive shakeup with over 35 changes, based on our general design philosophy and overall health of Standard. With so many changes at once, we built this extra balance window into this content patch to catch a couple power and playrate outliers that have cropped up since those changes. We still have our regular hotfix balance window planned for a little after this patch, once players have had a chance to play with the new mini-set cards and explore the new meta.*

#### Arena Updates

On May 14, all current Arena runs will end and a new Arena season will begin. The Arena rules and draft pools will remain the same, except that cards from Dr. Boom’s Incredible Inventions will be added to the pool. However, the random generation and Discover pools will be updated to only include cards from Ashes of Outland onward.

#### In-Game Event: Dr. Boom’s Aisle of Mischievous Toys

Dr. Boom’s inventions are, of course, incredible, but one or two of them have been known to... have a little extra boom. From May 21 to June 11, complete Event Quests to earn Event XP on the special event rewards track. Complete the rewards track to earn 6 Whizbang’s Workshop packs and the Thunder Ape Shaman Hero Skin.

#### Bug Fixes and Game Improvements

- *Dev Comment: As we continue iterating on our progression system, and our communications around it, we’re working on some updates that didn’t quite make it into this patch. Our next Weekly Quest update is planned for the next scheduled client patch, Patch 29.4.2.*
- [Hearthstone] Updated Photographer Fizzle to now load his Snapshot in existing hand order instead of timestamp order (meaning, for example, Mini cards will appear where they are in your hand when the Snapshot is taken, even though they were added to your hand later than other cards).
- [Hearthstone] Updated the order of Discover options in Illidan’s Gift to match the order on the card’s text.
- [Hearthstone] Fixed a bug where The Headless Horseman unfroze upon drawing Horseman’s Head (finding your head is nice, but your body’s still on ice).
- [Hearthstone] Fixed a bug where Open the Cages would not trigger off Observer of Mysteries before disappearing.
- [Hearthstone] Fixed a visual bug on some mobile devices where the enemy Secret zone overlapped and was difficult to see with hover. Fixed another visual bug on some mobile devices where the friendly Secret Zone couldn’t be hovered to see at all.
- [Hearthstone] Fixed a bug where Dr. Stitchensew could cause disconnects in rare circumstances.
- [Hearthstone] Fixed a bug where a Spell Damage Chia Drake wouldn’t have the Spell Damage effect when control was swapped or a copy was summoned.
- [Hearthstone] Fixed a bug where a Cosplay Contestant cosplaying as Magtheridon, Unreleased would trigger its Dormant effect twice each turn. That’s not canon!
- [Hearthstone] Fixed a bug where Fairy Tale Slime didn’t update its text from the opponent’s perspective.
- [Hearthstone] Fixed a bug where Spirit of the Badlands’s Mirage can create an extra, fake Mirage in hand—that was a bit too immersive.
- [Hearthstone] Fixed a bug where Crimson Expanse wouldn’t create an exact copy under certain circumstances.
- [Hearthstone] Fixed a bug where Felscale Evoker’s effect sometimes wasn’t tracking properly.
- [Battlegrounds] Fixed a bug where Battlegrounds Ratings were showing as stuck on 0 on tablet devices.
- [Battlegrounds] Fixed a bug where tripling Marine Matriarch with Elemental of Surprise would turn off Marine Matriarch’s effect.
- [Battlegrounds] Fixed a bug where Slimy Felblood’s effect also applied to your Partner’s Tavern when activated in combat.
- [Battlegrounds] Fixed a bug where passing a minion to your partner while they were in the Choose One interface soft locked the client.
- [Battlegrounds] Fixed a bug where passing a minion to your partner while they are holding another copy of that minion, and the passed card forms a triple, blocks all functionality until the newly tripled card is played.
- [Battlegrounds] Fixed a bug where minions affected by Suspicious Stimulant couldn’t be passed from hand.
- [Achievements] Fixed a bug where the Tick, Tick, Boom! Achievement didn’t track properly after Boomboss Tho’grun’s effect was updated.
- [Cosmetics] Fixed a bug where Favorited Coins weren’t showing for Coins generated in-game.
- [Cosmetics] Fixed a bug with Halveria Darkraven’s Hero Power animation.
- [Cosmetics] Corrected a few Battlegrounds Hero skins that had the wrong rarity listed in the Collection.
- [Shop] Fixed a bug where Mac desktop clients couldn’t access the Shop.
- [???] Updated a secret puzzle to account for the new Core Set update.
- Misc. other bug fixes and game improvements.
