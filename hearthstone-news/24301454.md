## Introducing Tavern Rewards

![HS_37p0_Patch_Notes_InBlog_TavernTenders_760x428_SW01.jpg](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/6N19KGMYJB6Z1791309924816.jpg)

**Tavern Rewards** are a new way to get even more from your visits to the [Hearthstone Battle.net Shop](https://us.shop.battle.net/family/hearthstone).

The Hearthstone Battle.net Shop is already the place to pick up packs, bundles, cosmetics, Tavern Passes, and more. With Tavern Rewards, eligible purchases can also help you earn **Tavern Tender** and unlock even more Hearthstone rewards.

Starting October 20, you'll be able to unlock bonus Hearthstone rewards simply by purchasing eligible items in the Hearthstone Battle.net Shop. Whether you're looking to grow your collection, snag a new cosmetic, or save up for something special, Tavern Rewards gives you even more value from every visit.

### Unlock Bonus Rewards with Eligible Purchases

Tavern Rewards is simple: **Shop. Earn. Redeem.**

When you purchase eligible items through the Hearthstone Battle.net Shop, you'll earn Tavern Tender at no additional cost. Then, visit the Tavern Rewards Vault on Battle.net to redeem your Tavern Tender for bonus Hearthstone content.

### Explore the Tavern Rewards Vault

The Tavern Rewards Vault is where you'll redeem your Tavern Tender on a rotating collection of Hearthstone goodies.

Expect a mix of packs, signature cards, card backs, hero skins, and more. Some rewards may be community favorites making a return, while others will be brand-new additions available. With new items arriving over time, there's always something new to discover.

We'll have more details on Tavern Rewards soon, including a full look at the rewards available in the Tavern Rewards Vault.

*Tavern Rewards will be available in all Hearthstone regions except Mainland China.*

## Your Hearthstone Journey Begins Now

Journey is now live! Found in the Hearthstone Journal, Journey combines story, Journey Quests, rewards, events, and seasonal experiences into a single campaign that unfolds throughout an expansion.

Learn everything else you need to know in our [dedicated Hearthstone Journey blog](https://hearthstone.blizzard.com/news/24303865).

### Take a Tour of the Journal

Everything tied to this expansion lives in the Journal. Here's a quick tour of what you'll find inside.

- **Journey**: Follow the expansion story by watching voiced vignettes and reading lore.
- **Quests**: Complete Journey Quests to progress and earn rewards.
- **Rewards**: Collect rewards from your Journey and expansion activities.
- **Feats**: Achievements are now known as Feats. New name, same great challenges and rewards.
- **Profile**: View your progression and account details.
- **Bookmarks**: Access events, Tavern Brawls, and other limited-time experiences. Use "Go To" to open the experience.

## Get Ready for *Reign of the Black Empire* with Pre-Release Tavern Brawl and Community Days

![HS_37p0_CommunityEventsBlog_InBlog_PreReleaseBrawl_760x570_SW01.jpg](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/0Q4NV927E1ZY1790105421933.jpg)

Pre-Release Tavern Brawl is back, now with an updated format. This is an event where you get to open your *Reign of the Black Empire* packs early, build a deck, and compete to earn rewards. The new rules are:

- Build decks using only cards from *Reign of the Black Empire*
- Everyone starts with free trial copies of all *Reign of the Black Empire* Common and Rare cards\*
- Choose from predefined multi-class combinations before constructing your deck
  - Most options combine three classes, while one special two-class combination offers enhanced C'Thun synergies.
- Card Generation Pool: *Reign of the Black Empire*, Standard

\* *Access to the* Reign of the Black Empire *trial cards is temporary and limited to the Pre-Release Tavern Brawl, which starts October 13 at 10:00 a.m. PDT when Patch 37.0 goes live and ends on October 20 at 10:00 a.m. PDT when the expansion launches, after which they are removed.*

### New Pre-Release Tavern Brawl Feats

If you're looking for a challenge, we’ve got two feats for you to take a crack at!

- ***Reign of the Black Empire* Pre-Release Champion**: Finish a Pre‑Release Tavern Brawl run with 6 wins for the *Reign of the Black Empire* expansion.
  - **Rewards**: 2x Tavern Tickets, 1x Reign of the Black Empire Pack
- ***Reign of the Black Empire* Pre-Release Pro**: Finish a Pre‑Release Tavern Brawl run without losing a game for the *Reign of the Black Empire* expansion.
  - **Rewards**: Godlike Brawler Card Back, 2x Tavern Tickets

![CardBack_TavernBrawl_BlackEmpire_01.png](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/4LB1R6YGD2XC1791310153666.png)

### How to Play in the *Reign of the Black Empire* Pre-Release Tavern Brawl

All players will get one entry into the Pre-Release Tavern Brawl for free. After that, each entry will cost 2 Tavern Tickets\*, 300 Gold, or 400 Runestones. On each run, lock in a deck and play until you get either 6 wins or 3 losses and earn more new cards based on how well you do!

*\* You can obtain 2 additional Tavern Tickets during Community Days! More details in the next section.*

## Arena Updates

On October 13, with Patch 37.0, all ongoing Arena runs will end, and **a new Arena season will begin**. This season will use cards from the following sets:

- Reign of the Black Empire
- Across the Timeways
- TITANS
- Showdown in the Badlands
- Perils in Paradise
- Core Set
- Event Set

Cards from *Reign of the Black Empire* will be incorporated into the card pool on October 13, a week before the new expansion releases on October 20.

### Arena Rewards

With the new season comes a fresh reward for achieving 12 wins in Underground: **the Il'gynoth Hero Skin for Demon Hunter class**. Starting on October 13, it will replace the previous reward Hero Skin. After unlocking it, any subsequent 12-win runs will earn you an alternate prize of 2 Golden *Reign of the Black Empire* packs.

![HS_DEMONHUNTER_HERO_10cb_enUS_Ilgynoth-133202_NORMAL.png](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/gm/GMXJXYOM7YAR1791486497210.png)

When *Reign of the Black Empire* releases on October 20, all Arena and Underground pack rewards will update from *Escape from Violet Hold* to *Reign of the Black Empire*.

### New Arena Feats

Five new Arena feats have been added for *Reign of the Black Empire*. For each achievement, Part 1 must first be completed to do Part 2.

- **Empire Combatant**
  - **Part 1**: Open 5 Reign of the Black Empire packs.
  - **Part 2**: Finish an Underground run with 6+ wins.
  - **Reward**: 2x Tavern Tickets
- **Empire Challenger**
  - **Part 1**: Open 10 Reign of the Black Empire packs.
  - **Part 2**: Finish an Underground run with 6+ wins.
  - **Reward**: 2x Tavern Tickets
- **Empire Rival**
  - **Part 1**: Open 20 Reign of the Black Empire packs.
  - **Part 2**: Finish an Underground run with 6+ wins.
  - **Reward**: 2x Tavern Tickets
- **Empire Duelist**
  - **Part 1**: Open 35 Reign of the Black Empire packs.
  - **Part 2**: Finish an Underground run with 6+ wins.
  - **Reward**: 2x Tavern Tickets
- **Empire Gladiator**
  - **Part 1**: Open 50 Reign of the Black Empire packs.
  - **Part 2**: Finish an Underground run with 6+ wins.
  - **Reward**: 2x Tavern Tickets

## Bug Fixes

- Hearthstone
  - Fixed a bug where Flames of Infinity always triggered first at the end of an opponent's turn.
  - Fixed a bug where Shadow of Demise interacted incorrectly with Cultist Map.
  - Fixed a bug where Beastspeaker Taka could Discover Colossal minions.
  - Fixed a bug where Spikeridged Steed failed to grant Taunt to the targeted minion.
  - Fixed a bug where adding the Signature version of Reanimate the Terror to a deck could result in an invalid deck being generated by deck auto-complete.
  - Fixed a bug where Haunt could behave like a persistent Aura effect.
- Battlegrounds
  - Fixed a bug where Deities transformed by Mask of Ancient Ones were summoned with reduced stats.
