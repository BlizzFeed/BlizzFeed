The genius inventor and toymaker, Whizbang, has opened the doors to his magical workshop—and all of Azeroth’s invited! This place is teeming with plushies, Miniatures, and more. With nonstop nostalgia and options galore, you’re bound to find a new favorite toy—or even build one yourself. Welcome to a world of whimsy and wonder, welcome to Whizbang’s Workshop!

![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_folder_media/ENG4GYF9WKLP1707267693593.jpg)

Whizbang’s Workshop launches worldwide on March 19, with 145 new cards! You can find the Whizbang’s Workshop reveal schedule, and all cards that have already been revealed, by visiting the official [Card Library](https://hearthstone.blizzard.com/cards). Check back often—Whizbang is restocking the shelves with new reveals daily!

![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_folder_media/7V9UE5C4PJ901707438828988.png)

#### New Keyword: Miniaturize

Some of Whizbang’s creations come with their own fun-sized copy! Whenever you play a card with Miniaturize, you get a 1-mana 1/1 copy added to your hand. Play the Mini version right away for tempo, or save it for a cheap, powerful effect when the time is just right.

![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_folder_media/PK461ESWQ1LP1707267673820.png)![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_folder_media/0NF40DG3IP8U1707267673867.png)

#### Throwback Cards and Iconic Characters

Whizbang’s creations are inspired by 10 years of Hearthstone history. His workshop’s full of familiar faces and new takes on mechanics from Hearthstone’s past. Will your favorites make an appearance?

#### Customize Your Own Card with Zilliax Deluxe 3000

Can’t find the exact card you’re looking for? Build your own! Zilliax Deluxe 3000 is fully customizable. While building your deck, choose two Zilliax Modules to combine their costs, stats, and effects into your perfect Zilliax. Then, finish your Zilliax off with your choice of cosmetic finishes. How will you build your Zilliax?

![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_folder_media/1T7QIH8QJDB61707267673869.png)

#### Colifero the Artist Login Reward Available Now!

Picture this: all you need to do is login now and you’ll get the Legendary minion, Colifero the Artist!\* Colifero is one of a few talented Artist cards in Whizbang’s Workshop, all of whom have a special effect based on the card they draw. Colifero has a special talent for capturing your drawn card’s likeness and conveying it to your minions in play. What will your masterpiece be?

![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_folder_media/A3VFSCF7ILE11707267673961.png)

*\* Limited one per account. After Patch 29.6, Colifero the Artist will be granted upon opening your first Whizbang’s Workshop pack, instead of upon login.*
