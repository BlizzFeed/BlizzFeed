## Hearthstone Updates

*Dev Comment: Well met! We've been closely monitoring player feedback and gameplay data since the last balance update. While the current top decks are relatively close in power level, many players have expressed frustration with some of the format's most common play patterns and the lack of variety among competitive options. Since our balance strategy is about more than just card stat power levels, we're making several significant changes aimed at reducing those frustrations and opening the door for a wider range of strategies to succeed.*

*Dev Comment: On the buff side, our focus was primarily on underperforming decks and classes that have struggled to keep pace with the current format. Rather than relying entirely on straightforward stat increases, many of these changes reshape cards to better support the strategies they're meant to enable. In some instances, the mana cost of cards increased so that we could significant increase the overall power of the card's effect!*

*As with any patch intended to significantly shake up the metagame, there's always some uncertainty about where things will ultimately land. We'll be paying especially close attention to how the format develops after these changes go live. The next major update will be the release of Class Sets, followed by another scheduled balance pass, giving us additional opportunities to make adjustments as the meta continues to evolve. ## Battlegrounds Updates

*Dev Comment: We hope you've been enjoying the new season so far! There are a lot of balance changes in this patch, so let's jump right in.*

*Starting with the biggest change: Tasty Lobster. Tasty Lobster has been both highly prevalent and remarkably consistent in the early meta, to the point where it can feel like it's crowding out other strategies. As a result, we've gone with a substantial nerf by halving the power of its Deathrattle. We've also decided to keep Hoarding Hyena banned for now. Once we see how these and the other changes settle, we may revisit that decision if it turns out we've hit Lobsters too hard.*

*Most of the other minion changes are focused on helping underperforming minion types while spreading power more evenly among those that are already succeeding. One of the more ambitious changes in this patch is aimed at Quilboar. As a minion type, Quilboar have generally been underperforming, with Jailbird Juggernaut being the notable exception. We're trying something big by moving Jailbird Juggernaut to Tier 7 while moving both Sanguine Refiner and Sanguine Champion down to Tiers 5 and 6. Our goal is to give Quilboar a meaningful boost without concentrating too much of that power into a single minion.*

*Most of our Trinket and Dark Gift changes this patch are focused on smoothing out power levels by raising the floor and lowering the ceiling on some outliers. This should help reduce some of the high-roll moments while still leaving plenty of room for the wild and memorable games that make Battlegrounds special.*

*As always, we'll continue monitoring the meta and making adjustments as needed. We'll see you in the Tavern!*
