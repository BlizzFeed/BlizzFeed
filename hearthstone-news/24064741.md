# The Masters Tour Spring Championship is Here!

- URL: https://hearthstone.blizzard.com/en-gb/news/24064741/the-masters-tour-spring-championship-is-here
![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/8q/8QYSS3KN7SIH1712088537870.jpg)

> The first Masters Tour of 2024 is starting soon, streaming on Twitch and Youtube!

---

The first Masters Tour of the year is this weekend, and Hearthstone's best have come to play! Sixteen of the top players from the February and March Ladder rankings will compete for their share of the $50,000 (USD) prize pool and two invites to the 2024 World Champion. This year, we're bringing more Hearthstone action with a third day of streaming action! Read on for everything you need to know to enjoy the action, only on [Twitch](http://www.twitch.tv/playhearthstone) and [YouTube](http://www.youtube.com/playhearthstone).

What You Need to Know

- **Dates**: Friday, April 12 – Sunday, April 14 at 9 am PDT (6 pm CEST)
- **Format**: 4-deck, best-of-5 Conquest with a ban

  - **Day 1**: Group play
  - **Day 2**: Group play
  - **Day 3**: Top 8, Single Elimination Bracket
- **Prize Pool**: $50,000 (USD)
- **Drops**: Earn up to three Whizbang's Workshop packs while watching on Twitch all weekend (up to one pack per day).
- **Casters**: [Dragonrider](https://twitter.com/dawniedk), [Lorinda](https://twitter.com/LorindaGames), [Raven](https://twitter.com/RavenCasts), [Sottle](https://twitter.com/Sottle), and [TJ](https://twitter.com/Azumoqt)
- **Streams**:

  - **Twitch**: <https://www.twitch.tv/playhearthstone>
  - **Youtube**: <https://www.youtube.com/playhearthstone>
  - **Co-Streamin**g: If you are interested in co-streaming future Hearthstone Esports events, live or during off-broadcast times more conducive to your local region, be sure to apply [here](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSdPWAFj9F5fSAdnGlN9sxkivINRuNYWzZyXgDtsUnxOgu4jUQ/viewform).
