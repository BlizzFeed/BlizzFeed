Ready to begin your **Journey**? From weekly story content and quests to rewards, events, and all-new experiences, here's everything you need to know about what's waiting for you in the **Hearthstone Journal**.

## What Is Journey?

Journey is a brand-new way to experience Hearthstone.

Found within the Hearthstone Journal, Journey brings together story, Journey Quests, rewards, events, and seasonal experiences into a single campaign that unfolds throughout an expansion.

As you progress, you'll encounter major characters, unlock new content tied directly to the season, and experience Hearthstone's story in a deeper and more immersive way than ever before. From voiced vignettes featuring key characters to text-driven narrative moments, Journey tells its story through a variety of interactive experiences.

## Chapter 1: The Road to Ny'alotha

![HS_BlizzConRecap_InBlog_UpdatedJournal_760x428_SW01.jpg](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/H8G4OWI0FS571789249576959.jpg)

Your Journey begins on October 13 with **Chapter 1: The Road to Ny'alotha**.

This seven-part chapter unfolds over seven weeks. Each week, a new part unlocks with story content and Journey Quests to complete. Previously unlocked parts remain available until Patch 38.0, so you can complete them at your own pace.

Complete each part to earn:

- 7x Reign of the Black Empire Packs (1 pack per completed part)

As the chapter progresses, you'll uncover more about the Old Gods ruling over ancient Azeroth.

## Events Become Part of Your Journey

Many familiar Hearthstone events now play a larger role in Journey.

Rather than existing as separate experiences, events are now woven directly into the seasonal narrative. They offer deeper immersion and new insights into the world explored throughout the season. New events run over the course of the expansion, each featuring its own objectives and rewards while diving deeper into the lore through the bookmarked pages.

Event bookmarks let you preview event details from the Journal. When you're ready to jump in, select "Go To" on the bookmarked page to open the event hub.

Beginning with the launch of *Reign of the Black Empire*, you'll encounter a variety of seasonal experiences integrated into Journey, with more content planned throughout the expansion.

## The Journey Is Just Beginning

Journey is a living experience that grows over time.

In addition to new story chapters and events, future updates will introduce new ways to play, including:

- The return of the Black Market with new rewards
- New Tavern Brawls, including 2v2 and Hearthstone Warchief
- Additional story chapters, quests, rewards, and activities
- And coming with our next expansion: all-new Boss Fight events

Many of these experiences are featured on our six-month roadmap, with even more surprises planned along the way.
