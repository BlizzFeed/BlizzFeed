# The Hearthstone Summer Championship Is Here!

- URL: https://hearthstone.blizzard.com/en-gb/news/24293049/the-hearthstone-summer-championship-is-here
![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/qe/QEXCQIS4WYLZ1784751445140.png)

> Everything you need to know to enjoy all the action on Twitch and YouTube.

---

The road to BlizzCon is almost over, with just one final chance for competitors to qualify: the Summer Championship! Next weekend, from July 31-August 2, the top ladder players will join the qualifiers from the Summer Playoffs to battle it out for their share of $50,000 and one of three spots in the 2026 Hearthstone World Championship. Read on for everything you need to know to catch all the action on Twitch and YouTube.

## What you need to know

- **Dates:** Friday, July 31 – Sunday, August 2, starting each day at 7:00 a.m. PDT (4:00 p.m. CEST, 11:00 p.m. KST)
- **Format:** 4-deck, Best-of-5 Conquest with a ban
  - **Day 1:** A&B Groups Play, Double Elimination Bracket
  - **Day 2:** C&D Groups Play, Double Elimination Bracket
  - **Day 3:** Top 8, Single Elimination Bracket, with 3rd place match.

The top three competitors will qualify for the Masters Tour World Championship at BlizzCon later this year!

- **Prize Pool:** $50,000
- **Drops:** Earn up to 3 Escape from Violet Hold packs while watching on Twitch (1 each day)
- **Choose Your Champion:** Choose a competitor in the Summer Championship. The better they finish, the more packs you'll earn!

Choose Your Champion is live now, so head over and make your pick to earn a pack just for voting [here](http://hearthstone.blizzard.com/vote/choose-your-champion)!

- **Co-streamers:** If you’re interested in co-streaming the Hearthstone Summer Championship then [please fill out the following form](https://forms.gle/wW5rZhDJSVSo4MKg8) as soon as possible to be considered for costreaming.
