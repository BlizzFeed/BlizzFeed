# Showdown in the Badlands is NOW LIVE!

- URL: https://hearthstone.blizzard.com/en-gb/news/24008695/showdown-in-the-badlands-is-now-live
![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/r9/R9JTY3A8CBYN1699989730561.jpg)

> Eureka! Hearthstone’s newest expansion is now live. Chase outlaws and bandits, Excavate new cards, and be quick on the draw in this wild west bonanza!

---

Saddle up, partner! Showdown in the Badlands is now here, with 145 new collectible cards, legendary outlaws, new Quickdraw and Excavate keywords, and more!

[![Video](https://markdown-videos-api.jorgenkh.no/youtube/A5___jYm6vs?width=1280&height=720)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=A5___jYm6vs)

#### What’s Included: Legendary Outlaws and Terrible Townsfolk

The Bloodrock Mining Company is polluting the Badlands and awakening slumbering Elementals, putting everyone at risk! Enter the outlaws: these renegades are fighting to keep the peace. Demon Hunter, Druid, Hunter, Paladin, Priest, and Shaman each have a Legendary outlaw minion that has a powerful effect if your deck has no duplicates in it. To top it all off, Reno, Lone Ranger joins the outlaws as Hearthstone’s first Neutral Hero card!

#### What’s Included: New Keyword—Excavate

Cards with the Excavate keyword add Excavated Treasures to your hand! Talk about paydirt. The first time you Excavate, you get a random 1-cost Common Treasure, the second time you Excavate you get a random 2-cost Rare Treasure, and the third time you get a random 3-cost Epic Treasure. Once you’ve tapped out your mine, you can start the cycle over again from the beginning.

![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/77XDHPHASUW21698949144595.png)

Death Knight, Mage, Rogue, Warlock, and Warrior have teamed up with the Bloodrock Mining Company. These expert mining classes are also able to Excavate a fourth level of Treasures deep in the mine—a class-specific powerful Legendary!

![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/CLPKQ41IY0DC1698949154641.png)
![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/UX7OJKCYD9W21698949144807.png)
![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/ALDD05IN3SBH1698949144806.png)

*All Excavate Treasures have rarities, but are non-collectible and can only be gotten by Excavating.*

#### What’s Included: New Keyword—Quickdraw

In the Badlands, being fast on the draw can be the difference between life and death. Cards with the Quickdraw mechanic have a special bonus the turn they come into your hand. For best results, fire them off as soon as you... DRAW!
