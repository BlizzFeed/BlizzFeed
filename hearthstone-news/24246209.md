## Balance Preview for Patch 35.0 (coming March 10)

Rounding things out, we have a handful of balance changes coming to Standard with Patch 35.0. These updates will be active during our Theorycrafting and Pre‑Release Tavern Brawl events. They are in addition to our usual balance adjustments for cards rotating into Wild. Keep an eye out for the full 35.0 Patch Notes for details on those changes.

*Dev Comment: To give Deathwing the truly cataclysmic entrance he deserves, we are dialing back a few standout cards from previous expansions to raze the established meta and make room for new cards and strategies. Elise the Navigator is receiving the most significant updates across her kit, and Ursine Maul is also receiving a notable reduction in power, while the rest of the balance changes are on the lighter side. Our goal is for these cards to take a step back in the meta rather than disappear from it entirely. We hope you enjoy trying out the new cards in this refreshed landscape as we kick off a new year of Hearthstone!*

Elise the Navigator

- All locations' Durability: 3 → 2
- Bursting Geyser (Deathrattle: Deal X damage to all enemies.)
  - 10-Cost location (Fire Plume Ridge): 10 damage → 5 damage
  - 5-Cost location (Terror Run): 5 damage → 3 damage
  - 1-Cost location (Un'goro Jungle): 3 damage → 1 damage
- Nesting Grounds (Summon X 2/1 Raptors with Rush.)
  - 10-Cost location (Fire Plume Ridge): 6 Raptors → 4 Raptors
  - 5-Cost location (Terror Run): 3 Raptors → 2 Raptors
- Radiant Crystals (Summon a X/X copy of a friendly minion.)
  - 10-Cost location (Fire Plume Ridge): 10/10 copy → 5/5 copy
  - 5-Cost location (Terror Run): 5/5 copy → 3/3 copy
  - 1-Cost location (Un'goro Jungle): 1/1 copy → Radiant Crystals can no longer be selected on the 1-Cost location

![HS_NEUTRAL_TLC_100_enUS_ElisetheNavigator-117762_NORMAL.png](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/GLJW9GBQCD7C1772489162074.png)

Blob of Tar

- Old: [4 mana] 2/4. Deathrattle: Summon a 1/2 Blob with Poisonous and a 1/2 Blob with Taunt.
- New: [5 mana] 4/4. Deathrattle: Summon a 2/2 Blob with Poisonous and a 2/2 Blob with Taunt.
- *Dev Comment: While neutral outliers were a focus in this patch, we still believe there’s an important place for strong mid‑game defensive tools like Blob of Tar. We’re reducing its overall power and breaking up its repetitive play pattern with Ravenous Felhunter, while also providing a compensatory buff to support its board‑focused role. We’ll be keeping an eye on Felhunter as we evaluate its power level in a post‑rotation environment with fewer available targets.*
