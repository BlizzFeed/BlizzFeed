## Hearthstone Updates

*Dev Comment:* Escape from Violet Hold *has shaken up the meta in a big way, and we've loved seeing so many new cards making an impact. At the same time, we've seen a few classes start to fall behind, including Warlock, Death Knight, Priest, and Demon Hunter. Overall, class and deck diversity are lower than we'd like, especially at high ranks, so this patch is focused on opening up more options.*

*To help with that, we're improving a large number of* Escape from Violet Hold *cards, with a particular focus on supporting underperforming classes. Azalina Soulsever is also receiving one of the biggest updates we've ever given a card, complete with an all-new Start of Game effect.*

*We're also making adjustments to several of the current top-performing decks, such as Two-Cost Rogue and Mug Shaman, focusing on early blow-outs.*

*In Wild, Warlock was already quite powerful before Escape from Violet Hold, and Godfrey the Betrayer as further increased its overwhelming presence. Because of this, we're taking some larger swings, with an outright ban to Godfrey and nerfs to several other key cards for Warlock decks. We'll be monitoring to see what happens after these large changes and made continued follow ups as needed.*

*As always, we'll be watching closely to see how the meta develops.*

### Standard Balance Changes

Vanessa the Ringleader

- Old: [6 mana] 4/4.
- New: [7 mana] 4/3.
- *Dev Comment: Vanessa was one of the strongest enablers in Rogue and was creating powerful early snowballs too consistently. This change slows her down and makes those swing turns easier to answer.*

![NEUTRAL_JAIL_407_enUS_VanessatheRingleader-124150_NORMAL.png](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/ZY5WEGCU10L31784308008145.png)

Blazing Invocation

- Old: Discover a Battlecry minion. It costs (1) less.
- New: Discover a Battlecry minion.
- *Dev Comment: Blazing Invocation was accelerating some of Shaman's strongest plays too efficiently. Removing the discount slows those turns down while preserving the card's core identity. ‌Shaman's strength has put the class firmly on our radar, and we'll continue to monitor its performance as we see how this change plays out, including the impact of other early-game minion generators.*

![SHAMAN_GIL_836_enUS_BlazingInvocation-48393_NORMAL.png](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/3237TWU5N35A1784308009614.png)

Warptooth

- Old: If three friendly characters take damage on one of your turns, summon this from hand or deck.
- New: If four friendly characters take damage on one of your turns, summon this from hand or deck.
- *Dev Comment: Warptooth was hitting the battlefield too easily and creating explosive damage turns. Requiring additional setup should give opponents more time to respond.*

![WARRIOR_JAIL_421_enUS_Warptooth-125803_NORMAL.png](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/DZPNVF4725221784308009623.png)

Lotus Troublemaker

- Old: Battlecry: Deal 2 damage to a random enemy. Shoot 1 time! (While holding this, play cards for 2 Mana to shoot more!)
- New: Battlecry: Deal 1 damage to a random enemy. Shoot 1 time! (While in hand or deck play cards for 2 Mana to shoot more!)
- *Dev Comment: Lotus Troublemaker is a key piece in the new 2-cost Rogue. Its overall power level looks about right, but the difference between drawing it early and drawing it late can cause extreme gaps in that power, leading to frustration for both players. This change helps smooth out that draw variance while also reducing the speed of the burst damage.*

## Arena Updates

We’ve increased the appearance rate of **Escape from Violet Hold** cards in Arena to offer more opportunities to play with the new cards. We’ll continue monitoring how this change impacts the Arena experience and use those findings to help inform future Arena updates.

## Battlegrounds Updates

*Dev Comment: With the next Battlegrounds season just around the corner, we wanted to avoid making large-scale balance changes that could have unintended effects on the upcoming meta. Instead, we're taking a lighter approach and focusing on low-risk adjustments, along with a few significant power outliers.*

*Overall, the minion types are in a pretty healthy spot right now, so this patch is mainly focused on toning down Mechs and giving Elementals and Quillboar a boost.*

*There are also a few popular strategies we chose not to address this time, such as Rylak Metalhead and Back to Back, as we already have changes planned for them next season.*

*We'll have more to share very soon. Until then, we'll see you in the Tavern!*
