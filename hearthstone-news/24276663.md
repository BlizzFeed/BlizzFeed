The Hearthstone Spring Championship Is Here!  Learn how to watch the Spring Championship and earn rewards.

The road to BlizzCon continues with the Spring Championship next weekend! From June 5 - 7, the top ladder players will join the qualifiers from the Spring Playoffs to battle it out for their share of $50,000, as well as one of three spots in the 2026 Hearthstone World Championship.

Read on for everything you need to know to enjoy all the action on Twitch and YouTube.

## What You Need to Know

- **Dates**: Friday, June 5 - Sunday, June 7, starting each day at 7 a.m. PDT (4 p.m. CEST, 11 p.m. KST)
- **Format**: 4-deck, Best-of-5 Conquest with a ban
  - **Day 1**: A&B Groups Play, Double Elimination Bracket
  - **Day 2**: C&D Groups Play, Double Elimination Bracket
  - **Day 3**: Top 8, Single Elimination Bracket, with 3rd place match
- **Prize Pool**: $50,000
  - The top 3 competitors will qualify for the Masters Tour World Championship at BlizzCon later this year!
- **Drops**: Earn up to 3 CATACLYSM packs while watching on Twitch (1 each day)
- **Choose Your Champion**: Choose a competitor in the Spring Championship, and the better they finish, the more packs you’ll earn!
  - Choose Your Champion is live now, so [head here to enter](http://hearthstone.blizzard.com/vote/choose-your-champion) and get a pack just for voting.
- **Co-streamers**: If you’re interested in co-streaming the Hearthstone Spring Championship, please fill out the appropriate form as soon as possible to be considered.
- **Casters**: [Edelweiss](https://bsky.app/profile/edelweissccg.bsky.social), [Lorinda](https://bsky.app/profile/l0rinda.bsky.social), [PocketTrain](https://x.com/pocket_train), [Raven](https://x.com/RavenCasts), [Sottle](https://bsky.app/profile/sottle.bsky.social)
- **Streams**:
  - **Twitch**: <https://www.twitch.tv/playhearthstone>
  - **YouTube**: <https://www.youtube.com/Hearthstone>
