## Here’s What’s Inside

![Hearthstone_Standard_1920x1080.png](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/ag/AGFIYAXEOLWL1784241591603.png)

### Bloodmage Thalnos Signature Legendary

He’s in charge of the Annual Scarlet Monastery Blood Drive. Celebrate one of Hearthstone’s most iconic minions with menacing new art as Thalnos is reimagined in striking Signature form.

### Blade of the Black Empire Card Back

Brandish the power of the Black Empire with this gold-and-void card back that glimmers with dark energy. It should no longer try to tempt you with promises of power, but ignore any whispers you may hear, just in case.

### 5 Standard Card Packs

Crack open five Standard packs to grow your collection. Every pack holds five cards, with at least one Rare or better waiting inside.
