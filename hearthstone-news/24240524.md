# 33.6.2 Patch Notes

- URL: https://news.blizzard.com/en-gb/article/24240524/33-6-2-patch-notes
![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/kq/KQ6HP2BCJCAK1759781442899.jpg)

> Anomalies make their return, alongside balance updates to Heroes, Buddies, and more in Patch 33.6.2.

---

Patch 33.6.2 launches today with a variety of updates for Battlegrounds, including the return of Anomalies and balance changes to Heroes, Buddies, and more.

## Battlegrounds Updates

*Dev Comment: Well met!*

*We’re overall quite pleased with the initial reception to the midseason shakeup. This patch’s minion adjustments primarily focus on nerfing Quilboar while buffing Beasts, Undead, and Elementals. We also made several changes to various Buddies and Heroes based on early performance data to bring them more in line with each other.*

*This patch also brings Anomalies back to the game! There are six brand-new Anomalies that interact with the Buddy system in different ways. In addition to these, we’ve curated 20 returning Anomalies to rejoin the pool. One Anomaly not returning this time is Secrets of Norgannon (also known as “The Tier 7 Anomaly”). We’ve chosen to hold it back until we’ve had a chance to adjust the Tier 7 minion pool.*

*This patch introduces a significant rule change to Anomaly appearance rates: after you play with an Anomaly, it won’t appear again for at least three games. This should help you experience a wider variety of Anomalies and keep each game feeling fresh.*

*We don’t expect to make further gameplay adjustments until sometime after Across the Timeways launches, but we’ll be keeping an eye on the meta in the meantime. We hope you enjoy these updates and we’ll see you in the Tavern!*

### Anomalies Return

Anomalies are back in Battlegrounds! Anomalies are tavern-transforming effects that apply to all players from the start of each game.

![HS_NEUTRAL_BG33_Anomaly_002_enUS_SpiritofFriendship-123022_NORMAL.png](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/O07SFPIQAKYG1759858111979.png)![HS_NEUTRAL_BG33_Anomaly_003_enUS_ThirdNature-123081_NORMAL.png](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/D5WPB5QWFFFI1759858111948.png)![HS_NEUTRAL_BG33_Anomaly_005_enUS_ColorfulCamaraderie-123086_NORMAL.png](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/L2HE18SXQM251759858111775.png)

#### New Anomalies

- Summoning Pact: After you upgrade the Tavern, reduce the cost of your next Buddy by (2).
- Spirit of Friendship: After you press your Buddy Button, also Discover a Buddy.
- Third Nature: You can press your Buddy Button a 3rd time for a Golden Buddy. It costs half.
- Colorful Camaraderie: Buddies have all minion types.
- Partner in Crime: You have no Buddy Button. On turn 4, choose a Quest to get your Golden Buddy.
- Amicable Amendment: The Cost of your Buddy Button is 50% higher, but reduces by (1) after you buy a card.

#### Returning Anomalies

- A Faire Reward: Instead of a minion, Triple Rewards Discover a Tier 1 Darkmoon Prize. (Improves in 4 turns!)
- Audience's Choice: At the start of each turn, one player chooses a card for ALL players to get at end of turn.
- Bring Home the Bacon: Start with two upgrading 'Piggy Banks'. Break them open to gain Gold!
- Bring in the Buddies: Buddies are in the Tavern.
- Continuing Education: At the start of each turn, get an Evolving Scroll. Each turn, it transforms into a Tavern spell from a Tier higher.
- Double Header: The first time you buy a card each turn, get an extra copy of it.
- Eleventh Hour: The first time your hero takes fatal damage in combat, prevent it and gain 11 Gold next turn instead.
- Golden Arrow: Every 3 turns, get a Golden Arrow. (3 turns left!)
- Golganneth's Tempest: Minions cost 2 Gold. You cannot Refresh the Tavern. It Refreshes itself after you buy a card.
- Grapnel of the Titans: The first minion you buy each turn is free.
- Mimiron's Clockwork Stadium: You cannot upgrade the Tavern with Gold. It upgrades itself every 2 turns. (2 turns left!)
- Money Match: Start at 10 Gold.
- Overseer's Orb: After you upgrade the Tavern, Refresh it with minions of your most common type.
- Path of the Treasure-Seeker: After you Refresh 5 times, find the Golden Monkey! (5 left!)
- Perfected Alchemy: Start with a Goldenizer.
- Planar Alignment: At the start of each turn, get a random minion of your most common type. (Unlocks on Turn 2.)
- Scout's Honor: Start with a Golden Patient Scout in play.
- The Yogg-iseum: At the start of each turn, spin the SAME Wheel of Yogg-Saron.
- Treasure Hoard: On Turn [5/6/7/8/9], Discover a Golden Tier [3/4/5/6/7] minion. ([4/5/6/7/8] turns left!)
- Up-Prizing: After you upgrade the Tavern, Discover a Tier 1 Darkmoon Prize. (Improves in 3 turns!)
