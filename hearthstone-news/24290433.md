## New Gameplay System: Dark Gifts

**Dark Gifts** are coming to Battlegrounds! These mysterious boons infuse your minions with powerful enhancements steeped in dark magic. ‌You may already be familiar with Dark Gifts from Hearthstone, but we’re putting a new spin on them for Battlegrounds.

Starting on Turn 3, you can use the Dark Gift button to spend 3 Gold and Discover a minion with a Dark Gift. Each of the 3 minions offered will be paired with a unique Dark Gift.

![HS_36p2_BGSeason14_Announce_InBlog_DarkGifts_GIF_760x428_SW01.gif](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/w9/W9PLK8PE2BCZ1784581627490.gif)

Big stat boosts. Powerful keywords. Game-changing effects. With more than 40 Dark Gifts available, every game offers new ways to power up your warband.

The Dark Gift button can be used once per turn, up to 3 times per game. The minions and gifts offered improve each turn, becoming more powerful the longer you wait. Will you grab an early boost or hold out for something a little more tempting?

[Learn more about Dark Gifts in our Developer Deep Dive!](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/hearthstone/t/battlegrounds-developer-insight-dark-gifts/163606)

## New Keyword: Activate

![HS_36p2_BGSeason14_Announce_InBlog_KelpKeeper_GIF_760x428_SW02.gif](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/cd/CD3H0O5MYC4D1784755936040.gif)

**Activate** is a new keyword that gives certain minions a special ability you can trigger during the Recruit Phase by spending Gold, giving you a whole new way to invest your resources. While the minion is in your warband, simply click on it to activate its ability. Each Activate ability can be used once per turn, so choose your timing carefully and get the most value out of your Gold!

## General Updates

### Venomous

With Fishbait allowing minions to attack during the Recruit Phase, Venomous has been updated to destroy the first minion it damages each combat, ensuring the keyword remains effective once combat begins.

Venomous

- Old: Destroy the first minion this deals damage to.
- New: Destroy the first minion this deals damage to each combat.

### Chromadrake

Chromadrakes have been removed from the Tavern pool but can still be generated by certain minions and Trinkets. Their keyword has been updated to reflect that they are now only available through these effects.

Chromadrake

- Old: One of five Tier 3 Dragons with various Battlecries.
- New: One of five Dragons with various Battlecries.

### Easy-to-Read Stats

We all love seeing our minions reach absurdly high stats. To help keep your warband readable, numbers with four digits or more will now be condensed for a cleaner, easier-to-read display.
