> The 20.2 patch brings Quilboar to Battlegrounds as the next minion type!

---

The 20.2 patch, which will release on May 4, includes a major update for Battlegrounds, purchasable decks, and more!

#### Battlegrounds Updates

![HS_20p2_BG_FBTWsocial_1280x720_EK02.jpg](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/0f/0F8ASPXXBFFB1619482734119.jpg)

Quilboar have been added as a new Battlegrounds minion type! There will still be five minion types per Battlegrounds game once Quilboar are added, meaning that three minion types will be excluded from each game. Just like when Dragons, Pirates, and Elementals were added, Quilboar will show up in every Battlegrounds match until our next major content patch.

![NEUTRAL_BG20_GEM_enUS_BloodGem-70136_NORMAL.png](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/lb/LBCN1AK1Q3M31619713665293.png)

The Quilboar update includes 17 new minions, 3 new heroes, and a new mechanic: Blood Gems! Blood Gems are spells that give a friendly minion +1/+1. Some Quilboar give you Blood Gems, while others have special interactions when Blood Gems are played.

We’ve also added Battlegrounds support for your favorite Coin! As a reminder, you can set a Coin as your favorite by clicking on the Coin icon in the top right of your Collection.

Finally, prepare for a fresh playing field as all players’ Battlegrounds ratings will be reset to 0 when the 20.2 patch launches on May 4!

#### Diamond Ragnaros & Tavern Regular Achievement

![DiamondRag](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/fq/FQEIQ0LQDDJ31619480347149.png)

Diamond Ragnaros has been added to the Tavern! Diamond Ragnaros can be acquired by completing the Classic Legendary Collector 5/5 achievement (Collect all 36 Classic Legendary cards). Please note that players who have already completed and claimed this achievement must reclaim the achievement to obtain Diamond Ragnaros.

Battle-Ready Decks will be available for purchase for a limited time for $19.99 and will be limited to one per account
