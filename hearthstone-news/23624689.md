> The 19.6 patch includes a balance updates for Battlegrounds, Duels, and one minion in Standard.

---

The 19.6 patch brings pre-purchase for Hearthstone’s newest expansion, *Forged in the Barrens*, along with balance updates for Battlegrounds, Duels, and one card in Standard—High Abbess Alura.

#### Standard Balance Update

High Abbess Alura

- Old: [Costs 4] **→ New: [Costs 5]**

![NEUTRAL_SCH_141_enUS_HighAbbessAlura-59587.png](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/t6/T6N7J1RI420V1613516489805.png)![cardchanges_arrow.png](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/gallery/wx/WXH3UJH8MS061548722662334.png)![NEUTRAL_SCH_141_enUS_HighAbbessAlura-59587.png](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/1f/1FZST7FVHIDF1613516885051.png)

*Dev Comment: High Abbess Alura is going to 5 mana in order to push out the swing turns she enables. When an individual card contains a large amount of a deck's winrate, as Alura does, it breaks the promises of a Hearthstone match. Instead of having a natural back-and-forth flow between players, games with a turn 4 Alura can create seemingly insurmountable board states too early in the match. At 5 mana, Alura's identity as a big spell buildaround card remains intact but the change will give more room for games to occur before the swing and for responses to be ready after her Spellburst trigger.*

*High Abbess Alura will be eligible for a full dust refund for 2 weeks after the 19.6 patch goes live.*

#### Bug Fixes & Miscellaneous Updates

- Fixed a bug where completed Weekly Quests would not show awarded XP on the Rewards Track without relaunching Hearthstone.
- Fixed a bug where Transfer Student would not have a unique ability on the Darkmoon Faire gameboard.
- Fixed a bugged interaction between Bright-Eyed Scout and Shenanigans.
- Fixed a bugged interaction between Holy Wrath and Shenanigans.
- Fixed a bug where Sightless Watcher’s Battlecry would not offer a second copy of Sightless Watcher if one was in your deck.
- Fixed a bug where Keywarden Ivory’s Spellburst would only give one additional spell after Discovering two spells when played with Brann Bronzebeard.
- Fixed a bug where Dreaming Drake would not retain in-deck and in-hand modifications when Corrupted.
- Fixed a bug where history tiles were not showing after activating Spellburst for Glacier Racer and Imprisoned Celestial.
- Fixed a Battlegrounds bug where minions added to the warband via Embrace Your Rage would not be summoned by Bob’s Burgles the following recruit phase.
- Fixed a Battlegrounds bug where three Golden Faceless Taverngoers used on the same minion would combine to form another triple.
- Fixed a Duels bug where Staking the Claim would not count Keywarden Ivory as a Discover card.
- Fixed a Duels bug where Scrapmetal Demolitionist’s card text would not update throughout a run.
- Fixed a Duels bug where Instructor Fireheart would play two voice lines simultaneously.
- Duels Signature Hunter Treasure Bow of the Beast has been renamed to Carrot on a Stick.
- The Silas Darkmoon login reward will now be granted if you own a pack from Madness at the Darkmoon Faire and go to the Open Packs menu.
- Increased the font size on mobile devices for the XP bar on the Rewards Track.
- Search results for any minion type in the Collection Manager will now include Circus Amalgam.
