## Theorycrafting, Community Days and Pre-Release Tavern Brawl

### Theorycrafting

The plan is out in the wild and now everyone’s trying their own version of the breakout.

Theorycrafting brings together players from across the globe, all streaming their escape attempts live with brand-new decks, questionable strategies (players need to have a minimum of 10 *Escape from Violet Hold* cards in their decks), and some very unexpected results.

Theorycrafting participants will stream on their own channels in these slots:

- **NA/LATAM/EMEA players** will play on June 25, 9:00 a.m. - 9:00 p.m. PDT
- **APAC players** will play on June 26, 2:00 a.m. - 8:00 a.m. PDT
- **CN players** will play on June 28 4:00 p.m. - June 29 7:00 a.m. PDT

### Pre-Release Tavern Brawl

The reveals showed the plan. Theorycrafting tested the routes. Now it’s time to run your own breakout.

Crack open the *Escape from Violet Hold* packs you’ve been earning, then jump into the Pre-Release Tavern Brawl where only Escape from Violet cards are allowed. Everyone gets free trial copies of all Common *Escape from Violet Hold* cards to start, and the biggest rule is shattered: pick a class… then build your deck with cards from ANY class and no Rune restrictions.

- **When:** June 30, 10:00 a.m. PDT - July 7, 10:00 a.m. PDT
