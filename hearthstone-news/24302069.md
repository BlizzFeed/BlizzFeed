## Bargains, Mysteries, and More at the Black Market

Get ready for a trip to the mysterious Black Market. Simply play Ranked Hearthstone to earn a limited-time currency that can be spent on packs, cards, and a variety of cosmetics. Black Market prices are always shifting, so check back often for new deals, or try your hand at haggling for a better price.

The Black Market comes and goes like the wind, so don't miss your chance to visit September 15 - October 13. More details are coming in the 36.6 Patch Notes.

![HS_BlizzConRecap_InBlog_BlackMarket_DB_760x428_SW01.jpg](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/C4N1E6CTCR4A1789249575868.jpg)

The Black Market is just the first of many new experiences waiting to be discovered. Keep an eye on your Journal to see what's next!

## New Minion Type Coming to Battlegrounds

Meet Aberrations, the newest Battlegrounds minion type. Built around discard synergies, Aberrations grow stronger as they work to awaken their Deity, an ancient Old God of unimaginable power. Every Aberration that dies brings that moment closer, and you'll definitely know when it arrives.

Check out this breakdown of [all the Battlegrounds updates coming in Patch 36.6.1](https://hearthstone.blizzard.com/news/24302091) for more details. Aberrations make their Battlegrounds debut in Patch 36.6.1 on September 22.
