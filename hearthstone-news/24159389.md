# Announcing Battlegrounds Season 9

- URL: https://news.blizzard.com/en-gb/article/24159389/announcing-battlegrounds-season-9

> Enter the Technotavern in Battlegrounds Season 9 with a massive minion refresh, updates to the damage cap, Hero Reroll options, a reworked Rewards Track, and more!

---

In his travels across The Great Dark Beyond, Bob’s decked out the Tavern with cyberpunk style, new technologies, and all kinds of minion updates. Come see what the universe has to offer in Battlegrounds Season 9, Enter the Technotavern, blasting off on December 3!

![HS_31p2_BGSeason9_Announce_InBlog_BGMarquee_Seasonal_760x760_SW01.jpg](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/QIFA33KVEBDX1731625507663.jpg)

With Season 9, Battlegrounds Ratings will reset and Trinkets will rotate out of the Tavern. Season 9 will also bring with it an update to the Battlegrounds Track and all new Battlegrounds Tokens, which can be used to Reroll a Hero option at the start of your game—find out more, below.

#### Damage Cap Update

Starting with Season 9, the damage cap will be adjusted in solo Battlegrounds games (note: this change will only apply to solo Battlegrounds, not Duos). The damage cap will start at 5 damage, then go up to 10 damage on turn 4, and 15 damage on turn 8. The cap will still be removed once you’re in the Top 4. This change will allow games to progress more evenly, without big bursts of damage in the early game.

#### Get More Hero Choices with New Battlegrounds Tokens

One of the most common requests we hear about Battlegrounds is for more control over play direction. You have different favorite playstyles, preferred minion Types, and even Hero skins you want to see more of—not to mention some Heroes that you’re just not all that interested in playing.

Introducing Hero Rerolls! At the start of a game, during the hero selection screen, you can now Reroll one or more of your heroes to get more options on how you play. Each slot can be Rerolled once per game, for one Battlegrounds Token per Reroll.

![HS_31p2_BGSeason9_Announce_InBlog_RerollToken_GIF_760x495_SW01.gif](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/520SICB7VPBH1731625507823.gif)

Hero Rerolls (shown here with the Season Pass+)

Battlegrounds Tokens can be earned through the Rewards Track (both for free and with the Season Pass), as rewards from Events, or in the in-game Shop. Once the season goes live, there also will be a free intro gift of 2 Battlegrounds Tokens that will be automatically added to all player accounts and an additional 4 free Battlegrounds Tokens that can be claimed from the shop. Battlegrounds Tokens will also be included in viewership drops campaigns, including for the upcoming Season 9 Preview Event—more details on that below.

#### Get Even More Options with a Reworked Season Pass and the Season Pass+

The Battlegrounds Rewards Track is being extended to add Battlegrounds Tokens throughout both the free rewards and the Season Pass at no additional cost—giving players more rewards to earn throughout the Season. We will be closely monitoring this updated progression system and will adjust it further if it turns out this first version isn’t hitting right.

We are also adding the Season Pass+, a new option which will include all the perks and rewards of the Season Pass *and* makes your first Reroll each game not consume a Battlegrounds Token. This Reroll can’t be banked and follows the same Reroll rules as free-to-play players and players with the regular Season Pass—it just gives a consistent option for players who want to make the most out of this new system.

#### Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Why are you making these changes?**

A: Battlegrounds is a huge part of our Tavern, and we want to better serve you and make a more awesome experience for you all. We love the core Battlegrounds experience and want to be very careful about any changes we make. That’s why we are pursuing angles that provide you with more optionality rather than direct power. For example, we are not interested in selling perks like additional Armor at the start of the game or additional Gold or Tavern Refreshes during the game. By keeping Battlegrounds Tokens small, instead of all-or-nothing, you can engage however much you want. This also opens up a wider range of uses for us like drops, events, and freebies.

Informed by game data and direct player feedback, we have decided to shift some of our attention towards new offerings, like Battlegrounds Tokens. We’ll be watching for your feedback on these changes as we continue to refine this approach going forward.

**Q: How do I get Battlegrounds Tokens?**

A: The primary source of Battlegrounds Tokens is the Rewards Track. The free Track includes 8 Battlegrounds Tokens and the Season Pass adds an additional 25 Battlegrounds Tokens.

You can earn 4 Battlegrounds Tokens from Drops during our Preview Event. At Patch launch, all players will automatically get 2 Battlegrounds Tokens and be able to claim an additional 4 Battlegrounds Tokens from the shop. We also plan to include Battlegrounds Tokens as part of in-game Events.

Players who want even more Rerolls can also get Battlegrounds Tokens from the Shop (at a base price of 4 Battlegrounds Tokens for $0.99 or 100 Runestones; bundles will also be available), or pick up the Season Pass+ to get one Reroll per game without using up their Battlegrounds Tokens.

Since Hero Rerolls are an entirely new system, we’ll be watching play patterns and your feedback to see how this initial launch and distribution of Battlegrounds Tokens feels. Let us know what you think to inform our future iterations of this system.

**Q: How exactly do Hero Rerolls work?**

A: When you are on the Hero selection screen, there will be a Reroll button underneath each particular hero. The buttons under each Hero will indicate that it takes 1 Battlegrounds Token to Reroll (except, if you have the Season Pass+, your first Reroll won't cost a Battlegrounds Token). Your total Battlegrounds Tokens will be displayed in the corner of the screen as you decide.

Each hero slot is independent and can be Rerolled up to once (meaning that you can’t Reroll the same slot multiple times and you’ll have either 0-2 or 0-4 hero Rerolls per game, depending on whether you have the +2 Hero Options perk from the Season Pass). You can’t Reroll into a Hero you’ve already been offered, or a Hero that isn’t allowed in your game, and your Rerolls aren’t affected by the other players’ Rerolls (so you don’t have to try to game the timing of your Rerolls). Once you see a Hero you want to play, just stop Rerolling and select it like normal.

We limited it to one Reroll per Hero slot because we wanted to avoid games being too samey as everyone rolled for the most popular Heroes. Rerolls are done individually (instead of Rerolling all your Heroes at once) so that you can stop once you've found a Hero you like, and so that each Reroll isn't more valuable to people with 4 Hero Options than people with 2 Hero Options.

**Q: How will Rerolls work in Duos?**

A: Rerolls work the same in Duos as in regular Battlegrounds, with one major special case just for Duos: two-player Heroes, like Cho and Gall. We won’t force anybody to use a Battlegrounds Token when they don’t want to, so one player Rerolling the paired Hero won’t affect the other player’s Hero options. Similarly, if you Reroll a Hero, you won’t roll into one of the paired Heroes (which would be useless to you, because your partner wouldn’t have the other half). You’ll also never Reroll into a banned Hero pairing.

**Q: Why aren’t you changing the Damage Cap in Duos, too?**

A: We tested it out in Duos and found that it made the Mode worse instead of better. Duos already inherently rewards greedy scaling more than regular Battlegrounds does because your partner can help support you, so we didn’t want to further incentivize that strategy. It’s still our overall goal to make normal Battlegrounds and Duos overlap as much as possible, so players can smoothly take experience in one into the other, but we don’t want to restrict ourselves to that requirement if it makes the overall experience less than it can be.

**Q: How will the StarCraft cosmetic unlocks work?**

A: The StarCraft cosmetics are earned just like everything else on the Track, except that they won’t be unlocked until a later patch, planned for February 2025. If you earn that level of rewards prior to that patch, you won’t be able to claim them until they unlock.

**Q: What’s the bottom line for me?**

A: People who are free-to-play will continue to have that option. There are slightly fewer cosmetics on the free Rewards Track, but they’ll get some Battlegrounds Tokens from the Track, events, drops, and other free grants. So, they can keep engaging with Battlegrounds as they always have, with the option to occasionally Reroll a Hero they don’t feel like playing for a chance at one they like better.

People who are used to buying the Season Pass each Battlegrounds Season will get the +2 Hero Options and a range of cosmetics similar to what they’re used to from the Season Pass, with the addition of 25 more Battlegrounds Tokens on the Season Pass (plus the free Battlegrounds Tokens mentioned above).

People who want to maximize their Hero selection options can upgrade to the Season Pass+ to get one free Hero Reroll every game, plus the Rerolls they get from all the Battlegrounds Tokens on the Rewards Track and other sources.
