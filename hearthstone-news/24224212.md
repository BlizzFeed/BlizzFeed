## Hearthstone Updates

***Dev Comment:** With the launch of The Lost City of Un’Goro last week, our primary goals for this patch are to infuse power into several of the new cards and to tone down some older decks and early outliers. We’re doing buffs earlier in this expansion cycle because we can see from the early data that many of the new cards are not meeting expectations—and that nerfs alone won’t result in the changes we want to see.*

### Battlegrounds Updates

**Dev Comment:** *Well met! Patch 33.0.3 marks our final major balance update of the season. While we’ve made targeted adjustments to individual Trinkets and Anomalies, the core focus this patch is on minion tuning. We’ve addressed standout cards like Shoalfin Mystic and Captain Sanders, aiming to bring them into healthier positions within the meta. At the same time, we’ve given a boost to underperformers such as Fauna Whisperer and Champion of Sargeras to make them more rewarding to play. We hope you’ve enjoyed this season and look forward to sharing what’s coming next very soon.*

### Arena Updates

Arena draft rates have been adjusted so that the following cards appear less frequently:

- Dozing Dragon
- Desert Nestratron
- Trogg Gemtosser
- Voronei Recruiter

These cards will appear more often:

- Brasswing
- Libram of Divinity
- Libram of Faith
- Camouflage Mount
- Patchwerk Pals
- Slagclaw

Rheastrasza and Dirdra, Rebel Captain have been removed from the draft pool. Razidir can no longer be randomly generated.

### Bug Fixes and Game Improvements

- [Battlegrounds] Fixed a bug where Dragons adjacent to Persistent Poet sometimes did not keep enchantments from combat
- [Battlegrounds] Fixed a bug where Moroes, Steward of Death’s Deathrattle gave other minions the wrong attack number.
- [Battlegrounds] Fixed a bug where Golden Sinrunner Blanchy was not Reborn with full Health and enchantments in combat.
- [Hearthstone] Hamuul Runetotem's effect can no longer be stacked if in your deck multiple times.
