We’re about to blast off into The Great Dark Beyond, where a new Rewards Track awaits! When the Rewards Track refreshes with The Great Dark Beyond launch, all players will automatically be granted any unclaimed rewards on the current free Rewards Track and the Tavern Pass track (if acquired), before a completely new The Great Dark Beyond Rewards Track takes its place. **This time, we’ve improved both the free Rewards Track and the Tavern Pass track with an extra infusion of The Great Dark Beyond packs, so you get access to more new cards faster!\***

### The Great Dark Beyond Tavern Pass

With the launch of The Great Dark Beyond, a new and improved Tavern Pass will be available, featuring a universe of new cosmetic rewards, XP Boosts, and expansion packs!

![HS_31p0_RewardsTrack_InBlog_TavernPass_760x428_LO_DB01.jpg](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/u0/U0QD1XZGBJ1R1728609080014.jpg)

Getting the Tavern Pass at any time will immediately get you a 10% XP Boost that lasts the rest of the duration of the expansion, plus all items up to your current level on the paid track. As you progress along the Tavern Pass during The Great Dark Beyond expansion, you can earn: additional XP Boosts; the Archimonde Diamond Legendary card; the Nexus-Prince Shaffar and The Exodar Golden Legendary cards; 2 Ace Wayfinder Golden Epic cards; 2 Red Giant Signature Epic cards; 2 Supernova Signature Epic cards; 2 Galactic Crusader Signature Rare cards; a new cosmetic coin and card back; and 11 new Hero Skins; 2 Golden Standard Packs; and 4 The Great Dark Beyond Packs!\*

*\* All Tavern Pass card grants are unable to be crafted or disenchanted.*

### Two New Cosmic Cosmetic Coins

Two new cosmetic coins have been added with The Great Dark Beyond: the Planetary Coin and the Draenei Coin. The Planetary Coin is earned by reaching level 90 on the Rewards Track with the Tavern Pass. The Draenei Coin is earned by completing the Achievement for having 145 The Great Dark Beyond cards.

![zzNEUTRAL_GDB_COIN1_enUS_TheCoin-114074_NORMAL.png](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/SX5862WK9U2W1728608737663.png)![zzNEUTRAL_GDB_COIN2_enUS_TheCoin-114075_NORMAL.png](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/BARDFQ8V5XY51728608716815.png)

The Great Dark Beyond will have a special Mini-Set that will go where no Hearthstone set has gone before, and which will make it easier to complete the collection-based Achievements. We’ll share more information about the Mini-Set closer to when it comes out.
