# Get Ready for the 2025 Hearthstone World Championship!

![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/bm/BMSW1G9DOD5Y1765234292670.png)

> Here’s how to watch and earn rewards during the Hearthstone World Championship.

---

After a year of jaw-dropping plays, relentless practice, and unforgettable action, the 2025 Hearthstone Masters Tour is heading to its grand finale: the Hearthstone World Championship! From December 18 - 21, this four-day celebration of competitive Hearthstone will feature 16 of the world’s best players battling for their share of the $500,000 prize pool. We’ve got everything you need to know: how to watch, the tournament format, and how you can score some free packs along the way.

## What you need to know

- **Dates**:
  - Thursday, December 18 - Sunday, December 21
  - Starting each day at 7:00 a.m. PST (4:00 p.m. CEST)
- **Format**: 4-deck, Best-of-5 Conquest with one ban
  - **Day 1**: Groups A&B Initial and Winners matches
  - **Day 2**: Groups C&D Initial and Winners matches
  - **Day 3**: Elimination and Decider matches for all groups
  - **Day 4**: Quarterfinals through Grand Final

Thanks to the four-day broadcast, every single match will be shown, so you won’t miss a moment of the action.

- **Prize Pool**: $500,000
- **Drops**: Earn up to 8 Across the Timeways packs while watching on Twitch. Watch for 2 hours each day to claim your packs.
- **Choose Your Champion**: Pick a competitor in the World Championship. The better they finish, the more packs you earn, up to 5 packs\*.
  - [Choose your Champion is now live!](https://hearthstone.blizzard.com/vote/choose-your-champion)

*\* Please note: Packs from Choose Your Champion may be delayed due to the tournament’s proximity to the holiday season.*

- **Co-Streamers**: Want to share the World Championship action with your audience? Fill out [this application](https://forms.gle/RRP9EVcEh14abWPc9). If selected, you can co-stream the tournament on your channel.
- **Casters**: [Edelweiss](https://bsky.app/profile/edelweissccg.bsky.social), [Lorinda](https://bsky.app/profile/l0rinda.bsky.social), [Raven](https://x.com/RavenCasts), [Sottle](https://bsky.app/profile/sottle.bsky.social) + Special Guests!
- **Streams**:
  - **Twitch**: <https://www.twitch.tv/playhearthstone>
  - **YouTube**: <https://www.youtube.com/Hearthstone>
