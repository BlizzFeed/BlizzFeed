# Reignite Your Dreams with the Embers of the World Tree Mini-Set

![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/bp/BPF4B7JTWUFW1745352740092.jpg)

> Fire up smoldering cards, Imbue and Dark Gift synergies, and more!

---

The Druids of the Flame are attacking the World Tree, setting our Dreams ablaze! But through the flames, there remains a spark of hope. Prepare your defenses, it’s nearly time to harness new firepower with the Into the Emerald Dream Mini-Set: Embers of the World Tree!

![HS_32p2_MiniSet_Announce_InBlog_GoldenMiniSet_RN_760x428_SW01.jpg](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/qw/QW0U5BNKQT2E1745448968207.jpg)

The Embers of the World Tree Mini-Set, launching on May 13, has 38 new cards: 4 Legendary cards, 2 Epic cards, 16 Rare cards, and 16 Common cards. Those cards can either be opened in Into the Emerald Dream packs or purchased as a complete 72-card\* set. Normal versions of the Mini-Set can be purchased for $14.99 or 2000 Gold. The all-Golden version can be purchased for $69.99 or 10,000 Gold, and includes a bonus Diamond copy of the Fyrakk, the Blazing Legendary minion!

*\* Includes one copy of each Legendary card and 2 copies of each other card in the Mini-Set.*

#### Smoldering Cards Let You Play with Fire

New Smoldering cards get stronger each turn they are in your hand, but hold onto them for too long and they’ll flame out! Plan your turns out to maximize your value—just don’t get too greedy, or you might get burned.
