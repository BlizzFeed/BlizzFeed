> The Masters Tour World Championship is this weekend, live on Twitch!

---

The Masters Tour World Championship is this weekend! The top point earners from this year's ladder rankings join the top finishers of the Spring and Summer Masters Tours in a showdown for their share of the $500,000 (USD) prize pool, provided by Blizzard Entertainment® and NetEase Thunderfire! Read on for everything you need to know to enjoy the action, only on [Twitch](https://www.twitch.tv/playhearthstone) and [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/playhearthstone).

### What You Need to Know

- **Dates:** Friday, December 13 – Sunday, December 15, starting at 7 am PST (4 pm CET) each day
- **Format:** 4-deck, best-of-5 Conquest with a ban
  - **Day 1:** Group play
  - **Day 2:** Group play
  - **Day 3:** Top 8, Single Elimination Bracket
- **Prize Pool:** $500,000 (USD)
- **Drops:** Earn up to **six** *The Great Dark Beyond* packs while watching on Twitch all weekend (up to 2 per day)
- **Casters:** [Dragonrider](https://twitter.com/dawniedk), [Edelweiss](https://bsky.app/profile/edelweissccg.bsky.social), [Lorinda](https://bsky.app/profile/l0rinda.bsky.social), [Raven](https://twitter.com/RavenCasts), [Sottle](https://bsky.app/profile/sottle.bsky.social), and [TJ](https://twitter.com/Azumoqt)
- **Streams:**
  - Twitch: <https://www.twitch.tv/playhearthstone>
  - Youtube: <https://www.youtube.com/playhearthstone>
  - Co-Streaming: If you are interested in co-streaming Hearthstone Esports events, live or during off-broadcast times more conducive to your local region, be sure to apply [here](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSdPWAFj9F5fSAdnGlN9sxkivINRuNYWzZyXgDtsUnxOgu4jUQ/viewform?usp=sf_link).
