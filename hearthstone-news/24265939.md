# The Hearthstone Winter Championship Is Here!

- URL: https://news.blizzard.com/en-gb/article/24265939/the-hearthstone-winter-championship-is-here
![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/ny/NYOESCST1ADR1774463195852.jpg)

> Learn how to watch the Winter Championships and get free packs.

---

Hot on the heels of [*CATACLYSM’*s fiery launch](https://hearthstone.blizzard.com/news/24250357), things are cooling down with the first Masters Tour of the year: the Winter Championship! From April 10 - 12, the top ladder players will face off against the winners of the Winter Playoffs, competing for their share of $50,000 and one of three coveted spots at the [2026 Hearthstone World Championship](https://hearthstone.blizzard.com/news/24245108). Read on to learn how to catch all the action on Twitch and YouTube.

## What You Need to Know

- **Dates**: Friday, April 10 - Sunday, April 12, starting each day at 8 a.m. PDT (5:00 p.m. CEST)
- **Format**: 4-deck, Best-of-5 Conquest with a ban
  - **Day 1**: A&B Groups Play, Double Elimination Bracket
  - **Day 2**: C&D Groups Play, Double Elimination Bracket
  - **Day 3**: Top 8, Single Elimination Bracket, with 3rd place match.
- **Prize Pool**: $50,000
  - The top 3 competitors will qualify for the Masters Tour World Championship later this year!
- **Drops**: Earn up to 3 CATACLYSM packs while watching on Twitch (1 each day)
- **Choose Your Champion**: Choose a competitor in the Winter Championship, and the better they finish, the more packs you’ll earn!
  - Choose Your Champion is live now, so head [here](http://hearthstone.blizzard.com/vote/choose-your-champion) to enter and get a pack just for voting.
- **Co-streamers**: If you’re interested in co-streaming the Hearthstone Winter Championship then [please fill out this form](https://forms.gle/E3Tmsp4Fq3GaVk5J6) as soon as possible to be considered fo costreaming.
- **Casters**: [Edelweiss](https://bsky.app/profile/edelweissccg.bsky.social), [Lorinda](https://bsky.app/profile/l0rinda.bsky.social), [PocketTrain](https://x.com/pocket_train), [Raven](https://x.com/RavenCasts), [Sottle](https://bsky.app/profile/sottle.bsky.social)
- **Streams**:
  - **Twitch**: <https://www.twitch.tv/playhearthstone>
  - **YouTube**: <https://www.youtube.com/Hearthstone>
