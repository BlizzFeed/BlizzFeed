## *Reign of the Black Empire* Theorycrafting Streams October 6 - 9

![HS_37p0_CommunityEventsBlog_InBlog_TheorycraftingWeek_760x428_SW03B.jpg](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/3YUNF22N5WME1790105422007.jpg)

The Old Gods' corruption is spreading everywhere, and its reach knows no bounds! Theorycrafting has been infused with their dark power, expanding from a single day into nearly a full week of early access content. Join your favorite creators as they experiment with new decks, discover powerful interactions, and put *Reign of the Black Empire* through its paces before launch.

### Theorycrafting: Devs vs. Streamers

The battle for the Black Empire isn't limited to Azeroth.

During Theorycrafting, Team Dev and Team Streamer will clash for glory, bragging rights, and rewards. Every five victories earned by Team Streamer will unlock surprise rewards for randomly selected Theorycrafting participants to share with their communities, so every win counts.

To make sure more players can join the action, this year's Theorycrafting event spans multiple regions and multiple days. Participating creators will stream from their own channels throughout the event, while our developers enter the fray during the opening play session.

Check the schedule below to see when your favorite creators will be live, then tune in to watch new decks emerge, mind-bending combos take shape, and the first *Reign of the Black Empire* experiments unfold.

Drops enabled! Watch any stream in the Hearthstone category to earn rewards.

- **NA/LATAM/EMEA players** will play on October 6 (8:00 a.m. PDT – 1:00 p.m. PDT) and on October 7 (12:00 p.m. PDT – 6:00 p.m. PDT)
- **APAC players** will play on October 7 (2:00 a.m. PDT – 8:00 a.m. PDT)
- **CN players** will play on October 8-9 (4:00 p.m. PDT – 8:00 a.m. PDT)

## Pre-Release Tavern Brawl from Oct 13 - 20

![HS_37p0_CommunityEventsBlog_InBlog_PreReleaseBrawl_760x570_SW01.jpg](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/0Q4NV927E1ZY1790105421933.jpg)

Jump into *Reign of the Black Empire* early with our Pre-Release Tavern Brawl, now with an updated format!

- Play with only cards from *Reign of the Black Empire*
- Choose from predefined multi-class combinations before constructing your deck.
- Most options combine three classes, while one special two-class combination offers enhanced C'Thun synergies.
- Everyone starts with *Reign of the Black Empire* Common and Rare cards available as free Trial Cards\* during the event.

\* *Please note these* Reign of the Black Empire *trial Common & Rare Cards are available to play for this event only and not permanently added to your collection. The cards can be obtained for your collection by opening* Reign of the Black Empire *packs.*

All players will get one entry into the Pre-Release Tavern Brawl for free. After that, each entry will cost 2 Tavern Tickets, 300 Gold, or 400 Runestones. On each run, lock in a deck and play until you get either 6 wins or 3 losses and earn more new cards based on how well you do!

## Sneak Peek at What's Coming in Patch 37.0

Tune in during Theorycrafting for an early look at some of the new features, events, and collectibles arriving in Patch 37.0.

### The New Journal

The new Journal is your go-to destination for story content, events, Tavern Brawls, Arena seasons, free rewards, and more. Get a first look at what awaits within its pages before it opens to everyone in Patch 37.0.

### Celebrate 30 Years of Terror

Get ready to celebrate 30 years of terror with a special Hearthstone crossover featuring Diablo himself. From an all-new in-game event to the return of a fan-favorite Tavern Brawl and themed rewards in the Shop, there's plenty of fire and brimstone on the horizon. Players exploring the new Journal may uncover an early glimpse of what's to come, with full details arriving soon.

### The Old Gods' Finest Offerings

The influence of the Old Gods extends throughout the Tavern in a new collection of premium cosmetics and collectibles. Collectors will soon have the opportunity to add the Y'Shaarj Mythic Hero Skin, N'Zoth Pet, and C'Thun Signature Card to their collection. In Patch 37.0, a new Old God Card Back (seen below) featuring all four Old Gods will also become available through a special collector Achievement. More details will be revealed in the Patch 37.0 notes.
