#### Hearthstone’s Newest Mode: Twist

![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_folder_media/SQMIAUOJZM7M1687385412592.jpg)

Patch 26.6 will kick off the Beta seasons of Hearthstone’s newest Mode, Twist! Twist will be replacing Classic. Like the other Hearthstone Modes, Twist is a constructed format where you build a deck and compete in a ranked ladder climb. But, unlike Standard and Wild, each Twist season has a special *twist* on the eligible card pools or rules! In Twist, you can replay all your old favorite cards, but see them in a whole new light.

The Twist Beta period will run during Patch 26.6. For that Beta period, the following sets will make up the eligible Twist card pool (but with **no neutral cards**):

- Core
- Demon Hunter Initiate
- Ashes of Outland
- Scholomance Academy
- Madness at the Darkmoon Faire
- Forged in the Barrens
- United in Stormwind
- Fractured in Alterac Valley
- Voyage to the Sunken City
- Murder at Castle Nathria
- March of the Lich King
- Path of Arthas
- Festival of Legends

Don’t worry if you don’t have cards from all those sets, we’ll be offering 6 Battle-Ready Decks specially created for this Twist format, such as Libram Paladin, Weapon Rogue, and Questline Druid! These decks are perfect for players who don’t have the cards for the format and just want to jump right into the fun. Each of these Battle-Ready decks will be offered in regular (3200 Gold or $19.99) and all-Golden (12000 Gold or $79.99) versions.

You can learn more about our current and upcoming plans for Twist in the [official Twist announcement blog](https://hearthstone.blizzard.com/news/23968054).

**Bug Fixes and Game Improvements**

- [General] We’ve moved Tavern Brawl to the main menu and moved Mercenaries to the Modes button. Tavern Brawl, Mercenaries, and all other Modes are now unlocked by unlocking all Hearthstone classes.
- [General] Improved new player flow.
- [General] Fixed a bug where players could not adjust screen resolution.
- [Hearthstone] Fixed a bug where stealing Fizzle’s Snapshot from your opponent’s deck did not give any cards when played.
- [Hearthstone] Fixed a bug where triple-Rune cards were sometimes offered in Discover effects.
- [Hearthstone] Fixed a visual bug on mobile where the deck selection screen would remain blurry after a match.
- [Hearthstone] Fixed a visual bug where cards destroyed from your opponent’s deck would show as coming and being destroyed from yours.
- [Hearthstone] Fixed a visual bug where JIVE, INSECT! would sometimes not transform the art of the card it targeted.
- [Battlegrounds] Bassgill can no longer summon Ambassador Faelin’s locked minions.
- [Battlegrounds] Fixed a bug where Ambassador Faelin’s locked minions could be made playable when becoming Goldenized by Upbeat Impressionist.
- [Battlegrounds] Fixed a bug where Golden Octasari, Wrap God’s Deathrattle would not trigger correctly with Titus Rivendare.
- [Battlegrounds] Fixed a bug where Vaelastrasz (Alexstrasza’s Buddy) did not always give you a Dragon of your Tavern Tier.
- [Battlegrounds] Fixed a bug where “All”-type minions added to Cookie, the Cook’s pot could result in no-type minions being offered in the Discover options. Cookie’s now gotten better training on following dietary restrictions.
- [Solo Content] Fixed a bug where defeating The Lich King in the Icecrown Solo Adventure would cause the game to lock on some devices.
- [Features] Fixed a bug where the iOS keyboard would appear and block the ability to share a deck code with a friend on mobile.
- [Progression] Fixed a bug where total win counts would not update unless the client was restarted.
- [Cosmetics] Fixed Frostmonger Sai and Crimson Knight Sai text emotes and voicelines.
- [Shop] Added a confirmation window for shop purchases made with Gold (to avoid accidental just-a-buys).
- Misc. other performance updates, game improvements, and bug fixes.
