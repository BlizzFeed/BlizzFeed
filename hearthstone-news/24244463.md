![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/7z/7Z8B829570O31761931797623.png)

> Here is how you can watch Hearthstone competitors battle it out for the last spot in the World Championship.

---

The hourglass is nearly empty, and Hearthstone competitors are running out of time to qualify for the World Championship. Eleven players have already secured their spots through Masters Tours and Ladder points. Four more will join next month from China’s competitive system. That leaves just one final spot to be claimed.

That’s where the Last Chance Finals comes in. It’s a high-stakes, winner-takes-all, single-day event featuring over 100 players from this year’s competitive circuit. Each of them narrowly missed their shot at glory and will now battle for one thing: the last ticket to the World Championship.

### What You Need to Know

- **Date**: Sunday, November 16 at 9 a.m. PST
- **Format**: 4-deck, Best-of-5 Conquest with one ban
  - Day 1: 7-round Swiss to seed Top 8 (not broadcasted)
  - Day 2: Top 8 Single Elimination Bracket
- **Competitors**:
  - Any player who competed in a Masters Tour Championship and has not yet qualified for the World Championship
  - The Top 50 global Competitive Points earners across all three Qualifying Periods
  - All Top 4 finishers from Open Qualifiers during the Last Chance Qualifying Period
- **Prize**: The winner of the Last Chance Finals will earn a spot in the 2025 Hearthstone World Championship.
- **Drops**: Earn 2 Across the Timeways packs by watching at [twitch.tv/playhearthstone](http://twitch.tv/playhearthstone) (1 pack per hour)
