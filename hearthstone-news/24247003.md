# Setting Things Right with Ranked Rewards and Mister Clocksworth

![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/d7/D7V14RPSWXZU1762552588440.jpg)

> We’re sharing our plan to fix a pair of recent issues.

---

Hey all,

Hearthstone EP Nathan here. Recently we hit a few bumps, so I’m here to tell you how we’re going to smooth them out.

### Ranked Reward Packs

At the end of the last Ranked season, those who opened their rewards got Lost City of Un’Goro packs instead of the intended Across the Timeways packs.

#### Here’s how we’re making it right:

- Next week, we’ll be sending you the proper Across the Timeways packs you should have received.
- You’ll keep the Lost City of Un’Goro packs you already opened.

### Signature Mister Clocksworth

A bug caused the Signature Mister Clocksworth card to appear in Across the Timeways packs, and we removed it from collections. That was a mistake. Each collection is personal and represents your history of play and love for the game. While there may be extreme circumstances that call for altering a collection, this wasn’t one of them. We know how much your collection means to you, and we apologize for the frustration this caused.

#### Here’s how we’re making it right:

- As of this post, we’ve restored the Signature Mister Clocksworth card to everyone affected.
- You’ll keep the random Signature card you already received.
- We’re also sending you 5 Golden Across the Timeways packs.

Thank you for your patience as we implement these fixes. We’ll see you in the Tavern!

- Nathan
