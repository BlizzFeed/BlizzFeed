# Crack Open Ancient Power with the Day of Rebirth Mini-Set

> It’s time to shell-ebrate dinosaur hatching season with the Day of Rebirth Mini-Set!

---

The colossal Egg of Khelos begins to stir, and the Un’Goro Crater erupts in celebration! As dinosaur-hatching season begins, fireworks, feasts, and ancient stories mark the sacred Tortollan tradition where life begins anew and the fallen are honored. Prepare yourself: the Day of Rebirth Mini-Set is about to hatch!

![HS_33p4_Patch_Notes_InBlog_GoldenMiniSet_LO_760x428_SW01.jpg](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/o6/O67JUUXIDR731756826028196.jpg)

The Day of Rebirth Mini-Set, launching on September 2, has 38 new cards: 4 Legendary cards, 1 Epic card, 17 Rare cards, and 16 Common cards. Those cards can either be opened in The Lost City of Un'Goro packs or purchased as a complete 72-card\* set. Normal versions of the Mini-Set can be purchased for $14.99 or 2000 Gold. The all-Golden version can be purchased for $69.99 or 10,000 Gold and includes a bonus Diamond copy of the Beast Speaker Taka Legendary minion!

\* *Includes 1 copy of each Legendary card and 2 copies of each Common, Rare, and Epic card in the Mini-Set.*

## Awaken the Beast Within

She’s ancient, wise, and ready to roar. When you play Beast Speaker Taka, you channel her primal gift: gaining the strength of a Legendary Beast and unleashing it upon your foes!
