## *Escape from Violet Hold* Launches on July 7

[*Escape from Violet Hold*](https://hearthstone.blizzard.com/news/24276664), Hearthstone’s next expansion, launches on July 7! This 135‑card expansion features bold new cards that defy the very rules of Hearthstone, including a Legendary Rulebreaker minion for each class, the sneaky new Prepare keyword, Disguised minions, Bribe cards, and more! See every new card in the [Card Library](https://hearthstone.blizzard.com/cards) today, and grab your Pre‑Purchase bundles before the breakout begins!

![HS_35p6_Expansion_Announcement_InBlog_MegaBundle_MM_760x428_SW03.jpg](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/c9/C95TK60ZKN8N1781744843703.jpg)

Vanessa VanCleef is orchestrating the breakout, and now her Signature version is part of the Pre-Purchase Mega Bundle! Vanessa the Ringleader is seeing tons of play, so we’ve added this special bonus to help you generate more fun Signature cards with her ability. - Signature Vanessa the Ringleader is added on top of the existing Pre-Purchase Mega Bundle at **no extra cost.**

  - Anyone who purchased the *Escape from Violet Hold* Mega Bundle and logged into Hearthstone at least once between June 2 at 10:00 a.m. PT (Patch 35.6.0) and June 18 when Patch 35.6.3 releases will receive Signature Vanessa the Ringleader automatically within 24 hours.

## Get Ready for *Escape from Violet Hold* with Pre-Release Tavern Brawl and Community Days

We’ve got some exciting updates for the Pre-Release Tavern Brawl, now with a new format. This is an event where you get to open your E*scape from Violet Hold* packs early, build a deck, and compete to earn rewards. The new rules are:

- Build decks using **only** cards from *Escape from Violet Hold*. **No class or rune restrictions!**
  - Card Generation Pool: *Escape from Violet Hold*, Standard
- Everyone starts with **free trial copies** of all *Escape from Violet Hold* Common cards\*

\* *Access to the* Escape from Violet Hold *trial cards is temporary and limited to the Pre-Release Tavern Brawl, which starts June 30 at 10:00 a.m. PDT when Patch 36.0 goes live and ends on July 7 at 10:00 a.m. PDT when the expansion launches, after which they are removed.*

## Arena Updates

On June 30, with Patch 36.0, all ongoing Arena runs will end, and a new Arena season will begin. This season will use cards from the following sets:

- Escape from Violet Hold
- Across the Timeways
- Whizbang's Workshop
- Festival of Legends
- Murder at Castle Nathria
- Core Set
- Event Set

Cards from *Escape from Violet Hold* will be incorporated into the card pool on June 30, a week before the new expansion releases on July 7.

### Arena Rewards

With the new season comes a fresh reward for achieving 12 wins in Underground: the **Nefarious Nemsy Hero Skin**. Starting on June 30, it will replace the previous reward Hero Skin. After unlocking it, any subsequent 12-win runs will earn you an alternate prize of 2 Golden *Escape from Violet Hold* packs.

![HS_WARLOCK_HERO_07bq_Blank_NefariousNemsy-127685_NORMAL.png](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/GWVDYUW0MNQF1782236265908.png)

When Escape from Violet Hold releases on July 7, all Arena and Underground pack rewards will update from CATACLYSM packs to Escape from Violet Hold packs.

### New Arena Achievements

Five new Arena achievements have been added for *Escape from Violet Hold*. For each achievement, Part 1 must first be completed to do Part 2.

- **Jailbreak Combatant**
  - **Part 1**: Open 5 Escape from Violet Hold packs.
  - **Part 2**: Finish an Underground run with 6+ wins.
  - **Reward**: 2x Tavern Tickets
- **Jailbreak Challenger**
  - **Part 1**: Open 10 Escape from Violet Hold packs.
  - **Part 2**: Finish an Underground run with 6+ wins.
  - **Reward**: 2x Tavern Tickets
- **Jailbreak Rival**
  - **Part 1**: Open 20 Escape from Violet Hold packs.
  - **Part 2**: Finish an Underground run with 6+ wins.
  - **Reward**: 2x Tavern Tickets
- **Jailbreak Duelist**
  - **Part 1**: Open 35 Escape from Violet Hold packs.
  - **Part 2**: Finish an Underground run with 6+ wins.
  - **Reward**: 2x Tavern Tickets
- **Jailbreak Gladiator**
  - **Part 1**: Open 50 Escape from Violet Hold packs.
  - **Part 2**: Finish an Underground run with 6+ wins.
  - **Reward**: 2x Tavern Tickets

### New Arena Feature: Sideboards

Cards that use sideboards can now be drafted in Arena! Sideboard contents are fixed and cannot be changed during a run. Try out sideboards in Arena with the new *Escape from Violet Hold* Legendaries, King of the Underbelly and Commander Beatrix.

### What’s Next After Trial Cards

If you’ve been playing with the Dream City Trial Cards, we hope you’ve had a blast trying out new decks and discovering your favorite strategies. When the next Hearthstone expansion launches on July 7, these Trial Cards will be expiring. But don’t worry, we’ve got you covered. We’re making sure you have plenty of ways to build up your collection and stay ladder-ready without missing a beat.

To get started, be sure to claim these **two free Catch-Up Packs**. These oversized packs can grant **up to 50 cards** based on the size of your collection.

- **Catch-Up Pack #1**: Just log in any time after *Escape from Violet Hold* launches on July 7 to claim. Lasts until Patch 36.4.
- **Catch-Up Pack #2**: Participate in the Violet Hold in-game event detailed above, running from July 7 – 28. Complete event missions and check your progress to earn a Catch-Up Pack along with other rewards like free packs and cosmetics.

Check out [our guide](https://hearthstone.blizzard.com/news/24280282) for more tips on how to grow your collection and keep playing!

## Bug Fixes

[Hearthstone](#Hearthstone)

- Fixed a visual bug where Shandris’ bow was missing its string.
- Fixed a bug where Classic Collection achievements were incorrectly marked as retired.
- Fixed a bug where Eggbasher did not upgrade spells from Blackwing Experiment.
- Fixed a bug where Confront the Tol’vir could incorrectly target friendly minions when replaying 1-Cost Choose One cards.
- Fixed a bug where the target of Confront the Tol’vir could die before the spell resolved.
- Fixed a bug where minions could still be healed after exceeding base Health while Repulsive Gargantuan was on the board.
- Fixed a bug where Petal Peddler did not grant +1/+1 to another Dragon when it was the last end-of-turn effect minion played.
- Fixed a bug where Bashana Runetotem revealed spells to the opponent.
- Fixed a bug where Brilliant Macaw could gain Deathrattle effects from Timeway Warden when repeating the last Battlecry.
- Fixed a bug where cards could still be Discovered after transforming a Cultist Map result into a Coin with Agent of the Old Ones.
- Fixed a bug where Felwood Treant could still trigger after the opponent played Mana Burn.
- Fixed a bug where The T.N.T. could destroy the same cards in hand or on board multiple times.
- Fixed a bug where Howling Blast could be cast on friendly minions in unintended ways.
- Fixed a bug where Remornia, Living Blade did not retain Mega-Windfury from Air Support.
- Fixed a text bug where Spearheart Sentry and Shimmering Sunfish incorrectly capitalized “Spell” in their text.
- Fixed a bug where Nurturing Nature could be played without a valid target.
- Fixed a bug where Red Aspect Essence could deal damage to minions that were already dead.
- Fixed a bug where Twilight Egg token Accelerated Whelp appeared as a base 2/2 in Collection.
- Fixed a bug where Prepared cards copied by Zai the Incredible could be Prepared multiple times.

[Battlegrounds](#Battlegrounds)

- Fixed a visual bug where Skeletal Strafer’s Avenge animation was cut off during combat.
- Fixed a bug where the Full Stats tab did not display the correct Quest when playing as Sire Denathrius.
- Fixed a visual bug where buy cost icons and numbers could appear misaligned in the Tavern.
- Fixed a bug where Bottom Feeder only generated Tavern Spells and not minions.
- You can no longer be offered Designer Eyepatch when you pick Souvenir Stand as your Lesser Trinket.
- Fixed a bug where Timewarped Shivarra could trigger its effect while still in the Tavern.
- Fixed a bug where Rod of Roasting did not apply Pyrobuff when triggered through Yogg-tastic Pastry.

[General](#General)

- Fixed a bug where the “Tavern Regular” achievement could not be claimed if all Hero Skins were already owned.
- Fixed a bug where Arena and Underground Arena public ratings did not update until re-entering the mode.
- Fixed a bug where the first Rewards Track reward could not be claimed on certain Android devices.
- Fixed a bug where the main menu could play without default music.
- Fixed a visual bug where Mythic Dragonqueen Alexstrasza’s Hero Tray clipped into Deathling’s pet area.
