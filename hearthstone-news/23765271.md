> Whoa, now those are some patch notes.

---

This update, going live alongside the patch for [Voyage to the Sunken City](https://playhearthstone.com/expansions-adventures/voyage-to-the-sunken-city), includes numerous balance and system changes across Standard, Wild, Battlegrounds, and more!

#### General Updates

- The Priest class has been removed.
- The following cards have been banned from ALL formats: Forbidden Ancient, Forbidden Flame, Forbidden Healing, Forbidden Shaping, Forbidden Words, Forbidden Ritual.
- If you manage to have in hand the Skull of Gul'dan, two Hands of Gul'dan, and two of the rarely seen Legs of Gul'dan, the enemy hero will now be immediately destroyed.
- All players have been granted one (1) gold.
- The main Collectible Card Sets (3 per year) are being discontinued.
  - *Dev Comment: Alongside this new expansion cadence, we'll be introducing "Mega-Sets"! These card sets are comprised of 135 cards that will be released in-between Mini-Sets.*
- New Keyword: **Taunt Taunt**
  - *Dev Comment: Minions with **Taunt Taunt** protect Taunt minions. You must destroy all **Taunt Taunt** minions before you can attack Taunt minions.*
- The number of deck slots has been reduced to 9
  - *Dev Comment: We've found that players have been too confused by the deck slots increase.*

Boulderfist Ogre

- Old: [Cost 6] 6 Attack, 7 Health. **→ New: [Cost 4] 7 Attack, 7 Health, renamed to Flamewreathed Faceless, reads “Ogreload: (2)”**
  - *Dev Comment: Boulderfist Ogre has been a key player in several decks for its entire existence in Hearthstone. We didn't want to remove the card completely, so we felt a redesign was necessary. Moving the card to Shaman limits which classes have access to it, but it was still too powerful, so we gave it the downside of "Ogreload: (2)" to add more decision making when including it in a deck. Adjusting its stats and cost helped push it into a more balanced space, but the name no longer made sense, leading to the new name you see now.*

#### Battlegrounds Updates

- Explosive Trap has been added to The Great Akazamzarak's Secret Pool.
- Fixed a bug in Battlegrounds where Jandice Barov was sometimes offered in lobbies without tokens.
- Fixed a bug in Battlegrounds where Pyramad was sometimes offered.
- During her first 2 turns, A. F. Kay can no longer Freeze, emote, send messages to friends, change settings, concede, or quit.
- Diablo has returned to the Battlegrounds Hero Pool.
  - *Dev Comment: Diablo returns as permanent addition to Battlegrounds! He was a bit overtuned last time, so we're toning him down a bit in the special combat. (600 Health, down from 666)*
- All Murlocs now have Charge.
  - *Dev Comment:* *People have been asking why Old Murk-Eye has charge, so we're updating all other Murlocs for consistency.*

#### Bug Fixes and Game Improvements

- Fixed a bug where casting Windchill on a Chillwind Yeti would crash the game.
- Fixed a bug where Tyrantus couldn't be targeted by spells or Hero Powers.
- Fixed a bug where certain accounts would always queue into unfavorable matchups.
- Fixed a bug where Luna's Pocket Galaxy could also put lint and loose change into your deck.
- Fixed a bug where Golden cards didn't have +1 Attack.
- Fixed a bug by restoring a damaged Venomous Scorpid to full health.
- Fixed a bug where Crackle would deal less than lethal damage.
- Fixed a bug where Priests would sometimes not generate multiple copies of Soul Mirror. They will now always generate at least 1 extra copy.
- The Priest class has been re-added.
