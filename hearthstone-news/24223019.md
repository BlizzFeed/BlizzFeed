### New Keyword: Rally

Make every attack count with Rally! The new Battlegrounds keyword Rally triggers an effect whenever a Rally minion attacks. It’s primarily featured in Quilboar and Dragons this season, but you’ll find it across all minion types. Several existing minions, like Monstrous Macaw and Yrel, have also been updated to include the Rally keyword.

### Returning Mechanic: Quests

Battlegrounds Quests return in Season 11! On Turn 4 (the 6-Gold turn), choose one of three Quests. Complete it to unlock a powerful Reward that lasts the rest of the game.

### New Keyword for Pirates: Bounty

The Battlegrounds Pirates have plundered a new treasure: the Bounty keyword! Bounties are Tier 3 Tavern spells exclusive to Pirate lobbies. There are five types, with a combined appearance rate equal to two regular Tavern spells. Several new Pirates can generate Bounties, and Proud Privateer cashes them in for big rewards.

### Battlegrounds Combat Update

##### Combat Matchmaking

- While all players are alive, you will now face each opponent in the lobby once before facing any repeat opponents.
- After a player dies or concedes, matchmaking will revert back to the current system except that you cannot face a repeat living opponent for 3 rounds when possible (previously 2 rounds).

##### Combat Speed Adjustment

- The maximum combat speed cap has been lowered.

## Hearthstone Updates

**Dev Comment**: *Well met! We don’t typically roll out balance changes at this point in the patch cycle, but we wanted to use this opportunity to make a few adjustments. First up, we’re tuning down two standout performers since our last adjustments, particularly in high-rank play: Cycle Rogue and Agamaggan Warlock. These changes are aimed at slowing their pace and curbing how consistently they can execute their power plays. We’re also giving a gentle boost to Quest Mage and implementing a beneficial fix for Quest Demon Hunter. As always, we’ll be closely monitoring the meta and will follow up with additional changes in our next regularly scheduled balance patch.*

Playhouse Giant

- Old: 20 mana.
- New: 25 mana.

Cursed Catacombs

- Old: Discover a minion from your deck. Make it Temporary.
- **New**: Discover another card from your deck. Make it Temporary.

![17%20-%20HS_WARLOCK_TLC_451_enUS_CursedCatacombs-117892_NORMAL.png](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/K4PL7DV7OY381753824760483.png)

*The above disenchantable cards will be eligible for full dust refunds for two weeks following Patch 33.2.*

The Forbidden Sequence

- Old: Quest: Discover 8 cards. Reward: The Origin Stone.
- **New**: Quest: Discover 7 cards. Reward: The Origin Stone.

Gorishi Colossus (Demon Hunter Quest reward)

- Old: Effect only works on your turn.
- **New**: Effect also works on opponent's turn.

**Dev Comment**:*Community feedback highlighted a mismatch between the text and effect of the Demon Hunter Quest Reward, which was causing some confusion. Faced with the choice of updating the text to match the game play or adjusting the gameplay to match the text, we chose to revise the gameplay to improve both the card's clarity and effectiveness.*

## Introducing Dual Class Tavern Brawl

![20%20-%20HS_33p2_Patch_Notes_InBlog_TavernBrawlChalkboard_DualClass_760x570_SW01.jpg](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/FE85LYUKMP761753824760840.jpg)

In Dual Class Tavern Brawl, running from August 13 to 20, players get to build a deck with cards from ANY two classes from ANY standard expansion! You'll start by choosing a primary class, which determines your Hero Power and the first class you can add cards from. Then, you can add cards from any one additional class. This fusion allows for creative and unexpected synergies, enabling strategies that wouldn't normally be possible in standard play. Double the flavor, double the chaos!

## Wild Heroic Tavern Brawl

![21%20-%20HS_33p2_Patch_Notes_InBlog_HeroicBrawliseum_Wild_760x380_SW01.jpg](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/QH7BIFE7KJBL1753824760999.jpg)

Heroic Tavern Brawl returns from August 6 to 13—and this time things are getting Wild! Pay an entry fee, create a Wild deck using any cards in your collection, and compete in high-stakes games until reaching either 12 wins or 3 losses, with prizing based on how well you perform.

## In-Game Event: Umbra's Uprising!

![23%20-%20HS_33p2_Patch_Notes_InBlog_Event_UmbrasUprising_760x435_SW02.png](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/5SDML2IHL6JU1753824761279.png)

Umbra's Uprising runs from August 5 to 26 and follows the duplicitous Umbra as she betrays her fellow explorers and allies with the Cult of the Damned to raise undead dinosaurs and attack the Lost City of Un'Goro! You’ll complete co-op event quests—earning progress both when you meet the quest criteria and when your opponent does—to unlock rewards along the event track. Complete the entire event track to unlock Staff of the Endbringer (regular and golden), 4 The Lost City of Un'Goro Packs, 2 Random Epic Cards from The Lost City of Un’Goro, and the King Bone Krusher Hearthstone Hero Skin.

## Bonus In-Game Event: Lost City Fire Festival! (1 Week Long)

![24%20-%20HS_33p2_Patch_Notes_InBlog_Event_LostCityFireFestival_760x435_SW02.png](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/9DNVVOJHRKVU1753824761354.png)
