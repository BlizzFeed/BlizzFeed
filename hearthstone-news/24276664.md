Launching on July 7, Hearthstone’s next expansion follows a ragtag group of outlaws as they hatch a daring plan to break out of Dalaran’s maximum-security prison. Get ready to *Escape from Violet Hold!*

In this unprecedented expansion, you’ll break the very rules of Hearthstone with brand new mechanics. Running duplicate Legendaries, sneaking minions onto your opponent’s side of the board, and drawing an extra card each turn might sound illegal, but in this expansion, you’re playing by your own rules.

The mastermind behind this heist is **Vanessa VanCleef**, daughter of notorious Defias Brotherhood leader Edwin VanCleef. She's assembling the best of the worst for the ultimate prison break. With magically sealed cells, armored guards, and a prison that shifts into an arcane maze, escaping the Hold is said to be impossible. Vanessa and her crew plan to prove otherwise.

As if the traps and guards weren’t enough, the legendary Watcher **Maiev Shadowsong** has been summoned as the new Warden. You didn’t think getting out would be easy, did you?

## Rules Are Made to be Broken

Every class gets a Legendary **Rulebreaker** minion that warps a core Hearthstone rule in different ways. They were locked up for breaking the rules, and now Vanessa is pulling strings behind the scenes, arranging prison transfers to assemble the perfect crew and put those talents to work for the breakout. These cards push the limits further than ever before and open up entirely new ways to play.

### Hogger’s Flying Off the Chain

![WARRIOR_JAIL_384_enUS_ChainbreakerHogger-126002_NORMAL.png](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/RQYU04XKCFXP1779832765329.png)

At the start of the game, Chainbreaker Hogger duplicates all the Legendaries in your deck. Those one-copy limits? Gone. Hogger turns your strongest cards into double trouble, opening up previously unthinkable strategies.

## New Keyword: Prepare

The new **Prepare** keyword lets you set up explosive turns in advance. Drag a Prepare card onto your deck to spend your remaining mana and reduce its cost by that amount, plus one. It returns to your hand and can be played on a future turn. For example, if you have three mana left, it becomes four mana cheaper. When you play that Prepared card on a later turn, you can surprise your opponent with a much bigger play than expected!

![NEUTRAL_JAIL_407_enUS_VanessatheRingleader-124150_NORMAL.png](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/TP4W5UWH1AR31779832760523.png)

As a strategic mastermind, Vanessa VanCleef perfectly embodies the new Prepare keyword. With Edwin VanCleef out of the picture, it was his daughter Vanessa who rebuilt the Defias Brotherhood. So when she found herself behind bars, she was the one person with the leadership chops to wrangle a crew of misfits bold enough to orchestrate a breakout.

![HS_35p6_Expansion_Announcement_InBlog_VanessaPrepare_GIF_760x428_SW01.gif](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/9XJ851MBBU671779832696876.gif)

With a variety of effects at your disposal, Prepare will have you ready for anything when navigating the labyrinth of halls, chambers, and traps that is the Violet Hold.

### Case the Hold

The Violet Hold is a sprawling prison full of surprises. Scope out the forbidden artifact lockup, the medical ward, and heavily enchanted containment units holding some very dangerous magical entities. Down in the Demon Vaults, Moragg has been just waiting for a chance to break loose.![WARLOCK_JAIL_906_enUS_Moragg-124073_NORMAL.png](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/DZXEDKB5WUUS1779832765073.png)

Moragg is no mastermind, just an Observer demon who enjoys causing mayhem by opening cells and unleashing other demons. With Prepare, you can unleash them when it benefits you most.

![HS_35p6_Expansion_Announcement_InBlog_Moragg_GIF_760x428_SW01.gif](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/PXRKZU9YYL1K1779832747887.gif)

## The Warden Initiates Lockdown

Of course, no one is breaking out of the Violet Hold without getting past the Warden. And when things spiral out of control like this, they call in the best: Warden Maiev.

![NEUTRAL_JAIL_850_enUS_WardenMaiev-126600_NORMAL.png](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/NBVNUWX3OSJH1779832762733.png)

She instantly traps anything you play, locking it down and securing it for one turn.

![HS_35p6_Expansion_Announcement_InBlog_WardenMaiev_1_GIF_760x428_SW01.gif](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/T52UBODEKUCV1779832760422.gif)

Then it returns to play with a big buff. It can be handy to protect a card for a turn or let your smaller minions do a few push-ups before going into battle.

![HS_35p6_Expansion_Announcement_InBlog_WardenMaiev_2_GIF_760x428_SW01.gif](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/NFQJ3ZXW28ZH1779832762044.gif)

Maiev Shadowsong is the warden who kept Illidan imprisoned for *10,000 years*. Not exactly who you want guarding the exit when you're trying to fly the coop.

## Become a Master of Disguise

With the new Disguised minions, you can, for the first time in Hearthstone history, **play minions on your opponent’s side of the board**.

For the Warrior class, minions often become enraged when they take damage, so Disguised Watchman’s ability feels right at home on your side of the board.

![WARRIOR_JAIL_455_enUS_DisguisedWatchman-127260_NORMAL.png](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/28UARHF554PE1779832765528.png)

But if your opponent decides to go low and wide, you can play her onto their board to unleash a sneaky attack.

![HS_35p6_Expansion_Announcement_InBlog_DisguisedWatchman_GIF_760x428_SW01.gif](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/EORI3RLXRYMM1779832492785.gif)

## Pre-Release Tavern Brawl

Sneak into the Pre-Release Tavern Brawl to try *Escape from Violet Hold* early. We’re rolling out a new format\* where you build a deck using only cards from the set, and **you can use cards from any class together in one deck!** Everyone starts with trial copies of all *Escape from Violet Hold* Common cards\*\*, which you can use alongside any other *Escape from Violet Hold* cards you open from packs.

\* *In addition to no class restrictions, there will be no Rune restrictions. The Discover pool includes both Standard and Escape from Violet Hold.*
