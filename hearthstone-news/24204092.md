> Battlegrounds updates, bug fixes, and the Into the Emerald Dream Mini-Set, Embers of the World Tree, go live today!

---

Battlegrounds updates, bug fixes, and the Into the Emerald Dream Mini-Set, Embers of the World Tree, go live today!

### Hearthstone Updates

![HS_32p2_MiniSet_Announce_InBlog_GoldenMiniSet_RN_760x428_SW01.jpg](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/qw/QW0U5BNKQT2E1745448968207.jpg)

Scorch your opponents with the Into the Emerald Dream Mini-Set, Embers of the World Tree, unlocking today! The Embers of the World Tree has 38 all-new cards. Get them in Into the Emerald Dream packs, or as a complete 72-card\* Mini-Set. Get the normal version for $14.99 or 2000 Gold, or get the all-Golden version for $69.99 or 10,000 Gold. The all-Golden version comes with a bonus Diamond copy of the Fyrakk, the Blazing Legendary minion! You can see cards from the Embers of the World Tree Mini-Set in the official [Card Library](https://hearthstone.blizzard.com/cards).

*\* 4 Legendary cards, 2 Epic card (x2), 16 Rare cards (x2), and 16 Common cards (x2).*

*Dev Comment: There are no Hearthstone mode balance changes in this patch because we’d like the to see how the new mini-set cards influence the shape of the format before making other changes. The next balance window is coming shortly after launch, which is where we’ll take a look at meta outliers, new or existing. We also have another balance window before the next expansion, in early June, in case we need to make additional adjustments then.*

### Battlegrounds Updates

*Dev Comment: This set of balance changes is primarily aimed at strengthening underperforming compositions and reducing the power of the two strongest compositions in the current meta, Tavern spell Mechs and End-of-Turn Elementals. At the same time, we generally aim to have at least two different options within each type, so we’re also buffing the Tavern-buff cards in Elementals and returning Kangor’s Apprentice to the minion pool. We also made a few Tier swaps for Naga and Demons, in anticipation of their relative power after these adjustments.*

*We’ve also made a ton of adjustments to Trinkets and are trying out a rule change to Greater Trinket offerings. We’ll be watching game data and player feedback after this change to decide if we should make this a permanent change.*

#### Trinket Updates ([Skip to Hero and Minion Updates](#hero-updates))

##### Trinket Rules Update

The Greater Trinket slot that used to have a 50% chance to offer an untyped Trinket and a 50% chance of a discounted Trinket will now always offer an untyped Trinket. While the original intent of the "pivot" chance was to create opportunities for novel gameplay, we think the Turn 9 timing in the current meta will likely make a neutral Trinket the more compelling choice on average.
