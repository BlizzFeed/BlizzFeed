#### Mercenaries Updates

**GENERAL UPDATES**

- You will now be able to set different Equipment and Portrait loadouts for your different Parties using the same Mercenary; you will no longer be able to change these loadouts in the middle of a Bounty.
- When you get offered a Bounty Treasure for a Merc that already has a Treasure, you will be now offered the option to upgrade your current Treasure, instead of just keeping or replacing it.
- Chi-Ji is now available through all the normal ways of acquiring a Mercenary.

**NEW LIMITED TIME EVENT**

![Leeroy Jenkins is coming to Mercenaries](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/l9/L9DKF9LYBSEP1646701213288.jpg)

Time’s up! Let’s do this! On March 22, Leeroy Jenkins is charging into Mercenaries with his own special event. The event consists of 10 special Tasks starring Leeroy and the Black Dragonflight! For the first five days of the event, you can get up to two event Tasks each day, but you have until April 5 to complete all 10 Tasks. Complete all 10 Tasks to get over 750 Mercenary Coins, the Leeroy Jenkins Legendary Mercenary Card, a Leeroy Jenkins Golden Portrait, and a Leeroy Jenkins Diamond Portrait. This event is not for the faint of heart! Orcs, Humans, Dragons, and Fighters will all be needed as you form your raiding parties for this event.

#### Bug Fixes & Game Improvements

- Added the ability to play cards directly from your “mini-hand” on mobile, instead of first needing to tap to enlarge your hand.
- The Hunter Questline will no longer be progressed if the Hunter is damaged by a Cast When Drawn card that their opponent shuffled into the Hunter’s deck (such as Bombs or Agonies).
- Full-Blown Evil can now be played when there are no enemy minions for it to hit.
- Fixed a bug where Whelp Bonker was counted as a Whelp by Raid Boss Onyxia.
- Fixed a bug where Xyrella, the Devout’s Battlecry would not count Deathrattle minions that were Silenced before they died.
- Fixed a bug where Pride Seeker’s discount would not be consumed if the card played was a Choose One minion that transformed into a different minion when played.
- Fixed a bug where Pathmaker was creating game history tiles every turn while you had a Choose One card in hand.
- Fixed a bug where Cult Neophyte was increasing the cost of a Need for Greed that was drawn that turn.
- Fixed a bug where Tamsin’s Phylactery would not grant Rustwix Envoy’s Deathrattle effect.
- Fixed a bug where if you discovered a Ranked card from Palm Reading (such as Condemn), and the spelled ranked up, the spell would not get the Palm Reading discount.
- Fixed a bug where if Improved Ice Trap was played before Ice Trap, both would trigger off the same spell. Now, whichever is played first will trigger and the version played second will be saved for the next spell.
- Fixed a bug where Sir Finley and Sir Finley of the Sands’s effects could not be copied by Brilliant Macaw.
- Fixed a bug where Pilfered Power would sometimes refresh Mana Crystals instead of giving empty Mana Crystals when played with over 10 Mana Crystals.
- Fixed a bug where Unexpected Results did not account for temporary Spell Damage buffs.
- Friendly Wager, Saturday C’Thuns, I’ll Take That, and Spirit Swap hero powers will no longer be able to be triggered multiple times by Sous Chef and Elder Taggawag Buddies.
- Fixed a visual bug where minions stolen by the Loyal Henchman Buddy would sometimes show as Golden, but function like the plain copies they were supposed to be.
- Fixed a bug causing visual issues with Aranna Starseeker’s Buddy Meter the turn she transforms.
- Mercenaries event-locked Bounties will now include a “Coming Soon” message when selected.
- Updated Chi-Ji and Yu’lon’s Task 17s to both better match their respective Abilities.
- Fixed a bug where special event Tasks that required you to beat a Bounty with at least 3 Mercs of a particular type would not count if you reached the boss of that Bounty with less than that number of Mercs still alive.
- Fixed a bug where Balinda Stonehearth’s Frost Burn would not count as an Ability of either version’s spell school.
- Fixed a bug causing the Mercenaries Training Grounds search function to be unresponsive on tablet devices.
- Corrected a visual bug where Eudora’s “Prepare the Cannons” Ability stated a Cannon with 5 Attack would be summoned, instead of 0 Attack.
- Fixed a bug where Sinestra’s Wracking Swipe would activate off her own being a Dragon instead of checking for other Dragons.
- Fixed a bug where Gruul’s Dragonclaw Fist gave the wrong buff while an enemy Dragon was present.
- Fixed a bug where Edwin’s Assisted Strike Ability did not respect Taunt.
- Fixed a bug that sometimes prevented Duels achievements from tracking properly.
- Fixed a bug where the Warden’s Insight Duels Treasure did not refill all your mana if your maximum mana was more than 10.
- Fixed a bug where if Transfer Student was in your starting Duels deck, its effect would not update with each Board.
- Fixed a bug causing players to get stuck in Tombs of Terror if they choose the Sr. Tomb Diver Signature Treasure.
- The Floor is Lava and Spiked Arms will now give their Attack buff first and then deal their damage, to be consistent with cards like Inner Rage and Cruel Taskmaster.
- Added missing Mini-Set achievements for Rogue and Demon Hunter.
- The mysterious achievement that was added in 22.4, but could not be completed at that time, can now be completed.
- Fixed a bug with the “Future So Bright, Gotta Wear Shades” achievement where it was not tracking properly.
- Fixed a bug causing “phantom” achievement notifications.
- Fixed a bug causing the Collection Manager “NEW” tag to refresh after each time logging back into the client.
- Misc. other bug fixes, text improvements, and performance upgrades.
