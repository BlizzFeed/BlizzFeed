# Across the Timeways Rewards Track Refresh

- URL: https://news.blizzard.com/en-gb/article/24241547/across-the-timeways-rewards-track-refresh
![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/p7/P7J0RVFOX0W51761089705114.jpg)

> Collect timeless treasures from Across the Timeways with the new Rewards Track!

---

The sands of time shift and reveal a vision of the future: a brand-new Rewards Track! When the Rewards Track refreshes with the launch of [*Across the Timeways*](https://hearthstone.blizzard.com/news/24226328/warcraft-heroes-past-and-future-unite-in-across-the-timeways-hearthstone-s-next-expansion), all players will automatically be granted any unclaimed rewards on the current free Rewards Track and the Tavern Pass track (if acquired), before a completely new Across the Timeways Rewards Track takes its place.

## Across the Timeways Rewards Track

The free Rewards Track for *Across the Timeways* will include 14 Golden cards and the Chrono-Lord Deios Legendary card\*! Other spoils from across the ages include a random Legendary Fabled card, random Legendary card, random Epic card, Tavern Tickets, and a bounty of Gold and Packs.

![HS_34p0_RewardsTrack_InBlog_FreeTrack_760x428_LZO02.jpg](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/7c/7CYMQA0B6LI81762479301915.jpg)

\* *These 14 Golden cards (two copies of each of 7 cards from the set) and the Chrono-Lord Deios Legendary card earned on the Rewards Track can’t be crafted or disenchanted.*

## Across the Timeways Tavern Pass

With the launch of *Across the Timeways*, a new Tavern Pass arrives packed with cosmetic rewards, XP boosts, Signature cards, and expansion packs!

![HS_34p0_RewardsTrack_InBlog_TavernPass_760x428.jpg](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/HDNZWBD2LZB81761086094663.jpg)

Getting the Tavern Pass at any time will immediately grant you a 10% XP Boost that lasts for the rest of the *Across the Timeways* expansion, plus all items up to your current level on the paid track. Unlock your rewards even faster!

As you progress along the Tavern Pass during the *Across the Timeways* expansion, you can claim these timeless treasures\*:

- Additional XP Boosts
- Chromie Diamond Legendary card
- Azure Queen Sindragosa Signature Legendary Fabled Card
  - Azure King Malygos Signature Legendary Fabled Token Card
  - Azure Oathstone Signature Legendary Fabled Token Card
- Mister Clocksworth Golden Legendary card
- Chrono-Lord Deios Golden Legendary card
- 2 Circadiamancer Golden Epic cards
- 2 Futuristic Forefather Signature Epic cards
- 2 Paltry Flutterwing Signature Rare cards
- 2 Hourglass Attendant Signature Common cards
- 1 new cosmetic coin
- 1 new card back
- 8 new Hero Skins
- 2 Golden Standard Packs
- 4 Across the Timeways Packs

\* *All Tavern Pass card grants are unable to be crafted or disenchanted.*

## New Cosmetic Coins

Two new cosmetic coins will be added with *Across the Timeways*: the Clockwork Coin and the Sundial Coin. The Clockwork Coin is earned by reaching level 90 on the Rewards Track with the Tavern Pass. The Sundial Coin is earned by completing the Achievement for collecting 145 Across the Timeways cards.

![HS_NEUTRAL_TIME_COIN2_enUS_TheCoin-121674_NORMAL.png](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/ZSKYWQYQOQES1761173327885.png)![HS_NEUTRAL_TIME_COIN1_enUS_TheCoin-121673_NORMAL.png](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/120TQLWES04J1761173327894.png)

*Across the Timeways* will have a Mini-Set, coming in Patch 34.4, that will make it easier to complete the collection-based Achievements. We’ll share more information about that Mini-Set before it goes live. In the meantime, check out the [34.0 Patch Notes](https://news.blizzard.com/article/24243857) for more information about everything else coming soon!
