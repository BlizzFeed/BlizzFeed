Whizbang’s about to open the doors to his workshop, where a new Rewards Track awaits! When the Rewards Track refreshes with the Whizbang’s Workshop launch, all players will automatically be granted any unclaimed rewards on the current free Rewards Track and the Tavern Pass track (if purchased), before a completely new Whizbang’s Workshop Rewards Track takes its place.

![First Page of the Whizbang's Workshop Rewards Track](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_folder_media/VCFOHLLC457B1709242948055.png)

#### Whizbang’s Workshop Rewards Track

The free Rewards Track for Whizbang’s Workshop will include packs from Whizbang’s Workshop, a random Epic card, two random Legendary cards, Tavern Tickets, a new card back, and lots of Gold! There are also 14 Golden cards along with the Li’Na Shop Manager Legendary card from the set!\*

![Promo image of Whizbang's Workshop Free Track](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_folder_media/7A7CHHTXHJ6V1709242947994.jpg)

*\* These 14 Golden cards (two copies of each of seven cards from the set) and the Li'Na Shop Manager Legendary card earned from the Rewards Track can’t be crafted or disenchanted.*

#### Whizbang’s Workshop Tavern Pass

With the launch of Whizbang’s Workshop, a new Tavern Pass will be made available for purchase, featuring a toychest full of fun new cosmetic rewards and XP Boosts!

![Promo image of Tavern Pass](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_folder_media/4G46X74RPX991709242948162.jpg)

Purchasing the Tavern Pass at any time will immediately give you a 10% XP Boost that lasts the rest of the duration of the expansion, plus all items up to your current level on the paid track. As you progress along the Tavern Pass during the Whizbang’s Workshop expansion, you can earn: additional XP Boosts; the Joymancer Jepetto Diamond Legendary card; the Li’Na Shop Manager and Colifero the Artist Golden Legendary cards; 2 Playhouse Giant Golden Epic cards; 2 Corridor Sleeper Signature Epic cards; 2 Watercolor Artist Signature Epic cards; 2 Rambunctious Stuffy Signature Common cards; a new cosmetic coin and card back; a new Hero Skin for each class; and 2 Golden Standard Packs!\*

\* *All Tavern Pass card grants are unable to be crafted or disenchanted.*

![Whizbang's Workshop Divider](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_folder_media/7V9UE5C4PJ901707438828988.png)

#### Achievements Updates

Unlike prior expansions, Showdown in the Badlands Achievements will not stop awarding XP with Patch 29.0 and will, instead, keep granting XP on the next Rewards Track! There will be fewer new Achievements in Whizbang’s Workshop, but each of those new Achievements will each grant more XP than main-set Achievements have typically given in the past.

Additionally, the progression on the Legendary Collector Achievement will now grant copies of two Signature cards, instead of a Diamond card:

![Toyrannasaurus Signature Card](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_folder_media/4V6OWB7MK0GO1709242974687.png)
![Lab Patron Signature Card](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_folder_media/U8BSW2373XWN1709242974860.png)

#### Flip for Two New Cosmetic Coins

Two new cosmetic coins have been added with Whizbang’s Workshop: the Wondrous Workshop Coin and the Candy Coin. The Wondrous Workshop Coin is earned by reaching level 90 on the Rewards Track with the Tavern Pass. The Candy Coin is earned by completing the Achievement for having 145 Whizbang’s Workshop cards.

![Workshop Coin](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_folder_media/2JQCBRH89CA61709242974681.png)
![Candy Coin](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_folder_media/EHDZ6732IXAP1709242974687.png)

Whizbang’s Workshop will have a 38-card Mini-Set that will make it easier to complete the collection-based Achievements. We’ll share more information about that Mini-Set in the coming months.
