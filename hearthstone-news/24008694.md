Harth is pulling out all the stops this BlizzCon, opening up the Tavern and inviting some fun friends to the show. Here’s what you need to know as you plan your visit!

#### Show Schedule

BlizzCon will be bustling with a jam-packed Opening Ceremony, presentations from all your favorite games, and more! Hearthstone fans won’t want to miss the Opening Ceremony or the **Hearthstone: What’s Next panel, on the Main Stage on Friday, at 4:30 p.m. (PDT)**—including discussion of both Traditional Hearthstone and Battlegrounds. The action’s not all on the stage! The Tavern’s always a place to gather, make new friends, and have a great time. Here are the other activities and attractions that will be taking place for Hearthstone throughout BlizzCon:

Come check out Hearthstone’s demo area for some fun, new, and exciting gameplay. We’re not ready to share what this will entail just yet, but you won’t want to miss it!

***Influencer Meet and Greet***

Come meet—and greet—your favorite Hearthstone streamers and content creators in the designated Meet and Greet area!

***Duel-a-Dev***

The fan favorite is back! Meet one of Hearthstone’s developers and challenge them to a one-on-one face-off, to show them how it’s really done. Duel-a-Dev will be taking place on both days of BlizzCon, in the Warcraft Tavern area, so come on by whenever you’re up for a game!

***Developer Signings***

After you’ve taken on your favorite dev, catch up with them at one of three dedicated Developer Signing sessions throughout BlizzCon:

- Friday, 2:00 p.m. - 4:00 p.m. (PT)
- Friday, 5:30 p.m. - 6:30 p.m. (PT)
- Saturday, 11:00 a.m. - 2:00 p.m. (PT)

Posters will be provided for devs to sign—check in for each signing session, since the groups will change up throughout the show.

***Awesome Artists***

Sometimes you need the perfect draw. Sometimes you need the perfect drawing! Check out some of your favorite Hearthstone artists in the Artist Nook, open throughout BlizzCon.

#### Don’t Miss the Show

BlizzCon’s just around the corner, but it’s not too late to get your [in-person tickets for the show](https://www.axs.com/blizzcon2023) or your [BlizzCon Collection](https://us.shop.battle.net/product/blizzcon-collection) of in-game goodies! We hope you’ll join us, because we can’t wait to see you in the Tavern. For those of you who can’t make it, you’ll still be able to watch for free on Blizzard’s [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/@Blizzard/featured) and [Twitch](https://www.twitch.tv/blizzard) accounts. Head on over to [BlizzCon.com](https://www.blizzcon.com) for more information, including the full [BlizzCon schedule](https://blizzcon.com/news/24021890) and more!
