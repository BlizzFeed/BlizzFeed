# Tune In: Battlegrounds Season 12 Developer Streams

- URL: https://news.blizzard.com/en-gb/article/24244882/tune-in-battlegrounds-season-12-developer-streams
![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/uz/UZYG8FUW1B201763681301291.png)

> Here are all the details you need to watch the upcoming stream revealing Battlegrounds Season 12!

---

Battlegrounds Season 12 is almost here, and we’ll be kicking things off with a series of streams to showcase all the new features! Join the Battlegrounds developers and special guests as they reveal a fresh spin on the Tavern inspired by Across the Timeways and give you your first look at the new cards in action. The new season begins December 2.

## The Tavern at the Edge of Time Reveal Stream

![HS_34p2_Amplification_Reveal_TuneIn_InBlog_760x428_DB01.png](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/J72JRREQJMAE1763681407471.png)

Join Battlegrounds Lead Designer Mitchell Loewen and our special guest Shadybunny as they guide you through the biggest Battlegrounds patch of all time!

- **When**: December 1 at 10:00 a.m. PST
- **Where**: Hearthstone’s [Twitch](https://www.twitch.tv/playhearthstone) and [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/Hearthstone) streams
- **Drops**: Get 1 Year of the Raptor Pack and 4 Battlegrounds Tokens by watching on Twitch or YouTube (not both)

## The Tavern at the Edge of Time Dev Co-Streams

![HS_34p2_Amplification_DevCo_LineUp_TuneIn_InBlog_760x428_DB01.png](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/S93LS799KX9R1763681410501.png)

### Dogdog and BoarControl

Watch Battlegrounds Designer George Webb (BoarControl) as he joins DogDog on his stream for a one-hour Q&A and play session. Will DogDog take BoarControl's play advice? Let's find out.

- **When**: December 2 at 2:00 p.m. PST
- **Where**: [DogDog’s Twitch stream](https://www.twitch.tv/dogdog)
- **Drops**: 2 Battlegrounds Tokens

### HapaBear and Mitchell Loewen

Check out Battlegrounds Lead Designer Mitchell Lowen joining HapaBear on her stream for a one-hour Q&A and play session. Let's see if Mitchell can still talk through the cards with Hapa's APM!

- **When**: December 2 at 3:00 p.m. PST
- **Where**: [HapaBear’s Twitch stream](https://www.twitch.tv/hapabear)
- **Drops**: 2 Battlegrounds Tokens
