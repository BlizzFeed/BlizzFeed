# Winter Veil Brings Old Gods to Battlegrounds

![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/tu/TU608113659B1607553964883.jpg)

---

Though many attempt to safeguard their sanity within Battlegrounds, nowhere is free from the corruption of the Old Gods! N’Zoth, C’Thun, and Y’Shaarj are headed to the Battlegrounds as playable Heroes alongside 10 new minions! [Read the 19.2 patch notes to learn more.](https://playhearthstone.com/en-us/news/23584141)

![HS_19p2_BG_heroes_EK01.jpg](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/w5/W5NQ9K45ZWQ21607559550018.jpg)

#### Darkmoon Prizes

We’re introducing a new limited-time mechanic—Darkmoon Prizes! Every four turns you’ll Discover a Darkmoon Prize and add it to your hand. As the game progresses, you’ll be offered increasingly powerful Darkmoon Prizes to play with. From immediately impactful buffs to game-lasting effects, Darkmoon Prizes add a new twist to every Battlegrounds match!

#### Winter Veil Quests

![HS_19p2_DMF_09_Winter_Veil_4Pack_Event_Social_TW_1280x720.jpg](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/iu/IU0HHDDU66IY1607551794674.jpg)

Winter Veil is underway! Get into the holiday spirit by completing a Legendary Quest chain that rewards a total of 4 *Madness at the Darkmoon Faire* card packs! These two quests will appear one at a time on your Quests Log and will not override any of your other quests.

#### Gift Exchange Tavern Brawl

![HS_TB_GiftExchange_760x572_EK01.jpg](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/m9/M9G20JDDIZGV1607553946717.jpg)

The Great Father is dropping crates on the battlefield! Get a gift each time you smash your opponent’s crate or your own! Completing the Gift Exchange Tavern Brawl will reward the Winter Veil Treat card back! If you’ve already collected the card back, you’ll earn 1 Classic card pack instead.

#### Wacky Waxy Winter Veil Tavern Brawl

![HS_TB_WaxyBrawl_760x572_EK01.jpg](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/d6/D6KCBXYJG2AI1607553929630.jpg)

Greatfather Kobold got a little carried away with the presents. He’s wrapped up the strongest creatures and Heroes in Azeroth! Free them! Completing the Wacky Waxy Winter Veil Tavern Brawl will reward 1 Classic card pack.

#### Winter Veil Wreath Card Back Returns

![HS_19p2_DMF_06_WVCB_Social_Twitter_1280x720_JS01.jpg](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/lw/LW4HESTRO9YZ1607553900990.jpg)

The Winter Veil Wreath card back is returning to the in-game shop! You can purchase this festive card back for 500 Gold of $2.99 at any point between December 15-29.

#### Book of Heroes Garrosh

![10_16_HS_DMF_Blog_19p0_PatchNotes_enUS_InlineBlogImage_BoH_Garrosh_760x500_JY_v01.jpg](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/yk/YKUUQAL7OZHI1607547862930.jpg)

Follow Garrosh’s journey to becoming the Warlord of the Warsong clan in the next installment of Hearthstone Book of Heroes, releasing on December 16! Defeating all 8 bosses in this linear adventure will reward 1 Warrior Pack, containing only Warrior cards from Standard!

#### Login Rewards

![HS_19p2_Login_Reward_Social_TW_1280x720.jpg](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/le/LEXYMM7FPRZ71607553868355.jpg)

Finally, we’re giving all players 500 Gold and 5 Madness at the Darkmoon Faire card packs just for logging in after the 19.2 patch goes live!\* [There are a ton of balance updates and game improvements included in this patch as well—check out the full patch notes for details.](https://playhearthstone.com/en-us/news/23584141) Happy Feast of Winter Veil!\
*\*All rewards will only be granted once per account. Must log in before January 18, 2021.*
