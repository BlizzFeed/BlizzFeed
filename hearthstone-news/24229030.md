## Day of Rebirth Hatches on September 2!

The Day of Rebirth is a vibrant Tortollan festival in Un’Goro Crater that marks the start of dinosaur-hatching season and celebrates the cycle of life. At its heart is the ancient Egg of Khelos, a mighty turtle-like beast incubating for generations and finally ready to hatch. The jungle comes alive with bonfires, parades, fireworks, and storytelling, ushering in the Day of Rebirth Mini-Set, launching September 2!

![HS_33p4_Patch_Notes_InBlog_GoldenMiniSet_LO_760x428_SW01.jpg](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/o6/O67JUUXIDR731756826028196.jpg)

The Day of Rebirth Mini-Set includes 38 new cards. Get them in The Lost City of Un’Goro packs or as a complete 72-card\* Mini-Set. Pick up the normal version for $14.99 or 2000 Gold, or go all-in with the all-Golden version for $69.99 or 10,000 Gold, which includes a bonus Diamond copy of the Beast Speaker Taka Legendary minion! You can see all of the new cards in the official [Card Library](https://hearthstone.blizzard.com/en-us/cards).

\* *Includes 1 copy of each Legendary card and 2 copies of each Common, Rare, and Epic card in the Mini-Set.*

## Arena Updates

With Patch 33.4, all ongoing Arena runs will end and a new Arena season will begin.

The updated draft pool now consists of these sets:

- The Lost City of Un'Goro + Day of Rebirth Mini-Set
- Perils in Paradise
- Festival of Legends
- March of the Lich King
- Murder at Castle Nathria
- Core

Escape the Underfel has been removed from the draft pool.

For this season, the guaranteed Legendary pick during the second Redraft on Loss in Underground Arena has been replaced with a normal draft pick.

We've made a small improvement to Underground rewards at 9 and 11 wins:

- 9 Wins: Now includes a guaranteed Pack\*.
- 11 Wins: Instead of granting a random reward of either Gold or 3 Packs, players now receive 1 guaranteed Pack plus a random reward of either Gold or 2 additional Packs.

[Please visit our Arena information page for more details on rewards](https://support.blizzard.com/article/32545).

\* *Card Packs for 9 and 11 wins will be from the latest Hearthstone expansion.*

## Day of Rebirth Event

## Hallow's End Special Event

![HS_33p4_Patch_Notes_InBlog_Event_HallowsEnd_760x435_SW01.png](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/ZWA3ZQK4D12G1756318842543.png)

Hallow’s End returns to Hearthstone with a chilling celebration of tricks and treats! Complete the event quests (if you’re not too afraid) to earn 2 The Lost City of Un'Goro Packs, 2 Random The Lost City of Un'Goro Common cards, 2 Random The Lost City of Un'Goro Rare cards, 2 Random The Lost City of Un'Goro Epic cards, a Random The Lost City of Un'Goro Legendary card, and the Nightmare Zail Hearthstone Hero Skin. This event runs from September 23 to October 14.

## Ladder Ready Deck Updates

“Ladder Ready Decks” is the new name for “Loaner Decks.” Ladder Ready Decks for new and returning players\* are getting updated with the launch of Patch 33.4. These new decks are tuned to let you immediately jump onto the ladder and include cards as new as The Lost City of Un’Goro main set!

\* *Returning players who have been away for 90 or more days are eligible for a free deck just for logging in. Returning players can claim these returning decks each time they are eligible. New players are eligible for their free deck after completing the tutorial and Apprentice Track.*

## New Arrivals: Void-Touched Cosmetics Now in the Shop

![HS_33p4_WOW_Midnight_Collab_Phoenix_Franchise_ArtOnly_1920x1080_MM05.png](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/TBVLLKLR8EG61756318846125.png)

From September 2 to 30, the shop will feature new items inspired by The War Within and Midnight. These include the Alleria Epic Bartender Skin and Xal’atath Epic Bartender Skin, each bringing a touch of the Void to Battlegrounds. ## Rabbit’ath Hops Into Darkmoon Faire Treasures

![HS_33p4_Patch_Notes_InBlog_DMFTPrizeSpread_760X428_SW02.png](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/VKC37QSHA7GW1756318842186.png)

Hearthstone’s newest interactive Pet, Rabbit’ath, is a mischievous, Void-touched bunny that reacts to your gameplay, emotes, and more with sassy animations and sounds. Pet her, feed her Void-berries, or play with her Xal’atath dagger toy to see her burrow under the gameboard! Available from September 2 to 23, Rabbit’ath is one of 10 prizes in the returning Darkmoon Faire Treasures, a random pull system that also rewards Signature cards, Hero Skins, and more. [Read more details on Darkmoon Faire Treasures pulls and odds.](https://support.blizzard.com/article/000380896)

## Bug Fixes and Game Improvements

- [Hearthstone] Improved Spirit of the Mountain’s display of played minion types.
- [Hearthstone] Fixed a visual bug where Volcanic Thrasher’s VFX didn’t appear correctly from the opponent’s view.
- [Hearthstone] Fixed a visual bug where upgrading Ritual of the New Moon was visible to the opponent.
- [Hearthstone] Fixed a visual bug where minions summoned by Cycle of Hatred showed mana cost on board.
- [Hearthstone] Fixed a visual bug where Axe of the Forefathers VFX and SFX played on the enemy Hero.
- [Hearthstone] Fixed a visual bug where Twilight Mender's card creation had a delayed VFX while Baron Rivendare was on the board.
- [Hearthstone] Fixed a visual bug where Everbloom’s come-into-play VFX didn’t display.
- [Hearthstone] Fixed a visual bug where playing a Hero Card while using the Arachnid Kerrigan Hero Skin caused the Hero Power asset to appear darker.
- [Battlegrounds] Fixed a bug where Rapid Reanimation summoned minions in the wrong place.
- [Pets] Fixed several bugs related to Pets: added in the missing XP bar shadow; made it so disconnections don’t revert XP progress if Pet leveled up during the game; and corrected issue where Pet clipped into the Battlegrounds Murloc Hideaway Board.
