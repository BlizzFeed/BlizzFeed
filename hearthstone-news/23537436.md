# 18.4.2 Patch Notes

- URL: https://hearthstone.blizzard.com/en-gb/news/23537436/18-4-2-patch-notes
![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/6m/6MYLQTXZRGI21602200154898.png)

> The 18.4.2 patch includes balance updates for several Battlegrounds Heroes and minions!

---

On October 15, we’ll be releasing the 18.4.2 patch that includes numerous Battlegrounds balance updates! You can check out the full details of the patch below.

##### Battlegrounds Hero Updates

- Bartendotron has been removed from the Battlegrounds Hero Pool.

Ragnaros the Firelord

- *DIE, INSECTS!*
  - Old: After you kill 20 enemy minions, get Sulfuras. → **New: After you kill 25 enemy minions, get Sulfuras.**

![Old Ragnaros Battlegrounds Hero Power Base Version](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/qt/QT9PRKX5RKVQ1602200435908.png)![cardchanges_arrow.png](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/gallery/wx/WXH3UJH8MS061548722662334.png)![New Ragnaros Battlegrounds Hero Power Base Version](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/xz/XZLZO8TDTNLW1602200903486.png)

- *Sulfuras*
  - Old: At the end of your turn, give your left and right-most minions +4/+4. → **New: At the end of your turn, give your left and right-most minions +3/+3.**

![Old upgraded Ragnaros Battlegrounds Hero Power ](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/ey/EY8YYY2JWH3S1602200452103.png)![cardchanges_arrow.png](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/gallery/wx/WXH3UJH8MS061548722662334.png)![new upgraded Ragnaros Battlegrounds Hero Power](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/zz/ZZ7ZB65ULKFA1602200919973.png)

Maiev Shadowsong

- *Imprison*
  - Old: Make a minion in Bob's Tavern Dormant. After 2 turns, get it with +1/+1. → **New: Make a minion in Bob's Tavern Dormant. After 2 turns, get it with +1 Attack.**

![Old Maiev Battlegrounds Hero Power](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/90/9028AZC3POY51602200471851.png)![cardchanges_arrow.png](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/gallery/wx/WXH3UJH8MS061548722662334.png)![New Maiev Battlegrounds Hero Power](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/pr/PRRVI0TKOKYZ1602200936788.png)

Chenvaala

- *Avalanche*
  - Old: After you play 3 Elementals, reduce the cost of upgrading Bob's Tavern by (2). → **New: After you play 3 Elementals, reduce the cost of upgrading Bob's Tavern by (3).**

Patchwerk

- *All Patched Up*
  - Old: Start with 50 Health instead of 40. → **New: Start with 55 Health instead of 40.**
