> Yee-haw! It’s almost time for another Rewards Track refresh.

---

The sun is high and a new Rewards Track is on the horizon, ready to ride into the Tavern. When the Rewards Track refreshes with the Showdown in the Badlands launch, all players will automatically be granted any unclaimed rewards on the current free Rewards Track and the Tavern Pass track (if purchased), before a completely new Showdown in the Badlands Rewards Track of excavated treasures takes its place.

![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_folder_media/29LPBYOVUPEZ1698442894347.png)

*TITANS Achievements will stop awarding XP with the launch of Patch 28.0 on November 7, whereas the Rewards Track will swap over with the launch of the Showdown in the Badlands expansion on November 14.*

![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_folder_media/8GREUIK6L34X1696983093647.png)

#### Showdown in the Badlands Tavern Pass

With the launch of Showdown in the Badlands, a new Tavern Pass will be made available for purchase, featuring a mother lode of brand-new cosmetic rewards and XP Boosts!

![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_folder_media/SLBVYUMIYXUH1698859425336.jpg)

Purchasing the Tavern Pass at any time will immediately give you a 10% XP Boost that lasts the rest of the duration of the expansion, plus all items up to your current level on the paid track. As you progress along the Tavern Pass during the Showdown of the Badlands expansion, you can earn: additional XP Boosts; the Diamond Velarok Windblade Legendary card; the Golden Thunderbringer and Sheriff Barrelbrim Legendary cards; 2 Signature Wishing Well Epic cards; 2 Signature Pile of Bones Rare cards; 2 Signature High Noon Duelist Common cards; a new cosmetic coin and card back; a Hero Skin for each class; and 2 Golden Standard Packs!\*

*\* All Tavern Pass card grants are uncraftable and can’t be disenchanted.*

####

#### Diamond Cards in Showdown in the Badlands

Velarok Windblade and Elise, Badlands Savior are the set’s two progression-rewarded Diamond cards.

![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_folder_media/A6MQCS20OAOJ1698442901928.png)![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_folder_media/VKPQG7NY6FNY1698442901935.png)

![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_folder_media/IYJPSVF1B9CC1698442901858.png)

Velarok Windblade—Obtained by purchasing the Tavern Pass and reaching level 2 on the Rewards Track.

Elise, Badlands Savior—Obtained by completing the Showdown in the Badlands Legendary Collector Achievement.

#### Dig Up Two New Cosmetic Coins

Two new cosmetic coins have been added with Showdown in the Badlands: the Outlaw Coin and the Townsfolk Coin. The Outlaw Coin is obtained by reaching level 90 on the Rewards Track with the Tavern Pass. The Townsfolk Coin is obtained by completing the Achievement for having 145 Showdown in the Badlands cards.

![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_folder_media/D0N82EDAQ0E11698442901871.png)![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_folder_media/E4JJ4P56EW1J1698442901854.png)

Showdown in the Badlands will have a 38-card Mini-Set that will make it easier to complete the two collection-based achievements. We’ll have more information about the Showdown in the Badlands Mini-Set in the coming months.
