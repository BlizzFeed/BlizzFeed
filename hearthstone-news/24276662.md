## Hearthstone Updates

*Dev Comment: Well met! Our first-ever Class Sets are here, and we’re happy to see how excited people are to try out the new cards and archetypes. That said, we agree with your feedback that they’ve landed a bit soft, so we’re giving a couple of them a boost in this update.*

*Mage saw a lot of early experimentation, but the Leyline package fell short, so we’re buffing three cards to help it compete and encourage more exploration. For Hunter, the Animal Companion archetype has been popular and pretty strong, but it’s been missing a strong two-drop. We updated Migrating Elekk to help fill that role.*

*We’re also showing some love to the new event card, Dragon Soul, Shattered. Two of its Essences are being strengthened to better reward the effort of combining them.*

*The meta continues to look diverse with no major outliers, so this is a lighter balance pass aimed at keeping things stable. Demon Hunter, Druid, and Warrior are leading the pack right now, so we’re dialing back one card from each to keep them in check.*

*We’ll be watching how these changes play out and continue to tune things as needed. Keep the creative decks coming!*

## Battlegrounds Updates

*Dev Comment: There are a lot of balance changes in this patch, so we wanted to highlight our overall goals. For minion changes, we focused more on buffs than nerfs to lift up underperforming types. Most of these changes target Pirates and Mechs, with some buffs for Murlocs, Undead, and Quilboar as well.*

*On the nerf side, we swapped Famished Felbat with Twisted Wrathguard to keep Demons in check while lowering the power of Fodders. We also brought back P-0UL-TR-0N, with nerfs to it and Stomping Stegodon, to keep that strategy balanced.*

*We originally planned to nerf Dragons, but after reviewing the data, most of their overperformance came from Egg of the Endtimes Portrait and Chromatic Tear. As a result, we kept our balance adjustments focused on their Trinkets.*

*Speaking of Trinkets, our main goal was to smooth out the power between the highest and lowest performers. We also removed some of the least-picked options to keep your offerings compelling. We’ll keep an eye on these changes and make further adjustments as needed.*

*Our mid-season update is just around the corner, and we’re looking forward to sharing more very soon. We’ll see you in the Tavern!*
