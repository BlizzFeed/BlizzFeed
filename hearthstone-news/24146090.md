The final countdown is about to start as we prepare to blast off into The Great Dark Beyond! Join us for stellar community celebrations, a new type of Tavern Brawl, and more as we chart the unknown.

### The Great Dark Beyond Theorycrafting Streams October 24-25

Join your favorite content creators as they take their first steps into The Great Dark Beyond and theorycraft the new expansion. Featured players will be streaming their participation on their own personal streams, so make sure you’re following your favorite creators when they go live!

- NA/LATAM/EMEA players will play on October 24, 9:00 a.m. - 9:00 p.m. (PDT)
- APAC players will play on October 25, 2:00 a.m. - 8:00 a.m. (PDT)

All participants need to include at least 10 The Great Dark Beyond cards in their decks (that can be 10 individual cards, 5 duplicates, or anything in between).

Theorycrafters to mission control! Your favorite casters will be returning for their Theorycasting stream on the official Hearthstone Twitch channel. Join them as they watch our intrepid theorycrafters, casting over games across participating players.

- Sottle, Raven, Lorinda, and Edelweiss will return to Theorycasting on October 24, 9:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. (PDT)

Twitch Drops will be enabled category-wide from October 24, 9:00 a.m. to October 25, 9:00 a.m. (PDT), so you can watch any Hearthstone stream you’d like to earn up to 2 The Great Dark Beyond packs (make sure you link your Twitch and Battle.net accounts so that your viewing time counts towards the drops). On top of that, participating streams will have Pre-Purchase Mega Bundle codes to give away to their audiences during the event!

### Pre-Release Tavern Brawl from October 29-November 5

Starting with Patch 31.0, jump into The Great Dark Beyond early in the special new Pre-Release Tavern Brawl. Open your The Great Dark Beyond packs early and build a deck using cards from Core Set, Event Set, Whizbang’s Workshop, Perils in Paradise, and The Great Dark Beyond!

![HS_30p6_TGDB_Announcement_Blog_InBlog_Brawl_DB01.png](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/8VVKDI4SEIZ81727900217875.png)

All players will get one entry into the Pre-Release Tavern Brawl for free. After that, each entry will cost 2 Tavern Tickets, 300 Gold, or 400 Runestones. On each run, lock in a deck and play until you get either 6 wins or 3 losses and earn more new cards based on how well you do!

![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/us/US7II5M9QGJ01729526946991.jpg)

See our dedicated [Pre-Release Tavern Brawl Developer Insights](https://hearthstone.blizzard.com/news/24143782) blog for more information about the Pre-Release Tavern Brawl and the [Disclosure Page](https://us.battle.net/support/article/32545?_gl=1*nmjtiu*_gcl_au*MjUxNDY1MzIwLjE3MjU5OTgxNzg.*_ga*MjA1MTkyNTI2OS4xNjMwMzU4NjIw*_ga_VYKNV7C0S3*MTcyODYwMTcxMS44MC4xLjE3Mjg2MDMxNjIuMzEuMC4w) for more on specific rules.

### Community Days October 29-November 2

![HS_30p0_Patch_Notes_InBlog_CMTYDay_760x428_SW_DB01.jpg](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/MYLSZMG4HAJC1720142875024.jpg)

Community Days return with free packs you can use in the Pre-Release Tavern Brawl! From October 29, 10:00 a.m. (PDT) to November 2, 10:00 a.m. (PDT), you can get up to 10 packs just for watching any Hearthstone stream (2 hours per drop)!

- 1st Drop: 3 Whizbang’s Workshop Packs
- 2nd Drop: 3 Perils in Paradise Packs
- 3rd Drop: 4 The Great Dark Beyond Packs

Tune in to your favorite streamers or host your own party in this category-wide celebration of the Hearthstone universe!

### Creator Decks Return to the Client

You’ve watched their expeditions all reveal season. Now you can play like the pros! When The Great Dark Beyond launches on November 5, the in-game deck recipes will update to include decks from Hearthstone’s iconic content creators! Try them out to play among the stars:

- Cantelope’s Rainbow Starship Death Knight
- Kibler’s Starship Crew Demon Hunter
- Kibler’s Starcaneship Druid
- Day9’s Ceaseless Discover Hunter
- SwayBae’s Fiery Elementals Mage
- Roffle’s Libram Paladin
- MarkMcKz’s Mystified Draenei Priest
- Clark’s Schematic Heist Rogue
- Roffle’s Spacerock Shaman
- MarkMcKz’s Demon Starship Warlock
- Sloot’s Akamattack Warrior

In-game deck recipes will update with expansion launch on November 5. However, new and returning player loaner decks will instead update with the 31.4 Patch and will include cards from the main expansion as they did in the 30.4 update.
