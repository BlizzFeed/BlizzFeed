The Old Gods have arrived! *Reign of the Black Empire* is now live with 135 new collectible cards. This new Hearthstone expansion features the Old Gods at the height of their power, the new Assemble keyword, and the Titan-forged, mighty constructs created by the Titans to oppose the Black Empire.

## What’s In the New Expansion

### Old Gods

Command C'Thun, N'Zoth, Yogg-Saron, or Y'Shaarj at the height of their power. Each Old God comes with four unique abilities capable of dramatically shifting the battle, but you may include only one Old God in your deck, so choose wisely.

## Tavern Rewards Are Here!

![HS_37p0_Patch_Notes_InBlog_TavernTenders_760x428_SW01.jpg](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/8UEY7O973XZX1792004903526.jpg)

**Earn Tavern Tender. Bank it. Spend it when you’re ready.**

Tavern Rewards is an ongoing way to get more from eligible Hearthstone purchases in the [Battle.net Shop](http://shop.battle.net/family/hearthstone). Eligible purchases earn Tavern Tender, which you can save over time and redeem for bonus rewards you want in the Tavern Rewards Vault.

With Tavern Rewards, you can save your Tavern Tender and pick up the rewards you want at your own pace.

### How Tavern Rewards Works

Getting started is simple: **Shop. Earn. Bank. Redeem.**

1. Purchase eligible items in the Hearthstone Battle.net Shop.
2. Earn Tavern Tender from those purchases.
3. Bank your Tavern Tender over time.
4. Visit the Tavern Rewards Vault and redeem your Tavern Tender for rewards.

When you purchase eligible products, you’ll automatically earn Tavern Tender at no additional cost, making it easy to save up for rewards that catch your eye.

### Explore the Tavern Rewards Vault

The Tavern Rewards Vault is home to a rotating collection of Hearthstone rewards. Redeem Tavern Tender for packs, cards, cosmetics, returning favorites, and brand-new additions, with new rewards added over time.

#### Available Rewards

The current Tavern Rewards Vault lineup includes:

- Welcome Inn Card Back
- 1x Random Signature Legendary Card from Standard
- Wild God Omen Lite Hero Skin
- AF Kitsune Lite Hero Skin
- Annhylde Full Hero Skin and Card Back
- Ice Lord Malfurion Lite Hero Skin
- 5x Reign of the Black Empire Packs

![HS_37p0_ExpansionNowLive_InBlog_TavernTenderSpread_760x428_SW01C.jpg](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/YYKETPJ8NHJ81792004923341.jpg)

Rewards may come and go from the Vault, and some may return in future Vault rotations. Be sure to check back regularly to see what's available and discover the latest additions.

For more information, please visit the [Hearthstone Tavern Rewards disclosure page](https://support.blizzard.com/help/article/000386361).

*Tavern Rewards is currently available in all Hearthstone regions except Mainland China.*
