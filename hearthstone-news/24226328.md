# Warcraft Heroes Past and Future Unite in Across the Timeways, Hearthstone's Next Expansion

- URL: https://news.blizzard.com/en-gb/article/24226328/warcraft-heroes-past-and-future-unite-in-across-the-timeways-hearthstone-s-next-expansion
![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/c7/C7BXLIQAM3SN1758309386444.jpg)

> Learn about Rewind, Fabled, and how to manipulate time itself in our new set, Across the Timeways!

---

It’s about time... literally. Hearthstone’s **Across the Timeways** expansion launches November 4, adding 145 new cards and a time-warping storyline that pulls Warcraft heroes from every era into one epic showdown. Bend time to your will with new keywords Fabled and Rewind, harnessing the power of the greatest warriors of all time and even rewinding cards to play them again.

[![Video](https://markdown-videos-api.jorgenkh.no/youtube/yYIzqXIQ7MM?width=1280&height=720)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yYIzqXIQ7MM)

At the heart of the expansion is Chromie, the Bronze Dragonflight’s ever-cheerful chronomancer and protector of the Timeways. She made the shocking discovery that Murozond, Lord of the Infinite, is trying to derail all timelines and send them towards oblivion. To stop him, Chromie raced *across the timeways* (yes, really) to recruit Fabled heroes from past, future, and alternate realities. With a pre-banshee Sylvanas, a mech-piloting Gelbin, and the rest of her dream team of Warcraft icons assembled, Chromie is ready to rally against this chrono crisis.

View the Across the Timeways reveal schedule (and all cards already revealed), in the [Official Card Library](https://hearthstone.blizzard.com/cards). Journey through the Timeways with Chromie and uncover even more new cards by joining the [r/Hearthstone Discord](https://discord.gg/kuRptFJ3z6) for a special adventure to kick off our reveal season!

## New Keyword: Rewind

Time travel is coming to Hearthstone! Rewind is a new keyword that lets you replay cards with random effects for another shot at a better outcome. We've all had a spell go sideways or watched a minion’s effect miss the mark. Now, if a card has Rewind, you'll be offered the chance to rewrite history. Do you stick with what happened, or Rewind the timeline? The power to change ~~RNG~~ fate is in your hands.

## New Keyword: Fabled

Wield the powers of Warcraft icons from past, future, and alternate timelines! These storied heroes are so powerful, they bring extra Legendary cards with them that automatically slot into your deck. A Fabled Legendary arrives for every class, pulled from timelines both known and never seen before.

![HS_33p6_Expansion_Announcement_InBlog_SylvanasFabled_GIF_760x428_SW01.gif](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/K5Z1I6XHM1JE1758307561604.gif)

*Ranger General Sylvanas* rolls in with her sisters, Alleria and Vereesa, and she's looking... alive and well? That’s because this trio comes from a time before Arthas and the Scourge tore through Silvermoon City, so you get to unleash the full power of the Windrunner sisters in their prime!

![HS_33p6_Expansion_Announcement_InBlog_RangerGeneralSylvanas_700x330_SW01.jpg](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/ML0KR9OUH3HL1758307558557.jpg)

*Gelbin of Tomorrow* preserved his mind with Holy magic and now pilots a giant mech in a futuristic Gnomeregan. He brings two Legendary Auras that pair well with these other new Paladin cards. *Chronological Aura* summons a dragon guardian every turn, and *Manifested Timeways* hits all enemies if you’ve got an Aura in play. Gelbin really gives Aura farming a whole new meaning.

## Bend Time to Your Will

Rewind and Fabled are just the beginning. Across the Timeways offers even more ways to control time itself!

*Timeway Warden* imprisons an opposing minion for 10,000 turns (the average length of a Control Warrior mirror match) or until the Warden dies. It’d be a shame if the Warden were silenced...

![HS_DEMONHUNTER_TIME_442_enUS_TimewayWarden-120988_NORMAL.png](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/N22FNQB41XZQ1758306628044.png)

*Divergence* takes a minion in your hand and splits it in two, perfect for cards with powerful effects.

## Ally With the Protectors of the Timeways

And of course, what’s a time travel expansion without *Timelord Nozdormu*? As leader of the Bronze Dragonflight and Guardian of Time (not to mention Chromie’s boss), he’s at the center of this temporal catastrophe. Technically, he’s on both sides of it. You may already know he and Murozond are one and the same. (If you’re good at anagrams, you saw this coming a mile away.) Murozond is Nozdormu’s corrupted future self. Still, Nozdormu’s willingness to fight his future self reminds us that no matter how bad the future looks, no fate is inevitable.

![HS_NEUTRAL_TIME_063_enUS_TimelordNozdormu-120981_NORMAL.png](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/HDSQOJICTNLL1758306630303.png)

In fact, the theme of duality runs throughout this set. *Soldier of the Bronze* and *Soldier of the Infinite* represent opposite sides of the time war, and their card designs mirror each other in several ways.

![HS_NEUTRAL_TIME_720_enUS_SoldieroftheBronze-120847_NORMAL.png](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/DFDT3EY4W4SB1758306630588.png)![HS_NEUTRAL_TIME_051_enUS_SoldieroftheInfinite-120949_NORMAL.png](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/FLMUFW0P0FJ31758306630045.png)
