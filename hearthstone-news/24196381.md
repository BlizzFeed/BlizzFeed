# Announcing Battlegrounds Season 10 – Second Nature!

- URL: https://news.blizzard.com/en-gb/article/24196381/announcing-battlegrounds-season-10-second-nature
![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/u5/U591LX2YCBFT1744661735540.jpg)

> Appeal to your second nature with the return of Trinkets, a massive minion shakeup, and more!

---

See the other side of the Emerald Dream, with revamped Trinkets, a huge minion refresh, and more in Battlegrounds Season 10, Second Nature, going live on April 29!

![HS_32p2_BGSeason10_Announce_InBlog_BGMarquee_Seasonal_760x608_SW01.jpg](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/8U1N7ZX6A17U1744661578998.jpg)

*When Season 10 begins, Anomalies will be removed from the Tavern and turn lengths will be temporarily lengthened.*

### Returning Mechanic: Trinkets

Trinkets are back! Trinkets are special passive power-ups that you buy with Gold and use for the rest of the game. Trinkets are offered twice per game: on turn 6 (Lesser Trinkets) and turn 9 (Greater Trinkets). Each offering, you’ll get 4 Trinkets to choose from.

![HS_30p2_BG_Announce_Blog_InBlog_Trinkets_760x428_DB01.png](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/XYV05LYA1OQR1722624075955.png)

The Trinket system has been adjusted for their return, so that you now more frequently get Trinkets for your most common minion type. To keep things interesting, we’ve also infused the Trinket Shop with tons of new offerings: 100+ new Trinkets to go alongside 100+ returning Trinkets!

![NEUTRAL_BG32_MagicItem_801_enUS_HeartoftheForest-120138_NORMAL.png](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/8490RQVTZTZN1744660804968.png)![NEUTRAL_BG32_MagicItem_944_enUS_EmpowermentPortrait-121499_NORMAL.png](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/WJS5UGY3H5LV1744660805111.png)

Harness the power of Nature with the Druidic demigod, Cenarius, and ramp up your Gold reserves for powerful late-game plays!

Or celebrate the return of Trinkets with... more Trinkets! Buttons is a regular at the Trinket Shop, picking out an extra Greater Trinket to grab towards the end of his vacation.

### Quality of Life Improvement: New Minion Frames

We’re updating minion frames to make it easier to tell which minions belong to the same minion type, even when you’re quickly rerolling the Tavern! Each minion type’s frame will correspond to one of the Hearthstone class frames, and dual type minions will use the half and half frames that Hearthstone uses for dual-class cards.

### Creator Early Access Event (with Drops!)

![HS_32p2_BGSeason10_Announce_InBlog_EarlyAccess_TuneIn_760x428_SW01.jpg](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/PINVYX00O8EV1744661580306.jpg)

On April 25, watch your favorite content creators play the new Season live! Participating players will be streaming on their personal channels, so make sure you have notifications turned on. Time slots by region are:

- JP: 2:00 a.m. - 8:00 a.m. (PDT)
- Americas and EMEA regions: 9:00 a.m. - 3:00 p.m. (PDT)

Drops will be enabled category-wide on Twitch. Watch for a total of 2 hours to earn 4 Battlegrounds Tokens (2 total hours, 1 hour per drop).

### Battlegrounds Track Refresh

![HS_32p2_BGSeason10_Announce_InBlog_SecondPage_760x435_SW01.png](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/RQSE0RVI0YF11744661579961.png)

On April 29, Battlegrounds Season 9 will end and Season 10 will begin! With that, Battlegrounds Ratings will reset and the Battlegrounds Track will update. Any rewards that were already earned from the Season 9 Rewards Track, but not yet claimed, will be automatically claimed for you.

![HS_32p2_BGSeason10_Announce_InBlog_PremiumPass_LO_760x428_SW01.jpg](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/6UMELPPXW5NM1744661579869.jpg)

The Season 10 Battlegrounds Track includes afterlife-themed cosmetics. Pick up the Season Pass to instantly unlock +2 Hero options at the start of each game. Then, progress through the track to earn the Ardenweald Legendary Strike, 13 additional Hero Skins, a new Bartender, and more! Or the Season Pass + to get all the rewards from the Season Pass **plus one included Hero re-roll per game.**
