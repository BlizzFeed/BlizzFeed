# The Great Dark Beyond is NOW LIVE!

![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/9w/9W8SAPGQ56BB1730151245072.jpg)

> It’s time to set off into The Great Dark Beyond. Hearthstone’s latest expansion is now live with out-of-this world Starships, forward-looking Draenei, Spellbursts of power, and more!

---

3, 2, 1... Blast off! The Great Dark Beyond is now live, with 145 new collectible cards, out-of-this world Starships, forward-looking Draenei, powerful Spellburst cards, and more!

[![Video](https://markdown-videos-api.jorgenkh.no/youtube/RV0a47cZFsk?width=1280&height=720)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RV0a47cZFsk)

#### What’s Included: New Keyword-Starship

Explore The Great Dark Beyond in your very own Starship! You’ll find Starship Pieces floating around in The Great Dark Beyond. You can play them like regular minions, and when they die their stats and effects automatically get added to your Starship construction. Once you’ve built up the perfect ship, hit the button to launch it directly onto the board!

Each of the six classes with Starship cards (Death Knight, Demon Hunter, Druid, Hunter, Rogue, and Warlock) have their own iconic Starship visual appearance and name, but every class is welcome on The Exile’s Hope.

### What’s Included: New Minion Type-Draenei

Driven from their planet by the Burning Legion, these “exiled ones” fled to the stars in search of a new home. The Draenei explore all the far-reaches of The Great Dark Beyond as Hearthstone’s newest permanent minion type. Always hopeful, many Draenei have effects that impact the next Draenei you play. They rally around their powerful leader, Velen!

The Great Dark Beyond is bursting with cosmic energy. Cards with the returning Spellbust keyword get an effect after you play a spell. Play your cards in the right order for a quick burst of power!
