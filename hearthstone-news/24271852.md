## Arena Updates

### New Arena Mode: Under-Underground Arena

Think you’ve mastered Underground Arena? Great. Now dig deeper. **Under‑Underground Arena** is a hidden mode with intense new rules for players who enjoy ~~suffering~~ a challenge.

#### Unlock Requirements

- Retire a run at 11 wins.
- Once unlocked, enter the new mode by clicking on a 10x10-pixel sized button that appears somewhere on the Arena menu screen.

#### New Gameplay Features

- Every card you play shortens your turn timer by 5 seconds.
- One random card in your deck is upside down.

#### How to Win

- Achieve a 24‑game win streak.
- One loss will end your run, revoke your access, reset the unlock requirements, and hide the button again.
