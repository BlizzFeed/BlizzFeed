> The Spring Championship is just around the corner!

---

The Spring Championship is just around the corner! From June 6-8th, the top ladder players will join the winners of the Spring Playoffs to battle it out for their share of $50,000 as well as a spot in the World Championship. Read on for everything you need to know to enjoy all the action, only on Twitch and YouTube.

### What You Need to Know

- **Dates:** Friday, June 6 – Sunday, June 8, starting at 9 am PST (6 pm CEST) each day
- **Format:** 4-deck, best-of-5 Conquest with a ban
  - Day 1: Group play
  - Day 2: Group play
  - Day 3: Top 8, Single Elimination Bracket
- **Prize Pool:** $50,000 (USD)
- **Drops:** Earn up to 3 packs from the next expansion while watching on Twitch (1 each day).
- **Choose Your Champion:** Choose a player competing in the tournament and the more they win, the more packs you earn! Enter here: <https://hearthstone.blizzard.com/vote/choose-your-champion>
