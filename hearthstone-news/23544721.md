> Watch the first EMEA Battlegrounds Cup this weekend, beginning on October 16!

---

The first EMEA Battlegrounds Cup begins! Utilizing the new Battlegrounds Lobbies, the top 8 Hearthstone Battlegrounds players on the European Ladder and 8 invited streamers are ready to compete this weekend, October 16-18, for a share of the $10,000 (USD) prize pool and bragging rights as the best Battlegrounds players in the region.

### Details

**Dates:** Friday, October 16 – Sunday, October 18

**Broadcast Start Time**: 9:00 a.m. PDT

**Where to Watch**: [YouTube.com/HearthstoneEsports](http://www.youtube.com/HearthstoneEsports), [Twitch.tv/PlayHearthstone](https://www.twitch.tv/playhearthstone), and co-streamed by the players!

**Casters**: Collins “[EducatedC](https://twitter.com/educatedcollins?lang=en)” Mensah, Jia “[Jia](https://twitter.com/jiadee_)” Dee, Simon “[Sottle](https://twitter.com/coL_Sottle)” Welch

**Prize Pool**: $10,000 (USD)

- Team 1: $5,000 (USD) - $2,500 each
- Team 2: $2,400 (USD) - $1,200 each
- Team 3: $1,600 (USD) - $800 each
- Team 4: $1,000 (USD) - $500 each

**Language Streams on YouTube:**

[English](https://www.youtube.com/HearthstoneEsports)

[Russian](https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCv4IKw9phF1NSxXD1gMZjqg)
