Patch 36.6.3 releases today with balance updates for Hearthstone and Battlegrounds.

## Hearthstone Updates

*Dev Comment: Well met! Overall, we think the current Standard meta is in a healthy spot, with a few outliers, namely Dragon Warrior and Attack Druid. This patch aims to bring those decks more in line with the rest of the field as we head into Reign of the Black Empire. We expect the arrival of the Old Gods to shake up the meta in a big way, so we’re taking a measured approach with this patch. We'll be keeping a close eye on how things develop after launch.*

Windpeak Wyrm

- Old: Kindred: Costs (3) less.
- New: Kindred: Costs (2) less.

Infest the Scullery

- Old: Summon two random 3-Cost minions.
- New: Summon two random 2-Cost minions.

## Battlegrounds Updates

*Dev Comment: Most of the changes in this patch, along with our update earlier this week, are focused on bringing Aberrations down a notch. Our goal is to reduce their early-game tempo while keeping them competitive later in the game. To support that, Deities now require 4 deaths instead of 3 to summon, making early summons less frequent. We're also moving all Deities from Tier 3 to Tier 1 to reduce hero damage taken from Aberrations.*

*When we introduce a new minion type, we normally guarantee it appears in every lobby for two weeks so players have plenty of opportunities to try it out. However, we've heard your feedback that this can make games feel a bit too repetitive. As a result, we're ending the Aberration guarantee early, and they will now have the standard 50% chance to appear in lobbies. Going forward, we expect new minion types to have a one-week guarantee, while new Heroes will continue to have a two-week guarantee. We've also returned new Trinket appearance rates to normal.*

*Outside of Aberrations, most of our minion and Trinket changes are focused on helping underperforming strategies catch up. We took a conservative approach here since Aberrations have been shaping the meta so heavily, but we'll continue making adjustments as needed.*

*Thanks for all your feedback on Aberrations and the Midseason Update! We don't plan on making any additional major balance changes before Reign of the Black Empire launches, so we'll see you again after the Old Gods arrive. See you in the Tavern!*

[General Changes](#General-Changes)

- Aberrations are no longer guaranteed in every lobby.
- Sinister Invitation now offers higher-Tier minions more frequently.
- Maw Caster can no longer be offered Echoing Voice.
- Arch-Villain Rafaam and Victim Specter can no longer be offered in Aberration lobbies.
- The increased appearance rate for new Trinkets has been removed. New Trinkets now appear at the same rate as all other Trinkets.
- Updated the VFX for Living Azerite and Seafood Stew to resolve more quickly.
