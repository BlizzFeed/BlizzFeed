# Celebrating Lunar New Year in Hearthstone!

- URL: https://hearthstone.blizzard.com/en-gb/news/23620128/celebrating-lunar-new-year-in-hearthstone
![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/xz/XZ58KEVF70E41612301686764.jpg)

> Lunar New Year brings new quests, a fan favorite tavern brawl, and new Three Kingdoms Hero skins!

---

Lunar New Year kicks off in the Tavern on February 9 with a new Legendary Quest line, a fan favorite Tavern Brawl, and a special set of Three Kingdoms themed Hero skins in the shop!

#### Legendary Quests

Good fortune is coming your way! New Legendary quests rewarding a total of 9 card packs from Madness at the Darkmoon Faire and Scholomance Academy will be available from February 9 through February 23!

![HS19p4_LNY_EventsBlog_Inline_3_Rewards_760x600_BK02.jpg](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/4c/4C49YSR1EA2J1612301818229.jpg)

- Play 1 game of Battlegrounds to earn 2 *Madness at the Darkmoon Faire* card packs and 1 *Scholomance Academy* card pack.
- Play 5 games of Duels to earn 2 *Madness at the Darkmoon Faire* card packs and 1 *Scholomance Academy* card pack.
- Play 5 Ranked Standard games to earn 2 *Madness at the Darkmoon Faire* card packs and 1 *Scholomance Academy* card pack.

#### Three Kingdoms Heroes

![HS19p4_LNY_EventsBlog_Inline_2_Skins_760x600.jpg](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/h6/H6H4P3Q6DTPN1612301834749.jpg)

The Three Kingdoms skins are inspired by *Romance of Three Kingdoms*, an epic Chinese tale of war, heroes, and betrayal where three successor kingdoms rose to power after the collapse of the Han Empire and battled to reunify the country. The Three Kingdoms skins will be available in-game and on the Blizzard web shop from February 9 until February 19!

#### Lunar Blessings Tavern Brawl

![HS19p4_LNY_EventsBlog_Inline_1_TavernBrawl_760x600_BK01.jpg](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/p0/P0HJ88EB9BJR1612301849167.jpg)Returning to the Tavern is the Lunar Blessing Tavern Brawl! Choose a class, get a random deck, and choose an animal to receive its blessing and power!

*Be sure to give your opponents a festive greeting with Lunar New Year emotes! Lunar New Year ends on February 23!*
