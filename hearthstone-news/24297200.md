---

Launching on October 20, *Reign of the Black Empire* takes Hearthstone back to the earliest age of Azeroth, long before the Old Gods were imprisoned and long before the world as we know it existed. This was the age of the Black Empire, when C’Thun, N’Zoth, Yogg-Saron, and Y’Shaarj ruled a corrupted world and waged war against the Titan-forged armies sent to stop them. Now it's your turn to step into that conflict. Command the most horrifying Old Gods Hearthstone has ever created and wield the power of creation to Assemble your own custom minions.

## Wield Ancient Power

*Reign of the Black Empire* features the four Old Gods as Legendary Neutral minions:

- **C'Thun, Eye of the Empire** rewards unwavering devotion and the fulfillment of its dark designs.
- **N'Zoth, Dream's End** spreads corruption through patient planning and inevitable decay.
- **Mastermind Yogg-Saron** seizes control, unleashing chaos through those who serve it.
- **Y'Shaarj, Titan Bane** overwhelms its enemies through sheer size, strength, and domination.

This is the age of the Black Empire, when these nightmarish rulers stood at the height of their power and their influence shaped all of Azeroth. Their strength is so immense that you may include only one Old God in your deck. Each Old God possesses four potent abilities and, instead of attacking, can invoke two of those powers each turn. Once all four abilities have been exhausted, the Old God is free to attack and join the battle directly.

## New Keyword: Assemble

As the Old Gods and Titan-forged build their armies for war, you'll do the same with Assemble. Add a minion to your hand, then replace part of its card text with the text of another minion from a curated pool. Assemble creates unique combinations and lets you tailor your forces to fit your strategy.

![SHAMAN_BE_114_enUS_ShadowSculpt-131061_NORMAL.png](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/LLHPIM8HABL01789246759675.png)

## The Titans Strike Back

Long before heroes walked Azeroth, the world was shaped by a war between cosmic powers. When the Titan Pantheon discovered Azeroth being corrupted by the Old Gods, they responded by creating an army of Titan-forged servants powerful enough to fight back. In *Reign of the Black Empire*, you'll experience that legendary conflict from both sides. Command ancient Old Gods and wield their unfathomable powers, or fight alongside Azeroth's greatest defenders as legendary Titan Watchers and celestial magic enter the battle. The fate of the world is in your hands.

## Isiset Awakens

Among the Titan-forged champions who rose to oppose the Black Empire, few wielded power as devastating as Isiset. This Legendary Titan Watcher brings enormous late-game potential to Mage decks. Her Battlecry summons two massive 10/10 Constructs that remain Frozen until you've spent 15 Mana on spells. If you can unlock their power, your opponent will suddenly be staring down 20/20 worth of Titan-forged Constructs.

## Pre-Release Tavern Brawl

Get your first chance to play *Reign of the Black Empire* before launch in the returning Pre-Release Tavern Brawl. Open your packs early, build decks with the new cards, and compete for prizes before the expansion goes live.

The *Reign of the Black Empire* Pre-Release Tavern Brawl introduces a brand-new format. Build decks using only *Reign of the Black Empire* cards, with all Common and Rare cards available as free Trial Cards\* during the event. Choose from predefined multi-class combinations before constructing your deck. Most options combine three classes, while one special two-class combination offers enhanced C'Thun synergies.

The Pre-Release Tavern Brawl runs October 13 - 20.

\* Reign of the Black Empire *free trial Common and Rare cards can only be used in the Pre-Release Tavern Brawl and will be available until expansion unlock on October 20 at 10:00 a.m. PDT.*
