## Hearthstone Updates

### Standard

*Dev Comment: Well met! We’ve been excited to see how much* Reign of the Black Empire *has shaken up the metagame. You’ve been experimenting with tons of new decks and archetypes, and the Old Gods have certainly made their presence known!*

*After using temporary bans to address a few early outliers, this balance patch gives us an opportunity to make more permanent changes and create more room for different strategies to succeed.*

*C’Thun, Eye of the Empire has emerged as the strongest of the four Old Gods, so we’re shifting some of its power away from the cards that activate its abilities early and into C’Thun itself. C’Thun will now quadruple its abilities instead of tripling them, making it an even juicier late-game payoff.*

*Warlock was one of the strongest users of C’Thun, so those changes should help rein it in. We’re also adjusting some of Warlock’s strongest strategies, including combos with M.O.T.H.E.R., Bat Mask, and Divergence, as well as its early-game board presence with Ritual of Summoning and Godfather Kazakus.*

*For Priest, we’re increasing the requirement for Reach Equilibrium and reverting our previous buff to Egg of Khelos, while strengthening a few newer Priest cards to help compensate.*

*We’re also adjusting Unstable Prototype to reduce early board strength across a variety of classes, especially Handbuff Death Knight.*

*Finally, we’re buffing several underperforming cards and strategies, with a particular focus on the other Old Gods. We can’t let C’Thun have all the fun! We’ve also got new free event cards on the way that will give you even more ways to build around the Old Gods across different classes.*

*We’re looking forward to seeing where the metagame goes next!*

We temporarily banned Reincarnate to address one of those interactions with Mastermind Yogg-Saron, and with this patch, we’re making a more targeted change by preventing Reincarnate from targeting Legendary minions.*

*We’re also addressing an early-game combo involving Kobold Illusionist and N’Zoth, along with a few other cards and strategies that have been causing frustration in Wild for a while.*

### Assemble Pool Update

*Dev Comment: Assemble pulls from a curated pool of cards, with the goal of creating some fun and unexpected combinations. A few combinations went a little too far, though, resulting in things like infinite triggers and unkillable minions. We’ve adjusted the Assemble pool to keep those outcomes from showing up.*

### Event Card Acquisition

We’re moving up the availability of the Arrival of the Old Gods, Arrival of the Titans, and Dark Terraforming card rewards from The War of Old & New event. Starting with today's Patch 37.0.2, you can obtain these cards by owning an Escape from Violet Hold Pack.

*Dev Comment: The timing for automatically granting missed event cards can vary based on our content release schedule, and in this case, these cards would have remained unavailable for longer than we’d like. Rather than make you wait, we’ve decided to make a one-time change to shorten that window and grant them ahead of schedule.*

*We’ve heard your feedback on how event cards are made available, and we’re exploring ways to improve this process in the future. Thanks for sharing your thoughts with us, and we hope you enjoy getting your hands on these cards a little sooner!*

## Battlegrounds Updates

*Dev Comment: There are quite a few changes in this patch, so let’s jump right in!*

*Starting with minions, our main goals are to bring down some of the stronger types, like Quillboar and Demons, while giving some help to underperforming types like Pirates.*

*We also want to call out Murlocs and Elementals specifically. While both types are underperforming overall, each has a particular strategy that has become too dominant and is warping how the type is played. For Elementals, that’s Living Azerite, and for Murlocs, it’s the combination of Expert Aviator/Diremuck Forager and Bile Spitter.*

*To address these strategies, we’ve reduced Living Azerite to +3/+3 and removed Expert Aviator. At the same time, we’re making several buffs across both types, including to Twilight Tidehunter, Shamanic Tidecaller, Unbound Tempest, and Ultraviolet Ascendant. Combined with other adjustments to individual cards and Trinkets, we hope these changes open up more viable strategies for both types.*

*Now, onto Dark Gifts. Overall, we want players to feel like they can use their Dark Gifts whenever it makes sense throughout the game. We’ve heard feedback that players feel too incentivized to use them during the early turns, so we’re adjusting their power to make holding onto them a more viable option.*

*We’re reducing the power of some early-game Dark Gifts, specifically Fresh Perspective and Toughened Shield, while buffing several mid- and late-game options, including Hostility, Resistance, and Incubation. We’re also making a rule change for turns 7-9 that guarantees at least two minions of your most common type in each Discover. Turns 10+ will continue to offer at least two minions of your most common type, as before. Together, these changes should give players more freedom to use their Dark Gifts when they want rather than feeling pressured to use them early.*

*The next season of Battlegrounds is just around the corner, and we’re excited to share more about what’s coming very soon. We’ll see you in the Tavern!*
