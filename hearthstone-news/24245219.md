# Deathwing Strikes Back in CATACLYSM, Hearthstone’s Next Expansion

On March 17, *CATACLYSM* descends on Hearthstone, bringing back the legendary destroyer, **Deathwing**. Only this time, he’s not the Worldbreaker you remember. In the final moments of *Across the Timeways*, Murozond refused to accept defeat from Chromie and the Heroes of Time, so he smashed the Hourglass of Time and opened a portal to a nightmare timeline where Deathwing never fell. Now this undefeated Deathwing is invading Azeroth with six terrifying Lieutenants and they’re ready to break the world all over again!

Last time, Azeroth came within a hair’s breadth of total annihilation. This time, it’s even worse.

The skies of Azeroth fill with embers and ash as the first-ever **Deathwing Hero Card** rises, massive **Colossal** minions lay siege, and the **Herald** keyword signals the beginning of Azeroth’s end! To stand a chance, the heroes must rally the **five dragonflights**, unleash their new cards, and harness the power of the **Shatter** keyword.

An unprecedented threat demands an equally unprecedented response. Chromie is calling on the full power of her time magic to arm you for the battle ahead. She’s pulling reinforcements from the past and sending *two full sets of cards* straight into your collection!

Here’s everything you need to know about *CATACLYSM*! Shuffle up your deck and get ready to play with fire.

## Colossals Are Back and Stronger Than Ever with Herald

Colossals are BIG! First introduced to Hearthstone in *Voyage to the Sunken City*, they represent creatures of massive scale, making them perfect for Deathwing’s Lieutenants like Ragnaros and Azshara. When you play a Colossal minion, they expand and take up multiple spaces on the board with appendage cards that act as their huge arms, extra heads, gross tentacles, etc. These appendages have abilities that complement the main Colossal minion, creating a unified display of their enormous power.

![HS_35p0_Announce_Blog_GIF_Ragnaros_Resize_760x522_DB01.gif](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/1PWGDQFUFBQR1770167710345.gif)

All classes are getting a brand new Legendary Colossal card in *CATACLYSM*. The new Herald keyword is tied to Deathwing’s arrival, so only the six classes aligned with him reap its benefits: Death Knight, Demon Hunter, Rogue, Shaman, Warlock, and Warrior. Take a closer a look at the Warrior Colossal, Ragnaros, the Great Fire:

![HS_35p0_Announce_InBlog_Ragnaros_700x330_DB01.png](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/CMQHZIF2HTLC1770167713350.png)

But these aren’t the same Colossals as before. To turn the heat up for *CATACLYSM*, cards like Envoy of the End use Herald to make them even more effective.

![HS_DEATHKNIGHT_ROGUE_SHAMAN_WARLOCK_WARRIOR_DEMONHUNTER_CATA_722_enUS_EnvoyoftheEnd-122639_NORMAL.png](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/87BTHBEAJKX01770167714013.png)

Deathwing’s servants herald the coming of the Worldbreaker and his Colossal cronies, and the Herald keyword showcases that in two key ways. First, when you play a minion with Herald, a Soldier of your class’s Colossal is summoned to the battlefield. These Soldiers share the same effects as that Colossal’s appendages. Since Colossal minions typically arrive later in the game due to their high mana cost, Soldiers let you tap into those abilities earlier. A preview of the destruction your Colossal will unleash.

![HS_WARRIOR_CATA_580t_enUS_SoldierofRagnaros-127082_NORMAL.png](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/BG390FN7O79A1770167714713.png)

But that’s not all! The more you use Herald, the mightier Deathwing and your Colossal Soldiers and appendages become. Herald twice to double their power. Herald two more times to double it again.

![HS_35p0_Announce_Blog_GIF_Herald_Ragnaros_760x522_DB02.gif](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/9G2SKOAFROMS1770240845549.gif)

The undefeated version of Deathwing now wields the Dragon Soul, a source of power that once destroyed him but now answers to his command. His Colossal allies have also been strengthened, making them more dangerous than ever. In their alternate timeline, Ragnaros burned down the World Tree and fused with its charred branches to reach an entirely new level of power. The Herald keyword reflects that rising strength and the escalating danger that builds over the course of a game.

To summarize Herald:

- Herald delivers instant value by summoning a Colossal Soldier and keeps scaling in strength throughout the game.
- Playing a Herald minion summons a Soldier that has the same ability as your class Colossal's appendages.
- Each Herald use upgrades both the Soldier and your Colossal’s appendages. Herald twice to double their power, four times to double it a final time.
- Herald also empowers Deathwing, Worldbreaker, increasing the number of Cataclysms he unleashes when played. Herald twice to play two Cataclysms, four times to use all four.

## New Keyword: Shatter

Deathwing’s destruction is spreading across Azeroth, and our heroes are doing their best to pick up the pieces. Enter Shatter, a new keyword for the dragonflight-aligned classes. When you draw a card with Shatter, it breaks into two pieces that move to opposite ends of your hand. You can play the pieces separately, but if you manage to bring them back together by playing the cards in between, they recombine into a single card that’s doubly strong.

![HS_35p0_Announce_InBlog_Supply%20Run_760x330_DB01.png](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/PP2HBI0YKQ641770167713550.png)
