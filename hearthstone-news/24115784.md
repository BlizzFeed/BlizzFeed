Marin’s about to cut the ribbon for the grand opening of his new resort, where a new Rewards Track awaits! When the Rewards Track refreshes with the Perils in Paradise launch, all players will automatically be granted any unclaimed rewards on the current free Rewards Track and the Tavern Pass track (if purchased), before a completely new Perils in Paradise Rewards Track takes its place.

![HS_30p0_RewardsTrack_InBlog_RewardsTrack_760x438_DB01.png](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/RR1WE1I3ZUT81720196063175.png)

#### Perils in Paradise Rewards Track

The free Rewards Track for Perils in Paradise will include packs from Perils in Paradise, a random Epic card, two random Legendary cards, Tavern Tickets, a new card back, and lots of Gold! There are also 14 Golden cards, along with the Gorgonzormu Legendary card from the set!\* For finishing the Reward Track, you get to choose one of 21 Hero Skin options.

\* These 14 Golden cards (two copies each of seven cards from the set) and the Gorgonzormu Legendary card earned from the Rewards Track can’t be crafted or disenchanted.

#### Perils in Paradise Tavern Pass

With the launch of Perils in Paradise, a new Tavern Pass will be made available for purchase, featuring island souvenirs and XP Boosts!

![HS_30p0_RewardsTrack_InBlog_TavernPass_760x428_SP_DB01.jpg](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/WD1SVAGCU6X71720196063208.jpg)

Purchasing the Tavern Pass at any time will immediately give you a 10% XP Boost that lasts the rest of the duration of the expansion, plus all items up to your current level on the paid track. As you progress along the Tavern Pass during the Perils in Paradise expansion, you can earn: additional XP Boosts, the A.F. Kay Diamond Legendary card, the Marin the Manager and Gorgonzormu Golden Legendary cards, 2 Seaside Giant Golden Epic cards, 2 Mixologist Signature Epic cards, 2 Hydration Station Signature Rare cards, 2 Tidepool Pupil Signature Common cards, a new cosmetic coin and card back, a new vacation-themed Hero Skin for each class, and 2 Golden Standard Packs.\*

\* All Tavern Pass card grants are unable to be crafted or disenchanted.

#### New Cosmetic Coins

Two new cosmetic coins have been added with Perils in Paradise: the Sand Coin and the Seafarer’s Coin. The Sand Coin is earned by reaching level 90 on the Rewards Track with the Tavern Pass. The Seafarer’s Coin is earned by completing the Achievement for having 145 Perils in Paradise cards.

![zzNEUTRAL_VAC_COIN1_enUS_TheCoin-114503_NORMAL.png](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/ZEKRXCI2LPK41720196076586.png)![zzNEUTRAL_VAC_COIN2_enUS_TheCoin-114504_NORMAL.png](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/ZIIMLNQPQS2E1720196076624.png)

Perils in Paradise will have a 38-card Mini-Set that will make it easier to complete the collection-based Achievements. We’ll share more information about that Mini-Set in the coming months.
