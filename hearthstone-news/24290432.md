## Hearthstone Updates

*Dev Comment: Well met! We've been closely following the impact of the latest balance update, and it's become clear that the Demon Hunter buffs overshot the mark. Our goal was to help the class reach a healthier power level ahead of the new support arriving in this patch, but it has since become one of the strongest performers and is having an outsized impact on the meta. As a result, we're making a small unscheduled adjustment.*

*In particular, Vicious Voidscale has proven to be a clear outlier. We're reverting the buff to bring both the card and the class more in line with the rest of the meta.*

*With Soul Immolation becoming available through the "Zuramat's Rampage!" event, we look forward to seeing how you use its unique effect in Void Soul Demon Hunter.*

*We'll continue monitoring the meta and evaluating whether further changes are needed during our next regularly scheduled balance window. We'll see you in the Tavern!*

- New: Deathrattle: Get a Void Soul

## Battlegrounds Updates

Something strange has been smuggled out of Dalaran, and it's about to reshape every lobby. **Battlegrounds Season 14: Dark Gifts of Dalaran** introduces powerful Dark Gifts, the new Activate keyword, an expanded Trinket pool, new Heroes, and the debut of the Lockbox and Fishbait mechanics. Discover what's inside when the new season begins on August 4 at 10:00 a.m. PDT.

![HS_36p2_BGSeason14_Announce_InBlog_BGMarquee_Seasonal_760x665_SW01.jpg](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/zj/ZJW9URATAO0S1784585812627.jpg)

### What's Coming in Season 14

- **New Gameplay System: Dark Gifts**
  - Spend Gold to Discover minions paired with powerful Dark Gifts.
  - Starting on Turn 3, spend 3 Gold to Discover 1 of 3 minions, each paired with a unique Dark Gift.
  - Use the Dark Gift button once per turn, up to 3 times per game.
  - Offerings improve each turn, so waiting can lead to stronger options.
  - 43 unique Dark Gifts
- **New Keyword: Activate**
  - Spend Gold during the Recruit Phase to trigger special minion abilities.
  - Click an Activate minion in your warband to use its ability.
  - Each Activate ability can be used once per turn.
- **New Pirate Synergy: Lockbox**
  - Pirates can uncover Lockboxes containing Golden minions that unlock on later turns, unless you break them out early.
- **New Beast Synergy: Fishbait**
  - Beasts can now attack during the Recruit Phase, with a big reward for the minion that takes the bait.
- **Seasonal Minion Refresh**
  - Minions
    - 96 minions removed
    - 62 new minions
    - 31 returning minions
  - Tavern Spells
    - 5 Tavern spells removed
    - 5 new Tavern spells
    - 4 returning Tavern spells
- **Refreshed Trinket Pool**
  - Trinkets are sticking around for another season and will be available alongside Dark Gifts!
  - 23 new Lesser Trinkets
  - 24 new Greater Trinkets
- **Two New Heroes**
  - Nightmare Lord Xavius – Feel Devastation
  - Tras'tath, Soul Parasite – Void Power

### General Battlegrounds Updates

#### Venomous Change

With Fishbait allowing minions to attack during the Recruit Phase, Venomous has been updated to destroy the first minion it damages each combat, ensuring the keyword remains effective once combat begins.

Venomous

- Old: Destroy the first minion this deals damage to.
- New: Destroy the first minion this deals damage to each combat.

#### Chromadrake Change

Chromadrakes have been removed from the Tavern pool but can still be generated by certain minions and Trinkets. Their keyword has been updated to reflect that they are now only available through these effects.

Chromadrake

- Old: One of five Tier 3 Dragons with various Battlecries.
- New: One of five Dragons with various Battlecries.

#### Easy-to-Read Stats

We all love seeing our minions reach absurdly high stats. To help keep your warband readable, numbers with more than four digits will now be condensed for a cleaner, easier-to-read display.

## Bug Fixes and Game Improvements

[Unity](#Unity)

- Hearthstone has been upgraded to a newer version of Unity. The game's minimum system requirements have changed as a result. For the latest requirements, visit the [Hearthstone System Requirements support page](https://us.support.blizzard.com/en/help/article/28991) and select your operating system for more information.

- Fixed a bug where Shadow of Demise could be played after a non-spell card was played, despite having no valid effect.
- Fixed a bug where the Essence generated by Dragon Soul, Shattered could be cast twice by Sinestra.

- Fixed a bug where Prepare incorrectly triggered Agamaggan's effect.

- Fixed a bug where Ascendance allowed a damaged Time-Twisted Seer to grant permanent Spell Damage for the rest of the game.
- Fixed a bug that prevented Tombs of Terror from being started.
- Fixed a bug where Secrets cast by The Origin Stone could be seen by opponents through the history bar.

- Fixed a bug where Memoriam Manifest summoned Onyxia's Wing before Arisen Onyxia appeared.
- Fixed a bug where Violet Punisher failed to steal Taunt from certain minions.
- Fixed a bug where The Hero's Welcome did not trigger Rangari Scout's effect.
- Fixed a bug where Siphoning Growth granted 16 Armor instead of 8 while Niri of the Crater was in play.

- Fixed a bug where Dire Fate effects that grant Windfury, Taunt, or Charge did not apply to summoned minions.
- Fixed a bug where Conflux Crasher could still deal damage to an enemy minion after it had already died.
- Fixed a bug where Zilliax Deluxe 3000 was guaranteed to be drawn after being shuffled into the deck by its own Deathrattle.
- Fixed a bug where Helpless Hatchling's Deathrattle could reduce the Cost of Beasts that already cost 0.
- Fixed a bug where Headmaster Kel'Thuzad did not add Mr. Bigglesworth to hand under certain board conditions.
- Fixed a visual bug where Rampaging Hound displayed the Beast type instead of Elemental Beast.
- Fixed a visual bug where Winterspring Whelp's Frost effect could trigger twice while The Origin Stone was in play.
