Marin the Manager has unlocked his treasure vault as a part of our ongoing 10-Year Anniversary celebrations. Over the next four weeks, check in to the Hearthstone in-game shop for special gifts that can be claimed for free for a limited time only\*.

Here’s what you can look forward to:

![Golden Harth Stonebrew Card](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/er/ER1WQ3Y9GH261722384595637.png)

**Week 1 and 2: Golden Harth Stonebrew\*\***

- **Start Time:** 1 August 18:00 BST
- **End Time:** 14 August 17:59 BST

![Golden Perils in Paradise Pack](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/4EH5HNHV99441722383091518.jpg)

**Week 2**: **1 Golden Perils in Paradise Pack**

- **Start Time:** 7 August 18:00 BST
- **End Time:** 14 August 17:59 BST

![Standard Perils in Paradise Pack](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/QKYM0UV7JQKH1722383091470.jpg)

**Week 3**: **1 Standard Perils in Paradise Pack**

- **Start Time:** 14 August 18:00 BST
- **End Time:** 21 August 17:59 BST

**Week 4**: **1 Standard Perils in Paradise Pack**

- **Start Time:** 21 August 18:00 BST
- **End Time:** 28 August 17:59 BST

![Mana%20Crystals.png](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/U3TAQLGQRTS31662475366928.png)

*\*Weekly gifts only available between 1 August and 28 August during above the listed time periods.\
\*\*Players who previously received the Golden Harth Stonebrew will instead receive 1000 Runestones as an automatic grant to your account no later than 28 August 2024.*
