Experience the magic of the Warcraft 30th Anniversary celebration firsthand at one of six intimate gatherings around the world. During the months of February through May 2025, we're uniting the Warcraft community in London, Seoul, Toronto, Sydney, São Paulo, and Boston.

Enjoy an evening of live entertainment, meet Warcraft developers, and experience unique activities. Attendance will be limited, so make sure to obtain your free ticket for your chance to join the celebration in person!

Don't miss this epic celebration of all things Warcraft! Visit our [official site](https://blizz.ly/Warcraft30thAnniversary) to learn more about each location.
