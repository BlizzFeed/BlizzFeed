## Battlegrounds Updates

Get ready for **Season 15 of Battlegrounds: Warped Empire**! This new Battlegrounds season launches December 1 with three new keywords, two new heroes, the return of the Timewarped Tavern, and new cards for all Minion types.

### New Keyword: Resilient

With the new Resilient keyword, your minion takes half damage when attacked (rounded up).

![WARLOCK_BG37_005_enUS_HardyFelhound-134521_NORMAL.png](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/16WEVCXYPUFL1794437158623.png)![WARLOCK_BG37_006_enUS_NonchalantNapper-134523_NORMAL.png](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/WRBNS64XGM2J1794437159041.png)

With the addition of Resilient to the pool of Bonus Keywords, we're rotating Stealth out for the time being. ‌

### New Keyword: Mission

The new Mission keyword gives your minion a goal to complete for a one-time reward.

### Returning Gameplay System: Timewarped Tavern

Turn back the clock, because the Timewarped Tavern is back! Trinkets are rotating out as we welcome back this returning gameplay system. On Turns 6 and 9, visit the Timewarped Tavern to spend Chronum on special cards you won’t normally find in Bob’s Tavern.

#### Hero Changes

King Mukla - Bananarama

- Old: At the start of your turn, get 2 Bananas and give everyone else one.
- New: At the start of each turn, get 2 Tavern Dish Bananas and give everyone else one.

#### Hero Lobby Changes

- **Zerek, Master Cloner**: Timewarp restriction removed.
- **Sindragosa**: Now banned from Pirate lobbies.
- **Millhouse Manastorm**: Now banned from Pirate lobbies.
- **Murozond, Unbounded**: No longer banned from Aberration lobbies.
- **Morchie**: No longer banned from Aberration lobbies.

### Deity Update

Deities no longer share one summoning rule. Each one now has its own number, X, of friendly Aberrations that must die each combat. C'Thun and Y'Shaarj still need 4, and the newest Deity, N'Zoth, needs just 3. Also, the Deity now summons where the most recent Aberration died.

#### 2v2 Gameplay Rules

- **Separate Health**: Each player has their own Health pool.
- **Victory**: If either opponent’s Health reaches 0, your team wins!
- **Simultaneous Turns**: Allies take their turn at the same time.
- **Communicate**: Right-click a card or minion to ping your ally.
- **Portal**: Click the Portal to view your ally’s board.
- **Pass**: Drag a card from your hand to the Portal to pass a card to your ally. You must wait until the end of your next turn to pass again.

#### Event Card Acquisition Update

We've heard your feedback about missed event cards. Those cards can matter for competitive decks, and waiting for them to become available can be frustrating. We're working on longer-term changes for future events, and in the meantime, we're making a temporary change to help everyone keep their collection up to date.

Starting with this patch, you'll be able to obtain any event cards you missed one week after the event ends. To be eligible, you need to own at least one card pack from the expansion tied to that event's patch. Then visit the Open Packs screen to receive your cards. For example, if you miss the upcoming "Battle of Y'Shaarj" event, you'll need to own a *Reign of the Black Empire* pack to obtain its cards. (You don't need to open the pack. It just has to be in your account.)
