## New Keyword for Demons: Fodder

Fodder minions are added to your personal minion pool. Whenever you Refresh into one, a hungry friendly Demon consumes it, gains its stats, and refills the slot.

## Returning Mechanic: Trinkets

Trinkets return in Battlegrounds Season 13 with an enhanced system. You’re now guaranteed a trinket of your most common type in both Lesser and Greater selections, making it easier to find trinkets that fit your strategy and see stronger options more often.

Visit [this forum post](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/hearthstone/t/battlegrounds-developer-insights-updated-trinket-rules/158955) for a detailed explanation of the updated Trinket system.

## Battlegrounds Track Refresh

![HS_33p2_BGSeason13_Announce_InBlog_First4_760x435_SW01.png](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/NVKHFFHHP0AK1774553292203.png)

On April 14, Battlegrounds Season 12 will end, and Season 13 will begin! With that, Battlegrounds Ratings will reset, and the Battlegrounds Track will update. Any rewards that were already earned from the Season 12 Rewards Track, but not yet claimed, will be automatically claimed for you.

![HS_35p2_BGSeason13_Announce_InBlog_BGPremiumPass_DB_760x428_SW01.jpg](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/N63L5DDOWR1R1774553293127.jpg)

The Season 13 Battlegrounds Track includes brand new cosmetics featuring Deathwing, Sindragosa, and more. Pick up the Season Pass to instantly unlock +2 Hero options at the start of each game. Then, progress through the Season Pass track to earn the new Cataclysmic Strike, 13 additional Hero Skins, a new Bartender, and more! Or upgrade to the Season Pass + to get all the rewards from the Season Pass **plus one included Hero re-roll per game**.

## Creator Early Access Event (with Drops!)

On April 9, watch your favorite content creators play the new Season live! Participating players will be streaming on their personal channels, so make sure you have notifications turned on. Time slots by region are:

- **Americas, LATAM, EMEA regions**: April 9 at 9:00 a.m. - 3:00 p.m. PDT
- **JP**: April 10 at 2:00 a.m. - 8:00 a.m. PDT
- **CN**: April 9 at 6:00 p.m. PDT - April 10 at 8:00 a.m. PDT

Participating creators will be giving away Battlegrounds Season Pass + codes.

Drops will be enabled category-wide on Twitch. Watch for a total of 2 hours to earn 4 Battlegrounds Tokens (2 total hours, 1 hour per drop).
