Patch 32.2, launching April 29, brings Battlegrounds Season 10, fires up the Embers of the World Tree Mini-Set, and more!

#### Embers of the World Tree Ignite on May 13th!

![HS_32p2_MiniSet_Announce_InBlog_GoldenMiniSet_RN_760x428_SW01.jpg](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/qw/QW0U5BNKQT2E1745448968207.jpg)

The battle for the Emerald Dream rages on, culminating in conflagration! The Druids of the Flame have invaded the Dream and set the World Tree ablaze. Sift through the ashes for fresh firepower and reignite your hope in this hot new Mini-Set, unlocking on May 13!

The Embers of the World Tree has 38 all-new cards. Get them in Into the Emerald Dream packs, or as a complete 72-card\* Mini-Set. Get the normal version for $14.99 or 2000 Gold, or get the all-Golden version for $69.99 or 10,000 Gold. The all-Golden version comes with a bonus Diamond copy of the Fyrakk, the Blazing Legendary minion! You can see cards from the Embers of the World Tree Mini-Set in the official [Card Library](https://hearthstone.blizzard.com/cards).

*\* 4 Legendary cards, 2 Epic card (x2), 16 Rare cards (x2), and 16 Common cards (x2).*

#### Quality of Life Improvement: Updated Minion Frames

Battlegrounds minions have been given a refresh to make them easier to spot and understand, especially when quickly re-rolling through the Tavern. Now, each minion type will have one of the Hearthstone class card frames associated with it, as follows:

- Beasts – Hunter Frame
- Demons – Warlock Frame
- Dragons – Warrior Frame
- Elementals – Mage Frame
- Mechs – Paladin Frame
- Murlocs – Shaman Frame
- Naga – Demon Hunter Frame
- Pirates – Rogue Frame
- Quillboar – Druid Frame
- Undead – Death Knight Frame
- Typeless and All – Neutral Frame

Dual-Type minions will have Dual-Class card frames. These changes only apply to minions, not Tavern spells, Trinkets, or anything else that might associate to a minion or minion type.

*Dev Comment: Previously, all Battlegrounds minions had the Neutral frame, except for exact copies of Hearthstone-Mode cards, which would have their corresponding Hearthstone frame in Battlegrounds for consistency between the two Modes. We think this change lets the cards in each Mode better serve the gameplay and player experience of that Mode.*

#### Arena Update

When Patch 32.2 launches all ongoing Arena runs will end and there will be one last Arena season under the current system. The eligible sets for that season will be:

- Into the Emerald Dream (including the Embers of the World Tree Mini-Set, even before it releases, and the Fleeing Treant Arena-only card)
- Showdown in the Badlands
- Voyage to the Sunken City
- Forged in the Barrens
- Scholomance Academy
- Core

#### Loaner Decks Update

With Patch 32.2, the new/returning player loaner decks\* will update to include cards from the Into the Emerald Dream expansion (the main set, not the Mini-Set). The loaner decks that will be available for this expansion cycle will be Death Knight, Druid, Mage, Paladin, Shaman, and Warlock.

*\* Returning players who have been away for 90 or more days are eligible for a free deck just for logging in. Returning players can claim these returning decks each time they are eligible. New players are eligible for their free deck after completing the tutorial and Apprentice Track.*

#### Bug Fixes, Rules Updates, and Game Improvements

- **[General] Minimum iOS version has been increased to iOS 14.0.**
- [Hearthstone] The Dark Gift keyword is being adjusted so that Dark Gifts with keywords can’t be applied to minions that already have one or more of those keywords (previously, Dark Gifts were only blocked if fully redundant). Additionally, 0-Attack minions won’t be able to get the Sleepwalker Dark Gift (which gives Charge).
- [Hearthstone] Normal game rules are that a card moving backwards in a zone (such as from the board to hand, or hand to deck) loses its enchantments. Wallow the Wretched now has a specific exception to this rule: if Wallow the Wretched is moved from your hand to the top of your deck due to the Sweet Dreams Dark Gift, Wallow will now retain all its Dark Gift enchantments (but not other enchantments, and not any enchantments if it is moved backwards in a zone due to anything other than its own effect).
- [Hearthstone] Adjusted the evolve/devolve rules. The full rule is now: When evolving, if a minion costs 10 or less, it is capped at 10. When evolving, if a minion costs more than 10, it evolves into a random minion of its same cost (which could just be the same minion). When devolving, if a result is not available then the minion will continue devolving until a result is found (for instance, devolving a 15-cost minion would result in a 12-cost if there was a 12-cost minion in the pool, but no 13- or 14-cost minions).
- [Hearthstone] Starship Piece “when launched” effects will now always trigger in a random order.
- **[Hearthstone] Effects that have the same resolution trigger will now always resolve in timestamp order. (This means Briarspawn Drake’s effect will now always trigger its own effect before the "return to deck" effect given to it by Cliff Dive).**
- [Hearthstone] Replaced Flint Firearm with Astalor Bloodsworn in the Splendiferous Whizbang in the Deck of Legends (Hunter) Experimental Deck. Flint was firing blanks in Standard after Quickdraw rotated out of the format.
