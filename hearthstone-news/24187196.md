# 32.0 Patch Notes

- URL: https://news.blizzard.com/en-gb/article/24187196/32-0-patch-notes

> Patch 32.0 starts the Year of the Raptor with Hearthstone’s latest expansion, Standard rotation, Core Set updates, a huge balance patch, community days, and more!

---

Patch 32.0, launching tomorrow, makes the final preparations for Hearthstone’s next expansion and the Year of the Raptor!

### Step Into the Emerald Dream on March 25

Into the Emerald Dream, Hearthstone’s next expansion, launches on March 25! The new 145-card expansion includes the new Imbue and Dark Gift keywords, Legendary Wild Gods, and more! See all the new cards in the [Card Library](https://hearthstone.blizzard.com/cards) today. Don’t forget to grab your [Pre-Purchase bundles](http://shop.battle.net/product/hearthstone-into-the-emerald-dream-mega-bundle) before you wake up and they’re gone!

![HS_31p6_Expansion_Announcement_InBlog_MegaBundle_RN_760x428_SW01.jpg](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/W41GDC8PJRYT1739316145680.jpg)

With the launch of Into the Emerald Dream comes a new Rewards Track, filled with dreamy goodies like XP boosts, cards, Gold, card packs, and more! Check out the [Rewards Track Refresh](https://hearthstone.blizzard.com/news/24187142) blog for details. Then grab some tasty nappetizers to get the expansion started: a launch gift of five free packs!\*

*\* 4 Year of the Pegasus Packs and 1 Into the Emerald Dream Pack. Free packs will be available to claim from the in-game Shop from March 25 to April 15.*

### Pre-Release Tavern Brawl and Community Days

The Into the Emerald Dream Pre-Release Tavern Brawl begins tomorrow, with Patch 32.0, and runs until expansion launch on March 25. Crack open your packs early, build a deck, and compete in a mini competition to win more Into the Emerald Dream prizes!

![HS_30p6_TGDB_Announcement_Blog_InBlog_Brawl_DB01.png](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/8VVKDI4SEIZ81727900217875.png)

Everyone will get one free entry into the Pre-Release Tavern Brawl. Ater that, entries will cost 2 Tavern Tickets, 300 Gold, or 400 Runestones. In each run, you will lock in a deck and then play until you get 6 wins or 3 losses, and then get prizes based on how well you do.

![HS_32p0_CommunityEvents_InBlog_PreReleaseBrawlChart_760x439_SW01.jpg](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/SLWEJOW0WR4C1741212719873.jpg)

The eligible card pool for the brawl will be:

- Core Set (before rotation)
- Event Set (before rotation)
- The Great Dark Beyond (without Heroes of StarCraft)
- Into the Emerald Dream

See what your favorite creators are dreaming up and earn more packs to use in the Pre-Release Tavern Brawl during an extended 5-day Community Days event! See the dedicated [Community Activations blog](https://hearthstone.blizzard.com/news/24185348) for more information about the Pre-Release Tavern Brawl and Community Days.

### Bug Fixes and Game Improvements

- [General] Fixed a bug causing missing text on Pixel devices.
- [Hearthstone] Cards that transform minions into ones that cost more (commonly called “Evolve” effects) are now capped at 10 mana. If the result would go over 10, then it results in a random 10-mana minion, instead.
- [Hearthstone] Using the last charge on a location that summons a minion will now clear the location away first and then summon the minion (meaning there will now be space for the minion if your board is full).
- [Hearthstone] Fixed a bug where Planetary Navigator didn’t work with Murmur.
- [Hearthstone] Fixed bugs with The Ceaseless Expanse’s tracking not counting cards destroyed when overdrawn, or destroyed in hand or deck by Flik Skyshiv.
- [Hearthstone] Fixed a bug where cards that tracked cards played (such as Rat Trap) sometimes counted Countered cards and sometimes did not. Countered cards should not count towards this kind of tracking.
- [Hearthstone] Fixed a bug where Liberator (the Starship Piece) stopped dealing its damage to functionally dead minions (relevant if the minion has an effect that is triggered each time it is damaged, like Acolyte of Pain).
- [Hearthstone] Fixed a bug where Multi-Strike's extra attack wouldn’t carry over after a hero skin transformation (like Metamorphosis Illidan’s).
- [Hearthstone] Fixed a bug where Chaotic Consumption wouldn’t be playable in certain edge situations.
- [Hearthstone] Fixed a visual bug where playing a multi-class StarCraft card in a Tourist deck triggered the Tourist animation.
- [Hearthstone] Fixed a visual bug where Signature Zergling, SCV, and Void Ray did not glow green when playable.
- [Hearthstone] Fixed a visual bug where launching a Starship could cause the End of Turn button to turn green when additional moves could still be made.
- [Battlegrounds] Fixed a bug where minions couldn’t be sold on turn 1 while the Lay of the Land Anomaly was active.
- [Battlegrounds] Fixed a bug where Knockoff Wisdomball didn’t respect the Bob-blehead Trinket.
- [Battlegrounds] Fixed a bug where Catacomb Crasher’s effect was triggered when Tavern spells couldn’t spawn into a Tavern (like if the Tavern was fully frozen or when Tess Greymane’s hero power spawned a Tavern without a Tavern spell).
- [Battlegrounds] Fixed a bug where Loyal Mobster wouldn’t activate Tough Tusk.
- [Battlegrounds] Fixed a bug where Blade of Ambition could fail to copy the minion with the highest stats in the Warband.
- [Battlegrounds] Fixed a bug where the Golden Hammer Spellcraft Spell was castable on Professor Putricide’s creations.
- [Battlegrounds] Fixed a bug where Lurker retained its base stats upon being Goldenized via the Golden Hammer.
- [Battlegrounds] Fixed a visual bug where rerolling a hero would give you a new hero with the default hero skin, instead of a toggled favorite.
- [Battlegrounds] Fixed a visual bug where the rope burned during combat when the Match Fixing Anomaly was active.
- [Collection] All in-client deck recipes and loaner decks will be updated with the launch of Into the Emerald Dream, so that they only include Standard cards after set rotation.
- [Collection] Fixed a bug where Signature Prince Renathal could rarely drop from Packs.
- [Progression] Fixed a bug where Quests and Tavern Brawl would show “0 days” in their tracking if less than one day remained.
- [Cosmetics] Fixed bugs where various hero skins lacked hero power animations, lacked their golden frame when the class was golden, or lacked the “Happy New Year” fireworks.
- [Cosmetics] Fixed a bug where transforming hero skins were temporarily untargetable during their transformations.
- Misc. other bug fixes and game improvements.
