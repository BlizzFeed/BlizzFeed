# Host a Summer Tavern Hero Qualifier!

- URL: https://hearthstone.blizzard.com/en-gb/news/20130369/host-a-summer-tavern-hero-qualifier
- Date: 2016-05-26T17:00:00Z
![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/nv/NVT5RXP2J07Y1456875838100.jpg)

We’ve heard your call, aspiring champions—and now there are more ways to get involved with the Tavern Heroes program than ever before! Starting Saturday, June 4, approved Fireside Gatherings will be able to host Tavern Hero Qualifiers. Winners of these competitions will be eligible to participate in the Tavern Hero tournament for a chance to earn a spot at the 2016 Hearthstone Europe Summer Preliminary. Find out more below!
