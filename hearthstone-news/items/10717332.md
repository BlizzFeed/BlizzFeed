# Gamers Get Their Hands on Hearthstone

- URL: https://hearthstone.blizzard.com/en-gb/news/10717332/gamers-get-their-hands-on-hearthstone
- Date: 2013-08-22T17:18:09Z
![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/N1WCQWS0GKIR1377176537626.jpg)

Today, for the first time in Europe, players were able to get their hands on Blizzard’s upcoming strategy card game Hearthstone. It’s a big draw at this year’s gamescom, with hours-long queues forming instantly when the show opened this morning.
