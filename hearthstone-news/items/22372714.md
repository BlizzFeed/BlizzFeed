Roboboom has—as is usually the case with Dr. Boom’s projects—run amok! Kicked out of Boom Labs and relegated to a rocky rookery somewhere in Netherstorm, the real Dr. Boom is left to lament his losses. Can he de-throne Roboboom to reclaim his lab, his pride, and his lady love? Find out!
