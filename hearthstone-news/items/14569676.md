# Garrosh Hellscream, the Warrior

- URL: https://hearthstone.blizzard.com/en-gb/news/14569676/garrosh-hellscream-the-warrior
- Date: 2014-06-26T17:00:00Z
![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/connect_thumbnail/NX01YK6J3VF81395183546708.jpg)

Garrosh Hellscream's meteoric rise and tragic descent into despotism and power hunger marked the path of a complex figure: an uncertain orc, out of his element and trying to live up to his father’s legacy. Garrosh sought to bring honor, glory, and prosperity to his people, but, for him, good intentions only paved a path of destruction.
