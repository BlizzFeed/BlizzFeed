# Community Tournament License Update

- URL: https://hearthstone.blizzard.com/en-gb/news/13184832/community-tournament-license-update
- Date: 2014-03-06T22:00:00Z
![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/K5413ASNPIEH1394135240627.jpg)

Want to run a Hearthstone tournament with a prize pool over $10,000 USD? That’s awesome! We’d love to work closely with you to ensure the success of your tournament. For larger tournaments like this, we can often help promote your event in the appropriate region, or offer other assistance. That process starts with obtaining a tournament license from Blizzard.
