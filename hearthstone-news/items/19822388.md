# Hearthstone Patch Notes - 2.8.0.9554

In this Hearthstone patch we’ve added the ability to pre-purchase a discounted bundle of 50 The Grand Tournament card packs that includes The Grand Tournament card back as a bonus! We also added the Darkspear card back and fixed a few bugs.
