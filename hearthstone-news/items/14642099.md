Gather round, fellow inn dwellers! Grab a drink and settle down by the hearth. Listen as the innkeeper shares the tales of The Yogscast’s Simon “the noob,” the brave adventurer who recently embarked on a long and perilous journey to vanquish the scourge “tryhard” Lewis. Can he overcome the challenges that await him and become a Hearthstone master?
