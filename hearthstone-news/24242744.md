> This balance update includes changes for Standard, Wild, and Battlegrounds.

---

Patch 34.6.2 launches today with balance changes to Standard, Wild, and Battlegrounds.

## Hearthstone Updates

*Dev Comment: Well met! We’ve loved seeing the excitement around the Hearthstone Spotlight and what’s ahead with [CATACLYSM](https://hearthstone.blizzard.com/en-us/news/24245219). If you haven’t already, make sure to check out the free goodies in the [Dragon’s Hoard](https://hearthstone.blizzard.com/en-us/news/24242864/your-guide-to-cataclysm-card-reveals-and-free-rewards-in-the-dragon-s-hoard) and claim your login reward, Warmaster Blackhorn, who has already begun making waves in the meta.*

*Warmaster Blackhorn offers an early taste of CATACLYSM‘s devastation, and he may have brought a little too much of it. We’ve seen some players love his power, while others have found facing him frustrating. We enjoy cards that spark strong reactions and see plenty of play, but Blackhorn has proven to be a bit too impactful. To address this, we are adjusting him to only destroy cards in decks, preserving his destructive identity while allowing for more back-and-forth gameplay.*

*In Wild, these changes target a handful of decks that have become too common or that create especially sharp play patterns. Updates to Discard Warlock keep the archetype intact while lowering its power and consistency. Changes to Egg Warlock slow its strongest turns, and an adjustment to the Ysiel Druid combo strategy reduces games ending in a single explosive turn.*

*Looking ahead, a much larger balance update is planned for Patch 35.0, alongside rotation and Core Set updates. We look forward to sharing more details soon!*

## Battlegrounds Updates

*Dev Comment: Most of the Battlegounds balance changes in this patch focus on individual cards that were either over- or underperforming, but we wanted to highlight a few key updates.*

*First up is Silky Shimmermoth. This card was a significant power outlier and was temporarily removed some time ago. It’s now returning with a redesign that should feel much less oppressive. To account for its return, we’ve also made adjustments to Turquoise Skitterer and Forest Rover.*

*Next, let’s talk about Mechs. Volumizers have been somewhat underperforming since the return of Apexis Guardian, but that card is tricky to adjust without pushing the archetype too far. Instead, we’ve chosen to buff the Volumizers and bring back Metal Dispenser, which has also received a buff compared to its previous iteration.*

*Lastly, we’ve removed the Eleventh Hour anomaly so we can keep the anomaly pool more curated and exciting.*

*We’ll be keeping a close eye on how the meta develops and will make further adjustments as needed. Thank you for all your feedback, and we’ll see you in the Tavern!*
