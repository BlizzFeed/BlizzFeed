Heroes, the battlefield is calling, and the fight is evolving. Overwatch 2 - Season 15: Honour and Glory is here and pulling no punches with the debut of the Perks system, a fresh Competitive Year, and a treasure trove of rewards that make every victory count. This season is bursting with content that will have you rallying your squad to claim rewards like never before. From a fun Perked Up event with weekly challenges, to Loot Boxes returning, this is your moment to fight for Honour and Glory. It’s time to step up, prove your skill, and make this season your own.

## Upgrade. Adapt. Dominate... with Perks!

Season 15 introduces Perks, the biggest evolution to Overwatch 2’s gameplay yet! Imagine taking your favorite hero’s abilities and cranking them up mid-match, creating new strategies and playstyles on the fly for each match in Quickplay and Competitive. That’s Perks. You’ll unlock Minor boosts that give you just the edge you need, and Major upgrades that can completely turn the tide of battle. Available for all 42 heroes, there will be a total of 168 Perks at launch with each hero having two Minor and two Major Perks to choose from.

But Perks are more than just upgrades; they’re a new way to experience Overwatch 2. Whether you’re chasing victory in Competitive or diving into Quickplay with friends, Perks add depth, strategy, and a whole lot of excitement to every match. The Perk system will continue to evolve with regular updates, ensuring it stays balanced, dynamic, and fun for all.

## A New Competitive Year Begins

It’s a new year and a new era for Overwatch 2’s Competitive scene. With a rank reset, everyone has a fresh slate to prove their skill and climb the ladder. Earn your bragging rights and show off every hard-fought victory with incredible rewards like Galactic Weapon Skins that bring a cosmic level of style to your arsenal and all-new Weapon Charms that let you flaunt your achievements in a way that’s uniquely yours.

The addition of Perks takes the competitive grind to the next level, creating new strategies and ways to dominate your matches. Whether you’re battling your way to Grandmaster or just testing your skills, this Competitive Year is all about leveling the playing field, refreshing the fun, and making every win feel more rewarding than ever.

## Get Perked Up!

Get ready for the Perked Up Event, a season-long celebration of the new Perks system and everything that makes Overwatch 2 special. Weekly challenges let you unlock up to 10 Loot Boxes filled with fun cosmetics, and the exclusive Rainy Day Mercy skin is yours to earn as well. But that’s not all - with Twitch Drops, you can grab the cozy Rainy Day Mei skin, and the Support-a-Streamer event lets you claim the sleek Rainy Day Tracer skin while supporting your favorite creators.

This event is all about keeping the fun alive week after week, giving you reasons to hop back into the fight. Every match you play, every challenge you complete, and every streamer you support gets you closer to unlocking some of the best seasonal rewards yet.

## Premium Battle Pass: Locked and Loaded with Loot

Speaking of seasonal rewards, the Season 15 Premium Battle Pass is packed with new content that’ll make every match feel like a win. Leading the charge is the Mythic Pixiu Zenyatta skin, a fully customizable masterpiece inspired by the mythical creature Pixiu and unlockable by earning Mythic Prisms. With four progression levels, you can upgrade from base armor to celestial effects that radiate good fortune and divine energy. Customize your look with three unique masks and colorways, including green/gold, purple/gold, and red/gold, to ensure Zenyatta shines like never before.

And it doesn’t stop there. Rainy Day Kiriko, Bi’an Orisa, Goumang Hanzo, Toxic Venture, and Cyber Enforcer Zarya bring the heat as Legendary skins, while Tianma Lucio adds an Epic touch. And, as you may have heard, Loot Boxes are back, including two Legendary Loot Boxes with the Premium Battle Pass, giving you even more chances to unbox standout cosmetics for your collection!***\**** Every level of this pass is a celebration of style and creativity, offering something for everyone.

Play for free to earn a Legendary Loot Box, 600 coins, 1500 credits, plus the Suanni Mei and Sindri Torbjörn skins.

***\*****Loot Boxes are not available from the Free Battle Pass or the Premium Battle Pass in Belgium.*\
