# New This Season

- We’re got [new team gear for 2020](https://shop.overwatchleague.com/?_s=bm-OWLcom-OWL-OWL_HP-MainNav-2019). Iconic streetwear designer [Jeff Staple worked with players and the league](https://overwatchleague.com/en-us/news/23277659) to reimagine esports gear.
- The future of Overwatch League stats has arrived. [Stats Lab](https://overwatchleague.com/statslab) gives fans access to a host of player, team, match, and teamfight statistics.
- The league is implementing Hero Pools in March. [Here’s how it will work](https://overwatchleague.com/en-us/news/23299542).
- Fan who attend home matches will get access to exclusive in-game team sprays. [Find out more here](https://overwatchleague.com/en-us/news/23284098).
- Celebrate your team spirit with [Fan Fridays](https://overwatchleague.com/en-us/news/23303229). We’ll see you online and in you gear!
- And finally, plan your next Overwatch League outing with our redesigned [2020 schedule](https://overwatchleague.com/en-us/schedule) and [tickets hub](https://overwatchleague.com/en-us/tickets).
