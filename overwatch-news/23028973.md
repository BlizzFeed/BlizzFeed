# Jump into action with Baptiste’s Reunion Challenge

- URL: https://overwatch.blizzard.com/en-gb/news/23028973/jump-into-action-with-baptistes-reunion-challenge
![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/56/56F1N5EMHHLV1560476743757.jpg)

> Explore Baptiste's war-torn past in "What You Left Behind," a new short story by Alyssa Wong. Through July 1, earn awesome rewards—including the epic Combat Medic Baptiste skin—by winning games and watching Overwatch on Twitch.

---

Jump into action and prove yourself by completing Baptiste’s Reunion Challenge! We’re bringing Baptiste’s war-torn past revealed in the *Overwatch* short story, “What You Left Behind,” into the game with story-inspired content available to unlock for a limited time.

[![Video](https://markdown-videos-api.jorgenkh.no/youtube/tRz7i5F22Xg?width=1280&height=720)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tRz7i5F22Xg)

From **now** until **July 1**, players will be able to earn a player icon, eight sprays, and an **epic Combat Medic Baptiste skin,** for a total of 10 new rewards.

- [COMBAT MEDIC](#header1) - Learn how to unlock brand-new Baptiste sprays and skin
- [WATCH THEIR BACKS](#header2) - Earn special Baptiste-themed sprays by watching Twitch streams
- [AN UNEXPECTED REUNION](#header3) - Read the new short story, “What You Left Behind”

## COMBAT MEDIC

We’re bringing the excitement of the action-packed short story into *Overwatch* by making some of the special content in the story available to unlock in-game. Wins in Quick Play, Competitive Play, or Arcade will all earn you a limited-time player icon, two sprays, and a new epic skin: Combat Medic Baptiste!

**These special Baptiste’s Reunion Challenge rewards are a bonus on top of your normal earnings for playing in the Arcade.**

![OW_Baptiste-MicroEvent_InGameRewards_Embedded_JP.png](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/gallery/7x/7XLL8AXIH2SL1560476290690.png)

## WATCH THEIR BACKS

Between matches, you can watch Overwatch streams on Twitch to unlock even more Baptiste-themed cosmetics. Tune in to participating broadcasters while they’re playing Overwatch anytime through July 1 to earn six Baptiste’s Reunion Challenge sprays!

**IMPORTANT:** To be eligible to receive these [Twitch Drops](https://help.twitch.tv/customer/en/portal/articles/2754380-how-to-earn-drops) you must first link your Blizzard account to your Twitch account. Visit the [Twitch Connections](https://www.twitch.tv/settings/connections) page to log in and complete the link now.

## AN UNEXPECTED REUNION

After Jean-Baptiste Augustin left Talon, he wandered the world using his medical talents to make a difference where he could. But international criminal syndicates don't forget those who owe them debts. In "What You Left Behind," by Alyssa Wong, Baptiste's past catches up to him, with explosive results.

[![OW_Baptiste-MicroEvent_StoryCover.PNG](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/gallery/mn/MNHFPGHB7M0X1560476586816.PNG)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/gallery/MH2T6XNHIXXZ1560535557651.pdf)

Protect your allies in-game and keep your eye on the participating broadcaster channels so you can add all of these limited-time cosmetics to your collection.

Now get in there—this mission ends July 1!
