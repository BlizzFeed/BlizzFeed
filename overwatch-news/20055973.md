Your watch is about to begin. Overwatch™ arrives in stores starting **May 24** on PC, PlayStation 4, and Xbox One!

[![Pre-PurchaseNow_Button_OW_JP_320x80.png](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_folder_media/BNWEE45LI3NA1446520756966.png)](http://blizz.ly/BuyOverwatch)

Assemble your team and prepare your defenses, then head over to [www.buyoverwatch.com](http://www.buyoverwatch.com) to learn more about the different versions of Overwatch™—including the Origins Edition, which comes with special “origin” skins for five heroes and a cache of extra content for several Blizzard Entertainment games. You’ll also find links to prepurchase Overwatch on all three platforms.

As an added bonus, if you pre-purchase digitally or at participating retailers (GAME in the UK), you’ll unlock **early access to the upcoming Open Beta** for you and a friend starting May 3.
