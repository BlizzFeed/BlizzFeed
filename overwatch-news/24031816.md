# The Overwatch 2® Twitch Support-A-Streamer is back!

- URL: https://news.blizzard.com/en-gb/article/24031816/the-overwatch-2-twitch-support-a-streamer-is-back
![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/sw/SWJP593RFPOC1701221377698.png)

> Show off your colors with the Bird of Paradise Epic skin for Echo when you purchase or gift subscriptions to your favorite Overwatch® 2 Content Creators on Twitch!

---

Overwatch® 2 Content Creators are some of the best players in our community. Whether showing off their awesome skills, creating awesome custom games, or building incredible experiences with their fans, they bring Overwatch 2 to thousands of viewers worldwide on Twitch. This is why we’re celebrating our Content Creators through the return of Twitch Support-A-Streamer for Overwatch 2!

From November 30 through December 10, support your favorite eligible streamers when you subscribe or gift subscriptions on their channel, and you will receive the Epic Bird of Paradise Echo skin that will unlock in-game!

## Event Duration

From November 30 at 11:00 a.m. PST until December 10 at 11:59 p.m. PST.

## How to Participate

Log in to your [Twitch account](https://www.twitch.tv/login) (if necessary, first create a Twitch account for free at [Twitch.tv](https://www.twitch.tv/))

Visit one of the eligible participating Twitch channels listed below

While the channel is live streaming Overwatch, subscribe or gift a cumulative of two (2) subscriptions of any tier to earn the reward (Subscriptions gifted anonymously will not count toward the campaign).

\*Please note that the campaign will begin on November 30, 2022, at 11:00 a.m. PST. Any subscriptions or gifted subscriptions purchased before the start time will NOT be counted toward the campaign. ONLY subscriptions or gifted subscriptions purchased while the channel is live streaming Overwatch 2 with the category set to “Overwatch 2” will count towards the campaign.

## Reward for Supporting

The reward for participating in the Support a Streamer event is the Epic Bird of Paradise Echo skin! Once you purchase two (2) subscriptions or gifted subscriptions, this skin will unlock immediately in-game.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q. Can I participate in the campaign if I do not own Overwatch?**

A. Any viewer with a Twitch account can participate in the campaign to receive the Overwatch 2 Bird of Paradise Epic Skin for Echo. However, the free skin can only be redeemed to a Battle.net account and used on Overwatch 2 in regions where the game is available to download and play.

**Q. How can I confirm which channels are eligible for the campaign?**

A. The full list of eligible participating channels and the campaign period dates are listed above. If a Twitch channel does not appear on that list, they are not eligible for the campaign. Double-check the list to ensure that the Twitch channel you are watching is currently live, is streaming Overwatch during the campaign period, and its name is listed in the campaign.

**Q. How do I gift a subscription on Twitch?**

A. Users must first log into Twitch, using either a PC or mobile device. Subscriptions cannot be gifted anonymously if you wish to participate in this campaign. Purchases may be made on either a mobile device or your PC.

Detailed information on gifting subscriptions, including how to purchase them can be found [here](https://help.twitch.tv/s/article/gift-subscriptions).

**Q. How many subscriptions do I need to purchase? Which Tier(s) are eligible for the campaign?**

A. To qualify for the in-game reward, users must purchase two (2) subscriptions or gifted subs of any tier, or any combination of tiers towards an eligible channel, as long as their cumulative support is two (2) total gift subscriptions. The two (2) subscriptions or gifted subscriptions may be purchased at once or one at a time over the course of the campaign, across multiple eligible participating channels, or done on a single eligible participating channel.

**Q. I received two (2) gift subscriptions prior to or during the campaign. Am I eligible for the Overwatch 2 Bird of Paradise Echo Epic skin in-game reward?**

A. No. The Bird of Paradise Echo Epic skin in-game reward can only be earned by purchasing two (2) subscriptions or gifted subs to an eligible participating channel that is streaming Overwatch during the campaign period. Receiving a Gift Subscription during this period does not qualify for the in-game reward.

**Q. What should I do if I have a problem completing subscription purchases on Twitch?**

A. First, make sure that you are trying to complete a purchase in one of the eligible participating Twitch channels that appear above. Most commonly, refreshing the page, or trying a different payment method can help. For mobile purchases, please allow a moment to refresh. If you require further assistance, contact the Twitch Support team by reading common troubleshooting FAQs and common fixes [here](https://help.twitch.tv/s/article/purchase-support-faq).

**Q. Can I track how many eligible subscription purchases I have completed for the campaign?**

A. While there is not a centralized location to track progress in the campaign, users may refer to their [Twitch Payment History](https://www.twitch.tv/wallet?tab=purchase-history) to review recent purchases of subscriptions to eligible channels. This should be used only as a reference for successful subscription purchases and does not guarantee the subscriptions qualified for the campaign.

**Q. Where can I find my campaign rewards once I've earned them?**

A. The Bird of Paradise Echo Epic skin reward will be delivered in-game in Overwatch 2 once it has been earned  and displays in the Twitch notification inbox. The notification inbox can be found to the right in the top navigation bar on Twitch.

**Q. What should I do if I’m having a problem receiving my reward on Twitch?**

A. In-game reward delivery can take up to 72 hours to be delivered once earned.

If after waiting 72 hours and verifying that your purchases were made towards an eligible streamer that was live streaming, please contact Twitch Support [here](https://link.twitch.tv/SubsGiftingIssues) while logged in.

**Q. What regions are participating in the Support a Streamer campaign?**

A. Participating regions include North America, Latin America, Europe, The Middle East, Africa, South Korea, Japan, Taiwan, Southeast Asia, Australia, and New Zealand.
