# Overwatch Digital Comic: "Train Hopper"

![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/2r/2R7FKO02BM691461151559925.jpg)

> Infamous outlaw Jesse McCree needs to get out of town and lay low for a while. That’s a shame. His train ride to Houston is going to be anything but "low profile.”

---

*Justice ain't gonna dispense itself.*

It’s high noon as McCree takes center stage in **Train Hopper**—the first in a series of free digital comic shorts that further illuminate the characters and world of Overwatch.

In "Train Hopper," former Blackwatch operative-turned-vigilante Jesse McCree hitches a ride on a hypertrain to Houston in the hopes of skipping town and laying low for a while. But soon after the engine starts, he realizes his trip is going to be anything but "low profile."

[![OW_McCree_p03_thumb.jpg](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_folder_media/0w/0W0QKXMSREMX1461151831947.jpg)](http://comic.playoverwatch.com)

Written by Blizzard's own Robert Brooks and illustrated by Bengal (*Spider-Gwen, All-New Wolverine, Batgirl*), "McCree: Train Hopper" is now available to [read in full](http://comic.playoverwatch.com/en-us/mccree-train-hopper). We hope you enjoy it, and stay tuned for more Overwatch digital comics coming soon!
