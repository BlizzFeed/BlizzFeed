Now through **March 22**, win games to earn delightful new rewards and become the ultimate Pachimari enthusiast—second only to Roadhog, of course.

[![Video](https://markdown-videos-api.jorgenkh.no/youtube/w_AEBtnCf3c?width=1280&height=720)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=w_AEBtnCf3c)

Collect a slew of pachi-swag, a **player icon**, a **Junkrat emote,** **six unique sprays**, and **Pachimari Roadhog (epic)** for a total of **nine** new rewards.

- **ONE MAN A-PACHI-LYPSE** - Learn how to unlock rewards
- **LOG IN TO EARN** - Earn special Pachimari-themed cosmetics by logging into Overwatch

### ONE MAN A-PACHI-LYPSE

Become the ultimate pachimari fan and collect special pachimari-themed content available to unlock in-game. Wins in Quick Play, Competitive Play, or Arcade will all earn you a limited-time player icon, a spray, and a new epic skin: **Pachimari Roadhog**!

**These special PachiMarchi Challenge rewards are a bonus on top of your normal earnings for playing in the Arcade.**

Now through March 22, log in at any time to unlock 6 unique sprays featuring pachimar-ized versions of heroes!
