![BlogSectionBar-CheckBox-InstallAndPlay_v2.png](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_folder_media/HGBRV90JUMW41445962636342.png)

Before your watch can begin, you'll first need to install Overwatch on your Nintendo Switch System. To install and play on October 15, please follow the instructions below.

**Step 1:** Have a current [Nintendo Switch Online Membership](http://nintendo.com/switch/online-service/).\
**Step 2:** Turn on your Nintendo Switch System and log in to your preferred local user.\
**Step 3:** Overwatch should begin downloading as soon as it's available.\
**Step 4:**Once installed, select the Overwatch icon and press "A" to begin!.

![BlogSectionBar-CheckBox-InstallAndPlay_v2.png](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_folder_media/ES7P5SD3EHDZ1463677613737.png)
