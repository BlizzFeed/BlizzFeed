## After weeks of intense competition, Stage 1 is coming to its dramatic conclusion at Champions Clash!

Champions Clash brings together eight of the top teams from OWCS Asia, China, North America, and EMEA, marking the grand finale of Stage 1. From May 22–24, they’ll compete in a double-elimination bracket at Tachikawa Tachihi Arena in partnership with [GANYMEDE](https://gnmd.com/en/news/event-info/1524/). Every region is chasing glory, and a share of the crowdfunded prize pool, so expect nonstop, high-stakes action.

## Stage 1 Recap

The 2026 OWCS Season has gotten off to a fantastic start. All four regions have been fighting tooth and nail for their chance at a trip to Tokyo - and the race isn’t over yet! There are still two spots up for grabs, but China, North America, and EMEA have all locked their teams.

![CC Qual Teams 16x9 [Apr 27].png](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/gallery/dv/DVDM0OSJRPXI1777493315119.png)

**North America**

Dallas Fuel enter Champions Clash as NA’s #1 seed, having made a dominant return to Overwatch Esports in Stage 1. They went undefeated in the regular season and playoffs, utilizing off-the-wall team comps to disrupt their opponents’ game plans and defy the region’s meta. NA’s #2 seed, Spacestation, came in close behind off of the sheer strength of their star-studded roster. Their only regular season loss was to Dallas, who eked out the win in a [Map 5 nailbiter](https://youtu.be/NkhPh-56KFI?t=18424) in our final weekend of regular season play. The two teams met twice more in playoffs - and while SSG drew blood where they could, Dallas ultimately came out on top with a [3-1 Upper Finals](https://youtu.be/HnFJBXRmXLk?t=16176) win and a [4-1 Grand Finals](https://youtu.be/Q998tVE7B-w?t=18580) victory to cap off the Stage.

**EMEA**

Our reigning World Champions Twisted Minds extended their undefeated streak to take the region’s #1 seed with ease. Virtus.pro [fended off](https://youtu.be/E0xzLwD9sS0?t=5512) two strong [playoffs efforts](https://youtu.be/Q998tVE7B-w?t=1681) from Geekay Esports to meet TM in the [EMEA Grand Finals](https://youtu.be/Q998tVE7B-w?t=7052), and are joining them in Tokyo as the only team to have taken even a single **map** off of them since Al Qadsiah last year - firmly slating themselves as serious Champions Clash contenders in their own right.

**China**

Weibo Gaming continued their dominance in the China region, becoming the first team to secure a qualification spot from that region. The final qualification spot came down to a battle between JD Gaming and All Gamers, with the two teams neck and neck in score, fighting all the way to the fifth game. In the final overtime teamfight, players from both teams gave it their all, and ultimately All Gamers clinched the last qualification spot.

![CC Rosters 4x5 [Apr 27].png](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/gallery/g5/G5S1YP5E0RY21777493371949.png)

**Asia (Korea, Japan, & Pacific)**

Asia’s regional championship kicks off May 4th (in Pacific Time), where Korea’s Top 4, Japan’s Top 3, and the regional champs from the Pacific will fight for two spots at Champion’s Clash. The first two days of competition on May 4 and 5 (Pacific Time) will split the eight teams into two groups. The two best teams in each group will move on to the 4-team, double-elimination playoff bracket on May 8-10.

For full format and schedule details, be sure to follow the OW Esports Asia accounts on [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/ow_esports_kr) and [X](https://twitter.com/OW_Esports_asia)!

## Tournament Overview

Once our full slate of teams is confirmed, our first Champions Clash match-ups will be slotted into the schedule, and then we’re off! Three days. Double elimination. Global bragging rights and a crowdfunded prize pool on the line. This is your chance to see the world’s best go head-to-head.

![OWCS26 CC Bracket v2 [Apr 27].png](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/gallery/ou/OUWOR5YSPZFF1777493514184.png)

Each day of Champions Clash competition kicks off at **7pm PT/3am CEST.** Competition takes place on Patch 2.1, featuring the debut of Sierra in international play. With big changes since the last World Finals, teams will need sharp strategies to make a deep run.

Follow us on socials for your latest daily reminder of when games are on!

## Special Events

Whether you’re watching Champions Clash from home or sitting in the arena, we’ve got some truly special extras coming your way! Keep an eye on our socials for details about event co-streamers, and a live on-stage showmatch giving you an exclusive first look at what’s coming your way in Season 3.

Champions Clash will also have meet & greets with your favorite teams all weekend long - ticket holders will have a chance to apply to a draw for a meet & greet from May 9 to May 16 (PT), and successful applicants will be notified before the event.

The meet & greets will take place over three days, and will feature a rotating lineup of teams. On the first day, attendees will have the opportunity to meet Dallas Fuel, Twisted Minds, and a team from Asia. The second day will include the second team from China, another team representing Asia, and Virtus.Pro. On the final day, SSG will appear alongside one of the teams from China. Application is free, but subject to processing fees.

Also, a little birdie told us that there might be some Overwatch World Cup news sprinkled in over the course of the weekend… Be sure to tune in so you don’t miss it!
