> From Hero shuffling chaos to a molten Mythic and more, Season 20’s midcycle is packed with ways to warm your winter in Overwatch!

---

Winter might be settling in, but Season 20’s midcycle is keeping the heat turned all the way up and the action burning bright. Showdown Shuffle throws structure out the window, Quick Play Hacked brings Assault mode back with a modern edge, and Junkrat’s Loot Hunt turns a weekend into a reward-filled explosion. Add in a molten Mythic, Esports flair, fresh Shop collections, plus Drives, and this midcycle stays lively with multiple ways to warm your winter.

Let’s bundle back up and break it down!

## Showdown Shuffle

Another Showdown hits January 13 - 26, and it’s built around one simple idea: *let’s see what happens*. In Showdown Shuffle, you’ll lock into a role but your Hero changes constantly, keeping every fight fresh and every round unpredictable. Kills, assist milestones, and even your own elimination (twice) can all trigger swaps, turning each match into a fast-moving remix of the roster.

The result is a mode that feels light, loose, and surprisingly freeing. There’s no pressure to master every Hero, but the opportunity to try someone new. You’re encouraged to experiment, adapt on the fly, and enjoy the moments where things go sideways in the best possible way. Not knowing who you’ll be playing or what comes next is the best part!

Showdown Shuffle is available across Control, Hybrid, Escort, Push, and Flashpoint in a 5v5 format. Match modifiers rotate throughout the game, adding just enough unpredictability to keep things interesting without disrupting the flow of fun.

## Quick Play Hacked: Assault Returns!

From January 8 to 11, Assault steps back into the rotation with a Quick Play Hacked weekend that blends nostalgia with modern Overwatch pacing. Temple of Anubis, Hanamura, and Volskaya Industries return exactly where you remember them, but the fights feel very different with today’s Heroes, balance changes, and team flow.

This is a chance to revisit a familiar mode and see how the current roster reshapes your strategies. Jump in, vote on the map, and see how old-school layouts hold up in the OW2 era. It’s a celebration of where Overwatch started, tuned to feel fast, readable, and right at home.

## Junkrat’s Loot Hunt

January 22 to 25 belongs to Junkrat, and you can bet that he’s bringing explosions and prizes! Junkrat’s Loot Hunt turns the weekend into a straight-shot reward chase where every match counts and progress never resets. Play a little or play a lot. Either way, every game gets you closer to another Loot Box.

Challenges stay flexible, letting you grind everything out in one long session or spread the chaos across the weekend. There’s no daily pressure, no strict schedule, and no tricks. Just matches, milestones, and up to nine Loot Boxes waiting to be cracked open before the event wraps. Oh, and the explosions, obviously. It’s loud, generous, and exactly as Junkrat would want it. Boom first, ask questions later (or never and just unbox your rewards).

## Mythic Magma Titan Doomfist Hero Skin

Introducing **Magma Titan Doomfist**, a Mythic built around raw strength, control, and unmistakable presence. This skin channels blazing energy through a sleek streetwear style, turning Doomfist into a walking inferno.

Across four upgrade tiers, the look gets refined with additional gauntlets, colorways, and effects that let you shape just how much heat you bring into every fight. Custom ability VFX and unique sound effects make every punch land a fiery statement, reinforcing you and Doom as the unstoppable force you always have been.

Start with a complete Mythic form, then tune it piece by piece, dialing in the exact balance of burn and spectacle befitting your unique style. Become a storm of molten fury and unyielding defiance with Mythic Magma Titan Doomfist!

## Last Chance Rank Reset

The Competitive reset is closing in, and these are the final moments to lock things down! Titles, Galactic skins, and Competitive Points are all on the line, and every match carries that extra weight.

Your ending rank reflects your final push, so finish strong and leave your mark. Stack points, secure rewards, and finish on your terms before placements reset and the climb begins again. The clock is ticking… rally your squad, queue up, and make the last stretch count!

## Cosmic OWL Skins

Esports pride arrives with **Cosmic OWL** skins that rework iconic looks for **Widowmaker, Lúcio,** and **Reinhardt**. These Legendary recolors take fan-favorite designs and push them into cosmic territory, blending competitive legacy with bold new visual flair.

Timed alongside live Esports moments in Season 20, the Cosmic OWL Collection bridges the gap between just watching the action and being part of it yourself. It’s a stylish way to show your support, celebrate the scene, and bring a bit of pro energy to the battlefield.

## Season 20 Drives

From January 30 to February 4, Drives return with a sharp burst of progression controlled by your commitment and performance. Push through checkpoints, take on themed objectives, and climb toward exclusive Season 20 rewards, like a new green Signature.

Wins matter more as you climb, bonuses kick in when you play with friends, and losses stay forgiving early before scaling higher up the ladder. Drive Score can be earned across Core Competitive and Stadium, giving you multiple ways to push forward and prove your drive to rise.

## Shop Collections

### Borealis

Officially available January 8 to 19, the **Borealis Collection** steps beyond traditional holiday vibes into high-fantasy winter legends**. Mercy, Wuyang, Lifeweaver, Sojourn,** and **Echo** take on icy, celestial forms shaped by frost, starlight, and Arctic power. They’re elegant, magical, and designed for the coldest battles.

### Heist

From January 13 to 26, the **Heist Collection** flips the seasonal script. **Roadhog, Reaper,** and **Ashe** suit up in gritty underworld looks built for high-stakes jobs and loud exits. Sharp silhouettes, criminal attitude, and zero holiday cheer make this the perfect contrast to the season’s brighter offerings. Crime doesn’t take a holiday, and neither do these skins.
