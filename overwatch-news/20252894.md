> The votes are in—here are your Overwatch World Cup national teams!

---

Earlier this month, we announced that the first-ever **Overwatch World Cup** would be hosted at BlizzCon 2016, and we called on YOU—our players—to help us select the dream team that would represent your country. After tabulating over 3 million votes and gathering suggestions from each of the Team Captains, we're happy to reveal the final rosters for all the nations and regions participating in this international exhibition!
