# Friendship, but Make It Heroic with Overwatch x Hello Kitty & Friends

> Team up to make Overwatch a little brighter as Hello Kitty & Friends join the fray!

---

Sometimes the world doesn’t need saving with abilities and Ults alone... sometimes it needs a little kawaii kindness and charm!

From February 10–23, Overwatch teams up with Hello Kitty and Friends to deliver a feel-good crossover that brings the beloved characters into the world of Overwatch. This delightful collaboration is making the world a little cuter—because you can be powerful and adorable, even when things get chaotic. <3

## Six Heroes, Six Friends

Overwatch x Hello Kitty and Friends introduces six new themed Hero skins:

- Always ready to help, kindness meets courage and looks cute doing it when **Juno** steps in as **Hello Kitty**.
- Light on her feet and sweet as ever, **Kiriko** floats through as **Cinnamoroll**.
- Bringing comfort, care, and cozy charm to every clutch save, **Mercy** suits up as **Pompompurin**.
- Confident and ready to shine with playful superstar energy, **D.Va** drops in as **My Melody**.
- Sweet on the surface, sharp underneath, **Kuromi**’s mischievous edge pairs perfectly with **Widowmaker**’s cool precision.
- Fast beats, big smiles, and nonstop good vibes flow freely! **Lucio** hops into action as **Keroppi**.

![KawaiiCatIllustration.jpg](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/3i/3I3Q0ABVTXIJ1770235869720.jpg)

Each skin is crafted with care, celebrating the personality of both the Hero and their Hello Kitty and Friends counterparts. And this collaboration is more than skin deep, with premium Play of the Game moments that feature fully realized 3D Hello Kitty and Friends characters! These joyful highlights turn your big plays into heartwarming commemorations and create some of the most visually distinctive collab moments Overwatch has ever seen. Yes, it’s adorable... but also, still feels epic.

## Collect Your Favorites, Your Way

You can pick up individual Hero bundles for the characters you love most or go all-in with the Mega Bundle to collect the full Hello Kitty and Friends experience.

Plus, conquer challenges to earn 2D cosmetics like name cards, sprays, and player titles inspired by both universes, plus 12.5k Battle Pass XP. Cute wins count too!
