> Enjoy the globetrotting music from Overwatch’s maps and missions with the Overwatch: Cities & Countries soundtrack!

---

*What is that melody?* A brand new collection of Overwatch music, featuring the themes from the game’s maps and missions. *Overwatch: Cities & Countries* releases worldwide starting Today! Get a taste of the album with the [YouTube playlist](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLAYFVhxsaqDszyxCBNVKd5IC6RvtzTkkj)!

[![Video](https://markdown-videos-api.jorgenkh.no/youtube/videoseries?width=1280&height=720)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=videoseries)

*Overwatch:Cities & Countries* is undergoing rolling releases across several popular music streaming platforms, going live in each region on July 13. Check back in on [PlayOverwatch.com/music](https://www.playoverwatch.com/music/) to see where you can listen to the soundtrack as it becomes available in your region.\*

Plus, tune up your instruments and prepare for a brand new in-game event; Sigma’s Maestro Challenge begins on July 14!

\*Streaming platform availability may vary by region.
