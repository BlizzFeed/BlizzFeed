# Join us for the Summit1g vs. iiTzTimmy Showdown

![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/wb/WBZ8CIPEY2DV1669234679138.png)

> Watch iitzTimmy and Summit1g go head-to-head in an Overwatch 2 Showdown showcase event!

---

On November 25 at 2 PM PST, Summit1g and iiTzTimmy are each putting together their best team of five to face off in a first to three showcase match. You will be able to catch all the action on both [Summit1g’s](https://www.twitch.tv/summit1g) and [iiTzTimmy’s](https://www.twitch.tv/iitztimmy) Twitch channels, as they compete for a $25,000 prize pool. You can also watch it on the PlayOverwatch Twitch channel featuring Soe and Mr. X calling all the action.

[Twitch drops](https://overwatch.blizzard.com/en-us/news/23865971/) will be live so don’t forget to connect your Twitch account through [Battle.net](https://account.battle.net/connections) to earn your rewards. Players who changed their password since the last campaign need to sign into their account again to claim future Twitch drops. The Halloween Terror Twitch drops are also available on all platforms, and participating regions include North America, Europe, Latin America, Australia/New Zealand, Taiwan, Korea, and Southeast Asia.
