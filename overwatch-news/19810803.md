# Play Overwatch @ gamescom 2015

- URL: https://overwatch.blizzard.com/en-gb/news/19810803/play-overwatch-gamescom-2015
![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/YDC4AWWEX5261435174031120.jpg)

> Join us August 5–9 in Cologne, Germany!

---

Looking for your next shot at playing Overwatch? Make like Reinhardt and charge hammer-first into **[gamescom 2015](http://www.gamescom-cologne.com/)**, the world's largest interactive entertainment trade fair taking place August 5–9 at the Koelnmesse in Cologne, Germany.

This year's gamescom marks the first time Blizzard Entertainment's 6v6 shooter will be playable publically in Europe—so if you want to get in on the action, assemble your team in our official booth located in Hall 7.

[![gamescom_2015-BoothLocation_OW_LightboxThumb_JP01_700x200.jpg](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_folder_media/1S5WE3WEAESG1435099683719.jpg)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_folder_media/2PN7ZZ6472801435099685948.jpg)

### Bonus Objectives:

In between battles, Hall 7 will also host a variety of activities around all of Blizzard’s games, including developer signing sessions, showmatches, quizzes, community contests, and more. On top of that, the latest versions of Heroes of the Storm, Hearthstone, StarCraft II, and World of Warcraft will be playable on more than 500 game stations in Blizzard's sector of the show.

We'll have more details to share on all of our convention-deployment plans as gamescom gets closer—in the meantime, be sure to visit [www.blizzgc.com](http://www.blizzgc.com/) and [www.gamescom-cologne.com](http://www.gamescom-cologne.com/) for more event details!
