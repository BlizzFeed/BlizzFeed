# Official Overwatch Novel: The Hero of Numbani

- URL: https://overwatch.blizzard.com/en-gb/news/23429411/official-overwatch-novel-the-hero-of-numbani
- Date: 2020-05-26T19:55:00Z
![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/ot/OTNCJQKE4PNH1590521209293.png)

The first official Overwatch novel from critically acclaimed author Nicky Drayden is coming June 2! The Hero of Numbani tells the full story behind Efi and Orisa’s heroic origins, offers a glimpse into Talon’s machinations in Numbani, and provides some must-know hints at things to come.
