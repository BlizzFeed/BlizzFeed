# Overwatch: Origins Edition Digital Goodies Now Available

- URL: https://overwatch.blizzard.com/en-gb/news/20098642/overwatch-origins-edition-digital-goodies-now-available
- Date: 2016-04-19T21:30:00Z
![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/rp/RP481H9PAGDF1461029025909.jpg)

In-game extras for World of Warcraft, Diablo III, StarCraft II, Heroes of the Storm, and Hearthstone are now available to players who pre-purchased Overwatch: Origins Edition on Windows PC from the Battle.net Shop.
