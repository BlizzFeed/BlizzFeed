> Calling all heroes of Overwatch®—we're partnering with Twitch and Amazon to bring you a special Golden Loot Box!

Calling all heroes of Overwatch®—Blizzard Entertainment is excited to announce that we’re partnering with Twitch and Amazon to bring you a special Golden Loot Box\*!

From **June 20–August 20**, anyone who links their Amazon Prime and Twitch account (as well as all active Twitch Prime members) will be eligible to receive a Golden Loot Box in Overwatch. This promotional in-game Loot Box contains four random items from our classic collection, including one guaranteed legendary. Of course, you'll also enjoy all the benefits that come with Prime—from streaming movies and TV shows on Amazon Prime Video, to ad-free viewing of your favorite streamers on Twitch, to special in-game rewards like this for Overwatch and other Blizzard titles.

[Click here to activate your Twitch Prime account or start a free trial.](https://twitch.amazon.com/prime/loot/overwatch?ref_=SM_CM_BE_OWBP)

## How to Claim Your Golden Loot Box Offer Redemption Instructions

**Claim your Prime Loot code:**

**Step 1:** Visit [twitch.tv](http://twitch.tv/) and log into your Twitch account.\
**Step 2:** Click on the Prime Loot icon located in the upper-right of the screen.\
**Step 3:** Locate the Overwatch Golden Loot Box offer from the drop-down menu.\
**Step 4:** Select "Claim Code" and follow the instructions provided.

**Once you've claimed your Golden Loot box code:**

**Step 5:** [Create](https://battle.net/account/creation/) or [log in](https://battle.net/login/?ref=https://battle.net/account/management/&app=bam&cr=true) to your Blizzard account.\
**Step 6:** Visit <http://blizzard.com/code>\
**Step 7:** Enter your code, and then follow the steps for your platform below.

**Redeem your Golden Loot Box on PC:**

**Step 8:** [Download](http://battle.net/app/) and install the Blizzard app.\
**Step 9:** Once the Blizzard app launches, click the **Overwatch** icon.\
**Step 10:** Select your region from the dropdown menu, then click **Install**.\
**Step 11:** Once installed, click “Play” and open your Loot Box!

**Redeem your Golden Loot Box on PS4 or Xbox One:**

**Step 8:** [Link your PlayStation Network or Xbox Live account to your Blizzard account.](https://battle.net/support/article/Linking-your-Console-Account-on-Battle-net)\
**Step 9**: On your console, log in to the game and open your Loot Box!

*\*Offer valid only in regions where Twitch Prime is available. Must own Overwatch to redeem. Loot Box does not include seasonal event items. Content of Loot Box are random. Each Blizzard account may only redeem one code per promotional item.*
