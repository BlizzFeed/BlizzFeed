Earn new rewards, including **Marammat Symmetra**, by winning games and watching *Overwatch* on Twitch. Plus, embark on a journey of understanding with Symmetra and Zenyatta in Christie Golden’s new Overwatch short story, “Stone by Stone.”

[![Video](https://markdown-videos-api.jorgenkh.no/youtube/sMkQiQ5mA_8?width=1280&height=720)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sMkQiQ5mA_8)

From now until November 30, players will be able to earn a **player icon**, **seven unique sprays**, and **Marammat Symmetra (epic)** for a total of **nine** new rewards.

- **EVERYTHING BY DESIGN** - Learn how to unlock brand-new Symmetra rewards
- **WATCH TO EARN** - Earn special Symmetra-themed cosmetics by watching Twitch streams
- **NEW COMIC** - Learn more about Christie Golden’s new short story, “Stone by Stone”

## EVERYTHING BY DESIGN

Bend reality to your will to earn exceptional new content available to unlock in-game. Wins in Quick Play, Competitive Play, or Arcade will all earn you a limited-time player icon, a spray, and a new epic skin: **Marammat Symmetra**!

**These special Symmetra’s Restoration Challenge rewards are a bonus on top of your normal earnings for playing in the Arcade.**

![Symmetra's Restoration Challenge In-Game Rewards. 3 wins unlocks a Player Icon, 6 wins unlocks a Spray, 9 wins unlocks the epic Marammat Symmetra skin](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/gallery/q8/Q8EQWDX5NTZ41605140050051.png)

## WATCH TO EARN

Unlock additional rewards by watching Twitch streamers before, during, or after matches to get even more Symmetra-themed cosmetics. Tune in to any Twitch streamer while they’re playing Overwatch now through November 30 to earn six Symmetra’s Restoration Challenge sprays!

**IMPORTANT**: To be eligible to receive these [**Twitch Drops**](https://help.twitch.tv/customer/en/portal/articles/2754380-how-to-earn-drops), you must first link your Blizzard account to your Twitch account. Visit the [**Twitch Connections**](https://www.twitch.tv/settings/connections) page to log in and complete the link now.

![Symmetra's Restoration Challenge Twitch Drops Rewards. Earn one spray by viewing 2 hours of eligible streams, an additional two sprays by viewing 4 hours of eligible streams, and an additional three sprays by viewing 6 hours of eligible streams.](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/gallery/c7/C7BP588W3JYV1605140895444.png)

Symmetra's Restoration Challenge drops will be available **November 17 - 30**. All channels streaming in the [**Twitch Overwatch game category**](https://www.twitch.tv/directory/game/Overwatch) will be available for rewards.

We’ll be spotlighting Overwatch content creators each day throughout Symmetra’s Restoration Challenge! Catch some of your favorite creators throughout the event to unlock new cosmetics.

## NEW SHORT STORY

### "Stone by Stone" by Christie Golden

When Vishkar Corporation inadvertently destroys a sacred statue of Aurora, the first sentient omnic, the brilliant hard-light architech Satya Vaswani—known also as Symmetra—is sent in to avert a PR disaster. She’s been instructed to give the village whatever it wants—but she doesn’t know what that might be. When she accepts Tekhartha Zenyatta’s invitation to stay at the temple and learn about Aurora and the omnic faith, Symmetra embarks on a journey of understanding, compassion, and acceptance that results in the perfectly imperfect gift to the village, to Zenyatta… and to herself.
