## The First Melee DPS Hero

Every good winter brings a storm. Ours is Vendetta, the first melee DPS Hero in Overwatch history! She arrives with cold ambition in her grip and Talon’s legacy in her wake. Once denied her inheritance as Antonio Bartalotti’s daughter, she fought her way through the Colosseo pits with nothing but ruthlessness and a hunger for more. Now she stands on the battlefield with blade in hand and a plan unfolding.

![OVR_S19_H45_Vendetta_Ultimate_SunderinghBlade_002.png](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/rs/RSJQTCJHTL0M1764204035074.png)

Vendetta thrives where the fight gets tight. She lunges, slices, leaps, and closes distance with a style that feels regal (if royal families still solved their problems with live steel). Each strike carries her spite. Each movement carries her confidence. And every victory will feel like you earned it. Her kit blends mobility and power, flair and fury. She is inevitable. And she’s here to claim vengeance and carve her place in your highlight reel. *Arriva la punizione!*

## Stadium Report

While the chilly wind whistles through the wintery streets, Stadium will be evolving again. New Heroes Doomfist and Wuyang leap into the fray, with a full Cassidy rework to sharpen your plays. A new Top 500 tier arrives to give you a goal worthy of your grind and the rank system will reset for a fresh powdery climb. As you ascend, the **League 6 Reaper Chase** Skin awaits, and a China Control map will allow all the new chaos to take shape.

Stadium continues to grow as a living mode that thrives on clever tactics and confident plays, and every season aims to give you more tools to make each match matter.

## Competitive Updates

Season 20 will raise the stakes in Competitive, too. Challenger Score fuels the race for Top 500, turning every match into forward momentum. Win streaks will help you soar, while queue bonuses keep your climb warm. And for the first time, top-ranked Heroes have integrated social visibility so that streamers and competitive hopefuls shine more brightly across the community.

Cheaters can’t hide from the cold, either. When they are banned, you’ll regain any rank progress stolen by unfair losses, making the ladder sturdier and more honest. And as Challenger continues to tie itself more closely to our Esports ecosystem, your victories might lead to opportunities in FACEIT tournaments, OWCS scouting, and more. If you’ve ever dreamed of going pro, 'tis the season to make your mark.

## Winter Wonderland

### Mischief & Magic Mode

Now onto the part of the season that jingles, sparkles, and occasionally bursts into confetti. Winter Wonderland is back from December 9 – January 7 with Mischief & Magic, the beloved prop hunt game mode, on a familiar winterized map. For the first two weeks you will hide as props or hunt down your disguised foes with as much holiday spirit as you can muster while firing a weapon indoors (\*please *don’t actually fire weapons inside*).

Halfway through the event, the magic shifts. Props wake up, stretch their little limbs, and decide they have had enough. Mischief & Magic Wonderland breathes life into your disguises, letting you fight back with explosive charm. Gingerbread bites. Snowmen detonate. The holiday spirit positively combusts. Through it all you can earn **Frozen Demon** **Junkrat** and **Frozen Mountaineer** **Cassidy**, track your progress in the Event Hub, and enjoy holiday chaos with friends who definitely did not see that candy cane coming.

### Winter Wonderland Event

This year also introduces a festive three-path reward system that lets you chase the cosmetics that speak to your style. Complete challenges across Winter Wonderland and other modes to earn Winter Cookies, then spend them on three tracks: Jingle Tracer, Krampus Junkrat, and Santa Claus Torbjorn. Each path has its own unique rewards, milestones, and personality to let you earn at your own pace and build that warm fuzzy sense of holiday joy while you play.

Collect your Cookies and bring some cozy winter fun to your fights December 9 through January 7.

## Mythics

### Divine Desperado Cassidy Hero Skin

If golden hour had a favorite Hero, it would be Cassidy. The **Divine Desperado** Hero skin will turn you into a radiant desert deity with blazing visual effects, immersive sound, and all the swagger of a cowboy who stared directly at the sun and won. You can unlock new hairstyles, the bold Purple Dusk and Ice Blue Dawn colorways, then illuminate the world around you with ambient sky glow in the final tier.

This Mythic is drenched in drama, Cassidy’s cool confidence, and a sacred celestial blaze. When you unholster this one, you won’t just feel powerful. You feel divine.

![WEB_S20_Mythic_Cassidy_ux.png](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/rc/RC44IU4REY0E1764205743844.png)

### Capsule Cannon Orisa Weapon Skin

Orisa’s latest upgrade is pure candy-coated joy. Efi has turned her weapon into the Mythic **Capsule Cannon**, sparkling with shine and adorable trinkets that flutter and pop like tiny surprises. Every elimination powers up your cannon with brighter effects, and every flourish animation feels like cracking open a new capsule prize. And once you reach the final level, the starry kill effects light up the battlefield like a neon skyline. It’s unbelievably cute, surprisingly deadly, and everything you deserve.

## Bring Balance Back to the Battle

The harmony of the Four Nations returns to the frontlines as the Overwatch 2 x Avatar: The Last Airbender collaboration makes a comeback! Immerse yourself in the elemental arts as iconic Overwatch Heroes channel the power of Water, Earth, Fire, and Air once more.

- **Mei as Katara** – Flow into fights as the Water Tribe’s wise and fluid defender. Complete challenges to unlock this skin and bend the battle in your favor.
- **Zenyatta as Aang** – Channel the Air Nomad’s legacy of peace and resilience as you glide into combat with the spiritual grace of the Avatar himself.
- **Orisa as Appa** – Fly into the fray with your steadfast protector, embodying loyalty and strength to carry your team to victory. Yip yip!
- **Venture as Toph** – Shake your enemies' foundations as the unyielding, earthbending master who stands strong against all odds.
- **Genji as Zuko** – Fuel your flames with the determined firebender whose heart burns with purpose and redemption.
- **Kiriko as Suki** – Strike with precision and grace, honoring the unbreakable spirit of a Kyoshi Warrior.

The returning crossover will bring back the celebrated cosmetics, sprays, and flair inspired by the spirit of Team Avatar, so don’t miss this chance to complete your collection! Gather your Gaang, master the elements, and bring balance to the battlefield again from December 16 through 29.

‘Tis the season... to give gifts right inside the game! Battle Passes, currency bundles, and premium packs can be gifted to friends with a few clicks. Available only through Battle.net, this marks the start of a gifting system that’s just beginning to unfold, with more ways to share and surprises ahead.
