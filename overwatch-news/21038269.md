> Where can you watch the best Overwatch esports this month? We've got you covered.

---

Overwatch esports ramps up this month with more live events and streams around the globe:

- [Overwatch Pacific Championship](twitch.tv/fullcircle)—broadcast from the Blizzard E-Stadium in Taipei—will complete its regular season and head into the playoffs to crown its Season 2 champions on October 6.
- [Overwatch Contenders](http://twitch.tv/overwatchcontenders) Season One also reaches its final checkpoint this month. Join us for the NA and EU playoffs October 7 & 8.
- [APAC Premier](https://twitch.tv/playoverwatch) kicks off October 19. Teams from Europe, the United States, South Korea and the Asia-Pacific will join four Chinese teams in this epic intercontinental showdown.
- [Overwatch APEX](https://twitch.tv/ognglobal) heats up as some of South Korea’s finest players continue to fight for dominance in their fourth season.

Never miss a moment! Here's our full schedule of official events for October 2017:
