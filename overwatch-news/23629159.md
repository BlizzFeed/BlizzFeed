# Overwatch at BlizzConline: A recap

- URL: https://overwatch.blizzard.com/en-gb/news/23629159/overwatch-at-blizzconline-a-recap
![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/tt/TT04L5SWOK0P1613608468021.png)

> In case you missed the show, we’ve got you covered. Check out panel recaps and segment links to catch up on all things Overwatch.

---

### Behind the Scenes of Overwatch 2

The Overwatch 2 team [dove into the game’s development process](https://youtu.be/ixf-k3miyDg) and shared some sneak peeks of upcoming content, including new maps, PvP role updates, hero missions, revamped hero looks, and story.

[![Video](https://markdown-videos-api.jorgenkh.no/youtube/ixf-k3miyDg?width=1280&height=720)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ixf-k3miyDg)

### The Voices of Overwatch

Voice actors Darin De Paul (Reinhardt), Fred Tatasciore (Soldier: 76), Charlet Chung (D.Va), Matthew Mercer (McCree), and Jeannie Bolet (Echo) [shared their experiences bringing the characters of Overwatch to life](https://youtu.be/kuBrIrnaD4), discussed doing voice recording from their home offices, and put on an in-character improv performance for viewers.

[![Video](https://markdown-videos-api.jorgenkh.no/youtube/kuBrIrnZaD4?width=1280&height=720)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kuBrIrnZaD4)

### Creating a Soundtrack

Blizzard music director Derek Duke [spoke with composers](https://youtu.be/6SIep1CGxqI) responsible for creating some of the most iconic pieces that bring the future-possible world of Overwatch to life.

[![Video](https://markdown-videos-api.jorgenkh.no/youtube/6SIep1CGxqI?width=1280&height=720)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6SIep1CGxqI)

### Overwatch League: Shock vs. The World

The two-time Overwatch League champion [San Francisco Shock took on a dream team](https://youtu.be/lRvsR22uwr8) of pro players, streamers, community figures, and other surprise guests.

[![Video](https://markdown-videos-api.jorgenkh.no/youtube/lRvsR22uwr8?width=1280&height=720)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lRvsR22uwr8)

### Community-Made Creations in the Workshop

Overwatch team engineers Dan Reed and Zach Metcalf [interviewed some of the game’s most ambitious community creators](https://youtu.be/H8JTG2DH_k4) about their workshop endeavors, including the creators of McCree’s Hot Potato, PMAJellies Aim Trainer, Conquest, and more.

[![Video](https://markdown-videos-api.jorgenkh.no/youtube/H8JTG2DH_k4?width=1280&height=720)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=H8JTG2DH_k4)

### Overwatch League: Crafts with Soe

Watchpoint host Soe Gschwind gave players a masterclass in pachimari perfection, teaching them step-by-step how to create their very own clay pachi-pal.

![BC2021_OW_Soe-Crafts_JP_Social_700x393.png](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/gallery/5w/5W6S1857CUN71613783745449.png)

### Blizzard Art Studio: Overwatch

Overwatch team artists Arnold Tsang, Morten Skaalvik, Qiu Fang, and Andrew Menjivar taught players step-by-step how to draw [**Tracer**](https://youtu.be/NSISUK9I7ps), [**Roadhog**](https://youtu.be/MPAbPNF1ihQ), [**Reaper**](https://youtu.be/VpGjCeOfe4s), and [**Zenyatta**](https://youtu.be/RZrUhCf9Q2w).

[![Video](https://markdown-videos-api.jorgenkh.no/youtube/NSISUK9I7ps?width=1280&height=720)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NSISUK9I7ps)
