> Celebrate Overwatch's 100 million player milestone with a personalized recap video.

---

You don’t need to use Recall to look back on all the good memories you’ve made since the launch of Overwatch in 2016. Instead, head over to your official Overwatch 2 Recap Video!

To celebrate surpassing 100 million players—and to give thanks to the heroes who made that possible—we’ve put together a personalized recap tailored to your gameplay. [Watch now](https://recap.overwatch.com/) to see your achievements honored, your teammates commended, and your contributions recognized!

Access your Recap Video by visiting [recap.overwatch.com](https://recap.overwatch.com)\*. There, you can view your awe-inspiring highlights and share your triumphs with the community. After all, with so many points captured, backlines dove, and endorsements received, you deserve to capitalize on some hard-earned bragging rights.

When the world needed heroes most, you answered the call. Indiscriminate of role, rank, or main, you’re as valuable an addition to Overwatch as any in-universe agent. Resurrect your fondest memories of queuing and join the community in celebration today!

Cheers to the game we’ve built together. See you in your next match—GLHF!

*\*Currently, the Overwatch 2 Personalized Recap Video is exclusively available to players in the United States who are opted into receiving Marketing Communications and have actively played Overwatch 2. Opting in to receive Blizzard marketing communications will not make this video immediately available and will only make you eligible to receive future campaigns.*
