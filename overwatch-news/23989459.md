# Overwatch 2 - What to Expect at BlizzCon® 2023

- URL: https://news.blizzard.com/en-gb/article/23989459/overwatch-2-what-to-expect-at-blizzcon-2023
![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/3n/3NIL60FM1TEF1698280480474.png)

> BlizzCon® is just around the corner, and there is a packed schedule of events for every Overwatch 2 fan including panels, live demos, the Overwatch® World Cup, and more!

---

Millions of you have answered the call to play *Overwatch* 2 since we launched a year ago. New heroes—Sojourn, Junkerqueen, Kiriko, Ramattra, Lifeweaver, and Illari—have joined the roster. You explored new parts of the world, including Esperança, Shambali Monastery, and Survassa. Teams worked together to stop Null Sector attacks on Rio, Toronto, and Gothenburg. However, we can’t wait to show you what’s coming next to *Overwatch* 2 at *BlizzCon* 2023.

Whether you’re attending in-person in Anaheim, CA, or watching from the comfort of your home, there’s going to be a lot of *Overwatch* 2 news, reveals, and competition to check out!

## BlizzCon 2023 – Opening Ceremonies

*November 3 – 11:00 AM PT – BlizzCon Arena*

Alongside exciting announcements from World of Warcraft®, Diablo® IV, Hearthstone®, and Warcraft: Rumble, *Overwatch* 2 has a few surprises in store, including introducing the next Hero.

## Overwatch World Cup Finals

*November 3-4 - All Weekend Long – World Cup Hall*

The Overwatch World Cup reaches its epic conclusion! The top 8 teams from around the world will compete in a single-elimination bracket to bring the trophy home. Be sure to read our [World Cup blog](https://overwatch.blizzard.com/en-us/news/24017502/) to learn more about how you can watch from home and earn cool rewards throughout the weekend.

## Developer Meet & Greet

*November 3-4 - All Weekend Long – Overwatch Hall*

If you are attending BlizzCon in-person, you’ll be able to meet many of the developers who work behind the scenes on your favorite Overwatch 2 features, Heroes, maps, and more. Posters will be available, but you can also bring your favorite collectible or autograph book for them to sign!

## New Hero Deep Dive

*November 3 – 2:30 PM PT – BlizzCon Arena*

Join Overwatch 2 developers as they explore the art, backstory, and abilities of our newest hero, including the premiere of their origin story cinematic.

## Overwatch 2: What’s Next

*November 4 – 1:30 PM PDT – BlizzCon Arena*

Tune in on Saturday as we look toward what you can expect in Season 8 and beyond. Get a first look at future changes for Competitive Play and even bigger surprises that we can’t wait to reveal.

## Community Night

*November 4 – 4:45 PM PDT – BlizzCon Arena*

Community Night is back as we celebrate our fans across all Blizzard games through performances, art, and cosplay.

## Live performance by LE SSERAFIM®

*November 4 – 6:15 PM PDT – BlizzCon Arena*

To wrap up an epic weekend, hit K-Pop sensation LE SSERAFIM® will be performing their new single, *Perfect Night*, along with other fan-favorite hits.

We can’t wait to see you at BlizzCon, and if you are still looking to go, [you can find tickets available here](https://www.axs.com/blizzcon2023#tickets). You can also watch all of the festivities for free via live stream. Either way, get ready for a rockin’ good time!
