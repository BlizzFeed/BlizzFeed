Season 19 lit the candles, cast the shadows, steeped in mystery, style, and spooky spectacles. But we’re not fading into the dark just yet! The midcycle masquerade is here, and it’s primed to keep the party going! This round brings a fresh limited-time mode, new skin drops that drip, and more.

## Spirit Showdown

Your mains can’t save you here. Spirit Showdown is a fast-paced fight where every elimination swaps your current Hero instantly. You’ll also change Heroes if you’re eliminated twice without getting a kill of your own. So, you’ll need to think fast, adapt faster, and flex your skills across the entire Hero lineup to survive this skirmish. Each battle pushes you to mastery, forcing teams to find balance as the Hero pool continuously shifts. It’s giving pure Overwatch energy: skill, adaptability, and a whole lot of hilarious panic. Every kill changes the tide, every respawn rewrites your story, and no two rounds will play out alike. Slay, swap and master the roster in Spirit Showdown, available now through November 24!

## Mythic Cyber Fuel Junkrat

The demolition man gets wired for mayhem as he goes digital and drifts into the void with his chaotic new Mythic form!

Dialed into the netstream of the future, **Mythic Cyber Fuel Junkrat** turns hacking into havoc and explosive eliminations into an artform. This chaotic icon is pulsing with void energy and punk defiance as he plants bugs in the system—literally—blowing up BigTech’s best-laid plans. This new Mythic is part punk demolitionist, part digital void ghost, and all mayhem. Junkrat’s new look makes every explosion a statement, and every spark a warning.

From the moment you unlock Mythic Cyber Fuel Junkrat, you’ll wield his full Mythic form, complete with explosive VFX and SFX that hum with glitchfire and anarchy. Then you’ll amplify and refine your style as you unlock new colorways, tires, and an eerie ambient glow worthy of the void. Junkrat’s always been loud, unpredictable, and over the top… but now, he’s transcended to become Mythical. Rage through the battlefield as the radical cyber rebel you were coded to be!

## Junkrat’s Loot Hunt

The demolition doesn’t stop at the Mythic—Junkrat’s Loot Hunt is back for another round of pure, chaotic fun, letting you snag up to 9 Loot Boxes just by playing. This time, every 5 matches in any mode earns a new Loot Box, and wins count double, so it pays to play loud, proud, and just a little unhinged.

Progress carries through the whole weekend, so you can play your way, stack your boxes, and let the explosions rain down. Log in, light the fuse, and unbox your rewards from November 20-23!

## Story Feature

Step into the stories that built the Heroes, the villains, and everything in between!

The world of Overwatch is ever expanding, and now you can explore it easier than ever. The new Story feature brings the universe to life inside your game, creating a dedicated space for stories and lore to unfold season by season. Covering the history of Overwatch’s fallen and reborn Heroes, this is your central hub to catch up and dive deep into the legends that bind them all together.

Accessible from the Main Menu, the Story feature shows cinematic content, motion comics, and lore-driven videos to be your window into the world, with new stories and cross-links to be added every season. The fight for the future has always been about more than the battlefield, and now you can see exactly how it all connects. For die-hard lore junkies and curious newcomers alike, these stories are where Overwatch’s universe truly comes alive!

## Overwatch Champions Series World Finals

The Overwatch Champions Series is heading toward an epic season finale as the world’s best face off for glory on the DreamHack stage in Stockholm, with fierce competition and new ways to rep your teams. OWCS isn’t just a tournament; it’s a celebration of the best moments our players create, the roar of the crowd, and the energy that keeps this community (and the competition) electric.

The OWCS World Finals bring the top teams together for their final clash of skill and strategy. Watch live to earn Loot Boxes, skins, and other exclusive drops. Then support the OWCS World Finals further when you deck out your Heroes in the new crowd-funded Scarlet Flare bundle, featuring cosmetics for Freja, Mercy and Juno! Whether you’re cheering from your couch or grinding ranked in solidarity, this is your chance to celebrate your favorite players and witness the future of Overwatch competition.

Suit up, watch your favorite matches, and look the part while you play your own. Plus, tune in to get the first look at what’s coming in 2026!

The best players on Earth, one arena, all glory on the line, starting November 26 with the live event finals November 28-30.

## Drives

From December 4 to 9, buckle up for a burst of high-stakes play as Drives return to rev up the momentum before the season wraps. These short, mission-based progression tracks give you the perfect excuse to hop back in and chase rewards that won’t be around for long. You’ll rack up XP, snag exclusive cosmetics, and relive that sweet sense of Overwatch chaos where every win feels personal. Queue into Competitive or Stadium, stack those W’s, and cross your checkpoints to claim the Season 19 Signature before the clock runs out!
