Grab your bricks and build yourself up by completing [Bastion’s Brick Challenge](https://blizz.ly/BrickBastion)! Play games and watch streams to earn new content inspired by LEGO® – including the new legendary Brick Bastion skin.

[![Video](https://markdown-videos-api.jorgenkh.no/youtube/X2B5WESlCIU?width=1280&height=720)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=X2B5WESlCIU)

From **now** until **September 30**, players will be able to earn five sprays, six player icons, and the **legendary Brick Bastion skin,** for a total of twelve new rewards.

- [BUILD 'EM UP](#header1) - Learn how to unlock brand-new Bastion rewards
- [WATCH TO EARN](#header2) - Earn special Bastion-themed cosmetics by watching Twitch streams

## BUILD 'EM UP

We’re bringing the magic of LEGO® into *Overwatch* with some LEGO®-inspired content available to unlock in-game. Wins in Quick Play, Competitive Play, or Arcade will all earn you limited-time player icons, sprays, and a new legendary skin: BRICK BASTION!

**These special Bastion’s Brick Challenge rewards are a bonus on top of your normal earnings for playing in the Arcade.**

![OW_Bastion-MicroEvent_InGameRewards_Embedded_JP.png](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/gallery/gc/GCBVUBCCT04D1568679247131.png)

## WATCH TO EARN

Want more? You can watch Twitch streamers before, during or after matches to unlock even more Bastion cosmetics – we’re bringing you more Twitch Drops every time you tune in to participating broadcasters through **October 1 at 09:00 CEST** to earn six Bastion's Brick Challenge sprays!

**IMPORTANT:** To be eligible to receive these [Twitch Drops](https://help.twitch.tv/customer/en/portal/articles/2754380-how-to-earn-drops) you must first link your Blizzard account to your Twitch account. Visit the [Twitch Connections](https://www.twitch.tv/settings/connections) page to log in and complete the link now.

![OW_Bastion-MicroEvent_TwitchDropsRewards_Embedded_JP.png](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/gallery/rl/RL8XUKTYVYIL1568679269268.png)
