# Overwatch Digital Comic: "Destroyer"

---

The sixth in our series of digital comic shorts is here: **Destroyer**! In this installment, gear up for a larger-than-life tale featuring Overwatch's ingenious engineer and weapons specialist, Torbjörn Lindholm.

In "Destroyer," a giant mech is terrorizing a small country, leveling skyscrapers and taking innocent lives—and only one man possesses the know-how and skill to go toe-to-toe with the colossal robotic monster.

[![OW_TORBJORN_Comic_EN-p05.jpg](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_folder_media/zx/ZXMJVC5INGEJ1464287249142.jpg)](http://comic.playoverwatch.com/en-gb/torbjorn-destroyer)

Written by Blizzard’s Micky Neilson and illustrated by Gray Shuko (*Fruity Frags*), "Destroyer" is now available to read at [comic.playoverwatch.com](http://comic.playoverwatch.com/en-gb/torbjorn-destroyer). We hope you enjoy it!
