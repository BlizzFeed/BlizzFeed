*"You want to survive, you follow my rules. Got it?"*

The latest installment in our series of digital comic shorts, Binary, takes place in rural Sweden shortly after the events of our holiday comic, [Reflections](http://comic.playoverwatch.com/en-gb/tracer-reflections), where a terror from the past has recently reemerged.

In "Binary," locals have spotted a Bastion unit roaming the woods, and they believe it's only a matter of time before the dangerous omnic tries to destroy them. As they assemble to discuss what to do, a mysterious stranger arrives… someone who has experience fighting Bastions and is eager to hunt them down.

[![OW_011_BASTORB_comic_EN-p001.jpg](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_folder_media/i6/I6GD2XJ0L6S21488845324062.jpg)](http://comic.playoverwatch.com)

Written by Blizzard's own Matt Burns and James Waugh and illustrated by Joe Ng and Espen Grundetjern, "Binary" is now available for download at [comic.playoverwatch.com](http://comic.playoverwatch.com/en-gb/bastion-binary/). You can also experience the action and sound of this story through the [Madefire motion book](http://reader.madefire.com/work/w-5f5bc7acb982495e89712680b43e1f38/read) on desktop, mobile, and Apple TV.

We hope you enjoy it—and stay tuned for more Overwatch comics coming soon!
