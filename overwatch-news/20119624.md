# Overwatch Digital Comic: "Mission Statement"

- URL: https://overwatch.blizzard.com/en-gb/news/20119624/overwatch-digital-comic-mission-statement
![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/wm/WM0STNX3PA501463720469592.jpg)

> Pharah leads her squad to neutralize a dangerous artificial intelligence intent on wreaking havoc in Egypt.

---

*"I will protect the innocent."*

Pharah soars into action in **Mission Statement**—the fifth entry in our series of free digital comic shorts exploring the heroes of Overwatch. In this installment, we follow Helix security chief Fareeha Amari into a high-stakes battle for her home country.

In "Mission Statement," when omnic sentries are taken over by an AI intent on wreaking havoc in Egypt, Pharah must lead her squad of Helix agents into the fray. In the process, she learns what matters most on the battlefield.

[![005-OW_PHARAH_Comic_EN-5.jpg](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_folder_media/vl/VLJ4DH3FB6A11463720630835.jpg)](http://comic.playoverwatch.com/en-gb/pharah-mission-statement)

Written by Blizzard's Andrew Robinson and illustrated by Nesskain (*R.U.S.T.*), "Pharah: Mission Statement" is now available at [comic.playoverwatch.com](http://comic.playoverwatch.com/en-gb/pharah-mission-statement). We hope you enjoy it, and stay tuned for more Overwatch digital comics coming soon!
