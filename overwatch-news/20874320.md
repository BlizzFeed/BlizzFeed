*"War between the omnics and humans seems all but inevitable."*

The latest installment in our series of digital comic shorts has arrived: [**Masquerade**](http://comic.playoverwatch.com/doomfist-masquerade)!

Years after suffering defeat at the hands of Overwatch, Akande Ogundimu—better known to the world as Doomfist—has escaped imprisonment and reclaimed his place within Talon's inner council. After getting up to speed on recent events, he decides to take matters into his own hands and charts a deadly new course for the shadowy organization...

[![DoomfistComic_Pg01.jpg](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_folder_media/0g/0G2QH7AIMTO91499991826922.jpg)](http://comic.playoverwatch.com/doomfist-masquerade)

Written by Blizzard's own Michael Chu and illustrated by Ryan Benjamin and Anthony Washington, "Maquerade" is now available for download at [comic.playoverwatch.com](https://comic.playoverwatch.com/doomfist-masquerade). You can also experience the action and sound of this story through the [Madefire motion book](https://reader.madefire.com/work/w-a2da1239584645338362109be0e7f1fd/read/) on desktop, mobile, and Apple TV.

We hope you enjoy it—and stay tuned for more Overwatch comics coming soon!
