## FORGE YOUR VICTORY

An all-new way to play Overwatch 2, Stadium is about to open once and for all, as the third pillar alongside Quick Play and Competitive. That means it’s time to strategize, customize and rise, permanently!

Stadium is a best-of-seven, round-based brawl where you’ll earn Stadium Cash each round, which you can spend to transform your heroes in the Armory.  With 17 heroes available at launch plus regular updates bringing more heroes, items, abilities, maps, and balances, no two matches will ever be alike. Oh, and did we mention you can switch between first-person and third-person perspective?  Every match is a chance to experiment, evolve, and rule Stadium’s new standalone ranking system.

## NEW DPS HERO: FREJA

There's a new DPS hero officially arriving! Freja will be here to stay and fair warning once more—she never misses her mark. Armed with her Revdraw Crossbow and an arsenal of high-mobility abilities, Freja rewards precision, positioning, and the pursuit of targets.

Her crossbow fires with both rapid precision and explosive power, letting you control any engagement. Need to reposition? Her Quick Dash and Updraft abilities let you escape danger, chase down enemies, or set up the perfect shot. And when it’s time to really lock down a fight, her devastating Bola Shot Ultimate will tangle enemies together to create massive playmaking opportunities.

Prepare to master those movements and show your enemies that a true hunter is never caught off guard. Freja goes live at launch!

## HERO BANS ARE HERE!

The winds of change continue to sweep through Overwatch 2. For the first time, you'll have the power to directly influence the fight before the first shot is fired… arriving by popular demand, Hero Bans are here for Competitive Play!

This new feature will launch with Season 16 and bring collaborative strategy to the frontline. Before each match, both teams will vote to remove up to four heroes from play (two per team, no more than two per role) to tweak team compositions and add an extra layer of mind games before the real fighting begins. It’s a system built for strategic thinkers and team players, which rewards coordination and understanding of hero synergies.

Hero Bans don’t just level the playing field, they shape it. Are you tired of seeing the same heroes dominate every match? Want to throw a wrench in the enemy’s go-to strat? Coordinate with your team and swing the vote to remove a high impact pick, block that one counter that’s keeping your best in check, lock out a problematic flanker, or protect your support duo. Whoever you choose, Hero Bans give your squad more power to play your way.

The Hero Ban system is designed to breathe new life into every game so no match will play the exact same as the pool of heroes will shift based on your votes. We’ve crafted Hero Bans to keep things fresh, fair, and fun for everyone. So, rally your team, plan your bans, and get ready to shake things up. Hero Bans are going live tomorrow and it’s time to drop the hammer.

For a deeper dive into Hero Bans,check out our [Director’s Take: Introducing Hero Bans for Competitive Play](https://overwatch.blizzard.com/en-us/news/24197272) blog.

## MYTHIC HEART OF HOPE JUNO

Enter the world of Dokiwatch, where chosen heroes rise as the Heroes of Hearts. Each one is bonded to animal companions and gifted with Magitech, a fusion of magic and technology. Together, the Heroes of Heart fight valiantly to protect the world from Woe — and the evil Hollow Hearts who spread its corrupting influence.

Juno is joining the fight as the newest Hero of Heart! Her Mythic Heart of Hope skin features four stages of customization that evolve alongside her radiance. From a bright-eyed protector to an unstoppable warrior, and even a corrupted version lost to Woe in between, her progression tells a story of resilience and hope. As Mythic Juno grows with you in battle, you’ll unlock three distinct forms complete with her Magitech familiars—the Sharks of Dreams, Deimos and Phobos.

- **Level 1, Base:** Spread hope with a signature pink colorway, sparkly ability VFX, and a cute bun hairstyle.
- **Level 2, Doki Princess:** Don a regal outfit, glowing yellow colorway, and a chic bob hairstyle that’s fit for a magical Dokiwatch Princess.
- **Level 3, Corrupted Evil:** Woe’s influence takes hold with a sharp twin tails hairstyle and red/black colorway that oozes heartless energy.
- **Level 4, Radiant Protector:** Mythic Juno's final form radiates pure Heart with ambient VFX for the ultimate magical girl glow-up.

And if you want to shine even brighter, an Iridescent Aspect adds a prismatic glow to her radiant energy, coming at midseason. Mythic Heart of Hope Juno’s transformation is more than cosmetic; it’s a magical journey that’s yours to shape.
