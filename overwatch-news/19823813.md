# Play Overwatch at the 2015 European Road to BlizzCon

- URL: https://overwatch.blizzard.com/en-gb/news/19823813/play-overwatch-at-the-2015-european-road-to-blizzcon
![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/2g/2GAM4WY5B1GW1437660762574.png)

> Get some hands-on time with Blizzard’s newest game at the final stop on the European Road to BlizzCon in Prague, Czech Republic on October 3–4!

---

Looking for a chance to try out *Overwatch*? Join us for some epic Blizzard eSports at the 2015 European Road to BlizzCon on October 3–4 and get the chance to fight for the future between matches!

Overwatch will be available to play at the final stop of the 2015 European Road to BlizzCon, taking place at the O2 Arena in Prague, Czech Republic. We’ll have 120 gaming stations set up on both days of the event, where attendees will be able to team up and accomplish the incredible with their favorite heroes on two different maps—Volskaya Industries and Numbani.

![O2 Arena in Prague](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/9f/9FLFGM8PMSOD1440168597143.jpg)

This is only the second time that Overwatch has been publically playable in Europe, so you won’t want to miss it! To learn more about the game, be sure to check out our official hero pages on [playoverwatch.com](http://eu.battle.net/overwatch/en/) as well as our [Ability Overview](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jo4QF2lpEHU&list=PLqviSFwR--yqfth2e5VbUSfRvcTT5Tzrq) and [Gameplay Preview videos on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=URzB2SHhlEg&list=PLqviSFwR--yr7UoIGUIn_VzmYsDHlos4f).

For more info on the 2015 European Road to BlizzCon, including details on the World of Warcraft, Hearthstone, and Heroes of the Storm World Championship tournament series, be sure to visit our [dedicated website](http://www.eurtb2015.com/?utm_source=battle.net&utm_medium=blog&utm_content=en-blog&utm_campaign=marketing-eu-eurtb2015). We hope to see you in Prague!
