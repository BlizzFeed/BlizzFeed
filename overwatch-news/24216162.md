## Pick Your Plastic

Loadouts don’t get much louder or more colorful than this! The Overwatch x NERF collab is packed with blaster-inspired weapon skins styled after some of NERF’s most iconic gear. Cassidy headlines the collab with a Legendary Hero skin that comes with custom visuals, unique sound effects, the NERF **Maverick** blaster *and* a **Vortex Football** to boot.\
![OVR_PK_S17_BlasterCollab_WeaponSkins_Cassidy_001.png](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/b2/B29Q8ECVJ24Y1753897154304.png)\
He’s joined by Tracer’s earnable **Gelfire Pros**, Genji dual wielding a **Slingerang** plus **Power Blast Bat**, and Mei bringing the pressure (literally) with the classic **Super Soaker XP100**. Additionally, Hanzo’s got a **Big Bad Bow**, Baptiste runs the Galactic **Commander**, the newest NERF blaster from the Loadout line, and Soldier: 76’s precision gets the plastic treatment with **N Series Pinpoint**.

![OVR_PK_S17_BlasterCollab_WeaponSkins_Baptiste_001.png](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/0t/0TXTWRNCZRQW1753897213607.png)

Each NERF weapon skin can be equipped on top of your existing Hero skins—so no matter how you style your fit, your foam is fixin’ to be fully loaded.

## NERF or Nothin’ Mode

This isn’t your typical showdown. It’s as chaotic and high-energy as a living room grudge match between friends and family with every blaster, bat, or bow you could find in the garage.

NERF or Nothin’ is a fast-paced free-for-all where eight players drop in, and the first to hit 20 eliminations claims the win. You’ll start with a random Hero (minus Mercy and Lifeweaver, who are sitting this one out), and every elimination earns you a new Hero. Some of them will come with a new NERF blaster skin to master in combat. Can’t snag a kill? You’ll still rotate after three deaths. No repeats either: once you’ve played a Hero, you won’t get them again.

Cooldowns? Basically gone. Everyone’s working with 80% cooldown reduction, so the action is constant. Your Ultimate charge sticks around after death, and if you swap Heroes, you’ll start with 15% Ult charge, ready to go.

![OVR_PK_S17_BlasterCollab_WeaponSkins_Mei_001.png](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/2n/2NOVAWLJXDQB1753897250692.png)

The maps? It’s a greatest hits list including Chateau, Petra, Kanezaka, Malevento, King’s Row, Hollywood, Dorado, Eichenwalde, Blizzard World, and even Paris.

Think of it as a weapon mastery trial with a lot more plastic, a lot less time to think, and seriously fun NERF  foamfighting in every direction. Adapt or get blasted.

## Conquer to Score the Gear

In this collab, the foamfights equal real rewards. Conquer event challenges to unlock a themed spray, player icon, foam dart weapon charm, and Tracer’s Gelfire Pro weapon skin, plus up to 40k Battle Pass XP. NERF or Nothin’ is the only place to earn event progress and rewards, so if you want the goods, blast through those challenges.
