The countdown to the OWCS Midseason Championship in Riyadh is on, and the battle for glory is heating up. As the top Overwatch Teams prepare to throw down, you’ll have a new way to show your team pride and support via the OWCS Partner Team Skins and the brand new Legendary Radiant Angel Mercy Skin. These OWCS Partner Team bundles are coming to the game on July 22!

## Your Purchase, Their Glory.

Show your passion in-game with exclusive Partner Team Skins and Legendary Skin Bundles—available now!

This year, Partner Teams directly receive YOUR support through a portion of the revenue earned by the sale of these items. These teams have provided us with so many incredible memories, and this is a great opportunity to show them some love and directly support them as they prepare for the next unforgettable moment.. These items will only be available for a limited time, so don’t miss your chance to rep your team.

## Partner Team Packs

Each OWCS Partner Team has their own bundle available now for purchase. Whether you're all about supporting your home region, backing your favorite OWCS team, or simply picking the skin that matches your vibes—there's something for everyone in this collection.

All nine OWCS Partner Teambundles will include:

- Venture Team Skin
- Ramattra Team Skin
- Ana Team Skin
- Team Weapon Charm
- Team Spray
- Team Icon
- Team Name Card

## Legendary Radiant Angel Mercy

![Mercy%20MTX%20TW%20v1.png](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/6FEEKGR8574J1752880194131.png)

The Legendary Radiant Angel Mercy Skin joins the lineup as part of the OWCS offerings, available for a limited time starting July 22. Whether your duo is a Pharah main or you’re just a dedicated OWCS fan, this is your chance to help Partner Teams shine!

Let’s find out more about this design from artist Shu Lee:

“The Radiant Angel Mercy skin was developed by building upon the ideation and story originally crafted by TB Choi. Radiant Mercy is a divine being from a parallel world, who has come to protect the human realm. To reflect this, I focused on conveying a sense of holiness, warmth, and powerful energy. Her glowing halo and eye covering emphasize this divine presence. I also designed patterns and motifs that evoke the feeling of an advanced parallel-world civilization.

The blue gemstones embedded throughout her weapon serve as conduits, channeling energy and healing power to her.”
