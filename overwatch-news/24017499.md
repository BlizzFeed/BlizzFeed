> We’re bringing friendly competition this Holiday season with the Overwatch 2: FlashOps Holiday Showdown.

---

The holidays are on their way, and we can’t wait to ring in the festivities with the FlashOps Holiday Showdown tournament! Registration for North America (NA), Europe, Middle East, and Africa (EMEA), and Asia Pacific (APAC) regions is now live. With participation rewards and BIG regional prize pools, you don’t want to miss it!

## NA AND EMEA EVENT OVERVIEW

Eligible Players from the NA and EMEA regions will compete for their spot in regional FlashOps Holiday Showdown Finals. The top 8 qualifying teams from each region will advance, where they will have a shot at a $50,000 USD prize pool.

Think you have what it takes? Sign up today and prove it!

- [North America (NA)](http://start.gg/FlashOpsNA)
- [Europe, Middle East, and Africa (EMEA)](http://start.gg/FlashOpsEMEA)

*Registration closes for both regions Thursday, November 9 at 11:59 PM PST*

### NA AND EMEA Competition Format

The NA and EMEA FlashOps Holiday Showdown is split into 2 stages

- Stage 1: Regional Qualifiers
- Stage 2: Flash Ops: Holiday Showdown Regional Finals

### NA and EMEA Regional Qualifiers

Teams in NA and EMEA can register for their respective Region’s Qualifier event. After registration closes, eligible teams will compete in a Swiss Play tournament format over 10 rounds with a first-to-three map win series. The top 8 teams from each region’s Swiss Play will advance to that region’s Finals.

## APAC EVENT OVERVIEW

Eligible Players from Japan (JP, South Korea (KR), and Southeast Asia (SEA) will compete in regional qualifier events for a spot in the APAC Flash Ops Holiday Showdown. 6 total teams will qualify for a live LAN Studio Finals event at WDG Esports Studio Seoul, where they will compete for a $50,000 USD prize pool.

**Sign up now and earn your spot!**

- [Korea (KR)](https://www.wara.gg/bbs/board.php?bo_table=official&wr_id=224)
- [Japan (JP)](https://www.wara.gg/bbs/board.php?bo_table=official&wr_id=225)
- [Southeast Asia (SEA)](https://www.wara.gg/bbs/board.php?bo_table=official&wr_id=226)

*Registration closes for APAC Thursday, November 9th at 11:59PM KST*

### APAC Competition Format

The APAC Flash Ops Holiday Showdown is split into 3 stages

- Stage 1: WDG Open Regional Qualifiers.
- Stage 2: WDG Regional Knockouts.
- Stage 3: FlashOps: Holiday Showdown APAC Finals live from WDG Studio in Seoul.

![OW2_FlashOps_East_StageCalendar_ML02.png](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/it/ITXASKMZBY471697589994727.png)

### APAC Regional Qualifiers

Teams in KR, JP and SEA will register for their respective region’s qualifier event. After registration ends, eligible teams will be split into groups of 8 where they will play round-robin matches. The top 4 teams from each group after the round-robin matches will be placed into 1 of 8 single elimination tournament brackets where they compete for their spot in stage 2. The top team from each single elimination bracket will advance to their region’s stage 2. The Qualifiers format is subject to change based on the number of participating teams

### APAC Finals

A total of 6 teams including the top 3 teams from KR, the top 2 teams from JP and the top team from SEA will be invited to Seoul, for the WDG Esports Studio Finals. These 6 teams will be split into 2 groups where they will play round-robin format with first-to-three map win series. The top 2 teams from each group will advance to a Single Elimination playoffs bracket which will determine the winner of the APAC tournament. Semifinals of the playoffs will be first-to-three while the Grand Finals match will be a first-to-four map win series.
