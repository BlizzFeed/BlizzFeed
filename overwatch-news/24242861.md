### Intro

Overwatch Champions Series is closing out an epic 2025 Season with an expanded five days of competition from **Wednesday, November 26 to Sunday, November 30**. We’re excited to return to DreamHack Stockholm to showcase the biggest OWCS competition of the year!

The OWCS 2025 World Finals brings the 12 best teams from our 4 regions - Asia, China, EMEA, and NA - to battle for glory and their share of the crowdfunded prize pool.

Read on to learn everything you need to know to enjoy World Finals in full - including where to watch, the drops to earn, and the incredible new in-game bundles! World Finals weekend will be full of fun surprises, announcements, performances, plus some Season 20 details you won’t want to miss.

### Tournament Overview

![25 WF Bracket 16x9 [Fewer Timeslots].png](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/gallery/84/84K2AYKH5R761762797328345.png)

The OWCS 2025 World Finals will host five days of competition in a 12-team double-elimination bracket. The first two days of competition will cover Upper Bracket Round 1 and 2 broadcasted live from our Stockholm studio. Our first on-stage games will begin Friday, November 28, where fans have the opportunity to watch each team play on the main stage in front of our amazing crowd at DreamHack Stockholm.

![S3 Map Pool 16x9 v2.png](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/gallery/kp/KPB1IK6WDT6J1762797370063.png)

If you want to see your favorite teams play in-person, don’t forget that you can grab your tickets to DreamHack Stockholm - including prime seats, merch, and VIP add-ons - [right](http://dreamhack.com/stockholm/overwatch) [here](http://dreamhack.com/stockholm/overwatch)!

Games across Wednesday, November 26, Thursday, November 27, and Friday, November 28 start at **12pm CET.** Games on the 29th and 30th start at **11am CET.** You can tune in to our usual [Twitch](http://twitch.tv/ow_esports) and [YouTube](http://youtube.com/c/ow_esports) feeds to catch the action across all five days of competition!

Doors to the DreamHack festival area will open at 10am CET on Friday, Saturday, and Sunday. Doors will close at midnight CET on Friday and Saturday, and 7pm CET on Sunday. You can check the [DreamHack Stockholm FAQ](https://dreamhack.com/stockholm/faq/) and [full event rules](https://dreamhack.com/stockholm/rules/) for more information, including details on the BYOC schedule, accommodations for guests with disabilities, the venue address, permitted and prohibited items, and more.

That’s not all, though - to celebrate an amazing year of Overwatch, fans will have a chance to win tickets for BlizzCon 2026 to watch Overwatch World Cup live! Follow Overwatch Esports socials to find out more information.

### Attendee Rewards and How to Grab a Ticket

A variety of [OWCS Add-On packages](https://dreamhack.com/stockholm/overwatch/) are available alongside your DreamHack ticket. Each includes ALL of the Viewership Rewards listed above, prime seating in the forward sections of the viewing hall, and the following in-game Attendee Rewards:

- Flight to Finals - Stockholm Icon
- OWCS Boarding Pass Spray
- OWCS Flight Info Spray
- And all 5 days worth of OWCS World Finals Viewership Rewards

OWCS Add-Ons are the only way to guarantee yourself a seat to watch World Finals - any attendees who don’t purchase an Add-On may still be able to sit and watch the action, but unreserved seats are only available on a first come, first serve basis. Higher-tier Add-On packages also include additional goodies, including merch, prime seating, and more!

You can grab your DreamHack Stockholm tickets and OWCS Add-Ons [here](http://bit.ly/owcswf) while supplies last. Don’t forget both viewers and ticketholders will all get a chance to enter a giveaway for BlizzCon tickets to see Overwatch World Cup live!

### Special Events

Whether you’re watching at home or with us in Stockholmsmässan, DreamHack Stockholm will be packed with special events and updates that every kind of Overwatch fan will enjoy.

In the OWCS Hall, we’ll be hosting meet and greets with players, team staff, and developers all weekend long, including an exclusive meet-up and behind-the-scenes tour for [VIP Add-On](http://bit.ly/OWCSWF) holders.

On November 29, we’ll be sharing future plans for Overwatch Esports in 2026. On the 30th, we’ll sit down with Overwatch 2 Game Director Aaron Keller and Senior Narrative Designer Jude Stacey in a live, on-stage Developer Update!

Once we’ve chatted with the devs, we’ll have a Sunday Showmatch just before Grand Finals to showcase Season 20 updates - in the hands of your favorite OWCS players and creators!
