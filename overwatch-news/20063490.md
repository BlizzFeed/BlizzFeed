# Overwatch Animated Short Incoming: "Recall"

- URL: https://overwatch.blizzard.com/en-gb/news/20063490/overwatch-animated-short-incoming-recall
![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/ff/FFLXQAIBU5961458187874768.jpg)

> Discover the story of Winston in our first-ever Overwatch animated short—premiering this Monday, March 21 at 20:00 CET on Xbox.com/Overwatch!

---

*Imagination is the essence of discovery. . . .*

Next Monday, delve deeper into the world of Overwatch with the premiere of our first animated short: **Recall**.

"Recall" tells the story of Winston—a genetically engineered gorilla and brilliant scientist who longs for the days of heroism to return. In this episode, we explore Winston's thoughts and memories as he wrestles with the decision to recall the agents of Overwatch, all while the forces of Talon stage an attack on his laboratory in the abandoned Watchpoint: Gibraltar.

![WinstonShort-v03_OW_Embedded_JP_700x350.jpg](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/gallery/EB01EKT3S7EZ1458233514774.jpg)

Mark your calendars: "Recall" premieres exclusively on [Xbox.com/Overwatch](http://www.xbox.com/en-GB/games/overwatch#gallery) **March 21 at 20:00 CET**.
