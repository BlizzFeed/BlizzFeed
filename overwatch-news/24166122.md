## OVERWATCH CLASSIC: MOTH META

From February 4 to 17, Overwatch Classic: Moth Meta brings you back to one of the most iconic and chaotic chapters in Overwatch history. Mercy’s legendary “Moth” gameplay takes the spotlight as her lightning-fast Resurrect redefines team fights, while heroes like DPS Doomfist and Symmetra with her Shield Generator make a triumphant return to the battlefield. Step onto beloved maps Oasis, Eichenwalde, and Junkertown, where every skirmish is packed with nostalgic strategies and unpredictable mayhem.

The Moth Meta isn’t just about the meta—it’s about the memories. Complete special challenges to earn exclusive rewards while reliving the glory days of Overwatch. Whether you’re soaring through the skies as Mercy or coordinating team fights on classic battlegrounds, this is your chance to revisit the meta that defined an era. Let the chaos take flight!

## 6V6 MIN 1, MAX 3

Step back into Overwatch’s origins even more with a fresh twist on the beloved six-player team format. The 6v6 Min 1, Max 3 Mode is a unique experiment that blends nostalgia with modern gameplay dynamics, offering a structured composition alongside the flexibility to switch roles mid-match.

This mode provides a unique balance between strategy and adaptability. Whether you’re rekindling the joy of the original six-player team format or exploring how updated Overwatch heroes thrive in a classic setup, 6v6 Min 1, Max 3 delivers an engaging, dynamic experience. And your participation in this experiment will help shape the future of Overwatch 2’s gameplay—so don’t miss this chance to leave your mark. Jump in from January 21 to February 3!

## MYTHIC LEAD ROSE ASHE WEAPON SKIN

Elegance meets danger with the new Mythic Lead Rose Ashe Weapon Skin. Inspired by Ashe’s aristocratic roots at Lead Rose Manor, this stunning weapon evolves through four levels of rebellion, blending her refined upbringing with her fearless Deadlock Gang persona.

Each progression step introduces breathtaking new elements: a reactive blooming rose texture at Level Two, a taunting flourish at Level Three, and a striking petal-burst kill effect at Level Four. These dynamic visuals and auditory effects turn every takedown into a work of art.

With this weapon, you’re not just playing as Ashe—you’re embodying her transformation from a poised socialite into a daring outlaw leader. Available now, this Mythic weapon skin brings both beauty and brutality to the battlefield.

## YEAR OF THE SNAKE BRINGS MISCHIEF AND MAGIC

Celebrate the Lunar New Year - Year of the Snake - in Overwatch 2 with the return of Mischief & Magic, a festive game mode that blends strategy, stealth, and skill. This hide-and-seek battle pits Rogues against Knights in a thrilling showdown where every move counts.

As a Rogue, you’ll transform into props, scaling walls and using double jumps to escape your pursuers while taunting them with playful voice lines. Meanwhile, the Knights must hunt down the Rogues, uncovering their hiding spots and defeating them before time runs out. Mischief & Magic is the perfect combination of lighthearted fun and intense competition, bringing teams together in the spirit of Lunar New Year celebrations. Other festive Lunar New Year event modes including Capture the Flag and Bounty Hunter also return.

Complete event challenges to unlock exclusive rewards, including Lunar New Year-themed cosmetics that capture the joy and vibrancy of the season. Then head for the shop to check out new remixes of some of your favorite Lunar New Year skins. The fun begins today and runs through February 3—don’t miss your chance to outwit, outplay, and outlast your opponents!

## VALENTINE’S DAY 2025

Take aim and fall in love again with the return of the Love of Geometry game mode. This team Deathmatch brawl pits teams of four Cupid Hanzos against each other to determine which will reign supreme! Be sure to watch out as hiding around corners is not safe since everyone can use the original Scatter Arrow ability for Hanzo. Love is in the air and the ricochet angles in Love of Geometry, available from February 4 to February 18.

## SEASON 14 DRIVES: DOUBLE POINTS, FINAL REWARDS

Season 14 Drives are coming for one final push, offering players a chance to secure their legacy in Overwatch’s competitive scene and finish the year strong. From February 11 to 16, every match matters as you earn double Competitive Point bonuses, with the opportunity to collect up to a whopping 6,000 Competitive Points during this limited-time event.

This is your last shot to dominate the battlefield and end the 2024 competitive season in style. Whether you’re climbing the ranks or solidifying your place among the elite, Drives are your ticket to finishing strong. Along the way, spend those Competitive Points to claim the coveted Jade Weapon Skins, stunning exclusives that will be sent to the vault after the season ends.

Win or lose, every match contributes to your Drive Score and competitive rewards, so rally your team, focus your strategy, and leave it all on the battlefield. Don’t miss this chance to maximize your rewards and make your mark.
