Fly into action and take up the mantle of the Valkyrie in Mercy’s Recall Challenge! Bring the new Overwatch short story, “[Valkyrie](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/x3/X38BR3N7HTDP1573261672394.pdf)” to life with story-inspired content available to unlock for a limited time.

[![Video](https://markdown-videos-api.jorgenkh.no/youtube/Ya-l3E_Ls1w?width=1280&height=720)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ya-l3E_Ls1w)

From now until December 2, players will be able to earn a player icon, 8 sprays, and a legendary Dr. Ziegler Mercy skin, for a total of 10 new rewards.

- [DR. ZIEGLER](#header1) - Learn how to unlock brand-new Mercy rewards
- [WATCH TO EARN](#header2) - Earn special Mercy-themed cosmetics by watching Twitch streams
- [HEROES NEVER DIE](#header4) - Read the new short story, "Valkyrie"

## DR. ZIEGLER

We’re bringing the excitement of the action-packed short story into Overwatch by making some of the special content in the story available to unlock in-game. Wins in Quick Play, Competitive Play, or Arcade will all earn you a limited-time player icon, two sprays, and a new legendary skin: Dr. Ziegler!

These special Mercy’s Recall Challenge rewards are a bonus on top of your normal earnings for playing in the Arcade**.**

![OW_MercysRecallChallenge_InGameRewards_Embedded_JP.png](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/gallery/bx/BXFZAUCJEM9X1573524279515.png)

## WATCH TO EARN

Unlock additional rewards by watching Twitch streamers before, during or after matches to unlock even more Mercy cosmetics – we’re bringing you more Twitch Drops every time you tune in to participating broadcasters through December 2 to earn six Mercy’s Recall Challenge sprays!

**IMPORTANT:** To be eligible to receive these [Twitch Drops](https://help.twitch.tv/customer/en/portal/articles/2754380-how-to-earn-drops) you must first link your Blizzard account to your Twitch account. Visit the [Twitch Connections](https://www.twitch.tv/settings/connections) page to log in and complete the link now.

![OW_MercysRecallChallenge_TwitchDropsRewards_Embedded_JP.png](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/gallery/4j/4J7U7OYDP7FY1573524318137.png)

Mercy’s Recall Challenge Twitch Drops will be available November 12 – December 2 on all channels streaming in the [Twitch Overwatch game category](https://www.twitch.tv/directory/game/Overwatch) will be eligible for rewards.

## HEROES NEVER DIE

Following the end of Overwatch, Dr. Angela Ziegler attempted to escape her past and help those in need. It would only be a matter of time before she learns that her battle was not over. In “[Valkyrie](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/x3/X38BR3N7HTDP1573261672394.pdf)” by Michael Chu, Mercy regains her wings and begins to rebuild the future.

Embody the Valkyrie with Mercy’s Recall Challenge, now live in-game! Earn 9 wins and watch participating broadcaster channels so you can add 10 limited-time cosmetics to your collection.
