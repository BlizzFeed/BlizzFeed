This season brings Overwatch into a city caught between beauty and collapse, where neon lights cut through drifting smoke and crowds. The streets pulse with life, while old grudges, criminal empires, and dangerous ambitions grow beneath the surface.

At the center of it all stands Shion, leader of the Hashimoto Clan and one of the most dangerous new Heroes to emerge in the wake of Talon's growing influence. But this season is more than just who controls the streets. It's about identity. Belonging. And the people we choose to stand beside when the world feels determined to pull us apart.

From the new Damage Hero and Hybrid map to a seasonal story event, fresh Mythics, creator collaborations, plus a city full of secrets to uncover, Season 3 is taking you Into the Tiger’s Den.

## Ride Fast and Hit Hard

Some Heroes arrive with a dramatic speech... Shion arrives on a motorcycle. Which you can throw at your opponents. It’s awesome.

And when subtlety stops being useful or fun, you can simply ride straight through the battlefield before turning your bike into a projectile weapon.

## Welcome to Neon Junction

You’ve been asking for a new Hybrid map for a long time, and we're finally answering !

Introducing the Neon Junction map, set beneath glowing storefronts on the streets of Tokyo, where the Hybrid format lets the story unfold naturally as you push closer to the heart of Hashimoto territory. The city feels alive at every turn; packed with arcades, capsule machines, late-night shops, and hidden alleyways.

Oh, and our payload robot is the one and only ANDROMEDA III from the hit series Super Cosmic War: Star☆Infinite! Andromeda is piloted by the illustrious Pia. Together, they are defending Future Earth from the evil machinations of the Centauri Empire and their nefarious champion, SAGITTARIUS IV!

## Community Crafted

All the best Overwatch discussions start with a simple question: "*What if we tried this?*"

Community Crafted brings four creators directly into the design process alongside the Hero team. Guxue, Ocie, Apply, and mL7support each helped shape gameplay experiments designed to explore different possibilities for familiar Heroes.

- Guxue's version of Reinhardt leans harder into his brawler fantasy, rewarding aggressive hammer play and encouraging you to stay in the fight.
- mL7support reimagines Baptiste's kit by transforming Immortality Field into an Ultimate while creating more flexible tactical opportunities with Amplification Matrix.
- Apply's Ashe changes give B.O.B. a bigger battlefield presence while rewarding smart setup and combo-driven gameplay.
- Ocie revisits Sombra's disruptive flanker roots, restoring some of her dive identity while creating more interactive counterplay around Hack.

Some changes push aggression further, some open fresh tactical options, and others revisit playstyles you may have been missing or open entirely new ways to think about the game.

Think of it as more of a conversation than a balance patch. And for two weeks from June 30 – July 13, everyone gets to join in the fun!
