# Take Flight in the Flash Ops: Echo Showdown

- URL: https://overwatch.blizzard.com/en-gb/news/23391566/take-flight-in-the-flash-ops-echo-showdown
![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/8r/8RVEXDEUHYRC1586566074450.png)

> Take part in a $50,000 open tournament featuring Echo in every match or tune in to earn in-game rewards!

---

Focus your beams, prime your sticky bombs, and get ready to glide into the Echo Showdown. To celebrate the introduction of the new damage hero, Echo, and to give players a chance to show off their skills with this advanced evolutionary combat robot, we’re hosting a tournament that’s open to everyone!

---

#### Tournament Details

- Signups begin April 14 and extend until April 17. Matches will be held on **April 18 and 19 PDT** using the GameBattles tournament platform.
  - Day one (April 18th) matches will begin in EU at 3:00 AM PDT and NA at 1:00 PM PDT
  - Day two (April 19th) matches will begin in EU at 3:00 AM PDT and NA at 1:00 PM PDT
- Participating teams will ALWAYS be required have an Echo in play. Tri-shot your hardest, we want to see what you’ve got!
- Overwatch League and Contenders players will be eligible to compete, though only two can play on a team at any given time.
- Prizes:
  - We’ve booted up the duplication assimilation matrix and mirrored our prizes across both NA and EU, with **$25,000** in rewards per region.

**To sign up and view a full breakdown of the requirements, regulations, and prizing, choose your region below:**

[North America](https://gamebattles.majorleaguegaming.com/pc/overwatch/tournament/echo-overwatch-tournament-na/info) [Europe](https://gamebattles.majorleaguegaming.com/pc/overwatch/tournament/echo-overwatch-tournament-emea/info)

#### Watch, Learn, and Earn

Anyone who tunes in to watch Overwatch on Twitch.tv during the Flash Ops: Echo Showdown tournament (April 14-20) will receive 6 Echo-themed sprays (two rewarded at each 2, 4, and 6-hour time watched benchmarks) to blast around the battlefield between executing their usual flight routines. Help your community earn their Echo sprays by streaming your matches!

**IMPORTANT:** To be eligible to receive these rewards, you must first link your Blizzard account to your Twitch account. Visit the Twitch Connections page to log in and complete the link now.

[Link Your Accounts](https://www.twitch.tv/settings/connections)
