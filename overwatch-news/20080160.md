*One shot, one kill. . . .*

Did our  [first animated short](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_jKn_GbYe68), "Recall," leave you eager to explore more of the world of Overwatch? Well, get ready—three more are on the way! Next up: **Alive**.

"Alive" weaves a tale of Widowmaker, the peerless Talon assassin who stalks her prey with deadly efficiency. In this episode, we spend a fateful night in London's King's Row—where you'll discover how one death can change everything.

![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/gallery/6SHDHEGRJAKX1459436948166.jpg)

Set your sights: "Alive" premieres exclusively on [PlayStation's YouTube channels](http://blizz.ly/aliveuk) **April 3 at 21:00 CEST**.
