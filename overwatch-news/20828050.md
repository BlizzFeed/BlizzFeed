> In celebration of the Overwatch Anniversary, Blizzard Entertainment in partnership with ARTtitude unveiled 48 console controllers for PS4 and Xbox One representing our 24 heroes.

---

In celebration of the Overwatch Anniversary, Blizzard Entertainment in partnership with ARTtitude unveiled 48 console controllers for PS4 and Xbox One representing our 24 heroes.

These handmade controllers were created by talented European street artists and each one is a unique numbered piece. Itching to get your hands on one? Rejoice, you’ll have a chance to win these controllers through various contests and giveaways organized across Europe. Keep an eye on our official channels for news of these opportunities in the coming days and through the year.\
In the meantime, you can check out a few of them in detail below:
