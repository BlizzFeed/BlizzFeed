# Sink your teeth into Overwatch's BlizzCon Reveals!

- URL: https://overwatch.blizzard.com/en-gb/news/24294376/sink-your-teeth-into-overwatch-s-blizzcon-reveals
![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/gb/GBB69UK9ZAIH1788902346335.png)

> Overwatch brings a new Hero, map, musical performances, esports, and more to BlizzCon 2026!

---

From the reveal of Doctrine as Hero 54, to a Mythic thank you to all our players, and even a clue as to who Hero 55 may be, this year’s Overwatch experience at BlizzCon is all about celebrating our community!

![OW_2026_Full_Lineup.png](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/gallery/HOKLURVU8GWL1788902299535.png)

We can’t have a BlizzCon without a new Overwatch Hero, or should we say, villain? Doctrine, a vampire-inspired Support Hero and ally of Doomfist, is out of the shadows and already in your hands with his limited-time Hero Trial, starting now! His true debut in Season 5 pushes the Reign of Talon story toward its exciting conclusion.

Also featured in today’s BlizzCon Opening Ceremony is the new Escort map, Watchpoint: Grímsvötn, an Icelandic volcano prison and the setting of Doomfist and Doctrine’s reconnection.

But that’s not all. Major Sombra and Roadhog reworks are coming, as well as the long-awaited continuation of Dokiwatch. We’ll keep the BlizzCon festivities going with the latest LE SSERAFIM encore collab, crown the next victor of the Overwatch World Cup from 8 finalists, host a live YOASOBI performance, provide a Mythic thank-you Gift, and more throughout the celebration weekend!

Here’s a more detailed look at everything announced and coming for Overwatch:\
\
![Grimsv%C3%B6tn_Beauty_2.png](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/gallery/ZVJY459E50DI1788902299498.png)

- Doctrine, Overwatch's next Support Hero, is playable now during his limited-time Hero Trial through September 14 before officially joining the roster in Season 5.
- Doctrine brings Overwatch's ultimate vampire fantasy to Support, manipulating lifeforce with his scepter, infusions, and bat-like drones.
- The Reign of Talon reaches its conclusion in Season 5, closing out Overwatch's first year-long story arc. From there, we reveal a *clue* about where 2027 is headed with a Hero 55 tease.
- A new Escort map, Watchpoint: Grímsvötn, takes the fight to a remote Icelandic prison, where Doomfist breaks Doctrine free and sets the final chapter in motion.
- Sombra and Roadhog receive major Hero reworks in Season 5, including Sombra moving from Damage into the Support role.
- Play one game after the Opening Ceremony to earn a BlizzCon Mythic Gift, redeemable for one eligible complete Mythic bundle from Seasons 1-14.
- The Overwatch World Cup reaches its exciting climax as the world's top eight teams compete for the ultimate championship live from BlizzCon.
- Musical performances by Overwatch fans LE SSERAFIM (closing ceremony) and YOASOBI (Overwatch World Cup Day 2)!
- Overwatch Spotlight returns in February 2027, where we’ll share a roadmap for the year ahead!
- There's plenty happening live in the BlizzCon broadcast! Tune in for live competition, panels, playable experiences, and community moments continuing throughout the show!

**QuestWatch!**

![questwatch_makeawish_16_9.png](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/gallery/3KMD4UHZW99D1788902299264.png)

Forget team comps, this fight calls for character sheets! QuestWatch brings Overwatch to the tabletop for its first-ever live TTRPG one-shot, with Matt Mercer as Game Master Cassidy guiding Sombra (Carolina Ravassa), Symmetra (Anjali Bhimani), Reinhardt (Darin DePaul), and Vendetta (Chiara Preziosi) through a new non-canon adventure. Tune in live at 12 pm PT tomorrow, September 13, then stick around as the party rolls for something bigger with an online charity auction supporting Make-A-Wish.

**A Mythic Thank-You**

![OW_2026_BlizzCon_MythicVoucher_1920x1080.png](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/gallery/cl/CLQQJ3MBNE091788902240915.png)

BlizzCon wouldn't be the same without the Overwatch community showing up, so we've got a Mythic Gift to say thanks to our amazing community. Play one game after the Opening Ceremony to earn a Mythic Voucher, then choose one eligible complete Mythic bundle from Seasons 1-14 to make your own. You have until October 5 to earn and redeem your voucher, so the hardest part might just be picking a favorite!

- Play one game after the Opening Ceremony to earn a Mythic Voucher as our thank-you for being part of the Overwatch community.
- Redeem your Mythic Voucher in the Mythic Shop for one eligible complete Mythic bundle from Seasons 1-14.
- Your chosen bundle includes the base Mythic Skin and all of its standard customization levels, so you'll get the complete Mythic experience in one pick.
- Aspects are not included in Mythic Voucher bundles.
- Already own the base level of every eligible Mythic Skin? We've got you covered with 50 Mythic Prisms instead.
- Earn your Mythic Voucher starting September 12 after the Opening Ceremony, then choose and redeem your eligible Mythic bundle by October 5.

There is so much happening all weekend, but the fun doesn’t stop there! Season 5 launches on October 6, so stay tuned for more info in the coming weeks, and keep an eye out for Spotlight 2027 early next year!
