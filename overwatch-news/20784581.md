# Join Us May 24 to Celebrate 1 Year of Overwatch

- URL: https://overwatch.blizzard.com/en-gb/news/20784581/join-us-may-24-to-celebrate-1-year-of-overwatch

> To celebrate Overwatch’s first anniversary, we invited some of our friends from across Europe to join us for a day of fun, games, streaming and prizes!

---

What a year it has been! Since Overwatch launched on May 24, 2016, we’ve met new heroes and traveled to new (sometimes exotic) locations. We played our fair share of Lúcioball and Capture the Rooster, heard Reinhardt retell the terrible tale of Junkenstein’s Revenge, and relived Tracer’s first mission during the King’s Row Uprising.

To celebrate what this year has meant to us, we’ve invited some of our friends from across Europe to join us at the TaKeTV studios in Krefeld, Germany, for a day of fun, games, and prizes!  This livestream celebration will begin May 24th at 10:00 CEST, and the party (can’t stop) won’t stop until approximately 20:00 CEST.

So be sure to join us on [Twitch](https://www.twitch.tv/playoverwatch) as we get ready to fight for the future and jump into our second year of Overwatch...and beyond.
