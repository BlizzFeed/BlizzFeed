> When Overwatch’s hands are tied, Blackwatch moves through the shadows to deliver retribution.

*“No one will ever know Blackwatch was there.”*

The latest installment in our series of digital comic shorts has arrived: **Retribution**!

Following a large-scale attack on Overwatch’s facilities in [Oslo, Norway](https://playoverwatch.com/blog/21658542), Blackwatch gets to work gathering intel so the peace-keeping organization can pursue justice. Their findings point to one of Talon’s high-ranking members but taking him down will prove to be a complicated affair.

Blackwatch agents are tasked with apprehending the mastermind, but their plans are quickly interrupted…

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/fu/FUDD4Y0BT4HI1522786161581.jpg)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/FQLQ050SCKG11521835732280.pdf)

Written by Blizzard Entertainment’s Matt Burns and illustrated by Gray Shuko, “Retribution” is now available for [download](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/FQLQ050SCKG11521835732280.pdf).

We hope you enjoy it—stay tuned for more Overwatch comics coming soon!
