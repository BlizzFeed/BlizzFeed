Calling all heroes! This summer, Blizzard Entertainment is partnering with Twitch Prime to bring you a series of exciting in-game rewards.

Starting today, all Twitch Prime members can claim two Wrecking Ball Loot Boxes in Overwatch\*. Available through September 3, these promotional Loot Boxes contain four random items for the newest Overwatch hero – [Wrecking Ball](https://playoverwatch.com/heroes/wrecking-ball/)! Twitch Prime members can also claim a Golden Loot Box in September as well as another Golden Loot Box in October.

## Link Your Accounts Getting Started

**Link Your Twitch and Blizzard Accounts:**

**Step 1:** If you don't already have a Twitch account, create one **[here](https://twitch.amazon.com/prime?ref=sm_CM_OWBlog)**. \
**Step 2:** **[Log in](https://www.twitch.tv/login)** to your Twitch account. \
**Step 3:** If you don't already have a Blizzard account, create one **[here](https://battle.net/account/creation)**.\
**Step 4:** Navigate to the Twitch [**Connections page**](https://www.twitch.tv/settings/connections) in the Settings menu.\
**Step 5:** Locate the **Blizzard Battle.net** section and chose your gameplay region.\
**Step 6:** Log in to the Blizzard account you wish to link and click **Log in to Blizzard**.

**(Console Only)** **Link Your Console and Blizzard Accounts:**

**Step 7:** [Link your PlayStation Network or Xbox Live account to your Blizzard account.](https://battle.net/support/article/000065162)\
**Step 8:** Success! You are ready to redeem your Overwatch offers.

Before linking your Twitch and Blizzard accounts, check to see that you’re not logged into any other Blizzard accounts at the same time in the same browser, as this could cause complications. For the best results, complete the process in "private browsing" or "incognito" mode.

## How to Claim Your Rewards Offer Redemption Instructions

**Claim Your Twitch Prime Offer:**

**Step 1:** Visit the [**Twitch Prime Rewards**](https://twitch.amazon.com/prime/loot/overwatch) page for Overwatch.\
**Step 2:** **[Log in](https://www.twitch.tv/login)** to your Twitch account and authorize.\
**Step 3:** Verify your linked Blizzard account and click **Claim Your Loot**.\
**Step 4:** Success! You are ready to claim your items in the game.

**Claim Your Rewards In-Game:**

**Step 5:** Launch **Overwatch** on PC, PlayStation 4, or Xbox One.\
**Step 6:** Click on the **Loot Boxes** menu option. \
**Step 7:** Locate your loot boxes and open them to see what you unlocked!

Once you claim an Overwatch reward, and if you remain an active Twitch Prime member, all future rewards in the campaign will be automatically applied to the same Blizzard account and cannot be transferred. Be sure that your preferred Blizzard account is connected to your Twitch account at the time of enrolling!
