# Overwatch 2 x Avatar: The Last Airbender Collab

> Master the elements and bring balance to the battlefield with the Overwatch 2 x Avatar: The Last Airbender event!

---

The harmony of the Four Nations has come to the frontlines in Overwatch 2! Immerse yourself in the elemental arts from *Avatar: The Last Airbender* in an epic collaboration that unites iconic heroes with Water, Earth, Fire, and Air bending. Whether you’re gliding through the battlefield like an Air Nomad, holding strong like the Earth Kingdom, or igniting your team’s spirit like the Fire Nation, the power of balance and unity is in your hands. From the serene strength of Zenyatta as Aang to the fierce resolve of Genji as Zuko, this limited-time event offers unforgettable ways to master the elements and bring balance to every battle.

## Master the Elements and Bring Balance to the Battlefield

Each skin captures the timeless legacy of *Avatar: The Last Airbender*, blending Overwatch’s dynamic gameplay with the enduring spirit of the elements. Unleash your inner bender with:

- Mei as Katara – Flow into battle as the Water Tribe’s wise and fluid defender. Complete special challenges to unlock this skin and bend the flow of battle in your favor.
- Zenyatta as Aang – Channel the Air Nomad’s legacy of peace and resilience as you glide into combat with the spiritual grace of the Avatar himself, a beacon of hope in any fight.
- Orisa as Appa – Fly into the fray with your team’s steadfast protector, embodying loyalty and strength to carry your team to victory. \**Yip yip!*\*
- Venture as Toph – Shake your enemies' foundations as the unyielding, earthbending master who stands strong against all odds. Show them what true strength looks like, whether they see it coming or not.
- Genji as Zuko – Fuel your flames with the determined firebender whose heart burns with purpose and redemption, embracing his fierce determination and resilience.
- Kiriko as Suki – Strike with precision and grace, honoring the unbreakable spirit of a Kyoshi Warrior and blending resolve, strength, and loyalty.

## Complete Challenges to Earn Rewards

Harness the elements and fight like Team Avatar by completing event challenges to immerse yourself in bending mastery and unlock Mei as Katara. Plus earn themed sprays, voice lines, and player icons that capture the wisdom and iconic moments of *Avatar: The Last Airbender*.  Equip your heroes with the strength, style, and heart of the Four Nations.

From December 17 to 30, gather your Gaang and summon the strength of Water, Earth, Fire, and Air in Overwatch 2. This collaboration honors the bonds between teammates, the resilience to overcome challenges, and the harmony we all strive for—both on and off the battlefield.

Master the elements, unite your team, and bring balance to Overwatch 2.
