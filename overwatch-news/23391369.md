### WATCH TO EARN

Unlock Echo-themed rewards by watching Twitch channels streaming in the [Twitch Overwatch game category](https://www.twitch.tv/directory/game/Overwatch). Tune in to any Twitch streamer while they’re playing Overwatch now through April 20 to earn six Echo sprays!

IMPORTANT: To be eligible to receive these [Twitch Drops](https://help.twitch.tv/customer/en/portal/articles/2754380-how-to-earn-drops), you must first link your Blizzard account to your Twitch account. Visit the [Twitch Connections](https://www.twitch.tv/settings/connections) page to log in and complete the link now.

### FLASH OPS: ECHO SHOWDOWN

![OW_2020_Hero32_Blog_Embedded-EchoTournament.png](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/gallery/62/62US0MD0ERV91586566972350.png)

The Flash Ops: Echo Showdown tournament is presented by Open Division and available for all players, content creators, and aspiring professional players! Join us on April 18 - 19, 2020 to compete for the $50,000 ($25,000 NA / $25,000 EMEA) prize pool. Throughout the tournament, all teams will be required to have an Echo on their team at all times – This tournament will be a great way to explore all the possibilities she brings to Overwatch!

Learn more about the tournament and how to sign up in our [tournament blog](https://playoverwatch.com/news/23391566)!

### ECHOED ORIGINS
