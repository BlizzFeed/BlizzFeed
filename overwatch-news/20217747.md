# The Overwatch Open by ELEAGUE

- URL: https://overwatch.blizzard.com/en-gb/news/20217747/the-overwatch-open-by-eleague
![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/zj/ZJIHQGLDP2JK1469797239154.jpg)

> ELEAGUE and FACEIT have partnered to bring you the Overwatch Open. Sign up for your chance at the largest prize pool in competitive Overwatch history!

---

On July 22 ELEAGUE and FACEIT announced their partnership to bring you the Overwatch Open—with $300,000 USD up for grabs this is the largest prize pool in the Overwatch competitive scene to date!

The initial online phase of the competition will be run by FACEIT after which players will compete in their respective regional finals live on Twitch. The action culminates with the grand finals on Friday, September 30 which will be held in the ELEAGUE arena at Turner Studios in Atlanta and will be broadcast on live TV on TBS as well as Twitch.
