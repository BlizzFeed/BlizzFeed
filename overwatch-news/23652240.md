##### UP FOR A CHALLENGE?

Journey into familiar territory with dangerous new twists. This year, we’re adding brand new Challenge Missions to the Archives Brawls:

- **New Uprising Challenge Mission:**
  - Bulletproof Barriers - Enemy barriers are invulnerable.
- **New Retribution Challenge Mission:**
  - Sympathy Gains - Damaging enemies heals other enemies.
- **New Storm Rising Challenge Mission:**
  - Thunderstorm - Enemies damage all nearby players.

You can find more details on Archives brawls and their challenge missions on the [Overwatch Archives Event site.](https://playoverwatch.com/events/archives/)

---

##### NEW WEEKLY REWARDS--NEW WAYS TO EARN THEM

Take on Archives Missions, earn stars, and unlock player icons, sprays, and sensational skins like Corredor Lucio, Subaquatic Zenyatta, and Camouflage Mercy. Choosing harder difficulties and Challenge Missions will grant additional stars. Each week introduces new rewards to earn!
