# Claim your Honor in the Overwatch League Flash Ops: Kanezaka Community Tournament

- URL: https://overwatch.blizzard.com/en-gb/news/23609152/claim-your-honor-in-the-overwatch-league-flash-ops-kanezaka-community-tournament
![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/ai/AIM60JWQRJLW1610569529741.jpg)

> Take part in a $10,000 open 4v4 Team Deathmatch tournament!

---

Ready your shuriken, nock your arrows, and get ready to brawl in the Overwatch League Kanezaka Community Tournament. To celebrate the launch of the new Kanezaka map, and to give players a chance to show off their skills, Overwatch League is hosting a tournament that’s open to everyone!

---

## Tournament Details

- Signups begin January 15 and extend until January 21. Matches will be held on **January 22 - 24** using the [GameBattles tournament platform](https://gamebattles.majorleaguegaming.com/pc/overwatch/tournament/flash-ops-kanezaka-emea/info).
- All matches will be played in the 4v4 Team Deathmatch format.
- Two (2) Overwatch League players will be eligible to compete per team, and only two can play on a team at any given time.
- Prizes:
  - The Overwatch League is hosting regional tournaments, each with their own $10,000 prize pools.

---

## Watch the Finals on the Overwatch League

Though many teams will enter the battle, few will rise to the top. You’ll be able to catch all the action of the semi-finals and finals on the official [Overwatch League YouTube channel](https://www.youtube.com/overwatchleague), produced by our friends at [Monkey Bubble](https://monkeybubble.gg/).
