> Gear up and fight for freedom or chaos on the frontlines in an elite Overwatch 2 x G.I. Joe collaboration!

---

The battle lines are drawn. Conflict is no longer a question of if or when, but who you fight for. So suit up, pick a side, and earn some tacti-cool rewards as Overwatch 2 teams up with the legendary world of G.I. Joe in a limited-time collaboration. From July 1–14, it’s up to you to answer the call: join the elite ranks of G.I. Joe or throw in with the ruthless Cobra organization as heroes and villains clash.

## Fight for Freedom or Strike for Chaos

This mission comes equipped with style and strength as Legendary skins straight out of the G.I. Joe arsenal bring some of the most definitive characters onto the Overwatch 2 battlefield. While you slice through enemy lines with precision or plot world domination from the shadows, this roster has your back (or your next betrayal).

- Genji as **Snake Eyes**: The elite G.I. Joe operative, a silent blade with unmatched discipline. Deadly, sworn to the mission, never misses.
- Freja as **Scarlett**: G.I. Joe’s counterintelligence extraordinaire. The sharpest mind in the room, backed by tactical accuracy and firepower. Crossbow locked. Field-ready. Strategic genius with a mean uppercut.
- Widowmaker as **Baroness**: Striking for Cobra with brains, bullets, and betrayal, this femme fatale is lethal. Cold, calculated, always in control and couture.
- Reaper as **Cobra Commander**: Unhinged ambition in a chilling new form, schemes sharpened by madness. The face of terror. The voice of chaos. Commands Cobra.
- Doomfist as **Destro**: Not just muscle for Cobra, but a mastermind in metal, made of profits, power, and pure strength. Brawler. Steel-plated menace. Built for punching.

Gear up with your favorites through five individual Hero Bundles or grab the G.I. Joe Mega Bundle to command the full force of this collab.

## Complete Special Ops to Earn Rewards

Complete in-game special ops challenges to unlock exclusive rewards before they’re decommissioned. With signature voice lines, sprays, and a weapon charm all inspired by the classic arsenal, every reward is a salute to the iconic G.I. Joe legacy. Claim the G.I. Joe player title, rack up 25,000 Battle Pass XP to fuel your seasonal grind, and show the world you’re mission ready. Challenges are live across Quick Play, Competitive, and Stadium, and wins score you double progress. You’ve got your orders—lock in and get to work.
