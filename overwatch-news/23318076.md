Grab your beads and join the bacchanalia now through March 9 to earn unique, Mardi Gras-inspired rewards. *Laissez les bons temps rouler!*

[https://youtu.be/zZK0-92UJMs](https://youtu.be/zZK0-92UJMs)

From now until **March 9**, players will be able to earn two limited-time player icons, 7 sprays, and the epic **Mardi Gras Ashe skin,** for a total of 10 new rewards.

- **GET IN THERE** - Learn how to unlock brand new Ashe rewards
- **WATCH TO EARN** – Earn special Ashe-themed cosmetics by watching Twitch streams
- **COURTESY OF THE DEADLOCK GANG** - Learn more about Ashe merchandise

### GET IN THERE

Don your purple, green, and gold, and enjoy the celebration with special Mardi Gras content available to unlock in-game. Wins in Quick Play, Competitive Play, or Arcade will all earn you two limited-time player icons, a spray, and a new epic skin: Mardi Gras Ashe!

**These special Ashe’s Mardi Gras Challenge rewards are a bonus on top of your normal earnings for playing in the Arcade**

![OW_2020_AshesMardiGrasChallenge_Blog_InGameRewards_700x1146_MB01.png](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/gallery/nf/NFI0Y2DZRX1K1582653094456.png)

### WATCH TO EARN

Unlock additional rewards by watching Twitch streamers before, during, or after matches to unlock even more Ashe cosmetics. Tune in to participating broadcasters while they’re playing Overwatch any time through March 9 to earn six Ashe’s Mardi Gras Challenge sprays!

IMPORTANT: To be eligible to receive these [Twitch Drops](https://help.twitch.tv/customer/en/portal/articles/2754380-how-to-earn-drops), you must first link your Blizzard account to your Twitch account. Visit the [Twitch Connections](https://www.twitch.tv/settings/connections) page to log in and complete the link now.

![OW_2020_AshesMardiGrasChallenge_Blog_TwitchDrops_700x1146_MB01.png](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/7f/7FC3H2UDK2QK1582412179685.png)

Ashe’s Mardi Gras Challenge drops will be available February 25 – March 9. All channels streaming in the [Twitch Overwatch game category](https://www.twitch.tv/directory/game/Overwatch) will be available for rewards.
