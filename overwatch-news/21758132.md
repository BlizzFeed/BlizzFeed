# Unlock Pink Mercy and Help Support Breast Cancer Research

- URL: https://overwatch.blizzard.com/en-gb/news/21758132/unlock-pink-mercy-and-help-support-breast-cancer-research
![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/pf/PFXG6OECFAJ61525400895294.jpg)

> Show your support for the Breast Cancer Research (BCRF) Foundation as Overwatch's brilliant scientist and guardian angel, Mercy, in her new Pink skin—available for a limited time.

---

We believe in creating a better world, and as part of that mission we’ve teamed up with the Breast Cancer Research Foundation to release a special charity skin in Overwatch: [**PINK MERCY**](https://playoverwatch.com/charity)!

Available for purchase in-game and online through May 21, all sales of this limited-time legendary skin will benefit BCRF and their mission to end breast cancer.

[![Video](https://markdown-videos-api.jorgenkh.no/youtube/WSMmgo2v1ZI?width=1280&height=720)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WSMmgo2v1ZI)

But this isn’t the only way to show your support! We’ve also created a limited-edition Pink Mercy charity shirt, unleashed a unique collection of Pink Mercy sprays and player icons you can get as Twitch Drops, and rallied an epic team of Overwatch content creators to stream for the cause.

As a brilliant scientist and the guardian angel of Overwatch, Dr. Angela Ziegler—codename Mercy—has dedicated her life to helping and healing others. Now you can help BCRF work toward a cure for breast cancer by purchasing the new [PINK MERCY skin](https://playoverwatch.com/charity), available for a limited time on PC, PlayStation 4, and Xbox One.

![PinkMercySkin-PierScreenshot_OW_JP_thumb.png](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_folder_media/mh/MH10NQHU0SZ41525400656244.png)

Blizzard Entertainment will donate 100% of the proceeds that it receives from all Pink Mercy skin sales to BCRF, with a guaranteed minimum donation of $250,000 (USD). The total donation amount will be disclosed after the campaign concludes on May 21. Please visit our shop page for more details.

![PinkMercySkin-BlogSectionBar-WearForAwareness_OW_JP.png](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_folder_media/2e/2EWHE0CLESPO1525400462740.png)

Show your support on and off the battlefield with a new, limited-edition Pink Mercy charity shirt.

Log in to Overwatch any time between May 8 and May 21 and unlock a BCRF-themed player icon!

We've also partnered with community artist [ONEMEGAWATT](https://twitter.com/onemegawatt) to create a series of themed Twitch Drops, including a new player icon and four all-new sprays. Available only during this event, you can earn these special Pink Mercy cosmetics by viewing select streams on Twitch during the two-week promotional period.

**IMPORTANT:** To be eligible to receive [Pink Mercy Twitch Drops](https://help.twitch.tv/customer/en/portal/articles/2754380-how-to-earn-drops) you must first link your Blizzard account to your Twitch account. Visit the [Twitch Connections](https://www.twitch.tv/settings/connections) page to log in and complete the link now.
