# One-Punch Man x Overwatch 2

> The collab returns with new characters, returning icons, and rewards that pack a punch!

---

Get ready to style yourself as anime’s fiercest heroes (or villains) with the return of the One-Punch Man x Overwatch 2 collaboration from **October 16 to 29.**With fresh faces joining fan-favorite comeback skins, brave rewards to earn, and the show resuming with it's third season, it’s time to squad up and punch your way to victory!

![OW2_S3_OPM_Legal_BeautyShot_Saitama.png](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/52/52QET7VHCXH61760626082736.png)

## Punch with New Heroes

At the heart of this collab’s second run are two daring debuts, as new characters suit up to fuse Overwatch 2’s flair with One-Punch Man’s unbeatable energy.

- Wuyang dons the mantle of **Garou**, the Hero Hunter mirroring Wuyang’s rebellious streak. With Garou’s spectral flair and chaotic flow, this skin adds an enigmatic current to Wuyang’s fluid grace.
- Meanwhile, Ashe transforms into **Hellish Blizzard** (with B.O.B. looking sharp). Her aura of confidence and authority commands respect from both allies and enemies alike. The powerhouse leader of the Blizzard Group finds her perfect echo in Ashe, whose cool, commanding presence rules the battlefield.

![OVR_S19_OPMCollab_DuoShot_AquaGarouAsheFubuki_enUS_001.png](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/r4/R4EZ9WQR20UV1760116979037.png)

Of course, the original collab Heroes are making a comeback too!

- Doomfist as **Saitama**. The cape. The gloves. The punch. Enough said.
- Genji as **Genos** embodies an unrelenting pursuit of strength and ninja precision.
- Kiriko as **Terrible Tornado** blends fox magic with some psychic panache.
- Soldier: 76 as **Mumen Rider** is justice on two wheels with unshakable courage.

Every collab skin is crafted with care to carry personality and unique details of the iconic inspirations from our first crossover into our second. Saitama’s comically straightforward power, Genos’ precision, and now Hellish Blizzard’s psychic grace, and Garou’s dangerous edge, all folded into Overwatch’s stylized aesthetic. We’ve got heroes, we’ve got villains, and we’re exploring the thin line between them through bold, expressive design.

So, if you missed the originals the first time or want to add the newcomers to your collection, now is your chance to assemble the full crossover squad.

![OVR_S19_OPMCollab_GroupShot_enUS_001.png](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/et/ET4JAD75C8QW1760117248721.png)

## Punch Through Challenges

Gear up and get grinding—justice doesn’t earn itself and these rewards aren’t about to either! Complete event challenges in game to earn the Soldier: 76 Mumen Rider skin, plus 15k Battle Pass XP, and a heroic lineup of crossover cosmetics for your collection. But act fast, because these valiant rewards will vanish when the collab rides off into the sunset again.

![OW2_S3_OPM_Legal_BeautyShot_Mumen.png](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/lz/LZPKDUWPUIDQ1760117332577.png)

## Punch Harder Together

Overwatch has always been where the unexpected and unlikely can thrive. Where rocket-hammer-wielding crusaders and time-jumping pilots team up to make a difference. Both One-Punch Man and Overwatch exalt in the weird and wonderful, that’s what makes the crossover click. This is a playful collision where courage, absurdity, heroism, and even villainy all have a place. Ready your fists from **October 16 to 29**!

The first collab set the bar. The second aims to take it higher. Punch hard. Play Overwatch.
