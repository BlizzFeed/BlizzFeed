As Season 13: Spellbinder continues, we’re turning back time and introducing an experience that will bring players old and new together. Whether you're craving a nostalgic trip to Overwatch's origins or eager to leave your mark among the competition, Season 13 has something for everyone. Let’s get into the action!

## Overwatch: Classic – Relive the Thrill!

For a special three-week event starting today (November 12), Overwatch 2 takes you back to where it all began. This is your chance to experience the thrilling origins of Overwatch, complete with iconic maps, beloved heroes, and classic gameplay. Whether you're a veteran from the early days or a new player discovering the roots of the game, this event will immerse you in the original joy that defined Overwatch.

With the pioneering hero roster and their classic abilities back in the spotlight, you’ll experience the pleasure of Overwatch’s 6v6 format and no-limit team compositions from November 12 – November 14. Then, Single Hero Limits will be applied for the rest of the Overwatch: Classic event, from November 15 – December 2. Step into battle on legendary maps like Hanamura, Temple of Anubis, and Volskaya Industries for a flashback to the moments that made Overwatch special, allowing you to relive the experiences that first captured the gaming world.

Gather your old friends or make new ones on the battlefield as you reconnect over the camaraderie that Overwatch is known for. This is a celebration of fun, friendships, team strategies, and the thrill of every victory. But hurry—this event is a limited time opportunity, so mark your calendars and join the fun from November 12 to December 2! Relive the thrills that made Overwatch legendary. Check out our [Overwatch: Classic blog](https://overwatch.blizzard.com/en-us/news/24146047/) to learn more.

## Dive into Drives for Glory

Competitive Drives is back from November 29 to December 2, bringing new challenges and exclusive rewards for those bold enough to take on the competition. This high-octane event will push your limits as you battle through fresh challenges to secure your place in Overwatch 2 history.

Showcase your skill and claim the coveted Season 13 Signature, a collectible reward that lets everyone know you mean business. But it won’t be easy - checkpoints along the way ensure you don’t lose progress, but more wins are required to lock in those hard-earned rewards. Want to increase your Drive Score? Team up with friends and receive bonus points for group victories. Strength in numbers will help you dominate the competition.

And for those who completed the last Drive, prestige your Signature to show off your unmatched dedication and prove that you’re a top-tier competitor. The stakes are high, and glory awaits—are you ready to make your mark?

## New Mythic Deliverance Soldier: 76 Weapon Skin

Soldier: 76 is stepping into the spotlight with the new Mythic Deliverance Weapon Skin, a weapon that evolves with your gameplay. Inspired by the Six Realms of Rebirth in Buddhism, this skin reacts to every elimination, collecting the souls of defeated enemies and transforming visually as you use it.

As you defeat enemies, the Mythic Deliverance Weapon Skin weapon progresses through four stages of reactivity, each offering a new visual and auditory effect. Start with a ghostly aura around the weapon, progress to ghostly reactivity, add a flourish, and finish with the haunting sight of enemy souls being dragged into a lantern for a truly chilling effect. The more you perform, the more this weapon evolves, rewarding your gameplay with intense, satisfying feedback.

The Mythic Deliverance Soldier: 76 Complete Weapon Bundle is now available, featuring the complete Mythic Weapon Skin (base + all level upgrades) and 20 tier skips to boost your progress.
