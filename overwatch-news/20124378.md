> With less than a week to go until the launch of Overwatch, our celebrity teams are getting ready for the ultimate showdown!

---

With less than a week to go until the launch of *Overwatch*, our celebrity teams are getting ready for the ultimate showdown. With glory and bragging rights on the line, two teams of athletes, celebrities, and YouTubers will face off against each other in exciting live *Overwatch* All-Stars matches in the UK and the Netherlands!

At the **Oval Space in East London** **on May 25**, it’ll be **Team Sports** versus **Team Entertainers.** The two world class footballers, Everton FC’s and Spain’s U21 captain [Gerard Deulofeu](https://twitter.com/gerardeulofeu) and Arsenal FC and England star [Kelly Smith MBE](https://twitter.com/kelly_smith10?lang=en-gb), will be joined by influencers including [Ali-A](https://twitter.com/OMGitsAliA), [Vikkstar 123](https://twitter.com/Vikkstar123), [Slogoman](https://twitter.com/Slogomanify), and [Mantrousse](https://twitter.com/PalaGilroySen) to form the unstoppable Team Sports squad.

They’ll battle a team fronted by famous comedian and actor [Rufus Hound](https://twitter.com/RufusHound) and icon [Jamal Edwards MBE](https://twitter.com/jamaledwards), backed by [Lalna](https://twitter.com/YogscastLalna)and [Sjin](https://twitter.com/YogscastSjin) from The Yogscast, [Moreconsole](https://twitter.com/MoreConsole), and PlayStation Access’ very own [Hollie Bennett](https://twitter.com/HollieB). Together they make up Team Entertainers, hoping to show the world how they shine in *Overwatch*.

The members of both teams have trained hard in the beta and promise an exciting match at the live *Overwatch* All-Stars London event, in which they’ll strive to take down their rivals and prove who the real heroes are!

The challenge is also on in the Netherlands, where **Team Yarasky** will face off against **Team TheDutchTerms** in **Utrecht on May 30**. [Yarasky](https://www.instagram.com/officialyarasky/?hl=en) has recruited Twitch streamer [Nysira](https://twitter.com/Nysira) to bolster his ranks, as well as Praga from Belgium gaming website 9Lives, Sven from [GameMania Netherlands](https://www.facebook.com/GameManiaNederland/?fref=ts), [Willie Wartaal](https://twitter.com/Wiwatron) from [De Jeugd van Tegenwoordig](https://www.facebook.com/dejeugd/), and Ado The Hague football player [Ruben Schaken](https://twitter.com/rubenschaken).

Joining [TheDutchTerms](https://www.instagram.com/vincent.dik/) will be [LowkoTV](https://twitter.com/LowkoTV), alongside Ajax Amsterdam player [Nemanj Gudelj](https://www.instagram.com/nemanjagudelj/?hl=en), Shelly of the television show [Gamekings](https://www.facebook.com/gamekings/), [Faberyayo](https://twitter.com/faberyayo) from [De Jeugd van Tegenwoordig](https://www.facebook.com/dejeugd/), and Tom from [GameMania Belgium](https://www.facebook.com/GameManiaBelgie/?fref=ts).

A limited number of tickets to the live *Overwatch* All-Stars events will be available through social media—be sure to follow our celebrity players’ channels and keep an eye on the **#OverwatchAllStars** hashtag for a chance to snag a spot. In addition to watching the matches live, event attendees will be able to win some loot in activities like costume contests and giveaways.

A total of nine *Overwatch* All-Stars events are being held across Europe, and you’ll be able to catch the action of each of the matches online. We hope you’re hyped!
