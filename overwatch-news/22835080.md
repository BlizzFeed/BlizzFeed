# Get Back in the Fight with Ana’s Bastet Challenge

- URL: https://overwatch.blizzard.com/en-gb/news/22835080/get-back-in-the-fight-with-anas-bastet-challenge
![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/kf/KFP48OQYTDCC1546636148466.jpg)

> Prove yourself as a powerful guardian by completing Ana’s Bastet Challenge! Unlock new content inspired by the latest Overwatch short story “Bastet” in-game for a limited time.

---

Prove yourself as a powerful guardian by completing Ana’s Bastet Challenge! We’re bringing the fierce protector from the new *Overwatch* short story, “Bastet,” into the game with story-inspired content available to unlock for a limited time.

[![Video](https://markdown-videos-api.jorgenkh.no/youtube/6cNobbufrVU?width=1280&height=720)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6cNobbufrVU)

From **now** until **January 22**, players will be able to undertake a special trial to earn five sprays, a player icon, a victory pose, and an **epic Bastet Ana skin,** for a total of eight new rewards.

- [BECOME BASTET](#header1) - Learn how to unlock brand-new Ana sprays and skin
- [WATCH THEIR BACKS](#header2) - Earn special Ana-themed sprays by watching Twitch streams
- [GUISE OF A GUARDIAN](#header3) - Read the new short story, “Bastet”

![OW_BastetBlog-BecomeBastet_Header%20_B01.png](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/gallery/ASX43SSDW95D1546635681639.png)

We’re bringing the excitement of the action-packed short story “Bastet” into *Overwatch* by making some of the special content in the short available to unlock in-game. Wins in Quick Play, Competitive Play, or Arcade will all earn you a limited-time player icon, victory pose, and a new epic skin: BASTET ANA!

**These special Ana’s Bastet Challenge rewards are a bonus on top of your normal earnings for playing in the Arcade.**

![OW_BastetBlog-InGameRewards_B01.jpg](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/gallery/VC2YQ7AYR8W41546635682949.jpg)

![OW_BastetBlog-WatchTheirBacks_Headers_B01.png](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/gallery/AYW55CSWWQGV1546635682667.png)While fulfilling the protector’s legacy in-game, you can watch Twitch streamers between matches to unlock even more Bastet-themed cosmetics. Tune in to participating broadcasters while they’re playing Overwatch anytime through January 22 to earn five Ana's Bastet Challenge sprays!

**IMPORTANT:** To be eligible to receive these [Twitch Drops](https://help.twitch.tv/customer/en/portal/articles/2754380-how-to-earn-drops) you must first link your Blizzard account to your Twitch account. Visit the [Twitch Connections](https://www.twitch.tv/settings/connections) page to log in and complete the link now.

![OW_BastetBlog-TwitchDrop_B01.jpg](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/gallery/V4SQQFE3983D1546635682947.jpg)

Ana’s Bastet Challenge Twitch Drops will be available January 8 – 21 on select Twitch broadcaster channels—view the full list of participating channels below. Tune in during the highlighted dates and times to support a specific broadcaster, or watch their streams at any point during the campaign to unlock these rewards. For more information on Twitch Drops, click [here](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/overwatch/t/d-va-nano-cola-challenge-faq/199311?u=tompowers).
