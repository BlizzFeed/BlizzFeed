# Take the stage in Sigma’s Maestro Challenge!

> Join the ensemble and earn limited-time rewards, including Maestro Sigma (legendary), by winning games and watching Overwatch on Twitch.

---

It’s your turn to take the stage! Take part in Sigma’s symphony to earn limited-time rewards, including **Maestro Sigma (legendary)**.

[![Video](https://markdown-videos-api.jorgenkh.no/youtube/PFm5dzs3fCs?width=1280&height=720)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PFm5dzs3fCs)

From now until July 27, players will be able to earn a**player icon**, a**legendary emote**, **six unique sprays**, and **Maestro Sigma (legendary)** for a total of **nine new rewards.**

- **THE MAESTRO** - Learn how to unlock brand-new Sigma rewards
- **WATCH TO EARN** - Earn special music-themed cosmetics by watching Twitch streams
- **AMP IT UP!** - Learn more about the new soundtrack, *Overwatch: Cities & Countries*

### THE MAESTRO

Take part in Sigma’s symphony and shine under the spotlight with special concerto-themed content available to unlock in-game. Wins in Quick Play, Competitive Play, or Arcade will all earn you a limited-time player icon, a legendary emote, and a new legendary skin: Maestro Sigma!

**These special Sigma’s Maestro Challenge rewards are a bonus on top of your normal earnings for playing in the Arcade.**

![Sigma's Maestro Challenge In-Game Rewards Overview](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/gallery/l2/L20XI3E0U23S1594072900440.png)

### WATCH TO EARN

Unlock additional rewards by watching Twitch streamers before, during, or after matches to unlock even more musical cosmetics. Tune in to any Twitch streamer while they’re playing Overwatch now through July 27 to earn six Sigma’s Maestro Challenge sprays!

**IMPORTANT**: To be eligible to receive these [**Twitch Drops**](https://help.twitch.tv/customer/en/portal/articles/2754380-how-to-earn-drops), you must first link your Blizzard account to your Twitch account. Visit the [**Twitch Connections**](https://www.twitch.tv/settings/connections) page to log in and complete the link now.

![Sigma's Maestro Challenge Twitch Drops Rewards Overview](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/gallery/lk/LKXYECLW4WZ51594073149557.png)

Sigma’s Maestro Challenge drops will be available **July 14 – 27**. All channels streaming in the [**Twitch Overwatch game category**](https://www.twitch.tv/directory/game/Overwatch) will be available for rewards.
