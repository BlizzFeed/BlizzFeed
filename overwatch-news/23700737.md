### MAKE A SPLASH

Bon voyage! It’s time to celebrate summer with the ultimate getaway: [the Overwatch Summer Games](https://playoverwatch.com/events/summer-games/). Earn weekly rewards, get new cosmetics, and bring your A-game in the Lúcioball arena.

### AMP IT UP

Overwatch’s futuristic spin on soccer is back! Team up to boop the ball into the opponent’s goal. The team that scores the most points before time runs out wins the game.

### EARN WEEKLY REWARDS

Unleash a wave of new weekly rewards by playing games in Quick Play, Competitive Play, or the Arcade to unlock new player icons, sprays, and summer-themed skins like Ocean King Winston, Sunset Pharah, and Nihon Hanzo. Wins count as two games. Each week brings new rewards to earn!

![Summer Games Loot Box Rewards: Buy 50, get 10 extra](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/sp/SPTEMQ97M7QI1626725511533.png)

### SNAG SOME SUMMER SWAG

Stock up for the summer and receive 10 bonus Summer Games Loot Boxes when you purchase 50. Don’t wait too long, though—like any vacation, this deal will be over before you know it!
