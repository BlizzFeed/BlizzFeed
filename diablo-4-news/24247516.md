## Unleash Your Rage with Killstreak System

Indulge in bloodthirsty carnage to grow progressively stronger—maintain a fast, efficient slew of kills to increase your power and earn rewards.

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/B5SOFETSPQTD1769645253377.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/B5SOFETSPQTD1769645253377.png)

The Killstreak system changes the way you play across every activity in Diablo IV.

- Slaughter your enemies in quick succession to begin your Killstreak,
  - As your killstreak begins, a pop-up will start tracking your kill count.
- Once the Killstreak begins, there’ll be a short period of time before your Killstreak begins to deplete.
- Continue your Killstreak with merciless pace:
  - Refresh your Killstreak timer with additional kills, direct damage, or on initial damage-over-time application.

Killstreaks are marked by five distinct tiers based off current streak’s total:

- Killstreak
- Carnage
- Devastation
- Bloodbath
- Massacre

When the timer bar fully depletes, the Killstreak is over.

- Experience and seasonal reputation are gained at the end of a Killstreak.
- The amount of Experience and seasonal reputation gained are based on the total number of monsters slain during the Killstreak, with a bonus based on the Killstreak Tier reached.
  - Note: seasonal reputation will not be present in the PTR.
- If you should perish while in a Killstreak, it will immediately end without rewards.

## Rain Crimson with Bloodied Items

Bloodied Items are a new Item Quality you can harness in combination with your Killstreaks to heighten the bloodshed.

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/3SLM6Z149COZ1769645246594.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/3SLM6Z149COZ1769645246594.png)

Bloodied Items have affixes which scale in power based off your current **Killstreak Tier**.

- Any item that can drop can potentially appear as **Bloodied**.
- This Item Quality tag is non-exclusive, and an item can be both Ancestral and Bloodied.

Bloodied Affixes come in three categories:

- **Rampage:**
  - Increase in power based off current **Killstreak Tier**
  - Applies to Armor
  - Ex: *Killstreak: 7%[+] Move Speed per Killstreak Tier in your Killstreak*
- **Feast:**
  - Apply Combat bonuses based off total kills in your current Killstreak
  - Applies to Weapons
  - Ex: *Every 40 Kills in your Killstreak, reset 1 random Cooldown*
  - These bonuses are constant and are not tied to Killstreak Tier
- **Hunger:**
  - Customizes reward offerings, which scale with Killstreak Tiers
  - Ex: *Elite kills will drop Runes more frequently*
  - Applies to Jewelry

As with other Affixes, Bloodied Affixes stack their effects.

## Unlock Slaughter with Bloodied Sigils

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/7BYTVC3FWWNQ1769645250756.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/7BYTVC3FWWNQ1769645250756.png)

Evoke the power of the Burning Hells and set a fearsome combatant on your trail. Bloodied Sigils unlock your ability to increase the heat and increase the ferocity of gameplay while rewarding you handsomely for this increased effort.

- Nightmare Dungeons, Infernal Hordes, and Lair Bosses each have Bloodied Sigils.
- These potent Sigils only become available once you reach Torment I.

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/NODDFC6FRPHD1769645246805.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/NODDFC6FRPHD1769645246805.png)

Bloodied Sigils

- Each Bloodied Sigil carries a Bloodied Affix, **Relentless Butcher**. The infamous purveyor of human charcuterie hunts you endlessly, hounding your every move. Should you manage to fell the Butcher, he will immediately return. He will never cease!
- This does not replace the existing chance to have Butcher spawns, there is the chance to fight both Butchers simultaneously.
- Expect the activity in the Bloodied Sigil to be around a Torment Tier higher in difficulty than your current Torment Tier.

Bloodied Sigils reward this significant challenge with guaranteed drops of Bloodied Items.

## How to Install the PTR

Here are the steps to join the PTR:

- Open the Blizzard Battle.net App and select *Diablo IV* from your *Games* list.
- In the selector above the Play button, there is a game version drop down menu.
  - Select the Public Test Realm option
- Click *Install* to Install the PTR client. This becomes a *Play* button when ready.
- Click Play to log in to the PTR, and select any of the available Test Servers.
- Create a new test character.
  - *Note: Create a Seasonal Character for any testing related to the Seasonal themes.*
- Enter the game and test the 2.6.0 PTR!

If you’re a PC Game Pass or Game Pass Ultimate user, you’ll be able to access the PTR through the same flow as other Battle.net users. Access the Battle.net client through the Xbox App, then continue to access the PTR through Battle.net.
