Rise, wanderer, and stand triumphant before your hoard of spoils.

An alluring glow descends across the land, increasing the clarity and temperament of all stalwart warriors. Scholars have unlocked new secrets in their ancient tomes, while coffers threaten to burst with gold unearthed from long forgotten crypts.

How will you seize your fortunes in this brief glimpse of splendor? Make haste to hone your blade across a weekend of adventure and plunder to become the deadliest force in all of Sanctuary.

#### Blessings Aplenty: Earn Increased Experience and Gold

From **September 1, 10 a.m.– September 5, 10 a.m. PDT**, all players will earn XP and Gold at a **25%** increased rate. The Mother blesses all of her children, as this bonus applies to both Seasonal and Eternal Realms, and all World Tiers. This bonus stacks, so make sure to pair them with Elixirs and the Urn of Experience to maximize your experience gains.

For those curious where to attribute this newfound boon, look for the new in-game icon beside your potion count that signifies the increased rate of earning Gold and XP. Use this boost to carve through ranks of the Battle Pass, progress through the Season Journey, and climb to higher Levels and World Tiers with burning swiftness.

Stoke the fire, gather round, and rally your most savvy of companions to slay demons with renewed fervor. The Blessed Mother’s cruelty is matched by her generosity, but not for long. Rise to new heights and earn more Gold for your bloodlust in the Mother’s Blessing Weekend!
