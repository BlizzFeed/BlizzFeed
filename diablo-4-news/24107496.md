# Tune in to the Spiritborn Reveal Livestream

- URL: https://news.blizzard.com/en-gb/article/24107496/tune-in-to-the-spiritborn-reveal-livestream
![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/0d/0DV7Z7LOH6ZA1720731090245.png)

> Join us on July 18 at 11 a.m. PDT to learn more about the Spiritborn, Diablo IV’s first new class.

---

Get an in-depth look at the Spiritborn, a class entirely new to the Diablo series, during a special livestream on **July 18 at 11 a.m. PDT**!

Join community director Adam Fletcher, game director Brent Gibson, associate narrative designer Eleni Rivera-Colon, art director Nick Chilano, and game designer Bjorn Mikkelson, as they reveal details about the Spiritborn’s story, art, design, gameplay, and more. There will also be a Q&A segment at the end of the stream for players to field their questions directly to the team.

Visit our official Diablo [Twitch](https://www.twitch.tv/diablo), [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/Diablo/featured) and [X](https://twitter.com/Diablo) channels to watch live! Following the Spiritborn Reveal Livestream’s conclusion, we’ll post another article with a video of the livestream so you can catch up on the details if you miss them.
