## Unleash Your Rage with the Killstreak System

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/6w/6W6LXSPFAC101772686474441.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/6w/6W6LXSPFAC101772686474441.png)

Indulge in bloodthirsty carnage to grow progressively stronger—maintain a fast, efficient slew of kills to increase your power and earn rewards.

The Killstreak system changes the way you play across every activity in Diablo IV. Slaughter your enemies in quick succession to begin your Killstreak. As your killstreak begins, a pop-up will start tracking your kill count.

After it begins, you have a short window before your Killstreak starts to decay. Continue your Killstreak with merciless pace, refresh your Killstreak timer with additional kills, direct damage, or on initial damage-over-time application.

Killstreaks are marked by five distinct tiers based off current streak’s total:

- Killstreak
- Carnage
- Devastation
- Bloodbath
- Massacre

When the timer bar fully depletes, the Killstreak is over. Experience and seasonal reputation are gained at the end of a Killstreak, **a pop-up will indicate the amount of seasonal reputation and experience gained**. These are based on the total number of monsters slain during the Killstreak, with a bonus based on the Killstreak Tier reached.

Stay alive if you hope to reap your rewards. The bigger the slaughter, the better the bonus.

## Rain Crimson with Bloodied Items

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_folder_media/zs/ZS9F4QAAE8SD1772686711042.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_folder_media/zs/ZS9F4QAAE8SD1772686711042.png)

A warrior consumed by bloodlust can become the embodiment of unbridled rage if they know how to direct and sustain their rampage.

**Based on PTR feedback, we made several adjustments to bring Bloodied Items more in line with the power of Chaos Armor from Season of Infernal Chaos and Sanctification bonuses.**

Bloodied Items are a new Item Quality you can harness in combination with your Killstreaks to heighten the bloodshed.

Bloodied Items have affixes which scale in power based off your current **Killstreak Tier**. Any item that can drop can potentially appear as **Bloodied**. This Item Quality tag is non-exclusive, and an item can be both Ancestral and Bloodied.

Bloodied Affixes come in three categories:

- **Rampage:**
  - Increase in power based off current **Killstreak Tier**
  - Applies to Armor
  - *Ex: Killstreak: 7%[+] Move Speed per Killstreak Tier in your Killstreak*
- **Feast:**
  - Apply Combat bonuses based off total kills in your current Killstreak
  - Applies to Weapons
  - *Ex: Every 40 Kills in your Killstreak, reset 1 random Cooldown*
  - These bonuses are constant and are not tied to Killstreak Tier
- **Hunger:**
  - Customizes reward offerings, which scale with Killstreak Tiers
  - *Ex: Elite kills will drop Runes more frequently*
  - Applies to Jewelry

## Unlock Slaughter with Bloodied Sigils

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/gallery/al/AL7WNIGWRN1S1772687819824.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/gallery/al/AL7WNIGWRN1S1772687819824.png)

Evoke the power of the Burning Hells and set a fearsome combatant on your trail. Bloodied Sigils unlock your ability to increase the heat and increase the ferocity of gameplay while rewarding you handsomely for this increased effort.

Nightmare Dungeons, Infernal Hordes, and Lair Bosses each have Bloodied Sigils. Note: these potent Sigils only become available once you reach Torment I. **When playing in Bloodied activities, enemy health bars will display the level difference between their current level and how difficult they are in the Bloodied content.**

#### Bloodied Sigils

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/gallery/0l/0LJBIMV43GDL1772687937072.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/gallery/0l/0LJBIMV43GDL1772687937072.png)

Each Bloodied Sigil carries a Bloodied Affix, **Relentless Butcher**. The infamous purveyor of human charcuterie hunts you endlessly, hounding your every move. Should you manage to fell the Butcher, he will immediately return. He will never cease!

This does not replace the existing chance for Butcher spawns. You may even face both Butchers at once. Expect the activity in the Bloodied Sigil to be around a Torment Tier higher in difficulty than your current Torment Tier.

Bloodied Sigils reward this significant challenge with guaranteed drops of Bloodied Items.

#### Bloodsoaked Sigils

For those who seek suffering and glory in equal measure, Bloodsoaked Sigils are the truest test of strength. Scaled to be roughly close in difficulty to Pit Tier 100, Bloodsoaked Sigils carry incredible challenge, with remarkable rewards.

Bloodsoaked Sigils reward you with guaranteed drops of multiple Bloodied Items.

- Affixes:
  - 51-65 All Stats

  - 8.3-10% Attack Speed
  - 40-50% Vulnerable Damage

## Earn new Items from Season Rank

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/ip/IPTZAL1M4PU81772689397347.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/ip/IPTZAL1M4PU81772689397347.png)

As you endlessly defend Sanctuary from demonkind, you’ll earn rewards from Season Rank.

Completing the Rank 5 Capstone Dungeon and unlocking Rank 6 will reward you Bloody Billy the Pet. Remember that you do not need to complete all objectives in Rank 5 to earn Bloody Billy.

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/gallery/1b/1B4C7N1C8PZK1772689466379.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/gallery/1b/1B4C7N1C8PZK1772689466379.png)

#### Season of Slaughter Season Blessings

Within Season Rank, you’ll have the ability to unlock Season Blessings and Smoldering Ashes. Smoldering Ashes can be earned as Chapter Rewards for completing objectives in the Season Rank. Once you’ve earned Ashes, they can be spent on these Seasonal Blessings:

- Urn of Butchery: Increase rate of Seasonal Reputation earned from all sources.
- Urn of Slaughter: Boosts the chances of finding additional Slaughterhouse Keys.
- Urn of Bloodied Keys: Boosts the chances of finding additional Bloodied Activity Keys.
- Urn of Meat: Boost the amount of Fresh Meat dropped from all sources.
- Urn of Glyphs: Grants a chance to earn an extra upgrade when improving glyphs.
