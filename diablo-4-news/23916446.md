Hail wanderer! We’ve returned with our second Developer Update Livestream. On June 6, [Diablo IV](https://diablo4.blizzard.com/en-us/#pre-purchase) will inject new gameplay elements into the Diablo universe, while simultaneously capturing the nightmarish aesthetic of its predecessors that originally enamored players.

For this livestream, we were joined by game director **Joe Shely**, lead systems designer **Meng Song**, and lead world designer **Art Peshkov**—some of our resident Diablo IV experts—who dug into [details about our upcoming Open Beta](https://news.blizzard.com/diablo4/23916442/) and Early Access weekends (including Beta rewards), Strongholds, and the game’s Armor system. We also held a live Q&A session where players could field questions directly to our developers.

#### Play the Open Beta, Lead the Pack

![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/n4/N43ZLDK6HAMM1677608077080.gif)

As mentioned in the livestream, a cache of **unique rewards can be earned as you play Diablo IV during the Open Beta and Early Access weekends**. Showcase your wanderer’s prowess at saving Sanctuary by collecting all three. Players who unlock these Open Beta rewards will receive them on June 6 once Diablo IV launches.

- *Initial Casualty* Title: earned by **reaching Kyovashad** with one character.
- *Early Voyager* Title: earned by **reaching Level 20** on one character.
- **Beta Wolf Pack Cosmetic Item:** earned by reaching Level 20 on one character.
