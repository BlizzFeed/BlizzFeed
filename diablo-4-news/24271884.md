Purchase the Diablo IV BlizzCon Bundle and unlock:

Visit the [Battle.net Shop](https://shop.battle.net/product/diablo-iv-blizzcon?pfm=fran-diablo-d4-news) to purchase these items as your own, until September 28, 2026.

### BlizzCon Ultimate Collection Bundle ($49.99 USD)

Hungry for more BlizzCon rewards? Check out the [BlizzCon Ultimate Collection](https://news.blizzard.com/article/24272113/) for more items fit for a wanderer of great renown. Alongside the items that come in the Diablo IV Digtal Bundle, you’ll also unlock:

This BlizzCon Ultimate Collection contains everything from the five individual digital bundles from each franchise.

The BlizzCon Ultimate Collection is included in the purchase of a **BlizzCon Pass**, so if you’re attending the show, you’re getting it all. Be sure to [link your BlizzCon Pass](https://blizzcon.com/blizzcon-pass-linking/) to your **Battle.net Account** to receive your items.
