## Diablo V: Coming Spring 2029

Sanctuary has fallen.

Diablo won.

Diablo V is officially in development and coming Spring 2029. For the first time, players will enter a Sanctuary where Diablo has triumphed and its heroes have been defeated. At the heart of Diablo V lies a defining question: what must you become when the world has already fallen?

Join us for **Diablo V: Forging the Next Era** panel on **September 12, 1:45 p.m. PT** for an early look at the world, classes and creative vision shaping the chapter of Diablo. You can also sign up for future updates at [Diablo5.Blizzard.com](https://diablo5.blizzard.com/).

## The Amazon Enters the Battle in 2027

The Amazon is coming to Diablo IV in the first half of 2027. She hails from the island of Skovos, revealed for the first time in Diablo IV in Lord of Hatred.

Her return preserves the disciplined, athletic, and highly trained warrior fantasy players remember while reimagining the class for Diablo IV. Across four gameplay archetypes—Champion, Skirmisher, Ranger, and Commander—the Amazon can wield the returning bow and javelin, which introduces an entirely new weapon type to Diablo IV.

Get a closer look at the Amazon in the **Diablo IV: What's Next** panel on **September 13, 11:00 a.m. PT**. Additional class pack details will be shared at a later date.

## Celebrate 30 Years with the Season of Hell’s Legacy

More than 130 million players worldwide have entered Sanctuary across the Diablo franchise. Now, three decades of darkness return in Season of Hell’s Legacy, a playable tribute to Diablo’s most iconic characters, foes, locations and loot.

In Season of Hell's Legacy, the lost soul of Deckard Cain returns to guide you through an epic journey to redeem the dark wanderers from the torment of the Prime Evils. Confront the Prime Evils Baal, Mephisto, and Diablo, reclaim splinters of their souls, and bear the burden of one of the Splinters to assume a portion of their power. But remember: no demonic gift is bestowed without a steep price.

#### Season of Hell's Legacy begins rolling out on September 15, 9:30 a.m. PT.

Read more of the legacy the Burning Hells has wrought in our Season Overview blog [here](https://news.blizzard.com/article/24295394/), and join us for the **Diablo IV: What's Next** panel on **September 13, 11:00 a.m. PT**.

## Diablo IV on Nintendo Switch™ 2

[![Video](https://markdown-videos-api.jorgenkh.no/youtube/W64QNl4VlLs?width=1280&height=720)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=W64QNl4VlLs)

Hell has arrived on Nintendo Switch™ 2. The Diablo IV: Age of Hatred Collection is coming to Nintendo Switch™ 2 on September 15. The Diablo IV: Age of Hatred Collection bundles the base game, Vessel of Hatred expansion, and Lord of Hatred expansion.

## More Diablo Panels at BlizzCon 2026

The 30th anniversary celebration continues throughout BlizzCon weekend with panel highlighting the voice talent behind some of Diablo’s iconic characters and the making of Diablo IV’s cinematics. Join us live at the Anaheim Convention Center or watch from home on the following channels (exceptions noted below):

- [YouTube.com/Diablo](https://www.youtube.com/diablo)
- [Twitch.tv/Diablo](https://www.twitch.tv/Diablo)

- **Diablo V: Forging the Future –**  Main Stage, 1:45 p.m. PT

  The future of Diablo begins here! Host Johanna Faries sits down with the Diablo V development team to share an early preview of the game's story, world-building approach, class design philosophy, and the vision that will define the next era for Diablo.

- **Voice Actors' Panel –**  Main Stage, 3:15 p.m. PT\*

  Hear the voices behind beloved Blizzard characters share stories from the booth, revisit memorable performances, and bring favorite moments to life on stage.
