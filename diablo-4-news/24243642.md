# Goblins and Gifts Galore in Goblin Greedings

- URL: https://news.blizzard.com/en-gb/article/24243642/goblins-and-gifts-galore-in-goblin-greedings
![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/7d/7DHK4EKXEBKK1765834957507.png)

> Goblin Greedings descends on Sanctuary as Treasure Goblin variants roam in increased numbers and bountiful spoils. Earn bonus Whisper Caches and claim free holiday cosmetics.

---

While the destitute denizens of Sanctuary scrape what they can for holiday celebrations, something else scuttles and scrapes amidst the shadows. Could there be even a moment of respite for these weary laden souls? Could the whispers of an abundance descending into Sanctuary be true? Will the fading embers of their hearths be lit with the Light that has returned to Estuar? Only you can stoke the embers of their dwindling hope.

### Crush Goblins, Hoard Rewards

From **December 18—January 6**, delight in a grim celebration of bountiful gifts.... and persnickety Treasure Goblins. Across all realms, beat back the tides of Treasure Goblins and reach different Tiers of gilded rewards.

Complete the March of the Goblins reputation board by personally slaying these foul gremlins. Unlocking new tiers within the March of the Goblins reputation board will improve the loot laden bags that drop upon slaying them.

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/ZRKCD8GQH3S71765834426778.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/ZRKCD8GQH3S71765834426778.png)

Upon each Tier unlock, visit the Goblin Square in Kyovashad to claim your reward caches. The final, repeatable, Tier rewards a Resplendent Spark on first completion!

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/MAZMB6BGRWKY1765834426447.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/MAZMB6BGRWKY1765834426447.png)

Claim all rewards by **January 6**, before they vanish back into an unknown hovel of Treasure Goblin origin.

### Holiday Gifts of the Tree

While Goblin Greedings is live, returning Grim Favors will grant you a bonus Gift of the Tree cache—a Collection of Chaos—for your festive deeds done in service of the Tree. Open your selected Cache to reveal the Gift of the Tree within.

### Claim Free Gifts in the Shop

It appears a benevolent hand has provided gifts to you, wanderer. Could the Light be returning to Sanctuary with new whispers speaking of Paladins among our lands?

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/0RBT1QB89ML71765834426584.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/0RBT1QB89ML71765834426584.png)

Claim free Holiday Gifts in the in-game Shop:

- **December 18: Crook of First Snow (Mount Trophy)**
- **December 19: Fallow-ward (Mount Trophy)**
- **December 20: Tackle of the First Snows (Mount Armor)**

Claim all rewards by January 6 before they disappear in a puff of smoke amongst Tejal’s wares.

May your Goblin slaying tools stay sharp, and ready.\
