# Carve through Sins of the Horadrim with Seasonal Splendor

> Bolster your efforts to claim Horadric Phials with the Seasonal Splendor limited-time boost.

---

A most auspicious glow descends across Sanctuary, bringing a rare boon to the desolate wastes of this land. From **August 26—September 2**, reap Horadric Phials at an improved rate across all sources for a limited-time.

Slay through Dungeons to earn increased Phials, use this improved source to enhance your Horadric Spellcraft and accelerate your seasonal progress.

#### Exchange a Grand Amount of Horadric Knowledge at the Horadric Lapidary

Introduced in Patch 2.3.3., the Horadric Lapidary vastly increased the amount of Horadric Knowledge gained from converting Horadric Phials by a staggering amount. For example, Substantial Horadric Knowledge now grants 4,000 Horadric Knowledge in exchange for the unchanged cost of 2,000 Horadric Phials.

This new exchange rate is not tied to Seasonal Splendor and lasts until the end of Season 9. Harness this vastly improved source of Horadric Knowledge in the Reputation tab of the Horadric Lapidary.

Gather your closest allies, slaughter demons, and carve through the final ranks of the Seasonal reputation board in Seasonal Splendor.
