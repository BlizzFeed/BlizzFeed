> Faster than a Zerg rush, StarCraft storms into Sanctuary for a limited time.

---

Starting on **September 26**, at 12:00 p.m. PDT, purchase iconic StarCraft-themed cosmetics emblematic of their legendary factions. Suit up as Jim Raynor with the Barbarian, channel Kerrigan’s fearsome style with the Spiritborn, outfit your Druid into Tychus Findlay, and more. Earn bold emblems from Twitch Drops starting September 26—October 10, and keep an eye out for free gifts available in the in-game shop.

## Observe Twitch Drops for Worthy Emblems

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/ZVRQ8GL9Q1YF1758583984531.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/ZVRQ8GL9Q1YF1758583984531.png)

Much like the best Protoss Observers, plenty can be learned from studying those around you. From **September 26—October 10**, watch eligible content in the Diablo IV category while this Twitch Drop is active to earn emblems worthy of the Royal Guard.

Watch for 2 Hours to earn the Ancient of the Void Emblem, 4 hours to earn The Teeming Brood Emblem, and 6 hours to earn the Forward unto Dominion emblem.

For frequently asked questions on Twitch Drops, see our blog [here](https://news.blizzard.com/article/24199285/).
