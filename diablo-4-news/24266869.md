[2.6.0 Build #70982 (All Platforms)—March 11, 2026](#the-260-patch-notes-at-a-glance)

**Note:** Additional changes made since the PTR’s conclusion are highlighted in **blue**.

#### The 2.6.0 Patch Notes at a glance:

- On the seasonal realm, summon more powerful versions of Nightmare Dungeons, Lair Bosses, and Infernal Hordes with Bloodstained Sigils. To dial up the heat even further, use Bloodsoaked sigils for an incredibly powerful test.
- Several new uniques that span many classes are here for your demon-slaying needs. These all drop from the new Butcher Lair Boss.
- Boss Stagger mechanics have been upgraded. Bosses can be staggered twice as quickly before, but now have a cooldown for 30 seconds where it is five times harder to stagger them.
- New enemies, layouts have been added to the Tower and Bosses should hit much harder. The amount of enemies needed to complete a floor has been reduced.

# Updates from PTR

## Game Updates

- Killstreak multipliers are now highlighted in the UI.
- Killstreaks no longer reset when going between floors in dungeons.
- Bloodstained now grant better rewards, including guaranteed Bloodied and Ancestral items when defeating Bloodsoaked bosses.
- The following Massacre Affixes have been tuned.
  - Attack Speed per Killstreak Tier increased from 6% to 8%.
  - Cooldown Reduction per Killstreak Tier increased from 3% to 6%.
  - Critical Strike Chance per Killstreak Tier increased from 5% to 8%.
  - Life on Hit per Killstreak Tier changed to 100.
  - Maximum Life per Killstreak Tier changed to 3% Maximum Life.
  - Movement Speed per Killstreak Tier increased from 7% to 10%.
  - Resource Cost Reduction per Killstreak Tier increased from 6% to 10%.
- All Classes can now encounter a Bloodied Affix that increases their Primary Core Stat by 3% per Killstreak Tier. This affix appears on Bloodied Items of at least 750 Item power.
- Necromancer Balance Updates
  - Fueled by Death Passive
    - Duration increased from 6 to 8 seconds.
  - Bloodbath Legendary Paragon Node
    - Previous: Deal 20%[x] increased Overpower damage. After Overpowering, deal 50%[x] increased Physical Damage for 5 seconds.
    - Now: Deal 20%[x] increased Overpower damage. After Overpowering, deal 50%[x] increased Damage for 5 seconds.
- The Incense of Vitality has been removed.
- The Mythic Prankster Nightmare Dungeon rewards have been reduced from 5 Mythic Uniques to 1.

## Balance Updates

### All Classes

#### The Butcher's Cleaver

- Previous - Lucky Hit: Critically Striking an enemy has up to a 100% chance to Fear and Slow them by 61-90% for 4 seconds.
- Now - Your attacks Critically Strike Injured Enemies. Killing Elites Fears and Slows nearby enemies by 65-95% for 2 seconds.

#### Endurant Faith

- Damage threshold for activation reduced from 40% to 30% of Maximum Life.
- Damage over time duration increased from 3 to 4 seconds.

#### Tassets of the Dawning Sky

- Resistance increased from 20-40% to 80-160%.

#### X'Fal's Corroded Signet

- Previous: Lucky Hit: Your damage over time effects have up to a 75% chance to erupt, dealing 1350-1950 damage of the same type to surrounding enemies.
- Now: Each Damage over Time type you apply explodes every second, dealing 3000-4000 damage over 4 seconds to surrounding enemies.

#### Soulbrand

- Previous: Your Healing Potion no longer heals and instead grants a Barrier for 200% of its healing for 4 seconds. You gain 20-40% Damage Reduction while you have a Barrier and may drink your Healing Potion while at full Life.
- Now: Your Healing Potions grant a Barrier for 100% of your Maximum Life for 1 second. You gain 20-40% Damage Reduction while you have a Barrier and you may drink your Healing Potion while at full Life.

#### Lorcan's Talisman

- Previous: Your Skills gain [0.10 - 0.40]%[+] Critical Strike Chance per point of Primary Resource you have when cast, up to [10 - 40]%[+]. Each point of Primary Resource above 100 grants Skills 0.2%[x] Critical Strike Damage instead, up to 500 Resource.
- Now: Critical Strikes deal 50-100%[x] increased damage but your Critical Strike Chance is reduced by 50[+]%.

#### Crown of Lucion

- Duration increased from 4 to 6 seconds.
- Maximum stack count increased from 5 to 7.

#### Godslayer Crown

- Previous: When you Stun, Freeze, or Immobilize an Elite, you pull all Nearby enemies and deal 30-60% increased damage to them for 3 seconds. Can only happen once every 12 seconds. Against Bosses, this affect triggers when dealing damage instead.
- Now: When you attempt to Stun, Freeze, or Immobilize an enemy you mark them and all Nearby enemies, pulling them in and dealing 20-40% increased damage to them.

#### Andariel's Visage

- Previous: Lucky Hit: Up to a 20% chance to trigger a poison nova dealing (4000% weapon damage) damage over 5 seconds to enemies in the area.
- Now: Lucky Hit: Up to a 20% chance to trigger a poison nova dealing 40% of the damage you dealt over 5 seconds to enemies in the area.

### The Tower

- New monster types have been added to the Tower.
- New tilesets and art assets have been added for the Tower.
- Your best Tower rank now always shows in the top right of the leaderboard menu.
- Tower bosses now deal significantly more damage.
- The number of enemies defeated needed to complete the Tower has been reduced.
- A new ‘Jump to Me’ button has been added so you can find your entry more easily.
- An “End of run” screen appears to summarize key stats around your run.
- New Tower bosses have been added.
- Treasure Goblins killed in the Tower now drop better rewards.

### Quality of Life Improvements

- How fast you complete a traversal now scales with movement speed, including mount speed for traversals that can be jumped over while mounted.
- Using a traversal now resets your mount cooldown.
- Potion cooldown is no longer different when in Fields of Hatred.
- Masterworked Greater Affixes now have their own icon to make them stand out.
- Added a new Hide Weapon option in the Wardrobe, allowing you to conceal sheathed weapons for a cleaner, more personalized appearance.
- All non-objective doors in Nightmare Dungeons now automatically open.
- Channeling times for repeatable actions, such as summoning the Town Portal, have been reduced and now max out at .5 seconds.
  - Additionally, the threshold for damage taken before stopping a channelled action has been increased from 5% life to 20% life.

### Combat

- The Stagger mechanic for bosses has been updated.
  - Bosses now build Stagger approximately twice as fast as before.
  - Stagger buildup no longer decays over time. Once you make progress toward a Stagger, it will not drain.
  - After a boss is Staggered, they gain increased Stagger Resistance for 20 seconds.
  - While this resistance is active, the boss is 5 times harder to Stagger.
  - Each additional party member increases the Stagger threshold by 50%.
- The Legendary Monster affix now only appears in Expert Difficulty and higher.
- The Unique Monster affix now only appears in Penitent Difficulty and higher.
- The number of bosses in the Fell Council encounter in Infernal Hordes has been reduced to one in Pre-torment difficulties.
- A new monster affix has been added.  The Sapper Affix causes affected enemies to drain the your resource.
- The following monster affixes have been removed from the Pit.
  - Shielded
  - Unstoppable
  - Teleporting

> **Developer’s Note:** *We've heard feedback that players may opt to restart runs if they encounter enemies with these affixes. We are opting to remove them from these activities.*

- Visual effects from Fire Enchanted and Fire Orb monster affixes have been updated to bring great attention to them.
- Minion monsters are now visually distinct from other monsters.

### Miscellaneous

- Updated DLSS Super Resolution presets, taking advantage of DLSS 4.5 features when applicable. This improves image quality when using Ultra Performance, Performance, and DLAA quality modes.
- Obducite rewards in Horadric Strongrooms have been reduced significantly.
- The cap for Obducite has been increased from 999,999 to 99,999,999.
- Experience rewards have been reduced for defeating World and Lair Bosses.
