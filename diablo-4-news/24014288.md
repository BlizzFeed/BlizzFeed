# Catch Up on Our Latest Campfire Chat

![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/7x/7X6BCQRZZSKR1696545042016.png)

> Today, the development team sat down to preview upcoming updates around Classes, Resistances, damage balance, items, and more coming with Patches 1.2.0 and 1.2.1.

---

On October 10, we held our Campfire Chat centered on updates coming with Patches 1.2.0 and 1.2.1. Associate game designer Charles Dunn, lead balance designer Adam Jackson, and associate director of community Adam Fletcher introduced the design philosophy behind Class and damage balance changes, resistance and item updates, new Unique items, and more. The team also answered questions live from the community. If you’d like to join us by the campfire to experience the chat firsthand, see the video below.

[![Video](https://markdown-videos-api.jorgenkh.no/youtube/GNDN2Ql_x0E?width=1280&height=720)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GNDN2Ql_x0E)

To read the full Patch 1.2.0 notes, which go live on October 17, visit [this article](https://news.blizzard.com/diablo4/23964909/). Later this month, we’ll be publishing the full patch notes for 1.2.1 in that same article, giving you a closer look at the changes to come.
