Sanctuary is a volatile place—danger lurks at every turn and the reappearance of Mephisto’s hatred has ushered in a new era of hardships for those who call these lands home. Learn how Belial, Lord of Lies returned to Sanctuary in a new short story. Use its knowledge to prepare for your fight against him on April 29 in [Season 8: Belial’s Return](https://news.blizzard.com/article/24189530/).

### “All Who Lie” By Matthew J. Kirby

Under the cover of night, a father, a mother, a priest, a broker, and a Sorceress gather to raise a child from death. But is it the soul of the child they are summoning . . . or something far more foul? In this tale about the lies we tell the world—and the ones we tell ourselves—no one can be trusted.
