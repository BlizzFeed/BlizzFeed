> Catch up on our latest Campfire Chat, where we discussed the Public Test Realm for the 2.2.0 PTR.

---

On **March 7**, we held a Campfire Chat that talked about the Public Test Realm (PTR) for the 2.2.0 Update, which runs from **March 11—March 18**, for players with a PC Battle.net account.

Members of the development team sat down to give a preview of the upcoming 2.2.0 PTR, which features the upcoming Lair Boss fights, new powerful abilities to steal from these fearsome foes, and more. We displayed new systems coming to the Seasonal realm including these new Boss Powers and Apparition Incursions. We also detailed upcoming changes to the Midseason updates, Class balance changes, and new Unique Items.

For details on the 2.2.0 PTR and all that will be included, you can read our blog and the Patch Notes  [here](https://news.blizzard.com/article/24179278). If you’d like to watch the full Campfire Chat to catch up on all the details, see the video below.
