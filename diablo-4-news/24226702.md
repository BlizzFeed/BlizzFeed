# Rally Against Hell in Season of Infernal Chaos

- URL: https://news.blizzard.com/en-gb/article/24226702/rally-against-hell-in-season-of-infernal-chaos
![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/dk/DK7IBK1T14081757628524756.png)

> Earn powerful Chaos Armor, wield fearsome Chaos Perks, and fight in a revamped Infernal Hordes in Season of Infernal Chaos.

---

On **September 23**, the veil shifts between Sanctuary and the Burning Hells in Season of Infernal Chaos. Hunt down powerful Chaos Armor and unlock deadly Chaos Perks to wield incredible power and build-defining possibilities. Mephisto’s presence weakens the boundary between worlds, allowing the Fell Council to escape Hell and spread destructive Chaos Rifts across Sanctuary. Delve into the Seasonal Questline to discover the Fell Council’s plot to sow destruction across the land.

This season brings permanent updates to Eternal and Seasonal Realms in the Infernal Hordes with Chaos Waves, and Bartuc, Lord of Chaos, an all-new boss fight to master.

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/FRZJSSBA35UI1757608925680.jpg)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/FRZJSSBA35UI1757608925680.jpg)

### Season of Infernal Chaos At a Glance

Earn Seasonal progression, Viz-Jaq'taar Veneration, by sealing Chaos Rifts in Helltide and Nightmare Dungeons. Earn Magic, Rare, and Legendary Chaos Perks by progressing through the Seasonal Reputation board. Earn Unique Chaos Perks by defeating Bartuc in Torment Tiers. Upgrade Chaos Perks by earning Infernal Warp through sealing Chaos Rifts and defeating Chaos Monsters.

Chaos Armor can be earned from many sources across the game, while Bartuc is an excellent source of Chaos Armor in Torment Tiers with a guaranteed drop chance in Torment III and IV.

## Infernal Hordes Reborn

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/C8CIPCOP163H1757628502263.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/C8CIPCOP163H1757628502263.png)

Bartuc’s return to Sanctuary has stoked the flames of the Infernal Hordes and the fires of the Burning Hells are hotter than they’ve even been before. The Infernal Hordes is shaking up in several key ways: Chaos Waves, the Fell Council, Bartuc, and more.

### Chaos Waves

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/JTC30Y3O5ITI1757628509512.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/JTC30Y3O5ITI1757628509512.png)

Among the Infernal Offerings within the Infernal Hordes, Chaos Waves enter to throw your visit to hell into a bloody frenzy. Chaos Offerings will occasionally appear between waves, transforming a standard wave into a volatile crucible of randomized, rule-breaking encounters. These high-intensity combat trials aren’t for the faint of spirit, with each type imbuing havoc and intense challenge. These waves will have significantly increased difficulty, but reward far more Aether should you be able to survive.

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/y6/Y6YRCG87AH011757633213848.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/y6/Y6YRCG87AH011757633213848.png)

Chaos Waves will have many different combinations to discover and master. Dodge deadly projectiles from Chaos Rifts, slay a rampaging Hellwyrm, or hunt down erratic Aether Goblins. Chaos awaits!

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/3JYF692EOSXK1757628509169.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/3JYF692EOSXK1757628509169.png)

### Confront Bartuc, Lord of Chaos

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/092MXU8CPVDU1757628488924.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/092MXU8CPVDU1757628488924.png)

At the end of your Infernal Hordes run, you’ll be met with a choice. Choose to fight against the tested Fell Council, or up the ante and throw down against the insidious Bartuc. Read on for more details on the Lord of Chaos’ return.

### Dialing up the Heat in the Infernal Hordes

We’ve changed the moment-to-moment combat within the Infernal Hordes in a few different ways. First, we've done an overall re-work of the Infernal Offerings revealed between each Wave. Our goal is to try and make all offerings feel valid as choices for you as you start to stack them to focus on your run. This includes additional balancing to ensure that Infernal Offerings are more balanced across the board compared to the Hellborne wave, which has been seen as essential for the best possible Infernal Hordes run.

Hell has unearthed a new monster family—Chaos Monsters—from deep within its horrid maw. Born of smoke and brimstone, some of these Chaos Monsters provide a mighty shrine-like buff when killed. Slay Chaos Bringers to earn a temporary buff, which can be increased in duration through the Urn of Sustain from Seasonal Blessings. For the advanced demon slayers, if you can manage to kill another Chaos Bringer before your first buff expires, they’ll stack.

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/4GM2PO7FOVC41757628496019.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/4GM2PO7FOVC41757628496019.png)

Additionally, the door to enter the Infernal Hordes now opens much more quickly than before. Usually, people are slightly more reserved to enter the Burning Hells, but not you, wanderer.

### The Fell Council

The Fell Council has had some promotions. When fighting them, one of their members will now be the designated Council Speaker and gain enhanced abilities.

### Slake your Bloodthirst in The Fleeting Hordes

We’re introducing a warming version of the Infernal Hordes to burn through earlier in your character journey. You can play this trimmed down version of the Infernal Hordes in pre-Torment Tiers.

Fleeting Hordes will be four waves in total, with limited Infernal Offerings to keep the packs of vicious monsters under relative control. Upon completing the final wave, you’ll fight two of the Fell Council members before you can spend your hard-earned Aether for rewards.

## Bartuc Beckons

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/LODW50K0JJTP1757628502227.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/LODW50K0JJTP1757628502227.png)

An infamous Vizjerei Warlord known for his acts of cruelty performed in the Mage Clan Wars, Bartuc’s perversion knows no bounds. He returns to Sanctuary with a new title, the Lord of Chaos.

To fight Bartuc, each member of your party will need to spend Aether for the chance to eliminate this cruel threat. The first member of your party who selects Bartuc decides for the rest of the party.

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/GBCYV4T1H3MJ1757628488318.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/GBCYV4T1H3MJ1757628488318.png)

Bartuc will harness Chaos portals to shoot projectiles at your location and deploys an arsenal of malevolence to claim your life. Master his tactics and use the teleport pads strategically to gain the upper hand.

Similar to other bosses starting with Season of Infernal Chaos, Bartuc’s invulnerability phase can only occur after 10 seconds of the fight has elapsed.

Upon defeat, Bartuc’s rewards will appear on the ground, so you do not have to spend additional Aether to earn these rewards.

## Harness Entropy with Chaos Armor

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/PVQCNAOX278S1757628505595.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/PVQCNAOX278S1757628505595.png)

The only certainty is that nothing is certain. No entity is free from Chaos’s grasp—human, Horadrim, Angel, or Demon. All dance in its path, so rather than succumb to its erratic forces, harness this untapped power for yourself.

A new Unique Armor type has entered Sanctuary. Chaos Armor is a more fearsome version of existing Unique items that come with a diabolical twist. Chaos Armor are existing Unique items which appear as more formidable counterparts fit for other item slots, leading to entirely new ways of dispensing carnage.

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/NQIB4EVZHN0H1757628489090.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/NQIB4EVZHN0H1757628489090.png)

Chaos Armor can appear in the following item slots:

- Helm
- Chest
- Gloves
- Pants
- Boots

Chaos Armor will only appear as an item fit for anything other than its original slot, and the color of their item names are distinct. For example, Flickerstep won’t have a Chaos Armor version in the Boots slot. The aspects on Chaos Armor are also more potent than their normal counterpart, always appear at Maximum power, and have at least one Greater Affix.

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/E8BQWYS0N8GK1757628504539.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/E8BQWYS0N8GK1757628504539.png)

Note: you won’t be able to equip the same Unique item between Chaos Armor and standard Unique.

For example, you could have Fists of Fate drop as a Helm instead of Gloves, allowing you to equip a different set of Unique Gloves to compound those affixes in a combination that was previously unavailable. Theorycrafting is the name of the game this season.

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/LTG8LHL2VK5X1757628504753.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/LTG8LHL2VK5X1757628504753.png)

To earn Chaos Armor, defeat Bartuc in Torment III and IV for a guaranteed drop. Defeating Bartuc in Torment I and II also has a smaller chance to drop Chaos Armor as well.

## Seal Chaos Rifts

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/4ZPWJV73AF1Q1757628508351.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/4ZPWJV73AF1Q1757628508351.png)

Strange portals of crackling energy have appeared across Sanctuary, unleashing the Burning Hells’ legions through their wicked rifts. Chaos Rifts materialize to terrorize the denizens with untold fury.

Chaos Rifts appear within Helltide and Nightmare Dungeons. Put down the demons pouring from them and seal them away for seasonal reputation and **Infernal Warp**, which is used to level up your Chaos Perks. They’re also a great source of chaotic loot, granting additional experience, gems, and Chaos Armor in Torment difficulties.

In extremely rare cases, when playing in Torment difficulties, you may even come across a Chaos Rift of Mythical proportions...

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/Z43LTKSS1NXD1757628507657.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/Z43LTKSS1NXD1757628507657.png)

#### Chaos Rift Nightmare Dungeons

Chaos has seeped into the fabric of Sanctuary’s Dungeons, delivering bloodthirsty Chaos Monsters through these grim halls of carnage. These Nightmare Dungeons are rife with Chaos Rifts, which are great sources of fleeting Hordes Compasses. Since the PTR, these Chaos Rifts now provide similar rewards to their overworld Helltide counterparts. Additionally, the stacking getting hit by an attack from a Chaos Portal no longer increases your overall damage taken.

## Wield Devastating Chaos Perks

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/IP4D4SF6XV841757628492789.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/IP4D4SF6XV841757628492789.png)

Something incredible has descended upon Sanctuary, imbuing your abilities with devastating effect. Chaotic magic distorts all that it touches, even you! Chaos is of course, chaotic, and these Perks often come at a price for the power they provide.

Chaos Perks change how your skills function, harness your finest buildcraft to create devastating combinations. There are 4 different rarities of Perks: Magic, Rare, Legendary, and Unique. Magic, Rare, and Legendary Chaos Perks twist and empower your skills, while Unique Perks dramatically reshape your entire build.

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/8W2J9FCESWFS1757628506513.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/8W2J9FCESWFS1757628506513.png)

You can equip three non-Unique perks at once, and one Unique Perk. In addition to the Perks every class can use, each class has their own set of exclusive perks. Overall, each class has access to 16 perks, four of each rarity, and four class-specific perks.

Progress through the Seasonal Reputation board to unlock Chaos Perks, and defeat Bartuc within the Infernal Hordes in Torment I or higher for a chance to unlock Unique Perks. Upgrade Chaos Perks by collecting **Infernal Warp**, earned by defeating Chaos Family monsters.

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/LNH2U50AG1ZI1757628507207.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/LNH2U50AG1ZI1757628507207.png)

The following are class agnostic Chaos Perks:

### Magic

#### Invigorating Attacks

Every 5 seconds, your next Basic Skill generates an additional 50-100% of your Maximum Primary Resource, but your Basic Skills deal 30% reduced damage.

#### Accelerating Chaos

Lucky Hit: Damaging an enemy has up to a 10% chance to trigger a Chaotic Burst on the enemy, dealing 200-400% damage. Your Chaotic Bursts that hit an enemy reduce a random active Cooldown by 0.5 seconds.

#### Unstable Power

Chaotic magics erupt from your Basic Skills, making them guaranteed to Critically Strike and Overpower for 50-100% increased damage, but the power is unstable, giving them a 3 second cooldown.

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/I76J3YDDG8671757628495747.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/I76J3YDDG8671757628495747.png)

### Rare

#### Erupting Chaos

Chaotic Bursts randomly erupt around you, dealing 200-400% damage of your highest damage type. Your Chaotic Bursts also deal 100% of their initial damage over 4 seconds.

#### Power Siphon

Your Core Skills deal 50-100% increased damage, but also reduce your Maximum Current Resource by 20 for 6 seconds. This reduction stacks but does not refresh.

#### Alternating Alterations

Casting a Corse Skill empowers your next non-Channeled Cast of a different Core Skill, refunding 50% of its Resource cost and dealing 10-30% increased damage.

### Legendary

#### Marred Guard

You can no longer be healed above 50% Life. You gain 5-10% Maximum Resistance to All Elements, 10-20%[x] ~~20-40%[x]~~ Armor, and 50-100% increased Barrier and Fortify Generation.

Casting a Skill with a Cooldown will randomly set its Cooldown between 2 seconds and its base cooldown. You no longer benefit from Cooldown Reduction. Using a Cooldown unleashes a Chaotic Burst, dealing 300-500% damage of your highest damage type.

#### Crazy Brew

Your Healing Potion is infused with the power of Chaos, granting you 100% Resource Cost Reduction, 40-80%[+] increased Attack Speed and 40-80[+] increased Movement Speed for 5 ~~4~~ seconds. Your Healing Potion has a 15 ~~10~~ second Cooldown and you can drink it at full Life. Cooldown is now affects by Cooldown Reduction.

Killing an enemy generates 2 stacks of Chaos and dealing direct damage to a boss generates 5 stacks of Chaos. At 100 stacks, unleash a salvo of 9 Chaotic Bursts, dealing 300-500% of your highest damage type.

### Lair Boss Invulnerability Changes

Based off resounding community feedback, we are changing the way invulnerability phases work across Lair Bosses. When fighting Lair Bosses, their invulnerability phase cannot be triggered until after 10 seconds of the fight has elapsed. Invulnerability phases can now effectively bypassed if the player exceeds certain damage thresholds.

At 2/3 and 1/3 Life the boss gets a shield, which lasts for five seconds. At 2/3 of remaining Life, the shield’s value is 1/3 the boss’ max life. When the boss is at 1/3 Life, the shield’s value is 2/3 the boss’ max Life. If they player breaks the shield with those five seconds it’s active, the boss does not become invulnerable.
