### Druid

> **Developer’s Note:** *The primary goal for Druid in Season 16 is to address tag stacking. We want to reduce the necessity for Druids to stack Skill Tags, so that more Skill Variants can easily benefit from four legendary boards. In addition, we want to carve a clear division between Resolve and Fortify. Instead of both Earth and Bear skills getting access to both of these mechanics, we have separated the two; Earth gets Fortify, and Bear gets Resolve. Finally, we wanted to even-out the importance of Overpower and Vulnerable, so we have reduced the reliance on using the Hero of the Storm Lightning Storm variant as a means to apply Vulnerable, and have increased the benefit of using Overpower, and Druid's access to it.*

#### Skills

- Removed the ability for skills to inherit the Nature Magic or Shapeshifting Tag. These tags will become tied to the base skill.
  - For example, Lightning Claw only grants the Storm tag, not the Nature Magic Tag.

  - Additionally, this ability spawns Tectonic Spikes more frequently, and this frequency scales with attack speed.
