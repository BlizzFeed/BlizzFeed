# The Divine Bovine Community Challenge

- URL: https://news.blizzard.com/en-gb/article/24295385/the-divine-bovine-community-challenge
![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/js/JSOLXBCOVL0G1788128185054.png)

> Gather your herd and band together to take down Moophisto. End his Mad Cow rage to unlock a reward for all who oppose his reign of udder chaos.

---

From now until **September 14**, the global World Boss has been taken over by Moophisto—a hulking brute of cattle carnage. If players can defeat Moophisto 300,000 times to complete **The Divine Bovine Community Challenge**, a cow-veted reward will unlock for all players in Season of Hell’s Legacy—the **Moomento Mori Mount Trophy**.

Every player who participates in any Moophisto kill contributes 1 point toward his “cownter”. Contribute more points by killing him more than once. Once Moophisto's death “cownter” hits 300,000 defeats, this steer will finally be put to pasture.

Form a herd with your fellow Wanderers and strike down this mad cow. Each time he is sent back to the grass, it will eat away at his tyranny. Skim him to 1%, 2%, until you can remove him whole.

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/iz/IZIE6H3K0ET21788128085757.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/iz/IZIE6H3K0ET21788128085757.png)

The reasons surrounding Moophisto’s unannounced arrival are shrouded in “moostery”. Whispers talk of a cult with bovine inclinations who summoned him to make Sanctuary his unending pasture to violently graze upon.
