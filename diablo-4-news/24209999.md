# Anniversary Celebrations Erupt in Sanctuary

- URL: https://news.blizzard.com/en-gb/article/24209999/anniversary-celebrations-erupt-in-sanctuary
![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/l1/L1HO3PNDG42I1748558355584.png)

> Celebrate the 2nd anniversary of the Blessed Mother’s return by unlocking gifts in a new Event Reliquary.

---

Rise, wanderer. It’s time to celebrate.

This marks the second year since you rose against the unflinching tides of evil to thwart Lilith’s blessed legions. From **June 3—June 17, 10 a.m. PDT** gather round the campfire, collect your most trusted demon slaying companions, and toast these celebrations with a shower of demon blood.

*“Hell is coming,” went the refrain, yet the dawn still breaks on the horizon... Could it be the Light? Or the final fire at last?*

---

## Earn Rewards in the Anniversary Event Reliquary

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/2HI9E4R9DJ721748558112530.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/2HI9E4R9DJ721748558112530.png)

To celebrate this occasion, gilded rewards present themselves in a new Event Reliquary. While the anniversary celebrations are live, trade in your heaps of gold for these tantalizing rewards.

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/M6JQI7EOJFMX1748558112730.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/M6JQI7EOJFMX1748558112730.png)

The following items can be traded for gold in the 2nd Anniversary Event Reliquary:

- Sunflame Lantern Mount Trophy
- Burning Dawn Banner Mount Trophy
- Sunraiser Sword Cosmetic
- Sunflame Sword Two-Handed Sword Cosmetic
- Burning Barbs Polearm Cosmetic
- Burning Dawn Bow Cosmetic

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/5CZVVZRKP5IJ1748558112728.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/5CZVVZRKP5IJ1748558112728.png)

Until **June 17**, collect all items in the 2nd Anniversary Event Reliquary to earn **The Burning Dawn Mount Armor**.

## Mother’s Blessing Returns

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/H3Y7E39RGXR71748558114821.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/H3Y7E39RGXR71748558114821.png)

Stand triumphant before your hoard of spoils—Mother’s Blessing has returned under the wrathful gaze of the Tree of Whispers for anniversary celebrations.

During the anniversary celebrations in Sanctuary, whenever you return your Grim Favors, you’ll also receive an additional Gift of the Tree cache. Open the cache you receive to find the Gift of the Tree cache inside. The contents within are as unpredictable as their namesake—enjoy the Collection of Chaos items that lie within.

In addition to your bonus Caches, while Mother’s Blessing is active, all players will earn **35% experience and 50% Gold at a (multiplicative) increased rate**. Lilith blesses all her children, as this bonus applies to both Seasonal and Eternal Realms, and all World Tiers.

Use these powerful boons to make your final stand in Season 8: Belial’s Return, strike down the forces of this colossal con artist.

May you dance in the flames of celebration.\
