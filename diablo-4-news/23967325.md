> The livestream may have concluded, but you can catch up on all the fun with our video on demand. Watch it and see what is coming to Diablo Immortal and Diablo IV in July.

---

Multiple planes of Hell converged during the **Diablo Developer Update Livestream** on **July 6**. We kicked off the livestream with a deep dive provided by senior narrative designer **Ryan Quinn** and lead user experience designer **Chris Liao** on the new Blood Knight Class coming to Diablo Immortal on July 13. Our host, associate director of community **Adam Fletcher**, then introduced associate game director **Joseph Piepiora** and lead game producer **Timothy Ismay** who detailed what to expect from Season of the Malignant—the **first Diablo IV Season**—which begins on **July 20**. We also held a live Q&A session where players fielded questions directly to our developers.

To see this unfold, watch the video on demand below.
