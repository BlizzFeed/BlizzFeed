We’re dialing up the heat in the next Sanctuary Sitdown! In anticipation of the upcoming 2.5.0 update and PTR (Public Test Realm), we’re sitting down with Rhykker to talk all the details coming with this content update.

On **October 16**, at **11 a.m. PDT**, join Aislyn Hall, Owen Leach, Art Peshkov, where they’ll be talking all things Diablo IV. For this Sanctuary Sitdown, we’re shaking up the format. First, you’ll watch Rhykker discuss the upcoming update with the developers. Following this discussion, there’ll be a live Q&A segment in the [Sanctuary Discord](https://discord.gg/GRrZACUdKx) where we’ll answer your questions.

Save the date and join us on [Twitch](https://www.twitch.tv/rhykker), [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/@Rhykker), or the [Sanctuary Discord](https://discord.gg/GRrZACUdKx).

Tune in live to get their insights on anything under the dark sun of Sanctuary, we’ll see you soon.
