Diablo IV Inside the Game: A New Saga

Join members of the development team as they set the stage for Diablo IV’s story in the last Inside the Game video.

Over the course of our **Inside the Game** video series, we sat down with our resident Diablo IV experts—members of the development team—to walk you through elements of the game. Our next video in this series is A **New Saga**—an exploration of how the story between Lilith and Inarius is the darkest we’ve ever told in a Diablo game.

Join Diablo game director **Joe Shely**, associate writer **Eden Trujillo**, senior manager of lore **Sean Copeland**, and principal writer **Matt Burns** as they expound upon Sanctuary’s creation by Inarius and Lilith, your role as the wanderer, key characters you’ll interact with, and more.

[![Video](https://markdown-videos-api.jorgenkh.no/youtube/edba6zNBznY?width=1280&height=720)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=edba6zNBznY)

The story of Diablo IV takes place 50 years after the events of Diablo III, presenting a new plane of Hell for both veteran wanderers and newcomers alike to slay in. See it unfold firsthand by [pre-purchasing Diablo IV for your platform of choice](https://diablo4.blizzard.com/#pre-purchase).

Thank you for watching the Inside the Game video series thus far. There will be a pause on new videos until sometime after the launch of Diablo IV.

We’ll see you in Sanctuary on June 6!\
-The Diablo IV Team
