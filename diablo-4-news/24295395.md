# Diablo IV Patch Notes

> We will continually update this article with all patches the development team implements for Diablo IV.

---

The Diablo IV team has been monitoring your feedback. As we introduce patches to keep your experience in Sanctuary smooth, we will update the below list and denote whether the fixes are intended for PC, Xbox, PlayStation™, or all platforms.

---

[3.2.2 Build #73764 (All Platforms)—September 30, 2026](#season-of-hells-legacy)

## Season of Hell's Legacy

- Fixed an issue where the Seasonal questline could not be completed if the portal to the Worldstone Chamber was entered too quickly.
- Fixed an issue where going down the hive too quickly in Lester's Farm could block dungeon progression.
- Fixed an issue where completing the Chamber of Bone did not give rewards.
- Fixed an issue where the Edge Runeword allowed gaining two items at a time from the Purveyor of Curiosities.
- Fixed an issue where wearing a Lore Runeword Helm made the helm invisible.
- Fixed an issue where Nihlathak could teleport outside of the Boss Arena.
- Fixed an issue where the Henri's Perquisition Unique Charm could not be crafted in the Horadric Cube.

## Class Changes

### Druid

- Fixed an issue where Pulverize could indefinitely immobilize a Staggered boss.

### Sorcerer

- Fixed an issue where burning damage from the Incinerate Enchantment didn't trigger the Tal rune.

## Gear and Equipment

### Runes

- Fixed an issue where the damage reduction from the Sol Rune didn't activate when expected.

## Game Features

### The Tower

- Fixed an issue where the Artificer's Obelisk did not show rewards were available on the Map and Minimap Icon.

## UI/UX

- Fixed an issue where the button to check the leaderboard was still visible in Eternal when playing on Controller.
- Fixed an issue where pinned currency wasn't highlighted in the Stats & Materials tab.

## Miscellaneous

- Fixed an issue where mounted players could not interact with the Horadric Post.
- Various stability, performance, and visual improvements.
