# The Infernal Hordes Blazes with Compass to Carnage

> For a limited time, earn increased Infernal Compasses and endure more Chaos Waves in Compass to Carnage.

---

Deep within the chasms of the Burning Hells, the Warlord of Chaos awaits your presence… some would dare say he invites it.

From **November 4, 10 a.m.—November 11, 10 a.m. PST**, Sanctuary ignites with increased Infernal Compasses, increased Chaos Waves in the Infernal Hordes, and more. During Compass to Carnage, gaining an audience with the Fell Council has become easier than ever, as drop rates for Infernal Compasses are increased.

When fighting in the Infernal Hordes, Chaos seeps further into the marrow of this hellish dance. Expect increased Chaos Waves for every Infernal Hordes run, and you’ll earn loads of additional Aether when sealing Chaos Rifts.

Compass to Carnage is an excellent chance to climb the final ranks of the Viz-Jaq’taar Veneration reputation board!

Bartuc anxiously awaits your presence.\
—The Diablo IV Team
