> Catch up on our latest Developer Update Livestream, where we revealed Season 8: Belial’s return, new Boss Powers, and more.

---

On April 24, we held a Developer Update livestream that revealed what to expect in Season 8: Belial’s Return, which begins on **April 29** at **10 a.m. PDT**. Members of the Development Team sat down to discuss the return of the fearsome Belial, new Stolen Boss Powers, a [collaboration with Kentaro Miura’s dark fantasy manga Berserk](https://news.blizzard.com/article/24189531), and more.

The team dove into upcoming balance changes, quality-of-life changes, feedback learnings from our recent Public Test Realm, and more.

Join us now on our first Sanctuary Sitdown with Community Host, [Lurkin](https://www.youtube.com/@Lurkin17) and our very own Charles Dunn talking all things Diablo IV. They’ll be in the [Sanctuary Discord](https://discord.gg/GdJfkuMtVh) to answer your burning questions.

If you’d like to watch the full Developer Update livestream to catch up on all the details, **see the video below**.
