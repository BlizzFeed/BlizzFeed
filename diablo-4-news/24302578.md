> Preview significant changes to War Plans, the new Horadric Items system, and more in the upcoming 3.3.0 PTR.

---

The 3.3.0 Public Test Realm (PTR) for Diablo IV’s Season 16 will be available from October 27, 10:00 a.m. PDT to November 3, 10 a.m. PST, offering you the chance to test upcoming changes and features coming in the next season for the base game, shaking up how you play cross both Eternal and Seasonal Realms.

The purpose of the PTR is to test updates and features before the launch of the next season, where we’ll be focusing on significant additions to the War Plans system for both Eternal and Seasonal play, reworked Infernal Hordes, Pit, and Lair Boss activities, and powerful new Horadric Items that create fresh synergies between your equipped gear and Talisman system.

Using your reports, we’ll make adjustments before the next season goes live. This helps us to fine-tune balance changes, address eliminate bugs, and create the best experience possible while testing new systems. Thank you for playing through the PTR and sharing your thoughts with the team.

**We heard your feedback: some features from the Season of Hell's Legacy will be carrying forward into Season 16.** However, these will not be part of this PTR, as we want to focus on new features for testing. Next week, we'll have more information about which favorite features will continue into the new season.

Here is what the 3.3.0 PTR will preview ahead of next season.

## Customize Your War Plans

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/0H8H7SFVYO8Z1792675714307.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/0H8H7SFVYO8Z1792675714307.png)

**War Plans Tactics** is a new meta progression system that allows you to increase the likelihood of getting the content and rewards from War Plans that matter most to you.

Earn points from completing War Plans activities, then invest these points into nodes to increase the chance of preferred activities or rewards appearing. This allows you to tailor your War Plans more closely with both your goals and playstyle.

Spend up to 3 points into any War Plan Activity node to give it a better chance to appear in your offered War Plans over other types. Allocating at least 1 point into Helltides or Nightmare Dungeons also opens a choice to increase the chances for Greater Helltides or Escalation Dungeons.

Assign up to 3 points into any type of Reward node for better chances to pick your preferred rewards:

- Armor
- Weapons
- Jewelry
- Obducite
- Salvage
- Gems
- Gold
- Activity Keys
- Horadric Cube Materials
- Talisman Items

War Plans Tactics will be available for both Seasonal and Eternal content.

## Respec War Plans With Marks of Wisdom

When you turn in completed War Plans, you’ll be rewarded with a new currency, Marks of Wisdom. Instead of spending gold, you’ll use Marks of Wisdom to change nodes in both your War Plans Tactics tree and the individual Activity trees.

In the PTR, Marks of Wisdom are capped at 20.

## Power Your Season with Tyrael’s Boons

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/B5U9358M4IQZ1792675716293.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/B5U9358M4IQZ1792675716293.png)

Starting with Season 16, a new Season-themed War Plans tree called **Tyrael’s Boons** allows you to tailor your War Plans rewards and activities for that season. For example, you’ll have season-specific choices such as gaining more Seasonal reputation or currency, or more generalized perks such as reducing Obols costs at the Purveyor of Curiosities. All Seasonal players can access Tyrael's Boons; owning the Lord of Hatred expansion is not required.

You can earn up to 132 points, enough to choose up to 4 of 6 available selections within the four available paths.

In Season 16, the four paths are as follows:

- **Curious Addition:** Modifies the Purveyor of Curiosities to increase chances for a second item, and offer either new item types or improve the chances for higher-quality items. You can also select an options to make the Purveyor far more expensive, but increase the likelihood of getting Ancestrals.
- **Merchant of Mystery:** Adds a new merchant at Torment difficulty selling caches for Forgotten Souls. Invest points to increase the chances of Ancestral Caches. Select a path to buy caches with Runes or Gems or increase the amount and quality of Ancestral Caches on offer.
- **Scent of Gold:** Increase the amount of gold and experience you get. One path also increases the number of Horadric Cube materials and Gem Fragments you find, and the other improves monster drops of Gold and Gems. You can also increase your chances of finding a Legendary.
- **Trash for Treasure:** Transmuting costs at the Alchemist are reduced, and you can choose to invest in a better chance for Forgotten Souls. The first path gives more Obducite and Scrolls of Restoration, and the other gives monsters a chance to drop more Obducite and Salvage. At the end, salvaging Ancestral Items gives a chance to grant a Scroll of Restoration. It also opens a new Horadric Cube recipe, Greater Temper, which upgrades the Tempered Affix on an item to a Greater Affix for the cost of 30 Scrolls of Restoration and 1,000 Obducite.

You can unassign points in Tyrael's Boons for 5,000,000 gold per point.

The Tyrael’s Boons tree does not replace Seasonal Blessings. Season Blessings will become a static system with the same five Blessings on offer each time. Together, these combine a solid foundation of reliable perks to personalize your journey.

## Supercharge Charm Bonuses with Horadric Items

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/2WCCIB4WP8YI1792675719145.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/2WCCIB4WP8YI1792675719145.png)

The Death Cult of Ullo Sar introduces Horadric Items, Legendary items that create a direct synergy between your equipped items and your Talisman set bonuses. Horadric affixes modify a specific Talisman set, with varying effects depending on the item type.

Jewelry items with Horadric affixes increase your set bonus count by 1 for the specific Talisman set. Unlike Seal of the Diamond Mind, you only need to have 1 charm equipped to enjoy the 2-piece set bonus with a corresponding Horadric jewelry item. You’ll be able to activate a larger bonus with fewer charms, freeing up slots for multiple set bonuses or new charm combinations.

Armor and Weapon items with Horadric affixes add new effects that activate if you have at least a two-set bonus from a defined Talisman set. These can include perks such as skill rank bonuses, bonus damage, and improved defensive capabilities.

## How to Install the PTR

Here are the steps to join the PTR:

- Open the Blizzard Battle.net App and select *Diablo IV* from your *Games* list.
- In the selector above the Play button, there is a game version drop down menu.
  - Select the Public Test Realm option
- Click *Install* to Install the PTR client. This becomes a *Play* button when ready.
- Click Play to log in to the PTR, and select any of the available Test Servers.
- Create a new test character.
  - *Note: Create a Seasonal Character for any testing related to the Seasonal themes.*
- Enter the game and test the 3.3.0 PTR!

If you’re a PC Game Pass or Game Pass Ultimate user, you’ll be able to access the PTR through the same flow as other Battle Net users. Access the Battle.net client through the Xbox App, then continue to access the PTR through Battle.net.

## Druid

> **Developer’s Note:** *The primary goal for Druid in Season 16 is to address tag stacking. We want to reduce Druid's ability to stack tags and their reliance on them, so builds that pick only one tag can still benefit from 4 legendary boards. Additionally, we wanted to carve out more identity for Resolve and Fortify. Instead of having both Earth and Druid access both keywords, we can split them up and use the extra space for different upgrade designs.*

## Paladin

Aura Potency now increases the power of auras based on the value including Skill Ranks.

## Helltides

- Helltides now start on the hour for base game zones and on the 30 minute mark in expansion zones, so a Helltide is always at least active in non-expansion zones.
- Aberrant Cinders no longer reset between Helltides.

## Gameplay

- Effects that deal damage over time in a specific area, such as Upheaval's Trailblazing Variant, now have their duration increased by bonuses to Damage Over Time Duration, such as those granted by Shattered Vow.
- Mount speed has been adjusted to be faster baseline, but benefits less for spurring, such that the maximum speed is only slightly increased.

# UI & UX

- Updated the Poison Enchanted affix description to better communicate that its damage increases the longer a player remains in the affected area.

# Miscellaneous Bug Fixes

- Fixed an issue where players had higher base Block Damage Reduction than intended.
- Fixed an issue where certain Monster Affixes did not properly interact with Weaken under specific circumstances.
- Fixed an issue where the Suppressor Affix could stop slowing ranged projectile skills after an enemy disengaged from combat.
- Fixed an issue where some appearances previously unlocked during the Season of Death Awakening did not carry over.
- Fixed an issue where the Screen Reader would malfunction in the Friends list menu.
