# Earn Cosmetics for Diablo IV and World of Warcraft

- URL: https://news.blizzard.com/en-gb/article/24161248/earn-cosmetics-for-diablo-iv-and-world-of-warcraft
![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/mw/MWD9P8TKQYFP1734401085371.jpg)

> Receive coveted cosmetic rewards when you purchase both Diablo IV: Vessel of Hatred and World of Warcraft: The War Within—up to 30% off until January 7.

---

For a limited time, the lush Jungles of Nahantu and stony structures of Khaz Algar collide in the Vessel of Hatred and The War Within Expansion Collaboration! From **December 17, 10 a.m. PST–January 7, 2025, 10 a.m. PST**, when you purchase both Diablo IV: Vessel of Hatred and World of Warcraft: The War Within (or have purchased them in the past), we’ll sweeten the pot by giving you a bounty of must-have cosmetic rewards to add to your wanderer’s or champion’s collection\*.

For Diablo IV: Vessel of Hatred, you’ll receive:

- Invincible Mount Bundle (Invincible Aspect, Bard of Undying Loyalty, Domination Helm, Echo of Frostmourne)

And for World of Warcraft: The War Within, you’ll receive:

- Baa'lial Soulstone\*\*
- Enmity Bundle\*\* (Enmity Cloak, Enmity Hood)
- Livestock Lochaber Axe\*\*
- Nightmare Banner\*\*
- Twelve-String Guitar\*\*
- Wirt's Fightin' Leg\*\*
- Wirt's Haunted Leg\*\*
- Wirt's Last Leg\*\*
- Tome of Town Portal\*\*
- Amalgam of Rage mount\*\*

Cosmetic items from this promotion will be delivered by **January 13**.

*\*Must purchase Vessel of Hatred and The War Within and link the same [Battle.net](http://battle.net/) account for both games at any time prior to January 7, 2025. Purchases outside of Battle.net require at least one login to Diablo IV after the purchase to link the account. Must claim offer via the Battle.net desktop app prior to July 7, 2025. Bonus items already purchased or claimed via a previous promotion cannot be claimed again from this promotion.*

*\*\*Not available for WoW® Classic games.*

#### How to Claim Rewards

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/ws/WS4MZZSGGVQY1734401039215.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/ws/WS4MZZSGGVQY1734401039215.png)

First, you must ensure that your Battle.net account is linked to any Xbox, PlayStation, or Steam accounts that you wish to purchase these expansions for. Please make sure to login to Vessel of Hatred before the end of the promotion.

Once the rewards have been distributed (after January 13, 2025, rewards expire July 7, 2025), log in to the [My Gifts](https://account.blizzard.com/gifts/) page on [Battle.net](http://battle.net/) to [claim your rewards](https://support.blizzard.com/article/000370165)). You can also use the Gifts button in the Battle.net Desktop App.

#### Two Distinct Universes, Two Great Deals

The stars have aligned! During the collaboration’s run, all editions of [Vessel of Hatred](https://diablo4.blizzard.com/#purchase) for all supported platforms will be 25% off, and [The War Within](https://shop.battle.net/product/world-of-warcraft-the-war-within) for all supported platforms will be 30% off. Begin your quest to prevent Mephisto’s return to Sanctuary and start your defense of Azeroth from the shadows below before this deal disappears.
