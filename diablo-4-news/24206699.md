> Catch up on our latest Campfire Chat, where we revealed details about the upcoming 2.3.0 PTR.

---

On May 22, we held a Campfire Chat that showed what to expect from the 2.3.0 PTR, which begins on **May 27, 10 a.m. PDT**. Members of the Development Team sat down to discuss the features arriving with Season 9: Sins of Horadrim.

The team dove into Sins of the Horadrim’s core gameplay loop, Horadric Powers, upcoming balance changes, and more. If you’d like to watch the full Campfire Chat, **see the video below**.
