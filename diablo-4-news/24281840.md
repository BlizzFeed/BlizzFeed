> Celebrate Diablo IV’s 3rd anniversary with resplendent free gifts, March of the Goblins, Mother’s Blessing, and more.

---

Celebrations are upon us, wanderer! To mark another gilded age of Sanctuary, free gifts and events to savour this brief period of respite are here. The festivities begin on **June 2** until **June 9**, with limited-time events and free weapon cosmetics to claim.

While this week has festivities to celebrate the 30th anniversary of Diablo, **much** is brewing on the horizon throughout the year. The celebrations continue...

#### Bask in the Blessing of the Mother and Strike down Goblins

Starting on **June 2**, at 10:00 a.m. PDT, Mother’s Blessing arrives to grant you increased multiplicative XP in both Seasonal and Eternal realms.

For those loathsome of greed, strike down a deluge of greedy pests with March of the Goblins. The event reputation board is back, completing the final rank will earn you the **Regalia of the Sacred Creed**.

#### Free Gifts Await

On June 2, at 12:00 p.m. PDT, a new gift arrives in the in-game shop each day until **June 6**. You have until **June 9** to claim all of these fearsome weapon skins for demonic dismemberment.

The following gifts will unlock in the in-game Shop:

- Day 1 - Blood Raven's Talon (One-handed Sword cosmetic)
- Day 2 - King Kanai's Last Stand (Shield cosmetic)
- Day 3 - Nangari Wounder (Dagger cosmetic)
- Day 4 - Overlord's Odium (Two-handed Axe)
- Day 5 - Flamefinger's Claws (Glaive cosmetic)

Thank you all for fighting Hatred and defending Sanctuary throughout all seasons.\
—The Diablo IV Team
