# Behind the Scenes: The Journey | Hearthstone

Sit down with Hearthstone developers Alicia Cornelia, Ryan Hickman, Dez Bencosme, and Clay Howell as they discuss The Journey, our new system that revamps the Journal and brings quests, events, and game modes together into one evolving experience.

Learn how the team built The Journey, how it will help tell the story of future Hearthstone expansions, and get a sneak peek at upcoming features, including the new 2v2 Tavern Brawl.

The Journey begins on October 13, followed by the launch of Reign of the Black Empire on October 20.
