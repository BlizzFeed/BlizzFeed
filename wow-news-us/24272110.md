In the Midnight: Revelations content update, we’re introducing a new single-boss raid located in Harandar. You’ll face the fungal giant Rotmire in Raid Finder, Normal, Heroic, and Mythic difficulties. Players can also undertake Mythic difficulty in flexible groups of 15-25 players.

---

## Sporefall

![Fungal living space with large pathway in shades of green, blue, purple and fuchsia with mushroom type creatures.](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/rw/RW6D9CN4ZY5R1779141447190.png)

> **Location:** Harandar\
> **Raid Bosses:** 1\
> **Difficulties:** Raid Finder, Normal, Heroic, Mythic\
> **Level:** 90\
> **Raid Finder Minimum Item Level:** 240

*Deep in the fungal Valleys of Harandar, the fungarian sporecallers have been growing a new threat for The Grudge Pit. Shaded by towering mushrooms, Sporefall is the perfect site to empower their new creation.*

### Rotmire

![Giant mushroom type creature reaching out and towering over a combat area. There are large mushroom canopies and mushroom-inspired living spaces surrounding it.](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/I18I51JU7JRN1777415621455.png)

*The fungarians of Sporefall have nurtured a fungal giant to grow far beyond its natural growth cycle. Rotmire is a beast of brute strength whose spores can catalyze the growth of surrounding fungi, sprouting new dangers for the unwary.*

### Rewards

Take on Rotmire to unlock a range of enticing rewards, including the **Luminous Rotshroom** housing decor item, **Luminous Sporeglider** mount, **Sporefused** gear, and more.

[![alternate-image-name](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/nw/NW9II4XHRVMY1779300342309.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/nw/NW9II4XHRVMY1779300342309.png)

**Luminous Sporeglider Mount**\
*“This sporeglider’s voracious appetite for luminescent spores has lent it an exquisite glow.”*

This mount is earned by collecting 4 x **Delicious Sporesnacks**, which you can earn once per week, per account, by defeating Rotmire in Sporefall on any difficulty.

#### Sporefused Gear

Gear up with items infused with Rotmire’s fungal power, with an ilvl ranging from 259 on Raid Finder difficulty to 298 on Mythic difficulty.

- **Sporefused Armor Pieces**: Rotmire’s loot includes two Sporefused armor pieces for every armor type.
- **Rotmire’s Sporeheart** (Neck): Your healing has a chance to spawn protective toadstools that absorb damage for up to 12 sec. If destroyed, they burst, dealing Nature damage to nearby enemies.
- **Sporelord's Mycelial Insignia** (Trinket): Your spells and abilities have a chance to nourish the mycelium, granting a random secondary stat and Leech for 12 sec.
- **Sporecaller’s Blooming Loop** (Ring): Dealing Nature damage has a chance to inflict Rotbloom, dealing Nature damage over 15 sec. If the enemy is already afflicted by your Rotbloom, the effect is consumed to deal 120% of its remaining damage.

Whether adventurers are chasing armor upgrades or distinctive fungal-infused effects, Sporefused gear offers fresh rewards for every raid difficulty, with Rotmire’s corruption woven into every piece.

#### Madcap Redcap Toy

[![alternate-image-name](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/Y5Q5KHQ9GKKS1779231887131.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/Y5Q5KHQ9GKKS1779231887131.png)

**Madcap Redcap Toy**\
*“Share a delicious mushroom treat with your friends and become one with the fungus.”*

The buff from the Madcap Redcap toy lasts 20 minutes, has a 1-hour cooldown, and persists through death—perfect for keeping the fungal festivities going.

#### Luminous Rotshroom Housing Decor Item

Rotmire’s loot wouldn’t be complete without something to remember him by while you rest your weary bones after an intense battle. Light up your home with a bit of fungal fun by adding a **Luminous Rotshroom** to your abode.
