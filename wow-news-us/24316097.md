## Stay Connected in World of Warcraft with Discord

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_header/m8/M8JNOUTFZDPK1781723339591.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_header/m8/M8JNOUTFZDPK1781723339591.png)

Keep the conversation going in modern *World of Warcraft* and *World of Warcraft: Forever* with Discord account linking and guild chat mirroring. Whether you’re coordinating your next raid run, recruiting more players for PvP, or catching up with your guildmates, you can send messages between Discord and your guild chat.
