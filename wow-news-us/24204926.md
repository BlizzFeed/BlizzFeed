# Revisit Horrific Visions for New Challenges and Rewards

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_header/mo/MOAF7ORNENOD1747441584203.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_header/mo/MOAF7ORNENOD1747441584203.png)

Madness stirs once more! Return to N'Zoth's twisted versions of Stormwind and Orgrimmar in Horrific Visions Revisted and take on the new challenges within—complete objectives, collect Displaced Corrupted Mementos, and claim a dark trove of mounts, toys, transmogs, weapon enchantments, and more!

Whether powering up your Warband or venturing into the nightmare for the first time, now is the moment to walk willingly into the void and seize what lies beyond—maintain your Sanity or ultimately fail.

Speak with Soridormi, who has set up camp on the Coreway overlook in Dornogal to deliver you to the Tenebrous Gateway, where the Image of Wrathion will help you enter the void-corrupted visions of Orgrimmar or Stormwind.

Do you think you can stand the horrors? Everything you need to know about Horrific Visions can be found in our [article](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/news/24204919). Gather your allies to stand steadfast against the ancient corruption spreading across Azeroth.

# The AWC & MDI Season 2 Global Finals Start May 23!

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/ke/KEKHH8KQROGE1747438895652.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/ke/KEKHH8KQROGE1747438895652.png)

The War Within Season 2 Arena World Championship and Mythic Dungeon International Global Finals are here! Over two weekends, celebrate the top Arena Gladiators and Dungeon Runners as they compete for their share of $520,000 (USD).

Tune in live between May 23 and June 1 on [Twitch](https://www.twitch.tv/warcraft) and [YouTube](https://youtube.com/warcraft)!

Everything you need to know can be found in our [viewer guide](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/news/24206294).

# Pre-order World of Warcraft: Blood Ties
