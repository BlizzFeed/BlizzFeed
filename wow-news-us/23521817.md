# Introducing the World of Warcraft Classic Fall Conquest

- URL: https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/en-us/news/23521817/introducing-the-world-of-warcraft-classic-fall-conquest
![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/fg/FGAH862JGZS31599761058148.jpg)

> Registration is open for the World of Warcraft Classic Fall Conquest!

---

WoW Classic esports returns this October with the Fall Conquest!

The WoW Classic Fall Conquest will take place over three weeks from October 3-18 within the 15v15 Arathi Basin. With $12,000 (USD) in total prizing, teams in North America and Europe will battle for their share of each region’s $6,000 (USD) prize pool!

The tournament will be split into two regions: North America and Europe. All games will take place on live servers for WoW Classic, using the War Game feature. Here’s how it all works:

- Teams of 15 players will sign up for their region. Signups are open to eligible players with a level 60 character on live servers for WoW Classic—check eligibility and sign up for NA [here](https://gamebattles.majorleaguegaming.com/pc/world-of-warcraft/tournament/wow-classic-fall-conquest-na) and EU [here](https://gamebattles.majorleaguegaming.com/pc/world-of-warcraft/tournament/wow-classic-fall-conquest-eu)!
- The tournament will be split into two stages for each region: qualifiers and finals.
  - The qualifiers are open signups.
  - The finals will feature the top six teams within each region.
- Teams will battle against each other on live servers using the War Game feature, allowing players to queue up battlegrounds against other teams in their region.
- For the top six teams in North America and Europe, there will be a total of $6,000 (USD) in separate prizing for each region.
- More information can be found in our rulesets, which can be found on our [NA](https://gamebattles.majorleaguegaming.com/pc/world-of-warcraft/tournament/wow-classic-fall-conquest-na) and [EU](https://gamebattles.majorleaguegaming.com/pc/world-of-warcraft/tournament/wow-classic-fall-conquest-eu) sign-up pages.
