## New Dungeon: Eco-Dome Al'dani Available Week of August 5

> **Where**: K'aresh\
> **Bosses**: 3\
> **Level**: 80\
> **Difficulties**: Normal, Heroic, Mythic

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/8BLN7LGBWEGZ1750209628365.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/8BLN7LGBWEGZ1750209628365.png)

Season 3 of *The War Within* brings with it a new three-boss dungeon—Eco-Dome Al'dani—immediately available with the launch of the Ghosts of K'aresh. The post-raid story continues in Eco-Dome Al'dani, and it will also be featured in the Season 3 Mythic+ rotation.

This dungeon is available in Normal, Heroic, and Mythic 0 difficulties, and offers the following item level gear:

- **Normal Difficulty**: Item Level 639 (Non-Upgradeable)
- **Heroic Difficulty**: Item Level 665 (Adventurer 4/8)
- **Mythic Difficulty**: Item Level 681 (Champion 1/8)

On your journey to face three new boss encounters, you'll encounter a variety of creatures, including life-consuming devourers, and learn about the Wastelanders sworn to protect what remains of their shattered homeland and their purpose within the eco-dome.

### Tazavesh, the Veiled Market Returns

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/gs/GSS5O9VZY29A1751561707265.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/gs/GSS5O9VZY29A1751561707265.png)

Shadowlands' Veiled Market is back in The War Within Season 3 as an eight-boss Mega-dungeon that counts as a Mythic+10 in the weekly Great Vault, two separate Mythic+ dungeons, and a player hub at the heart of K'aresh that will be your launchpad for new adventures.

Tazavesh M0 is Hard Mode by default and is available on August 12—both wings will be available in Heroic Group Finder, and drop Hero track gear.

### New Delve: The Archival Assault

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/BVOV40XYETXB1750209628317.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/BVOV40XYETXB1750209628317.png)

Found within the Azure Vaults within the Dragon Isles, you'll encounter ethereals, ethereal pirates, and brokers who are plundering the Delve.

Use the Reshii Wraps to unlock unique challenge gateways to bosses or challenge rooms. You may also catch a glimpse of your friends within the Delve, or even *mourn* them as you observe their ghost from where they died.

This Season's Nemesis is Nexus-Princess Ky'veza, and she has unfinished business. With a shady disappearance back in Nerub'ar Palace, you'll get your chance to face her one-on-one.

Along the Delver's Journey progression, unlock customizations for the new Season 3 mount—**Delver's Mana Skimmer** to help you delve straight into adventure, or maybe other dimensions. There are also two new toys to fill out your Collection—the **Delver's Mana-Bound Ethergate**, which opens a portal to return to the Explorers' League home base in Dornogal, and the **Etheric Brannmorpher**, which summons an Ethereal Transmorpher that can change your appearance.

## Undertake a New Challenge in PvP Beginning August 12
