As a part of our ongoing series of articles featuring many of the collectible's in World of Warcraft, we've set our sights on Warlords of Draenor. The many riches of this expansion are just waiting for you to claim them. There are a variety of ways to lay claim to these mounts, pets, and toys, whether it's from defeating a rare boss, collecting specific currencies, or becoming friends with the natives, you're sure to expand your stash of treasures.

#### Currencies

There are a variety of currencies used in Warlords of Draenor. Here are some of the primary types that you'll want to make sure you have at hand.

- Gold
- [Apexis Crystals](https://www.wowhead.com/currency=823/apexis-crystal&brl): Apexis Crystals can be earned in a variety of ways via quests and defeating creatures or collecting treasures within Tanaan Jungle.
- [Blackfang Claw](https://www.wowhead.com/item=124099/blackfang-claw&brl): Collected from Blackfang saberon in Tanaan Jungle.
- [Timewarped Badge](https://www.wowhead.com/currency=1166/timewarped-badge&brl): Earned in Timewalking dungeons and used in exchange for goods from Timewalking vendors in capital cities.
- [Draenic Seeds](https://www.wowhead.com/item=116053/draenic-seeds&brl): Collected from Herbalism.
- [Nat's Lucky Coin](https://www.wowhead.com/item=117397/nats-lucky-coin&brl): These lucky coins can be earned with fishing and can be exchanged for rare goods with Nat Pagle.
