### The Midnight Beta Test is Now Live!

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_header/67/67POK3SV37RD1761694580379.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_header/67/67POK3SV37RD1761694580379.png)

New systems, the complete leveling experience, zones, dungeons, delves, Housing, and the new playable Haranir and Devourer Demon Hunter are all available in the beta. Raid testing will come at a later time. As with the alpha test, we’ll be adding content through the initial weeks and running focused tests as needed. Keep an eye on the [test forums](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/c/in-development/midnight-alpha-test/328) for more information.

Over the course of the test, we'll be inviting community veterans, press, fansites, friends, and family to check out the upcoming expansion and give us feedback. Players who [pre-purchase](https://shop.battle.net/product/world-of-warcraft-midnight) or who upgrade to the Epic Edition of Midnight will also gain access to the beta. If you would like more details on the *Midnight* beta, including the testing roadmap, you can just head over to our [previously published article](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/news/24245100) to learn more.

### Midnight Prologue: “The Warning” Content Update Goes Live December 2!

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_header/ka/KANSUX5W77BQ1762904612354.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_header/ka/KANSUX5W77BQ1762904612354.png)

With the Ghosts of K’aresh behind us, Azeroth stands on the precipice of a future overshadowed by Xal’atath and the relentless advance of the Void. In "The Warning", the stage is set for what comes next before continuing the story in the Midnight expansion. Players will also be welcomed home with the start of Housing Early Access for those who have pre-purchased the expansion!

Take your next step into the continuing story of The War Within and prepare for the arrival of *Midnight* by reading our previously[published article](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/news/24247509).

### Stake Your Claim with Housing Early Access and Come Home December 2!

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_header/m1/M1I7V4KO8MSZ1762892987777.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_header/m1/M1I7V4KO8MSZ1762892987777.png)

With the release of the “The Warning” *Midnight* Prologue content update, players who have purchased [any edition of the Midnight expansion](https://shop.battle.net/product/world-of-warcraft-midnight?utm_source=wow_site&utm_campaign=mktg-wow-global&utm_medium=homepagepanel) can join a neighborhood, purchase a house, and start customizing their new home with Housing Early Access! If you don’t have *Midnight*yet, don’t worry. You’ll still be able to begin adding to your Housing collection through a variety of activities and achievements, or by purchasing them from vendors throughout Azeroth.

Head over to our [previously published article](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/news/24244464) to learn more. Your new home in Azeroth awaits!
