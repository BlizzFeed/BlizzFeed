#### Here are this week's highlights:

#### BlizzCon® 2023

- BlizzCon® 2023 returns November 3 and 4, and [additional tickets](https://www.axs.com/blizzcon2023) are still available. Can’t attend? Don’t worry. You can watch all the content from the Anaheim Arena for free. To commemorate the return of BlizzCon, you can also purchase the [BlizzCon Collection](https://shop.battle.net/product/blizzcon-collection?_gl=1*105nmam*_ga*Njc5ODY4OTUwLjE2ODQ4NjIwNjA.*_ga_VYKNV7C0S3*MTY5Njk4NTY2NS4xMDMuMS4xNjk2OTg2Mjk3LjU3LjAuMA..) with a new set of digital goodies for World of Warcraft and other Blizzard games.

#### Dragonflight

- **Guardians of the Dream PTR Updated:** On Tuesday, the Guardians of the Dream PTR was updated to the latest build. This week’s [development notes](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/guardians-of-the-dream-ptr-development-notes/1666552/13) included updates for Monks, Priests, and power reductions for certain items. Updates were also posted for the [Mythic+](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/feedback-mythic-testing-october-26th-october-30th/1694863) and [Raid](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/amirdrassil-the-dream%E2%80%99s-hope-raid-testing-schedule/1669121/12) testing schedules.

#### WoW Classic

- **Wrath Classic Dungeon Finder Adjustments:** A [hotfix was deployed](http://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/wrath-of-the-lich-king-classic-hotfixes-updated-october-25/1500326/270) this week, and [details](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/wotlk-classic-dungeon-finder-dungeons-missing-in-level-range/1686449/106) were shared on how the level ranges for available dungeons should now be working in the game.
- **Wrath Classic Instance Limit Increased:** The hourly [instance limit](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/hh-instance-limit/1690738/18) for the Headless Horseman encounter was increased this week.
