Calling all college and university student adventurers and artists! Blizzard Entertainment's university relations and World of Warcraft development teams are proud to present the annual [World of Warcraft Student Art Contest](https://careers.blizzard.com/global/en/wow-student-contest)!

Online submission forms are available now, and ***we will accept entries until 11:59 PM PST on December 13, 2026***.

Unleash your creativity and craft an original masterpiece inspired by the world of Azeroth—something that we’ve never seen before and fits within the *World of Warcraft* universe.

Submit your work in one of four categories: Animation, Character Art, Environment Art, and FX. Category descriptions are on the entry form and in the official contest rules, so please read them before submitting.
