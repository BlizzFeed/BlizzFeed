#### Here are this week's highlights:

- **Fractures in Time Update on the PTR:** On Tuesday, the latest [PTR update](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/dragonflight-fractures-in-time-ptr-development-notes/1587226/9) was released for Fractures in Time. This included various class tuning changes, damage reductions to the Onyx Annulet Primordial Stones, and a skip through the Siege of Orgrimmar raid.
- **Damage Reduction to the Onyx Annulet:** With the release of the Fractures in Time update, we’re reducing the damage dealt from the [Onyx Annulet Primordial Stones](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/onyx-annulet-changes/1612330) by 40%. Some specs have some changes being made to offset this loss in damage; see the PTR notes for complete details.
- **Siege of Orgrimmar Raid Skip:** With the release of Fractures in Time, players who have defeated Garrosh Hellscream in the [Siege of Orgrimmar](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/legacy-raid-content/1557320/2) raid will be able to skip directly to The Inner Sanctum on all characters on their account.
