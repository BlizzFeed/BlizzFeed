For a limited time, we’re granting a Dark Portal Housing decor item\* for all players with an active *World of Warcraft* subscription or Game Time who log in between now and the Midnight launch.

![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/3o/3O3EVU149YYR1769192395032.png)

Players who have collected this item can purchase additional copies from Gabbi in Orgrimmar and Tuuran in Stormwind City (near the Trading Post.) Impress your friends at your next house party with this real showstopper!

\*Only available for modern World of Warcraft.
