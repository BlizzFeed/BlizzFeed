### What's Inside Mists of Pandaria Classic

Level through new zones, new dungeons, and new raids, engage in PvP combat, and dive further into the mysteries of Pandaria.

- **New Level Cap**: Ascend to new heights of power as you defend Azeroth, earning new talents every step of the journey to level 90 with extensively reworked talent trees.
- **Lush New Lands:** The Wandering Isle (Levels 1–10), The Jade Forest (Levels 85–86), Valley of the Four Winds (Levels 86–87), Krasarang Wilds (Levels 86–87), Kun-Lai Summit (Levels 87–88), Townlong Steppes (Levels 88–89), Dread Wastes (Levels 89–90), Vale of Eternal Blossoms (Level 90)
- **Dungeons and Raids:** Brave defenders of Azeroth can battle their way through six new dungeons in Pandaria, four revamped Heroic dungeons, and three new raids.
- **New PvP Arena and Two New Battlegrounds:** The gates will open when PvP Season 12 begins the week following launch.
- **Sunsong Ranch**: Embrace a farmer’s life with your own farm, allowing you to plant and harvest crops. Head to the Valley of the Four Winds and start earning reputation with the Tillers to earn a plush plot of soil to till.
- **Scenarios:** Join friends to achieve a common goal, such as mounting a defense against invading monsters, in a flexible new type of queueable "micro-dungeon" PvE challenges in these seven three-player Scenarios.
- **Challenge Mode Dungeons**: Master the ultimate 5-player time trial and earn prestige rewards in a new dungeon mode that will put your resolve and coordination to the test.

### Meet the Tillers

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/JZEJ08L6WITY1752021257787.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/JZEJ08L6WITY1752021257787.png)

Claim your own slice of paradise in the Valley of the Four Winds, where you shape a thriving farmstead and leave your mark on the land.  A faction of dedicated farmers committed to nurturing Pandaria’s rich agricultural traditions, the Tillers welcome you into their ranks. As you grow your farm and befriend the Tillers, you build relationships with a vibrant cast of characters while contributing daily to their shared goals. Your rising reputation with them unlocks new opportunities—plant rare crops and embrace the steady rhythm of life in Halfhill. When you join the Tillers, you don’t just pursue rewards but immerse yourself in the spirit of camaraderie, hard work, and adventure at the heart of Pandaria.

### Soar the Skies with a Sage Serpent

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/h0/H0M63MFEM58V1750802411309.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/h0/H0M63MFEM58V1750802411309.png)

Play through the Pandaren starting experience from pre-patch starting on July 1 through July 30 to earn the mysterious **Shaohao's Sage Serpent** Skyriding mount in modern *World of Warcraft* by following a few simple steps:

- Install *[World of Warcraft: Cataclysm Classic](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/classic)*
- Create a Pandaren character.
- Complete the Pandaren starting experience, including choosing the path of either Alliance or Horde.
- Once complete, **Shaohao's Sage Serpent** will be delivered to your Collection in modern WoW.

**[Pandaria Awaits](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/classic)**

Shaohao's Sage Serpent is only available in Modern World of Warcraft and requires a Subscription or Game Time.

### Mists of Pandaria Classic: Enter the Mogu'shan Vaults Raid July 31!

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_header/zx/ZXCQG9KMYNGT1750794356113.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_header/zx/ZXCQG9KMYNGT1750794356113.png)

Gather your allies and prepare to explore the hidden secrets of the elusive Mogu empire in the Mogu’shan Vaults—available globally on July 31 at 3:00 pm PDT.

*The mogu, proud of their supremacy, kept lengthy records of their accomplishments within a great complex of vaults. The construction of these vast chambers predate any pandaren written records, shrouding their depths in mystery. Rumors once circulated of a mighty army locked within; for this reason, the vaults were the first target of the ancient pandaren rebellion, and they have been sealed ever since the mogu empire was overthrown.*

> **Where:** Kun Lai Summit\
> **Level:** 90\
> **Difficulties:**(10) Normal and Heroic, (25) Normal and Heroic\
> **Bosses:** 6
