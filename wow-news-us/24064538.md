#### Here are this week's highlights:

#### Dragonflight

- Hotfixes
  - We implemented [many bugfixes](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/wow-hotfixes-february-14/1769365) for dungeons, such as Darkheart Thicket, Waycrest Manor, and Dawn of the Infinite: Rise of Murozond.
- Skybox Shenanigans
  - Players noticed that [Dragonflight's daytime/nighttime](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/whered-the-sun-go/1787055/150) cycle is different than before in all realms hosted in North America. We're developing a fix, and the fix will require realm restarts.

#### WoW Classic

##### Season of Discovery

- Improvements
  - On February 12, we announced [several improvements to Season of Discovery](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/season-of-discovery-adjustments-coming-soon-feb-12-2024/1786210) issues, such as overcrowding and streamlining the Blood Moon event.
- Class Tuning Changes
  - With scheduled weekly maintenance, [class tuning adjustments are coming](http://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/season-of-discovery-class-tuning-incoming-february-20/1788506) for druids, shamans, and mages, with paladin tuning already implemented on February 14.

- Hotfixes
  - [Some fixes have been implemented](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/season-of-discovery-hotfixes-february-14/1788040), addressing issues such as Battle for Ashenvale quests providing XP and players losing their stacks of Blood for the Blood Loa.

##### Wrath of the Lich King Classic

- Teleport Services to Shadowfang Keep
  - To speed up access to the Love is in the Air special dungeon encounter, NPCs in Stormwind and Orgrimmar now offer [teleport services to Shadowfang Keep](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/faster-travels-to-shadowfang-keep/1786416).
