# Arena World Championship returns October 3!

- URL: https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/en-us/news/24222919/arena-world-championship-returns-october-3
![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/i5/I5LK6A3O0ENP1758735324283.png)

> Enter the coliseum and cheer on the competition, AWC Season 3 is here!

---

***Beginning October 3, the Arena World Championship roars back into action for the final season of The War Within!***

Steel yourself for the battles ahead as the Arena World Championship once again calls the greatest gladiators of Azeroth to prove their might. Every clash is a test of skill, resolve, and strategy, as the best of the best fight for their place in PvP history.

Season 3 of the Arena World Championship will feature three qualification Cups, in which teams compete for points. From there, the top three point-earning teams will advance to the culmination of the competition: the Grand Finals. The following top five point-earning teams will have another chance at making it big, as they’ll face off in the Gauntlet and compete for the final spot in their region’s Grand Finals.

### Details

- **Broadcast Dates:**
  - Cup 1: October 3–5
  - Cup 2: October 10–12
  - Cup 3: October 17–19
  - Gauntlet: November 14
  - Grand Finals: November 15–16
- **Broadcast Start Time:** 10:00 am (PDT) / 7:00 pm (CEST)
- **Format:** Double Elimination, Best-of-5. Grand Finals Best-of-7.
