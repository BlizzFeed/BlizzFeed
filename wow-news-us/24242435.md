Are you prepared to tread once again through the Dark Portal and into the Fel-scarred realm of Outland? The *WoW Burning Crusade Classic Anniversary Edition* pre-expansion patch arrives on January 13. Join fellow heroes of Azeroth and rally to face the might of the Burning Legion as you adventure across the shattered skies and torn lands of an exiled world. Burning Crusade brings the playable races of Blood Elves and Draenei to World of Warcraft, along with flying mounts that enable you to explore Outland’s most remote and dangerous regions.

## Anniversary Realm to Classic Era Realm Transfers

For those Classic Anniversary players who do not want their characters to continue to Burning Crusade Classic Anniversary, we will open up free character transfers from Anniversary Realms to Classic Era realms for a limited time starting November 25. This choice to move Classic Anniversary characters to Classic Era realms will last until January 12, 2026.

After January 12, 2026, the transfer option will no longer be available, and you will not be able to transfer your Classic Anniversary characters to Classic Era realms. Any characters remaining on Classic Anniversary realms will continue to *Burning Crusade Classic Anniversary*.
