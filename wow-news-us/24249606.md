### The Midnight Pre-Expansion Content Update Goes Live January 20!

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/8u/8U01KX0A706R1766079582754.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/8u/8U01KX0A706R1766079582754.png)

The first shadows of the Void fall upon Azeroth as the *Midnight* Pre-Expansion Content Update goes live January 20.

**What’s Inside:**

- New Race and Class Combination: Void Elf Demon Hunters
- Transmogrification Updates
- Player vs. Player (PvP) Training Grounds
- Housing Early Access
- Pre-expansion Event
- The Winds of Mysterious Fortune Returns

Players can also explore the new User Interface (UI) updates, experiment with new combat design changes across all classes, walk the path of the Demon Hunter Devourer, and more! Head over to our previously published [pre-exansion overview](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/news/24245217) for all the details.
