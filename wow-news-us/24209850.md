## Rise of the Red Dawn

> **Where**: Receive quest from Faerin Lothar standing outside Stonelight Rest in Dornogal.\
> **Difficulty**: Level 80

While attention has been focused on Khaz Algar, a new enemy has risen in the Arathi Highlands, threatening to shatter the tenuous peace between Stromgarde and Hammerfall. Join Faerin, Danath, and Geya'rah as they attempt to expose a conspiracy, defuse tensions, and grapple with the legacy of Arathor. Experience this new storyline with branching viewpoints based on your character’s faction as you brave the journey ahead.

## Rewards

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/b6/B60LS879TX8M1749586486850.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/b6/B60LS879TX8M1749586486850.png)

Don the tabard of your allegiance when you complete the "Rise of Red Dawn" quest:

- **Alliance**: Stromgarde Tabard
- **Horde**: Hammerfall Tabard

Look as brave as those who wield the Sacred Flame when you complete the "Past Glory" quest. Earn the **Lamplighter's Pauldrons** transmog, inspired by Fearin's lamplighter shoulders, and the "Rise of the Red Dawn" achievement and Title:

- **Alliance**: Rise of the Red Dawn achievement and title "<Name> of Stromgarde"
- **Horde**: Rise of the Red Dawn achievement and title "<Name> of Hammerfall"
