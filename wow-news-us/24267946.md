### Play Midnight for a Classic Anniversary Edition Flying Mount

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_header/3f/3FSI0HCBB7SM1773436207775.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_header/3f/3FSI0HCBB7SM1773436207775.png)

Earn the menacing Voidfeather Dragonhawk flying mount to carry you through the fel-scarred realm of Outland in *Burning Crusade Classic Anniversary Edition* after completing the introduction scenario in *World of Warcraft: Midnight*.

**When**: March 17 through May 15

Quel'Thalas is under siege! Complete the intro scenario of *Midnight*, pitting you against Void forces during their invasion of the home of the Blood Elves, and earn the "Echoes of Midnight" achievement before the promotion period ends to get the Voidfeather Dragonhawk flying mount for use in your *Burning Crusade Classic Anniversary* adventures.

Details on how you can obtain this mount for the Burning Crusade Classic Anniversary characters, including how you can get to the Midnight scenario, can be found in our [previously published article](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/news/24267942).

### Take Your Turn on the Trial of Style Catwalk

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_header/nc/NCR32LBI4R971773787706390.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_header/nc/NCR32LBI4R971773787706390.png)

Fierce fashionistas and dashing defenders of the Horde and Alliance, synchronize your wardrobes and prepare for a fashion battle. The Trial of Style has arrived, and it’s time to put your transmogrification skills to the test.

*Dust off those pauldrons and compete in a fashion competition of Azerothian proportions! Speak to a transmogrifier in any major city to participate in the Trial of Style!*

**When:** March 20–27\
**Where:** Queue for the 6-player scenario at your friendly neighborhood transmog NPC.

For a fantastic fashion challenge, find eight new "***[Work It!](https://www.wowhead.com/quest=76214/work-it?brl)***" quests around hubs in the four main zones of Quel’Thalas—Eversong Woods, Zul’Aman, Harandar, and Voidstorm. Exercise your design eye with six new themes to show off your prowess:

- Dungeon Dangers
- Elemental Extremes
- Mechanical Menace
- Jungle Jam

Look for new themes, decorations, and winning displays as the Trial of Style runway extends into Quel’Thalas. Start your fashion journey by picking up the "[***Fashion Week***](https://www.wowhead.com/quest=76361/fashion-week?brl)" quest from a Holiday Enthusiast chilling near the Trading Post in Silvermoon City, which will draw you to Belli, the Trial of Style Envoy in Silvermoon.

Also, earn extra for being ***extra***. While the Trial of Style is active, completing certain Trial of Style-related activities while trotting around in your best transmogs will help you earn progress towards filling your bar at the Trading Post.

Learn more from our [previously published article](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/news/24267943).

### Unveiling the AWC & MDI Road to BlizzCon in 2026!

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_header/e9/E9YG409J5INY1772756748291.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_header/e9/E9YG409J5INY1772756748291.png)

Return to the epic action as we unveil the Arena World Championship and Mythic Dungeon International programs. The clock has officially struck *Midnight*, and we're kicking off the future of competition for 2026!

The Arena World Championship (AWC) and Mythic Dungeon International (MDI) are back with programming leading into BlizzCon for the first time in 7 years. [Registration is now open](http://raider.io/tournaments). Read below to learn how you can get involved and mark your entry on the Road to BlizzCon!

All AWC and MDI programming will be available live on the official [Warcraft Twitch](https://www.twitch.tv/warcraft) and [Warcraft YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/@Warcraft) channels, alongside perspectives from participating co-streamers. For those attending BlizzCon in person, we're thrilled to share the 2026 Grand Finals live from Anaheim, California—check out everything coming to the event at [BlizzCon.com](http://blizzcon.com/)!

Event details, including registration and prize pools, are available in our [previously published article](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/news/24247519).
