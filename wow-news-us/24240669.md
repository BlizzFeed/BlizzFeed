Raise *fel* in the violet vibrance of a void-infused transmog set while you ride high astride your Void-Razed Elekk, with a smoldering Fel Snooter pet in tow! All are available together through January 12, 2026, when you purchase the Amethyst Felfire Pack\*.

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/66/66QEZDECYMMB1759863862376.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/66/66QEZDECYMMB1759863862376.png)

The Void-razed Elekk is a ground mount shared across all present and future WoW® Modern characters and automatically scales to the fastest riding skill known by each character.

The Amethyst Felfire Pack includes the following:

- Void-razed Elekk (Ground Mount)
- Mustyfur Fel Snooter (Pet)
- Ensemble: Felreaver's Arcane Attire (Transmog Set)
  - Felreaver's Arcane Helm
  - Felreaver's Arcane Shoulderguards
  - Felreaver's Arcane Jacket
  - Felreaver's Arcane Belt
  - Felreaver's Arcane Legguards
  - Felreaver's Arcane Treads
  - Felreaver's Arcane Gauntlets

Content within the Amethyst Felfire Pack may arrive in a future rotation of the Trading Post, but no sooner than three months after the offer leaves the Shop.
