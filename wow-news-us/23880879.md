# The Second Phase of the Dragonflight Pre-Expansion Patch is Now Live!

- URL: https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/en-us/news/23880879/the-second-phase-of-the-dragonflight-pre-expansion-patch-is-now-live
![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/5e/5EU72O6TRG1J1668531977067.png)

> With the second phase of the pre-expansion patch, players with Dragonflight can experience the new dracthyr Evoker and begin leveling through the Forbidden Reach. The second phase of the pre-expansion patch also brings with it a new event— the Tempest Unleashed.

With the second phase of the pre-expansion patch, players with *Dragonflight* can experience the new dracthyr Evoker and begin leveling through the Forbidden Reach. The second phase of the pre-expansion patch also brings with it a new event— the Tempest Unleashed.

### Scale Up with the New Dracthyr Evoker

![Green Dracthyr Evoker Hovering Above the Ground](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/ee/EE8336PJGVSH1658370586642.jpg)

With the release of the second phase of the pre-expansion patch, players with Dragonflight can create and play the new dracthyr Evoker, a hero class that serves as both a race and a class in combination. [[Learn More](https://worldofwarcraft.com/en-us/news/23878723/)]

---

### Awaken in the Forbidden Reach

![Ornate Looming Entryway into the Dracthyr Starting Area](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/5o/5O4W8T2T05T41658190184039.png)

Awaken as a dracthyr Evoker in the Forbidden Reach and dive into the mysteries of the past as enemies and potential allies, new and old, are drawn to this place of ancient power. [[Learn More](https://worldofwarcraft.com/en-us/news/23877536/)]

---

### The Tempest Unleashed Pre-Expansion Event

![Primal Elemental Made of Rocks with Green Crystals](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/WG4SJ5X5BOFC1668202023353.jpg)

As a new age of adventure approaches, Primalists compel the very elements to strike all over Azeroth. Quell their rage and venture once more into Uldaman to recover Tyr's lost memories. It is up to you to stop the elements from revolting all over the Eastern Kingdoms and Kalimdor, and delve into the Uldaman: Legacy of Tyr dungeon to recover an ancient titan relic. [[Learn More](https://worldofwarcraft.com/en-us/news/23873864)]

---

### Finding Your Way to the Dragon Isles

![Male Draenei Casting next to a Kneeling Female Night Elf](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/zw/ZWXPQJOMQRR71667363320994.jpg)

The path to the Dragon Isles, ancestral home of the dragonflights, opens November 28. Whether you’re returning to the land of Azeroth or new to these magical lands, we’ve put together some helpful information to prepare you for your journeys ahead. [[Learn More](https://worldofwarcraft.com/en-us/news/23874313/)]

### Dragonflight Pre-Expansion Phase 2 Update Notes

![Purple Dracthyr with Red Horns](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/2u/2U2C416P9FAX1668110121411.jpg)

The Dragonflight Pre-Expansion Phase 2 update is around the corner! Get ready to create a dracthyr Evoker and journey through the Forbidden Reach, ward off attacks from the Primalists in the Tempest Unleashed in-game event, and brave the new dungeon, Uldaman: Legacy of Tyr. [[Learn More](https://worldofwarcraft.com/en-us/news/23874318/)]

Catch up on all the changes introduced in phase 1 of the Dragonflight pre-expansion patch in our previously published article.
