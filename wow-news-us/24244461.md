# Face New Challenges and Disrupt Ritual Sites in 12.0.5

- URL: https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/en-us/news/24244461/face-new-challenges-and-disrupt-ritual-sites-in-12-0-5
![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/m8/M8SFZO14KAY51776140852580.png)

> Whether you’re going it solo, or gathering allies to join you, a new challenge awaits at the Ritual Sites in Eversong Woods and Zul’Aman. You’ll put a stop to rituals of power-hungry naga and Twilight’s Blade cultists in small one-to-five-player instances and face new challenges for new rewards.

---

Ritual Sites introduce new repeatable instanced content similar to Delves designed for flexible group sizes and allowing one to five players. Each offers escalating challenges in a bid for the heroes of the Alliance and Horde to disrupt the rituals of the naga in Eversong Woods or the Twilight’s Blade cultists in Zul’Aman.

- Eversong Woods: Daggerspine Point
- Zul’Aman: Broken Throne

These sites alternate between the two zones. To enter the Ritual site, players will interact with the Curious Obelisk which allows you to choose your difficulty tier and the challenges you will face.

![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/0e/0E8M1534UGAK1776143373505.png)

As you progress through these Ritual Sites, you can choose your level of challenge from Tiers 1 through 5. Players must complete each Tier before unlocking the next. Tiers 3 through 5 will also require you to select 1, 2, or 4 challenges respectively in order to enter.

A new currency called "**Field Accolades"** is earned in Ritual Sites along with **Ritual Spoils** which contain Crests, currencies, and rare cosmetic items. **Field Accolades** and **Voidlight Marl** can be spent at several new vendors found on the second level of The Bazaar in Silvermoon City. You will also find Lady Darkglen, Rae’ana, Ranger Captain Lilatha, and Kul’amara the Fierce in this location for quest turn-ins or to pick up new quests.

![alternate-image-name](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/BWIII8J5HVGV1776140895664.png)
![alternate-image-name](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/61ZSFSRPP4RD1776140895863.png)

The level of challenge you undertake and how well you succeed will impact the amount of rewards you get. You can also keep track of your Spoils and Deaths throughout the site within the User Interface as you progress through the instance. A performance score is displayed at the end of the encounter showing your total rewards.

- **Spoils:** Combined spoils gained from thwarting enemy forces in the Ritual Site.
- **Deaths:** The number of times players have been overpowered in a Ritual Site. Your first 2 deaths will not affect your final spoils. Every death after that will reduce the spoils by 5%, up to a maximum of 50%.

Each week the tier requirement will be one lower as players learn more about the rituals. This will allow all players to unlock the bonuses in the lower levels over time.
