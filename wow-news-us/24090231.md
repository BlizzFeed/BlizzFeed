# Mount Up for the Northrend Cup!

- URL: https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/en-us/news/24090231/mount-up-for-the-northrend-cup
![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/48/48ZJ4Y36MKEE1659374272702.jpg)

> Take to the skies of Northrend for a new dragonriding racing event!

---

Make your way through the icy skies of Northrend in a chilly, new dragonriding racing event.

*"The Riders of Azeroth invite dragonriders to compete in races throughout Northrend! Talk to Lord Andestrasz in Valdrakken for more information.."*

**When:** April 30–May 13\
**Where:** Begins in Valdrakken

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/q4/Q41TFR3PFM2T1691778452604.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/q4/Q41TFR3PFM2T1691778452604.png)

With several races spread across Northrend, dragonriders will have a skyfull of options for challenging courses in Normal, Advanced, and Reverse variations. Trek to Valdrakken and speak to Lord Andestrasz near the Rostrum of Transformation to pick up the initial quest for **Riders of Azeroth Badges**, which can be exchanged for unique items and rewards from Maztha, also in Valdrakken.

#### Rewards For a Fancy Flight

Complete all Northrend Cup races on Gold to earn the *Northrend Racing Completionist: Gold* achievement, "Northrend Racer" title, and the **[Frosted Riders of Azeroth Tabard](https://www.wowhead.com/item=213593/frosted-riders-of-azeroth-tabard?brl)**. In addition, some rewards you can choose from include a **[Vial of Endless Draconic Scales](https://www.wowhead.com/item=212518/vial-of-endless-draconic-scales?brl)** that randomizes mount customizations each time you mount, pieces to build the fashionably adventurous **Icy Drake Racer's** set, and more!
