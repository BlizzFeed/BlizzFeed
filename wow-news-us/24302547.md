Join us for the *WoW: Forever* Poster Signing on November 2, 2026, at the Irvine Improv.

### Get Free Tickets

Attendance is limited, so make sure to obtain your free ticket from [TIXR](https://www.tixr.com/groups/bc/events/world-of-warcraft-forever-poster-signing-210889) beginning Tuesday, October 27 at 12:00 pm PDT for your chance to attend this special event.

### Meet the Developers

Meet developers behind the game, connect with fellow adventures, and celebrate the upcoming release of *WoW: Forever* on November 4, 2026.

### Get There On Time

Guests are strongly encouraged to arrive early at their designated arrival window. Entry will be granted to all guests that registered in advance with a valid QR code for their time slot. The QR code must be scanned at the check-in tent located outside the venue. Due to the limited time and space within the venue, we will be unable to accommodate anyone without a ticket. Irvine Improv is also ADA compliant.

Visit our [event page](https://www.tixr.com/groups/bc/events/world-of-warcraft-forever-poster-signing-210889) for more information.

We anticipate a fun and busy celebration and can’t wait to see you there!
