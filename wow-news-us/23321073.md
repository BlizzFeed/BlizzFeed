# Announcing the World of Warcraft Student Art Contest 2019 Winners!

- URL: https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/en-us/news/23321073/announcing-the-world-of-warcraft-student-art-contest-2019-winners
![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/cr/CR1WE1CZ4LUL1584035040488.jpg)

> The 2019 Student Art Contest has come to an end, and after intense scrutiny and some impassioned deliberation, we’ve identified this year’s winners.

---

Presented by Blizzard Entertainment’s University Relations and World of Warcraft development teams, the ninth annual [World of Warcraft Student Art Contest](https://worldofwarcraft.com/en-us/news/23226196) has drawn to a close. After some fervent debate and impassioned deliberation, our judges sifted through the many remarkable entries and identified the winning entries.

Check out the winners and runner-ups below for each of the four categories.

---
