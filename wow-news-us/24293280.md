- **Feats of Strength**

  - **Cutting Edge: Chimaerus, the Undreamt God**
    - Defeat Chimaerus, the Undreamt God in the Dreamrift on Mythic difficulty during Season 1.

  - **Dreamrift Vanquisher's Argent Trophy**: 100% drops to all raid members from defeating Chimaerus, the Undreamt God on Raid Finder/Normal difficulty.

  - **Dreamrift Vanquisher's Argent Trophy, Dreamrift Vanquisher's Gleaming Trophy, and Dreamrift Vanquisher's Aureate Trophy**: 100% drops to all raid members from defeating Chimaerus, the Undreamt God on Mythic difficulty.

### The Voidspire

- **Feats of Strength**
  - **Ahead of the Curve: Crown of the Cosmos**
    - Defeat Crown of the Cosmos in The Voidspire on Heroic difficulty or higher during Season 1.
  - **Cutting Edge: Crown of the Cosmos**
    - Defeat Crown of the Cosmos in The Voidspire on Mythic difficulty during Season 1.
- **Decor Items**
  - **Voidspire Vanquisher's Argent Trophy**: 100% drops to all raid members from defeating Crown of the Cosmos on Raid Finder/Normal difficulty.
  - **Voidspire Vanquisher's Argent Trophy, and Voidspire Vanquisher's Gleaming Trophy**: 100% drops to all raid members from defeating Crown of the Cosmos on Heroic difficulty.
  - **Voidspire Vanquisher's Argent Trophy, Voidspire Vanquisher's Gleaming Trophy, and Voidspire Vanquisher's Aureate Trophy**: 100% drops to all raid members from defeating Crown of the Cosmos on Mythic difficulty.

### March on Quel'Danas

- **Feats of Strength**
  - **Ahead of the Curve: Midnight Falls**

  - **Cutting Edge: Midnight Falls**

### Achievements

With the upcoming changes to Temple of Sethraliss in Midnight Season 2, the following achievements will be going away:

- **Snake Eyes**
  - Activate both eyes to exit the Bazaar in the Temple of Sethraliss within 10 seconds on Mythic difficulty.
  - Upon the beginning of Season 2, Temple of Sethraliss, Kings' Rest, and Ruby Life Pools on Heroic and Mythic will only be available to players level 90 and above. Upon conclusion of Season 2, these dungeons on Heroic and Mythic will become available again at their original level requirement.
- **Snake Eater**
  - Force the Solid Egg to hatch during the Merektha encounter, defeat the Solid Snake, and become the Snake Eater in the Temple of Sethraliss on Mythic difficulty.
- **Good Night, Sweet Prince**
  - Restore the Avatar of Sethraliss with the Prince active in the Temple of Sethraliss on Mythic difficulty.

## Join the Final Surge for Decor Duels

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_header/uu/UUPD6XOSRR2H1776296285850.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_header/uu/UUPD6XOSRR2H1776296285850.png)

Heed the final call to join Decor Duels before the doors close in this final surge before the Curse of Ula’tek goes live. To help you get the most out of it, we’ve made a few additional changes.

- Hiders will no longer be automatically revealed when thirty seconds remain in the match.
- Flies have been removed from Hiders who stay in one location for too long.
- The energy increase rate for Seekers has been reduced by 20%.
- Players will earn double the rate of Illusionary Coins

Once the dust settles and Decor Duel is no longer available, players will still be able to purchase Decor items from the vendors in Silvermoon City using Voidlight Marl.

## Don’t Forget this *Goal*den Opportunity

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/45/45GW9A1Q8LKY1784764583743.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/45/45GW9A1Q8LKY1784764583743.png)

A limited-time vendor, Richmond, is hanging out near Thalassian University within Silvermoon City, and you’ll want to score some goodies before he blows the final whistle.

Those with a passion for sports can *net* themselves some kickin’ rewards to represent their faction’s colors on the field and in their home. Simply get out on the nearby practice field and score three goals with the **Kickable Practice Ball** while donning the **Tabard of Participation** (either Alliance or Horde) to unlock the [*Goal!*](https://www.wowhead.com/achievement=63343/goal?brl) Achievement, earning you rewards to kick off your collection: a **Prized Orb of Azeroth** trophy and faction pride decor—**Framed Horde Pride** or **Framed Alliance Pride**.

If you want to score duplicates of these items, Richmond is happy to sell them to you for a fee. Once he heads off for greener fields, you can still pick them up from [Dennia Silvertongue](https://www.wowhead.com/npc=256828/dennia-silvertongue?brl) in Silvermoon City, [Gabbi](https://www.wowhead.com/npc=261262/gabbi?brl) in Orgrimmar, or [Tuuran](https://www.wowhead.com/npc=261231/tuuran?brl) in Stormwind—provided you’ve already earned them through gameplay. Don’t wait too long to get started; this opportunity won’t go into overtime.
