Whether you're a lifelong player, or a new adventurer, this is your chance to relive the nostalgia, connect with other community members, and immerse yourself in Warcraft like never before. Don't miss out on this final wave of free tickets to the event!

Join us at the Royale Boston on May 10th for an evening celebrating 30 years of gaming history. Doors will open at 7pm EDT with the event activities continuing until 11pm EDT.

### Get Free Tickets!

The final wave of tickets is here and you can claim yours on **Wednesday, April 30 at 1:00 pm EDT** [here](https://tixr.com/e/136482). Tickets will go fast, so plan accordingly.

### Meet us at the Royale Boston

Located in the heart of the theater district, the Royale Boston is conveniently located near multiple parking garages within walking distance of the venue and a short 1-2 block walk from public transit. For any Uber and Lyft rides, please use Royale (279 Tremont Street Boston  02116) to get dropped off right at the front door!

### Get There Early

We strongly encourage all guests to arrive on time when doors open at 7pm EDT, as entry will be granted on a first-come, first-served basis. We want as many of you as possible to experience the magic of this celebration, so be sure to plan accordingly!

Visit our [event page](https://www.tixr.com/groups/warcraft30-world-tour) for more information.
