# The Arena World Championship Begins February 2!

- URL: https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/en-us/news/24061005/the-arena-world-championship-begins-february-2
![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/vx/VXXGL6TF40IO1706297786429.png)

> Prepare for close-quarter combat and awesome action from the top World of Warcraft© teams as AWC returns in Season 3!

---

Beginning February 2, the arena gates will open for the 2024 Dragonflight Season 3 Arena World Championship (AWC)! Tune in to the first two Cups on [Twitch](https://twitch.tv/warcraft) and [YouTube](https://youtube.com/warcraft) to see how far the gladiators will go.

There’s a great deal at stake in Season 3, with only two opportunities for the top gladiators to emerge victorious for a spot in the cross-region Mid-Season Clash! Tune in to the tournament for exhilarating brawls, brutal combat, and thrilling encounters!

[![Video](https://markdown-videos-api.jorgenkh.no/youtube/nZBW8-vLS6M?width=1280&height=720)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nZBW8-vLS6M)

#### Details

- **Venue:**  Online: Twitch.tv/Warcraft and YouTube.com/Warcraft
- ***Co-streaming:*** *Co-streaming is returning in 2024, and AWC Watch Party registration is now open. Check with your favorite competitor to see if they will stream their POV during the tournament! To sign up for an AWC Watch Party, use the* [*AWC Watch Party Form*](http://blizz.ly/CostreamAWC)*.*

- **Dates:**
  - Cup 1: February 2–4
  - Cup 2: February 9–11
  - Mid-Season Clash: March 1–3
- **Broadcast Start Time:**  10:00 a.m. (PT) / 7:00 p.m. (CET)
- **Format:** Double Elimination Best-of-5 and Grand Finals Best-of-7
