# Weekly Timewalking Holidays Incoming!

- URL: https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/en-us/news/23442724/weekly-timewalking-holidays-incoming
![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/ut/UTY3UXCGB7QO1559068487985.jpg)

> Beginning June 23, players will be able to experience five consecutive weeks of Timewalking and earn 50% bonus reputation during each Timewalking event.

---

Gather your party and prepare for five consecutive weeks of Timewalking adventure. Beginning Tuesday, June 23, players will be able to step into the first week of Timewalking with the Burning Crusade. While Timewalking is active, you will gain a 50% bonus to reputations earned from quests and creature kills for all reputations associated with the corresponding expansion.

**Weekly Schedule of Timewalking Events**

- June 23-30: [Burning Crusade Timewalking](https://worldofwarcraft.com/en-us/news/20324470/weekly-bonus-event-burning-crusade-timewalking)
- June 30-July 7: [Wrath of the Lich King](https://worldofwarcraft.com/en-us/news/21510319/wrath-of-the-lich-king-timewalking-returns-march-17-24)
- July 7-14: [Cataclysm](https://worldofwarcraft.com/en-us/news/23237898/step-into-the-firelands-during-cataclysm-timewalking)
- July 14-21: [Mists of Pandaria](https://worldofwarcraft.com/en-us/news/20564529/weekly-bonus-event-mists-of-pandaria-timewalking)
- July 21-28: [Warlords of Draenor](https://worldofwarcraft.com/en-us/news/22994613/weekly-bonus-event-warlords-of-draenor-timewalking)

This reputation bonus also applies to all future regularly scheduled Timewalking events.
