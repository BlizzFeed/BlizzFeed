> Revel in owlmazing escapades on the ground and in the skies when you climb into the saddle of the Charming Courier mount (with Skyriding!) and invite the Swoopy owl pet along for the ride!

---

Expand your collection of mounts and pets with some *owl*some friends—the Charming Courier Mount and the Swoopy owl pet—in the skies and on the ground. Your adventures will *owl*ways be a hoot.

Add the [Charming Courier](https://shop.battle.net/product/world-of-warcraft-mount-charming-courier) owl mount (with Skyriding!) to your multitude of mounts, spread your wings, and swiftly swoop into the skies! Get a [6-Month Subscription](https://shop.battle.net/product/world-of-warcraft-subscription) and receive the Charming Courier mount for Modern, along with the [Swoopy](https://shop.battle.net/product/wow-classic-pet-swoopy) owl pet to journey with your *Cataclysm Classic™* characters. The mount and pet are also available separately through the Battle.net Shop.

![charming-courier-mount](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/on/ONVYU5KBWM3G1719952197024.png)

Players on existing 6-Month or 12-Month recurring subscription plans with a future renewal date will receive the Charming Courier mount and the Swoopy pet at no additional charge, no later than July 23, 2024 (must be redeemed by January 31, 2025) through the Battle.net launcher.

Have a hoot of an *owl*venture on the ground and in the skies aboard the Charming Courier mount (with Skyriding!) hooo will sagaciously deliver you to your destination!

The Charming Courier mount is not available for World of Warcraft Classic games and is shared across all present and future WoW Modern characters. It automatically scales to the fastest riding skill known by each character, and if characters aren’t at a high enough level to use a flying mount, Charming Courier will also act as a ground mount.
