#### Here are this week's highlights:

##### Dragonflight

**Final Campaign Quest for Embers of Neltharion:** Access to the final campaign quest for Embers of Neltharion currently requires players to defeat Scalecommander Sarkareth. It will [open to all players](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/campaign-quest-locked-behind-raiding/1596736/15), regardless of progression, when the final Raid Finder wing releases in the coming weeks.

**A Greedy Emissary In-game Event:**

- Higher drop rates for two items: The Tyrael’s Charger mount and Horadric Haversack bag will have a [significantly higher chance of dropping](http://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/world-of-warcraft-pays-homage-to-diablo-with-the-greedy-emissary-event/1598195/128) the first time a Treasure Goblin is defeated each day.
- Charms disabled in instanced content: The charms that players can earn from the Treasure Goblin will be [disabled in instanced content](http://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/diablo-event/1598783/41).

**Latest Fractures in Time PTR Updates:** The latest [PTR development notes](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/dragonflight-fractures-in-time-ptr-development-notes/1587226/5) and feedback thread for the [new Kalimdor Cup Dragonriding feature](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/feedback-kalimdor-cup/1596833) were posted this week.

**PTR Client Install:** Updates on the [install locations for PTR clients](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/new-ptr-install-locations/1597162) were shared, explaining the differences and current availability across the different games.

##### WoW Classic

**Thoughts on the WoW Token:** Additional thoughts were posted to [explain why](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/thoughts-on-wow-token-in-wrath-classic/1598255) the WoW Token was implemented in Wrath Classic and provide some visibility into actions against exploitative accounts.

**Classic Era Honor Reset:** Updates to the Classic Era version of the game will require a complete [reset of the current honor system](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/classic-era-pvp-rank-reset-coming-with-patch-1144/1596356).

**Call of the Crusade PTR Testing:** An explanation was posted on how the Anub’arak encounter [works as intended](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/anub-hc-nerubian-burrower-incorrect-interaction-with-block/1582516/15) with the block ability based on a bug fix done late in the original Wrath of the Lich King cycle.
