> Here you will find a list of hotfixes that address various issues related to World of Warcraft: The War Within, Cataclysm Classic, Season of Discovery, WoW Classic Era, and Hardcore.

---

Here you'll find a list of hotfixes that address various issues related to World of Warcraft: The War Within, Cataclysm Classic, Season of Discovery, WoW Classic Era, and Hardcore. Some of the hotfixes below take effect the moment they were implemented, while others may require scheduled realm restarts to go into effect. Please keep in mind that some issues cannot be addressed without a client-side patch update. This list will be updated as additional hotfixes are applied.

## Hotfixes

---

**April 28, 2025**

**Classes**

- **Paladin**
  - **Retribution**
    - Resolved an issue that caused Gleaming Rays to drop off and not be reapplied until specific events occurred.
- **Shaman**
  - **Restoration**
    - Fixed an issue causing damage from Acid Rain to not generate threat or aggro enemies.
- **Warrior**
  - **Arms**
    - Juggernaut is now a Tracked Buff on the Cooldown Manager.

**Items**

- Explosive Hearthstone - Fixed an issue where parts of the spell visual could be hidden from other players.
- Conqueror's Prized Lacquer and Conqueror's Prized Varnish will now supersede a lower PvP item level if present on the target item.
- Fixed an issue preventing Amirdrassil tier tokens from dropping.

**Nightfall Scenario**

- Implemented additional tuning to help players that are finding it hard to earn points for their Nightfall scenario Service Award score.
- Added improved tracking for Nightfall scenario Service Award that should make scoring behavior more consistent.
- Implemented additional stability improvements for Nightfall scenario objective assignment and for completing the Nightfall scenario.

**Player versus Player**

- Fixed an issue that caused the wrong Field Medic's Hazard Payout to be awarded at end of match.

**Quests**

- The Earthen Prototype and Overcharged batteries in the Siren Isle Introduction quest "To Scan a Crystal" should once again be interactable.

**Season of Discovery**

- The Caladboulder will now lose 1 durability every time the Chance on Hit stun proc activates.
- **Scarlet Enclave**
  - There are now three Torn Pages for Putress' Diary per Scarlet Enclave instance, as intended (was incorrectly 1).
  - The Hoard of the Pirate Lord containing the Lucky Doubloon will now require you to kil Mason the Echo before being able to loot it (was: required killing Balnazzar before looting, and was also using his tap list - creating issues for Master Looters and not having a chest if a soft reset happened between Balnazzar and Mason).
  - The Hoard of the Pirate Lord will no longer despawn if there is a soft reset done on the instance after Balnazzar.
  - The Hoard of the Pirate Lord will no longer despawn if the chest is left open for too long.
  - **Caldoran**
    - Enemies will no longer show the Panic debuff while they are dead.
    - Enemies will no longer ignore Panic if they have Curse of Recklessness active when it is cast.
    - Enemies will (hopefully) no longer stay alive while at 1 health.
  - **Lilian Voss**
    - Ignite has had its initial damage reduced and the scaling amount reduced.

  - **Hunter**

    - Increases and decreases to Judgement will no longer affect Seal of Command.

    - The base physical damage reduction from Just a Flesh Wound has been reduced to 20% (was 30%).
    - *Developers’ notes: We've lowered the flat damage reduction portion of Just a Flesh Wound. When this was increased (when the defense scaling was introduced), this number being 30% was used to help bridge the shift from tanking in damage gear to tanking in tank gear. We feel enough time has passed to lower this number, as players have had adequate access to gear they could swap over to or get a new set of if they had geared for damage.*
  - **Shaman**
    - The Scarlet Enclave Elemental 2-piece set bonus will no longer chain to other targets while Burn is engraved (was casting a Flame Shock tick that chained even to targets that didn't have a Flame Shock active and also chaining multiple times across targets if Chain Lightning was the triggering spell).

    - The Scarlet Enclave Elemental 2-piece set bonus will no longer play a Flame Shock animation from the caster whenever it activates.
    - The Scarlet Enclave Elemental 2-piece set bonus will now correctly strike targets with an instant tick of Flame Shock that are out of normal Flame Shock range of 20 yards (was: enemies needed to be within the normal Flame Shock).
  - **Warlock**
    - Shadow Bolt Volley now causes Shadow Bolt to deal 30% reduced damage (was 5%).
    - Shadow Bolt Volley no longer deals reduced damage if there is only one target that is struck by your Shadow Bolt cast (was: always deals reduced damage while the rune is engraved).
- **Scarlet Enclave**
  - **Mason the Echo**
    - The Berserk timer has been increased to 7 minutes.
    - Scarlet Cannonballs are now properly removed when the boss leashes and resets.
  - **Alexei the Beastlord**
    - Enervate and Enkindle have had their periodic damage reduced slightly.
    - The spell visuals for Wild Fissure have been improved.
  - **Reborn Council**
    - Blast Wave’s vertical arc from the knockback has been lessened.

- Hardened Frostguard now has an internal cooldown on the Frost Nova chance on hit, to prevent it from extensively reactivating on the same ability cast.
- **Naxxramas**
  - Ignite will no longer incorrectly tick on targets while the player is under the effect of a Mind Control.
  - The health increases from Lifegiving Gem, Rallying Cry, Last Stand, Aegis of Preservation, Vengeance, and Survival Instincts no longer behave incorrectly while under the effects of the Seal of the Dawn.
  - While inside Naxxramas, the 6-piece bonuses for tank warrior, tank warlock, and tank druid will now dynamically update your Last Stand, Vengeance, and Survival Instincts if the new value would be lower and you do not have a debuff that lowers your health active.
- **Rogue**
  - The tank T1 6-piece set bonus now provides a 20% damage reduction (was 50%).
  - The animation for Crimson Tempest has been changed to be less chaotic.

**February 26, 2025**

**Achievements**

- Reading the Misplaced Work Order will now reliably give credit for *Read Between the Lines*.

**Delves**

- Fixed an issue that could prevent players who had never entered Delves before from being able to configure Brann's role and abilities.

**Items and Rewards**

- Fixed a bug preventing delves and world activities from providing credit towards the Great Vault. As a reminder, next week, the Great Vault will provide Season 1 rewards.
- The Surge Pricing world event now awards Extravagant Tip Chest for 150 Shipping and Handling Tasks (was 250).
- Fixed an issue where the Adventurer's Warbound items sold by Finley for Undercoin would generate gear at the wrong item level.

**Quests**

- Players who selected a Cartel during "Contract Work" but were not given work to do can now reselect their Cartel for their Warband.
- Grimla should no longer unintentionally disappear while players are on “The Grimla Express”, “Split Venture”, or “Welcome to Undermine”.

**Undermine**

- The Suspicious Book and Fireworks Hat should no longer disappear prematurely, and players should be able to loot them as expected. Players who were previously affected by this bug will find the loot in their mail after about an hour.
- Lowered the number of tasks required to be completed to earn the highest Surge Pricing reward to 150, down from 250.
- Newspaper Bundles for the Paper Delivery job should no longer remain on the player if the job is canceled. This should also resolve the issue for players that have already completed or canceled the quest and had the bundles on their back.
