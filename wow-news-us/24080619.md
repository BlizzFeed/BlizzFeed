#### Here are this week's highlights:

##### General

- **April Fool's Patch notes**

  - **​**An April Fool's tradition, the [10.2.6 patch notes](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/world-of-warcraft-1062-patch-notes/1820477/1) went up on April 1st.

##### Dragonflight

- **Amirdrassil, the Dream’s End**:
  - A post was made about [Mythic Tindral and Fyrakk adjustments](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/amirdrassil-the-dreams-hope-mythic-tuning-incoming-april-2/1821047) and the reduction of damage, targets, and health of select abilties.
- **Noblegarden**
  - [The mount drop rate](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/noblegarden-new-rare-mount-post/1821099/6) from the new boss was increased and a first account kill bonus was added.
- **Dragonflight Season 4 Public Test Realm Dungeon Test Schedule**
  - A call has been made for players to join in the dungeon test schedule from [April 2 – April 8](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/feedback-dungeon-testing-april-1-april-8/1821588/1).

##### WoW Classic Season of Discovery Phase 3

- **Season of Discovery Phase 3**
  - The full [Content Update Notes](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/season-of-discovery-phase-3-content-update-notes/1821471) are now available.
- **Nightmare Incursions**
  - This week we [shared more information](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/nightmare-incursions/1821465) on Nightmare Incursions, what players can expect, and how they work.

##### Wath of the Lich King Classic

- **Wrath Classic Season 8 Ends Soon**
  - The [end of Wrath Classic Season 8 is coming](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/wrath-classic-pvp-season-8-ending-soon/1821435) and with it Arena Points will be converted to Honor.
