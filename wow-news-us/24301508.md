Azeroth is calling once more. *World of Warcraft: Forever* invites you to begin again in a reimagined original Azeroth, where familiar roads lead to new discoveries, the journey matters as much as the destination, and the world is ready to grow for years to come.

When *World of Warcraft: Forever* launches globally on November 4, 2026, at 3:00 p.m. PST, access will be included with your *World of Warcraft* Subscription or Game Time. Optional upgrade editions are available to pre-purchase now on the Battle.net Shop, including the Skyborne Heroic Pack, Skyborne Epic Pack, and the limited-time Warcraft Forever Collection. If you’re eager to get a hands-on preview ahead of launch, you can choose the Skyborne Epic Pack or Warcraft Forever Collection for access to the beta beginning September 17.

## Bring a Friend Along for Launch Week

Adventuring is always better with company. Qualifying purchases include one or more Invite-A-Friend Launch Codes that players can share with friends so they can join *World of Warcraft: Forever* during launch week, from November 4 through November 11, 2026. The Skyborne Heroic Pack includes one Invite-A-Friend Launch Code, while the Skyborne Epic Pack and Warcraft Forever Collection include three total codes.

Codes will be delivered by email beginning October 20, 2026, PST. Once received, copy the code from the email and send it directly to the friend you want to invite. Eligible invitees can redeem the code through Battle.net to access *World of Warcraft: Forever* without a subscription or game time during the launch-week window. These Invite-A-Friend Launch Codes do not include beta access. [Learn more from our support article.](https://support.blizzard.com/help/article/000385883)

## Step Into Beta and Prepare for Launch

*World of Warcraft: Forever* Beta begins September 17, 2026, PST, and runs through October 21, 2026, PST. Beta access is included with the Skyborne Epic Pack and the Warcraft Forever Collection, giving you an early look at the roads, stories, and systems waiting in this new World of Warcraft experience.

Early Name Reservation will be available from October 27 through November 3, 2026, PST, included with any upgrade purchase. You can create and customize up to three characters during this period, with character names reserved on a first-come, first-served basis. Desired names cannot be guaranteed.
