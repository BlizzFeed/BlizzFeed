## Discover the Complete Gameplay Expansion

Azeroth is calling, and this time, the story is yours to tell. D&D: World of Warcraft invites you to bring the legends, factions, and iconic adventures of World of Warcraft to your own campaign table, where your choices shape every battle, every alliance, and every legend. Recreate your main player character, roll a new alt, or play a hero the world has never seen.

### Six New Subclasses and Thirteen Class Skins

*D&D: World of Warcraft* brings the fantasy of all thirteen World of Warcraft classes to the tabletop.  Each class builds on a foundation from the *Player's Handbook,* then layers on alterations, new options, and guidance inspired by their World of Warcraft specializations.

The book adds **six new subclasses:** the **Oath of the Ebon Blade** and **Oath of the Holy** Paladin, the **Demon Hunter** Ranger, the **Shadow Domain** and **Discipline Domain** Cleric, and the **Path of the Elements** Barbarian.

Rounding out the roster are **three updated subclasses** (**Swashbuckler** Rogue, **Path of the Spiritual Guardian** Barbarian, and **Warrior of Mystic Brews** Monk).

### Sixteen Species Straight From Azeroth

In addition to new WoW-mapping rules for five existing species, *D&D: World of Warcraft* adds **eleven new species** —Dracthyr, Draenei, Earthen, Forsaken, Haranir, Mechagnome, Pandaren, Tauren, Troll, Vulpera, and Worgen—to help you build a character that feels unmistakably World of Warcraft.

From Azeroth's ancient races to newcomers from distant realms, these species bring a wide range of histories, cultures, and perspectives to your adventures. Your choice of species can even grant a starting Renown Score with one of Azeroth's factions—so whether you ride out “For the Horde!” or “For the Alliance!”, your allegiance is baked into your origin.

### Sixty Spells, Twenty-Five Magic Items, and Ten Plus Professions

*D&D: World of Warcraft* contains **60+ new spells** to equip your champion with their signature abilities, including well-known spells like Rune Strike, Demon Spikes, Moonfire, and more!

Every adventure deserves a reward! This book contains a treasure haul of popular **magic items**, **mounts,** and **companion creatures** to reward your exploration across Azeroth.

And questing is only half the story: choose from over ten **professions** to customize your hero between quests, turning downtime into a space to craft gear, tinker on trinkets, and prep upgrades.

### Fifty Monsters and Thirty Dungeon Bosses

Face Azeroth's greatest threats with **50+ new monsters** and **30 dungeon boss stat blocks** built for the climactic encounter peaks your party won't stop talking about.

From mysterious allies to legendary foes, your campaign will never be lacking for the creatures that make Azeroth unforgettable.

### Seven Iconic Dungeons Reimagined

Revisit your favorite encounters in Azeroth with **seven iconic World of Warcraft dungeons** rebuilt for tabletop play.

Each one is packed with boss encounters worthy of the legends — whether you're exploring a dungeon you’ve never tried before or diving into a fan-favorite you know by heart.

- Azjol-Nerub
- Blackfathom Deeps
- Deadmines
- Dire Maul
- Onyxia's Lair
- Scarlet Monastery
- Shadowfang Keep

### Twenty-Five Factions to Fortify Your Legacy

*D&D: World of Warcraft* introduces **20+ factions,** each with its own renown rewards for securing your legacy across Azeroth.

Built on the renown rules from the *Dungeon Master's Guide,* each faction tracks your reputation and pays out tangible and intangible rewards as it grows—new allies, safe passage through hostile territory, and perks reserved for those who've earned their trust.

Grind your rep with a smaller group for a quick favor, or pledge to one banner and rally to your chosen cause.
