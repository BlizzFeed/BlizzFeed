Hallow’s End celebrates the break of the Forsaken from the Scourge. Fun and mischief reign as the Innkeepers of Azeroth dole out treats and tricks for those seeking to partake in the festivities.

> **When:** October 18–November 1\
> **Where:** Trick-or-Treat throughout the Inns of Azeroth, Northrend, Pandaria, Outland, Draenor, the Broken Isles (Dalaran), the Dragon Isles, and Khaz Algar for tasty treats and currency to buy Hallow’s End goodies.

---

## Brave the Headless Horseman

> *“Prepare yourselves, the bells have tolled! Shelter your weak, your young, and your old! Each of you shall pay the final sum. Cry for mercy, the reckoning has come!” – The Headless Horseman*

Face the Headless Horseman’s haunting challenge daily for a chance to receive the **Headless Horseman's Ghoulish Charger** Skyriding mount, and you're guaranteed to gain ghastly pieces of the **Headless Horseman's Ghoulish Armor** transmog set to add to your spooktacular Collection.

[![Headless Horseman's Ghoulish Charger and Headless Horseman's Ghoulish Armor transmog set](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/hi/HI6MVYMCTA1M1760484415074.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/hi/HI6MVYMCTA1M1760484415074.png)

**Headless Horseman's Ghoulish Charger Skyriding mount, and Headless Horseman's Ghoulish Armor**

## Grab Some Ghastly Goodies

Hallow's End vendors sell a host of festive goods, which you can obtain by trading in your Tricky Treats.

### New on the Hollow's End Vendor

Use your Tricky Treats to procure new items, and old, including the **Patched Harvest Golem Transmog** set pieces that previously dropped from the Headless Horseman encounter.
