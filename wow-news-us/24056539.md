### Season of Discovery Phase 2 Goes Live February 8

![A Human standing near ruins in Stranglethorn Vale with an orange sky](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/y2/Y2ZRAS4NQX3L1704749964556.jpg)

It won’t be long before the Season of Discovery Phase 2 arrives. Adventurers can level from 25 to 40, earn up to 31 additional talent points, take on the challenge of Gnomeregan as a new raid dungeon, discover new runes and abilities, and more!

Realms go live with this exciting new update February 8 at 1:00 p.m. PST (21:00 GMT) around the world.

### Time is Running Out to Sign Up for the AWC and MDI in 2024

![AWC and MDI Logos along with a banner to the right](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/7u/7UVHRULDSP711705023032295.png)

Time is running out before the close of signups for the Arena World Championship (AWC) and Mythic Dungeon International (MDI) Season 3. It won’t be long before the competition swings into action. Don’t miss out on this opportunity for you and your team to climb your way into the winner’s circle!

> - Sign up for the MDI Dragonflight Season 3 on [Raider.IO](https://raider.io/tournaments) **by January 29, 2024**, at 11:00 a.m. PST (19:00 GMT).
> - Sign up for the AWC on [Raider.IO](https://raider.io/tournaments) **by January 26, 2024**, at 12:00 p.m. PST (20:00 GMT).

Learn more about the 2024 esports season in our [previously published article](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/news/24021893/unveiling-awc-mdi-in-2024).
