# Make Murtastic Memories with the Murkmorpher Toy

- URL: https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/en-us/news/24055601/make-murtastic-memories-with-the-murkmorpher-toy
![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/8g/8G09WB5LVZZ21706663330095.png)

> Deploy the Murkmorpher toy to make a mighty morphing moment for yourself or your party members.

---

Make a mighty morphing moment for yourself or your party members when you deploy the Murkmorpher toy.\* Deploying the toy will temporarily change your appearance into an ever-popular Westfall Story Murloc or other random, colorful variant of the amphibious biped variety.

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/6p/6PBLB6CVSR0N1706663521317.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/6p/6PBLB6CVSR0N1706663521317.png)

Players can morph themselves and other party members into playful murlocs once every 60 minutes. The transformation lasts 20 minutes and stays active in combat, even after death.

[Make Murloc Morphing Magic](https://shop.battle.net/product/world-of-warcraft-toy-magical-murkmorpher)

---

\* Not available in World of Warcraft Classic games and requires World of Warcraft® Subscription or Game Time.
