#### Here are this week's highlights:

- **Latest Class Tuning Changes:** Details on the latest round of [class tuning](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/class-tuning-incoming-with-embers-of-neltharion-patch/1577047/) includes a variety of power increases for specs, including Havoc Demon Hunters, Arcane Mages, Assassination Rogues, Fury Warriors, and Windwalker Monks.
- **The Twitch Drop Issue Has Been Resolved:** Players are now correctly receiving the [Ethereal Portal Toy](http://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/twitch-portal-blazing-hippogryph/1579575/18) for this week’s Twitch Drop and can get the [Blazing Hippogryph Mount](http://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/en-us/news/23938290/dragonflight-twitch-drops-get-your-ethereal-portal-now) next week.
- **The Embers of Neltharion Storyline Continues:** [Two new quest chapters](http://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/time-gate-or-renown-locked-story-and-slitherdrake/1580061/6) will be available next week, along with access to the Slitherdrake Mount.
- **Vault of the Incarnates Tuning:** Due to an unintended increase in difficulty, [changes have been made](https://twitter.com/WarcraftDevs/status/1653616351175643137) to the Broodkeeper Diurna and Raszageth encounters to bring the difficulty of those encounters back in line.
- **Sparks of Shadowflame in Season 2:** Details on how [Sparks of Shadowflame](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/sparks-of-shadowflame-for-altsreturning-players/1577701/2) will work for professions in Season 2 were shared, along with how catchup mechanics will work.

##### Wrath of the Lich King Classic

- **Free Character Transfers Available:** A new set of [free character transfers](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/character-transfers-update-may-1/1558792/100) are now available for Wrath of the Lich King Classic.
- **Call of the Crusade on PTR:** Details on the Call of the Crusade [Patch 3.4.2 PTR](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/wrath-of-the-lich-king-classic-patch-342-testing-schedule/1580625) were shared this week, along with a schedule on when certain content will be available for testing.
