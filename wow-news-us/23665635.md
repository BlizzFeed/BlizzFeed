# Arena World Championship Season 1 Finals are Here!

- URL: https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/en-us/news/23665635/arena-world-championship-season-1-finals-are-here
![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/8u/8UD31U0WBWEI1619452713730.jpg)

> The AWC Season 1 Finals are live on YouTube May 1 - 2, beginning at 10 am PT!

---

​

***The top teams from the Arena World Championship (AWC) Circuit are competing this weekend for their share of the $200,000 (USD) global prize pool in the Season 1 Finals!***

From more than 200 teams competing in Cup 1, to only four teams remaining in both Europe and North America, the final contest for which teams will reign supreme is here. The top four teams in each region from the AWC Circuit face off this weekend for their share of the $200,000 (USD) global prize pool and title of Regional Champions. You can catch all of the PvP action on [YouTube.com/Warcraft](https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCbLj9QP9FAaHs_647QckGtg), beginning at 10 am this Saturday!

### Details

- **Venue:** Online - [YouTube.com/Warcraft](https://www.youtube.com/Warcraft)
- **Dates:** EU – Saturday, May 1 / NA – Sunday, May 2
- **Broadcast Start Time:** 10:00 AM PT
- **Prize Pool:** $200,000 (USD) total, with $100,000 (USD) per region
- **Format:** 3v3, Best of 5 / Best of 7 Finals
- **Language Streams on YouTube:**
  - [English](https://www.youtube.com/Warcraft)
  - [German](http://youtube.com/warcraftDE)
  - [Portuguese (LATAM)](http://youtube.com/worldofwarcraft/PTBR)
  - [Spanish (EU)](https://www.youtube.com/channel/UC6whzKjm0XOAh15s1DNL7yA)
