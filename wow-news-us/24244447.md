Decorate your new home with a pack of eight adorable plushies: four Beloved Lions and four Beloved Wolves. Perfect for shelves, sofas, or cozy corners, they can bring a touch of charm to every room in your home.

These small Housing decor items arrive for the cost of 500 Hearthsteel and contain 4 of each plush in the pack.

The Plushie Decor Pack\* includes the following:

- 4 Beloved Lion Plushie (Alliance)
- 4 Beloved Wolf Plushie (Horde)

![a yellow lion plush toy](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/8D8UP3IFIMFS1770834936118.png)
![a gray wolf plush toy](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/K8DH374Y6XMD1770834936165.png)

You can also purchase the Beloved Lion Plushie and Beloved Wolf Plushie individually from the in-game shop or the Battle.net shop.

Hearthsteel can be purchased in various denominations at the rate of 100 Hearthsteel per $1.

[Buy Now](https://shop.battle.net/family/world-of-warcraft/items/2025865/)

### What is Hearthsteel?

Hearthsteel is a new in-game currency for *World of Warcraft* that is purchased with real money using your Battle.net balance to acquire Housing items from the Battle.net shop and within the game. Visit the shop to see the available Hearthsteel bundle options.

Learn more about the Hearthsteel virtual currency in our [previously published article](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/news/24242736/).

*\*Not available in World of Warcraft Classic® games. Requires World of Warcraft® Subscription or Game Time.Use and placement of decor items is limited to the Housing feature, which requires access to World of Warcraft®: Midnight.*

*Bundled pack offer available through March 4, 2026.*
