## The Reven Pack Raises Over $2 Million to Support CureDuchenne —Thank you!

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/av/AVW2H42IPCKU1729721575389.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/av/AVW2H42IPCKU1729721575389.png)

Thanks to your overwhelming generosity, kindness, and compassion, we’ve raised over 2 million USD from sales of the Reven Pack to support CureDuchenne!

The Reven Pack was inspired by  the incredible story captured in “The Remarkable Life of Ibelin” documentary about Mats “Ibelin” Steen—[now live on Netflix](https://www.netflix.com/title/81759420)— a Norwegian WoW® player who suffered from Duchenne muscular dystrophy. You can learn more about Mats “Ibelin” Steen and his story on the [official Microsoft news site](https://news.microsoft.com/source/features/work-life/his-own-body-weakened-by-rare-disease-gamer-adopts-stronger-one-leaves-legacy-within-world-of-warcraft/).

During the campaign we saw a groundswell of support from all over the world as you gathered together to celebrate Mats’ life. Many took time to visit his in-game memorial— joining with his friends of many years in their annual run in his memory. It was a true testament to your incredible caring and compassion which has resonated throughout the world.

All proceeds from The Reven Pack sales support CureDuchenne, a global nonprofit leader in research, patient care, and innovation for improving and extending the lives of individuals with Duchenne muscular dystrophy, a progressive neuromuscular disease.

## Season of Discovery Phase 7— Naxxramas Now Live!

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_header/3t/3TZJ0MZDFU4C1606891276802.jpg)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_header/3t/3TZJ0MZDFU4C1606891276802.jpg)

Are you ready to face the terrors within Naxxramas?

Floating above the Plaguelands, the necropolis known as Naxxramas serves as the seat of one of the Lich King’s most powerful officers, the dreaded lich Kel’Thuzad. Horrors of the past and new terrors yet to be unleashed are gathering inside the necropolis as the Lich King’s servants prepare their assault.

Tuned for 20 players, you can venture into these hallowed halls with up to 40 players to take on the terrors within and reap the rewards with new mechanics and added “Empower” difficulty. You’ll discover Sanctified gear which can be improved with the new Seal of the Dawn trinket to take on the dangers in Naxxramas at the highest difficulty. Players will also receive more loot drops when a quarter is empowered, including new cosmetic items for your collection.

Within the Frostwyrm Lair, raid bosses Sapphiron and Kel’Thuzad have more secrets to uncover when all bosses are defeated at their highest difficulty.

We can’t wait to see you battle your way through the halls of Naxxramas and anywhere danger takes you in Azeroth!

- **Format:** Mythic Keystone Pushing against the clock. Details on format changes are below!

## Carve Out a Rewarding Career in Undermine Cartels

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_header/75/75UK771KIZ7D1738623211675.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_header/75/75UK771KIZ7D1738623211675.png)

Get a glimpse into goblin culture, society, and unparalleled creativity as you navigate the intricate politics and confront the sinister plans of Xal'atath; you'll meet the four major goblin cartels: Bilgewater, Steamwheedle, Blackwater, and the Venture Company.

Notorious goblins like Monte Gazlowe, Baron Revilgaz, Marin Noggenfogger, and more run each cartel. A secret fifth cartel waits in the wings for players to learn about a bit later. Which of these cartels you align with is up to you; your experience will change within the Undermine depending on which one you choose every week. Around Undermine, knowing the right people is crucial to a rewarding career with plenty of unique opportunities.

Dig through [our article](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/news/24173073) to prepare for a lucrative career working for the Cartels of Undermine.
