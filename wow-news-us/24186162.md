### The Liberation of Undermine is Now Live!

[![Video](https://markdown-videos-api.jorgenkh.no/youtube/4Saa-UkuF2k?width=1280&height=720)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4Saa-UkuF2k)

**Raid Unlock Schedule:**

- **Week of March 4:** Raid Finder Wing 1: Shock and Awesome (Cauldron of Carnage; Rik Reverb), Normal, Heroic, Mythic Difficulty

- **Week of March 25:** Raid Finder Wing 4: The Chrome King (Chrome King Gallywix)

### Go Inside the 11.1.5 Content Update

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_header/km/KMSEZSHJ7KOW1741129032909.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_header/km/KMSEZSHJ7KOW1741129032909.png)

The 11.1.5 content update is making its way to the PTR, bringing some new changes and additions that will roll out in the weeks after the update is released.

**Defend the Arathi in the Nightfall Scenario**

Players will take up arms alongside a new faction, Flame’s Radiance, to defend the Arathi against the Sureki forces following the defeat of Queen Ansurek.

**Dastardly Duos**

Players can experience a new six-week event providing a variety of cosmetics, toys, and even some dungeon items based on the bosses you encounter. Step up to take on ‘animatronic’ versions of past bosses, gain powerups, and strive for higher and higher scores for bragging rights. You and your party will fight two (or more) bosses at a time to rack up your highest score.

**Horrific Visions Revisited**

Return to N’Zoth’s corrupted versions of Stormwind and Orgrimmar, complete objectives, and take on the challenges within. As you endure these encounters, you’ll need to maintain your sanity or fail.

**UI Update**

We are implementing a new UI update that introduces a Cooldown Manager. This feature allows players to better track class ability cooldowns. This additional functionality can be personalized through the edit mode within the UI.

We expand on each of these features in our [previously published article](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/news/24179070).

We will share more details on the content schedule in the weeks ahead, and we look forward to seeing you on the PTR!
