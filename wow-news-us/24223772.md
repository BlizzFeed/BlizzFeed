*The War Within* Season 2 wraps up with the release of the Ghosts of K’aresh content update.  If you’re a completionist, then this handy list of all the things that will become unavailable or change at the end of Season 2 on August 5 and with the release of The War Within Season 3 on August 12 is for you!

---

### *The War Within* Season 2 Meta-Achievements

![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/ARDCZXKH6G3Y1739306004582.png)

There are two Meta-Achievements that players may want to complete for rewards before August 5.

***The War Within* Season 2: Master Blaster**- Accomplish one of the following feats during Season Two of *The War Within* on your current character:

- Attain 1600+ PvP Rating
- Attain 2000+ Mythic+ Rating
- Defeat Chrome King Gallywix on Heroic or Mythic Difficulty

Completing this Meta-Achievement will reward Mark of the Spelunker Supreme which can be traded for either a PvE or PvP item.

**Dressed to the Mines:** Complete one of the following achievements or Feats of Strength listed below.

- **Mythic: Chrome King Gallywix:** Defeat Chrome King Gallywix on Mythic difficulty.
- **The War Within Keystone Hero:**Season Two: Attain a Mythic+ Rating of at least 2500 during The War Within Season 2.
- **Elite: The War Within Season 2:** Earn the rank of Elite during *The War Within* season 2. Rewards the seasonal character title, “the Elite.”

For completing this Meta-Achievement, you’ll receive the Crystallized Essence of Kaja'mite. “Grants one Essence of Kaja'mite capable of imbuing armor with incredible power at the Catalyst in Dornogal.”

Once the season ends, the only way to earn this achievement is by completing the Mythic raid achievement criteria. The other two criteria options will only be available during Season 2.

### *The War Within* Season 2 Raid Rewards

![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/vg/VGE17XNT1AJN1739306940332.jpg)

As the season draws to a close, players will want to make sure they finish a few achievements in Liberation of Undermine before they become Feats of Strength and can no longer be earned.

- **Ahead of the Curve:** Chrome King Gallywix- Defeat Chrome King Gallywix on Heroic difficulty or higher before the release of the next raid tier.
- **Cutting Edge:** Chrome King Gallywix- Defeat Chrome King Gallywix on Mythic difficulty before the release of the next raid tier.
- **Hall of Fame:** Chrome King Gallywix: Be among the first two hundred guilds worldwide to defeat Chrome King Gallywix on Mythic difficulty.

For completing the Hall of Fame: Chrome King Gallywix achievement, players will earn the title **Famed Slayer of The Chrome King**.

The Keys to the Big G will still be available as a reward for killing Chrome King Gallyiwx and the drop chance will remain the same until the release of the next expansion when it will become a very rare drop.

Puzzling Cartel Chips, their quests, and the vendor will no longer be available with the end of Season 2.
