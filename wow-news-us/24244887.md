## Add the Omnium Folio to Your Library of Tools Against the Void

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/4o/4OEWSZ3AHHRO1777415546081.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/4o/4OEWSZ3AHHRO1777415546081.png)

Join Magister Umbric and Grand Magister Rommath to restore the reawakened Sunstrider Omnium, an ancient elven relic once created by Dath’remar Sunstrider to study and preserve the schools of magic.

Once unlocked, the Omnium Folio grants access to potent runes for battle; continue activities to imbue additional power and unlock its full potential against the forces of the Void. These rune powers will be available to players throughout the rest of the *Midnight* expansion.

You can learn more about the Sunstrider Omnium and its history by heading over to our [previously published article](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/news/24277442).

## The MDI Groups Stage Continues this Weekend

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/jd/JD5PBLAKARV11777587619285.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/jd/JD5PBLAKARV11777587619285.png)

The Groups Stage is the month-long period during which the top 24 teams compete in weekly matches.

Each weekend, eight teams compete in a head-to-head speedrunning format in the hopes of securing one of the top two placements and qualifying further in the competition.

This weekend, from May 29–31, Group China matches will be broadcast live on the official [Warcraft Twitch](https://www.twitch.tv/warcraft) and [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/warcraft) channels starting at 10:00 am PDT / 19:00 CEST each day.

Learn more from our previously published [MDI overview article](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/news/24276958).
