# Viewer's Guide: WoW Classic Summer Bowl

- URL: https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/en-us/news/23453342/viewer-s-guide-wow-classic-summer-bowl
![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/ge/GE2AHH0J8L8B1592433957793.jpg)

> Tune in beginning Saturday, June 20 at 10:00 a.m. PDT to watch the first WoW Classic esport event of the summer.

---

The World of Warcraft Classic Summer Bowl is about to get underway! Are you ready to get HYPE as teams battle in Warsong Gulch, putting their combat skills and flag capturing abilities to the test? The time for waiting is over, the WoW Classic Summer Bowl starts this weekend. Watch live on [YouTube.com/Warcraft](https://www.youtube.com/Warcraft) and [Twitch.tv/Warcraft](http://www.twitch.tv/Warcraft) beginning Saturday, June 20 at 10:00 am PDT.

---

### Sign up now

Sign-ups are still open for EU competitors until Thursday, June 18 at 10:00 am PDT and NA competitors until Thursday, June 25 at 10:00 am PDT. Eligible players with a level 60 character in WoW Classic can still sign-up for a chance to compete for a separate $4,000 (USD) prize pool for North America and Europe — distributed amongst the best two teams in the region’s finals. Check eligibility and rules, then register your team for NA [here](http://gamebattles.majorleaguegaming.com/pc/world-of-warcraft/tournament/wow-classic-summer-bowl-na) and EU [here](https://gamebattles.majorleaguegaming.com/pc/world-of-warcraft/tournament/wow-classic-summer-bowl-eu)!
