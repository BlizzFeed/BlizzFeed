# Collect Trader’s Tender in Midnight’s Winds of Mysterious Fortune

> The forecast calls for fortune! Log in during the Winds of Mysterious Fortune event to earn Trader’s Tender, collect Mysterious Satchels, and breeze through your adventures with bonus experience and reputation gains.

---

The Winds of Mysterious Fortune are blowing in your favor, so tie down your hat and prepare to collect the fortunes whirling about.

> **When**: September 8 through 22 (the event begins and ends with weekly realm restarts)

---

## The Forecast Calls for Trader’s Tender

While the gales are gusting during the Winds of Mysterious Fortune event, complete the Feat of Strength achievement “Winds of Particularly Mysterious Fortune” to earn 500 Trader’s Tender.

Simply log in with any character on any five days during the event, and you’ll be able to collect 500 Trader’s Tender in the Collector’s Cache chest to spend at the Trading Post.

---

## A Buff to Speed Things Along

Take advantage of the Winds of Mysterious Fortune 20% experience buff to get your level 10–89 characters up to speed. Catch up on your alts and finish missed content and unlocks while facing the dangers in *World of Warcraft: Midnight*.

This buff is automatically applied at login during the event and provides a 50% reputation gain with all factions worldwide, excluding Zul’jarra’s Forces and Tokka’s Crew.

---

## You Get a Satchel, and You Get a Satchel

As the wind blows, so do satchels chock-full of valuable and unique items. While questing and adventuring, players below level 90 can earn **Mysterious Satchels** bursting with goodies by looting creatures or as a bonus reward for completing quests.

**Mysterious Satchels** contain various unique and valuable items, including temporary stat boosts, visual alterations, a chance for bonus gear appropriate to your level and specialization, and 10% experience potions that stack with the 20% experience buff, to a maximum of 30%. Experience potions are Warbound items that can be consumed during the event or saved until after the event for use on your other level 10–89 characters. However, any stat or elemental potion effects will not work in Mythic+ raids or rated PvP content.

If you think Mysterious Satchels are amazing, you’ll love **Pristine Mysterious Satchels**. The ***first*** Mysterious Satchel drop you earn each day will be Pristine quality, giving you a greater chance to earn even more valuable rewards.
