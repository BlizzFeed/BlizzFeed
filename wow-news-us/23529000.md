# Viewer’s Guide: WoW Classic Fall Conquest

- URL: https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/en-us/news/23529000/viewers-guide-wow-classic-fall-conquest
![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/jm/JM4LTYU4FJ0G1600715455911.jpg)

> Tune in beginning Saturday, October 3 at 10:00 a.m. PDT to watch the WoW Classic Fall Conquest!

---

***The World of Warcraft Classic Fall Conquest is ready to invade the Arathi Basin! Watch live on*** [***YouTube.com/Warcraft***](https://www.youtube.com/Warcraft) ***and*** [***Twitch.tv/Warcraft***](http://www.twitch.tv/Warcraft) ***beginning Saturday, October 3 at 10:00 a.m. PDT.***

The 15v15 Arathi Basin WoW Classic Fall Conquest begins this Saturday with the EU qualifiers! Eligible players with a level 60 character in WoW Classic can still sign-up for a chance to compete for a separate $6,000 (USD) prize pool for North America and Europe – distributed amongst the best two teams in each region’s finals. Sign-ups are still open for EU competitors until Thursday, October 1 at 10:00 a.m. PDT and NA competitors until Thursday, October 8 at 10:00 a.m. PDT. Check eligibility and rules, then register your team for NA [here](https://gamebattles.majorleaguegaming.com/pc/world-of-warcraft/tournament/wow-classic-fall-conquest-na) and EU [here](https://gamebattles.majorleaguegaming.com/pc/world-of-warcraft/tournament/wow-classic-fall-conquest-eu)!
