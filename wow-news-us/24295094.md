## Curse of Ula'tek Housing Updates: New Blueprints, Pets, and More Arrive for Your Home

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_header/xy/XYZ3V4XL8Z9R1786146532533.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_header/xy/XYZ3V4XL8Z9R1786146532533.png)

The Curse of Ula’tek update brings new ways to shape, share, and settle into your Housing space. With Blueprints to save and swap your favorite builds, Pet Beds to welcome your trusted companions indoors, and a variety of new updates to expand your decorating options, your home is ready for a little more personality.

Blueprints let you capture a favorite Housing layout and keep it ready for whenever inspiration strikes. Save a single room, your interior, your exterior, or your entire home, then swap between builds any time you want to refresh a space, test a new idea, or return to an old favorite without rebuilding it from scratch.

Visit your neighborhood vendors, gather your favorite companions, and stop by the [official forums](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/c/gameplay/housing-blueprints/) to find and share Blueprints with other players—then let your creativity run wild as you make your home into a space worth saving, sharing, and showing off with the Housing updates in Curse of Ula’tek.

## Undertake Four New Endeavors in Your Neighborhood!

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_header/b4/B4IVGNSRSDLG1786058996864.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_header/b4/B4IVGNSRSDLG1786058996864.png)

Your neighborhood is about to receive new visitors, each bringing a variety of tasks, stories, and items to purchase. Meet back up with Griftah for “fine Amani items”, lend a helping hand to the Ohn’ahran centaurs, attend a candle-making workshop with the kobolds of the Ringing Deeps, and relax with the worldly tortollans.

With the launch of Curse of Ula’tek, the Knock-off Amani Endeavor is available immediately and ties into activities on [the Coiled Isle](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/news/24293963/). After that, you’ll be able to choose from an array of Endeavors as new visitors make their way to your neighborhood.

Players may also notice new additions around their Neighborhood based on the Endeavors they have completed, both old and new, so keep an eye out during your visits. We look forward to seeing you in the neighborhood!
