### Link Your Battle.net Account to Discord in the Curse of Ula’tek Content Update

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_header/m8/M8JNOUTFZDPK1781723339591.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_header/m8/M8JNOUTFZDPK1781723339591.png)

It won’t be long before you’ll be able to speak with your guildmates both in and out of the game by connecting your Battle.net and Discord accounts to chat with your guild no matter where they are.

Whether you’re coordinating your next raid run, looking for more to join you in game for PvP, or just hanging out in your neighborhoods together, you’ll be able to send messages between the two quickly and easily. This new feature will be available to test soon on the PTR and once it goes live, it will be easy to get set up and started.

Learn more from our [previously published article](http://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/news/24286284).

### Twitch Drop Now Live! Get the Cuddly Cotton Candy Grrgle Housing Decor Item

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_header/q4/Q40HKVUI2W0J1780944349760.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_header/q4/Q40HKVUI2W0J1780944349760.png)

Watch any eligible World of Warcraft stream on Twitch.tv from June 16 at 10:00 am PDT until July 14 at 10:00 am PDT to claim the **Cuddly Cotton Candy Grrgle** Housing decor item. Players who have claimed this new item from the Twitch Drop will also be able to visit Dennia Silvertongue in Silvermoon City to purchase more copies of this adorable decor. Additional copies can also be purchased from Tuuran in Stormwind and Gabbi in Orgrimmar (near the Trading Post).

**Start Time:** June 16 at 10:00 am PDT\
**End Time:** July 14 at 10:00 am PDT

Watch **4 hours** of any *World of Warcraft* content stream on Twitch.tv while these Twitch Drops are active to claim the **Cuddly Cotton Candy Grrgle** Housing decor item.

Frequently asked questions and details on linking your Twitch account can be found in our [previously published article](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/news/24267734).
