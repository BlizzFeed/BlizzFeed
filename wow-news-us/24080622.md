#### The Worldsoul Saga Begins Here!

*[World of Warcraft®: The War Within™](http://thewarwithin.blizzard.com/)* arrives this year1, and the wings of a grand new adventure will spread widely across the next three expansions.2 Be a part of the epic story that commemorates the first 20 years of *World of Warcraft* and sets new foundations for Azeroth's future!2

The [*World of Warcraft: The War Within* 20th Anniversary Collector's Edition](https://gear.blizzard.com/products/wowccl0003-world-of-warcraft-the-war-within-collectors-edition) is now available for pre-purchase and comes with an array of rare and commemorative items to aid in your journey into the depths of Azeroth.

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/68/68ROJICPJ8ZY1713288060997.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/68/68ROJICPJ8ZY1713288060997.png)

- ***The Art of The War Within***—Leaf through the visual evolution of *The War Within* expansion with this beautifully bound lexicon filled with exclusive art.
- **Collectible Pin**—Featuring Thrall, Anduin, and Alleria primed for battle.
- **World of Warcraft 20th Anniversary Gryphon Rider Statue**—Perched and ready to defend from above.

[Pre-Purchase today!](https://gear.blizzard.com/products/wowccl0003-world-of-warcraft-the-war-within-collectors-edition)

The collector's edition is available for pre-purchase exclusively on the Blizzard Gear Store. It includes everything in ***The War Within* Epic Edition**—an Enhanced Level 70 Character Boost, 1,000 Trader's Tender4, 30 Days of Game Time, plus more!

Already pre-purchased an edition of *The War Within*? No problem! These three new commemorative collectibles can still be added to your trove. When you purchase the [*World of Warcraft: The War Within* 20th Anniversary Collector's Edition](https://gear.blizzard.com/products/wowccl0003-world-of-warcraft-the-war-within-collectors-edition), you’ll receive a *Battle.net®* balance reimbursement for your previous purchase to use in-game or on *Battle.net*.

#### Get in the Beta

Players who pre-purchase the World of Warcraft®: The War Within™ 20th Anniversary Collector's Edition from the Blizzard Gear Store will receive a beta code\* emailed to the registered email they purchase the Collector’s Edition from. Those who have already pre-purchased the Collector’s Edition can expect to start receiving emails in the next week.

Please make sure to check your spam filter for your email. If you have issues receiving your key, please contact Legends Support for more assistance. To redeem your code, log into your account and navigate to your Account Overview and enter your code for the account you are claiming it on. Visit our support page for more information.

Purchasers in Australia and New Zealand will need to contact their local retailer for additional information on obtaining their beta key.

\*One beta key per purchase while supplies last.
