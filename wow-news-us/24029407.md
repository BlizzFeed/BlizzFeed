#### Here are this week's highlights:

#### Dragonflight

- **Seeds of Renewal PTR Begins**: Check out the current [development notes](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/seeds-of-renewal-ptr-development-notes/1713534/2) for this upcoming content update, including Follow Dungeons, Azerothian Archives, worldwide Dragonriding, and the Reclaiming of Gilneas. A [forum](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/c/in-development/seeds-of-renewal-ptr-discussion/301) was also opened for player feedback after they’ve tested the new content.
- **Display Issues for the Great Vault**: Some players who complete tasks, like Dungeons, are not seeing the credit in the Great Vault—this is a [display issue](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/great-vault-dungeon/1705984/23) and is actively being looked into.
- **Consumables in Unrated Battlegrounds**: Some changes were made to how [consumables](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/changes-to-consumables-in-unrated-battlegrounds-with-season-3/1710727/51) will work in Season 3 Unrated Battlegrounds.

#### Classic

- **Classic Era 1.15.0 Update**: [Patch notes](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/wow-classic-era-version-1150-patch-notes/1711439) for this update were posted earlier this week.
- **Classic Era Patch 1.15.0 Known Issues**: A [known issues](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/patch-1150-known-issues-list-updated-11142023/1711158) thread for this update was posted earlier this week.
