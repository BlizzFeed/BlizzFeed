Send shivers down the spines of your enemies in modern *World of Warcraft*\* astride the **Herald of Sa'bak** skyriding mount\*\*. You'll also have no shortage of adorable adventures in WoW Classic Progression (soon to be *Mists of Pandaria Classic™)*\* with a tiny **Sa'bak's Favored** pet\*\*\* tagging along.

[![Video](https://markdown-videos-api.jorgenkh.no/youtube/hngk0O1X8Yk?width=1280&height=720)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hngk0O1X8Yk)

Get a [6-Month Subscription](https://shop.battle.net/product/world-of-warcraft-subscription) and receive the **Herald of Sa'bak** skyriding mount*\*\** to set scale for the horizons in modern *World of Warcraft*, and the tiny **Sa'bak's Favored** pet in WoW Classic Progression. The mount and pet are also available separately through the Battle.net Shop.

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/lo/LOYN4CFXLUCR1752087772608.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/lo/LOYN4CFXLUCR1752087772608.png)

Players on existing 6-Month or 12-Month recurring subscription plans with a future renewal date will receive the **Herald of Sa'bak** mount and the **Sa'bak's Favored** pet at no additional charge, no later than July 31, 2025 (must be redeemed by July 31, 2026) through Battle.net.
