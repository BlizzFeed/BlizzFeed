#### Here are this week's highlights:

##### Dragonflight

- **Fury Incarnate PTR Update:** On Tuesday, the [latest development notes](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/dragonflight-fury-incarnate-ptr-development-notes/1631163/5) accompanied a new build of the PTR. These outlined class changes and updates to the Hallow’s End annual event.
- **Cross Realm Trading Re-enabled:** Players can now use the [Cross Realm Trading](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/update-cross-realm-trading-is-available/1631978/99) feature in-game.
- **Latest PvP Class Changes:** The final [PvP changes](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/class-tuning-incoming-%E2%80%93-july-25/1636876/300) for this week were posted on Monday.

##### WoW Classic

- **Classic Era 1.14.4 PTR Update**: On Thursday, [new development notes](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/wow-classic-era-1144-development-notes/1621723/8 "https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/wow-classic-era-1144-development-notes/1621723/8") accompanied a new build of the PTR. These outlined the bug fixes and adjustments made in the latest build.
- **Coming Soon to 1.14.4**: Several minor improvements to Classic Era and Hardcore are [in the works](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/coming-with-future-ptr-updates/1640858 "https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/coming-with-future-ptr-updates/1640858").
