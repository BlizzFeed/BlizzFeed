#### Here are this week's highlights:

##### Dragonflight

- **Class Tuning Changes and Hotfixes:** Class tuning adjustments were announced for [Holy Paladins](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/updated-mage-and-paladin-tuning-incoming-july-18/1631728/210) on Monday, and on Tuesday, Augmentation Evoker updates were posted with the [hotfixes](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/en-us/news/23973739/hotfixes-july-19-2023).
- **Latest Fury Incarnate PTR Notes:** On Tuesday, the latest Fury Incarnate 10.1.7 PTR [development notes](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/dragonflight-fury-incarnate-ptr-development-notes/1631163/4) were posted, including details on Dragonriding Challenge Courses.

##### WoW Classic

- **Latest Classic Era 1.14.4 PTR Notes:** The latest WoW Classic Era 1.14.4 PTR [development notes](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/wow-classic-era-1144-development-notes/1621723/7) were posted on Thursday, including the Classic Hardcore PTR coming back online, PvP Honor System updates, and UI updates.
- **Brawl with the Blues Event Rescheduled:** A [Brawl with the Blues event](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/brawl-with-the-blues-part-two-july-21/1634873) was announced this week but was delayed until August 4 while additional updates are being worked on.
