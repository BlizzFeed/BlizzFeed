# Cleverly Soar the Skies with The Wings and Wisdom Pack

- URL: https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/en-us/news/24194654/cleverly-soar-the-skies-with-the-wings-and-wisdom-pack

> Climb atop glorious Great Ravens and adorn yourself in feathery garb inspired by Khadgar and Medivh.

---

Climb atop glorious Great Ravens and adorn yourself in feathery garb inspired by Khadgar and Medivh when you sweep up the Wings and Wisdom Pack\*. You will receive the Feathered Archmage and Feathered Prophet transmog sets, including staff weapon transmogs\*\* (staff weapon transmogs are not available for the following classes: Death Knight, Demon Hunter, Paladin, and Rogue), along with the two Great Raven mounts.

Both Great Raven mounts and transmog sets are shared across all present and future WoW® Modern characters, and the mounts automatically scale to the fastest riding skill known by each character. If characters aren't at a high enough level to use a flying mount, the Great Raven mounts are also ground mounts.

The Wings and Wisdom Pack includes: Prophet's Great Raven, Feathered Cowl of the Prophet, Feathered Mantle of the Prophet, Feathered Drape of the Prophet, Standard of the Prophet, Archmage's Great Raven, Feathered Cowl of the Archmage, Feathered Mantle of the Archmage, Feathered Drape of the Archmage, Standard of the Archmage.
