#### SEPULCHER OF THE FIRST ONES

![WoW_9-2Sepulcher_Blog-Thumb_1200x675_JM01.jpg](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/DHH6DUVGBAO61644355507774.jpg)

Pursue Zovaal's forces into the Sepulcher of the First Ones and prepare for the most epic Shadowlands raid yet—coming with the start of Season 3.

The Jailer has breached the Sepulcher, the mysterious heart of the Shadowlands. Should he control its primordial power, he will reshape the cosmos with Death at the fore. As Zovaal prepares to dominate reality, champions of Azeroth must charge forth to stop him.

Read more about the new Sepulcher of the First Ones raid and raid schedule, rewards, and more coming in Eternity's End with Season 3 of Shadowlands [here](https://worldofwarcraft.com/news/23774003/).

#### ETERNITY’S END CLASS SETS

With the start of Shadowlands Season 3, players will have an opportunity to start collecting an all-new 5-piece Class Set; giving you bonuses based on your specialization when you equip two or four pieces of the set. There are several ways to acquire set pieces—defeat bosses in the new Sepulcher of the First Ones raid in any difficulty, get them directly from the Great Vault after Season 3 begins, or use the Creation Catalyst to turn any non-set pieces of the appropriate slot into a new set piece of the same item level.

Learn more about the Class Set [here](https://worldofwarcraft.com/news/23778646/).

#### NEW REWARDS FOR MYTHIC+, KEYSTONE MASTER, AND ARENA

Mythic+ in Shadowlands includes a brand-new affix: *Encrypted*—enemies throughout the dungeon possess relics of the First Ones. Destroy the relics to summon the First Ones' Automa and gain powerful bonuses based on the order in which they are destroyed.

Two new titles will be available: *The Cryptic* and *The Cryptic Hero*, and those able to earn the Season 3 Keystone Master achievement will earn the Wastewarped Deathwalker mount.

![WoW_9-2Mounts_Blog-Lightbox01_1280x720_JM01.jpg](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/zf/ZF741AE4YOLO1645673323588.jpg)

A new Arena title: *Cosmic Gladiator*, along with a new weapon illusion, a new color variation of the Cosmic Gladiator Soul Eater mount, and new Vicious mounts–Alliance and Horde themed Warstalker and Croaker—are part of the rewards lineup this season.

#### NEW ARENA: MALDRAXXUS COLISEUM

![WoW-9.2-Maldraxxus-Coliseum-3.jpg](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/w2/W2Y4BXH08C821645064905884.jpg)

For eons, the Necrolords of Maldraxxus honed their craft at the Maldraxxus Coliseum in the Theater of Pain. Now the Arena is open to any would-be challengers—and there's no shortage of seasoned gladiators and newcomers willing to test their mettle in brave combat against their foes. #### NEW BRAWL: SOLO SHUFFLE

![Solo-Shuffle-Thumb-v2.jpg](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/vt/VTYTUBGAB7EJ1637200884854.jpg)

The Solo Shuffle brawl pits six players (two healers, four damage dealers) against each other in exciting 3v3 Arena matches. As the name implies, you can only queue for this brawl solo. Play a series of short matches as teams are shuffled and earn rewards based on your performance. Queue for Solo Shuffle beginning March 1, and unlike other Brawls, Solo Shuffle will be permanently available and not on a rotating schedule. Learn more [here.](https://worldofwarcraft.com/news/23745827/)
