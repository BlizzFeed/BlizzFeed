As Manaforge Omega opens its doors in World of Warcraft: Ghosts of K'aresh, the ultimate test begins as top players and guilds race to face a series of arcane challenges culminating in a final encounter with Dimensius the All-Devouring. This demanding Race to World First demands teamwork, strategy, and resilience as competitors strive to overcome every hazard to secure Azeroth's future.
