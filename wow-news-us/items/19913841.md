# Don’t Miss the European Road to BlizzCon this Weekend

- URL: https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/en-us/news/19913841/dont-miss-the-european-road-to-blizzcon-this-weekend
- Date: 2015-10-02T23:50:00Z
![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/BQMGIXD70HPC1443019841508.jpg)

The 2015 European Road to BlizzCon gets underway in Prague, Czech Republic this weekend, October 3-4, and it’s going to be epic! If you’re an eSports fan, you won’t want to miss the Hearthstone, Heroes of the Storm, and World of Warcraft European Championships being held at the event.
