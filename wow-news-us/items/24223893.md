# Explore the Zones of Midnight

- URL: https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/en-us/news/24223893/explore-the-zones-of-midnight
- Date: 2025-09-02T23:45:00Z
![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/1c/1CSC8U6BN5XR1756517579684.png)

Battle your way to a new level cap of 90 as you explore four reimagined and new zones in Midnight. You’ll return to the homelands of the blood elves in Eversong Woods, explore the capital of the Amani trolls in Zul’Aman, travel beneath the roots of the world trees to Harandar, and face the all-consuming Void in The Voidstorm.
