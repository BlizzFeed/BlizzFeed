# Monumentally Playing Favorites – A Poll

- URL: https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/en-us/news/19919132/monumentally-playing-favorites-a-poll
- Date: 2015-10-12T14:00:00Z
![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/eh/EHB1V0IJWKEO1444660799725.jpg)

Look around your Garrison and you might notice the three big, round pedestals placed in prime decorative locations. They’re made for holding Monuments that stand as great, gleaming symbols of your grand accomplishments in Draenor. Monuments aren’t easy to come by, but if you rise to the challenge, they can add a very nice look to your home base.
