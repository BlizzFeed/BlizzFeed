# The Day of the Dead is Here!

- URL: https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/en-us/news/20353389/the-day-of-the-dead-is-here
- Date: 2016-11-01T18:00:00Z
![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/q3/Q31EWZIXTZEZ1477972664809.jpg)

During the Day of the Dead, the people of Azeroth gather in graveyards to celebrate the spirits of those they have lost. The festivities occur in the cemeteries of any major city where celebrants cook, drink, don costumes, and more.
