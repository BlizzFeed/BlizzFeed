# Not all Choices are Black and White at the August Trading Post

- URL: https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/en-us/news/24224366/not-all-choices-are-black-and-white-at-the-august-trading-post
- Date: 2025-07-30T19:00:00Z
![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/lw/LWLN99J0NQ3F1753812671753.png)

You can make all the right moves in either dramatic black and red or classic black and white at the Trading Post. This month you can add the Grandmaster’s Prophetic Board mount to your collection. Just like the chess event from Karazhan, your adventures won’t be contained when you soar over the landscape.
