# Link Your Battle.net Account to Discord in the Curse of Ula’tek Content Update

- URL: https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/en-us/news/24286284/link-your-battle-net-account-to-discord-in-the-curse-of-ulatek-content-update
- Date: 2026-06-18T15:59:00Z
- Image: https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/f2/F28C0LCILH8B1781714504793.png

With the release of the Curse of Ula’tek content update, players will be able to link their Battle.net and Discord accounts to chat and collaborate with their guild no matter where they are.
