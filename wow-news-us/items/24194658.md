# WoW Classic 20th Anniversary Edition Phase 4 Now Live

Within the jungles of Stranglethorn Vale, distant drums beat in an ancient ritual, calling forth the Blood God, Hakkar, to the ancient troll city of Zul’Gurub. Beyond the jungle, ancient emerald dragons rise, the insidious Silithid start to swarm, the most dangerous sport of all gets underway— Stranglethorn Fishing Extravaganza, and the Tier 0.5 Class Armor Sets (Dungeon Set 2) become available.
