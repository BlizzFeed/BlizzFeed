# Preview: New Warfront — Battle for Darkshore

- Date: 2018-12-20T17:00:00Z

In the ashen shadow of Teldrassil, hostilities between the Horde and the Alliance ramp up once more along the western coast of Kalimdor. With the release of Tides of Vengeance on December 11, prepare to clash in an all-new Warfront: The Battle for Darkshore.
