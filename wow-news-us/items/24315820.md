# World of Warcraft: Forever Goes Live Globally November 4!

- Date: 2026-10-29T21:00:00Z
![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/yz/YZRX9YZ5KHDV1789250918244.png)

Begin your first adventure or return to a new and reimagined Azeroth in World of Warcraft: Forever, launching worldwide November 4 at 3:00 pm PST. Adventure to level 60 across the original continents, discover new stories, and meet fellow adventurers in a permanent home alongside modern and Classic WoW where community comes first and the journey matters as much as the destination.
