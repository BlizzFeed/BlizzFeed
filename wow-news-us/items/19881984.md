# Prepare for Lift-Off – Patch 6.2.2 Takes Flight September 1

- URL: https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/en-us/news/19881984/prepare-for-lift-off-patch-6-2-2-takes-flight-september-1
- Date: 2015-08-31T17:00:00Z
![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/tb/TB40RVIID2LX1440793309353.jpg)

Patch 6.2.2 arrives September 1 and with it, a new way to get into the PvP action will become available with Mercenary Mode. Those who earn the Draenor Pathfinder achievement will be also be able to take flight over the skies of Draenor.
