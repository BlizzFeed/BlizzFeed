# Demon Hunter Early Access Now Available!

- URL: https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/en-us/news/20231211/demon-hunter-early-access-now-available
- Date: 2016-08-09T17:00:00Z
![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/bc/BCNYP78N86TZ1470437569299.jpg)

Early access to the new Demon Hunter class is now available to those who have pre-purchased the Legion expansion and have at least one level 70 character on their realm. To help you prepare for your new role as one of the Illidari, we’ve sent scouts into Mardum to learn more about this powerful hero.
