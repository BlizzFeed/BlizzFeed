# The Remote Auction House Is Retiring

- URL: https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/en-us/news/21655806/the-remote-auction-house-is-retiring
- Date: 2018-04-04T17:20:00Z
![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/cj/CJE5I0XTX8A21522786331143.jpg)

The WoW Remote Auction House will be deactivated on April 18, 2018. On that date, you will no longer be able to view Auction House listings or make Auction House transactions through the official WoW website or the WoW Armory app, and all links will be removed from our website.
