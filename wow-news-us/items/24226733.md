# New or Returning, Take Your Character to the Next Level!

- URL: https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/en-us/news/24226733/new-or-returning-take-your-character-to-the-next-level
- Date: 2025-09-02T23:45:00Z
![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/40/40S6G6NBI8EU1657669349914.png)

Whether you’re new to Azeroth or returning to undertake the next chapter in the evolving story within Midnight, we’ve got you covered when the 11.2.7 content update arrives later this year. New players will find a smoother transition to get caught up with an updated New Player Experience. Players looking to come back will find a guided Returning Player Experience to help you remember how to play the game and get you on the path to the latest content in World of Warcraft.
