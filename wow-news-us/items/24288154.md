# Run, Leap, and Spread Cheer—the Darkspear Dash is Here!

- Date: 2026-06-27T07:01:00Z
- Image: https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/bs/BSJ66856UNSP1782239030643.png

From isles to spires, the Darkspear Dash begins! Join the Darkspear trolls and their Zandalari allies as they celebrate individuality and identity. Race up Kalimdor and across the Eastern Kingdoms, from Echo Isles to Silvermoon City, for an in-game event inspired by the annual Running of the Trolls community-led event while you run, leap, and spread rainbow cheer along the route.
