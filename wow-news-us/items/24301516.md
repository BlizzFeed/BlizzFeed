# Gather Around the Campfire in World of Warcraft: Forever

- URL: https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/en-us/news/24301516/gather-around-the-campfire-in-world-of-warcraft-forever
- Date: 2026-10-21T17:00:00Z
![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/b0/B0HPKSFCFCDC1792455657405.png)

The Camping system in World of Warcraft: Forever lets players create shared campsites where they can rest, craft, and gain useful benefits from profession-made features. Beginning with the Cooking profession’s Basic Campfire, the system gives adventurers new reasons to prepare together and connect while traveling through Azeroth.
