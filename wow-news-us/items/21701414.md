The heart of the troll empire, Zandalar is known as Azeroth’s cradle of life. There are three main territories in this region: the lush jungle of Zuldazar, the dank marshland of Nazmir, and the storm-scorched desert of Vol’dun. Read on as we take you on a brief tour of the first of these areas: Zuldazar.
