# Take a Trip Down Forsaken Memories Lane During the July Trading Post

- URL: https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/en-us/news/24214499/take-a-trip-down-forsaken-memories-lane-during-the-july-trading-post
- Date: 2025-06-26T19:00:00Z
![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/qz/QZA52OVHJJ791750892250752.png)

Just when you think it’s safe to go to the July Trading Post, the Forsaken have infiltrated the month’s offerings. This month’s bar is extended to include three new Forsaken-themed rewards to add to your collection—Forsaken’s Grotesque Cauldron, Ensemble: Forsaken’s Grotesque Collection, and Forsaken’s Grotesque Charger. Players can also earn an additional 500 Trader's Tender.
