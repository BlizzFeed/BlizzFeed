# Saddle Up for the Eastern Kingdoms Cup

- URL: https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/en-us/news/24229241/saddle-up-for-the-eastern-kingdoms-cup
- Date: 2025-08-26T07:01:00Z
![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/6v/6V3OZSWHNQS51696281302241.png)

Launch into action as the Eastern Kingdoms Cup sweeps across Azeroth! From August 26 to September 9, new and veteran riders can race through fourteen thrilling courses, and take on challenges in Normal, Advanced, and Reverse modes—each offering excitement and a test of skill. Begin your quest with Lord Andestrasz to dive into the aerial action!
