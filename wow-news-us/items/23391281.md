# The 2020 Arena World Championship is Changing

- URL: https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/en-us/news/23391281/the-2020-arena-world-championship-is-changing
- Date: 2020-04-14T21:00:00Z
![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/0b/0B2CB05E5K1X1586884196825.jpg)

To help protect the health and safety of players, staff, and partners, the 2020 Arena World Championship (AWC) Battle for Azeroth season will be held entirely online.
