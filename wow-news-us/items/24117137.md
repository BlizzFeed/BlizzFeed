# Paid Character Transfer Service Update Ahead

- URL: https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/en-us/news/24117137/paid-character-transfer-service-update-ahead
- Date: 2024-07-12T00:00:00Z
![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/9s/9SSJ8VVRB3FK1645205310091.png)

With the launch of The War Within™ pre-expansion content update arriving on July 23, we will be disabling the Paid Character Transfer (PCT) service for character transfers between Battle.net® accounts.
