Prior to the release of World of Warcraft: Legion, the Burning Legion swept into Azeroth and began their invasions anew; vying once more to destroy all that stood in their way. Now that the heroes of the Horde and Alliance have pushed their forces on into the Broken Isles to repel these demonic forces, a fresh spate of attacks are on the horizon. Read on to learn more.
