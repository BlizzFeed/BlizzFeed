# Check Out the New WoW Esports Site!

- URL: https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/en-us/news/20859943/check-out-the-new-wow-esports-site
- Date: 2017-07-05T17:00:00Z
![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/71/71XDHTEQY2AP1499106052899.jpg)

The new WoW esports site is dedicated to the people and players who live and breathe esports, like us. The information you’ll see there focuses entirely on competitive tournaments, talented teams and their stories, live events and recaps, video and streaming content, leaderboards, announcements, and more.
