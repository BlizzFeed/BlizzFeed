# Celebrate 19 Years of World of Warcraft!

- URL: https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/en-us/news/24025834/celebrate-19-years-of-world-of-warcraft
- Date: 2023-11-16T18:00:00Z
![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/in/IN2CX6568GW61604015292093.jpg)

The 19th-anniversary celebration of World of Warcraft is upon us! Log in to World of Warcraft between November 16 and December 7 to earn the WoW’s 19th Anniversary achievement, then check your mail for a special Anniversary Gift Package along with the Lil' Frostwing pet.
