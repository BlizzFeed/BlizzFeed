# Patch 11.1.5 Now Live!

- URL: https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/en-us/news/24186695/patch-11-1-5-now-live
- Date: 2025-04-22T20:52:00Z
![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/a3/A391FSRNZ54T1745337251799.png)

The 11.1.5 content update ushers in a host of new activities rolling out beginning with the launch of the update with more to come in the weeks that follow. It all begins with the Nightfall Scenario, UI updates with the new Cooldown Manager, and the Winds of Mysterious Fortune buff bringing with it an in-game experience boost and reputation boost for characters levels 10 through 79.
