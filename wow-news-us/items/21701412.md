# World of Warcraft Arena Esports Levels Up in Australia and New Zealand

- Date: 2018-04-16T23:15:00Z
![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/bi/BIM24KFM8IAG1500068793057.jpg)

The 2017 season was a phenomenal year for Australian and New Zealand World of Warcraft Arena esports fans. The region sent three teams to compete in the Asia-Pacific (APAC) Arena World Championship, and for the first time ever, a team from Australia—Team Rock—competed on the global stage at BlizzCon.
