Get ready to welcome four new groups of visitors to your neighborhood with the introduction of new Endeavors featuring the Amani trolls, kobolds, Ohn’ahran centaur, and tortollan. Take on new activities, earn more Community Coupons, and shop for new Housing Decor items—including rugs—to enhance your home.
