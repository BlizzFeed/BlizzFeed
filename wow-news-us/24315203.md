---

Azeroth has always been more than a place to play—it is a world shaped by the people we meet along the way. The WoW Portal Room offers a starting point for making those connections, whether you’re searching for familiar camaraderie or an entirely new circle of adventurers.

The WoW Portal Room is ready to welcome World of Warcraft: Forever players and help them build on the friendships, stories, and shared adventures that make Azeroth feel like home. Alongside players at every stage of their journey, you can turn that enduring connection into new conversations, groups, and communities.

## A Gathering Place for Every Kind of WoW Player

The WoW Portal Room is the official World of Warcraft Discord server, bringing players and community Ambassadors together in an inclusive hub. Whether you’re learning the ropes, returning to the game, sharing years of knowledge, or looking for a new group for your next chapter, the Portal Room can point you in the right direction. Like any portal, it is a gateway—not the final destination.

## Meet the WoW Ambassadors

WoW Ambassadors are experienced players who represent their Discord communities in the WoW Portal Room. They help players of all backgrounds and experience levels find welcoming spaces that match how they enjoy Warcraft—whether that means tackling content together, swapping stories, learning from others, or simply staying connected to the world and its community.
