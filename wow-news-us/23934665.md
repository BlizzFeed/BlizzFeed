#### Here are this week's highlights:

- **Updates and Reminders for Dragonflight Season 2:** [Updates and reminders](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/season-2-updates-and-reminders/1576036) on transitioning to Dragonflight Season 2 are available, including how Mythic Keystones and the Revival Catalyst will work.

([NA](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/m-thundering-hero-title-updated-april-27/1576546), [EU](https://eu.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/m-thundering-hero-title-updated-27-april/444828))
