# Set Your Campfire Ablaze with the Pouch of Violet Dust!

- URL: https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/en-us/news/24317678/set-your-campfire-ablaze-with-the-pouch-of-violet-dust
![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/im/IMSJQENHJ6TZ1793835712755.png)

> Watch 4 hours of any eligible World of Warcraft stream on Twitch.tv during the World of Warcraft: Forever launch week to collect the Pouch of Violet Dust toy.

---

Players can collect and use an ephemeral new launch-celebration toy to enhance their campfire and spread the benefits of camaraderie as they begin their journey through Azeroth in World of Warcraft: Forever.

Watch any eligible World of Warcraft stream on Twitch.tv from November 4 at 4:30 pm PST to November 12 at 4:30 pm PST to claim the Pouch of Violet Dust Toy. This ephemeral item is available only within WoW: Forever and can only be used during the “WoW: Forever Launch Celebration” event period, which expires in early December. After that point, the toy will be removed from players’ collections and will no longer be available in game.

![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/bj/BJA1IFS2DYL51793835840867.png)

The Pouch of Violet Dust toy allows players to turn a campfire purple, increasing the duration of all camp benefits by 1 hour (for a total of a 2-hour duration). Once activated, this benefit is shared, and any other players resting at the Camp will receive the same duration extension.

---

### Eligibility

To participate, you must link your Twitch account by visiting <https://account.battle.net/connections>. If you have changed your password or made any changes to your Twitch or Battle.net account, you will have to relink your accounts. When you relink, there will be a 7-day cooldown before you can relink a new Twitch account. After you’ve claimed your rewards on Twitch, please make sure you’re logging into the Battle.net region you’d like to receive the drop(s) on, as the first region you log into will be where your items will be delivered. It can take up to 24 hours to receive your reward(s) in-game after claiming. Read our Frequently Asked Questions section for more information.

---

### How do I Earn Twitch Drops?

**Start Time:** November 4, at 4:30 pm PST  
**End Time:** November 12, at 4:30 pm PST  
**Where to Watch:**  Eligible World of Warcraft streams.

Watch four hours of eligible *World of Warcraft* content while Drops are active on any channel.

### Frequently Asked Questions

**Q. How do I link my battle.net and Twitch accounts?**  
A. Sign into your Battle.net account at <https://account.battle.net/connections> and click the Connect link for Twitch, then follow the instructions to sign in and link your Twitch account to Battle.net.

**Q. How do I link my Battle.net account to my YouTube account?**  
A. You can [link your Battle.net account](https://news.blizzard.com/article/23653192/link-your-battle-net-account-with-youtube-today) to your YouTube account through the YouTube [Connected Apps page](https://www.youtube.com/account_sharing) to earn game rewards and drops.

**Q. What devices can I use to earn and claim drops?**  
A. You can earn and claim drops using your PC/Mac web browser or on the Twitch or YouTube app for Android or iOS. Twitch apps or YouTube apps on game consoles, smart TVs, and other TV apps do not support drops.

**Q. Do I need to watch the same channel the entire time to earn my drops?**  
A. You can earn progress towards a drop on any eligible channel in the *World of Warcraft* category on Twitch. You can, for example, watch half an hour on one channel and then switch to another channel without losing your progress. You cannot earn progress faster by watching more than one channel at a time. If watching on YouTube, you’ll need to watch the World of Warcraft official channel for the entirety.

**Q. Will I receive my drop rewards in-game automatically?**  
A. To receive your rewards, you must claim the drop on the channel you’re watching or in the Drops Inventory menu on Twitch. YouTube rewards will be delivered automatically in-game if the Battle.net and YouTube accounts are connected while viewing.

**Q. Do Twitch Drop rewards expire?**  
A. Earned Twitch Drop rewards will expire seven (7) days after they’ve been claimed if a Battle.net account has not been linked.

**Q. As a streamer, how do I get Twitch Drops on my channel?**  
A. All you need to do is ensure your Twitch and Battle.net accounts are linked and that you’ve opted into Drops (see Drops Streamer Home).

**Q. What regions are eligible for these Twitch Drops?**  
A. Participating regions include North America, Latin America, Europe, The Middle East, Africa, Japan, Taiwan, Southeast Asia, Australia, and New Zealand.

**Q. What regions are eligible for these YouTube Rewards?**  
A. Participating regions include North America, Latin America, Europe, The Middle East, Africa, Japan, Taiwan, Southeast Asia, Korea, Australia, and New Zealand.
