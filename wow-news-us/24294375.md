## Earn World of Warcraft Rewards

![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/w5/W5YL0UQZ7L5M1789077968622.png)

Tune in to eligible Warcraft streams on [Twitch](https://www.twitch.tv/warcraft) or the official [World of Warcraft YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/warcraft) channel to earn rewards during the show! If you’re attending BlizzCon in person, you can link your BlizzCon Pass to your [Battle.net Account](https://battle.net/) no later than September 18, 2026\*\* to claim all the available rewards. Rewards and eligibility vary by game, stream, and platform, so check out [the World of Warcraft Rewards overview](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/news/24295396) to learn more.

## Watch the AWC and MDI Grand Finals Live

![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_header/rn/RNJJDCTPXE8V1787866983845.png)

We’re bringing the best talent in the world to BlizzCon 2026 to put their skills to the test with the Arena World Championship (AWC) and Mythic Dungeon Invitational (MDI) Grand Finals live from the convention.

Come join us in person from the BlizzCon Stage or enjoy every exciting moment from home on the official World of Warcraft [Twitch](https://www.twitch.tv/warcraft) or [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/warcraft) channels as Azeroth's top heroes compete for honor, the BlizzCon trophy, and a share of the $600,000 combined AWC & MDI prize pool on September 12 and 13.

Learn everything you need to know about these events in our [overview article](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/news/24293057).
