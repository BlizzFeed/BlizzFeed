# Celebrate with the Dead During Day of the Dead!

- URL: https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/en-us/news/22646756/celebrate-with-the-dead-during-day-of-the-dead
![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/q3/Q31EWZIXTZEZ1477972664809.jpg)

> The time has come once more to gather in the graveyards for the Day of the Dead and celebrate with and cherish the spirits of those who have been lost.

---

### Event Details

> **When:** November 1–3\
> **Where**: Graveyards of all Capital Cities

Don’t wait for your invitation to arrive and **/dance** with Catrina to take the form of a skeleton and earn the achievement [Dead Man’s Party](http://www.wowhead.com/achievement=3456?brl).

A little [Bread of the Dead](http://www.wowhead.com/item=46691?brl) can go a long way to restoring your health, so make sure to purchase the recipe from Chapman, who can also be found in any city graveyard. He’s got a few other goodies that you can purchase to make it a truly festive holiday, so be sure to speak to him to see his wares.

You could be a contender and earn the achievement — [To the Afterlife](https://www.wowhead.com/achievement=9426/to-the-afterlife?brl) — during this holiday by defeating a contender while wearing a contender’s outfit purchased during Day of the Dead.

Contender costumes include: ”[Blooming Rose](https://www.wowhead.com/item=116856/blooming-rose-contenders-costume?brl)” Contender’s Costume, “[Night Demon](https://www.wowhead.com/item=116888/night-demon-contenders-costume?brl)” Contender’s Costume, “[Purple Phantom](https://www.wowhead.com/item=116889/purple-phantom-contenders-costume?brl)” Contender’s Costume, “[Santo’s Sun](https://www.wowhead.com/item=116890/santos-sun-contenders-costume?brl)” Contender’s Costume, and “[Snowy Owl](https://www.wowhead.com/item=116891/snowy-owl-contenders-costume?brl)” Contender’s Costume and will add to your toy collection.

Learn more about the Day of the Dead in-game holiday on Wowhead’s [holiday guide](http://www.wowhead.com/day-of-the-dead-guide?brl).

### WoW Classic

Come and honor the dearly departed in *WoW Classic*.

> **When:** November 1–2\
> **Where:** Graveyards of all Capital cities

During the Day of the Dead celebration, shed your skin when you /dance with Catrina to take the form of a skeleton and earn the *Dead Man's Party* achievement.

Take some **Bread of the Dead** with you on your adventures to restore your health in tight times. Visit Chapman in any city graveyard to purchase the recipe.
