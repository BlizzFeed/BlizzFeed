## 20th Anniversary Realms: Molten Core and Onyxia's Lair Now Live

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/0s/0S3NMSM8ELGD1733942226594.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/0s/0S3NMSM8ELGD1733942226594.png)

Who's ready to handle the heat? Players on new *WoW Classic 20th Anniversary Edition* Realms can now leap into the fire and face off against the Firelord, Ragnaros, in Molten Core and Onyxia in her lair. As a reminder, Anniversary Realms have no buff and debuff limits, so you can feel free to use every spell you can cast in a raid.

Prepare to face the heat in Molten Core and Onyxia's Lair when you read our [detailed overview](http://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/news/24165121).

Looking for a new adventure in the WoW Classic 20th Anniversary Edition realms? More information can be found in our [previously published article](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/news/24156594).

## Heed the Call of Siren Isle Beginning December 17

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_header/2i/2IOEMVBR981J1733877504644.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_header/2i/2IOEMVBR981J1733877504644.png)

Prepare to dive into mystery and mayhem with the newest content update in *The War Within*— Siren Isle.

Explore an enigmatic outdoor zone, procure new items and rewards for you and your alternate characters, engage in new campaign chapters—Dalaran epilogue and Lingering Shadows prologue to Undermine(d)—and more!

An overview of what's coming ahead can be read in [our article](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/news/24165119).
