## Sign Up Now for The War Within Season 3 Esports: AWC & MDI!

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_header/js/JSFBKIP726CA1757049894311.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_header/js/JSFBKIP726CA1757049894311.png)

The **Ghosts of K’aresh** content update is here, and the Arena World Championship (AWC) and Mythic Dungeon International (MDI) return for ***The War Within* Season 3**! Prepare for fierce Arena clashes beginning **October 3**, and join the race in Mythic+ with Time Trials starting **October 1.**

- [**Register on Raider.IO**](http://raider.io/tournaments) to secure your spot in AWC or MDI.
- **Access Tournament Realms** with fully geared characters at max item level.
- Earn the all-new **Season 3 banners (*Unbound Banner of the Algari & Astral Champion’s Prestigious Banner*).**
- Tune in live on [**Twitch**](https://www.twitch.tv/warcraft) and [**YouTube**](https://www.youtube.com/@Warcraft) to cheer for your favorite competitors.

In Season 3, the AWC will once again feature **three qualification Cups per region**, leading into the AWC Gauntlet and Grand Finals with a combined **$200,000 (USD) prize pool**.

The MDI also returns to full force in Season 3, continuing its focus on **Mythic+ key pushing** as the cornerstone of competitive Dungeons. Registration is now open, with **Time Trials beginning October 1**.

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/gallery/qi/QIYDQ8VFET201757049922252.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/gallery/qi/QIYDQ8VFET201757049922252.png)

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/gallery/5s/5SZ9AZZQFIYA1757049946556.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/gallery/5s/5SZ9AZZQFIYA1757049946556.png)

We can’t wait to get ***The War Within* Season 3** esports competitions underway. Don’t miss a moment, [sign up now on](http://raider.io/tournaments) [**Raider.IO**](http://raider.io/), subscribe to our [**Twitch**](https://www.twitch.tv/warcraft) and [**YouTube**](https://blizz.ly/Subscribe) channels, and follow **WoW Esports on** [**Twitter**](https://twitter.com/wowesports) for the latest updates.

Additional details about the tournaments can be found in [our article](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/news/24234067/). Good luck, and have fun!

## World of Warcraft®: Midnight™ Beta Sign-Ups

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_header/nn/NNM2XDVG7PGE1755621970639.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_header/nn/NNM2XDVG7PGE1755621970639.png)

Sign-ups for the Midnight beta test are now open. Over the course of the test, we'll be inviting community veterans, press, fansites, friends, and family to check out the upcoming expansion and give us feedback. Players who purchase or who upgrade to the Epic Edition of Midnight will also gain immediate access to the beta.

Players selected to participate will receive an email invitation directing them to download the game client directly from the Battle.net desktop app. Players who purchase the Epic Edition may also receive email notifications. As always, please be aware of phishing attempts—if you're unsure whether your invitation is legitimate, please log in to your Battle.net account to verify that there is a Midnight beta license attached (under Your Game Accounts).

[Sign Up Now](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/midnight)
