In *Mists of Pandaria Classic*, players can engage in Pet Battles—strategic 3v3 turn-based encounters that feature hundreds of collectible battle pets from across Azeroth. This beginner-friendly guide introduces the fundamentals of Pet Battles, from taming wild companions to mastering type matchups and ability combos.

---

### What are Pet Battles?

Pet Battles allow players to turn their companion pets into battle-hardened allies. Unlock the feature through Battle Pet Training and take your team of three pets into tactical, turn-based encounters against wild creatures, quest trainers, and other players.

Each pet has its own family type, abilities, and progression path.

### How to Get Started

![Human female character surrounded by pet cages](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/83Y23YWLD3WP1751231072701.png)

**Audrey Burnhep in Stormwind**

![Orc male character surrounded by pet cages](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/UCFJE97DESOB1751231072672.png)

**Varzok in Orgrimmar**

1. **Train the skill:** Visit any major city and speak with a Battle Pet Trainer to learn *Battle Pet Training*. This unlocks Pet Battles and the ability to track nearby wild pets on your minimap.
2. **Tame your first pet:** Choose from your current companions or tame a level 1 wild pet in a starting zone like Elwynn Forest or Durotar.
3. **Enter your first battle:** Right-click a wild pet to begin. Reduce its health to attempt a capture or win the match to gain experience.
4. **Level and customize:** Pets level up to 25, unlocking up to six unique abilities—three of which can be slotted at a time.

### Pet Families and Their Strengths

Every pet belongs to a family type that determines its strengths and vulnerabilities. Understanding matchups is key to winning tougher battles.

| **Family** | **Strong Against** | **Weak Against** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Beast | Critter | Flying |
| Aquatic | Elemental | Flying |
| Flying | Aquatic | Magic |
| Mechanical | Beast | Elemental |
| Magic | Flying | Mechanical |
| Undead | Humanoid | Aquatic |
| Dragonkin | Magic | Undead |
| Elemental | Mechanical | Critter |
| Humanoid | Dragonkin | Undead |
| Critter | Undead | Beast |

Each family also has a passive bonus—for example, Flying pets act first when above 50% health, and Undead pets return to life for one final round after defeat.

### Tips for New Tamers

> - Equip the [Safari Hat](https://www.wowhead.com/mop-classic/item=92738/safari-hat?brl) (from [Taming the World](https://www.wowhead.com/mop-classic/achievement=7499/taming-the-world?brl) achievement) to boost pet experience
> - Aim for *rare-quality* pets when capturing in the wild
> - Rotate multiple pets for faster leveling and better matchups
> - Complete daily trainer quests for steady rewards and experience
> - Use the Pet Journal to view families, abilities, and manage loadouts

### Beyond Level 25

After leveling your core pets, you can:

- Collect unique pets from dungeons, world drops, and achievements
- Challenge master trainers across Pandaria
- Explore hidden encounters tied to weather, time, or special events
- Queue for PvP Pet Battles to test your strategy
