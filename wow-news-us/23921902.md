# The AWC & MDI Dragonflight Season 1 Global Finals Start March 31!

- URL: https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/en-us/news/23921902/the-awc-mdi-dragonflight-season-1-global-finals-start-march-31
![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/go/GO0SYM36BYYW1678907758153.jpg)

> Everything you need to know to celebrate the 2023 WoW Esports Finals beginning March 31 on Twitch and YouTube in Patch 10.0.7!

---

The *Dragonflight* Season 1 Arena World Championship & Mythic Dungeon International Global Finals are here! Over two weekends, celebrate the top Arena Gladiators and Dungeon Runners as they compete for their share of $600,000 (USD). Tune in live between March 31-April 9 on [Twitch](https://www.twitch.tv/warcraft) & [YouTube](https://youtube.com/warcraft)!

### Mythic Dungeon International 2023 Global Finals, March 31-April 2

Following some incredible strategies and routes in the new *Dragonflight* Dungeons, the top eight teams from across the globe will test their mettle in the Mythic Dungeon International (MDI) 2023 Global Finals. Winners of each MDI Group and the Last Stand Tournament will do battle in the *Dragonflight* Season 1 Dungeons one last time for their share of $300,000 (USD). This is the ultimate opportunity for dungeoneers to showcase their speed running prowess. Teams will face off starting March 31 in a series of Best of 3, double-elimination matches on their path to the Grand Finals. Each broadcast day will begin at 10 am PDT / 7:00 pm CEST, live on [Twitch](https://www.twitch.tv/warcraft) and [YouTube](https://youtube.com/warcraft).

### Arena World Championship Gauntlet & Grand Finals, April 7-9

With an updated program format to go alongside the fast-paced game meta, the first season of *Dragonflight* has brought a breath of fresh air into the Arena World Championship (AWC). The AWC Finals weekend starts with AWC Gauntlet on April 7, a fierce single-elimination bracket to determine each Region’s last Finalist. The next day, the AWC Grand Finals begin, with the top 4 European & North American teams clashing to establish the first *Dragonflight* Champion of each region with $300,000 (USD) in combined prizing on the line.

### Earning the D.I.S.C.O. Toy In-Game!

From March 21–April 2, any eligible Twitch channel broadcasting in the World of Warcraft section will have Twitch Drops active so that viewers can earn the D.I.S.C.O Toy in-game shortly after 10.0.7 launch. This will be active on the Warcraft [Twitch](https://www.twitch.tv/warcraft) channel & eligible Watch Parties. For more information, read our [previously published article](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/en-us/news/23923488/).
