### Twitch Drop: Get the Beetriz Pet April 22

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_header/ti/TIW0JR1HXACJ1744740079957.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_header/ti/TIW0JR1HXACJ1744740079957.png)

Yellow maidenbugs are often mistaken for other yellow insects—this happens even more often to Beetriz. You can claim this bold little bug by watching any *World of Warcraft* stream on Twitch.tv.

Watch 4 hours of *World of Warcraft* content on any channel from ***April 22, 10:00 am PDT***, until ***May 20, 10:00 am PDT*** while these Twitch Drops are active to claim the ***Beetriz the Maidenbug*** pet.

### Spring into Action—Noblegarden Begins April 21!

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_header/ox/OX29EVDIAXR11744900402536.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_header/ox/OX29EVDIAXR11744900402536.png)

Hop to it and visit Azure Watch, Dolanaar, Goldshire, Kharanos, Brill, Razor Hill, Bloodhoof Village, Falconwing Square, Valdrakken, or Dornogal to join the festivities. Noblegarden brings new and returning rewards to claim for your celebratory efforts.

**When:** April 21—27\
**Currency:** Noblegarden Chocolates are used as currency during this holiday to purchase garments, mounts, pets, and other special rewards. These can be collected by completing quests and opening the eggs found in the event areas.

##### What's New in 2025

- Khaz Algar welcomes the feast of Noblegarden with great aplomb and festooned Dornogal with festive decorations, as well as a new egg hunt for hidden **Brightly Colored Eggs**.
- The stack count for Brightly Colored Eggs has been increased to 18.
- Festive new weapon transmogs to help you spring into action.

More details will be posted on [worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/news](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/en-us/news) when the event begins.
