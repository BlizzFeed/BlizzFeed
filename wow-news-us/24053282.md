#### Here are this week's highlights:

#### Dragonflight

- **Trading Post Achievement Reward**: The additional 500 Trader’s Tender that some players received is part of the Trading Post Enthusiast achievement, which requires players to earn the monthly bonus reward 12 times. An [issue is being looked into](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/the-extra-500-tenders-was-an-easy-w/1756074/10) where this additional currency isn’t listed as one of the rewards.

#### WoW Classic

- **Classic Era Realms Aren’t Being Layered**: Currently, Classic Era Realms, like Whitemane, [do not have layering enabled](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/blizzard-you-can-have-my-or-have-layering-on-era-not-both/1745962/4) and have not for months.

#### General

- **WoW Community Council:** The [application](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/apply-to-join-the-wow-community-council-in-2024/1745455) form for this program is currently open. We look forward to talking with players this year about World of Warcraft.
