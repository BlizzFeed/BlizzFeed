We're bringing back the Greedy Emissary event\*, and Azeroth's bravest must band together to deliver a swift end to greedy, saw-toothed thieves. And, alongside the Greedy Emissary event, take advantage of the Collector's Bounty buff when you defeat legendary foes once again to collect their bountiful rewards!

> **When**: Begins July 1 at 8:00 am PDT and ends July 29 at 8:00 am PDT\
> **Where**: Stormwind, Orgrimmar, Dornogal, and Undermine\
> **Currency**: Hellstone Shards earned during the Greedy Emissary event.

During the Greedy Emissary event, you'll encounter even more of these deviously greedy Treasure Goblins in Stormwind, Orgrimmar, Dornogal, and Undermine for new diabolical rewards— including the **Inarius' Charger** mount.

## Collector's Bounty Buff

Alongside the Greedy Emissary event, take advantage of the Collector's Bounty buff. Foes in legacy dungeons and raids find themselves overloaded with extra treasure. Defeat them once again to collect their bountiful rewards for you and your Warband.

> **When**: Begins July 1 at 8:00 am PDT and ends July 29 at 8:00 am PDT

If you've been on the hunt for rewards that have been eluding you, now's the time to get them. The following will have drop rates increased by up to 10 times (and in one case, 25 times!), while your chance for Transmog loot drops is doubled in legacy (pre-Dragonflight) raids and dungeons:

- All pre-Dragonflight legacy mounts from legacy dungeons and raids
- Eye of Sulfuras (Molten Core)
- Golden Hearthstone Card (The Nighthold)
- Bindings of the Windseeker (Molten Core)
- Warglaives of Azzinoth (Black Temple)
- Thori'dal, The Stars' Fury (Sunwell Plateau)

Some exclusions do apply—for instance, all Timewalking content is excluded, as are Pets and Toys.

## Portals-A-Plenty

Portals spawn every 60 minutes on the hour, and an icon of the Portal location appears on the Stormwind, Orgrimmar, and Dornogal maps if you're in that zone.

- **Dornogal**: Portals spawn by the southeastern city exit after the target dummy area.
- **Orgrimmar**: Portals spawn outside Orgrimmar, by the entrance gate.
- **Stormwind**: Portals spawn by the Stormwind Docks.

### Portals and Portal Storms in the Undermine

Portals abound in the Undermine, but spawning times and locations are a bit different. Killing any Undermine rare has a chance to trigger a Portal spawning a Treasure Goblin.

Treasure Goblins have gotten word that great loot can be nabbed in the Liberation of Undermine raid—defeat certain bosses, and there's a chance that it will trigger a Portal to spawn.

If that isn't enough, try to survive the Portal Storms! At the quarter-hour every hour, a Portal Storm can spawn another five Portals. Not only are these Portals enhanced with additional Affixes, but they each have a chance to hand-deliver pesky Treasure Goblins for even more chances to grab loot. So, keep an eye on your map for the icon to lead you where you need to go.

## Treasure Goblin Affixes and Elixirs

Treasure Goblins are more dangerous now that they carry more affixes from their world. Icons on top of both the summoning Portal and the Treasure Goblin itself will indicate what affixes the Treasure Goblin currently has applied.

Customize what affixes a Treasure Goblin will have by tossing special Elixirs on the Goblin summoning portals, but beware, adding more Affixes to a Portal will increase the number of goblins that can spawn.

| **Affix** | **Elixir** | **Effect** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Arcane | Arcane Elixir | Spawns Arcane Overloads, inflicting Arcane damage to enemies in the area. If no enemies are in the area, inflicts Arcane damage to enemies within 50 yards. |
| Blistering | Blistering Elixir | Summons Blistering Eruptions, which deal Shadowflame damage to players in the impact area. |
| Connected | Connected Elixir | Occasionally spawns hordes of Fallen and Fallen Shaman. |
| Cursing | Cursing Elixir | Inflicts Sanctuary Tongues, forcing players to speak Diabolic and reducing cast speed by 10% until the Goblin is defeated. |
| Deafening | Deafening Elixir | Casts Howl From Hell, knocking back all enemies within 50 yards. |
| Explosive | Explosive Elixir | Conjures Explosive orbs at enemies. After a short delay, they explode, dealing Fire damage to enemies caught in the blast. |
| Healthy | Healthy Elixir | Spawns health globes that heal players who step on them. |
| Magma | Magma Elixir | Hurls Magma at enemies, dealing Volcanic damage. Each impact summons a ball of lava that fixates on an enemy and deals Volcanic damage on impact. |
| Rallying | Rallying Elixir | Occasionally spawns hordes of Demonic Bovines. |
| Rich | Rich Elixir | Spawns pools of money that give Greedy Motivation when stepped on, increasing Haste and Movement Speed for 15 seconds. |
| Storming | Storming Elixir | Spawns pools of lightning that deal damage to enemies who step on them. |
| Tempestuous | Tempestuous Elixir | Pulls enemies to the Goblin with Tempest and then attempts to hit them with Arcane Gust, dealing Arcane damage and knocking enemies back. |
| Trapper | Trapper Elixir | Spawns Goblin Zapper traps, which deal 11% max health damage to enemies stepping on them. |
| Windforce | Windforce Elixir | Spawns Wind Vortexes that deal Nature damage and knock back players in the impact area. |

## Diabolical Rewards

Earn **Hellstone Shards** from **Hellcaller Chests** dropped by Treasure Goblins and completing activities during the Greedy Emissary event, then exchange them at Hellhunter Baranar, located in The Foreground in Dornogal, for hellish armor sets and other goodies. Your first ten **Hellcaller Chests** weekly, across your Warband, will always yield **Hellstone Shards**. **Hellcaller Chests** can also contain **Valorstones** and otherworldly items.

You won’t want to miss some truly iconic rewards, including recolors of classic Tier 2 armor sets for your characters.
