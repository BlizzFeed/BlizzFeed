### Patch 11.1.5 Arrives on April 22

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_header/s0/S0QSCBKG0Y6A1743811402033.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_header/s0/S0QSCBKG0Y6A1743811402033.png)

The 11.1.5 content update ushers in a host of new activities rolling out beginning with the launch of the update with more to come in the weeks that follow. It all begins with the Nightfall Scenario, UI updates with the new Cooldown Manager, and the Winds of Mysterious Fortune bringing with it an in-game experience boost and reputation boost for characters levels 10 through 79.

**Schedule:**

- **April 22:** Nightfall Scenario, Cooldown Manager, and Winds of Mysterious Fortune Buff
- **May 20:** Horrific Visions Revisited
- **June 3:** Dastardly Duos

We look forward to sharing more adventures with you. learn more details from our [previously published article](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/news/24186695/). See you in Azeroth!

### Season of Discovery Phase 8 is Now Live!

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_header/mt/MT4JBF5GQ3VB1742309098767.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_header/mt/MT4JBF5GQ3VB1742309098767.png)

Experience new outdoor content, a new Legendary weapon questline, new stories and surprises, and the Scarlet Enclave raid dungeon.

**Infiltrate New Avalon**

Go behind enemy lines in disguise as you infiltrate the town of New Avalon within the Scarlet Enclave. Gain the trust of the citizens and tradesmen, learn new recipes, earn new rewards, and more.

**New Legendary Weapon Questline**

Warriors, Hunters, and Paladins can undertake an epic quest to restore a fallen weapon to its former glory. Will you succeed, or will this storied weapon remain shrouded in darkness? The future is uncertain.

**New Raid: Scarlet Enclave**

A splinter faction of the Scarlet Crusade needs your aid in fighting an insidious evil at Tyr’s Hand. They need your assistance in retaking the Scarlet Enclave for good, glory—and new rewards. Players can engage with eight new bosses as they make their way through this new raid dungeon and collect new eight-piece armor sets for their class.
