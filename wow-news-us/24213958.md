Hidden by mists for millennia, Pandaria emerges on the horizon—a land of ancient secrets, breathtaking landscapes, and soul-stirring new adventures. Mists of Pandaria Classic invites heroes to step ashore and discover a world that is both serene and savage, where harmony and conflict dance in equal measure.

Each zone of Pandaria is a chapter in a saga that unfolds across rolling jade hills, mist-shrouded peaks, and vibrant villages. Whether you are a fresh-faced wanderer or a seasoned champion, Pandaria’s seven main zones teem with stories to unravel, challenges to conquer, and wonders to explore.

## The Jade Forest: Gateway to Discovery

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/x6/X6CH1SALHXYO1752022600374.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/x6/X6CH1SALHXYO1752022600374.png)

The journey begins in the lush expanse of the Jade Forest, where towering bamboo groves sway beneath the watchful gaze of jade statues and cloud-piercing mountains. This is the gateway to Pandaria—a place where the Alliance and Horde’s first steps are met with both opportunity and danger.

The Jade Forest embodies the balance between peaceful tradition and the disruption brought by outsiders. Pandaren monasteries radiate tranquility, while the invading forces of the Alliance and Horde threaten to upset centuries-old serenity.

**What Players Can Do:**

- Embark on introductory questlines that set the tone for the expansion and reveal the tension between local denizens and foreign invaders.
- Assist the enigmatic Pandaren and the noble Jade Serpent, Yu’lon, in restoring harmony to the land.
- Battle the mischievous Hozen and cunning Jinyu as each faction seeks new allies among Pandaria’s inhabitants.
- Venture into the Temple of the Jade Serpent dungeon, a sanctum overtaken by sha corruption.

## Valley of the Four Winds: Bounty of the Land

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/JZEJ08L6WITY1752021257787.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/JZEJ08L6WITY1752021257787.png)

Travel westward and you’ll find yourself in the Valley of the Four Winds, a patchwork of golden fields, bustling farmlands, and tranquil villages. Here, the Pandaren’s deep connection to the earth is celebrated at every turn.

The Valley pulses with life and warmth, but lurking beneath its pastoral calm are dangers waiting to uproot the peace.

**What Players Can Do:**

- Help farmsteads thrive by tending crops, rallying against mischievous vermin, and defending against the destructive Mantid swarms from the east.
- Join the legendary Brewmasters in crafting Pandaria’s finest ales, and participate in quirky, memorable quests filled with humor and heart.
- Journey through the Stormstout Brewery dungeon, where players aid Chen Stormstout in reclaiming his family’s legacy from chaos and corruption.
- Explore Halfhill Market, the heart of Pandaria’s agriculture and the central hub for the beloved Tillers faction—your home away from home for farming and daily activities.

## Krasarang Wilds: Jungle Secrets and Open War

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/11/11LMMW4M338I1752020310092.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/11/11LMMW4M338I1752020310092.png)

Krasarang Wilds stretches along Pandaria’s southern coast, its humid jungles and misty shores teeming with life—and strife. What begins as exploration quickly erupts into open warfare as the conflict between Alliance and Horde intensifies.

The wild is home to ancient ruins, mysterious caves, and the first skirmishes of a greater war that will consume Pandaria’s southern shores.

**What Players Can Do:**

- Explore hidden temples and lost lore deep within the jungle’s heart.
- Join the battle at the Domination Point or Lion’s Landing, pivotal strongholds for Horde and Alliance players, respectively.
- Engage in world PvP, dailies, and questlines that drive the story of faction warfare forward.

## Kun-Lai Summit: The Roof of Pandaria

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/5y/5YN2XUIEXAYU1752022402135.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/5y/5YN2XUIEXAYU1752022402135.png)

Beyond the tranquil fields rises Kun-Lai Summit, a region of jagged peaks, snow-capped cliffs, and ancient monasteries perched above the clouds. Here, the air is thin, and the challenges are many.

Kun-Lai Summit is home to hermits, monks, and legendary warriors who have trained for generations atop its mountains.

**What Players Can Do:**

- Scale perilous heights to seek the wisdom of the monks at the Shado-Pan Monastery and aid them in their eternal vigil against darkness.
- Take part in epic quests involving the mountain-dwelling Yaungol and the fearsome Mogu warlords.
- Challenge the Shado-Pan Monastery dungeon, confronting the sha and testing your worth in the eyes of Pandaria’s guardians.
- Venture into the Burlap Trail and encounter the Grummles—resourceful traders with a penchant for good luck charms and adventure.

---

## Townlong Steppes: Frontier of War

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/JBE47F5IJ74P1752021265946.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/JBE47F5IJ74P1752021265946.png)

To the west lies the Townlong Steppes, a windswept wilderness where grasslands meet barren tablelands, and the land bears the scars of ancient wars.

The Shado-Pan hold the line here, standing against the relentless Mantid and the insidious sha that threaten to spill over Pandaria’s borders.

**What Players Can Do:**

- Stand with the Shado-Pan as they wage a desperate campaign to contain the Mantid advance.
- Unravel the secrets of the long-lost Mogu empire and confront new adversaries rising from the shadows.
- Complete challenging daily quests and secure a foothold in the ongoing struggle for Pandaria’s survival.

---

## Dread Wastes: Heart of Corruption

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/0s/0SWFNG98D0ZI1752021680447.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/0s/0SWFNG98D0ZI1752021680447.png)

South of the Townlong Steppes, Pandaria’s serenity gives way to the corrupted depths of the Dread Wastes. Here, the Mantid swarm is at its strongest, and vile sha energies twist the land.

The Dread Wastes is a stark reminder of the ever-present threat of chaos; hope flickers in the darkness, guarded by the brave Klaxxi.

**What Players Can Do:**

- Ally with the Klaxxi Paragons—ancient Mantid champions fighting to preserve their people from utter madness.
- Delve into a web of intrigue, sabotage, and heroics as you take up the mantis blade in the war against the Empress and her sha-corrupted followers.
- Earn reputation with the Klaxxi through daily quests, unlocking powerful augments and unique storylines.

## Vale of Eternal Blossoms: Cradle of the Titans

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/d2/D2ZPMRA2O6E81752022714113.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/d2/D2ZPMRA2O6E81752022714113.png)

At the core of Pandaria lies the Vale of Eternal Blossoms, a valley of golden light, sacred waters, and ancient secrets. Long sealed against outsiders, the Vale is revered as the heart of the continent’s spiritual power.

The Vale is a place of hope and healing, but also the stage for some of Pandaria’s most dramatic confrontations.

**What Players Can Do:**

- Gain favor with the august Celestial Guardians and the Golden Lotus, Pandaria’s elite defenders.
- Protect the Vale from new and old threats, as the shadow of war and the corruption of the sha threaten to consume this sacred ground.
- Unlock powerful dailies, story quests, and—later—raid content within the [Mogu’shan Vaults](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/news/24216437/) and the Heart of Fear.

## The Adventure Awaits

From the tranquil terraces of the Jade Forest to the shadowed wastes of the Dread Wastes, Pandaria is a continent of contrasts—a harmonious blend of beauty, danger, and legend. Whether forging alliances with the Pandaren, battling ancient evils, or carving your own path through untamed wilds, Mists of Pandaria Classic offers endless adventures for those bold enough to step beyond the mists.
