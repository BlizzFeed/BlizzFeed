Heroes of Azeroth, the Horde and the Alliance are putting their rivalry to the ultimate test—and you're invited to tune in and join the excitement.

---

### The Showdown: Two Teams, One Escape Room

Brace yourself for an epic experience as WoW®: Foreverpresents Escape Together comes to Toronto, where thrills await!

**When:** Friday, October 23\
**Where:** Official [Warcraft Twitch](https://www.twitch.tv/warcraft "https://www.twitch.tv/warcraft") and [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/warcraft "https://www.youtube.com/warcraft") Channels

Watch the action unfold live as real-world champions of the Horde and the Alliance battle it out in a series of head-to-head challenges before facing their final test together—an Escape Room inspired by the iconic Scarlet Monastery, where many early adventurers tested their mettle against the Scarlet Crusade’s large garrison of fanatics.

We'll reveal our stream time and competitors soon, so stay tuned to find out who's been selected and which faction will outthink, outmaneuver, and out-escape the other.

---

### Escape the Scarlet Monastery in Person in Toronto

Do you and your closest allies have what it takes to defeat the Scarlet Monastery escape room on your own? Prove your skills and experience the challenge firsthand.

If you're in the Toronto area, bring friends and assemble your dungeon party to see if you can outwit the Scarlet Monastery-inspired escape room before time runs out.

**When**: October 8–11\
**Where**: Escape Manor, King Street, Toronto, Ontario

[Get Your Tickets](https://www.escapemanor.com/toronto/scarletmonastery/booking)
