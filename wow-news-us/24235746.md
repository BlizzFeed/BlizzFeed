Engage with *World of Warcraft* content in a meaningful way thanks to a brand-new feature tab in their Adventure Guide—Journeys—with a significant update to how you track your in-game progress, once Midnight launches.

The Journeys tab, located in the Adventure Guide, aims to be your one-stop shop to help keep your Renown, Delves, and Prey progress more intuitive and streamlined, and there's even a shortcut to check out your Great Vault rewards.

Where you once found yourself looking to the Renown Track for your progress, you'll now see a fresh hub, with tabs to track your progress through activities and culture reputation in Midnight and beyond, sectioned off for easier viewing.

## Key Changes and Features

- **Centralized Progress Tracking**: The Journeys tab consolidates all your progression-related information, making it easier to see how far you've come in activities and culture reputation within Midnight and previous expansions.
- **Delves Integration**: The Delves tab will be relocated from the Group Finder panel to the Journeys panel. Players can now configure their Delve companion directly within the Delves section of Journeys, streamlining the process.

## Looking Ahead

As World of Warcraft continues to evolve, the addition of the Journeys tab represents Blizzard's commitment to improving accessibility and the overall user interface. Whether you're a completionist or casual adventurer, this new feature promises a more organized way to track your journey through Azeroth and beyond.

## Journeys in Alpha

For those participating in Public Alpha 1, only previous expansion Renowns and Midnight Delves will be available. Artwork for Journeys is still a work in progress, and further visual enhancements can be expected in future updates.
