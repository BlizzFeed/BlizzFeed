# Announcing the World of Warcraft Student Art Contest 2022 Winners!

- URL: https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/en-us/news/23921898/announcing-the-world-of-warcraft-student-art-contest-2022-winners
![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/5k/5K5UMZSUBB1C1677795639217.png)

> The 2022 Student Art Contest has come to an end, and after intense scrutiny and some impassioned deliberation, we’ve identified this year’s winners.

---

Presented by Blizzard Entertainment’s Early Careers and World of Warcraft development teams, the twelfth annual [World of Warcraft Student Art Contest](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/news/23873399/) has drawn to a close. After some fervent debate and impassioned deliberation, our judges sifted through the many remarkable entries and identified the winning entries.

Check out the winners and runners-up below for each of the four categories.

---
