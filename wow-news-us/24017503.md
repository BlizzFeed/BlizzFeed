### Three Startling Steeds

Scour the skies upon the monstrous Iron Skyreaver and lurk the lands atop the Illidari Felstalker or Warforged Nightmare ground mounts.

![Irion Skyreaver hovering above with (from left to right) Nibbles and Mischief pets, Warforged Nightmare, and Illidari Felstalker](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/ei/EIFJBJHDNUYB1697231659741.png)

### Two Cute and Creepy Companions

Nibbles and Mischief are ready to join you on your latest adventures in the Dragon Isles and anywhere you go in Azeroth—and beyond. You’ll have nothing to fear with these fearsome companions at your side.

[Buy Now](https://shop.battle.net/product/world-of-warcraft-hallows-end-pack)

All mounts from the Hallow’s End Pack automatically scale to the fastest riding skill known by each character.  Once activated, the mounts and pets will be applied to present and future World of Warcraft characters on a single regional Battle.net account and will appear in each character’s Collections interface.  If you already own one or more items from the Hallow’s End Pack, its price will automatically adjust. All items are available as standalone purchases.

\*All items included in the bundle leave the Battle.net Shop indefinitely on October 31, 2023.\
\*\*Items from the Hallow’s End Pack not available in WoW Classic Titles.
