# Climb Atop a Clever Corvus with the Wings of the Guardian Pack

- URL: https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/en-us/news/24235741/climb-atop-a-clever-corvus-with-the-wings-of-the-guardian-pack
![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/wk/WK793ZQ1GJSN1758575339187.png)

> Sweeping across Azeroth in the saddle of a glorious Great Crow is caws for celebration!

---

Sweeping across Azeroth in the saddle of a glorious Great Crow is ***caws*** for celebration when you ***peck*** up the Wings of the Guardian Pack\* containing the Prophet's Great Crow (black) and Archmage's Great Crow (white) flying mounts.

The Wings of the Guardian Pack is only available for *WoW® Classic* progression realms (currently *Mists of Pandaria™ Classic*) and includes both the Prophet's Great Crow and the Archmage's Great Crow.

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/po/POU7M9R0BKF21758575372387.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/po/POU7M9R0BKF21758575372387.png)

You can share both Great Crow mounts across all present and future *WoW® Classic* Progression characters. The mounts automatically scale to the fastest riding skill known by each character. If characters aren't at a high enough level to use a flying mount, the Great Crow mounts are also ground mounts.
