### Twitch Drop: Get the Cuddly Void Grrgle Housing Decor Item Now!

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_header/20/20YWVPQYNRKT1773874502811.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_header/20/20YWVPQYNRKT1773874502811.png)

Get the **Cuddly Void Grrgle** Housing decor item! From now through April 23, 3:00 pm PDT watch **4 hours** of any *World of Warcraft* content stream on Twitch.tv while these Twitch Drops are active to claim this limited-time Housing decor item.

Players who have claimed this new item from the Twitch Drop will also be able to visit Dennia Silvertongue in Silvermoon City to purchase more copies of this adorable decor. Additional copies can also be purchased from Tuuran in Stormwind and Gabbi in Orgrimmar (near the Trading Post).

Frequently asked questions and details on how to link your Twitch account can be found in our [previously published article](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/news/24271368).
