> Fill your ears with the sounds of Azeroth with some Synthwave beats to help carry you to BlizzCon®.

---

BlizzCon® is only one week away, and we can’t wait to see you! Join Chromie and Nozdormu as they observe the timeways while folks gather for in-person and virtual festivities.

These Synthwave tunes were mixed by Jason Charles Miller (who is also the voice of Prince Valanar in Wrath of the Lich King and High Commander Goodchilde in Legion) and Jamison Boaz of Rezodrone.
