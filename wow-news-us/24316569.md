Step into the macabre world of Talebound in a PvE action RPG game mode where you become part of the dark chronicles of a mysterious Storyteller, chillingly brought to life, and where danger is written on every page.

> **When**: December 1 (with regional maintenance)

Take a rest, hang your cap, and sip some hot cocoa, but don’t get too comfortable; the village and the storyteller don’t appreciate a story unfinished, so you’ll have to strategize for the horrors to come.

## Twisted Tales, Rewritten Fates

Talebound has four stories across Normal, Veteran, and Heroic difficulties to explore, each with its own settings, enemies, and boss encounters, including witches, giants, marshals, bishops, deep-sea horrors, and nightmare royalty.

[![alternate-image-name](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/L5JT6JVCLY8H1792714301515.jpg)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/L5JT6JVCLY8H1792714301515.jpg)

**Darkhaven**

Darkhaven, home to Konrad von Sonnesheim, the Lord Marshal, and to Alexandre Dozsenc, the Blood Bishop, is cut off from the rest of the world.

It is the only place that survived a devastating plague that wiped out everyone beyond its borders.

[![alternate-image-name](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/MPEDD671MGNB1789246423424.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/MPEDD671MGNB1789246423424.png)

**Greenthistle Wood**

According to the tale, two humble villages—Shady Hollow and Riverton—lie in Greenthistle Wood.

It is a place overrun and terrorized by two witches, a giant, and a host of vile, predatory fiends who have filled the wood with unspeakable cruelty and mayhem.

[![alternate-image-name](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/S2DBRKT1OFUN1792714324421.jpg)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/S2DBRKT1OFUN1792714324421.jpg)

**Greywake's Expedition**

Greywake’s Hill was once a thriving and prosperous island, but something sinister has happened.

Follow the tale of the many who have fallen to Zzyg'th, the Hadal Tyrant, and to Chathoa, the Herald from Beyond.

[![alternate-image-name](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/g7/G7UCIE9XR0BC1794512828458.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/g7/G7UCIE9XR0BC1794512828458.png)

**Grove of the Night Court**

A frightening chronicle of Tilywth, The Fairy Queen, and Maernok, The Nightmare King.

Discover the aftermath of when dream and nightmare intertwine as night moves through the world.

Collections of these stories are also available in town, if you wish to hear them again.

## Talebound Classes and Talents

Across all classes, movement and defense are central, and every class can double jump, dodge, Evade (gaining a moment of temporary immunity), Sprint, and Guard, making positioning a key part of combat.

Three paths lie before you, each one steeped in shadow. Choose your role in Talebound, complete with a talent tree of fearsome abilities waiting to be claimed. Each class’s talent tree holds 27 points, and you’ll spend **Tales of Valor** at the Scribe in Quillgrave to unlock extra abilities, then to upgrade the talents that make them more fearsome, empowering you to stand against whatever your enemies can throw at you.

### WITCH

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/B7O0M5L7E0ZF1794506821502.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/B7O0M5L7E0ZF1794506821502.png)

The Witch uses lightning, poison, spiders, moonlight, thorns, and ravens to overwhelm enemies with a darker, spell-driven style.

- **Brewing Storm**: A sequence of three attacks that inflict lightning damage, with the last strike dealing more serious damage.
- **Thorn**: Launches thorns that erupt beneath the two nearest enemies.
- **Moonlight Ravens**: A channeled spell used while moving to summon a flock of ravens that each seek out nearby targets, inflicting damage to them.
- **Bubbling Cauldron**: Summons a plague of frogs from your cauldron. Frogs leap to a random enemy and explode, inflicting poisonous damage in a small area.
- **Winter Spiders**: Summons spiders that skitter forward, including a brood mother that explodes and inflicts serious damage in a small area.
- **Feline Communion**: Blinks forward and transforms you into a cat for a short time. While in this form, a shower of stars inflicts damage to all nearby enemies, and you may cast Crooked Path to blink forward, escaping through moonlight.
- **Coven**: Summons a coven of witches to assail your enemies. When you strike enemies with Bubbling Cauldron, Winter Spiders, or Moonlight Ravens, your Coven will attack those enemies with a similar spell.

### SLAYER

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/AHCS3VPFF63C1794506821192.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/AHCS3VPFF63C1794506821192.png)

The Slayer's martial might and monster hunter knowledge give him the rare physical tools to combat the beasts of folklore. Wield a sword to cut down eldritch foes and a gun for when a blade doesn’t suffice

- **Slash**: A sequence of three attacks that damage enemies directly in front of you, with higher damage and stun on the final blow.
- **Throwing Knives**: Moves the player quickly and strikes nearby enemies. It can cancel other attacks for fast movement.
- **Shrapnel Round**: Fires multiple rounds at enemies, bouncing between foes and inflicting damage to each it hits.
- **Explosive Shot**: Fire your shotgun at enemies in front of you. Closer foes take damage from multiple shots. Empower this ability to deal greater damage.
- **Minefield**: Allows you to leap backward and hurl an arc of mines in front of you. These mines inflict damage and slow your enemies.
- **Silver Bolt**: Leap into the air and fire several silver bolts into your foe, immediately inflicting damage on the enemy with the highest health in range. While in the air, you are temporarily immune to damage.
- **Dense Macabre**: Become a flurry of blades and shadows, striking enemies at random for heavy damage and gaining temporary immunity.

### INQUISITOR

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/F1EWHQKVRXNF1794506821264.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/F1EWHQKVRXNF1794506821264.png)

The Inquisitor’s zeal lights every corner and burns the face of evil, no matter the illusions it uses to hide.

- **Purify**: A sequence of three attacks that damage enemies in front of you, each strike more powerful than the last.
- **Radiance**: Can be cast while moving to hurl fire at a nearby target that explodes on impact, burning all nearby enemies.
- **Light’s Wrath**: Conjures a wrathful sun above an enemy, burning enemies nearby.
- **Rapture**: Wreathes enemies in damaging flames for a short time. These flames detonate on your command.
- **Condemn**: Damages enemies in front of you, inflicting holy damage and reducing their movement speed. Crucially, it is castable while moving.
- **Exorcism**: Perform an exorcism upon the highest health enemy in front of you. The longer you empower this ability, the more damage you will inflict. Roots you in place but inflicts high damage.
- **Divine Punishment**: Channel Divine Punishment to inflict damage against the nearest enemy and become immune to damage for a period of time. When the duration ends, you leap at the targeted enemy and inflict damage to all surrounding enemies.

## A Telling Tale Isn’t Without Its Rewards

[![alternate-image-name](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/5I2OED6VT8SA1789246420177.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/5I2OED6VT8SA1789246420177.png)

**Left to Right:** Witch Hunter's Shadow Attire Transmog Set, Quillgrave Storyteller’s Lectern Decor, Shadowbound Storybook Mount, and Quillgrave Talebound Shadowtome Decor

As you temper the horrors of the tales before you, earn Bound Silver to spend on fashionable rewards to use in *Midnight* and beyond.

Along the way, you’ll also collect Ducats, which you can convert into the resources needed to progress through Talebound. Once your tale is complete, return to Quillgrave to receive Bound Silver and other rewards for your efforts.

Spend your Bound Silver on transmog sets, individual pieces, and other cosmetic rewards in the Talebound store, accessible from the character selection screen, the Game Menu, or by visiting Nib in Quillgrave.

A full list of rewards can be seen in our [Talebound rewards article](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/news/24317867).

## To Begin Your Adventure

To start playing Talebound, open the game mode from the World of Warcraft menu. Create your hero and choose your archetype—Witch, Slayer, or Inquisitor.

Upon completing the Red Hollow introduction and learning your way around Quillgrave, your hero is now ready for the daunting adventures ahead. Choose your story and set out on the journey in Normal, Veteran, or Heroic difficulties.

Between stories, return to Quillgrave to make any upgrades, get supplies, and accept quests that earn currency for cosmetic rewards.
