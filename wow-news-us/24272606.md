### Explore the Lands, Legends, and Legacy of The Burning Crusade to Midnight

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_header/bk/BKNCW75FGTU71775527468877.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_header/bk/BKNCW75FGTU71775527468877.png)

Whether you joined *World of Warcraft* from the beginning, took your first steps through the Dark Portal into Outland in *The Burning Crusade*, or arrived later to pick up on the story of Azeroth through Lorewalker Cho, we’re all on this journey together—and what a journey it’s been… and continues to be.

In *Midnight*, we find ourselves back where many of our fondest memories were forged and where new ones are made with familiar places, faces, and stories brought to life once more. From Eversong’s gold-lit boughs to Zul’Aman’s shadowed ruins, the road to *Midnight* is paved with echoes.

Join us as we connect the threads of time in our [previously published article](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/news/24264003/), and discover how familiar lands and legends return—changed by time, scarred by war, and poised to face the Void.

### The Arena World Championship is Underway

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/ks/KSBYXVMFYSAT1775242315837.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/ks/KSBYXVMFYSAT1775242315837.png)

Gather round and gear up, the Arena World Championship has officially kicked off in Season 1 of *Midnight,*and the premiere PvP Program for WoW is now underway, with a loaded lineup of competitions for hopeful heroes around the world.

The matches from each week’s Top 8 Tournament will be streamed and broadcast live on the [Warcraft Twitch](https://www.twitch.tv/warcraft) and [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/@Warcraft) channels starting at 10:00 am PDT / 19:00 CEST each day:

- Cup 1 - April 10–12
- Cup 2 - April 17–19
- Cup 3 - May 1–3

Find out what to expect and when to tune in as the gladiators embark on the road to BlizzCon from our [previously published article](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/news/24266796/).

### *Noblegarden Ends April 13: There's Still Time to Spring into Action*

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_header/f8/F8WR348W1M1Z1775161798147.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_header/f8/F8WR348W1M1Z1775161798147.png)

Hop to it and visit Azure Watch, Dolanaar, Goldshire, Kharanos, Brill, Razor Hill, Bloodhoof Village, Falconwing Square at the edge of the Ruins of Silvermoon (Pre-Midnight), Valdrakken, Dornogal, or Silvermoon City in Eversong Woods (Midnight) to join in the festivities.

**When:** April 6–13

**What's New in 2026**

- Quel’Thalas welcomes the feast of Noblegarden with great aplomb in Silvermoon City, festooned with festive decorations, as well as a new egg hunt for hidden **Brightly Colored Eggs**.
- The festive new Spring Reveler’s Cornsilk transmog set will help you spring into action. Pieces of this darling set drop from Daetan Swiftplume.
- Those who wish to brighten up their day with a splash of color can rejoice! If you have the Duck Disguiser toy, colors now drop from Daetan Swiftplume and can be shared across your Warband.

Bring the festivities home by hiding **[Noblegarden Eggs](https://www.wowhead.com/item=44818/noblegarden-egg?brl)** around your Neighborhood for your friends and neighbors to find! Spring on over to our [Noblegarden overview](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/en-us/news/24272601) for all the details.
