Follow the glow and take wing through your *World of Warcraft*® adventures astride the crimson-speckled **Sunflare Driftmoth\*** Skyriding mount, with the **Sunflutter Driftmoth** pet drifting cheerfully alongside.

[![Video](https://markdown-videos-api.jorgenkh.no/youtube/EaGzj9tPQZ0?width=1280&height=720)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EaGzj9tPQZ0)

Don’t let this bundle flutter away. Unlock these special items with the purchase of a [6-Month or 12-Month Subscription](https://shop.battle.net/product/world-of-warcraft-subscription):

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_header/wx/WXB6T3TV11Z91783539584011.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_header/wx/WXB6T3TV11Z91783539584011.png)

- **Sunflare Driftmoth Mount\*\***: Glide through the skies on the radiant wings of the Sunflare Driftmoth. Bathed in warm light and glowing embers, this luminous companion brings a brilliant touch to every journey. Drift through the lands of *World of Warcraft®* in luminous style with this enchanting new mount.
- **Sunflutter Driftmoth Pet\*\*\***: Add a spark of brilliance to every adventure with the radiant Sunflutter Driftmoth, a tiny companion aglow with shimmering wings. Bring this fluffy little companion along for your *WoW*® Classic progression adventures, currently in *Mists of Pandaria Classic*™.

Players already on an existing 6-Month or 12-Month recurring subscription plan with a future renewal date will automatically receive the mount and pet at no additional charge, no later than July 31, 2026 (must be redeemed by July 31, 2027) through [Battle.net](https://shop.battle.net/).
