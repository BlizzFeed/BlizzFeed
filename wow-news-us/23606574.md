> Just in time for BlizzConline, commemorate 30 years of Blizzard games and adventures yet to come with the Celebration Collection.

---

> ***UPDATED FEB 19:** Just announced at BlizzConline: When you purchase any of the three Celebration Collections in the Blizzard Shop, you’ll also get the Blizzard Arcade Collection! You can view the [full list of available goodies for all your Blizzard games here](https://shop.battle.net/product/celebration-collection)! Those of you who purchased the Celebration Collection prior to the announcement will also receive the newly announced goods—no further purchase required.*

This February, Blizzard® is celebrating our 30th year of creating worlds, exploring new universes, and sharing countless epic gaming experiences together.

[![Video](https://markdown-videos-api.jorgenkh.no/youtube/9vIo_zF74gU?width=1280&height=720)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9vIo_zF74gU)

We’re looking forward to marking the occasion with everyone later this month at BlizzConline™—and if you’d like to summon forth some anniversary spirit in your favorite Blizzard games, you can pick up one of three **[Celebration Collection bundles](https://shop.battle.net/product/celebration-collection)** filled with in-game goodies and extras, available starting today in the Blizzard Shop. When you purchase the Celebration Collection, you can bring a piece of BlizzConline with you into whatever world or universe you call home, and mark the occasion in-game alongside your friends, allies, teammates, guildmates, broodmates, and rivals.

For *World of Warcraft* players, purchasing these bundles will reward a pet, a mount, and 30 days of game time.

Snag the **Essentials Pack ($19.99)** to unlock the Moon-Touched Netherwhelp pet, adorned with scales said to reflect the night sky of another world—perhaps one of the many you’ve explored on your own far-reaching journey.

Upgrade to the **Heroic Pack ($39.99)** to add the mighty Snowstorm bear mount to your WoW collection. Thunder across the tundras of Azeroth and the fields of Bastion astride this mighty frost-shrouded mount bearing the sigil of an epic anniversary. This Blizzard-born bear leaves behind snow drifts from time to time, so don’t be too surprised if a snowball fight breaks out in the middle of Maldraxxus.

Going for the gold with the **Epic Pack ($59.99)** will grant you all of the above along with 30 days of World of Warcraft time to continue your adventures in World of Warcraft and World of Warcraft Classic.

![Celebration Collection](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/gallery/aa/AAWM12ZJOKMZ1611965148058.png)

The Celebration Collection packs include the above along with goodies for Heroes of the Storm®, Overwatch®, StarCraft®, Diablo®, and Hearthstone®. Get a closer look in the [full preview](https://www.blizzcon.com/en-us/news/23617534), and head to the [Blizzard Shop](https://shop.battle.net/product/celebration-collection) to pick up a pack.

*Note that Moon-Touched Netherwhelp and Snowstorm are only available in World of Warcraft and not accessible in WoW Classic.*
