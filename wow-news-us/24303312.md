# Submit Your Questions for the World of Warcraft Live Q&A September 17

- URL: https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/en-us/news/24303312/submit-your-questions-for-the-world-of-warcraft-live-q-a-september-17
![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/xq/XQHA2LAWZ50S1789241337987.png)

> Have a question about what you learned at BlizzCon 2026 after the show reveals? We're collecting your questions and hosting a live Q&A with members of the World of Warcraft modern and Forever development teams to answer as many as we can on September 17.

---

Have a question about what you learned at BlizzCon 2026 after the show reveals? Head over to [the World of Warcraft forums](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/submit-your-question-for-the-live-wow-qa-on-september-17/2347251 "https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/") to share it with us.

Then, during the week following BlizzCon 2026, we’re hosting a live Q&A with members from across the World of Warcraft modern and *Forever* development teams as we take a closer look at the compiled questions players have been eager to ask.

**When:** Thursday, September 17 at 10:00 a.m. PDT for modern, and 10:30 am PDT for *Forever*\
**Where:** Official [Warcraft Twitch](https://www.twitch.tv/warcraft "https://www.twitch.tv/warcraft") and [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/warcraft "https://www.youtube.com/warcraft") channels

If you miss the live Q&A, a VOD will be available after the stream so you can catch up.

---

We look forward to answering your questions and exploring your next adventures in Azeroth together.
