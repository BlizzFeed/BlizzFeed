### WoW Classic News and Development

#### Wrath of the Lich King Classic™ Arrives September 26

[![Video](https://markdown-videos-api.jorgenkh.no/youtube/J7VBka-A3hc?width=1280&height=720)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=J7VBka-A3hc)

Embark on a treacherous journey through Northrend's wintry forests and snowy peaks when World of *Warcraft®: Wrath of the Lich King Classic™* goes live beginning September 26 at 3 pm PDT globally. Check out our article for [more details](https://worldofwarcraft.com/news/23833251/).

#### Become a Death Knight in Wrath of the Lich King Classic

![A Death Knight with a rune-covered sword stands ready for combat](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/OQ756BMQYMEX1658190867421.jpg)

You stood boldly against the shadow and purchased another dawn for the world...with your life. The specter of death looms over the world again, and it is the hour of your dark rebirth. For more details on this class, [read our article](https://worldofwarcraft.com/news/23818249/).

#### Learn the Art of Inscription in Wrath of the Lich King Classic

![A gnome with an inkwell and feather quill](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/7IN8A7MT0UB71658277308597.jpg)

Scribes live and die by the pen, weaving words of power into parchment through sheer magic. Wrath of the Lich King Classic brings back the first iteration of a beloved profession—Inscription. Learn how to become a master scribe and get the most ink for your book [from our detailed article](https://worldofwarcraft.com/news/23818250/).

#### The Battle for Wintergrasp in Wrath Classic

![Wintergrasp keep amid looming mountains covered in snow](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/oz/OZ2RJTFJSOI11658277259556.png)

An iconic part of the original Wrath of the Lich King, the WoW Classic team shares some insights on the Battle for Wintergrasp development in Wrath Classic. Read more about our plans [in our article](https://worldofwarcraft.com/news/23833249/).

**THIS WEEK IN SHADOWLANDS**

- **PvP Brawl: Shado-Pan Showdown**
  - Step into Tiger's Peak Arena and get ready for the Shado-Pan Showdown. You'll race for control of a center control point to activate one of two bosses. The first to kill the other team's boss wins!
- **Weekly Bonus Event: Warlords of Draenor**
  - Prime your minds for an especially tangled chrono-spatial conundrum. Players can adventure into six Warlords of Draenor's dungeons to reap new rewards during this week's Bonus Event.

**COMING UP IN SHADOWLANDS**

Beginning Next Week:

- **Battleground Bonus Event**
  - While this event is active, random battlegrounds will award Honor at an increased rate. Glory awaits in the fields of battle!
- **PvP Brawl: Warsong Scramble**
  - Warsong Gulch has always been the scene of an epic capture-the-flag battle, but what happens when you can capture your enemy's flag without your own at base? Or if there are multiple flags at each base? Find out when you step into this PvP Brawl!

Beginning the week of August 7:

- **Darkmoon Faire**
  - Meet Silas Darkmoon and his troupe, play games that test mind and nerve, and behold exotic sights from the four corners of Azeroth…and beyond!
- **Torghast: Chorus of Dead Souls**
  - Dead souls swarm within Torghast, Tower of the Damned!
- **PvP Brawl: Cooking Impossible**
  - Nomi is hosting a Pandaren Chili Cookoff in the Valley of the Four Winds between the Alliance and the Horde! The first to bring Nomi all of the required ingredients wins.
- **Shadowlands Dungeon Event**
  - While this event is active, the final boss of each Shadowlands dungeon awards an extra item when defeated.

Check your in-game calendar for a list of the current and upcoming in-game event start times!
