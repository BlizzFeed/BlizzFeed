# Keep Track of Potions, Trinkets and More with New User Interface Updates

- URL: https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/en-us/news/24294064/keep-track-of-potions-trinkets-and-more-with-new-user-interface-updates
![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/0n/0N58B5OUOBKA1785545378437.png)

> Curse of Ula’tek brings new user interface updates to help you keep track of cooldowns, consumables, and key combat information. Track potions and trinkets in the Cooldown Manager, ping key spell and health information to your party, and adjust Raid Frames to keep the healing effects you rely on front and center.

---

Curse of Ula’tek introduces user interface updates designed to help you better track your cooldowns, consumables, and key combat information when it matters most.

---

## Stay in Control With the Cooldown Manager

With the new updates, the Cooldown Manager will now be able to track trinkets and potions helping with consumable management. It can also track racial ability cooldowns and durations.

[![alternate-image-name](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/M1M89EZ7RIRQ1785542985583.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/M1M89EZ7RIRQ1785542985583.png)
[![alternate-image-name](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/wu/WU4WM7RCGS7V1785546055007.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/wu/WU4WM7RCGS7V1785546055007.png)

For managing other resources, more updates arrive to the ping system. Players can ping their action bar or the Cooldown Manager directly providing your team with the status of your spells.

![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/3VJGZN1HMV3O1781742463706.png)

Your own unit frames can also get pinged to convey the status of your health to the group and players who are acting in a healing role will also share the status of their mana saving them from needing to say, “OOM” before the next pull.

![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/R9CMDQBL0TQ71785542986859.png)

---

## Keep the Heals Flowing with New Configurations

[![alternate-image-name](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/1B658N9IDSFZ1785542985574.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/1B658N9IDSFZ1785542985574.png)

Healers can now configure or hide the buffs shown on Raid Frames, so the healing effects that matter most to you stay visible.

---

We’ll see you in-game as you put these updates to use with your party and raid team.
