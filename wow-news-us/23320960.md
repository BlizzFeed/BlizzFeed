# Arena World Championship and Mythic Dungeon International 2020 Sign-Ups Now Open

- URL: https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/en-us/news/23320960/arena-world-championship-and-mythic-dungeon-international-2020-sign-ups-now-open
![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/y5/Y5Z535VKI6GF1583173468639.png)

> Do you and your team have what it takes to make it the top spot in the Arena World Championship or Mythic Dungeon International? Sign-ups for both competitions are now open!

---

Do you have what it takes to be a champion? It’s time to get your best crew together and sign up for the Arena World Championship (AWC) or Mythic Dungeon International (MDI) to test your mettle against the best of the best the world (of Warcraft) has to offer.

---

#### Mythic Dungeon International

The Mythic Dungeon International (MDI) is making its return with a new dungeon and a new Affix to challenge competitors and put them through their paces. Gather your best dungeon runners and [sign up today](https://battlefy.com/mythic-dungeon-international) on Battlefy.

---

#### Arena World Championship

We’re shaking things up for the 13th year of the Arena World Championship (AWC), and you and your crew can vie for a piece of the prize pool and the right to brag across all of Azeroth. To sign up, just head over to [Battlefy](https://battlefy.com/world-of-warcraft-arena-world-championship) today.

---

Learn more about the 2020 esports season in our [original announcement](https://worldofwarcraft.com/en-us/news/23299641), then join the discussion [on the forums](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/458924).
