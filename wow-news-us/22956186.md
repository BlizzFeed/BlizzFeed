# Announcing the Blizzard Entertainment Student Art Contest 2018-2019 Winners!

- URL: https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/en-us/news/22956186/announcing-the-blizzard-entertainment-student-art-contest-2018-2019-winners
![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/wr/WR9GGZZV43BF1555104471062.jpg)

> The 2018-2019 Student Art Contest has come to an end, and after intense scrutiny and some impassioned deliberation, we’ve identified this year’s winners.

---

---

Presented by Blizzard Entertainment’s University Relations and World of Warcraft development teams, the eighth annual [Blizzard Student Art Contest](https://worldofwarcraft.com/en-us/news/22548003) has drawn to a close. After some fervent debate and impassioned deliberation, our judges sifted through the many remarkable entries and identified the winning entries.

Check out the winners and runner-ups below for each of the four categories.

---
