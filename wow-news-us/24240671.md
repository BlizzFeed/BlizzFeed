### Legion Remix: Skies of Fire Now Live!

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_header/g7/G735Q9YL88IC1757956537624.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_header/g7/G735Q9YL88IC1757956537624.png)

Get ready for fast frenetic fun in Legion Remix as you meet up with the infinite dragonflight once more, level new characters, and earn a variety of items to add to your collection. Take to the skies of the Broken Isles with the original Legion content and the Emerald Nightmare and Trial of Valor raids.

Legion Remix is a time-limited seasonal event where players can create a Timerunner as one of the original 12 Legion classes and replay the entire Legion expansion at an accelerated pace from level 10-80 with some major gameplay twists. Players will collect power, face epic challenges, and earn a mountain of cosmetics such as pets, mounts, and transmog gear.

We can’t wait to see you on the Broken Isles in Legion Remix! Read [our article](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/news/24239491/) to learn more.

### Midnight Prologue: The Warning Content Update and Housing Early Access

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_header/hk/HK3BNKK5YV2K1759970928084.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_header/hk/HK3BNKK5YV2K1759970928084.png)

With the Ghosts of K’aresh behind us, Azeroth stands on the precipice of a future overshadowed by Xal’atath and the relentless advance of the Void. In The Warning, the stage is set for what comes next before continuing the story in the *Midnight* expansion. Players will also be welcomed home with the start of Housing Early Access!

Head over to our [previously published article](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/en-us/news/24241096) to learn more—we look forward to seeing you on the PTR!

### In Development: Phase 2 of the Midnight Alpha

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/xp/XPVJMB808VRS1758555003001.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/xp/XPVJMB808VRS1758555003001.png)

Phase two of the Midnight Alpha is here, and with it, players in the test will be able to level from 83 to 88, explore the Zul’Aman zone, and more.

**Midnight Alpha Phase 2 Content:**

- Level 83-88
- Zone: Zul’Aman
- Story and Quests
- Dungeons: Den of Nalorakk and Maisara Caverns
- Delves: Twilight Crypts and Atal’Aman

Over the course of the test, we’re inviting community veterans, press, fansites, and friends and family to check out the upcoming expansion and give us feedback.

Players selected to participate will receive an email invitation directing them to download the game client directly from the Battle.net desktop app. As always, please be aware of phishing attempts—if you’re unsure whether your invitation is legitimate, please log in to your Battle.net account to verify that there is a Midnight Alpha license attached (under Your Game Accounts).
