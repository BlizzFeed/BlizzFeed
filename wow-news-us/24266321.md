### Seasonal Rewards

![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/y8/Y8XPNODF7SOU1772751051908.png)

**(left) Convalescent Carrion and (right) Calamitous Carrion**

- Midnight Keystone Explorer: Season 1
  - Complete a Mythic+ dungeon within the time limit during Midnight Season One.
- Midnight Keystone Conqueror: Season One
  - Title: the Umbral
    - Attain a Mythic+ Rating of at least 1500 during Midnight Season One.
- Midnight Keystone Master: Season One
  - Mount Reward: [Calamitous Carrion](https://www.wowhead.com/item=262620/calamitous-carrion?brl)
    - Attain a Mythic+ Rating of at least 2000 during Midnight Season One.
- Midnight Keystone Hero: Season One
  - Armor Visual Effect: [Gleaming Sunmote](https://www.wowhead.com/item=265071/gleaming-sunmote?brl)
    - Attain a Mythic+ Rating of at least 2500 during Midnight Sason One.
- Midnight Keystone Legend: Season One
  - Mount Reward: [Convalescent Carrion](https://www.wowhead.com/item=262621/convalescent-carrion?brl)
    - Attain a Mythic+ Rating of at least 3000 during Midnight Season One.

End *Midnight* Season 1 with a Mythic+ Rating in the top 0.1% of all players in your region to earn the title "the ***Umbral Hero"***.

Players can also gain access to Dungeon portals for each of the eight dungeons when they complete Mythic 10 or higher within the time limit.

## PvP Season 1

The season brings a new PvP ladder, increased item level gear, fresh rewards, a new weapon illusion, tabard, cloak, pennant, and more!

#### Season 1 PvP Elite Transmog Set

- Combatant I: Midnight Season 1 (1000): Cloak
- Combatant II: Midnight Season 1 (1200): Legs, Bracers
- Challenger I: Midnight Season 1 (1400): Gloves, Boots
- Challenger II: Midnight Season 1 (1600): Chest, Belt
- Rival I: Midnight Season 1 (1800): Shoulder, Helm
- Rival II: Midnight Season 1 (1950): Weapon Illusion
- Duelist: Midnight Season 1 (2100): Prestige Cloak
- Elite: Midnight Season 1 (2300+): Galactic Gladiator's Tabard

#### Weapon Illusion

Earn the PvP Weapon Illusion “Galaxy” when you unlock the Rival II Feat of Strength.

#### Bloody Tokens & War Mode Gear

Bloody Tokens are now obtainable and can be used to purchase War Mode gear.

#### PvP Mounts

![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/ul/ULPC4HUEMZI71772692083563.png)

**Galactic Gladiator’s Goredrake**\
*“Originating from the void, fully armored and ready for combat.”*

- **Vicious Snaplizard Mount:** Earned as part of the [Galactic Combatant](https://www.wowhead.com/achievement=61403/galactic-combatant?brl) Achievement—win Rated PvP matches during Season 1 while at 1000 rating or higher. These mounts come in an [Alliance](https://www.wowhead.com/item=257504/vicious-snaplizard?brl) and [Horde](https://www.wowhead.com/item=257502/vicious-snaplizard?brl) version, with faction-colored armor.
- [Galactic Gladiator's Goredrake](https://www.wowhead.com/item=260228/galactic-gladiators-goredrake?brl) Mount: Win 50 games while at 2300 rating or higher as a Gladiator in Midnight Season in 3v3.

#### Titles

- Titles are earned based on PvP rating:
  - Combatant: Earn a PvP rating of 1000
  - Challenger: Earn a PvP rating of 1400
  - Rival: Earn a PvP rating of 1800
  - Duelist: Earn a PvP Rating of 2100
  - Season Character Title "the Elite": Earn the rank of Elite during Midnight Season 1.
  - Legend: Win 100 Rated Solo Shuffle rounds at Elite rank during Midnight Season 1.
  - Galactic Legend: End Season 1 in the top 0.1% of the Solo Shuffle ladder. Requires 50 games won in Season 1.
  - Galactic Gladiator: End Season 1 in the top 0.1% of the 3v3 arena ladder. Requires 150 games won in Season 1.
- Battleground Blitz Title Rewards
  - **Strategist**: Win 25 Rated Battleground Blitz matches at Elite rank during Midnight Season 1.
  - **Galactic Warlord** (Horde Only): End Midnight PvP Season 1 in the top 0.1% of the Rated Battleground Blitz ladder (requires 50 games won in Midnight Season 1).
  - **Galactic Marshall** (Alliance Only): End Midnight PvP Season 1 in the top 0.1% of the Rated Battleground Blitz ladder (requires 50 games won in Midnight Season 1).

#### Season 1 PvP Pennant, Tabard, and Cloak

- Galactic Gladiator's Tabard
  - Earned from achieving the Elite Rank during Midnight Season 1.
- Galactic Gladiator's Prestigious Cloak
  - Earned from achieving the Duelist Rank during Midnight Season 1.
- Galactic Legend's Pennant
  - Earned from achieving either the Legend: Midnight Season 1 Achievement or Strategist: Midnight Season 1 Achievement.
- Nightfall Legend's Pennant
  - Earned from achieving the Solo Shuffle: Medic: Midnight achievement.
  - *This pennant is earnable starting with Season 1 but is obtainable all expansion.*
- Nightfall Strategist's Pennant
  - Earned from achieving the Battleground Blitz: Medic: Midnight achievement.
  - *This pennant is earnable starting with Season 1 but is obtainable all expansion.*
