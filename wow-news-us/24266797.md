## Travel to Val and Naigtal to Quell Leaders of the Void

![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/l2/L2WPUVXBXPQK1780417593228.png)

Facing the forces of the Void in Void Assaults and Ritual Sites isn’t enough to stop their machinations. To cut the threat off at its source, you’ll need to hunt down the leaders behind these brutal attacks on Azeroth. Track them through a portal in Voidstorm leading to two new locations—Val and Naigtal.

![alternate-image-name](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/BZ4MWORXCDQC1777415621947.png)

**Naigtal:** A fungal, arcane-rich world now occupied by an insidious ethereal faction known as the Hal'hadar.

![alternate-image-name](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/8FDMQTC5QKFP1777415625345.png)

**Val**: An icy world once inhabited by the Legion where the Domanaar Imperator Pertinax has established his bastion of power.

The portal to these worlds is unstable and switches destinations every week, bringing a rotating set of challenges and experiences. Players can undertake World Quests, defeat rare enemies, take part in events, and ultimately have a showdown with the leaders as world bosses. Learn more in our [overview article](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/news/24271372).

## Gain New Power with the Omnium Folio

![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_header/36/36GFTEVTPMY91777415569123.png)

Join Magister Umbric and Grand Magister Rommath on a mission to reconfigure and restore the Sunstrider Omnium. This ancient elven relic was originally created by Dath’remar Sunstrider to study and observe the various schools of magic and to serve as an academic repository. After years of silence, the Sunstrider Omnium has now reawakened, unsettling the Magisters and sowing disarray among them.

Once unlocked, you’ll be entrusted with the Omnium Folio, a runic ledger containing powerful runes of power you can use in battle. Continue taking part in weekly activities to imbue more runes—customizing these initial runes and unlocking their maximum potential to aid you in the fight against the Void. Learn more in our [previously published article](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/news/24277442).

## Get More with Ritual Sites and Void Assault Updates

![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/WXY9ABHDVH791776187753426.png)

With the content update, players will find increased rewards from Ritual Sites and Void Assaults.

### Void Strikes and Incursions Grant More Experience

Experience earned from Void Strikes, Void Incursions, and weekly quests is increased. Drop rates for Dark Particles have also increased, along with the chance to receive Bulging Satchels upon completing these activities.

### Shop with Trima Dawnsetter and Maren Silverwing

Trima Dawnsetter in Silvermoon City now offers pouches for purchase that contain the same cosmetics found in Bulging Satchels, available for 150 Dark Particles. These pouches include items normally dropped from Ritual Site end chests.

Maren Silverwing now sells slot-specific Bind-on-Pickup Heroic caches for 750 Field Accolades, as well as slot-specific Warbound Champion caches for 100 Field Accolades.

### Challenge Yourself in Ritual Sites with a New Tier 6 Difficulty

Face greater challenges in Ritual Sites with the new Tier 6 difficulty. Select six challenges as you take on enemies within. Completing these encounters grants additional rewards, including 5 Mythic Dawncrests and 10 Heroic Dawncrests.

New weekly quests are also available at Tier 6 when specific challenges are selected. Players can earn an additional Bonus Roll from these quests during weeks 3 and 6.

Earn two new achievements:

- *Advanced Ritual Site Studies* — Complete the six advanced challenges for Lady Darkglen.
- *Pinnacle Ritual Work* — Complete each Ritual Site at Tier 6 with all eight challenges active.\
  **Reward:** Title — *Ritual Breaker*

## Turbulent Timeways Returns the Week of June 30

![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/FXF3XG2HSFRL1777415624005.png)

- Week of June 30: Dragonflight

- Week of July 14: Shadowlands
