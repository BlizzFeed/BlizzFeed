Cackle with glee as you sweep through the skies atop two bewitching Skyriding broom mounts and 600 Trader's Tender in your pocket with the Enchanted Sweeper Bundle\*.

Which Witch's sweeper will you choose to send you skyward?

The Enchanted Sweeper Bundle is available for a limited time from the Battle.net® or in-game Shop through March 31, 2025, and includes the Twilight and Sky Witch's Sweeper Skyriding mounts and 600 Trader's Tender to use in the Trading Post.

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/jk/JKFGD9ES2WH41738598986018.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/jk/JKFGD9ES2WH41738598986018.png)

[Visit the Shop](https://blizz.ly/4hk1r85)

Share the Twilight Witch's Sweeper and Sky Witch's Sweeper across all present and future WoW® Modern characters. They automatically scale to the fastest riding skill known by each character; however, if they aren't a high enough level to use a flying mount, the Sweepers make excellent ground mounts.

You don't need to be a thaumaturgist to make yourself look magical; complete the look when you stir a [Sky Witch Pack](https://blizz.ly/3Z3zWHL) containing the [Ensemble: Sky Witch's Attire](https://www.wowhead.com/item=212221/ensemble-sky-witchs-attire?brl) into your fashion cauldron before it evaporates into the ether.

---

\*Requires a *World of Warcraft*® subscription or Game Time and is unavailable in *World of Warcraft* Classic progression games.
