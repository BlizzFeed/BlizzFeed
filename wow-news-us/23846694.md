# Get A Northrend Upgrade Before Your Journey to Icecrown

- URL: https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/en-us/news/23846694/get-a-northrend-upgrade-before-your-journey-to-icecrown
![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/69RYHVLU1VW11661551779112.jpg)

> World of Warcraft®: Wrath of the Lich King® Classic™ is just around the corner. Enhance your experience with the Northrend Heroic Upgrade and Northrend Epic Upgrade, available the week of August 30.

---

The frozen wastes of Northrend await you, hero! *World of Warcraft®: Wrath of the Lich King® Classic™* launches **September 26**, and the pre-expansion patch arrives **August 30**. Wrath of the Lich King Classic will be included with every WoW players’ subscription at no additional cost.

However, for those who haven't played *Burning Crusade Classic™* recently, or if you're looking to enhance your experience, we’re offering optional upgrades—along with a trove of rare items to aid you in your coming journey.

---

Starting the week of August 30, after the weekly scheduled realm maintenance, everyone who wants to get an early start to join the adventure when Northrend opens on September 26 will be able to purchase the [Northrend Heroic Upgrade](https://shop.battle.net/product/world-of-warcraft-wrath-classic-upgrades), allowing them to boost one character of their choosing on a Wrath of the Lich King Classic realm to level 70 (limit one per World of Warcraft account; **not usable on Death Knight characters**). The boosted character will receive:

- Expert Riding skill
- A race-specific epic ground mount
- A faction-specific rare flying mount
- Weapon skills up to level 70
- Access to Azeroth and Outland flight paths
- A set of level 70 Uncommon (green) quality gear
- Up to four Draenic Leather Pack bags (14 slots each)
- A little bit of gold to start their journey

In addition, the Northrend Heroic Upgrade includes Pebble, a penguin pet and the Fishspeaker's Lucky Lure, a toy to summon your very own tuskarr fishing companion.

![A forsaken caster receives a level-up boost, a tuskarr companion and a penguin with her.](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/gallery/VRRELTDOAXOO1661551793151.jpg)

---

Intrepid explorers looking to further enhance their Wrath of the Lich King Classic experience can purchase the optional [Northrend Epic Upgrade](https://shop.battle.net/product/world-of-warcraft-wrath-classic-upgrades). This edition includes the Level 70 Character Boost\* and all the items from the Northrend Heroic Upgrade, in addition to:

- The Kalu’ak Whalebone Glider, a flying mount for your Classic characters for use only in Wrath of the Lich King Classic and designed to match the look of the original expansion
- The Tuskarr Shoreglider, a flying mount variant for use in modern World of Warcraft (including Shadowlands and Dragonflight) and reflecting the game’s current visual style
- 30 days of World of Warcraft game time, providing access to WoW Classic Era realms, Wrath of the Lich King Classic, and the modern game

![A forsaken character receives a character boost, around her a tuskarr companion, a penguin, two flying mounts and a floating WoW logo](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/gallery/9XXNHDDV2VOT1661551793114.jpg)

For more information on World of Warcraft: Wrath of the Lich King Classic, visit the [official site](https://wowclassic.blizzard.com/).

---

*\*The same restrictions as the Northrend Heroic Upgrade apply; upgrading from the Northrend Heroic Upgrade to the Northrend Epic Upgrade does not grant a second boost.*
