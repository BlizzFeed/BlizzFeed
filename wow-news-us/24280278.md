### Time is Running Out—Adopt Roofus and Support Habitat for Humanity!

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/va/VA4M2OAFL12I1773251795469.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/va/VA4M2OAFL12I1773251795469.png)

Introducing Roofus, an industrious builder and lovable new companion pet ready to accompany you on all your adventures in both *Mists of Pandaria Classic* and modern *World of Warcraft*.

The pack includes new Housing decor items for your home in modern *World of Warcraft*, including a customizable dog house, bed, and bowl! Perfect for relaxing after a *ruff* day.

By purchasing The Roofus Pack, you'll be supporting [Habitat for Humanity](https://www.habitat.org/), an organization that has helped provide housing and shelter for more than 65 million people across the globe. During the promotional period, 100% of the purchase price of **The Roofus Pack** will support Habitat for Humanity.

### The Mythic Dungeon International Returns!

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/jd/JD5PBLAKARV11777587619285.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/jd/JD5PBLAKARV11777587619285.png)

Speedrunning is back, and the season is underway—gather round and prepare to tune in for the Mythic Dungeon International in Season 1 of Midnight!

This peak Dungeon programming premieres this weekend, kicking off the year of competitions for dedicated dungeoneers across the globe. Matches will be streamed and broadcast live each day of competition on the official [Warcraft Twitch](https://www.twitch.tv/warcraft) and [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/warcraft) channels starting at 10:00 am PDT / 19:00 CEST.

Here’s what to expect and when to tune in as the speedrunners set off on the [road to BlizzCon 2026](http://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/news/24247519/):

- **Group A**: May 8–10
- **Group B**: May 15–17
- **Group C**: May 22–24
- **Group China**: May 29–31

Learn more from our previously published [MDI overview article](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/news/24276958).
