### Marvelous Meeksi Pack

Find your cup of tea and take a sip of magic with the Marvelous Meeksi Pack\*—available for a limited time through April 28.

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/AOBPN0PDX5731740513689767.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/AOBPN0PDX5731740513689767.png)

The Marvelous Meeksi Pack includes five ***brewteaful*** dynamic panda mounts to carry you through the skies or on the ground. Each tea-toting panda is steeped in tenderly curated colors that blend perfectly with any journey in Azeroth, whether on land or in the air.

The pack includes the following mounts:

- Meeksi Teatuft (Red)
- Meeksi Brewthief (Purple)
- Meeksi Softpaw (Gray)
- Meeksi Rufflefur (Blue)
- Meeksi Rollingpaw (Orange)

All Meeksi mounts are flying mounts shared across all present and future WoW® Modern characters and automatically scale to the fastest riding skill known by each character. If players aren’t at a high enough level to use a flying mount, Meeksi’s make an excellent ground mount.The Meeksi Teatuft mount is also available as a standalone purchase, and the price of the Marvelous Meeksi Pack will automatically adjust if you already have it.

Certain restrictions apply. Visit shop.battle.net for more information and detailed promotion end dates.
