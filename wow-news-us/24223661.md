### Tune in for Opening Night Live for the Midnight Reveal

Gather your watch party together to bear witness to the unveiling of a truly monumental opening cinematic for World of Warcraft®: Midnight, at Opening Night Live on August 19! You won’t want to miss a single moment of the first salvo to WoW’s next chapter. It’s bound to make an impact.

---

### Watch the Midnight Developer Panels

We’re pleased to announce that we will now be sharing the full VoDs of the developer panels from the gamescom 2025 show floor shortly after the conclusion of the panels. These developer presentations will serve the same purpose as the previously announced Midnight Expansion Reveal broadcast. You’ll have the opportunity to learn more about the zones, quests, stories, and features of the Midnight expansion as presented directly from the developers. We look forward to sharing from gamescom all the exciting details the expansion has to offer! Stay tuned to the official World of Warcraft site.

Check back here on the official site for more details in the upcoming weeks.

### Attending in Person?  Come Home to the World of Warcraft Booth!

> **Where:**World of Warcraft booth in Hall 8\
> **When:**Wednesday, August 20 – Sunday, August 24 in Cologne, Germany

### Housing Hands-On

Welcome! It’s time to step through the door of your home away from home. For the first time ever, players can get their hands on the all-new Housing system as a public demo at gamescom 2025! Stop by the World of Warcraft booth in Hall 8 to build, decorate, customize, and get an early look at one of the most anticipated features to come to World of Warcraft.
