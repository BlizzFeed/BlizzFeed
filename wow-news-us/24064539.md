#### Here are this week's highlights:

#### Dragonflight

- Mythic Tindral Sageswift, Seer of the Flame
  - [Adjustments to this raid](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/mythic-tindral-adjustments-incoming-february-27/1794333) are coming on Tuesday, February 27, during our next scheduled weekly maintenance.
- Embers of Fyr’alath
  - Embers of Fyr’alath now provide a [significantly increased chance](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/embers-of-fyralath-empowered-february-20/1792751/) to get started on the legendary axe.

#### The War Within

- Oracle Hero Talents
  - Based on feedback, we’re [revisiting the Oracle Hero Talents](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/feedback-oracle-in-the-war-within/1782831/343) that we previewed on February 7.

#### WoW Classic

##### Season of Discovery

- Hotfixes
  - Several classes in Season of Discovery received [tuning updates and bug fixes](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/season-of-discovery-hotfixes-february-21/1788040) this week.
- Revered Reputation Rewards for Factions
  - We hit a snag deploying the [revered reputation rewards](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/there-no-revered-rewards-for-supply-faction/1788116/3) for the Azeroth Commerce Authority and Durotar Supply and Logistics factions, but they’re coming soon.
