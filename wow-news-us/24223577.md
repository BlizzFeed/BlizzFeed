Take on new challenges through ten new Delves and one new  seasonal Nemesis Delve alongside a new NPC companion—legendary blood elf rogue Valeera Sanguinar. You’ll experience more of the story of Midnight interwoven through new quests and even experience the great outdoors—taking the adventure outside for the first time.

---

We’re introducing new layouts and enemies while removing some of the things that we found didn’t work as well in The War Within. Players will still find the same variety and replayability as they’ve grown to love, however.

We’re also staging a couple of Delves in outdoor spaces where players can use their ground mounts to get around more easily. You’ll also experience new quests to carry you through the overarching story in the zone.

In place of Brann Bronzebeard, the legendary blood elf rogue Valeera Sanguinar will join us as a new NPC companion taking on all three support roles players are familiar with. She will lend her aid in many of the same ways as Brann but, hopefully, will avoid standing in things that she shouldn’t.
