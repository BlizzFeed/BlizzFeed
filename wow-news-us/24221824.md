### What Are Scenarios?

Scenarios are instanced, story-driven adventures designed for groups of three players. These flexible, fast-paced encounters immerse you in the pivotal moments and hidden corners of Pandaria’s rich narrative tapestry. Unlike dungeons or raids, Scenarios offer a streamlined, cooperative experience with no rigid role requirements—tank, healer, or damage dealer, any combination will do.

Scenarios are perfect for players seeking engaging content that’s accessible and rewarding. Each run unfolds with its own objectives, challenges, and unique lore, inviting players to participate in the living history of Pandaria.

##### Key Features of Scenarios

- **Flexible Grouping:** Participate with any mix of classes and specializations—no need to wait for a tank or healer.
- **Quick to Queue, Quick to Complete:** Scenarios typically last 10 to 30 minutes, perfect for fitting in a quick adventure.
- **Story-Focused:** Each scenario highlights significant events, characters, or battles, deepening your connection to Pandaria.
- **Scalable Rewards:** Earn Valor Points, achievements, and gear upgrades tailored to your level and performance.

##### How to Access Scenarios

To join a scenario, simply queue using the Group Finder (**default hotkey “I”**) and select the **Scenarios** tab. You can enter with friends or be matched with others looking for adventure. Players will need to be level 90 with a minimum item level of 425 to queue for a Scenario.

##### Scenarios at a Glance

- **A Brewing Storm** (Thunderpaw Peak, Jade Forest)**:** Help Brewmaster Blanche create her masterpiece, the Boomer Brew.  Join her search for the brew’s secret ingredient at the tallest peak in Jade Forest.
- **Arena of Annihilation** (Temple of the White Tiger, Kun-Lai Summit)**:** Prove your combat prowess by facing a series of increasingly difficult opponents in a gladiatorial arena. Defeat all the adversaries and emerge victorious, showcasing your skills and teamwork.
- **Brewmoon Festival** (Binan Village, Kun-Lai Summit): Defend villagers from sprites and spirits in the Jade Forest.
- **Crypt of Forgotten Kings** (Vale of Eternal Blossoms): Venture into the Vale of Eternal Blossoms to uncover and defeat ancient evils lurking within the crypt.
- **Greenstone Village** (Greenstone Village, Jade Forest): Defend villagers from hozen raiders in the Jade Forest.
- **Theramore’s Fall** (Theramore, Dustwallow Marsh)**:** Witness the devastation wrought by the mana bomb in Theramore and its aftermath. Available as separate experiences for Horde and Alliance.
- **Unga Ingoo** (Unga Ingoo, Krasarang Wilds): Put an end to the mischievous monkey pirates’ chaos.

Each scenario brings unique challenges and mechanics. These are just the first we’ll have in store for you with more Scenarios and even Heroic Difficulty for some Scenarios in future updates.

### Challenge Modes: The Ultimate Test

For those who crave a greater test of skill and coordination, Challenge Modes beckon. These time-limited, scaled dungeons require precision, teamwork, and a dash of daring. Challenge Modes transform familiar dungeons into gauntlets where only the swift and the skilled rise to the top.

##### How Challenge Modes Work

- **Gear Normalization**: Your gear’s stats are scaled down to a uniform item level, ensuring the focus is on strategy and execution rather than raw power.
- Timed Runs: Beat the clock to earn medals—Bronze, Silver, or Gold—based on your completion time.
- **Leaderboards:** See how your group stacks up against others on your realm and beyond.
- **Exclusive Rewards:** Unique transmogrification sets, mounts, titles, and more await those who conquer the challenges.

#### Participating in Challenge Modes

Gather a group of five players and travel to the entrance of a Challenge Mode dungeon. Interact with the Challenge Mode console to begin your run; once inside, the timer starts, and the true test begins. Communicate, coordinate, and make every second count.

##### Which Dungeons Offer Challenge Modes?

All Mists of Pandaria dungeons feature Challenge Mode variants, including:

- Temple of the Jade Serpent
- Stormstout Brewery
- Shado-Pan Monastery
- Mogu’shan Palace
- Siege of Niuzao Temple
- Gate of the Setting Sun
- Scholomance (Mists of Pandaria Classic version)
- Scarlet Monastery (Mists of Pandaria Classic version)

Each dungeon brings distinct routes, mechanics, and opportunities for speed-running mastery.

##### Rewards and Recognition

Victory in Challenge Modes is rewarded with more than just pride. Achieve Gold times and unlock transmogrification sets inspired by the theme of each dungeon. Silver and Bronze completions also yield special cosmetics and achievements. Top teams may find their names etched on leaderboards for all to see, and mounts like the Jade Pandaren Kite await those who complete every dungeon with distinction.
