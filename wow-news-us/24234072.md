# Midnight: Stay Awhile and Listen in the Arcantina!

- URL: https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/en-us/news/24234072/midnight-stay-awhile-and-listen-in-the-arcantina
![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/rl/RL1POGO2HZTO1758848527264.png)

> A refuge to the road-weary and those seeking a respite from the trials and travails of the life of an adventurer, the Arcantina serves as a crossway for all kinds. It is a magical tavern at the crossroads of nowhere and everywhere catering to travelers and heroes from all walks of life. It’s here you’ll meet up with some familiar faces for stories and quests sending you back out into Azeroth once more.

---

Initially introduced in the [Seasons](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/media/comic/seasons) comic, the Arcantina has become a not-so-secret haven for both Horde and Alliance. Within its cozy walls, the space is teeming with life, from the upbeat music, laughter between friends, to the reminiscing of old adventures. If the Void is the bleak and cold, the Arcantina is its opposite: a warm spark in the heart of every patron. It is a place to rest, to gather, and soak in the atmosphere of a bustling watering hole.

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/GJI7M0CETQAV1758849854197.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/GJI7M0CETQAV1758849854197.png)

Yet even here, adventure finds you. Familiar faces stop by from time to time, offering quests, and a story to tell, that send you far across Azeroth in search of relics of their past. You’ll encounter nine different group rotations of visitors and you’ll never know who you’ll see next. Return to familiar lands to discover a fresh perspective and earn rewards to add a well-travelled charm to both the tavern and coordinating decor for your house.

To enter you’ll need an Arcantina Key and yours awaits you at the end of [Arator’s Journey questline](https://worldofwarcraft.com/news/24234071). Forged from the remnants of Dalaran, it can return you to the Arcantina whenever you need respite from the endless fight against Xal'atath and her sleepless army.

Whether you’re there to unwind, reconnect with old friends, listen, and learn historic stories—the best Tavern not-of-this-world is only one turn of a key away!
