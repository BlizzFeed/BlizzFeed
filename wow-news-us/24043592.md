#### Here are this week's highlights:

#### Dragonflight

- **Shaman Trading Post Armor Issue**: The **Imminence of Krag’wa’s Executor** was charging more than intended in the Trading Post. This was [resolved](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/imminence-of-kragwas-executor-price-in-trading-post/1733061), and a fix was being worked on for affected players.
- **Trading Post Update**: Some announced items were unavailable on the vendor; this was [resolved](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/en-us/news/24033715/updated-126-make-merry-at-the-december-trading-post) this week.

#### Wrath of the Lich King Classic

- **Actions Against Exploitative Accounts**: A summary of [recent actions](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/recent-actions-against-exploitative-accounts-%E2%80%93-november-2023/1734212) was posted on Wednesday.
- **Joyous Journeys Buff**: It was not intended to have this buff disappear with maintenance. It is being looked into and will be [reactivated](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/where-is-joyous-journeys/1732726/27) once issues are resolved.

#### WoW Classic Season of Discovery

- **Ashenvale PvP Changes**: Check out the latest changes to the [PvP event in Ashenvale](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/season-of-discovery-%E2%80%93-ashenvale-pvp-changes-december-7/1735147).
- **Reputation at Revered**: It is [intended](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/battle-for-ashenvale-no-reputation-gained-for-leaders-past-revered/1735156/4) that defeating faction leaders no longer rewards reputation once a character is Revered.
- **Notes Bout Gameplay**: Some [clarifications](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/season-of-discovery-gameplay-notes-december-1/1729185) were posted on December 1 to avoid confusion on how the game works.
- **PvP Rank Cap**: With the initial max level of 25, the [PvP rank](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/season-of-discovery-pvp-rank-caps/1726478) will be capped at 3.
- **Hotfixes**
  - **December 1**: Some [changes were made](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/season-of-discovery-initial-adjustments/1729198) to the game the day after release, which included adjustments to avoid bottlenecking, reductions in power for abilities that trivialized content, and a redesign for the Explosive Shot rune ability.
  - **December 4**: These hotfixes at the start of the week included a change to “[The Orb of Soran’ruk](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/season-of-discovery-hotfixes-updated-december-6/1732235/73)” so that it didn’t require entering the Blackfathom Deeps raid.
  - **December 5**: [Updates](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/season-of-discovery-hotfixes-updated-december-6/1732235/73) included changes for Paladins, Shaman, and Rogues.
  - **December 6**: Several changes were made to the [PvP event in Ashenvale](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/season-of-discovery-hotfixes-updated-december-6/1732235/93).
