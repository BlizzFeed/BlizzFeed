## About the Soundtrack

![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/u5/U5DJB5RJ8NC31784239474185.png)

*The World of Warcraft: Azeroth Housing Soundtrack introduces an all-new collection of music created to bring Azeroth’s most personal spaces to life. The music supports player-driven spaces with compositions that emphasize warmth, atmosphere, and customization, shifting focus from large-scale adventure to personal expression. Each piece was crafted to complement the evolving nature of player housing, blending seamlessly with different environments and styles while staying grounded in the musical identity of Azeroth.*
