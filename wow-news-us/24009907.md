> Fill your ears with the sounds of Azeroth with some Synthwave beats to help carry you to BlizzCon®.

---

There are two weeks until BlizzCon®, so we’re bringing some energy-infused beats from Azeroth. Join Thrall on his ride through Durotar as you wait.

These ethereal Synthwave tunes were mixed by Jason Charles Miller (who is also the voice of Prince Valanar in Wrath of the Lich King and High Commander Goodchilde in Legion) and Jamison Boaz of Rezodrone.
