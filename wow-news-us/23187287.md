The next expansion, World of Warcraft: Shadowlands, has been announced!

[*World of Warcraft: Shadowlands*](https://blizz.ly/Shadowlands), the eighth expansion for Blizzard Entertainment’s acclaimed massively multiplayer online role-playing game, sends players to the ultimate undiscovered frontier—the Shadowlands, an infinite plane where the souls of the dead go to be reborn, serve, do battle, or endure endless torment. There, Azeroth’s heroes must forge bonds with the powerful beings who hold dominion over the Shadowlands—among them some of the most iconic figures in the Warcraft® universe—and acquire the unearthly powers they need to uncover the extent of Sylvanas’ designs.

#### Leveled-up Leveling

*World of Warcraft: Shadowlands* introduces a smoother leveling experience to better prepare new adventurers for what’s to come. When players step into Shadowlands their characters’ levels will be adjusted, with the current level cap of 120 becoming level 50, and 60 being the new maximum level.

---

#### Character Boost Conversions

Planning to create a new character for Shadowlands using a current 110 Character Boost or the recently revealed 120 Character Boost unlocked with pre-purchase\*? When the upcoming expansion goes live, both options will create level 50 characters that allow players to jump straight into the new expansion content.

In the future we will go into further detail of how the Character Boost level conversions work. To learn more about level adjustments and the Shadowlands leveling experience, stay tuned for the What’s Next panel at X PT.

*\*120 Character Boost is available in the Heroic and Epic Shadowlands digital copies and in Complete Collection Heroic and Epic Editions physical copies.*

#### New Charity Pet

Meet Dottie, our newest in-game charity pet, available for purchase in December. Sales of this delightful new companion will benefit charities aiding children in need: the Make-A-Wish Foundation and We.org. [Make-A-Wish](https://wish.org/) creates life-changing wishes for critically ill children in the U.S. and around the world, and [We.org](https://www.we.org/) works to provide clean water, food, and education to children in need. Check back on the World of Warcraft site in the weeks to come for more details!
