#### Here are this week's highlights:

#### Dragonflight

- **New Public Events Coming with 10.2**: In the 10.2 Guardians of the Dream patch, [new public events](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/new-public-events-superbloom-and-emerald-bounty/1674596/1) that feed into each other in the Emerald Dream zone.
- **10.2 Updates on the PTR**: A series of [updates were deployed](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/guardians-of-the-dream-ptr-development-notes/1666552/5) to the Guardians of the Dream PTR, including updates to Classes, Items, PvP, and more.
- **10.2 Updates to Demon Hunter and Balance and Feral Druids:**
  - **[Demon Hunter](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/feedback-demon-hunters/1674608/1)**:  Demon Hunter talent updates will roll in as they become ready, starting with Havoc tree changes in this week’s PTR build.
  - **[Balance and Feral Druids](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/feedback-druids/1666448/211)**: A couple of important changes to Balance and Feral Druids are coming in the PTR build next week.

#### WoW Classic

- **Wrath Classic Season 7 Ending Soon**: The current PvP season in Wrath Classic is ending soon. Additional details can be found in the [forum post](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/wrath-classic-season-7-ends-october-2/1674430/1).
- **3.4.3 Testing Update Includes a Special Reward**: An [update to the 3.4.3 PTR](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/wrath-classic-343-ptr-development-notes/1660806/7) has been deployed, which includes a meta-achievement and special reward new to Wrath of the Lich King Classic for completing it—the adorable ghostly pup, Arfas.
