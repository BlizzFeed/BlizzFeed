# Add the 30th Anniversary Mount Bundle to Your Collection Today

- URL: https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/en-us/news/24145981/add-the-30th-anniversary-mount-bundle-to-your-collection-today
![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/uz/UZ1MRDUQXVHT1729626818843.png)

> The Warcraft 30th Anniversary Mount Bundle includes iconic mounts for World of Warcraft and World of Warcraft Classic. Whether defending Azeroth or conquering new lands, these mounts will ensure you ride in style and honor the legacy of Warcraft

---

Celebrate 30 years of epic adventures with the *Warcraft®* 30th Anniversary Mount Bundle\*, which includes four iconic mounts for *World of Warcraft®* and  *World of Warcraft Classic*. Whether defending Azeroth or conquering new lands, these mounts will ensure you ride in style and honor the legacy of *Warcraft*!

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/ra/RA9H0DS28KU21729626344764.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/ra/RA9H0DS28KU21729626344764.png)

Here’s what’s available in the Warcraft 30th Anniversary Bundle:

- Four Chaos-Forged Skyriding mounts for *World of Warcraft*\*\*\*
  - Chaos-Forged Gryphon
  - Chaos-Forged Hippogryph
  - Chaos-Forged Dreadwing
  - Chaos-Forged Wind Rider
- Four Chaos-Born mounts for *World of Warcraft Classic*\*\*\*\*
  - Chaos-Born Gryphon
  - Chaos-Born Hippogryph
  - Chaos-Born Dreadwing
  - Chaos-Born Wind Rider

All *Warcraft* 30th Anniversary Mount Bundle\*\* mounts are now available to add to your in-game collection through January 6, 2025. Mounts are not faction-locked, so characters can experience how the other side rides on the ground and in the skies.

[Visit the Shop](https://blizz.ly/3BWEvMg)

---

Visit the Battle.net Shop or the in-game shop to add these mounts to your collection.

*\*Offer available through January 6, 2025*\
*\*\*Requires World of Warcraft Subscription or Game Time*\
*\*\*\*Not available in World of Warcraft Classic games.*\
*\*\*\*\*Only available in World of Warcraft Classic progression games.*
