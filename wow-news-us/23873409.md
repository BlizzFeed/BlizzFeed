Gather your friends and allies and prepare to soar into new adventures when the World of Warcraft: Dragonflight expansion goes live on November 28.

#### The *Dragonflight* Pre-season Has Begun

![Alexstrasza](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/ce/CECBGHE1T9KW1669242952081.jpg)

Once the expansion is live, the Valor, Conquest, and Honor currencies will be converted to gold. While rated activities (Mythic Keystone Dungeons, Rated Battlegrounds, Arenas, Solo Shuffle) will be unavailable, you can still participate in non-Keystone Mythic, Heroic, and Normal Dungeons, Random Battlegrounds, Skirmishes, and Brawls.

Season 1 of *Dragonflight* kicks off December 13 with regularly scheduled maintenance.

#### Expansion Features and Updates

![...](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/ee/EE8336PJGVSH1658370586642.jpg)

##### Scale Up with the New Dracthyr Evoker

Created by Neltharion the Earth-Warder, the essence of dragons was combined with the adaptability of the mortal races to create an elite soldier capable of switching from a draconic form to humanoid form. Long in a magical stasis, they are now awakening to a new threat within the Dragon Isles. [[Learn More](https://worldofwarcraft.com/news/23878723/)]

![...](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/5o/5O4W8T2T05T41658190184039.png)

##### Awaken in the Forbidden Reach

Begin your journey as a dracthyr Evoker in a nature-claimed caldera lost to the ravages of time, and dive into the mysteries of the past as enemies and potential allies, new and old, are drawn to this place of ancient power. [[Learn More](https://worldofwarcraft.com/news/23877536/)]

![...](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/5f/5FIPB5X0KSGW1586222804013.jpg)

##### New Adventurers Needed at Exile's Reach

A new expansion presents a unique opportunity to start your adventures in World of Warcraft, check the new expansion out, or bring new friends to Azeroth. Available to both veteran players and new, the introductory excursion to Exile's Reach is a great way to begin forging your path through the world. Here's everything you need to start your journey. [[Learn More](https://worldofwarcraft.com/news/23873862/)]

![...](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/C6ZU8L1EYWAF1666228328222.jpg)

##### Let Your New Talents Shine in Dragonflight

The Talent system has been an integral part of World of Warcraft from its inception. Over the years it has changed several times, always with the goal of bringing new and interesting choices for adventurers everywhere. The Dragonflight expansion debuts the biggest change to the Talents system yet, giving you more freedom to experiment and more options to choose from than ever before. [[Learn More](https://worldofwarcraft.com/news/23865972/)]

![...](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/li/LIJLOXBWAI7C1664915954590.jpg)

##### Meet the Major Factions

Dragonflight ushers in a unique way to progress with Major Factions. Earn Reputation to unlock Renown ranks and access to rewards when you assist denizens of the Dragon Isles. [[Learn More](https://worldofwarcraft.com/news/23823105/)]

![...](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/4x/4XT394NID4FM1657150350983.png)

##### Making It with Professions

Take your crafting game to the next level with the new and updated profession system. Dragonflight introduces a new and updated profession system into World of Warcraft, providing greater depth and engagement while allowing the crafters of Azeroth a way to differentiate themselves and stand out from their peers. [[Learn More](https://worldofwarcraft.com/news/23876529/)]

![...](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/t4/T49QAA3793YJ1666710374784.png)

##### Get Into the Grid of Things with the Updated UI and HUD

We have modernized the player’s layout and view with a sleek appearance while maintaining the charm and personality of *World of Warcraft’s* original design. Explore a fresh-looking Heads-Up Display (HUD) and User Interface (UI) designed to be adjustable, effective, attractive, and easily accessible for everyone. [[Learn More](https://worldofwarcraft.com/news/23837944)]

![...](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/34/349TJC1J2KF41669066710805.jpg)

##### Accessibility Updates in Dragonflight

New accessibility updates in Dragonflight allow you to further enrich your gameplay in World of Warcraft. Check them out! [[Learn More](https://worldofwarcraft.com/news/23876525/)]

![...](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/zw/ZWXPQJOMQRR71667363320994.jpg)

##### Finding Your Way to the Dragon Isles

Welcome, explorer! Whether you’re returning to the land of Azeroth or new to these magical lands, we’ve put together some helpful information to prepare you for your journeys ahead. [[Learn More](https://worldofwarcraft.com/news/23874313/)]

![...](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/jv/JVUFA2OVK4FW1660180901734.jpg)

##### Adventure Lies Ahead in the New Dragonflight Dungeons

Four new level-up dungeons and four maximum-level dungeons await players who venture into the Dragon Isles. Whether you’re looking for loot, a new challenge, or to learn more about this ancient land, you’ll need to gather your courage— and your allies— for the trials ahead. [[Learn More](https://worldofwarcraft.com/news/23874315/)]

![...](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/CP50UAVJ4W051664316210064.jpg)

##### Dragonflight Season 1 and Raid Schedule

Dragonflight Season 1 goes live starting the week of December 12, bringing a new raid, an updated rotation of challenging Mythic+ dungeons, and a new PvP Season. Mark your calendars for the road ahead and get ready to take on all new challenges. [[Learn More](https://worldofwarcraft.com/news/23841486/)]

![...](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/bl/BLUHM85FWKU41668825353054.jpg)

##### Dragonflight Update Notes

Head over to Stormwind or Orgrimmar to begin your journey into the Dragon Isles and experience the new content the expansion has to offer. [[Learn More](https://worldofwarcraft.com/news/23882864)]

#### Zone Previews

### Explore the Untamed Lands of the Waking Shores

![The Waking Shores](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/sn/SN1BLTPIWY351658795813018.jpg)

The Waking Shores are the beginning of your adventures as you take your first steps into the Dragon Isles. Heroes of the Alliance and Horde are invited by Alexstrasza to aid the red dragonflight in this vibrant and menacing land teeming with elemental magic. [[Learn More](https://worldofwarcraft.com/news/23874316/)]

### Step Foot into the Magnificence of the Ohn’Ahran Plains

![The Ohn'arahn Plains](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/qh/QHZJNXNW72JZ1660085886230.png)

Discover a verdant region filled with sweeping plateaus, mesas, groves, and valleys abundant with life where the green dragonflight once roamed. [[Learn More](https://worldofwarcraft.com/news/23874317/)]

### Brave the Chill of the Azure Span

![The Azure Span](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/40/40S6G6NBI8EU1657669349914.png)

Journey into the Azure Span, an ancient frost-touched land, where you'll aid Kalecgos as he ventures into the Azure Archives on a mission to reclaim the Azure Oathstone. [[Learn More](https://worldofwarcraft.com/news/23872579/)]

### Take Flight to the Ancient Seat of Power, Thaldraszus

![Thaldraszus](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/cz/CZX5V1K7VPJV1659376831743.jpg)

Thaldraszus, a wonderous mountain region in the Dragon Isles, is the cradle of dragon civilization. From formidable structures to complex waterways, titan and dragon influence can be found throughout its diverse biomes. [[Learn More](https://worldofwarcraft.com/news/23879372/)]
