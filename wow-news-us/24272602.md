## *Mists of Pandaria Classic: Escalation* Now Live!

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_header/ye/YETDYBMLRKRP1773246324348.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_header/ye/YETDYBMLRKRP1773246324348.png)

It is time to fight back against the aggressive reign of Garrosh Hellscream, once proud Warchief of the Horde in *Mists of Pandaria Classic: Escalation*.

As tensions rise and the fate of Azeroth hangs in the balance, this latest content update unleashes a wave of new features and challenges for heroes across Pandaria and beyond. ## April’s Trading Post Positively Sprouts with Garden Delights

[![alternate-image-name](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/yy/YYNI9780F14B1774895002406.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/yy/YYNI9780F14B1774895002406.png)

**Ensemble: Forest Dweller’s Butterfly Attire**\
**Head, Shoulder, Chest, Waist, Legs, Feet, and Hands**\
*“Fungus is my fashion.”*

April’s Trading post arrives in full bloom, heralding in the rejuvenation of sun-dappled days and tending the garden. Take part in a variety of activities to earn this month’s reward—Ensemble: Forest Dweller's Butterfly Attire transmog set.

Visit T&W (Tawney and Wilder) just outside the Mage District in Stormwind or the Zen’shiri Trading Post next to Grommash Hold in Orgrimmar to check out all the latest items they have available.

Players can also visit the Trading Post in the capital cities of Dornogal (The Foregrounds) on the Isle of Dorn and in the renewed [Silvermoon City](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/news/24243213) (with the launch of *Midnight*) where representatives of both Tawney and Wilder and the Zen’shiri Trading Post are open for business.
