#### Here are this week's highlights:

##### Dragonflight

- **Mind Soothe Hotfix**: NPCs with True Sight will now be immune to the Priest ability, [Mind Soothe](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/why-do-we-need-a-priest-for-mythics/1656110/24).
- **Fury Incarnate PTR Update**: Adjustments continue for the upcoming [Ping system](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/dragonflight-fury-incarnate-ptr-development-notes/1631163/9) in Fury Incarnate.

##### Classic

- **Classic Era 1.14.4 PvP System**: An overview of how the [PvP ranking system](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/wow-classic-era-1144-pvp-ranking-update-examples/1656142) works was posted on Tuesday.
- **Best Tips to Play Classic Hardcore**: Players and Blizzard employees share their [best tips](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/whats-your-best-tip-for-hardcore/1657193) for playing in the Classic Hardcore ruleset. Let people know what you’ve learned!
- **Lost Hardcore Character Destinations:** Details about the Classic Era [transfer destinations](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/classic-era-transfer-destinations-for-lost-hardcore-characters/1657619) ([EU link](https://eu.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/classic-era-transfer-destinations-for-your-hardcore-characters/463953)) for lost Classic Hardcore characters were posted on Thursday.
