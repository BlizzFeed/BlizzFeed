## Enter the Mountain for New Adventures

Within the mountain on the Coiled Isle, you’ll undertake a variety of new activities as you explore this corrupted eco-system filled with poisonous waters and venomous foes.

Plunge Into the Vaults of Atal’Utek primed for challenging group content and rotating public events. Taking part in these public events will ultimately build up to a boss fight.

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/SOO0EC38ZX7Q1781742464429.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/SOO0EC38ZX7Q1781742464429.png)

While in the zone players will have access to a custom talent tree that will provide different perks of player power along with quality-of-life perks. These perks can include things such as something to reduce the potency of the venom found around the isle as you explore.

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/6DZT00QD47NJ1781742462968.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/6DZT00QD47NJ1781742462968.png)

## Encounter Curse Surges and Face Rare Elites

As you adventure through the zone, you’ll come across Curse Surges which will regularly spawn rare elites at five rotating locations throughout the isle.

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/VOCV0321SOPS1781742459758.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/VOCV0321SOPS1781742459758.png)

Killing a rare elite unlocks **Cursed Fishing** in that location. You’ll also discover a Local story with a tortollan sea captain named Tokka, gain reputation with his crew, and learn to fish in more cursed waters around the isle.

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/917U8DY34KWM1781742463698.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/917U8DY34KWM1781742463698.png)

## Step Into Lairs and Face the World Bosses Inside

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/X0MQBJPBDS5J1781742460649.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/X0MQBJPBDS5J1781742460649.png)

Introducing Lairs an evolution on world bosses located in instanced encounters with scaling difficulty up to flexible Mythic with 15-25 players. These will be found in specific locations similar to Delves and will come complete with a summoning stone outside the lair.

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/RMVFSPOWXXSQ1781742460809.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/RMVFSPOWXXSQ1781742460809.png)

The goal is to make it easier for players to gather and take on these bosses by encountering them at specific locations where you can choose your difficulty and use the summoning stone to bring others to you.

In the Curse of Ula’tek content update, players will be able to take on three new Delves with one of these Delves as a new Nemesis Delve. ## Cooldown Manager and User Interface Updates Ahead

The Cooldown Manager is getting some additional updates and will be able to track trinkets and potions, helping with consumable management.

For managing other resources, we’re making more updates to the ping system. Players will be able to ping their action bar or the Cooldown Manager itself providing your team with the status of your spells.

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/OYG8DV11DHCB1781742457641.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/OYG8DV11DHCB1781742457641.png)

You can also ping your own unit frames to convey things like your health to the group.

[![alternate-image-name](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/3VJGZN1HMV3O1781742463706.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/3VJGZN1HMV3O1781742463706.png)
[![alternate-image-name](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/T5RRUAV48IP61781742463911.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/T5RRUAV48IP61781742463911.png)

New configurations for healers on Raid Frames are also arriving allowing you to configure which kinds of healing buffs and what the priorities of them are for the ones you want to show up there. This will allow you to track what types of healing spells they’ve applied to the group.

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/6MRJSTQT3YPU1781742459396.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/6MRJSTQT3YPU1781742459396.png)

More class changes are also on the way. Keep an eye on the [PTR notes](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/midnight-curse-of-ulatek-ptr-development-notes/2317811) to learn more in the weeks ahead.

## Housing Updates: Make the Most of Housing with New Blueprints and Pets!

For players who want to share their cool builds with others, you’ll be able to export the entirety of your exterior and your interior, or your interior, your exterior, or a single room alone as you like.

[![alternate-image-name](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/H2Y0TWKQBE3O1781742457327.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/H2Y0TWKQBE3O1781742457327.png)
[![alternate-image-name](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/1S919V7LCTL21781742462937.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/1S919V7LCTL21781742462937.png)

Other players will be able to import your exports to use with their own homes. If you want to try something new, you’ll also be able to save what you currently have in place, play around with additional layouts, and then reload if you change your mind.

If you want to start with a clean slate completely, there will also be a new Reset option which places all your items back in your storage and lets you start new.

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/LTVBXI1JTEPY1781742460114.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/LTVBXI1JTEPY1781742460114.png)

If you’re hoping to import a blueprint but don’t have everything in that blueprint already, the system will show you a list of what is in a blueprint and tell you what you have or are missing. You will still be able to import even if some items are missing.

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/FAR5GHAWQNQV1781742457527.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/FAR5GHAWQNQV1781742457527.png)
