### Embrace the Chaos of the Final Weekend of Legion Remix

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_header/dt/DTSR3W7C4JZE1765234231417.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_header/dt/DTSR3W7C4JZE1765234231417.png)

Log in anytime from Friday, January 16, 8:00 am PST through to the end of Remix to earn the Feat of Strength, **Cloudy With a Chance of Infernals.**

When the dust settles and you find yourself returned to the main timeline in Azeroth, any Remix-specific items, equipment, and quests will be removed. Each transferred character will receive 2,000 gold and a set of item level 655 (102 after the squish) armor and weapons along with retaining any gold earned during Legion Remix.

Like sand through the hourglass, time is running out before the timeline in Legion Remix collapses. Before that happens, make sure to get in there and spend your Bronze on the items you want most to add to your collections before January 19.

While a vendor will remain available to purchase Legion Remix Housing decor items afterward, purchasing them during Legion Remix offers the best value!

More details, including the cost of items, can be found in our [article](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/news/24242738/).

### Twitch Drop: Get the Cuddly Green Grrgle Housing Decor Item January 20!

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/so/SOOQ1VUA3O0V1768329928484.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/so/SOOQ1VUA3O0V1768329928484.png)

Get the **Cuddly Green Grrgle** Housing decor item starting January 20! Players who have claimed this new item from the Twitch Drop will also be able to visit Dennia Silvertongue in Silvermoon City to purchase more copies of this adorable item after the launch of Midnight on March 2. Additional copies can also be purchased from Tuuran in Stormwind and Gabbi in Orgrimmar (near the Trading Post) beginning with the release of the Midnight Pre-Expansion Content Update.

Watch **4 hours** of any *World of Warcraft* content stream on Twitch.tv from January 20 at 10:00 am PST until February 17 at 10:00 am PST while these Twitch Drops are active to claim the **Cuddly Green Grrgle** Housing decor item.

**Start Time:** January 20, 10:00 am PST\
**End Time:** February 17, 10:00 am PST

To participate, you must link your Twitch account by visiting <https://account.battle.net/connections>. If you have changed your password or made any changes to your Twitch or Battle.net account, you will have to relink your accounts. When you relink, there will be a 7-day cooldown before you can relink a new Twitch account. After you’ve claimed your rewards on Twitch, please make sure you’re logging into the Battle.net region you’d like to receive the drop(s) on, as the first region you log into will be where your items will be delivered. It can take up to 24 hours to receive your reward(s) in-game after claiming. Read our Frequently Asked Questions section for more information.

Frequently asked questions can be found in our [previously published article](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/news/24244883).
