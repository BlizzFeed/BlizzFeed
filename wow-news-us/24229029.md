#### Continue The War Within Campaign, Go Phase Diving, and More

In The Ghosts of K’aresh, the campaign continues as more pieces to a troubling puzzle fall into place. Players  immerse themselves in The War Within story as it leads up to the next chapter of the Worldsoul Saga—Midnight.

There’s some unfinished business to be had on K’aresh and the specter of an old enemy looms large. Forces with ill-intent are determined to bring forth Dimensius, the All-Devouring once more to finish off the ethereals. Should he arise,  Azeroth will also be in peril from this dangerous Void Lord.

Learn about Phase Diving, the new Reshii Wrap artifact, the Manaforge Omega Raid dungeon and more from our [previously published article](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/news/24226730/).

### Twitch Drop: Get the Shadefur Brewthief Pet - Now Live!

![Purple Panda standing in a field of flowers](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/ho/HO10BPQ9P9641753938446222.png)

“If you have milk tea, keep an eye on it. Brewthieves love to pop out of the shadows and steal tea. Even worse, once in paw they love to show off their newly acquired beverage.”

Watch any World of Warcraft stream on Twitch.tv from August 5 at 3:00 pm PDT until September 16 at 10:00 am PDT to claim the Shadefur Brewthief pet.

### How do I Earn Twitch Drops?

**Start Time:** August 5, 3:00 pm PDT\
**End Time:** September 16,  10:00 am PDT

Watch **4 hours** of *World of Warcraft* content while these Twitch Drops are active on any channel to earn the Shadefur Brewthief pet.

Learn more about how to participate, eligibility, and more from our [previously published article](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/news/24227049/).

### Mists of Pandaria Classic: Raids and Unlock Schedule

![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/ki/KIOVMJER74Z81754337598293.png)

Prepare to defend against the corrupted dangers that threaten Pandaria.

And, in a change from the original release of Mists of Pandaria, Mists of Pandaria Classic includes Achievements for 10-Player Raids in the challenge for realm first for both the Heart of Fear and Terrace of Endless Spring Raids.

> ##### **Release Schedule**
>
> *All unlocks are with each region's weekly maintenance reset, unless otherwise noted.*
>
> - **August 12**: Celestial Dungeons unlock, and Mogu'shan Vault (MSV) Celestial rewards are available.
> - **August 21, 3:00 pm PDT**: Heart of Fear (HoF) raid unlocks.
> - **August 26**: Sha of Anger drops Tier 14 gear
> - **September 2**: Celestial Dungeons HoF Celestial rewards available.
> - **September 4, 3:00 pm PDT**: Terrace of Endless Spring (ToES) raid unlocks.
> - **September 16**: Celestial Dungeons ToES Celestial rewards available.
