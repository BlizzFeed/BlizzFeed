With the release of the Mists of Pandaria pre-expansion patch, a new race—Pandaren—along with a new class—the Monk—become available. As a new Pandaren, you’ll start your adventure on the Wandering Isle and begin learning about this noble race and culture.

---

### The Myth and Magic of the Wandering Isle

![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/dz/DZP9XYZE5LJW1750704030051.png)

The Wandering Isle is the breathtaking starting zone for Pandaren players, offering a distinctive experience unlike any other in World of Warcraft. This is no ordinary landmass—but a living turtle named Shen-zin Su that roams the vast oceans of Azeroth. This unique starting zone serves as the birthplace and cultural hub of the Pandaren. According to lore, Shen-zin Su’s unending journey symbolizes the Pandaren’s connection to nature and their quest for enlightenment.

The isle is a lush and vibrant ecosystem, teeming with cherry blossom trees, bamboo forests, serene temples, and bustling villages. Designed to reflect a harmony between nature and civilization, it embodies the Pandaren philosophy of balance. Despite its idyllic appearance, the Wandering Isle is far from a utopia, and it is here that players begin their journey to uncover the troubles brewing beneath its surface.

### Choosing Your Path: Meet the Pandaren

![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/ol/OLT9B7B5DN041750703402836.png)

Pandaren characters begin their journey on the Wandering Isle as neutral to both factions and level to 10 before choosing between Alliance or Horde.

The Pandaren culture is rich and diverse, reflecting a deep respect for nature, community, and self-discipline. Their society is built on the principles of balance, introspection, and harmony with the natural world. This philosophy is evident in their architecture, which blends seamlessly with the lush landscapes of their homeland, and in their daily practices, which emphasize mindfulness and physical mastery.

- **Available Classes:** Warrior, Hunter, Rogue, Priest, Shaman, Mage, and Monk.
- **Racials:**
  - **Bouncy (Passive)**: You take half falling damage.
  - **Epicurean (Passive):** Your love of food allows you to receive double the stats from Well Fed effects.
  - **Quaking Palm:** Strikes the target with lightning speed, incapacitating them for 4 seconds, and turns off your attack.

### Learning the Ways of the Monk

While players can choose any class for their Pandaren, the monk class is uniquely tied to the narrative of the Wandering Isle. The monk represents discipline, balance, and physical mastery—qualities deeply ingrained in Pandaren culture. Through quests and interactions, players become attuned to these principles, which resonate throughout their experience on the isle.

##### Available Races

The new Monk class is available to many races outside of the Pandaren including Human, Orc, Dwarf, Tauren, Gnome, Troll, Draenei, and Blood Elf.

### Core Mechanics and Playstyle

![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/vr/VR85ZOT8YRWV1750717198906.png)

Monks are defined by their fluidity and adaptability, able to fill multiple roles depending on specialization: Brewmaster for tanking, Mistweaver for healing, and Windwalker for dealing damage. The class revolves around energy and chi, resources that fuel their abilities. Monks excel in mobility, with skills such as **Roll** and **Flying Serpent Kick** allowing them to traverse battlefields with ease.

The **Brewmaster** specialization brings a unique flavor to tanking, focusing on avoiding and mitigating damage using abilities like Stagger, which delays incoming damage over time rather than absorbing it outright. This specialization captures the essence of the Pandaren's resilience and cleverness in combat.

**Mistweaver** monks are graceful healers, using their chi to channel soothing mists and restore health to their allies. They blend traditional healing spells with melee abilities, creating a hybrid playstyle that sets them apart from other healers.

**Windwalker** monks, on the other hand, are experts in martial offense. Their strikes are precise and explosive, utilizing combos and chi to deliver devastating damage. The Windwalker embodies the Pandaren's mastery of martial arts and their philosophy of finding strength and balance.

### The Heart of the Conflict

As players progress through the Wandering Isle, they discover that Shen-zin Su is in distress. A shipwreck on the turtle’s back has caused an infection, threatening his health and, in turn, the lives of those who call him home.

Players collaborate to heal Shen-zin Su, demonstrating the Pandaren values of teamwork and compassion. However, the events on the Wandering Isle underscore the need for the Pandaren to reconnect with the larger world, ultimately leading them to join either the Alliance or Horde.
