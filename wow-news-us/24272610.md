### Face New Challenges and Disrupt Ritual Sites in 12.0.5

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_header/f7/F7TKRE2DBXQZ1776140872865.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_header/f7/F7TKRE2DBXQZ1776140872865.png)

Whether you’re going it solo, or gathering allies to join you, a new challenge awaits at the Ritual Sites in Eversong Woods and Zul’Aman. You’ll put a stop to rituals of power-hungry naga and Twilight’s Blade cultists in small one-to-five-player instances and face new challenges for new rewards.

Ritual Sites introduce new repeatable instanced content similar to Delves, designed for flexible group sizes and allowing one to five players. Each offers escalating challenges in a bid for the heroes of the Alliance and Horde to disrupt the rituals of the naga in Eversong Woods or the Twilight’s Blade cultists in Zul’Aman.

As with Delves and Prey, players will unlock Great Vault rewards in the World content row. Only you can determine what challenges you’ll undertake and which rewards you’ll choose to claim as your own. Ritual Sites are available in the 12.0.5 content update, and you can learn more about them in our [previously published article](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/news/24244461).

### Power Up with the Voidforge

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_header/yh/YH8H6HS3GLK61776301615499.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_header/yh/YH8H6HS3GLK61776301615499.png)

Help aid domanaar Decimus to build a Voidforge to begin transmuting Nebulous Voidcores, which are transmutable into empowered equipment upon defeating foes in Midnight Season 1 raids or completing Mythic+ dungeons, Bountiful Delves, and Prey Hunts on Nightmare difficulty. Once the Voidforge is constructed, you’ll also assist him in crafting the Ascendant Nilhammer to transmute Ascendant Voidcores to empower worthy equipment.

Domanaar Decimus may have ulterior motives, but you’ll want to take advantage of the Voidforge to enhance your adventures. Unearth the details in our [previously published article](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/news/24271858).

### Hunt or be Hunted in Decor Duels

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_header/uu/UUPD6XOSRR2H1776296285850.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_header/uu/UUPD6XOSRR2H1776296285850.png)

Lift your spirits and take a break from all the serious threats the Void and their agents present with a little team vs team hide-and-seek in the 12.0.5 content update. Don the guise of a random Decor Item or seek them out in this fast-paced duel for fun—and some new rewards.

Amidst all the danger that swirls above Azeroth, there’s something to be said about having a little fun. Join in a team vs team hide-and-seek style game taking place in Silvermoon City (*Midnight*). Previously announced in the State of Azeroth, this fast-paced player vs. player activity puts you right in the thick of it whether you’re a harmless cheese platter or some other seemingly innocuous item.

No matter your level, it’s an even playing field once the match begins in this 5v5 matchup.

You might not defeat the Void and save Azeroth by taking part in this event, but it’s a great way to find a bit of fun in the face of danger. Learn more from [our overview](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/news/24271369), and maybe we’ll see you…or maybe not… in Decor Duels.

### WoW BCC Anniversary Edition: Overlords of Outland Arrives May 14

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_header/1TUY2D1QHA5B1631231409250.jpg)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_header/1TUY2D1QHA5B1631231409250.jpg)

The battle for Outland heats up on May 14 as *WoW Burning Crusade Classic Anniversary Edition: Overlords of Outland* launches globally on all realms at 3:00 PM PDT / 23:00 BST.

Take the fight to Illidan Stormrage’s lieutenants, brave two new raids, and chase new rewards across the shattered world of Outland.

- **Two New Raids**: The Eye and Serpentshrine Cavern
- **New Reputation Factions**: Ogri’la and Sha’tari Skyguard
- **Arena Season 2**
- **New Profession Recipes**
- **Druid Swift Flight Form**

Get ready to adventure across the shattered skies and torn lands of Outland as you continue to protect Azeroth from the threat of destruction, beginning May 14.

### Mists of Pandaria Classic: Expect Joyous Journeys Beginning April 21

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_header/jc/JC0LPVXNIX5V1750870178685.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_header/jc/JC0LPVXNIX5V1750870178685.png)

Stalwart champions in *World of Warcraft: Mists of Pandaria Classic*, take advantage of the Joyous Journeys experience buff to enhance your odyssey through Azeroth, increasing experience gains by 50% up to level 90!

**Duration**: April 21 until the next content update for *Mists of Pandaria Classic* arrives.

The mystical lands of Pandaria are swirling with danger and need your help. Create a new character, finish leveling a forgotten one, or invite friends to join you in legendary Pandaria.

The Joyous Journeys buff is active by default; however, you can visit any innkeeper in the capital cities and Dalaran to turn it on or off. The Joyous Journeys buff is not available in modern *World of Warcraft®* (currently *Midnight*), *World of Warcraft®: Burning Crusade Classic Anniversary Edition*, or *World of Warcraft® Classic* *Era*.

See you in Pandaria!
