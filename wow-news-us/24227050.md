### Claiming Your Reshii Wraps

![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/4IVGG4MCQUB01750209631370.png)

Undertake the new campaign in K’aresh to lay claim to a powerful new artifact—Reshii Wraps. You’ll need to work to gain the trust of some local denizens through a series of quests where you will collect what are known as Reshii Ribbons. These are dangerous and powerful relics from old K’aresh that were once used by the Ravel.

Once these Reshii Ribbons are combined, you’ll use them to create your Reshii Wrap which is used for new challenges in K’aresh—including taking on Dimensius, the All-Devouring. This artifact is unlocked at the completion of “The Tabiqa” quest at the end of Chapter 3 in the campaign—Desert Power. Completion of the quest will also provide you a choice of Fibers to slot into the cloak (much like you can slot gems on gear) and 3 Ethereal Strands. Fibers provide a secondary stat on your cloak when slotted into your Reshii Wrap.

Travel to Overlook Zo’Shuul to speak with Hashim who will help you enable your Reshii Wraps to harness ethereal energies. You can also purchase other Fibers from Hashim to slot into your wrap.

- Fiber can be purchased from Hashim or can be created with Khaz Algar Tailoring.

To upgrade your Reshii Wraps, you’ll need to use Ethereal Strands to purchase additional enhancements by speaking with Hashim. These strands can be earned in Delves, Mythic Keystone Dungeons, and PvP matches across Khaz Algar. In K’aresh, you can additionally seek them within the Manaforge Omega and by completing Warrants. You can earn up to 15 strands to empower your Reshii Wraps. Each empowerment costs 3 strands each and have 1 rank each.

- Reshii Wraps will upgrade and scale up to five ranks and include new visuals as you progress through content.

Your harmful spells and abilities have a chance to focus latent energies around you to inflict Arcane damage split between your target and nearby enemies. Enemies below 35% health take 20% additional Damage.

An ethereal presence now lingers in Khaz Algar Delves, revealing secret rooms.

You may now find phase orbs that increase your primary stat while Phase Diving. Phase orbs have a chance to recover a Phase-Lost appearance.

**Orbs of Power**

Imbue more energy into your Reshii Wraps, enhancing its power.

Enemies killed while Phase Diving now have a chance to drop a phase orb that increases your primary stat. Phase orbs have a chance to recover a Phase-Lost appearance.

You may now ride Phase-Lost Slatebacks when Phase Diving.

**What Lies Beyond**

Imbue more energy into your Reshii Wraps, enhancing its power.

**Regalia of the Reshii**

Imbue more energy into your Reshii Wraps, enhancing its power.

Unlock the appearances of the Reshii Cowl and Reshii Scrolls.

Players will be able to claim their Reshii Wraps starting the first week of the content update. Once completed, alternate characters can travel immediately to Overlook Zo'Shuul to obtain their cloak without having to progress through the campaign quests a second (or more) times.

### Go Phase Diving

![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/SA9L7OPNS8CJ1753925977917.png)

Once you have your new artifact, you are sent to meet up with Shad’anis in Overlook Zo’Shuul to complete the quest, “Another World.” Here you’ll learn more about Phase Diving and how the Reshii Wraps can help you step into Untethered Space and the sub-realm to experience K’aresh a new way.

![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/HV8V17SYYO5K1753925976591.png)

Using Phase Conduits enables you to enter and exit Untethered Space so that you can move between phases of being. Travel between the conduits the same as you would with any flight paths.

![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/7H2LYWKG1JA31753925976579.PNG)

You can find these conduits throughout K’aresh as follows:

- Shadow Point
- Untethered Space
- Fracture of Laacuna
- Shan’dorah
- The Oasis
- North Sufaad
- Lunnall River
- Serrated Peaks
- Naakroa
- Hosaas’ Rest
- Ruins of Yaathron
- Tazavesh, the Veiled Market

You’ll also learn about (and collect) Phase Energy when you take your first Phase Dive.

> **Phase Energy:** “Consumed by creatures in Untethered Space. They are also sought after as one of the ingredients used to power the eco-domes.”
