#### Here are this week's highlights:

##### BlizzCon 2023

- Additional [BlizzCon 2023 tickets](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/additional-tickets-for-blizzcon-2023-go-on-sale-september-29/167864) are now available. Get yours today, and we’ll see you in Anaheim on November 3-4!

##### Dragonflight

- **Guardians of the Dream PTR Update**:
  - The latest [development notes](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/guardians-of-the-dream-ptr-development-notes/1666552/6) are available, including changes for Demon Hunters, Monks, Rogues, Shaman, Warlocks, and more.
- **Mythic+ Dungeon Testing**: The [latest PTR testing for Season 3 Mythic+](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/feedback-mythic-testing-september-28th-october-2nd/1678628) dungeons has started and goes through the weekend.
- **Raid Testing Schedule Updated**: [Raid testing on the Guardians of the Dream PTR](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/amirdrassil-the-dream%E2%80%99s-hope-raid-testing-schedule/1669121) is ongoing for the upcoming raid, Amirdrassil, the Dream’s Hope.
- **Demon Hunter Updates**: The latest [changes to Demon Hunter](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/feedback-demon-hunters/1674608/571) on the Guardians of the Dream PTR were discussed this week, along with some new changes that are currently planned.
- **Trinkets in Season 3**: Some of the [latest changes for Trinkets](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/trinket-feedback-in-season-3/1673239/52) in the upcoming season were recently discussed.

##### Classic

- **Wrath Classic 3.4.3 PTR Updated**: This [update to the 3.4.3 PTR](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/wrath-classic-343-ptr-development-notes/1660806/9) includes bug fixes and additional testing for Icecrown Citadel.
