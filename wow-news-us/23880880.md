*Dragonflight* is nearly here, and you and your allies can prepare to embark on a new adventure. This weekend, we're giving all players with inactive *World of Warcraft* accounts full access to the game\* and all your characters without a subscription. You’ll be able to access all expansions, including *Shadowlands,*so that you can play the most recent content—including the Tempest Unleashed pre-expansion event— and get ready for *Dragonflight* when it launches on November 28, 2022, at 3 p.m. PST. Rejoin your guild, rally your comrades, and prepare to take flight.

During the Welcome Back Weekend, you can also prepare for all your adventures when you [save up to 30% off](https://us.shop.battle.net/en-us/family/world-of-warcraft#game-services) [Character Transfer](https://us.shop.battle.net/product/world-of-warcraft-service-character-transfer), [Faction Change](https://us.shop.battle.net/product/world-of-warcraft-service-faction-change), [Race Change](https://us.shop.battle.net/product/world-of-warcraft-service-race-change?p=11692), and [Name Change](https://us.shop.battle.net/product/world-of-warcraft-service-name-change?p=11690) services.

Return to World of Warcraft FREE from November 17 to November 20, 2022.\*\*

### Get Back in Action

If you’ve been away for a while, you can play through the [new starting experience](https://worldofwarcraft.com/en-us/news/23873862) starting at level 1 to learn the ropes.

You can also choose to opt out of the starting experience and begin instead at level 1 in the starting area for your race. Upon reaching level 10, you can either continue into Battle for Azeroth or speak with Chromie to choose a Timewalking Campaign. Choose an expansion and continue leveling until you're ready to enter the Dragon Isles at level 60 when Dragonflight goes live. Players with Allied race characters will begin at level 10. Non-Allied race Death Knights and Demon Hunters will automatically begin at level 8 and play through their own starting experience before moving on around level 10.

If you'd like to select a different Timewalking Campaign, simply speak to Chromie again. With phase 2 of the *Dragonfligh*t pre-expansion patch, Timewalking Campaigns scale to level 60 and include *Shadowlands*.

### Need Help Logging Back In?

- Download the [Battle.net desktop app](https://www.blizzard.com/en-us/apps/battle.net/desktop)
- Launch the app and log in to your account.
  - [Need to recover your account information](https://battle.net/account/support/login-support.html)?
- Within the app, click on the World of Warcraft tab, then either Install or Update.
  - [Latest system requirements](https://battle.net/support/article/76459)
- When the installation or update is complete, click Play.
- Play free through November 20, 2022!
