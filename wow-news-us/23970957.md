#### Here are this week's highlights:

##### Dragonflight

- **Fractures in Time Known Issues:** A list of [known issues](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/patch-1015-known-issues-and-information/1629118) was published on Tuesday.
- **Coming Next Week: Conquest Cap Changes:** The PvP [Conquest currency cap](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/conquest-cap/1628791/14) will be lifted next week, and boxes will be available for purchase with the currency.
- **“Sell All Junk” Button Delayed:** The “[Sell All Junk](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/dragonflight-fractures-in-time-content-update-notes/1625960/61)” button was not released with Fractures in Time as originally intended and is now targeted for the next content update.
- **Glyph of Shadow is Working as Intended:** After the release of Fractures in Time, players noticed that the functionality of this glyph had changed, and a post was made to confirm that this was [working as intended](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/glyph-of-shadow-buffed/1629044/16).
- **Fury Incarnate PTR Development Notes**: The latest [development notes](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/dragonflight-fury-incarnate-ptr-development-notes/1631163) for the Fury Incarnate PTR were posted, including details on the Dreamsurge feature, character customization updates, UI updates, and more.

##### Classic

- **WoW Classic Hardcore PTR is Offline:** The WoW Classic Hardcore PTR is now [offline](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/hardcore-ptr-realm-going-offline-today/1631002). Stay tuned for future updates!
- **Classic Era PvP Changes:** An overview that detailed new [Classic Era 1.14.4 PvP changes](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/classic-era-1144-pvp-update/1631287) coming to the PTR was posted.
