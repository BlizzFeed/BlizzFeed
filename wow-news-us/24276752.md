## BCC Anniversary Edition: Overlords of Outland Arrives May 14

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_header/1TUY2D1QHA5B1631231409250.jpg)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_header/1TUY2D1QHA5B1631231409250.jpg)

The battle for Outland heats up! Take the fight to Illidan Stormrage's lieutenants, brave two new raids, compete in Arena Season 2, and pursue new rewards across the shattered world of Outland.

**Global Launch Date/Time:** May 14 at 3:00 PM PDT / 23:00 BST

- **New raids**
  - Tempest Keep: The Eye (Kael'thas Sunstrider) and Serpentshrine Cavern (Lady Vashj)
- **Arena Season 2**
  - New rewards and updated progression.
- **Swift Flight Form**
  - Druid questline (including the Idol of the Raven Goddess) culminating in a showdown with Anzu the Raven God in Heroic Sethekk Halls
- **New daily factions**
  - Earn rewards with Ogri'la and the Sha'tari Skyguard
- **New profession recipes**
  - Engineering epic goggles and new Alchemy discoveries (including raid-friendly cauldrons)

Get ready to adventure across the shattered skies and torn lands of Outland as you continue to protect Azeroth from the threat of destruction, beginning May 14. For more details, read through our [previously published](http://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/news/24276751) overview.

## Last Chance for Children's Week

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_header/74/74ILRO6XHJA81745609179292.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_header/74/74ILRO6XHJA81745609179292.png)

Be the champion when you give back to the children of Azeroth during Children's Week—take an orphan under your wing and show them what the hero's life is like!

**When**: April 27–May 4

***What's New in Midnight***

- Children's Week comes to Midnight's reimagined Silvermoon City in Quel'Thalas!
- New Toy: [Nap Mat](https://www.wowhead.com/item=272287/nap-mat?brl)
- A new Children's Week vendor is standing near the Holiday Enthusiast, between the Auction House and the Trading Post, in the reimagined Silvermoon City of *Midnight*.

Visit Orphan Matron Nightingale in Stormwind, Orphan Matron Battlewail in Orgrimmar, Orphan Matron Mercy in Shattrath, Orphan Matron Aria in Dalaran, Caretake Padae in Dazar'alor, or Orphan Matron Westerson in Boralus, or Matron in Training Ullna in Khaz Algar, and make a child's dream come true!

***WoW Classic***

The WoW Classic version of Children's Week is also available for the giving souls of the Horde and the Alliance to help make orphan dreams come true.

**When:** April 27–May 4

Players level 10 and above can visit Orphan Matron Nightingale in Stormwind (Alliacne), Orphan Matron Battlewail in Orgrimmar (Horde), Orphan Matron Mercy in Shattrath (Neutral), or Orphan Matron Aria in Dalaran (Neutral) and make a child's dream come true.

Upon completing the requested tasks from a chosen orphan, WoW Classic progression players have their choice of a cute, non-combat pet! These pets are account-wide, so feel free to share them with your alts.
