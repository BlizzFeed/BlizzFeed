## WoW Classic Under Development

*WoW Classic Season of Mastery* Open Beta is Now available. Check out the development notes to [learn more](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/wow-classic-season-of-mastery-design-intentions-and-details/1108436/1).

#### WoW Classic Season of Mastery Design Intentions and Details

Go deeper into the design intentions and details for the upcoming *WoW Classic Season of Mastery*, what it is, how the PvP Honor System will work, and more in our [forum post](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/wow-classic-season-of-mastery-design-intentions-and-details/1108436).

> **About WoW Classic Season of Mastery:** Players will have a chance to start fresh on a level playing field allowing new and current Classic players to start anew at level 1 for a complete reset of *WoW Classic's* content. Learn more details in our [news post](https://worldofwarcraft.com/news/23730850/).

#### The Return of Large Groups in Burning Crusade Classic Battlegrounds

All "pre-made" groups will again be allowed to queue for BCC Battlegrounds. Read our [forum post](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/the-return-of-large-groups-in-battlegrounds/1109705) and [hotfix notes](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/burning-crusade-classic-hotfixes-%E2%80%93-updated-october-12/974064/288) to learn more.

## Shadowlands Under Development: 9.1.5 Content Update

The 9.1.5 content update continues to be available for testing on the PTR with more updates added, including:

- Several Monk Spells have been adjusted
- The new Nightborne customization "Luminous Hands" is now available on male and female characters.
- An additional correction to the way male Nightborne mouths look has been made.

Read more details in the [PTR Notes](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/ptr-915-development-notes/1082616/18) on the forums.
