#### Event Overview

The Dastardly Duos Creator Clash is a single-day showcase bringing rosters from two of Azeroth's greatest guilds against one another in the gritty new Dastardly Duos event. The fiercest fighters from Echo and Liquid will face off in the Undermine's Demolition Dome, battling against villainous menaces from Azeroth's past to earn the most points and be crowned the Warcraft-Weight World Champions!

This evening of awesome action will be split into three rounds of competition, with points awarded for each match win.

#### Broadcast

The Dastardly Duos Creator Clash will be broadcast live on the Warcraft [Twitch](https://www.twitch.tv/warcraft) and [YouTube](https://youtube.com/warcraft) channels, starting at 10:00 am PDT / 7:00 pm CEST on Saturday, June 7.
