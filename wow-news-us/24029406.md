# WoW Lofi Mixes Now on Spotify, Apple Music, and Deezer

- URL: https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/en-us/news/24029406/wow-lofi-mixes-now-on-spotify-apple-music-and-deezer
![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/gc/GC7XANJ5W4GB1696978705704.png)

> Send your ears on an auditory odyssey through World of Warcraft.

---

Send your ears on an auditory odyssey through *World of Warcraft* with ethereal Lofi tunes mixed by Rezodrone’s Jason Charles Miller (the voice of Prince Valanar in Wrath of the Lich King and High Commander Goodchilde in Legion) and Jamison Boaz.

All remixes, including one bonus track, “Orc 2,” originally from Warcraft 3, can now be streamed directly through Spotify, Apple Music, and Deezer.
