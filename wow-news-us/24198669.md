The fall of Queen Ansurek has spurred the Sureki forces into action within Hallowfall as they seek revenge upon the Arathi. Take up arms beside a new faction, Flame’s Radiance, and defend against the invading forces for Azeroth, glory—and a variety of new rewards.

# A Radiant Call

![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/3SCL8GIDVOBN1723551694829.png)

Players will begin their journey when they speak with the Flame’s Radiance Recruiter in Dornogal near the map table. You’ll travel to Hallowfall to meet up with Mylton Wildbraun at the Flame’s Radiance camp at Morgaen’s Tears to the southwest of Mereldar near the Veneration Grounds. Speaking with Mylton will offer a variety of quests including Daily Quests to take up around Hallowfall and Azj’Kahet along with a weekly quest for the Nightfall Scenario. You’ll earn Flame’s Radiance Reputation and Resonance Crystals along the way to gain access to and purchase new rewards.

---

# Nightfall Scenario

![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/SAOWW7DU6FXC1744852463144.png)

Each hour, players will be called forth to take part in the Nightfall scenario which has several phases players can participate in until you ultimately defeat the Sureki champion. You’ll be able to check within the **Quest Log (L)** in the Events tab for when the next scenario begins if you’re not sure. You can also mouse over the Nightfall symbol on the map to view a countdown timer until the next round of the event.

Additional rewards are earned with your participation during the scenario— which can be tracked and viewed on the **Service Award** progress bar at the top of the screen. The more participation, the greater the reward.

Players can also undertake a weekly quest *The Flames Burn Eternal* to earn Valorstones, Carved Undermine Crests, and a Radiant Service Satchel. The satchel contains additional Valorstones, Resonance Crystals, and 1 Radiant Emblem of Service to spend at the vendors in Morgaen’s Tears.

**Service Awards:**

- Arathi Soldier’s Coffer
- Arathi Cleric’s Chest
- Arathi Champion’s Spoils

## Support the Flame’s Radiance

A variety of additional Incursion quests are available to undertake from Mylton Wildbraun and will send you throughout Hallowfall and Azj’Kahet as a part of the Flame’s Radiance’s efforts to push back the Sureki. As you progress through these various activities, you’ll also gain powerful buffs to help you combat the powerful forces brought forth from these nerubian invaders.

## You Are Invincible… Sort of

While you adventure in Hallowfall and Azj’Kahet, you’ll also be able to gain additional buffs to help you in the outdoor areas within the zones as you battle against the Sureki such as the Liquid Divination elixir you can “test” for the Sacred Alchemist or the Sacred Flame’s Ward.

- **Liquid Divination:** While in Arathi’s End your healing, damage, and health are increased by 30%.
- **Sacred Flame’s Ward:** While in Khaz Algar, dealing damage has a high chance of burning the enemy with righteous fire dealing bonus Radiant damage while taking less damage.
