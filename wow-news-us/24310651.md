Something wickedly stylish is stirring in Azeroth. Four bewitching bundles are in the **Hexed Witchwick Collection**\*, each pairing a spellbinding transmog ensemble with its own enchanted mount. Summon your favorite bundle on its own or toss them all into your cauldron and claim the complete collection.

If a little Witchwick magic is already tucked away in your in-game Collection, the price will automatically adjust so you only pay for the bundle items you haven’t yet claimed.

Uncover the Transmog pieces waiting in the shadows of each Witchwick Ensemble. The Witchwick Cat and Jack hats each include a “Flowing” option, so you can decide whether to keep your hair tucked away or let it fly free:

- Witchwick Cat Hat
- Witchwick Flowing Cat Hat
- Witchwick Jack Hat
- Witchwick Flowing Jack Hat
- Witchwick Shoulderpads
- Witchwick Shouldergourds
- Witchwick Chestpiece
- Witchwick Gloves
- Witchwick Belt
- Witchwick Skirt
- Witchwick Boots

Witchwick’s Rider mounts are shared across all present and future WoW® Modern characters, matching the fastest riding skill each character has mastered. For those not yet ready to take to the skies, these enchanted brooms will glide across the ground instead.
