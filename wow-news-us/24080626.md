# Embrace Two New Races in Cataclysm Classic

- URL: https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/en-us/news/24080626/embrace-two-new-races-in-cataclysm-classic
![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/my/MY3B724BNZH01713987560519.png)

> Two new races—worgen and goblin—join the Alliance and Horde, each bringing their unique edge to Azeroth.

---

Heroes are called to once again experience a dramatically reforged Azeroth and defend it from utter devastation with a smoother and accelerated leveling experience. Two new races—worgen and goblin—join the Alliance and Horde, each bringing their unique edge to Azeroth.

---

#### The Cursed Worgen

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/GOCYVK27QSLF1713987690578.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/GOCYVK27QSLF1713987690578.png)

Behind the formidable Greymane Wall, a terrible curse transformed many of the stalwart citizens of the isolated kingdom of Gilneas into lupine beasts known as worgen. Unable to cure their affliction and besieged by the dreaded Forsaken, these worgen learned to control their ferocity thanks to the help of the night elves.

The worgen now stand ready to unleash the beast within at a moment’s notice as **Death Knight**, **Druid**, **Hunter**, **Mage**, **Priest**, **Rogue**, **Warloc**k, and **Warrior** in defense of the Alliance.

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/29V5KSX9S3KS1713918168367.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/29V5KSX9S3KS1713918168367.png)

A veteran of several wars against the Horde, Genn Greymane, acts as a trusted advisor to the young King Anduin Wrynn of Stormwind. Stalwart and strong, Greymane seeks to secure a lasting peace for the Alliance and remains intent on reclaiming his lost kingdom one day.

##### Racial Traits

- [Aberration](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=68976/aberration?brl) (Passive): Reduces Shadow and Nature damage taken by 1%.
- [Darkflight](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=68992/darkflight?brl): Activates your true form, increasing movement speed by 40% for 10 seconds.
- [Enable Worgen Altered Form](https://www.wowhead.com/cata/spell=94293/enable-worgen-altered-form?brl): Enables Worgens to switch between human and Worgan forms
- [Flayer](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=68978/flayer?brl) (Passive): Increases your Skinning Skill by 5 and Deftness by 25%. Deftness allows you to skin more quickly.
- [Running Wild](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=87840/running-wild?brl): Drop to all fours and run as fast as a wild animal.
- [Two Forms](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=68996/two-forms?brl)- Turn into your currently inactive form. (Awarded by [Neither Human Nor Beast](https://www.wowhead.com/quest=24593/neither-human-nor-beast?brl))
- [Viciousness](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=68975/viciousness?brl) (Passive): Increases Critical Strike chance by 1%.

##### Home City: Gilneas

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/j4/J4FY10LUSZW11713987816245.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/j4/J4FY10LUSZW11713987816245.png)

Ruled by Genn Greymane, the winding cobblestone streets and narrow alleyways served its citizens well, even as the city fell victim to the curse of the Worgen. Now, forced to flee their home during the Cataclysm, the Worgen seek to reclaim their beloved city one day.

##### Racial Mount: Mountain Horse

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/6PNJ8TQGFR511713987691770.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/6PNJ8TQGFR511713987691770.png)

These wild horses have served Gilneans since before the First War. Known for their strength and ferocious demeanor, the mountain horses are comfortable with their riders—and their proclivities to embrace their feral side.

#### The Cunning Goblins

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/8TQHMRJOLIWS1713987691138.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/8TQHMRJOLIWS1713987691138.png)

The goblins’ craftiness and greed make them masters of mercantilism. Reforging old pacts with their one-time allies, the goblins of the Bilgewater Cartel have been welcomed into the Horde as **Death Knight**, **Hunter**, **Mage**, **Priest**, **Rogue**, **Shaman**, **Warlock**, and **Warrior**. Honing their inherent cunning to a razor’s edge, the goblins hope to use their explosive inventions to rule the world—or at the very least own a profitable percentage of it.

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/WADW6XRPR3GP1713918168309.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/WADW6XRPR3GP1713918168309.png)

After the abrupt departure of Trade Prince Gallywix at the conclusion of the Fourth War, the goblins of the Bilgewater Cartel were left temporarily leaderless. Now Trade Prince Gazlowe leads the goblins with his clever wits, representing them on the Horde council.

##### Racial Traits

- [Best Deals Anywhere](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=69044/best-deals-anywhere?brl) (Passive): Always receive the best possible gold discount regardless of reputation standing.
- [Better Living Through Chemistry](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=69045/better-living-through-chemistry?brl) (Passive): Alchemy skill increased by 15.
- [Pack Hobgoblin](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=69046/pack-hobgoblin?brl): Allows you bank access for 1 min. (Awarded by [Message for Saurfang](https://www.wowhead.com/quest=25267/message-for-saurfang) after the end of the Lost Isles quests)
- [Rocket Barrage](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=69041/rocket-barrage?brl): Launches belt rockets at an enemy, dealing Fire damage based on character level.
- [Rocket Jump](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=69070/rocket-jump?brl): Activates a rocket belt to jump forward.
- [Time is Money](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=69042/time-is-money?brl) (Passive): Increases attack and casting speed by 1%.

##### Home City: Bilgewater Harbor

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/72M9P0WWCFIX1713987691261.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/72M9P0WWCFIX1713987691261.png)

Bilgewater Harbor sprang up in record time, and its quintessential goblin construction has fostered an atmosphere of debauchery and disorder, replete with gambling dens, pleasure houses, and other luxuries.

##### Racial Mount: Trike

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/YQ6HCB515JMY1713987690475.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/YQ6HCB515JMY1713987690475.png)

The remarkably aged Jibidiah “No Fingers” Gragglefutz created the sturdy transport trike. Years of product-related mishaps bore fruit in this speedy, durable, and mostly un-explodable vehicle that remains very popular among goblins.
