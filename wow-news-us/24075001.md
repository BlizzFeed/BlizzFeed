#### The 10.2.6 Content Update Arrives March 19

![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/3l/3LPJVF61BIFZ1709854692175.png)

Mark your calendar for March 19 when the 10.2.6 content update goes live with a bold, new, limited-time event. You won't want to miss out on the fun. It's important to note that *Dragonflight* season 4 will go live several weeks after the release of the 10.2.6 content update. For heroes working toward the [Amirdrassil Feats of Strength](https://www.wowhead.com/guide/raids/amirdrassil-the-dreams-hope/rewards-mounts-achievements#:~:text=AMIRDRASSIL%20FEATS%20OF%20STRENGTH?brl), you'll need to obtain [***Ahead of the Curve: Fyrakk the Blazing***](https://www.wowhead.com/ptr-2/achievement=19350/ahead-of-the-curve-fyrakk-the-blazing?brl) before Season 4 begins, and the achievement is no longer available to earn.

#### Hearthstone’s Anniversary Event in World of Warcraft Extended to March 22

Join in Hearthstone’s 10-year anniversary celebration beginning March 11. Players can log into Hearthstone to collect a new Fiery Hearthsteed mount in *World of Warcraft* for free!\*

![Flaming Hearthsteed](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_folder_media/d8/D85O5FEZMKD41707265549425.jpg)

Players can also seek out special “Hearthstone” matches in capital cities from March 11 to March 22. Stick around long enough and you’ll face a special encounter where you can defeat iconic minions to add to your deck and earn other fun new Hearthstone-themed rewards! Learn more about collecting the Fiery Hearthsteed and the in-game events in [our latest article](https://worldofwarcraft.com/news/24072150).

Visit the Hearthstone [official site](https://playhearthstone.com/blog/24056181) to learn more about the full slate of festivities.

*\* No additional purchase necessary for the Fiery Hearthsteed mount. Limited-time offer only available to those who login to Hearthstone between March 11 and May 14. Mount not available in World of Warcraft® Classic games.*
