## Every Tale Has Its Price: Earn and Spend Bound Silver

Outfit yourself with a dark trove of cosmetic rewards using **Bound Silver**, a Warband-bound currency earned by completing quests, achievements, and progress in the *Tales of the Storyteller*.

Along the way, you’ll also collect **Ducats**, which can be converted into the resources needed to progress through Talebound, and Bound Silver. Once your tale is complete, return to Quillgrave to trade in your Ducats for Bound Silver and other rewards for your efforts.

Spend your Bound Silver on transmog sets, mounts, and other cosmetic rewards through the Talebound store, accessible from the character selection screen or the Game Menu, or by visiting Nib in Quillgrave.

Read on for a list of items to add to your collection.
