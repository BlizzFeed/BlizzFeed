# The Pet Battle Bonus Event Has Begun

- URL: https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/en-us/news/23901944/the-pet-battle-bonus-event-has-begun
![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/ep/EP8JN93JX20T1672943605965.png)

> Prepare your most stalwart companions and get out there and battle!

---

Prepare your most stalwart companions for battle; the Pet Battle Bonus Event is underway. While this event is active, your pets will earn experience at double the usual rate.

To get in on the action, open your Pet Journal (Collections Shift+ P) and click the “Find Battle” button to match up against an opponent and their own team of well-trained companions.

#### This Week

Look for the following:

- Pick up the quest "The Very Best" from Kazra in Valdrakken or start it within the Adventure Journal.
  - Quest requirement: Win five pet battles versus other players with a team of three level-25 pets.
  - Reward: One Ultimate Battle-Training Stone.
- Passive bonus: All Pet Battle experience gains are increased by +100%

#### Every Week

The Bonus Events system consists of a rotating schedule of different activities, currently scheduled to run each week beginning on Tuesdays. Each Bonus Event grants a passive bonus to a particular game activity and offers a once-per-event quest with a noteworthy reward for accomplishing a related goal. Check the in-game calendar as your one-stop reference for the event schedule. The Adventure Guide also offers a direct link to active Bonus Events, allowing you to easily accept any associated quests.
