### WoW 20th Anniversary Edition Phase 3 Honor Cap, Arathi Basin, and More Now Live!

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_header/rq/RQHS2D0SAJGM1583543679956.jpg)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_header/rq/RQHS2D0SAJGM1583543679956.jpg)

The PvP Honor Rank 11 cap has been removed, giving access to titles and awards for ranking up to 14, the gates of Arathi Basin are open for a resource race, and more opportunities for Honor and Rewards can be had during Battleground holiday weekends.

**Get Ready for Arathi Basin**

**Players:**15 vs.15\
**Level Range:**20+\
**Capture Points:**Farm, Mine, Blacksmith, Lumber Mill, and Stables\
**Resources Needed to Win**: 2000

Players must hone their team coordination to a keen edge to capture and hold five different bases throughout the map and accrue resources. Alliance players will start close to the Stables at Trollbane Hall, while Horde players will begin near the farm in the Defiler’s Den. The first team to collect 2000 resources wins.

**Battleground Holidays: More Honor and Rewards**

Every weekend from Thursday night at midnight until early Tuesday morning, performing objectives in the featured battleground gains increased Honor and Faction rewards. Battleground holidays are on a weekly rotation cycle, starting the weekend of April 4 with Arathi Basin. Seek out the emissary in major cities to get the objective for the featured battleground to kick off your battle weekend.

Learn more details from our [previously published article](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/news/24182548/).

### Arena World Championship Returns on April 4!

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/gallery/38/386PRZKZK6V41741724680812.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/gallery/38/386PRZKZK6V41741724680812.png)

Beginning April 4, the gladiatorial gates will open again for the Arena World Championship in Season 2 of The War Within!

- **Broadcast Start Time:** 10:00 am PDT / 7:00 pm CEST
- **Format:**Double Elimination, Best-of-5. Grand Finals Best-of-7.
- **Venue:**Online: Twitch.tv/Warcraft and YouTube.com/Warcraft
- **Co-streaming:** *Check with your favorite competitor to see if they plan on sharing their perspective during the tournament or gather your own audience and host a watch part of your own! Sign up for co-streaming using the*[*AWC Watch Party Form*](http://blizz.ly/CostreamAWC)*.*
- **Open Bracket:**For the first time, the Open Bracket will be available for co-streaming! Join the [AWC Discord server](http://blizz.ly/AWCDiscord) for more information.

Tune into all three Cups to find out who will win! Crack open a cool Kaja'Cola and kick back; it's time to catch the latest close-quarter combat from the greatest gladiators in (and under) Azeroth! The pinnacle of PvP content has returned in the newest season of The War Within and has followed our competitors into the depths of the Undermine.

While Cup 1 is underway, registration is still open for the remaining Cups. [Register on Raider.IO](http://raider.io/tournaments) today, and get a chance to earn the new PvP-exclusive reward "Prized Champion's Prestigious Banner" by competing in a full AWC Cup! Find out all the details from [our article](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/en-us/news/24187199).
