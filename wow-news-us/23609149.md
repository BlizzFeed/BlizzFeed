# Torghast: Chorus of Dead Souls Bonus Event Now Live

- URL: https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/en-us/news/23609149/torghast-chorus-of-dead-souls-bonus-event-now-live
![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/a4/A46Q5YIX92AW1610427020629.jpg)

> Venture into the halls of Torghast, Tower of the Damned to take part in a new Shadowlands Bonus Event.

---

Shadowlands introduced a series of three new bonus events within Torghast, Tower of the Damned, offering unique new event Anima Powers. These new powers will provide a variety of wild new abilities to utilize during your adventure through the halls. If you choose the event-themed Anima Power at the start of your dungeon run, the remaining choices for that run will change to include additional event-themed powers to choose from along the way.

For example, choosing the maw pet power during the Beasts of Prodigum event will alter any remaining Anima Power discoveries in Torghast to include powers that buff your maw pet. Should you not choose to take this new power and choose a normally available power, you will not get any abilities associated with the event within Torghast to use for your run.

#### Schedule of Events:

##### **Torghast: Beasts of Prodigum—***Beasts of Prodigum have been unleashed upon Torghast, Tower of the Damned!*

Journey through Torghast with a maw pet which can be enhanced with additional Anima powers found throughout the dungeon.

- January 25
- April 19
- July 12
- October 4
- December 27

##### **Torghast: Chorus of Dead Souls—***Dead souls swarm within Torghast, Tower of the Damned!*

Assume the form of a Deadsoul Chorus, granting special abilities, additional damage, healing, and movement.

- February 22
- May 17
- August 9
- November 1

##### **Torghast: Unbridled Darkness—***Unbridled Darkness has been unleashed upon Torghast, Tower of the Damned!*

Plunge into the darkness with the vial of darkness. While under this effect, you'll gain additional damage and movement bonuses.

- March 22
- June 14
- September 6
- November 29

#### Every Week

The Bonus Events system consists of a rotating schedule of different activities, currently scheduled to run each week beginning on Tuesdays. The in-game calendar can serve as your one-stop reference for the event schedule.
