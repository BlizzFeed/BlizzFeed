### WoW Esports Sign-Ups Open for North America and Europe

![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/u5/U5TFBO287D6D1685720237312.jpg)

Sign-ups for the Arena World Championship & The Great Push for Dragonflight Season 2 are now open! Eligible participating players will be invited to Tournament Realms and can earn the in-game toy “Smoldering Banner of the Aspects.”

Don’t delay, sign up on [GameBattles](https://gamebattles.majorleaguegaming.com/pc/world-of-warcraft/tournament) before it’s too late. Sign-ups for the first AWC Cup of the Season close on June 9, and sign-ups for TGP Season 2 close on June 30.  Learn more about these events from our [previously published article](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/en-us/news/23934874).
