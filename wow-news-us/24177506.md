Take on all new challenges in *The War Within* Season 2 with a new raid, new Delves, an updated rotation of challenging Mythic+ dungeons, and a new PvP season. This season also features familiar exclusive rewards such as the **Ahead of the Curve** and **Cutting Edge** achievements for raids, Keystone Master achievements, rewards for Mythic+, and Gladiator rewards for PvP.

Read on for more details to help you prepare to take on all new challenges in *The War Within: Undermine(d)*.

### New Raid: Liberation of Undermine

Despite the best efforts of the united cartels, Jastor Gallywix has holed himself away in the Gallagio and called in every favor ever owed him... leaving a mountain of would-be oppressors between the goblins of Undermine and the Chrome King. Gazlowe and the rest of goblinkind will need to find their way into the Gallagio to rid themselves of Gallywix for good.

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/xt/XT4VRQHMSYZW1739218869708.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/xt/XT4VRQHMSYZW1739218869708.png)

The Liberation of Undermine is an 8-boss raid that will unlock one week after the launch of The War Within: Undermine(d), along with the Story Mode version.

**Where:** The Ringing Deeps\
**Raid Difficulties:** Story Mode, Raid Finder, Normal, Heroic, Mythic\
**Raid Finder iLevel:** 606\
**Bosses:** 8

Check out our [raid preview article](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/news/24178099) for more information on the new raid, including boss encounters, achievements, and release schedule.

### New Dungeon: Operation: Floodgate

It's a race against time to stop the Darkfuse and Venture Co. from sabotaging a dam that would have disastrous effects on the Ringing Deeps. Operation: Floodgate is a four-boss dungeon in the Ringing Deeps and will be available across Heroic, Mythic, and Mythic+ difficulties.

##### Mythic+ Affixes

- **New! Xal'atath's Bargain: Pulsar**
  - While in combat, Xal'atath summons pulsars that orbit players and must be intercepted within the time limit.
- **Xal'atath's Guile**
  - Will cause Keystones above level 12 to increase health and damage of enemies by 12% per level.
- **Xal'atath's Bargain: Voidbound**
  - Dark Prayer is no longer a channel, and the Void Emissary no longer grants damage reduction to creatures while active.
  - Dark Prayer now has a 15-second cast time and grants all nearby creatures in combat Xal'atath's Gift when it finishes casting.

The potency of Xal'atath's Gift granted by Dark Prayer is based on the remaining shield percentage of the Void Emissary.

### Season 2 PvP

#### New PvP Arena: Cage of Carnage

Found in the Demolition Dome, the Cage of Carnage is a close-quarter gladiatorial arena where players are dropped in through trap doors separated only by a haphazard metal platform equipped with ramps at every corner. This map is available in Arenas and Rated Solo Shuffle.

### New World Boss: The Gobfather

Once a source of pride for the Bilgewater Cartel, this mech was a force to be reckoned with in the Demolition Dome. A pinnacle of goblin innovation…until someone forgot to give it an oil change. Now, the King of the Demo Dome has gone haywire, running amok and threatening to unleash havoc upon the citizens of Undermine.

### Season 2 Meta-Achievements

##### *"The War Within Season 2: Master Blaster"*

Accomplish one of the following feats during Season 2 of *The War Within* on your current character to receive the **Crystallized Essence of Kaja'mite** that grants one Catalyst charge:

- Attain 1600+ Rated PvP Rating
- Attain 2000+ Mythic+ Rating
- Defeat Chrome King Gallywix on Heroic or Mythic difficulty.

##### *"Dressed to the Mines"*

Complete one of the following Achievements or Feats of Strength listed below before the season ends to get the Warbound-wide **Gallagio Highroller's Bombstone** that adds visual effects to the Liberation of Undermine armor set.

- **"Mythic: Chrome King Gallywix"**: Defeat Chrome King Gallywix on Mythic difficulty.
- **"The War Within Keystone Hero: Season 2"**: Attain a Mythic+ Rating of at least 2500 during The War Within Season 2.
- **"Elite: The War Within Season 2"**: Earn the rank of Elite during The War Within Season 2. Rewards the seasonal character title, "the Elite."
