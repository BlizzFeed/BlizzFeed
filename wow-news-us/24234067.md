The **Ghosts of K’aresh** content update is here and the Arena World Championship (AWC) and Mythic Dungeon International (MDI) return for ***The War Within* Season 3**! Prepare for fierce Arena clashes beginning **October 3**, and join the race in Mythic+ with **Time Trials starting October 1.**

## How to Get Involved

- [**Register on Raider.IO**](http://raider.io/tournaments) to secure your spot in AWC or MDI.
- **Access Tournament Realms** with fully geared characters at max item level.
- Earn the all-new **Season 3 banners (*Unbound Banner of the Algari & Astral Champion’s Prestigious Banner*).**
- Tune in live on [**Twitch**](https://www.twitch.tv/warcraft) and [**YouTube**](https://www.youtube.com/@Warcraft) to cheer for your favorite competitors.

## ARENA WORLD CHAMPIONSHIP

In Season 3, the AWC will once again feature **three qualification Cups per region**, leading into the AWC Gauntlet and Grand Finals with a combined **$200,000 (USD) prize pool**.

Teams in **Europe** and **North America** can [register now on](http://raider.io/tournaments) [**Raider.IO**](http://raider.io/tournaments) for *The War Within* Season 3 Cups, where they’ll not only fight for their share of **$20,000 (USD)** per Cup but also for valuable points toward qualification. Competitors who sign up will earn the new **Season 3 Astral Champion’s Prestigious Banner**, exclusively available through AWC participation.

Following the Cups and Gauntlet, the top four teams from each region will advance to the **AWC War Within Grand Finals**, where they’ll battle for their share of the **$200,000 (USD)** prize pool and the title of Season 3 champions.

## MYTHIC DUNGEON INTERNATIONAL

The MDI also returns to full force in Season 3, continuing its focus on **Mythic+ key pushing** as the cornerstone of competitive Dungeons. Registration is now open, with **Time Trials beginning October 1**.

Players who [sign up via](http://raider.io/MDI) [**Raider.IO**](http://raider.io/MDI) will gain access to the Tournament Realms and unlock the in-game **Season 3 Unbound Banner of the Algari**. From the Time Trials, the **top 18 global teams** will advance to the MDI Groups.

The Groups format has been adjusted slightly to feature **six teams competing at once** with daily eliminations, new dungeons, and a modified spec variety rule with **$30,000 (USD)** in prizing on the line for each Group. The **six best teams** from the Group Stage will join **two representatives from China** to face off in The War Within MDI Global Finals for their share of the **$300,000 (USD)** prize pool and the title of **MDI Global Champion**!
