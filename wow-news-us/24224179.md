Whether you’re joining us live in Cologne or from the comfort of your home, you’re in for an unforgettable experience!  The Creator Clash is a series of stellar showcases spotlighting the newest content in *World of Warcraft* by bringing together prominent personalities and pitting them against one another in various competitions.

Join us from Wednesday, August 20 to Friday, August 22 by visiting the [World of Warcraft booth at gamescom in Hall 8](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/news/24228403) or tune in to the official Warcraft [Twitch](https://www.twitch.tv/warcraft%22%20/t%20%22_blank) and [YouTube](https://youtube.com/warcraft%22%20/t%20%22_blank) channels to enjoy each spectacular show!

The Housing Showcase will be broadcast on multiple days during gamescom 2025! The first show will take place at 10:00 CEST on Wednesday, August 20 (1:00am PDT), while the second show will take place at 20:00 CEST on Friday, August 22 (11:00am PDT).

#### Format

A laid-back and casual competition, each day, four creators will take part in several rounds of interior designing using the new World of Warcraft Housing feature. They’ll showcase their interior design chops for the first time live from the gamescom halls and race against the clock— and each another— to decorate a domicile fitting the theme provided to earn your favor! Each round will bring with it a new and unique theme, with categories including in-game lore, characters, cultures, and many more!

#### Creators

The Dungeons Showcase will take place starting at 13:00 CEST (4:00 am PDT) on Wednesday, August 20.

#### Format

Four teams each representing their region will team up and face off in dungeon speedruns in the newly available Mythic+ Dungeons from the Ghosts of K’aresh content update. Teams will go head-to-head to fight their way through a single-elimination bracket for the championship title! Each match will pit two teams against each other in a best-of-three series, up until the final showdown for a best-of-five finale.

After the Dungeons have concluded, a bonus showcase of the new Archival Assault Delve will take place featuring three captains from each region. They’ll take part in a relay race with a few fun twists to be revealed live at gamescom – stay tuned!

#### Teams

![CC_Dungeon_Participants (2).png](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/gallery/na/NA79J509J6DI1755204636378.png)

The Arenas Showcase will take place beginning at 10:00 CEST (1:00 am PDT) on Thursday, August 21.

#### Format

This showcase amps things up to a more serious competitive showdown! Four teams composed of the world’s greatest gladiators will face off in an action-packed cross-region arena finale! They’ll be going head-to-head and fight their way through a 3v3 double-elimination bracket for the global championship title.
