#### Here are this week's highlights:

#### Dragonflight

- **Secrets of Azeroth Event**: Players [can still complete](https://twitter.com/WarcraftDevs/status/1697341168722596257) the Inquisitive and meta-achievements now and after the event ends.

#### WoW Classic

- **Wrath Classic 3.4.3 PTR**: The latest [Wrath Classic PTR](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/wrath-classic-343-ptr-development-notes/1660806/4), including the Icecrown Citadel raid dungeon, is now available.
- **Non-Hardcore Classic Era Realms**: After recent restarts, non-Hardcore Classic Era realms were returned to [a single layer](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/hotfix-incoming-for-layers-on-non-hardcore-classic-era-realms/1658634/41) and [confirmed](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/whitemane-still-has-layers-after-reset/1659090/3).
- **Classic Era Honorable Kill Counts**: A fix was implemented this week, and characters should now see their accurate [Honorable Kill](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/hks-missing-after-classic-era-patchpvp-update/1656678/32) count.
