The Sky-Captain’s Masquerade Attire includes:

- Sky-Captain’s Masquerade Hat (Head)
- Sky-Captain’s Masquerade Coat (Chest)
- Sky-Captain’s Masquerade Pants (Legs)
- Sky-Captain’s Masquerade Boots (Feet)
- Sky-Captain’s Masquerade Cape (Cloak)

[Purchase Now](https://shop.battle.net/product/world-of-warcraft-sky-captains-masquerade-attire)

The Sky-Captain’s Masquerade Attire will be available in the Battle.net® Shop for a limited time\*\*. Items from the Sky-Captain's Masquerade Attire transmog set will arrive in a future rotation of the Trading Post, but no sooner than three months after the set leaves the Shop.

*\*Not available in World of Warcraft® Classic games  \
\*\*The Sky-Captain's Masquerade Attire leaves the Battle.net Shop indefinitely on January 1, 2024.*
