#### THIS WEEK IN SHADOWLANDS

- **Fated Raids: Sepulcher of the First Ones**
  - This week, bosses in Sepulcher of the First Ones are empowered with increased difficulty and a special affix. Loot drops are upgraded to a higher item level.
- **PvP Brawl: Shado-Pan Showdown**
  - Step into Tiger's Peak Arena and race for control of a center control point to activate one of two bosses. The first to kill the other team's boss wins!
- **Timewalking Dungeon Event: Cataclysm**
  - While this event is active, players level 50 or higher may access a special Timewalking Dungeon Finder queue, which scales players and their items down to revisit past dungeons from the Cataclysm expansion.
- **Hallow's End**
  - Hallow's End celebrates the break of the Forsaken from the Scourge. Fun and mischief reign as the Innkeepers of Azeroth give treats, and tricks, to those who ask.

#### COMING UP IN SHADOWLANDS

##### Beginning Next Week:

- **Fated Raids: Castle Nathria**
  - This week, bosses in Castle Nathria are empowered with increased difficulty and a special affix. Loot drops are upgraded to a higher item level.
- **Arena Skirmish Bonus Event**
  - While this event is active, Arena Skirmishes will award Honor at an increased rate. Will you answer the call to battle?
- **PvP Brawl: Warsong Scramble**
  - Do you have a flag? Warsong Gulch has always been the scene of an epic capture-the-flag battle, but what happens when you can capture your enemy's flag without your own at base? Find out when you step into this PvP Brawl.
- **Timewalking Dungeon Event: Wrath of the Lich King**
  - While this event is active, players level 50 or higher may access a special Timewalking Dungeon Finder queue, which scales players and their items down to revisit past dungeons from the Wrath of the Lich King expansion.

##### Beginning the week of October 31:

- **Torghast: Chorus of Dead Souls**
  - Dead souls swarm within Torghast, Tower of the Damned!
- **Fated Raids: Sanctum of Domination**
  - This week, bosses in Sanctum of Domination are empowered with increased difficulty and a special affix. Loot drops are upgraded to a higher item level.
- **PvP Brawl: Cooking Impossible**
  - Nomi is hosting a Pandaren Chili Cookoff in the Valley of the Four Winds between the Alliance and the Horde! The first to bring Nomi all of the required ingredients wins.
- **World Quest Bonus Event**
  - While this event is active, completing World Quests awards bonus reputation with the appropriate faction.
- **Day of the Dead**
  - During Day of the Dead, people gather in graveyards to celebrate with and cherish the spirits of those they have lost. One can find festivities in the cemeteries of any major city, where celebrants cook, dance, don costumes, and more!

Check your in-game calendar for a list of the current and upcoming in-game event start times!
