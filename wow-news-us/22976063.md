# BlizzCon 2019 Commemorative Collectible Celebrates 25 Years of Warcraft

- URL: https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/en-us/news/22976063/blizzcon-2019-commemorative-collectible-celebrates-25-years-of-warcraft
![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/2o/2O8M32DFXR9G1556173269982.jpg)

> Choose between the mighty Orc Grunt or bold Human Footman when you buy a ticket to BlizzCon® 2019, and celebrate a quarter century of conflict in Azeroth.

---

November 2019 marks the 25th anniversary of the Warcraft® universe, and instead of doing a typical BlizzCon® Goody Bag as we have in past years, we’d like to commemorate the occasion by providing BlizzCon attendees a distinctly Azerothian memento.

We’re excited to announce that everyone who buys a ticket to attend this year’s [BlizzCon](https://blizzcon.com/en-us/news/22955736/) will receive their choice of a special **BlizzCon 2019 Commemorative Collectible**—one of two premium statues in the style of the Blizzard Legends series, celebrating a quarter century of Warcraft’s timeless central conflict. Take up arms alongside the mighty **Orc Grunt**, or side with the bold **Human Footman!**

**IMPORTANT!** **You’ll need to decide which statue you want at the time you purchase your tickets on May 4 or May 8**. We’ll be producing quantities for pickup at BlizzCon based on what people choose, and locking in your selection now helps us ensure we’ll have everyone’s statue ready in time for the show!

[![Orc Grunt](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/fu/FUO5GTSAU2K21556082180154.jpg)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/fu/FUO5GTSAU2K21556082180154.jpg)

**Zug zug! Mark the 25th anniversary of Warcraft with this mighty axe-wielding Orc Grunt.**

[![Human Footman](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/ll/LLWF2MD9LHJF1556082391240.jpg)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/ll/LLWF2MD9LHJF1556082391240.jpg)

**Ready for action! Honor the legends of Azeroth with the fierce and noble Human Footman.**

Since statue choices are locked in at the time of ticket purchase, make sure to sync up with family and friends in advance to determine which one everyone wants. BlizzCon attendees will pick up their preselected statues with their badges at the show—and keep in mind that if you choose to transfer tickets ([find out more](https://blizzcon.com/en-us/event-info/attendees/badge)), the selected statue will stay connected to that ticket.

In addition, *anyone* who wants either statue (or both!) will be able to purchase the Orc Grunt and Human Footman for a limited time on the Blizzard Gear Store starting May 4. Statues purchased during this window will be shipped directly to your home around the time of BlizzCon. Stay tuned for more details.

Here’s to 25 years of Warcraft—we’re looking forward to celebrating with you at BlizzCon!
