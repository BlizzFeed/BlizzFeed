# Herald in the Heavens Astride Ash'Adar, Harbinger of the Dawn

> Get the color-shifting Ash'adar, Harbinger of the Dawn, for a limited time through the in-game shop.

---

Herald in the heavens astride the ethereal, color-shifting ***Ash'adar, Harbinger of the Dawn***\* now available for a limited time\*\* through the in-game shop.

> Kaldorei legends whisper of a relentless hunter prowling amongst the deepest shadows of Kalimdor, never sleeping. By the light of the sun or the moon, Ash'adar now hunts with you.

Add Ash'adar, Harbinger of the Dawn, to your collection of mounts before it leaves for otherworldly adventures on March 31. And, for the fashion-forward, this mount also pairs well with the color-shifting [Burden of Unrelenting Justice Transmog Set](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/news/24064542/green-is-good-during-marchs-trading-post#:~:text=Get%20the%20Burden%20of%20Unrelenting%20Justice%20Transmog%20Sets).
