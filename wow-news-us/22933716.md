> The skies of Kul Tiras and Zandalar can be decidedly unfriendly to the uninitiated. To help you on the first part of your journey, we’ve put together a brief guide.

---

If you’ve got your eye on the skies where Pterrodax rule, you’ll need to complete five achievements as a part of earning **Battle for Azeroth Pathfinder, Part One.** On completion, you will earn this achievement account-wide.

---

### Where to Start

##### I’m not 120 yet

Don’t worry – if you haven’t reached maximum level yet, you have time. Go questing, run dungeons, level up your professions. In the end, you will hit that magic number. You can always take a look at one of the many leveling guides available on various fansites, like [this one](https://www.wowhead.com/guides/battle-for-azeroth-leveling-and-consumables&brl).

If you’re still working your way through the Horde and Alliance War Campaigns, don’t worry. We’ve put together a [brief walkthrough](#readyforwar)below of what you need to do to complete it.

#### Reputations

**Azerothian Diplomat:**Reach Revered with all the reputations listed.

Whether you’re Horde or Alliance, you’ll want to reach Revered with specific factions to earn this achievement.

- **Horde:** Zandalari Empire, Talanji’s Expedition, Voldunai, The Honorbound, Champions of Azeroth, Tortollan Seekers
- **Alliance:** Proudmoore Admiralty, Order of Embers, Storm’s Wake, 7th Legion, Champions of Azeroth, Tortollan Seekers

**Tip:** There are a lot of ways to maximize your Reputation gains. Make sure you’re keeping on top World Quests and completing Emissaries. (You can check which ones are active with the [WoW Companion App](https://worldofwarcraft.com/en-us/news/22933715/keep-azeroth-close-with-the-wow-companion-app).) You’ll also want to make your way to the [Darkmoon Faire](https://worldofwarcraft.com/en-us/news/22938940/welcome-to-the-darkmoon-faire) when it’s around to purchase a Darkmoon Hat (or two) or go for a ride on the Darkmoon carousel or the new Darkmoon roller coaster to get a buff to your reputation gains. Check out [Wowhead’s guide](https://www.wowhead.com/guides/battle-for-azeroth-reputation-overview&brl) to learn more.

#### World Quests

**Wide World of Quests:**Complete 100 different World Quests in Kul Tiras and Zandalar.

From slaying bosses to taking part in profession quests, there are a variety of activities in Kul Tiras and Zandalar to participate in. World Quests become available at level 120 and can be rewarding if you’re looking for some new gear, a little extra gold in your pocket, War Resources, or some Azerite to boost up your Heart of Azeroth.

Left-clicking on Emissaries on the Map (Hotkey- M) will cycle through the zones that have quests available for that specific Emissary. You can also mouse-over the quests in the map to see which Faction it corresponds to, the task they're asking of you, and the rewards you’ll earn by completing them.

#### War Campaign

If you haven’t started the War Campaign for your faction, don’t worry. We’ve put together a brief guide of what you need to do to earn the Ready for War achievement. You’ll also earn the reward of increased mount speed while in Kul Tiras and Zandalar when you’ve completed it.

- [Horde War Campaign](#ready-for-war-achievements)
- [Alliance War Campaign](#ready-for-war-achievements-1)

At level 110 and after you’ve completed the opening quest lines of Battle for Azeroth, you’ll be introduced to the new War Campaign and begin the road to opening new Footholds over time in the opposite faction’s territories.

Your first quest will be The War Campaign, which introduces you to War Resources. After the quest Mission From the Warchief, you’ll be introduced to the mission table, which will allow you to begin sending followers you gain through your adventures out to carry out missions.

Access to Footholds will become available as you level through Zuldazar, Nazmir, and Vol’dun. At each key point in your journey (level 110, 114, and 118) you’ll be able to establish a new Foothold in Drustvar, Stormsong Valley, and Tiragarde Sound. At level 120, you’ll begin focusing more on reaching reputation milestones with The Honorbound.
