#### About AURORA

Over the past ten years, Norwegian art-pop artist AURORA has carved out a singular space in global music with her ethereal voice, fierce vulnerability, and visionary aesthetic. She first captivated audiences in 2015 with her debut EP *Running With the Wolves*, followed by critically acclaimed albums including *All My Demons Greeting Me as a Friend* (2016) *Infections of a Different Kind* (2018) *A Different Kind of Human* (2019), and *The Gods We Can Touch* (2022). Her music, both delicate and defiant, has earned her over 2.6 billion streams, a dedicated global fanbase, and praise for her haunting performances. In support of her latest album *What Happened to the Heart?* AURORA has toured over 50 countries, selling nearly 300,000 tickets worldwide; her most ambitious and far-reaching campaign to date. Known for her otherworldly stage presence and outspoken activism, she remains a creative force redefining what pop can be: poetic, political, and profoundly human.
