# Add the Living Heartsbane Cauldron Mount to your Collection

- URL: https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/en-us/news/24304164/add-the-living-heartsbane-cauldron-mount-to-your-collection
![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/zp/ZPH0AWA2BZ1C1792810084201.png)

> Take command of this cursed creation and ride across Azeroth in wicked style. The Living Heartsbane Cauldron is a delightfully macabre mount, ready to whisk you into adventures by ground or sky.

---

Rising from the remnants of a dark ritual, this living cauldron bears the marks of the witchcraft that awakened it. Now, this cursed construct answers your command.

![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/ed/EDJ2OFGL7LD01792810132434.png)

**[Visit the Shop](https://shop.battle.net/product/world-of-warcraft-mount-living-heartsbane-cauldron)**

The Living Heartsbane Cauldron mount\* is shared across all present and future WoW® Modern characters and automatically scales to the fastest riding skill known by each character. If their level isn’t high enough to use a flying mount, the Living Heartsbane Cauldron is also a ground mount.

*\*Not available in World of Warcraft Classic® games or World of Warcraft: Forever. Requires World of Warcraft® Subscription or Game Time.*
