> Join Constable Aonda of the Skyborne as she travels into a remote settlement in the shadow of a growing threat. To find out why, she plays along, but quickly finds herself overwhelmed by hidden agendas and fanatic devotion. And in a land ruled by wind, blind devotion can become a storm.

---

Constable Aonda of the Skyborne travels into a remote settlement that appears to be under strict control. Prisoners march under close guard, strangers smile with an eerie conviction, and an uncomfortable silence provides cover for a growing threat. As she plays along to get closer to the truth, she finds herself overwhelmed by hidden agendas and fanatic devotion. And in a land ruled by wind, blind devotion can become a storm.

Join Constable Aonda for a special reading of World of Warcraft: Forever's newest motion comic, “Al'Aketh Rising”.
