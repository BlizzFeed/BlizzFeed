The young Amani troll, Zul’jan, finds himself caught between his people’s fading legacy and the dangerous temptation of ancient power. As memories of his childhood clash with the harsh realities of the present, Zul’jan is guided down this new path by a mysterious mentor, exploring his grandfather’s legacy, fractured loyalties, and the promise of a new Amani rise—but it remains to be seen whose benefit this journey is truly for.
