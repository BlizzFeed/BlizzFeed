Stay up to date with *World of Warcraft* when you join us each week as we round up some of the latest top blue posts and discussions.

#### Here are this week's highlights:

#### Dragonflight

- **WoW Community Council Live Chat**: The latest live chat occurred with Dragonflight developers on Tuesday. [Watch the full recording](http://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/wow-community-council-live-chat-december-12/1740160) and check out discussions happening in the council forum.
- **Echoed Ephemera Changes:** Multiple fixes have been [deployed](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/echoed-ephemera-not-getting-mailed-pending-solutions/1738035/10) around this quest currency. This should allow all players who maximize this currency each week to purchase the enchantment on week five.
- **Tindral Sageswift Adjustments**: These [changes](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/tindral-sageswift-adjustments-incoming-december-12/1739019) went live earlier this week.
- **Solo Shuffle Updates**: Active PvP concerns are being closely watched, and a recent change [went live](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/retail-pvp-is-at-a-breaking-point-already/1735628/2) to try and improve queue times for the Solo Shuffle PvP Brawl.

#### Wrath of the Lich King Classic

- **Joyous Journeys Reactivated**: This buff was [reactivated](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/where-is-joyous-journeys/1732726/185) late last week in all regions.
- **Ruby Sanctum PTR**: The [PTR testing](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/ruby-sanctum-ptr-testing-december-15-21/1742138) for this content is planned for December 15–21.

#### World of Warcraft Classic: Season of Discovery

- **Class Tuning Changes:** A summary of the [changes](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/season-of-discovery-class-tuning-incoming-december-12/1739014/199) that went live earlier this week included [adjustments](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/season-of-discovery-class-tuning-incoming-december-12/1739014/199) to Hunters and the Blackfathom Deeps raid.
- **Latest Ashenvale PvP Updates**: The latest [updates to this event](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/battle-for-ashenvale-fixes-more-coming/1741369) were posted on Thursday, including layering adjustments and the progress bar.
- **WoW Community Council Live Chat**: A live chat occurred with the WoW Classic developers late last week. Watch the [full recording](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/wow-community-council-live-chat-december-8/1736513/5) here and check out the [ongoing discussion](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/wow-community-council-live-chat-december-8/1736513/5) with [further updates](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/wow-community-council-live-chat-december-8/1736513/7) in the thread.
- **This Week’s Hotfixes**: The latest [updates](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/season-of-discovery-hotfixes-updated-december-13/1732235) to the game were posted, including drop rates for high-level Waylaid Supplies and a reduction in gold cost for several runes.
