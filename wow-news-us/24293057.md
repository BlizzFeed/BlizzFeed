# The AWC and MDI Grand Finals Arrive Soon at BlizzCon 2026!

> WoW Esports returns in full to BlizzCon 2026 for the AWC & MDI Grand Finals!

---

After months of preparation and hard-fought battles, the world’s best WoW teams have risen to the challenge to compete in the Grand Finals returning to BlizzCon for the first time since 2019.

The Arena World Championship and Mythic Dungeon International Grand Finals are here! Come join us live from the BlizzCon Stage or enjoy from home as Azeroth's top heroes compete for honor, the BlizzCon trophy, and a share of the $600,000 combined AWC & MDI prize pool on September 12 and 13.

### Mythic Dungeon International

Following [a year-long journey](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/en-us/news/24282984/the-mdi-season-1-finals-are-this-weekend), the four top teams will face off in an exciting single-elimination best-of-3 bracket with best-of-5 Grand Finals featuring the new Season 2 dungeon maps, tougher keystones, and record-breaking runs as they race to become the MDI Champions.

The dungeon races begin on September 12 at 12:45 pm PDT / 9:45 pm CEST for the Mythic Dungeon International Grand Finals, with the full schedule for WoW at BlizzCon available [here](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/news/24294375).

Get ready to cheer as the world’s most strategic speedrunners come together for an unforgettable showdown, racing against time and each other!

### Arena World Championship

Six fantastic teams will compete in a thrilling single-elimination best-of-5 bracket (Grand Finals best-of-7), featuring the return of cross-region matches between the best of Europe, North America, and China in a bid to come AWC BlizzCon Champions.

The epic arena action unfolds with the Arena World Championship Grand Finals on September 13 starting 10:45 am PDT / 7:45 pm CEST.

Prepare to cheer as the world’s most legendary combatants go head-to-head in some of their fiercest battles yet.
