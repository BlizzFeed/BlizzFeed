Brave adventurers, set your sights on the horizon! Calling all those who hold Azeroth dear to their hearts. Mark your calendars for an intimate 30th Anniversary of Warcraft celebration on Saturday, March 15th at The Rec Room in Toronto.

The event begins at 3:30pm EST and will honor thirty years of Warcraft’s rich history and lore with Toronto-based adventurers and heroes alike.

---

Prepare yourselves, adventurers, for the celebration’s events:

- Exclusive signing opportunities with Warcraft developers including Holly Longdale, Tina Wang, and more.
- Share a celebratory toast with Warcraft Vice President and Executive Producer, Holly Longdale.
- Delectable Warcraft-themed bites and non-alcoholic drinks fully included, along with a cash bar available.
- A Warcraft cosplay showcase to show off your best Warcraft style.
- A photobooth to pose with fellow members of the Horde, or Alliance loyalists, to take home memories from the evening.
- Commemorative surprises in a Warcraft swag bag (while supplies last.)
- Access and a wristband loaded with game tokens to The Rec Room’s many immersive activities and games.

---

> **Ticket to this exclusive and intimate celebration are available TOMORROW February 19th at 7am PST/10am EST.**
