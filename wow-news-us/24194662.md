# The Winds of Mysterious Fortune are Gusting In

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_header/gl/GLWXHNV233VJ1745271983228.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_header/gl/GLWXHNV233VJ1745271983228.png)

**When**: April 22– May 20

The winds of fortune blow in your favor during this event. While questing and adventuring in certain *Dragonflight* and *The War Within* zones, players below level 80 can gain **Mysterious Satchels** bursting with goodies while looting creatures or as a bonus reward from completing quests.

**Mysterious Satchels** contain various unique and valuable items, including temporary stat boosts, visual alterations, a chance for bonus gear appropriate to your level and specialization, and 10% experience potions that stack with the 20% experience buff to a maximum of 30%. If you think Mysterious Satchels are amazing, then you’ll love **Pristine Mysterious Satchels**.

Also, take advantage of the Winds of Mysterious Fortune 20% experience buff to get characters level 10–79 up to speed. Catch up on your alts, finish missed content and unlocks, and dive into the latest content in *The War Within*.

Read [our article](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/news/24188493/) to learn all the details about the Winds of Mysterious Fortune event.

# Spring into Action for Noblegarden!

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_header/ox/OX29EVDIAXR11744900402536.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_header/ox/OX29EVDIAXR11744900402536.png)

Hop to it and visit Azure Watch, Dolanaar, Goldshire, Kharanos, Brill, Razor Hill, Bloodhoof Village, Falconwing Square, Valdrakken, or Dornogal to join the festivities. Noblegarden brings new and returning rewards to claim for your celebratory efforts.

**When:** April 21—27\
**Currency:** Noblegarden Chocolates are used as currency during this holiday to purchase garments, mounts, pets, and other special rewards. These can be collected by completing quests and opening the eggs found in the event areas.

##### What's New in 2025

- Khaz Algar welcomes the feast of Noblegarden with great aplomb and festooned Dornogal with festive decorations, as well as a new egg hunt for hidden **Brightly Colored Eggs**.
- The stack count for Brightly Colored Eggs has been increased to 18.
- Festive new weapon transmogs to help you spring into action.

More details can be found in our [previously published article](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/news/24194659).
