# Set Out with the Hearthkeeper’s Wandering Caravan Mount

![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/at/ATOFXAUA7KKB1787179943197.png)

> Bring comfort and convenience on every ox-pedition with the Hearthkeeper’s Wandering Caravan, a handy ground mount equipped with repair and mailbox access wherever your journey takes you.

---

Bring a little extra comfort on every journey with the **Hearthkeeper’s Wandering Caravan**\* ground mount—it’s the coziest way to hoof it.

Whether you’re pushing deeper into dangerous territory, tending to your bags between adventures, or checking your mail far from town, the Hearthkeeper’s Wandering Caravan keeps repair and mailbox access at hand. Click the Caravan Home Entrance to put your character into the hut; click it again to move your character into the saddle. You’ll be ready to go the distance, with no ox-cuse to cut your expeditions short.

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/lv/LVKDH53N5NQH1787179845941.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/lv/LVKDH53N5NQH1787179845941.png)

Once added to your collection, the Hearthkeeper’s Wandering Caravan will be available to all current and future *World of Warcraft* Modern characters on your Battle.net account, traveling as fast as each character’s highest riding skill allows.

[Claim Your Caravan](https://shop.battle.net/product/world-of-warcraft-mount-hearthkeepers-wandering-caravan)

Ready to make camp wherever adventure calls? Add the Hearthkeeper’s Wandering Caravan ground mount to your collection through the in-game Shop or at [Battle.net](https://shop.battle.net/). For full details, visit [shop.battle.net](https://shop.battle.net/).
