# Chill to WoW Lofi Beats: Waiting for BlizzCon®

> Fill your ears with the sounds of Azeroth with some chill Lofi beats to help carry you to BlizzCon®.

---

There are three weeks until BlizzCon®, so we’re bringing chill beats from Azeroth. Join Jaina in Stormwind and fill your ears as you as you wait.

These ethereal Lofi tunes were mixed by Jason Charles Miller (who is also the voice of Prince Valanar in Wrath of the Lich King and High Commander Goodchilde in Legion) and Jamison Boaz of Rezodrone.
