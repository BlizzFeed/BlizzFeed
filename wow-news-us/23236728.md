# Purchase a 6 Month Subscription and Get a New Mount!

- URL: https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/en-us/news/23236728/purchase-a-6-month-subscription-and-get-a-new-mount
![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/mo/MOS7V3VNZCUC1579123943208.jpg)

> Add some mischief to your mount collection with the brand-new Squeakers, the Trickster mount, available now for purchase in-game and from the Blizzard Shop. For a limited time, you can also get this clever rat with your purchase of a 6 Month World of Warcraft subscription.

---

Introducing the brand-new Squeakers, the Trickster\* mount—now available to add to your collection! This clever new addition can be purchased in-game or from the Blizzard Shop; you can also get it free for a limited time\*\* when you purchase a **6 Month** subscription to World of Warcraft.

---

#### Stir Up Some Trouble

*It’s not quite certain where Squeakers found his incredible wings of jade. Perhaps he outwitted the Jade Serpent herself. Now Azeroth’s first flying rat is ready to set out and spread mischief wherever he goes!*

[![Video](https://markdown-videos-api.jorgenkh.no/youtube/yWD6bpOHrfI?width=1280&height=720)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yWD6bpOHrfI)

Once you’ve purchased the **6 Month** subscription from the Blizzard Shop, the Squeakers, the Trickster mount will be automatically added to your in-game collection. Welcome this mischievous addition to your assortment of mounts!

Players on an existing 6-month recurring subscription with a future renewal date will receive the Squeakers, the Trickster mount at no additional charge, no later than January 28, 2020.

[Purchase 6 Months Now](https://shop.battle.net/product/world-of-warcraft-subscription)

#### Now in the Shop!: Squeakers, the Trickster!

Take the mischievously clever Squeakers, the Trickster with you everywhere you go when you purchase in-game or from the [Blizzard Shop today](http://shop.battle.net/product/world-of-warcraft-mount-squeakers)!

[Purchase Now](https://us.shop.battle.net/en-us/product/world-of-warcraft-mount-squeakers)
