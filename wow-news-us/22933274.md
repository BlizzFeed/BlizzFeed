# Support WoW Esports With Your Toy Purchase

- URL: https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/en-us/news/22933274/support-wow-esports-with-your-toy-purchase
![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/rs/RSFVKLB69HY11552938250539.jpg)

> You can help support World of Warcraft esports by purchasing two new in-game toys!

---

Show your support for WoW esports with two new toys, now available in the Blizzard shop and in-game shop.

For a limited time, every purchase of the Transmorpher Beacon or Lion’s Pride and Horde’s Might Fireworks, 25% of the proceeds will contribute toward the year’s finals LAN event prize pool for the Arena World Championship (AWC) and the Mythic Dungeon International (MDI) with a guaranteed minimum prize pool of $500,000 USD ($250,000 USD for each event.) Your support will help take the WoW esports prize pool to the next level.

#### Play like a boss with the Transmorpher Beacon

![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/i7/I7WPRMR2IV9S1552952785252.jpg)

The ethereals have a twisted new trick up their sleeves, and they’re ready to traverse the nether to help you spread some chaos and confusion on the battlefield. Use this beacon to summon an Ethereal Transmorpher who can turn you and your allies into some of the most fearsome foes Azeroth has ever known.

[Buy Now](https://us.shop.battle.net/en-us/product/world-of-warcraft-toy-transmorpher-beacon)

#### Lion's Pride and Horde's Might Fireworks

![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/f6/F6SB1NWA2X5G1552945068332.jpg)

What’s more satisfying than vanquishing a worthy foe in honorable hand-to-hand combat? Planting an explosive in their freshly felled corpse, that’s what! Light up the sky above a recently defeated enemy player with these reusable fireworks and send an unmistakable signal to the other faction.

[Buy Now](https://us.shop.battle.net/en-us/product/world-of-warcraft-toy-fireworks)

Your chance to support WoW esports and donate to the prize pool ends on October 15, 2019.

Learn more about WoW Esports for 2019 in [our post](https://worldofwarcraft.com/en-us/news/22862590/) and follow all of the [AWC](https://worldofwarcraft.com/en-us/esports/arena) and [MDI](https://worldofwarcraft.com/en-us/esports/mythic) action on the esports site.
