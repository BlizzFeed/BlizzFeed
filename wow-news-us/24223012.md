## New Dungeons

Brave defenders of Azeroth can battle their way through six new dungeons in Pandaria.

[Temple of the Jade Serpent](#achievements)

- **Level Requirement**: 85
- **Recommended Level:** 85-90
- **Dungeon Finder Level Requirement**: 85-87
- **Item Level Required**: 325
- **Location**: The Jade Forest, Pandaria

Once a sacred site dedicated to Yu'lon, the Jade Serpent, the Temple of the Jade Serpent has been overrun by the Sha, malevolent entities born from negative emotions. As the Sha's corruption spreads, it twists the Temple's serene halls, distorting its guardians and defiling its sacred waters. Players are tasked with cleansing the Temple, battling both possessed caretakers and manifestations of fear and doubt.

The dungeon features four boss encounters, each reflecting the Temple's corruption:

- **Wise Mari**: The revered Jinyu waterspeaker, Wise Mari has found knowledge and foresight countless times in the Fountain of the Ever-Seeing. But when the Sha of Doubt corrupted the familiar currents, Mari was overwhelmed with limitless distrust and suspicion.
- **Lorewalker Stonestep**: The Sha of Doubt's minions gleefully invaded the Temple of the Jade Serpent's library, creating twisted, destructive versions of heroes and other characters of pandaren lore. Despite his best efforts, Lorewalker Stonestep has been unable to restore these manifestations of the past to their uncorrupted forms.
- **Liu Flameheart**: Since she was a cub, the priestess Liu Flameheart has studied the cycle of rebirth for the Jade Serpent, Yu'lon. When the Sha of Doubt attacked, Liu was the first to resist, but she was also the first to succumb to corruption. Now she is consumed with uncertainty, fearing she may not have the strength to ensure Yu'lon's rebirth.
- **Sha of Doubt**: The physical manifestation of suspicion was trapped in the Jade Forest after Emperor Shaohao defeated the entity 10,000 years ago. The Sha of Doubt then grew obsessed with the Jade Serpent--Yu'lon, the Spirit of Wisdom and Foresight--and now delights in the thought of corrupting her home.

#### Achievements

- **Cleaning Up**: Defeat the Sha of Doubt in Temple of the Jade Serpent while under the effects of Purified Water.
- **Seeds of Doubt**: Defeat the Sha of Doubt in Temple of the Jade Serpent while under the effect of 4 Seeds of Doubt on Heroic difficulty.
- **Hydrophobia**: Defeat Wise Mari without being hit by Corrupted Water, Hydrolance, or Wash Away in Temple of the Jade Serpent on Heroic Difficulty.

[Stormstout Brewery](#achievements-1)

- **Level Requirement:** 85
- **Recommended Level:** 85-90
- **Dungeon Finder Level Requirement:** 85-87
- **Item Level Required:** 325
- **Location:** Valley of the Four Winds, Pandaria

Stormstout Brewery is located in the lush Valley of the Four Winds. Once a proud brewery run by the Stormstout family, it has fallen into chaos, overrun by mischievous Hozen, virmen, and the corrupted ale spirits known as the Alementals. Players must help Chen Stormstout reclaim his ancestral brewery by battling through waves of drunken Hozen, squashing virmen infestations, and purging the tainted brews. The dungeon's atmosphere is both lighthearted and chaotic — Stormstout Brewery offers a mix of humorous encounters and unique mechanics.

This dungeon features three boss encounters, each with distinct mechanics:

- **Ook-Ook**: Even for a hozen, Ook-Ook has a reputation for being uncouth and raucous. The monstrous creature and his cronies recently broke into the Stormstout Brewery and cracked open its precious beer casks to throw the most fabulous party of all time: Ookapalooza!
- **Hoptallus**: Hoptallus is the bane of farmers throughout the Valley of the Four Winds - a vermin of mythic proportions that can consume entire fields of crops in a single night. Word has spread that the giant pest and his offspring have infested the brewery and begun devouring its stores of rice and barley.
- **Yan-Zhu the Uncasked**: In a desperate attempt to save the family brewery, Uncle Gao set about concocting what he believed would be the ultimate ale, a full-bodied elixir of depthless flavor. Yet his bold experiment resulted in a bitter and extremely volatile *ale*mental creature, dubbed Yan-Zhu the Uncasked.

#### Achievements

- **Keep Rollin' Rollin' Rollin'**: Kill Ook-Ook after assaulting him with 20 Rolling Brew Barrels in Stormstout Brewery on Heroic Difficulty.
- **How Did He Get Up There?**: Find a way to get the Hozen Clinger to let go in Stormstout Brewery on any difficulty.
- **Ling-Ting's Herbal Journey**: Collect all 30 Golden Hoplings hidden around the Stormstout Brewery on any difficulty.
  - Complete this achievement to earn the [Hopling](https://www.wowhead.com/item=86562/hopling?brl) pet.
- **Hopocalypse Now!**: Smash 100 Virmen with a single hammer swing in Stormstout Brewery on Heroic Difficulty

[Mogu'shan Palace](#achievements-2)

- **Level Requirement:** 87
- **Recommended Level:** 87-90
- **Dungeon Finder Level Requirement:** 87-89
- **Item Level Required:** 366
- **Location:** Vale of Eternal Blossoms

Once a grand seat of mogu power, Mogu'shan Palace now serves as a battleground where rival factions seek to reclaim the legacy of their ancient empire. The mogu warlord Xin the Weaponmaster has gathered his forces within the palace, aiming to restore the mogu's dominance over Pandaria. Players must fight through the halls of this once-glorious stronghold, facing hostile mogu warriors and opportunistic raiders as they work to put an end to Xin's ambitions.

The dungeon features three boss encounters, each representing a different faction vying for control:

- **Trial of the King**: Ming the Cunning, Kuai the Brute, and Haiyan the Unstoppable have journeyed to the palace with their clans, vying to prove their worth to their king. Yet, these Mogu factions are plagued by bitter rivalries. Violence threatens to flood the palatial grounds unless the bickering clans can find a way to test their strength without coming to blows with one another.
- **Gekkan**: As the mogu clans battle for supremacy, the cunning Gekkan leads his fearsome saurok raiding party into the palace's fabled treasure caches. Loyalty is not usually ascribed to these ruthless reptilian beings, but Gekkan's courage and tactical brilliance inspire a sense of honor in his soldiers.
- **Xin the Weaponmaster**: The Title of Weaponmaster has many definitions among Azeroth's inhabitants. To Xin, it means that Mogu weaponry obeys his every command. The king is more than willing to demonstrate his deadly skills to anyone who questions his right to rule Pandaria.

#### Achievements

- **Quarrelsome Quilen Quintet**: Collect all the Jade Quilen located throughout Mogu'Shan Palace on Heroic Difficulty.
- **What Does This Button Do?**: Use Xin the Weaponmaster's secret defense mechanism against him in Mogu'shan Palace on Heroic difficulty.
- **Glintrok N' Roll**: Defeat Gekkan and his Glintrok entourage in Mogu'shan Palace on Heroic difficulty within 5 minutes of being spotted by the Glintrok Scout.

[Shado-Pan Monastery](#achievements-3)

- **Level Requirement:** 87
- **Recommended Level:** 87-90
- **Dungeon Finder Level Requirement:** 87-88
- **Item Level Required:** 366
- **Location:** Kun-Lai Summit, Pandaria

As the stronghold of the Shado-Pan, Pandaria's most elite defenders, Shado-Pan Monastery has been overrun by the Sha, corrupting its warriors and threatening its sacred halls. The Shado-Pan, trained to resist negative emotions, have struggled against the overwhelming influence of the Sha, leaving the monastery in disarray. Players are called upon to purge the corruption, battling possessed monks, twisted spirits, and the Sha itself to restore balance to this once-honored sanctuary.

The dungeon consists of four boss encounters, each representing a different stage of the monastery's fall:

- **Gu Cloudstrike**: Before the escape of the sha, Gu Cloudstrike was entrusted with training the elite Shado-Pan spellcasters. Now this formerly gentle and reserved pandaren revels in unleashing his astounding powers, with devastating consequences. He has asserted control over the mystical Azure Serpent, and his students are helpless in the wake of Cloudstrike's lethal attacks.
- **Master Snowdrift**: Master Snowdrift recognizes that the battles between the Horde and the Alliance have contributed to the monastery's dire state. Heroes seeking this wary pandaren's loyalty must first best his students. Only those who prevail can face Snowdrift himself, a paragon of martial arts who has dedicated his life to attaining perfection of body and mind.
- **Sha of Violence**: Bloodthirsty, ruthless, and savage, the Sha of Violence is unfettered within the Shado-Pan Monastery. Feeding on the Horde and Alliance's brutality, the entity gleefully ravages the monks who struggle to impede the monstrous onslaught.
- **Taran Zhu**: The Sha of Hatred has tainted Taran Zhu, the Shado-Pan's pragmatic and mighty leader. Once devoted to protecting his beloved home, Zhu now burns with seething hatred for all life, twisting his perception of everything—and everyone—around him.

#### Achievements

- **Hate Leads to Suffering**: Defeat Taran Zhu while every party member is at maximum hatred in Shado-Pan Monastery on Heroic Difficulty.
- **Respect**: Bow to the Shado-Pan Novices when they bow to you after being defeated during the Master Snowdrift event in Shado-Pan Monastery on Heroic Difficulty.
- **The Obvious Solution**: Eliminate the remaining Sha imprisoned in the Sealed Chambers and then defeat the Sha of Violence in Shado-Pan Monastery on Heroic Difficulty.

[Gate of the Setting Sun](#achievements-4)

- **Level Requirement:** 88
- **Recommended Level:** 88-90
- **Dungeon Finder Level Requirement:** 88
- **Item Level Required:** 393
- **Location:** Vale of Eternal Blossoms, Pandaria

The Gate of the Setting Sun is located on the Serpent's Spine, the massive wall that separates Pandaria from the Mantid Empire. The dungeon takes place during a full-scale mantid assault, as the insectoid army breaches the gate and threatens to spill into the Vale of Eternal Blossoms. Players join the Shado-Pan defenders in a desperate battle to repel the invasion, fighting through swarms of mantid warriors and their monstrous war machines while working to prevent the fortress from falling.

The dungeon consists of four boss encounters, each escalating the siege:

- **Saboteur Kip'tilak**: The mantids are no strangers to warfare, using technology to turn the tide of battle. The pandaren stand little chance against munitions experts such as Kip'tilak, who strike deep behind enemy lines and threaten to destroy the already weakening fortifications from within.
- **Striker Ga'dok**: The strikers are a key aerial component of the Mantid Empire's massive army. Among them are seasoned aces such as Ga'dok. Able to single-handedly rain fiery devastation upon scores of enemies, Ga'dok and his minions seek to bring death from above to the gates' defenders.
- **Commander Ri'mok**: Commander Ri'mok oversees the mantid ground forces relentlessly besieging the towering gates. Unimpressed by the defenders, Ri'mok calls upon countless reinforcements to vanquish those foolish enough to stand in his way.
- **Raigonn**: This colossal mantid spearheads the assault against the gates. Acting as a living battering ram, Raigonn mercilessly hammers the pandaren defenses, seeking to decimate any and all obstacles barring the mantid's ruthless invasion of Pandaria.

#### Achievements

- **Bomberman**: Trigger 15 explosions within 15 seconds during the Saboteur Kip'tilak encounter in Gate of the Setting Sun on Heroic Difficulty.
- **Conscriptinator**: Gain three stacks of Resin Residue by defeating Mantid Conscripts in Gate of the Setting Sun on Heroic Difficulty.
- **Mantid Swarm**: Defeat Raigonn without killing any of the Mantid reinforcements

[Siege of Niuzao Temple](#achievements-5)

- **Level Requirement:** 88
- **Recommended Level:** 88-90
- **Dungeon Finder Level Requirement:** 88
- **Item Level Required:** 393
- **Location:** Townlong Steppes, Pandaria

Niuzao Temple, a sacred site protected by the Niuzao Pandaren, has come under attack by the mantid, who seek to breach its defenses. As the battle rages, the Shado-Pan defenders struggle to hold their ground against waves of invaders. Players must assist in the Temple's defense, repelling the mantid forces and eliminating their commanders before the siege can succeed.

The dungeon features four boss encounters:

- **Vizier Jin'bak**: Vizier Jin'bak roams just beyond the mantid's natural bridge, collecting the tree root's precious sap. Using his vast alchemical knowledge, this formidable being is transforming the substance into weapons and other tools that will aid in advancing the siege on Niuzao Temple.
- **Commander Vo'jak**: What Commander Vo'jak lacks in strategic brilliance, he makes up for with raw ferocity. His devastating strikes were crucial to the mantid's exploitation of their insidious bridge, allowing them to infiltrate the island. Yet devising battle plans is for the mantid general; Vo'jak craves only blood.
- **General Pa'valak**: The pragmatic General Pa'valak does not fear the pandaren or their allies, and he does not differentiate between using his own might and that of his mantid armies to defeat his enemies. Victory is the only honor he seeks, and Pa'valak will not tolerate failure should his minions fall short of their goal.
- **Wing Leader Ner'onok**: Wing Leader Ner'onok's decisive blows have won a hundred battles. The small gains General Pa'valak has made are nothing compared to the victory Ner'onok believes he could achieve if he were in command. Although his aerial attacks have thus far devastated the Temple's guardians, the winged terror still longs for a chance to prove his superiority over the general.

#### Achievements

- **Return to Sender**: Defeat General Pa'valak while he has 20 stacks of Detonate in the Siege of Niuzao Temple on Heroic Difficulty.
- **Run with the Wind**: Defeat Ner'onok in Siege of Niuzao Temple on Heroic Difficulty while under the effect of Tenwu's Resolve.
- **Where's My Air Support?**: Defeat Commander Vo'jak after having killed the Sik'thik Amberwing in Siege of Niuzao Temple on Heroic Difficulty

## Heroic Dungeons

In addition to the six dungeons arriving in Mists of Pandaria Classic, there are heroic dungeons available for players to reap greater rewards. All heroics have a required item level of 440 and drop item level 463 loot. The final boss of each heroic also has a chance to drop an item level 476 epic.

[Scarlet Halls (Heroic)](#panel-group-name7)

- **Level Requirement:** 90
- **Dungeon Finder Level Requirement:** 90
- **Item Level Required:** 435
- **Location:** Tirisfal Glades, Eastern Kingdoms

The Scarlet Halls are located in the former stronghold of the Scarlet Crusade within the Eastern Kingdoms. After the fall of the original order, the remaining Crusaders have fortified their halls, training new recruits and stockpiling weapons to continue their fanatical crusade against the undead—or any they deem impure. Players must fight their way through the halls, facing well-trained warriors and powerful leaders determined to uphold the Scarlet Crusade's brutal ideology.

The dungeon consists of three boss encounters:

- **Houndmaster Braun**: The quickest and surest way to detect the undead is by the scent of rot they carry, and even the most veteran soldier cannot match the olfactory sense of a hound. Houndmaster Braun has bred and trained a savage pack of canines to aid the crusaders in hunting down their foes—living and undead.
- **Armsmaster Harlan**: The weapons of the Scarlet Crusade are many, and new initiates must be indoctrinated in the use of blade, hammer, arrow, and shield--effective tools against the undead. Armsmaster Harlan has dedicated his life to the art of wielding these indelicate instruments, and he is pleased to provide a demonstration for the Crusade's blasphemous enemies.
- **Flameweaver Koegler**: Flameweaver Koegler trains the Scarlet Crusade's more magically minded adepts in the subtle applications of the arcane. Fire is his specialty, and he has used his skills to burn the order's historical tomes, erasing the Crusade's past failures. Apart from books, Koegler's flames have reduced many undead, students, and trespassers alike to piles of smoking ash

[Scarlet Monastery (Heroic)](#panel-group-name8)

- **Level Requirement:** 90
- **Dungeon Finder Level Requirement:** 90
- **Item Level Required:** 435
- **Location:** Tirisfal Glades, Eastern Kingdoms

Located in Tirisfal Glades, the Scarlet Monastery was once the heart of the Scarlet Crusade's operations. It remains a bastion of the fanatical order, now under the leadership of its most zealous warriors. Players must infiltrate the monastery, battling its fanatical defenders and eliminating its high-ranking leaders to weaken the Scarlet Crusade's influence.

The dungeon features four boss encounters:

- **Thalnos the Soulrender**: Thalnos earned repute in the Scarlet Crusade for the brutal torture methods he used to "purify" new recruits. Ultimately, the sadistic mage contracted the plague of undeath and arose as a fearsome skeletal being. The vengeful Thalnos now prowls the monastery's graveyard, commanding apparitions and zombified creatures to attack anyone who trespasses on his unholy domain.
- **Brother Korloff**: Brother Korloff first learned of the monk's deadly arts from pandaren ambassadors as they journeyed through Azeroth. When the crusader demonstrated this martial style to his superiors, they unanimously ordered Korloff to train an entire generation of initiates in the ways of the monk.
- **High Inquisitor Whitemane**: At a young age, Sally Whitemane witnessed her family succumb to the horrific plague of undeath as they were traveling through northern Lordaeron. She was then forced to destroy her parents and siblings when they rose as mindless Scourge minions, leaving her racked by guilt and rage. Ever since that day, the fearsome priestess has found fulfillment and pleasure in only one thing: the destruction of the undead

[Scholomance (Heroic)](#panel-group-name9)

- **Level Requirement:** 90
- **Dungeon Finder Level Requirement:** 90
- **Item Level Required:** 435
- **Location:** Western Plaguelands, Eastern Kingdoms

Scholomance is found in the ruined keep of Caer Darrow in the Western Plaguelands. Once a school for necromancy under the control of the Cult of the Damned, the academy remains a breeding ground for dark magic and undead horrors. Though the Lich King has fallen, his legacy persists within these halls, where necromancers continue their forbidden studies. Players must navigate the twisted corridors of the academy, battling undead instructors, twisted experiments, and powerful cultists to purge the lingering Scourge influence.

The dungeon features five boss encounters, each representing a different aspect of the dark arts practiced within Scholomance.

- **Instructor Chillheart: Course: Introduction to the Dark Arts:** Instructor Chillheart journeyed from Northrend to teach aspiring necromancers discipline, harshly punishing those who disappoint her. She commands the icy chill of the north and masterfully uses Scholomance's libraries to deadly effect.
- **Jandice Barov – Course: Advanced Illusions**: Barov's spirit lurked in the recesses of Scholomance for years, but now she has taken a more active role in educating the next generation of magi. Her vast knowledge of illusions has made her both a valued educator and a dangerous opponent to anyone foolish enough to draw her ire.
- **Rattlegore – Course: Reanimation 101**: Rattlegore was thought to have been destroyed in Andorhal, but Gandling immediately began preparations to reanimate the clattering terror so he could guard against intruders. The Darkmaster sought to make Rattlegore more ferocious than ever, imbuing him with an insatiable desire to harvest raw materials from his enemies.
- **Lilian Voss – Course: Reeducation**: The undead Lilian Voss strangled her father--a high priest of the Scarlet Crusade--for treachery, and then started a rampage that eventually led her into the shadowy corridors of Scholomance. Rather than destroy the intruder, Darkmaster Gandling graciously decided to show Voss how to embrace her grim fate. The anger and runeblades she had once directed at the school are now turned toward Gandling's enemies.
- **Darkmaster Gandling – Course: Advanced Studies**: The recent defeat in Andorhal has put Darkmaster Gandling in the foulest of moods. More than ever, all students of Scholomance are advised to impress him at every turn. Pupils who dissatisfy the headmaster will be removed from class to perform lab work... or become lab work
