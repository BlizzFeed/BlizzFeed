Slide into the saddle and ride the waves astride the Wondrous Wavewhisker mount, a *fin*tastical fish that will take you on a mesmerizing journey around Azeroth and beyond.

Add the Wondrous Wavewhisker\* fancy-finned mount to your muster of mounts! Purchase this mount from in-game or the Blizzard Shop, or get it with a [6-Month or 12-Month Subscription](https://us.shop.battle.net/product/world-of-warcraft-subscription) along with the adorable Glub pet\*\* to keep you company in World of Warcraft®: Wrath of the Lich King Classic™ \*\*\*. The mount is also available as a separate purchase.

#### Rollick in Fancy-Finned Adventures

> Once hunted nearly to extinction, wavewhiskers were carefully cultivated by the Ankoan, who revere them to this day.

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/jb/JBC6HBIN4H101688659110384.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/jb/JBC6HBIN4H101688659110384.png)

Wondrous Wavewhisker will be automatically added to your Collection once you purchase the [6-month subscription](https://us.shop.battle.net/product/world-of-warcraft-subscription) from the Blizzard Shop. Welcome this fancy-finned addition to your assortment of mounts!

The 6-Month Subscription offer includes the bubbled-buddy, Glub pet for your *Wrath of the Lich King Classic* characters.

If you’re currently on a 6-month or 12-month recurring subscription with a future renewal date, you’ll receive the mount and the pet as a gift in your [Battle.net](http://battle.net/) desktop app at no additional charge no later than July 24, 2023 (must be redeemed by January 31, 2024).
