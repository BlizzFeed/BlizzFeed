# The End of Burning Crusade Classic PvP Season 2 is Nigh!

- URL: https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/en-us/news/23751522/the-end-of-burning-crusade-classic-pvp-season-2-is-nigh
![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/ic/ICZ7P0U2GOXU1639610005356.jpg)

> It’s time for one last push with your Arena team to see who will rise to the top of the leaderboards.

---

It’s time for one last push with your Arena team to see who will rise to the top of the leaderboards. Arena teams will have two weeks to do their best work before the Burning Crusade PvP Season 2 becomes history on January 11, 2022.

Burning Crusade Classic PvP Season 3 and Phase 3 of Burning Crusade Classic content will arrive at a later date in early 2022. Stay tuned to the official site and the forums for more information.

Do you have what it takes to finish the season strong?
