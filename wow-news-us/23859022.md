#### THIS WEEK IN SHADOWLANDS

- **Fated Raids: Sanctum of Domination**
  - The week, bosses in Sanctum of Domination are empowered with increased difficulty and a special affix. Loot drops are upgraded to a higher item level.
- **Battleground Bonus Event**
  - While this event is active, random Battlegrounds will award Honor at an increased rate. Glory awaits in the fields of battle!
- **PvP Brawl: Arathi Blizzard**
  - Put away your bathing suit, pull out your parka, and enter a winter wonderland as the Arathi Basin you know and love gets a fresh coat of ice and snow.
- **Brewfest (also in WoW Classic)**
  - First celebrated by the dwarves, Brewfest now holds appeal among all the races of Azeroth! Visit the Alliance camp outside Ironforge or the Horde camp outside Orgrimmar and join the party!

#### COMING UP IN SHADOWLANDS

##### Beginning Next Week:

- **Fated Raids: Sepulcher of the First Ones**
  - This week, bosses in Sepulcher of the First Ones are empowered with increased difficulty and a special affix. Loot drops are upgraded to a higher item level.
- **PvP Brawl: Gravity Lapse**
  - Is gravity getting you down? Step into the Eye of the Storm and prepare for a new en-lighten-ing experience.
- **Timewalking Dungeon Event**
  - While this event is active, players level 50 or higher may access a special Timewalking Dungeon Finder queue, which scales players and their items down to revisit past dungeons from the Wrath of the Lich King expansion.

##### Beginning the week of October 3:

- **Darkmoon Faire**
  - Meet Silas Darkmoon and his troupe, play games that test mind and nerve, and behold exotic sights from the four corners of Azeroth… and beyond!
- **Fated Raids: Castle Nathria**
  - This week, bosses in Castle Nathria are empowered with increased difficulty and a special affix. Loot drops are upgraded to a higher item level.
- **Torghast: Beasts of Prodigum**
  - Beasts of Prodigum have been unleashed up Torghast, Tower of the Damned!
- **PvP Brawl: Comp Stomp**
  - Jump into Arathi Basin for a head-to-head battle against AI opponents.
- **Shadowlands Dungeon Event**
  - While this event is active, the final boss of each Shadowlands dungeon awards an extra item when defeated.
- **The Great Gnomeregan Run**
  - Join the Gnomes as they celebrate their unrelenting moxie! Race across the Eastern Kingdoms, from Gnomeregan down to Booty Bay!
