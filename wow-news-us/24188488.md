### The Liberation of Undermine Raid Finder Wing 4 is Now Live!

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_header/la/LAKZTCFQQXFF1739218896817.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_header/la/LAKZTCFQQXFF1739218896817.png)

Things are heating up in Undermine, and the cartels are ready to take the battle right to the heart of Gallywix’s stronghold in the Gallagio. Players will encounter eight new bosses— including the Chrome King Gallywix himself—as they fight their way through the glitz and glam to get straight down to business.

**Raid Details:**

- **Where:** Undermine
- **Raid Bosses:** 8
- **Level:** 80
- **Difficulties:** Story Mode, Raid Finder, Normal, Heroic, Mythic
- **Raid Finder Minimum Item Level:** 606

**Raid Unlock Schedule:**

- **Week of March 4:** Raid Finder Wing 1: Shock and Awesome (Cauldron of Carnage; Rik Reverb), Normal, Heroic, Mythic Difficulty
- **Week of March 11:** Raid Finder Wing 2: Maniacal Machinist (Sprocketmonger Lockenstock; Stix Bunkjunker), Story Mode
- **Week of March 18:** Raid Finder Wing 3**:**Two Heads Are Better **(**Vexie and the Geargrinders; One-Armed Bandit; Mug’Zee)
- **Week of March 25:** Raid Finder Wing 4: The Chrome King (Chrome King Gallywix)

No pressure or anything, but goblin society depends on you, so learn how you can prepare for the Liberation of Undermine from [our article](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/news/24178099/).

### April’s Trading Post is Faire and Bright

Shop the April Trading Post to look your Faire best as you Spring into the season.  This month, join the carnival with the festively festooned **Ensemble: Topsy Turvy Mask** transmog set.

![ ](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_header/ch/CHKFXP16TJOT1742931386395.png)

**Ensemble: Topsy Turvy Mask Set (Head)**\
*“Join the carnival in your favorite flair of purple or green.”*

Complete activities to fill the bar at the top of the**Traveler’s Log** found in the **Adventure Guide (Shift-J)**, and you'll receive this month's bonus reward — the **Ensemble: Topsy Turvy Mask**transmog set. Join the carnival with these festively festooned masks.

Visit T&W (Tawney and Wilder) just outside the Mage District in Stormwind or the Zen’shiri Trading Post next to Grommash Hold in Orgrimmar to check out all the latest items they have available.

Players can also visit the Trading Post in the capital city of Dornogal (The Foregrounds) on the Isle of Dorn, where representatives of both Tawney and Wilder and the Zen’shiri Trading Post are open for business.
