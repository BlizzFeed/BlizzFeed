# WoW Classic: Arathi Basin Now Available!

- URL: https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/en-us/news/23320961/wow-classic-arathi-basin-now-available
![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/6f/6FS5NXAUE1DC1583543663580.jpg)

> The gates into Arathi Basin are now open in this 15 vs.15 resource battle. Do you and your crew have what it takes to take control and win?

---

The gates into Arathi Basin are now open in this 15 vs.15 resource battle. Players will need to hone their team coordination to a keen edge in order to capture and hold five different bases throughout the map and accrue resources. Alliance will start close to the Stables at Trollbane Hall while Horde begin near the farm in the Defiler’s Den. The first team to collect 2000 resources wins.

---

![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/0q/0QG3XJNGXEI11583543715658.jpg)

**Players:** 15 vs.15\
**Level Range:**20+\
**Capture Points:** Farm, Mine, Blacksmith, Lumber Mill, and Stables\
**Resources Needed to Win**: 2000

---

#### Reputations

There are two reputations associated with this battleground: [The Defilers](https://classic.wowhead.com/faction=510/the-defilers&brl) (Horde), and the [League of Arathor](https://classic.wowhead.com/faction=509/the-league-of-arathor&brl) (Alliance). Gaining reputation with either faction will unlock additional rewards for purchase from common resources such as potions and bandages at Friendly to epic pieces of armor and weapons at Exalted.
