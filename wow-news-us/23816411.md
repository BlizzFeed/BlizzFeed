Grab your parka and gloves and prepare for an adventurous return to the frozen tundra of Northrend. Take a sentimental journey with art director Ely Cannon, Lead Level Designer Sarah Boulian-Verrall, and *Overwatch* game director Aaron Keller to the time when they all worked together as a part of the level design team on *Wrath of the Lich King*. You’ll learn more about how the team built Northrend and how this icebound world was brought to life.
