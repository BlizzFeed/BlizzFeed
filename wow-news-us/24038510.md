Get back into action when you purchase the Enhanced Level 70 Boost\* and jump into Dragonflight endgame-ready.

There comes a time in every hero’s quest when they need a helping hand to get them over the hump and right back into the action. Now you can instantly grant a single character a one-time boost to level 70 and take your adventure to new heights right away. To boost multiple characters, buy a Character Boost pack and use them all at once or save some for later.

![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/eb/EB2E8I9WKH701701310373871.png)

### What You Get

With the enhanced Level 70 Boost, you’ll get the following:

- **Upgraded Gear:** Get started with Mythic dungeons or jump right into Raid Finder Difficulty in Amirdrassil, the Dream’s Hope, the final raid dungeon of *Dragonflight,* with epic new gear (item level 424) and battle your way to face Fyrakk and his allies.
- **Renown Progress:** Get a leg up with 20 Renown levels with the Valdrakken Accord, Iskaara Tuskarr, Maruuk Centaur, and Dragonscafle Expedition. Get 12 Renown levels with the Niffen.
- **Bags and Reagent Bags:** 4 Shrouded Cloth Bags (30 slot) and 1 Gatherer’s Reagent Pouch (26 slot)
- **Dragonriding Mounts:** Take flight with Five Dragonriding Mounts including the Cliffside Wylderdrake, Highland Drake, Windborne Velocidrake, Renewed Proto-Drake, and the Winding Slitherdrake Mount.
- **Guardians of the Dream Campaign Access:** Get right into the story in the Guardians of the Dream content update with immediate access to the start of the campaign.
- **Zones and Flight paths Discovered:** All areas and flight paths for The Waking Shores, Ohn’ahran Plains, The Azure Span, Thaldraszus, The Forbidden Reach, and Zaralek Cavern discovered and available to travel at will.
- **Explore Zaralek Caverns**: Explore the Zaralek Caverns right from the start without needing to complete the lead-up campaign quests.
- **Renown Systems Unlocked**:
  - Access the Dragon Isles Travel Network Waygates.
  - All Dragonscale Expedition talents purchased allowing for treasure finding, rock climbing, and photography minigames.
  - All NPC craftable Tuskarr Fishing Gear unlocked (Dragon Isles Fishing still required.)
- **5000 Dragon Isles Supplies**

Players can also get an Enhanced Level 70 Character Boost included in all editions of the upcoming [World of Warcraft®: War Within](https://shop.battle.net/product/world-of-warcraft-the-war-within?utm_source=worldofwarcraft.com&utm_campaign=mktg-wow-global-11-prepurchase&utm_medium=referral&utm_content=wow_blog&_gl=1*1vt8v50*_ga*Njc5ODY4OTUwLjE2ODQ4NjIwNjA.*_ga_VYKNV7C0S3*MTcwMDAwNTE3Ni4xODcuMC4xNzAwMDA1MTc2LjYwLjAuMA..)™\*  expansion.
