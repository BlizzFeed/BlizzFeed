## K'aresh, in Pieces

Home of the ethereals, K'aresh was once a vibrant world, but now it only exists as a fragmented void-touched world waiting for players to explore what remains of this little-known land. Though there are multiple points of interest for heroes to explore, including ethereal Eco-Domes where life still thrives.

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/e4/E4Z191KBN3FZ1752616296877.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/e4/E4Z191KBN3FZ1752616296877.png)

With shadowy forces working toward bringing Dimensius back into the world, players will need to gather their allies in a bid to put a stop to him and his followers.

### Main Hub: Tazavesh, the Veiled Market

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/hj/HJ7UEM2EHELZ1752608207188.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/hj/HJ7UEM2EHELZ1752608207188.png)

Launch your adventures, forge through the campaign, gather allies, hunt down the mounting threats found in K'aresh, and set the stage to take on Dimensius himself, all from this iconic ethereal and broker hub of activity. Tazavesh is also home to two Mythic+ dungeons—Streets of Wonder and So'leah's Gambit.

Follow the campaign from Locus-Walker, completing quests along the way, to open a portal after you complete the "A Shadowy Invitation" quest. This marks the starting point of your journey into the new zone, K'aresh.

Once inside the market, find vendors, more quests, and portals. There are even portals to each of the four daily active Bountiful Delves.

### Primary Faction: K'aresh Trust

A coalition of Broker cartels and ethereals formed to accomplish an impossible goal—save their destroyed world from the All-Devouring.

The K'aresh Trust is the seventh major faction of The War Within, and is based out of Tazavesh, located in what remains of K'aresh.

Get faction rewards from Renown Quartermaster Om'sirik found in Tazavesh, the Veiled Market.

### Phase Dive in K'aresh with Reshii Wraps

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/4IVGG4MCQUB01750209631370.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/4IVGG4MCQUB01750209631370.png)

Harness the power of the ethereals and see the world through their eyes. An artifact cloak, Reshii Wraps, can be used to transform into an energy form, much like the ethereals, to interact with the world in a new way. You'll use these wraps to take on a new type of content—Phase Diving— as well as use them in the Manaforge Omega raid, the open world, delves, and more!

Reshii wraps will upgrade and scale with you, including new visuals as you progress through the content.

## New Dungeon: Eco-Dome Al'dani

The storyline of Al'dani takes place following the events of the Manaforge Omega raid. The eco-domes represent the best hope for restoring life to K'aresh, and Eco-Dome Al'dani was built on the site of one of the most sacred locations on K'aresh. A Wastelander invasion has ripped open the dome, exposing it to destruction. Ve'nari has called for help to keep K'aresh's great hope from being destroyed. Join the ethereals' efforts to regain control by clearing the new three-boss dungeon, earning rewards, and exploring the latest content.

- **Where**: K'aresh
- **Bosses**: 3
- **Level**: 80
- **Difficulties**: Normal, Heroic, Mythic

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/8BLN7LGBWEGZ1750209628365.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/8BLN7LGBWEGZ1750209628365.png)

On your adventures, you'll encounter a variety of creatures, including life-consuming devourers and Wastelanders. This dungeon is available in Normal, Heroic, and Mythic 0 difficulties, and offers the following item level gear:

- **Normal Difficulty**: Item Level 639 (Non-Upgradeable)
- **Heroic Difficulty**: Item Level 665 (Adventurer 4/8)
- **Mythic Difficulty**: Item Level 681+ (Champion 1/8)

##### Azhiccar

Devourers often travel in the wake of the Great Devourer Worms to reach new realms to feast on. Azhiccar is no exception. It burrows into a new land, seeking to satiate its endless hunger. Sometimes even other devourers fall prey, but the devourers still answer Azhiccar's call—to consume or be consumed in its vortex of endless hunger.

##### Soul-Scribe

The Soul-Scribe once led the oracles of K'aresh, but the destruction of her world drove them into the wastelands, where they began a crusade of revenge. She has given ancient oaths to destroy the enemies of Salhadaar and all they have built. With her ancient powers rediscovered, the fate of this dome looks grim.

### Dungeon Achievements

- **Eco-Dome Al'dani**: Defeat Soul-Scribe in Eco-Dome Al'dani.
- **Heroic: Eco-Dome Al'dani**: Defeat Soul-Scribe in Eco-Dome Al'dani on Heroic difficulty or higher.
- **Mythic: Eco-Dome Al'dani**: Defeat Soul-Scribe in Eco-Dome Al'dani on Mythic or Mythic Keystone difficulty.
- **Keystone Hero: Eco-Dome Al'dani**: Complete Eco-Dome Al'dani at Mythic Level 10 or higher, within the time limit.

### Find Your Way With Waystones

Dungeon checkpoints are evolving into Waystones, with a more visual representation of unlock checkpoints. A new icon will appear on the map showing you where Waystones within the zone can be found to make your post-wipe life easier.
