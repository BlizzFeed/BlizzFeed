#### Here are this week's highlights:

##### Dragonflight

- **Loot from the Zaqali Elders:** The loot from this world boss encounter appears to be [dropping as expected](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/world-boss-zaqali-elders-bug/1587890/29); however, it doesn’t show in the chat logs due to how the loot is distributed in an encounter with multiple NPCs.
- **Latest Fractures in Time Update:** On Wednesday, the latest [PTR update](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/dragonflight-fractures-in-time-ptr-development-notes/1587226/6) was released for Dragonflight Fractures in Time. This update includes the new Mega-Dungeon: Dawn of the Infinite and class tuning changes.

##### Wrath Classic

- **Arena Points Fixed:** After investigating, a [fix was deployed](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/0523-0529-arena-points-given-as-honor/1602967/66) so players affected by this issue could log in to get their missing Arena Points.
