### Go Lorewalking With the Help of Lorewalker Cho

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_header/fy/FY0A5R69R5OY1748978786457.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_header/fy/FY0A5R69R5OY1748978786457.png)

Meet with Lorewalker Cho in Stormwind, Orgrimmar, or Dornogal to experience iconic lore moments in Azeroth in the Legacy of Arathor content update.

Players level 10 and above can explore three storylines carefully curated by Lorewalker Cho to learn more about Xal’atath, the Ethereals, and The Lich King. Each storyline consists of previous quests and a few new ones.

More details on iconic Lorewalking moments await you in [our article](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/news/24210000).

### It’s Electric: Power Up with Overcharged Delves!

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_header/am/AMR95Q1PDJ1H1749081921026.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_header/am/AMR95Q1PDJ1H1749081921026.png)

Power up with Titan machinery to explore new depths and face new challenges within six Delves throughout Khaz Algar in the Legacy of Arathor content update. You’ll be able to amp up the challenge with a new Delve-specific affix and earn some shiny loot.

- Fungal Folly: Isle of Dorn
- Kriegval’s Rest: Isle of Dorn
- Nightfall Sanctum: Hallowfall
- Skittering Breach: Hallowfall
- The Spiral Weave: Azj’Kahet
- Sidestreet Sluice: Undermine

Amp up the challenge with a new Delve-specific affix and earn some shiny loot. Two Bountiful Delves each day will feature the Overcharged Affix, though Overcharged Delves can be completed as many times as you like. These Delves are easily identifiable on the map with a little extra spark added to the icon.

To get started, you can pick up the first of a short quest chain— **Titanic Rumblings**— from your Adventure Journal, then continue by speaking with Dagran Thaurissan II in Dornogal for the **Titan Consoles** quest to unlock access to the Overcharged Console.

We’re ecSTATIC to send you and Brann into this new challenge. Get charged up with more details from [our article](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/news/24206305).

### Presenting the Dastardly Duos Creator Clash Coming June 7!

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/o2/O23UEWHEY2RA1748651306546.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/o2/O23UEWHEY2RA1748651306546.png)

Take your seat and get ready to witness the chaos! Introducing the Dastardly Duos Creator Clash, a special showcase pitting Azeroth's most headstrong heroes against one another in a cage-match showdown for the ages! Catch this special event live on [Twitch](https://www.twitch.tv/warcraft) and [YouTube](https://youtube.com/warcraft) on Saturday, June 7, starting at 10:00 am PDT / 7:00 pm CEST.

The Dastardly Duos Creator Clash is a single-day showcase bringing the fiercest fighters from Echo and Liquid to face off in the Undermine's Demolition Dome, battling against villainous menaces from Azeroth's past to earn the most points and be crowned the Warcraft-Weight World Champions!

For more information about the Dastardly Duos Creator Clash, check out the  [broadcast overview](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/news/24208339)!
