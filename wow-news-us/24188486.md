# Announcing the World of Warcraft Student Art Contest 2024 Winners!

- URL: https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/en-us/news/24188486/announcing-the-world-of-warcraft-student-art-contest-2024-winners
![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/x5/X57W00K1CMJU1742490617181.jpg)

> The 2024 Student Art Contest has come to an end, and after intense scrutiny and some impassioned deliberation, we’ve identified this year’s winners.

---

Presented by Blizzard Entertainment’s Early Careers and World of Warcraft development teams, the 14th annual [World of Warcraft Student Art Contest](https://careers.blizzard.com/global/en/student-contest-2024) has drawn to a close. After some fervent debate and impassioned deliberation, our judges sifted through the many remarkable entries and identified the winning entries.

Check out the winners and runners-up below for each of the four categories.

---
