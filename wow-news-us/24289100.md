> Forge a mount that’s uniquely yours. The Stoneforged Sentinel is ready to take shape with over 300,000 possible customization combinations, letting every champion tailor a distinct flying companion reflecting their own style.

---

Chiseled from enduring stone and awakened by ancient power, the Stoneforged Sentinel mount is yours to shape.\* With a wide array of customization options—over 300,000 unique mount variations—you can craft a truly personal flying guardian unlike any other.

Visit any Rostrum of Transformation to customize your Stoneforged Sentinel. Rostrum locations can be found in multiple cities and camps. Look for the gold dragon head on your minimap or ask a city guard for directions.

Stoneforged Sentinel customization options include:

- 4 x Stone Colors
- 4 x Metal Colors
- 5 x Gem Colors
- 3 x Saddle Styles
- 6 x Head Styles
- 6 x Wing Styles
- 6 x Claw Styles
- 6 x Tail Styles

**[Visit the Shop](https://shop.battle.net/family/world-of-warcraft/items/2065183)**

Use of the Stoneforged Sentinel mount is shared across all present and future characters in modern *World of Warcraft®* (currently *Midnight™*). The mount automatically scales to the fastest riding skill known by each character, and if a character’s level isn’t high enough to use a flying mount, the Stoneforged Sentinel makes a sturdy ground mount.
