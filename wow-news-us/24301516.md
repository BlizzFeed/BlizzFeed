# Gather Around the Campfire in World of Warcraft: Forever

- URL: https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/en-us/news/24301516/gather-around-the-campfire-in-world-of-warcraft-forever
![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/b0/B0HPKSFCFCDC1792455657405.png)

> The Camping system in World of Warcraft: Forever lets players create shared campsites where they can rest, craft, and gain useful benefits from profession-made features. Beginning with the Cooking profession’s Basic Campfire, the system gives adventurers new reasons to prepare together and connect while traveling through Azeroth.

---

There is something special about seeing another adventurer on the road. Maybe they are headed toward the same quest objective, recovering after a close fight, or simply taking the long way through a dangerous zone. In *World of Warcraft: Forever*, the Camping system gives those moments more room to grow by turning a simple Cooking Fire into a place where players can rest, prepare, and connect.

The system expands the Cooking profession’s simple Cooking Fire into a Basic Campfire. Once placed, the Basic Campfire creates a shared campsite where nearby players can rest and gain benefits from profession-made camp features before setting out again. Those reasons to linger can turn a roadside pause into a helpful encounter, a trading opportunity, or the start of new friendships.

Camp Features give professions a more visible role in the world**.** A Basic Campfire can support up to three special crafted objects contributed by nearby players, with each player able to add one. Each profession brings its own form of utility, such as a blacksmith placing a Sharpening Wheel that increases Strength for an hour, a tailor placing a Faction Banner that increases Spirit, or an herbalist placing an Incense Candle that increases Intellect. These effects are variants of buffs normally provided by specific classes and are exclusive with them.

Players sitting or crafting at a campsite for at least 1 minute will gain the benefits from other camp features. This provides an opportunity to get to know your fellow adventurers, and perhaps embark on a journey together.

---

## How Camping and Profession Camp Features Work

Early in your adventures, a simple quest introduces the Camping system and shows how a campfire can become a useful gathering place for players out in the world. Once a profession reaches skill level 20, you can acquire its first campsite items through starter zone quests or purchase them from a profession trainer. Items that require skill level 140 or higher are found through drops in dungeons and raids.

### About Professions

> **Secondary Professions**
>
> You can learn all three secondary professions: Cooking, Fishing, and First Aid.
>
> **Primary Professions**
>
> You can learn two primary professions. These include the gathering professions Skinning, Mining, and Herbalism, as well as the crafting professions Alchemy, Blacksmithing, Enchanting, Engineering, Leatherworking, and Tailoring.

To learn a profession, visit a trainer in the world or in a major city. To unlearn one, open the **Professions** tab (K) or select it from the menu bar at the bottom of the screen, then click the “X” next to the profession you want to remove. Profession ranks progress from Apprentice to Journeyman, Expert, and finally Artisan.

The **Professions** tab also shows available recipes and blueprints, their required materials, and whether crafting an item will increase your skill.

The table below lists a sample of each profession’s camp features, required skill levels, and benefits. All Camping features require a campfire to be nearby and share a cooldown of 1 hour.

| **Profession** | **Item** | **Skill Level** | **Details** |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Cooking** | Basic Campfire | 1 | Builds a **Basic Campfire** that allows cooking and the placement of up to 3 additional camp features. Players sitting or crafting nearby for 1 minute will gain benefits from other camp features |
| **First Aid** | First Aid Kit | 20 | Unpacks a **First Aid Kit** that allows you and others sitting nearby to gain 3-56 increased Stamina (level dependent), exclusive with **Power Word: Fortitude**. |
| **Fishing** | Fishing Bowl | 20 | Places a small **Fishing Bowl** that allows you and others sitting nearby to gain 8% increased stats, exclusive with **Blessing of Kings**. |
| **Alchemy** | Mana Well | 20 | Constructs a **Mana Well** that allows you and others sitting nearby to regenerate 10-29 Mana every 5 seconds (level dependent), mutually exclusive with **Blessing of Wisdom**. |
| **Blacksmithing** | Sharpening Wheel | 20 | Constructs a **Sharpening Wheel** that grants you and others sitting nearby 6-34 increased Strength (level dependent), mutually exclusive with **Strength of Earth Totem.** |
| **Enchanting** | Enchanted Lute | 20 | Summons an **Enchanted Lute** that allows you and others sitting nearby to gain 28-260 Armor, 0-13 increase to all stats, and 0-22 increase to all resistances (level dependent), mutually exclusive with **Mark of the Wild**. |
| **Engineering** | Reagent Bot | 20 | Summons a **Reagent Bot** that allows you and others nearby to purchase reagents. |
| **Herbalism** | Incense Candle | 20 | Ignites an **Incense Candle** that allows you and others sitting nearby to gain 2-25 increased Intellect (level dependent), mutually exclusive with Arcane Intellect. |
| **Leatherworking** | Camp Tent | 20 | Builds a **Camp Tent** that allows you and others sitting nearby to increase Rested experience by 5% of a level. No effect if Rested experience already exceeds that value. |
| **Skinning** | Camp Chair | 20 | Assembles a **Camp Chair** that allows you and others sitting nearby to gain 2% increased critical strike chance with all spells and attacks, exclusive with **Moonkin Aura**. |
| **Tailoring** | Faction Banner | 20 | Unrolls a **Faction Banner** that allows you and other members of your faction sitting nearby to gain 14-32 increased Spirit (level dependent), mutually exclusive with **Divine Spirit**. |

Cooks can eventually learn upgraded Campfires that allow five or even 10 objects to be placed at a camp, while other professions unlock helpful conveniences such as Alchemy Laboratories and Tanning Racks.

To receive campsite buffs, players need to sit by the campfire briefly and become rested before returning to the road. That pause makes rest, preparation, and community part of the journey, supporting a playstyle built around traveling at your own pace instead of rushing between capital cities and your objectives.

---

Choose your campsite carefully. Some places in Azeroth are better suited to a quiet rest than others, and danger can still find you when you stop. On a PvP Ruleset, that risk increases when enemy players come upon your camp.

Whether you are repairing gear, preparing your group, or sharing a fire with passing adventurers, Camping offers another way to live in Azeroth—not just move through it. We’ll see you by the campfire!
