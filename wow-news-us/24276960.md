## BCC Anniversary Edition: Overlords of Outland Now Live!

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_header/1TUY2D1QHA5B1631231409250.jpg)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_header/1TUY2D1QHA5B1631231409250.jpg)

Lady Vashj and Kael'thas Sunstrider, the cunning Overlords of Outland, have long served Illidan Stormrage, the Betrayer. As the battle for Outland heats up, it's time to bring their wretched rule to an end. Take the fight to Illidan Stormrage's lieutenants, brave two new raids, compete in Arena Season 2, and pursue new rewards across the shattered world of Outland.

- **New raids**: Tempest Keep: The Eye (Kael'thas Sunstrider) and Serpentshrine Cavern (Lady Vashj)
- **Arena Season 2**: New rewards and updated progression.
- **Swift Flight Form**: Druid questline (including the Idol of the Raven Goddess) culminating in a showdown with Anzu the Raven God in Heroic Sethekk Halls
- **New daily factions**: Earn rewards with Ogri'la and the Sha'tari Skyguard
- **New profession recipes**: Engineering epic goggles and new Alchemy discoveries (including raid-friendly cauldrons)

The overlords of Outland await you, champions. Ready your weapons and rally your allies as you continue to defend Azeroth from destruction. For more details, read through our [previously published](http://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/news/24276751) overview.

## Mists of Pandaria Classic: The Siege of Orgrimmar Arrives June 2

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_header/mj/MJUA05XTI0231778615821624.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_header/mj/MJUA05XTI0231778615821624.png)

Azeroth's heroes have stood against the Thunder King, yet a darker storm now gathers in Garrosh Hellscream. With the Vale of Eternal Blossoms corrupted and Orgrimmar in chaos, the Warchief must be stopped before all are dragged into darkness.

**When:** June 2, with regional maintenance.

Get ready for new content and features, including a new zone—Timeless Isle, a new multi-part raid—Siege of Orgrimmar, five new World Bosses, the final push for the Legendary Cloak, Proving Grounds, and more!

- **New Zone**: Uncover the Mysterious Timeless Isle and Claim Its Treasures
- **New Faction**: Earn Reputation with Emperor Shaohao for New Rewards
- **Legendary Cloak**: Claim Your Legendary Cloak as the Questline Comes to an End
- **New Raid**: Put an End to Garrosh in the Siege of Orgrimmar Raid
- **Five New World Bosses**: Travel to the Timeless Isle to Test Your Mettle Against World Bosses
- **PvP Season 14**: Claim New Armor and Rewards
- **Celestial Dungeon Season 3**: Bear Witness in Season 3's Celestial Dungeons With Powerful Buffs
- **Season 3 Challenge Mode Dungeons: Diamond Mode**: Chase Glory in Season 3 Challenge Mode Dungeons
- **Proving Grounds**: Enter the Proving Grounds for Solo Role-Based Challenges

Learn more about what's coming in the final chapter of the Pandaren campaign from our [Siege of Orgrimmar overview](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/news/24264421). The fate of Azeroth awaits on the Timeless Isle, and within the walls of Orgrimmar.

## Play Midnight for a Classic Anniversary Edition Flying Mount

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_header/3f/3FSI0HCBB7SM1773436207775.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_header/3f/3FSI0HCBB7SM1773436207775.png)

Earn the menacing Voidfeather Dragonhawk flying mount to carry you through the fel-scarred realm of Outland in *Burning Crusade Classic Anniversary Edition* after completing the introduction scenario in *World of Warcraft: Midnight*.

**When**: March 17 through May 17

Quel'Thalas is under siege! Complete the intro scenario of *Midnight*, pitting you against Void forces during their invasion of the home of the Blood Elves, and earn the "Echoes of Midnight" achievement before the promotion period ends to get the Voidfeather Dragonhawk flying mount for use in your *Burning Crusade Classic Anniversary* adventures.

Details on how you can obtain this mount for the Burning Crusade Classic Anniversary characters, including how you can get to the Midnight scenario, can be found in our [previously published article](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/news/24267942).
