## Classic Race Change Packs\*

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/hu/HU5B08MBA4M51758239018153.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/hu/HU5B08MBA4M51758239018153.png)

Sometimes, a simple touch-up of your 'character's looks isn't enough; maybe you want to go all the way and change which race you belong to! And with a Race Change service, you can do just that.

To change multiple characters' races, purchase Race Change packs and use them all at once or save some for later. The final step of a Race Change requires choosing a new appearance and includes an optional Name Change.

Available Race Change pack options:

- 1 for $17.50 (Regularly $25.00)
- 3 for $35.00 (Regularly $50.00)
- 6 for $56.00 (Regularly $80.00)

The Classic Race Change service is not available for classes with only one race option within a faction—such as Druids, Blood Elf Paladins, or Draenei Shaman. Also, the Classic Race Change is not available for *WoW Classic Era* and cannot be used on a character that completed a race or faction change within the past 30 days.

To learn more about the Race Change service and any restrictions, visit [our support page](https://battle.net/support/article/23684).
