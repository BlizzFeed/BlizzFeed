# Get a Look at the Legacy of Arathor

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_header/j7/J79MC2LZBGCH1746055759658.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_header/j7/J79MC2LZBGCH1746055759658.png)

Take your adventures further in the Legacy of Arathor, our next content update. You’ll amp up the challenge in Overcharged Delves, get a helping hand with the new Combat Assistant and Single-Button Assistant, and more.

- **New Arathi Highlands Campaign**
  - Join Faerin and Danath as they attempt to quell the rising tides of hostility in a new multi-quest campaign.
- **Go Lorewalking with Lorewalker Cho**
  - Step back into some iconic lore moments in Azeroth with both previous quests and a few new ones.
- **Overcharged Delves with Additional New Rewards**
  - We’re introducing a new Delve-specific affix to six Delves to push you to new feats of heroism.
- **Get a Helping Hand with Combat Assistant and Single-Button Assistant**
  - Two new features will be introduced to aid in lowering the barrier of entry for new and returning players.
- And more!

Everything you need to know about 11.1.7 can be found in our [article](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/news/24199286), with more details on the timing for these events in the coming weeks. In the meantime, you can head into the Public Test Realm to check them out.

# Mythic Dungeon International is Now Live!

[![Video](https://markdown-videos-api.jorgenkh.no/youtube/ui98lNylzBA?width=1280&height=720)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ui98lNylzBA)

Spread the word and gather at the Gallagio—it’s time to watch Azeroth’s most daunting dungeoneers redefine reality and shatter world records! The premiere dungeon competition has returned in the latest season of *The War Within* and has followed our competitors into the depths of the Undermine.

For three weeks, hopeful heroes will compete in the MDI Groups for gold, glory, and their spot in the Global Finals. With $300,000 and the Championship title up for grabs, these teams will surely bring their best to the weekend!

- **Venue:** Online: [Twitch.tv/Warcraft](https://www.twitch.tv/warcraft) and [YouTube.com/Warcraft](https://www.youtube.com/warcraft)
- **Co-streaming:** *Check with your favorite competitor to see if they plan on sharing their perspective during the tournament, or gather your own audience and host a watch part of your own! Sign up for co-streaming using the [MDI Watch Party Form](https://blizz.ly/CostreamMDI).*
- **Dates:**
  - **Group A**: May 2– 4
  - **Group B**: May 9– 11
  - **Group C**: May 16–18
  - **Global Finals**: May 30– June 1
- **Broadcast Start Time:** 10:00 am (PDT) / 7:00 pm (CEST)
- **Groups Format:**Four teams push keys simultaneously each day, and the top two from each day compete on day three

Everything you need to know can be found in our [viewer guide](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/news/24196001).

# Welcome to Critter Country During May’s Trading Post

May’s Trading Post will bring you joy with new pets to add to your collection and more. This month, welcome Weechi the customizable pet to your collection. You can dress this adorable little buddy in several available costumes.

![ ](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/TCFDIHRZBZB81745874362501.png)

**Weechi**\
*“Weechi adores attention and what better way to get attention than wearing a costume?”*

Complete activities to fill the bar at the top of the**Traveler’s Log** found in the **Adventure Guide (Shift-J)**, and you'll receive this month's bonus reward—Weechi, the customizable pet. Dress this companion up in a variety of adorable costumes by visiting a Pet Grooming Station near the Trading Post to change up the look!

Visit T&W (Tawney and Wilder) just outside the Mage District in Stormwind or the Zen’shiri Trading Post next to Grommash Hold in Orgrimmar to check out all the latest items they have available.

Players can also visit the Trading Post in the capital city of Dornogal (The Foregrounds) on the Isle of Dorn, where representatives of both Tawney and Wilder and the Zen’shiri Trading Post are open for business.
