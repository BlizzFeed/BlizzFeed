# Get a Slew of Hearthstone Goodies and More with the Purchase of a 6-Month Subscription!

- URL: https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/en-us/news/23716425/get-a-slew-of-hearthstone-goodies-and-more-with-the-purchase-of-a-6-month-subscription
![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/6a/6ACN1WFNQKSM1630379854070.png)

> For a limited time, you can get a slew of Hearthstone goodies, the brilliant Sapphire Skyblazer mount, and the Imp in a Ball toy, with the purchase of the 6-Month subscription to World of Warcraft.

---

For a limited time\*, you can get a slew of *Hearthstone* goodies when you purchase a *World of Warcraft* 6-Month Subscription from the Blizzard Shop.

**What you Get:**

Once you’ve purchased your subscription, you’ll receive 15 card packs to *United in Stormwind*, a Tavern Pass, and Battleground Perks. You’ll receive an additional 15 Mercenaries card packs with the release of Mercenaries on October 12.

As an additional bonus, you’ll also receive 15 card packs from a future *Hearthstone* expansion along with 15 Mercenaries card packs, a Tavern Pass, and Battleground perks at a future date.

![Image of Hearthstone Chest and United in Stormwind Card Back](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/48/48Q12CXGI4DL1630376430709.png)

For those who already own a 6-Month subscription, please visit our [support article](https://support.blizzard.com/article/000296393)to learn more about your eligibility to claim these *Hearthstone* goodies.

---

#### Like a Blue Streak

*It’s not quite certain why Sapphire Skyblazers flocked to Azeroth, but what’s known for sure is that they burn brighter (and hotter!) than any avian to ever soar across the skies. Fast as lightning and armored to the beak, this is a perfect mount to accompany you in your daring adventures.*

[![Video](https://markdown-videos-api.jorgenkh.no/youtube/6esNqTmPhdQ?width=1280&height=720)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6esNqTmPhdQ)

Once you’ve purchased the [**6-Month** subscription](https://shop.battle.net/en-us/product/world-of-warcraft-subscription) from the Blizzard Shop, the Sapphire Skyblazer mount\*\* will be automatically added to your in-game collection. Welcome this avian addition to your assortment of mounts!

#### Imp in a Ball

![Imp in a Ball Toy](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/f1/F1MC0LQCATZ71621307297738.jpg)

Players who purchase a recurring 6-month subscription will not only get the *Hearthstone* goodies and Sapphire Skyblazer mount for *World of Warcraft*, but also receive the Imp in a Ball toy\*\*\* to use in *Burning Crusade Classic*. This fel-infused toy can tell your fortune as you make your way through the shattered lands of Outland!

Please visit our [support article](https://support.blizzard.com/article/000286922) to learn more about claiming this toy.
