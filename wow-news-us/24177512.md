Long before Renzik the Shiv came to Stormwind, he was the bloody fist of the Krackslagger Cartel. But when his grim work led him to cross daggers with an Alliance spy, Renzik was forced to look back on the lifetime of brutality he carved out of the Undermine and the future laid before him.

Join Mathias Shaw for a special fireside telling of our newest short story, "The Tipping Point."
