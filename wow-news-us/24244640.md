# The Arena World Championship and Mythic Dungeon International Grand Finals are here!

- URL: https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/en-us/news/24244640/the-arena-world-championship-and-mythic-dungeon-international-grand-finals-are-here
![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/we/WEHRG97ES5GI1762567826843.png)

> Get ready for the ultimate showdown as the Arena World Championship (AWC) and Mythic Dungeon International (MDI) reach their electrifying finales—where legends are made and champions crowned! The world’s fiercest gladiators and dungeoneers will clash for glory, record-breaking feats, and a share of over $500,000 USD in prizes.

---

It’s been an enthralling season of WoW esports, and both the Arena World Championship (AWC) and the Mythic Dungeon International (MDI) are preparing to round out the expansion with an exciting conclusion. The two pinnacle programs have spanned several weeks of competitions, featuring the world’s most devoted *World of Warcraft* players challenging one another to reach new heights in some of the most intense tournaments yet. Now that the groups and cups have drawn to a close, it’s time to prepare for the epic finales in the realms of PvP and PvE alike.

---

### Tune in for the Mythic Dungeon International November 21-23!

The MDI is also approaching its epic end for the season! In the Global Finals, eight teams of the world’s most dedicated dungeoneers will face off for their share of the $300,000 prize pool and the title of worldwide champions. Tune in to the competition live on official [Warcraft Twitch](http://Twitch.tv/Warcraft) and [YouTube](http://Youtube.com/Warcraft) channels from November 21 to November 23!

- **Dates**: November 21-23 starting at 9.45 a.m. PST
- **Format**: Keystone Pushing Format, Highest Keys Pushed Wins
- **Competitors**: Find the full list of qualified teams on [Raider.IO](https://raider.io/events/mdi-the-war-within-season-3/the-great-push-leaderboard/global-finals)
- **Prize Pool**: $300,000 USD
- **Drops**: Earn the Ensemble: Violet Sweatsuit Transmog set by watching MDI! [More information is available in this blog post](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/en-us/news/24246288/new-twitch-drop-ahead-get-the-ensemble-violet-sweatsuit-transmog-set-11-november).
- **Streams**: <Twitch.tv/Warcraft> and <YouTube.com/Warcraft>
- **Co-streaming**: Interested in co-streaming the MDI Global Finals? Please fill out [this form](https://blizz.ly/CostreamMDI) ahead of the event.
- **Casters:**Dratnos, Meeix, Naguura, Rookuri, Tettles, Xyronic

### Watch the Arena World Championship Live November 14-16!

The AWC is nearing its finale! The exciting end of the season starts with a fierce single-elimination bracket, where groups of gladiators enter the gauntlet with the hopes of securing the final spot in the Grand Finals. From there, four teams of the greatest gladiators in each region will compete in a spectacular showdown for the championship title and their share of the $200,000 (USD) prize pool. Catch the action live on the official [Warcraft Twitch](http://Twitch.tv/Warcraft) and [YouTube](http://Youtube.com/Warcraft) channels from November 14 to November 16!

- **Dates**: November 14-16 starting at 10 a.m. PST
- **Format**: Single-Elimination Gauntlet into Double-Elimination Grand Finals
- **Teams & Brackets:** Follow all the teams and Grand Finals brackets live on [Raider.IO](https://raider.io/events/awc-the-war-within-season-3-grand-finals/brackets/)
- **Prize Pool**: $220,000 USD
- **Drops**: Earn the Ensemble: Violet Sweatsuit Transmog set by watching AWC! [More information is available in this blog post](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/en-us/news/24246288/new-twitch-drop-ahead-get-the-ensemble-violet-sweatsuit-transmog-set-11-november).
- **Streams**: <Twitch.tv/Warcraft> and <YouTube.com/Warcraft>
  - **Co-streaming**: Interested in co-streaming the AWC Grand Finals? Please fill out [this form](https://blizz.ly/CostreamAWC) ahead of the event.
- **Casters:**Eiya, Supatease, Venruki, Ziqo
  - **Guest Talent:**Azael, Dmachine
