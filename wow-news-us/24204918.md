# Rev up Your Adventures with Turbo Boost—Now Live!

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/6U7T83N5WN8Y1739219892263.jpeg)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/6U7T83N5WN8Y1739219892263.jpeg)

We’re turning things up a notch during The War Within Season 2 with increased rewards and improvements to help you gear up your Warband with Turbo Boost.

During this event, Hero and Myth upgrade tracks will extend 2 upgrades (from 6 to 8), the cap for Crests is lifted, and Valorstones become Warbound for the remainder of the season. Mythic+ drop rates for Warband until Equipped gear are also being doubled.

- Take up one of two new weekly quests—**Turbo-Boost: Powerhouse Challenges** or **Ultra Prime Deluxe Turbo-Boost: Powerhouse Challenges**, and earn 3 Puzzling Cartel Chips upon the first completion of the quest and one chip per week for subsequent weeks. You can earn a maximum of 9 Puzzling Cartel Chips per character.
- Gear Vendor—grab carefully curated items from the Liberation of Undermine raid, including weapons, trinkets, and other special items. Head over to our [overview](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/en-us/news/24201310/rev-up-your-adventures-with-turbo-boost-may-13#Raiditems) for a complete list.
- Delve Improvements—Get a leg up on adventure with a boost to Brann’s experience, Delver’s Journey progress, and improvements to rewards from the Delver’s Bounty.
- And more!

Everything you need to know about Turbo Boost can be found in our [article](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/news/24201310). We’ll see you in Azeroth!

# Last Chance: The Winds of Mysterious Fortune Blow Away May 20

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_header/gl/GLWXHNV233VJ1745271983228.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_header/gl/GLWXHNV233VJ1745271983228.png)

The winds of fortune blow in your favor during this event. While questing and adventuring in certain *Dragonflight* and *The War Within* zones, players below level 80 can gain **Mysterious Satchels** bursting with goodies while looting creatures or as a bonus reward from completing quests.

Also, take advantage of the Winds of Mysterious Fortune 20% experience buff to get characters level 10–79 up to speed. Catch up on your alts, finish missed content and unlocks, and dive into the latest content in *The War Within*.

Read [our article](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/news/24188493/) to learn all the details about the Winds of Mysterious Fortune event.
