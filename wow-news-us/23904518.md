# Arena World Championship Lands in Dragonflight!

- URL: https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/en-us/news/23904518/arena-world-championship-lands-in-dragonflight
![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/mw/MWH5WA3HKOSJ1673630880639.png)

> The Arena World Championship is making its premiere in Dragonflight and kicking off the new year of WoW Esports!

---

*Beginning January 20-22, the gates will open on the 2023 Dragonflight Season 1 Arena World Championship (AWC)! . Tune in to all four Cups to see which teams will qualify to continue in the tournament, on* [*Twitch*](https://www.twitch.tv/warcraft) *and* [*YouTube*](http://youtube.com/warcraft)*.*

If the competition wasn’t already exciting enough, these Cups will present a plethora of new additions to the Arena World Championship, including both The Nokhudon Proving Grounds and Evokers! Competitors will battle for the title of both the champion of their series and the clan’s greatest warrior in The Nokhudon Proving Grounds, while tackling the tricky terrain that includes ramps, pillars, and a middle wall. Evokers are ready to prove themselves, as these scale-coated-competitors enter the AWC for the first time!
