## Steep Your Adventure in the Marvelous Meeksi

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/AOBPN0PDX5731740513689767.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/AOBPN0PDX5731740513689767.png)

Find your cup of tea and take a sip of magic with the Marvelous Meeksi Pack that includes five ***brewteaful*** dynamic panda mounts to carry you through the skies or on the ground. Pack is available for a limited time through April 28.

All Meeksi mounts are flying mounts shared across all present and future WoW® Modern characters and automatically scale to the fastest riding skill known by each character. If players aren’t at a high enough level to use a flying mount, Meeksi’s make an excellent ground mount.

The Meeksi Teatuft mount (red) is also available as a standalone purchase, and you can have plen***tea*** of fun and elevate your journey into a magical experience—carrying delicious teas and delivering joy and warmth with adorable aplomb. The price of the Marvelous Meeksi Pack will automatically adjust if you already have it.

## Dash, Dodge, and Dive During Kalimdor Cup

The Riders of Azeroth invite skyriders to compete in races throughout Kalimdor!

**When:** February 25 – March 10\
**Where:** Begins in Valdrakken

With a total of sixteen races spread across Kalimdor, riders will have a sky full of options for challenging courses in Normal, Advanced, and Reverse variations. Head to Valdrakken and speak to Lord Andestrasz near the Rostrum of Transformation to pick up the initial quest.

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_header/nh/NH8D31KYNGJZ1691778094114.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_header/nh/NH8D31KYNGJZ1691778094114.png)

Kalimdor’s brave racers can earn achievements and **Riders of Azeroth Badges** for completing races. Exchange your badges for unique items and rewards from Maztha in Valdrakken so that you can dash through the skies with panache. Nothing else to collect? Trade your badges in for **Valdrakken Accord Insignias** to gain reputation with the Valdrakken Accord.

The sky’s the limit (unless you’re trying to beat the timer); may the winds be at your back as you go for the gold.
