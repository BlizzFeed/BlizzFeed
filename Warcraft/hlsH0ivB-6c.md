# “Al’Aketh Rising” Motion Comic | World of Warcraft: Forever

Follow Constable Aonda of the Skyborne in the World of Warcraft: Forever Comic, “Al'Aketh Rising”

In a land ruled by wind, blind devotion can become a storm. 

A local constable travels into a remote settlement that appears to be under strict control. 

Prisoners march under close guard, strangers smile with an eerie conviction, and an uncomfortable silence provides cover for a growing threat. As she plays along to get closer to the truth, she finds herself overwhelmed by hidden agendas and fanatic devotion. 

Read by Sam Slade as Constable Aonda
Written by Robert Brooks
Art & Cover by Stefano Porcu
Colors by Mauro Gulma
Letters by AndWorld Design
