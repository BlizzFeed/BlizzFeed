# Skyborne Lore & Abilities | World of Warcraft: Forever

First, these High Elven exiles fled Kalimdor under the threat of certain death. The protection of powerful wind spirits provided refuge…for a time. 

After their benefactors vanished, the Skyborne have begun seeking out a new path. One that takes them out of hiding and into greater Azeroth.
