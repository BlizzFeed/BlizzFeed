# Heroes Brawl of the Week, October 12, 2018: Industrial District

> This week’s Heroes Brawl is an Overwatch-inspired single-lane slugfest: Industrial District!

---

This week’s Heroes Brawl is Industrial District! Slide around and slug it out in the heart of Volskaya in this Overwatch-themed, single-lane Battleground featuring lots of conveyor belts and tons of action.

---

### Rules:

- Shuffle pick - Choose from one of three Heroes before entering the battle.
- Standard play - no talent or level restrictions.
- The first team to destroy the enemy Core wins!

### Rewards:

- Complete two matches of the Industrial District Brawl to earn a **Loot Chest!**

---

Find out more about the Heroes Brawl game mode on our [Heroes Brawl site](http://us.battle.net/heroes/en/game/heroes-brawl/); and as always, you can find more information on this week’s Brawl by clicking the Brawl Info button at the bottom of the play screen when preparing to queue for the Brawl game mode.
