# Blog Comments to be Disabled

- URL: https://news.blizzard.com/en-gb/article/21656680/blog-comments-to-be-disabled
![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/fa/FAFJEGXGTI941523997985733.jpg)

> Blog comments will soon be disabled on the Heroes of the Storm website. Read within for more info.

---

In the coming days, we’re planning to implement some new infrastructure that will greatly improve our back-end support for blogs on the Heroes of the Storm website. Unfortunately, this technology isn’t compatible with our aging blog comment system, and it will take significant resources and developer time to ensure comments will remain functional into the future. After much deliberation, we’ve decided to remove the comment system from Heroes blogs.

Removing blog comments allows us to move forward with ongoing back-end website improvements, and also offers us an opportunity to consolidate even more of your conversations into the [official Heroes of the Storm forums](https://battle.net/forums/heroes/index). The forums serve as one of the primary sources of constructive feedback and discussion about the game for the development team. They’re also a great place to discuss news and announcements, as well as connect with other players in the Heroes community. We hope you’ll continue to share your thoughts with us there, because your feedback is important to us as we continue to make Heroes of the Storm better than ever.

**Until Next time, we'll see you in the Nexus!**
