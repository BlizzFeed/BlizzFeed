#### Abilities

As a mighty dragon aspect, Deathwing has the power to switch between two forms in battle: Destroyer form, which grants him access to the Incinerate and Onslaught abilities, and World Breaker form, which allows him to use the Lava Burst and Earth Shatter abilities.

Also, those enormous, powerful wings aren't just for show; if the need arises, Deathwing can soar high into the sky, replenishing his health as he surveys the battlefield from above the carnage. When he's ready to rejoin the action, Deathwing can slam into the ground, dealing damage and assuming one of this two forms.

TRAIT: Aspect of Death

\
Deathwing is fitted with 4 Armor Plates which grant 10 Armor each. Every 25% Health lost causes Deathwing to lose a plate. Plates are only restored when **Dragonflight** is used, based on the amount of health Deathwing has when landing.\
\
Deathwing is also a mighty Dragon Aspect who is permanently Unstoppable and deals 100% additional damage to Heroes, but views his allies with contempt and is immune to any of their friendly effects.\
\
*click to switch forms*

Dragonflight

\
After 3 seconds, fly into the sky, becoming immune to all effects and gaining 2.5% Health per second. When Deathwing deals or takes damage, Dragonflight is put on a 4 second cooldown.

Molten Flame

\
Breathe a stream of flame, dealing damage every second. Drains energy each second.

Incinerate

\
Deathwing flaps his wings, dealing damage to nearby enemies.

Onslaught

\
Lunge in a direction dealing damage to enemies and slowing them. Deathwing bites enemies at the end of the lunge, dealing additional damage.

HEROIC ABILITY: Cataclysm

\
Deathwing flies across the target path, dealing damage on impact and leaving scorched ground that persists for a short time, damaging enemies.

Lava Burst

\
After a short delay, deal damage to all enemies in the area and create a pool of magma that persists for a short time, damaging and slowing enemies.

Earth Shatter

\
Create a fissure that deals damage to enemies and stuns them.

Destroyer

\
**While airborne:** Slam down to the ground, dealing damage to enemies in the area and gaining access to Destroyer abilities: **Incinerate** and **Onslaught**.\
\
*click to switch to Destroyer form*

World Breaker

\
**While airborne:** Slam down to the ground, dealing damage to enemies in the area and gaining access to World Breaker abilities: **Lava Burst** and **Earth Shatter**.\
\
*click to switch to World Breaker form*
