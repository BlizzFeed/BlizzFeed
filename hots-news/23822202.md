> Everything is better with friends—Heroes included. Learn more about the best places to find new teammates and chat with fellow community members.

Connect with fellow mains of your eccentric one-trick hero pick, search for some laid-back teammates to queue with, or find the fated other half of your dream duo in the Heroes of the Storm community. Regardless of what brought you here, other Heroes players will welcome you with dependable advice and exquisite memes.

Check out the sites below to start connecting with fellow community members:

- [Official Forums](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/heroes/)
- [Reddit](https://www.reddit.com/r/heroesofthestorm/)
- [Discord](https://discord.com/invite/heroesofthestorm#discord)
- [Twitter](https://twitter.com/blizzheroes)
- [Twitch](https://www.twitch.tv/directory/game/Heroes%20of%20the%20Storm)
