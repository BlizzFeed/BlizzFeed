We’ll get into how we are approaching design for Heroes of the Storm, both now and into the future, but first, let’s take a closer look at our newest Hero!

**Xal’atath, Harbinger of the Void – Ranged Assassin**

A Void-wielding Ranged Assassin joins the Nexus. Xal’atath externalizes her power through a summonable object, the Dark Heart, rewarding spatial control, foresight, and extended fights: she grows more dangerous the longer a battle lasts.

[![Video](https://markdown-videos-api.jorgenkh.no/youtube/QwXQEtGlkc4?width=1280&height=720)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QwXQEtGlkc4)

A quick thank you to everyone that has been testing her out in the PTR! Due to your feedback, we updated her ability bindings from what was originally announced, with her mobility now on E to match most other Heroes.

- **Role:** Ranged Assassin
- **Difficulty:** Medium
- **Resources:** Mana (primary); Soul Essence (secondary, via her trait)

**Ability kit:**

- **Trait — Void Volley.** Basic attacks build Soul Essence; at full Essence, unleash a barrage of void **orbs** at a target area while moving freely. Tied to the Void Whispers quest, which rewards range, faster charge, piercing, and empowered void damage as it progresses.
- **Q — Shadow Mark.** Cast a large Void Orb that marks enemy Heroes hit by its inner radius; the Dark Heart moves to the impact point and detonates on marked enemy Heroes.
- **W — Dark Heart Ritual.** Summon the Dark Heart talisman to pulse void energy at nearby enemies, then follow and detonate on any Shadow Marked Hero.
- **E — Void Step.** Blink between three points in a triangular pattern, gaining attack damage at each stop; can be cancelled early to reposition or escape.
- **Heroic — Void Eruption.** Channel a growing void orb, launch it to blind enemies and dominate space, then detonate in a large area — cast on the Dark Heart for an instant, fully empowered eruption.
- **Heroic — Void Convergence.** Summon a portal that spawns relentless void minions to overwhelm nearby enemies, replacing fallen minions until the portal ends or is destroyed.

**Evolving the Nexus**

A driving philosophy for the Heroes of the Storm team is sustainability. We want to ensure we all can continue enjoying this game for a long, long time, and that requires a thoughtful approach that avoids overextending ourselves while also keeping the game feeling fresh for the community.

This started back in 2022, when the game had essentially gone into maintenance mode. As part of that transition, the team focused on optimizing the game to run better on newer systems while reducing workload on the team to keep things moving smoothly. As that work progressed, the key work the community saw was dedicated to bug fixing, spread over two patches in 2023.

In 2024, with tools and systems behind the game updated and bugs heavily reduced, the team began to focus on balance. If you [go back to those patch notes](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/heroes/t/the-patch-history/395), you’ll see a gradual increase in balance updates from patch to patch: from LiLi’s Jug of 1000 Cups healing slightly increasing to Samuro receiving updates across multiple abilities. Modes, queue changes, and events all started to receive increasing attention, all with the goal of improving the game across the board.

After about a year and a half of balance updates, we were ready to start doing bigger and bigger things. Some changes we've made include:

- Mythic Quest Rewards that provide an exciting, yet rare reward for your performance

- Hero reworks and talent updates that create new builds, stronger fantasy, and memorable moments
- Revamps and targeted updates across nearly every battleground
- The return of Haunted Mines
- Shorter respawn times, meaning less waiting and more time in the fight
- Many other changes to improve clarity and consistency

Then our 10-year anniversary arrived in 2025. Enter Gear Head Thrall, the first new cosmetic release in years. In addition to the team testing updated tools for releasing new content, this also allowed us to prove that there is a community eager for new things. The reception was incredible, and gave us the encouragement to finally dream of what we all really wanted…new Heroes.

**Design Pillars**

We spent the last year developing not only Xal’atath, but the process of creating a new Hero. All these updates, from balance changes to Xal’atath herself, have followed six key pillars.

- **Hero Fantasy:** bring each Hero’s power fantasy to life.
- **Unconventional Abilities:** lean into what makes the Nexus and its Heroes distinct.
- **Team Success:** keep the competition between rival teams, not within your own.
- **Mythical Power:** matches should offer moments where players feel uniquely powerful, brilliant, or legendary.
- **Meaningful Games:** matches are either meaningfully contestable or decisively ending.
- **Fun:** Heroes of the Storm is *fun*, and that is something we can’t ever forget.

We could write 5000 more words on all of these and exactly how we have aimed to implement them into the game, but for now we’ll spare you all (though if you want more, let us know and we’ll dive even deeper). The core of it is that we look at Heroes of the Storm as having three distinct design eras:

- **Wacky**: There were Heroes that were bold, unconventional. Characters like Abathur, Murky, Lost Vikings, and Cho’gall. They pushed the boundaries of what a Hero could be, while also immersing us in the real fantasy of these characters. For example, Cho’gall is perhaps, at first glance, potential comic relief, but their strength and power can become terrifying, and having two players in perfect sync can manifest that in a ridiculously fun way.
- **Esports**: Heavy focus on competitive optimization, matchmaking, and viewership. Many incredible matches happened during this time. We were even on national TV and one of the first major collegiate esports broadcasts. The [Classic Cup match](https://www.youtube.com/live/SNhx-G6JZb8?si=kWoGmKbf5sc-DJdp&t=9615) at BlizzCon 2026 between Dignitas and Gen.G was a brilliant showcase of what this era brought to the game.
- **Today**: In this new era, we’re focusing on the chaotic fun of HOTS that makes it different from other MOBAs: being whimsical while also making sure matches are contestable and fun, which helps lead to great competition amongst friends and strangers.

**What’s Next**

At BlizzCon, Johanna mentioned another possible Hero and we have something in the works, but aren’t ready to share details yet. Much like the journey from 2022 to now, our focus is ensuring that we are sustainable while making meaningful updates. There will be a minor balance patch or two over the next few months, during which we’ll assess how things are going and then determine potential release plans for Hero 92. We’ll keep you updated once we know more.

Toward that end, our focus right now is on Patch 57. Keep sharing your thoughts and feedback on the forums, Reddit, and social media. Your voices have kept this game we all love alive and that means more to us on the Heroes of the Storm team than we could ever truly express.

Let’s have some fun and we’ll see you in the Nexus!
