## New Hero: Xal'atath

Xal'atath has arrived in the Nexus!

**Base**

- **Shadow Mark [Q]**
  - Shoot a single Void Orb that deals 45 damage to enemies hit. Enemies hit by the center take an additional 90 damage.
  - Enemy Heroes directly hit are Marked for 4 seconds.
  - Marked Heroes are Revealed and draw the Dark Heart and Void Minions toward them.
- **Void Step [W]**
  - Cast to teleport Xal'atath 3 times in a triangular pattern.
  - Each time Xal'atath teleports, her Basic Attacks deal +25% damage while the ability is active.
  - Reactivate to end Void Step early and remain at the current location.
- **Dark Heart Ritual [E]**
  - Summon the Dark Heart, which pulses for 35 damage per second to nearby enemies for 8 seconds.
  - The Dark Heart moves to Shadow Mark's impact location.
  - The Dark Heart pursues Marked Heroes and detonates on impact, dealing 120 damage to nearby enemies and an additional 60 damage to the Marked target.
- **Void Volley [Trait]**
  - Void Volley is charged by Xal'atath's Basic Attacks. Unleash a barrage of Void Orbs at a target location, each dealing 99 damage on impact.
  - **Quest:** Hit enemy Heroes with Void Volley. Each Hero hit grants 1 stack and increases all Void damage by .1%. Xal'atath loses all stacks when killed
    - **Reward (30 Stacks):** Increase Void Volley's range by 2. Upon death, Xal'atath now only loses 6 stacks, but cannot fall below her current milestone minimum
    - **Reward (55 Stacks):** Void Volley requires 1 fewer Basic Attack to charge Void Volley.
    - **Mythic Reward (90 Stacks):** Increase Void Attack and Ability damage by 50%. Void Volley projectiles Pierce all targets.

**Talents**

- **Level 1**
  - **Anchored Core**
    - Increase Dark Heart's area by 50% and duration by 50%.
    - Dark Heart's damage bounces 2 times from the primary target to nearby enemies, dealing 18 damage.
  - **Cloak of Shadows**
    - Activating Void Step grants 50% Movement Speed and 25 Spell Armor for 2 seconds. This bonus refreshes each time Xal'atath teleports.
  - **Toxic Mark**
    - Increase Shadow Mark's duration by 1 second.
    - Damage over time dealt to Marked Heroes is increased to 1% of their maximum Health. Marked enemies have -15 Armor while Marked.
- **Level 4**
  - **Dark Barrier**
    - Each time Dark Heart damages an enemy, Xal'atath gains a Shield equal to 2% of her maximum Health, up to 10%.
  - **Void Acolyte**
    - Completing Void Step fully charges Void Volley.
    - The next Void Volley deals 50% increased damage and costs no Mana.
  - **Cold Feet**
    - Increase Shadow Mark's duration by 1 second.
    - Marked enemies are Slowed by 25%.
- **Level 7**
  - **Void Summoner**
    - Activating Void Step summons a single Void Minion in its center. Void Minions are attracted to Shadow Marked Heroes.
    - Void Minion Basic Attacks deal 95 damage per hit. Void Minions lose Health over 30 seconds.
  - **Void Adept**
    - **Quest:** Detonate the Dark Heart on Marked enemy Heroes to increase Shadow Mark's damage by 5 each time a target is hit, up to 100 additional damage.
  - **Silence of the Lamb**
    - Activating Void Step summons a seal of Void magic on the ground. When the seal completes, it deals 99 damage to enemies inside and Silences them for 2 seconds.
- **Level 10**
  - **Void Eruption [R]**
    - Channel a single powerful Void Orb that grows over time until reaching maximum size after 2 seconds. Recast to launch the Orb early.
    - Enemies that come into contact with the Orb take up to 175 damage and are Blinded for 1 second.
    - When the Orb reaches the end of its path, it detonates in a large explosion, dealing up to 700 damage.
    - The Orb deals contact damage every 0.125 seconds.
  - **Void Convergence [R]**
    - After channeling for 2 seconds, summon a Void Portal that spawns up to 4 Void Minions to attack nearby enemies.
    - Enemies near the Void Portal are Slowed by 25%.
    - Void Minions are attracted to Shadow Marked Heroes.
    - The Portal loses health over 20 seconds.
- **Level 13**
  - **Crushing Cascade**
    - Enemies hit by Void Volley are Slowed by 6% for 2 seconds per projectile, up to 50%.
  - **Dark Heart's Protection**
    - Casting Dark Heart Silences enemies inside its radius for 1 second.
    - Xal'atath and allied Heroes gain 30 Spell Armor while inside Dark Heart's radius.
  - **Relentless Whispers**
    - While Void Step is active, Xal'atath's Basic Attack rate is increased by 200%.
- **Level 16**
  - **Pull of the Dark Heart**
    - Linked enemies are Slowed by 10% for each pulse, stacking up to 5 times and decaying over 2 seconds.
  - **Marked Resilience**
    - Basic Attacks against Marked Heroes grant a 480 point Shield that rapidly decays.
  - **Fractured Reality**
    - Increase Void Volley projectile damage by 25%.
    - Void Volley projectiles leave a trail of fire along their path for 3 seconds, dealing 15 damage over time.
    - The trail deals damage every 1 second.
- **Level 20**
  - **Harbinger's Consumption**
    - Xal'atath's Void Eruption splits into 3 orbiting Void Orbs.
    - Cancelling the ability early causes the orbs to travel faster.
  - **Rift Invasion**
    - Void Convergence's Portal no longer loses health over time and can spawn and sustain up to 6 Void Minions.
  - **Dueling Orbs**
    - Gain 1 additional Shadow Mark charge.
  - **Unstable Core**
    - When the Dark Heart expires or detonates on a Marked Hero, it leaves behind a pool of void flames for 5 seconds.
    - Enemies in the flames take 15 damage over time.
    - The flames deal damage every 1 second.
  - **Paradox Loop**
    - After completing Void Step, Xal'atath immediately performs a second Void Step in the opposite direction. Added effects are triggered again.

## Balance Update

### Heroes

#### Abathur

**Talents**

- **Level 13**
  - **Bombard Strain**
    - Health decay reduction lowered from 50% to 40%.
- **Level 16**
  - **Survival Instincts**
    - Now provides 50% damage reduction against non-Heroes, instead of +100% Health.

#### Alexstrasza

**Base**

- Health increased from 1698 to 1780.

**Talents**

- **Level 16**
  - **Overprotective**
    - Shield reduced from 70% of healing to 50%.

#### Garrosh

**Talents**

- **Level 1**
  - **Unrivaled Strength**
    - Damage bonus reduced from 125% to 75%.
- **Level 4**
  - **In For The Kill**
    - Damage bonus increased from 70% to 100%.
- **Level 7**
  - **Oppressor**
    - Spell Power reduction decreased from 30% to 25%.
- **Level 13**
  - **Bloodcraze**
    - Heal duration increased from 3 seconds to 4 seconds. Total healing remains the same.
  - **Double Up**
    - Armor duration increased from 3 seconds to 5 seconds.

#### Mal'Ganis

**Base**

- **Night Rush [E]**
  - Cast time reduced from 0.75 seconds to 0.625 seconds.
- **Vampiric Touch [D]**
  - Healing based on damage against Heroes increased from 45% to 50%.
  - Healing based on damage against non-Heroes increased from 10% to 15%.

#### Qhira

**Base**

- **Blood Rage [W]**
  - Damage bonus per stack decreased from 0.25% to 0.2%.
  - Blood Rage base damage per tick lowered from 8 to 7.

**Talents**

- **Level 1**
  - **Fatal Wounds**
    - Damage bonus against low Health targets increased from 50% to 70%.
- **Level 13**
  - **Pulsing Pain**
    - Damage increased from 135 to 150.
- **Level 16**
  - **Lingering Ailment**
    - Armor reduction decreased from -35 to -25.

#### Whitemane

**Base**

- **Desperate Plea [Q]**
  - Base mana cost increased from 40 to 45. Mana scaling with each use is unchanged.

**Talents**

- **Level 7**
  - **Intercession**
    - Cooldown increased from 60 to 75 seconds.
- **Level 16**
  - **Radiance**
    - Healing increased from 175 to 190.

#### Yrel

**Base**

- **Vindication [Q]**
  - Mana cost increased from 50 to 65.

**Talents**

- **Level 13**
  - **Aldor Peacekeeper**
    - Damage reduction increased from 40% to 50%.
    - Damage reduction duration increased from 3 seconds to 4 seconds.
  - **Velen's Chosen**
    - Damage bonus per stack decreased from 10% to 8%.
- **Level 20**
  - **Seraphim**
    - Cooldown increased from 20 to 30 seconds.
