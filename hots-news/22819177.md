> Two teams, two mechs, two pilots, one victor! This week’s Brawl is Bash ‘Em Smash ‘Em Robots!

---

This week’s brawl is Bash ‘Em Smash ‘Em Robots! Control one of two Triglav Protector mechs on our new one-lane Overwatch themed Battleground, Industrial District, and get ready to bash ‘em and smash ‘em!

---

![Heroes_Volskaya_enUS_GIF.gif](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_folder_media/re/RE57X0X6KOG81506467810605.gif)

---

### Rules:

- Choose from one of three randomly selected Heroes before entering the Battleground. Be quick about it though, you only have 30 seconds to choose!
- Everyone will start at level 1 with no talent restrictions.
- Triglav Protector mechs will spawn for both teams at their friendly structures.
  - Protectors have infinite duration.
  - Protectors will respawn 15 seconds after they’re destroyed.
- **The first team to destroy the enemy Core will win the match!**

### Rewards:

- Complete three matches of the Bash ‘Em Smash ‘Em Robots to earn a Loot Chest!

---

Find out more about the Heroes Brawl game mode on our [Heroes Brawl site](http://eu.battle.net/heroes/en/game/heroes-brawl/); and as always, you can find more information on this week’s Brawl by clicking the Brawl Info button at the bottom of the play screen when preparing to queue for the Brawl game mode.
