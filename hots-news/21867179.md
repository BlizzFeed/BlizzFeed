We’ve just released a new hotfix for Heroes of the Storm in order to apply a few bug fixes.

---

### Bug Fixes

**Heroes, Abilities, and Talents**

- **E.T.C.:** Mosh Pit’s sound effects will no longer continue to play if casting is interrupted after learning the Tour Bus Talent.
- **Garrosh:** Can no longer use Into the Fray to throw an allied Boss.
- **Ragnaros:** Fixed an issue that could allow allied displacement Abilities to move Ragnaros while he was in Molten Core form.
