> Interested in trying Heroes but unsure where to begin? Read on to learn more about beginning your MOBA journey.

---

Divebomb your enemies from above as Deathwing, duke it out side by side with heroes and villains like Kerrigan, Kael’thas, Diablo, or D.Va, and unleash anarchy in a battle of multiversal madness.

If the above sounds like the mayhem you’ve been missing from your gaming experience, hit that download button—the Nexus awaits you in Heroes of the Storm.

Heroes is a Multiplayer Online Battle Arena, more commonly known as a ‘MOBA.’ Whether you’re familiar with the genre or a newcomer, you’ll find that Heroes puts its own unforgettable spins on MOBA gaming. It’s a unique hybrid of familiar and fresh, fusing fun new twists with recognizable and easy-to-learn gameplay. Play as legendary Heroes from your favorite games, customize their abilities on the fly, and battle it out on objective-based Battlegrounds. Read on to learn more about starting your Heroes journey.

---

#### Test out different talents

Instead of the one-size-fits-all item shop featured in most MOBAs, each hero has a **custom talent tree** tailored to their abilities. Every few levels, starting at level one, you’ll receive a set of talent choices that allow you to customize your hero according to your preferred playstyle and react to both your enemies and the Battleground you’re playing on.

Once you hit level 10, you’ll be able to select from two powerful **Heroic** talents. These are potent and potentially game-changing abilities that often have long cooldowns when used. Picking the best one from the offered selection can be the difference between a win and a loss.

![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/cu/CUFAGOLZ0UEQ1661381324900.png)

#### Brawl it out on Battlegrounds

The core structure of most MOBA games is the same: 5v5 player versus player combat that takes place on a static three-lane map. Heroes of the Storm, however, brings a unique twist to the genre standard: **Battlegrounds**. Queue up and traverse a multitude of maps that offer diverse objectives and themes. Whether it’s pushing Overwatch-style payloads while fending off enemy assaults, duking it out alongside draconic allies, or building a Zerg army in the StarCraft-themed Braxis Holdout, each Battleground brings something new and wild to the Nexus.

#### Make the most of modes

The Nexus takes many forms. Clash in a slew of different objective-driven modes that cater to all types of players. Maybe you’re looking to kick back with a casual match, or perhaps you’re on a glory-seeking quest to test yourself in ranked play; regardless of your reason, there’s a game mode that’s right for you.

**Quick Play**

Pick your hero, queue up, and charge into battle against other players on a random Battleground.

**Unranked**

Do you enjoy the Ranked Play experience but dislike the ladder-climbing pressure that accompanies it? Hop into Unranked to preserve the Draft and Ban format without having to worry about your rank.

**Versus A.I.**

Train against A.I.-controlled opponents to hone your skills on your hero of choice.

**Custom**

Choose a Battleground, invite your friends, and play Heroes of the Storm *your* way with a fully-customized match.

**ARAM**\
Unleash your chaotic potential in this All Random, All Mid mode. Choose from three heroes and brawl on a single-lane Battleground.

**Ranked Play - Storm League**\
Join the Nexus’s greatest contest and prove your worth by conquering enemies and ascending the ranked ladder.

#### Choose a role

Before you step onto the Battleground, you’ll need to choose a hero. At the beginning of each match, you’ll be prompted to take your pick of familiar faces from the Blizzard pantheon. Choose the role that best fits your playstyle, harness customizable talents and abilities to bolster friends and eliminate enemies, and endeavor to become a master of your favorite hero—or, if you’re bold enough, attempt to master them all.

##### Tank

If you’re the type of player that enjoys utilizing formidable strength to protect your team, consider taking some Tank heroes to the Nexus. Tanks often lead the vanguard in fights, absorbing attacks that would wipe out their more fragile team members and harnessing stuns, slows, and other crowd-control abilities to prevent enemies from retreating.

**Tank Heroes:** Mei, Mal’Ganis, Blaze, Garrosh, Cho, Johanna, Anub’arak, Arthas, Diablo, Muradin, Tyrael, E.T.C., Stitches

##### Bruiser

Bruiser heroes are masters of surviving rough fights and dishing out damage, making them great choices for players who like a good balance of tenacity and power in their picks.

**Bruiser Heroes**: Hogger, Deathwing, Imperius, Yrel, Malthael, D.Va, Ragnaros, Varian, Dehaka, Xul, Artanis, Rexxar, Leoric, Thrall, Chen, Sonya, Gazlowe

##### Support

Buffs, boosts, and other benefits are the staples of Support heroes. While their niche might be a small one, the boons provided by Support heroes, like teleportation or increased experience gain, can be immense for their teams.

**Support Heroes**: Zarya, Medivh, The Lost Vikings, Abathur

##### Healer

For those fond of being benevolent aides to their allies or holding their teammates’ lives in their hands, the Healer role offers many diverse options. Healers focus primarily on restoring health and providing potent mitigation; the utility they bring often makes them indispensable members of any team.

**Healer Heroes**: Anduin, Whitemane, Deckard, Alexstrasza, Ana, Stukov, Lúcio, Auriel, Lt. Morales, Kharazim, Rehgar, Brightwing, Li Li, Malfurion, Tyrande, Uther

##### Melee Assassin

These heroes specialize in dispatching enemies with swift and precise power. If you enjoy dealing high-octane, in-your-face damage and aren’t afraid to place yourself in precarious positions to eliminate whatever enemies stand in your way, consider checking out the Melee Assassin lineup.

**Melee Assassin Heroes**: Qhira, Maiev, Valeera, Samuro, Alarak, The Butcher, Murky, Illidan, Kerrigan, Zeratul

##### Ranged Assassin

Ranged Assassins are lower-health heroes that are skilled at picking off their foes from a safe distance. Despite their fragility, they dish out potent damage and excel at wearing down their enemies from afar.

**Ranged Assassin Heroes**: Orphea, Mephisto, Fenix, Hanzo, Junkrat, Kel’Thuzad, Genji, Cassia, Probius, Zul’jin, Gul’dan, Chromie, Tracer, Li-Ming, Greymane, Lunara, Gall, Kael’thas, Sylvanas, Jaina, Azmodan, Zagara, Tychus, Nazeebo, Nova, Raynor, Sgt. Hammer, Valla, Falstad, Tassadar
