## New Event: CraftWars

![card-hero-template.jpg](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/gallery/fy/FY7OB19GJC681599250984154.jpg)

The reemergence of the Dark Nexus has led to fiercer competition for Singularities than ever before—but a small group of Heroes is transforming the conflict. The Realm Crafter they’ve developed doesn’t just bring Heroes from multiple dimensions together in the Nexus—it actually fuses them, along with their friendships, rivalries, and powers.

Does Mei have it in her to be a Death Knight? What are the odds that Gazlowe will excel as a pro SCV pilot? Who’s the biggest heartthrob: Anduin Wrynn, Valerian Mengsk, or a combination of their wonderfully symmetrical faces?

While some body-melded Heroes take the time to learn from one another and their new circumstances, others are double-mindedly focused on the war to become MVP. The CraftWars are spiraling throughout the realms, and you’ll need to choose a side carefully—the King of Blades waits to absorb stragglers from every corner of the battlefield.

Prove you’re more than the sum of your parts as mash-ups of your favorite Heroes in the wildest matchup the Nexus has ever seen.

## New Nexus Anomaly: Gladiator's Medallion

![H52_MedallionBlog_Lightbox_ActionShots.gif](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/gallery/4m/4M3QDNMFDNP31599252925873.gif)

Each Nexus Anomaly is a unique limited-time Event where you can test out potentially permanent gameplay changes to Heroes. With our fourth and newest Nexus Anomaly, Gladiator’s Medallion, the power to prevent crowd control and disabling effects is directly in your hands. In this Anomaly, every Hero will be equipped with an activated ability that instantly clears most disabling effects and grants a brief Unstoppable buff, though it comes with a hefty 5-minute cooldown.

As with our previous iterations, this Nexus shake-up is only available for a limited time. Read about the details in our [overview blog](https://news.blizzard.com/en-gb/heroes-of-the-storm/23513930/become-unstoppable-during-the-gladiator-s-medallion-nexus-anomaly?cmsMode=draft), and try it out in-game before it's gone!

## Hero Reworks: D.Va and Gazlowe

As the Nexus continually changes and evolves, so do the combatants. Two of your favorite misfit Heroes have received major updates to their kits to help promote both gameplay diversity and team synergy, all while preserving their unique identity! This time around, D.Va and Gazlowe are getting reworks to fully cement their role as Bruisers.

Take a look at the full details of [D.Va's rework](https://news.blizzard.com/en-gb/heroes-of-the-storm/23494820) and [Gazlowe's rework](https://news.blizzard.com/en-gb/heroes-of-the-storm/23509394/gazlowe-s-hero-rework-a-bruiser-you-want-to-scrap-with) in their very own blogs.
