# Tune in as We Reveal the Next Hero and Battleground

> We’re gearing up to unveil the next Hero and Battleground to enter the Nexus. Be among the first to learn about them by tuning in to our live broadcast on Monday, June 4!

---

We’re gearing up to unveil the next Hero and Battleground to enter the Nexus during a live broadcast on Monday, June 4, and we hope you’ll tune in for our live reveal celebration! Lead Content Designer **Matt Cooper** and Lead Hero Designer **Matt Villers** will be joined by HGC Europe casters **[Tim 'Trikslyr' Frazier](https://twitter.com/Trikslyr)** and [Thomas ‘Khaldor’ Kilian](https://twitter.com/Khaldor) to bring you all the details.

---

#### How to Watch

**When:** 19:00 CEST on Monday, June 4

**Where:** twitch.tv/blizzheroes

[Join us on Twitch](https://www.twitch.tv/blizzheroes)

---

Stick around for the entire hourlong show for an epic showmatch on the new Battleground with members of the Heroes development team. We can’t wait to show you what we’ve been working on, so be sure to set a reminder for 19:00 CEST on June 4. Until then, we’ll see you in the Nexus.
