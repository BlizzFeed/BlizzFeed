We’ve recently released a Heroes of the Storm patch in order to address a few live bugs. Read on for details.

---

### Bug Fixes

**General**

- **AI:** A.I. Heroes will no longer immediate run to Mercenary Camps after they spawn.
- Fixed a rare issue that could cause the client to crash.

**Battlegrounds**

- **Boss Mercenaries:** Fixed an issue allowing Boss mercenaries to be slowed.
