# Everything You Need to Know About the Crucible

- URL: https://news.blizzard.com/en-gb/article/21702093/everything-you-need-to-know-about-the-crucible
![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/ky/KYYDY1LRCHWE1525113707300.jpg)

> After each Crucible match, one team will be in Phase 2 of the HGC, while the other will be in the Open Division.

---

For the teams skilled enough to advance through the Open Division Playoffs in Europe and North America—Granit Gaming, Worst Positioning, King’s Gambit, and XD—the Crucible is the final proving ground to see if they have what it takes to compete in premier league play. These four promising challengers will face off against the bottom two teams from HGC Europe and North America in tense best-of-seven battles that will determine who enters (or remains) in the HGC.

As the winners of the North America Open Division Playoffs, King’s Gambit have chosen to play HGC North America eighth-place finishers [No Tomorrow](https://esports.heroesofthestorm.com/en-gb/teams/2011/no-tomorrow). This means Open Division runner-up XD will play [LFM Esports](https://esports.heroesofthestorm.com/en-gb/teams/4675/lfm-esports), who finished seventh in HGC NA.

In Europe, HGC Open Division Playoff winner Worst Positioning have chosen to play seventh-place finishers [Diamond Skin](https://esports.heroesofthestorm.com/en-gb/teams/2013/diamond-skin). This leaves Open Division runner-up Granit Gaming against HGC Europe's last-place team, [Leftovers](https://esports.heroesofthestorm.com/en-gb/teams/4476/leftovers).

The winners of each of those four series will be in HGC North America or HGC Europe for the second half of the season.
