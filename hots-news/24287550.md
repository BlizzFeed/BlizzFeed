> Our next Heroes of the Storm patch has just hit the Public Test Realm and will be available for playtesting.

---

Our next Heroes of the Storm patch has just hit the Public Test Realm and will be available for playtesting. As always, if you encounter any bugs during your PTR play sessions, please stop by the [PTR Bug Report forum](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/heroes/c/ptr-bug-report) to let us know about your experiences.

## Balance Update

### Heroes

#### Abathur

**Base**

- **Spawn Locust [D]**
  - Health reduced from 350 to 225.
  - Locusts now heal for 20% of the damage they deal, and heal for 20% of their maximum Health when a nearby Minion dies.

**Talents**

- **Level 1**
  - **Envenomed Nests**
    - Armor reduction increased from 10 to 15.
  - **Locust Brood**
    - Moved from Level 16 to Level 1.
    - Cooldown increased from 45 to 75 seconds.
- **Level 4**
  - **(New) Slapathur**
    - Abathur's Basic Attacks now Stun non-Heroes and deal 500% additional damage.
- **Level 10**
  - **Evolve Monstrosity**
    - Now displays as a Quest.
    - Abathur now has a Monstrosity unit frame, for tracking Health and stacks of the unit.
- **Level 16**
  - **Survival Intincts**
    - Moved from Level 1 to Level 16.
    - Health bonus increased from 100% to 200%.

#### Alexstrasza

**Base**

- **Dragonqueen [D]**
  - Cooldown now starts after Dragonqueen has expired.
  - Cooldown reduced from 150 to 130 seconds.
  - Talents that affect Gift of Life also apply to Breath of Life.
  - Talents that affect Abundance also apply to Preservation.
- **Breath of Life [Dragonqueen Q]**
  - Healing reduced from 20% to 15%.
- **Preservation [Dragonqueen W]**
  - Healing reduced from 30% to 25%.

**Talents**

- **Level 1**
  - **Live and Let Live**
    - Now also provides 25% recharge rate for Breath of Life.
  - **Flames of Fury**
    - **NEW MYTHIC REWARD:** Hit an additional 40 Heroes without dying to cause Dragonqueen's duration to be refreshed when damaging an enemy Hero.
- **Level 7**
  - **Life Blossom**
    - Now makes Breath of Life remove Slows and Roots.
    - Transforming into Dragonqueen will no longer remove the collected Lifeblossom.
- **Level 16**
  - **Draconic Dicipline**
    - No longer affect's Dragonqueen's duration.
    - For each allied Hero healed with Dragonqueen's Basic Attacks, reduce the cooldown of Preservation by 1.25 seconds.
- **Level 20**
  - **On Ruby Wings**
    - Rework: If at least 3 Heroes are in an area hit by a Fireball, that Fireball charge is not consumed.
  - **Ancient Flame**
    - Does not provide Quest stacks while active.

#### Blaze

**Talents**

- **Level 1**
  - **Adrenaline Stim**
    - Active Attack Speed bonus reduced from 100% to 85%.
- **Level 10**
  - **Bunker Drop**
    - Cooldown increased from 80 to 85 seconds.

#### Cho

**Talents**

- **Level 7**
  - **Calloused Hide**
    - Health threshold required to trigger reduced from 60% to 40%.
- **Level 7**
  - **Firestarter**
    - Cooldown reduction per Basic Attack increased from 1 to 1.5 seconds.

#### Gall

**Talents**

- **Level 4**
  - **Rising Dread**
    - Bonus per bounce reduced from 25% to 20%.
  - **Bomb's Away**
    - No longer has a cap on stacks.
    - Damage bonus per stack lowered from 5 to 4.
- **Level 7**
  - **Double Trouble**
    - Shadowflame's cooldown reduction from Quest Reward increased from 0.5 to 1 second.
- **Level 16**
  - **Leaden Orb**
    - Stun duration reduced from 0.75 seconds to 0.6 seconds.

#### Chromie

**Base**

- **Dragon's Breath [W]**
  - Now shows a radius preview to enemies.
- **Time Trap [E]**
  - Health reduced from 400 to 250.

**Talents**

- **Level 1**
  - **Once Again The First Time**
    - Quest requirement reduced from 40 to 20 stacks.
    - Quest must now be completed without dying.
  - **Deep Breathing**
    - Damage per stack reduced from 1% to 0.5%.
    - No longer has a cap on stacks.

- **Level 4**
  - **Chronic Conditions**
    - Now also causes Time Trap to activate automatically upon touching an enemy Hero.
    - Slow reduced from 30% to 20%.
    - Movement Speed bonus reduced from 30% to 20%.

- **Level 13**
  - **Here and There**
    - Range increased from 20 to 40.
    - Snap targetting is now used. The Echo nearest the player's target position will be used.

#### Diablo

**Base**

- **Shadow Charge**
  - Damage increased from 40 plus 130 with a collision, to 45 plus 140 with a collision.
- **Fire Stomp**
  - Damage decreased from 12 on expansion and 36 on return to 11 on expansion and 34 on return.
- **Overpower**
  - Damage increased from 73 to 80.

**Talents**

- **Level 7**
  - **Diabolical Momentum**
    - Cooldown reduction per Basic Attack increased from 1.5 to 2 seconds.
- **Level 20**
  - **Dying Breath**
    - Cooldown reduction per Hero hit increased from 25 to 30 seconds.
  - **Lord of Terror**
    - Health stolen increased from 10% to 12% maximum Health.

#### Genji

**Talents**

- **Level 1**
  - **Swift as the Wind**
    - Movement Speed bonus increased from 30% to 40%.
- **Level 4**
  - **Strike at the Heart**
    - Damage increased from 134 to 142.
- **Level 7**
  - **Perfect Defense**
    - Cooldown reduction per hit increased from 2 to 2.5 seconds.
- **Level 13**
  - **Flow Like Water**
    - Cooldown reduction per hit increased from 3 to 4 seconds.
- **Level 16**
  - **Final Cut**
    - Damage reduced from 125 to 115.

#### Johanna

**Base**

- **Shield Glare [E]**
  - Cooldown increased from 12 to 13 seconds.

**Talents**

- **Level 10**
  - **Blessed Shield**
    - Cooldown increased from 60 to 65 seconds.

#### Mal'Ganis

**Talents**

- **Level 1**
  - **Will of Tichondrius**
    - Stun's duration increase reduced from 0.5 to 0.25 seconds.
- **Level 16**
  - **Blind as a Bat**
    - There is now a 0.2 second reactivation delay upon finishing 3 swipes of Fel Claws. This matches the time between other swipes.

#### Qhira

**Base**

- **Revolving Sweep [Q]**
  - Once attached, Revolving Sweep can no longer be detached by the target becoming Unstoppable.

- **Blood Rage [W]**
  - **NEW BASELINE QUEST:** Deal damage to enemy Heroes with Blood Rage's bleed to permanently increase its damage by 0.5% per stack.
  - Blood Rage's bleed damage reduced from 11 to 8.

**Talents**

- **Level 1**
  - **Fatal Wounds**
    - Damage bonus against low Health targets increased from 30% to 50%.
    - Quest removed.

#### Ragnaros

**Base**

- **Blast Wave [W]**
  - Damage increased from 104 to 120.
- **Molten Core [D]**
  - Cooldown increased from 120 to 180 seconds.
  - Health increased from 3996 to 5000.
  - Duration increased from 18 to 23 seconds.
  - Ragnaros is now Stunned for 1 second upon exiting.
- **Meteor Swing [Molten Core Q]**
  - Damage increased from 161 to 250.
  - No longer deals increased damage to Minions.
  - Cooldown increased from 6 seconds to 9 seconds.
  - Now also knocks enemies back.
- **Meteor Shower [Molten Core W]**
  - Damage increased from 151 to 200.
  - Cooldown increased from 2.5 to 6 seconds.
  - Area of effect increased by 66%.
  - Delay between hits significantly increased.
  - No longer deals increased damage to Minions.
  - Slow increased from 25% to 35%.
  - Range reduced from 35 to 20, but now it extends up to 35 over time.
  - Now deals 75% reduced damage to Structures.
- **Explosive Rune [Molten Core E]**
  - Cooldown decreased from 4 to 2.5 seconds.
  - Now applies a DOT that deals 88 damage per second for 5 seconds to impacted targets. No longer deals instant damage.
  - Range reduced from 35 to 20, but now it extends up to 35 over time.
  - No longer deals increased damage to Minions.
  - Now deals 75% reduced damage to Structures.

**Talents**

- **Level 1**
  - **Engulfing Flame**
    - Damage bonus reduced from 75% to 50%.
- **Level 10**
  - **Sulfuras Smash**
    - **NEW MYTHIC QUEST:** Hit 8 enemy Heroes with the center of Sulfuras Smash. Mythic Reward: Sulfuras Smash gains an extra charge.
- **Level 20**
  - **Lava Surge**
    - No longer reduces the cooldown of Lava Wave by 30 seconds.
    - Now also gives +200% Movement Speed to allied Minions and Mercenaries in its path.
  - **Heroic Difficulty**
    - **NEW FUNCTIONALITY:** Decrease the cooldown of Molten Core by 110 seconds when used on a dead Fort or Keep. In addition, the starting range of abilities in Molten Core are increased by 100% and will continue to grow.

#### Whitemane

**Base**

- **Searing Lash [E]**
  - **NEW QUEST:** Hit Heroes with Searing Lash to increase Whitemane's maximum Health by 3 and Mana by 2.

**Talents**

- **Level 1**
  - **Pity the Frail**
    - Cooldown reduction per hit reduced from 1.5 to 1 second.
  - **Inquisitor's Prayer**
    - **REWORK:** For each Hero hit by Searing Lash, restore 25 Mana. Passive: Zeal's duration is increased by 2 seconds.
- **Level 7**
  - **Intercession**
    - Cooldown increased 45 to 60 seconds.
- **Level 13**
  - **Scarlet Wrath**
    - Mana restore increased from 0.5% to 1%.
- **Level 20**
  - **Guiding Light**
    - Now also increases the Spell Power gained from Zeal's activation by 15%.

#### Yrel

**Base**

- **Righteous Hammer [Q]**
  - Heal increased from 320 to 370.
  - Damage increased from 140 to 160.
- **Righteous Hammer [W]**
  - Stun duration increased from 0.75 1 second.
  - Damage increased from 125 to 140.
- **Avenging Wrath [E]**
  - Slow increased from 50% to 60%.
  - Damage increased from 225 to 260.
- **Divine Purpose [D]**
  - Cooldown increased to from 8 to 16 seconds.
  - Cooldown is now reduced by 8 seconds whenever Yrel's Basic Abilities are interrupted.

**Talents**

- **Level 4**
  - **Hand of Freedom**
    - Cooldown increased from 30 to 40 seconds.
    - Movement Speed bonus reduced from 30% to 25%.
- **Level 20**
  - **Seraphim**
    - Cooldown increased from 10 to 20 seconds.

## Bug Fixes

### General

- Attack beams from Forts, Keeps, and the Core will now be more visible when attacking Heroes.

### Maps

- **Haunted Mines**
  - Fixed an issue where the player would not auto-mount when using the Hearthstone from inside the mines.
- **Warhead Junction**
  - Fixed an issue that caused Heroes to not be revealed while launching a Warhead.
  - Fixed an issue that caused the Warhead's channel beam to persist when interrupted.

### Heroes

- **Ana**
  - **Level 10**
    - **Eye of Horus**
      - No longer reveals in a global line when hitting a Structure.
      - Removed a minor delay when dealing damage to enemy Heroes.

  - **Base**
