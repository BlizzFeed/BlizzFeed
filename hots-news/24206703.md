## General

- All Heroes will be free starting from now until June 15th.
- **NEW:** All players will receive two new portraits to celebrate the 10 year anniversary of Heroes.
- Players who are unable to purchase gems in-game can now purchase gems in the Battle.net shop.
- Gem quantities have been standardized in all regions and prices have been adjusted to affect this change. Previous bonus value has been incorporated in the new price.
- Updated Homescreen and Startup Music. **CAUTION:** May cause nostalgia.
- Updated Startup Loading Screen.

## Bug Fixes

### Heroes

- **Brightwing**
  - **Base**
    - **Arcane Flare [Q]**
      - Minimum cast range removed.
      - No longer bypasses flight time when cast inside Brightwing's space.
- **E.T.C.**
  - **Level 20**
    - **Power of the Horde**
      - Fixed missing cast animations.
      - Fixed missing Power of the Horde cast visual.
    - **Ride the Lightning**
      - E.T.C. will now face the direction that Ride the Lightning is cast in.
      - Fixed missing cast animations.
- **Gazlowe**
  - **Level 10**
    - **Grav-O-Bomb 3000**
      - Minimum cast range removed.
      - No longer bypasses flight time when cast inside Gazlowe's space.
- **Hanzo**
  - **Base**
    - **Sonic Arrow [E]**
      - Minimum cast range removed.
      - No longer bypasses flight time when cast inside Hanzo's space.
- **Murky**
  - **Base**
    - **Pufferfish [W]**
      - Minimum cast range removed.
      - No longer bypasses flight time when cast inside Murky's space.
- **Orphea**
  - **Base**
    - **Shadow Waltz [Q]**
      - Reverted fix that caused Orphea to not dash when moving after hitting an enemy Hero with Shadow Waltz. We will continue to investigate.
- **Tychus**
  - **Base**
    - **Frag Grenade [W]**
      - Minimum cast range removed.
      - No longer bypasses flight time when cast inside Tychus' space.
