---

### Bug Fixes

**General**

- **Reconnect:** Fixed an issue causing some players to lose control of their Heroes after reconnecting to a game.

**User Interface**

- **Whitemane:** Clemency will now appear under Quick Cast settings in the Hotkeys menu.
- **Observer:** Fixed an issue that could cause game crashes if messages were sent as an observer.
- **Profile:** Fixed an issue that caused the Profile pane to stop displaying individual seasons after playing a game.
- **Profile:** Fixed an issue that caused the statistics pane to fail to update when viewing a mode with no games played.
