The Warchrome Wastes are chaotic and desolate. Anyone hoping to survive their next scavenging operation will need to be determined, desperate, and even a little mad. Lucky for you, we ventured far and wide to round up some of the deadliest—and shiniest—creatures to inhabit the Wastes and wrangled them into the Collection! Read on to preview new Warchrome skins and other cosmetics we’re bringing to Heroes in our next patch.
