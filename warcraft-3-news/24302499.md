## Wage War Once More

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/3x/3XGTM1BMF42T1789238995043.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/3x/3XGTM1BMF42T1789238995043.png)

Before there was a world, there was war. Return to the conflicts that helped forge Azeroth’s future with *Warcraft III Reforged: Forsaken Kingdom*, a new campaign for *Warcraft III: Reforged* that begins in the ruins of Lordaeron and leads into the first days of *World of Warcraft*.

Step into the untold story of the Forsaken as Lordaeron’s fallen wrest free of the Scourge and fight for a future beneath the shattered surface of their former kingdom. Led by heroes such as Sylvanas Windrunner, the newly risen Forsaken face enemies familiar and new, establish a foothold in the Undercity, and make choices that will echo into the era of *World of Warcraft: Forever.*

## Choose Your Path on the Battle.net Shop

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_folder_media/S4Y0MWPTJ0YF1789238760860.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_folder_media/S4Y0MWPTJ0YF1789238760860.png)

*Warcraft III Reforged: Forsaken Kingdom* is available to purchase on the Battle.net Shop. Players who already own *Warcraft III: Reforged* can add the Forsaken Kingdom campaign to play the new storyline. New players can begin with *Warcraft III: Reforged* or choose the *Warcraft III Reforged:* *Forsaken Kingdom* combo, while fans looking for the broadest Warcraft experience can pick up the limited-time Warcraft Forever Collection, including *Warcraft III Reforged: Forsaken Kingdom* and *World of Warcraft: Forever*.

The Forsaken Kingdom campaign unlocks a prologue, three campaign chapters, a neutral Forsaken Paladin hero unit, and story content that connects the fall of Lordaeron to the rise of a new era in Azeroth. The Definitive Edition update for *Warcraft III: Reforged* also brings performance and graphical improvements to new and returning players at no additional cost for those who already have access to *Warcraft III: Reforged*.

### What’s Included

- *Warcraft III Reforged: Forsaken Kingdom*—Add the new prologue and campaign to your collection.
- *Warcraft III: Reforged* — Experience the real-time strategy classic with a visual overhaul, modern social and matchmaking features, and the new Definitive Edition update.
- *Warcraft III Reforged: Forsaken Kingdom* Combo — Play through *Warcraft III: Reforged* and the Forsaken Kingdom campaign in one bundle.
- Warcraft Forever Collection — Celebrate BlizzCon 2026 with a limited-time collection that includes *Warcraft III: Reforged*, *Warcraft III Reforged: Forsaken Kingdom*, the *World of Warcraft: Forever* Skyborne Epic Pack, and Forsaken and Human Figurine Housing decor.

## Prepare for World of Warcraft: Forever

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_folder_media/3GOH92PYRHRK1789238762841.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_folder_media/3GOH92PYRHRK1789238762841.png)

The story continues beyond the fall of Lordaeron. *World of Warcraft: Forever* continues shortly after the events of the Forsaken Kingdom campaign, inviting players to carve a new path through a reimagined original Azeroth when it launches on November 4, 2026. Access is included with a World of Warcraft subscription or Game Time, and optional digital packs unlock the new Skyborne race, Zephras Isle starting experience, Beta access, early name reservation, in-game cosmetics, and more.

Fans who want the broadest Warcraft experience can pre-purchase the Warcraft Forever Collection, which includes *Warcraft III Reforged: Forsaken Kingdom*, *Warcraft III: Reforged*, the *World of Warcraft: Forever* Skyborne Epic Pack, 30 days of WoW Game Time, and additional in-game benefits. [Pre-purchase *World of Warcraft: Forever* digital packs on the Battle.net Shop](https://us.shop.battle.net/en-us/product/warcraft-3-reforged?pfm=fran-warcraft-news-article-shop-forsaken-kingdom-combo).

*Warcraft III Reforged: Forsaken Kingdom requires Warcraft III: Reforged to play. Internet connection, Battle.net Account, and Battle.net desktop app required. World of Warcraft, including World of Warcraft: Forever, requires WoW subscription or Game Time. Some in-game benefits apply only to the specific WoW account purchased for. Included in-game items are not available in WoW Classic games.*

*\*Warcraft Forever Collection will be available through January 11, 2027.*
