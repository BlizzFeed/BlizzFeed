# Warcraft® III: Reforged’s Multiplayer Beta Begins This Week!

- URL: https://news.blizzard.com/en-gb/article/23150112/warcraft-iii-reforgeds-multiplayer-beta-begins-this-week
![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/7f/7FGANKKGH48J1571781074670.jpg)

> Learn about the multiplayer beta and everything included in the Warcraft® III: Reforged Spoils of War Edition.

---

The multiplayer beta for [*Warcraft III: Reforged*](https://playwarcraft3.com/) begins this week\* with limited access to the orc and human races within 1v1 and 2v2 matchmaking. The undead and night elf races, and more game modes will be added in the weeks ahead.

Invitations to the multiplayer beta will be sent out over the coming weeks. Initial invitations will go out in waves to those who have pre-purchased the [Spoils of War](https://eu.shop.battle.net/en-gb/product/warcraft-iii-reforged) edition of the game and subsequent waves of invitations will go to additional players, such as those who have pre-purchased the standard edition of *Warcraft III: Reforged*.

---
