Another exciting new feature in *StarCraft: Remastered* is making its debut. Profiles will be home to multiplayer statistics, replays, ladder ranks (for the new matchmaking system), and more!

The Summary tab is your base camp. Create a personalized message and see an overview of your *StarCraft: Remastered* career:

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/gallery/T030OBUBWEKI1506533979283.jpg)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/gallery/T030OBUBWEKI1506533979283.jpg)

Stats lets you dive into data. Sweet, sweet data . . . Sometimes you just *need* to know your lifetime total resources collected.

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/gallery/UB0WXYZBISVC1506533981161.jpg)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/gallery/UB0WXYZBISVC1506533981161.jpg)

Cloud saves keep your replays a click away on any computer. Access an overview or rewatch the action!

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/gallery/BZU7Q6LOLCCD1506533982927.jpg)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/gallery/BZU7Q6LOLCCD1506533982927.jpg)

The Seasons tab helps you stay on top of your rank, view past standings, and reminisce about your greatest achievements:

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/gallery/5LOS40GHLDNY1506533986993.jpg)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/gallery/5LOS40GHLDNY1506533986993.jpg)

Collections houses your spoils of war. Unlock unique portraits for your race, and wear them proudly to intimidate opponents on the field of battle:

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/gallery/QIQRKUV8EAJU1506533990296.jpg)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/gallery/QIQRKUV8EAJU1506533990296.jpg)

*These pages have been edited to more accurately reflect how these features work in StarCraft: Remastered.*
