Chat Channels have been a core part of the *StarCraft* experience since 1998, and starting this summer, we’re making them even better! *StarCraft: Remastered* will allow you to access multiple Chat Channels—from more places throughout the game—without changing the classic functionality that you know and love.

---

Persistent Chat: remain in the same channel no matter where you go in the menus. Continue conversations even when searching for a Lobby to join!

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/gallery/XM4UFF1JZPUR1497623858545.jpg)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/gallery/XM4UFF1JZPUR1497623858545.jpg)

In addition to classic chat commands and functionality, StarCraft: Remastered connects to your Blizzard Friends list, making it easier to find and join games with your friends.

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/gallery/RM96G3CD05Z21497623858576.jpg)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/gallery/RM96G3CD05Z21497623858576.jpg)

Modern resolutions offer more space for Chat. We’re also adding increased color variety, and better support for global keyboards and languages.
