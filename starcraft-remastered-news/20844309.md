# Feature Preview: Leaderboard

- URL: https://news.blizzard.com/en-gb/article/20844309/feature-preview-leaderboard
![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/3f/3F13UYJG5KSY1496961148404.jpg)

> It’s not just StarCraft’s graphics that are receiving an overhaul this summer—StarCraft: Remastered will also add modern matchmaking and ladder functionality, including a shiny new Leaderboard! At launch, StarCraft: Remastered will include leaderboards for Friends and IGRs.

---

It’s not just *StarCraft*’s graphics that are receiving an overhaul this summer—*StarCraft: Remastered* will also add modern matchmaking and ladder functionality, including a Leaderboard! *StarCraft: Remastered* will launch with Leaderboards between Friends, and expand all the way up to global standings as we hone our matchmaker.

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/gallery/VNLBIT4WKUZS1506533973683.jpg)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/gallery/VNLBIT4WKUZS1506533973683.jpg)

View the top of the Leaderboard, or search for a specific player by Profile Name or BattleTag.

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/gallery/883QDDLE5V3I1506533973791.jpg)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/gallery/883QDDLE5V3I1506533973791.jpg)
