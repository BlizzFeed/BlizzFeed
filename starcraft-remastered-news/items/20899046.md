# From StarCraft Pro Gamer to a Seat at Blizzard: TheBOy Interview

With StarCraft: Remastered launching on August 14, it’s never been a more appropriate time to look back on almost two decades of StarCraft history. This week we shine a spotlight on Kook ‘TheBOy’ Kibong, who was 18 years old when StarCraft came out. He soon became one of the best players in Korea, placing highly in numerous tournaments. Years later, he found himself at Blizzard Korea, where he currently helps manage StarCraft and StarCraft II esports.
