# Feature Preview: Hotkeys

- URL: https://news.blizzard.com/en-gb/article/20726737/feature-preview-hotkeys
- Date: 2017-05-19T17:00:00Z
- Image: https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/99/99BGMUT54RSS1495156081234.jpg

The first implementation of Hotkeys in StarCraft: Remastered during the early 1.18 Public Test Realm (PTR) drew a lot of great feedback and discussion. The Classic Games team took the community’s input to heart, and today we’re excited to unveil the version of Hotkeys you can expect when StarCraft: Remastered comes out this summer.
