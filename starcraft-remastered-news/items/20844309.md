# Feature Preview: Leaderboard

It’s not just StarCraft’s graphics that are receiving an overhaul this summer—StarCraft: Remastered will also add modern matchmaking and ladder functionality, including a shiny new Leaderboard! At launch, StarCraft: Remastered will include leaderboards for Friends and IGRs.
