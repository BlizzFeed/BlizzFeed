# Cost of Life Story Time with Tashinga Bepete | Overwatch

Sometimes the man becomes the monster.
 
Join Tashinga Bepete, the voice actor behind Doctrine, for a reading of our newest comic, ‘Cost of Life’.  

Doctrine, a brilliant cybernetic engineer, rejects the limits of life and death to save the one person he cares about. As his work begins to consume him, he is forced to face the consequences of his ideology.
