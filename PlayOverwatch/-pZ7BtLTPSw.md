# Fracture Story Time with Carolina Ravassa | Overwatch

A volatile weapon, a broken genius, and one impossible rescue.
 
Join Carolina Ravassa, the voice actor behind Sombra, for a reading of our newest comic, “Fracture”. 
 
When a dangerous experiment spirals out of control, Sombra is forced to act as personal ties draw her in. As Talon and Overwatch collide around an unstable Sigma, the battle erupts into chaos, pushing everyone to their limits.
