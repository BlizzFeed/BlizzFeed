# Terms of Justice Story Time with Cherise Boothe | Overwatch

What are the next steps for Overwatch?

Join Cherise Boothe, the voice actor behind Sojourn, for a reading of our newest short story, “Terms of Justice.”

In the wake of the International Justice Commission's decision to revoke Overwatch's charter, Sojourn reflects on the integrity of leadership when the unexpected arrival of an IJC dropshop promises to complicate what comes next…
