![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/gallery/9AA33FC3YIXD1456525140808.png)

Eight players are left standing after one of the most incredible weekends we've seen all year. Now, the finale begins.

Beginning at noon PDT on Friday, November 3rd, the world's greatest eight StarCraft II players will vie for the most prestigious title any one gamer could hold: the StarCraft II World Champion. Such a title comes with a rich reward as well, as the first place winner will take home a bountiful $280,000 cash prize.

It's going to be the greatest StarCraft II event of the year... read below so you don't miss a moment.
