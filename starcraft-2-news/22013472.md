# StarCraft II 4.5.1 Patch Notes

- URL: https://news.blizzard.com/en-gb/article/22013472/starcraft-ii-4-5-1-patch-notes
![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/ai/AI2488BMOWLI1534218865365.jpg)

> We’ve added all new weekly Mutations with our latest patch.

---

### General

- New weekly Mutations have been added with ‘Bannable Offense’ kicking off the new challenges.
- A new Viking Trophy Top has been added for tournament winners.

### Bug Fixes

- Fixed an issue that added a delay for when players could control their workers at the beginning of a Versus match.
- Zerglings using the Primal skin will no longer show the fire death animation when being killed by non-fire type attacks.
- Fixed several issues with War Chest 3 cosmetics.
