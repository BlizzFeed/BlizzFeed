# StarCraft II: Legacy of the Void 3.16.1 Patch Notes

> A new patch has come to StarCraft II: Legacy of the Void.

---

![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/XD6JO47M2O7B1495231911264.png)

- Fixed a display issue with experience in War Chest node dialogs.
- The Leviathan Brood Ultralisk, Baneling and Queen now have more pronounced team color, and glow their team color properly.
- Zealot using the Forged skin can now be properly selected while warping in.
