# War Chest Phase 2 Unlocked

- URL: https://news.blizzard.com/en-gb/article/20988563/war-chest-phase-2-unlocked
![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/z6/Z6MW468E20LO1502862572233.jpg)

> The next phase of the War Chest is now available. Get in game to start earning new skins and rewards.

---

Phase 2 of the [War Chest](https://warchest.starcraft2.com/en-gb/) has been unlocked! It’s time to construct additional pylons, spread your creep, and drop manner MULES to earn new skins, emoticons, and sprays. You can refer to our [handy guide](http://eu.battle.net/sc2/en/blog/20859945/war-chest-visual-guide-7-15-2017) to see what rewards will be available in this next phase or you can jump directly into StarCraft II and mouse over each node within the War Chest itself.

We were both humbled and excited to see the community rally around supporting esports and reaching the $700,000 goal so quickly. As a sign of gratitude, we wanted to add more content for those who participated in the first ever War Chest and created new portraits from the Shadow Wars comic. These portraits will be deployed in a future patch and will be awarded to anyone who purchases this season’s War Chest.

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/LJQL2R6JSLE51502862682395.jpg)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/CLXSQ3ISQ6F71502862683822.jpg)

Now that prize pool has been maxed out, 25% of future [War Chest](https://warchest.starcraft2.com/en-gb/) earnings will go towards WCS 2017 and 2018 operations. We’ve already been able to put this money to work by doubling the prizepool of TotalBiscuit’s recent SHOUTcraft Kings tournament. Thanks to your efforts, players competed for a prizepool of $10,000. You can watch the entire series [here](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLS67L5dgs2EYeyFxwhFMBRWyUnqORjN16) if you missed it.

Thank you again for everyone’s continued support of StarCraft II and StarCraft II esports.
