> Here are all the times, talent, streams, and more for BlizzCon 2018, including Opening Week.

---

The 2018 WCS Global Finals starts this Friday, Oct. 26 at BlizzCon Opening Week, with three days of group-stage competition before moving on to BlizzCon proper. Here’s everything you need to know so that you don’t miss a minute of the action.

# Dates, Times, and Groups

**WCS Global Finals Group Stage Day 1:** Oct. 26, 9 a.m.–3 p.m. PDT

- Group A: Maru vs. Lambo, TY vs. Neeb
- Group B: Zest vs. HeRoMaRinE, sOs vs. Serral

**WCS Global Finals Group Stage Day 2:** Oct. 27, 9 a.m–3 p.m. PDT

- Group C: Stats vs. Has, Dark vs. ShoWTimE
- Group D: Classic vs. Nerchio, Rogue vs. SpeCial

**WCS Global Finals Group Stage Day 3:** Oct. 28, 9 a.m.–5 p.m. PDT

**WCS Global Finals at BlizzCon Day 1:** Nov. 2, noon–8:15 p.m. PDT

**WCS Global Finals at BlizzCon Day 2:** Nov. 3, 1:30 p.m.–7:30 p.m. PDT\*

\**The Pylon Show* podcast begins at 11:30 a.m. PDT on [iNcontroLTV](https://www.twitch.tv/incontroltv).
