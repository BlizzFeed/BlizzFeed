# StarCraft: Remastered Is Live

- URL: https://news.blizzard.com/en-gb/article/20977640/starcraft-remastered-is-live
![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/7t/7T4JLV54DCR81502502460793.jpg)

> The real-time strategy game that started a legacy is back. Jump in and relive the rush.

---

Almost 20 years ago, Blizzard Entertainment released a game that would set the standard for the real-time strategy genre, inspire a generation of gamers, and show the world how powerful esports could be.

Today, we are honored to bring a classic game loved by so many into the modern era. Today we launch StarCraft: Remastered.

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/EUP3BM3E71421458604450055.png)](https://eu.battle.net/shop/en/product/starcraft-remastered)

If you haven’t been following our development closely, here are some of the features that we are modernizing or adding to StarCraft:

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/C9ALAI9NI2QE1502502544312.jpg)](https://starcraft.com/en-gb/articles/20844309) [![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/20P0BT9YFL6W1502502544126.jpg)](https://starcraft.com/en-gb/articles/20814255)

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/4L1Z1T89TFQH1502502544136.jpg)](https://starcraft.com/en-gb/articles/20852854) [![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/C5W9AIDY03UZ1502502544150.jpg)](https://starcraft.com/en-gb/articles/20726737)

Along with these new and improved features, we’ve enhanced the game’s original audio and dialogue and updated the graphics to 4K UHD. We hope these updates will allow our community to continue to enjoy StarCraft for many years to come.

Thank you to everyone in the community who has helped us shape Remastered into the game it is today. We’re far from done, however, and we’ll still need your help as we continue to make changes and fix those pesky bugs. Here’s to another 20 years of StarCraft!
