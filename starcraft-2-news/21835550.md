> The talent, the times and the streams in your language are all here.

---

We’re getting to the heart of the 2018 WCS season with this weekend’s WCS Austin event jumpstarting what should be a busy summer of pro StarCraft II. From the skill and tradition of the Global StarCraft II League (GSL), to the spectacle of IEM Katowice, the first half of this season has already given us plenty of high profile moments. In Austin, some of the best players in the WCS Circuit series will be looking to add their names to list of players who have thrilled us this season.

Top players like SpeCial, Neeb and ShoWTime are anxious to get their hands on some WCS points in hopes of qualifying for the WCS Global Finals at BlizzCon. Of course, the most direct route would be to win WCS Austin outright and claim the automatic berth at the Global Finals, and $20,000 top prize, that comes with it.

Fans looking to catch all of the WCS Austin action can check out the up-to-date schedule and live streams in your preferred language below:
