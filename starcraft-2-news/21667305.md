# Fenix Warps in for Everyone

- URL: https://news.blizzard.com/en-gb/article/21667305/fenix-warps-in-for-everyone
![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/w7/W7HQ1LE5N2N61522156009743.jpg)

> March has been the month of StarCraft, so now we are giving away the Co-op Commander Fenix for free!

---

March has been the month of StarCraft, and we’ve had a blast celebrating with all of you. We kicked it off with our new [SC20 website](https://starcraft2.com/en-gb/sc20) which is full of in-depth articles about the [history of the franchise](https://starcraft2.com/en-gb/news/21509420), the impact that StarCraft has had on [esports](https://starcraft2.com/en-gb/news/21574697), and the fascinating things that the DeepMind team is [doing with StarCraft II](https://starcraft2.com/en-gb/news/21509421). And there’s still a lot more in store!

Today, we’re thrilled to announce that we are giving away the Co-op Commander Fenix for free! From **March 29 to April 3**, you’ll find the Protoss champion sitting in the gift tab ready to be claimed.

For those of you who already own Fenix, we have a gift for you, too: A Co-op Stimpack! Collect this gift and you’ll earn double experience when you play Co-op Missions for 30 days. You’ll earn those sweet, sweet mastery levels twice as fast.

We hope everyone has been enjoying the 20th Anniversary celebration as much as we have. Here’s to 20 more years of StarCraft!
