# Caster Call to Action

> Apply to cast the 2019 WCS Challenger Season 3 Open Qualifiers!

---

The 2019 WCS Challenger Season 3 Open Qualifiers are almost here, and we are now taking applications for Community Casters looking to cover the event.

As a reminder, please be sure to fill out your application in full and include a summary of your StarCraft casting experience in your email!

### How to Apply

Send your application to [SC2admin@blizzard.com](mailto:SC2admin@blizzard.com?subject=Community%20Caster%20WCS) by July 18th. Ensure that the subject line reads “Community Caster WCS “, and that the following information is included

- Full name
- Discord handle
- Email address
- Broadcast language
- Broadcast channel
- Summary of your casting experience
- Link to a clip of your casting

### Other Requirements

- Casters must broadcast the Open Qualifiers exclusively on Twitch.
- Casters must use a 3-minute delay for all matches.
- Those selected to cast will be doing so from their own streaming channel.
- Tournament streams must comply with Blizzard’s advertising and sponsorship policy.
- All languages are welcome!

### Open Qualifier Dates and Times

- July 22: EU Qualifiers Day 1 - starts at 16:00 CEST
- July 22: NA Qualifiers Day 1 - starts at 16:00 PDT
- July 23: EU Qualifiers Day 2 - starts at 16:00 CEST
- July 23: NA Qualifiers Day 2 - starts at 16:00 PDT

For all our competitors, this could be the start of earning a coveted spot at the WCS Finals at BlizzCon, and you could be there at the start of their journey. Remember, the deadline to send in applications is July 18th. Apply today!
