# 2017 Ladder Season 3 Begins

- URL: https://news.blizzard.com/en-gb/article/20887939/2017-ladder-season-3-begins
![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/8g/8G3C5BT67NJ01453918571047.jpg)

> The start of our third season of the 2017 StarCraft II Ladder has arrived! Click through for the details.

---

It’s time for the third 2017 Season to begin!

To get things started, bonus pools and league standings have been reset, and 2017 Season 2 milestones have been awarded. You can check out your results for the past Season by visiting the "Ladder" section of your character profile.

Season 3 introduces new maps to the 1v1 and team map pool, and also brings back a few fan favorites to be revisited. Be sure to check out [this guide](http://battle.net/sc2/blog/20890403) for more details before you press the "Find Match" button in the game.

We’ve also applied a free character name change to all accounts that do not have one available. Remember, character name changes do not stack (you can only have one free name change at a time).

Good luck, commanders — and have fun!
