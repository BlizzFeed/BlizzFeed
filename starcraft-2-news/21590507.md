> Share your favorite StarCraft memories with #SC20!

---

To celebrate the 20th anniversary of StarCraft, we’re asking people around the world to share what makes StarCraft special to them. Grab your phone and record a short video sharing your most powerful feelings about StarCraft. What does StarCraft mean to you? What was the most impactful memory you have? What is your favorite moment in the game or in esports? We want to know!

To kick it off, we have our very own Mike Morhaime sharing his favorite memories.

[![Video](https://markdown-videos-api.jorgenkh.no/youtube/1g-DmlUgt-U?width=1280&height=720)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1g-DmlUgt-U)

After you’ve recorded your video, share it on Twitter with the hashtag #SC20 on Twitter so we can revel in all of the StarCraft love.

Thank you to everyone who loves and supports StarCraft. Here’s to another 20 years. Cheers!
