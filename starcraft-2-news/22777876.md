Expectations were smashed and history written anew at the 2018 World Championship Series Global Finals at BlizzCon. The paths of eight pro StarCraft II players twisted and turned until we were treated to an unexpected finals rematch of GSL vs. The World, resulting in a groundbreaking, emotional finish.
