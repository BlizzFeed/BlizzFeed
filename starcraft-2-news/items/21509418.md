# iNcontroL and Artosis Bring Years of StarCraft History to Their New Show

- URL: https://news.blizzard.com/en-gb/article/21509418/incontrol-and-artosis-bring-years-of-starcraft-history-to-their-new-show
- Date: 2018-05-02T17:30:00Z
![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/vh/VHF2F2NTJ01F1519425153633.jpg)

Geoff “iNcontroL” Robinson and Dan “Artosis” Stemkoski are two of the StarCraft community’s most beloved personalities. In the early 2000s, they competed in StarCraft: Brood War; later, they became professional StarCraft II players, shoutcasters, and expert denizens of the analyst desk. Now they’ve started The Pylon Show, which focuses on the competitive scene of both StarCraft games. Four episodes have already been recorded, with new broadcasts every Thursday at 2:00 CEST. We caught up with Geoff and Dan to find out more.
