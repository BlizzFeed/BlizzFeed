# Announcing the StarCraft II AI Workshop

- URL: https://news.blizzard.com/en-gb/article/21048078/announcing-the-starcraft-ii-ai-workshop
- Date: 2017-10-05T01:00:00Z
![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/t5/T5EMYCD85FUG1502220066903.jpg)

On November 3 and 4, Blizzard and DeepMind will co-host an invite-only StarCraft II AI Workshop for those in the AI community to collaborate on all things AI.
