# StarCraft and StarCraft II have landed on Game Pass

- URL: https://news.blizzard.com/en-gb/article/24172797/starcraft-and-starcraft-ii-have-landed-on-game-pass
- Date: 2024-11-12T20:00:00Z
- Image: https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/0w/0WK4AAOS65871737070416438.jpg

We’re excited to welcome Game Pass members now joining us in the Koprulu Sector. While the entirety of the original StarCraft and StarCraft II’s multiplayer components are free-to-play, StarCraft: Remastered and StarCraft II: Campaign Collection are now available to anyone with a PC Game Pass or Game Pass Ultimate subscription.
