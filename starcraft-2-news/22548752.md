# StarCraft BlizzCon In-Game Goodies Are Here

![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/xj/XJAABNPG1T3F1539711171046.jpg)

> Check out the BlizzCon loot you can now equip in StarCraft II and StarCraft: Remastered.

---

BlizzCon is just around the corner, and starting today, those who purchased a BlizzCon ticket or a BlizzCon Virtual Ticket will have access to new content in StarCraft: Remastered and StarCraft II.

We all know how important it is to maintain a high APM when fighting for ladder points, but did you know you can also be super stylish while doing so? The BlizzCon 2018 SCR Console Skin not only sports a sleek Umojan-style outer layer, it also changes color based on your actions-per-minute. From a cool green to a blazing red, the console will react to every single one of your actions just like a slick new gaming keyboard.

[Get the Virtual Ticket](https://eu.shop.battle.net/en-gb/product/blizzcon-virtual-ticket)

![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/TTWD0U0FGJFE1539711889927.gif)

Warping to StarCraft II, we look back at the legacy that started it all with brand new ‘Classic’ skins. The workers from each race can now be equipped with a skin inspired by the original StarCraft game. Make your enemies question what year it is as you send them to their doom with a vintage worker rush!

![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/U9IT8WC74J461539711890337.gif)

You’ll also receive a new portrait that fuses all three races into one hellish, grotesque, and majestic creature.

![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/pw/PWZTH3OJMYHB1539712101627.png)

On top of the StarCraft loot, there’s a whole cache of loot waiting for you in other Blizzard games as well:

**[Overwatch](https://blizzcon.com/en-gb/news/22484224/strike-from-the-shadows-as-demon-hunter-sombra)** **–** Demon Hunter Sombra Legendary Skin

**[Heroes of the Storm](https://blizzcon.com/en-gb/news/22533809/ride-into-the-nexus-in-style-with-the-blizzcon-virtual-ticket)** **–** Nexus Razorgrin Mount and Spray, BlizzCon 2018 Banner and Portrai**t**

**[World of Warcraft](https://blizzcon.com/en-gb/news/22551243/bring-home-the-blizzcon-wow-classic-demo-with-the-virtual-ticket)** **–** Mantle of the Horde and Alliance, Stormwind and Orgrimmar Champion’s War Banners; plus, play the BlizzCon WoW Classic Demo at home this November.

**[Hearthstone](https://blizzcon.com/en-gb/news/22549776/beef-up-your-hearthstone-collection-with-the-blizzcon-virtual-ticket) –** BlizzCon 2018 card back and a total of 10 card packs for your collection.
