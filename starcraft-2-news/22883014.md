# WCS Winter Round of 32: A Viewer’s Guide

- URL: https://news.blizzard.com/en-gb/article/22883014/wcs-winter-round-of-32-a-viewers-guide
![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/g3/G3Y5I1ZOP17Z1545094662928.jpg)

> Competition in Europe and the Americas begins January 31.

---

After six qualification events and ladder races in Europe and the Americas, the top 32 players in each region have emerged for the StarCraft II World Championship Series (WCS) Winter event. These players will be fighting for their share of a USD $60,000 prize pool per region—for more information about WCS Winter, check out [the program announcement](https://starcraft2.com/en-gb/news/22838228) and the [official rules](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/gallery/ng/NGUSGRSGRE6G1545262444649.pdf).

The first matches for the Round of 32 begin this Thursday, January 31. Here’s what you need to know.

## Dates and Times:

The WCS Winter Ro32 matches for both Europe and the Americas will be broadcast Thursdays through Sundays for the next two weeks, beginning Thursday, January 31, and concluding Sunday, February 10.

- WCS Winter Europe Ro32: 4 p.m. to 8 p.m CET/7 a.m. to 11 a.m. PT
- WCS Winter Americas Ro32: 11 a.m. to 3 p.m. PT/8 p.m. to 12 a.m. CET

Be sure to download the Blizzard Esports Mobile App, available on [iOS](https://itunes.apple.com/us/app/blizzard-esports/id1435533595?mt=8) and [Android](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.blizzard.esports&hl=en_US), to easily check the broadcast times for your time zone. You can also set up your notifications for StarCraft II, so that you will receive a push when each day goes live.
