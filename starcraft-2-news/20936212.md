# StarCraft II 7th Anniversary Portrait

- URL: https://news.blizzard.com/en-gb/article/20936212/starcraft-ii-7th-anniversary-portrait
![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/i6/I6FERLTB9DDW1500602470177.jpg)

> StarCraft II turns seven on July 27 and we’re giving away a free portrait to celebrate!

---

It’s been seven years since StarCraft II stormed into the hearts and minds of gamers around the world. Wings of Liberty kicked off the epic trilogy and the Ghosts of the Past trailer gave everyone a taste of what was to come. Do you remember how it made you feel when you saw it for the first time?

[![Video](https://markdown-videos-api.jorgenkh.no/youtube/C_E83GfWM-A?width=1280&height=720)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=C_E83GfWM-A)

It’s been an amazing journey since then and through it all, the community has been instrumental in the path that StarCraft II has taken. Hundreds of balances changes and two expansions later, you all have helped shape the game that we see today.

Even though we’ve been working hard to bring the first [War Chest](https://warchest.starcraft2.com) into your hands, we also wanted to say thank you by giving all players who complete one game in Co-op or Multiplayer mode a custom 7th Anniversary Portrait. This promotion will be available starting today and ending on August 3.

![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/page_media/a4/A4S6WPY9E98E1500662317413.png)

We can’t thank you enough for the support that you’ve shown us in these seven years. We hope you have found epic moments in StarCraft II, whether it was in the single-player campaign, pushing for that next ladder promotion, or watching amazing esports action. GLHF!
