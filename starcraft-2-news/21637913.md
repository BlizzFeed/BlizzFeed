# StarCraft II Balance Update – March 19, 2018

- URL: https://news.blizzard.com/en-gb/article/21637913/starcraft-ii-balance-update-march-19-2018
![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/fl/FLSSDCPRQR6I1513621123819.jpg)

> We’re changing the Zerg early-game.

---

Today’s balance change is focused on reducing the early aggression of Zerg that was particularly problematic for Protoss players. For more insight on the thoughts behind these changes, browse through our last two [community](https://eu.battle.net/forums/en/sc2/topic/17618093547) [updates](https://eu.battle.net/forums/en/sc2/topic/17618044160#1).

Zerg

- The Overlord’s Mutate Ventral Sacs upgrade now requires a Lair instead of an Evolution Chamber.

With this change now live, we’ll also be taking down the balance testing queue. Thanks for everyone who helped by playing and giving us feedback. We’ll see you on the ladder.
