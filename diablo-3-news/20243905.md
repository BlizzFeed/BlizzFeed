Patch 2.4.2 and Season 7 are in the wild, and the community has been busy creating helpful guides to make the most of them! Today, we’d like to highlight some of the best we’ve seen — including a few gems you may have missed.

---

**Patch 2.4.2 Guides**

For those who haven't explored the most recent patch and need a summary, Curse created a video highlighting the main changes. If you need a no-frills recap, this should get you caught up!

[![Video](https://markdown-videos-api.jorgenkh.no/youtube/u-Z-cyRayn4?width=1280&height=720)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u-Z-cyRayn4)

Want more great patch videos? Bluddshed has an excellent guide to Torment XIII [rewards](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XI4gy9MWW7Y), and Rhykker rounded up [top builds](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1qbgx81wBSI) to try in Patch 2.4.2. As always, our [patch notes](http://us.battle.net/d3/en/blog/20210379) list all the patch changes.

**Season 7 Guides**

Deadset recently collected Season 7 progression videos for each class [in one place](https://www.reddit.com/r/Diablo/comments/4warx7/deadsets_guide_to_242_detailed_beginnergroupsolo/), along with a handy [beginner’s guide](http://www.icy-veins.com/d3/beginners-guide-to-seasons-in-diablo-3) to Seasons for those who haven’t tried them. If you’re curious how to get Haedrig's Gift in Season 7, Bluddshed does an excellent job of breaking down the process in this helpful video.

[![Video](https://markdown-videos-api.jorgenkh.no/youtube/lnLjfPNKXv0?width=1280&height=720)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lnLjfPNKXv0)

If all you need is a quick leveling guide, [Uthred](https://www.reddit.com/user/Uthred)’s [spreadsheet](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1I4qvNabBnI9LoDkp9o2_kP2j4P7LD-MvfTLhuNldUz8/htmlview?usp=sharing&sle=true) is worth viewing. It lets you know when to swap skills and craft gear as you progress toward level 70, and includes helpful tips. The guide’s minimalist approach is its strength, helping you focus on slaying and making changes as needed.
