> The Diablo III PC Battle Chest has arrived just in time for the holidays, bundling both Diablo III and Reaper of Souls at a new low price.

---

This holiday season, gather your friends and prepare for the defense of the mortal realms . . . the Diablo III PC Battle Chest is available now at a new low price of 29.99 €! The Battle Chest includes both Diablo III and the Reaper of Souls expansion, together at last in one definitive volume.

You can [purchase or gift a digital copy in](https://eu.battle.net/shop/en/product/game/diablo) the Shop, or you can pick up a physical box at retail, arriving in stores starting November 29th, just in time for the holidays. Check your local retailer for availability.
