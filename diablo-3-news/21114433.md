If the ways of the Necromancer remain a mystery to you, here’s your chance to experience them first-hand just in time for the spookiest season of the year. Diablo III is on sale now until October 31, including the [Rise of the Necromancer pack](https://eu.battle.net/shop/en/product/diablo-iii-rise-of-the-necromancer) for the first time on PC!

You can unearth a trove of new Diablo III content, such as the Necromancer hero class, two additional character slots and stash tabs, the Wings of the Crypt Guardian, the Half-Formed Golem pet, and more, for just €9.99/£8.49. That’s more than 30% off the original price.

New to Diablo? You can also grab the Diablo III: Battle Chest, which includes the original game and the Reaper of Souls expansion set, for just €19.99/£16.99—the perfect opportunity to join your friends in Sanctuary. Visit the [Blizzard Shop](https://eu.battle.net/shop/en/product/game/diablo) for all discounted pricing before the sale ends on October 31.

Console players can also take advantage of the sale for PlayStation 4 and Xbox One, including the Eternal Collection, which combines Diablo III, Reaper of Souls, and the Rise of the Necromancer in one definitive volume. See the [PlayStation Store](http://store.playstation.com/) or [Xbox Store](http://store.xbox.com/) for pricing and details.
