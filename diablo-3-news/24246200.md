# Blend Devilish Brews with Diablo: Drinks, Potions, & Elixirs

> Ghoulish concoctions and bold tinctures await in Diablo: Drinks, Potions, & Elixirs, a new book from Insight Editions.

---

As darkness threatens to engulf Sanctuary, precious few are willing to stand against it. Those who do should take heed of this tome, which contains collected knowledge of Sanctuary’s most skilled alchemists.

*Diablo: Drinks, Potions, & Elixirs* is a cocktail book that showcases over 60 recipes for drinks and provisions from across Sanctuary.

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/gallery/2l/2L8R4NMHK3I71762216884677.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/gallery/2l/2L8R4NMHK3I71762216884677.png)

Written by Senior Narrative Designer Ryan Quinn and Cassandra Reeder, author of *The Video Game Chef*, this book features recipes inspired by classes, creatures, and characters across nearly 30 years of Diablo games. This guide of potent mixtures are sure to excite the palate of the most discerning gourmands.

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/gallery/27/27AFF0OJ6C8R1762216995004.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/gallery/27/27AFF0OJ6C8R1762216995004.png)

*Diablo: Drinks, Potions, & Elixirs* is certain to mystify and entice you to try potions made of the darkest arts. Slake your thirst with the chilling Frost Nova, coat your insides with the savory Abomination, or surrender to Mother’s Kiss.

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/gallery/my/MY8UUPHHVE8H1762217078007.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/gallery/my/MY8UUPHHVE8H1762217078007.png)
