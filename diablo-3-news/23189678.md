## Diablo IV Unveiled

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/gallery/4r/4R1U2U3XELCL1572621035551.jpg)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/gallery/4r/4R1U2U3XELCL1572621035551.jpg)

We revealed the next major entry in the Diablo franchise today to an incredible reception. With a new darkly gruesome cinematic, fully playable demo on the convention floor, and an exciting list of panels to continue the deluge of information tomorrow, we can’t wait to show you more about what awaits the fate of Sanctuary.

[![Video](https://markdown-videos-api.jorgenkh.no/youtube/K8bsv2fUnDM?width=1280&height=720)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=K8bsv2fUnDM)

We’ve prepared a more comprehensive blog with today’s details, so check it out if you haven’t already!

## Lilith Wings Revealed

We know you’ve been waiting eagerly to find out more about the Diablo III Virtual Ticket goodie. Wait no longer and feast your eyes upon Lilith’s Embrace, modeled after the wings of Lilith, Daughter of Hatred.

![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/gallery/fk/FKTIPB5C0BT61572656251446.gif)

All Virtual Ticket and BlizzCon pass holders will receive these wings automatically in Diablo III as soon as Patch 2.6.7 launches later this month.
