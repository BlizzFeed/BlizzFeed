You’ve heard of One Punch Monk—but now it’s time to deliver a swift boot to the head! In today’s build, [Davlok#1847](http://us.battle.net/d3/en/profile/Davlok-1847/hero/1205254) props up [Lashing Tail Kick](http://us.battle.net/d3/en/class/monk/active/lashing-tail-kick) as the star of this fiery flurry of kicks to debut the return of Play Your Way!

---

### About the Build

Be prepared for a bit of investment—Davlok’s One Kick Monk centers around the [Legacy of Nightmares](http://us.battle.net/d3/en/item/litany-of-the-undaunted) ring set, which means collecting a whole lot of Ancient items. Still, the results are an extremely satisfying line of combustion bursting from rapid-paced kicks that will melt just about anything encountered.

[![Video](https://markdown-videos-api.jorgenkh.no/youtube/E7NV4f_ERkY?width=1280&height=720)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E7NV4f_ERkY)

This is a great change of pace for fulfilling your basic tasks, like farming bounties and Torment X Nephalem Rifts. With the inclusion of [Nemesis Bracers](http://us.battle.net/d3/en/item/nemesis-bracers), it’s a particularly ideal method for gathering up Death’s Breath’s without resorting to the [Sage’s Journey](http://us.battle.net/d3/en/artisan/blacksmith/recipe/sages-apogee) set while still feeling like a total martial arts badass.

![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/gallery/A5H22NN3MDKU1466546079337.gif)\
*Davlok was* [*way luckier*](https://youtu.be/UzaSDWMxg5U) *than I was while testing his build!*

You will want to make a couple of tweaks for survivability if you intend to tackle higher-end Greater Rifts, however. Try changing [Epiphany](http://us.battle.net/d3/en/class/monk/active/epiphany) (Insight) to [Epiphany](http://us.battle.net/d3/en/class/monk/active/epiphany) (Desert Shroud) or working in [Cyclone Strike](http://us.battle.net/d3/en/class/monk/active/cyclone-strike) with [Lefebrvre’s Soliloquy](http://us.battle.net/d3/en/item/lefebvres-soliloquy) for that extra defense.
