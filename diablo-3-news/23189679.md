---

BlizzCon 2019 has come and gone, but not without incredible announcements and unforgettable memories! Thank you from the bottom of our hellish hearts to our community, whether you joined us live in Anaheim or from the comfort of your couch via Virtual Ticket. We miss you already and can’t wait to get back to work!

In case you missed any of the info extravaganza we threw your way, we’ve compiled all the Diablo-oriented highlights right here in one handy guide. We’ll be keeping it updated throughout the week as more recaps, interviews, and community features become available. Let us know if we missed something—we’ll be happy to add it in!

***Last Updated: November 4***
