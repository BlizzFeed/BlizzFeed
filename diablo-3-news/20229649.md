Pets, turrets, and chakrams. If that’s grabbed your interest, then you’re bound to love this week’s Play Your Way build from [Sightless#11241](http://us.battle.net/d3/en/profile/Sightless-11241/hero/42022737)! He’s taken a Demon Hunter who’s armed to the teeth and run a slight twist on a traditional [Embodiment of the Marauder](http://us.battle.net/d3/en/item/marauders-carapace) build. Let’s dive right in!

---

### About the Build

Before we get started, I want to make sure to mention this build was submitted prior to the launch of Patch 2.4.2, which went live [earlier this week](http://us.battle.net/d3/en/blog/20210379/). Originally, [Spike Trap](http://us.battle.net/d3/en/class/demon-hunter/active/spike-trap) was part of the build. However, with the changes made to [Spike Trap](http://us.battle.net/d3/en/class/demon-hunter/active/spike-trap), there was no way to detonate the traps with this build, so we’ve swapped in [Marked for Death](http://us.battle.net/d3/en/class/demon-hunter/active/marked-for-death) for some added damage and utility. That said, what you can expect here is a fairly straightforward [Marauder’s](http://us.battle.net/d3/en/item/marauders-carapace) build, where your [Sentries](http://us.battle.net/d3/en/class/demon-hunter/active/sentry) and [Companions](http://us.battle.net/d3/en/class/demon-hunter/active/companion) do a fair share of heavy lifting while you hang back and spam [Chakram](http://us.battle.net/d3/en/class/demon-hunter/active/chakram) to your heart’s content!

[![Video](https://markdown-videos-api.jorgenkh.no/youtube/b3pUsAE1O4Y?width=1280&height=720)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=b3pUsAE1O4Y)

\
*A tale of close calls and severed limbs.*

The combination of [Sword of Ill Will](http://us.battle.net/d3/en/item/sword-of-ill-will) and [Spines of Seething Hatred](http://us.battle.net/d3/en/item/spines-of-seething-hatred) means never losing damage from lack of Hatred, and [Chakram](http://us.battle.net/d3/en/class/demon-hunter/active/chakram) (Twin Chakrams) leaves behind a beautifully massacred mess wherever it goes. [Vault](http://us.battle.net/d3/en/class/demon-hunter/active/vault) serves as some much needed utility to keep yourself out of danger, as this build does suffer from being a bit squishy. Still, there’s room for improvement, and as-is it ~~severs~~ serves itself quite well in Torment X or below.
