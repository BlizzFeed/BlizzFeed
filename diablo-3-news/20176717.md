Masters of the mightiest elements, Barbarians can take advantage of multiple damage types to devastating effect. In this week’s Play Your Way, [Sindro#1947](http://eu.battlenet/d3/en/profile/sindro-1947/hero/76849656) boosts his generator build through the diversity of [Convention of Elements](http://eu.battlenet/d3/en/item/convention-of-elements) in a way that’s a [Bash](http://eu.battlenet/d3/en/class/barbarian/active/bash) to play!

---

### About the Build

Utilizing a combination of [Cleave](http://eu.battlenet/d3/en/class/barbarian/active/cleave) and [Bash](http://eu.battlenet/d3/en/class/barbarian/active/bash), Sindro has propped up these two primary skills into major damage dealers with their respective supplementary Legendary items. To keep his fury fueled (and his Hardcore hero well-protected), he’s donned the full [Immortal King’s Call](http://eu.battlenet/d3/en/item/immortal-kings-eternal-reign) set for both its defensive and offensive capabilities. Lastly, timing plays a key role, as you’ll want a rhythm of rotating between [Cleave](http://eu.battlenet/d3/en/class/barbarian/active/cleave) and [Bash](http://eu.battlenet/d3/en/class/barbarian/active/bash) for when Physical and Fire elements are being boosted by your [Convention of Elements](http://eu.battlenet/d3/en/item/convention-of-elements).

[![Video](https://markdown-videos-api.jorgenkh.no/youtube/d22al2RriL0?width=1280&height=720)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=d22al2RriL0)

\
*Not going to lie—if I could have held this build for BlizzCon season just for the pun, I probably would have.*

As a Hardcore player, it’s important to play conservatively, and Sindro has pushed through Greater Rift 60 as of the time of this blog. Incredible amounts of splash damage from [Battle Rage](http://eu.battlenet/d3/en/class/barbarian/active/battle-rage) (Bloodshed), [Bash](http://eu.battlenet/d3/en/class/barbarian/active/bash) (Pulverize), and some supplemental Area Damage allows the Convention Barb to tear through large groups with relative ease. If attacks are timed correctly, the potential for burst damage is astounding!
