*We regularly see amazing fan art in the Diablo community. In order to give those who create these legendary works the attention they deserve, we’re giving them the spotlight in this column.*

The Lord of Terror has been portrayed in a variety of ways, but it’s rare to see art depicting him make a powerful visual statement using a very limited color palette. “Diablo” by Laisen Bi does just that, showing the Prime Evil in all of his destructive glory.

Click the image below for the full view, and be sure to check out Laisen’s other works here: [www.artstation.com/artist/leizhenbi](http://www.artstation.com/artist/leizhenbi)
