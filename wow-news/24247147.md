Adventurers, your banks and bags are about to get lighter! With the arrival of “The Warning” content update, reagents you’ve stashed since Classic World of Warcraft—right up through The War Within—have a renewed purpose. Crafters can transform their collections of herbs, ores, leathers, and more into unique Housing decor items.

Whether you’re looking to personalize your own home or make a tidy profit on the auction house, there’s never been a better time to revisit your old materials and see what new creations await. From enchanting string lights to sturdy blacksmith benches, the possibilities span every expansion and every crafting table. What will you create first?

We've also introduced lumber as a new resource that can be collected and used for various crafting recipes.
