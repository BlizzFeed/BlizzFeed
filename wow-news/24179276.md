# Get in on the Last Wave of Free Tickets for the 30th Anniversary Event in London!

> Heroes of Azeroth, the final chance to claim a free ticket to the Warcraft 30th Anniversary Celebration in London is coming on the 18th of February at 15:00GMT!

---

It’s almost time! We are calling all champions to gather for the Warcraft 30th Anniversary Celebration in London on 22 February at Soane Hall, London. The doors open at 17:00 GMT for a celebration well worth of the three decades of Warcraft history.

---

### Last Chance to Secure Your Place in History!

The final opportunity to claim your free ticket to the London event is on the 18th of February at 15:00 GMT. Follow [this link](https://www.tixr.com/groups/warcraft30-world-tour/events/world-of-warcraft-30-world-tour-128156) to secure your free ticket.

The Warcraft 30th Anniversary Celebration is shaping up to be a night of revelry, music, and epic moments! We cannot wait to greet you all!

### Meet the developers

World of Warcraft developers Holly Longdale, Ion Hazzikostas, and Maria Hamilton will join Hearthstone developers Christian Scharling and Cora Georgiou in a developer meet & greet.

Before embarking on this grand adventure, make sure your inventory is stocked with the essentials:

- Your battle-hardened spirit – Whether you fight for the Horde or the Alliance, bring your undying passion for Warcraft and prepare for an evening of epic proportions!
