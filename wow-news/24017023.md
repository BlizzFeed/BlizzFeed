#### Here are this week's highlights:

#### BlizzCon® 2023

- BlizzCon® 2023 returns November 3 and 4, and [additional tickets](https://www.axs.com/blizzcon2023) are still available. Can’t attend? Don’t worry. You can watch all the content from the Anaheim Arena for free. To commemorate the return of BlizzCon, you can also purchase the [BlizzCon Collection](https://shop.battle.net/product/blizzcon-collection?_gl=1*105nmam*_ga*Njc5ODY4OTUwLjE2ODQ4NjIwNjA.*_ga_VYKNV7C0S3*MTY5Njk4NTY2NS4xMDMuMS4xNjk2OTg2Mjk3LjU3LjAuMA..) with a new set of digital goodies for World of Warcraft and other Blizzard games.

#### Dragonflight

- **Details on the Headless Horseman’s Mount**: The first drop of the day receives a [bonus chance](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/headless-horseman-and-the-hallows-end-event/1690342/67) to earn the mount, which scales with the number of curses applied for the encounter.
- **Guardians of the Dream PTR Update**: This week’s [development notes](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/guardians-of-the-dream-ptr-development-notes/1666552/11) included updates for Demon Hunters, Monks, Rogues, and more. Updates were posted for the [Mythic+](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/feedback-mythic-testing-october-19th-october-23rd/1691272) and the [Raid](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/amirdrassil-the-dream%E2%80%99s-hope-raid-testing-schedule/1669121/11) testing schedules.
- **Hallow’s End Feedback**: With the start of Hallow’s End, the WoW Community Council began to share their experiences and [feedback](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/feedback-recent-updates-to-holiday-events/1690956/5) on the event.
- **Acquiring The Scarlet Key**: Some additional [details](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/scarlet-monestary-keys-old-dungeon/1690733/2) were confirmed on how players can earn this item, which gives people access to an older version of the Scarlet Monastery to all characters on the account.

#### Classic

- **Weekly Raid Quest**: An issue prevented the quest from being available; this was [not intentional](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/no-weekly-raid-quest-after-maintenance/1690350/10) and has since been resolved.
