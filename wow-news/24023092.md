On stage at BlizzCon 2023, *World of Warcraft* Executive Producer Holly Longdale took to the stage to announce the next expansion for *World of Warcraft Classic*. Next year, Classic progression realms will advance from Wrath Classic to Cataclysm Classic, with a few changes based on player feedback and requests.

Prepare to face Deathwing in Cataclysm Classic in the first half of 2024.
