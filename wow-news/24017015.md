#### Here are this week's highlights:

#### BlizzCon® 2023

- BlizzCon® 2023 returns November 3 and 4, and [additional tickets](https://www.axs.com/blizzcon2023) are still available. Can’t attend? Don’t worry. You can watch all the content from the Anaheim Arena for free. To commemorate the return of BlizzCon, you can also purchase the [BlizzCon Collection](https://shop.battle.net/product/blizzcon-collection?_gl=1*105nmam*_ga*Njc5ODY4OTUwLjE2ODQ4NjIwNjA.*_ga_VYKNV7C0S3*MTY5Njk4NTY2NS4xMDMuMS4xNjk2OTg2Mjk3LjU3LjAuMA..) with a new set of digital goodies for World of Warcraft and other Blizzard games.

#### Dragonflight

- **Guardians of the Dream PTR:** We released the latest build for Guardians of the Dream PTR on Wednesday. This week’s [development notes](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/guardians-of-the-dream-ptr-development-notes/1666552/10) included changes for Demon Hunters, Monks, Rogues, Shaman, and more. Updates were also posted for the [Mythic+ testing schedule](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/feedback-mythic-testing-october-12th-october-16th/1687359), the [raid testing schedule](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/amirdrassil-the-dream%E2%80%99s-hope-raid-testing-schedule/1669121/9), and a new [Brawl with the Blues](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/brawl-with-the-blues-in-battleground-blitz-on-october-13/1686800) PvP event.

#### WoW Classic

- **Icecrown Citadel:** On Thursday, some final [notes](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/final-icc-notes-thank-you-for-testing-the-343-ptr/1687398) were posted for Wrath Classic regarding the Icecrown Citadel Raid, including implementing a Heroic difficulty attempts counter and a fix for Lord Marrowgar. Thanks to everyone who participated in the PTR!
