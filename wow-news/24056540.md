It’s a Leap Year and February gains an extra day for our Community Discords members to help keep you warm inside with a variety of new activities.

---

### New Members Here! Lord of the Reins Discord

The Community Discords Program is pleased to welcome the Lord of the Reins Discord to the program.

Lord of the Reins is a mount collecting community specializing in Group Finder activities. People can host and find groups on Lord of the Reins for instanced and open world activities for mount drops and rewards.

**Join Lord of the Reins** - <https://discord.gg/lordofthereins>

---

### Dungeon Dojo

Seven Days a week the Dojo is helping players learn the dungeons in *Dragonflight* Season 3. In addition, experienced players will run all key levels, pushing to the highest keys,  achieving Keystone Master, and Keystone Hero, with the added bonus of unlocking all portals!

Every Friday the Dojo organizes weekly raid nights in Amirdrassil, the Dream's Hope, Normal and Heroic Difficulty, for those who want to experience this end game content without the stress. These raid sessions are open to EU and NA players. The groups are generally a mixture of both very experienced players and new players.

##### The Dungeon Dojo Invitational is coming back!

On the first of February, the Dungeon Dojo will open to interested players who want to take part in the seasonal tournament! Please contact any Sensei in the Dojo for further information. Registration for the teams and a full description of the event will be published on the Discord toward mid-February.

Join Dungeon Dojo - <https://discord.com/invite/gsF3TSx7ju>

### GoldGoblinNET

GoldGoblinNET is organizing an exciting quiz for their community!

The quiz consists of 15 questions from all areas of *World of Warcraft.* If you get 12 out of 15 questions right, you qualify for a prize draw!

If you fail the quiz, you can, of course, try again.

> **Start Quiz:** 03.02.2024  12:00 Uhr German Time\
> **End Quiz:** 10.02.2024 12:00 Uhr German Time

Join GoldGoblinNET - <https://discord.gg/goldgoblinnet>

### Starship February Events

##### Four Into The Fire** **- Starship x Timewalking Discord Collaboration

> **Date & Time:** February 4th, Live at [Twitch.tv/starship\_eu](https://twitch.tv/starship_eu)

Starship and the Timewalking Discord proudly present "Four Into The Fire," a thrilling 4-player Firelands Speed Run challenge! Tune in on Twitch to witness teams compete for the fastest Timewalking Firelands raid completion. The event boasts an impressive prize pool for the top 3 teams, including The War Within™ Epic Editions, BlizzCon Digital Packs, and 2.4 million in gold!

![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/0XO88NXCVJBJ1705965838062.png)

#### Sunday Raid School for New Players and Alternate Characters

> **Date & Time:** Every Sunday from 20:30 Realm time

Are you new to raiding or looking to enhance your skills in a supportive and non-judgemental environment? Join Starship every Sunday for their Raid School. All skill levels are welcome as their experienced raid leaders guide you through current content raid strategies. If you can't make it in-game, catch the action on Twitch.

##### Starship Stream Team Launch - February

Starship is excited to announce the upcoming launch of their own Stream Team in February. If you're a content creator and believe you'd be a great fit, reach out to them! Various opportunities, including casting roles, are available. Stay tuned for more details.

**Join Starship - Discord.gg/starshipeu**
