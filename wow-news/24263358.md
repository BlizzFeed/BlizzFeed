## Large Starter Decor Pack

Bring big changes to your new home with the Large Starter Decor Pack. Includes two Gazebos plus 2,500 Hearthsteel to help decorate the home of your dreams.

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/5O9LU4O2M8BE1772220352282.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/5O9LU4O2M8BE1772220352282.png)

The Large Starter Decor Pack*\** includes the following:

- 2,500 Hearthsteel
- 2 Spring Blossom Gazebo

You can also purchase decor items in the Lush Garden Decor Pack individually from the in-game shop or the Battle.net shop.

Purchase Hearthsteel in various denominations at the rate of 100 Hearthsteel per £0.90.

## What is Hearthsteel?

Hearthsteel is a new in-game currency for *World of Warcraft* that is purchased with real money using your Battle.net balance to acquire Housing items from the Battle.net shop and within the game. Visit the shop to see the available Hearthsteel bundle options.

Learn more about the Hearthsteel virtual currency in our [previously published article](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/news/24242736/).
