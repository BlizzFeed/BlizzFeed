# Introducing the World of Warcraft® BlizzCon Guild Clash!

- URL: https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/en-gb/news/24008688/introducing-the-world-of-warcraft-blizzcon-guild-clash
![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/4g/4G6S8EBO88WJ1695069193207.png)

> WoW® Esports is excited to present the latest competitive showcase live from BlizzCon® 2023, featuring Echo, Golden Guardians, Liquid, and Method! These top guilds will be pitted against one another in a series of World of Warcraft® competitions to determine which guild will emerge victorious!

---

Greetings, Blizzard fans and esports enthusiasts! Get ready to mark your calendars and clear your schedules for the newest event at this year's BlizzCon - the World of Warcraft Guild Clash! Guild Clash is an all-day, three-event tournament featuring four of the most formidable guilds from World of Warcraft. Prepare for an action-packed, adrenaline-fueled day filled with epic battles, camaraderie, and unforgettable moments. Guild Clash starts Saturday, November 4, 2023 starting when BlizzCon doors open at the Anaheim Convention Center.

---

### BlizzCon Guild Clash Format

##### Event 1: 3v3 Arena PVP

The 4 Guilds PvP rosters will enter the gates of the arena, but only one can emerge victorious! For this event, each guild will field their roster of gladiators to face-off in a single-elimination bracket. Matches will be best-of-five, with a team knocked out each round.

##### Event 2: 5v5 Mythic Dungeon Races

This will be the first time seeing the latest Dragonflight Season 3 Dungeons live on broadcast. In this event, four teams will race in an MDI-style head-to-head single-elimination format to determine which team has the most determined dungeoneers!

##### Event 3: New Content Showcase

Watch this space for more information on a 3rd event showcase to wrap the show!
