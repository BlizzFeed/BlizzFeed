# Shop Updates: Appearance Change Service and Battle for Azeroth

- URL: https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/en-gb/news/23523208/shop-updates-appearance-change-service-and-battle-for-azeroth
![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/bo/BOWAVJGBBSWH1578452828763.jpg)

> The Appearance Change Service has retired from the Blizzard Shop and with the release of the Shadowlands pre-expansion patch, Battle for Azeroth will be included with your World of Warcraft subscription.

---

With the release of the pre-expansion patch, players will be able to seek out a Barber Shop for a variety of character-customization options, including the ability to change your character’s gender, hair, facial features, and more. Due to this upcoming new in-game option, the Appearance Change service for Shadowlands has now been retired from the Blizzard Shop.

#### Battle for Azeroth Included In Your WoW Subscription

With the release of the Shadowlands pre-expansion patch, Battle for Azeroth will be included with your World of Warcraft subscription—no additional purchase required.
