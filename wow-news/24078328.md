### Starship

#### The MD-WHY?

- Date: 28th April

Starship community proudly presents "The MD-WHY" – A new Mythic+ tournament. Teams gather in the usual Mythic+ contest format, but with a twist. Starship are adding their own unique Affixes! Join the action on [Twitch](https://www.twitch.tv/starship_eu) on 28 April. Want to take part? Head to their Discord and say, “hi!”

#### Sunday Raid School for New Players and Alts

- Date & Time: Every Sunday from 20:30 realm time.

Are you new to raiding or looking to enhance your skills in a supportive and nonjudgemental environment? Join Starship every Sunday for their Raid School. All skill levels are welcome as our experienced raid leaders guide you through current content raid strategies. If you can’t make it in-game, catch the action on Twitch.

#### Streamers Wanted!

Starship is looking for streamers to represent the Starship community. If you're a content creator and believe you'd be a great fit, reach out to the team via the Starship Discord! Various opportunities, including casting roles, will be available.

[Join the Starship Discord today.](https://discord.gg/starshipeu)

### ChromieDE (DE)

ChromieDE Discord has two exciting events coming in April!

#### Plunderstorm screenshot contest

- Dates: from April 8th to April 15th

Join the community and post your coolest wins, most epic deaths, or the best time you spent hiding in a bush! The three screenshots rated best by the community will receive prizes.

#### 2nd Timelost Event >Storming Haala<

- Dates: End of April

The Timelost Project is still running as the ChromieDE community continues to roam around Outland!

What is the Timelost project? Learn in [their article here](https://www.chromie.de/erlebe-wow-neu-timelost-chromiede-kommt/).

ChromieDE is delighted to invite you to a new Timelost Project event at the end of April - Storming Haala's!

Two raid groups, one Horde and one Alliance are about to replay the Burning Crusade Haala opening PvP event. The goal of this event is to relive the experience in a more relaxed, role-play (RP) focused way.

### Dungeon Dojo

Last but absolutely not least, Dungeon Dojo has a lot in store this April!

#### Mythic+ Monday

Every Monday the whole community comes together to help each other learn how to do Mythic+ content. All are welcome including beginners, returning players, or those who are learning a new class or spec. This is also the perfect time to achieve the Key Stone Master and Key Stone Hero titles before the end of the season.

#### Ahead Of The Curve Achievement in collaboration with the guild Honolulu

- Date: 5th of April

With the help of Purke and the guild Honolulu, they will be taking members of the Dungeon Dojo into Amirdrassil, the Dream’s Hope raid in Heroic difficulty to kill Fyrakk, score a nice achievement, and get a wonderful Dragonriding skin for flying mounts! They will be running as many attempts as people sign up for!

#### A World Anew - Taivan Meta-Achievement Run!

With the introduction of this new achievement to obtain Taivan, the good boy, the Dojo will be running several sessions to team up and do meta-achievements that can be done while in a group!

- On the 12th of April, The Dojo will run Vault of the Incarnates, on Normal difficulty, followed by an evening farming Cobalt Assembly reputation.
- On the 19th of April, the Dojo will run the Aberrus, the Shadowed Crucible raid dungeon on Normal difficulty, and then will run several Mythic 0, for another meta-achievement.

#### Break The Meta

As with every season so far, the Dungeon Dojo will be participating in Break the Meta, an event organized by Raider.IO, to showcase all those classes that usually don't get enough of the spotlight. Several groups will run, helping everyone who wants to obtain the Mythic Dungeon International banner toy for *Dragonflight* Season 3.

[Join](https://discord.com/invite/gsF3TSx7ju)[the Dungeon Dojo Discord.](http://discord.com/invite/gsF3TSx7ju)
