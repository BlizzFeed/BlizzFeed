#### Here are this week's highlights:

- **Fractures in Time PTR Updated:**On Tuesday, the latest [PTR update](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/dragonflight-fractures-in-time-ptr-development-notes/1587226/4) was released for Dragonflight Fractures in Time. This included class tuning changes, [Augmentation Evoker](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/feedback-augmentation-evokers/1591583) updates, and [Time Rifts](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/feedback-time-rifts/1592372).
- **Legendary Cracked Titan Gem Availability:**The Cracked Titan Gem, which leads to a [legendary Evoker weapon](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/evoker-legendary-quest-mythic-sarkareth-drop/1590392/99), has a chance to drop in all difficulties after Mythic Scalecommander Sarkareth is defeated in the region.
- **Item Upgrade System:**While nothing is set in stone at this time, the worries about bag space and needing to run lower-level content [are being discussed](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/feedback-new-upgrade-system/1585679/11) as potential system changes in Dragonflight Season 3.
- **Quest and Achievements:**An explanation was posted in the WoW Community Council forum for why the count for [quests completed](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/updated-seeker/1566438/5) doesn't always align with the achievement progress and how this will change in Fractures in Time.

#### WoW Classic

- **Official Classic Hardcore is Coming:**The development team is working to bring official [Hardcore Classic](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/official-classic-hardcore-is-coming/1588836) gameplay to WoW Classic. More details will be coming soon.
