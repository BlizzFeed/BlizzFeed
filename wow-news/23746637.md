# Shadowlands: New Arenas Coming in Eternity's End

- URL: https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/en-gb/news/23746637/shadowlands-new-arenas-coming-in-eternity-s-end
![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/yu/YUTFQ8Z0EDDW1636680113337.jpg)

> Seek glory in two new Arenas in Eternity's End: Maldraxxus Coliseum and the Enigma Arena.

---

> *Update: We previously planned to bring two new Arenas to the game in the Eternity’s End content update: the Maldraxxus Coliseum and the Enigma Arena. Upon further review, we’ve decided to devote more development time to make Enigma Arena as good as we expect it to be. This new Arena includes moving features, and we want to make sure we get it working right before release. While we’re very excited about it, we’re now planning to add it to the game in a subsequent content update, and not with the beginning of Season 3.*

Eternity's End brings two brand-new Arenas, calling upon seasoned gladiators and newcomers alike to conquer their foes in valorous combat. Seek glory in the Maldraxxus Coliseum and the Enigma Arena.

#### Maldraxxus Coliseum

![Maldraxxus Coliseum](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/gallery/CV90MS03NPCZ1636679789641.JPG)

Only the strong survive the Maldraxxus Coliseum.

![Maldraxxus Coliseum](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/gallery/WMMO6QRQCIIA1636679727631.JPG)

Maldraxxus Coliseum in the Theather of Pain.

For eons, the Necrolords of Maldraxxus honed their craft at the Maldraxxus Coliseum in the Theater of Pain. Now the Arena is open to any would-be challengers—and there's no shortage of champions willing to test their mettle. Prove yourself worthy. Only the strong will survive.

---

#### Enigma Arena

![Enigma Arena](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/gallery/DLNASXSB019I1636679728028.jpg)

Harness the power of the First Ones and change the field of battle.

![Enigma Arena](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/gallery/N23K7YN1NYQI1636679740619.jpg)

Enigma Arena in Zereth Mortis.

The mysterious Enigma Arena from Zereth Mortis was created by the Progenitors and serves as an ever-changing battlefield. A switch located at the center of the Arena can be used to create cover for yourself and your allies—or deny it from your opponents. Clever strategists will control and dictate the flow of battle; those who cannot adapt will surely be defeated.

#### Meet Your Foes in Battle

Look forward to these two new Arenas coming soon to the PTR and later on when Eternity's End launches. We'll see you in the Shadowlands, champion!
