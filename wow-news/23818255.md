# Building Azeroth: Making Death Knights

> Take a journey beyond the grave to discover more about the making of the Death Knight class.

---

In this episode of "Building Azeroth," *Wrath of the Lich King* Game Director Tom Chilton and Principal Game Designer Kris Zierhut take us on a journey beyond the grave into the making of *World of Warcraft’s* first iconic hero class—the Death Knight.

[![Video](https://markdown-videos-api.jorgenkh.no/youtube/TcG2NI4LFzw?width=1280&height=720)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TcG2NI4LFzw)

Harness the powers of darkness as a Death Knight to help combat the evil that threatens Azeroth in *World of Warcraft: Wrath of the Lich King Classic*. Learn more about becoming a Death Knight [here](https://worldofwarcraft.com/news/23818249/), and read more details on the pre-patch timeline of events, [here](https://worldofwarcraft.com/news/23833257/).
