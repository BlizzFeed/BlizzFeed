Expand your horizons in Azeroth, or join new friends or old on their latest adventures when you transfer your character or characters with the [Paid Character Transfer Service](https://shop.battle.net/product/wow-classic-service-character-transfer-era). You can now purchase multiple character transfers\* at a discount to use them all at once or as needed.

For a limited time\*\* save 30% when you purchase 1 character transfer or 3-character transfers. That’s two extra Paid Character Transfers for the regular price of a 6-character bundle. Once the sale ends, this bundle will revert to the regular 6-character transfer bundle.

#### How it Works

Once you’ve purchased your Character Transfers within the Battle.net® shop or the in-game shop, an icon will appear on your character selection screen showing how many available Character Transfers you have. You’ll then be able to activate a transfer by clicking on the token and selecting the character you wish to transfer and the realm you wish to move them to. Any unused transfers will remain on your account when you choose to use them at a future time.

To learn more about the Paid Character Transfer service and its restrictions, visit our [support page](https://battle.net/support/en/article/000083440).

Ready to make your move? Purchase Paid Character Transfers from the [Battle.net shop](https://shop.battle.net/product/wow-classic-service-character-transfer-era) or launch World of Warcraft and click the Shop button on the character select screen.
