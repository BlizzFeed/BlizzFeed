#### Here are this week's highlights:

#### Dragonflight

- **Dragonflight Season 3**: We [posted a clarification](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/wheres-are-8-trophies-of-strife-from-sparks-quest/1705989/28) of how the PvP currency Trophy of Strife is going to work in Dragonflight Season 3.
- **Dream Wardens Renown**: [Earning Dream Wardens Renown](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/todays-dreamseed-hotfixes/1706123/) via Dreamseeds now works a little differently than it did when Guardians of the Dream first launched.

#### WoW Classic

- **PTR Development Notes**: The 1.15.0 PTR is now open for testing and development notes are [here](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/classic-era-version-1150-development-notes/1706960).
- **Season of Discovery**: Oceanic players will have [dedicated local realms](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/sod-oceanic-server-please/1700571/48) in Season of Discovery.
