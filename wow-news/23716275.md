# Mythic Dungeon International: Shadowlands Season 2 Begins!

- URL: https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/en-gb/news/23716275/mythic-dungeon-international-shadowlands-season-2-begins
![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/ju/JUIPKB2GBA0G1629926013696.jpg)

> The Mythic Dungeon International returns this Friday, September 3, at 10 a.m. PT, only on YouTube!

---

***The Mythic Dungeon International (MDI) is back for its second season of Shadowlands, bringing a new format as teams compete for their shot at the 2021 Global Finals. Watch starting this Friday, only on*** [***YouTube***](https://www.youtube.com/Warcraft)***!***

MDI is making its glorious return to Shadowlands as season two begins this weekend. The top 24 teams from time trials have been divided into three separate groups, with eight teams competing each weekend for their share of the weekly $20,000 (USD) prize pool. In addition to prizing, the top two teams in each group will advance to the Global Finals October 29 – 31 where they will battle for the title of MDI World Champions and a $300,000 prize pool!

Following the three group weekends will be the Last Stand Tournament, October 1 - 3! The top six teams from a final open registration Time Trials have 15 hours to speed run keystones across multiple dungeons, with the top team securing a spot in the Global Finals.    Open registration for the Last Stand Tournament lasts until September 20, only on [GameBattles](https://gamebattles.majorleaguegaming.com/pc/world-of-warcraft/tournament/mdi-shadowlands-season-2/info). Are you ready for the start of the latest MDI season? Here is everything you need to know to enjoy the dungeon-running action!
