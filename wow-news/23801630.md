#### Burning Crusade Classic Character Clone Service is Retiring Soon

The World of Warcraft®: Burning Crusade Classic™ Character Clone service will soon be cycled out of the Battle.net® Shop. The service will be on sale until its retirement. You'll be able to [get a Character Clone for up to 66% off](https://us.shop.battle.net/product/wow-classic-service-character-clone) to keep those characters active on both Burning Crusade Classic and WoW Classic Era realms. Read more details [here](https://worldofwarcraft.com/news/23783592/the-wow-burning-crusade-classic-character-clone-service-is-retiring-soon).

**THIS WEEK IN SHADOWLANDS**

- Torghast: Chorus of Dead Souls
  - Dead souls swarm within Torghast, Tower of the Damned. Assume the form of a Deadsoul Chorus, granting special abilities, additional damage, healing, and movement.
- Weekly Bonus Event: Arena Skirmishes
  - Sharpen your swords and reinforce your boards because you're called to the PvP Arena for the hectic mayhem of Skirmishes.
- PvP Brawl: Cooking Impossible
  - Nomi is hosting a Pandaren Chili Cookoff in the Valley of the Four Winds between the Alliance and the Horde! The first to bring Nomi all of the required ingredients wins.

**COMING UP IN SHADOWLANDS**

##### Beginning Next Week:

- PvP Brawl: Temple of Hotmogu
  - Enter the Temple of Kotmogu for this fast-paced match. For a whole new twist, players will be able to throw the Orb of Power to friendly players in a game of hot potato.
- Timewalking: Wrath of the Lich King
  - Looking to withstand the fires of Igniss the Furnace Master? Withstand the terrific tantrum of XT-002 Deconstructor? Or is it your very mind you want to test against the master of nightmares, Yogg-Saron? Opportunity awaits—earn transmog sets, the pets you need to earn the Raiding for Leashes IV achievement, and more.
- Glowcap Festival
  - For one day only, the sporelings of Sporeggar in Zangarmarsh hold their annual mushroom festival. Help them keep the great Fshoo alive as long as possible!

##### Beginning the Week of May 30:

- PvP Brawl: Southshore vs. Tarren Mill
  - This Brawl harkens back to the early days of WoW PvP, with an epic battle between the two towns of Tarren Mill and Southshore. In this game of war, you'll need to work as a team and deplete your foes' resources to claim victory.
- Weekly Bonus Event: World Quests
  - Chart a course across the Shadowlands for this Week's Bonus Event – World Quests. All week, open your map (default hotkey-"m") and select any of the regions in Shadowlands to view the World Quests available to you.

Be sure to check your in-game calendar for a list of the current and upcoming in-game event start times!
