#### MYTHIC AND RAID FINDER WING 1

Sepulcher of the First Ones Mythic and Raid Finder Wing 1 is live, and the Jailer has breached the Sepulcher, the mysterious heart of the Shadowlands. Should he control its primordial power, he will reshape the cosmos with Death at the fore. Pursue Zovaal's forces into the Sepulcher of the First Ones and prepare for the most epic Shadowlands raid yet. ### WoW Esports

#### ARENA WORLD CHAMPIONSHIP

AWC is starting 2022 off with its first season cups beginning on March 18, with signs up for [Europe](https://gamebattles.majorleaguegaming.com/pc/world-of-warcraft/tournament/AWC-Shadowlands-S3-EU-Cup-1/info) and [North America](https://gamebattles.majorleaguegaming.com/pc/world-of-warcraft/tournament/AWC-Shadowlands-S3-NA-Cup-1/info) open now! Like last year, teams across North America and Europe will compete in four separate open Cups for $10,000 in weekly prizing per region and points to advance to the AWC Circuit. Learn more [here](https://worldofwarcraft.com/news/23764235/setting-our-sights-on-2022-for-the-15th-anniversary-of-wow-esports#awc).

#### MYTHIC DUNGEON INTERNATIONAL

The Groups format from Season 2 is returning for MDI Season 3, and dungeon-running teams can [register](https://gamebattles.majorleaguegaming.com/pc/world-of-warcraft/tournament/mdi-shadowlands-season-3/info) until March 28 for the season Time Trials that begin on March 30. Read on for the [specifics](https://worldofwarcraft.com/en-us/news/23764235/setting-our-sights-on-2022-for-the-15th-anniversary-of-wow-esports#mdi).

Stay connected by following WoW Esports on [Twitter](https://twitter.com/WoWEsports?ref_src=twsrc%5Egoogle%7Ctwcamp%5Eserp%7Ctwgr%5Eauthor) and subscribing to the Warcraft [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/warcraft) channel.
