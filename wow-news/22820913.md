# New Mount: Vulpine Familiar

- URL: https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/en-gb/news/22820913/new-mount-vulpine-familiar
![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/nx/NXPLYCRS22C41544721424456.jpg)

> It’s hard to resist the charm of the newest mount to grace the Blizzard Shop and in-game shop—the Vulpine Familiar.

---

Fiercely intelligent and insatiably curious, this species of fox has travelled the Great Dark Beyond to seek the companionship of the greatest heroes of Azeroth. Vulpine Familiars are born daredevils, and any one of them would make a great companion on your adventures throughout Azeroth and beyond.

[![Video](https://markdown-videos-api.jorgenkh.no/youtube/HI47Dke8U6E?width=1280&height=720)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HI47Dke8U6E)

You’ll be ready to take on new adventures wherever you go with this clever new mount. Now available in the [Blizzard shop](https://eu.shop.battle.net/en-gb/product/world-of-warcraft-mount-vulpine-familiar) and in-game shop.
