The Arena World Championship Regional Finals concludes this weekend, beginning Saturday at 10 a.m. PDT!

Following four intense weeks of the Arena World Championship (AWC) Battle for Azeroth, the top four Arena teams in North America and Europe are facing off this week to decide once and for all the best team in each region. The remaining teams are fighting for their share of $100,000 (USD) in regional prizing, and the titles of Battle for Azeroth Regional Champions! Watch the AWC Battle for Azeroth Regional Finals beginning on September 26 at 10 a.m. PDT, only on [YouTube Gaming](https://www.youtube.com/Warcraft).
