## Two New Raids

Deep within the halls of Tempest Keep, and the watery depths of Serpentshrine Cavern, Kael'thas Sunstrider and Lady Vashj await any who challenge Illidan's power.

It falls to you to put an end to their plans and weaken Illidan's grip on Outland. Gaining access to Serpentshrine Cavern and The Eye in Tempest Keep requires preparation and a trusty band of heroes at your side. Muster your forces and get ready for battle.

### The Eye: Vanquish the Sun King

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/MBT9DKC695LD1776990219008.jpg)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/MBT9DKC695LD1776990219008.jpg)

Infiltrate the heart of a crystalline fortress to end the madness of Kael'thas Sunstrider and his warped loyalists.

- **Raid Bosses**: 4
- **Level**: 70
- **Location**: Tempest Keep, Netherstorm

Located within Tempest Keep in the eastern part of Netherstorm, this crystalline fortress is ruled by Kael'thas Sunstrider.

Face off against Void Reaver, Al'ar, High Astromancer Solarian, and eventually the Sun King himself, Kael'thas Sunstrider.

##### Rewards

Three Tier 5 tokens each drop from Void Reaver and Kael'thas Sunstrider.

### Serpentshrine Cavern: Confront the Sea Witch

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/KWC86Z2TKVAS1776990217400.jpg)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/KWC86Z2TKVAS1776990217400.jpg)

Slay the twisted Lady Vashj and her vicious fiends in her watery lair.

- **Raid Bosses**: 6
- **Level**: 70
- **Location**: Coilfang Reservoir, Zangarmarsh

Found in the depths of Coilfang Reservoir in Zangarmarsh, it is here that Lady Vashj has constructed her watery lair.

Face off against five bosses—Hydross the Unstable, The Lurker Below, Leotheras the Blind, Fathom-Lord Karathress, and Morogrim Tidewalker—before confronting Lady Vashj herself.

##### Rewards

Three Tier 5 tokens each drop from Leotheras the Blind, Fathom-Lord Karathress, and Lady Vashj.

## Arena Season 2

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/UU0B4J6RVOGN1776990217461.jpg)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/UU0B4J6RVOGN1776990217461.jpg)

Brutalize your faction's enemies on the battlefield with the advent of Arena Season 2!

- When Season 1 ends, there will be a two-week off-season.
- Honor will not be reset at the beginning of Season 2.
- When Season 2 begins, remaining unspent Arena Points are converted to Honor Points at a rate of: 1 Arena Point = 10 Honor Points.

Victorious players will earn Arena Points to exchange for powerful gear, climb the ranks against similarly skilled opponents, and compete for an exclusive Gladiator mount awarded to the top 0.5% of players.

## Druid: Swift Flight Form

Druids, your destiny lies in Heroic Sethekk Halls.

In Phase 2 of Burning Crusade Classic Anniversary Edition, the questline that rewards Swift Flight Form and the Idol of the Raven Goddess becomes available. Seek out Loganaar in Moonglade to begin once you meet the prerequisites. From there, meet with Morthis Whisperwing, a former member of the Druids of the Talon, at Cenarion Refuge in Zangarmarsh. The final chapter pits you against Anzu the Raven God in Heroic Sethekk Halls, who has a chance to drop the Reins of the Raven Lord.

> ##### ***Prerequisites***
>
> - **Level Requirement**: 70
> - **Riding Skill**: Artisan Riding (300 skill, 280% flying speed)
> - **Reputation**: At least Honored with the Lower City faction to access Heroic Sethekk Halls
> - **Material**s: 10 Bogblossoms, 10 Ancient Lichen, 10 Dreaming Glory for early quest stages

Only druids who are currently on, or have completed, the questline can summon Anzu.

## Two New Factions for Daily Quests and Rewards

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/HB5RRQI3I3I01776990217695.jpg)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/HB5RRQI3I3I01776990217695.jpg)

Make new allies in Outland with the Ogri'la and Sha'tari Skyguard factions. Complete daily quests to earn these factions' favor and, in turn, receive lucrative rewards.

### Ogri'la

A faction of ogres in the Blade's Edge Mountains, where their proximity to Apexis Crystals has allowed them to evolve beyond their brutish nature. They are currently fighting a war against both the Black Dragonflight and the Burning Legion, who seek the Apexis Crystals for their own purposes.

### Sha'tari Skyguard

The Sha'tari Skyguard is an air wing of the Sha'tar of Shattrath City, defending the capital from attackers in the hills as well as battling against the arakkoa of Terokk in the peaks of Skettis.

## New Profession Recipes

A variety of powerful new epic goggles can now be crafted with Engineering.

Characters with Alchemy can now discover recipes for cauldrons, special items that can provide protection potions for your entire raid when used.
