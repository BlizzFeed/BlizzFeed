Adventurers, mark your calendars! We’re kicking off the 30th Anniversary of Warcraft with an epic celebration in London, and you’re invited to join us for this unforgettable gathering on 22 February, at the Soane Hall, London.

Starting at 17:00 GMT, this gathering is designed for everyone who has Azeroth close to their heart. Whether you’re a proud member of the Horde, an Alliance loyalist, or a fan of Warcraft's rich history and lore, this is your chance to unite with fellow heroes from across the realm. The gathering also celebrates the 20th Anniversary of the February 2005 official launch of *World of Warcraft* in Europe.

Here’s what you can expect:

- Live entertainment that’ll bring the spirit of Warcraft to life.
- Developer meet-and-greets where you can connect with the minds behind your favorite stories and adventures.
- Exclusive giveaways and commemorative surprises.
- Specially crafted activities that celebrate 30 years of Warcraft.

*And, of course, the unparalleled camaraderie of the Warcraft community!*
