# World of Warcraft: Forever Early Name Reservations Are Now Live!

- URL: https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/en-gb/news/24303866/world-of-warcraft-forever-early-name-reservations-are-now-live
![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/cj/CJUNEMGJNYPJ1792458573707.png)

> Get ready to make your mark in Azeroth! World of Warcraft: Forever Early Name reservations will be available from 27 October through 3 November, offering an early opportunity to reserve names for up to three characters.

---

Early Name Reservations for *World of Warcraft: Forever* are available from 27 October through 3 November, giving players an opportunity to secure the names that will become part of their adventures in Azeroth.

---

## Name Reservation Start Times

- **Europe:** 27 October at 18:00 CET
- **North America:** 27 October at 13:00 PDT
- **Korea:** 28 October at 07:00 KST
- **Taiwan:** 28 October at 07:00 CST

During the reservation period, eligible players\* can create and reserve names for up to three characters total across all Rulesets. This means that if you create two on a PvP Ruleset, you’ll only be able to create one more character in a Normal or Role-Play Ruleset. This restriction only applies to Early Name Reservation.

### Prepare to Launch

1. Make sure your graphics drivers are up to date.
2. Download and install the [Battle.net desktop app](https://download.battle.net/desktop)
3. Open the Battle.net desktop app and allow it to update if needed.
4. Select *World of Warcraft* from the list of games.
5. In the Game Version menu above the Play button, select *World of Warcraft: Forever*, then select Install.
6. Once installation is complete select Play and [choose your Ruleset](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/news/24302070/).
7. Create up to 3 characters with your chosen name.

Choose the names that will carry your heroes into a new age and prepare for your adventures in World of Warcraft: Forever.

---

Players who [pre-purchase](https://us.shop.battle.net/en-us/product/world-of-warcraft-forever?_gl=1*d7m9ad*) the Skyborne Heroic Pack, Skyborne Epic Pack, or limited-time Warcraft Forever Collection will have access to *WoW*®: Forever Early Name Reservation from 27 October through 3 November. Character names will be available on a first-come, first-served basis, and desired names cannot be guaranteed.

For additional information on how character names work, check out the previously published [Create a Name of Your Own in WoW: Forever](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/news/24304161) article.

*\* Purchase of World of Warcraft: Forever Digital Upgrade or Collector’s Edition required. This benefit only applies to the specific WoW® account it was purchased for. WoW®: Forever Early Name Reservation will be available starting October 27 through November 3. Character names are limited to a first-come, first-serve basis, and desired names cannot be guaranteed.*
