##### Explore the Dungeons of Shadowlands

New adventures await within the 8 new dungeons in Shadowlands—4 leveling dungeons and 4 max-level dungeons. [[Learn More](https://worldofwarcraft.com//news/23557609/)]

![...](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/o9/O9FSFPZMTDYZ1594226802755.jpg)

##### Meet the Shadowlands Covenants

In Shadowlands, players will be able to quest through four new zones ruled by ancient and powerful Covenants. Upon reaching the max level of 60, players will be able to join one of these Covenants as they continue to explore the Warcraft universe’s afterlife and the many mysteries and adventures that it holds. [[Learn More](https://worldofwarcraft.com/news/23537373)]

![...](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/gallery/x3/X31TGL0Z2J3Y1605218832548.jpg)

##### Torghast, Tower of the Damned

World of Warcraft: Shadowlands introduces a variety of new adventures for players to undertake, including Torghast, Tower of the Damned. Explore its ever-changing halls and chambers and do battle with the minions of the Jailer, Torghast’s vile ruler, to claim legendary treasures and free the heroic spirits trapped within. [[Learn More](https://worldofwarcraft.com/news/23389553/)]

![...](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/gallery/n1/N1SPWCFWN1BR1605218664809.jpg)

##### Adventures

Prepare to put your tactical skills to the test. In the expansion’s new Adventures feature, you’ll send your allies into the Shadowlands to tackle unique challenges for your chosen Covenant. You’ll need to be strategic about how you take on these combat puzzles, including who you’ll send to face them and how you’ll use their skills to claim victory. [[Learn More](https://worldofwarcraft.com/news/23492548/)]

![...](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/lp/LPP3II5TYPBX1602157934548.jpg)

##### Tell Your Story with Character Customization

With hundreds of new additions with thousands of new combinations, Shadowlands’ new character customization options will help you better create the characters you envision in your mind. [[Learn More](https://worldofwarcraft.com/news/23537372/)]

![...](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/e4/E4QEYLEBPJII1598571117297.jpg)

##### Shadowlands: Castle Nathria and Season 1 Goes Live December 8

Shortly after the release of Shadowlands, Season 1 will begin and the gates of Castle Nathria will open as the expansion’s first raid experience. [[Learn More](https://worldofwarcraft.com/news/23557932/)]

![...](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/gallery/pb/PBVAO3D0ET8T1600905759570.jpg)

##### Adventure Awaits in the New Starting Experience

Adventure awaits in Azeroth in an all-new starting experience arriving with Shadowlands. Available to both veteran players and new, this introductory excursion is a great way to begin forging your path through the world. [[Learn More](https://worldofwarcraft.com/news/23380363/)]

Take World of Warcraft anywhere you go with multiple expansion support. [[Learn More](https://worldofwarcraft.com/news/23492552/)]

#### Zone Previews

![...](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/xk/XKSRZ8LTJ5TW1586252499652.jpg)

##### Bastion and Kyrian Covenant

Journey deeper into the resplendent fields of Bastion. [[Learn More](https://worldofwarcraft.com/news/23379368/)]

![...](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_header/fb/FBKPJZIMKGN91591826533957.jpg)

##### Maldraxxus and the Necrolord Covenant

Within war-torn Maldraxxus, might—of all kinds—makes right. Here, in the birthplace of necromantic magic, those who master the powers of death turn legions of ambitious souls into relentless immortal armies. [[Learn More](https://worldofwarcraft.com/news/23451089/)]

![...](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/18/18ZDMRTPB0FG1590490032969.jpg)

##### Ardenweald and the Night Fae Covenant

A twilight-tinged afterlife for those closely connected to the wild, Ardenweald is a domain of restoration tended by the mystic night fae. Within this eternal forest, massive dream trees draw in precious anima to feed the spirits of nature awaiting rebirth. [[Learn More](https://worldofwarcraft.com/news/23434527/)]

![...](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/KI9S0T8N76UZ1587520622607.jpg)

##### Revendreth and the Venthyr Covenant

As you explore four otherworldly zones—Bastion, Maldraxxus, Ardenweald, and Revendreth—you will meet the Covenants that rule the afterlife realms in the Shadowlands. Your final destination in the narrative arc is the shadowy realm of Revendreth. [[Learn More](https://worldofwarcraft.com/news/23391567/)]

![...](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/zf/ZFWDIHZLV7FN1594336931943.jpg)

##### The Maw

The Maw is a tumultuous, hopeless land where the vilest souls in the cosmos are imprisoned forever. Should the ancient evil chained here break free, all of reality will be consumed. [[Learn More](https://worldofwarcraft.com/news/23463303/)]
