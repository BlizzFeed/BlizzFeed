# Midnight: Embrace the Void with the Devourer Specialization

> Introducing the Devourer Demon Hunter, a third specialization that utilizes the power of the Void. This glaive-wielding, soul-harvesting, and planet crushing spellcaster operates from mid-range with the full suite of mobility you'd expect from Demon Hunters.

---

Introducing the Devourer Demon Hunter, a third specialization that utilizes the power of the Void instead of Fel, like other Demon Hunters. This glaive-wielding, soul-harvesting, and planet crushing spellcaster operates from mid-range with the full suite of mobility you'd expect from Demon Hunters. Use specialization and Hero Talents to augment your playstyle, enabling you to dive into melee and unleash devastating combo attacks.

![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/9a/9ADWZZ4P3H8Q1756514780772.png)

For a little extra Void, when in the Devourer specialization, all Demon Hunter abilities will use Void effects and icons, even abilities that are shared across all three specializations.

---

## New Hero Talents

![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/0z/0ZTCIUVAKUUO1772052798836.png)

With the introduction of a new specialization, Demon Hunters also gain a new Hero Talent tree—Annihilator. This new tree is shared between Devourer and Vengeance Demon Hunters. Devourer Demon Hunters also gain access to the Void-Scarred Hero Talent tree (formerly called Fel Scarred).

---

## New Race/Class Combination: Void Elf Demon Hunter

![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/gt/GTHSZ9VWDRWJ1759163716054.png)

Players also gain access to the new Void Elf Demon Hunter combination through a short quest line available now as a part of the Midnight pre-expansion content update, along with the Devourer specialization.
