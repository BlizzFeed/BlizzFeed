## Stake Your Claim with Housing Early Access—Now Live!

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_header/qx/QX0JDDVADZQM1764701321998.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_header/qx/QX0JDDVADZQM1764701321998.png)

With the release of the “The Warning” *Midnight* Prologue content update, players who have purchased [any edition of the Midnight expansion](https://shop.battle.net/product/world-of-warcraft-midnight?utm_source=wow_site&utm_campaign=mktg-wow-global&utm_medium=homepagepanel) can join a neighborhood, claim a house, and start customizing their new home with Housing Early Access!

If you don’t have *Midnight* yet, don’t worry. You’ll still be able to begin adding to your Housing collection through a variety of activities and [achievements](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/news/24247005/achievement-unlocked-decorating-your-azeroth-home), or by purchasing them from vendors throughout Azeroth.

Our [previously published overview](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/news/24244464) will provide everything you need to know to start building your dream home in *World of Warcraft*.

#### Housing 101: Getting Started

Join Jay Hwang, Design Lead for Housing decoration, as he guides you through the Housing system in World of Warcraft. You'll learn about picking a neighborhood, purchasing a home, decorating, collecting, and he'll even answer some common community questions.

[![Video](https://markdown-videos-api.jorgenkh.no/youtube/Nhshcr68CJ0?width=1280&height=720)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Nhshcr68CJ0)

#### Achievement Unlocked: Decorating Your Azeroth Home

There are many ways for players to collect Housing decor in World of Warcraft. One of these ways is through completing achievements. Players can earn a decor from achievements from myriad activities and many of these may have been earned already throughout your time in Azeroth.

You can learn more about these achievements and how to aquire them in our [previously published article](http://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/news/24247005).

#### Professions Unpacked: Craft Housing Decor with Reagents from Every Era

Adventurers, your banks and bags are about to get lighter! With the arrival of “The Warning” content update, reagents you’ve stashed since Classic World of Warcraft—right up through The War Within—have a renewed purpose. Crafters can transform their collections of herbs, ores, leathers, and more into unique Housing decor items.

Whether you’re looking to personalize your own home or make a tidy profit on the auction house, there’s never been a better time to revisit your old materials and see what new creations await. From enchanting string lights to sturdy blacksmith benches, the possibilities span every expansion and every crafting table.

What will you create first? Explore the posibilities when you dig through our [previously published article](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/news/24247147).

#### Legion Remix Housing Decor Now Available

Players can now collect Legion Remix Housing decor to use when decorating their new home in Azeroth. If you’re looking to add a touch of Fel green to the ambiance, then look no further. We’ve got you covered. Completion of several achievements in Legion Remix will grant Fel-themed Housing decore items, with an option to purchase additional items for Bronze.

New Housing décor is waiting for you in in Legion Remix, so get out there and raise some Fel! Learn more from our [previously pubished article](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/news/24244443).

## Take a Ride on Turbulent Timeways—Now Live!

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_header/6a/6AAS3WV4VFUB1763589037274.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_header/6a/6AAS3WV4VFUB1763589037274.png)

Once more, the Bronze Dragonflight has discovered growing disturbances in the timeways, with several rapidly intersecting our own!

Timewalk through a previous expansion each week for 10 consecutive weeks, beginning and ending with the Shadowlands. This grand tour through World of Warcraft's past delivers six new Timewalking Dungeons for players to undertake—De Other Side, Spires of Ascension, Plaguefall, Necrotic Wake, Sanguine Depths, and Halls of Atonement.

Master the Timeways for a new Mount! This season, you'll be granted **Ta’readon’s Mount Voucher**for completing the “Master of the Turbulent Timeways IV” achievement awarded to those distinguished champions who maintain “Mastery of the Timeways” for any five weeks during the event.

The voucher will grant the “Ta’readon’s Mount Voucher” quest, directing you to Overseer Ta’readon in the Hall of Curiosities, where you’ll choose one of the four Shadowlands mounts—**Bronze Wilderling**, **Bronze Corpsefly**, **Bronze Gravewing**, or **Bronze Aquilon**—for free, and the ability to purchase the other three mounts from the Timewalking vendor for Timewarped Badges.

To learn more about Turbulent Timeways, including a dungeon schedule and list of rewards, read through our [previously published article](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/news/24242437).

## Twitch Drop Now Live! Get the Topsy Turvy Joker’s Mask Transmog

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_header/jq/JQN7QD87ADSK1764012631092.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_header/jq/JQN7QD87ADSK1764012631092.png)

Watch **4 hours** of any *World of Warcraft* content stream on Twitch.tv from 2 December at 18:00 GMT until 30 December at 18:00 GMT while these Twitch Drops are active to claim the **Topsy Turvy Joker’s Mask transmog**.

**Start Time:** 2 December, 18:00 GMT\
**End Time:** 30 December 18:00 GMT

To participate, you must link your Twitch account by visiting <https://account.battle.net/connections>. If you have changed your password or made any changes to your Twitch or Battle.net account, you will have to relink your accounts. When you relink, there will be a 7-day cooldown before you can relink a new Twitch account. After you’ve claimed your rewards on Twitch, please make sure you’re logging into the Battle.net region you’d like to receive the drop(s) on, as the first region you log into will be where your items will be delivered. It can take up to 24 hours to receive your reward(s) in-game after claiming. Read our Frequently Asked Questions section for more information.

Frequently asked questions can be found in our [previously published article](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/news/24250380).
