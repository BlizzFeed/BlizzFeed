> The Great Push (TGP) returns in January 2025! Register today for the last chance to earn the Tempered Banner of the Algari.

---

*The Great Push (TGP) returns in January 2025! Register today for a chance to become the first Great Push champions of The War Within*™ and to earn the Tempered Banner of the Algari *by completing the Time Trials.*

The Great Push is back beginning on January 22 with the Time Trials, a five-day qualification period where any registered team can push keystones as high as possible on the Tournament Realms. The top sixteen teams from Time Trials advance to the Group Stage commencing January 31, where they’ll push pre-assigned keystones against the clock live on [Twitch](https://www.twitch.tv/warcraft) and [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/@Warcraft). The final eight teams will advance to the TGP Global Finals, where $200,000 (USD) will be on the line as well as the title of first TGP champions of *The War Within*™!

Registration for The Great Push is [now open on Raider.IO](https://rio.gg/tgptwws1) – Register by January 17 for your chance to earn the Tempered Banner of the Algari and a combined $260,000 (USD) in prizing.

### **TGP Format Changes in *The War Within****™*

We’re making some changes to the overall format of The Great Push in *The War Within™* ahead of the Time Trials starting on January 22. These changes are designed to keep the action exciting throughout all broadcast stages of the tournament, and we’re excited to see it playout through the Season.

- Each TGP Group will now feature eight teams, meaning the top sixteen teams will advance from the Time Trials.
- In the Group Stage, the eight teams per group will be split into two sets of four. On Day one and two of each Group, these teams will compete in three keystones, with the top two of each day advancing to Day three. On Day three the four remaining teams’ scores will reset, and they’ll face three new keystones to determine who wins.
- Two teams will advance from each TGP Group, joined by two teams from China to make up a six-team Global Final.
- The Global Finals will follow traditional TGP format, with all six teams competing simultaneously. They will compete for three days until we have a winner!
- We’ll be disabling dungeons to limit the practice time available on Tournament Realms prior to Time Trials and between Groups. The intent is to ensure we see strategies unfold live on broadcast and reduce the burden on teams during The Great Push season.

[Register today on Raider.IO](https://rio.gg/tgptwws1) and earn the Tempered Banner of the Algari by completing this season’s Time Trials.
