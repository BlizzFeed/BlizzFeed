## Save 30% on Select In-Game Services

For those looking to get a fresh start, save 30% on [select game services](https://shop.battle.net/family/world-of-warcraft#game-services)—Character Transfer Packs, Faction Change Packs, Race Change Packs, and Name Change Packs.\*

Purchase Race Changes, Faction Change Packs, and Character Transfers from the [Battle.net shop](https://shop.battle.net/). Or launch *World of Warcraft* and click the Shop button on the character select screen. Once you’ve purchased your Character Transfer, Faction Change Packs, Name Change Packs, and Race Change Packs, a new icon will appear on your character selection screen. To activate a game service, click on the token, then select the character you wish to move. Any unused Faction Change tokens or Character Transfers will remain in your account for future use.
