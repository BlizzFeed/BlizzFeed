# Arena World Championship Dragonflight Season 2 is here!

- URL: https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/en-gb/news/23961850/arena-world-championship-dragonflight-season-2-is-here
![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/gx/GXDL513DXBMC1686530962796.png)

> Brace yourself for intense battles and epic PvP action as AWC Season 2 approaches, bringing the world's top World of Warcraft© teams together in their quest for glory!

---

Beginning June 16, the gates will open to the 2023 Dragonflight Season 2 Arena World Championship (AWC)! Tune in to all four Cups on [Twitch](http://twitch.tv/warcraft) and [YouTube](http://youtube.com/warcraft) to see which teams will qualify to continue in the tournament.

AWC Season 2 promises to be a spectacular display of skill, teamwork, and strategy as the finest PvP players enter the arena. Brace yourself for a thrilling journey filled with intense battles, jaw-dropping plays, and nail-biting moments to keep you on the edge of your seat.

### Details

- **Venue:**
  - **​Online:** [Twitch.tv/Warcraft](http://twitch.tv/warcraft) and [YouTube.com/Warcraft](http://youtube.com/warcraft)
  - **German:**[Twitch.tv/Kimuh](http://Twitch.tv/kimuh)
  - *Gladiators can stream their own perspectives. Check with your favorite competitor to see if they will stream their POV during the tournament! To sign up for co-streaming or an AWC Watch Party, use the**[AWC Watch Party Form](https://blizz.ly/CostreamAWC).*
- **Date:**
  - **​Cup 1:** 16-18 June
  - **Cup 2:** 23-25 June
  - **Cup 3:** 30 June- 2 July
  - **Cup 4:** 7-9 July
- **Broadcast Start Time:** 18:00 BST
- **Prize Pool:**$400,000 (USD) across the entire season, $10,000 (USD) per Cup
- **Format:**Double elimination, Best of 5
