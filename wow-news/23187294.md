> The Mythic Dungeon International (MDI) pits the best dungeon runners head-to-head to see who has the skills to rise to the top. This year’s winner has done just that.

---

Thousands put their skills to the test at the start of this year’s MDI, but only one team could take the win and be crowned the BlizzCon 2019 Mythic Dungeon International champion. Congratulations go out to [Method EU](https://twitter.com/Methodgg) who made their way through fierce competition to come out on top in a final Method EU vs. Method NA battle royale.
