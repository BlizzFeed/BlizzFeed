### The Seeds of Renewal Content Update Goes Live This Week!

Discover the buried history of Azeroth and the Dragon Isles through the Azerothian Archives Public Event, explore Bel'ameth—a burgeoning new zone offering hope to the kaldorei, take dragonriding to the skies of old-world continents, storm dragonflight dungeons alongside NPC companions, complete new campaign chapters in the continuation of the Dragon Isle epilogues, and more! ### Sign Up for the AWC and MDI in 2024

![AWC and MDI Logos along with a banner to the right](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/7u/7UVHRULDSP711705023032295.png)

Signups for the Arena World Championship (AWC) and Mythic Dungeon International (MDI) Season 3 are underway for just a little longer before the competition will swing into action. Don’t miss out on this opportunity for you and your team to climb your way into the winner’s circle!

> - Sign up for the MDI Dragonflight Season 3 on [Raider.IO](https://raider.io/tournaments) **by 29 January 2024**, at 19:00 GMT.
> - Sign up for the AWC on [Raider.IO](https://raider.io/tournaments) **by 26 January 2024**, at 20:00 GMT.

Learn more about the 2024 esports season in our [previously published article](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/news/24021893/unveiling-awc-mdi-in-2024).
