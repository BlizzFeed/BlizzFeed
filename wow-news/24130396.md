> Prepare yourself for your latest adventures and get back into the fight in The War Within with the new Enhanced Level 70 Character Boost.

---

There comes a time in every hero’s journey when they need help jumping back into the fray. Prepare your hero for the journey into the wild lands of *The War Within™* with the new **Enhanced Level 70 Character Boost\*** and harness the power of level 463 gear (up from 424) and an expanded Reagent bag (32-slot up from 26-slot).

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/de/DETIY3Y5IO5F1724275140308.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/de/DETIY3Y5IO5F1724275140308.png)

### Bring Your Hero Up to Speed

Grant a single character a one-time boost to level 70 and jump into the latest Dragonflight content endgame-ready! To boost multiple characters, buy a Character Boost pack and use them all at once or save some for later.

An Enhanced Level 70 Character Boost is included in all editions of the upcoming [The War Within™](https://us.shop.battle.net/product/world-of-warcraft-the-war-within) expansion.

The Enhanced Level 70 Character Boost includes:

- Max level for Dragonflight (level 70)
- Immediate access to the start of the Guardians of the Dream campaign
- Endgame-ready item level 463 gear
- Four 30-slot bags and one 32-slot reagent bag
- Renown System unlocked at Renown 20 with the four initial Dragonflight major factions and Renown 12 with the Loamm Niffen faction
- Five dragonriding mounts, including the Winding Slitherdrake dragonriding mount
- All Dragon Isles Travel Network Waygates available, flight paths unlocked, Dragonscale Expedition talents purchased, and NPC craftable Tuskarr fishing gear unlocked
- 5000 Dragon Isles Supplies

[Get Back in the Fight](https://shop.battle.net/product/world-of-warcraft-service-character-boost)

---

Purchase boosts from the [Battle.net shop](https://shop.battle.net/family/world-of-warcraft#:~:text=%2420.00-,Game%20Services,-Customize%20your%20gameplay), or launch *World of Warcraft* and click the Shop button on the character select screen. Once purchased, a new icon will appear at the top of your character selection screen. Click on the token, then select the character in your Warband that you wish to boost. Any unused tokens will remain in your account for future use.

Please note that as the character selection screen shows all characters on the Battle.net account in one convenient location, some may show up as “gray” in the listing and are not available to select to use a Character Service on. To use a service for that character, you will need to select the realm that the character is from first by clicking on the “Realm” selection at the top of the screen and then apply the service to that character. You can also hover your mouse cursor over a character listed on the right side of the screen to see additional information including the realm it is currently on.

The Character Boost can only be used on the WoW® game account on which it was purchased. Boosting an Allied Race will not unlock their Heritage Armor set. Some [restrictions](https://us.battle.net/support/en/article/000345591) do apply.
