### New Zone to Explore

[![Video](https://markdown-videos-api.jorgenkh.no/youtube/vbXcFW7g-o0?width=1280&height=720)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vbXcFW7g-o0)

As raging tempests batter against Siren Isle, set off on an expedition to face off against a weekly rotation of adversaries while unraveling baffling new mysteries surrounding the abandoned Kul Tiran Azerite mine.

Seek out Dawn, located in Foundation Hall, for the questline, [*The Expedition Awaits*](https://www.wowhead.com/ptr/quest=84719/the-expedition-awaits?brl), to begin your journey to Siren Isle. It's up to you, intrepid adventurer, to discover the fate of those who once lived and worked here. Explore alongside goblin, earthen, and Arathi expedition teams to learn more about the "singing crystal" at Siren Isle's heart.

While battling through enemies new and old with daring bravado, earn a new currency—**Flame-Blessed Iron**—to trade for items like mounts, pets, gear, and transmogs. It can also be used to open one of three Excavation sites on the island, presenting a face-off against a formidable foe to earn unique rewards.

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/h4/H41O8AG012R61733879101438.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/h4/H41O8AG012R61733879101438.png)

A powerful new ring—**Cyrce's Circlet**—is available by completing the Siren Isle introductory quest line—[The Radiant Vault](https://www.wowhead.com/ptr/quest=84724/the-radiant-vault?brl?brl). Complete a series of quests that will teach you how to upgrade the ring over time to increase its item level and gain new powers to help you journey around Siren Isle—keep reading to learn more.

While on your adventure, earn **Singing Citrines** that can be socketed in **Cyrce's Circlet**. These Citrines can be mixed and matched to create powerful combinations, and the gems can be socketed and unsocketed as you see fit.

##### Earn Crackling New Wings

Add a little spark to your adventure when you collect items from around the island to help release Thrayir, Eyes of the Siren—a new electrifying storm crow mount—from its bonds.

#### Getting Around on Land, Sea, and Air

While flying is possible after fully upgrading Cyrce's Circlet, due to the raging Seafury Tempests, the winds of the Siren Isle make it a no-fly zone at the outset.

Master the less severe winds by completing a series of quests to upgrade the circlet, giving you the power to face all but the most punishing tempests.

To upgrade Cyrce's Circlet, a single quest unlocks weekly, and completing it offers a new power that helps you on the island while wearing the ring. Don't fret if you miss a week or two; you can complete the missed quests in the following week along with the new one.

> - **Week 1**: Finish [The Radiant Vault](https://www.wowhead.com/ptr/quest=84724/the-radiant-vault?brl) questline to get Cyrce's Circlet and learn how to socket and unsocket gems.
> - **Week 2**:  Complete [Dipping a Toe](https://www.wowhead.com/ptr/quest=85653/dipping-a-toe?brl) to get [Blessing of the Seadweller](https://www.wowhead.com/ptr/spell=470197/blessing-of-the-seadweller?brl), which will allow you to walk on the seafloor, find treasures underwater, breathe underwater, and swim much faster in the surrounding waters of Siren Isle.
> - **Week 3**: Complete [Juicing Up and Storming Out](https://www.wowhead.com/ptr/quest=85654/juicing-up-and-storming-out?brl) to get [Superjuiced](https://www.wowhead.com/ptr/spell=470167/superjuiced?brl), allowing you to activate the TR-06 Pummeler and N.U.K.U.L.A.R Target Painter while in the Seafury Tempest on the Siren Isle.
> - **Week 4**: Complete [Unleashing Her Power](https://www.wowhead.com/ptr/quest=85655/unleashing-her-power?brl) and earn [Cyrce's Zephyr](https://www.wowhead.com/ptr/spell=470168/cyrces-zephyr?brl), allowing your spells and abilities to have a chance to summon Cyrce's Zephyr, which will cast every existing Singing Citrine ability over three seconds while the ring is equipped and you're on the Siren Isle.
> - **Week 5**: Complete [The Singing Bandit Catcher](https://www.wowhead.com/ptr/quest=85656/the-singing-bandit-catcher?brl) to gain the [Thieves of the Siren](https://www.wowhead.com/ptr/spell=470169/thieves-of-the-siren?brl) power to illuminate invisible enemies attempting to steal Siren Isle's riches. They are often found trying to pilfer Flame-Blessed Iron from the strongest creatures on the isle.
> - **Week 6**: Complete the final quest in this series—[Cyrce Would Be So Proud](https://www.wowhead.com/ptr/quest=85657/cyrce-would-be-so-proud?brl)—to earn [Wings of the Siren](https://www.wowhead.com/ptr/spell=470170/wings-of-the-siren?brl), giving you the passive ability to control the winds, allowing you to take flight on Siren Isle.

### New Events for the Adventurous

##### New Special Assignment: Storms A Brewin

Siren Isle is regularly lashed by horrible storms. This Seafury Tempest stirs up powerful creatures of all types from deep in the roiling ocean to assault the island.

##### New Public Event: Excavations

Three Major Excavations hosted by the expedition teams on the island await eager treasure hunters. Contribute **Flame-Blessed Iron** to open Shuddering Hollow, The Drain, or The Drowned Lair. Once inside, complete the event to earn valuable loot and rewards!

### Cross-Faction Queuing

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_header/x9/X9ZEJ03PBJAA1600844224978.jpg)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_header/x9/X9ZEJ03PBJAA1600844224978.jpg)

Gone are the days when someone from your cross-faction premade group had to face the wilds of Azeroth on the trek to a Meeting Stone to summon their companions from across faction lines—the time has come for you to grab your nearest group of premade frenemies and queue for Dungeons and unrated PvP through the Group Finder tool.

However, Dungeons will require a fully premade group, and party members who leave will not be replaced.
