Azeroth is calling once more. *World of Warcraft: Forever* invites you to begin again in a reimagined original Azeroth, where familiar roads lead to new discoveries, the journey matters as much as the destination, and the world is ready to grow for years to come.

*World of Warcraft: Forever* access will be included with your *World of Warcraft* Subscription or Game Time. Optional upgrade editions are available to purchase now on the Battle.net Shop, including the Skyborne Heroic Pack, Skyborne Epic Pack, and the limited-time Warcraft Forever Collection.

- Warcraft III: Reforged
- *Warcraft III Reforged: Forsaken Kingdom* campaign

Purchase *World of Warcraft: Forever* upgrade editions on the [Battle.net Shop today](https://shop.battle.net/product/world-of-warcraft-forever).

## Explore Warcraft III Reforged: Forsaken Kingdom

[![Video](https://markdown-videos-api.jorgenkh.no/youtube/I1kXClUCyuA?width=1280&height=720)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=I1kXClUCyuA)

*Warcraft III Reforged: Forsaken Kingdom* is also available as a digital purchase for Warcraft III players. This all-new campaign returns Warcraft’s RTS roots to the front line with a story set around the fall of Lordaeron, the founding of the Undercity, and the first Forsaken Paladin. Players can purchase the campaign on its own, pick up a combo that includes Warcraft III: Reforged, or choose the Warcraft Forever Collection to get both the campaign and WoW: Forever upgrade benefits in one limited-time bundle.

Purchase [*Warcraft III Reforged: Forsaken Kingdom*](https://shop.battle.net/product/warcraft-3-reforged) separately on the Battle.net Shop or get it as an included benefit of the Warcraft Forever Collection.
