A new playable race arrives in The War Within—the Earthen. Unlock playable Earthen characters after completing their quests and the overall level-up campaign. Enlist this titan-forged race made of living stone by aiding them in their endeavors.

Champions of Azeroth must aid the Earthen and seek a way to restore their harmony and population. They can also be unlocked as a playable race, and you can choose Alliance or Horde to begin your journey starting at level 10 in their capital city of Dornogol.

---

### The Titan-Forged Earthen

We've met Titan-forged Earthen before in Ulduar and other places. Still, this society of Earthen has been isolated within Khaz Algar for a long while and has developed its own manners and customs. They are ancient denizens freshly freed from the edicts that bound them; they now venture forth with limitless curiosity to ally with the Alliance and Horde and explore the world.

![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/9DFSD3URF2ML1717472040721.png)

As you aid them in restoring the Coreway and venture below Dornogol, tread lightly—Earthen politics are complicated, and danger lurks in the shadows.

### Becoming One with the Earthen

The Earthen can also be unlocked as a playable race, and you can choose Alliance or Horde to begin your journey.

Completion of the following will make them available:

- [The War Within storyline](https://www.wowhead.com/beta/achievement=20597/the-war-within?brl):
  - *Against the Current*
  - *News from Below*
  - *A Light in the Dark*
  - *Ties that Bind*
  - *The Machines March to War*
- Level-up campaign, [*Beginning Anew from Rock Bottom*](https://www.wowhead.com/beta/quest=78248/beginning-anew-from-rock-bottom?brl)
- Help Machinist Kittrin mend broken tools for [*Reforged Purpose*](https://www.wowhead.com/beta/quest=78564/reforged-purpose?brl)
- Assist Councilward Merrix in returning stability to Dornogal in Bad Business.
- Help Urtago and Korgran perform a ceremony for [*As He Departs*](https://www.wowhead.com/beta/quest=79342/as-he-departs?brl)
- Help Urtago at Fallside Outpost take on her new role in [*The Weight of Duty*](https://www.wowhead.com/beta/quest=82895/the-weight-of-duty?brl)

### Racial Traits and Class Combinations

- [Azerite Surge](https://www.wowhead.com/beta/spell=436344/azerite-surge?brl): The earthen draw upon their inner strength using an empowered spell that, when released, invokes the power of Azerite in a cone in front of them, dealing Fire damage. This spell is on a 2-minute timer and gains additional effects as it is empowered:
  - I: Deals Fire damage
  - II: Heals allies hit by the effect
  - III: Deals additional fire damage to the enemy's health, most affected by the effect.

- [Hyper Productive](https://www.wowhead.com/beta/spell=436339/hyper-productive?brl): Increases Finesse by 2%. Finesse increases the chance of gathering additional materials when gathering.
- [Ingest Minerals](https://www.wowhead.com/beta/spell=436341/ingest-minerals?brl): You are always Well Fed but cannot consume food. Activate to consume a Khaz Algar gem and change the benefit granted to you by Well Fed:
  - Amber (Stamina)
  - Emerald (Haste)
  - Onyx (Mastery)
  - Ruby (Critical Strike)
  - Sapphire (Versatility)
- [Titan-Wrought Frame](https://www.wowhead.com/beta/spell=436340/titan-wrought-frame?brl): Base armor from equipped items is increased by 10%.
- [Wide-Eyed Wonder](https://www.wowhead.com/beta/spell=448924/wide-eyed-wonder?brl): When you gain experience for exploring a location, gain 200% additional exploration experience.

### Racial Mount: Slatestone Ramolith

Unlock the earthen mount, [Slatestone Ramolith](https://www.wowhead.com/beta/item=223572/reins-of-the-slatestone-ramolith?brl), upon completing the [Allied Races: Earthen](https://www.wowhead.com/beta/achievement=40306/allied-races-earthen?brl) achievement. This mount is usable by any race and is available account-wide.

### Heritage Armor

For reaching 50 on the Earthen Allied Race, you will receive the [Heritage of the Earthen](https://www.wowhead.com/beta/achievement=40309/heritage-of-the-earthen?brl) achievement and the Earthen Heritage Armor. This armor can only be transmogged by Earthen characters.
