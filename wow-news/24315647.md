## Bundle Up for the December Trading Post

[![alternate-image-name](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/uj/UJ3DT7RG0F2P1795552796466.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/uj/UJ3DT7RG0F2P1795552796466.png)

**Left to Right**: Festive Eve pet, Ensemble: Seafarer’s Winter Collection, Ensemble: Ice Walker’s Guise of the Wight, and Brrrgl pet

Winter has arrived at the December Trading Post, and while things may get icy, you'll find a variety of transmog appearances to keep you warm. This month, bundle up in the **Ensemble: Seafarer’s Winter Collection**.

Visit T&W (Tawney and Wilder) just outside the Mage District in Stormwind, or the Zen’shiri Trading Post next to Grommash Hold in Orgrimmar, to check out the latest items they have available. Players can also visit the Trading Post in the capital cities of Dornogal (The Foregrounds) on the Isle of Dorn and in the renewed [Silvermoon City](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/news/24243213), where representatives of both Tawney and Wilder and the Zen’shiri Trading Post are open for business.

Champions new to or returning to *World of Warcraft* and *Midnight* can [tour the Trading Post](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/news/24131507) to learn everything you need to know, from where to find it to how you can earn additional Trader's Tender through the Traveler's Log.

## Get Myth 2/6 Gear in Labyrinth of Kindo'jan

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/UDVKV95593QV1786560513837.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/UDVKV95593QV1786560513837.png)

Each week, Wah’du has tasked you with clearing out a specific chamber, and for your efforts, a reward of 1 **Mythical Soul Fragment** and 2,000 **Undercoin**. Have you missed a few weeks? No worries, Wah'du has you covered. He'll hand out each skipped quest, one after another, so you can wrap up several in a single visit and get right back on pace. Missed all of them? Now's the time to complete all six of Wah'du's Labyrinth quests in order in one run to earn six **Mythical Soul Fragments** worth two Myth 2/6 gear pieces of your choice.

**New to the Labyrinth of Kindo’jan?**

If you've completed the campaign quests, the Labyrinth is already unlocked for you. If not, no problem! Head north of Amani Pass in Eversong Woods and pick up "The Long Way Around" from Liadrin, or travel to Tokka's Landing and accept "The Labyrinth" from Kul'amara the Fierce. Then, complete the Chamber of Rites with Valeera Sanguinar and speak with Kinduru to unlock the full Labyrinth for your entire Warband.

Once completed, follow Wah’du’s quests and clear only the chambers he sends you to, in the order he asks. Do that, and you’ll earn enough **Mythical Soul Fragments** to claim any two pieces of Myth 2/6 gear (your choice) from Wah’du, Servant of de Loa.
