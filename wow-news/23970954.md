#### Here are this week's highlights:

#### Classic

- **Classic Era - 1.14.4 PTR Development Notes**: The latest [updates](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/wow-classic-era-1144-development-notes/1621723/5) were posted on Thursday, which included bug fixes, changes to the String of Ears buff, PTR downtime, and the upcoming “Brawl with the Blues” event (more details coming soon).
- **Classic Hardcore - PTR Jumping Issue Resolved:** On Monday, an issue where PTR characters could be disconnected while jumping was [resolved](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/disconnect-deaths-on-jumpstuns/1622074/9).
- **Actions Against Exploitative Accounts:** Over [120,000 actions](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/recent-actions-against-exploitative-accounts-june-2023/1625327) were taken against exploitative WoW accounts in June.
