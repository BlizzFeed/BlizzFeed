# Guild Services Retiring

- URL: https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/en-gb/news/24107641/guild-services-retiring
![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/jf/JFSHO4U0VWEO1490035260875.jpg)

> We will be retiring Guild Services currently available in the in-game shop as of 22 July 2024, at 18:00 BST.

---

We will be retiring Guild Services currently available in the in-game shop as of 22 July 2024, at 18:00 BST.

Services that will be retired include Guild Realm Transfers and Guild Faction Transfers. After 22 July 2024, these services will no longer be available. The Guild Name Change service will continue to be available.
