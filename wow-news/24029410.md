#### Here are this week's highlights:

#### Dragonflight

- **Latest Class Tuning Changes and Process**: Check out the latest [class tuning adjustments](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/class-tuning-incoming-november-28/1723436) and some details on the [process](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/pveretail-lack-of-class-tuning/1717839/10) behind these changes.
- **Rares in Zaralek Caverns and Forbidden Reach**: Recent changes were made to the rares in Zaralak Caverns and the Forbidden Reach. The [reason](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/rare-elites-in-zaralek-caverns-and-forbidden-reach/1719458) for these changes was shared, and other adjustments are being discussed.
- **Legendary 2-Handed Axe, Fyr’Alath the Dream Render**: Details on [how to earn](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/bad-luck-protection-on-legendary-axe-and-how-it-works/1724426/2) Fyr’Alath the Dream Render were posted, along with details to increase the chances of getting it.
- **Seeds of Renewal PTR Update**: Check out the [latest details](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/seeds-of-renewal-ptr-development-notes/1713534/4) for this PTR, including a first look at the new area of Bel’ameth.
- **Rated PvP Gear Changes**: Some changes are being made to [Rated PvP gear](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/update-coming-to-gear-from-rated-pvp/1719592), affecting how this gear will work in PvE content.
- **Accessibility Adjustments**: Some adjustments are being looked into to help make the game more [accessible](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/blind-accessability-range-sound-notifications-blocked-by-blizzard/1720911/3).

#### Classic

- **Season of Discovery PvP Rank Cap**: With the initial max level of 25, the [PvP rank](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/season-of-discovery-pvp-rank-caps/1726478) will be capped at 3.
- **Classic Era PvP Changes**: Starting next week, PvP ranks will [no longer decay](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/classic-era-pvp-update-december-5/1724481).
