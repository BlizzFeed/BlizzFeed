# The Mythic Dungeon International Returns October 24!

- URL: https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/en-gb/news/24243637/the-mythic-dungeon-international-returns-october-24
![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/jx/JXJY8CQ9CX2M1760650295485.png)

> Prepare for broken records and crowned champions —MDI Season 3 is here!

---

The countdown has begun, and you’d better be ready. The Mythic Dungeon International (MDI) is here—catch the epic finale of *The War Within* starting October 24!

After several seasons of spectating speedy runs and celebrating champions, you may think that you’ve seen everything *The War Within* has to offer—but you couldn’t be more wrong!

Tune in for Season 3’s live broadcast to catch our most-celebrated champions and contenders return for an action-packed finale to *The War Within* as they dive into the most daunting dungeons yet to shatter records and redefine the realm of possibility!

While they’ll be facing off against familiar foes, the competition will be more challenging than ever! This season is shaping up to be a spectacular showcase of skill and strategy—our challengers are back and better than ever before, equipped with the expansion’s most elite armaments through turbo-boosts and tertiaries, while also bearing an elite understanding of mobs and mechanics bestowed upon them from extensive first-hand experiences. You won’t want to miss a second of the action!

This season’s tournament continues the new era of pinnacle PvE programming, challenging competitors to push their limits through the format formerly known as “The Great Push”. Over the next three weeks, hopeful heroes and determined dungeoneers will compete in the MDI Groups for glory, gold, and a spot in the Global Finals! Six teams will compete each weekend to push the assigned Mythic+ Keystones as high as possible within the allotted time of five hours per day. The top two teams from each group will qualify for the highest-stakes competition yet - the Global Finals. Over the final weekend, the qualified competitors will face off to earn global renown, the championship title, and their share of the $300,000 prize pool!

### Details

- **Venue:**  Online Broadcasts - [Twitch.tv/Warcraft](http://twitch.tv/Warcraft) and [YouTube.com/Warcraft](http://youtube.com/Warcraft)
- **Co-streaming:** *Check with your favorite competitors to see if they plan on sharing their perspective during the tournament, or gather your own audience and host a watch party of your own! Sign up for co-streaming using the [MDI Watch Party Form](http://blizz.ly/CostreamMDI).*
- ***Dates:***
  - ***Group A - Oct. 24th to Oct. 26th***
  - Group B - Oct. 31st to Nov. 2nd
  - Group C - Nov. 7th to Nov. 9th
  - Global Finals - Nov. 21st to Nov. 23rd
- **Broadcast Start Time:**  10:00 am (PDT) / 7:00 pm (CEST)
- **Format:**
  - 6 Teams per Group Weekend
  - 3/2/1 Daily Dungeon Distribution
  - Spec Variety Rule
