# Meet the Teams Competing at the AWC Spring Finals

- URL: https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/en-gb/news/22986425/meet-the-teams-competing-at-the-awc-spring-finals
![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/6c/6CFVVMBME0IJ1557188791304.jpg)

> Join us this weekend as we crown the champions!

This weekend, the Spring season of the Arena World Championship (AWC) will reach its epic conclusion as the top four teams from North America and the top four teams from Europe descend on Ohio to battle it out in person on LAN. After two days, one team will stand as the AWC Spring Champions!

## Meet the Teams

### Wildcard Gaming

- Maro-Simon “Maro” Franke
- Martin “Loony” Pettersson Moazzez
- Emil “Zeepeye” Ek
- Maros “Blizo” Molcan

> "What do you enjoy about competing in the AWC?"
>
> The game can be very fun at times. Very nice community (teammates/other teams/GCD and Blizzard team). But above everything else, hanging out/traveling with my teammates is probably the thing I enjoy the most about competing.
>
> "What impact has World of Warcraft had on your life?"
>
> Very big impact. Obviously competing is very time consuming but getting to know all the people and travel around the world is really cool. Also, some people I met through the game are really good friends to me now.
>
> Maro-Simon “Maro” Franke ([Twitter](https://twitter.com/maro_wow)|[Twitch](https://www.twitch.tv/maro))

### ABC

- Alexander “Alec” Sheldrick
- Niki “Niksi” Hietala
- Kristoffer “Asgarath” Wilhelmsen
- James “Jaime” McKeever

> "Are there any teams you are afraid of in the Top 8?"
>
> Personally, I'm never afraid of any team. I've beaten every player at the LAN at some point or another, and I'm not saying we will win every team or even win a series, but there is no point being stressed out about other teams. Everyone sucks, including us. Just who sucks less on the day will do the best.
>
> James “Jaime” McKeever ([Twitter](https://www.twitter.com/jme_gg)|[Twitch](https://www.twitch.tv/jaime))

### Never Lucky

- Brandon “Vellido” Jennings
- Zachary “Zach” Knights
- Noah “Packratts” Moreside
- Christopher “Kolo” Moniz

> "How do you feel about Sylvanas burning Teldrassil?"
>
> Honestly, I was really sad to see that. I've been an Alliance player since I was young, but I've grown more to the Horde side as I've grown up.
>
> "What impact has World of Warcraft had on your life?"
>
> It's impacted everything in my life. I've got to travel to Europe, play on a stage with 40k people in front of me cheering for me, I've made so many friends and had so many highs and low memories on this game. But at the end of the day, we're all playing a game that brought us all together and I think we all have found enjoyment out of WoW in some form.
>
> Christopher “Kolo” Moniz ([Twitter](https://twitter.com/Kolohots)|[Twitch](https://www.twitch.tv/kolowavex))

### Method Orange

- Charles “Cdew” Dewland
- Ryan “Trill” Costello
- Richard “Mes” Simpson
- Michael “Samiyam” Hlubek

> "What do you enjoy about competing in the AWC?"
>
> Not only do I just enjoy playing with my teammates, but it's also the feeling of winning. Winning just feels amazing and winning the 2018 AWC was almost too good to be true at the time. It also feels good when you're doing something you know others aren't doing to gain an advantage and knowing you're one step ahead of your competition.
>
> "Are there any teams you are afraid of in the Top 8?"
>
> I think every team has a reason to be feared, but our hardest match is probably our first one vs Wildcard Gaming. They're an incredible team that have a lot of momentum going into this tournament and will be on fire. That's not to say Method Black or Cloud9 won't put up a fight either though.
>
> Ryan “Trill” Costello ([Twitter](https://twitter.com/TrilltkoWW)|[Twitch](https://www.twitch.tv/trilltko))
