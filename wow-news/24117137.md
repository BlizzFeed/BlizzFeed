# Paid Character Transfer Service Update Ahead

- URL: https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/en-gb/news/24117137/paid-character-transfer-service-update-ahead
![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/9s/9SSJ8VVRB3FK1645205310091.png)

> With the launch of The War Within™ pre-expansion content update arriving on 24 July, we will be disabling the Paid Character Transfer (PCT) service for character transfers between Battle.net® accounts.

---

With the launch of The War Within™ pre-expansion content update arriving on 24July, we will be disabling the [Paid Character Transfer](https://shop.battle.net/product/world-of-warcraft-service-character-transfer) (PCT) service for transfers between Battle.net® accounts.

This service will still be available to use between realms and between World of Warcraft® accounts within a single *Battle.net* account. Please be aware that transfers between *Battle.net* accounts will not be available after the pre-expansion update has gone live. Any transfer tokens that you have saved to use on a transfer between *Battle.net* accounts should be used before this time.
