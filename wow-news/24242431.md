### New Twitch Drop Ahead!

![A green orc flexing while wearing a purple shirt and matching pants](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/tj/TJS0RP4C30YY1762282527282.png)

*“The ideal outfit for a hard workout.”*\
(This ensemble comes as separate chest and leg pieces and will show up in your in-game collection under those categories.)

Get the **Ensemble: Violet Sweatsuit Transmog** set starting 11 November!

Watch **4 hours** of any *World of Warcraft* content stream on Twitch.tv from 11 November 18:00 GMT until 2 December 18:00 GMT while these Twitch Drops are active to claim the **Ensemble: Violet Sweatsuit Transmog**.

**Start Time:** 11 November 18:00 GMT\
**End Time:** 2 December 18:00 GMT

To participate, you must link your Twitch account by visiting <https://account.battle.net/connections>. If you have changed your password or made any changes to your Twitch or Battle.net account, you will have to relink your accounts. When you relink, there will be a 7-day cooldown before you can relink a new Twitch account. After you’ve claimed your rewards on Twitch, please make sure you’re logging into the Battle.net region you’d like to receive the drop(s) on, as the first region you log into will be where your items will be delivered. It can take up to 24 hours to receive your reward(s) in-game after claiming. Read our Frequently Asked Questions section for more information.

Frequently asked questions are available in our [previously published article](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/news/24246288).
