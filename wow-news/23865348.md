> The first esports tournament in Wrath of the Lich King Classic is here, live on YouTube and Twitch beginning Saturday, October 22 at 10 am PT!

---

***The Wrath of the Lich King Classic™ Undying Challenge begins this weekend!  Get ready to watch the first-ever Classic WoW® Raid Race as some of the top guilds in the world duke it out through gearing, grinding, and raiding!  Watch live on*** [***YouTube***](https://www.youtube.com/user/WorldofWarcraft) ***and*** [***Twitch***](https://www.twitch.tv/warcraft) ***beginning Saturday, October 22 at 10:00 am PT.***

Progress (EU) and SPACEFORCE (NA) will start the weekend having to gear new characters throughout the dungeons of Northrend. On Day 1, they'll run through every Northrend dungeon to strengthen their characters as much as they can, before coming back on Sunday where they will only be able to further gear up in the Eye of Eternity and Obsidian Sanctum raids. Once each team has completed gearing up, they’ll face off in one final race through Naxxramas!

October 22:

- **10:00 am PT**: Gearing up - Tour of all Heroic Dungeons in Northrend

October 23:

- **10:00 am PT - ~11:00 am PT**: Gearing up -  Eye of Eternity & Obsidian Sanctum
- **~11:00 am PT**: Race to the finish - Naxxramas
