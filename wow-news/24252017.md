# Mists of Pandaria Classic: Siege of Orgrimmar Raid Now Live

- URL: https://news.blizzard.com/en-gb/article/24252017/mists-of-pandaria-classic-siege-of-orgrimmar-raid-now-live
![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/30/30K15I8Z9F591780081410347.png)

> Garrosh Hellscream’s arrogance has brought ruin to the Vale of Eternal Blossoms. Champions of the Alliance and the Horde must march on his capital and bring the ruthless Warchief’s reign to an end.

---

The tensions stoked by Garrosh Hellscream’s arrogance have reached a boiling point with the devastation unleashed upon the Vale of Eternal Blossoms. Champions of the Alliance and the Horde must answer for Hellscream’s destruction, lay siege to his capital, and topple the ruthless Warchief once and for all!

Heroes of the Alliance and the Horde have tested their mettle against the might of the sha, the Thunder King, and each other, yet neither side could have foreseen the atrocities the Horde’s Warchief would unleash.

Garrosh Hellscream’s reckless thirst for power has unleashed horrors upon the Vale of Eternal Blossoms, once the sacred heart of the pandaren empire.

---

## Bring Down the Warchief

The Siege of Orgrimmar is a sprawling, two‑part raid that takes players from the scarred ruins of the Vale of Eternal Blossoms to the heart of the Horde's capital, Orgrimmar in Durotar.

> **Location:** Begins inside the shattered pillar before Mogu’shan Palace on the southwest side of the Vale of Eternal Blossoms and ends in Orgrimmar, Durotar.\
> **Level**: 90\
> **Difficulty**: Normal, Heroic\
> **Total Bosses**: 14

---

## Deal with the Vile Events in the Vale of Eternal Blossoms

The first order of business is to confront the aftermath of Garrosh’s foul machinations. His poisoning of the Pools of Power has brought devastation to the land and to those who have long defended it.

### Vale of Eternal Sorrows

Venture through the scarred Vale of Eternal Blossoms, exploring unearthed mogu ruins to discover the source of the destruction.

![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/XBGM6E90E1R91378247009220.jpg)

Immerseus

The ancient inhabitants of Pandaria recognized the vital importance of the lifegiving Pools of Power, building an underground system of aqueducts to safeguard the waters and nurture life in the Vale of Eternal Blossoms. The touch of corruption has animated and twisted these waters, and Immerseus stands as an unnatural embodiment of the Vale's sorrow.

![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/3SJT0OL26B351780092111375.png)

The Fallen Protectors

The Golden Lotus and Shado-Pan guardians of the Vale of Eternal Blossoms were caught in the epicenter of the devastating blast that scarred the Vale and were torn apart by the dark energies. Their spirits linger in the place they once protected, confused and tormented by their failure.

![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/RBQO9QLQ6RPT1378247009623.jpg)

Norushen

Some say that the mogu race was created in the image of this titanic construct, left deep beneath Pandaria to watch over and guard the continent's darkest and most dangerous secret.

![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/J3FMSUQMV2E51780092112954.png)

Sha of Pride

The seventh sha, the Sha of Pride, was the final burden to which Emperor Shaohao clung, shrouding the land in mist and biding its time for millennia. When Garrosh awakened the Heart of Y'shaarj, the force of his arrogance caused this dark energy to coalesce in the chamber where the Heart was unearthed.

## Storm the Stronghold and Confront the Encounters Within

With the crisis in the Vale contained, heroes of the Horde and the Alliance must take the war to Garrosh’s doorstep in Durotar, push past his outer defenses, and confront the evil festering within the gates of Orgrimmar.

### Gates of Retribution

War has come to the gates of Orgrimmar, as Alliance forces and the Darkspear Rebellion seek revenge against Garrosh Hellscream.

![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/4EOM5ECDSAAM1780092111408.png)

Galakras

Warlord Zaela formed a close bond with Garrosh during events in the Twilight Highlands, and she and her Dragonmaw orcs have pledged loyalty to Garrosh's cause. Riding atop the fearsome Galakras, a direct descendant of the cataclysmic progenitor of all dragonkind, Zaela oversees the naval defense of Orgrimmar.

![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/GUE32LUPR5AF1378247009400.jpg)

Iron Juggernaut

This mechanical terror, designed nearly as much for intimidation as destruction, is the centerpiece of Garrosh's siege weaponry. Crafted in the image of the mighty Kor'kron war scorpion, the Iron Juggernaut guards the gates of Orgrimmar, crushing any who would rise to challenge Garrosh's True Horde.

![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/QTPWTEF4TL871780092112905.png)

Kor'kron Dark Shaman

Haromm and Kardris trained thousands of shaman to whisper reverently to the elements to requisition their aid. The army of Garrosh, however, does not ask; it takes what it desires in the name of the True Horde. Dark shamanism forces the elements into servitude, twisting them into burned-out ash, corrupted waters, and toxic air.

![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/ISNV0UHKSI821780092112851.png)

General Nazgrim

Once a grunt in the service of the former Warchief, Thrall, General Nazgrim rose quickly through the ranks after overwhelming victories in Grizzly Hills and the sunken city of Vashj'ir. Fiercely loyal to the Horde and bound by a rigorous code of honor and duty, Nazgrim will hold the line for his Warchief until his dying breath.

![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/5QA79GSPH4F71378247009431.jpg)

Malkorok

Malkorok has been Garrosh's most loyal and trusted lieutenant throughout the Pandaria campaign. When the Warchief needed a volunteer to infuse with the power of Y'Shaarj, it was only natural for Malkorok to offer without hesitation.

![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/5HVPMGD8130P1780092113577.png)

Spoils of Pandaria

When Garrosh gazed across Pandaria, he saw untapped power. During the course of his campaign, Garrosh has plundered weapons, treasures, and artifacts of the pandaren, the mogu, and the mantid. They are kept in a warehouse deep within his underground base, guarded by a mysterious security system that appears to be of Titan origins.

![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/17IRRBC5DP6E1378247009947.jpg)

Thok the Bloodthirsty

When the Isle of Giants was discovered off the coast of Pandaria, teeming with primal devilsaurs, Garrosh sent men to capture some of the most fearsome specimens, hoping to subjugate them and use them as beasts of war. Countless orcish beastmasters have fallen to Thok's jaws as they struggle to tame him, yet the creature's thirst for blood remains unslaked.

### Downfall

Wielding the dark power that lay dormant beneath Pandaria, Garrosh seeks to bring a new order to Azeroth. He must be stopped!

![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/3GZ0AZ5PKUTL1378247008763.jpg)

Siegecrafter Blackfuse

Helix Blackfuse was the only goblin with the combination of engineering prowess, professionalism, and ruthlessness to satisfy Garrosh in his search for the engineer of the True Horde. A mercenary at heart, Blackfuse's love for his creations (and the gold they fetch) has forever linked his fate with that of his patron and Warchief.

![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/XN98BY2NY3RJ1378247009796.jpg)

Paragons of the Klaxxi

The nine surviving Klaxxi'va Paragons are ancient champions of the mantid who fought alongside the Wakener against the madness of Empress Shek'zeer. But the paragons, as do all mantid, hold a far deeper loyalty. When Garrosh unearthed the heart of Y'Shaarj, the paragons followed the whispers of their ancient master to the iron halls beneath Orgrimmar.

![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/9TEHJNLPAR9D1780092111436.png)

Garrosh Hellscream

Garrosh, son of Grommash Hellscream, first learned of his father’s heroism when Thrall encountered the young orc in Outland. With the seeds of pride planted, Garrosh spearheaded Horde victories in Northrend and, as Warchief, consolidated Horde power amid the chaos of the Cataclysm. But his vision of orc supremacy at any cost has brought the armies of the world crashing down upon Orgrimmar... and a reckoning Garrosh himself awaits with brutal relish.

## Seize Victory and Claim Your Rewards

Bring the siege to its end and claim rewards forged in the downfall of Garrosh Hellscream.

The Siege of Orgrimmar unfolds across two difficulties, each offering rewards for champions who endure the assault.

| Difficulty | Item Level | Warforged |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Normal** | 553 | 559 |
| **Heroic** | 566 | 572 |

### Claim the Power of Tier 16 Gear

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_header/TRBMZM64KS8C1378488303563.jpg)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_header/TRBMZM64KS8C1378488303563.jpg)

Siege of Orgrimmar introduces Tier 16 raid gear, with the full set available within the raid, and Garrosh Hellscream also has a chance to drop **Essences**, which can be exchanged for any Tier 16 piece.

Here are the specific bosses that drop Tier Set armor in Siege of Orgrimmar:

| Token | Boss |
| --- | --- |
| Helm | Thok the Bloodthirsty |
| Shoulders | Siegecrafter Blackfuse |
| Chest | Sha of Pride |
| Legs | Paragons of the Klaxxi |
| Gloves | General Nazgrim |

The following classes share Armor Tokens:

- **Vanquisher**: Death Knight, Druid, Mage and Rogue
- **Conqueror**: Paladin, Priest and Warlock
- **Protector**: Hunter, Monk, Shaman, and Warrior

Exchange Armor Tokens for Tier 16 set pieces at vendors on the second-floor balconies of the faction shrines in the Vale of Eternal Blossoms:

- **Alliance**: Clarice Chapmann (Normal) and Lorry Warmheart (Heroic) at the Shrine of the Seven Stars
- **Horde**: Ki’agnuu (Normal) and Tu’aho Pathcutter (Heroic) at the Shrine of the Two Moons

### Claim Glory and Take to the Skies

Complete all fourteen challenge achievements to earn the “Glory of the Orgrimmar Raider” meta-achievement for the [**Reins of Galakras**](https://www.wowhead.com/mop-classic/item=104208/reins-of-galakras?brl), teaching brave champions how to summon the **Spawn of Galakras** flying mount.
