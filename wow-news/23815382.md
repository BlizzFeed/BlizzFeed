# Get the Nether-Gorged Greatwyrm with a 6-Month Subscription

- URL: https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/en-gb/news/23815382/get-the-nether-gorged-greatwyrm-with-a-6-month-subscription
![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/jt/JT5VCMLY1DGN1657652851316.png)

> Glide around on the saddle of this gluttonous mount—available now for purchase in-game or from the Blizzard Shop.

---

Bristling with arcane energy, the serpentine Nether-Gorged Greatwyrm\* is now available to add to your mount collection. Glide through the nether on this gluttonous mount when you purchase directly from in-game or the Blizzard Shop, or receive it at no additional charge with a 6-Month Subscription, along with the Tabard of Flame\*\* for Burning Crusade Classic.

---

#### A Netherworldly Appetite for Adventure!

The Nether-Gorged Greatwyrm will carry you to the far-off horizons of whatever plane of existence your adventures take you. Embark on an electrifying journey and discover new sources of delicious arcane power along the way.

[![Video](https://markdown-videos-api.jorgenkh.no/youtube/v8p-GvswHPc?width=1280&height=720)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=v8p-GvswHPc)

This arcane power-hungry mount will automatically be added to your Collection when you purchase the [6-month subscription](https://shop.battle.net/product/world-of-warcraft-subscription) from the Blizzard Shop. Welcome this serpentine addition to your assortment of mounts!

Players on an existing 6-month recurring subscription with a future renewal date will receive the Nether-Gorged Greatwyrm mount, and Tabard of Flame for Burning Crusade Classic at no additional charge, no later than July 22, 2022 (must be redeemed by December 31. 2022).

---

#### A Blazing Addition to Your Wardrobe

An epic Tabard of Flame will turn every outfit into a fierce fashion statement! Once you purchase the 6-Month Subscription, redeem your item by accepting a special quest from Innkeepers in any capital city, as well as Landro Longshot in Booty Bay. The quest is available for all Burning Crusade Classic characters attached to a single regional Battle.net account.

[Purchase 6 Months Now](https://shop.battle.net/product/world-of-warcraft-subscription)

#### Now on the Shop: Nether-Gorged Greatwyrm

Glide around on the saddle of a serpentine mount of greatness when you purchase the Nether-Gorged Greatwyrm!
