## The Plunderstorm® Creator Royale returns 25 January!

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_header/3f/3FNPE1ZWYO3J1736812123848.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_header/3f/3FNPE1ZWYO3J1736812123848.png)

Avast ye sailors, scallywags, and spectators alike— the Plunderstorm® Creator Royale lies dead ahead! Prepare for pirates, prizes, and plenty of plundering as sixty of your favorite content creators clash once more within the storm-plagued peaks of Arathi Highlands.

This unique event sets sail on [Twitch](http://twitch.tv/warcraft) and [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/@Warcraft) on 25 January, starting at 18:00 GMT.

The *Plunderstorm Creator Royale* will be broadcast live from Blizzard Headquarters in Irvine, California, with First Mates [Goldenboy](https://x.com/GoldenboyFTW), [Soe](https://x.com/Soembie), and [Esfand](https://x.com/EsfandTV) manning the helm!

During the competition, each of the sixty content creators will also stream third-person perspectives from their own port of call worldwide on their personal channels.

Additional details can be found in our [article](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/news/24171477).

## Enter the Hub of Goblin Ingenuity: Undermine(d) Zone Overview

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/D3DE0XTSK0HN1737512372543.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/D3DE0XTSK0HN1737512372543.png)

As the adventure continues in *The War Within*, travel deep into the capital of the goblin's trade empire—Undermine. Continue the pursuit of Xal'atath and confront Gallywix's forces in the sprawling city streets of a city suffering from the conflict between the four major Cartels—Bilgewater, Blackwater, Steamwheedle, and Venture Co.

Welcome to Undermine! A city like none other, it stands as a testament to goblin ingenuity and creativity as experts in both technology and alchemy.

While in Undermine, learn more about goblin culture and society, what's important to them, the problems they are trying to solve, everything that makes them truly unique, and what they're capable of as a society. We'll dig deeper beyond their humor and explosions, though there will still be explosions and a LOT of mayhem to be found.

A full overview of the Undermine zone, Cartels you'll work for, the new Season 2 dungeon—Operation: Floodgate—can be found in our [article](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/news/24174172/).

## The Joyous Journeys 50% Experience Buff Returns for a Limited Time

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_header/m1/M19GLSH7OZCQ1728501403315.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_header/m1/M19GLSH7OZCQ1728501403315.png)

It’s not too late to join the adventure in Cataclysm Classic. For all stalwart champions beginning a new journey or continuing their odyssey in Azeroth, take advantage of the Joyous Journeys experience buff which increases experience gains by 50% for all players up to level 85!

Finish leveling a character, create a new one, or invite friends to join you in *Cataclysm Classic*. The buff is active by default, but you can visit any innkeeper in the capital cities and Dalaran to turn it off or on again.

Take advantage of the Joyous Journey’s experience buff now until the next major *Cataclysm Classic* content update. It’s an excellent opportunity for new and returning players to level up and join in the fight— for Azeroth!
