# Hotfixes: March 4, 2021

- URL: https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/en-gb/news/23580183/hotfixes-march-4-2021
![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/mv/MVKK5EJOGPU61470421579108.jpg)

> Here you will find a list of hotfixes that address various issues related to World of Warcraft: Shadowlands and WoW Classic.

---

Here you'll find a list of hotfixes that address various issues related to World of Warcraft: Shadowlands and WoW Classic. Some of the hotfixes below take effect the moment they were implemented, while others may require scheduled realm restarts to go into effect. Please keep in mind that some issues cannot be addressed without a client-side patch update. This list will be updated as additional hotfixes are applied.

## Hotfixes

---

####

#### March 4, 2021

**Dungeons and Raids**

- **Castle Nathria**
  - **Hungering Destroyer**
    - Expunge now shows up in the combat log when it is applied to players.
    - Volatile Ejection now shows up as a debuff when a player is targeted with this spell.
- **The Necrotic Wake**
  - **Blightbone**
    - Fixed an issue that would cause Carrion Worms to explode unexpectedly.

**Items and Rewards**

- Aspirant's Stretcher can no longer be used while in instanced areas.

#### March 1, 2021

**Dungeons and Raids**

- **Castle Nathria**
  - **Lady Inerva Darkvein**
    - [*With regional restarts*] Healers are now chosen as a last resort target of Shared Suffering and Concentrate Anima on Raid Finder, Normal and Heroic difficulties.
    - [*With regional restarts*] The ambient damage of Loose Anima for open containers has been reduced on Raid Finder, Normal and Heroic difficulties.
    - [*With regional restarts*] Targets of Shared Suffering no longer take damage from the beams they are wearing on Raid Finder, Normal and Heroic difficulties. Passing through the opposite edge will continue to damage targeted players.
  - **Hungering Destroyer**
    - [*With regional restarts*] Health reduced by 5% on Mythic difficulty.
    - [*With regional restarts*] Reduced the damage players wearing Gluttonous Miasma take by 10% on Mythic difficulty.
    - [*With regional restarts*] The damage increase of Essence Sap has been reduced by 20% per stack.
  - **Sun King’s Salvation**
    - [*With regional restarts*] Increased the amount of healing Essence Overflow heals Kael'thas to 2.5% of his maximum health on all difficulties (was 2%). The tooltip will be updated to reflect the change in a future hotfix or patch.
    - [*With regional restarts*] Increased the healing done bonus from Sinfall Boon to 100% per application on Mythic difficulty (was 50%).
    - [*With regional restarts*] Reduced the strength of the Cloak of Flames damage shield on Shade of Kael'thas by 10% on Mythic difficulty.
- **Sanguine Depths**
  - [*With regional restarts*] Wicked Oppressor’s Wrack Soul cast time increased to 2 seconds (was 1.5 seconds) and is cast less frequently.
  - [*With regional restarts*] Regal Mistdancer’s Echoing Thrust damage reduced by 20% and is cast less frequently.
  - [*With regional restarts*] General Kaal’s health reduced by 10% during the gauntlet event.
