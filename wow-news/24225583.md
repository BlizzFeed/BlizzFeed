Go further into Midnight in our Deep Dive broadcast with an introduction from Creative Director Chris Metzen and Game Director Ion Hazzikostas. You’ll take a closer look at the zones, story, and features of the expansion.

[![Video](https://markdown-videos-api.jorgenkh.no/youtube/WmG98apVwKw?width=1280&height=720)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WmG98apVwKw)

Stay tuned immediately following the broadcast for the Midnight live Q&A with the lead developers of World of Warcraft.

Game Director Ion Hazzikostas, Associate Game Director Paul Kubit, and Design Director Maria Hamilton will join Community Manager Randy Jordan to answer your biggest questions about World of Warcraft: Midnight.

The Q&A will be streamed live to the official [Warcraft Twitch](https://www.twitch.tv/warcraft) and [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/warcraft) channels.
