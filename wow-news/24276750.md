### Twitch Drop Now Live! Get the Cuddly Pearl Grrgle Housing Decor Item

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_header/t6/T6TFE3PMLCA61776365595985.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_header/t6/T6TFE3PMLCA61776365595985.png)

Watch any eligible *World of Warcraft* stream on Twitch.tv from April 23 at 3:00 pm PDT until May 21 at 3:00 pm PDT to claim the Cuddly Pearl Grrgle Housing decor item. Players who have claimed this new item from the Twitch Drop will also be able to visit Dennia Silvertongue in Silvermoon City to purchase more copies of this adorable decor. Additional copies can also be purchased from Tuuran in Stormwind and Gabbi in Orgrimmar (near the Trading Post).

**Start Time:** 23 April 23:00 BST\
**End Time:** 21 May 23:00 BST

Watch **4 hours** of any *World of Warcraft* content stream on Twitch.tv while these Twitch Drops are active to claim the **Cuddly Pearl Grrgle** Housing decor item.

Frequently asked questions and details on how to link your Twitch account can be found in our [previously published article](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/news/24266872).

### *Mists of Pandaria Classic*: Joyous Journeys is Now Live

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_header/jc/JC0LPVXNIX5V1750870178685.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_header/jc/JC0LPVXNIX5V1750870178685.png)

Stalwart champions in *World of Warcraft: Mists of Pandaria Classic*, take advantage of the Joyous Journeys experience buff to enhance your odyssey through Azeroth, increasing experience gains by 50% up to level 90!

**Duration**: 21 April until the next content update for *Mists of Pandaria Classic* arrives.

The mystical lands of Pandaria are swirling with danger and need your help. Create a new character, finish leveling a forgotten one, or invite friends to join you in legendary Pandaria.

The Joyous Journeys buff is active by default; however, you can visit any innkeeper in the capital cities and Dalaran to turn it on or off. The Joyous Journeys buff is not available in modern *World of Warcraft®* (currently *Midnight*), *World of Warcraft®: Burning Crusade Classic Anniversary Edition*, or *World of Warcraft® Classic* *Era*.

See you in Pandaria!
