# WoW Roofus Pack Raises More Than $1.3M for Habitat for Humanity

- URL: https://news.blizzard.com/en-gb/article/24298585/wow-roofus-pack-raises-more-than-1-3m-for-habitat-for-humanity
![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/va/VA4M2OAFL12I1773251795469.png)

> Thanks to the World of Warcraft community, the Roofus Pack raised more than $1.3 million USD for Habitat for Humanity, turning an in-game companion and cozy Housing Decor into real-world support for families and communities.

---

Earlier this year, players adopted Roofus through [the Roofus Pack promotion](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/en-us/news/24267940) in support of Habitat for Humanity. The cheerful builder, Roofus, joined players across modern *World of Warcraft* and *WoW Classic* progression realms as an in-game companion pet linked to a real-world cause, and Paw Pal Housing Decor items helped make players’ houses feel a little more like home.

## Thank You, Champions

During the promotion, Blizzard donated 100% of the purchase price\* of The Roofus Pack to Habitat for Humanity. Thank you to everyone who adopted Roofus, shared the promotion, or helped spread the word. Your support raised more than $1.3 million USD for Habitat for Humanity.

We’re also grateful to the Blizzard and WoW team members who volunteered with Habitat for Humanity in Altadena, California, helping build homes for families impacted by the fires.

## About Habitat for Humanity

Habitat for Humanity is a movement of people in local communities and around the world, working together to build more prosperous and vibrant communities by ensuring everyone has a safe, affordable place to call home. Since its founding in 1976, Habitat for Humanity has helped more than 65 million people globally build their futures on their own terms through access to decent housing. This is achieved by working alongside people of all walks of life to build, repair, and finance homes, by innovating new ways of building and financing, and by advocating for policies that make constructing and accessing housing easier for everyone. Together, Habitat for Humanity builds homes, communities, and hope.

To learn more, visit [www.habitat.org](https://www.habitat.org/).
