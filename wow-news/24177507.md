### The Undermine(d) Content Update Goes Live February 26

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_header/n7/N737YKGKRDIM1739233678760.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_header/n7/N737YKGKRDIM1739233678760.png)

As the adventure continues in *The War Within*, travel deep into the capital of the goblin's trade empire—Undermine. Continue the pursuit of Xal'atath and confront Gallywix's forces in the sprawling city streets of a city suffering from the conflict between the four major Cartels—Bilgewater, Blackwater, Steamwheedle, and Venture Co.

Travel deep into the goblin city of Undermine and learn to D.R.I.V.E., join a cartel, adventure in two new delves, a new dungeon: Operation Floodgate, face Gallywix in the Liberation of Undermine raid, battle in a new PvP Arena— and more!

### The Liberation of Undermine Raid Overview and Schedule

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_header/la/LAKZTCFQQXFF1739218896817.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_header/la/LAKZTCFQQXFF1739218896817.png)

Things are heating up in Undermine, and the cartels are ready to take the battle right to the heart of Gallywix’s stronghold in the Gallagio. Players will encounter eight new bosses— including the Chrome King Gallywix himself—as they fight their way through the glitz and glam to get straight down to business.

**Raid Details:**

- **Where:** Undermine
- **Raid Bosses:** 8
- **Level:** 80
- **Difficulties:** Story Mode, Raid Finder, Normal, Heroic, Mythic
- **Raid Finder Minimum Item Level:** 606

**Raid Unlock Schedule:**

- **Week of March 5:** Raid Finder Wing 1: Shock and Awesome (Cauldron of Carnage; Rik Reverb), Normal, Heroic, Mythic Difficulty
- **Week of March 12:** Raid Finder Wing 2: Maniacal Machinist (Sprocketmonger Lockenstock; Stix Bunkjunker), Story Mode
- **Week of March 19:** Raid Finder Wing 3**:**Two Heads Are Better **(**Vexie and the Geargrinders; One-Armed Bandit; Mug’Zee)
- **Week of March 26:** Raid Finder Wing 4: The Chrome King (Chrome King Gallywix)

No pressure or anything, but goblin society depends on you, so learn how you can prepare for the Liberation of Undermine from [our article](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/news/24178099/).
