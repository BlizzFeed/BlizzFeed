## Take a Look Ahead at the 12.0.5 Content Update

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_header/28/289EZGPAAG791773245286805.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_header/28/289EZGPAAG791773245286805.png)

## Welcome to the WoW Portal Room

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_header/cv/CVV0VMZMS5WJ1769616867241.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_header/cv/CVV0VMZMS5WJ1769616867241.png)

The WoW Portal Room is the official World of Warcraft Discord server and your gateway to connection—a hub where Ambassadors and players unite and where every adventurer feels included.

Whether you're new or returning, seeking tips for mythic dungeons, or simply wanting to chat, the WoW Portal Room is where you'll take the first steps to finding your people. Just like using a portal in *World of Warcraft*, the Portal Room is meant to be a way stop on your journey and not the final destination.

A team of WoW Ambassadors representing partnered community Discord servers is ready to meet you and help you with finding a space that fits your needs. Participating Discords enjoy special recognition, access to exclusive events, and direct support from Blizzard—making every server a more vibrant place to connect.

## Monthly Event Sponsored by Partnered Discord, WOWKEA

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/v9/V9KMBEX3CLMU1773427954387.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/v9/V9KMBEX3CLMU1773427954387.png)

WOWKEA is one of the largest WoW Housing Communities, and a place to showcase Housing builds, find design inspiration, and connect with Neighborhoods of like-minded housing sickos.

Every month, WOWKEA will sponsor a fresh event to build around,  and to kick off the month of March with our new friends, the Haranir, WOWKEA is challenging players to build the best Haranir-themed room for a chance at a selection of available digital prizes!

**What**: Haranir Monthly Challenge\
**When**: The Month of March\
**Where**: WOWKEA Community Discord - <https://discord.gg/pGUNg3hTFx>

This monthly event is sponsored and hosted solely by WOWKEA and is not affiliated with Blizzard Entertainment.
