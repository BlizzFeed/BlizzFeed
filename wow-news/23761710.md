#### Eternity’s End PTR Updates

Development continues on the next content update for Shadowlands: Eternity’s End, where players will be able to step into a new land—Zereth Mortis— and face Zovaal, the elusive Jailer and ruler of the Maw in Shadowlands.

On Friday, January 28, starting at 14:00 PST (17:00 EST, 23:00 CET), come join members of the World of Warcraft development team on the Broxigar PTR realm as we playtest the new Solo Shuffle Brawl that’s being added in the 9.2 Eternity’s End update! #### Development Update Posts on the Forums

- [**Belt of Unity and Legendary Updates**](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/92-double-legendary-acquisition/1167080/28)**:** Learn more about the ongoing changes to the Belt of Unity, the acquisition of additional Legendaries, and more.
- [**Encrypted Affix Updates**](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/feedback-new-seasonal-affix-encrypted/1171571/13): PTR feedback has led to additional design updates to the Encrypted Affix which mostly changes how the buffs work, including significant improvements to the two weaker effects, and some more love for healers.

### Raider.IO Celebrates 5-Years

On January 25, community site and popular add-on Raider.IO [celebrated their 5-year anniversary](https://raider.io/news/324-announcing-our-new-recruitment-system) as a part of the World of Warcraft community and announced a new project—their official Recruitment System. You can learn all about it on their site. If you’re a fan of their work, be sure to stop by and wish them a happy anniversary.

*Raider.IO was founded by Jah and Ulsoga with the vision of becoming a platform for player recruitment for World of Warcraft PvE content. Instead of creating only a recruitment platform, they wanted to make a website that players would enjoy for other reasons as well. This led to progression tracking, the creation of Raider.IO Score, working with the WoW Esports team on the MDI, hosting and facilitating community events, and more!*
