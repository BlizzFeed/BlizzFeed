## About Midnight

![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/33/333FZSZTWBKX1755721739173.png)

In *Midnight*, players will experience the next chapter in the Worldsoul Saga as things become more dire for the world of Azeroth. Xal’atath, the Harbinger, poses our greatest threat as she unleashes chaos in her wake with the ensuing invasion striking into the heart of the blood elf homelands in Eversong Woods.

Arriving over the glittering elven kingdom of Quel'thalas, the looming Voidstorm threatens to extinguish all light and cover the world in darkness. Players will level up to 90 and explore four new and reimagined zones to uncover the heart of Xal'atath's machinations while seeking out new allies among the light and shadows alike.

---

### Midnight Leveling and Zones

Play through four reimagined and new zones as you dig deeper into the ongoing story, meet new allies, and amass power to push back against the Void.

![...](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/0AHWE1190TFY1756517969034.png)

##### Eversong Woods

Immerse yourself in the revitalized Eversong Woods, with graphic updates breathing new life into this iconic blood elf homeland. A combination of the original Eversong Woods and the Ghostlands, the zone has been slightly expanded and connected to the Eastern Kingdoms. Players can take to the skies and Skyride through the zone, without loading screens, while experiencing the majesty of the glittering spires of blood elf architecture and golden foliage of the forests from above.

![...](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/6EZ7OIPNC5LP1756519258871.png)

##### Zul’ Aman

The volatile neighbors to the blood elves, the Amani trolls reside in their capital of Zul’Aman. They are a proud people who have fought hard for their land and overcome significant hardships and challenges. Comprised of old forests and tall mountains with periodic rainfall, you’ll take a journey deeper into their culture and history as you explore all this zone has to offer.

![...](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/8HS55FPH90RV1756519275830.png)

##### Harandar

The home of the haranir is a fungal jungle where the roots of all the world trees converge. The haranir travel through the rootways to move around the world, observing Azeroth in secret but never intervening openly in the world’s affairs. In the zone lies the Rift of Aln, a primordial wound where the barrier between dreams and reality grow thin.

![...](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/L0WZ4KKL8GA81756519287342.png)

##### Voidstorm

Suffused with Void, this hostile land is host to Void creatures of all varieties. Everything here is hungry and ready to devour and consume weaker creatures for their power. Xal’atath and her Devouring Host rule over this world of cosmic predation. Chief among her followers are the domanaar: some of the most powerful void creatures the world has ever known.

![...](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/7j/7JLXOU8JBS1Q1758845411704.png)

##### Arator’s Journey

One of the available paths through the Midnight campaign, players will undertake a new questline alongside Arator, son of Turalyon and Alleria Windrunner. At the request of Alonsus Faol, you’ll recruit Arator for this continent-spanning adventure to seek out relics of the Light used by priests and paladins at the Sunwell when their reserves run dry.

You’ll first begin your journey in Eversong Woods, but it’s up to you how your story progresses beyond this first zone. There are three available paths you can tackle in any order: brave the ancient troll kingdom of Zul'Aman, investigate the gnarled hidden roots of Harandar, or accompany Arator on his journey to better understand his relationships with both his family and the Light. Learn more about [the zones](https://worldofwarcraft.com/news/24223893) and [Arator’s Journey](https://worldofwarcraft.com/news/24234071) in our previously published articles.

### Meet and Befriend the Haranir

![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/ot/OTVIIXALP99R1759441447922.png)

Earn the trust of the Haranir through a series of quests to unlock this new allied race. Haranir will be able to join either the Horde or the Alliance and play as a Druid, Hunter, Mage, Monk, Priest, Rogue, Shaman, Warlock, or Warrior.

An unfamiliar people from a forgotten time, Haranir will arrive with a variety of customizations available to them. They can be adorned with bioluminescent paint on their face and bodies in a variety of patterns that can be selected when customizing your character. Learn more from [our previously published article.](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/news/24232758)

### More Features

- **Level to 90**
- **New Talents**-Expanded talent trees with exciting new abilities and introducing Apex talents. [Learn more.](https://worldofwarcraft.com/news/24230699)
- **Renewed City Hub: Silvermoon City**- Horde and Alliance will fortify within the streets of Silvermoon City, which will serve as a hub for both factions. Approximately one-third of the city will be designated exclusively for the Horde, while the remaining two-thirds will be accessible to both Horde and Alliance members. The city has been rebuilt from the ground up and the path of destruction the Scourge left behind mostly a memory.
- **Prey**-Track powerful targets throughout the zones of Midnight in three difficulties: Normal, Hard, and Nightmare. Successfully find and defeat your quarry to earn valuable new rewards as you progress through the system. [Learn more.](https://worldofwarcraft.com/news/24225580)
- **Arcantina**-A refuge to the road-weary and those seeking a respite from the trials and travails of the life of an adventurer, the Arcantina serves as a crossway for all kinds. It is a magical tavern at the crossroads of nowhere and everywhere catering to travelers and heroes from all walks of life. It’s here you’ll meet up with some familiar faces for stories and quests sending you back out into Azeroth once more. [Learn more.](https://worldofwarcraft.com/news/24234072)
- **PvP Updates**
  - **Take Arms in a New Battleground: Slayer’s Rise**-Set in the Voidstorm, this 40 vs 40 epic battleground will pit the Horde and Alliance against each other in a race for control and conquest. Players with War Mode turned on will also be able to engage in World PvP in the outdoor area.
  - **Learn the PvP Ropes in Training Grounds**-For players new to PvP, learn the ropes in the Training Grounds. [Learn more.](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/news/24223778)
- **New Activities**- Whether you’re looking for a challenge or just want to do a little more exploration into the stories found in Midnight, we’ve got you covered with some new activities that we’re sure will appeal to many playstyles. [Learn more.](https://worldofwarcraft.com/news/24223312)
- **User Interface (UI) Updates**- We’re making improvements to Nameplates, adding Damage Meters to the base UI, and integrating boss alerts into our raid design in Midnight. [Learn more.](https://worldofwarcraft.com/news/24223311)
- **Transmogrification Updates**- Take your transmog game further with the ability to save item slot appearances and outfits for any occasion. Whether you’re fighting in a dungeon, exploring a new area, or taking a rest in your house, you’ll be able to dress for the moment. [Learn more.](https://worldofwarcraft.com/news/24223776)
- **Updated Leveling Experience for New and Returning Players**-Whether you’re new to Azeroth or returning to undertake the next chapter in the evolving story within Midnight, we’ve got you covered beginning with the release of the Midnight Prologue “The Warning “Content Update on December 2.
