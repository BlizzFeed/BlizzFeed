Introduced on June 8, the Community Discords Program’s first group of community Discords have been going strong and are kicking off a variety of activities, giveaways, and more planned in the days ahead. There’s a little something for everyone, and they’re just waiting for you to join in the fun.

# Classic Hardcore Giveaways

![Bloodsale Buccaneer Ship Floating near the Kalimdor shoreline with the Hardcore Classic Banner announcing the program giveaway](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/9i/9IAPI7BJN6H71693241364465.jpg)

We’ve partnered with [Dungeon Dojo](https://discord.com/invite/gsF3TSx7ju), [GoldGoblinNet](https://discord.gg/goldgoblinnet) (German), and [ChromieDE](https://discord.gg/y6qUXhY5b5) (German) to run community giveaways to celebrate the launch of Hardcore Classic.  Players will be asked to share their best or funniest death in WoW Hardcore in one of the channels from 24 August through 7 September for a chance to win some great prizes!

Join them to learn more.

---

## Starship: Race to World WORST

From 3 September, Starship is asking players to form teams of three to take on dungeons locked at level 30 using only gear from a select expansion. Will you take on their challenge?

But that’s not all! Starship’s first annual meetup is happening in London on the 14th of October. [Join the Discord channel to Learn more.](http://discord.gg/starshipeu)

---

## Accademia di Scholomance

**6/9— Dreamsurge Public Event**

Join Accademia di Scholomance for the Dreamsurge public event beginning on Wednesday, 6 September, at 21:30 to take on the Dreamsurge events together. They’ll teach you everything you need to know to achieve success.

**19/9— Mythic Plus Night**

Esteemed Professors of the Accademia di Scholomance will be available to show you how to tackle Mythic Plus in the most efficient ways.

Accademia di Scholomance has much more in the works, including community raid and achievement runs. [Join the Discord channel to learn more.](https://discord.gg/tqnnbktrzv)

---

## Dungeon Dojo

**Dungeon Dojo Hardcore Challenge**\
24th August - 7th September\
To celebrate the release of the Hardcore Servers, the Dungeon Dojo is organizing a two weeks event to see who survives the longest in Hardcore Classic! Expect lots of laughs and deaths!

**Dungeon Dojo Invitational Season 2**\
29th - 30th of September and 1st of October\
Teams all across the globe compete against each other to see who can push the highest keys, and put to practice all the training done in the Dojo over the course of the past months!

[Join the Discord channel to learn more](http://discord.com/invite/gsF3TSx7ju).
