New land, new adventures, and new treasures await to be discovered in the Dragon Isles, along with a variety of mounts, pets, and toys to add to your collection.

#### Currencies/Resources:

The various factions in the Dragon Isles accept many different items in exchange for their goods, but here are some of the main currencies you'll want to have on hand.

- **Gold**
- **Polished Pet Charm**
- **Dragon Isles Supplies**: Various resources and building materials that can assist the friendly factions of the Dragon Isles in their fight against the Primalists. Used to purchase items unlocked from renown.
- **Magmote:** A tiny piece of magmatic fire energy, used as a currency by the Worldbreakers.
- **Elemental Overflow:** The essence of fallen foes imbued with the power of a primal storm.

You'll need to use the [Magma Shell](https://www.wowhead.com/beta/item=201883/empty-magma-shell?brl) item on an [Empowered Snail](https://www.wowhead.com/beta/npc=199010/empowered-snail?brl). Getting to it is going to take a little ingenuity (and maybe the help of a [toy](https://www.wowhead.com/beta/item=200116/everlasting-horn-of-lavaswimming?brl)). Enjoy the swim!
