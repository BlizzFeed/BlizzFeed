#### Here are this week's highlights:

#### Dragonflight

- **Guardians of the Dream 10.2 PTR:** The Guardians of the Dream PTR is open for testing; hop on and share your feedback. Check out the latest [development notes](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/guardians-of-the-dream-ptr-development-notes/1666552/1), and focused feedback threads are available for [Hunters](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/feedback-hunters/1666453), [Warlocks](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/feedback-warlocks/1666451), [Rogues](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/feedback-rogues/1666450), [Priests](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/feedback-priests/1666449), [Druids](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/feedback-druids/1666448), [healing changes](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/healing-throughput-and-mana-generation-in-guardians-of-the-dream/1666444), [class sets](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/class-sets-in-guardians-of-the-dream/1666443/2), and the [Battleground Blitz Brawl](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/guardians-of-the-dream-preview-battleground-blitz-brawl/1666439).
- **Fury Incarnate Known Issues:** Check out the list of the [latest known issues](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/patch-1017-known-issues-and-information/1665105) in Fury Incarnate.
- **Polished Pet Charms:** The [charm rewards](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/polished-pet-charm-reward-reduction/1608461/7) for Dragonflight quests should be back to where they were before a bug that reduced the number rewarded was found. Additional ways of earning this currency are being looked into, but there are no further details to share.

#### WoW Classic

- **Exploitive Account Actions:** The latest monthly summary of [actions against exploitative accounts](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/recent-actions-against-exploitative-accounts-%E2%80%93-august-2023/1666294) was posted on Wednesday.
- **Wrath Classic PTR Update:** The latest [Wrath Classic 3.4.3 PTR Development Notes](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/wrath-classic-343-ptr-development-notes/1660806/5) were posted on Thursday and included details on the first iteration of the Random Dungeon Finder feature.
