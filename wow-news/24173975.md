The time has come to gather once more, heroes of Azeroth! The Warcraft 30th Anniversary Event in London is just around the corner, and we can’t wait to celebrate with you in true Warcraft style.

---

### Calling All Heroes of Azeroth!

On 22nd February , we invite you to step into the heart of the celebration at the Soane Hall, London. To enter, you must hold a valid ticket, so come prepared! [The second wave of tickets](https://www.tixr.com/groups/warcraft30-world-tour) will be available from 15:00 GMT on Wednesday, the 5th February.

### Arrive Early – First Come, First Served!

While the halls of Soane are grand, space is still limited. We strongly encourage all guests to arrive on time when doors open at 17:00, as entry will be granted on a first-come, first-served basis. We want as many of you as possible to experience the magic of this celebration, so be sure to plan accordingly!

#### What Awaits You?

Prepare for an exclusive experience unique to the London stop of the Warcraft Anniversary World Tour. Here’s just a glimpse of what’s in store:

- **Developer Signing Sessions** – get ready to meet World of Warcraft developers Holly Longdale, Ion Hazzikostas and Maria Hamilton and Hearthstone developers Christian Scharling and Cora Georgiou in person.
- **Live Music – Wind Rose**, the mighty dwarven metal band, will take the stage with their thunderous anthems. More special guests will be announced soon.
- **Fortune of Azeroth** – test your luck and win epic prizes from Blizzard Gear Store, MSI, Razer and more!
- **Tavern for Guild & Community Meetups** – connect with old friends and forge new alliances
- **Trading Post open for business** – bring your pin collection and trade with fellow adventurers!
- **Goody Bags** packed with London-exclusive treasures as no event - no one will leave empty handed!
- **Food, Drinks, and Personalized Loot** – because every hero needs sustenance and souvenirs.

---

This is more than just an event—it’s a celebration of camaraderie, nostalgia, and the incredible Warcraft community. Whether you're a seasoned veteran or a newcomer to Azeroth, this night will be one for the books.

So, dust off your armor, rally your guildmates, and prepare to make memories that will last a lifetime. We’ll see you at Soane Hall—For the Horde! For the Alliance! For Azeroth!
