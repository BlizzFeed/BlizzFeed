## Travel to Val and Naigtal to Quell Leaders of the Void

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/9f/9FX9ZUZR7YJ51780334964712.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/9f/9FX9ZUZR7YJ51780334964712.png)

Join the Illidari demon hunters and the Army of the Light to venture into two new locations—Val and Naigtal—to cut off the threat of the Void and their leaders at the source. You’ll undertake new World Quests, defeat rare enemies, take part in events, and put a stop to the brutal attacks on Azeroth.

To begin, speak with Riftblade Maella in Silvermoon City to start a new questline, then travel through the portal in Voidstorm in Howling Reach to your destination. Each area rotates weekly, becoming available for one week at a time.

Group up, gear up, and meet us on the front lines in Val and Naigtal. Full details can be found in our [previously published overview](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/news/24271372/). We'll see you there!

## Mists of Pandaria Classic: The Siege of Orgrimmar Now Live

Azeroth's heroes have stood against the Thunder King, yet a darker storm now gathers in Garrosh Hellscream. With the Vale of Eternal Blossoms corrupted and Orgrimmar in chaos, the Warchief must be stopped before all are dragged into darkness.

## Mists of Pandaria Classic: The Siege of Orgrimmar Raid Now Live

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_header/b7/B7JS4GHJIP0A1780081450090.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_header/b7/B7JS4GHJIP0A1780081450090.png)

The Siege of Orgrimmar Raid is a sprawling, two‑part battle that takes players from the scarred ruins of the Vale of Eternal Blossoms to the heart of the Horde's capital, Orgrimmar in Durotar.

The tensions stoked by Garrosh Hellscream’s arrogance have reached a boiling point with the devastation unleashed upon the Vale of Eternal Blossoms. Garrosh’s reign stands on the brink of ruin, and champions of the Alliance and the Horde must press the assault forward and topple the ruthless Warchief once and for all!

Full details on the rewards and achievements to claim after your victory are in [our raid overview](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/news/24252017).

## Soar Through Turbulent Timeways 1 July to 12 August

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/sk/SKSOIR48XY231780083361377.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/sk/SKSOIR48XY231780083361377.png)

Once more, the Bronze Dragonflight has discovered growing disturbances in the timeways as several rapidly intersect our own.

**When**: 1 July–12 August

Timewalk through a previous expansion each week for six consecutive weeks, beginning and ending with *Dragonflight*. This grand tour through *World of Warcraft’s* past features six Timewalking Dungeons: Algeth’ar Academy, Halls of Infusion, Neltharus, Ruby Life Pools, The Azure Vaults, and Brackenhide Hollow. Players can also shop at a new *Dragonflight* vendor, Xydan, located in the Bronze Enclave in Valdrakken.

Don't let time run out—gather your allies and step into the timeways for adventures across Azeroth’s past. More information can be found in our [previously published article](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/news/24264422/).
