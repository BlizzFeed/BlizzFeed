# Mobilize Your Allies and Thwart Incoming Void Assaults

- URL: https://news.blizzard.com/en-gb/article/24266324/mobilize-your-allies-and-thwart-incoming-void-assaults
![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/ve/VEOWD82KESE51776188244388.png)

> In the 12.0.5 content update, players can push back against the Void as they begin powerful Void Strikes and Void Incursions in Eversong Woods and Zul’Aman— for Azeroth, for fun, and for new rewards.

---

The forces of the Void are vying for position and control amongst themselves in a grasp for more power. As they seek every opportunity they can, attacks begin in the Eversong Woods and Zul’Aman zones. Succeed in putting a stop to them, and you reap new rewards.

---

## Put a Stop to Void Strike****s

Void Strikes take part in Eversong Woods or Zul’Aman on a weekly rotation. Once completed, a new one will begin in another part of the zone and continue to rotate through the zone as they are completed.

![alternate-image-name](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/WXY9ABHDVH791776187753426.png)
![alternate-image-name](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/M049S3WC2PD21776187753355.png)
![alternate-image-name](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/LH6CY91IPLZP1776187753437.png)

Completing Void Strikes provides a Field Pouch with each completion containing **Field Accolades**, gold, **Voidlight Marl**, and more, acknowledging your acumen in the field.

---

## Brace for the Void Incursion

![alternate-image-name](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/WQ6QXGWEJJAX1776204412341.png)
![alternate-image-name](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/6VNIFL2S6A7U1776204412308.png)

Void Incursions occur when enough Void Strikes have been completed in the zone in which they are active. A progress bar will show on the map allowing you to track when it begins. Gather your allies and head to the gates of Silvermoon City in Eversong Woods or Speaker's Rest in Zul'Aman to take part, as it will take the might of many of Azeroth’s champions to defeat them.

---

## A Quest!

Ranger Captain Lilatha and Kul’amara the Fierce located on the second floor of The Bazaar will also have quests for you to complete rewarding a Ranger’s Cache for completion.

> *“Cache containing Field Accolades, Relic Coffer Key shards, materials for upgrading gear, gold, and more.”*

---

## Reap Your Rewards

When the dust settles and you’ve collected your just rewards, head back to the second level of The Bazaar in Silvermoon City to purchase a variety of items with your **Field Accolades** and **Voidlight Marl**. You can purchase new Housing Decor, armor appearances, Heroic and Champion level items, mounts, and more. Visit the [Ritual Sites article](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/news/24244461) to see the full list of vendor items.

---

Whether you’re working on your first character or an alternate character, Void Assaults offer a way to get up to speed and get into the action in *Midnight*.
