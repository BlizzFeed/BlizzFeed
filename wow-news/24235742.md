## Launch into Turbo-Boost 21 October

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_header/cu/CU34UJQAITR61758834663965.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_header/cu/CU34UJQAITR61758834663965.png)

We’re turning things up a notch during The War Within Season 3 with increased rewards and improvements to help you gear up your Warband with Turbo-Boost. With the initial 11.2.5 release, Hero and Myth upgrade tracks will extend 2 upgrades (from 6 to 8), and crafted items will be recraftable with an Augmentation Matrix for additional power.

As of the week of 21 October, the cap for Crests is lifted, and Valorstones become Warbound for the remainder of the season. Mythic+ drop rates for Warband until Equipped gear are also being doubled. Ethereal Voidsplinters may be earned in Mythic+ dungeons, Manaforge Omega, Delves, and rated PvP to fuel the Catalyst.

Visit [our overview](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/news/24230698) for more details.

After you’ve claimed your rewards on Twitch, please make sure you’re logging into the Battle.net region you’d like to receive the drop(s) on, as the first region you log into will be where your items will be delivered. It can take up to 24 hours to receive your reward(s) in-game after claiming. Read our Frequently Asked Questions section for more information.

## Make Sure to Don Your Kilts, Brewfest is Here!

![Dwarves](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_header/wc/WC7LQ12RFPZV1758324462367.png)

“First celebrated by the dwarves, Brewfest now holds appeal among all the races of Azeroth!”

Eat delicious food, drink bountiful beverages, and ride rams around barrels of apples! Brewfest has arrived, and you can enjoy all that and more!

**When**: 23 September–9 October\
**Where**: Dornogal, Valdrakken, Ironforge, and Orgrimmar\
**Currency:** Brewfest Prize Tokens—used to purchase most items at the Brewfest vendor.

Brewfest comes to *The War Within*! Strike up a conversation with the Holiday Enthusiast in Dornogal to begin the *brewfest*ivities. In addition to enjoying the usual fun in Ironforge and Orgimmar, new Brewfest quests can be completed throughout Khaz Algar, and the new Brewfest Banquet Outdoor Scenario will keep you hopping.

What's Brewfest without some stout rewards? Fresh items are available on the holiday vendor, but you can take the best of the fest with you wherever you go by bringing down Coren Direbrew—once daily, you'll have a chance to get the **Brewfest Bomber** mount.

You’ll also have an opportunity to get the Brewer's garb, including kilts, as a reward for completing the Brewfest Banquet. Customize yourself for adventure with other festive kilt colors available from the holiday vendor, or show off your Brewfest charm with a **Brewer’s Balloon**.

Stop, colla*brew*ate, and listen—grab your allies and hustle to get the most points possible during the new Brewfest Banquet Outdoor Scenario.
