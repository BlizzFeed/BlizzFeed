Leap into new adventures astride Jade, Bright Foreseer—Pandaria's *hop*timistic rabbit spirit that will carry you around Azeroth and beyond with pure joy.

Add the Jade, Bright Foreseer\* hoppy rabbit spirit mount to your muster of mounts! Purchase this mount from in-game or the Blizzard Shop or get it with a 6-Month Subscription along with the Hoplet pet in World of Warcraft®: Wrath of the Lich King Classic™. The mount is also available as a [separate purchase](http://shop.battle.net/product/world-of-warcraft-mount-jade).

#### Hop into a Bright Future

The presence of this majestic Pandaren rabbit spirit brings joy and happiness and is heralded as a wonderful omen. Legend says that in its eyes, you can glimpse the greatest happiness of your life, even if it is yet to come.

[![Video](https://markdown-videos-api.jorgenkh.no/youtube/EwM8gYvkjpA?width=1280&height=720)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EwM8gYvkjpA)

Jade, Bright Foreseer will be automatically added to your Collection once you purchase the [6-month subscription](https://shop.battle.net/product/world-of-warcraft-subscription) from the Blizzard Shop. Welcome this *hop*some addition to your assortment of mounts!

The 6-Month Subscription offer includes the *ear*resistible Hoplet pet\*\* for your *Wrath of the Lich King Classic* characters.

Players who purchased the 12-Month subscription will automatically receive the Jade Bright Foreseer and the Hoplet pet. Players on an existing 6-Month recurring subscription with a future renewal date will receive these items at no additional charge, no later than January 31, 2023 (must be redeemed by July 31, 2023) through their Battle.net launcher.
