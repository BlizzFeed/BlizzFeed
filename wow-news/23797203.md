### Upcoming World of Warcraft®: Dragonflight Expansion

The dragonflights of Azeroth have returned, called upon to defend their ancestral home, the Dragon Isles. Surging with elemental magic and the life energies of Azeroth, the Isles are awakening once more, and it’s up to you to explore their primordial wonder and discover long-forgotten secrets.

- For a closer look, join a full panel of World of Warcraft developers in the [Dragonflight Deep Dive](https://worldofwarcraft.com/news/23785116/) as they take you into the Dragon Isles and share insights about the new lands to explore, the creatures you’ll meet, and take you deeper into the various systems and changes that will come with the expansion.

### WoW Classic News and Development

#### Return to the Icy Realm of Northrend

[![Video](https://markdown-videos-api.jorgenkh.no/youtube/racf0luFU8Y?width=1280&height=720)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=racf0luFU8Y)

Later this year, you will again get to explore bone-chilling lands, storm Icecrown Citadel, and halt the Lich King’s baleful efforts. Only the most stalwart can challenge the Lich King and end his reign of terror in this rerelease of the 2008 expansion. [Check out the overview for more details](https://worldofwarcraft.com/news/23793177/), and don’t forget to sign up for the [upcoming beta](https://wowclassic.blizzard.com/).

#### Season of Mastery: The Ahn’Qiraj War Effort Begins April 21

![WoWClassic_AhnQiraj_Blog_Thumb_1200x675.png](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/ng/NGB0T0D3PMMR1650497265126.png)

The next Content Update for WoW Classic Season of Mastery will be available worldwide on April 21 at 3:00 p.m. PDT. The Ahn’Qiraj War Effort will begin at that time, and both factions must heed the call. Any hero brave enough to undertake the epic quest to become a Scarab Lord will be able to begin their journey. Learn more about what is [new in the Season of Mastery](https://worldofwarcraft.com/news/23785119/).

#### Mythic Dungeon International

MDI returns on Friday, April 22, only on [YouTube](http://youtube.com/Warcraft)! The fun begins with the same format as last season: 24 of the top teams from the Time Trials are sorted into three illustrious groups of eight, with a different group competing for $30,000 in prizing each weekend. The top two teams from each group will advance to the MDI GLOBAL FINALS! Everything you need to know to watch MDI Season 3 and other key dates and info can be found in the [Viewer’s Guide](https://worldofwarcraft.com/news/23785088/).
