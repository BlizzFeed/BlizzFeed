#### Pets Available for Adoption

![An adorable Quilen Cub waits patiently to be adopted.](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/WDYNW8J2IMXP1593473182509.jpg)

Quilen Cub

This charming pet from Pandaria is back!

Although Lucky Quilen Cubs resemble animated statues, their hard-as-stone skin conceals an affectionate heart. These trusted companions will look up to you and faithfully follow your every step—and might even bring some good fortune along the way.

![Alterac Brew Pup](//bnetproduct-a.akamaihd.net//dg/701/95454D9189ABBED941017B8AB75EAE70BEBE1C9E.jpg)

##### Alterac Brew Pup

Descended from a long pedigree of champion rescue hounds, the Alterac Brew Pup has a nose for finding travelers in distress in the high altitudes of the Alterac Mountains. However, it’s the massive pup’s ability to reignite your soul with its "warm spirits" on a cold night that will make this furry friend your most trusted and treasured companion.

![Cinder Kitten](//bnetproduct-a.akamaihd.net//dg/262/A9DF26E76653BF6340F867A4F49C219B232F4B24.jpg)

##### Cinder Kitten

Combining the cuteness of a newborn cat with the searing magma of the Elemental Plane of Fire, the Cinder Kitten arrives on scene as the hottest companion pet. Get each of your guildmates to get one of their own, and turn every raid night into a fiery feline frenzy.

![Lil’ Ragnaros](//bnetproduct-a.akamaihd.net//dg/264/5571CCC01A295A94669EA9226DD9679A2E32B8D4.jpg)

##### Lil’ Ragnaros

Get your guildmates fired up with Lil' Ragnaros, the mini-elemental lord. Not only can he serve as a cooking fire, but he'll also sporadically ignite nearby critters that wander too close to his burning form. As a being of pure elemental fire, he will even submerge into the world's molten core when you start moving and then reemerge next to you wherever you stop.

![Soul of the Aspects](//bnetproduct-a.akamaihd.net//dg/267/E83EBF32CDD411EDA65CCB517BF10FD0BDE27CD9.jpg)

##### Soul of the Aspects

As your faithful companion pet, the Soul of the Aspects flies by your side through the skies of Azeroth, celebrating your adventures together with aerial somersaults and twisting corkscrews.

![Twilight](//bnetproduct-a.akamaihd.net//fbe/2b8e4267d4e90112a43d8deccf8b047e-wow-twilight-feature-01.jpg)

##### Twilight

This cuddlesome little critter can’t wait to curl up by the campfire after your latest campaign. She’ll captivate your comrades with her chromatic color-changing coat and climb her way into the core of your collection…and she’s especially cunning in combat. Adopt this cute little creature today!

#### Spectacular Mounts

![The Majestic Imperial Quilen is fearless and fiercely loyal.](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/D0HAVS2H4NSC1593473182510.jpg)

Imperial Quilen

The majestic flying mount from Pandaria is back.

Powerful mogu sorcerers create their favorite companions — fearless and ferociously loyal quilen — by breathing life into a stone using an ancient, secret magic. If you ever find yourself in a need of a devoted mount with magically conjured wings, the gold-plated Imperial Quilen is patiently waiting.

![Enchanted Fey Dragon](//bnetproduct-a.akamaihd.net//dg/681/9E78DFD82E5F2C060DDB5D379411D3A8967684D3.jpg)

##### Enchanted Fey Dragon

Entrusted with protecting the Emerald Dream, the Enchanted Fey Dragon absorbs magic and exudes style—with a chroma-shifting twist. Saddle up on one of these iridescent sprites and enjoy the instant street (and sky) cred that comes with being one of the last bastions of cool.

![Heart of the Aspects](//bnetproduct-a.akamaihd.net//dg/270/82C94E738CF3D82EF3A0F46C6C95D66D0C1AF144.jpg)

##### Heart of the Aspects

When attacking from the air, it’s best to advance from the angle of the sun so as to blind your foes. Or, just ride a luminous dragon that glows like a thousand suns. Either way, you’re good.

![Hogrus, Swine of Good Fortune](https://bnetproduct-a.akamaihd.net//2b/cb18880fcef4e666d7ee1fe3ed45308b-Product_Feature_MOUNT_Showcase_Hogrus_MN01.jpg)

##### Hogrus, Swine of Good Fortune

Legends say that in his hour of deepest doubt, Emperor Shaohao, last ruler of the Pandaren Empire, was visited by Hogrus, Swine of Good Fortune, as he meditated upon his people’s future. Today, pandaren across Azeroth consider Hogrus to be a sign of prosperity and great things to come. You would be wise to seek his blessing as you pursue your own fortune, wherever the four winds may take you.

![Luminous Starseeker](//bnetproduct-a.akamaihd.net//fae/d5fdfb036c4964c314c22747a6dfab7c-wow-luminous-starseeker-feature-01.jpg)

##### Luminous Starseeker

Drawing on the shifting void, fel, and otherworldly energies surrounding him, the Luminous Starseeker lets you travel in style astride this mount’s undulating hues. Saddle up, because this polychromatic beast will travel as fast as your riding skill will take you.

![Warforged Nightmare](//bnetproduct-a.akamaihd.net//dg/1422/1BA67BA6FDBC6FCD68F36279E889FCDA5CE4629F.jpg)

##### Warforged Nightmare

Charge into battle astride a Warforged Nightmare, unleashing unbridled chaos upon your enemies! Ownership of this terrifying steed, crafted from cold steel and vengeful spirits by grandmaster smiths, allows you to place a Nightmarish Hitching Post. This mysterious artifact has the power to summon a Warforged Nightmare for every member of your war party strong enough to handle the reins and worthy enough to be given the honor.
