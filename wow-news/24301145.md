## World of Warcraft Midnight: Eclipse Revealed

[![Video](https://markdown-videos-api.jorgenkh.no/youtube/tlJZzTcHrH0?width=1280&height=720)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tlJZzTcHrH0)

The next major content update for Midnight, Eclipse, brings players face-to-face with Xal’atath as she hovers just within reach of her ultimate objective: the Worldsoul. With Azeroth’s fate hanging in the balance, players will need to confront her directly and stop her from claiming victory. The events of Eclipse will unfold in the worldcore, setting the stage for a revelation and conflict that will carry the Worldsoul Saga toward its final chapter in The Last Titan.

## The Final Chapter Begins in The Last Titan

[![Video](https://markdown-videos-api.jorgenkh.no/youtube/F9Xb5PhF5pc?width=1280&height=720)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F9Xb5PhF5pc)

*The Last Titan* is more than a return to Northrend—it’s a reflection on generations of courage, sacrifice, and unforgettable deeds across Azeroth. From the Shifting Sands of Ahn’Qiraj to the Frozen Throne, the Worldbreaker, the Burning Legion, and even the realms beyond life and death, heroes have fought time and again to defend our home. As Azeroth calls on us one last time, this next chapter will offer a glimpse at the challenges waiting ahead when more is revealed early next year, after the events of *Midnight: Eclipse*.

For the first time in more than two decades, a new campaign has arrived in *Warcraft III: Reforged*. *Forsaken Kingdom* follows the fall of Lordaeron and its transformation into becoming the Undercity, introducing new faces alongside familiar figures, like Sylvanas, as players discover what it truly means to be Forsaken. *Warcraft III: Reforged—Forsaken Kingdom* is live now and playable on the show floor at BlizzCon.

Before there was a world, there was war. Return to the conflicts that helped forge Azeroth’s future with *Warcraft III Reforged: Forsaken Kingdom*, a new campaign for *Warcraft III: Reforged* that begins in the ruins of Lordaeron and leads into the first days of *World of Warcraft*.

## World of Warcraft: Forever Announced

![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/wt/WT7SF76UI28P1789240743326.png)

[![Video](https://markdown-videos-api.jorgenkh.no/youtube/xXaXYxcbA6A?width=1280&height=720)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xXaXYxcbA6A)

[![Video](https://markdown-videos-api.jorgenkh.no/youtube/uQuKE0KDgiM?width=1280&height=720)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uQuKE0KDgiM)

*World of Warcraft: Forever* launches November 4, with beta beginning Thursday, September 17. Built as a new permanent home alongside modern and Classic WoW, *Forever* invites past, current, and new players into a familiar yet fresh Azeroth where the world takes center stage, community comes first, and the journey matters as much as the destination. Set after the events of *Warcraft III Reforged: Forsaken Kingdom* and before Molten Core, players will return to the original continents, adventure to level 60, and continue progressing through new stories, experiences, and updates inspired by years of player passion and feedback.

Return Sunday, September 13 at 10:00 am PDT to watch the live stream of the World of Warcraft: Forever –Deep Dive panel where the World of Warcraft development team will take you deeper into the philosophy, systems, and design choices for a reimagined Azeroth.
