Changing your faction also includes a free race change for your class, so you’ll fit in with your new friends when you join them on their adventures all over Azeroth.

Available Faction Change pack options:

- 1 for £13.30 (Regularly £19.00)
- 3 for £25.90 (Regularly £37.00)
- ~~6~~ 8 for £60.00

To learn more about the Faction Change service and any restrictions, visit our [support page](https://eu.battle.net/support/en/article/32063).

That’s two extra Paid Character Transfers for the regular price of a 6-Character Transfer bundle. Once the sale is over, this bundle will revert to the regular 6-character transfer bundle.

Ready to make your move? Purchase Faction Change Packs, Paid Character Transfers, Name Changes, or Race Changes from the [Battle.net shop](https://us.shop.battle.net/en-us/product/world-of-warcraft-service-character-transfer) or launch World of Warcraft and click on the Shop button on the character select screen.

Once you’ve purchased your Faction Changes, Character Transfers, Name Change, or Race Change within the Battle.net shop or the in-game shop, a new icon will appear on your character selection screen. To activate a game service, click on the token, then select the character you wish to move. Any unused Faction Change tokens or Character Transfers will remain on your account for use at a future time.
