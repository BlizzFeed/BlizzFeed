---

The battle for Outland heats up on 14 May as *WoW Burning Crusade Classic Anniversary Edition: Overlords of Outland* launches globally on all realms at 23:00 BST.

Take the fight to Illidan Stormrage’s lieutenants, brave two new raids, and chase new rewards across the shattered world of Outland.

- Two New Raids: The Eye and Serpentshrine Cavern
- New Reputation Factions: Ogri’la and Sha’tari Skyguard
- Arena Season 2
- New Profession Recipes
- Druid Swift Flight Form

---

Get ready to adventure across the shattered skies and torn lands of Outland as you continue to protect Azeroth from the threat of destruction, beginning 14 May.
