## Scurrywind Groveglider Collection

The Scurrywind Groveglider Collection\* is only available on modern *WoW®* realms. Grab onto the reins of these mounts with the complete Collection in one bundle.

The mounts are also available individually, so you can pick out your favorites or get the complete collection at a reduced price. If one or more mounts in the bundle are already part of your in-game Collection, the reduced bundle price is dynamic and will automatically adjust to exclude those items from the bundle.

## Scurrysoar Groveglider Collection

The Scurrysoar Groveglider Collection\* is only available on *WoW® Classic* progression realms (currently *Mists of Pandaria Classic™)*.

Like their Scurrywind counterparts, the mounts are also available individually, so you can pick out your favorites or get the complete collection at a reduced price. If one or more mounts in the bundle are already part of your in-game Collection, the reduced bundle price is dynamic and will automatically adjust to exclude those items from the bundle.
