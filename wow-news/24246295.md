In *Midnight*, we’ve opened the doors to boundless creativity with the introduction of Housing. To shine a light on this new feature, we’re bringing in some of the biggest creator names and best builders alike to go head-to-head in a delightful interior design competition!

You don’t have to be a competitor to get in on the fun. Share your own Housing creations on social media by using the hashtag #AzerothInteriors.

---

### Format

We’re calling up six pairs of prominent creators from around the world to create a concept, execute on their ideas, and produce exemplary player houses worth coming home to.

Each pair will contain a “Homeowner” and a “Builder” who work together to design their dream interior in Azeroth.

- **Phase One:** each of the teams will gather inspiration into a personal Pinterest board.
- **Phase Two:** The building portion of the challenge begins bringing ideas to life.
- **Phase Three:** Join us for a live stream where we’ll join the judges for walk-throughs of each home to determine who will claim victory.

We’ll be looking for the build that best exemplifies the spirit of Azeroth and *World of Warcraft*. When the dust settles, the winner will be featured as a custom listing on Real Estate site [Zillow](https://www.zillow.com/) and will be available for you to explore.

> ### **Schedule**
>
> - 17 February - Phase One: Ideation on Pinterest
> - 19-21 February - Phase Two: Building
> - 24 February - Phase Three: Walkthroughs and Judging
