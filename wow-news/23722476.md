> Chains of Domination introduced even more new mounts, pets, and treasures throughout the Shadowlands and the Maw. Will you brave the challenges within to claim them?

---

There are various collectibles available to be discovered in World of Warcraft and many ways to lay claim to these mounts, pets, and toys.  Whether it's from delving into the Sanctum of Domination, perusing the wares in Tazavesh, the Veiled Market, or venturing deep into Korthia, the City of Secrets, you're sure to expand your store of treasures in the Shadowlands.

#### Currencies

There are many kinds of currencies used in Shadowlands. Here are just some of the types that you'll want to make sure you have at hand.

- **Gold**
- **Reservoir Anima:** A measure of the Anima your Covenant has at its disposal.
- **Polished Pet Charms:** Redeemable at pet battle masters.
- **Cataloged Research:** As you bring the Archivist relics, he will catalog it, which will allow you to start Field Studies to learn more about them or identify relics in his care that you can use in the field.
- **Stygia**: The byproduct of the violent destruction of a Soul within the Maw.
- **Phantasma:** This extinguished strand of anima can be used to gain power within Torghast. Leaving Torghast will destroy all Phantasma.
