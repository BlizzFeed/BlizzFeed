# Garb Yourself in Greatness with the Gladiator’s Ragged Armor

> Intimidate your foes when you enter the battlefield garbed in ragged armor crafted in the style of the great gladiator Lo'Gosh!

---

Intimidate your foes when you enter the battlefield garbed in the **Gladiator's Ragged Armor**1 crafted in the style of the great gladiator Lo'Gosh, but grab it before it leaves the Battle.net® Shop2 indefinitely on 30 April 2024.

[Garb Yourself in Greatness](https://shop.battle.net/product/world-of-warcraft-gladiators-ragged-armor)

The Gladiator's Ragged Armor will arrive in a future rotation of the Trading Post, but no sooner than three months after it leaves the Shop. Certain restrictions apply. Visit [shop.battle.net](https://shop.battle.net/) for more information and detailed promotion end dates.

---

1 Requires World of Warcraft® Subscription or Game Time.\
2 Not available in World of Warcraft Classic games.
