## World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives

Every class in World of Warcraft: Forever is being revisited with an approach rooted in the original World of Warcraft, keeping the foundation players remember while making thoughtful updates that open up more choice, identity, and room to experiment.

[![alternate-image-name](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_header/t4/T46ED5B4T7V11791309695380.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_header/t4/T46ED5B4T7V11791309695380.png)

### Mage and Shaman

Learn how Mage updates expand Arcane, Fire, and Frost magic with new hybrid possibilities like Frostfire Bolt, and how Shaman changes make totems easier to manage, weapon imbues longer-lasting, and Elemental Combat, Enhancement, and Restoration builds more versatile.

Whether you prefer mastering spell timing as a Mage or shaping the battlefield through totems and elemental power as a Shaman, these updates give each class more room to grow while preserving the strengths that set them apart.

### Rogue and Warlock

Explore how updates to Rogues and Warlocks build on each class's defining tools, tactics, and playstyles. Whether striking from the shadows or wielding forbidden power, Rogues and Warlocks offer distinct ways to approach every encounter.
