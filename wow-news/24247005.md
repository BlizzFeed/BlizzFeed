---

There are many ways for players to collect Housing decor in World of Warcraft. One of these ways is through completing achievements. Players can earn a decor from achievements from myriad activities and many of these may have been earned already throughout your time in Azeroth.

Some achievements award decor once (and again, only once per account) and then unlock the ability to acquire more copies elsewhere. To purchase additional copies of that item you’ll need to earn the achievement first. All non-trophy decor (trophies will be introduced with the release of Midnight) can be reacquired from a vendor if the player wants more of them. Usually, the item carries the same name as the decor itself, but not always. For example, if the player wants additional copies of the **Head of the Broodmother** decor after the first from earning the achievement More Dots (25 Player), the goblin innkeeper Axle in Mudsprocket will sell the player a **Definitely Real, Not At All Fake Head of the Broodmother** that may not be real but when used as decor is indistinguishable.
