While soaring the skies in the land of dragons, you'll want to ensure you complete the activities from around the Dragon Isles before their rewards are no longer available (or not as easily obtained) when *The War Within™* arrives on 26 August.

---

### Dragonflight Season 4

##### Raids

- Raids will no longer be "Awakened" when *The War Within* launches, and the following  rewards, achievements, and teleports tied to them will no longer be attainable:
  - [**Voyaging Wilderling**](https://www.wowhead.com/item=217340/voyaging-wilderlings-harness?brl) mount attained from [*Awakening the Dragonflight Raids*](https://www.wowhead.com/achievement=19574/awakening-the-dragonflight-raids?brl).
  - "*Awakened Hero*" title attained from [Heroic: Awakening the Dragonflight Raids](https://www.wowhead.com/achievement=19575/heroic-awakening-the-dragonflight-raids?brl).
  - Raid teleports [Path of the Primal Prison](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=432254/path-of-the-primal-prison?brl), [Path of the Bitter Legacy](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=432257/path-of-the-bitter-legacy?brl), and [Path of the Scorching Dream](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=432258/path-of-the-scorching-dream?brl) earned from [Mythic: Awakening the Dragonflight Raids](https://www.wowhead.com/achievement=19576/mythic-awakening-the-dragonflight-raids?brl).
  - Season 4 Awakened Raid Achievements:
    - Awakened Storms: [Normal](https://www.wowhead.com/achievement=19564/awakened-storms?brl), [Heroic](https://www.wowhead.com/achievement=19565/heroic-awakened-storms?brl), [Mythic](https://www.wowhead.com/achievement=19566/mythic-awakened-storms?brl)
    - Awakened Shadows: [Normal](https://www.wowhead.com/achievement=19567/awakened-shadows?brl), [Heroic](https://www.wowhead.com/achievement=19568/heroic-awakened-shadows?brl), [Mythic](https://www.wowhead.com/achievement=19569/mythic-awakened-shadows?brl)
    - Awakened Flames: [Normal](https://www.wowhead.com/achievement=19570/awakened-flames?brl), [Heroic](https://www.wowhead.com/achievement=19571/heroic-awakened-flames?brl), [Mythic](https://www.wowhead.com/achievement=19572/mythic-awakened-flames?brl)
- After *The War Within* launches, the ability to obtain the [**Reins of Anu'relos, Flame's Guidance**](https://www.wowhead.com/item=210061/reins-of-anurelos-flames-guidance?brl) will become much more difficult. Learning the mount on any class also awards the [**Feather of the Blazing Somnowl**](https://www.wowhead.com/item=210754/feather-of-the-blazing-somnowl) and the [**Cinder of Companionship**](https://www.wowhead.com/item=211314/cinder-of-companionship).

##### Dungeons

- Mythic+ Season 4 ends, and the **Font of Power** will no longer appear in the Dragonflight Season 4 Dungeons.
- All Season 4 Achievements and rewards will no longer be attainable:
  - [**Dragonflight Keystone Conqueror: Season 4**](https://www.wowhead.com/achievement=19781/dragonflight-keystone-conqueror-season-four?brl)achievement and ***"***[***the Draconic***](https://www.wowhead.com/title/the-draconic-806?brl)***"*** title: Attain a Mythic+ Rating of 1500 during Dragonflight Season 4.
  - [**Dragonflight Keystone Master: Season 4**](https://www.wowhead.com/achievement=19782/dragonflight-keystone-master-season-four?brl) achievement and its [**Infinite Armoredon**](https://www.wowhead.com/item=213438/infinite-armoredon?brl) mount reward: Attain a Mythic+ Rating of 2000 during Dragonflight Season 4.
  - [**Dragonflight Keystone Hero: Season 4**](https://www.wowhead.com/achievement=19783/dragonflight-keystone-hero-season-four?brl)achievement: Attain a Mythic+ Rating of 2500 during Dragonflight Season 4.

Keystone Hero Dungeon Teleports:  [The Azure Vault](https://www.wowhead.com/achievement=16645/keystone-hero-the-azure-vault?brl), [Halls of Infusion](https://www.wowhead.com/achievement=16646/keystone-hero-halls-of-infusion?brl), [Neltharus](https://www.wowhead.com/achievement=16644/keystone-hero-neltharus?brl), [Algeth'ar Academy](https://www.wowhead.com/achievement=16643/keystone-hero-algethar-academy?brl), [Uldaman: Legacy of Tyr](https://www.wowhead.com/achievement=16639/keystone-hero-uldaman-legacy-of-tyr?brl), [Brackenhide Hollow](https://www.wowhead.com/achievement=16642/keystone-hero-brackenhide-hollow?brl), [Ruby Life Pools](https://www.wowhead.com/achievement=16640/keystone-hero-ruby-life-pools?brl), and [The Nokhud Offensive](https://www.wowhead.com/achievement=16641/keystone-hero-the-nokhud-offensive?brl).

### Complete WoW Remix: Mists of Pandaria Before 19 August

Relive epic adventures and experience the wonders of Pandaria anew. World of Warcraft Remix: Mists of Pandaria ends on August 19, so be sure to get your Remix rewards before they are whisked away into the mists.

Once WoW Remix ends, characters will be converted to regular characters, and all Remix-specific items and perks will be removed. However, those characters will still be playable in *The War Within* Pre-Expansion and beyond.

Learn more about WoW Remix: Mists of Pandaria in our [overview article](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/news/24092672/).

### Radiant Echoes Pre-Expansion Event Ends 27 August

Confront the memories of Azeroth during the new Radiant Echoes event and collect Residual Memories to purchase new items to add to your Warband collection.

**When:** 31 July – 27 August\
**Where:** Dalaran (hub), Dragonblight, Dustwallow Marsh, and Searing Gorge\
**Currency:** Residual Memories – Half-forgotten memories can be glimpsed in the light, playing across this iridescent powder.\
**Levels:** 10-70

Players with and without *The War Within* can participate in this event. Learn more about this event in our [overview article](https://worldofwarcraft.com/news/24057483).

---

*The War Within* goes live on August 26 at 23:00 BST globally. Players who have purchased the [Epic Edition](https://shop.battle.net/product/world-of-warcraft-the-war-within) of *The War Within* can participate in Early Access beginning 22 August.

See you in Azeroth!
