# Curse of Ula’tek: Raid Finder Wing 3 Now Live!

- URL: https://news.blizzard.com/en-gb/article/24294062/curse-of-ulatek-raid-finder-wing-3-now-live
![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/up/UPDIQUORMGVK1785170967947.png)

> Descend into the Venomous Abyss and confront Ula’tek within the inner sanctum of her lair. Awakened by Zul’jan’s actions, this ancient creature of hatred, corruption, and venom waits at the heart of a new eight-boss raid dungeon, where champions will battle through her venomous brood before facing Ula’tek herself.

---

Within the depths of the Venomous Abyss, Ula’tek stirs once more. Awakened by Zul’jan’s actions, this ancient force of hatred, corruption, and venom threatens to unleash her brood from the heart of an eight-boss raid dungeon. Gather your allies, carve a path through the vaults, and prepare to face Ula’tek in her inner sanctum.

---

### Raid Details:

- **Where:** The Coiled Isle
- **Raid Bosses:** 8
- **Level:** 90
- **Difficulties:** Raid Finder, Normal, Heroic, Mythic, Story Mode
- **Raid Finder Minimum Item Level:** 273

**Raid Unlock Schedule:**

All unlocks are with each region’s weekly maintenance reset, unless otherwise noted.

- **Week of 19 August:** Normal, Heroic, Mythic Difficulty, Raid Finder Wing 1- The Soulcoilers (Nek’zali the Soulcoiler; The Twin Fangs)
- **Week of 26 August:** Story Mode, Raid Finder Wing 2- The Essence of Venom (Entombed Sentinels; Vashnik the Malignant)
- **Week of 2 September:** Raid Finder Wing 3- The Serpent Warren (The Lost Explorers; Sszorak)
- **Week of 9 September:** Raid Finder Wing 4- The Tomb of Ula’tek- (The Coiled Altar; Ula’tek)

### The Venomous Abyss

![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/SSA6NR4LD1VX1785170429186.png)

*Guided by the dark whispers of Malacrass, Zul’jan has freed the forgotten broods of Ula’tek from their prison. As the monster’s power grows, Zul’jarra calls upon the Champions of Azeroth to descend into the Vaults of Atal’Utek to stop her brother before the weapon is unleashed upon Azeroth.*

## Bosses

### The Soulcoil Well

![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/8I2TB0943KH41785170425628.png)

**Nek’zali the Soulcoiler**

*Long ago, the shadow hunter Nek’zali and her followers were entombed for the blasphemous act of summoning Ula’tek. Restored by the very power she unleashed, she calls the other Soulcoilers from their tombs to serve her once more.*

### The Vile Crypt

![alternate-image-name](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/OGY3E4QOPMGM1785170425675.png)

**Entombed Sentinels**

*The Blood and Breath of Ula’tek bar the way to Vashnik’s inner sanctum. Ancient golems that long stood guard within the vaults, now corrupted by the venom of the nightmarish monster they imprisoned.*

![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/1JPXPRUFBTVD1785170425727.png)

**Vashnik the Malignant**

*Within the Chamber of Virulence, Vashnik the Malignant distills Ula’tek’s venoms into ever more lethal forms. The creature lurks in its noxious chamber, brewing foul toxins to empower its brood.*

### Crypt of the Soulcoilers

![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/07LOV1FQZA8U1785170427744.png)

**The Lost Explorers**

*A band of tortollan, possessed by spirits, have come to their senses near the sarcophagus of an ancient Soulcoiler. Trapped in the vault, they seek ancient artifacts to protect themselves from further possession by vengeful spirits.*

![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/jm/JM6UI92TKUGN1785170646735.png)

**Sszorak**

*A horrifying creature, warped by Ula’tek’s toxic venom, Sszorak is a brutal instrument of vengeance. Calling upon the winds that howl deep within Atal’Utek, it tears apart any living creature it encounters.*

### Pit of Fangs

![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/LGZY63UNHBIR1785170427943.png)

**The Twin Fangs**

*The first of Ula’tek’s blood, Vexhul and Ithraz brood beneath a roiling sea of venom deep within the vault of Atal’Utek. Any foolish enough to enter the prison’s inner chamber risk their monstrous jaws and toxic strikes.*

### The Coiled Altar

![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/KAG9F71K9QHH1785170428925.png)

**The Coiled Altar**

*The spirit of Malacrass will not release his grip on Zul’jan so easily. At the Coiled Altar, the shaman commands the warrior to enact the final ritual to unleash Ula’tek on the Amani—for revenge, and to gain their promised reward.*

![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/ec/ECNJHYP6XUCH1785170798414.png)

**Ula’tek**

*The great shame of the Amani empire. The vast complex of Atal’Utek was created to imprison the weapon that proved to be as much of a threat as the enemy she was intended to destroy. Ula’tek has risen from her ancient slumber and threatens all of Azeroth if she should escape.*

Learn more about each boss, their complete list of loot, and their abilities in the Adventure Journal (**Shift-J**).
