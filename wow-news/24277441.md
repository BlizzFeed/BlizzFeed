## Prepare to Face Rotmire in the Sporefall Raid

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_header/y7/Y71J3BRVK6ZC1779141312527.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_header/y7/Y71J3BRVK6ZC1779141312527.png)

Deep in the fungal Valleys of Harandar, the fungarian sporecallers have been growing a new threat for The Grudge Pit. Shaded by towering mushrooms, Sporefall is the perfect site to empower their new creation.

In the next content update, we’re introducing a new single-boss raid located in Harandar. You’ll face the fungal giant Rotmire in Raid Finder, Normal, Heroic, and Mythic difficulties. Players can also undertake Mythic difficulty in flexible groups of 15-25 players.

Take on Rotmire to unlock a range of enticing rewards, including the **Luminous Rotshroom** housing decor item, **Luminous Sporeglider** mount, **Sporefused** gear, and more.

Call up your allies and prepare for the new challenge in Sporefall arriving with the launch of *Midnight: Revelations*. Full details can be found in our [previously published overview](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/news/24272110/).

## The MDI Groups Stage Continues this Weekend

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/jd/JD5PBLAKARV11777587619285.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/jd/JD5PBLAKARV11777587619285.png)

The Groups Stage is the month-long period during which the top 24 teams compete in weekly matches.

Each weekend, eight teams compete in a head-to-head speedrunning format in the hopes of securing one of the top two placements and qualifying further in the competition.

This weekend, from 22–24 May, Group C matches will be broadcast live on the official [Warcraft Twitch](https://www.twitch.tv/warcraft) and [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/warcraft) channels starting at 18:00 BST each day.

Learn more from our previously published [MDI overview article](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/news/24276958).
