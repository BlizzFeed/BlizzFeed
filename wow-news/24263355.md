Are you a new player, returning after a break, or seeking to level up your play style? We invite you to join WoW Portal Room, a Discord server where you can meet just the right people. Community is the heart of WoW, and Discord servers have become the modern taverns where players gather, strategize, and make friends.

### WoW Portal Room: Your Gateway to Connection

The WoW Portal Room is the official World of Warcraft Discord server—a hub where Ambassadors and players unite and where every adventurer feels included. Whether you’re seeking tips for mythic dungeons or want to chat, the Portal Room is where you will take the first steps to finding your people. Just like using a portal in World of Warcraft, the portal room is meant to be a way stop on your journey and not the final destination.

A team of WoW Ambassadors representing 21 partnered community Discord servers is ready to meet you and help you with finding a space that fits your needs. Participating Discords enjoy special recognition, access to exclusive events, and direct support from Blizzard—making every server a more vibrant place to connect.

### What is the WoW Ambassadors Program?

The WoW Ambassador Program is designed to help the community help itself by breaking down barriers, creating long-lasting bonds, and recognizing the enormous efforts and endless expertise of our players. Our vision is simple: foster engagement and connection among players, ensuring everyone feels at home, whether they are a veteran raider or just starting out in Azeroth.

### Who are WoW Ambassadors?

WoW Ambassadors are experienced and dedicated WoW players who represent their Discord communities in the WoW Portal Room. Their mission is to ensure no one is left behind and to build new bonds in a safe and inclusive environment. They welcome new players, organize activities, and amplify the sense of belonging that sets WoW apart.

### Choose Your Path

The launch of the WoW Ambassador Program marks a new opportunity for players to connect in meaningful ways and is built for everyone. Whether you’re leading raids, crafting stories, or just seeking new friends, this is your chance to become part of something bigger. Step into the Portal Room, connect with Ambassadors, and help us create a world where every player is recognized, included, and celebrated.
