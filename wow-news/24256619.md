Through the span of their long lives, former Magister Umbric and Grand Magister Rommath forged an unlikely friendship. As their kingdom faced a series of trials unlike any other, Rommath hardened into the person he’d always been, while Umbric became something new. With word of a new threat rising against Silvermoon, Umbric must return from exile to offer his old friend an alliance.

Join Grand Magister Rommath for a special fireside telling of our newest short story—*The Void Between*—and find out if these old friends can forge that alliance ahead of *World of Warcraft: Midnight.*
