The War Within™ is almost upon us and with it, a new season  will begin with new dungeons, new rewards, and new challenges.

> - **22/8**: *The War Within* Early Access begins, Normal Dungeons open

> - **25/9:** Raid Finder Wing 3 Opens

### Dragonflight™ Season 4 End Dates

The current season of PvP content will end at 10:00 pm PDT the night before maintenance in each region—22 July at 22:00 PDT for NA, 23 July at 22:00 CEST for EU, and 24 July at 22:00 in Korea and Taiwan.

With the season’s end, it will no longer be possible to earn PvP seasonal rewards, titles, or Dragonflight Season 4 rankings, and titles will be awarded in the weeks after the season ends:

- [Draconic Gladiator](https://www.wowhead.com/achievement=19454/draconic-gladiator-dragonflight-season-4?brl): End Dragonflight PvP Season 4 in the top 0.1% of the 3v3 arena ladder (requires 150 games won in Dragonflight Season 4).
- [Draconic Legend:](https://www.wowhead.com/achievement=19453/draconic-legend-dragonflight-season-4?brl) End Dragonflight PvP Season 4 in the top 0.1% of the Solo Shuffle ladder (requires 50 games won in Dragonflight Season 4).

Players can still queue for rated PvP modes for Honor, Conquest, and progress toward the faction-themed **Vicious Dreamtalon** mount for Horde or Alliance.

Mythic+ 0.1% title rating qualification ends will also end on 23 July at the same time as the PvP season ends.

All PvE rewards currently available within Dragonflight Season 4 will no longer be available as of 26 August.

### *The War Within*

With the start of Early Access on 22 August, Normal difficulty dungeons will become available.

##### Dungeons

- Ara-Kara, City of Echoes (Levels 70-80)
- Priory of the Sacred Flame (Levels 70-80)
- The Rookery (Levels 70-80)
- The Stonevault (Levels 70-80)
- Cinderbrew Meadery (Level 80)
- City of Threads (Level 80)
- Darkflame Cleft (level 80)
- The Dawnbreaker (Level 80)

With the launch of the expansion on 26 August, Heroic difficulty dungeons will be available to play.

On 11 September, *The War Within* Season 1 will begin with Heroic and Raid Finder Wing 1 of Nerub-ar Palace opening, Mythic 0 dungeons also become available along with Heroic Seasonal dungeons and the new World bosses.

##### World Bosses

- Kordac, the Dormant Protector
- Aggregation of Horrors
- Shurrai, Atrocity of the Undersea
- Orta, the Broken Mountain

On 18 September, Mythic raids, Raid Finder Wing 2, and Mythic+ dungeons will open and raid Story Mode opens.

On 25 September Raid Finder Wing 3 will open.
