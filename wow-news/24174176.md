## Season of Discovery Phase 7 Now Live!

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/4b/4BIMS8ARP05F1736901868597.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/4b/4BIMS8ARP05F1736901868597.png)

Are you afraid of the dark? In Season of Discovery Phase 7, you’ll need to face your fears and go boldly into an all-new dungeon— Karazhan Crypts. Begin your journey toward attunement for Karazhan Crypts by visiting the Lights Hope Chapel in Eastern Plaguelands to pick up the starting quest.

Face the Scourge as they invade Azeroth once more, visit the Rune Brokers to purchase runes and rune-related discovery items, and on 6 February, journey to the famed ziggurat, Naxxramas, to defeat the horrors within.

- **29 January:**
  - Karazhan Crypts Dungeon Opens
  - Scourge Invasions Begins
- **6 February at 21:00 pm GMT worldwide:**
  - Naxxramas Opens

Get all the Season of Discovery Phase 7 details from our [previously published article](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/news/24171906).

## Undermine(d): No License Needed to D.R.I.V.E.

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_header/md/MD2TIJTS16RP1738117660596.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_header/md/MD2TIJTS16RP1738117660596.png)

Earn a new customizable vehicle and engage in D.R.I.V.E. as you drift at high speed through the streets of Undermine.

A cool acronym needs a cool meaning, and the goblins haven’t skimped on the meaning of D.R.I.V.E.’s. Standing for Dynamic and Revolutionary Improvements to Vehicular Experiences, you’ll want to make sure you have your sweet ride to roll around town in.

Players can earn the customizable G-99 Breakneck vehicle to use exclusively in their adventures within Undermine. You’ll push the boundaries of locomotion on the roads speeding through its causeways and byways at a higher rate than a ground mount can.

Learn more about D.R.I.V.E. from [our overview](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/news/24174933/).

## Celebrate Two Years of the Trading Post Under the Lunar Light

Join us for the second anniversary celebration of the Trading Post in February and shop new and previously released items at special vendors in Dornogal. This month, bring in the Lunar New Year in style with the Ensemble: Ornate Pink Lunar Festival Attire.

We’re celebrating the second anniversary of the Trading Post with an extension to the Reward Bar this month, allowing you to earn an additional 500 Trader's Tender through a variety of activities and expand your spending power on a wide variety of selections—including previously released transmogs, mounts, pets, toys, and monthly rewards.

Includes: Head, Shoulder, Chest/Robe, Waist, Feet

## The Great Push 2025 is Happening Now!

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_header/ty/TY6EGN48LYRI1738117097514.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_header/ty/TY6EGN48LYRI1738117097514.png)

The premiere Mythic+ Dungeon pushing competition features the top sixteen teams globally pushing Keystones against the clock live on [Twitch](https://www.twitch.tv/warcraft) and [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/@Warcraft). Each weekend, the top two teams will advance to the TGP Global Finals, where $200,000 (USD) will be on the line.

TGP 2025 features new format updates compared to previous years, primarily focused on the Group Stages commencing now. These changes are designed to keep the action exciting throughout all broadcast stages of the tournament, and we’re excited to see it play out through the Season.

- **Venue:**  Online: [Twitch.tv/Warcraft](http://www.twitch.tv/Warcraft) and [YouTube.com/Warcraft](http://www.youtube.com/Warcraft)
- **Co-Streaming:** *Each team will feature 1 or more perspectives of their gameplay during TGP. Additionally, Watch Parties are welcome during the event! Fill out the* [*MDI Watch Party Form*](http://blizz.ly/CostreamMDI) *to participate in the broadcast as a co-streamer,*
- **Dates:**
  - Group A: 31 January – 2 February
  - Group B: 7-9 February
  - Global Finals: 14-16 February
- **Broadcast Start Time:** 10:00am (PT) / 18:00pm (GMT)
- **Format:** Mythic Keystone Pushing against the clock. Details on format changes are below!

Additional details can be found in our [previously published article](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/news/24175284).

## Honor Your Elders with Celebration During Lunar Festival

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_header/oa/OA3GBOAUMYFK1737599141622.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_header/oa/OA3GBOAUMYFK1737599141622.png)

Every year, the druids of Moonglade celebrate their city’s great triumph over an ancient evil. During the Lunar Festival, citizens of Azeroth have the opportunity to honor their elders by celebrating their sage wisdom, sharing in magnificent feasts…and, of course, lighting fireworks.

The celebration has begun in Moonglade, and your Lunar Festival Invitation is waiting. Join in the fun by dressing up in your Lunar Festival finery, launching fireworks, and paying homage to the Elders throughout Azeroth.

**When:** 28 January–11 February\
**Where:** Moonglade, with camps in Ironforge, Stormwind, Darnassus, Thunder Bluff, Orgrimmar, Dragon Isles zones, Khaz Algar zones, and the Undercity\
**Currency:** Coin of Ancestry

Khaz Algar is joining in Lunar New Year festivities as eight new Elders (two per zone) make their way to the island, bringing with them a new Achievement, "Elders of Khaz Algar," and a new reward for trading in Coins of Ancestry, the [Lunar Launcher](https://www.wowhead.com/item=232901/lunar-launcher).

Learn more about how you can celebrate [in our overview](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/news/24174173/).

## The War Within Season 2 Mythic+ Updates Ahead

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_header/fj/FJXF04Y596JA1738005885609.jpg)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_header/fj/FJXF04Y596JA1738005885609.jpg)

We’re rolling out some changes to Mythic+ with the release of *The War Within* Season 2 and wanted to share a few more insights on what you can expect to see when those changes arrive on the Undermine(d) Public Test Realms (PTR). Looking ahead to Season 2, our aim is to make the Mythic dungeon system more rewarding from the outset and to smooth out the difficulty progression so that each step feels approachable.

More detailed information about these changes, including a breakdown of the new rewards and affixes at each level, can be found in [our previsouly pulbished article](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/news/24174877/).

These changes are rolling out on the Public Test Realm over the course of the next week or two. We look forward to your feedback!
