#### Here are this week's highlights:

##### Dragonflight

- Hearthstone’s 10th Anniversary Event
  - [Updates](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/hearthstones-10th-anniversary-event-updates/1807566/) have been made to this event, including an extension to 22 March, and bosses now spawn more often.
- 10.2.6 Content Update
  - This [content update will arrive](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/the-1026-content-update-arrives-march-19/1807582) with each region’s scheduled weekly maintenance the week of 19 March.
- Hot Fixes
  - [Hotfixes were deployed](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/wow-hotfixes-march-12/1769365/) during weekly maintenance this week, and class tuning adjustments were made to many classes in both group content and PvP.
