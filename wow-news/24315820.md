# World of Warcraft: Forever Goes Live Globally 4 November!

- URL: https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/en-gb/news/24315820/world-of-warcraft-forever-goes-live-globally-4-november
![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/yz/YZRX9YZ5KHDV1789250918244.png)

> Begin your first adventure or return to a new and reimagined Azeroth in World of Warcraft: Forever, launching worldwide 4 November at 23:00 GMT. Adventure to level 60 across the original continents, discover new stories, and meet fellow adventurers in a permanent home alongside modern and Classic WoW where community comes first and the journey matters as much as the destination.

---

Whether you’re stepping into *World of Warcraft* for the first time or finding your way back to Azeroth, there’s a place for you in *World of Warcraft: Forever*. Explore a new and reimagined Azeroth at your own pace, discover new places or revisit old favorites, and meet fellow adventurers along the way. This permanent home alongside modern and Classic WoW puts community first and makes the journey as meaningful as the destination.

Set after the events of *[Warcraft III Reforged: Forsaken Kingdom](https://shop.battle.net/product/warcraft-3-reforged?pfm=warcraft-rts-family-page-campaigns&returnFamilyUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fus.shop.battle.net%2Fen-us%2Ffamily%2Fwarcraft-rts%3Fpfm%3Dfrom-checkout)* and before Molten Core, *WoW: Forever* takes you across the original continents on an adventure to level 60. Your journey continues through new stories, experiences, and updates inspired by years of player passion and feedback.

## Explore New and Reimagined Zones

![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/t2/T2J3BJNES7241793235431765.png)

*WoW: Forever* expands the Eastern Kingdoms and Kalimdor with new and reimagined zones and more than 1,000 new quests across the level 1–60 journey. Explore new destinations including Mount Hyjal, Shen’dralas, the Riverglades, and Zephras Isle.

Mount Hyjal invites you to explore the aftermath of Archimonde’s defeat, journey through Darkwhisper Gorge, and help shape the future of a recovering land. In Shen'dralas, a long-hidden mystery between Mulgore and Desolace becomes a new adventure tied to the Shen’dralar, Eldre’Thalas, Dire Maul, and the centaur tribes of the region.

In the Riverglades, you’ll discover a rugged frontier of rivers, grasslands, trade routes, struggling communities, and old grudges, with more than 150 new quests across much of the mid-30s to mid-40s experience. On Zephras Isle, you’ll begin an elemental skybound starting experience from levels 1 through 12, blending Classic Warcraft aesthetics with architecture inspired by Skywall.

## Create the Hero You Want to Be in World of Warcraft: Forever

![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/vi/VI9AY6LNA97H1790051485653.png)

Race and class shape the hero you become in *World of Warcraft: Forever*, from the fantasy you embody to the role you take on in the world. You can return to a familiar favorite, explore a newly available combination, or begin your journey as a Skyborne character, with each choice offering a different way to step into Azeroth’s next chapter.

The Skyborne are a new playable race in *World of Warcraft: Forever*, bringing their own identity to the unfolding story. When choosing to play this new race, players can choose between Horde-aligned Windshapers which can become Shaman, or an Alliance-aligned High-Order Skyborne which can become Mages.

*World of Warcraft: Forever* opens new ways to build your character, including Orc Mages, Troll Warlocks, Undead Paladins, Human Hunters, Gnome Priests, Dwarf Shamans, and the debut of the Skyborne. Explore the possibilities and learn more in our [article](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/en-us/news/24304075).

## Meet the New Skyborne

![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/dd/DDKT72QJD7Z31790054782460.png)

The Skyborne—also known as the shen’dorei, or hidden people—are a new playable race in World of Warcraft: Forever. ## Select Your Experience Preset

![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/8u/8U6EJ8RI8SG21793236601260.png)

The first time you log in to World of Warcraft: Forever, you’ll be asked to Choose Your Experience Preset. You’ll make this choice before selecting your Ruleset.

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| **Classic**    A more traditional presentation with simpler visuals, standard character models, and fewere modern interface features enabled by default for a cleaner, more original-style experience with less automatic guidance.    **Starting Settings:**   - Standard Defiition character models - Quest points of interest and objective blobs off - One-bag off - Transmog off - Defaulted to camera turning keyboard binds - Classic screen UI layout | **Enhanced**    An updated presentation with higher visual fidelity and additional interface features enabled by default.            **Starting Settings:**   - High Definition character models - Quest points of interest and objective blobs on - One-bag on - Transmog on - Defaulted strafe movement to keyboard binds - Centralized screen UI layout |

If you are not sure which to choose, start with Enhanced. The preset gives you a convenient starting point rather than locking in your choices, and you can change its individual settings later under Game Menu > Options. After your initial log in to the game, you will no longer see this screen.

Each time you create a new character, you can select either **High Definition (HD)** or **Standard Definition (SD)** character models and animations. ## Choose Your Ruleset

Every journey through Azeroth begins with a choice about the kind of world you want to step into. In *World of Warcraft: Forever*, your journey starts with a Ruleset rather than a traditional realm-selection screen.

Instead of picking the “right” realm before creating a character, you choose the kind of experience you want in *World of Warcraft: Forever*. Each Ruleset functions as a broad player ecosystem rather than a single traditional realm, making it easier to find others who share your preferred playstyle while preserving the feeling of a living, active Azeroth.

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/xs/XSQAB61WGWTN1793252767387.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/xs/XSQAB61WGWTN1793252767387.png)

- **Normal:** This ruleset allows you to focus on adventuring and fighting monsters while allowing you to fight other players at your own discretion.
- **PvP:** This ruleset allows you to focus on player versus player combat; you are almost always at risk of being attacked by opposing players.
- **Roleplay:** This ruleset has strict naming conventions and behavior rules for players interested in immersing themselves as a character in a fantasy-based world.
- **Roleplay PvP:** This ruleset has strict naming conventions and behavior rules for players interested in immersing themselves as a character in a fantasy-based world focused on player versus player combat; you are almost always at risk of being attacked by opposing characters.

For players seeking the ultimate challenge, where every decision matters and death carries lasting consequences, the Hardcore ruleset will become available sometime after launch.

Learn more about rulesets in our [previously published article](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/news/24302070/).

## Create a Name of Your Own

![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/ji/JIBF1BNWRKSE1789963404849.png)

A name is one of the first choices you make when creating a character. It can be humorous, heroic, mysterious, deeply personal, or something that only makes sense to the friends who have adventured with you for years. In *World of Warcraft: Forever*, character names gain more room to breathe through a new two-name structure designed for a larger, realmless Azeroth.

Instead of relying on a single name tied to a specific realm, each character will have two names. Together, those parts form a full name that is unique within a region, creating more possibilities for players who want something that fits their character, history, or story in Azeroth. Learn more in our [previously published article](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/news/24304161).

## Set Up Camp with the Camping System

![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/b0/B0HPKSFCFCDC1792455657405.png)

The Camping system in World of Warcraft: Forever turns the Cooking profession’s Basic Campfire into a shared campsite where adventurers can rest, prepare, and connect. Nearby players can contribute up to three profession-crafted Camp Features, with each player adding one. Sit or craft at a campsite for at least 1 minute to gain benefits from its Camp Features, including buffs that are mutually exclusive with their corresponding class buffs. Take a moment to meet fellow adventurers before returning to the road. Learn more in our [previously published overview article](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/article/24301516/).

## Progress with the Legacy System

![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/n2/N2DYDUNG8N9B1790133979334.png)

*World of Warcraft: Forever* introduces an exciting new account-wide horizontal progression system—the Legacy System. It’s a whole new way to progress, focusing on exploring everything the game has to offer, rather than power progression alone.

This new system encourages players to level multiple characters of different classes and tradeskills and experience the journey all over again, however leveling alternate (alts) characters is not required to benefit from the system. All character advantages can be unlocked on a single character. The Legacy System has three major components: Legacy Challenges, Legacy Perks, and Legacy Rewards. Learn more in our [overview article.](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/news/24307383)

Off the coast of Dustwallow Marsh lies the Darkspear Islands, where a new battle is taking shape. In this 15 versus 15 battleground the Darkspear Raiders and Theramore Expeditionary Force clash for control of strategic footholds, a central flag, and the rewards waiting for those willing to fight for their faction. Whether you’re charging into battlegrounds with your faction or setting your sights on long-term PvP (Player vs. Player) goals, *World of Warcraft: Forever* brings a refreshed sense of purpose to every match-up.
