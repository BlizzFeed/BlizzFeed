There’s no better pilot than a S.A.F.E. pilot, and Gnomelia Gearheart is the best. Add a dash of charisma and a lotta moxie to your adventures when Gnomelia joins you throughout Azeroth and beyond!

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/uk/UK1KAJ81AVDU1698803492974.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/uk/UK1KAJ81AVDU1698803492974.png)

Those who complete the *Warcraft Rumble* tutorial and defeat Hogger will unlock the Gnomelia S.A.F.E. Pilot Battle Pet in World of Warcraft. You must have an account connected to Battle.net®

[Warcraft Rumble™](https://warcraftrumble.blizzard.com/) is a mobile action strategy game ready for you to engage in joyful chaos. Hop into the Warcraft® universe to build armies with your favorite heroes and villains from *Warcraft*  and battle it out in exciting scenarios designed to test your tactical wits.
