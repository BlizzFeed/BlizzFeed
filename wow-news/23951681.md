Each week we’ll look ahead to what’s in store for World of Warcraft, share the latest news, and keep you in the know byte by byte.  The Wrath Classic PvP Season is winding down and ends at the end of the month. Do you have what it takes to finish strong?

### Wrath of the Lich King Classic Season 6 Ends 30 May

The second Wrath Classic PvP season will end at 21:00 BST on 30 May.

During maintenance on  31 May, Arena Point costs for Season 6 gear will be reduced, and rating requirements will be removed for most items, though will still be required for elite weapons. During the offseason, all ranked Arena play will be unavailable. Once Arena Season 7 begins, Arena Points will be converted to Honor.

Good luck on a strong finish!
