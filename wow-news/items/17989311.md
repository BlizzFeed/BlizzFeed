# Gearing Up for Apexis Changes in Patch 6.1

- URL: https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/en-gb/news/17989311/gearing-up-for-apexis-changes-in-patch-6-1
- Date: 2015-02-19T18:00:00Z
![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/TCFWD3TELPEI1383166149202.jpg)

In Warlords of Draenor, players can use Apexis Crystals to buy new gear, collectable items, and even Followers. With enough Crystals, even those who are having no luck with the RNG goblins can get new and powerful gear to help them take their adventures even further.
