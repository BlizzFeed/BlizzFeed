# A Found Memento: Raising a King

- URL: https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/en-gb/news/20732238/a-found-memento-raising-a-king
- Date: 2017-05-17T17:45:00Z
![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/93/93I7DIO5QF9U1494609226488.jpg)

For those of the Alliance, the loss of King Varian Wrynn at the Broken Shore continues to echo throughout Azeroth. But for his son, Anduin Wrynn, that loss is felt most keenly as he faces not only the loss of a father, but takes on new responsibilities as a king. Watch the ending cinematic for the Alliance-only quest line A Found Memento to see Anduin rise to the new challenge before him.
