Patch 7.2: The Tomb of Sargeras brings with it a variety of new content and updates. Demon Assaults rise within the Broken Isles and a return to the Broken Shore is imperative for the Class Orders. This time, they assault the shore as a more prepared force wielding great weapons of power. But, will it be enough to carve a way to the Tomb of Sargeras?
