# Save the Date! BlizzCon is Coming 3-4 November

- URL: https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/en-gb/news/23951683/save-the-date-blizzcon-is-coming-3-4-november
- Date: 2023-05-17T17:00:00Z
![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/31/3114I3XBPS1S1684342486463.jpg)

After four years of gathering online–adventuring through Azeroth, teaming up in Overwatch, traversing the fields of Sanctuary, or warming yourself by the fire in the Tavern–it’s finally time to gather in person once more. We are happy to announce that BlizzCon will be returning to the Anaheim Convention Center on 3-4 November (PT)!
