# Support Ending for World of Warcraft 32-Bit Client This Summer

- URL: https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/en-gb/news/21788100/support-ending-for-world-of-warcraft-32-bit-client-this-summer
- Date: 2018-06-04T17:00:00Z
![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/di/DILTN5MM6PIG1527786985289.jpg)

When the World of Warcraft: Battle for Azeroth™ pre-patch is live, we will no longer support the World of Warcraft game client for 32-bit operating systems. The vast majority of World of Warcraft players are already running the 64-bit version of the game, and this change will allow us to focus on making the 64-bit version as stable and efficient as possible.
