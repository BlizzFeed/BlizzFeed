# [Updated 7/22] Join the Final Surge for Decor Duels

- Date: 2026-07-21T17:00:00Z

Decor Duels are winding down and will end with the release of Curse of Ula’tek. To help you get the most out of the final push, we’ve made a few additional changes to the event. Once the dust settles and Decor Duel is no longer available, players will still be able to purchase Decor items from the vendors in Silvermoon City using Voidlight Marl.
