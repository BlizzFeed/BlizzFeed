# Mythic Dungeon Invitational Global Finals Begin June 22!

It began with 32 teams going head-to-head over a series of four weekends—and now it’s come down to this. The top two teams from the Americas, Europe, Asia-Pacific, and China will take the stage once more in a battle to become the Mythic Dungeon Invitational Global Champions.
