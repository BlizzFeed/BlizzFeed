# Honor Your Legacy with New Forsaken and Night Elf Heritage Armor

- URL: https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/en-gb/news/23988137/honor-your-legacy-with-new-forsaken-and-night-elf-heritage-armor
- Date: 2023-08-16T17:00:00Z
![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/0o/0OPN1I56ZZ6J1692052247127.png)

Honor your legacy with two new Heritage Armor sets—one for undead and one for night elves—with the release of the Fury Incarnate content update. Players will also have new skin colors to choose from for undead, and night elves will have new facial markings and body tattoos to choose from.
