# The WoW Mobile Armory App Support Ending

- URL: https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/en-gb/news/21901726/the-wow-mobile-armory-app-support-ending
- Date: 2018-06-22T15:00:00Z
![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/nr/NRN8VQGHU31B1529538230091.jpg)

The World of Warcraft Mobile Armory app is retiring and will no longer be supported after July 11, 2018. But don’t worry—we have an updated app coming your way with Battle for Azeroth. Read on to learn more.
