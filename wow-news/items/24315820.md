# World of Warcraft: Forever Goes Live Globally 4 November!

- Date: 2026-10-29T21:00:00Z

Begin your first adventure or return to a new and reimagined Azeroth in World of Warcraft: Forever, launching worldwide 4 November at 23:00 GMT. Adventure to level 60 across the original continents, discover new stories, and meet fellow adventurers in a permanent home alongside modern and Classic WoW where community comes first and the journey matters as much as the destination.
