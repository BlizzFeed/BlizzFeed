# Patch 6.2 Zone Preview: Tanaan Jungle

- URL: https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/en-gb/news/18882366/patch-6-2-zone-preview-tanaan-jungle
- Date: 2015-05-01T01:00:00Z
![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/t5/T5Z44VBWR38V1430407576760.jpg)

Deep within Tanaan Jungle, the manipulations of the warlock Gul’dan have twisted what remains of the Iron Horde into a sinister new form—an army of fel orcs brimming with demonic strength and insatiable bloodlust. Now Gul’dun continues his machinations from inside the seemingly impenetrable Hellfire Citadel, plotting to open a portal for a massive invasion force.
