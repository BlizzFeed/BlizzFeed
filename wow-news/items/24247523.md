# Experience the Twilight Ascension Pre-Expansion Event

- URL: https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/en-gb/news/24247523/experience-the-twilight-ascension-pre-expansion-event
- Date: 2026-01-26T21:00:00Z
![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/y5/Y5EF7ELCF0VS1769216812451.png)

Travel to the Twilight Highlands to push back the threat of the Twilight's Blade cult and stop them from summoning more forces by disrupting their rituals and defeating rare enemies across the highlands. Available to characters level 10 and above, you’ll also take on World Quests and reap the rewards of a job well done during this repeatable event.
