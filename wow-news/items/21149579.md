# Arena World Championship at BlizzCon: Meet the Teams

- URL: https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/en-gb/news/21149579/arena-world-championship-at-blizzcon-meet-the-teams
- Date: 2017-10-25T21:00:00Z
![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/e7/E7IZZLCH60C61508939625446.jpg)

12 teams, from all over the world, will meet at BlizzCon and fight to claim the Arena World Champion title. Over three days of match play - Monday 30 October, and BlizzCon 3-4 November - with a team of expert hosts, you'll see the best of the best of PvP in World of Warcraft. Tune in!
