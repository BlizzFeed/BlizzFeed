# PvP: Rewarding Prestige

- URL: https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/en-gb/news/20732235/pvp-rewarding-prestige
- Date: 2017-05-15T17:00:00Z
![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/xh/XH8ZIBSW0JBD1494606564682.jpg)

In World of Warcraft: Legion, players can earn new rewards in PvP through the updated Honor system. At Honor level 50, a new path of advancement opens in which players begin earning Prestige levels. These Prestige levels increase with every 50 Honor levels and provide new rewards for each level gained. Read on to learn more.
