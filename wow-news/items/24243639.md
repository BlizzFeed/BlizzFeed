# World of Warcraft®: Midnight™ Goes Live 2 March!

- URL: https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/en-gb/news/24243639/world-of-warcraft-midnighttm-goes-live-2-march
- Date: 2025-11-26T18:00:00Z
![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/i8/I8R5R7VD16D81764095239566.png)

Prepare to face Xal’atath, the Harbinger and the Void in World of Warcraft®: Midnight™ launching worldwide on 2 March, 2026 at 23:00 GMT. Players who have purchased any edition of the Midnight expansion can secure Housing Early Access starting 3 December 2025.
