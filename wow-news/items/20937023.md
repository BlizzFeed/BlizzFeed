# Team Rock wins the 2017 APAC World of Warcraft Arena World Championship!

- URL: https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/en-gb/news/20937023/team-rock-wins-the-2017-apac-world-of-warcraft-arena-world-championship
- Date: 2017-07-31T08:12:00Z
![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/35/35UIX3VMEDMW1501488441031.jpg)

After a fierce two-day tournament, Australia’s Team Rock rose above all, bringing home the $15,000 USD prize and one of the 12 tickets to BlizzCon, the final stage of the Arena Championship.
