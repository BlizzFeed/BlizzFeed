# Warcraft Short Story: “Dark Mirror”

Nathanos Blightcaller has faithfully served the Banshee Queen, Sylvanas Windrunner, both in life and in undeath. This service has demanded great sacrifice—and difficult choices. Read “Dark Mirror” by Senior Game Designer Steve Danuser to learn more of Blightcaller's harrowing journey.
