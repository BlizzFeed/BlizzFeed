# The World of Warcraft 2016 Road to BlizzCon

- URL: https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/en-gb/news/20090403/the-world-of-warcraft-2016-road-to-blizzcon
- Date: 2016-04-08T17:00:00Z
![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/n8/N8ZTYN4T82QZ1460051230824.jpg)

Once again, the top World of Warcraft 3v3 Arena teams from around the globe will compete for a chance to face off in the WoW Arena World Championship. This year’s action-packed showdown will culminate at BlizzCon 2016 on November 4-5 in Anaheim, California.
