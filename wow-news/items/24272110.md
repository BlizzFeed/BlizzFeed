# Prepare to Face Rotmire in the Sporefall Raid

- URL: https://news.blizzard.com/en-gb/article/24272110/prepare-to-face-rotmire-in-the-sporefall-raid
- Date: 2026-05-20T17:00:00Z
- Image: https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/3w/3WC56CILED5Y1779141282319.png

In the Midnight: Revelations content update, we’re introducing a new single-boss raid located in Harandar. You’ll face the fungal giant Rotmire in Raid Finder, Normal, Heroic, and Mythic difficulties. Players can also undertake Mythic difficulty in flexible groups of 15-25 players.
