# Get Ready for the Molten Front and Season of Discovery Phase 5!

- URL: https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/en-gb/news/24135893/get-ready-for-the-molten-front-and-season-of-discovery-phase-5
- Date: 2024-09-10T17:00:00Z
![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/96/96TH88C0V5QO1725933781730.png)

There’s a bounty of adventure waiting for players in Cataclysm Classic and in Season of Discovery Phase 5. Players will be able to begin preparing to face Ragnaros in the Firelands when the Molten Front goes live early in Cataclysm Classic on 18 September. Those taking part in Season of Discovery can venture into danger within Blackwing Lair and Zul’Gurub on 26 September.
