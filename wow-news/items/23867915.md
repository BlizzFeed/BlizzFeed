# The Dragonflight Pre-Expansion Patch Phase 1 is Now Live

- Date: 2022-10-26T07:01:00Z

The first phase of the Dragonflight pre-expansion has arrived and with its arrival, players will be able to experience the new talent system, UI and HUD changes—including new accessibility features, Rated Solo Shuffle, and new class and race combinations.
