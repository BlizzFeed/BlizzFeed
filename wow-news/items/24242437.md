# Take a Ride on Turbulent Timeways—Now Live!

- Date: 2025-12-03T16:55:00Z
![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/ji/JI9Z1IXBWVYG1763589017685.png)

Once more, the Bronze Dragonflight has discovered growing disturbances in the timeways, with several rapidly intersecting our own! Timewalk through a previous expansion each week for 10 consecutive weeks, beginning and ending with the Shadowlands. This grand tour through World of Warcraft's past delivers six new Timewalking Dungeons for players to undertake—De Other Side, Spires of Ascension, Plaguefall, Necrotic Wake, Sanguine Depths, and Halls of Atonement.
