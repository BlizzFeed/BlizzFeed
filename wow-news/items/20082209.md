# World of Warcraft Takes to the Streets of Europe

- URL: https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/en-gb/news/20082209/world-of-warcraft-takes-to-the-streets-of-europe
- Date: 2016-04-04T21:12:00Z
![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/xf/XFV210M31R3N1459802091737.jpg)

On Tuesday, March 29, a group of urban artists from ARTtitude, in partnership with Blizzard Entertainment, set their sights on London’s Shoreditch neighborhood to bring a bit of World of Warcraft’s night elf magic to the walls along Great Eastern Street.
