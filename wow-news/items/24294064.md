# Keep Track of Potions, Trinkets and More with New User Interface Updates

- Date: 2026-08-03T17:00:00Z
- Image: https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/0n/0N58B5OUOBKA1785545378437.png

Curse of Ula’tek brings new user interface updates to help you keep track of cooldowns, consumables, and key combat information. Track potions and trinkets in the Cooldown Manager, ping key spell and health information to your party, and adjust Raid Frames to keep the healing effects you rely on front and center.
