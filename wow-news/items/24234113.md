In Midnight, players can acquire decor in a variety of ways. Whether it’s from purchasing an item from a vendor, earning an achievement, crafting with a profession, or getting a reward from a quest; there’s a multitude of options opening the way for you to decorate your home. In this article, we provide more insights into how you can add to your collection and express your unique style.
