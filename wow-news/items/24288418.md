# Quality-of-Life Improvements Coming in Curse of Ula'tek

- URL: https://news.blizzard.com/en-gb/article/24288418/quality-of-life-improvements-coming-in-curse-of-ula-tek
- Date: 2026-07-01T17:00:00Z
- Image: https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/qk/QKYKM4CWAE4W1772587409960.png

Since the 12.0.5 content update, we’ve been building on your feedback with targeted improvements aimed at enhancing the play experience. Recent hotfixes along with Midnight: Revelations changes have already addressed some issues with rewards, looting, quest experience, and durability loss. More updates are planned in the Curse of Ula’tek content update, including account-wide user interface settings, Auction House improvements, a one-time Profession Knowledge reset, and fixes for common combat and travel issues.
