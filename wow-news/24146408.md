## Don't Miss AWC Cup 2 This Weekend

Celebrate the top Arena Gladiators as they compete for their share of $400,000 (USD). The eight best teams from each region are competing live on [Twitch](https://www.twitch.tv/warcraft) and [YouTube](https://youtube.com/warcraft), and the teams with the best results will move to the Regional Finals, where the top team in Europe and the top team in North America will be determined.

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/gallery/la/LASAE6K9LFQL1726789559261.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/gallery/la/LASAE6K9LFQL1726789559261.png)

- **Venue:** Online – [Twitch.tv/Warcraft](https://www.twitch.tv/warcraft) | [YouTube.com/Warcraft](https://www.youtube.com/Warcraft)
  - Register to co-stream AWC with the [AWC Watch Party Form](https://blizz.ly/CostreamAWC).
- **Dates:**
  - AWC Cup 1: 27–29 September
  - AWC Cup 2: 4–6 October
  - AWC Cup 3: 11–13 October
  - AWC Cup 4: 18–20 October
  - AWC Grand Finals: 15–17 November
- **Broadcast Start Time:** 10:00 am PDT / 7:00 pm CEST
- **Prize Pool:** $400,000 (USD)
- **Format:** Best of 5, double-elimination / Best of 7 Grand Final
- **Talent:** AWC Season 1 will feature our returning star talent Eiya, Venruki, Ziqo, Supatease, Lythi, and Dmachine, alongside a special guest who will be revealed ahead of the AWC Grand Finals in November!

## Character Restoration Update

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/86/86QO3TX2ZYKJ1689095653189.jpg)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/86/86QO3TX2ZYKJ1689095653189.jpg)

Players are now able to restore characters that have been deleted.

Please note that at this time, there is a 60-second delay between each undelete. You will need to refresh the character screen, change realms, or log into a character between each character restored.

On 15 October, we will be re-instituting the 7-day cooldown period between character restorations.
