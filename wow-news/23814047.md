### WoW Classic News and Development

![BCC_Overlords_of_Outland_Ogrila_1200x675.png](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/03/03HK5Q3JIBOZ1655249893153.png)

#### Classic Development Update Posts on the Forums (English Only):

**New Fresh Start Realms with Wrath Classic**

With the launch of the Wrath of the Lich King Classic pre-patch, we expect many new Classic players to join us for the first time. We intend to open "Fresh Start" realms for players who wish to experience the game anew and have a chance to feel as though they're on an even playing field with everyone else. Additional details can be found in the forum post. [[Learn More](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/new-fresh-start-realms-with-wrath-classic/1262634)]

**Burning Crusade Classic Realm Consolidation**

Burning Crusade Classic™ realm consolidation and retirement coming to some low population realms on August 9. A list of affected realms is in the forum post. We encourage players to take advantage of Free Transfers before August 9. [[Learn More](https://eu.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/burning-crusade-classic-realm-consolidation-coming-august-10/362352)]

**THIS WEEK IN SHADOWLANDS**

- **Torghast: Unbridled Darkness**
  - Unbridled darkness has been unleashed upon Torghast, Tower of the Damned!
- **PvP: Packed House Warfare**
  - We've put more PvP in your PvP! In Packed House, there's nowhere to hide as you face not 2, not 3, not 5, but 15 adversaries in this 15 vs. 15 Arena battle.
- **Timewalking: Cataclysm**
  - Gather your staunchest allies and step into the Firelands raid dungeon to take on lord Ragnaros and his cohorts—and six dungeons from Deathwing's era— during Cataclysm Timewalking.

**COMING UP IN SHADOWLANDS**

##### Beginning Next Week:

- **Midsummer Fire Festival**
  - A time of merriment and celebration, dedicated to the hottest season of the year.
- **PvP Brawl: Deep Six Resource Race**
  - Deep Six pits teams of six players against each other in three iconic Battlegrounds: Warsong Gulch, Silvershard Mines, and Temple of Kotmogu.
- **Shadowlands Dungeon Event**
  - While this event is active, the final boss of each Shadowlands dungeon awards an extra item when defeated.

##### Beginning the Week of June 27:

- **Pet Battle Bonus Event**
  - While this event is active, your pets will earn experience at triple the rate. Get out there and battle!
- **PvP Brawl: Frozen Arathi Blizzard Resource Race**
  - Put away your bathing suit, pull out your parka, and enter a winter wonderland as the Arathi Basin you know and love gets a fresh coat of ice and snow.
