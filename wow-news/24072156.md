#### World of Warcraft Joins Hearthstone’s 10-Year Anniversary Festivities

Join in Hearthstone’s 10-year anniversary celebration beginning March 11. Players can log into Hearthstone to collect a new Fiery Hearthsteed mount in *World of Warcraft* for free!\*

![Flaming Hearthsteed](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_folder_media/d8/D85O5FEZMKD41707265549425.jpg)

Players can also seek out special “Hearthstone” matches in capital cities from 11 March to 18 March. Stick around long enough and you’ll face a special encounter where you can defeat iconic minions to add to your deck and earn other fun new Hearthstone-themed rewards! Learn more about collecting the Fiery Hearthsteed and the in-game events in [our latest article](https://worldofwarcraft.com/news/24072150).

Visit the Hearthstone [official site](https://playhearthstone.com/blog/24056181) to learn more about the full slate of festivities.

*\* No additional purchase necessary for the Fiery Hearthsteed mount. Limited-time offer only available to those who login to Hearthstone between 11 March and 14 May. Mount not available in World of Warcraft® Classic games.*
