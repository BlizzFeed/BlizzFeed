## Advance Your Scholarly Pursuits

You’ll look quite brilliant in the mystical High Scholar's Arcana transmog set as you while away the hours deep in the tomes and librams of many an arcane athenaeum.

In addition, take a bonus 500 Traders Tender with you when you visit the Trading Post.\*\*

![Blood elf female wearing the High Scholar's Arcana while standing on and surrounded by crystals. A Three Trader's Tender coins sit near her feet with the number 500](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/5n/5N4SYKA3HTVW1693432552640.png)

What you get:

- High Scholar’s Circlet (head)
- High Scholar’s Mantle (shoulder)
- High Scholar’s Gown (chest/robe)
- High Scholar’s Sash (waist)
- High Scholar’s Slippers (feet)
- High Scholar’s Gloves (hand)
- High Scholar’s Drape (cloak)
- 500 Trader’s Tender

Complete your ensemble with the High Scholar’s Grand Staff, available to purchase from the Trading Post for 500 Tender during the months of September and October. Visit our [Trading Post article](http://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/news/23989453/) to view this truly enchanting staff.
