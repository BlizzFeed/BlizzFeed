# The Classic Arena Tournament Viewers Guide

- URL: https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/en-gb/news/23877460/the-classic-arena-tournament-viewers-guide
![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/og/OGOQROAW4YTU1667947056229.png)

> The Wrath of the Lich King Classic Arena Tournament is here, live on YouTube and Twitch beginning Friday, November 11 at 10 am PT!

---

***The Wrath of the Lich King Classic™ Arena Tournament crowns a champion this weekend!  Get ready to watch as the top 8 teams in Europe and the Americas compete for the championship on*** [***YouTube***](https://www.youtube.com/user/WorldofWarcraft) ***and*** [***Twitch***](https://www.twitch.tv/warcraft) ***beginning Friday, November 11 at 10:00 am PT.***

The Classic Arena Tournament is back with the launch of Wrath of the Lich King! The best 3v3 Arena teams from Europe and North America have been engaged in battle and only the top 8 teams from each bracket remain. Watch each team fight for the top spot in their region to take home the championship title and their share of $100,000 (USD) in total prizing.

### Details

- Dates:
  - Friday, November 11: Europe Top 8
  - Saturday, November 12: Americas Top 8
  - Sunday, November 13: Semi-Finals & Finals
- Broadcast Start Time: 10:00 AM PT
- Prize: $50,000 prize pool per region
  - 1st: $17,000
  - 2nd: $12,000
  - 3rd: $8,000
  - 4th: $5,000
  - 5th/6th: $2,500
  - 7th/8th: $1,500
- Format: 3v3, Best of 7, Double-Elimination
- Language Streams:
  - English - YouTube.com/Warcraft and Twitch.tv/Warcraft
  - French - [Twitch.tv/kusa](http://twitch.tv/kusa)
  - German - [Twitch.tv/mowx](https://www.twitch.tv/mowx)
