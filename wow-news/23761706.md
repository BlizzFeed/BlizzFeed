The fearsome Wen Lo, the River’s Edge\* is now available to add to your muster of mounts! Earn your stripes with the purchase of this tiger mount from in-game or from the Blizzard Shop; or get it at no additional charge with the purchase of a 6-Month Subscription, along with the Goblin Gumbo Kettle toy for Burning Crusade Classic.

---

#### Go with the Wen Lo Flow!

With courage forged by the fiercely unpredictable waters of Pandaria, Wen Lo will carry you wherever the journey takes you! Hop into the saddle and let this armored water tiger help you embrace the thrill of the flight on your daring adventures across Azeroth.

[![Video](https://markdown-videos-api.jorgenkh.no/youtube/ZiQYBgz9egs?width=1280&height=720)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZiQYBgz9egs)

Wen Lo, the River’s Edge will be automatically added to your Collection once you purchase the [6-Month subscription](https://us.shop.battle.net/product/world-of-warcraft-subscription) from the Blizzard Shop. Welcome this rawrsome addition to your assortment of mounts!

Players on an existing 6-month recurring subscription with a future renewal date will receive Wen Lo, the River’s Edge mount, and Goblin Gumbo Kettle toy at no additional charge, no later than February 11, 2022 (must be redeemed by August 31, 2022).
