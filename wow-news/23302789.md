#### Level 50 Class Quests

Players level 50 and above can speak to their class trainers in any major city to begin a quest chain that will ultimately lead them into the belly of the Temple of Atal’Hakkar located in the Swamp of Sorrows.

*Thousands of years ago, the Gurubashi empire was plunged into civil war by a powerful sect of priests, the Atal’ai, who sought to summon to Azeroth an avatar of their god of blood, Hakkar the Soulflayer. The Gurubash people exiled the Atal’ai to the Swamp of Sorrows where the priests built the Temple of Atal’Hakkar. Ysera, Aspect of the green dragonflight, sank the temple beneath the swamp and assigned wardens to ensure that the summoning rituals never be performed again.*

Druids will be sent to meet up with [Towa Pathfinder](https://classic.wowhead.com/npc=9619/torwa-pathfinder&brl) in Un’Goro Crater who will give you a series of quests: [Blood Petal Poison](https://classic.wowhead.com/quest=9052/bloodpetal-poison&brl), [Toxic Test](https://classic.wowhead.com/quest=9051/toxic-test&brl), and [A Final Ingredient](https://classic.wowhead.com/quest=9053/a-better-ingredient&brl).

At the completion of this questline, you’ll be able to choose between three rare-quality rewards: [Grizzled Pelt](https://classic.wowhead.com/item=22274/grizzled-pelt&brl), [Forest’s Embrace](https://classic.wowhead.com/item=22272/forests-embrace&brl), and [Moonshadow Stave](https://classic.wowhead.com/item=22458/moonshadow-stave&brl).

Hunters will be sent to Azshara along the cliffs near the eastern shore to meet up with [Ogtinc](https://classic.wowhead.com/npc=8405/ogtinc&brl) for the quest [Hunter’s Charm](https://classic.wowhead.com/quest=8151/the-hunters-charm&brl). He’ll ask you to undertake a series of quests: [Courser’s Antlers](https://classic.wowhead.com/quest=8153/courser-antlers&brl), [Wavethrashing](https://classic.wowhead.com/quest=8231/wavethrashing&brl), and [The Green Drake](https://classic.wowhead.com/quest=8232/the-green-drake&brl).

At the completion of this questline, you’ll be able to choose between three rare-quality rewards: [Hunting Spear](https://classic.wowhead.com/item=20083/hunting-spear&brl), [Devilsaur Eye](https://classic.wowhead.com/item=19991/devilsaur-eye&brl), and [Devilsaur Tooth](https://classic.wowhead.com/item=19992/devilsaur-tooth&brl).

Mages will pick up the quest [Magecraft](https://classic.wowhead.com/quest=8250/magecraft&brl) which will send them to [Sanath Lim-yo](https://classic.wowhead.com/npc=8395/sanath-lim-yo&brl) in Azshara. He’ll provide a teleport to [Archmage Xylem](https://classic.wowhead.com/npc=8379/archmage-xylem&brl) who will give the next series of quests:  [Magic Dust](https://classic.wowhead.com/quest=8251/magic-dust&brl), [The Siren’s Coral](https://classic.wowhead.com/quest=8252/the-sirens-coral&brl), and [Destroy Morphaz](https://classic.wowhead.com/quest=8253/destroy-morphaz&brl).

At the completion of this questline, you’ll be able to choose between three rare-quality rewards: [Glacial Spike](https://classic.wowhead.com/item=20035/glacial-spike&brl), [Arcane Crystal Pendant](https://classic.wowhead.com/item=20037/arcane-crystal-pendant&brl), and [Fire Ruby](https://classic.wowhead.com/item=20036/fire-ruby&brl).

Paladins will begin their journey with the quest [Chillwind Camp](https://classic.wowhead.com/quest=8415/chillwind-camp&brl), which sends them to [Commander Ashlam Valorfist](https://classic.wowhead.com/npc=10838/commander-ashlam-valorfist&brl) in Western Plaguelands. From there they’ll embark on a series of quests that ultimately leads to the Temple of Atal’Hakkar: [Dispelling Evil](https://classic.wowhead.com/quest=8414/dispelling-evil&brl), [Inert Scourgestones](https://classic.wowhead.com/quest=8416/inert-scourgestones&brl), [Forging the Mightstone](https://classic.wowhead.com/quest=8418/forging-the-mightstone&brl)

At the completion of this questline, they’ll be able to choose between three rare-quality rewards: [Lightforged Blade](https://classic.wowhead.com/item=20504/lightforged-blade&brl), [Sanctified Orb](https://classic.wowhead.com/item=20512/sanctified-orb&brl), and [Chivalrous Signet](https://classic.wowhead.com/item=20505/chivalrous-signet&brl).

Priests will receive the quest [Cenarion Aid](https://classic.wowhead.com/quest=8254/cenarion-aid&brl) from their trainer which will lead them to Azshara to speak with [Ogtinc](https://classic.wowhead.com/npc=8405/ogtinc&brl) on the cliffs near the eastern shore. Ogtinc will give them the next series of quests: [Of Coursers We Know](https://classic.wowhead.com/quest=8255/of-coursers-we-know&brl), [The Ichor of Undeath](https://classic.wowhead.com/quest=8256/the-ichor-of-undeath&brl), and the [Blood of Morphaz](https://classic.wowhead.com/quest=8257/blood-of-morphaz&brl).

At the completion of this questline, they’ll be able to choose between three rare-quality rewards: [Blessed Prayer Beads](https://classic.wowhead.com/item=19990/blessed-prayer-beads&brl), [Woestave](https://classic.wowhead.com/item=20082/woestave&brl), [Circle of Hope](https://classic.wowhead.com/item=20006/circle-of-hope&brl).

Rogues will be given the quest [A Simple Request](https://classic.wowhead.com/quest=8233/a-simple-request&brl), which sends them to Alterac Valley to meet up with Lord Jorach Ravenholdt. He’ll give the next quest, [Sealed Azure Bag](https://classic.wowhead.com/quest=8234/sealed-azure-bag&brl), which has them traveling to Azshara to visit [Archmage Xylem](https://classic.wowhead.com/npc=8379/archmage-xylem&brl). [Sanath Lim-yo](https://classic.wowhead.com/npc=8395/sanath-lim-yo&brl) will be able to help them teleport to him. Archmage Xylem will give them the quest [Encoded Fragments](https://classic.wowhead.com/quest=8235/encoded-fragments&brl), followed by [The Azure Key](https://classic.wowhead.com/quest=8236/the-azure-key&brl)

At the completion of this questline, they’ll be able to choose between three rare-quality rewards: [Ebon Mask](https://classic.wowhead.com/item=19984/ebon-mask&brl), [Whisperwalk Boots](https://classic.wowhead.com/item=20255/whisperwalk-boots&brl), and [Duskbat Drape](https://classic.wowhead.com/item=19982/duskbat-drape&brl).

Shaman will be given the quest [Elemental Mastery](https://classic.wowhead.com/quest=8410/elemental-mastery&brl), which sends them to collect samples of air, fire, earth, and water to take to [Bath’rah the Windwatcher](https://classic.wowhead.com/npc=6176/bathrah-the-windwatcher&brl) in Chillwind Point. They can get there by following the river north from Tarren Mill in Hillsbrad Foothills. These samples can be gathered from the elementals in Alterac Valley.  Once they’ve turned in the quest, he’ll give them the quest [Spirit Totem](https://classic.wowhead.com/quest=8412/deprecated-spirit-totem&brl), which will send them into the Western Plaguelands. Once the quest is turned in, he’ll give another quest, [Da Voodoo](https://classic.wowhead.com/quest=8413/da-voodoo&brl), which will send them into the depths of the Temple of Atal’Hakkar.

At the completion of this questline, they’ll be able to choose between three rare-quality rewards: [Azurite Fists](https://classic.wowhead.com/item=20369/azurite-fists&brl), [Wildstaff](https://classic.wowhead.com/item=20556/wildstaff&brl), and [Enamored Water Spirit](https://classic.wowhead.com/item=20503/enamored-water-spirit).

To begin this quest line, warlocks will pick up the quest [An Imp’s Request](https://classic.wowhead.com/quest=8419/an-imps-request&brl) from their trainer, which will have them travel to Felwood to meet up with Impsy. They can also take up the quest [Hot and Itchy](https://classic.wowhead.com/quest=8420/hot-and-itchy&brl) directly from Impsy who will give them the next series of quests: [The Wrong Stuff](https://classic.wowhead.com/quest=8421/the-wrong-stuff&brl) and [Trolls of a Feather](https://classic.wowhead.com/quest=8422/trolls-of-a-feather&brl), which leads into the Temple of Atal’Hakkar.

At the completion of this questline, they’ll be able to choose between three rare-quality rewards: [Soul Harvester](https://classic.wowhead.com/item=20536/soul-harvester&brl), [Abyss Shard](https://classic.wowhead.com/item=20534/abyss-shard&brl), and [Robes of Servitude](https://classic.wowhead.com/item=20530/robes-of-servitude&brl).

Warriors will pick up the quest A Troubled Spirit which sends them to the Swamp of Sorrows to speak with the [Fallen Hero of the Horde](https://classic.wowhead.com/quest=8417/a-troubled-spirit&brl). They’ll find him along the path into the Blasted Lands. The Fallen Hero of the Horde will give them the next series of quests: [Warrior Kinship](https://classic.wowhead.com/quest=8423/warrior-kinship&brl), [War on the Shadowsworn](https://classic.wowhead.com/quest=8424/war-on-the-shadowsworn&brl), and [Voodoo Feathers](https://classic.wowhead.com/quest=8425/voodoo-feathers&brl).

At the completion of this quest line, they’ll be able to choose between three rare-quality rewards: [Fury Visor](https://classic.wowhead.com/item=20521/fury-visor&brl), [Diamond Flask](https://classic.wowhead.com/item=20130/diamond-flask&brl), and [Razorsteel Shoulders](https://classic.wowhead.com/item=20517/razorsteel-shoulders&brl).
