#### Details

**Venue:**Online: [Twitch.tv/Warcraft](https://twitch.tv/warcraft) and [YouTube.com/Warcraft](https://youtube.com/warcraft)

- *Sign up to be a live Watch Party during TGP using the [TGP Co-Streaming Form](https://blizz.ly/CostreamMDI)!*

**Language Streams:**

- [French](https://www.twitch.tv/kusa)
- [German](https://www.twitch.tv/kimuh)
- [Spanish](https://www.twitch.tv/phobyac)

**Dates:**

- Group A: July 21-23
- Group B: July 28-30
- Group C: August 4-6
- Global Finals: August 18-20

**Broadcast Start Time:**10:00 AM PT / 7:00 PM CET

**Prize Pool:**$30,000 USD per Group, $300,000 USD Global Finals

**Format:**Teams push the highest possible Mythic Keystones against the clock with daily eliminations.

**Brackets:**Follow the live dungeons each weekend on [Raider.IO](https://raider.io/)!
