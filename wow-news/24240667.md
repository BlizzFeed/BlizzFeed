Slay in sanguine style with this Lana'thel-inspired transmog set and swirling Crimson Cascade mount in the Crimson Court Pack\* available through 12 January 2026.

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/fi/FIQL9I9MO4BR1759852917492.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/fi/FIQL9I9MO4BR1759852917492.png)

Lana'thel's Crimson Cascade is shared across all present and future WoW® Modern characters and automatically scales to the fastest riding skill known by each character. If they aren't at a high enough level to use a flying mount, Lana'thel's Crimson Cascade is also a ground mount.

The Crimson Court Pack includes the following:

- Lana'thel's Crimson Cascade (Mount)
- Ensemble: Lana'thel's Crimson Couture (Transmog Set)
  - Lana'thel's Crimson Crown
  - Lana'thel's Crimson Mantle
  - Lana'thel's Crimson Vestments
  - Lana'thel's Crimson Belt
  - Lana'thel's Crimson Legguards
  - Lana'thel's Crimson Sarong
  - Lana'thel's Crimson Treads
  - Lana'thel's Crimson Gloves

[Buy Now](https://shop.battle.net/product/world-of-warcraft-crimson-court-pack)

The Crimson Court Pack is a limited-time bundled offer available through 12 January 2026. After that date, the cosmetics included in the pack (Lana'thel's Crimson Couture and Lana'thel's Crimson Cascade) will be offered as individual, standalone items.
