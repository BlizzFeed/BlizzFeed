# World of Warcraft: Forever- The Future of Skyborne Visual Updates

![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/sr/SRGU8HIDWYQU1791312885139.png)

> Get insights from the World of Warcraft development team on refining the Skyborne visual style, revisiting High-Definition Druid forms, and expanding player choice with Standard-Definition character models.

---

The World of Warcraft development team began our journey into *World of Warcraft: Forever* with ambitions that grew and evolved over time. Our aim has always been to uphold the original WoW era aesthetic, while giving players more choice in how they experience Azeroth.

Over the course of development, we have been aligning on the right range of fidelity and what feels like it belongs in the *WoW: Forever* world and what does not. The team has learned much in the process, and that will continue to strengthen more with future content.

Along the way, there were some ideas the team wanted to explore that ultimately didn’t quite meet the consistency and cohesion we are aiming for. To align with our goals better, we are taking aim at updating some of those visuals for the future. Here is one example of how we are revisiting the High Definition (HD) Skyborne Druid forms.

![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/3CO27CTTA8N61791312527480.png)

We removed a bit of the softness, added more detailed fur textures, and adjusted the color saturation. The Skyborne are from an exotic environment, and their Druid forms reflect that, but we don't want that to be dissonant with the visual language of other creatures in the world.

![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/3JGEMZ4SRX0Z1791312527556.png)

---

## Skyborne Standard Definition (SD) Player Character Update

In choosing to offer an SD or HD toggle for player characters, we focused on the pre-existing races from original *World of Warcraft* since many people have an attachment to those models. However, we have heard feedback that the HD Skyborne can be jarring by contrast when placed in the same world as the SD models and world, and we agree.

So, for those of us that prefer the look of SD models and want more choice, Skyborne player characters (and yes SD Druid forms) are in development and arriving early 2027. We look forward to sharing more information closer to that date. For now, here is an early work in progress image.

![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/zc/ZCUXX92LNDGI1791312851795.png)
