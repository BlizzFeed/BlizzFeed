# Dragonflight Zone Preview: The Azure Span

- URL: https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/en-gb/news/23823429/dragonflight-zone-preview-the-azure-span
![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/40/40S6G6NBI8EU1657669349914.png)

> Traverse and explore the snowy peaks, misty forests, and icy tundra of the Azure Span.

---

Journey into the Azure Span, an ancient frost-touched land, where you'll aid Kalecgos as he ventures into the Azure Archives on a mission to reclaim the Azure Oathstone.

---

#### Cold Comfort

When you first arrive in the Azure Span, you'll be greeted with snowy peaks, mist-covered forests, and icy tundra. The Azure Span stretches from coastal tuskarr villages through sprawling forest tundra to looming spires of ice and snow. Regal blue dragons once roamed these lands, but with the passage of time, several tribes of tuskarr, gnolls, and furbolg now call these ancient lands their home.

![A picture of a small village covered in snow at the edge of a river with a frozen waterfall in the background](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/22WXZP5O13DO1657584488771.png)The Azure Span is one of the largest zones created in *World of Warcraft* to date and offers the greatest visual elevation change players will see in the Dragon Isles.

![A picture of a road in a forest of trees with a crumbling tower](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/5XIIE09TMNWK1657584488829.png)

Much of the inspiration for this zone comes from one of our favorites in *Wrath of the Lich King*, Grizzly Hills, along with the California Bay area's coastal fogs and redwoods. Though, there's more than just redwoods to be found throughout Azure Span, as you'll learn when you trek through this diverse wilderness.

![A picture of a forest covered in mist withj a river and waterfall](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/DEWY8DR9L6CJ1657584488773.png)

Much of the zone is filled with varied biomes covered in snow and ice, giant frozen waterfalls, ice rivers, and forests shrouded in mist that beckon eager heroes hungry for adventure.

---

#### Azure Span's Denizens

As you progress on your adventures, you'll encounter many inhabitants of this snow-touched biome who have made the icy peaks, forests, and shores their homes. Here are some that you'll meet on your journey.

##### Tuskarr

![A picture of a tuskarr villagers standing in a village on an icy shore](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/TTXEWJNEJAX21657584489345.png)

Like their Northrend relatives, the tuskarr of the Dragon Isles are fishers and traders and have inhabited the land since the days when blue dragons roamed the Azure Span. Their tribes can be found throughout as they aid Kalecgos.

##### Blue Dragons

![A picture of a large blue dragon with snow and trees](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/XTSOGOWRQB3J1657584489339.png)

The Dragon Isles have been rejuvenated, but many blue dragons are still scattered across Azeroth. Kalecgos will need all the help he can get to reunite them—and an unexpected source appears to offer that aid.

##### Gnolls

![A picture of several gnolls around a campfire in a forest](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/IOQ0JK8HBS8M1657761523866.jpg)

A savage clan of shamanistic gnolls that have harnessed the power of decay, darkening their wetland home with vile pockets of thorns and roots. They are a force to be reckoned with.

##### Furbolg

![A picture of a furbolg in a forest](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/754KVFQT2I7Y1657761523892.jpg)

The forest-dwelling furbolg are dealing with conflicts that have splintered their clans. Many have been lured with the promise of being able to bend the elements to their will.
