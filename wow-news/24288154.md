> From isles to spires, the Darkspear Dash begins! Join the Darkspear trolls and their Zandalari allies as they celebrate individuality and identity. Race up Kalimdor and across the Eastern Kingdoms, from Echo Isles to Silvermoon City, for an in-game event inspired by the annual Running of the Trolls community-led event while you run, leap, and spread rainbow cheer along the route.

---

Run, leap, and spread rainbow cheer as a new surge of activity and celebration sweeps across Azeroth from the tropical shores of the Echo Isles to soaring spires of Silvermoon City with the Darkspear Dash.

> **Starts**: 27 June, 00:01 BST\
> **Ends**: 28 June, 23:59 BST

This vibrant new micro-holiday invites champions to join the Darkspear trolls and their Zandalari allies in a race across Azeroth, their path marked by celebration, spectacle, and a touch of mischievous magic.

Take a moment to honor tradition, community, and camaraderie in this race across Azeroth inspired by the [Running of the Trolls](https://runningofthetrolls.carrd.co/) community event—a charity-driven fun run held in support of the Trevor Project.

---

## Find the Festivities and Follow the Rainbow

Accepting the quest "Doing the Dash" from a Holiday Enthusiast in any capital city will lead you to festivities and straight into the splash, sparkle, and booming beach party on the tropical shores of the Echo Isles.

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/vc/VCLGDMRG3NV91780448265598.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/vc/VCLGDMRG3NV91780448265598.png)

From the Echo Isles, it’s full speed ahead through Durotar, Western Plaguelands, Eastern Plaguelands, Eversong Woods, and Silvermoon City as rainbow-revelers race across continents in a burst of color and chaos, ending in an extravagant pool party within the city’s grand fountain.

Passing each gate has the possibility of granting the “Ready to Roll” buff to fleet-footed runners, allowing them to leap into the air and help increase the movement speed of nearby allies.

Along the way, players can and should drink from the Horn of Mojo at water stations to become Hydrated (and faster on their feet).

---

## A Burst of Brilliant Rewards

Completing the “Darkspear Dash” quest rewards the new **Troll Scroll of Rainbow Roll** toy, allowing you to leap through the air in colorful style. You’ll be a racing rainbow as you roll like a troll with this toy!

You’ll also get a 24-Hour buff that grants a 30% increase to outdoor movement speed and a colorful rainbow that trails behind you, and the “Darkspear Dasher” temporary title to let everyone know that you ran with the rainbow during the Darkspear Dash.

Colorful new items are available during the dashing festivities, and Naz'do and Taz'do also sell temporary celebration items for 10 gold each. Bonus: Many rainbow-themed toys will have their cooldowns greatly reduced during the Darkspear Dash.

- **Darkspear Dash Tabard**: Available for 30 gold from Naz’do in the Echo Isles or Taz’do in Silvermoon City.
- **Pocket Rainbow**: Imbue the target with rainbow power!
- **Pocket Sand**: Be a revelry buzzkill and disorient your target for 3 seconds.
- **Rainbow Runners**: Put some color in your step!

You can also complete some Trading Post activities throughout the dash:

- **Darkspear Dash: Participate in the Darkspear Dash**
  - Complete the “Darkspear Dash” quest.
  - **Rewards**: 100 Travel Log Points
- **Darkspear Dash: Complete the Dash as a low-level Troll in Style**
  - Join the footrace at Echo Isles in Durotar as a level 1-15 Darkspear or Zandalari troll while wearing the Darkspear Dash Tabard.
  - **Rewards**: 100 Travel Log Points
