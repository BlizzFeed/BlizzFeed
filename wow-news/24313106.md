## Choose Your Ruleset in World of Warcraft: Forever

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_header/1p/1PD2ZLVROZOB1789961389530.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_header/1p/1PD2ZLVROZOB1789961389530.png)

Every journey through Azeroth begins with a choice about the kind of world you want to step into. We’re changing how that journey begins by moving away from the traditional realm-selection screen and introducing rulesets, letting you choose the kind of experience you want to have first.

Each ruleset functions more like a broad player ecosystem than a single traditional realm, making it easier to find others who share your preferred playstyle while preserving the feeling of a living, active Azeroth.

[Click to Learn More](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/news/24302070)

## Gather Around the Campfire in World of Warcraft: Forever

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_header/mi/MIORB2TEVZIK1792455672807.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_header/mi/MIORB2TEVZIK1792455672807.png)

There is something special about seeing another adventurer on the road. Maybe they are headed toward the same quest objective, recovering after a close fight, or simply taking the long way through a dangerous zone. The Camping system gives those moments more room to grow by turning a simple Cooking Fire into a place where players can rest, prepare, and connect.
