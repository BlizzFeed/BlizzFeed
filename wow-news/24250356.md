## About the Soundtrack

![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/87/872K48268PHD1772585950356.png)

**World of Warcraft: Midnight (Original Soundtrack)** is the official musical companion to Blizzard Entertainment’s *World of Warcraft: Midnight* expansion.

The opening track, “Midnight,” serves as the expansion’s main title suite—an epic, 19‑minute composition that introduces the expansion’s core musical themes. Drawing from *World of Warcraft’s* long-standing orchestral legacy, the track blends heroic melodies, choral textures, and darker tonal elements that reflect the narrative tension at the heart of *Midnight*.

The soundtrack features music from Blizzard Entertainment’s in‑house composition team alongside long‑time franchise contributors, continuing a lineage of iconic *World of Warcraft* scores that have defined the franchise for over two decades. *Midnight* was composed specifically to support in‑game storytelling, cinematics, and world exploration, while also standing as a complete listening experience, tracing the narrative arc of *Midnight's* gameplay for fans both inside and outside the game.
