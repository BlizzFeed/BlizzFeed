## Battle World Bosses in Lairs

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/X0MQBJPBDS5J1781742460649.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/X0MQBJPBDS5J1781742460649.png)

> **Week of 12 August:** World Difficulty\
> **Week of 19 August:** Normal, Heroic, and Mythic (Flexible-Scaling) Difficulties

Step into new Lairs, an evolution of world bosses that places them into a dedicated instanced encounter with scaling difficulty up to flexible Mythic with 15-25 players, making it easier than ever to gather your allies and earn epic loot, without the wait.

Season 2 delivers brave champions a challenge within the Tidebound Grotto, with a new World Boss, **Nymrissa Wavecaller**.

Similar to Delves, Lairs are found in specific locations with a summoning stone available outside the instance.

Rally your allies, step into the lair, and face legendary foes on your own terms. Learn more by heading over to our [previously published article](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/news/24295085).
