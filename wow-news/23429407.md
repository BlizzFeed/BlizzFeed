# New 4k Digital Backgrounds Available

- URL: https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/en-gb/news/23429407/new-4k-digital-backgrounds-available
![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/cu/CUEOLTWOXMSR1589910830436.jpg)

> Whether you’re doing a little at-home streaming or are working from home, we have just the thing you’re looking for—new 4k digital backgrounds.

---

We’ve recently seen some requests from the community for some high-quality digital backgrounds for use in home streaming or work-from-home setups. To help you in your quest to set the right mood for your streaming audience, or to just add a bit more Azeroth to your work-from-home setup, we’ve put together some 4k digital backgrounds that we hope will hit the mark.

These digital backgrounds include scenes from Visions of N’Zoth and iconic settings from World of Warcraft, as well as a peek into upcoming Shadowland zones.
