Burning Crusade Classic provides players the opportunity to go beyond the Dark Portal to face the fel forces of the Burning Legion, but no one needs to go it alone. We’ve gathered some of the best resources from around the community to help.

#### World of Warcraft Official Site News and Forums

Stay on top of all the official World of Warcraft Classic news by visiting the [official site](https://worldofwarcraft.com/).

**World of Warcraft Official forums:** Make your way over to the official WoW Classic forums to meet up with players like you.

- [The Burning Crusade Classic Discussion](https://eu.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/c/wow-classic/the-burning-crusade-classic-discussion/288): Discuss your experiences with others, get help, and meet new friends.
- [WoW Classic Technical Support](https://eu.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/c/support/technical-support/13): You can report bugs within WoW Classic, or discuss on the Technical Support forum.

#### Fansites and Communities

- [TBC Wowhead](https://tbc.wowhead.com/): Wowhead has a suite of tools including [talent calculator](https://tbc.wowhead.com/talent-calc?brl), [gear planner](https://tbc.wowhead.com/gear-planner?brl), and a variety of strategy guides available for players looking to maximize their experience in Burning Crusade Classic.
  - [Burning Crusade Classic Reputation Guides](https://tbc.wowhead.com/guides/reputation?brl)
  - [Burning Crusade Classic Leveling Guides](https://tbc.wowhead.com/guides/leveling?brl)
  - [Burning Crusade Classic Class Guides](https://tbc.wowhead.com/guides/classes?brl)
- [**Icy Veins**](https://www.icy-veins.com/tbc-classic/): Covering the latest WoW Classic news, Icy Veins has a variety of strategy guides and information on classes, professions, dungeons, and reputations.
- [**WoW Classic Reddit**](https://www.reddit.com/r/classicwow/): Join other WoW Classic community members to discuss your experiences in the game, swap stories, or get help and support from others.
- [**Petopia Burning Crusade Classic**](https://www.wow-petopia.com/classic_bc/): Learn what Hunter pets are available, their strengths, their diet, and more.
- [**Kargoz**](https://www.wowisclassic.com/en/): Helpful video guides and discussion on all things Burning Crusade Classic. Don’t miss out on Good Morning Azeroth.
- [**Warcraft Tavern**](https://www.warcrafttavern.com/): Here you can find and submit Burning Crusade Classic Guides, a [Burning Crusade ClassicTalent Calculator](https://www.warcrafttavern.com/tbc/tools/talent-calculator/), [leveling guides](https://www.warcrafttavern.com/tbc/guides/type/leveling/), and more!
- [**Silent Shadows**](https://www.silentshadows.net/?utm_source=worldofwarcraft.com&utm_medium=news23657324)  is the ultimate go-to resource for the BC Classic rogue players. Be it talents, gear theorycraft, arena strategies, or PvE under the microscope, the guide section has it covered.
- [**WoWClassic.cz**](https://wowclassic.cz/): Každodenní dávka zajímavých informací z WoW Classic v češtině, včetně nejrůznějších návodů pro profese, povolání, dungeony či raidy.
- [**Ścieżki Azeroth**](https://sciezkiazerotth.blogspot.com/): Blog poświęcony World of Warcraft , w którym znajdziecie informacje na temat lore w szczególności dotyczące dodatku The Burning Crusade Classic - opisy krain, lokacji, frakcji oraz ciekawostek.
- [**WestenOfficial**](https://www.youtube.com/user/aranyi34) Burning Crusade guides in Hungarian.
