> The wildest pack filled with in-game items that invoke the Spirit of Rexxar awaits your modern World of Warcraft (currently Midnight™) characters. For a limited time, get a transmog set, a toy to make Hunter combat pets look like Rexxar’s loyal combat pets, and a housing decor item worthy of a Beastmaster!

---

From now through 8 November 2026\*, invoke the spirit of legendary hunter and champion of the Horde, Rexxar, with a beastly pack of items that challenge you to embrace the untamed wilds in modern *World of Warcraft®* (currently *Midnight™*).

With the **Champion of the Wilds Pack**\*\*, you can take on the appearance of legendary beastmaster, Rexxar, decorate your home with a trophy of the hunt, and give your Hunter combat pets the appearance of Rexxar’s trusty companions Misha and Huffer.

The **Champion of the Wilds Pack** includes the following in-game items:

- Ensemble: Rexxar's Fabled Attire Transmog Set
  - Rexxar’s Fabled Cowl (Head)
  - Rexxar’s Fabled Spiked Shoulder (Shoulder)
  - Rexxar’s Fabled Padded Shoulder (Shoulder)
  - Rexxar’s Fabled Chestguard (Chest)
  - Rexxar’s Fabled Belt (Waist)
  - Rexxar’s Fabled Leggings (Legs)
  - Rexxar’s Fabled Boots (Feet)
  - Rexxar’s Fabled Gloves (Hands)
  - Rexxar’s Fabled Cloak (Back)
- Rexxar's Companion Transformation Toy (Hunter Only Toy)
  - This toy has a 5-minute cooldown and randomly gives any type of Hunter combat pet the appearance of either Misha (bear) or Huffer (boar) for 60 minutes. For modern WoW Beast Mastery Hunters with two pets out simultaneously, both combat pets will be transformed—one Misha and one Huffer.
- Zhevraskin Rug Housing Decor Item
  - If one Zhevraskin rug isn’t enough (and it rarely is), [additional rugs](https://shop.battle.net/family/world-of-warcraft/items/2132971) can be purchased from the in-game shop.
