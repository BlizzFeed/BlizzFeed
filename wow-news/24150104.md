Hallow’s End celebrates the break of the Forsaken from the Scourge. Fun and mischief reign as the Innkeepers of Azeroth dole out treats and tricks for those seeking to partake in the festivities.

> **When:** 25 October – 8 November\
> **Where:** Trick-or-Treat throughout the Inns of Azeroth, Northrend, Pandaria, Outland, Draenor, the Broken Isles (Dalaran), the Dragon Isles, and Khaz Algar for tasty treats and currency to buy Hallow’s End goodies.

---

### Brave the Headless Horseman

Face the Headless Horseman’s haunting challenge daily to collect pieces for the **Patched Harvest Golem** transmog set. A random piece is guaranteed to drop each day—no duplicates. Throughout Hallow’s End, characters level 10-80 can queue up for this ghoulish encounter in the Dungeon Finder.

> *“Prepare yourselves, the bells have tolled! Shelter your weak, your young, and your old! Each of you shall pay the final sum. Cry for mercy, the reckoning has come!” – The Headless Horseman*
