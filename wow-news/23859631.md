### Discover the Dragon Isles

![Dragon Isles Concept Art](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/4u/4UINC9ZS62CK1657746310179.jpg)

Discover the Dragon Isles and explore four new zones—[the Waking Shores](https://worldofwarcraft.com/news/23822253/d), roiling with elemental power; the vast expanse of the [Ohn’ahran Plains](https://worldofwarcraft.com/news/23837377/); the frosty peaks of the [Azure Span](https://worldofwarcraft.com/news/23823429/); and the majestic spires and temples of [Thaldraszus](https://worldofwarcraft.com/news/23837935/d)—each themed around and revealing more about the ancient dragon aspects.

---

### Meet the Dracthyr Evoker

![Dracthyr Evoker Hovering Over a Snowy Landscape](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/ee/EE8336PJGVSH1658370586642.jpg)

Players will menace their foes (or empower their allies) as the new [dracthyr Evoker](https://worldofwarcraft.com/news/23833250/), *World of Warcraft’s* first-ever race and class combination. Able to switch between a humanoid visage and a fearsome draconic form, the dracthyr are highly mobile, and their unique Evoker class can specialize in ranged damage-dealing or in aiding their allies as a healer by harnessing the mystical gifts of dragonkind.  They’ll awake from their slumber in the [Forbidden Reach](https://worldofwarcraft.com/news/23832633/), where they’ll take up their training to join the forces of dragonkind in the defense of the isles.

---

### Ascend to New Heights

![Player Riding on the Back of a Drake](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/ZRH610Q7YAUH1658953523750.jpg)

*Dragonflight* introduces [Dragonriding](https://worldofwarcraft.com/en-us/news/23818251/), an all-new skill-based method of aerial movement that allows players to take to the skies on the back of their personal Dragon Isles Drake. Players will customize their drake as they progress through the expansion, collecting more appearance options and enabling them to fly farther and faster.

---

### New Talent System

![Talent Tree Interface](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/5e/5EXAQLJQ8M8L1653669962444.jpg)

*Dragonflight* contains major updates to World of Warcraft classes, centering on the re-introduction of talent trees. The new talent system empowers players to make creative and meaningful talent choices without compromising their effectiveness—and most importantly, it gives them meaningful options at every level.

### New Dungeons

![The Azure Vault Dungeon](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/jv/JVUFA2OVK4FW1660180901734.jpg)

On 28 November , *Dragonflight* will release with [eight dungeons](https://worldofwarcraft.com/news/23816413/)—four level-up dungeons, which can be taken on with friends as you work toward the **new level cap of 70**— and four maximum-level dungeons.
