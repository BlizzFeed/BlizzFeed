> Rise of Azshara is nearly here! Learn more about the latest content update and prepare for the Queen of Tides’ grand reemergence June 26.

---

Something sinister churns beneath the waves—the Queen of Tides’ return is imminent! Rise of Azshara, the next major Battle for Azeroth content update goes live June 26 and is packed with many new features, including:

- Addition of [Essences to the Heart of Azeroth system](https://worldofwarcraft.com/en-gb/news/22956187/)
- Two new zones to explore, Nazjatar and Mechagon
- [Heritage Armor for Tauren and Gnomes](https://worldofwarcraft.com/en-gb/news/22994612/a)
- [New Benthic gear](https://worldofwarcraft.com/en-gb/news/22994614/)
- New raid that takes players into the depths of [Azshara’s Eternal Palace](https://worldofwarcraft.com/en-gb/news/23021200), and much more.
