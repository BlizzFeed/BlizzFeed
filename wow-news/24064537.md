#### Here are this week's highlights:

#### Dragonflight

- **Cross-Realm Mythic Difficulty**
  - With the weekly reset in each region this week, [cross-realm groups were enabled](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/cross-realm-mythic-amirdrassil-begins-feb-6/1781305/) for Mythic difficulty Amirdrassil, the Dream’s Hope.
- **This Week’s Hotfixes**
  - Updates were made throughout the week, including some impactful [adjustments to Mythic Fyrakk](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/wow-hotfixes-february-8/1769365).

#### WoW Classic

- **WoW Classic Era Notes**
  - Important [adjustments to WoW Classic Era](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/wow-classic-era-maintenance-notes-february-6-2024/1781972/) during this week’s maintenance were explained.

#### The War Within

- **Latest Hero Talents Revealed**
  - With the latest Hero Talents reveal, we asked players for feedback on the four classes outlined:
    - [Keeper of the Grove Druid](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/feedback-keeper-of-the-grove-in-the-war-within/1782828)
    - [Dark Ranger Hunter](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/feedback-dark-ranger-in-the-war-within/1782829)
    - [Frostfire Mage](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/feedback-frostfire-in-the-war-within/1782830)
    - [Oracle Priest](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/feedback-oracle-in-the-war-within/1782831)
