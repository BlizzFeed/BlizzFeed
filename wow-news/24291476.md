## Storm Two Legendary Raids

Journey into the heart of Outland's most dangerous conflicts as two legendary raids open their gates once more. Along the way, earn faction reputation, claim powerful rewards, and overcome unforgettable boss encounters.

### Confront the Betrayer in The Black Temple

Storm the shadowed halls of the Black Temple and confront Illidan Stormrage in his seat of power.

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/fp/FPWCVVKTD3T11784221575221.jpg)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/fp/FPWCVVKTD3T11784221575221.jpg)

- **Level**: 70
- **Location**: Shadowmoon Valley, Outland
- **Reputation**: Ashtongue Deathsworn
- **Boss Encounters**: High Warlord Naj'entus, Supremus, Shade of Akama, Teron Gorefiend, Gurtogg Bloodboil, Reliquary of Souls, Mother Shahraz, The Illidari Council, and Illidan Stormrage

Before you can enter the Black Temple, you'll need to complete the attunement process—learn how in [this guide](https://www.wowhead.com/tbc/guide/attunement-dungeons-raids-burning-crusade-classic?brl).

Improve your standing with the Ashtongue Deathsworn and earn additional rewards from Okuno within the Black Temple.

### Stand Against Archimonde in The Battle for Mount Hyjal

Journey through the Caverns of Time to preserve the outcome of the pivotal Battle for Mount Hyjal and ensure Archimonde meets his fated end.

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/g9/G9YDMOWFCBGO1784221646386.jpg)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/g9/G9YDMOWFCBGO1784221646386.jpg)

- **Level**: 70
- **Location**: Caverns of Time, Tanaris
- **Reputation**: Scale of the Sands
- **Boss Encounters**: Rage Winterchill, Anetheron, Kaz'rogal, Azgalor, and Archimonde

To enter the Battle for Mount Hyjal, complete the “[The Vials of Eternity](https://www.wowhead.com/tbc/quest=10445/the-vials-of-eternity?brl)” questline.

Improve your standing with the Scale of the Sands and earn rewards from Indormi within the Battle for Mount Hyjal raid instance. You do not need to be in a raid group to enter.

## Earn the Trust of the Netherwing

Begin earning Netherwing reputation through daily quests on Netherwing Ledge, the floating crystalline outpost high above southeastern Shadowmoon Valley. Flying mounts are a must to reach it—or a willingness to take a very strategic fall.

Upon arrival, complete daily quests to earn reputation with the Netherwing. Collect Netherwing Eggs along the way to accelerate your reputation gains.

Once you reach Exalted with the Netherwing, you can obtain epic Netherwing Drake mounts ([Azure](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Reins_of_the_Azure_Netherwing_Drake?brl), [Cobalt](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Reins_of_the_Cobalt_Netherwing_Drake?brl), [Onyx](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Reins_of_the_Onyx_Netherwing_Drake?brl), [Purple](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Reins_of_the_Purple_Netherwing_Drake?brl), [Veridian](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Reins_of_the_Veridian_Netherwing_Drake?brl), and [Violet](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Reins_of_the_Violet_Netherwing_Drake?brl)) from Drake Dealer Hurlunk in the Dragonmaw Base Camp, provided the required Artisan Riding skill is achieved, and the “Blood Oath of the Netherwing,” part of the Netherwing questline is completed.

Quest givers and merchants can also be found mingling in the camp, including Gug the Armorsmith, Horus the Innkeeper, and Rumpus for your poison and reagent needs.

## Enhance Your Gear with Epic Gems

Earn epic gems through mining in the Battle for Mount Hyjal with a Mining skill of 375, or discover them as rare drops within the Black Temple.

Looking to enhance your gear with epic gems? Purchase Jewelcrafting Designs from Indormi, Keeper of Ancient Gem Lore, inside the Battle for Mount Hyjal raid instance.

## Prove Your Worth in Arena Season 3

Arena Season 2 ends the week of 16 August at 22:00 local time, the night before each region's weekly reset. Prepare for a new season of competition as Arena Season 3 begins, bringing new rewards and the latest Arena gear available from vendors in Shattrath and Nagrand.

> **When**: 2 September (with regional weekly content resets)

At the end of Season 2, any remaining unspent Arena Points are converted to Honor Points (1 Arena Point = 10 Honor Points), and Arena Season 2 gear will be available from Area 52 vendors at a discount, with no Arena rating requirement. Arena Season 1 gear will also become available from Honor vendors in the Hall of Legends in Orgrimmar and the Champion’s Hall in Stormwind.
