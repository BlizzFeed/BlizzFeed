#### Here are this week's highlights:

- **Evoker Legendary Weapon Drop Chances:** Defeating Scalecommander Sarkareth on [lower difficulties](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/evoker-legendary-quest-mythic-sarkareth-drop/1590392/235) does not offer additional chances to receive this item. Players get the same chance to receive the item if they do only [Heroic or Normal then Heroic](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/evoker-legendary-quest-mythic-sarkareth-drop/1590392/330).
- **Scalecommander Sarkareth’s “Oblivion” Ability:** A recent change to Scalecommander Sarkareth’s “Oblivion” ability was more significant than intended. However, after a closer look, we’ve decided to [leave it as-is](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/yesterdays-change-to-sarkareths-oblivion/1616995).
- **Hall of Fame Is Closing for Aberrus, the Shadowed Crucible:** It was announced on Thursday that the Aberrus, the Shadowed Crucible Hall of Fame is [now full](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/aberrus-the-shadowed-crucible-hall-of-fame-is-now-full/1617492) and will be closed with next week’s resets.
- **Latest PTR Update for Fractures in Time:** On Thursday, the latest [PTR update](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/dragonflight-fractures-in-time-ptr-development-notes/1587226/10) was released for Dragonflight Fractures in Time. This included a variety of class tuning changes and the introduction of cross-realm trading.

#### Wrath of the Lich King Classic

- **Wrath Classic Realm Transfer Restrictions Lifted:** NA and EU [players may now transfer](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/realm-transfers-no-longer-restricted/1616294) to and from realms which were previously unavailable in the realm transfer system.
