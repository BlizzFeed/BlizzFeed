> Catch up on everything announced for World of Warcraft: Forever during BlizzCon, including a first look at the next chapter for Azeroth and the adventures waiting on the horizon.

---

Built as a new permanent home alongside modern and Classic WoW, World of Warcraft: *Forever* invites past, current, and new players into a familiar yet fresh Azeroth where the world takes center stage, community comes first, and the journey matters as much as the destination.

Set after the events of *Warcraft III Reforged: Forsaken Kingdom* and before Molten Core, players will return to the original continents, adventure to level 60, and continue progressing through new stories, experiences, and updates inspired by years of player passion and feedback. *World of Warcraft: Forever* launches November 4 at 3:00 p.m. PDT globally, with beta beginning Thursday, September 17.

## Pre-Purchase World of Warcraft: Forever Upgrades and Begin Your Next Journey in Azeroth

![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/7r/7RQ22ZLPRJFD1789240876327.webp)

Azeroth is calling once more. *World of Warcraft: Forever* invites you to begin again in a reimagined original Azeroth, where familiar roads lead to new discoveries, the journey matters as much as the destination, and the world is ready to grow for years to come.

When *World of Warcraft: Forever* launches globally on November 4, 2026, at 3:00 p.m. PST, access will be included with your *World of Warcraft* Subscription or Game Time. Optional upgrade editions are available to pre-purchase now on the Battle.net Shop, including the Skyborne Heroic Pack, Skyborne Epic Pack, and the limited-time Warcraft Forever Collection. If you’re eager to get a hands-on preview ahead of launch, you can choose the Skyborne Epic Pack or Warcraft Forever Collection for access to the beta beginning September 17.

[![Video](https://markdown-videos-api.jorgenkh.no/youtube/OQrvLbiLXcU?width=1280&height=720)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OQrvLbiLXcU)

### Choose Your Edition

Pre-purchase optional *World of Warcraft: Forever* upgrade editions now on the Battle.net Shop to prepare for launch on November 4. The **Skyborne Heroic Pack** includes access to the new Skyborne race and Zephras Isle starting experience, Early Name Reservation, the Cerulean Prideclaw ground mount (players will need to purchase the riding skill in-game to access and use this mount), cosmetics, housing decor, and one Invite-A-Friend Launch Code.

The **Skyborne Epic Pack** includes everything in the Skyborne Heroic Pack, plus beta access beginning September 17, 30 days of World of Warcraft Game Time starting November 4, additional cosmetics, pets, tabards, and three total Invite-A-Friend Launch Codes. For the broadest Warcraft experience, the limited-time **Warcraft Forever Collection** includes everything in the Skyborne Epic Pack, along with **Warcraft III: Reforged**, the **Warcraft III Reforged: Forsaken Kingdom** campaign, and Forsaken and Human Figurine decor for Housing in modern World of Warcraft.

Visit the Battle.net Shop to learn more and pre-purchase *World of Warcraft: Forever* upgrade editions on the [Battle.net Shop today](https://shop.battle.net/product/world-of-warcraft-forever)!
