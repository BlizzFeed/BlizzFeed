### Season of Discovery Phase 2 Goes Live 8 February

![A Human standing near ruins in Stranglethorn Vale with an orange sky](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/y2/Y2ZRAS4NQX3L1704749964556.jpg)

Season of Discovery Phase 2 arrives this week. Adventurers can level from 25 to 40, earn up to 31 additional talent points, take on the challenge of Gnomeregan as a new raid dungeon, discover new runes and abilities, and more!

Realms go live with this exciting new update 8 February at 21:00 GMT around the world. Check out our [previously published overview here](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/news/24056979/).
