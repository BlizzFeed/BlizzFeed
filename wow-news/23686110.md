# Get the New Sapphire Skyblazer Mount with the Purchase of a 6 Month Subscription!

- URL: https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/en-gb/news/23686110/get-the-new-sapphire-skyblazer-mount-with-the-purchase-of-a-6-month-subscription
![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/sd/SDB41NVR6CU71625006797876.jpg)

> Soar through the sky on the brilliant Sapphire Skyblazer mount when you add it to your collection. Available now for purchase in-game or from the Blizzard Shop. You can also get this brilliant blue avian mount and the Imp in a Ball toy with the purchase of a 6-month subscription.

---

Introducing the brand-new Sapphire Skyblazer\* mount—now available to add to your collection! This brilliant, blue-plumed armored companion can be purchased in-game or from the Blizzard Shop; you can also get it free when you purchase a 6-month subscription to World of Warcraft.

---

#### Like a Blue Streak

*It’s not quite certain why Sapphire Skyblazers flocked to Azeroth, but what’s known for sure is that they burn brighter (and hotter!) than any avian to ever soar across the skies. Fast as lightning and armored to the beak, this is a perfect mount to accompany you in your daring adventures.*

[![Video](https://markdown-videos-api.jorgenkh.no/youtube/jMKJepcQ0QY?width=1280&height=720)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jMKJepcQ0QY)

Once you’ve purchased the [**6-Month** subscription](https://eu.shop.battle.net/en-gb/product/world-of-warcraft-subscription) from the Blizzard Shop, the Sapphire Skyblazer mount will be automatically added to your in-game collection. Welcome this avian addition to your assortment of mounts!

Players on an existing 6-month recurring subscription with a future renewal date will receive the Sapphire Skyblazer mount and Imp-in-a-Ball at no additional charge, no later than July 13, 2021.

---

#### Imp in a Ball

![Imp in a Ball Toy](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/f1/F1MC0LQCATZ71621307297738.jpg)

Players who purchase a recurring 6-month subscription will not only get the Sapphire Skyblazer mount for World of Warcraft, but also receive the Imp in a Ball toy\*\* to use in Burning Crusade Classic. This fel-infused toy can tell your fortune as you make your way through the shattered lands of Outland!

Please visit our [support article](https://support.blizzard.com/article/000286922) to learn more about claiming this toy.
