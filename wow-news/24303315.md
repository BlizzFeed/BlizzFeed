## Get to Know World of Warcraft: Forever

![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/yz/YZRX9YZ5KHDV1789250918244.png)

Catch up on the latest from *World of Warcraft: Forever*, including choosing your ruleset, naming your character, class and race combinations, the Skyborne, and the Legacy System as you prepare to carve your path through Azeroth.

![...](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/sa/SAUYK7VOFLAB1789961357048.png)

##### Choose Your Ruleset

Every journey through Azeroth begins with a choice about the kind of world you want to step into. In World of Warcraft: Forever, rulesets help you choose the kind of experience you want first as you begin carving your path through Azeroth. All you need to know is in [our overview](http://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/news/24302070).

![...](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/ji/JIBF1BNWRKSE1789963404849.png)

##### Create a Name of Your Own

A name is one of the first choices you make when creating a character. In World of Warcraft: Forever, character names gain more room to breathe through a new two-name structure designed for a larger, realmless Azeroth. More details can be found [here](http://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/news/24304161).

![...](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/vi/VI9AY6LNA97H1790051485653.png)

##### Create the Hero You Want to Be

Race and class shape the hero you become, from the fantasy you embody to the role you take on in the world. You can return to a familiar favorite, explore a newly available combination, or begin your journey as a Skyborne character, with each choice offering a different way to step into Azeroth’s next chapter. Your hero awaits—learn more about it [in our article](http://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/news/24304075).

![...](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/dd/DDKT72QJD7Z31790054782460.png)

##### Meet the New Skyborne

A new adventure rises above Azeroth. The Skyborne invite you into a story of wind and ancestry—one that begins on Zephras Isle and continues as you decide whether your Skyborne will join the Horde or the Alliance. Learn more about the Skyborne [here](http://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/news/24302071).

![...](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/n2/N2DYDUNG8N9B1790133979334.png)

##### Get to Know the Legacy System

Take a peek at an account-wide horizontal progression system—the Legacy System. It’s a way to progress by exploring more of what the game has to offer, while every character you love contributes to your journey. Learn more about this feature [here](http://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/news/24307383).

## Raise Your Stein! Brewfest Returns with Battle-Brewed Rewards

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_header/r7/R7RY7W80YS0I1789674347343.jpg)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_header/r7/R7RY7W80YS0I1789674347343.jpg)

Eat delicious food, drink bountiful beverages, and ride rams around barrels of apples! Brewfest has arrived, and you can enjoy all that and more!

**When**: 20 September 20–6 October\
**Where**: Silvermoon City, Valdrakken, Dornogal, Valdrakken, Ironforge, and Orgrimmar\
**Currency:** Brewfest Prize Tokens—used to purchase most items at the Brewfest vendor.

Strike up a conversation with the Holiday Enthusiast in Silvermoon City to begin the *brewfest*ivities. In addition to the usual fun in Ironforge and Orgrimmar, you can complete new Brewfest quests throughout Quel’Thalas, and the Brewfest Banquet Outdoor Scenario will keep you hopping.

What's Brewfest without some stout rewards? Head over to our article to see what's freshly tapped for *Midnight*.

## Dress to Distress with Costumed Calamity in October’s Trading Post

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/og/OGA0360880WY1790273229235.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/og/OGA0360880WY1790273229235.png)

**This Month's Bonus Reward: Thornshadow Assassin's Attire Transmog Set**\
*“For missions that don't officially exist.”*

October’s Trading Post is cloaked in calamity, with fiendish finds ready to add chaos to your collection. Complete activities to earn Trader’s Tender, fill your Traveler’s Log, and slink into the shadows with the **Ensemble: Thornshadow Assassin's Attire**.

Visit T&W (Tawney and Wilder) just outside the Mage District in Stormwind, or the Zen’shiri Trading Post next to Grommash Hold in Orgrimmar, to check out the latest items they have available. Players can also visit the Trading Post in the capital cities of Dornogal (The Foregrounds) on the Isle of Dorn and in the renewed [Silvermoon City](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/news/24243213), where representatives of both Tawney and Wilder and the Zen’shiri Trading Post are open for business.

Champions new to or returning to *World of Warcraft* and *Midnight* can [tour the Trading Post](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/en-us/news/24131507) to learn everything you need to know, from where to find it to how you can earn additional Trader's Tender through the Traveler's Log.

The [September Trading Post Outlet](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/news/24295383) closes soon! Get in your last purchases before the vendors change over to their October inventory.
