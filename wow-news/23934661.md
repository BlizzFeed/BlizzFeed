#### Here are this week's highlights:

- **Live Class Tuning for Nine Classes:**With weekly maintenance in each region, [most classes received adjustments](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/wow-hotfixes-updated-april-12/1550427/136 "https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/wow-hotfixes-updated-april-12/1550427/136") to their performance in both general and PvP play.
- **Embers of Neltharion PTR:**On Wednesday, the latest [development notes](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/dragonflight-embers-of-neltharion-ptr-development-notes/1541379/9 "https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/dragonflight-embers-of-neltharion-ptr-development-notes/1541379/9") were published and the Embers of Nelthation PTR was updated and opened for testing. Updates this week included class tuning, additional PvP tuning, Professions updates, [scheduled raid testing](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/aberrus-the-shadowed-crucible-raid-testing-schedule/1545266/21 "https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/aberrus-the-shadowed-crucible-raid-testing-schedule/1545266/21") in Aberrus, the Shadowed Crucible, and scheduled testing in [Season 2 Mythic+ Dungeons](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/feedback-mythic-testing-april-13th-april-17th/1568304 "https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/feedback-mythic-testing-april-13th-april-17th/1568304").
- **Changes to Mythic+ Affixes Coming in Season 2:**In response to feedback from players, [Mythic+ Dungeon Affixes will be adjusted](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/ptr-mythic-status-update-week-of-april-13th/1567610 "https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/ptr-mythic-status-update-week-of-april-13th/1567610") in Dragonflight Season 2, with the goal of further lessening the impact of affixes on Mythic+ dungeon gameplay.

##### Wrath of the Lich King Classic

- **No Further Adjustments to Feral Druids:** On Monday, an additional change to Feral Druids was deployed and then rescinded, and then [explained in the WoW Community Council forum](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/wotlk-stealth-change-to-feral/1566375/5 "https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/wotlk-stealth-change-to-feral/1566375/5").
- **Bear Druid Threat Increased:**In Wrath Classic, Bears received [a small increase to their threat generation](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/wrath-of-the-lich-king-classic-hotfixes-updated-april-10/1500326/178 "https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/wrath-of-the-lich-king-classic-hotfixes-updated-april-10/1500326/178").
