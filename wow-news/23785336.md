### Shadowlands News and Development

#### Mythic and Raid Finder Wing 2

![WoW_9-2Sepulcher_Blog-Lightbox-Dausegne_1280x720_JM.jpg](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/gallery/XJBQDXNV9UYP1644355527950.jpg)

Sepulcher of the First Ones Mythic and Raid Finder Wing 2 is live. Prepare yourself for the fight against Dausegne, the Fallen Oracle, Prototype Pantheon, Lihuvim, Principal Architect by reading [our article](https://worldofwarcraft.com/news/23774003/).

#### Mage Tower Returns Permanently

![WoW_MageTower_embed.png](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_folder_media/NTNPO0E8CP7H1648137105828.png)

When introduced in Legion, the Mage Tower challenged players to conquer unique solo class-specific encounters. Starting the week of March 29, the Mage Tower will no longer be tied to the Legion Timewalking Bonus Event. Those seeking to prove their tenacity will be able to access it any time. Learn more about it [here](https://worldofwarcraft.com/news/23736141/).

#### Legendary Belt of Unity

Those completing Chapter 7 of the Zereth Mortis campaign should be getting the [Belt of Unity](https://youtu.be/V3J7bYAOP0o?t=113)—a new Legendary belt empowered with your covenant's Runecarving Effect. Complete the campaign and become Revered with the Enlightened faction to gain access to the Memory of Unity, allowing you to craft the new covenant swapping Runecarving Effect on a different armor slot or help your other characters get an Enlightened Legendary right away.

### Shadowlands Development Update Posts on the Forums

- **9.2.5 PTR Now Live**
  - Cross-faction Instances are now available for testing on the PTR, and players can directly invite members of the opposite faction to party. Check out the [forum post](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/925-ptr-development-notes/1205733) for details on these changes and more.
- **Great Vault Rewards Update on March 15**
  - Changes outlined [in this post](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/great-vault-rewards-update-march-15/1201225) were aimed at improving the feeling of opening your Great Vault even on weeks when, for whatever reason, you don't see a direct upgrade in the other items you can choose.
- **Sandworn Relics and Raid/Dungeon Tuning**
  - Dive into [this post](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/tuning-changes-%E2%80%93-raid-tazavesh-and-sandworn-relics/1201747) for detailed changes made to the Sandworn Relics and notable changes to our dungeons and raids.
- **Flying In Zereth Mortis**
  - Time to take to the skies! The campaign chapter for completing the Unlocking the Secrets achievement is available, and Zereth Mortis Flying can now be unlocked.
- **Castle Nathria Legendary Memories**
  - Legendary Memories previously obtainable only from Castle Nathria can now also be purchased from Rendle, who appears in both Castle Nathria and in Haven. You can read more details [here](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/legendary-memories-from-castle-nathria/1199617).
- **WoW Developer Response**
  - Desvin responds to feedback from the WoW Community Council in the Solo Shuffle Feedback thread and [explains the idea](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/solo-shuffle-feedback/1193325/12) behind the increase to ilvl 239 while in Solo Shuffle Brawl.
