### WoW Classic News and Development

#### The Wrath of the Lich King Beta Has Begun!

![WoW_C_WotLK_BlogThumb_1200x675.png](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/u2/U27SQGEBSZD71650384162498.png)

Throughout the test, we'll be inviting [opt-in beta testers](https://wowclassic.blizzard.com/en-us/#beta) and more to check out the upcoming Classic expansion and give us feedback.

Players selected to participate in the beta test will receive an email invitation directing them to download the beta client directly from the Battle.net desktop app. If you haven't received an invite this time, don't forget to [sign up for the beta](https://wowclassic.blizzard.com/en-us/#beta)\* for your chance to participate. Additional invite waves will happen as we get further into development. Learn more in our article [here](https://worldofwarcraft.com/news/23817630/).

#### Developer Update: What's Ahead for Wrath of the Lich King Classic

![WoW_SS_HowlingFjord_Blog_Thumbnail_1200x675.jpg](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/1s/1SXFYJZYVTUL1655771000950.jpg)

*Wrath of the Lich King Classic* provides new opportunities to make a few changes that will improve the gameplay experience in the next chapter of Classic WoW.

In [this Developer Update](https://worldofwarcraft.com/news/23813563/), the *World of Warcraft Classic* Team takes you through some of those changes and what they can mean for the future of Wrath Classic.

#### Building Azeroth: Northrend

![wow-building-azeroth-wotlk-editorial-suite-blog-1200x675.png](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/8k/8KLMPQ3M1S0C1656552404423.png)

Grab your parka and gloves and prepare for an adventurous return to the frozen tundra of Northrend in a new episode of Building Azeroth—[watch now](https://www.worldofwarcraft.com/en-us/news/23816411)!

#### Classic Development Update Posts on the Forums (English Only):

- **Dungeon Creature Behavior and Group XP**
  - Adjustments to the behavior of creatures in most BCC dungeons (including the new dungeons in Wrath of the Lich King Classic) are coming, as well as a reduction to group XP earned. [[Learn More](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/adjustments-to-dungeon-creature-behavior-and-group-xp/1267344)]
- **Joyous Journeys—Summer XP Buff Event**
  - As we continue work on Wrath of the Lich King Classic, we want to celebrate the defeat of Kil'Jaeden and the Burning Legion (again!) with a special event to help prepare players for their adventures in Northrend. [[Learn More](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/joyous-journeys-%E2%80%93-summer-xp-buff-event/1267341)]

**THIS WEEK IN SHADOWLANDS**

- **Midsummer Fire Festival — Ends July 5**
  - A time of merriment and celebration, dedicated to the year's hottest season.
- **Pet Battle Bonus Event**
  - While this event is active, your pets will earn experience at triple the rate. Get out there and battle!
- **PvP Brawl: Arathi Blizzard**
  - Put away your bathing suit, pull out your parka, and enter a winter wonderland as the Arathi Basin you know, and love gets a fresh coat of ice and snow.

**COMING UP IN SHADOWLANDS**

##### Beginning Next Week:

- **Darkmoon Faire**
  - Come see Silas Darkmoon and his troupe, play games that test the mind and nerve, and behold exotic sights from the four corners of Azeroth…and beyond!
- **Fireworks Spectacular One Night Only!  – July 4**
  - After the sun sets, the end of Midsummer is celebrated with an hourly firework extravaganza. To watch the show, visit any capital city or Booty Bay.
- **PvP Brawl: Gravity Lapse**
  - Gravity getting you down? Step into the Eye of the Storm and prepare for an en-lighten-ing experience.
- **Weekly Bonus Event: Mists of Pandaria Timewalking**
  - Players can venture into six of Mists of Pandaria's storied dungeons to reap new rewards all week during this Bonus Event.

##### Beginning the Week of July 11:

- **Torghast: Beasts of Prodigum**
  - Beasts of Prodigum have been unleashed upon Torghast, Tower of the Damned!
- **Arena Skirmish Bonus Event**
  - While this event is active, Arena Skirmishes will award Honor at an increased rate. Will you answer the call to battle?
- **PvP Brawl: Comp Stomp**
  - Jump into Arathi Basin for a head-to-head battle against AI opponents. Use the Group Finder tool (default hotkey: i) in the Player vs. Player tab to participate.
- **Luminous Luminaries One Day Only! – July 13**
  - GG Engineering has discovered luminescent creatures in the depths of Vashj'ir. They need your help researching the creatures before they disappear into the deep sea again!

Check your in-game calendar for a list of the current and upcoming in-game event start times!
