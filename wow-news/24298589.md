## Get the Drop on New Rewards During BlizzCon 2026

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_header/wh/WHLSXEERPDQV1788216107991.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_header/wh/WHLSXEERPDQV1788216107991.png)

BlizzCon 2026 will stream select programming live for everyone, and we’ve got a bucketful of rewards just waiting for you to claim.

Watch any eligible World of Warcraft stream on Twitch.tv from 12 September at 17:30 BST to 27 September at 18:00 BST or on the [official World of Warcraft YouTube channel](https://www.youtube.com/warcraft) from 12 September at 17:30 am PDT until 14 September at 03:00 pBST to claim a bucket full of rewards including: 200 Trader’s Tender, Cuddly Blue Grrgle Housing decor item, Fluffy Comfy Flying Quilt Mount, and the Venomous champion’s Illustrious Banner.

Attending BlizzCon in person? You can still receive these rewards by [linking your BlizzCon Pass](https://blizzcon.com/blizzcon-pass-linking/) to your Battle.net Account no later than 18 September 2026.

Rewards and eligibility vary by game, stream, and platform.

## WoW Roofus Pack Raises More Than $1.3M for Habitat for Humanity

[![Video](https://markdown-videos-api.jorgenkh.no/youtube/_yT62TNxaq4?width=1280&height=720)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_yT62TNxaq4)

Earlier this year, players adopted Roofus through [the Roofus Pack promotion](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/en-us/news/24267940) in support of Habitat for Humanity. The cheerful builder, Roofus, joined players across modern *World of Warcraft* and *WoW Classic* progression realms as an in-game companion pet linked to a real-world cause, and Paw Pal Housing Decor items helped make players’ houses feel a little more like home.

During the promotion, Blizzard donated 100% of the purchase price of The Roofus Pack to Habitat for Humanity.

Thank you to everyone who adopted Roofus, shared the promotion, or helped spread the word. Your support raised more than $1.3 million USD for Habitat for Humanity.
