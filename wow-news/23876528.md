### How to Enter:

Visit [shareyourdragon.com](https://shareyourdragon.com/), use the WoW AR app to create an image of you and your dragon, and upload your image to create your own custom Shout. We’ll randomly choose three first-place winners and three second-place winners from each social platform for a total of 6 winners.
