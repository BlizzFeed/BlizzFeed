## September is a Great Month to Celebrate Friendship at the Trading Post

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/x4/X406FFWOJFWE1787251052436.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/x4/X406FFWOJFWE1787251052436.png)

**This Month's Bonus Reward: Crested Ember Leafmimic Mount**\
*“*This sturdy leafmimic can be found only in the warmest of areas. Its red scales allow it to hide undiscovered in the heated alcoves it prefers.*”*

Friendship takes center stage at the Trading Post this September as Azeroth celebrates BlizzCon 2026. Complete activities in the Traveler’s Log, found in the **Adventure Guide** (Shift-J), to fill the monthly bar and earn the **Crested Ember Leafmimic** mount.

Visit T&W (Tawney and Wilder) just outside the Mage District in Stormwind or the Zen’shiri Trading Post next to Grommash Hold in Orgrimmar to check out all the latest items they have available. Players can also visit the Trading Post in the capital cities of Dornogal (The Foregrounds) on the Isle of Dorn and in the renewed [Silvermoon City](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/news/24243213), where representatives of both Tawney and Wilder and the Zen’shiri Trading Post are open for business.

## Carve, Climb, and Cruise Through the Skies of Northrend

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_header/25/259KQZ61TKEN1659374293133.jpg)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_header/25/259KQZ61TKEN1659374293133.jpg)

Buckle up and blaze through the chilly skies of Northrend! The Northrend Cup returns with daring Skyriding races, exclusive rewards, and courses across some of the region’s most iconic locations.

**When:** 25 August to 8 September\
**Where:** Begins in Valdrakken, Stormwind City, Orgrimmar, and Northrend

Trek to Valdrakken in the Dragon Isles to speak with Lord Andestrasz near the Rostrum of Transformation to pick up the initial quest.

With several races spread across Northrend, racers will have a skyfull of options for challenging courses in Normal, Advanced, and Reverse variations.

The sky's the limit (unless you're trying to beat the timer); may the winds be at your back as you go for the Gold.
