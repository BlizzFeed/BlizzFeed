## Raid Sets

Players can undertake the challenge in The Voidspire and The Dreamrift raids to collect their new class sets. Four pieces are available within The Voidspire with the chest piece available from The Dreamrift.

> Players can also collect a [Chiming Void Curio](https://www.wowhead.com/item=249367/chiming-void-curio?brl) from L’ura to purchase set items when the March on Quel’Danas raid becomes available. *“Find Kirana near the entrance to the March on Quel'Danas raid to trade this for powerful class set armor."*

Don’t forget, when you unlock a set appearance in a higher difficulty, it will unlock all preceding set variants for your collection.

## PvP Sets

Take part in the Midnight PvP season to earn new Gladiator and Elite armor class sets.

- **Galactic Gladiator’s Silk Armor** (Cloth): Mage, Priest, Warlock
- **Galactic Gladiator’s Plate Armor** (Plate)**:** Death Knight, Paladin, Warrior
- **Galactic Gladiator’s Leather Armor** (Leather): Demon Hunter, Monk, Rogue, Druid
- **Galactic Gladiator’s Chain Armor** (Mail): Evoker, Hunter, Shaman
