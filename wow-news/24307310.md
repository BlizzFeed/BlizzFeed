> Here you will find a list of hotfixes that address various issues related to World of Warcraft: Midnight, Mists of Pandaria Classic, Season of Discovery, The Burning Crusade Classic, WoW Classic Era, and Hardcore.

---

Here you'll find a list of hotfixes that address various issues related to World of Warcraft: Midnight, Mists of Pandaria Classic, Season of Discovery, Burning Crusade Classic, WoW Classic Era, and Hardcore. Some of the hotfixes below take effect the moment they were implemented, while others may require scheduled realm restarts to go into effect. Please keep in mind that some issues cannot be addressed without a client-side patch update. This list will be updated as additional hotfixes are applied.

## Hotfixes

---

**October 30, 2026**

**Housing**

- Tracking the source for the Nagrand plant decor sold by Botanist Gril'ka in Nagrand now properly leads to her location.

**Items**

- Azshara's Darkscale Cloak and Azshara's Deepscale Cloak were adjusted to resolve a dividing line that was visible under specific lighting.

**Labyrinth**

- Fixed an issue with the "Voices of Stone" bonus objective in the "Bear's Burden" scenario that caused it to become stuck on the UI.
- Fixed an issue with "The Wandering" scenario that caused some Tether icons to flicker erratically.

**October 27, 2026**

**Classes**

- **Druid**
  - **Feral**
    - All damage done increased by 5%. Does not affect PvP combat.
  - **Restoration**
    - *Developer’s note: These changes are intended to help Restoration Druid deal with burst damage in dungeons, as they are behind other healers. They are performing well in raid settings, so we are targeting talents and abilities that are favored when healing a 5-person group.*
    - Verdancy healing increased by 60%. Does not affect PvP combat.
    - Regrowth healing increased by 10%. Does not affect PvP combat.
    - Improved Swiftmend increases Swiftmend healing by 40% (was 30%). Does not affect PvP combat.
- **Hunter**
  - **Marksmanship**
    - *Developers’ notes: These changes are intended to boost the area damage effectiveness of Marksmanship Hunters.*
    - Trick Shots now increases the ricochet damage of Aimed Shot and Rapid Fire by 85% (was 75%).
    - Explosive Shot damage increased by 10%.
    - Volley damage increased by 10%.
- **Mage**
  - **Fire**
    - All damage done increased by 5%. Does not affect PvP combat.
- **Monk**
  - **Mistweaver**
    - Resolved an issue that caused the Mistline talent to sometimes not properly heal the lowest health target.
- **Rogue**
  - **Subtlety**
    - *Developers’ notes: The buff Subtlety received as compensation alongside changes in the 12.1.5 patch overshot expectations, so we’re rolling back a significant portion of that buff. In addition, the patch changes benefitted Trickster more than Deathstalker, so Trickster is receiving an additional smaller nerf.*
    - All ability damage reduced by 8%.
    - Trickster: Fazed now increases damage by 5% (was 7%).
- **Warlock**
  - **Affliction**
    - Unstable Affliction damage increased by 20%. Does not affect PvP combat.
  - **Destruction**
    - Rain of Fire damage increased by 20%.

**Items**

- Fixed an issue where tabards would hide the Rexxar armor set.

**Labyrinth**

- Fixed an issue where some scenarios were not granting Great Vault credit.

**Player versus Player**

- **Death Knight**
  - **Unholy**
    - *Developers’ notes: The last adjustments to San’layn did increase its viability, but the impact of Vampiric Strike has been higher than we would like.*
    - San’layn: Vampiric Strike damage reduced by 20% in PvP combat.
- **Demon Hunter**
  - **Devourer**
    - Collapsing Star damage increased by 20% in PvP combat**.**
- **Evoker**
  - **Preservation**
    - Chronowarden: Chronal Dynamo increases the healing and damage of Living Flame by 80% when not an instant cast in PvP combat (was 50%).
    - Chronowarden: Reverberations now causes Verdant Embrace to heal for an additional 80% over 8 seconds in PvP combat (was 40%).
- **Hunter**
  - **Marksmanship**
    - Kill Shot damage increased by 40% in PvP combat.
    - Deathblow’s chance to be activated by Aimed Shot increased to 25% in PvP combat.
    - Dark Ranger: Black Arrow damage increased by 40% in PvP combat.
- **Mage**
  - **Fire**
    - *Developers’ notes: Fire Mage has continued to overperform, so we’re decreasing its overall damage to bring it in line with expectations.*
    - All damage reduced by 5% in PvP combat.
  - **Frost**
    - *Developers’ notes: Frostfire has not been a competitive option for Frost Mages this season, so we’re increasing a few of its burst damage aspects to differentiate its damage profile from Spellslinger.*
    - Frostfire: Isothermic Core’s Meteor damage increased by 20% in PvP combat.
    - Frostfire: Duality’s Pyroblast damage increased by 20% in PvP combat.
- **Monk**
  - **Mistweaver**
    - *Developers’ notes: Conduit of the Celestials has not been a competitive option for Mistweavers this season, so we’re increasing its consistent and burst healing.*
    - Conduit of the Celestials: Celestial Conduit healing increased by 35% in PvP combat.
    - Conduit of the Celestials: Strength of the Black Ox absorb increased by 25% in PvP combat.
- **Paladin**
  - **Holy**
    - *Developers’ notes: Holy Paladin’s throughput has been underperforming and we’re decreasing the mana cost of Holy Light to promote casted healing.*
    - All healing increased by 5% in PvP combat.
    - Divine Favor now reduces the mana cost of Holy Light by 50% in PvP combat (was 30%).
- **Priest**
  - **Holy**
    - *Developers’ notes: These changes are aimed at further improving Archon’s viability in PvP with targeted increases to Prayer of Healing’s group healing and the power of proc effects from the tree.*
    - Prayerful Litany now causes the primary target of Prayer of Healing to be healed for 75% more in PvP combat (was 125%).
    - Prayer of Healing healing increased by 30% in PvP combat.
    - Archon: Resonant Energy now increases healing done by 4% per stack in PvP combat (was 2%).
    - Archon: Empowered Surges now increases the healing of spells affected by Surge of Light by 10% in PvP combat (was 5%).
    - Archon: Realized Potential now increases Flash Heal healing by 25% in PvP combat (was 20%).
- **Rogue**
  - **Assassination**
    - Deathstalker: Deathstalker’s Mark damage increased by 20% in PvP combat.
    - Deathstalker: Hunt Them Down damage increased by 20% in PvP combat.
    - Deathstalker: Darkest Night now increases the damage of the next Eviscerate by 15% in PvP combat (was 8%).
- **Shaman**
  - **Restoration**
    - Healing Stream Totem and Stormstream Totem healing increased by 20% in PvP combat.

**The Burning Crusade Classic**

- Heroic Dungeon keys now require Honored reputation instead of Revered.
