## Getting Started

Join us in testing *World of Warcraft: Forever*. Players who have opted in can be selected to participate, and invitations will go out regularly throughout the test. If selected, your Battle.net account is already flagged for access. Eligible digital bundle purchasers will also gain beta access—minimal delays of up to 30 minutes may be experienced, and a launcher restart may be needed.

- Make sure your graphics drivers are up to date.
- Run the Battle.net desktop app—it may need to update itself if you haven’t run it recently. If you don’t have the app installed, get started by downloading it from Battle.net.
- Once Battle.net is installed and updated, select World of Warcraft from the list of games.
- In the Game Version drop-down menu above the Play button, choose the World of Warcraft: Forever Beta option listed under In Development, then click Install.
- Allow installation to complete, click Play, and select any available test realm.
- Create a new test character when available.
- Enter the game and test the available content.

If you have issues installing or operating the test, please check our [Common Technical Issues and Solutions](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/common-technical-issues-and-solutions/22912) to begin troubleshooting.

## What’s Available in Beta

When the test begins, the level cap will be set to 20 and will be raised to 30 later in the beta.

#### Week 1

- New Race and Zone: Skyborne and Zephras Isle
- New Dungeon: Hall of Thanes (Level 13–18)
- New Dungeon: Ruins of Lordaeron (Level 15–20)
- All zone content up to level 20.

Additional zones, dungeons, and the new battleground will open for testing as the beta continues. At a later point during the test period, we’ll also open the servers for several hours so that anyone can access the *WoW: Forever* beta during that window for a “Server Slam.” We’ll share more details to closer to the test period.

The beta will be available through October 21, the last full day of testing, as we begin final preparations for the launch of *World of Warcraft: Forever*.
