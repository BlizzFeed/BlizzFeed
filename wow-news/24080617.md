#### Here are this week's highlights:

##### Dragonflight

- Season 4 Patch 10.2.6 PTR Open for Testing
  - We posted [extensive development notes](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/dragonflight-season-4-ptr-development-notes/1814041/3) on dungeon changes in Season 4.
- Dungeon Testing and Feedback
  - [Testing and feedback on dungeons is underway](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/feedback-dungeon-testing-march-26-april-1/1817323/) and ends Monday, 1 April at 10:00 a.m. PDT (13:00 EDT, 18:00 CEST). Please note that the test period may be adjusted for technical difficulties.

##### Plunderstorm

- Hotfixes
  - [26 March](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/wow-hotfixes-march-27/1769365/150): plunder was increased in Solos matches, reviving a player is faster, leveling rates were adjusted, and several spells were tuned.
  - [27 March](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/wow-hotfixes-march-27/1769365/169): More enemies spawn, some bugs are fixed, and the final stage of each match is more dangerous than before.
- Duos
  - [A fix](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/please-let-us-choose-who-steers-the-bird-in-duos/1818237/10) for who pilots the drop-in bird in Duos is coming.

##### WoW Classic

- Cataclysm Beta
  - Level 80-85 zones, leveling dungeons, and Archaeology are [now available for testing](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/cataclysm-classic-development-notes-and-known-issues-updated-march-28/1802826/4).
