### Make New Allies in Zaralek Cavern

While Raszageth has been defeated, the Primalists carry on in their bid to unravel all the Titans have wrought and must forge new alliances–or rekindle old ones.  As the heroes of Azeroth pursue enemies into the dangers of Zaralek Cavern, they are aided by the local inhabitants found under the Dragon Isles — the molefolk known as Niffen and the Drogbar. They may not know much of the surface wars, but their knowledge of the underground is key to players and their allies finding their footing within.

### New Dragonriding Mount and Racecourses

![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/S0XGMXEEQXDC1682538281534.png)

Discover 8 new Dragonriding Glyphs in Zaralek Cavern to unlock 2 new Dragonriding Traits—Ground Skimming and Land’s Blessing— take on new Dragonriding racecourses and World Quests within Zaralek Cavern, and soar through the cavern with the new Winding Slitherdrake mount. Players will be able to earn this new Dragonriding mount during the campaign chapters "Inherited Sin" and "Inevitable Confrontation" when they become available the week of May 10th.

#### FYRAKK ASSAULTS

![Fyrakk Assaults](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/AKRAJTOVOV0Z1682538281606.png)

Fyrakk’s forces are gathering resources at Suffusion Camps located in the Azure Span and Ohn’ahran Plains. It’s up to the heroes of Azeroth to disrupt their efforts to lure Fyrakk and his disciples out. Fyrakk Assaults will become available alongside *Dragonflight*: Season 2 the week of May 9.

### Aberrus, the Shadowed Crucible Raid Preview

![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/lz/LZYJ5ORIUU501681920480489.jpg)

Breach Neltharion’s secret laboratory hidden within Zaralek Cavern. It is here that he created the dracthyr and ultimately succumbed to the whispers of the Old Gods and their corruption. Players will face nine new raid bosses in an effort to thwart the incarnates as well as Scalecommander Sarkareth, whose forces seek to claim the legacy of the dracthyr. [Visit our article](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/en-gb/news/23935246) to learn more.

**Raid release schedule:**

- Week of 10 May: Raid Finder Wing 1, Normal, Heroic, and Mythic difficulties will open with the weekly maintenance for each region
- Week of 23 May: Raid Finder Wing 2 opens
- Week of 7 June: Raid Finder Wing 3 opens
- Week of 21 June: Raid Finder Wing 4 opens
