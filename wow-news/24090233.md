# Arena World Championship Returns 3 May!

- URL: https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/en-gb/news/24090233/arena-world-championship-returns-3-may
![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/aq/AQEU0B5LVNTH1714165106328.png)

> Get ready to watch the top World of Warcraft© teams clash in AWC Season 4.

---

***Beginning 3 May, the arena gates will open once more for the 2024 Dragonflight Season 4 Arena World Championship (AWC)! Tune in to the final two Cups on [Twitch](https://twitch.tv/warcraft) and [YouTube](https://youtube.com/warcraft) to see how far the gladiators will go.***

The high-stakes competition continues in Season 4, with only two opportunities for the top gladiators to emerge victorious from the arenas. Competitors will clash in Cups 3 and 4 for their chance to earn a spot in the Dragonflight Grand Finals! Tune in to the Tournament for close combat, near-death encounters, and exhilarating action.

---

### Details

- **Venue:**  Online: Twitch.tv/Warcraft and YouTube.com/Warcraft
- **Co-streaming:** Co-streaming is back! Check with your favorite competitor to see if they will stream their POV during the tournament, or gather your own audience and host a Watch Party. To sign up for co-streaming, use the [AWC Watch Party Form](http://blizz.ly/CostreamAWC).
- **Dates:**
  - Cup 3: 3–5 May
  - Cup 4: 10–12 May
  - Finals: 17–19 May
- **Broadcast Start Time:**  10:00 am (PDT) / 7:00 pm (CEST)
- **Format:** Double Elimination, Best-of-5. Grand Finals Best-of-7.
