#### Here are this week's highlights:

- **Mythic Keystone Issue Resolved:** Due to an issue with the Keystones in North America on Tuesday, [Mythic+ dungeons were temporarily disabled](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/mythic-keystone-dungeons-temporarily-disabled-resolved/1608091) while being investigated. Once resolved, the [Mythic+ dungeons were re-opened](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/mythic-keystone-dungeons-temporarily-disabled-resolved/1608091/134).
- **Revival Catalyst is Coming Next Week as Intended:** The “Catalyst’s Boon” quest that leads players to the Revival Catalyst in Dragonflight Season 2 became available one week earlier than intended, while the Catalyst itself remained unusable. This issue was explained, and the [quest was pushed to the week of June 13](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/revival-catalyst/1608050/26), when the Revival Catalyst will be available.
- **Latest Updates to Fractures in Time**: On Tuesday, the latest [PTR update](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/dragonflight-fractures-in-time-ptr-development-notes/1587226/7) was released for Dragonflight Fractures in Time and included class tuning changes and a [welcome-back gift](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/dragonflight-fractures-in-time-ptr-development-notes/1587226/8) for characters 10-60 that have not been logged into for an extended period.
- **Cobalt Shalewing Drop Rate Increased:** A hotfix was deployed to bring the drop rate of the [Cobalt Shalewing](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/drop-rates-in-zaralek-caverns-not-increased/1606509/10) mount in line with similar drops in the zone.
