#### Here are this week's highlights:

#### Dragonflight

- **Upcoming Prismatic Bauble Toy Changes:** The [Prismatic Bauble](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/prismatic-bauble-leaves-jepetto-joybuzzs-inventory-soon/1620559) toy, which leaves a rainbow trail behind characters, will return to its pre-June state soon, including a longer cooldown and purchasable from a more elusive vendor.
- **Fractures in Time PTR Update:** The latest [PTR update](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/dragonflight-fractures-in-time-ptr-development-notes/1587226/11) was released Tuesday for Dragonflight Fractures in Time and included a variety of class tuning changes and a new [Mythic track for item upgrades](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/dragonflight-fractures-in-time-ptr-development-notes/1587226/12).
- **Warlock Demon Names in Fractures in Time:** Some backend changes are in the works. However, the [demon names](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/warlock-customization-feedback-name-issues/1612486/34) may change depending on existing Glyphs and their interaction with the new customization options in Fractures in Time.

#### Wrath of the Lich King Classic

- **Lockout Issues for Trial of the Crusader:** The lockout for this raid isn’t working correctly for some players. While finishing the raid and [reclearing some bosses is acceptable](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/togc-weekly-lockout-not-working/1621115/17), any [abuse of this situation can lead to account actions](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/togc-weekly-lockout-not-working/1621115/22) at the development team’s discretion.
- **Classic Era Hardcore PTR is Open:** The [PTR for Classic Hardcore realms](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/c/classic-era-1144-ptr/296) was opened on Thursday. Check out the [development notes](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/wow-classic-era-1144-development-notes/1621723) or share your experiences after trying it out. A thread to collect [PTR availability changes](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/ptr-is-down-june-29-100-pm-pdt/1621759) has been started.
- **Development Approach to Hardcore Realms:** A [post](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/concerns-about-classic-hardcore/1621504/2) on the WoW Community Council forum focused on best-supporting player agency, some adjustments that will be made, and others that will not.
