---

Load up your favorite character, and prepare for an aerial adventure! You’ll explore the Dragon Isles, master the art of Dragonriding, take your professions to the next level, experience the early campaign quests\*\*, brave all-new dungeons, and more.

From now through June 4, 2023, we’re offering returning *World of Warcraft* players with active Game Time or Subscription access to all their characters along with access to the *Dragonflight* expansion during the *Dragonflight* Expansion Trial\*. For players who participated in the [previous Dragonflight Trial](https://worldofwarcraft.com/news/23891315), your dracthyr Evoker will be available to continue your adventures.

---

#### Spread the Word and Your Wings!

*Dragonflight* brings a number of exciting updates to *World of Warcraft*, including the dracthyr Evoker, the first ever class/race combo in WoW, a brand-new talent system, updates to professions with specializations, a new work orders system, a new form of aerial movement in Dragonriding, and much more. With a bigger-than-ever focus on exploration and player expression, *Dragonflight* rewards you for playing your way.

Bring your level 60 characters to the Dragon Isles, or play as the new dracthyr Evoker with complete access to the beginning of the new campaign in the Dragon Isles.

While you’re playing the trial, you won’t be able to progress your character beyond level 60\*\*\*, but don’t worry—any experience you earn through the trial period will be banked and applied to your character if you decide to purchase Dragonflight later. Get started on your adventure today!

---

#### Getting Started on Your Dragonflight Expansion Trial

- Download the Blizzard Battle.net [desktop app](https://www.blizzard.com/apps/battle.net/desktop).
- Launch the app and log in to your account.
- Click on the World of Warcraft tab within the app, then either Install or Update.
- While you're waiting, get your [Subscription](https://shop.battle.net/product/world-of-warcraft-subscription) or Game Time.
- When the installation or update is complete, click Play.
- Play the *Dragonflight* Expansion, included with your subscription or game time, through June 4, 2023.

#### Trouble Logging In?

If you're having trouble logging in, try [recovering your account information](https://battle.net/account/support/login-support.html), and check our Support articles for [login troubleshooting](https://battle.net/support/article/breaking/21791993) and the latest [system requirements](https://battle.net/support/article/76459) for *Dragonflight*.
