## Soar Through Turbulent Timeways—Now Live!

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/sk/SKSOIR48XY231780083361377.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/sk/SKSOIR48XY231780083361377.png)

Once more, the Bronze Dragonflight has discovered growing disturbances in the timeways as several rapidly intersect our own.

**When**: June 30–August 11

Timewalk through a previous expansion each week for six consecutive weeks, beginning and ending with *Dragonflight*. This grand tour through *World of Warcraft’s* past features six Timewalking Dungeons: Algeth’ar Academy, Halls of Infusion, Neltharus, Ruby Life Pools, The Azure Vaults, and Brackenhide Hollow.

Players can also shop at a new *Dragonflight* vendor, Xydan, located in the Bronze Enclave in Valdrakken.

Also, for the duration of Turbulent Timeways, the Timewalking quest available from the weekly quest giver will offer an increased reward—a Heroic **Cache of Quel’Thalas Treasures** with items level 259–276.

Learn more by heading over to our [previously published article](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/news/24264422).

## Quality-of-Life Improvements Coming in Curse of Ula'tek

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_header/d8/D84JV9ZJWDS01772587429618.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_header/d8/D84JV9ZJWDS01772587429618.png)

Since the 12.0.5 content update, we’ve been building on your feedback by making targeted improvements to enhance the play experience. Recent hotfixes, along with changes in *Midnight: Revelations*, have already addressed some issues with rewards, looting, quest experience, and durability loss.

More updates are planned in the *Curse of Ula’tek* content update, including account-wide user interface settings, Auction House improvements, a one-time Profession Knowledge reset, and fixes for common combat and travel issues.

In *Curse of Ula'tek* and beyond, we’ll continue looking for ways to make *World of Warcraft* easier to navigate and more enjoyable to play. We deeply appreciate all of your feedback on ways we can improve the *World of Warcraft* player experience and will continue to make efforts to address these improvement opportunities as they arise.

Dive into more details about the quality of life improvements ahead by reading our [previously published article](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/news/24288418).

## July’s Trading Post Ignites a True-Blue Celebration

[![alternate-image-name](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_header/zs/ZSONMQRPBIPZ1782406178978.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_header/zs/ZSONMQRPBIPZ1782406178978.png)

**Bilgewater X-TREME**\
**Mount**\
*"The engineers of Undermine were so preoccupied with whether or not they COULD make a faster rocket, they didn't stop to think if they should."*

July’s Trading Post is taking on a brilliant blue streak, with fresh finds ready to add a bold splash of color to your collection.

Complete activities to earn Trader’s Tender, fill your Traveler’s Log, and rocket away with the Bilgewater X-TREME mount.

Visit T&W (Tawney and Wilder) just outside the Mage District in Stormwind or the Zen’shiri Trading Post next to Grommash Hold in Orgrimmar to check out all the latest items they have available.

Players can also visit the Trading Post in the capital city of Dornogal (The Foregrounds) on the Isle of Dorn, the renewed Silvermoon City, where representatives of both Tawney and Wilder and the Zen’shiri Trading Post are open for business.

Read through our [article](http://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/news/24287639/) for a detailed list of ***all*** the items available from the July Trading Post and how to get them.

Champions new or returning to *World of Warcraft* and *Midnight* who want to know more can [take a tour of the Trading Post](http://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/news/24131507) to learn everything you need to know, from where to find it to how you can earn additional Trader's Tender through the Traveler's Log.

## Shape Your Destiny, and Your Mount, with the Stoneforged Sentinel

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_header/s0/S0FAGGEZ5Q1L1782516424949.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_header/s0/S0FAGGEZ5Q1L1782516424949.png)

Chiseled from enduring stone and awakened by ancient power, the Stoneforged Sentinel mount is yours to shape. With a wide array of customization options—over 300,000 unique mount variations—you can craft a truly personal flying guardian unlike any other. The mount automatically scales to the fastest riding skill known by each character, and if a character’s level isn’t high enough to use a flying mount, the Stoneforged Sentinel makes a sturdy ground mount.

Visit any Rostrum of Transformation to customize your Stoneforged Sentinel. Customization options include: 4 x Stone Colors, 4 x Metal Colors, 5 x Gem Colors, 3 x Saddle Styles, 6 x Head Styles, 6 x Wing Styles, 6 x Claw Styles, and 6 x Tail Styles.

Rostrum locations can be found in multiple cities and camps. Look for the gold dragon head on your minimap or ask a city guard for directions.
