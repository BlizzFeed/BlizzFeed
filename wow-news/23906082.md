# Mythic Dungeon International - First Dragonflight Season!

- URL: https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/en-gb/news/23906082/mythic-dungeon-international-first-dragonflight-season
![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/g7/G7I4UWO1HK2R1675962419901.png)

> The Mythic Dungeon International is making its premiere in Dragonflight and kicking off with the all-new Season 1 Dungeons!

---

Beginning February 17-19, the Dungeons will start for the 2023 Mythic Dungeon International (MDI) in Dragonflight! Tune in to the Group Stage live on [Twitch](https://www.twitch.tv/warcraft) and [YouTube](http://youtube.com/warcraft), where teams will race to qualify for the MDI 2023 Global Finals.

Each new season of Dragonflight brings an entirely new set of Mythic Dungeons. Each MDI will feature new teams, new strategies, and new battlefields in the race to the finish line. Teams have already soared through Time Trials to get this far, now, the top 24 compete head-to-head to prove themselves the best dungeoneers of Dragonflight. MDI 2023 starts with three weekends of MDI Groups, each pitting eight teams against one another, with the top two qualifiers moving on to the MDI Global Finals. After one final chance in the Last Stand Tournament, the top eight groups will clash in early April to determine Dragonflight’s first Mythic Dungeon Champion!

  - Group A: February 17-19

  - Group C: March 3-5

    - Sign-ups for The Last Stand are open until February 27 on [GameBattles](https://gamebattles.majorleaguegaming.com/pc/world-of-warcraft/tournament/mdi-dragonflight-season-1/info)
- **Broadcast Start Time:** 10:00 a.m. (PST) / 7:00 p.m. (CET)
- **Prize Pool:** $420,000 (USD) across the entire season, $30,000 (USD) per Group
- **Format:** Head-to-head Dungeon Races. Double elimination, Best of three
