Join us in celebrating the artists whose efforts helped build the visually stunning *World of Warcraft®: Midnight™* in the [Blizzard Entertainment World of Warcraft: Midnight Art Blast](https://magazine.artstation.com/2026/04/blizzard-entertainment-world-of-warcraft-midnight/).

Adventure is calling you home in *World of Warcraft: Midnight*, where you’ll return to the re-envisioned elven kingdom of Quel’Thalas and adventure through new and reimagined zones in the battle between the Light and the Void as Xal’atath’s invasion of Azeroth grows stronger, and the Voidstorm threatens to cover the world in darkness.

*Midnight* is now live, and your hour has come—take up arms and embark on all-new stories, zones, and dungeons, and put down roots of your own with Housing.

Prepare to answer the call—[learn more](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/midnight).
