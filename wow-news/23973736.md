### Specialize with the new Augmentation Evoker

![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/ka/KA3IWV2U3H0D1687798323482.jpg)

Imbue your allies with the power of the Black dragonflight and twist fate in their favor with Bronze magic as the new Augmentation Specialization. Emberthal and Ebyssian are working together to release the Black dragonflight essence that had been siphoned from the Dracthyr during their long stasis, unlocking memories and powers forgotten to them. Learn more about this new specialization in [our article](https://worldofwarcraft.com/en-gb/news/23952478).

---

### Preserve the Timeline: Dawn of the Infinite Mega-Dungeon

![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/template_resource/QKBLKV3GQIGA1687915289582.jpg)

Rally your party and venture into a new eight-boss Mythic difficulty mega-dungeon: Dawn of the Infinite! At stake is the future of Azeroth, and only you can ensure the timeline remains preserved—hopefully with a minimal number of paradoxes. This Mythic-only instance can reward you with loot coveted by time benders everywhere, including a perennial assortment of powerful gear, Dragonriding customizations, and many other surprises. Learn more from [our article](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/en-gb/news/23976337).

---

### Unravel the Time Streams

![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/m1/M1MHKREPKTSH1686841156557.png)

The time streams have become increasingly more unstable with recent events. As the boundaries between the timelines weaken, minions from other timelines begin pouring through into the Dragon Isles. Push back against the encroaching chaos from other timelines in the new Time Rifts Events, then head over to Eon’s Fringe for new quests.  Learn more from our [overview article](https://worldofwarcraft.com/en-gb/news/23970947).

---

### Level-Up Your Warlock and Pets

![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/ke/KEWYPQTLY5BR1687816631241.png)

In the Fractures in Time content update, the Warlock Class expands to new races. You’ll also be able to undertake a new Warlock-specific questline and use the Barbershop to customize your pets. Learn more in [our article](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/en-gb/news/23973735/).

---

### Help Wanted: Inquire at the Whelp Daycare

![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/43/43QZZVZXY2G41687807097146.png)

The Ruby Lifeshrine has more eggs than it can manage and needs a little help hatching and teaching five of their dragon whelps, so they’ve called in the crew at the Little Scales Daycare to assist. The crew has become overwhelmed and needs you to help wrangle some whelps. Learn more about this near feature in [our article](https://worldofwarcraft.com/en-gb/news/23970951).

---

### Get Ready to Ride in the Kalimdor Cup

![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/664AC1MUD07A1687991691993.png)

Rediscover Kalimdor's skies with a new dragonriding racing event arriving to Azeroth and running from August 15–28. Challenge 16 races across Kalimdor in Normal, Advanced, and Reverse variations to earn Riders of Azeroth Badges to exchange for rewards from Maztha.
