#### Seeds of Renewal Zone Overview: Discover Bel'ameth

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_header/59/59PV5UUZO9C01703268545069.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_header/59/59PV5UUZO9C01703268545069.png)

Discover Bel'ameth, the arms of the Goddess, a much-needed place for the kaldorei to seek repose and solace. This new night elf settlement lies adjacent to the Ohn'ahran Plains in the Dragon Isles. It rests beneath the new World Tree, Amirdrassil's boughs. [[Learn More](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/news/24050148/)]

#### Dig Into the Dragon Isles with the Azerothian Archives

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/LX34U1IOV7GG1704850343259.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/LX34U1IOV7GG1704850343259.png)

Dig into the history of the Dragon Isles and meet a unique cast of characters, hear stories of old, and witness the iconography of a time before. Participate in Solo and Group activities within Traitor's Rest with an hourly Public Event, plenty of opportunity to explore, and earn cosmetic rewards like Battle Pets, Mounts, and a Transmog set. [[Learn More](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/news/24053285/)]

#### Gilneas Reclamation

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/3QMP333NPICV1704850342463.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/3QMP333NPICV1704850342463.png)

King Greymane, the Old Wolf, is ready to retake his kingdom, but Gilneas isn't as empty as expected. Help reclaim what was lost and return the kingdom to Gilnean hands. To begin this storyline, speak to Genn Greymane in Stormwind or Calia Menethil in Silverpine Forest.

---

#### Follower Dungeons

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/ED930ACGS3L81704850342544.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/ED930ACGS3L81704850342544.png)

Tank, heal, and DPS alongside follower NPC companions that join you on your Dungeon adventures. They will be available in all eight Normal difficulty *Dragonflight* Dungeons—queue solo or partner up with a friend or two. These companions will allow players to learn about *Dragonflight* dungeons at their own pace. [[Learn More](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/news/24054790)]

#### New Character Customizations

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_header/v5/V5HOGDB23IKY1704334515490.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_header/v5/V5HOGDB23IKY1704334515490.png)

Trolls, Draenei, Warlock Tyrant, and Darkglare demons get a glow-up in the Seeds of Renewal content update—get creative with your characters! Trolls, it's time to hit the salon and change things up with five new hair colors. Unique customization for Draenei, Warlock Tyrant, and Darkglare demons are incoming, along with customization achievements. [[Learn More](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/news/24038517/)]

#### Dragonriding Available Worldwide

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/IHUNUFMO0TNI1704850342444.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/IHUNUFMO0TNI1704850342444.png)

The skies of Azeroth and beyond can be yours while adventuring. In Seeds of Renewal, Dragonriding will be expanded to all old-world continents wherever flying is available.
