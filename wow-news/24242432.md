Join the celebration as *World of Warcraft* celebrates its 21st Anniversary with a variety of activities and a myriad of ways to earn currency to purchase a plethora of items—and this year, you can don the intrepid threads of the **Loyal Watchman** ensemble.

> **When**: 16 November–7 December\
> **Where**: Caverns of Time in Tanaris

Grab the quest from any Holiday Celebrants and hitch a ride to the Caverns of Time through portals located in Orgrimmar and Stormwind.

Players receive an automatic **10% Experience and Reputation buff** during the Anniversary Event. They must complete the repeatable **Celebrate Good Fun!** quest from& **Yllana** every day to add **another 10% to the total**.

The anniversary event buff stacks with other XP and Reputation buffs, and you can enjoy a& **75% XP buff** during the event!

- +20% XP and Reputation from the Anniversary Event.
- +25% XP buff from the [Five Warband Mentors: The War Within](https://www.wowhead.com/achievement=19477) achievement.
- +10% XP and Gold from War Mode.
- +10% from the [Draught of Ten Lands](https://www.wowhead.com/item=166750) potion. This works for characters **level 49 and lower**.

## A Rewarding Celebration

The WoW 21st Anniversary has a variety of activities and ways to earn currency, which can be used to purchase myriad items, including the new **Loyal Watchman** ensemble transmog set pieces—Helm, Gauntlets, Trousers, Cuirass, Spaulders, Belt, and Boots.

![An orc in armor](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/t6/T6LFG2I3Q3KM1762888528926.png)

*Ensemble: Loyal Watchman Transmog Set*\
(Helm, Gauntlets, Trousers, Cuirass, Spaulders, Belt, and Boots)

During the Anniversary, earn anywhere from 1 to 3 **Bronze Celebration Tokens** per activity over the course of the event each week. Quests and activity completions are tracked Warband-wide, can be completed on any character in your Warband, and will count toward the weekly amount of Warband Transferable Tokens your Warband can earn each week.

So, there’s no need to run the event with multiple characters every week—unless you want to spread the fun among your characters.

The top goblins have tallied everything up so that you know how to earn all the anniversary currencies and where they can be spent.

### Anniversary Currency

- **Timewarped Badges**: Earned from Timewalking events.
- **Marks of Honor**: Exchanged for legacy weapons and armor at PvP Vendors.
- **Ancient Mana**: Concentrated magic from the ancient leylines beneath Suramar.
- **Polished Pet Charms**: Redeemable at battle pet masters.

### Anniversary Vendors

Make the most of the festivities by visiting the many vendors on the grounds for tasty treats and delights.

- **Traeya <Armor Vendor>**: Tier 2 Armor Vendor (Timewarped Badges)
- **Time-Displaced Thanthaldis Snowgleam <Stormpike Supply Officer>**: Scaling PvP-themed weapons and armor (Marks of Honor and Timewarped Badges)
- **Time-Displaced Jorek Ironside <Frostwolf Supply Officer>**: Scaling PvP-themed weapons and armor (Marks of Honor and Timewarped Badges)
- **Historian Ma’di**: Anniversary Rewards Vendor (Timewarped Badges)
- **Notary Grably**: Bronze Celebration Titles

## Expect Joyous Journeys Ahead in Mists of Pandaria Classic

> **When:** 18 November–9 December

The mystical lands of Pandaria are swirling with danger. For our stalwart champions in *World of Warcraft Classic* progression (currently *Mists of Pandaria Classic*), take advantage of the Joyous Journey’s experience buff to enhance your odyssey through Azeroth, increasing experience gains by 50% up to level 90!

Create a new character, finish leveling a forgotten one, or invite friends to join you in legendary Pandaria. The Joyous Journeys buff is active by default; however, you can visit any innkeeper in the capital cities and Dalaran to turn it on or off, and it is not available in modern *World of Warcraft®*, *Legion Remix*, or *World of Warcraft® Classic Era*.

## WoW Classic Anniversary Edition Realms

From now through November 23, take advantage of the **Unrelenting Rallying Cry of the Dragonslayer** buff with an increase to critical chance to your spells by 10%, melee, and ranged by 5% and grants 140 attack power. This buff does not stack with **Rallying Cry of the Dragonslayer** and cannot be Chronobooned.
