## Subscribe to a Year of World of Warcraft® for a Get a Slew of Extra Goodies

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/S9ZDLH4LOYAI1728595486717.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/S9ZDLH4LOYAI1728595486717.png)

Be a part of *WoW*'s 20th anniversary year and get the mystical new Startouched Furline Skyriding mount and several additional mounts and pets to add to your *World of Warcraft* and *WoW* Classic collection! Get four new mounts, a pet, and two exciting future items by purchasing a [World of Warcraft® 12-Month Subscription](https://shop.battle.net/product/world-of-warcraft-subscription). Your subscription will give you access to Azeroth's expansive world, including ***World of Warcraft*** and **Cataclysm Classic**™.

For players currently on a 12-month recurring subscription with a future renewal date, receive the mounts as a gift in the Battle.net desktop app at no additional charge, no later than 31 October 2024 (must be redeemed by 30 April 2025).

## Twitch Drop: Scramble to Get the Sand Scarab Pet on 22 October!

![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/ki/KIROLQGK4WAE1728933794750.png)

“Easily mistaken for a common beetle, the sting of a sand scarab will quickly clear up the misconception should you be unlucky enough to be stung by one.”

Greetings, adventurers! With the release of the [*World of Warcraft 20th Anniversary Celebration*](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/news/24147266/), players can commemorate the occasion with new **Twitch Drops**! Watch any *World of Warcraft*stream on Twitch.tv from 22 October, 18:00 BST, until 5 November, 18:00 GMT, to earn the **Sand Scarab** pet.

**Start Time:** 22 October, 18:00 BST (19:00 CEST)\
**End Time:** 5 November, 18:00 GMT (19:00 CET)

Watch **4 hours** of *World of Warcraft* content while these Twitch Drops are active on any channel to earn the **Sand Scarab** pet.
