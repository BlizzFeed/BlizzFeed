## BCC Anniversary Edition: Black Temple Arrives 27 August

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_header/al/ALECJTLSVCUJ1784228602971.jpg)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_header/al/ALECJTLSVCUJ1784228602971.jpg)

Illidan Stormrage has claimed the Black Temple as his fortress and is amassing an army of fel warriors in his pursuit of power. Infiltrate the Black Temple and face the Betrayer in *Burning Crusade Classic Anniversary*beginning 27 August at 23:00 pm BST.

**Your Journey Ahead**

- **New Raids**: Black Temple (Illidan the Betrayer) and The Battle for Mount Hyjal (Archimonde)
- **New Daily Faction and Quest Hub**: The Netherwing in Shadowmoon Valley
- **Arena Season 3**: Earn new rewards, updated progression, and more gear from Outland quartermasters.
- **Epic Gems**: Enhance your gear!

The Betrayer awaits. Rally your allies, steel your resolve, and prepare to face Illidan Stormrage within the Black Temple.

## Join the Final Surge of Decor Duels

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_header/uu/UUPD6XOSRR2H1776296285850.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_header/uu/UUPD6XOSRR2H1776296285850.png)

Heed the final call to join Decor Duels before the doors close in this final surge before the Curse of Ula’tek goes live. To help you get the most out of it, we’ve made a few additional changes.

- Hiders will no longer be automatically revealed when thirty seconds remain in the match.
- Flies have been removed from Hiders who stay in one location for too long.
- The energy increase rate for Seekers has been reduced by 20%.
- Players will earn double the rate of Illusionary Coins

Once the dust settles and Decor Duel is no longer available, players will still be able to purchase Decor items from the vendors in Silvermoon City using Voidlight Marl.

These changes will be available after weekly resets.

Learn more from our [previously published article](http://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/news/24271369/).
