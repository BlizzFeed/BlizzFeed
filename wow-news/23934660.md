> Updated April 27: Added additional information on the Embers of Neltharion campaign.

Muster your courage as you continue your adventure through the Dragon Isles and pursue your enemies into the dangers of the Zaralek Cavern. Along the way, dive deeper into the mysteries of the Black Dragonflight, meet new allies and foes, discover treasures and rewards, and more.

---

To reach Zaralek Cavern, complete the “Embers of Neltharion” campaign that will put you onto the path which guides you down beneath the Dragon Isles and into a vast underground world. Entrances to Zaralek Cavern lie within the chasm between Valdrakken and Ohn’ahran Plains and are visible on your map. The journey is long, so be sure your drake is well rested.

#### Meet New Allies

As the heroes of Azeroth venture into the underground expanse of the Zaralek Cavern, they will be aided by and build Renown with the local inhabitants found within.

##### Drogbar

![Drogbar](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/o6/O65P9N4EE3521681248882256.png
)

The familiar faces of the drogbar can be found in Glimmerogg. They now have their own culture and embrace some unique interests—lifting heavy things and snail racing. Players can participate in their own race by nudging along a crystal-shelled gastropod named Brulee to gain the **Zoom!** Achievement and get their own snail pet. Watch out for the slime trail.

##### Niffen

![Niffen](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/p5/P56TKC2O2BN11681248684491.png
)

These busy molefolk might not see much with their inferior eyesight, but with their distinct nose, their sense of smell is mighty. The entrepreneurial and friendly niffen make their home in Loamm and they’re always willing to barter and trade with outsiders who pay them a visit. Players can unlock their Renown tracks for rewards once they complete the first chapter of the Embers of Neltharion campaign.

Once you finish the quest "Six Hundred Feet Under" on any character, concluding the "Breaking Ground" chapter of the Embers of Neltharion campaign, it opens up repeatable content in Zaralek Cavern for other characters on your account.

Drift and dive through complex caverns riddled with stalagmites and stalactites as you and your dragon navigate this vast underground world. Pay attention as your dragon senses six new Glyphs that will help you expand your Dragonriding skills with two new traits.

#### Aberrus, the Shadowed Crucible

![Aberrus](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/mf/MF1EFD7U3MWU1681242277583.png)

> Millennia ago, Neltharion carved chasms in the earth where he conducted world-altering experiments. After his death, these sites were lost to time until now. Forces seeking to claim the Earth-Warder’s legacy raid his dominion in search of powerful artifacts. Now the champions of Azeroth must venture into the darkness and ensure the crucible’s dangerous power doesn’t fall into the wrong hands.

A new raid comes with Embers of Neltharion. Gather your allies and breach Neltharion’s secret laboratory hidden within Zaralek Cavern. Here, he created the dracthyr and ultimately succumbed to the whispers of the Old Gods and their corruption.

Including Scalecommander Sarkareth, whose forces seek to claim the legacy of the dracthyr; players will face nine new encounters:

- Kazzara, the Hellforged
- The Amalgamation Chamber
- The Forgotten Experiments
- Assault of the Zaqali
- Rashok, the Elder
- The Vigilant Steward
- Zskarn, Magmora
- Echo of Neltharion
- Scalecommander Sarkareth
