#### Here are this week's highlights:

**Follower Dungeon Experience Increased**:

- Details were shared on an [increase](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/follower-dungeons/1766975/137) in experience earned from the feature that went live earlier this week.

**Conquest Cap**:

- With next week’s reset, the [Conquest cap](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/conquest-still-capped/1767131/9) will be removed. In addition, Spoils of War will become active, providing increases to Conquest and Honor earned.

**Mythic+ Affix Issue**:

- A hotfix was deployed, so [Afflicted Souls](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/afflicted-die-to-spiders-and-spawn-souls-on-yazma-fight/1768340/10) are no longer treated as players.
