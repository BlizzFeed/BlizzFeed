# Invoke the Cenarion Family in Season 7!

- URL: https://news.blizzard.com/en-gb/article/24117225/invoke-the-cenarion-family-in-season-7
![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/33/3319B80K7QRO1719938773912.png)

> Unlock the new Cenarion family during the Cenarion Festival starting on July 24.

---

Invoke the balance, harmony, and utility of the Cenarion, *Warcraft Rumble’s* newest family. This season, we’re changing things up; the new Cenarion Minis will all be earnable during the Cenarion Festival starting on July 24.

Cenarius and Onu will be available in the G.R.I.D during the first Cenarion week on July 28. The new Cenarion Troops will be available in the G.R.I.D in Season 8.

- **Name:** Cenarius
- **Role:** Leader
- **Family:** Cenarion
- **Deploy Cost:** 5 Gold
- **Health:** 600
- **Healing:** 28 health every 1.5 seconds
- **Damage:** 120
- **Root Duration:** 3 seconds
- **Traits:** Brambles, Tranquility, Elemental, Resistant, Healer

Cenarius exemplifies the Cenarion playstyle, including sustain, control, duality, and defense. By using the AOE Heal for allies and AOE Root for enemies, Cenarius gives his allies time to attack or deal with incoming threats.

##### Leader Ability

- **Grovekeeper**: Cenarion Minis gain 20% empty health.

##### Attack

- **Wrath**: Single target ranged attack.

##### New Traits

- **Brambles**: Enemies are Rooted for 3 seconds when first entering an AOE around Cenarius.
- **Tranquility**: Nearby allies are healed over time.

##### Talents

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_folder_media/jb/JBJO93RZR3L31719867777850.png)](#)

##### Force of Nature

Summon a Treant\* when a Rooted enemy dies.

[![...](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_folder_media/lx/LXPKQXO5P0N71719867813134.png)](#)

##### Revitalize

Allies entering Tranquility trigger an additional heal pulse.

[![...](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_folder_media/97/979CV8IDNK351719867833406.png)](#)

##### Wild Growth

On deploy, Root all enemies on the map for 5 seconds.

\*Treants have 200 health and each Treant deals 50 physical damage every 2 seconds.

- **Name:** Onu
- **Role:** Leader
- **Family:** Cenarion
- **Deploy Cost:** 6 Gold
- **Health:** 3000
- **Damage:** 190
- **Traits:** Safe Arbor, Tank, Vulnerable

Open up new strategies by utilizing Onu’s Safe Arbor during a big push. His slower speed allows supporting Minis to push ahead and protect him, and if he does reach a tower, seize the moment to deploy your arsenal and claim victory.

##### Leader Ability

- **Safe Arbor**: Allow friendly deploys and block enemy deploys near Onu.
  - Persistent deploy zones or an enemy Onu that overlaps with Onu’s Safe Arbor will override the blocking behavior and allow the enemy to deploy Minis.

##### Attack

- **Punch**: Single target melee attack.

##### Talents

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_folder_media/5u/5U7H3NMFE4AP1719868553591.png)](#)

##### Barkskin

Safe Arbor also grants Armor for 8 seconds.

[![...](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_folder_media/kd/KD4YE5W94F3Y1719868573471.png)](#)

##### Petrify

Safe Arbor persists for 8 seconds after death.

[![...](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_folder_media/o6/O6I3LC8IVAM11719868591570.png)](#)

##### From the Trees!

Minis deployed within Safe Arbor deal double damage on first attack.

- **Name:** Ancient of War
- **Role:** Troop
- **Family:** Cenarion
- **Deploy Cost:** Consumes all current Gold
- **Health:** 850 (1 Gold) - 3770 (10 Gold)
- **Damage:** 60 (1 Gold) - 267 (10 Gold)
- **Traits:** Surge, Tank, Armored

Ancient of War is a flexible tank that grows in size and power depending on how much Gold is spent to cast it. It can be utilized in various ways, including a strong push, as a last layer of defense, or as a strong counter for Unbound Minis and Spells using his Lightning Rod talent.

##### Attack

- **Punch**: Single target melee attack.

##### New Trait

- **Surge**: Consumes remaining Gold when deployed.

##### Talents

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_folder_media/em/EM3S9XUJM3UF1719869037386.png)](#)

##### Behemoth

If played for 6 Gold or more, gain Siege damage.

##### Lightning Rod

If played for 4 Gold or more, redirect enemy Unbound deploys to current location.

- **Name:** Moonkin
- **Role:** Troop
- **Family:** Cenarion
- **Deploy Cost:** 4 Gold
- **Health:** 450
- **Moonfire Damage:** 165
- **Hurricane Damage:** 80
- **Traits:** AOE, Elemental

Moonkin is a versatile Cenarion Mini that can act as both a single target and an AOE ranged damage dealer. The alternate attacks will work similarly to Chimaera’s, though they will generally acquire the same target until it dies. Use Moonkin to turn the tides of a battle, adding damage behind a push or to counter a group of enemies.

##### Attacks

- **Moonfire**: Ranged single target attack.
- **Hurricane**: Ranged AOE attack.

##### Talents

##### Moonglow

#### Cenarion Festival

Join in to celebrate the arrival of the Cenarion family to *Warcraft Rumble*. This event will run for 3 weeks, and each will have a new theme and different things to try with the new Cenarion Minis. Unlock legendary rewards including the new Star Tome, which will allow you to add a start point to a Mini.

Meet the new Cenarions! During the first week of the festival, quests will add two Cenarion Minis to your army.

| **Day 1** | **Day 2** | **Day 3** | **Day 4** | **Day 5** | **Day 6** | **Day 7** |

| Cenarius + Moonkin | Bog Beast + Ancient of War | Earth and Moon + Moonkin | Cenarius + Bog Beast | Onu + Faerie Dragon | Faerie Dragon + Ancient of War | Cenarius + Onu |

Week two is the Cenarion combo week, all quest armies will be replaced with dual-family armies.
