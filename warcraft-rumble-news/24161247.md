# Sow the seeds of victory in Pilgrim's Bounty

- URL: https://news.blizzard.com/en-gb/article/24161247/sow-the-seeds-of-victory-in-pilgrim-s-bounty
![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/qb/QBRYY73D3PXN1731969087361.png)

> Pilgrim's Bounty is coming to Warcraft Rumble on November 20, 2024. Earn 2 new Minis, player avatar, cosmetics and more!

---

Pilgrim's Bounty is coming to *Warcraft Rumble* on November 20, 2024. Once the event starts, you can complete challenges to earn tickets and then exchange those tickets for rewards of your choice. Control the moon and the sun with Eclipse and get your sapling squad ready to drop in with the new Treant Minis.

This feast may just G'oble you down instead! Be sure to use tickets before the 2 week long event ends on December 3, 2024, unspent tickets will not carry over and will be converted to Arclight Energy at a 20:1 ratio.

---

#### New Cosmetics

Unlock the new Harvest Avatar and "Roast" Beast Kobold Skin!

#### New Minis

- [Treant](#talents)
- [Eclipse](#talents-1)

![Treant_Statue068.png](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_folder_media/94/94UP3ZBERSUK1731969360150.png)

- **Role**: Troop
- **Family**: Cenarion
- **Deploy Cost**: Consumes all current Gold
- **Traits**: Squad, Armored, Surge, Vulnerable
- **Health**: 200
- **Damage**: 90

##### Talents

- **Composting**: On death, Heal nearby Minis.
- **Propagation**: Summon two extra Treants at your base for every 3 Gold spent on deploy.
- **Uproot**: On death, Root the target for 3 seconds.

![Eclipse_Statue_Talent_SolarFlare.png](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_folder_media/v9/V9DKSKJHATIE1731969445160.png)

- **Role**: Spell
- **Family**: Cenarion
- **Sunfire Deploy Cost**: 3 Gold
- **Moonfire Deploy Cost**: 2 Gold
- **Traits**: AOE, Elemental, Eclipse
- **Sunfire Damage**: 400
- **Moonfire Damage**: 130
- **Moonfire DoT**: 9 damage every 0.5 seconds for 5 seconds

##### Talents

- **Solar Flare**: Sunfire also Burns enemies.
- **Umbral Force**: Moonfire also Stuns enemies for 1 second.
- **Celestial Focus**: Spells gain 2 levels if played immediately after Sunfire or Moonfire.
