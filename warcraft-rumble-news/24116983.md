# Season 7 Patch Notes

- URL: https://news.blizzard.com/en-gb/article/24116983/season-7-patch-notes
![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/pk/PKS6ZBMY9JXS1720552406953.png)

> All features across the game will now reset at the same time! We've also included some Cenarion Mini balance changes, improvements to challenges, improvements to dungeons and new Tomes!

---

#### Season 7

Invoke the new Cenarion family in Season 7 with 5 new Minis! Earn the new Minis, new Emote, the new Star Tome and much more at the Cenarion Festival! [[Learn More](https://news.blizzard.com/warcraftrumble/24117225/)]

#### Cenarion Balance Changes

Since posting our Season 7 article, there have been a few changes and balance updates.

- **Ancient of War**
  - Sapling talent has been updated to "If played for 4 Gold or less, gain 60% attack speed." from "If played for 4 Gold or less, gain 50% increased movement speed."
- **Bog Beast**
  - Health increased to 1100, from 1000.
- **Cenarius**
  - Damage increased to 120, from 110.
  - Heal increased to 28, from 25.
- **Moonkin**
  - AOE damage increased to 80, from 75.
  - Moonfire damage increased to 165, from 140.
  - Moonglow talent has been updated to "Spells gain 2 levels if played immediately after Moonkin." from "After deploying, your next Spell gains 2 levels."

  #### **Events, Siege and Raid**

  - Some exciting new challenges are coming your way!
  - When both players in a raid mission have already earned rewards from that mission, victory will no longer consume one of their 3 assists.

  #### **Dungeons**

  - A forfeit button has been added to the dungeon run screen.
  - Dungeons will now show rewards as already claimed before starting the run if that Leader has already claimed max level dungeon rewards for the week.

  #### **Tomes**
- **New! Star Tome**
  - Star Tomes will be available during the Cenarion Festival and will allow you to add a Star Point to a Mini of your choice.
- **New! Cenarion**
  - Tomes have been added for the new Cenarion family.
- **Mythic Tomes**
  - Mythic Tomes have been updated to include 1 themed page for each family (including Cenarion), 2 Leader pages, and 3 unthemed pages. This adjustment does not change the total number of pages and XP provided.

  #### **User Interface**

  - All features across the game will now reset at the same time, 11:00 a.m. PDT (6:00 p.m. UTC). Changed features include Daily Rewards, the G.R.I.D, Arclight Surge, dungeons, and more.
  - We’ve updated the UI for events and challenges.

  #### **Bug Fixes**

  - Fixed an issue that would cause Arcane Blast to be uncastable after minimizing the game.
  - Windrunner’s Shroud should now properly affect Murk-Eye's Murloc Tidehunters.

  #### **Known Issues**

  - We are aware of an issue affecting the final map for each zone in Heroic Campaign. Players who complete these maps may be unable to claim new Sigils. Claiming the Core reward first should allow Sigils to be claimed afterwards.We're working to address this as soon as possible.
  - Dungeons rewards may show as completed or collected after reset. This is a visual bug and the correct rewards will be displayed once the dungeon is started.

  Thank you all for your feedback. We hear you and the team is working to improve current processes and implement new processes that will allow *Warcraft Rumble* to be updated and hotfixed more frequently.
