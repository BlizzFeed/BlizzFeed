# Claim your Daily Major XP Tome and Coins!

- URL: https://news.blizzard.com/en-gb/article/24113155/claim-your-daily-major-xp-tome-and-coins
![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/xx/XX87ZFJLKA6W1719345015329.png)

> Get 600 Coins instantly, log in every day for the next 30 days to claim your Daily Drop of 1 Major XP Tome and 120 Coins.

---

Azeroth’s Blessing brings Legendary value to *Warcraft Rumble*. After purchase, get 600 Coins instantly, then log in every day for the next 30 days to claim your Daily Drop of 1 Major XP Tome and 120 Coins. You must log in each day before midnight to claim your Daily Drops. Unclaimed Daily Drops are lost.

Azeroth’s Blessing is now available for purchase from the in-game shop!
