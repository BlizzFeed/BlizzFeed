# Summon Swarms of Carrion Beetles with Anub'arak

- URL: https://news.blizzard.com/en-gb/article/24166285/summon-swarms-of-carrion-beetles-with-anub-arak
![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/z4/Z4LD74CQLRIJ1733240756834.png)

> Burrow underground and plague your enemies with swarms of Carrion Beetles using Anub'arak! Unlock Anub'arak and the Winter Veil player avatar during his event from December 14 - December 23, 2024.

---

The Warcraft universe is built on rich and diverse characters that may not fit into any singular family, and because of this, we’ve introduced split Leaders! In Season 10 and Season 11, we will be adding additional Split Leaders, starting with Malfurion Stormrage as Alliance and Cenarion, Anub’arak as Undead and Beast, and Orgrim Doomhammer as Horde and Blackrock.

- [Malfurion Stormrage](https://news.blizzard.com/warcraftrumble/24164437/): December 4, 2024 – December 13, 2024
- Anub’arak: December 14, 2024 – December 23, 2024
- Orgrim Doomhammer: December 24, 2024 – January 2, 2025

Each of these Split Leaders will be available for a limited time. \* During Anub’arak’s 10-day event, you can unlock him as a new Split Leader -- earn additional copies and the items needed to upgrade this powerful new Mini.

---

![Anub_PC_Bundle.png](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_folder_media/z6/Z6UVHEYYYW1S1733848027815.png)

Anub’arak the last king of the nerubian empire of Azjol-Nerub, Leader of the Scourge nerubians and majordomo to the *Lich King* -- is a fearsome foe known to plague his enemies with swarms of Carrion Beetles.

Anub’arak will be available during the event from December 14, 2024, to December 23, 2024. Once the event ends, Anub’arak will continue to be available in G.R.I.D.\*\* until January 2, 2025. The current week’s family will not affect Anub’arak’s G.R.I.D. availability.

- **Name:** Anub'arak
- **Role:** Split Leader
- **Family:** Beast and Undead
- **Deploy Cost:** 6 Gold
- **Health:** 1600
- **Damage:** 190
- **Traits:** Armored, Tank

- **Name:** Carrion Beetle
- **Role:** Troop
- **Family:** Beast and Undead
- **Health:** 100
- **Damage:** Applies Poison on hit.
- **Damage Type:** Elemental
- **Traits:** Elemental, Fast

##### Attack

- **Swipe**: Melee cleave attack.

##### Leader Ability

- **Carrion Feast**: Undead and Beast allies spawn a Carrion Beetle on deploy and death. Limit of 12 active beetles from this Leader ability at a given time.

##### Ability

- **Burrow**: Enter combat by burrowing underground, charging, and then emerging. Cooldown is 8 seconds.

##### Talents

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_folder_media/or/ORJES2H5LKY31733848132687.png)](#)

##### Regenerate

When emerging from a burrow, generate health.\*\*\*

[![...](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/5v/5VJV0EORQHKD1733848209233.png)](#)

##### Explosive Shells

Beetles erupt on death, dealing damage to nearby enemies.

[![...](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/74/744DKZOSOVF21733848231873.png)](#)

##### Trap Door

At 50% health, summon 6 beetles and submerge, avoiding attacks.\*\*\*\*
