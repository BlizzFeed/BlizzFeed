# Patch Notes: Honorable Horde Balance Changes and Bugfixes

- URL: https://news.blizzard.com/en-gb/article/24213949/patch-notes-honorable-horde-balance-changes-and-bugfixes
![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/sj/SJ1WHJ5M4X031722358334326.png)

> We've made some adjustments to Arthas as well as fixed bugs with Thrall, Gnomeregan, and the Honorable Horde event.

---

#### Balance Changes and Bugfixes

##### Leaders

**Arthas Menethil, the Lich King**

- The range for the Death Grip talent has been reduced to 8, from 9.
- Arthas' cost has been increased to 5 Gold, from 4 Gold.

**Charlga**

- Updated her normal attack percentage damage to deal reduced amounts to buildings and bosses, similar to Execute.
- Fixed an issue causing her normal attack percentage damage to not scale with level differences.

**Thrall**

- Updated text on Feral Spirits (Talent) to clarify that it will trigger up to twice per deploy.
- Fixed an issue where Feral Spirits counted as Ranged minis and could be hooked by Abomination/Death Gripped by Arthas.

##### Dungeons

**Gnomeregan**

- Walking Bombs in the Mekgineer Thermaplugg encounter now deal 8% damage, up from 5%.
- Walking Bomb damage also now scales with level differences.

##### Honorable Horde Event

**Challenges**

- Barrens Chat (Play Horde Minis in a single match) requirement lowered to 5 matches per tier (from 5/10/15).
- Clarified that "win with Horde minis" means 4+.
- Added Thrall as an eligible Orc to heal.
- Fixed an issue with Chain Lightning sometimes not counting as a Horde Spell being played.

**Rewards**

- Added an additional 3 star Thrall reward to the bottom of the reward tree (no requirements, 1000 ticket cost).

**Info**

- Added text clarifying that Thrall will be available to use in dungeons for the duration of the event, regardless of family week.

##### Shop

- Fixed issues causing cosmetic skins to not be replaced with coins in offers when already owned. The Honorable Horde event bundles should now become available in the shop again.
