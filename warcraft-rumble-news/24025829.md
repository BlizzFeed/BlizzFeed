#### Learn More About Warcraft Rumble

If you haven’t yet had the chance to get to know *Warcraft Rumble*, don’t worry. We have what you need to start planning your Mini adventures—for big fun.

Set in the beloved Warcraft universe, Warcraft Rumble invites players to build armies with their favorite heroes and villains from *Warcraft* and battle it out in exciting scenarios designed to test their tactical wits.

Build your forces from collections of Minis, starting with your armies’ **Leaders**—which include *Warcraft* notables like the Warsong chieftain Grommash Hellscream and Archmage Jaina Proudmoore—and fill in your rosters with rank-and-file **Troop** minis and powerful **Spells**. Each single-player mission presents its own distinct, strategic puzzle to solve, and players will have to nimbly adapt their tactics in real-time to overcome these frantic challenges.

- Collect over 60 characters from across the Warcraft universe—brought to life as lovingly sculpted tabletop miniatures.
- Triumph through an epic single-player campaign with over 70 missions.
- Work with your Guild to earn seasonal rewards with War Chests.
- Play cooperatively with your friends or put their armies to the test in competitive player-versus-player mode.
- Engage in treacherous dungeons.
- Flex your skills and earn more rewards with the Heroic Campaign.
- Take on the [Arclight Surge](https://news.blizzard.com/warcraftrumble/23859632/) for new challenges and earn more rewards.
- Join a guild, engage in PvP, and much more.

##### *Warcraft Rumble*****Minimum Requirements

Available on iOS and Android.

**iOS**

- iPhone 6s and higher
- iOS 13 and higher
- RAM – 2GB of RAM and higher

**Android**

- CPU - Snapdragon 675 / Exynos 8890 and higher
- GPU - OpenGLES 3.1 and higher
- Adreno 612 / Mali-T880 MP12 and Higher
- Android 5.1 (API level 22) and higher
- RAM - 3GB of RAM and higher
