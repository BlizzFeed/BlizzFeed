# Plant the Seeds of Protection with Malfurion Stormrage

- URL: https://news.blizzard.com/en-gb/article/24164437/plant-the-seeds-of-protection-with-malfurion-stormrage
![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/z4/Z4LD74CQLRIJ1733240756834.png)

> Unlock Malfurion Stormrage between December 4, 2024 and December 13, 2024. This powerful limited-time Mini is the perfect harmony of healing and defense. Shan'do or Shan'don't. There is no try.

---

The Warcraft universe is built on rich and diverse characters that may not fit into any singular family, and because of this, we’ve introduced split Leaders! In Season 10 and Season 11, we will be adding additional Split Leaders, starting with Malfurion Stormrage as Alliance and Cenarion, Anub’arak as Undead and Beast, and Orgrim Doomhammer as Horde and Blackrock.

- Malfurion Stormrage: December 4, 2024 – December 13, 2024
- Anub’arak: December 14, 2024 – December 23, 2024
- Orgrim Doomhammer: December 24, 2024 – January 2, 2025

Each of these Split Leaders will be available for a limited time, \* beginning with Malfurion Stormrage on December 4, 2024. During Malfurion’s 10 day event, you can unlock him as a new Split Leader -- earn additional copies and the items needed to upgrade this powerful new Mini.

---

![Malfurion_PC_Bundle.png](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_folder_media/vi/VI6WII3Z9GR91733240325361.png)

Malfurion Stormrage, first of his name... and the druids, Arch-Druid of Moonglade, co-leader of the Night Elves, beloved partner to Tyrande Whisperwind, brother to Illidan Stormrage and honored Shan’do among his people. While Malfurion was the most dedicated of Cenarius’ disciples, learning druidism and mastering the art of defense and healing, their tact is quite different.

Malfururion will be available during his event from December 4, 2024, to December 13, 2024. Once his event ends, he will continue to be available in G.R.I.D.\*\* until January 2, 2025. The current week’s family will not affect Malfurion’s G.R.I.D. availability.

- **Name:** Malfurion Stormrage
- **Role:** Split Leader
- **Family:** Alliance and Cenarion
- **Deploy Cost:** 4 Gold
- **Health:** 1240
- **Damage:** 190
- **Traits:** Elemental, Healer, Seeds of Protection

##### Attack

- **Wrath**: Single target ranged elemental attack.

##### Leader Ability

- **Regeneration**: Cenarion and Alliance allies receive a heal over time at 30% health and Tank Minis gain extra protection.

##### New Trait

- **Seeds of Protection**: Protect a nearby building or Meeting Stone from the next 5 attacks. Ability has one charge.

##### Talents

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_folder_media/ha/HA63OXV2PK6V1733240376478.png)](#)

##### Natural Salve

Nearby Poison damage heals Malfurion.

[![...](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_folder_media/a1/A1WFSQD2IXMS1733240414162.png)](#)

##### Deep Slumber

Deploy asleep and stationary. Awaken once attacked.

[![...](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_folder_media/zt/ZT4EQREJJKA51733240443289.png)](#)

##### Germinate

After 30 seconds, if at least one Seed of Protection remains, apply Seeds of Protection to all allied buildings and Meeting Stones.

![Malfurion_Statue_Pose.png](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/2i/2IXGE6HPNXGF1733240530745.png)
