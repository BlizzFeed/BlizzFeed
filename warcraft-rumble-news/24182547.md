Ironforge, carved from the mountains of Dun Morogh in the central Eastern Kingdoms and named for the Great Forge that burns at the heart of the city, is the home of the dwarves. This bustling capital city is one of Azeroth's oldest and largest cities and ancestral home to the Bronzebeard Clan. This hidden gem boasts massive stone passageways carved deep into the stone, an ornate Throne Room, and an underground Deeprun Tram that connects Ironforge's Tinker Town to Stormwind City's Dwarven district.

#### Bosses and Rewards

Players will earn rewards in the "First Time Heroic Rewards" tab the first time they defeat a boss on Heroic difficulty. The next time Ironforge Siege is available, those players who have previously defeated a boss on Heroic difficulty will receive rewards in the "Repeat Heroic Rewards" tab for defeating them again. Normal difficulty must be cleared before Heroic difficulty will unlock.
