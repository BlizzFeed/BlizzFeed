Blingtron, that lovable little bucket of bolts, is at it again, giving out sweet rewards to everyone who logs in. Beginning on February 12, 2025, log in daily to claim tickets\* and then exchange those tickets for a lovely reward. Be sure to tap the island and spend tickets before the event ends on March 3, 2025.
