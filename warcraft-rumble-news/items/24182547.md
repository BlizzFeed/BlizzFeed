# Claim Epic Rewards in Normal and Heroic Ironforge Siege

- Date: 2025-04-16T17:00:00Z

Ironforge, carved from the mountains of Dun Morogh in the central Eastern Kingdoms and named for the Great Forge that burns at the heart of the city, is the home of the dwarves. This hidden gem boasts massive stone passageways carved deep into the stone, an ornate Throne Room, and an underground Deeprun Tram that connects Ironforge's Tinker Town to Stormwind City's Dwarven district. Prepare to lay siege to Ironforge and claim victory in the new Normal and Heroic difficulties to earn sparkling gold skins and epic rewards!
