A dead Mini is a useless Mini. Right? RIGHT?! Priestess is a powerful healing and support focused Mini that can keep high health Minis alive, but does minimal damage. Priestess will be available during her event from February 26, 2025, to March 11, 2025. During the 2 week event, you can unlock her as a new Troop -- earn additional copies and the items needed to upgrade her.
