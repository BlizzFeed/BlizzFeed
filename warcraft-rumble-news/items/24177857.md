Blingtron, that lovable little bucket of bolts, is at it again, giving out sweet rewards to everyone who logs in. Beginning on February 12, 2025, log in daily to claim tickets and then exchange those tickets for a reward of your choice. Some of the rewards will include Tomes, Coins, Tinker Gems, Troops of your choice and more!
