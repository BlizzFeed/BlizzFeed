# Complete Community Challenges to Earn Rewards!

- URL: https://news.blizzard.com/en-gb/article/24166078/complete-community-challenges-to-earn-rewards
![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/g3/G3HPSO5MFPN41736470766078.png)

> Work together as a community to complete three weeks of challenges and unlock rewards everyone can enjoy!

---

Work together as a community to complete three weeks of challenges. If the community completes the challenge, everyone gets the prize!\* Cheer on your friends and get ready to Rumble, it all starts this week!

#### Week 1: Upgrade Those Minis!

From January 15 to January 22, complete 500 unit upgrades as a community and everyone gets 500 Coins!

![WR_Coins_sack_2.png](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/YNN738UO6DB61736470684008.png)

#### Week 2: Fight Amongst Yourselves!

From January 22 to January 29, complete 1000 PvP matches as a community and everyone gets a Mega Leader Tome!

![WR_Tome_Leader_Mega.png](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/2212WQAYQG641736470684077.png)

#### Week 3: Dominate the Dungeons!

From January 29 to February 5, complete 1000 Dungeon battles and everyone gets an Epic Core!

![WR_Core_Epic_tilted.png](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/YUTWOKXC64IA1736470684041.png)

\* *If the challenge is completed within the time period of that challenge, all Warcraft Rumble active players during that challenge period will receive the corresponding reward, regardless of whether they directly participated in the challenge. For purposes of these challenges, "active players" means players who logged in at any point during the challenge period and took any game actions (completed a mission, collected rewards, upgraded a mini, etc.). Rewards will be directly granted in-game, no later than two weeks after the end of the challenge period.*
