# Patch Notes: Balance Changes

- URL: https://news.blizzard.com/en-gb/article/24167121/patch-notes-balance-changes
![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/sj/SJ1WHJ5M4X031722358334326.png)

> We’ve made some balance adjustments to Ragnaros, Ancient of War, Earth and Moon, Faerie Dragon and more. We’ve also adjusted the difficulty for Missions in Moonglade and the Winter Veil – Sleighing Season Quest.

---

The following changes will go live in an update that is expected to go out on December 16, 2024.

#### Leaders

**Ragnaros**

- Ragnaros will now gain the effect of his Molten Armor Leader ability, not just Armored Minis. Molten Armor reflects damage back to the attacker.
- Blast Wave will now cast around Ragnaros as well as the deploy location when used.
- The Sons of the Flame talent now lasts 12 seconds, up from 8 seconds.

#### Troops

**Ancient of War**

- Adjusted Ancient of War’s power scaling to be more comprable to Molten Giant.
- Increased the attack speed of Saplings spawned with the Sapling talent to 100%, up from 60%.
- We’ve removed the Gold requirement from the Behemoth talent.

**Arcane Blast**

- The Torrent talent requirement to level up has been reduced from 4 to 3. The talent now reads, “Gain a level after casting rank 3.”

**Blizzard**

- The damage bonus from the Brittle Ice talent has been increased to 50%, from 30%.

**Bog Beast**

- We’ve increased the healing bonus to the Flourish talent to 50%, from 30%.
- We’ve increased the armor duration from the Living Wood talent to 30%, from 50%. Talent now reads, “Gain Armour until 30% health.”
- The Rampant Growth talent will now trigger at 50% health and on death, instead of just on death.

**Earth and Moon**

- Starfall deploy cost has been reduced to 5 Gold, down from 6 Gold.
- Starfall damage has been reduced by 10%.

**Faerie Dragon**

- Speed has been reduced from Fast to Medium.

**Harvest Golem**

- The Trojan Chickens talent will now spawn 5 chickens on death, up from 4.
- The heal applied by the Bountiful Harvest talent has been increased by 25%.

**Maiev**

- Deploy cost has been reduced to 5 Gold, down from 6 Gold.

**Moonkin**

- Deploy cost has been reduced to 3 Gold, down from 4 Gold.
- Health has been reduced by 15%.
- Damage has been reduced by 15%.

#### Missions

**Moonglade**

- Reduced the Gold income for opponents in Heroic Missions and Quests.

#### Quests

**Winter Veil - Sleighing Season**

- Reduced the health of Drakes and Polar Bears by 20%.
