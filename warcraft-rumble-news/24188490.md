# Difficulty Adjustments for the Lich King Event

- URL: https://news.blizzard.com/en-gb/article/24188490/difficulty-adjustments-for-the-lich-king-event
![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/qp/QPZLI2BLKQYA1720496694773.png)

> We've reduced enemy income on all difficulties, reduced the level of Normal Missions by 1, and reduced the difficulty of Heroic Pit of Saron.

---

We've reduced enemy income on all difficulties, reduced the level of Normal Missions by 1, and reduced the difficulty of Heroic Pit of Saron.

In addition to the difficulty changes, we will be extending the Lich King event until April 15, 2025. Challenge and unlock the Lich King before it's too late!\
[[Learn More](https://news.blizzard.com/article/24177510/)]

---

##### All Difficulties

**All Maps**

- Reduced the income rate for enemies on all maps by approximately 8%. This change should reduce the pace at which enemies play Minis.

##### Normal Difficulty

- Reduced the level of all Missions by 1.
  - *Developer Note: We want Normal difficulty to be largely accessible and fun to all players. It is not our intention for Normal to be a grueling challenge.*

**Lower Spire**

- Surprise assaults on player Barracks (Base) have been signigicantly reduced.
  - The number of Skeleton Warriors has been reduced to 3.

##### Heroic Difficulty

**Pit of Saron**

- Reduced the spawn rate of Winter Storms.
- Lil Gish's Health has been reduced.
- Reduced the spawn rate of the Abomination.

##### Mythic Difficulty

**Pit of Saron**

- Lil Gish's Health has been reduced.
