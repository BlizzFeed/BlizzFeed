# Hotfix: April 1, 2025

- URL: https://news.blizzard.com/en-gb/article/24188491/hotfix-april-1-2025
![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/m3/M3PPZ7R159MS1711042752424.png)

> Tap the shark in Booty Bay for a jaws-ome surprise! We've fixed issues with Polymorph and the Lich King's Soulshatter ability that would cause the game to crash.

---

##### General

- Tap the shark in Booty Bay for a jaws-ome surprise.

##### Bugfixes

- Fixed an issue that would cause the game to crash when a Mini was Polymorphed during Orgrim Doomhammer's Mak'gora.
- Fixed an issue that would cause the game to crash when Soulshatter (Leader Ability) killed a Poisoned Mini, while the Lich King was under the effect of the Purgatory talent.

##### Known Issues

- This morning we discovered what looked likc C'luc creating chaos in the Rumble code. The team was quick to hop into action but players may still encounter some hare-y situations.
