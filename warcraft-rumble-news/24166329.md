# Defend and Support Allies with the Ferocity of a Bear!

- URL: https://news.blizzard.com/en-gb/article/24166329/defend-and-support-allies-with-the-ferocity-of-a-bear
![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/bs/BSTUYUQU7LRF1736282230096.png)

> Defend and support with the ferocity of a bear! Unlock the new shapeshifting Druid of the Claw Mini, during the Winds of Change event, starting on January 8, 2024. When times are tough, it's time to get tough!

---

Unlock the new shapeshifting Druid of the Claw Mini during the Winds of Change event starting on January 8, 2025. Druid of the Claw will defend and support your allies with the ferocity of a bear. When times are tough, it's time to get tough!

Druid of the Claw will be available during the event from January 8, 2025, to January 21, 2025. During the 2 week event, you can unlock him as a new Troop -- earn additional copies and the items needed to upgrade this powerful new Mini.

---

![DruidOfClaw_Statue025.png](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_folder_media/ee/EE0G6Q92EJJG1736283174714.png)

- **Name:** Druid of the Claw
- **Role:** Troop
- **Family:** Cenarion
- **Deploy Cost:** 3 Gold
- **Health:** 650
- **Damage:** 200
- **Traits:** Elemental, Armored, Shapeshift

- **Bear Form**
- **Health:** 1300
- **Damage:** 120
- **Damage Range:** Melee

##### Attack

- **Sunfire (Night Elf Form)**: Single target ranged elemental attack.
- **Swipe (Bear Form)**: Melee cleave.

##### New Trait

- **Shapeshift**: Morphs into an alternate form at 50% health.

##### Talents

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_folder_media/ac/AC4IAQLODG4W1736282313106.png)](#)

##### Regrowth

When entering Bear form, heal 20% health over 5 seconds.

[![...](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_folder_media/6e/6EWJ2UUW9T5V1736282496211.png)](#)

##### Rejuvenation

Night Elf form gains the ability to apply a heal over time to an ally.

[![...](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_folder_media/tl/TLN8GRK90CX41736282536818.png)](#)

##### Leader of the Pack

Bear Form grants a 15% damage boost to nearby allies.
