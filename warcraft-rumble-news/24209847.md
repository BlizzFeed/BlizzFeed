Blood and Thunder... and Chain Lightning? The Honorable Horde has taken over Season 15 and Thrall is ready for the challenge. Don't mistake Thrall's warm and wise demeanor for weakness, he will rain elemental fury upon his enemies.

Only the bravest heroes dare to strike the Horde where it hurts them most. Respectfully challenge Thrall at the gates of Orgrimmar to unlock him as a new Horde Leader!

#### Challenge the Honorable Horde

On June 11, step up to the gates of Orgrimmar and challenge the Honorable Horde! Defeat Thrall and a special guest in a series of 3 challenging maps with increasing difficulty.

During the 3 week event, you can complete challenges and missions to unlock Thrall as a new Horde Leader--earn additional copies and the items needed to upgrade this powerful new Mini. Thrall will not be available in the G.R.I.D until the start of Season 16.

#### Thrall

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_folder_media/JOJ16CI40QZW1748993402548.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_folder_media/JOJ16CI40QZW1748993402548.png)

Thrall, Go'el, son of Durotan and Draka, Warchief to the Horde, breaker of chains, shaman of the Earthen Ring, and victim of Gul'dan's cruelty. Do not mistake Thrall's warm heart and wise counsel for weakness—for within him stirs the fury of the elements, ready to be unleashed upon those who threaten peace or justice. Though his tale begins in the shadows of cruelty, captivity, and ruthless conditioning, Thrall rises beyond his pain. His journey is a testament to the strength found in honor, the power of redemption, and the wisdom gained from both our own missteps and the failings of those who came before us. Welcome to Thrall's ORC talk.

Not long after his birth, the orc child known as Thrall was marked by tragedy. Under the dark command of Gul'dan, assassins were dispatched to slay his parents, Durotan and Draka, leaving the newborn orphaned and abandoned in the wilds. Fate, however, had other plans. The infant was discovered by Aedelas Blackmoore, a cunning human who saw in the orcling a weapon to be forged.

Raised within the cold, stone walls of Durnholde Keep, Thrall was taught the ways of man—how to read, to write, and to sharpen his mind as much as his blade. Under Blackmoore's cruel tutelage, he was trained to become a warrior of unmatched ferocity. In the arenas, Thrall rose as an undefeated gladiator, feared and revered. Yet even in such grim confines, he found an unlikely light: Taretha Foxton, a kind-hearted human girl who became his dearest friend and taught him the true meaning of honor and compassion.

With Taretha's help, Thrall eventually escaped his captors and the prison that was Durnholde. Blackmoore scoured the land, seeking his lost weapon, but Thrall vanished like a shadow into the wild. Beyond the reach of his tormentors, he embarked on a new quest—to free his people. The orcs, broken and caged in internment camps, needed a leader. In liberating them, Thrall became a beacon of hope, a symbol of strength. With every camp he freed, the Horde was reborn.

Returning to Durnholde once more—not as a prisoner, but as a liberator—Thrall freed the orcs still imprisoned there. Durnholde would become the heart of the new Horde, their sanctuary through the end of the Second War.

One night, a vision came to Thrall—a mysterious prophet cloaked in shadow appeared in his dreams, urging him to lead his people westward to the continent of Kalimdor, where their destiny awaited. Upon arriving, Thrall encountered the tauren, proud and noble warriors led by Cairne Bloodhoof. A powerful alliance was forged, built on mutual respect and shared survival. The land they claimed would come to be named Durotar in honor of Thrall's fallen father.

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_folder_media/I9YAN8SGIVSH1748992762977.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_folder_media/I9YAN8SGIVSH1748992762977.png)

But Thrall's destiny did not end there.

He freed the Darkspear trolls and welcomed them into the Horde. He brokered pacts with the Forsaken of Lordaeron and the Blood Elves of Quel'Thalas. After the fiery chaos of the Third War, it was Thrall who worked to foster peace between the Horde and the Alliance, striving to end centuries of bloodshed.

When the frozen terror of the Lich King began to seep in from Northrend, Thrall was forced to act. Though reluctant, he sent an expedition led by Garrosh Hellscream to combat the Scourge. Meanwhile, he led the assault to reclaim the Undercity from the dread demon Varimathras, unaware that King Varian Wrynn had launched his own attack. The Battle for the Undercity saw the Alliance and Horde clash in a brutal confrontation—until Jaina Proudmoore intervened, teleporting the Alliance forces back to Stormwind.

Thrall's reign as Warchief was marked by both triumph and turmoil. When the Cataclysm shattered Azeroth, he chose to lay down his mantle. He named Garrosh Hellscream as the Horde's new Warchief and turned his focus to the Earthen Ring, joining fellow shamans in their desperate battle against Deathwing the Destroyer.

In a final, cataclysmic conflict, Thrall stood beside the Dragon Aspects as the ancient artifact known as the Dragon Soul—retrieved through time itself by valiant heroes—was unleashed. Thrall and the Dragon Aspects were able to use the Dragon Soul against Deathwing the Destroyer during a brutal battle across Azeroth's skies, and ending in the Maelstrom at the center of the world.

Thrall, a slave to his name no longer, embraced his destiny under the name his parents had bestowed upon him: Go'el.

Though no longer Warchief, Thrall remains a legendary figure—a shaman of immense power, a leader of unwavering resolve, and a symbol of the Horde's unbreakable spirit.

**Name:** Thrall\
**Role:** Leader\
**Family:** Horde

##### Stats\*

**New Horde Leader: Thrall**

- **Deploy Cost:** 4 Gold
- **Health:** 1500
- **Melee Damage:** 180
- **Chain Lightning Damage:** 125
- **Range:** Melee and Ranged
- **Movement Speed:** Medium
- **Traits:** Detect, Elemental, Tank

**Spirit Wolves**

- **Health:** 700
- **Damage:** 100
- **Range:** Melee

##### Attack

- **Chop**: Single target melee axe swing.
- **Chain Lightning**: After attacking twice with his axe, Thrall casts a bolt of lightning that strikes one target and then arcs to 2 other nearby enemies. This attack can hit flying enemies, towers, and Barracks (Bases).

##### Ability

- **Far Sight**: Can attack Stealth enemies.

##### Leader Ability

- **Warchief's Presence**: All nearby Horde and Elemental allies get +20% damage and Resistance. This ability has a radius of 6.

##### Talents\*\*

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_folder_media/4TG4VTU6MWQX1749054031572.png)](#)

##### Call of the Horde

Gain Bloodlust for 5 seconds and pulses lightning whenever a Horde ally is deployed.

[![...](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_folder_media/L0X8LRGBQEEP1749054031578.png)](#)

##### Feral Spirit

When Thrall's health drops to 50%, he summons 2 Spirit Wolves to fight by his side. This can trigger twice per deploy.

[![...](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_folder_media/XNF1JSB8VAPF1749054031530.png)](#)

##### Totemic Mastery

When deployed Thrall summons a totem as his location and a new one every 10 seconds. Each totem lasts for 10 seconds.

**Totems**

- **Healing Tide**: Heals allies map-wide over time, approximately 25 health per second.
- **Earthbind**: Slows enemies map-wide by 25%.
- **Searing**: Casts a fire missile per second at the nearest enemy. Each fire missile deals 25 damage.
- **Windfury**: Increases the attack speed of allies map-wide by 25%.

\

\*Mini stats, attack, abilities and talents may change before and after its initial release.\
\*\*Talents will continue to be available in G.R.I.D after the event ends.
