# Collect the New Split Leaders and Cosmetics During Winter Veil

- URL: https://news.blizzard.com/en-gb/article/24163624/collect-the-new-split-leaders-and-cosmetics-during-winter-veil
![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/vk/VK5RUJ4EF6AC1733154609840.png)

> Winter Veil is coming to Warcraft Rumble on December 4, 2024. Collect the new Split Leaders, Winter Veil player avatar, Jinglebold Kobold skin and more!

---

Winter Veil is coming to *Warcraft Rumble* on December 4, 2024. Once the event starts, you can complete challenges to earn tickets and then exchange those tickets for rewards of your choice. New Cosmetics will be available during the event, including a Winter Veil player avatar, Jinglebold Kobold Skin and more!

Winter Veil will run for 30 days, featuring a new split leader and reward tree every 10 days. Malfurion Stormrage followed by Anub’arak and Orgrim Doomhammer.

- Malfurion Stormrage: December 4, 2024 – December 13, 2024
- Anub’arak: December 14, 2024 – December 23, 2024
- Orgrim Doomhammer: December 24, 2024 – January 2, 2025

Tickets will be cashed out between reward trees. Be sure to use tickets before each 10-day event ends; unspent tickets will not carry over and will be converted to Arclight Energy at a 20:1 ratio.

During the event, collect Gifts and be sure to defeat the Rocket T.R.E.E. to send it off to its next destination to spread holiday cheer!

#### Open Queue

- Players will now have the option to be matched up with players outside of their Guild for Siege and Raids.

#### Arclight Booster

- We’ve added the ability to trial the Arclight Booster before purchase.
- We’ve added new profile flair for those who have the Arclight Booster.
- We’ve updated the animations and added more detailed descriptions about how the Arclight Booster works to the shop.
- We’ve added a new feature to player profiles to show how much they’ve earned using the Arclight Booster. This total will start from 0 and will not be retroactive.

#### Bug Fixes and Optimizations

- A pop up will now notify players when they need to update their version of the game so that they match with their partner. Being on different versions of the game can prevent players from being able to queue for a Siege or Raid together.
- Players can now tap to skip during the boss death animation at the end of a game.
- Minis should now stop attacking after a boss dies.
- We’ve improved load times and added a loading notification to the Shop tab.
- Events tab will now sort challenges that are ready to claim at the top.
- Raids and Sieges are now uncapped, players can assist friends as many times as they like.
- We’ve removed dynamic Quest difficulty scaling for Winter Veil and Arclight Surge. Quests should no longer become more difficult the more you complete.
- Japanese will no longer be searchable in the Guild Search feature.
- We’ve removed talent poses for all Minis, they will now use their base pose instead.

*Developers Note: We’ve decided to remove unique talent statues from our game. After gathering player feedback, we found that while these statues are a nice touch, they aren’t tied to core gameplay mechanics and aren’t essential to the experience. Additionally, we already use icons to communicate which talent is equipped, and we’ll emphasize these icons even more in a future update to make it easy to see at a glance which talents are equipped in your army. Although it’s bittersweet to let these go (we’ve had so much fun creating them!), this change brings several exciting benefits:*

- *Smaller download sizes, both for initial installs and updates.*
- *Increased capacity to develop more new Minis.*
- *Greater flexibility for Mini adjustments.*

 *We’re very excited about the opportunities this will create for the game moving forward!*

The Open Queue, Arclight Booster, and the Bug Fixes and Optimizations are currently scheduled for our next update.
