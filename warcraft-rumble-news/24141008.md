# Enter the Emerald Dream in Season 9

- URL: https://news.blizzard.com/en-gb/article/24141008/enter-the-emerald-dream-in-season-9
![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/ay/AY9BRT0CQ0UM1727282125136.png)

> Unlock Ysera, the new Cenarion Leader and upgrade her during the Ysera event! Don’t miss Gadget Bonanza, Hallows End, a new Siege and more coming in Season 9!

---

Gadget Bonanza is coming to Warcraft Rumble! Starting on September 26, complete challenges to earn Shinies then exchange Shinies for Coins, Big Red Buttons, Leader Tomes, Star Tomes and more! Be sure to claim your rewards before the event ends on September 29.

---

Enter the Emerald Dream with Ysera, *Warcraft Rumble’s* newest Cenarion Leader. Ysera is the Cenarion family's third Leader and is quite unique. She sends aid while slumbering in the Emerald Dream by adding 4 new Minis to your army.

Ysera won’t be available in the G.R.I.D until Season 10, but don’t worry; you can add her to your collection of Minis through the Ysera event, starting on October 2.

---

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_folder_media/za/ZAAVVKF21KZ91727280738722.png)](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_folder_media/za/ZAAVVKF21KZ91727280738722.png)

- **Name:** Ysera
- **Role:** Leader
- **Family:** Cenarion
- **Traits:** Emerald Dream

##### Leader Ability

- **Emerald Dream**: Stuck in the Emerald Dream, Ysera cannot be played, but adds unique Emerald Minis into your army.

##### Emerald Minis

- **Name:** Innervate
- **Role:** Spell
- **Family:** Cenarion
- **Deploy Cost:** 3 Gold
- **Traits:** Emerald Dream
- **Description:** Generate 4 Bonus Gold over the next 10 seconds.

- **Name:** Emerald Whelps
- **Role:** Troop
- **Family:** Cenarion
- **Deploy Cost:** 2 Gold
- **Health:** 130
- **Damage:** 110, plus 1 stack of Poison.
- **Traits:** Poisonous, Elemental, Emerald Dream
- **Description:** A menacing trio of Poisonous green Whelps.

- **Name:** Hibernate
- **Role:** Spell
- **Family:** Cenarion
- **Deploy Cost:** 2 Gold
- **Radius:** 8
- **Traits:** Emerald Dream
- **Description:** Puts enemies to sleep, preventing them from moving or attacking for 8 seconds. Taking damage will wake them.

- **Name:** Emerald Drake
- **Role:** Troop
- **Family:** Cenarion
- **Deploy Cost:** 3 Gold
- **Health:** 340
- **Damage:** 64 per tick, 5 ticks per breath attack. Applies 3 stacks of Poison per breath, one stack at tick 1, 3 and 5.
- **Traits:** Poisonous, Elemental, Emerald Dream
- **Description:** A fearsome green Drake protects the skies of the Emerald Dream, raining Poison down on its foes.

##### Recurring Dream

Ysera Minis are twice as likely to show up in hand.

[![...](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/xu/XUUAZJEYPC5K1727280845817.png)](#)

##### Shared Dream

Hibernate applies to all enemies on the map.

[![...](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/11/115A986UU02G1727280875306.png)](#)

##### Corrupted Dream

Emerald Whelps and Emerald drake apply twice as much Poison.

#### Ysera Mini Event

Starting on October 2, complete challenges to earn Tickets then exchange those Tickets for Cenarion Tomes, Ysera Stars, G.R.I.D Overrides and more! Be sure to claim your rewards before the event ends on October 15.

---

#### New Emotes

The Angry Chicken Emote will be available as a PvP reward and the Banshee Scream Emote will be earnable through the guild War Chest.

#### Coming Later in Season 9

**Hallows End**

The Headless Horseman will rise in Season 9, will you survive? Complete challenges and claim batty rewards including Tomes, new Minis, cosmetics, and more!

 **New Siege**

Want to lay siege to the city of Ironforge? Defeat dwarf assassins in the vault, face the mechanical marvel of Mekkatorque in the Deeprun Tram, and forge on into the heavily defended throne room to face Magni Bronzebeard.
