# Season 13 is Going to be Heroic!

- URL: https://news.blizzard.com/en-gb/article/24177511/season-13-is-going-to-be-heroic
![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/yq/YQ4702BKCBP51741111783613.png)

> Season 13 is coming, bringing Arthas Menethil, the Lich King, as a new Alliance and Undead Split Leader. We've also added Army Loadouts, Leader Skins, Normal and Heroic difficulty Siege, and an avalanche of balance changes. Arthas will be the first Leader to receive skins. Alongside Arthas, we have plans for additional Mini skins that will be coming soon, some sooner than others.

#### Arthas Menethil, the Lich King

![ArthasLeader_Statue045_Edit.png](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_folder_media/5BX6KQH4PFP11739923486124.png)

Take to the frozen wastes of Icecrown and battle Arthas at Icecrown Citadel. Defeat him and his minions in a series of 3 challenging maps with increasing difficulty to unlock this new Alliance and Undead Split Leader.

During the 2 week event, you can unlock Arthas as a new Alliance and Undead Split Leader -- earn additional copies, skins and the items needed to upgrade this powerful new Mini.\
[[Learn More](https://news.blizzard.com/article/24177510/)]

#### Army Loadouts

Army Loadouts are here! Each Leader can have up to 3 different army, talent, and cosmetic loadout configurations that can easily be swapped between at the press of a button. Unlock the second loadout at Sigil 40 and the third loadout at Sigil 50!

Want to use your friend or guildmates loadout? No problem! Copy and import loadout codes to share with others in Guild chat or to use on a different Leader of your own! To make sharing easy, we've added the ability to copy Guild chat messages, just tap the message to copy. When using these codes, any unowned Minis or talents will not be equipped.

#### Season 13 Patch Notes

We have resolved several issues, including Ragnaros' Blast Wave, which was not dealing damage or applying Burn to structures. Additionally, we have made adjustments to Leaders, Troops, and Missions. We also addressed an issue with Frostwolf Shaman in Player versus Player.

These changes will be going live at the start of Season 13 on March 19, 2025. For a complete list of balance changes and bugfixes, please check out our Patch Notes [article.](https://news.blizzard.com/article/24182549/)
