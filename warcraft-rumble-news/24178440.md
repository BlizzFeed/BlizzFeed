---

Priestess is a powerful healing and support focused Mini that can keep high health Minis alive, but does minimal damage. She is based on Holy and Discipline priests from the early expansions of *World of Warcraft*, mastering restorative magic instead of the destructive power of the shadows. A dead Mini is a useless Mini. Right? RIGHT?!

Priestess will be available during her event from February 26, 2025, to March 11, 2025. During the 2 week event, you can unlock her as a new Troop -- earn additional copies and the items needed to upgrade this powerful new Mini.

---

![Priestess_Statue_Edit.png](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/lq/LQI7CC5967ZU1739224377377.png)

**Name:** Priestess\
**Role:** Troop\
**Family:** Alliance

##### Stats

- **Deploy Cost:** 2 Gold
- **Health:** 300
- **Heal:** 200
- **Heal Cooldown:** 6 Seconds
- **Range:** 8
- **Movement Speed:** Medium
- **Damage:** 50 Elemental Damage
- **Traits:** Healer, Elemental, Cycle

##### Attack

- **Smite**: A ranged damaging attack. The priestess casts for a moment and then a small, weak, holy missile travels towards the enemy.

##### Ability

- **Greater Heal**: A strong heal ability that only heals one target at a time. Prioritizes allies with the most health missing.

##### Ability

- **Renew**: Priestess periodically places a heal over time on a nearby ally. This ability has higher priority than Greater Heal. Ability cooldown and Renew duration will allow approximatly 3 allies to have a Renew on them at any given moment.

##### Talents

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/GR99OUDN4JJ51739223904592.png)](#)

##### Power Word: Shield

Periodically apply a shield to an ally, absorbing all damage for 2 seconds.

[![...](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/A1JTUQ1K60US1739223904254.png)](#)

##### Empowered Renew

Casting Renew on an ally also heals them for 10% of their total health instantly.

[![...](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/S0FN3JM6EV2O1739223904362.png)](#)

##### Spirit of Redemption

On death, summon a Spirit of Redemption who heals nearby allies for 5 seconds.
