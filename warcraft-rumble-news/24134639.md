# Season 9 Patch Notes

- URL: https://news.blizzard.com/en-gb/article/24134639/season-9-patch-notes
![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/sj/SJ1WHJ5M4X031722358334326.png)

> Improvements to G.R.I.D, the tutorial and performance. We’ve added new G.R.I.D Overrides and included bug fixes for Moonkin’s Armoured trait and many more!

---

#### Season 9

Check out everything coming to Season 9, including the new Ysera Mini, Ysera event, Gadget Bonanza, the Hallow’s End event, a new Siege and more! [[Learn More](https://news.blizzard.com/warcraftrumble/24141008/)]

#### Bug Fixes

General

- Fixed a bug that would cause Unbound and Spell Minis to not work as intended in PvP.
- Moonkin should now be usable in Armoured upgraded slots.
- Fixed a bug that caused the game to crash when claiming the Mission reward before the zone reward in Heroic Missions.
- Completing Arclight Surge should now count towards the “Win a Battle with Unique Leaders” Challenge.
- Onu and Ancient of War visual effects should no longer linger on maps after they have died.
- Witch Doctor’s beam should no longer extend across the map when attacking Brightwing before she teleports.
- Heroic Mission reward indicators should now be displayed properly on the world map.
- Your partner’s spells should no longer level up your Bloodmage Thalnos in Siege and Raid.
- Fixed a bug that would cause the incorrect cost for Minis to be displayed for Charlga’s Spirit Tangle.
- Fixed a visual bug that would cause Coins acquired during events to not be immediately updated in currency, the event reward tree, claim window pop up and Arclight Booster bonus.
- Fixed a visual bug that would cause the level up effect to appear on Bloodmage Thalnos while polymorphed, even when he does not gain a level.

Talents

- S.A.F.E Pilot should now deal damage on impact to Ancient of War when Ancient of War is using the Lightning Rod talent.
- Fixed a bug that would cause Ancient of War to not be affected by Onu’s From the Trees! talent.
- Onu’s Barkskin talent should now correctly apply to S.A.F.E Pilot.
- Fixed a bug that would cause Deep Breath with the Double Dragon talent to not trigger Charlga’s Sprit Passage.
- Fixed a bug that would cause Witch Doctor’s Spirt Ward shield to not disappear on certain flying Minis after they take a hit.

Dungeons

- Fixed a bug that would sometimes cause Willful Instruction to decrease multiple Undead Mini’s cost by 1. It should only decrease the next Undead Mini by 1 Gold.
- Fixed an issue with Incendiary Rounds not properly applying to S.A.F.E Pilot.

#### Improvements and Optimizations

G.R.I.D

- Minis will now display the current/max Star Points for each Mini.
- Upgrade arrows are now animated to make them more visible.
- Leader crowns will now only display on Leaders that you do not own.
- A glitter effect has been added to Minis that will unlock on purchase.
- Max Star Minis should show up less often.

Performance

- Slightly reduced load times when switching between features and screens.

Tutorial

- We’ve updated the map for a better player experience.
- New Gnomelia pop ups have been added with animated information to improve clarity on gameplay, talents, upgrades and much more!
- New visuals and arrows have been added to the UI and maps to highlight what Gnomelia is talking about. Gnolls before Trolls as Gnomelia would say.
- We’ve reduced the number of Minis that players are forced to play, allowing new players to experiment and learn for themselves.
- Sigil Road has been updated allowing players to dive into the game and play Arclight Surge, Blingtron’s Bounties, Darkmoon Faire, Raids and Siege sooner.

#### Unlinked Accounts

Players who received a Connection Error and were unable to login to their Guest Account/Unlinked Account should now be able to login to that account instead of being stuck in a Connection Error loop. Please note, Guest Accounts/Unlinked Accounts are saved locally on your device.
