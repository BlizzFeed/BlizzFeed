> Collect the first playable split Leader, Sylvanas Windrunner, during a special event in Warcraft Rumble beginning Novermber 13.

---

Collect the first playable split Leader, Sylvanas Windrunner, during a special event in *Warcraft Rumble* beginning Novermber 13.

During the event, players will complete Challenges to earn Tickets and use those Tickets to unlock rewards that can be used to upgrade Sylvanas Windrunner.

![WR_Sylvanas_Blog_InBlog_1920x1080_update.png](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_folder_media/5q/5Q5KZ0PHAXLR1731513590855.png)

**Leader Name:** Sylvanas Windrunner\
**Family:** Horde and Undead\
**Health:** 480\
**Gold Cost:** 6

##### Base Abilities

- **Black Arrow:** Baseline attack is ranged, elemental damage, that pierces through enemies in a straight line.
- **Haunt:** On death, Sylvanas summons a Banshee unit which operates just like a normal Banshee.

##### Leader Ability

- **Windrunner Warchief (Passive):** Minis near Sylvanas gain a movement speed boost.

##### Talents

![Black Arrow Talent Icon](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/G5789KO6TFDL1698458253560.png)

##### Queen's Reach

Black Arrow travels 20% farther.

![Confusion Talent Icon](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/47MGD9J7F2IE1698458253188.png)

##### Banshee's Wail

Scream on Death, Stunning nearby enemies for 3 seconds.

#### Split Leaders

Additional details below about split Leaders and how they will fit into Warcraft Rumble.

[PvE Content](#week-1)

- Completing a Heroic Mission with a split Leader will award Sigil progress and the Family Tome for both families.
- Completing an Arclight Surge will award the normal amount of Family Crests, but will be split 50/50 between the two families.
- Split Leaders can be chosen during either family dungeon week and theoretically offer another avenue to earn additional Coins and rewards each week.
- Dungeon Relics and other effects that impact specific families will apply to either/both of the split Leader's families.

[PvP Content](#week-2)

- Split Leaders do not affect PvP progress. What would award 200 Honor before still awards 200 Honor, and 100 Crests for both families.

[Other](#week-3)

- Split Leaders will show up on the G.R.I.D. during both families' dungeon weeks.
- Split Leader will appear in XP Tomes across both families.
- Split Leaders fultill Challenge requirements for both familes during events.

#### Shop and Bundle Update

We're trying out some new things with shop bundles, this includes changing up the contents and how they're displayed in the shop. We're working to set standard values for items and improve the consistency and value of items within the shop.

We've also added new items to Daily Offers and increased the appearance of others. The Tinker Gems & Rare O Matics will now appear more consistantly in Daily Offers.

The cost of the following items have been adjusted while we work towards improving consistency within the shop.

- Legendary Core - 4000
- Mythic Tome - 1100
- Mini + Mega XP - 590
- Leader + Mega XP - 620
