# Get Ready to lay Siege to Ironforge!

- URL: https://news.blizzard.com/en-gb/article/24145860/get-ready-to-lay-siege-to-ironforge
![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/da/DA0A8EAUL6PV1730477737287.png)

> Get ready to lay siege to the Alliance city of Ironforge! You and a guildmate can battle through Ironforge to earn unique rewards.

---

**Siege Bosses:** 3\
**Maximum Level:** 22\*\
**Minimum Level:**

- **Thief Catchers**: 17 - 18
- **Mekkatorque**: 18 - 19
- **Magni Bronzebeard**: 19 - 20

###### *\*Players above level 22 will be synced down.*

**The Thief Catchers**

They protect the Ironforge Vault from anyone trying to infiltrate the city and steal the ritches stored within. Don't get ambushed by this group of stealthy assassins.

![Mekka_Boss_Statue.png](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_folder_media/vr/VRAGGS7R4NR31730478107250.png)

**Mekkatorque**

Mekkatorque is a mechanical marvel of gnomish engineering. Penetrate his shields and avoid the Disinta-Ray to defeat this large and in charge gnome.

![Magni_Boss_Statue.png](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_folder_media/9t/9TRY418F3SNI1730478165675.png)

**Magni Bronzebeard**

Magni Bronzebeard, King of the Dwarves, awaits your arrival in the Ironforge throne room. Avoid his heavenly hammer attacks to claim victory and spoils.

#### Valor

Use Valor to upgrade a Gold Army Slot to Diamond. Diamond Army Slots will grant +4 levels to a Mini.

---

Wildcard Slot

The Wildcard Slot will affect the third slot of a Leader’s army and allows any Mini placed there to activate the trait bonus for additional levels.

###### Players will receive rewards for clearing the first time each week and can clear it two additional times each week to help fellow guildmates.
