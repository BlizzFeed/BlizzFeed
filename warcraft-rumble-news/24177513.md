# Hotfixes: February 20, 2025

- URL: https://news.blizzard.com/en-gb/article/24177513/hotfixes-february-20-2025
![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/oe/OEY7DZW8FIE91706649024511.png)

> Emotes can now be used in Siege and Raids! We've fixed issues with store purchases, the PC client, Druid of the Claw, Doomhammer and more!

---

#### Siege and Raids

- Emotes can now be used in Siege and Raids!

#### Bugfixes

- Player Honor & Rank now shows the correct amount instead of triple the amount in the guild member view and player profile.
- Fixed an issue that caused a crash when trying to join a guild.
- Daily Offers now display the Star Points they grant more clearly.
- Fixed several issues around tutorials not resolving correctly.
- Fixed several issues around store purchases not resolving correctly.
- The following buffs will now be kept when Druid of the Claw or Doomhammer transform:
  - Armor from Frostwolf Shaman's Earth Shield talent
  - Resistance from Faerie Dragon's Fae Blessing talent
  - Armor from Onu's Barkskin talent
  - The damage bonus from Onu's From the Trees talent
  - Healing from Druid of the Claw's Rejuvenation talent
  - Will be added in a future update: Movement Speed from Dryad's Nature's Swiftness talent

**PC Client**

- Fixed an issue causing the camera to zoom in on the wrong area at the end of a match.
- Fixed an issue where UI hotkeys (1-5) were not deactivated when typing in guild chat.
- Fixed several crashes, including when attempting to log in on multiple devices at once.
