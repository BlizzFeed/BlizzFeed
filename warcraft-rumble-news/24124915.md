> **[Updated August 15]**
>
> Dryad will not be available in the guild WarChest in Season 8.

Don't underestimate this nimble little acorn, Dryad is *Warcraft Rumble’s* newest Cenarion Troop. As one of the children of the forest, Dryad prefers peace but wont hesitate to use her agile ranged attack to poison and ensnare foes, keeping enemies at bay.

Dryad won't be available in the G.R.I.D until Season 9, but don't worry; you can add it to your collection of Minis through ~~the guild WarChest,~~ PvP or three Store offers—one at season start, one mid-season, and another at the end of the season.

#### G.R.I.D Overrides

G.R.I.D overrides are a new feature that give more control over the G.R.I.D, allowing Mini rarity and XP to be changed before purchase.

**Types of Overrides**

- Rare-O-Matic
- Tinker's Gem

The Rare-O-Matic or ROM for short can be placed in any open spot in the G.R.I.D and will override Mini quality in the same row or column, changing it to Rare. The Gold cost of the Mini will also change to match the new rarity.

A Tinker's Gem can be placed in any open spot in the G.R.I.D to grant additional bonus XP to Minis in the same row or column when purchased. This can be used in combination with the already existing XP bonuses found naturally in the G.R.I.D.

Overrides are consumed once a Mini is purchased from the G.R.I.D. Only one type of override can be used at a time.
