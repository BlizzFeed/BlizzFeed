# Faerie Dragon is Ready for Danger

- URL: https://news.blizzard.com/en-gb/article/24073123/faerie-dragon-is-ready-for-danger
![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/kb/KB1K01AQVYUI1711478055988.png)

> Faerie Dragon, Warcraft Rumble’s newest Troop, is stealthing her way into your heart and your enemy's Barracks in Season 5. She’s bringing with her two new emotes, as well as something dark and mysterious.

---

Are you friend or enemy? *Warcraft Rumble’s* newest Troop, Faerie Dragon, will be coming to Season 5 beginning on April 17 and will be earnable as a reward from the guild War Chest and PvP.

**Please Note: The Warcraft Rumble team is not responsible for any ouchies your Minis may incur from Faerie Dragon.**

Faerie Dragon won't be available in the G.R.I.D until Season 6, but don't worry; you can add her to your collection of Minis through the guild War Chest, PvP, or three Store offers—one at season start, one mid-season, and another at the end of the season.

![WR_Season5_Article_FaerieDragon_Turnaround_1920x1080_MV_V5.5_Holding2.png](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_folder_media/sj/SJAAR4ZON26G1711478115897.png)

*While Faerie Dragon is a curious creature, her stealing and holding the Cenarion icon above does not reflect in game actions or abilities.*

- **Name:** Faerie Dragon
- **Family:** Cenarion
- **Deploy Cost:** 3 Gold
- **Health:** 180
- **Damage:** 170
- **Traits:** Flying, Elemental, Resist, Nullify, Detect Invisibility

##### New Traits

- **Nullify**: Immune to Poison, Burn, Stun and slowing effects.
- **Detect Invisibility**: Can attack Stealth enemies.

##### Ability

- **Spit**: Ranged single target attack.

##### Talents

[![...](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_folder_media/no/NOGF30M3H43L1711479023770.png)](#)

##### Phase Shift

Teleport backwards when attacked.

[![...](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_folder_media/z3/Z3TNVVGHXVA61711479057727.png)](#)

##### Invisibility

Gain Stealth.

[![...](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_folder_media/dm/DM6DM3WTVY1Z1711479083043.png)](#)

##### Fae Blessing

Grant Resist to a nearby Ally. Ability has one charge.

Cenarion’s roots couldn’t keep this murderously adorable dragon from stealthing her way into your heart... and your enemy’s Barracks.

#### New Emotes

The new Gnomelia Thumbs Up and Pyromancer Rock On emotes are perfect for telling that monkey how much you like his banini gun. The Thumbs Up emote will be available in the Guild War Chest and the Rock On emote is earnable through PvP at 12,000 Honor.
