> Bring the rumble to reality by 3D printing your own Warcraft Rumble Minis.

---

Bring the rumble to reality by **3D printing** your own ***Warcraft Rumble*****™** **Minis**. Every week for the next five weeks, we’ll release **new *Warcraft Rumble* Mini 3D Blueprint files** for you to print, paint, and post online to share with your friends, share with your family, heck, get crazy, and share with your friend’s family!

Start printing your collection of 3D-printed *Warcraft Rumble* Minis today, beginning with **Jaina Proudmoore** and fellow members of the **Alliance**, available now!

[BRING ON THE CHAOS](https://warcraftrumble.blizzard.com/collection)

#### Post Your Minis!

And don’t forget to share your Minis—print, paint, and post your creative creations to social using the #WarcraftRumble hashtag so we can celebrate in joyful chaos with you!

Please keep printed Minis away from all Arclight Energy Sources after Midnight. Do not incinerate; this is not Teldrassil. May contain Murlocs, definitely contains Joyful Chaos.
