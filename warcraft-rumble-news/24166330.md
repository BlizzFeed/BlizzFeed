# Get Swole with Swole Troll

- URL: https://news.blizzard.com/en-gb/article/24166330/get-swole-with-swole-troll
![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/blog_thumbnail/bs/BSTUYUQU7LRF1736282230096.png)

> Spin to win with Swole Troll, Warcraft Rumble's latest Siege Specialist. Unlock Swole Troll, during the Winds of Change event, starting on January 22, 2024. This cyclonic Mini has one thing on his mind, destroying enemy towers.

---

Unlock the new Swole Troll Mini during the Winds of Change event starting on January 22, 2025. This cyclonic Mini has one thing on his mind, destroying enemy towers. Use his beefy arsenal to knock back, stun, push and destroy enemies.

Swole Troll will be available during the event from January 22, 2025, to February 4, 2025. During the 2 week event, you can unlock him as a new Troop -- earn additional copies and the items needed to upgrade this powerful new Mini.

---

![SwollTroll_Statue.png](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_folder_media/7w/7W6ZZXMGZNKU1736284359409.png)

- **Name:** Swole Troll
- **Role:** Troop
- **Family:** Horde
- **Deploy Cost:** 4 Gold
- **Health: 2900**
- **Damage:** 250
- **Traits:** Siege Specialist, Unstoppable, Tank

##### Attack

- **Push**: Pushes enemies out of the way to get to buildings more efficiently.

##### New Trait

- **Unstoppable**: Can not be affected by any movement impairing abilities.

##### Talents

[![](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_folder_media/iv/IVRV8501Z8SL1736284249381.png)](#)

##### Trollnado

Uses a spinning attack to knock nearby enemy Minis far away every **7** seconds.

[![...](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_folder_media/gx/GXODV4EO0BF51736284282783.png)](#)

##### Swole Train

Dashes to an enemy Mini to stun them along his path.

[![...](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_folder_media/qk/QKR7N7S6WU9A1736284318743.png)](#)

##### Meatier Elbow

Now targets enemy Minis with a large damaging AOE.
