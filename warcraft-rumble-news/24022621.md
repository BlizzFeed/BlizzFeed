Collect the new playable Leader, Sylvanas Windrunner, during Season 1 of Warcraft Rumble beginning 28 October from the Guild Warchest and through PvP as a reward.

Take note that this Leader won’t be available in the G.R.I.D until Season 2. Don't worry though, you’ll be able to add her to your collection of Leaders through the Guild Warchest, PvP, or two additional Store offers— one at the start of the season and another at the end of the season.

**Leader Name:** Sylvanas Windrunner\
**Family:** Undead\
**Gold Cost:** 6

### Leader Abilities

![From left to right, Bowshot, Haunt, and Leader Ability poses](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/HXS4VJL25VAO1698458255819.png)

### Base Abilities

- **Bowshot:** Baseline attack is a ranged, single target, physical damage.
- **Haunt:** On death, Sylvanas summons a Banshee unit which operates just like a normal Banshee.
- **Leader Ability:** Windrunner Warchief (Passive): Horde and Undead minis near Sylvanas gain a movement speed boost.

### Talents

![Black Arrow Talent Icon](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/G5789KO6TFDL1698458253560.png)

#### Black Arrow

Pierce through enemies in a line, dealing Elemental damage.

![Confusion Talent Icon](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/47MGD9J7F2IE1698458253188.png)

#### Banshee's Wail

Scream on Death, Stunning nearby enemies for 3 seconds.

[![Forsaken Fury Talent Icon](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/IPDBJPVFOL2L1698458253447.png)](#)

#### Forsaken Fury

Horde and Undead near Sylvanas gain Fury.
